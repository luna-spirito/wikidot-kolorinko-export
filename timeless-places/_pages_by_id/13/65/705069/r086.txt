> +++ Used by administrators for classifying articles, allowing them to be sorted into the correct modules.
@@@@

[[=]]
@@@@
[[tabview]]
[[tab Article Tags]]
> || Tag || Purpose ||
> || article || Goes on all articles. ||
> || space || Goes on Spaces. ||
> || anomaly || Goes on Anomalies. ||
> || summary || Goes on Summaries. ||
> || joke || Goes on Joke articles. ||
[[/tab]]
[[tab Site Tags]]
> || Tag || Purpose ||
> || site || Goes on all pages. ||
> || information || Goes on official information pages. ||
> || guide || Goes on guide pages. ||
> || policy || Goes on official site policy pages. ||
> || author-page || Goes on all author pages. ||
> || sandbox || Goes on all sandboxes. ||
> || admin|| Goes on all admin pages ||
[[/tab]]
[[tab Other Tags]]
> || Tag || Purpose ||
> || single-author || Articles written by one author. ||
> || co-authored || Articles written by multiple authors. ||
[[/tab]]
