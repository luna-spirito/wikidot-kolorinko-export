> +++ Thoroughly read through the following rules both before and after joining the site, there are no second chances.
@@@@
-----
[[=]]
@@@@
[[tabview]]
[[tab General]]
> +++ **__Minimum Age__**
> +++ • You must be equal to or over the age of 15 years old.
@@@@
> +++ **__Alt Accounts__**
> +++ • You cannot have more than one Wikidot account on the site.
@@@@
> +++ **__Harassment__**
> +++ • If you feel another user is harassing you, contact an administrator.
@@@@
> +++ **__Rating__**
> +++ • You cannot give a rating for reasons besides the content of the article.
> +++ • You cannot vote on an article that you have created.
> +++ • You cannot call for downvotes against a particular article or author.
@@@@
> +++ **__Respect__**
> +++ • You must treat other members of the site with respect, including yourself.
@@@@
> +++ **__Discriminatory/Unsavory Language__**
> +++ • There is a zero tolerance policy against slurs of any form.
@@@@
> +++ **__The Backrooms__**
> +++ • We are not affiliated or related to the Backrooms in any way, shape, or form.
[[/tab]]
[[tab Writing]]
@@@@
> +++ **__Crediting__**
> +++ • You must credit **all** media that you use.
@@@@
> +++ **__AI-Generated Content__**
> +++ • The use of AI-generated content for images and other similar is strictly prohibited.
@@@@
> +++ **__NSFW Content__**
> +++ • You cannot write NSFW content.
[[/tab]]
[[tab Sandbox]]
@@@@
> +++ **__Editing__**
> +++ • Only edit your own sandbox, unless someone has given you permission.
@@@@
> +++ **__Number Of Sandboxes__**
> +++ • You may only have **ONE** sandbox at any given time, additional sandboxes will be removed.
@@@@
> +++ **__Removal__**
> +++ • Banned / inactive members will have their sandboxes deleted.
[[/tab]]
[[/tabview]]
[[/=]]
