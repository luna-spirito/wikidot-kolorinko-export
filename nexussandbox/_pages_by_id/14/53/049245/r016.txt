**Anomaly Type:** Spatial

**Protocol:**
A-X is to be kept in Site 32, Sector 4, Containment room 87. The members of Squad Sven-99 are to be assigned a maximum of 5 researchers at a time to observe A-X and protect A-X and stop A-X from growing. 

While protecting A-X, all Sven-99 members are to be equipped with Tau Cannons.


[[image Black%20hole size="original"]] Closeup picture taken of A-X


Threat Level: 
Green

**Description:**
A-X is theorised to be a piece of common rock or oak somehow pressed so anomalously hard it is almost condensed a black hole, but not quite. Around approximately every 10 days A-X will attempt grow 1 metre approximately. A-X's colour is black. A-X is currently 6 inches, kept in this length and state to ensure safety and studies.

Unlike a regular black hole, A-X does not consume any matter when growing and rather eats the colour and texture of any object. This still poses a threat to humans as loss of colour and texture or being around objects affected by A-X causes hallucination, death and mostly insanity. There is no current known way of how to reverse this effect. Items affected are to be pronounced instances of A-X-2.

[[image colournone size="small"]] An A-X-2 instance.

**Discovery:**
A-X was found inside the walls of Sector 4 of Site 98 on January 28th, 2996. Personnel were evacuated by order of level 1 member The Eye. Sven-99 was the on-site squad that responded, which ended up landing them the place of A-X duty. They were given regular guns, which weren't as effective as a Tau Cannon, but they were able to capture A-X before the whole site lost colour.
