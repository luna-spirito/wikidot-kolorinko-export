Name: The Head Crabs

Appearance: The Head Crabs are slightly sentient crab/spider parasites usually spotted with 4 legs and 2 extra teeth in front of them. They always have a gaping hole located where their stomach is. Surrounding this hole is 10 eyes that let them line up to jump on prey. Their prey usually consists of anything of equal matter to themselves.

Government: They are known to have a leader named The Gonarch, who is a female 35.8 foot spider with 4 legs. The Gonarch has no Head Crab hole, instead having a large testicle. This testicle is used to very quickly reproduce more Head Crabs. There is one known in existence, but it is known that more can grow.

History/culture: Early in the life of the species, they were hunted down by other specis very much until they fled to their homeworld that they are known for.

NESA operations: The Gonarch is quite close to NESA. Head Crabs are sometimes used for testing.
