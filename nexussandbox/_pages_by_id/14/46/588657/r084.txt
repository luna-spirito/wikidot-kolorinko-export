**Anomaly Type: Dimensional**

**Level Status: 1**

**Classification Levels**

+ Protocols

+ Description

Anomaly-001 is a collection of documents depicting an phenomenon that resulted in the formation of the Omniveres, and in turn the Beta Universe. It is important to note that the 9 documents are only the major, more important, ones discovered and that there are actually a huge number of A-001 Files from all over the Omniveres that are made from the perspective of various organizations and multiverses.

It is theorized that NESA’s Nexus is both the glue and the barrier that keeps every Multiverse from ever colliding into each other. The 9 documents are in this File due to them explaining both the start of this phenomenon and how the Nexus was first created.

**Date: June, 1st, 2018**
[[div style="background-color: #D9D9D9; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

It was cool morning, Dr. Miler sips her coffee as she begins the day. She walks past a rainbow-y SCP logo which is being displayed on the wall, “pride month already” she mumbled. Sometimes you forget what day it is within the walls of Site-106.

Everyday with new minor anomalous item to study, catalog and then put in storage; no containment breaches, no groups of interest, nothing too eventful.

Meanwhile personnel on site brings in another crate of anomalous items, 34 for today. Dr. Julian takes a small lamp which appears to be on despite not being plugged into anything and Dr. Yong who gets things setup.

**Dr. Julian:** “This one looks interesting, so I say let’s start with this one.”

**Dr. Miler:** “Take out the lightbulb first.”

**Dr. Yong:** “Did you guys felt that earthquake last night?”

**Dr. Julian:** “I was in Orlando last night, also since when does earthquakes happen in Florida?”

**Dr. Miler:** “I thought that was a dream, did the news say what might have caused it?”

**Dr. Yong:** “No, all they had were theories.”

**Dr. Miler:** “Interesting.”

After several hours of them looking through the crate, an earthquake happens followed by a containment breach from the west side of the facility. All on site personnel were evacuated to a bunker as STF Rho-7 (“Greeting Party”) begin putting the Site on lockdown. 

While in the bunker, Dr. Miler starts checking security footage from an old laptop of hers; she starts the screen recorder to document the incident. Meanwhile MTF Xi-13 (“Sequere Nos”) is also 15 minutes.

She flips through the cam’s quickly before stopping at one, seeing an arm group; her immediate thought was that they must be the Chaos Insurgency breaking in, but she looks closer, to realize that this isn’t them.

The group was then approached by STF members to which they shouted “Identify yourself!?” The STF members were slightly stumped for a second but they ended up raising all their weapons.

“First Identify yourselves?” The lead STF asked.

“Why would we identify ourselves? Are you the ACEL?”

The STF looks at each other. “We’ve never heard of them, now tell us who you are or we will shoot.”

Eventually one of the sides fired and the group head for cover, bullets start echoing throughout Site-106. Dr. Miler then notices the camera footage from a different bunker. 

Two researchers are in a fight; one having normal Foundation clothing, and the other wearing something slightly different. But most important was a logo on the front pocket “ODRIN” Dr. Miler whispered to herself.

**Date: June, 3, 2018**
[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

At approximately 2:00 AM British Summer Time (BST), an anomalous phenomenon has begun to spread all over the world effecting around 500 million civilians as well as over 900 thousand anomalous individuals.

Below is a few examples which has been estimated to be the worst of all of them by property damage or casualties.

**Incident Report 10**

At approximately 2:00 AM, parts of London, England began to change and transformed; more specifically in the heart of London. The first change being the Cheese-grader building which had a mega tower take its place. However with both buildings sharing the same space, it became unstable and resulted in both structures collapsing.

Many other parts of London suffered the same and was made worse as the excessive wight ceased SCP-1678 to cave in, making many sinkholes across the city.

------

**Incident Report 23**

Portland Oregon, known for being the largest centralized place for the anomalous due to Three Portland’s, was effected as a group called the Wayward Society appeared and begun having a run in with the UIU. This was also followed by Cryptic Anomalous entities and phenomenon’s that have effected the area and endangered the public.

------

**Incident Report 309**

Hong Kong, Macao, Shenzhen and Zhongshan were all effected similarly to London but lead to large property damage as buildings got weakened by structural collapse and the urban domino effect.

There was also the appearance of the Asian Union which has locked Hong Kong down is completely hostile to the Chinese government and other military or organizations. Recapturing Hong Kong has proven difficult as the AU is far more advanced in terms of weaponry and defenses.

**Date: June, 6, 2018**
[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

After the incident at Site-106, Zoey Miller was transported to a Safe-house in Orlando Florida when word got out that the Global Occult Coalition is currently at war with another organization in Spain. Not only that but there’s apparently an excessive number anomalies appearing from out of nowhere.

Though the GOC aren’t the only ones with a war going on, as large alien Tripods have been spotted in Orlando’s suburbs and the Shark Punching Centre is in an active engagement with them.

Miller honestly didn’t want to think about it too much since there’s really nothing she could do but to wait for MTF to be at their location. Eventually helicopters can be heard landing just outside.

Everyone in the building was instructed to but Miller was told to stay put, this confuses her but she didn’t question it. She was then lead to a large room in the upper floors.

**Zoey Miller:** “Why am I staying?”

**MTF:** “O5-1 wants to speak with you.”

**Zoey Miller:** “O5-1? This has to be a mistake, I…”

They shut the door standing outside of it on guard. Miller walks through room to a patio over looking a lake, she notices a man over looking the destruction cause by the entities.

**O5-1:** “Hello Sophia Park.”

**Zoey Miller:** “That’s not my name anymore.”

**O5-1:** “I know.”

**Zoey Miller:** “So why call me by my old name?”

**O5-1:** “Because you change your name the moment you join the Foundation. Your an interesting case.”

**Zoey Miller:** “I’m not that interesting of a person. I only changed my name because my initials were S,C,P; which would be weird.”

**O5-1:** “Lets for a moment assume that the whole name thing was a coincidence, how do you explain the fact that you didn’t exist before joining our organization.”

**Zoey Miller:** “What do you mean? I had a childhood and relatives, a whole life, how can any of that not exist?”

**O5-1:** “False memories are a thing, and that’s not the only anomalous thing about you.”

**Zoey Miller:** “I’m sorry, but I’m going to need some proof.”

**Anomaly Type: Dimensional & Reality**

**Protocol**

A-001 is not to be located, it must remain lost in the omniverse. Any attempt at locating A-001 will be disciplined accordingly.

**Description**

A-001 is a very unstable prisms that can rip entire --universes-- multiverses creating realities of chaos and everlasting destruction.

[[collapsible show="List of Universes affected by A-001" hide=" List of Universes affected by A-001 "]]

(All documents about --universes-- multiverses that have been affected had been wiped off the database, this is to keep the location of A-001 a secret from infiltrators like the Arachnids and stop our own personnel of looking for it.)

(Anyone reading this page in an attempt to locate A-001 will be terminated, no exception.)

[[/collapsible]]

**Discovery**

A-001 was discovered by the SCP Foundation in universe 1 **(the original)** as [[[*https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-048 | SCP-048]]], SCP-048 was a prism with the ability to affect its surroundings depending on what color light is going through it.

**White**

Ceases the room that it’s in to expand or contract.

**Red**

Ceases the temperature to rise Drastically depending on time and intensity.

**Orange**

Orange light can cease all living organisms to decay rapidly.

**Yellow**

Yellow the most dangerous as every time yellow light was used, it resulted in very extreme incidents as SCP-048 has ceased some psychological issues. Personnel affected would commit self harm, robberies, murder, arguments, arson and etc.

**Green**

all living organisms will evolve at a slow rate but will pickup speed as time goes on.

**Blue**

Water droplets will manifest in the room without a source, this is also possible in very dry rooms where condensation should be impossible.

**Purple**

Ice begins to form and spread, if the light doesn’t turn off then ice continue to spread until something turns it off.
