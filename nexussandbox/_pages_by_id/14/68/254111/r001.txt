**Anomaly Type: Technological**

**Classification Levels**
BI Level

**Protocol**



**Description**

Anomaly-033 is a patch that can be placed anywhere on the skin as long as it has enough nerves to connect to the brain, this device developed by the Scaly™ Corporation has the ability to send the user's conciseness to the cloud, as well as connecting them directly to GalaxyChat™. Scaly™ does not sell these nor give them to their lower ranked employees, A-033 seems to only be reserved to those involved with the more anomalous side of the company.
