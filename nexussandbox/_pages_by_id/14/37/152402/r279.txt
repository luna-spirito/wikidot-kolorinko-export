Anomaly-099

**Anomaly Type: Technological**

**Protocol**

A perimeter has been set up on the lower level’s of Northern Calgary, Alberta. Due to the size and location of A-099, transport and maximum containment is impossible.

SSD Beta-1 () is to monitor the area and keep civilians and unauthorized personnel out of Site-099-B.

**Description**

Anomaly-099 is the anomalous location hidden at the bottom levels of Northern Calgary, the area also known as Nose Hill Park seems to have an entire facility hidden under large rocks and dirt, A-099 also has yet another layer of metal with regenerative properties which protects the facility from the outside.

It’s entrance which is a large elevator shaft is located on the southeastern sector where Site-099-B is at, 

**Discovery**

A-099 was discovered in 2090 after the city made plans to start building in the area, the elevator shaft was unearthed at the beginning of construction stopping everything. The Canadian government was about to investigate the situation but members of the Temporal Research Division managed to cover up the situation and distributed MWA’s to everyone involved.

Seeing this as a threat to secrecy, the TmRD decided to build a temporary site until NESA fully comes into existence, as the city grew TmRD proposed and forced the Canadian government into building the largest indoor botanical garden and built it over A-099.

This makes it easier to keep the location from being unearthed by the public again and also allowed SSD Beta-1 () to monitor the area easily.

**Entrance**

At the bottom of the elevator shaft, we were met with a metallic door about 23 feet in height and 78 feet in length. The doors opened for us automatically without intervention, suggesting that some type of AI is running the place.

Upon entering A-099, there’s a security gate which we had to go through, though it was abandoned and we gotten through pretty quickly, no entities of any kind of seen.

**The Map**

Past security was a holographic map which appears to be heavily damaged so the majority of the map was missing. According to the map there were thousands of levels of A-099 possibly towards the core of the earth but we couldn’t find any elevator’s or staircases that would take us lower, so we took the hallway ahead and continued.

**The Hallway**

Entering the hallway we’ve discovered living quarters, a cafeteria, databases[[footnote]]Which were all empty or damaged.[[/footnote]], storage and dozens of other rooms that were unaccessible. We’ve noticed that the lights in the hallway were following us, doing it’s best to light our paths despite the damage, some of the lights would flicker only to burn out completely.

The door at the end were broken through using a laser, it took about 10 minutes before we got in, the doors themselves were about 19 inches thick.

**The Main Room**

At first we thought it was the control room but it’s not, we didn’t know what it was, a screen on one of the walls turns on showing a name, “The Department of Abnormalities” which is a group that exists in Multiverse 44.
