+ FASA Official Code of Conduct
++ General Rules:
* Do not post illegal, pornographic, or blatantly over-the-top content. **This will result in an instant ban with no attempt for appeal.**
* Raiding the SCP, RPC, AEP, or other Anomalous Writing Community is against the rules.
* Do not brigade articles or call for down-votes.
* Do not advertise other sites or products.
* Be civil, respectful, and courteous to all members of the community.
* Do not engage in targeted harassment.
* Do not vandalize, delete, or sabotage articles.
* Do not spam.
* Do not complain about politics.
* Do not role-play.
* Do not force your ideologies on others. This includes (but is not limited to) governmental and identical politics, lifestyle choices, and pseudo-controversial topics and concepts.
* Do not impersonate any member of staff.
* You must be 13 years or older to join.
++ Forum Conduct
* Keep threads on topic and relevant.
* Post threads in their proper forum page. (Ex. Article drafts in the Drafts forum page)
++ Writing Conduct
* Do not coldpost.[[footnote]] Coldposting is a term used to describe the act of posting an article directly into the main series without posting your draft to the draft forum or in the FASA Sandbox. To prevent coldposting, make sure to run your article past fellow community members and members of staff.[[/footnote]] If you are transferring from another site with permission from staff then it isn't considered coldposting. For your draft to be accepted into the main series, you must have it reviewed and approved by at least two people on the forum or discord. Do not attempt to use alts to fake approval, this will result in a permanent ban.
* Self-inserts are not allowed.
* Do not post low-effort or intentionally low-quality content.
* Do not post your fan-fiction. This is an anomalous writing group.
* Do not post Joke Anomalies into the main series unless they have been adapted to be serious.

//The rules listed in this code of conduct are subject to change at any time.//
