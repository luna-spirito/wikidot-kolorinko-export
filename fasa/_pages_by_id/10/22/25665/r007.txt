+ Join Our Site
[[/div]]
[[div id="account-rules-heading" style="text-align:center;"]]
++ Account Rules:
[[/div]]
* Your Wikidot account is your wikidot account. Don't share your wikidot account with anyone else as you will be held responsible for the actions taken on your wikidot account.
* You are only allowed to have one Wikidot account on our site. Any alternate accounts will be removed.
[[div id="roleplaying" style="text-align:center;"]]
++ Roleplaying:
[[/div]]
* We are not a roleplaying site. Don't pretend to be a fictional character, and don't pretend to be a member of the Agency.
[[div id="age-heading" style="text-align:center;"]]
++ Age:
[[/div]]
* This site contains mature themes, discussions, and content. We expect all members of this community to act in a mature and adult-like fashion. The age requirement for this community is 14 years of age.
[[div id="applying-heading" style="text-align:center;"]]
++ Applying:
[[/div]]
* Please write the Site Passcode in the application below, then submit the application. If you still don't know the Site Passcode, then read the [[[Guide for Newbies]]] linked in the Introduction Section, and you will find instructions letting you know what you need to do.
* Your application must also contain at least one single grammatically correct sentence explaining why you would like to write with us. If your application does not contain this introductory sentence and Site Passcode, it will be automatically denied.

[[=]]
**By submitting an application below, you're agreeing to follow the [http://fasa.wikidot.com/site-rules rules of this site]. Please give us time to review and accept your application.**
[[/=]]
