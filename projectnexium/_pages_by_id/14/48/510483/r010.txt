**//Introduction//**
[[/<]]

------

So, you want to be a member of the Project Nexium Wiki. Before you do that, there are a few things we expect all users of the site to know, and the process to join is a little less intuitive than you'd initially expect. This guide will hopefully make both aspects as clear as can be.

[[/<]]
------
++ //**Everything on the [[[site-rules|Site Rules]]]**//

There's a good number of rules that site members are expected to follow when interacting with the wiki, and we'll outline some of the more important rules here:

* **Don't Be A Dick.** This is Rule Zero of the Wiki. Don't act like a bastard towards your fellow members. This includes but not limited to - Xenophobia, Transphobia, Homophobia, harassment, personal targeting, racism, etc.

* **Don't Vandalise.** This is Rule Five of the Wiki, which means, you should not edit or arbitrarily change pages without permission from the original author via their preferred form of contact. You can, however, change minor spelling mistakes, e.g. "Cardy" to "Candy"  or "Chooclate" to "Chocolate" is fine.

* **Don't Roleplay.** Not on-site or your Wikidot profile at least. Members are expected not to behave like they live in the Project Nexium Universe.

**//Staff Procedures://**

------

The rules of this wiki site and the procedures that govern it are made to help the wiki run smoothly and make the user's experience as comfortable as it can be, so it would only make sense for the users to be able to see what happens and have a say in the proceedings.

All non-sensitive staff actions, along with any policy discussions, rule changes, etc, are logged on the [http://x9command.wikidot.com X9 Command] wiki, which is separate from the Sotheby's Auction Wiki. On here, you can see most things related to staff's work, including the individual teams' composition, recent changes to the site, internal discussions, and so on. While normal site members cannot directly comment on X9 Command proceedings, we should have the [http://projectnexium.wikidot.com/forum/c-8131449/staff-policy-discussions Staff Policy Discussion] forum category, where all users can comment on said policy discussions as they occur.

Additionally, once you've been active for a while and feel like you can help contribute to the site's functioning, consider signing up to one of the semi-persistent staff recruitment threads on the site's forum categories via threads.

**Wikidot is a platform**

Project Nexium is hosted on a platform called Wikidot, which houses a number of other wikis: [https://blackbeltwiki.com A Martial Arts Wiki],  [http://yuzuki.wikidot.com An Author's Fan Wikia], [http://community.wikidot.com The Wikidot Community Site], and [https://scpwiki.com Another creative writing project detailing the containment of its weird objects] for some reason... Within those stands this Wiki as well, so to join, you'll have to create a [http://www.wikidot.com Wikidot Account].

Membership to the Project Nexium Wiki doesn't automatically include membership to any of the wiki's sub-platforms, such as the [http://projectnexium-sandbox Project Nexium Sandbox] (a place to post Works-in-Progress) and the [https://discord.gg/uWk5dQU9q9 Discord Server] - self-explanatory, since they are technically separate locations. Joining the sandbox is explained on the sandbox proper. Any additional platforms, like Reddit, will also have their sign-up process.

**Creating An Account**

------

On the top-right corner of your screen, you should see a sign-up/log-in button. If not, [https://www.wikidot.com/ go here]. Sign up to the platform[[footnote]]. Finish reading the guide first. It has notes on how to sign up for the wiki further down. [[/footnote]], then apply for membership to this wiki by following the instructions in the next tab, titled "Submit Application". You'll see a small section where you can input text, where you're expected to include the sentence, "I acknowledge that AI-generated content is not permitted on the Project Nexium wiki; I will respect community members and follow site rules." Input that sentence, **nothing else**. Please be aware that it may take 2-3 days or more, depending on the discretion or availability of Site Staff.

Please Note:

The following bugs have not yet been fixed due to Wikidot not receiving an update in over a year, so heed with caution that your confirmation email might not come through:

* **Do not use any email address with numbers in it or any Hotmail address.** This is due to the fact that the site on which the Wiki was built maintains a bug that delays and/or stops confirmation emails from going through to the recipient (In other words, the confirmation email was never delivered).

* The 'Facebook Integration' offered by Wikidot is still unfortunately broken until somebody new buys the site. This essentially means that account registration via Facebook is non-negotiable. We strongly advise against its use as it could lead to issues when recovering and/or using your account.

**Username Policy**
------

The Project Nexium dictates a username policy as follows:

* No names with profanities, slurs, or connotations. The final decision on what constitutes offensive is made at the discretion of the Site Staff.

* No names implying official positions (e.g. **Staff, Admin, Administrator, Mod, Moderator, Master Administrator**, etc.)

* No names implying specific/unique names/affiliations within the Project Nexium Universe, and other fictional groups/universes that exist on our Wiki site. In addition, no names that have been based on pre-Project Nexium characters, from works found on our Wiki.

* For example, names that contain the use of existing characters would not be permissible. For a summary of existing characters, please see the Characters tab of the [[[Technical Guide Hub]]]. **(Note: Authors who are responsible for the creation of said characters are exempt.)**

* No names that impersonate or reference public figures are allowed. For example, "DrBarackObama" isn't allowed, but "DrBarack" is.

* No names that support or suggest Political movements. "AntiFascistAction" isn't allowed, but "AntiNarwhalAction" is.

The username policy is enforceable both for potential members and existing members of the Sotheby's Auction Wiki; enforcement for existing members should exist on a day-to-day basis, at such a time when an existing user with a non-permissible username posts to a page, or comments on a discussion thread.

------
**Account Requirements**
------

Keep in mind the following rules when creating your Wikidot account and applying to the Project Nexium Wiki:

* **Your username must follow our policy as listed above**

* If you've already created your account, to change your name, go [http://www.wikidot.com here], and click on Edit next to your username. You can only **change your account's username twice**, so be careful.

* **You must be at least 18.** This website contains mature themes and discussions, and we expect members to behave in a formal, coherent, and adult-like manner. All members are expected to be at least 16 years old and to have reached the age of adolescence within the nations in which they live. **There are no exceptions to this rule, ever.**

* **You must/may only have one account per person, and one person per account.** 

* Please note that membership in various sites that have had adversarial relationships with our own is cause for an application to be declined.
