++ LOG Template
The first thing is the template to use when writing. It is as follows:
[[code]]
[[>]]
[[module Rate]]
[[/>]]

**File:** LOG-XXXX

**Level:** [Level 1: Helpful, Level 2: Cooperative, Level 3: Neutral, Level 4: Uncooperative, Level 5: Dangerous]

**Category:** [what is the subject of the article about (sentient/place/object/entity/creature)]

**Observations:** [Description of observations in the field]

**Nexium Agent:** [If applicable, the researcher associated or most commonly working with the log if it is an entity or creature]

**Recommendations:** [ Optional Provide any recommendations for additional actions or improvements based 
on the file, such as revised procedures, training requirements, or equipment upgrades.]

**Attachments:** [If applicable, include any supporting documents, data logs, images, or other relevant materials 
that provide additional context or evidence related to the file.]
