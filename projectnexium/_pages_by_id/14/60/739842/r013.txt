The Guide Hub is the centralized location for all of the official guides an author might need to navigate and contribute to the site, as well as the rules that all members of the Project Nexium Wiki Community agree to follow.

If you have an idea for an informational page that you'd like to see added to this page, please [[[http://ProjectNexium.wikidot.com/contact-staff | contact a staff member]]]. The “[http://ProjectNexium.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/guide guide]” navigational tag should only be assigned by staff, or with staff permission.

+ Required Reading

These pages have information on the foremost basics of the site and its community.

+++ //For all community members://
* **[[[Site Rules]]]**: You will be expected to know and understand the rules of the wiki. Being ignorant of a rule does not excuse you if you break it.
* **[[[guide-for-newcomers|Guide for Newcomers]]]**: An introduction to the world of the Log and the wiki as a whole, with optional sections on how to find reading material or create an account.
* **[[[Discord Guide]]]**: The guide to the Project Nexium official Discord. Covers registering, connecting, bannable offenses, chat staff, discussion suggestions, and secondary chatrooms.
* **[[[Stoat Guide]]]**: The guide to the Project Nexium official Stoat. Stoat is a alterative to Discord for those who don't like the age verification that Discord uses, or other upcoming slop updates.
* **[[[anti-harassment-policy | Official Anti-Harassment Policy]]]**: Code of conduct applying to all Project Nexium Wiki Community spaces.
* **[[[FAQ]]]**: Frequently asked questions and answers about the Project Nexium universe premise, how the site/wiki works, and how to interact with the community.


+++ //For all site authors://
* **[http://projectnexium.wikidot.com/greenlights The Greenlight Policy]**:  An explanation of the Greenlight forum policy. Read this if you want to get an idea or draft critique, don't know what the Greenlight policy is, or want to get Greenlights.
