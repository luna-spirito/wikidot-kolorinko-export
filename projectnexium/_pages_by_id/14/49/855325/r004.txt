**Description:**  **Hobgoblin**, also known as the "Common Goblin", or simply the "Goblin" refers to a species of bipedal creatures of mammalian descent. They are among the most numerous and successful sentient species in the realm of Nexium, being found in numerous regions of the world. However, the hobgoblins are most numerous in the arid region of the Hob Desert, from which they get the prefix hob-. The suffix -goblin comes from the corruption of the word "Gobelan" which is the native language of the hobgoblins. Alongside Gobelan hobgoblins are often fluent speakers of Common and Elvan.

**//Sub-Description-1: Characteristics and Biology//**

Hobgoblins are bipedal mammals noted for their great height, which can go as tall as 9' for fully grown individuals, though rare sitings of hobgoblins as tall as 13' have been recorded before. Their pectoral and dorsal regions are of standard humanoid build. However, hobgoblins have noticeably elongated limbs with particular emphasis being placed on their arms, palms, digits, femurs, and knees. Hobgoblins have five digits on their hands and four toes on their feet, with the former being 5'' and the latter 1'' in length. The hobgoblin has an average arm span of 8' and 5'' with some going as far as 10'. The hobgoblin's cranial region is quite large compared to the rest of the body, having a cranium-to-body ratio of 1:4. A hobgoblin's teeth are noted to be quite sharp and serrated, with a frontal fang being on average 3' in length, they are also polyphyodonts with the average tooth lasting approximately 3 solar weeks at most. 

Hobgoblins have standard-sized ears in proportion to their bodies and are quite standard from the ears of other species except for a pointed lobe that provides them more exact hearing, this lobe becomes more pronounced as a hobgoblin ages. Hobgoblins have a muscular system considered beyond president for a sentient species, one of the few species known to have no known measurement in maximum strength. This combined with their stamina, which is considered average among most humanoid species, means that they have extraordinary strength for a creature of their size, even among juveniles.

Hobgoblin skin tones vary greatly, ranging from turquoise, cyan, indigo, and periwinkle. Notably, a female hobgoblin will start out green when young before becoming more cyan-colored when they age, and the opposite is true for males. Hobgoblins are notable for having blood-red scleras and jet-black irises, but their pupils can come in various shades or oranges, browns, greens, and blacks. The hair of the Hobgoblin is noticeably thick and matted, with both genders commonly gaining long baggy locks or varying shades of white, reds, and yellows. During the teen years, a hobgoblin will grow a pair of small beige keratin growths, often compared in shape to a cat's ears, which later grow in spirals when entering adulthood. The males of the species have darker, waxy horns while the females have smooth bright colored horns. 

Hobgoblins are near-completely carnivorous, having a diet made up of various meats, marrows, and bones. Their stomachs are unable to process starch properly so they eat various types of fungi rather than plants to gain their daily carbohydrate allotment. Their stomachs have adapted to eating the spores of various fungi considered highly toxic to other species. Hobgoblins give live birth and can eat solid foods only hours after being born. They reach sexual maturity at an average of 25 years old and with the right conditions can live up to 400 years.

**//Sub-Description-2: Society and Culture//**
Hobgoblins are often taught since their birth to be simultaneously self-sufficient, contribute to the local community, and be efficient in their actions. This mindset is a necessity when living in the harsh wilderness of the Hob Desert, where resources are often scarce and prone to being stolen by scavengers. Hobgoblin households are noted for their quite compact nature, usually only providing chambers for sleeping, cooking, and general hygiene. Hobgoblin households also have noticeably thick walls and a general cuboid structure, giving their households the nickname "Squareshells". Houses are often clumped together against each other, with new houses being built over older ones out of whatever raw materials the builder may find near their general area, creating towering structures that dot the desert these beings call home. These structures tend to hold many smaller spires, each representing different Hobgoblin families splitting off and forming their own households, creating spire-filled cities that are considered a marvel of structural engineering. The largest of these cities, called Garen'ken, acts as the unofficial capital for the Hobgoblin race, being among the largest and oldest Hobgoblin settlements. Hobgoblin cities rule in an elective monarchy, with a king or queen being elected for life via a democratic vote, however, once they die their successor is determined via another democratic vote. Hobgoblins are monogamous, with tight-knit family groups often representing different political alignments in Hobgoblin society. 67% of all Hobgoblins live in the Hob Desert, with 78% of that previous total being found in the city of Garen'ken. The remaining population of Hobgoblins are found dotted in small settlements of other regions of the world, where their great strength and great work ethic make them popular as contractors.

Hobgoblins often look down upon those who don't contribute to the society they are part of, these deviants are often unwelcome in general in Hobgoblin society and often attempt to strike out on their own. These deviants are often linked to criminal activities such as looting, smuggling, and assassination and often give the others a bad reputation of being mindless brutes. However many Hobgoblins strive to escape these stereotypes and so many have found success in fields such as research, finance, and rescue. Hobgoblins are overall neutral towards the Nexium Archival Organization. While they aren't commonly allied with the NAO, they are usually on good terms with the organization. Hobgoblins are mostly found working in freelance organizations performing jobs for the NAO, often revolving around the transportation and relocation of various artifacts. The most notable of these organizations is the Garen'ken Transportation Conglomerate, which is the NAO's preferred method of transporting items across the harsh Hob Desert.
