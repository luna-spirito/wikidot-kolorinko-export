**File:** LOG-379

**Object Class:** Irregular 

**Observations:** LOG-379 has been observed to prefer humid and wet climates to the dry and cold climate of Site-419. It has also been noted that the anomaly should be allowed a lake to drink from. It is most  comfortable within a forestry environment. 

**Description:** LOG-379 is known to be a humanoid male with a slimy and swampish skin color. It often is able to trade wit with strength and speed making it a common predator to K-Class individuals.
