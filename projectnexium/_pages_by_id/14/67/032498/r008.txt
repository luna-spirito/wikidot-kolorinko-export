++ ##white|Introduction##

If you want to create your own PNX, you'll need to get it greenlighted; if you don't, your file will be removed from Wikidot, and you will receive a message from staff.
A greenlight is what allows you to post your file for everyone to see and to get yourself greenlights. Read the instructions below.

++ ##white| Ideas Critique Forum##

Once you have an idea of what you want to make your file about, you can go to the channel over in Discord, or stoat. There, you can make a forum asking for some people to critique your idea. Once your idea has been critiqued, you move on to the next step.

++ ##white|Drafts Critique Forum##

Once you have a good idea that has been critiqued, you can begin your draft over in the [http://projectnexium-sandbox.wikidot.com/ Sandbox]. Once you have made your draft, you then make a forum in the Draft critique channel to get critique there. If your draft has been critiqued and the staff has greenlighted you, you can move on to the next step.

++ ##white|Posting##

Very easy step here, just go to the [http://projectnexium.wikidot.com/projectnexium-chronicle Chronicles] to find your file number, click it, make a page, then copy what you have from the sandbox over, and finally click Save. You have now posted your file. :)

++ ##white|Death Rating##

If your log got past all the Greenlights, but is still disliked by the majority, your log will be removed, and you will be messaged about the problem. The Death rating is when your file is rated -10 or below.
