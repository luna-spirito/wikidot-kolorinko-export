++++ What is this site?
[[/<]]

SRDDF is a creative writing project hosted on the Wikidot platform, structured as a fictional research archive documenting anomalies, alternate dimensions, and speculative phenomena.

At its core, SRDDF exists because of its contributors. Writers who construct detailed case files, and readers who explore, analyze, and expand the universe through discussion and collaboration. The world of SRDDF grows through shared imagination and disciplined creativity.

The project evolves steadily, shaped by its community. Members are encouraged to write reports, develop narratives, exchange feedback, and participate in worldbuilding activities. All while respecting established guidelines to maintain coherence and quality.

SRDDF welcomes diverse storytelling styles: scientific documentation, psychological horror, political alternate history, metaphysical speculation, and experimental formats. Innovation is encouraged. Creativity is valued. The strength of the archive lies in the uniqueness of each contributor’s vision.

[[<]]
++++ What Are We ?
[[/<]]

SRDDF is set in a world with a completely different historical trajectory from the real life universe. In this reality, the Soviet Union and the Eastern Bloc never collapsed, with the exception of East Germany. The Ottoman Empire also did not dissolve and managed to retain its Islamic territories. This alternate history makes the SRDDF world fundamentally distinct from real life, shaped by a tripolar balance of power between the United States, the Soviet Union, and the Ottoman Empire.

SRDDF, which stands for Secret Research Discovery Development Facility, is a research organization established to protect humanity and to push human knowledge toward the understanding of supernatural and anomalous phenomena within the unstable SRDDF universe. In this world, DD, short for Discovered Deviation, is the designation used to classify anomalous entities and phenomena.

SRDDF was founded on July 21, 1945. Shortly after its establishment, it was forced to confront the immensely powerful entity known as DD-001. At that time, the organization possessed only basic military equipment and nuclear weapons, and it was initially overwhelmed by DD-001’s immense power. However, with the assistance of allied entities, DD-001 was ultimately destroyed, laying the foundation for the long term development of SRDDF.

The Supreme Leader, the highest authority within the organization’s hierarchy, later introduced an ideology known as SRDDF Expansionism. This doctrine asserts that if humanity confines itself within a cage of fear and refuses to harness the power of anomalies, it will forever remain powerless before them. Therefore, SRDDF must continuously expand its influence in order to become a force equal to the anomalies it seeks to confront.

After many decades and four generations of Supreme Leaders, SRDDF has evolved far beyond a mere containment organization. It has become a vast empire that controls powers capable of standing on equal footing with anomalous entities. The organization has constructed a colossal stronghold housing supreme supernatural structures that reinforce its authority. This dimension is known as the Great SRDDF.
