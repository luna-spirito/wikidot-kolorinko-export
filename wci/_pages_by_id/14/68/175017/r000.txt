Welcome to Project Nexium, Researcher.

Today marks your first step into a world far larger—and far stranger—than anything you’ve ever known. Here at Project Nexium, we dedicate ourselves to researching, documenting, and containing the phenomena that emerge from the Nexium Realm through dimensional rifts. Understand this: it’s not a question of if an incursion will happen—it’s a question of when.

You’ve been selected because you are among the best at what you do. Your skills, discipline, and courage are now indispensable tools in humanity’s effort to understand and survive the unknown.

The Nexium Realm is not merely another dimension. It is a vast, shifting plane that exists below and beyond our own, entirely separate from our reality. As its name implies, it is a convergence point—a nexus where all possibilities intersect, collide, and take shape. It is chaos incarnate, and yet it obeys patterns we are only beginning to decipher.

Our records indicate that before time, before matter, before even the concept of nothingness, there was Nexium—and in it, monsters older than imagination and chaos older than creation itself. What we face is not new. It is ancient.

We welcome you to the front line of this effort. You are now a part of something larger than any one person—larger than history itself. We don’t just study the impossible; we protect the world from it.

Prepare yourself, Researcher. The rifts are unpredictable. The creatures and phenomena on the other side are beyond comprehension. But you’re here because you have the potential to face them—and to win.

Good luck, and stay sharp
[[/div]]

--The Messenger
