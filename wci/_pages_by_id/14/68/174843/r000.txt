[[size 130%]]**Castopia**[[/size]] is a fictional collective literature universe inspired by SCP Foundation and The Backrooms. The website focuses on practical summoning and communication with the otherworld through rituals. The events of the universe take place in the present day, but with a touch of mysticism - many of rituals in our world works by own rules.
----
The website (or database) was created by netstalkers, who found an old forum, **LiberDemonologis**, in 2016. This forum was created in early 1998 in Japan (according to one version, the website belonged to a religious group TibaSouth, whose members were arrested by the Chiba Prefectural Police in 2005 on suspicion of planning a terrorist attack). It is still unclear how the site was accessed by external users, and whether this was originally intended. **LiberDemonologis** did indeed publish instructions for conducting magical rituals, often including rather gruesome and sometimes deadly elements. For unknown reasons, the website's users disappeared after unknown incident, and the domain was soon deleted.
----
[[size 130%]]**Our time.**[[/size]] The site's encyclopedia has expanded. Early **Castopia**, which began with the investigation of **LiberDemonologis** content, gradually took on a new form and faced hidden challenges: New sources previously unknown on well-known forums on the World Wide Web; frightening Rituals in both their essence and execution; a unique system of Exorcism; released Documentation, and much more that called into question the safety of humanity. A new era of the occult had arrived and you can become part of this era.
