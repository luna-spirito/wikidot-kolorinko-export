++++* Wikidot Cooperation Initiative (WCI) 
+* [http://wci.wikidot.com/about-us/ About Us]
The Wikidot Cooperation Initiative (WCI) is a collaborative project made to help smaller Wikidot communities connect and support one another. It supports a shared space for sharing ideas, creative concepts, and technical resources such as CSS and site development help. WCI also helps members find and engage with other wikis they may not have encountered otherwise, making it easier to explore new projects, collaborate across communities, and strengthen the long-term health of participating sites.
