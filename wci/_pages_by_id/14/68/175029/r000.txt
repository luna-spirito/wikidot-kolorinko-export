Good evening **%%title%%**, and welcome to your introduction to the Nexus Exploratory Space Administration or NESA for short.
[[/module]]

NESA exist only to learn and keep learning about our place in the omniverse, our motto which is to **Explore, Analyze, Document and Preserve** are the driving factors of this organization.

Here you will learn firstly about the in’s, the out’s and beyond’s of NESA, specifically the many division’s, indexes, levels and anomalous groups.

------
**Explore**

Explore which is to find the edge of the Omniverse, as far as we’re aware of we haven’t found it yet.

But when we Explore we in turn discovered more about our place in the Omniveres, so we Document our findings in the Files Archives.

------
**Analyze**

Every now and then we come across Anomalies, these Anomalies can differ very widely so we have 7 Divisions for each type of Anomaly in order to learn of how they work, their usefulness or how dangerous it is.

Sometimes Level 9 personnel are needed to run test in order to learn more about the Anomalies without needing to put other personnel in danger.

------
**Document**

Level 4 personnel who are in charge of a team to Analyze a certain Anomaly will then Document it into the Anomalous Archives, this is so that we can keep information on our research and a historical record of our Omnivers.

------
**Preserve**

The other part of our operations are the Specialized Security Detachment or SSD’s, these guys are the ones that help in containment of Anomalies and Preserving life, with over a hundred different types of SSD’s they are prepared for everything.

------

[[size xx-large]]Out of Universe[[/size]]

NESA is a collaborative wiki of fictional works in the Confic and Sy-fi genres, NESA is what you’ll expect the SCP Foundation to look like in the far future.

That said this wiki is also inspired by the SCP Foundation as well as the RPC Authority, and of course hundreds of others which you’ll find in the [[[http://nesa-wiki.wikidot.com/dimensional-research-division | Dimensional Research Division]]].

The DRD serves 2 purpose’s, 1 being that it allows the possibility of cross over entries between NESA and other wikis and 2 that it is important to organize the “Omniverse” into a comprehensive list of fictional wikis in order to keep track of everything.

Of course I’m not into making complete rip-offs but there are wikis that are quite unique, that took what SCP have and made it their own.

NESA, the Wonderer’s Library, the Backrooms, Void Observation Group are examples of the SCP concept with a twist.

So if you’re here because you’re interested in contributing to the Future of confic then head to the rules if you haven’t already and then join the site.
