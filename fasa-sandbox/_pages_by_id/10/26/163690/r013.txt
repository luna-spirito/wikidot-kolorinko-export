[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Enclosure Protocols:**[[/span]]

Instances of DA/1E are to be kept and bred at Site-081, which is located approximately 2.61 kilometers below the Grand Canyon. Each instance is to be fed 100 grams of heavily salted raw meat once per day by members of security staff. Any injuries caused by an instance of DA/1E should be reported to members of personnel immediately, so that emergency surgery can begin.
------
[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Physical Description:**[[/span]]

Instances of DA/1E are 19mm x 19mm x 19mm dice with white bodies and black pips. All instances of DA/1E weigh precisely 4.1 grams, and change coloration depending on the spectrum of light they are exposed to. This gives them a shiny appearance depending on the lighting that they are exposed to. Additionally, instances of DA/1E has a unique scent that is best described as a "sweet, upbeat scent similar to that of an alcoholic rose."

[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Anomalous Description:**[[/span]]

Instances of DA/1E show signs of ant-like intelligence, and are capable of moving by rattling itself through an unknown means. Instances of DA/1E are capable of consuming food by phasing through substances with a high salt content, causing them to disappear. Instances of DA/1E emit a colorless, sweet smelling vapor (which has been named Compound-1E-01) that causes those that inhale it to feel invigorated and energized. Further research has confirmed that those affected by this gas require less sleep. 

When instances of DA/1E have not been fed, they stop emitting Compound-1E-01 and become aggressive. Skin contact with DA/1E while in this state will result in DA/1E injecting venom from one of it's edges. This venom, named Compound-1E-02, causes the body to ethanol in the place of hormones, resulting in death by alcohol poisoning. Compound-1E-02 spreads slowly through the body, and has no currently known cure outside of amputation. It takes Compound-1E-2 approximately 2 hours to spread from the hands to the brain.
------
[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Recovery:**[[/span]]

265 instances of DA/1E were recovered from the Acquisitions Division during the liquidation of all assets owned by the Midas in 1948. Additionally, schematics for a storage facility within the air ducts was found, suggesting that Compound-1E-01 was pumped into the casino floor of the Midas. Investigations into the origin of DA/1E are still ongoing. 
------
[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Proposal of Destruction:**[[/span]]

> "DA-1E itself provides no use to the Agency. However, Compound-1E-01 will allow members of the Agency to work for longer without experiencing any adverse side effects, and DA-1E is currently the only known way to get Compound-1E-01. Instances of DA-1E should be bred and Compound-1E-01 should be harvested and pumped into Agency facilities, to increase productivity without sacrificing morale." ~**Dr. Cox**,
