------
[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Enclosure Protocols**[[/span]]
 
DA/0D is to be treated as a level 4 biohazard, any interactions with DA/0D are to be done in full hazardous protection uniforms, unprotected organisms are to be kept at least 60 meters away from any uncontained live instance of DA/0D. Any cases of DA/0D are to be reported to the nearest instance of Level IV or higher personnel so that DA/0D-Lockdown can be placed into effect around the affected area.
 
[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Destruction Protocols**[[/span]]
 
Any live instance of DA/0D of stage 2 and beyond, regardless of the condition of the host, is to be incinerated by fire measuring at temperatures of at least 820 degrees Celsius over a period of 3-4 hours, with strict surveillance of the procedure to ensure no organisms survive, as this could lead to further infection. Stage 1 instances of DA/0D are to be swiftly attended to, with the host being placed within a dry environment of temperatures of a minimum of 30 degrees Celsius with the mother instance of DA/0D is to be burned to death with a small blowtorch, the host's brain is to then be monitored for 2 hours to see if any activity consistent with stage 2 cases of DA/0D, in this case, the host is to be subjected to incineration.
