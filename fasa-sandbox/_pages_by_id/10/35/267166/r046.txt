[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Physical Description:**[[/span]]

DA/03 refers to an arcade cabinet[[footnote]] The marquee and 'title screen' refers to it as "Polybius." [[/footnote]] created by a nondescript federal agency for the U.S. Government for the purpose of making children more susceptible to propaganda.

After a subject aged between 7 and 19 'play' DA/03 for a period a greater then 5 minutes, they appear to follow any commands, suggestions, or beliefs expressed/given to them by an employee or something created by/from any form of government contained within the United States of America, either federal, state or local. This suggestibility seems to have no known limit, though when asked to do any activity that would harm the United States government in any capacity, the subject will refuse, often violently.

The original history of DA/03 is not fully known, as outside agencies refuse to transfer known files about its development. However, it is known that is was developed by Sinneslöschen Incorporated, a known CIA front company, and that it has some relation with Project MKUltra[[footnote]] Project MKUltra was a research project started by the U.S. Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) for the purpose of mind-control. Full information regarding FASA involvement with MKUltra is classified and is only accessible for those with TOP SECRET clearance.[[/footnote]]
