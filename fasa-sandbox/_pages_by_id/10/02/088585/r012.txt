**DA Index:** DA-041
**Codename:** Iceburgh
**Current Status:** Allied & Quarantined

**Containment Level: Fox**
**Usability Level:** 4-Star
**Danger Level: Pearl** 
**Public Status: U**

**Containment Protocols:** 
DA-041 is to remain isolated from public accessibility indefinitely. Any residents of DA-041 that attempt to escape are to be quarantined and terminated. All exports from DA-041 are to be confiscated and contributed to the FASA's pool of anomalous resources. Upon request, trusted members of personnel can request vacation time into DA-041, so long as they document their vacation and remain vigilant for any changes to the nature of DA-041. Additionally, as of the beginning of Operation Integration, all members of personnel working in the country of Iceland can request to move their permanent place of residence to DA-041, so long as they are capable of performing their duties.

AET-Bootes-17 ("Mammoth Kings") is to be stationed at numerous locations surrounding DA-041 at all times, in order to prevent possible unauthorized traffic in or out of DA-041. All non-authorized persons that enter within 1 mile of a known entrance of DA-041 are to be detained and questioned by AET "Mammoth Kings" before being amnesticized and released.

**Description:** 
DA-041 is an urban metropolis underneath a glacier located in the northern regions of Greenland. All attempts to melt the ice composing the glacier that houses DA-041 have failed. Experimentation of this effect is currently under way, in an attempt to find a solution to global warming. The city, though not fully mapped, has been estimated to be double the size of Manhattan, including the top regions. The population of DA-041 is predicted to inhabit 8.14 million. All of the buildings composing DA-041 are made of a white stone that is unique to the underground sections of DA-041. This material is similar in strength to concrete commonly used in skyscrapers. 

DA-041 is inhabited by anomalous sentient humanoids with bright pale skin, white tinted eyes and anomalous clusters of ice emerging from muscle groups, such as the thighs, backs, hips, and necks. Inhabitants of DA-041 have anywhere from two to seven eyes, the average number of eyes being five. They show a heightened emotional response than average humans, showing almost anomalous levels of emotional understanding and empathetic reasoning. The population of DA-041 is pacifistic, and will only attack when threatened. Although they show no desire to leave the confines of their city, inhabitants of DA-041 are incredibly friendly toward outsiders, and immediately wanted to begin trade with the Agency upon our first encounter. Despite previously being believed to be an vulnerable species; polar bears are a cultivated farm animal in agricultural zones of DA-041.

DA-041 has a government that can only be described as a Democratic Meritocracy, where able-minded members of the community are selected by a figure known as "The Wise Immortal" (which will be referred to as DA-041-1). DA-041-1 is a 50 cubic meter ice crystal located in the government center of DA-041. Every three years, it will select 100 inhabitants of DA-041 and the remaining inhabitants will vote for them based upon a list of their merits and skills sent through the mail. Investigation involving the nature of DA-041-1 are still ongoing, though research is showing that the population of DA-041 are mentally connected to DA-041-1 through anomalous means.

> **Operation Repopulation** [[/size]] [[/div]]

> **Operation Status:** Ongoing
> **Description:** The preservation of polar bears in the wild is a goal that is slowly gaining traction around the world. By purchasing polar bears from DA-041 and using a proxy organization to release them into the wild, the Agency can simultaneously increase the global reputation of the United States, strengthen political bonds with DA-041, and earn funding through fundraising. Currently, Operation Repopulation has garnered the Agency $350,000 in fundraising campaigns and donations from various wildlife organizations.
[[/collapsible]]
[[collapsible show="Operation Integration" hide="Access Granted"]]
[[div style="text-align: center; color: #356f91;"]] 
[[size 125%]]
> **Operation Integration** [[/size]] [[/div]]

> **Operation Status:** Ongoing
> **Description:** By slowly integrating members of the Agency into DA-041 as permanent residents, the Agency could eventually form a proxy organization within DA-041 and merge it into the Agency, effectively allowing the Agency to use DA-041 as a containment site for various polar anomalies. Currently, government figures of DA-041 have accepted our initial proposal for an anomalous branch of DA-041's government, known simply as "Ice". Currently seven natives to DA-041 are members of AET "Mammoth Kings", having been recruited initially to test their capabilities but have since become permanent members of the Engagement Team.
[[/collapsible]]

**Proposal of Destruction:** "//Due to the nature of DA-041, destruction of it would only waste Agency resources.//" ~Dr. Shine, Lead Researcher #031

**Proposal of Usage:** "//DA-041 presents itself as a friendly ally to the Agency and the entirety of the United States. As such, they provide a very useful opportunity to expand the power of the Agency by establishing and using a proxy-division of the Agency within DA-041. This would allow safer and more remote containment of anomalies that require sub-zero temperatures to be properly contained, and would allow easy deployment into areas close to the Arctic.//* ~Dr. Noumen, Senior Researcher at Site-071 

**ASRC Verdict:** DA-041 is an ally of the Agency and action should not be taken to cause harm to or destroy DA-041. The usage proposal has been formally approved by the ASRC, and actions to begin the proposal (hereby to be called Operation Integration) should begin immediately.
