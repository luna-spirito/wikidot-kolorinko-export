**Overview:** Manifest Liberty is an anomalous civil militia that was founded in the year 1934 by James Torres. James Torres was raised in a military family that had retained knowledge of the Agency's existence through the generations.  The mentality of Manifest Liberty is to act as a counter to the FASA in the event of a Civil War to overthrow the standing government of the United States. Manifest Liberty has at least twenty five confirmed bases scattered across the continental United States, and has an unconfirmed number of anomalies in their possession. 

Manifest Liberty has a cold relationship with the Agency, only communicating with the Agency when deemed mutually beneficial. Though Manifest Liberty has never launched a full-scale attack on the Agency, it is unconfirmed whether or not they are engaged in covert operations within the Agency.

**Handling Procedure:** The FASA must be careful when dealing with Manifest Liberty, as the smallest provocation can cause a Civil War if done incorrectly. All Operations targeted toward Manifest Liberty are to be carried out by AET-Scorpius-09 "Minutemen". Additionally, all Operations against Manifest Liberty are only to be done so for the safety of the American people.
