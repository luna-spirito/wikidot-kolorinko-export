[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Times New Roman', 'Arial', serif;"]]__**Enclosure Protocols**__[[/span]]
[[div style="text-indent: 15px;"]]
DA-XXX is to be enclosed inside a 20m x 15m  x 5m  enclosure cell at Site-10. DA-XXX is to be given three meals a day, each one being enough to satisfy DA-XXXs nutritional needs. For additional information regarding DA-XXXs diet speak to Dr. Jessica Mason. DA-XXXs enclosure cell is to be decorated in such regard to resemble a five years old boys room, with all necessary toys and furniture to be made to accommodate DA-XXXs size. DA-XXX is to be given four hours of playtime from 09:00 to 13:00 hours, after which DA-XXX is to be put to sleep for a "midday nap". DA-XXX should be woken up at 15:00 hours and allowed to resume its activities. Personnel are not allowed to enter DA-XXXs enclosure unit outside of testing without level 4 or above. All personnel that are communicating with DA-XXX are to refer to it as by the name of "Jacob".

[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Times New Roman', 'Arial', serif;"]]__**Physical Description:**__[[/span]]
[[div style="text-indent: 15px;"]]
DA-XXX is a humanoid creature with the height of 3.2 meters and weight of 95 kilograms. DA-XXXs body shows signs of serious malnourishment with very little of both fat and muscle tissue being present. All DA-XXXs extremities are out of proportion compared to its body.DA-XXX possesses a pair of two white eye like organs on its face, and two holes on the side of its head most likely being some form of hearing organs resembling ears.It is proven that DA-XXX has a normal human vision, and hearing considering its age. It has been noted that there is serious scaring on DA-XXXs body in places where vital organs should be present. Theories suggest that all of DA-XXXs organs are transplanted from another body. This theory is backed up by series of DNA testing showing that DNA taken from DA-XXXs vital organs (liver, inestines, stomach, heart, brain, bladder, kidneys and spleen) doesn't match with other DA-XXXs organs (skin, bone, muscle).
It is yet unknown how there is no organ rejection taking the fact that the DNA of DA-XXXs so called "body" doesn't match with the DNA of its organs. It was observed that if in presence of a human being, DA-XXX will get visibly disturbed claiming that it wants to "play" with the said human, and it will often ask the human does he want to "play" with it. After the question regardless if the human accepts the request to play or not DA-XXX will proceed to cause physical harm and sometimes openly kill the human claiming that the said human didn't want to play with it.
[[/div]]

[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Times New Roman', 'Arial', serif;"]]__**Anomalous Description:**__[[/span]]
@@
@@
Although lacking a large portion of its muscle tissue DA-XXX possesses inhuman levels of strength and resistance to physical damage. All photos of DA-XXX present a strange form of distortion. This distortion presents itself in a form that the pictures after being taken look like they were drawn using crayons. This includes digital pictures and physical photographs as well, this property might be linked with a strange observance on the right frontal lobe of DA-XXXs brain, indicating some form of a tumor was formed suggesting that DA-XXX possesses some form of telepathic abilities. The research of impact this "telepathy" can have on DA-XXXs surroundings, and its reality bending properties is ongoing.

= **DISCOVERY LOG**
----
**DATE:** 28.7.2002.

**NOTE:** DA-XXX was discovered in the state of Montana in a town that goes by the name of ████████. After a series of sightings by the local populous The Agency was informed of DA-XXXs existence and a team was deployed. 
----

[BEGIN LOG]

**Alpha-1**: This is AET-Sagittarius-3 Alpha squad team leader ████ ██████. We have been alerted of anomalous activity in the area of ████████ Montana. Orders are to snatch and grab, preferably alive.

**Alpha-4:** Weapons hot ?

**Alpha-1:** I doubt it.

**Alpha-2:** What if we need to bail out ?

**Alpha-1:** Standard protocol. 

**Alpha-1:** Okay its time to move, NODs on.

(//**Alpha team exits the vehicle and proceeds to search multiple locations without the anomaly being found**//)

**Alpha-3:** I am noticing some disturbance here on my NODs.

**Alpha-2:** Me too, whats happening ?

**Alpha-4:** I think we are approaching it.

**Alpha-1:** Turn off the nods and turn on the lights.

**Alpha-4:** Guys rally on my location i think i fou....

**Alpha-1:** What did you say Four ?

(//**Screams and gunfire are heard**//)

**Alpha-4:** No, get off me, no (gunshots are heard) no !

**Alpha-1:** Four ? Four ? Alpha team listen we are gonna rally on Fours position!

(//**Alpha team proceeds to rally on Alpha-4s last known location**//)

(//**Alpha-3 is the first to arrive the scene and sees the DA-XXX on a slide with Alpha-4s body being visible in the background.**//)

**Alpha-3:** Oh God!

(//**Alpha-2 and Alpha-1 arrive on scene**//)

**Alpha-2:** Sir should we open fire ?

(//**DA-XXX is seen as it lifts its head in the direction of Alpha team and starts to wave its hand in a welcoming gesture**//)

**Alpha-1:** No 

**Alpha-1:** (//**Lowers his weapon**//) Two give me the syringe.

**Alpha-2:** (//**Pulls out the tranquilizer syringe and gives it to Alpha-1**//)

**Alpha-1:** Three stay here with Two and cover me.

**Alpha-1:** (//**Proceeds to slowly approach DA-XXX, and kneels down before the entity with the syringe behind his back**//) Hey buddy.

**DA-XXX:** Hi.

**Alpha-1:** Do you have a name ?

**DA-XXX:** Jacob.

**Alpha-1:** Oh Jacob, what a nice name. Tell me Jacob, why did you hurt this mister over there.

**DA-XXX:** He didn't want play with me.

**Alpha-1:** Oh what a bummer... 

**DA-XXX:** (//**Proceeds to look at Alpha-1**//) Do you want to play with me ?

**Alpha-1:** Oh, well uh...

**DA-XXX:** Do you want to play with me ?! (//**Proceeds to grab Alpha-1 by the arm.**//)

**Alpha-1:** Yes.

**DA-XXX:** Then lets play! (//**Proceeds to clench Alpha-1s arm and brake it in the process.**//)

**Alpha-1:** (//**Screams, proceeds to stab the syringe into DA-XXXs neck.**//)

**Alpha-1:** Shoot it !

**DA-XXX:** (//**Throws Alpha-1 and proceeds to go toward Alpha-2 and Alpha-3**//)

**Alpha-2/Alpha-3:** (//**Open fire on DA-XXX**//) 


**//After some time of combat DA-XXX collapses from the tranquilizer injected by Alpha-1//**

-----

[END LOG]

Alpha-1 and Alpha-4 were considered KIA and immediate termination order was issued upon containment of DA-XXX. It was observed that DA-XXX sustained minimum amount of damage from the confrontation.
