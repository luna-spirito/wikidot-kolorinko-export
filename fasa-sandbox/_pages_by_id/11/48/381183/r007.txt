[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Enclosure Protocols**[[/span]]
 
DA/XX is currently confined to Alcatraz Island in San Francisco Bay, 1.25 miles offshore from San Francisco, California, USA. The island is to be cordoned off from the public until DA/XX is successfully neutralized. 
 
[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Destruction Protocols**[[/span]]
 
DA/XX is to be "starved", wherein the island it inhabits is to be isolated from the general public until it expires naturally. 
------
[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Physical Description:**[[/span]]
 
DA/XX resembles a large contrail cloud, with several appendages protruding along its length. Several of these appendages retain a sharp, bright orange glow at the end of their extension. Several bright glowing lights can be observed at the ending of DA/XX's length.
 
[[span style="font-size: 150%; font-family: 'Public Sans', sans-serif;"]]**Anomalous Description:**[[/span]]
