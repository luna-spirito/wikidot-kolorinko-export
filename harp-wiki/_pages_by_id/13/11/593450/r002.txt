Extra-terrestrial Anomalies Report Division (EARD) associates with HARP mostly when an extra-terrestrial activity is involved in the HCA, it may be an ET HCA or connected to extraterrestrial activity. The clear history of the department is not clear. According to some sources EARD has its association with some department to deal with Anomalies in all planets/galaxies with life. The containment of anomalies is done based on the type of anomalies
