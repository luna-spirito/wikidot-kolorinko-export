++ **Inappropriate language**

* No slurs of ANY kind.

++ Inappropriate content

* No Pornographic or Illegal content.
* No Plagiarism.

++ Quality Control
If a page is tagged for rewrite, an author has one day to respond. If they do not, it will be removed or archived.

++ Age

You must be age over the age of 15 to join the site.
We are a community and are here to help each other grow, be open to criticism, and listen to other's inputs.

++ Forum

* Be respectful when criticizing. People put a considerable amount of work into their articles.
* No spamming.
