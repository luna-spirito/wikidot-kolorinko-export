HCA-001 The Plant Man
CAS
Class: Safe
type: Plant
Status: Deceased

Description: The Plant Man was a Korean man from the early Korean war. Taken in by H.A.R.P.s founder, Dr. Harnibens. Ji-ho was his first name, he was capable of forcing landscapes to grow at his will, even controlling plants if needed. his power came at a cost, the more he used his power, the more his physical form was taken over by plants. when he came into H.A.R.P.s custody, he had a 3% body coverage. he died at the age of 83, with a total of 83% body coverage.

Backstory: During the Korean War, Dr. Harnibens had discovered Ji-ho, after his families farm was bombed and Dr. Harnibens was sent to treat the wounded. his family had been keeping him hidden from the village. The village had known of Ji-ho's existence but not his power. which they had managed to keep secret, it was found that Ji-ho's family had never abused him and in fear of him being found. they asked Dr. Harnibens for help. With some help, Dr. Harnibens managed to get Ji-ho to the states and thus was the beginning of H.A.R.P.

Ji-ho had spent the rest of life with H.A.R.P. and being an agriculture manager for 68 years. Until he had died in his sleep due to old age. he was expected to live longer with his plant counter parts, but it was found. his plant counter parts had affected his life spand. no general health issues though.
