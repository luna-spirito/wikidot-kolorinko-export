**HCA-724: The Early-Warning System
Class: Habilis
Type: Psionic**

**Description:** HCA-724 stands at approximately 5'7 in height, resembling a teenage male, averaging at the age of 15 years, with pale blue-grey mottled skin, short, black hair and bright purple eyes. HCA-724 has an IQ of 167, however rarely takes this into account. 724 only seems to express his intelligence when alone, or if he deemed it necessary. 724 can speak English, as well as fluently speaking in Russian, Arabic and German- this had caused some confusion, until further research revealed that 724's mental abilities allowed him to detect the origins of other words, and from there was able to work out the other languages (why he had to go with the tricky ones, no clue. He could've just done French). 

**Background:** 724 came to H.A.R.P's attention on October 7th, 2017, after being detained by police in reported "hysteria", racing into the police station yelling about a murder. When local P.D inquired, 724 claimed there would be a murder, and described the time, place and circumstances. Four days later was the capture of HCA-848, as well as the death of Ethan Feilds- in the exact way 724 had described. In furter days to come, 724 accurately described events before they were to happen. No patterns of the timelines between 724's predictions and the following events have yet been identified. 724 has even prevented multiple security breeches, his predictions allowing for ample time to improve security on said possible breech-points.
Cont HCA-724 is to be kept in a standard containment cell, no reinforced walls needed, about 15x15x15 ft. Excessive security is not necessary (our budget will thank us later), however 724 will have a rotation of security detail every 4 hours. To enter containment, Researchers must punch in a simple code, as well as take a quick verification of identity, which involves a thermal thumbprint scanner, a speech analysis and an iris scan, in order to prevent anyone from gaining unauthorized access- because people seem to like the idea of going to a fortune teller that actually works- but for the last time THIS IS A SCIENTIFIC INSTITUTE NOT A CARNIVAL, JERRY!
