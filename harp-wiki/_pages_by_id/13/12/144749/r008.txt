**HCA-848: The Moon Man
Class: Warren
Type: Ignis/Aerium**

**Description:** HCA-848 is a middle aged caucasian male, with a height of 2 meters. HCA-848 bears a similarity to HCA-217, with retractable wings (Two pairs, black-to-silver color gradient) that have a wingspan of 7ft on the primary pair and 5ft for the secondary. He also has an ability to conjure fire in an almost identical way to 217’s- however the flames are of a bright blue color, and far hotter. HCA-848 has an apparently calm demeanour, never really acting out in violence. HCA-848’s eyes consist of a pitch black sclera, with a silver iris and no apparent pupil. The iris has darker silver splotches in particular spots, bearing a resemblance to the full moon. He has fang-like teeth, and despite his often chilled-out persona, has been known to manipulate and control (without any anomalous ability, apparently) his guards and doctors, as well as attacking them. While an attack from 848 is rare, they do happen rather brutally when they occur.
**Background:** HCA-848 was taken into police custody on the night of October 11th 2017, after the murder of a man named Ethan Feilds. 848 Was hostile and defensive at the scene, causing multiple fires and casualties. 848 was very heavily sedated and brought to H.A.R.P custody and secured. As 848 was being escorted to his containment area, HCA-217 was passing by on the way to an interview with another staff member following an incident a few days prior, and reportedly was thrown into a state of hysteria at the sight of 848, attempting to attack and burn him as she passed. 217 was directed back to containment and the interview was postponed. 848 did not deny any connection to her- nor did 848 claim the circumstances of their relationship regarding one another. By close examination, 848 proved to be manipulative, conniving, sadistic and a pathological liar, as well as showing severe sociopathic tendencies from time to time. Cannot be trusted at all.

**Containment:** HCA-848 must be kept with a 24/7 watch, in a 24ft x17ft x24ft containment cell, with tantalum carbide reinforced walls, with absolutely NO obvious possible escape points. His cell is to be completely empty at all times, spare for a bunk and a chair- both must be bolted to the floor. No seriously. He threw the chair at the wall last week just to show us he can and we do not want him throwing it at us. Cell must also have concrete reinforcements (I’m not paranoid, you are- Dr. Tori Florence).
To enter containment (for the insane ones who do), an experimental sleeping gas (which surprisingly works), must be let into the room to render 848 unconscious. Reinforced turnout gear similar to that worn by firefighters must be worn within containment at all times, should 848 wake. If HCA-848 does regain consciousness, the containment area must be evaluated immediately. Should 848 attack, foam fire extinguishers should be activated to temporarily stop 848 in his tracks. Such an attack is rare, but as said, brutal.
**Notes:** 217 has claimed having a previous rivalry in her teens with HCA-848, claiming 848 was involved in a relationship with one of her close friends, and was quickly discarded from the circle as he proved to be controlling and manipulative to said friend, who, 217 states, is in fact Ethan Fields.
