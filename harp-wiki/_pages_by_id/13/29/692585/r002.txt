**Item Name: A Small Nuisance
Item Class: Safe
Item Type: Turing **

**Description:** HCA-999 is a 2006 Dell Laptop with extensive damage to the screen and keyboard. 
HCA-999-A is a --Little Shit-- 240 gigabyte file named hi.exe.

**Containment:** HCA-999 is to be unpowered with the battery removed, and stored in a standard anomalous item locker within the H.A.R.P building in Helena, Montana in order to minimize the chance of HCA-999-A escaping into the internet if ever powered on.

**Anomalous Effects:** Upon clicking HCA-999-A on any computer, it will install itself on the computer. this will have no indication or discernable effect until approximately an hour after installation. After this "Grace Period" has passed, HCA-999-A will begin alterations to the host computer, particularly in ways that annoy the specific user or impede the user's specific line of work. This causes extreme amounts of discomfort in the user, leading the user to lash out and damage the computer more during each successive event.

**Background:** HCA-999 was taken into custody after H.A.R.P was called by the IT department of Allistair Technologies, a small startup in Portland, Oregon. The IT department was complaining about an anomalous computer virus destroying their computers and impeding their work. A small IT team was sent in to isolate the entity to a single laptop, HCA-999[[footnote]] Believed to be the original computer that held the anomaly [[/footnote]], and returned to H.A.R.P Processing. 

**Notes:** No attempt to interview HCA-999-A is to be made. H.A.R.P has already wasted ██ computers on this --Fucker-- anomaly
