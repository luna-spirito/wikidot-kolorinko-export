**File #:** 021

**Danger Class:** Haeresis

**Obscurity Class:** Hexa

**Description:** ADF-021 is a humanoid entity of the Afro-American descent. It presents itself as a male child, approximately 9 years of age. It's typically docile and acts appropriately for its age, however it has never shown any need for food or water. ADF-021's anomalous properties present themselves whenever an adult (aged 18 years and up) verbally interacts with ADF-021. After initial contact has been made, the subject, now referred to as ADF-021-1, will develop an irresistible urge to ask ADF-021 what nine plus ten is. Upon asking this question, ADF-021 will always answer with "Twenty-one". About 3 seconds after ADF-021 finishes this sentence, ADF-021-1 will begin laughing uncontrollably. This will last until the subject dies of asphyxiation. ADF-021 will then proceed to absorb ADF-021-1 into itself and disappear. ADF-021 will reappear in a random spot after exactly twenty-one days have passed. ADF-021 will stay in the area it has reappeared in until it absorbs another instance of ADF-021-1.
