The Escape Room is a secret Level of the Backrooms, it doesn't have a specific date of discovery.

The Level's layout is entirely unknown, save for one singular room. This level is known only as the “starting room”, with the rest being temporarily inaccessible. Hidden behind a puzzle that no one has yet been able to crack.

+ **Description:**
The Level starts out as just one room, there are windows on all sides, with a white void being mostly all you can see. Sometimes, a few instances of [[[Entity 2|the window entities]]] can replace the pre-existing windows, as seen in the attached image, though the windows here aren't usually a window. The walls are faint, concrete yellow, with signs of decay. The floor is also concrete, with replenishing puddles of almond water. The ceiling is metal, with three large, fluorescent lights. These lights are inactive, with the only light coming from the outside void.

When you enter, you seemingly just know what to do immediately. To escape into the later portion of the level. Though the current issue is that there currently is no clear-cut way to exit the room, since all previous attempts have come out fruitless. There is however some puzzle that has been documented, found out by the brave volunteers who gave their lives to find the exit. Their findings only being given to us by the Wi-Fi that strangely exists on this level. Since all items disappear once someone dies, and only one person can enter at a time.

+ **Bases, Outposts and Communities:**
There are no known Colonies or Outposts on this level.

+ **Entrances and Exits:**
**Entrances:**
To enter, you can find a blank “inverted room” in [[[Level 16]]]. Where the ceiling is the floor, the floor is the ceiling, and the wall design is upside down. All the lights on the floor will be no-clippable, and falling through them will lead you here. If someone is already in this Level however, this will be impossible.

**Exits:**
To exit, it can be presumed you leave by completing the puzzle. But since that has not been figured out, leaving at this current time is practically impossible.
