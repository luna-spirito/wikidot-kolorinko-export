**Level 45** is the 46th Level of The Backrooms.

+ **Description:**

This level takes the appearance of a seemingly endless IKE-I mean, Home Depot. It is unknown how long this level is, as its edges have not yet been found. This level is home to many entities, some exclusive ones in this level are the store manager or the vendors, which resemble Facelings with unnatural body proportions. They are normally friendly, but when night falls, or if you steal from them, they will exert Skin-Stealer behavior and hunt you down until you exit the level. Some other entities found here are Deathmoths, Smilers, The Windows, Hounds, and Facelings. This level has items in the aisles and furniture. One man claims he built a fort with these to escape from some entities following him. There are occasional cameras located around this level, but they cannot be viewed. The roof is proven to be miles high, because someone claims to have built 1/4 of a mile high and still did not see the roof. If you drink any coffee on this level, you are in immediate danger of being discovered.

+ **Bases, Communities, and Outposts**

++ **The Industrials:**
* There is one main camp, they call themselves "The Industrials." A group consisting of 51 people, friendly and willing to trade.
* The largest group in **Level 45**
* Fortified enough to gain a basic wall to defend from entities
* Trade food or weapons for furniture and decors.

+ **Entrances And Exits:**

++ Entrances

You can enter **Level 45** by entering a orange-ceiling store at [[[Level 11]]]. Another way of entry is going through a orange-themed store in [[[Level 33]]]. It is also speculated that [[[The Metro]]] can take you here, although it is only a theory.

++ Exits

You can exit this Level by finding an exit door, though it is extremely rare, and has a chance of disappearing whenever you get near it. This will take you to [[[Level 11]]].
