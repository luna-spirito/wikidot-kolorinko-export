Level -666 is the 667th negative level of The Backrooms

+ **Description:**
Level -666 is a distorted and entity infested version of the halls of Vatican City whose name "Peccata Imperdonabilia" (Unforgivable Sins) was given from reports from documents that somehow no-clipped out of the level.

When someone enters the level, it will appear laying down on a square (Possibly resembling St. Peter's Square). Unable to move any muscle, two shadowy figures which they are one of the two exclusive entities to this level, will drag the wanderer into a doorway and they will throw the wanderer into the halls of the level then two big doors will lock the wanderer into the main part of the level.

After being throwed into the hallways of **Level -666** the wanderer from this point will encounter any hostile entity within the backrooms, the most common ones being [[[Entity 3|smilers]]], [[[Entity 10|skin-stealers]]], [[[Entity 15|wretches]]], [[[Entity 67|partygoers]]] and [[[Entity 4|deathmoths]]]. From this point, the wanderer must stay focused on the entities and sanity because the further the wanderer ventures into the hallways of **Level -666**, more entities will be there for combat and the sanity decreases and increases spontaneously.

If the wanderer manages to survive all of this, it will start hearing the yells of the souls in pain and minutes later it will encounter the second exclusive entity of the level known as **The Sinister Pope**. **The Sinister Pope** has the appearance of all of the popes from St. Peter to Pope Francis, he will appoarch the wanderer slowly and then will say in latin **"Omnis anima quae per vestibula inferorum ambulat, damnata est ad patiendum in reliqua aeternitate."** (Every soul that walks on the hallways of hell, is condemned to suffer for the rest of eternity).
Then it will vanish the wanderer from existence, meaning that all of the things related to him will be forgotten like if the wanderer didn't existed.

It is theorised that the yells of the souls in pain are the wanderers who ended up on this level

+ **Bases, Outposts and Communities:**
There are no known bases, outposts or communities on this level

+ **Entrances And Exits:**
To enter, one must commit all of the seven deadly sins in Christianity (Pride, greed, wrath, envy, lust, gluttony and sloth) in any backrooms location or by spending a week on [[[Level 666]]]

**Exits:**
There is no escape from hell, any soul that enters hell is condemned to suffer for the rest of eternity..
