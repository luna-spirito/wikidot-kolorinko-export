+ **What are Secret Levels?**
[[f>toc]]
The secret levels are areas in the Backrooms which do not have an ordinal placement, and are very mysterious. Some levels here are safe as far as we know, but some can be dangerous, though it is less likely. These levels have stranger properties than most levels, giving them unique names and/or numbers. A lot of these levels were recently discovered; it is theorized there are still many more enigmatic levels to be found.
