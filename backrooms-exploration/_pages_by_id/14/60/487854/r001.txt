**Level 60** is the 61st level of the backrooms. It is a sprawling complex of rooms similar to a retro laundromat.

+ **Description**
**Level 60** is the 61st level of the Backrooms. It is a sprawling complex of rooms similar to a retro laundromat, complete with washing machines, drying machines, vending machines, and more detergent and dryer sheets than you could ever need. The machines are all functional, but only if you use Coins, Coins are flat round pieces of metal that function as a "currency" in **Level 60**. You can obtain some by breaking open any washing or drying machine.

The machines all use soapy water in their cleaning process, so don't drink it. You can buy fresh and [[[object-1|Almond Water]]] in the vending machines. Many people come in and out of this level to wash their clothes and resupply.

+ **Bases, Outposts and Communities**
While there are no known bases, outposts, or communities on this level, there are almost always at least a few people on this level. This is considered a safe area.

+ **Entrances And Exits**

++++ Entrances

**Level 60** can be found by entering any laundromat in [[[Level 11]]]. Just explore it for a few hours, if you find that you can't go back to the city, you're in. It can also be rarely accessed by no-clipping through the street floor in [[[Level 9]]].
++++ Exits

There are two ways to exit **Level 60**. The first, and safest, is to noclip directly into a running drying machine. This will take you back to [[[Level 11]]]. The other way is to travel deep into the laundromat, which could take several days. Eventually, you will encounter an entrance to a train station. Entering this train, and traveling down it, will take you to [[[Level 61]]].
