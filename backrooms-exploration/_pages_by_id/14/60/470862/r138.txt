+ What Are Levels?

Normal Levels are the basis of the Backrooms, and vary from safe, to very dangerous. Most of the lower Levels have been proven to exist, but some of these particular Levels are only rumored to exist; without enough substantial evidence, it cannot yet be proven. You can come and contribute your findings to The Exploration Database!

The Backrooms Senate hosts monthly competition for new levels!
https://discord.gg/nWSP2Qnacd
