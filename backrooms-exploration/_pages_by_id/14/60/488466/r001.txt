**Level -1** is the 2nd Negative Level of the backrooms.

+ **Description:**

**Level -1** is a presumably infinite hallway that has doors on either side. The Level consists of white-painted walls and numerous black doors. Each door leads to either [[[level-minus-2|Level -2]]], [[[Level 0]]], [[[the-whiteout|The Whiteout]]] or [[[Level 2]]].

Whilst inside this Level, one will no longer hear the hum-buzz of [[[Level 0]]]. Instead, one may hear slight piano noises in the distance; the source of the sound is unknown. The deeper one traverses through this Level, the slightly louder it gets, but it never seems to get any closer. Your vision seems to get more static-like effects while walking too far down the hall. During this time, you can hear some old advertisements from around the 1930's era through the static noise. Soon, your vision becomes black and white with the static effect. While this event plays out, you can see people in old business suits, although their human features are completely invisible to the human eye, the only thing visible is their attire. You may also hear the chatter of many beings mixed with the sounds previously mentioned. [[[object 1|Almond Water]]] puddles can also be found on the floor during this event, dripping from the ceiling.

The wanderers that made it this far have always turned back at this point, and when they do, they end up talking about an unknown Entity that they simply call 'Nutrisha'.  Further expeditions will be started to explore **Level -1**. This Level will, for now, have a designated difficulty level of Class 2 until further notice.

The basis of **Level -1** is that it is one of the smallest Levels in all of The Backrooms (relatively), as it seems to consist only of an endless hallway. You cannot access [[[Level 1]]] from here. It is recommended that you try to exit to [[[Level 0]]], though, if you are feeling particularly adventurous and have a ready supply of [[[object 1|Almond Water]]], it is safe to exit to [[[Level 2]]] and [[[the-whiteout|The Whiteout]]]. It is strongly advised to never go to [[[level-minus-2|Level -2]]]. From this level, it is very easy to slip into [[[level-minus-2|Level -2]]], which is extremely dangerous.

+ Bases, Outposts, and Communities:

There are no known bases, outposts, or communities on this Level.

+ **Entrances And Exits:**

+++ Entrances:

To enter, break a wall in [[[Level 0]]], or noclip through any dark wall. Generally, noclipping anywhere in The Backrooms is a great way to escape a Level with an unknown exit.

+++ Exits:

To exit, enter one of the many doors to enter [[[level-minus-2|Level -2]]], [[[Level 0]]], [[[The Whiteout]]] and [[[Level 2]]]. It is recommended to enter the Level you desire once the door is found, as walking through this level's halls has unknown consequences. More Level entrances are rumored to exist beyond this point, but this is unknown.
