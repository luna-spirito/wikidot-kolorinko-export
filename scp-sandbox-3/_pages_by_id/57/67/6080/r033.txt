content: "**Welcome to the SCP Wiki Sandbox!** You can use pages on this site to practice formatting, store works in progress, or simply have a place to put drafts so you can show others and get feedback before posting to the main site.\n\n[!--\nremoved, see http://scp-sandbox-3.wikidot.com/no-drafts\nwill snip this out for good after a trial period\n++ REMEMBER: when making edits, always use the \"SAVE\" function and NOT \"save draft\". Using \"save draft\" will retain a digital copy of the edits without actually changing the page itself. This can cause problems when attempting to make a new sandbox. \n--]\n\n* **Anyone can create and edit their pages here.** Please note that you must [http://scp-sandbox-3.wikidot.com/system:join JOIN THE SITE] to create new pages.\n* **All mainsite rules apply to the sandbox.** They can be found [https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/site-rules here.] \n * AI-generated content and AI-assisted content are not permitted, including use of AI to reword or revise material, use of AI to generate images which an artist then traces, and use of AI to produce content that an author then adds surface edits to.\n* **Staff reserve the right to remove material that violates our site rules and guidelines.** If mainsite rules are broken on the sandbox, disciplinary action may be taken.\n* **The SCP Wiki Sandbox is routinely monitored by staff.** If you find something that violates site rules, please notify a member of the [http://05command.wikidot.com/maintenance-and-ancillary-staff-team-main Sandbox Cleaning] team.\n* **Rating modules are disabled on the sandbox.** While the module will still appear if the wikitext is present, you cannot cast votes.\n* **You can make as many sandbox pages as you'd like.** We strongly recommend that you title your sandbox page(s) with your username, or something else unique. It's easier if people can tell who the sandbox belongs to by reading the title, please avoid titles like \"SCP-XXXX\" or \"The Monster\".\n* **Be reasonable with file uploads.** While the sandbox allows uploading files to assist with the drafting process, do not upload excessively large or excessively many files.\n* **Sandbox pages are automatically edit-locked.** Regular sandbox pages can only be edited by their creator.\n* **Sandboxes can be created to allow co-authored works.** If you'd like to collaborate with other authors, create the page with \"collab\" at the start, for instance {{collab:site-19-raid}}. For further reading on co-authoring, refer to [https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/zyns-co-authoring-tips Zyn's Co-Authoring Tips]. If you need further assistance, please contact a member of [http://05command.wikidot.com/staff-list site staff].\n* Happy writing! :)\n\n\n[[div class=\"content-panel standalone centered unmargined\" style=\"padding: 20px 20px 10px;\"]]\n**[[size 120%]]To create a sandbox page, enter your desired page name below:[[/size]]**\n[[module NewPage size=\"20\" button=\"Create Sandbox\"]]\n[[/div]]\n\n----\n++ Finding Pages\nTo list all of the sandbox pages you have created, use the following code, replacing YOUR_USERNAME with your Wikidot username.:\n[[code]]\n[[module ListPages created_by=\"YOUR_USERNAME\"]]\n%%title_linked%%\n[[/module]]\n[[/code]]\n\n----\n++ Tabs\n\nTo add tabs to your sandbox, use the following code:\n\n[[code]]\n[[tabview]]\n[[tab Title 1]]\nText goes here. Pretend this is a draft.\n[[/tab]]\n[[tab Title 2]]\nMore text in another tab! You can add lots of tabs.\n[[/tab]]\n[[/tabview]]\n[[/code]]\n\nIf done correctly, you should get something like this:\n\n[[tabview]]\n[[tab Title 1]]\nText goes here. Pretend this is a draft.\n[[/tab]]\n[[tab Title 2]]\nMore text in another tab! You can add lots of tabs.\n[[/tab]]\n[[/tabview]]\n\n----\n\n++ Useful Guides\n\n* **[https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/how-to-write-an-scp How to Write an SCP]**\n* **[http://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/doing-the-safety-dance Doing the Safety Dance]** - writing reasonable and interesting containment procedures \n* **[http://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/technical-writing Technical Writing]** - For authors, not scientists.\n* **[http://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/avoid-the-blackbox What Hides Beneath: the Black and White of Blackboxing]** - how to use Redaction.\n* **[http://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/crosslinks-guide Crosslinks Guide]** - how best to use links to other articles.\n\nAdditionally, check out **[http://www.wikidot.com/doc:wiki-syntax/ Wikidot Syntax]** for a basic guide to formatting text and adding elements like collapsibles, images and tables, and **[https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-style-resource SCP Style Resource]** for some SCP-specific templates.\n\nAll of these guides, and more, are hosted on the main **[http://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/ SCP Wiki]** on the **[http://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/guide-hub Guide Hub]**. Additional writing advice can be found on the **[https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/essay-resource-hub Essay and Resource Hub]**.\n\n[[html]]\n<a rel=\"license\" href=\"http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/\"><img alt=\"Creative Commons License\" style=\"border-width:0\" src=\"https://i.creativecommons.org/l/by-sa/3.0/88x31.png\" /></a>This work is licensed under a <a rel=\"license\" href=\"http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/\">Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported License</a>.\n[[/html]]"
