O5-1 strode out of the ruins of his bunker, his face a mask of rage and grief. He... they, had protected humanity for so long. Humanity was imperfect, it was chaotic, and it was the only thing he'd ever loved. And now he was finally alone. A truth that had been figurative for over two thousand years was now literal. The irony was bile in his throat.

He stared at the new blue star and let out a roar of frustration. He poured every sacrifice, every tragedy, every Montauk, and every victory into his voice. When he fell silent, little of him remained.

"I'd do it again. All of it, one thousand times over. Even if I knew I would still fail." He straightened up and began to pace. "I see you in the sky, rising to a final betrayal with a being that is nothing more than a mockery of true life. You abandon everything your ancestors have built, everything we have shed blood to protect. I know this is the end, I can feel Life itself leaving this world. But had you stood with us rather than against us we could have won. We could have endured as we've always endured, survived as we've always survived. But you were seduced by power. You may have become gods, but that is something less than human, not more. The present isn't worth the loss of the future and the past."

O5-1 stopped, and turned to face [[[scp-990| someone who wasn't there]. "Hello Fritz, did no one ever teach you that it's rude to interrupt a soliloquy."

Nobody said nothing.

"You always stood with us. But when I needed your guidance more than ever you were gone. Why?"

Nobody said Nothing.

"We'll both be dead soon. Just tell me why. For the sake of everyone we've sacrificed on the altar of our ideals, tell me why you abandoned them!"

"Joseph. It wasn't the greater good anymore."

Neither being spoke again. The Wanderer had ended his journey, and Nobody was never no one.

**Item #:** SCP-SCP-3874

**Object Class:** Neutralized

**Special Containment Procedures:** As no instances of SCP-3874-1 have surfaced since 1956, the anomaly is currently considered neutralized. Prior containment procedures consisted of standard disinformation protocols regarding the victims, and of monitoring the postal correspondence of noted explorers and travelers.

Should the anomaly resurface, no Foundation agent is permitted to be used in an attempt to explore SCP-3874-2.

**Description:** SCP-3874 is a series of related phenomenon that lead to individuals (termed SCP-3874-A) entering what is believe to be an alternate plane of reality (termed SCP-3874-2), where said individuals invariably disappear. A instances consist of professional or amateur explorers and traveling enthusiasts. All instances had at least a degree of public renown at the time of their disappearance. The process begins when the A instance receives an unstamped letter purporting to be from their "Uncle Dexter."

No attempt to trace the letters to their source was ever sucessful; the working theory at the time was that instances of SCP-3974-1 would appear spontaneously within mailboxes and letter slots. Each letter follows the same overall structure: it begins with a vague introduction that mentions meeting the A instance when the latter was a child, proceeds to inform them of an opportunity for exploration or discovery, and contains a detailed map on the reverse side. For reference, a 3874-1 instance addressed to Robert F. Scott in 1909 is provided below.

[[collapsible show="+ Show Letter" hide="- Hide"]]

> Dear Robert,
> 
> You most likely don't remember me; we last met when you were only a boy after all. I was a dear friend of your father's from his days as a magistrate. My work kept me away from your family at the time of his passing, but I have always made sure to watch over his son.
> 
> And what a vigil it has been, my boy! You have made us all very proud with your Arctic expedition, though I know you wanted it to end differently. I was an explorer myself, albeit one of far less renown. Your mistake was lingering around Ross island and trying to cross southwest, when you should have gone southeast across the shelf. I have attached a map showing a safe route through most of the crevice fields. You are not the only one to have tried and failed to reach the pole. But with this, you will be the one to succeed.
> 
> With Great Anticipation,
> Uncle Dexter
[[/collapsible]]

Following reception of the letter, all known recipients attempted to travel to the location specified within their instance of SCP-3874-1. It is unknown if this is due to a compulsive effect; Agent Carter maintained that he did not experience a compulsion prior to entering SCP-3874-2 on Foundation orders, but the unanimous nature of the departures caused suspicion among the contemporary research team. An opposing hypothesis proposed that victims were selected based on their likelihood of succumbing to the bait.

In November of 1938, Foundation Para-archeological specialist Howard Carter received an instance of SCP-3874-1. Following internal debate and the acquiescence of Agent Carter, it was decided that he would attempt to enter SCP-3874-2. Use of Anomalous Object EG-41[[footnote]]A pair of papyrus scrolls affected by an anomaly that allows for one way transmission of writing. Originally discovered by Agent Carter 12 years prior to his disappearance.[[/footnote]] was authorized to enable remote reports. Carter began following the map contained in his SCP-3874 instance on February 8th, 1939. Despite the agreed-upon reporting schedule, only three reports were ever received. The following month, Agent Carter was declared KIA and a false report of his death disseminated to the public.

**Item #:** SCP-3561

**Object Class:** Thaumiel

**Special Containment Procedures:** Traditional containment procedures are unnecessary due to the nature of the anomaly. Informational security regarding its nature and function is considered to be of highest priority. Under no circumstances can a living member of the O5 Council be permitted to understand SCP-3561.

Should a majority of the eight regional Ethics Chairs, the Administrator, and the Director of RAISA vote to enact Protocol Damocles, SCP-3561 will be utilized to complete the procedure. With a unanimous vote of this group, the anomaly may also be used to enact Protocol Iscariot.

**Description:** By order of the Founder, no data describing the nature and function of SCP-3561 is to be recorded. A full verbal briefing on the subject will be given to newly installed Chairs of the eight regional Ethics Committees by the Administrator.

// typewriter code by not_a_seagull
function Typewriter(el, str, delay) {
  if (!(this instanceof Typewriter)) return new Typewriter(el, str, delay);

  this.el = el;
  this.str = str || el.innerHTML || el.value;
  this.delay = delay || 100;
  this.i = 0;
}

Typewriter.prototype.type = function() {
  var i = this.i,
    char = this.str.charAt(i);

  if (!char) {
    clearInterval(this.intervalID);
    return this;
  }

  if (char === '<') this.isTag = true;
  if (char === '>') this.isTag = false;

  this.el.innerHTML += char;
  return this.i++;
};

Typewriter.prototype.start = function() {
  var self = this;
  if (this.i < 0) this.i = 0;
  if (this.el.innerHTML === this.str) this.clear();

  // this.emit('start');
  (function loop() {
    self.type();
    if (self.isTag) return loop();
    self.intervalID = setTimeout(loop, self.delay);
  }());

  return this;
};

Typewriter.prototype.stop = function() {
  this.i = -1;
  return this;
};

Typewriter.prototype.restart = function() {
  this.clear();
  this.i = 0;
  return this.start();
};

Typewriter.prototype.clear = function() {
  this.el.innerHTML = '';
  return this;
};
