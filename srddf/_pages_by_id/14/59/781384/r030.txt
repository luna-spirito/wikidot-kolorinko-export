**Containment Procedures:** DD-095 is kept in a large containment chamber, completely sealed, with no ventilation gaps or any openings that allow material exchange with the external environment. The entire inner surface of the chamber is smoothed to limit the accumulation of dust and uncontrolled particulate matter. An internal filtration system is designed to maintain the amount of free particles at a minimal level, providing just enough to ensure the structural stability of the entity.

The chamber is equipped with basic entertainment devices, including multiple novels or chemical research books in French. In addition, an electronic device simulating visual chemical experiments is provided to meet the research needs of the entity. These items must be periodically inspected to ensure they do not decompose into small particles that could be absorbed by DD-095 unintentionally.

Every 48 hours, an assigned researcher must enter the chamber to conduct direct interaction with DD-095 through written conversation. These interaction sessions must not exceed 60 minutes. The content of the exchanges mainly revolves around the psychological state, cognition, and memory of the entity, while maintaining behavioral stability.

All objects introduced into the chamber must have their mass precisely recorded before and after contact with DD-095. In the event that a mass discrepancy exceeding the allowed threshold is detected, the chamber must be immediately locked down for inspection.

Fine particulate materials such as sand, ash, or industrial dust must not be supplied in large quantities without approval from the Supreme Leadership Committee. Any experiments related to the anomalous abilities of DD-095 must be strictly supervised.

**Description of Subject:** DD-095 is an entity composed of a large quantity of microscopic black dust particles designated as DD-095-1. The subject continuously condenses these particles to form a unified humanoid body, which it maintains at all times. The subject's face is always in an unstable state, manifesting as floating dust particles that continuously disperse away from the head region in a manner that does not follow conventional physical laws. Observation attempts have not recorded any fixed facial structure for a sufficient duration to determine identifiable features.
DD-095 states that it experiences severe difficulty in recalling its previous face, resulting in an inability to fully reconstruct the head region. This area therefore remains in an incomplete state at all times.
The constructed body has the form of an adult male, but instead of biological components, every part of the body is entirely composed of dust particles. DD-095 typically wears an old suit along with black leather shoes. Analysis indicates that the manifested clothing resembles garments produced in the 1920s. DD-095 demonstrates a tendency to maintain this outfit at all times, even without external requirement. The subject has repeatedly refused to change or switch to other clothing when requested during testing procedures. This behavior is assessed as non-functional, leaning toward cognitive or memory-related factors. Based on current observations, the hypothesis is proposed that DD-095 may be deliberately preserving a specific trace from its previous state of existence, most likely related to the period when the subject still possessed human characteristics. The outfit, in this case, functions as an identity anchor, helping maintain continuity in the subject's consciousness or sense of self.
X-ray imaging shows that the black dust particles serve as substitutes for internal organs and biological systems. These particles connect and interact with each other in a manner that perfectly simulates the functions of a human body, allowing DD-095 to possess complete mobility.

The entity claims to possess visual perception of its surrounding environment. In addition, it does not require eating, drinking, or excretion like a normal human.
DD-095 possesses the ability to collect and manipulate particulate matter such as dust, soil, ash, and sand with high precision. However, this ability does not occur instantaneously or unconditionally. To establish control, the entity must actively create an intermediate structure in the form of extremely thin threads, composed of DD-095-1 separated from its main body.
These threads are of a size observable by the naked eye, maintaining a stable connective property between DD-095 and the target material source. Once contact is established, external particles will gradually synchronize with the entity's structure, allowing DD-095 to expand its control range and manipulate them as an extension of its own body.
According to descriptions from DD-095, the entity can establish a form of metaphysical connection with particles within its control range. Through this connection, DD-095's perception is no longer limited to its main body structure, but can be distributed and observe the environment through multiple points simultaneously, corresponding to the positions of the controlled particles.
Additionally, DD-095 has the ability to restructure controlled particle clusters into DD-095-1 units with structures and characteristics similar to its main body, although at a smaller scale and with lower stability.DD-095 exhibits a calm, consistent, and cooperative attitude in all recorded interactions. There are no signs of hostility, panic, or resistance behavior. The entity appears to be fully aware of its containment status but does not express any desire to escape.
DD-095 appears to be largely unaffected by conventional physical impacts such as hitting, striking, or strong collisions, due to its body structure not existing in a fixed solid state. When subjected to force, the affected area may temporarily deform, disperse, or "break" its physical structure, however the constituent particles will quickly reconnect and restore to the original state within a very short period of time.
Observations show that this recovery process occurs almost instantly for small to medium force impacts, leaving no lasting damage. Even when a portion of the body is dispersed away from the main mass, the particles still maintain connection and tend to return, reintegrating into the overall structure of DD-095.
The entity is incapable of producing sound through voice. When communicating, DD-095 uses dust particles to form written text on flat surfaces or uses paper and pen provided. The entity's handwriting has a clear structure, with a classical style and consistency over time. The only language that DD-095 understands and uses is French. Any attempts to communicate in other languages receive no response, or are completely ignored by the entity.

**Addendum 095-A1:**
[[div style="background:#f9f9f9; border:1px solid #ccc; padding:10px; font-family:monospace; font-size:130%;"]]
+ **Origin and Initial Discovery of the Subject**
**Date:** 6/8/2004
**Location:** ███, France
After receiving multiple unofficial reports from backpacking groups regarding the appearance of a “faceless man” moving alone in a remote area within ███, France, the foundation SRDDF proceeded to gather preliminary information and verify the credibility of the sources. Descriptions from witnesses showed a high level of consistency regarding the overall shape of the subject, especially the detail of the head lacking a stable structure and the abnormal phenomenon of “dust flowing upward.” After assessing the potential threat, an MTF unit was deployed for direct verification.
Upon approaching the area, agents recorded that DD-095 was moving slowly along an abandoned dirt path, with no clear sign of orientation. The subject did not interact with the surrounding environment beyond maintaining a stable humanoid form. Throughout an observation period lasting ██ minutes, DD-095 did not exhibit hostile behavior or clear awareness of the presence of the surveillance team.
Due to the absence of a face or any frontal identifying features, determining the subject’s viewing direction was carried out based on body posture, movement direction, and the arrangement of the clothing it manifested. The MTF team agreed to assume that the front direction of DD-095 corresponded to its current movement direction, thereby determining the relative rear position to proceed with the approach.
The capture plan was designed to utilize the element of surprise and speed, with the assessment that the subject could disperse its structure and escape containment if given sufficient reaction time. When the signal was given, agents simultaneously closed in from behind and used a specialized sack to cover the entire body of DD-095 in the shortest time possible.
At the moment of contact, DD-095 displayed a clear startled reaction, demonstrated by strong oscillation of its constituent particles, especially in the head region. However, the subject did not manage to deploy any defensive or escape response before being completely isolated within the sack. No large-scale dispersal phenomenon or organized resistance was recorded throughout the containment process.
After being covered, the structure of DD-095 tended to stabilize quickly within the enclosed space, with dust particles maintaining cohesion inside the material layer. The subject did not make any significant attempt to break or escape from the container during transport.
The operation concluded successfully with no casualties or material losses. Post-incident evaluation indicated that the decisive factors were deployment speed and the ability to isolate the subject immediately. Experts assessed that if delayed by only a few seconds, DD-095 could have dispersed its structure and escaped the control perimeter, making capture significantly more complex.

**Addendum 095-B1:**
[[div style="background:#f9f9f9; border:1px solid #ccc; padding:10px; font-family:monospace; font-size:130%;"]]

+ **Interview Log (conducted via written exchange)**

**Interviewer:** Dr. François Leclerc
**Interviewed Subject:** DD-095
**Date:** 8/8/2004
**Location:** Interview Room 6

**[BEGIN LOG]**

**Dr. François:** Good morning, DD-095.
**DD-095:** Good morning, Doctor.
**Dr. François:** How is your condition today?
**DD-095:** Stable, as usual. However… I am still not truly satisfied with the way you brought me here.
**Dr. François:** Are you referring to the initial capture process?
**DD-095:** Yes, sir. I had no intention of resisting. Being suddenly covered and isolated like that… was not a pleasant experience. If you had chosen a different approach, perhaps I would have cooperated without the need for those measures.
**Dr. François:** Noted. At that time, we did not have sufficient data to assess your level of cooperation. The measures were implemented based on the worst-case scenario.
**DD-095:** I understand. But I still believe it was… unnecessary.
**Dr. François:** I will note this feedback. Now, I would like to return to the main topic. Are you willing to recount what happened to you?
**DD-095:** Of course, sir. I will try to present it as clearly as possible.
(Dr. François hands additional paper to DD-095)
DD-095 : (pauses for a long period of time before writing a lengthy response)
"I was once a chemist, pursuing a research equation that I believed could change humanity. Unfortunately, I was never able to complete my research before my final moments. Before I died, I instructed my loved ones to bury me along with the chemical compound I was researching. I remember the moment of my death, it felt like falling into a deep sleep. My mind replayed the memories of my life, and when the film of my existence ended, my consciousness faded away. But one day, my awareness returned. I felt my body shatter into dust, but then I soon realized that I could reconstruct myself, allowing me to move once again. My vision returned, and I could see the sunlight. My burial site had been forcefully burst open, leaving behind a large hole. There was a scorched cavity inside my coffin. I quickly sat up and noticed the surrounding area had been burned, as if struck by lightning. I took my first steps under the morning sunlight. I wandered aimlessly due to being lost, when I accidentally picked up a broken mirror fragment left on the road and held it up to look, only to be horrified by my own reflection, it was terrifying and disgusting. Overwhelmed, I collapsed to the ground and my body violently shattered apart. After regaining consciousness and restoring my body, I wandered outside in uncertainty, not knowing what I should do and eventually encountered your personnel." (DD-095 stops writing. Pushes the paper toward Dr. François)
**Dr. François:** Thank you.
(reads for a period of time)
**Dr. François:** Do you believe your research was successful?
**DD-095:** No, sir. I do not believe so.
**Dr. François:** Reason?
**DD-095:** Sir, I do not think so. My current form is like something cursed, far from what I once dreamed of.
(pauses briefly, then continues writing)
**DD-095:** If this is the result of that research… then it can only be considered the most shameful failure of my life.
**Dr. François:** I understand.
(stands up)
**Dr. François:** Thank you for your cooperation today.
**DD-095:** My honor, Doctor.
**Dr. François:** Goodbye.
**DD-095:** Goodbye. And… if possible, I hope future exchanges will help me feel better
(Dr. François pauses for a moment, then nods slightly)
**Dr. François:** Noted.

**[END LOG]**
[[/div]]

**Addendum 095-B4:**
[[div style="background:#f9f9f9; border:1px solid #ccc; padding:10px; font-family:monospace; font-size:130%;"]]

On 16/8/2004, DD-095 proactively made an unusual request during a routine interaction session, specifically asking to be provided with a small potted plant to be placed inside the containment chamber. When asked about the purpose, the entity did not provide a detailed explanation immediately, only affirming that the request was “not harmful” and “serves its own mental state”. After considering risk factors, including the possibility of DD-095 interacting with granular organic matter and the risk of uncontrolled disintegration, the request was forwarded to a higher approval level. A small-sized potted plant, with soil treated to minimize free particle content, was selected and introduced into the chamber under strict control conditions. The total mass of the pot and the material inside was recorded prior to transfer. Throughout the receiving process, DD-095 did not exhibit abnormal behavior or material absorption beyond the permitted threshold. The entity approached the plant cautiously, maintaining distance for the first few minutes before initiating interaction by controlling a small amount of DD-095-1 separated from the main body, forming thin streams of dust moving around the stem and leaves. No signs of direct harm to the plant’s structure were recorded during the initial observation phase. After establishing stable interaction, an additional exchange session was conducted as proposed by Dr. François to record the entity’s cognitive and emotional responses to this phenomenon.
-----
+ **Interview Log (conducted via written exchange)**
**[BEGIN LOG]**
**Interviewer:** Dr. François Leclerc
**Interviewed Entity:** DD-095
**Location:** DD-095 Containment Chamber

**Dr. François:** I see you have started interacting with the potted plant. Can you describe what you are doing?
**DD-095:** I am just… observing, in my own way.
**Dr. François:** More specifically?
**DD-095:** I separate a small portion of my structure, form it into thin streams of dust and let them move around the plant. Once contact is established, I can perceive what is happening inside it.
**Dr. François:** “Perceive” in what sense?
**DD-095:** It is difficult to express precisely. It is not like vision. Nor entirely touch. It is as if… my awareness spreads into its structure.
**Dr. François:** You are saying you can “recognize” the internal state of the plant?
**DD-095:** Yes. I can sense a form of continuous movement. Very slow, but clear. Something is circulating inside it.
**Dr. François:** Sap flow, perhaps. A normal biological process.
**DD-095:** If that is what you call it, then yes. But to me… it feels like a flow of life.
(DD-095 pauses writing for several seconds)
**DD-095:** It is different from anything I can perceive from myself currently.
**Dr. François:** How do you feel when perceiving that?
**DD-095:** Not comfortable.
**Dr. François:** Not comfortable in a negative way?
**DD-095:** It reminds me that I was once part of that. A system with life, with internal movement, with a clear purpose of existence.
**Dr. François:** And now?
**DD-095:** Now I exist, but not alive in that way.
(stop writing)
**DD-095:** When I extend my awareness into this plant, I can feel its continuity. No interruption or disintegration. Something I no longer have.
**Dr. François:** You are comparing your current state to your previous biological state?
**DD-095:** Yes. And the difference is very clear.
**Dr. François:** Does that make you feel loss?
**DD-095:** Yes.
(DD-095 writes more slowly)
**DD-095:** I cannot return to that state. I am aware of that. But perceiving it again, even indirectly… makes this reality clearer.
**Dr. François:** I understand.
(DD-095 pauses longer than usual before continuing)
**DD-095:** There is something… I have not mentioned.
**Dr. François:** You may elaborate.
**DD-095:** For a very brief moment, while I was maintaining the connection with the plant… the structure of my head region stabilized.
**Dr. François:** You say “stabilized”, specifically what does that mean?
**DD-095:** The particles stopped dispersing. They maintained relative positions, forming a consistent shape… for a short duration.
**Dr. François:** How long?
**DD-095:** Under one second. Possibly less. I cannot measure precisely.
**Dr. François:** Have you achieved a similar state before?
**DD-095:** No. This is the first time.
**Dr. François:** What was different compared to previous attempts at restructuring?
**DD-095:** I did not intentionally do it. It occurred while I was focusing on the plant… on the movement inside it.
**Dr. François:** Were you trying to imitate its structure?
**DD-095:** No. I was only observing. Or more accurately… perceiving.
(stop)
**DD-095:** But in that moment, I felt something very strange.
**Dr. François:** Describe that sensation.
**DD-095:** As if there was something… familiar. Not a clear image, but a vague form of awareness.
**Dr. François:** A form of memory?
**DD-095:** Possibly. But I could not hold onto it.
**Dr. François:** And immediately after that the structure destabilized?
**DD-095:** Yes. As soon as my focus was disrupted, everything collapsed as before.
**Dr. François:** Have you tried to recreate those conditions?
**DD-095:** I attempted to repeat the same action. But the phenomenon did not occur a second time.
**Dr. François:** Do you think contact with the plant is the direct trigger?
**DD-095:** I am not certain. But it was the only variable that differed at that time.
(short pause)
**Dr. François:** However, your ability to perceive and analyze these differences indicates you have not completely lost your ability to define yourself.
**DD-095:** Perhaps. But that does not change the fact that I am no longer what I used to be.
**Dr. François:** True. But it shows you still have the capacity to adapt to your current state.
**DD-095:** Adaptation does not equal acceptance.
**Dr. François:** No. But it is a necessary first step.
(DD-095 stops writing for approximately 6 seconds)
**DD-095:** I understand your point.
**Dr. François:** From an experimental standpoint, your interaction has not caused harm to the plant. This is a positive sign.
**DD-095:** I have no intention of causing harm.
**Dr. François:** I believe that.
(short)
**Dr. François:** Do you wish to continue keeping this plant?
**DD-095:** Yes.
**Dr. François:** Reason?
**DD-095:** It is the only thing in this environment that I can perceive as a true form of life.
(stop)
**DD-095:** If possible, I would like to take care of it myself daily.
**Dr. François:** Do you understand that this request will require further evaluation?
**DD-095:** I understand.
**Dr. François:** I will propose approval under appropriate monitoring conditions.
(DD-095 stops writing, then continues)
**DD-095:** Thank you.
**Dr. François:** You are welcome.
**[END LOG]**
-----

**Note:** The request to retain the potted plant and personally care for it daily by DD-095 has been approved after evaluation showed no significant risk. A periodic monitoring schedule has been established to ensure no uncontrolled material absorption occurs.
