**Containment Procedures:**

DD-007 is currently being held in the South Pacific region at coordinates ██.████° █, ███.███° █, in a state of submersion on the seafloor. The United States Navy’s Pacific Fleet has established submarine units operating at a safe distance to observe the seafloor, tasked with continuously monitoring its movements. If the entity shows signs of leaving the containment area, the fleet must maneuver to lure DD-007 into a pursuit state and force it back to its original position. In the event that DD-007 shows signs of slowing or ceasing pursuit, the fleet is authorized to use heavy artillery to stimulate the entity in order to maintain containment. Support vessels must coordinate carefully during resupply operations, ensuring fuel, provisions, and logistical support are consistently available to the fleet. At designated intervals, active vessels are to be rotated out and replaced with new units to maintain mission efficiency.

**Description of Subject:**

DD-007 is a living structure resembling a high-rise building with a height of 150 meters, with multiple limb-like appendages composed of unidentified material protruding from its body. These appendages are divided into two types: smaller, less numerous tendrils located around the midsection, and much larger, more numerous limbs extending from the lower foundation area. The entity possesses extremely high durability and resistance, fully withstanding high-destruction attacks such as bombs or missiles without sustaining significant damage beyond shedding extremely small fragments of concrete.

Research and hypotheses have been proposed, but none have been able to explain how DD-007’s concrete appendages can move while retaining rigidity equivalent to a solid mass.

DD-007 moves using its lower limbs, exerting an estimated ground pressure of up to 320,000 tons. Its movement always causes destruction and devastation to all objects in its path. Periodically, DD-007 will insert its tendrils into the ground, appearing to “take root” and enter a dormant state, remaining completely motionless for an indeterminate period.

DD-007 exhibits extreme hostility when provoked, violently striking its surroundings with its tendrils and relentlessly pursuing aggressors, especially when sustaining significant damage from heavy artillery or comparable attacks.

Analysis from sonar monitoring and seafloor sensors reveals that DD-007 does not “swim” in any biological sense. Instead, it operates based on a core anomalous physical phenomenon: the ability to manipulate or nullify the gravitational force acting on its own structure when submerged in seawater.

When fully submerged beneath the ocean, an anomalous event is triggered. DD-007’s immense physical mass appears to no longer exist. It is no longer bound by gravity in the usual manner, achieving a state of near-neutral buoyancy. In this state, it becomes impossibly lighter than water.

**Addendum 007-A1:**

[[div style="background:#f9f9f9; border:1px solid #ccc; padding:10px; font-family:monospace; font-size:130%;"]]
DD-007 was first discovered in ████, Mexico, on 05/02/2001, following reports of abnormal seismic activity. Investigation determined that the tremors originated from DD-007’s movement and the destruction it caused. In response, the Organization cooperated with the United States SRDDF Branch to carry out Operation Boiled Octopus, intended to guide DD-007 toward the ocean and prevent further devastation. DD-129 was deployed to erase all reality-based memories related to DD-007’s appearance, ensuring absolute secrecy regarding the entity’s existence.

**Addendum 007-B1:**

[[div style="background:#f9f9f9; border:1px solid #ccc; padding:10px; font-family:monospace; font-size:130%;"]]
+ **After-Action Report of Operation Boiled Octopus**
**Executed by:** Captain Jonathan R. Hargreaves, United States Navy
**Operational Period:** 06/02/2001 – 20/02/2001

I begin this report in my capacity as the commanding officer of the primary decoy vessel in Operation Boiled Octopus, under joint orders between the United States Navy and the United States Branch of SRDDF. Our mission was delivered concisely and directly: maintain continuous firepower, retain the attention of entity DD-007, and draw it away from the mainland at all costs.

On 06/02/2001, at dawn, the fleet formation commenced fire from waters off the western coast of Mexico. From the very first artillery volleys, DD-007’s response exceeded initial projections. The entity shifted from slow movement to deliberate pursuit, completely destroying the coastal area behind it. Recorded seismic readings confirmed that DD-007 had designated our vessel as its primary target.

During the first three days, crew morale remained stable. Accompanying MTF units responsible for anomalous monitoring and psychological warfare support recorded rapidly increasing stress levels, particularly when directly observing the entity’s scale and irrational durability. DD-007’s attacks did not immediately strike the vessel, but instead manifested as prolonged intimidation. Day after day, it continued its pursuit, closing the distance and destroying everything behind us as a warning.

From the fifth day onward, conditions began to deteriorate. Maintaining sustained high speed caused fuel consumption to exceed initial calculations. Food reserves also diminished rapidly due to the prolonged pursuit. Many crew members and MTF personnel exhibited signs of exhaustion, prolonged insomnia, and severe nervous strain. Several individuals had to be temporarily withdrawn from combat positions due to psychological breakdown after witnessing DD-007 at extremely close range for extended hours.

By the ninth day, fuel levels had become critically depleted. Without reinforcement, the mission would fail and we would be crushed pointlessly beneath DD-007. An emergency resupply request was transmitted, and on the eleventh day, a supply vessel successfully reached formation under severe weather conditions. Refueling operations were conducted under combat status, with DD-007 only a few kilometers away. This marked the most tense moment of the entire operation; any error could have allowed the entity to overtake and annihilate us.

The thirteenth day marked a clear escalation. DD-007 began exhibiting uncontrolled rage behavior, increasing pursuit speed and destroying all objects within reach. According to SRDDF specialists, the entity’s behavior had become fully anchored to the decoy vessel. The final decision was made: my vessel would serve as the sacrificial target.

On 20/02/2001, at the predetermined containment location in the South Pacific Ocean, the evacuation order was issued. Helicopters approached under extremely hazardous conditions. The crew and MTF forces evacuated the vessel in a state of controlled chaos. No casualties occurred during evacuation, despite DD-007 being close enough to cause severe structural vibrations across the deck.

After the vessel was abandoned, DD-007 approached and completely destroyed the target. Immediately thereafter, the entity gradually lost pursuit momentum, sank to the ocean floor, and embedded its appendages into the seabed. Sensors confirmed that DD-007 entered a “rooted” state and became immobile.

Operation Boiled Octopus was declared successful.

As the direct commanding officer, I confirm that the sacrifice of the vessel was necessary and that no other viable alternative existed at that time. Had the operation failed, the consequences for the mainland would have been uncontrollable.

**Note:** A permanent maintenance containment procedure was subsequently established, ensuring that DD-007 remains isolated in the South Pacific under continuous monitoring.
