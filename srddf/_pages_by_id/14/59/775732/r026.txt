**Containment Procedures:**

All 15 instances of DD-104-2 are to be collectively housed in a spacious containment chamber equipped with all necessary amenities and recreational tools, including a fully stocked bookshelf, DVD players, and video game consoles. Cleaning and food supply personnel must be completely deaf and are strictly instructed not to look at any potentially suspicious text. Multiple surveillance cameras have been installed within the containment chamber; all footage is processed through an AI-based Phase 1 filtering system before being relayed to supervisors for monitoring any anomalies occurring within the chamber.

In the event of personnel within the containment chamber being affected by the anomalous properties of DD-104-2 individuals, anesthetic gas is to be immediately pumped into the chamber to neutralize the affected individuals, who will be secured on-site. A designated terminal exists within the containment chamber to receive requests from DD-104-2 instances; all requests are filtered through an AI system before being processed externally.

**Description of Subject:**

DD-104 is an anomalous memetic protocol with transmissible properties. On April 1, 2018, a research intern named Lain Eucher transmitted a message to surrounding doctors and interns, affecting a total of 13 individuals. According to AI analysis of security footage, all affected individuals simultaneously uttered the phrase, ''"Site-14 does not exist"'', repeating it 14 times. The 14 individuals were promptly anesthetized, taken into containment, and designated as DD-104-2.

At the same time, a secondary anomalous event was recorded: Site-14, which housed numerous research divisions, personnel, and advanced equipment, vanished without a trace. The location where Site-14 once stood was reduced to an empty, barren field, devoid of any physical evidence of its prior existence. The cause of this catastrophic loss remains undetermined, but prevailing theories suggest a connection to the unusual behavior exhibited by the DD-104-2 individuals. However, prior records indicate that all 14 affected personnel were of sound intellect and mental stability, with no prior records of anomalous tendencies.

DD-104 is hypothesized to possess reality-altering properties when a full set of 14 affected individuals exists, allowing it to materialize the contents of its propagated message. Its influence is strictly limited to verbal and written communication, meaning that its transmission can be controlled via third-party communication methods to prevent direct exposure. Experimental research on DD-104-2 is strictly prohibited due to the high risk of personnel loss, potential proliferation of additional DD-104-2 instances, and the extreme danger associated with its anomalous effects.

**Addendum 104-W3:**
A containment breach incident resulted in an additional individual being affected by DD-104, increasing the total number of DD-104-2 instances to 15. In response, the organization swiftly implemented and deployed new technological security measures to enhance containment protocols to their current state.
