**Special Containment Procedures:**

The area surrounding DD-050 in ████, Estonian SSR, Soviet Union, has been fenced off to prevent any living beings from entering. A maximum of six agents and two researchers are authorized to reside within DD-050 for research purposes. Necessary supplies, including food and equipment, are delivered via radio communication requests. All actions and movements within DD-050 must be carried out in groups.

**Description of Subject:**

DD-050 is a town located in ████, Estonian SSR, Soviet Union, covering an area of 1.5 km². The town contains numerous houses, yet no living creatures can be found within its vicinity. Research indicates that signs of past habitation exist, suggesting that residents lived there many years ago.

DD-050 exhibits anomalous properties wherein any group of fewer than three individuals entering the town will disappear along with any belongings they carry. This disappearance occurs when a group of fewer than three is out of sight of another group of three or more. Any recording devices attached to or carried by the Foundation's drones experience abnormal interference when monitoring DD-050. Satellite imagery targeting DD-050 is consistently misaligned, capturing only its surroundings without displaying the town itself.

**Addendum 050-A1:**

DD-050 was discovered following multiple reports of missing persons who were last known to have visited the area. The Foundation quickly secured the site and established containment measures after confirming the town's anomalous properties.


**Addendum 050-B8:**

During extensive examinations and investigations of DD-050's residences, agents discovered a house containing two long-decomposed corpses in the kitchen. A search of a kitchen cabinet revealed an old notebook with only two recorded pages:

[[div style="background:#000; border:1px solid #ccc; padding:10px; font-family:monospace; font-size:130%; color:black;"]]
 **''February 23, 1985**
 
 I am Märt Ojamets, currently living with my wife, Piret Ojamets, in the town of [DATA EXPUNGED]. We are in utter despair. One by one, people in the town have begun to disappear without any explanation. Search parties have been formed, but they have found nothing. The population is dwindling.
 
 **March 13, 1985**
 
 The village is becoming emptier. Something strange is happening to us. Some believe it to be a curse.
 
 **March 27, 1985**
 
 I must confess our sins. During a time of famine in the town, a unit of Soviet soldiers passed through on a mission. They ruled us with strict control and violence, and some people conspired to eliminate them, unable to endure their oppression. Many villagers supported the idea out of sheer hatred for these Ivans, though my wife and I did not participate. One night, as the soldiers slept in their assigned lodgings, the townspeople stormed their quarters, tied them up, and dragged them to the town square. With crude tools, they tortured and cursed them in a display of utter brutality. Before his death, the commander spat his last words: "Your entire town will not escape me. Glory to the great Soviet Union." Then, Toomas drove a pitchfork through his throat. I believe their spirits have cursed this place.
 
 **April 4, 1985**
 
 Our neighbors have disappeared. My wife and I are terrified beyond reason.
 
 **April 6, 1985**
 
 We do not dare to leave our house. The silence in the town is horrifying.
 
 **April 7, 1985**
 
 It seems that no one else is left in the town. I hope that is not the case.
 
 **April 8, 1985**
 
 Piret is in complete panic. She refuses to eat."
[[/div]]

The journal entries end here. The remains of the couple were buried within the town. In a place where solidified waste is found site within DD-050, multiple skeletal remains were discovered, believed to belong to the Soviet soldiers mentioned in the journal. The remains exhibited no anomalous properties and were buried after thorough deliberation. The anomalous nature of the town remains unchanged.
