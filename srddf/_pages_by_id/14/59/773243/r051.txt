+ Welcome to the SRDDF Foundation!
------

Welcome personnel to the Central Records Archive System of the SRDDF Foundation.

All data stored within this system is the property of SRDDF and is protected by current information security protocols. All activities involving the access, copying, modification, or distribution of data are logged and monitored automatically.

Please ensure that you only access records necessary for your assigned duties and within the scope of your authorization. Certain documents stored herein contain information regarding anomalies, supernatural phenomena, extranormal entities, or data capable of directly affecting the physical health, mental condition, cognition, and memory of the reader. Ignoring warnings attached to records may result in serious consequences, including cognitive damage, anomalous contamination, or other unidentified effects.

Any unauthorized access to records beyond an individual's clearance level will be handled in accordance with Foundation regulations. Disciplinary actions may include warnings, suspension of access privileges, reduction of clearance level, investigative isolation, or other measures depending on the severity of the violation.

If you encounter abnormal data, records appearing outside their authorized catalog, or content showing signs of self-alteration, cease reading immediately and report the incident to the nearest information management department.

Personnel safety begins with procedural compliance.

We wish you success in carrying out your assigned duties.
