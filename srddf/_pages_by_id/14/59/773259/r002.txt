+ **What Are We?**

[[div style="border: 2px solid #ccc; padding: 10px; margin-top: 15px; background: #f9f9f9; text-align: center;"]]
[[image http://srddf-vn.wikidot.com/local--files/about/SRDDF_Logo.png width="300px"]]
[[/div]]

SRDDF, short for **Secret Research Discovery Development Facility**, is a global anomalous research, management, and response foundation. The foundation's core mission is to protect human civilization from abnormal threats while expanding humanity's understanding of the undiscovered laws of reality.

Within the foundation's classification system, the term DD (Discovered Deviation) is used to designate anomalous entities, phenomena, locations, objects, or processes that have been discovered and confirmed. DDs may range from minor deviations from natural laws to phenomena capable of affecting reality on a global or inter-reality scale.

SRDDF was established on July 21, 1945, following an agreement reached at the Yalta Conference between the Soviet Union, the United States, the Ottoman Empire, and the United Kingdom. Initially, the foundation was only an international research agency with limited resources, tasked with collecting and analyzing abnormal phenomena that emerged during the final stages of the Second World War.

Not long after its establishment, SRDDF was forced to confront the first anomalous crisis in its history, designated DD-001. This event exposed the vast gap between humanity's capabilities and the anomalous threats existing beyond the scope of contemporary scientific understanding. Despite suffering significant losses, the foundation successfully neutralized the threat with assistance from multiple special resources, thereby laying the foundation for the modern anomalous management system that continues to operate to this day.

Following the DD-001 crisis, the Supreme Leader of the foundation proposed a doctrine known as SRDDF Expansionism. This doctrine argues that humanity cannot survive in the long term by merely attempting to isolate or avoid what it does not understand. Instead, the study, control, and selective utilization of anomalous resources are necessary to ensure the survival and advancement of civilization.

Based on this principle, SRDDF continuously expands its operational reach, network of research facilities, military capabilities, and scientific resources in order to maintain a position strong enough to manage phenomena beyond ordinary comprehension. To the foundation, knowledge is not merely a tool for explaining the world, but also a means of ensuring humanity's survival within a reality far larger and more complex than previously understood.

+ MISSION STATEMENT

SRDDF is a global anomalous research and management foundation. Established to confront phenomena beyond ordinary understanding, the foundation bears the responsibility of protecting human civilization, expanding the boundaries of knowledge, and ensuring that threats beyond the laws of nature do not become the cause of the collapse of the known world.

Throughout its operations, SRDDF upholds five fundamental principles embedded within the foundation's very name.

* **Secret** — Not all knowledge should be made public. Certain information has the potential to cause widespread panic, alter public perception, or enable hostile forces to exploit anomalous phenomena. Therefore, SRDDF maintains strict security systems to ensure that dangerous information is accessed only by individuals possessing the necessary authority and capability to handle it.

* **Research** — Every anomaly must be observed, analyzed, and documented. Rather than relying on speculation or superstition, SRDDF applies scientific methods to understand the nature of unexplained phenomena. Every piece of data collected represents a step toward narrowing the gap between humanity and the unknown.

* **Discovery** — Reality is far greater than humanity has ever imagined. Expeditions into unmapped regions of space, isolated dimensions, unknown civilizations, and newly emerging phenomena are an inseparable part of the foundation's mission. Only through continued exploration can humanity understand its place within the universe.

* **Development** — Knowledge has value only when transformed into practical capability. The results of SRDDF research are utilized to develop technologies, strategies, doctrines, and new response methods aimed at improving humanity's ability to survive increasingly complex threats. The foundation seeks not only to understand anomalies but also to adapt to them.

* **Facility** — Knowledge cannot endure without the infrastructure necessary to protect and sustain it. Hundreds of research facilities, archival centers, testing zones, and operational bases maintained by SRDDF form a network extending across the world and numerous regions beyond human territory. These installations serve as the foundation for all research, management, and development activities conducted by the foundation.

These five principles are not only the meaning behind the name SRDDF, but also the guiding framework behind every decision made within the foundation.

Protect humanity. Expand knowledge. Prepare for the unknown.
