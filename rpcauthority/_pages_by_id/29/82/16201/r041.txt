<img src="http://www.rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/component:icons/gamma-red.png" style="float: left; margin: 0 15px 25px 0"><strong>Registered Phenomena Code:</strong> 616
<br><br>
<strong>Object Class:</strong> Gamma-Red
<br><br>
<strong>Hazard Types:</strong> Extra-Dimensional, Extreme Temperature, Sensory, Animated
<br><br>
<strong>Containment Protocols:</strong> Under no circumstances are any non-level 4 or lower Authority Personnel allowed to visit Installation-616 (area in which RPC-616-1 is held) unless approved by a member of the Global Director Council. Testing applications are to be submitted no less than 2 months in advance with a complete list of goals, prospected outcomes, and test subjects.
<br><br>
Installation-616 is to be fenced off using electrically-powered voltage gates with a running 24/7 and domed off using phase change material with an opaque coloring to prevent any possible sight of RPC-616 by unauthorized agents or the public. <s>Authority agents are to patrol the perimeter of the fence, only stopping every 6 hours to recite the phrase: "Бог сохранит меня"</s> (<em>See Updated Containment Protocols for further information.</em>)
<br><br>
Installation-616 is to be monitored daily through the use of video-drones capable of resisting extreme temperatures. No fewer than three drones, piloted by Authority Personnel who have scored at least a 24 out of 32 on the Schweps-Bridger Neuropsychology Cognitohazard exam, are to monitor the area for any major temperature, geography, or population changes. If any of these changes arise, Authority personnel monitoring Installation-616 are to immediately report to the acting Head Researcher.
<br><br>
<strong>Updated Containment Protocols:</strong> As of 10/6/20██, no human Authority personnel are allowed to surround Installation-616 due to the effective cognitohazardous threat arising from RPC-616. All monitoring should be done remotely by drone. Any humans seen around the area of RPC-616 should be apprehended and taken to Site-008 and administered Class A amnesics. Any individual of the <em>Capra aegagrus hircus</em> species should be eliminated and immediately incinerated.
<br><br>
<strong>Description:</strong> RPC-616-1 is the classification given to an anomalous lava-filled crater currently located in the northern Siberian section of ████████, Russia. How the lava or hole formed in its current location is currently unknown to Authority personnel; however, research into the origins of RPC-616 is ongoing. Although the average temperatures around RPC-616-1 reach -62 degrees Celsius, the center of Installation-616 can reach temperatures up to 500 degrees Celsius. Even with this extreme temperature surrounding the area, the ice and snow around the area do not exhibit any signs of melting or evaporation.
<br><br>
From RPC-616-1, screaming and sounds of agony can be heard by personnel surrounding Installation-616; however, no recording of said screaming can be picked up though drone surveillance. Personnel surrounding Installation-616 who perceive the screaming steadily for more than 6 hours resolve to depression, anxiety, and in some cases, psychosis. This effect is not exhibited in Authority drone pilots who have monitored the area for more than 6 hours straight. The ongoing theory for this effect is that RPC-616 emanates a secondary Cognito-hazardous effect leading to various forms of mental degradation.
<br><br>
A tertiary effect arises in humans and individuals of the <em>Capra aegagrus hircus</em> (Domesticated Goat) species surrounding Installation-616. Humans are seen to experience auditory and visual hallucinations along with extreme mental degradation. Both humans and individuals of <em>Capra aegagrus hircus</em> are seen to migrate towards RPC-616 in an attempt to reach the crater. So far, no successful attempts have been observed. Due to observation and various incidents resulting in increasingly dangerous outbursts of lava, it is hypothesized that once a certain number of individuals reach the crater of RPC-616, [DATA EXPUNGED] will occur resulting in an <a href="http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-963/" target=" _blank">omega class-"End of Tribulation" event</a> which will eliminate approximately 95% of all species on Earth. 
<br><br>
After consultation with various Nivaldian texts and following discovery of RPC-███, it was hypothesized RPC-616 is a possible spawning ground for extra-dimensional beings capable of causing mass extinction events. Further investigation is necessary to determine the true origins and capabilities of RPC-616 and whether it creates these beings or if it is simply an opening from other dimensions. 
<br><br>
RPC-616-2 is an unidentifiable humanoid figure who originates from RPC-616-1. Every instance of RPC-616-2 is uniform save for the type of clothing each figure wears. Also, every instance of RPC-616-2 follows the same course of direction after leaving RPC-616-1: RPC-616-2 climbs out of RPC-616-1, it begins to walk towards the eastern wall of Installation-616 until it reaches the phase change material barrier at the end of Installation-616. Once it reaches this point, it fades away into a dust-like substance back into RPC-616-1. It is unknown whether instances of RPC-616-2 are recycled and used multiple times; however, no two instances have been seen to wear the same attire <s>and as of now, no more than 3 figures out at one time have been observed.
</s> (<em>Load Addendum 616-B for further information.</em>)
<br><br>
<strong>Discovery:</strong> RPC-616-1 came into Authority knowledge after reports of nearby villagers hearing screaming and sounds of torture coming from a nearby area. Following these reports of screaming, mass amounts of cases involving psychosis, newly onset anxiety, depression, and other degenerative mental illnesses arose. 
<br><br>
Along with the new cases of psychotic diseases, reports of villagers near RPC-616-1 having dreams of a massive horned figure surrounded by faceless human-like formations calling people towards Installation-616 have been found. Although sporadically seen during the initial reporting of RPC-616-1, such descriptions began to increase after Authority intervention. One such description written on a note was found in the house of a 76-year old Russian man. The man was found to be dead by hanging after cutting pentagram-shaped pictures into his forearms and legs. Although having cut major arteries during the process, barely any blood was seen under or around the man which could suggest murder through cult activity.

function toggleCollapse(collId) {
    activatorId = collId + "-activator";
    activator = document.getElementById(activatorId);
    collapsibleDiv = document.getElementById(collId);
    if (activator.dataset.status == "closed") {
         collapsibleDiv.className = "collapsible-open";
         activator.dataset.status = "open";
         activator.innerHTML = activator.dataset.opentext;
    } else if (activator.dataset.status == "open") {
         collapsibleDiv.className = "collapsible-closed";
         activator.dataset.status = "closed";
         activator.innerHTML = activator.dataset.closedtext;
    }
}
