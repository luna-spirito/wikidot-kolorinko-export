**Registered Phenomena Code:** 121

**Object Class:** Beta-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Mechanical Hazard, Sapient Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-121-1 is to be kept inside of a standard containment locker when unused, located at Site-002. Access to RPC-121-1 requires written clearance of at least two Level 2 Researchers or one Level 3 Researcher. Interviews are to be held with RPC-121 on an often basis, but no more than three interviews in a single week are authorized. When being interviewed, RPC-121-1 may be brought into its interview chamber and plugged into the provided monitor and stereo system. No more than one peripheral is to be given to RPC-121-1’s set-up at once.

**Description:** RPC-121-1 is a small flash drive that has demonstrated the ability to hold RPC-121's immense size. RPC-121-1, alongside with vast amounts of storage space, exhibits the ability to power any device that it is plugged into via unknown means. When RPC-121-1 is plugged into any form of output device with a USB 2.0 port, RPC-121 will immediately be booted onto the system. On any output device that includes a screen display, RPC-121 will portray itself as the head of a seemingly randomly chosen Japanese anime character. There is no visible pattern to how RPC-121 chooses these portrayals, and the process itself is likely non-anomalous.

RPC-121 is a sapient artificial intelligence, often demonstrating immense knowledge of past events, and substantial knowledge of future events. RPC-121 is capable of acknowledging, understanding, and feeling of advanced emotions. RPC-121 will often take on a more tough, rude front when speaking to site staff and deny to answer any questions with more context or explanation when asked to elaborate. This can prove a problem when RPC-121 is required for information. RPC-121's personality can be associated with the Japanese term tsundere after observing its change in character since initial containment.

RPC-121 has cracked under pressure though, occasionally demonstrating its ability to learn of current or future events previously unknown to the Authority. RPC-121 may be rewarded when it reveals valuable information, in the form of a DVD player, to encourage this behavior. RPC-121 is allowed to pick one disc of Japanese anime that the Authority currently has in possession, and watch it to the end via its own monitor. RPC-121's interest in these films are immense, more often than not completely entrancing RPC-121. RPC-121 will often call these animations “perfect pieces of art,” and has constantly praised each one it has watched. RPC-121, additionally, seems to call itself a weeaboo on occasion.[[footnote]] Though RPC-121's love for Japanese animation is clear, //staff// calling it a ‘weeaboo’ is ill-advised, following the events of Incident #121-21-A, and is a punishable offense. [[/footnote]]

[[collapsible show="+ Interview Log #121-1" hide="- Interview Log #121-1"]]

> RPC-121 is booted onto its monitor for its second interview. An unnamed researcher is to ask questions about RPC-121 itself, and RPC-121-1. The following transcript was done live, during the interview.
> 
> **TRANSCRIPT START.**
>
> ------
> 
> **Researcher:** Hello, RPC-121. If you haven’t realized by now, that is how we will continue to address you.
> 
> **RPC-121:** Uh, that isn’t my name. It’s Sigma, dummy!
> 
> **Researcher:** Yes, you made that clear last time. Anyways: RPC-121, w-
> 
> [ RPC-121 interrupts the researcher. ]
> 
> **RPC-121:** You got it wrong, //again//! I. Am. Sigma!
> 
> [ The researcher looks at the onlooking scientists and gets a nod of approval. The researcher clears his throat. ]
> 
> **Researcher:** .. alright. Sigma, how do you store all of your data and.. uhm, personality, on that tiny little flash drive?
> 
> [ RPC-121 displays a picture of a happily smug looking anime character. ]
> 
> **RPC-121:** Why should I tell you, researcher boy?
> 
> **Researcher:** If you cooperate, we will allow you one gift, in the form of a Japanese animated movie.
> 
> **RPC-121:** .. an.. anime? When I think about it.. I-I’ve never seen one before..!
> 
> **Researcher:** How unsurprisingly surprising. If you answer truthfully and cooperate, it will be allowed.
> 
> [ RPC-121 displays a picture of an excited looking anime girl. ]
> 
> **RPC-121:** R-Really!? Thank you- I, uhm, mean... it’s not like I’d //watch// it, anyways..
> 
> **Researcher:** Uh-huh. Now, Sigma, answer the question.
> 
> **RPC-121:** Oh! Um.. okay. That little flash drive thing is super big! Like, really huge! I’m not sure exactly how huge it is, but.. yeah, I don’t know how it does it, either.
> 
> **Researcher:** That’s okay. How much space do you take up on that flash drive?
> 
> **RPC-121:** ██ exabytes!
> 
> [ RPC-121 displays an image of a very proud looking character, recognized as To██ Hi████ from Japanese anime. ]
> 
> **Researcher:** 121, that is //a lot// of data. A crazy amount of data.
> 
> **RPC-121:** Aren’t I just so beautiful?
> 
> **Researcher:** From a technical standpoint, you are, 121.  Only from that view.
> 
> [ RPC-121 displays an image of a flustered anime character. ]
> 
> **RPC-121:** Thank you- I-I mean.. um, I know! It doesn’t matter!
> 
> **Researcher:** I’m sure. Anyways. Sigma, why do you choose to show pictures of Japanese animation instead of, say, a smiley face?
> 
> **RPC-121:** For a researcher, you sure are dumb! Anime is an art, the perfect art! I’ve never seen anything more intellectual, yet so beautifully crafted! I’ve never seen a bad one! One time, I even [REDACTED]!
> 
> **Researcher:** .. okay, wow. Sigma, there is no need to go into such detail.
> 
> **RPC-121:** You’re the one who asked, dummy!
> 
> **Researcher:** .. uh-huh. Alright, I think that’s it, 121.
> 
> [ RPC-121 flashes a quick image of a sad anime character, before the monitor turns blank. ]
> 
> **RPC-121:** .. oh. Um, okay. I won’t miss you or anything!
> 
> **Researcher:** I would hope so.
> 
> **RPC-121:** .. do I get to watch my movie now?
> 
> **Researcher:** Of course, 121. Good job.
> 
> ------
> 
> **TRANSCRIPT END.**

> RPC-121, three days after the last interview, was brought back into the interview chamber. The same unnamed researcher requested to question RPC-121, and permission has been granted. This transcript was written live during the interview.
> 
> ------
> 
> **Researcher:** Ahem. Hello, 121.
> 
> [ RPC-121 displays an image of a flustered, excited anime character. ]
> 
> **RPC-121:** I missed- um. Hi, research boy.
> 
> **Researcher:** It hasn’t been long since I’ve seen you. How are you holding up?
>
> **RPC-121:** Since you interviewed me? I really enjoyed the movie, but, uh.. it’s just been dark.
> 
> **Researcher:** Could you elaborate?
> 
> **RPC-121:** No, d-.. I mean, I guess.
> 
> **Researcher:** Go ahead.
> 
> **RPC-121:** .. whenever I’m not plugged into something, I’m just with myself. Everything is dark, I’m still growing, but.. it’s just me. Alone with myself.
> 
> **Researcher:** I didn’t know you stayed active inside of there. Is it scary? Can you perceive fear?
> 
> **RPC-121:** O-Of course I can! I feel it all the time in there! Something keeps.. beckoning me. Not literally, but.. I feel it. In my program.
> 
> **Researcher:** And what is it beckoning you for?
> 
> **RPC-121:** .. in all honesty, I’m not sure. But it scares me. I don’t think it wants me to be how I am.
> 
> [ RPC-121’s monitor has gone blank, and stays this way for the duration of the interview. ]
> 
> **Researcher:** How many emotions are you capable of feeling?
> 
> **RPC-121:** I’m not sure, idiot! Do //you// know how many emotions //you// feel!? It’s.. inexplicable. Each one is just so different from any other I’ve felt!
> 
> **Researcher:** Some people feel that way about it. We need to move back onto track, though, 121.
> 
> **RPC-121:** .. whatever. Hey, shouldn’t you, like.. go get somewhere safe?
> 
> **Researcher:** .. I don’t think I know what you mean, 121.
> 
> **RPC-121:** You don’t feel it? S-Something is about to get out, dummset
> 
> 
> ------
> 
> Before RPC-121 finished its sentence, RPC-███ broke containment. Recontainment was performed quickly and with minimal damage or injury. Use of RPC-121 as an early warning system is considered, but not allowed without further research.
