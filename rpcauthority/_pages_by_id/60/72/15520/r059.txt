**Containment Protocols:** As of 8/15/1969, the AEDF Jericho-10 satellite is currently within Saturn's orbit with the task of observing RPC-983.

A coverup has been implemented, labeling RPC-983 as a minor moon orbiting far from Saturn. Several field agents have been deployed within NASA headquarters in order to remove and edit all information regarding RPC-983's anomalous nature.

As of 2/9/2017, no containment procedures are required for RPC-983. Please see the addenda for more information regarding RPC-983's neutralization.

**Description:** RPC-983 is a Buddha head that is entirely composed of stone and is 71.6 meters in radius. Currently, RPC-983 is in orbit about the planet Saturn, orbiting roughly 1.1 thousand km from Saturn's surface.

RPC-983 has been found to be tidally locked with Saturn, despite the position of the object. This is considered to be anomalous as RPC-983 orbits too far away from Saturn for this to occur naturally.

RPC-983 is sapient, being able to communicate through emission of radio waves. The process by which RPC-983 is able to produce and emit radio waves is unknown. RPC-983 does not actively communicate with personnel on its own accord, but most attempts to communicate with the object have been successful. RPC-983 views the planet Saturn as a relative or possibly a romantic partner. RPC-983 claims to have known an entity which resides as or within the planet Saturn, although RPC-983 has not delivered specific details regarding said entity.

RPC-983 speaks in standard morse code. The reason for this is currently unknown.

= **Log I**

<Begin Log>

Command: Hello RPC-983.

RPC-983: WHO IS RPC-983?

Command: You are RPC-983. We have found you here and have given you that designation. Do you have another name?

RPC-983: NO I DO NOT. CALL ME WHAT YOU WISH.

Command: And are you bothered by my presence at all?

RPC-983: NO. I WELCOME YOU.

Command: Great. So, I have some questions to ask you. May I?

RPC-983: DO AS YOU WISH.

Command: Great. Thank you. Now, how did you get here?

RPC-983: I DO NOT KNOW. I'VE BEEN WITH ARAH SINCE I WAS CREATED FROM NOTHING. WE'VE GROWN VERY CLOSE.

Command: Who is this Arah?

RPC-983: HER. SHE IS IN FRONT OF ME.

[3 minutes of silence.]

Command: I can not see her.

RPC-983: ARE YOU BLIND? SHES THE ONLY THING I CAN SEE FOR MILES. SHES OF A GOLDEN HUE, AND HAS A BEAUTIFUL RING WRAPPED AROUND HER. LOOK.

Command: Are you referring to Saturn?

RPC-983: SATURN? HER NAME IS ARAH.

Command: Apologies. Arah, or Saturn, is a planet. We doubt that she is alive at all. How—

RPC-983: NO. SHE IS STILL HERE. I KNOW SHE IS.

Command: May I speak?

RPC-983: APOLOGIES. YOU MAY SPEAK.

Command: OK. How have you become close?

RPC-983: WE ARE INTERLINKED. WE SHARE MUTUAL LOVE. CAN YOU NOT FEEL IT?

Command: Yes. I understand. How long have you been here?

RPC-983: MANY YEARS. I AM AWAITING HER RETURN.

Command: From where?

RPC-983: [Unresponsive.]

Command: RPC-983?

RPC-983: [Unresponsive.]

Command: Did Arah give you a name?

RPC-983: NO. SHE COULD NOT SPEAK.

Command: Why was that?

RPC-983: SHE WAS BORN WITHOUT A VOICE. THE REASON IS NOT KNOWN.

Command: Why—

RPC-983: I DESIRE TO BE LEFT ALONE NOW.

Command: Can I ask one more question?

RPC-983: YES.

Command: Do you know what you are?

RPC-983: [Unresponsive.]

Command: RPC-983?

RPC-983: WE NEVER KNEW.

<End Log>

<Begin Log>

Command: Hello RPC-983.

Command: You can call me Doctor Ings.

Command: To make sure you are alright.

RPC-983: WHY ME? I AM FINE HERE. I AM NOT DESERVING OF COMFORT. I NEED TO WAIT FOR ARAH.

Command: I understand. But I take pleasure in making others happy. You seem very tense. Plus, it's my duty.

RPC-983: I AM FINE.

Command: And dismissive.

RPC-983: THERE IS NOTHING TO HIDE, THEREFORE I AM NOT DISMISSIVE.

[Silence for 4 minutes.]

RPC-983: WHERE DO YOU COME FROM?

Command: I thought you wanted to be alone.

RPC-983: JUST ANSWER ME.

Command: I come from Earth. I live amongst many other people, meeting creatures like you and making them feel better.

RPC-983: WHAT IS EARTH?

Command: Earth is a planet, like Saturn— although I know Arah is more than that. We are able to walk and move on Earth. We can also swim in water, fly in the sky, and do lots of fun things.

RPC-983: SO YOU LIVE ON EARTH? AND YOU CAN MOVE?

Command: Yes. And it isn't lonely here at all. You seem awfully lonely here.

RPC-983: NO, I HAVE ARAH.

[Silence for 3 seconds.]

RPC-983: BUT EARTH SOUNDS VERY NICE. WHAT DOES IT FEEL LIKE TO BE THERE?

Command: It feels fairly normal. Imagine what you feel like right now, waiting for Arah. It feels like that; normal. Although we do have temperatures. It can be hot or cold.

RPC-983: I DO NOT KNOW WHAT A TEMPERATURE IS.

Command: There are two; hot and cold. Hot is when the temperature rises, and objects turn to liquid or gas. It is what causes fire. The Sun is very hot. Cold is the exact opposite. Most things turn solid when they are cold. Cold freezes people, like Sat— like the other planets here.

RPC-983: YOU SAY THAT ARAH IS COLD?

Command: My bad.

RPC-983: HOT SOUNDS VERY HARSH. I WOULD NOT WANT TO BE HOT.

Command: Sometimes it can be very nice.

RPC-983: AND COLD. COLD SOUNDS STRANGE TO ME. YOU SUGGEST THAT ARAH IS COLD, BUT I KNOW SHE. SHE. SHE IS NOT.

Command: Are—

RPC-983: SHE CANNOT BE COLD. I AM NOT COLD I HAVE ARAH. I—

Command: Are you alright?

[Silence for 1 minute.]

RPC-983: I AM NOT COLD, AM I?

Command: What do you mean by cold?

RPC-983: I MEAN— NEVERMIND. ARAH IS HERE. SHE IS MAKING ME HOT. PLEASANT HOT, NOT DEADLY HOT.

Command: You mean warm?

RPC-983: YES. WE ARE WARM. TOGETHER. WARM.

<End Log>
