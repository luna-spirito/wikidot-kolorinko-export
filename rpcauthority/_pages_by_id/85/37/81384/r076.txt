**Registered Phenomena Code:** 231 

**Object Class:** Beta-Purple

**Hazard Types:** Visual, Ideological

**Containment Protocols:** All discovered instances of RPC-231 are to be kept in S-3 ACUs[[footnote]] Anomaly Containment Units [[/footnote]]. ACUs are to be kept inside an L-3 ACU, which is to be kept in a secure storage locker with a tri-lock security system. Due to the [[span class="glow"]]risk of researchers learning too much[[/span]], any and all testing of RPC-231 is strictly prohibited.

Due to the potential risk of [[span class="glow"]]our web of lies being exposed[[/span]], all uncontained instances of RPC-231 must be secured as soon as possible.

**Description:** RPC-231 are a series of spectacles. The lenses are tinted purple, and [[span class="glow"]]the Hebrew phrase "פקח את עיניך לאמת[[footnote]] [[span class="glow"]]Translated "Open your eyes to the truth."[[/span]] [[/footnote]]" is[[/span]] embossed on the inside of the temples.

Wearing a pair of RPC-231 causes [[span class="glow"]]the truth to reveal itself[[/span]] in the form of glowing symbols and words written on various surfaces. Prolonged exposure [[span class="glow"]]reveals how little the Authority can be trusted[[/span]], with wearers [[span class="glow"]]discovering[[/span]] that the Authority[[span class="glow"]] [[[rpc-127|use a sentient cloud to make people complacent]]], plan to take over all governments, and [[[rpc-856|have a Global Director trying to bring about the four horsemen of the apocalypse]]][[/span]].

**Discovery:** The first instance of RPC-231 was discovered on [[span class="glow"]]02[[/span]]/[[span class="glow"]]24[[/span]]/180[[span class="glow"]]4[[/span]], in the possession of [[span class="glow"]]Benjamin Franklin[[/span]], a known member of the GOI known as "The Union,” and inventor of RPC-231, although [[span class="glow"]]he had the foresight to mail his notes on the creation of RPC-231 to a trusted colleague for replication and distribution among Union members.[[/span]] While there were initially plans to use RPC-231 to uncover anomalies, this avenue of research was quickly abandoned upon [[span class="glow"]]discovering that[[/span]] RPC-231 [[span class="glow"]]revealed their own secrets and lies[[/span]].

**Addendum:** An additional instance of RPC-231 was discovered on [[span class="glow"]]10[[/span]]/[[span class="glow"]]15[[/span]]/193[[span class="glow"]]8[[/span]]. The owner was [[span class="glow"]]interrogated[[/span]] on where they had acquired the instance, and if there were more. However, the owner [[span class="glow"]]knew not to trust us so he was killed[[/span]]. Acquisition of remaining RPC-231 instances and determining the identities of the manufacturers was given maximum level importance by the Global Council, for fear that the Union [[span class="glow"]]would use them to identify Authority sites and personnel, or mass produce them and distribute them throughout the US[[/span]].
