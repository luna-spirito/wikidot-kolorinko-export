**Object Class:** Gamma-Red

[[include component:hazards

[[==]]
**Containment Protocols:** RPC-440 is to be contained within a standard humanoid containment chamber with basic furnishing and appliances. The chamber containing RPC-440 must be devoid of any form of observation and/or geological surveying devices, this includes the usage of sonar imaging systems near RPC-440's containment chamber. Inspection of the chamber for any form of breach that may allow a direct line of sight to the object is to be undertaken on a biweekly basis.

Feeding is done through a dumbwaiter three times a day; meals must contain the standard daily amount of nutrients needed for a teenage human female. RPC-440 may be granted a daily meal reduction at certain times of the year per religious reasons. RPC-440 is to be provided SSRI antidepressant pills on a biweekly basis, per Dr. Edward Mason's prescription.

Under no circumstances should personnel assigned to RPC-440 initiate conversations and/or maintain any form of relationship with RPC-440. Violation of this order will result in a temporary or permanent reassignment. Only selected individuals with direct approval from the assigned head researcher are exempt from this precedent. Communications is to be done through the integrated intercom inside RPC-440's containment chamber.

Personnel that are exposed to RPC-440 must be quarantined for at least one week. After full recovery, exposed personnel may be allowed to return to their duties. Any and all RPC-440-1 subjects are to be immediately terminated after a positive diagnosis.

Relocation of RPC-440 must be done by a modular containment chamber with no openings other than the access door. If possible, RPC-440 is to be instructed to wear a headwrap that covers the entirety of its face provided by the supervising personnel.

----

**Description:** RPC-440 appears to be a human female measuring 155cm in height, 54kg in weight with a Southeast Asian ethnicity. However, its appearance may vary from person to person depending on their response to RPC-440's memetic effects.

Experiments with CSD personnel reported all test subjects perceived RPC-440 as a young woman with average to exquisite characteristics within approximately 10 seconds of observation. The initial depiction of RPC-440 differs from each individual; subjects reported of perceiving RPC-440's face as their sister, former colleague, wife and at times, various celebrities.

However, after observing the object for longer than 10-30 seconds, subjects reported of perceiving RPC-440 as a faceless woman with all of its clothes currently worn morphing into a red //kebaya//[[footnote]]A type of Javanese Blouse usually worn in formal occassions.[[/footnote]]. This stage is considered as the lethal offset due to most experimented subjects were diagnosed with brain hemorrhage.

After approximately one minute of continuous observation, subjects started to bleed from every facial orifice. However, none of the subjects reported experiencing pain. This stage will progress into psychosis and—if the subject survives the ordeal—will show extreme devotion to RPC-440. Subjects that have reached this stage have a 67% chance of survival and are henceforth referred to as RPC-440-1.

RPC-440's memetic effects also manifest in every media depiction of the object (e.g. pictures, quick sketches and as far as sonic frequency readings). Furthermore, media depictions will always have RPC-440 show a sultry expression regardless of the situation it finds itself in (e.g. Image 440.1 is supposed to show RPC-440 pinned against the wall in distress yet personnel reported of seeing RPC-440 as relaxed).

[[collapsible show="+ Discovery Log #440" hide="-hide Discovery Log #440"]]

RPC-440 was apprehended after reports of "a faceless woman in red" wandering around the city of Madiun, East Java, Indonesia were received. MST Sierra-8 "Sundowners" investigators also received a report of a massacre in a village near the location of the first reported sighting. An autopsy of the corpses from the massacre indicated that the villagers were killed due to brain hemorrhage.

A local urban legend named "Pasuryan Abang"[[footnote]]//"Si Wajah Merah"//, "The Red Face".[[/footnote]] was also circulating around the city, which revolved around an entity matching descriptions of RPC-440. MST X-Ray-6 "Annullifiers" was tasked to expunge any and all data regarding sightings and urban legends of RPC-440 on ██/██/████, 2 weeks after RPC-440 was taken into custody.

> **Interviewed:** RPC-440
> 
> **Interviewer:** Dr. Freya Hildebrand
> 
> **Translator:** Junior Researcher R.R. Hidayat Suradiningrat
> 
> **Foreword:** RPC-440 was taken into Authority custody and transported to the temporary Southeast Asian ASF FOB on ██/██/██. Dr. Freya Hildebrand was assigned to process and transfer RPC-440 to its containment chamber in Site-002.
> 
> The entirety of this interview was done through an intercom. The questions are provided by Dr. Freya Hildebrand and the interview was translated from Indonesian with the help of Junior Researcher R.R. Hidayat Suradiningrat.
> 
> ----
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **JR Suradiningrat:** Please state your name, age, birthplace and occupation.
> 
> **RPC-440:** My name is Nur Lestari. I am twenty-five years old, born in Madiun, East Java. I don't have a job; I mostly tend my father's rice farm.
> 
> **JR Suradiningrat:** Are you aware of your condition?
> 
> **RPC-440:** Yes. Ever since I was born. My parents never let me get out of the house until I was eighteen years old.
> 
> **JR Suradiningrat:** So you never attended school? 
> 
> **RPC-440:** No. I never did. [sniffles] Look, is this about the farmers? I said I was sorry, I didn't mean to do it! [sobs]
> 
> **JR Suradiningrat:** What farmers?
> 
> **RPC-440:** The other farmers near our house knew about me. They have been demanding to... [sniffles] kill me ever since I was born. They thought I was a witch.
> 
> **JR Suradiningrat:** And what happened?
> 
> **RPC-440:** A few weeks ago, a man broke into our house demanding to bring me to him. I was scared, so I ran away. I didn't know how long I was gone but when I calmed down and returned, the farmers had... killed my parents. I became angry and... and I... [sobs]
> 
> **JR Suradiningrat:** Miss Lestari? How did you manage to survive after the incident?
> 
> **RPC-440:** I... thought of going to the city but I was so scared to look at people... I've met some on the road, they tried to help me but I... They... [RPC-440 broke down crying]
> 
> **JR Suradiningrat:** We understand Miss, but you must cooperate with us if you want our help. How did you kill those farmers?
> 
> **RPC-440:** I told you, I didn't know; I can't control it. Please madam, I don't want to hurt anyone; you must help me.
> 
> **JR Suradiningrat:** We will, Miss Lestari. We will.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> 
> ----
> **Closing Statement:** //RPC-440's is currently emotionally unstable. I would like supervising personnel to treat it as humanely as possible and request a psychiatrist to evaluate its emotional state immediately.// - Dr. Hildebrand
