**Registered Phenomena Code:** 993

**Object Class:** Alpha-Yellow

**Hazards:** Aggression Hazard, Animated Hazard, Grouped Hazard, Sapient Hazard, Transmutation Hazard, Extreme Temperature Hazard


**Containment Protocols:** RPC-993 is to be kept in a containment chamber furnished similarly to Room 28 of the ███ Apartment (London) to familiarise RPC-993-2-18 with its surroundings on [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/site-007 Site-007].[[footnote]]Psychological observations of RPC-993 reveals that familiarity degrades the threat of the anomaly[[/footnote]] Every night from 20:00-00:00 to 03:00-06:00, RPC-993 must be monitored cautiously for any RPC-993-2 instances; monitoring must continue until all instances disappear. If a new and unrecorded instance of RPC-993-2 appears, instance must be reported to the site manager immediately.

Testings involving CSD-class personnels must be filed under careful registration by personnel of Clearance level-2 or above. Subjects of lower prominence will be tested solely for behavioral analysis on the RPC-993-2 instances. Subjects of a higher prominence[[footnote]]RPC-993-2-18 grow an emotional attachment to test subjects tested with the most[[/footnote]] will be tested under the supervision of Director M█████ for psychological evaluations and potential interview of RPC-993-2-18. Class-A amnestic will be given to subjects if necessary.

**Description:** RPC-993 designates both the black stain beneath, and anomalies generated from it. The anomalies reside in a twin-size memory foam mattress bed with no other apparent anomalous property except for it's hinted "invulnerability" and the anomalous "shadow" underneath it. Beneath RPC-993, an irremovable black stain marks its perimeter: registered as RPC-993-1. If RPC-993 changes location, RPC-993-1 will change its location accordingly similar to a shadow. Despite its shadow-like property, no amount of light is able to illuminate RPC-993-1. It must be heavily emphasized that RPC-993 manifests itself into a physical nightmare every night. 

Every night from 20:00-00:00 to 03:00-06:00 the anomaly enters a state where RPC-993-1 undergoes a drastic shift in configuration creating a "gate". The purpose of said gate is to allow RPC-993-2 instances to materialize out of RPC-993-1. These phenomena all differ to one another in shape and form. --The purpose of these anomalies is currently unknown, for RPC-993-18 have proven itself to be difficult to cooperate with--. Instances have a tendency to indirectly interact with each other in a complementary manner. Multiple experiments reveal that RPC-993-2 instances are less aggressive towards more prominent test subjects. After 30-40 tests on the same human subject, RPC-993-2-05 - 10 will materialize allowing RPC-993-2-18 to enter.

[[collapsible show="List of RPC-993-2 Phenomena" hide="- Close"]]
> **RPC-993-2-01** Registers a voice heard from underneath RPC-993-2, these voices speak in an unknown language. The voices heard are that of a young female and elderly male having what appears to be an argument. RPC-993-2-01 fade away after 6-7 hours. Though RPC-993-2-01 can be heard by human ears, recordings of RPC-993-01 results in an empty file.
> ------
> **RPC-993-2-02** Is a white liquid that increases its mass by 1% every second. Growth continues until the volume reaches 330 L. RPC-993-2-02 completely evaporates after 2-3 hours. When examined, it is revealed that RPC-993-2-02 has a component similar to the ██████ of both sexes combined.
> ------
> **RPC-993-2-03** Is a hairless, malnourished humanoid lacking in pigmentation, nose, genital and eyes. Further examination of RPC-993-2-03 reveals its primitive nature, possessing equal intelligence to a chimpanzee. Subjects who come in contact with RPC-993-2-03, anomaly will [REDACTED] until subjects are unable to speak. RPC-993-2-03 will only manifest after RPC-993-2-02 instances materialized the previous night.
> ------
> **RPC-993-2-04** Designates a number of human arms reaching out of RPC-993-2. The quantity in which RPC-993-04 appears ranges from 4 to 350. RPC-993-04 will torment subjects by banging the bed, the floor or the wall until it bleeds. Once an arm bleeds, it will return back to RPC-993-1. Phenomena lasts after 3-5 hours. RPC-993-04 will avoid any physical contact with humans or objects moved by human means, thus making testing regarding RPC-993-2-04 difficult.
> ------

> **[RPC-993-2 unit 05-15 has been removed from the list as ordered by Director M█████]**

> ------
> **RPC-993-2-16** Registers a quantity of animate wooden antique dolls that will climb on top of subject and bounce on subject continuously until dematerialization. Further examination of RPC-993-2-16 reveals a mass of muscles, nerves and fat stuffed inside of the doll shell. Unlike all other instances, RPC-993-2-16 can't physically disperse unless disintegrated by RPC-993-2-17 the next night.
> ------
> **RPC-993-2-17** Is the most harmful of instances, registering an invisible odorless gas of an unknown elemental composition. After 11-66 minutes of materialization, RPC-993-2-17 will light itself on fire through unknown means, setting fire to the entire room, turning the room’s heat to 250-████.██°C. The fire expires after 3 hours of activity. Subjects laying on top of RPC-993-1 during an RPC-993-2-17 event experience tremendous pain, the most damage ever caused by RPC-993-2-17 is 1st degree burns on 40% of CSD-7989’s body. RPC-993-2-17 will only transpire after the materialization of RPC-993-2-16 the previous night.
> ------
> **RPC-993-2-18** Registers a human Caucasian girl around the age of █-1█. The object has proven itself to be cooperative and docile only to subjects who've slept on RPC-993. RPC-993-2-18 claims itself to be Jessica K██████, the former owner of RPC-993.
