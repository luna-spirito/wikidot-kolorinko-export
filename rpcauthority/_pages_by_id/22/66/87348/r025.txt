**Registered Phenomena Code:** 925

**Object Class:** Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** Bio-Hazard, Memory Alteration Hazard, Sensory Hazard, Ideological Hazard, Emotional Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** As there are no naturally occurring instances of RPC-925, all existing versions are currently being supplied to all personnel assigned to Cognito-hazardous RPCs for combative use/as a deterrent. All MST operatives are required to have an inhaler stored on their person during any operations. If supplies are low, personnel are encouraged to notify their Head Researcher for resupplying.

**Addendum-1:** All Experiments must be done in qualified testing facilities and under the observation of a head researcher following Experiment-76.

**Description:** The original RPC-925 was a form of bacteria that can affect the host's synapses and thought processes, making the victim act completely contradictory with their actions, statements, and mindsets. The origins of this bacteria can be traced to RPC-███ and these effects usually lasted for a day.

On behalf of the Authority, Nucorp Industries genetically re-engineered RPC-925[[footnote]] Note that 925 now refers to the new re-engineered strand as no original bacteria colonies exist  [[/footnote]] colonies into gaseous vapours that induce a cognitive state that renders the host immune to almost all mind-affecting anomalies. RPC-925 comes contained in an oral inhaler with 2 uses available with effects lasting for 1 hour. Nucorp Industries included an excerpt of ingredients used to make the mixture:

* Ar (Argon)
* Tetrahydrocannabinol (Found in cannabis)
* As (Arsenic)
* CH4 (Methane)
* Sucrose (Sugar)
* Ca (Calcium)
* C (Carbon)
* Acesulfame Potassium (Artificial sweetener)
* ████ (RPC-925)
* Acrylic Polymers (Used in paint)
* Polyisoprene (Rubber)
* and another 154 results

Subjects affected by RPC-925 express all 5 basic senses being heightened and an overall feeling of alertness, however prolonged use in a short time span (2/3 or 4 uses in a day) will result in an assortment of negative side effects. Some of the documented effects are listed below:

* Temporary memory loss

* Mood swings

* Periods of confusion

* Early onset dementia (occurred in 5 overusing cases for researchers over 40)

* Periods of paranoia

* Lack of cognitive awareness, specifically in surroundings

* Long bouts of sensory overload

Overdosing (5+ uses a day) on 925 will result in more severe side effects, such as:

* Temporary memory loss may become permanent, similar to untreated dementia.

* Mood swings elevate into Bipolar disorder

* Periods of paranoia can become severe panic attacks, and users could injure nearby personnel on "suspicions"

* Cognitive unawareness elevates to a permanent inability to function without support (symptom seems to bear a similarity to untreated Huntington's disease)

* Sensory bouts can either become permanent but minimal or in bouts but stronger than usual.

Overall long-term use with RPC-925 results in sharply diminishing results, with the body immunising itself. Researchers have found that after using RPC-925 safely for a span of 4 weeks, it would become usable again after approximately a year.

> **Head Researcher's note:** To all new staff and security personnel, please follow the instructions provided and DO NOT attempt to acquire more than one inhaler for emergency use. As RPC-925 is bacterial in origin, your body will eventually build a resistance to it and it will no longer become effective. This resistance takes a long time to break down, as in months up to a year long. 
> 
> If you're an ASF or MST member, this is integral: never use your 2 doses at once, no matter how scary the monster is. Your one required dose will be suitable. And only use dosage in emergency situations, your tolerance to RPC-925 could mean the death of everyone around you including yourself.
> 
> For Researchers: We don't need extra casualties in containment breaches because you think one dose will not be enough or you became tolerant. One dose will be enough, guaranteed. No need to overuse RPC-925 and start forgetting breach procedures, or even worse be affected by the RPC to further assist in the breach.

**Foreword:** RPC-925 has been tested with various RPCs, leading to both success and failure. Below are the declassified results of some RPCs. For the full list, please see Head Researcher Maxine about Document-925-3.

------

**Experiment-012:**
An instance of RPC-113 was viewed with RPC-925 administered to one CSD.

**Results:** The subject was completely exempt of 113’s properties, with no expression of even disorientation. Multiple instances of 113 were then brought in, with the same results.

------

**Experiment-020:**
An instance of RPC-041 was broken and the Subject was left in the area of influence. Subject was not supplied RPC-925 before in the area of effect and after a short amount of time, was given RPC-925 in the area of influence.

**Results:** Subject acted inebriated as expected, struggling to administer RPC-925 when provided. When finally taken, subject acted and performed more coherently in the area of influence, however, the subject inexplicably tripped over their own feet a total of 5 times during the remainder of the test.

------

**Experiment-021:**
An extra instance of 041 was supplied with the extent purpose of testing a higher strain of effects. The procedure was copied from the previous experiment, however with the addition of breaking a second instance of 041 after the administration of RPC-925.

**Results:** Subject acted normally after the administration of RPC-925, but slightly reverted to an inebriated state with the breaking of the second 041 instance. Subject could still process complex thoughts, but motor functions and reflexes were impaired[[footnote]] //I propose all tests with RPC-X now attempt to establish the limits of it, and who knows, we may find a pattern on RPCs that are and aren’t affected//-Junior Researcher Morgan

//Proposition Approved//-Head researcher Maxine [[/footnote]].

------

**Experiment-028:**
A CSD subject was administered RPC-925 and induced into a sleeping state in RPC-087’s containment room. An untreated CSD was outside in case of an attempted containment breach by 087.

**Results:** RPC-087 could not feed on the Subject for an entire hour. A breach was attempted until the untreated backup was brought in, and 087 was allowed to feed. With RPC-925 wearing off 2 hours later, 087 began feeding on the initially treated Subject. 

------

**Experiment-035:**
One //Suricata suricatta// (Meerkat) was administered RPC-925 and exposed to RPC-143.

**Results:** On administration of RPC-925, the Suricata immediately entered a comatose state and was later euthanized. All testing is now to exclude animals.

------

**Experiment-040:**
CSD subject was administered RPC-925 at the beginning of RPC-188’s second stage and was continually administered RPC-925 every 2 hours.

**Results:**
Subject expressed sensory overload bouts and temporary memory loss for over a day but showed no feelings of euphoria usually seen in 188-1 instances. When operated to extract the 188 seed, it was discovered in a seedling form and had died. Surgery was successful in removing everything but small parts of the roots, due to concern of damaging nearby organs. The Subject was diagnosed with Bipolar and paranoia/panic attacks later on.

------

**Experiment-044:**
CSD was administered RPC-925 and ordered to view RPC-135.

**Results:** Subject replied with little to no emotional response, but when asked to elaborate on the images or their feelings, the subject expressed a loss for words. After 30 mins of staring, the subject was able to look and walk away. When questioned later about their actions she replied: “I didn’t believe it."

------

**Experiment-049:**
CSD was Administered RPC-925 and ordered to read RPC-409.

**Results:**
Subject started displaying symptoms of obsession and eventually became a 409-1A instance. Experiment deemed a failure.

------

**Experiment-052:**
CSD was administered RPC-925 with confirmation on designation as 302-1.

**Results:**
5 mins after administering RPC-925, the 302 instances immediately left the Subject and flew around the experiment room presumably looking for a new host.

------

**Experiment-061:**
CSD was administered RPC-925 and ordered to view RPC-103.

**Results:**
CSD expressed feelings of comfort and peace, but when asked about visuals replied that they could only see a bright light. Any sounds heard by researchers were unheard to the Subject. Subject was later decontaminated for any of 103’s microbial compounds, where researchers found all forms of the biological matter in a state of complete dormancy, never triggered.

------

**Experiment-070:**
A group of CSD were tasked with re-containing RPC-793. All Subjects were administered RPC-925 and were given orders to surround 793’s influence zone with fence posts. All subjects were equipped with dim flashlights to find the area of influence.

**Results:**
Success in reestablishing 793’s area of influence. Nearly all subjects reported no aggressive emotions and heavily used the flashlights to accurately map out the area of influence. One subject against all orders traveled closer to 793, leading to heightened aggressiveness and similar effects. Subject returned and was terminated for not following orders. Construction on a new containment site for 793 has begun.

------

**Experiment-076:**
5 CSD administered RPC-925 before a planned containment breach.

**Results:**
Complete success. The CSD showed no sign of hive mind mentality as seen in other 212 instances while gaining the mutations of 212-1 to -3 instances. They however used the breach to break other RPCs out of containment and almost overtook Site-074. An MST was quickly dispatched and resolved the situation.
