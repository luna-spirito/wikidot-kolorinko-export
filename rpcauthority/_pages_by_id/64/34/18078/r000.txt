**Registered Phenomena Code:** XXXX

**Object Class:** Beta-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Organic Hazard, Regenerative Hazard, Sapient Hazard, Bio-Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-XXX is currently held at Site-002. RPC-XXX is to be contained in a standard airtight concrete cell[[footnote]]RPC-XXX is incapable of breaking concrete unless the surface is already heavily damaged.[[/footnote]] with several modifications. The room is equipped with a filtered ventilation system and 5 UV lights. One surveillance camera is to be positioned in the south-eastern corner. The walls of RPC-XXX’s cell is fitted with an automatic sprinkler system which hydrates RPC-XXX once every 24 hours at 3:00am lasting for a duration of 10 minutes. In the event of a power failure, RPC-XXX’s cell is connected to a back-up generator. Any personnel to enter RPC-XXX’s cell must be quarantined for 2 hours.

**Description:** RPC-XXX is a plant-based humanoid entity measuring 3.2 meters in height. RPC-XXX has a mouth and nostrils, but lacks all other external facial features. Analysis shows that RPC-XXX has a biological composition similar to that of  Delairea odorata. Thick vines can be produced by RPC-XXX to ensnare prey [[footnote]]The maximum recorded length of these vines is 10 meters.[[/footnote]]. Signs of sapience have been observed from RPC-XXX however, it is not capable of speech or any other form of advanced communication. During initial recovery, it was discovered that RPC-XXX possesses extreme regenerative capabilities.

RPC-XXX-1 is a microscopic spore released by RPC-XXX as a method of controlling a living brain. When inhaled, it takes effect within hours. After this time, RPC-XXX-1 multiplies and fully takes control of the subject’s brain putting it under direct control of RPC-XXX. The only known cure for those infected with RPC-XXX-1 is to move the infected patient at least 1.3 miles away from RPC-XXX causing RPC-XXX-1 to seemingly lose their connection to RPC-XXX and lose effect. Those infected with RPC-XXX-1 can be manipulated by RPC-XXX to speak and communicate with other humans though, they are only capable of forming simple sentences.

RPC-XXX is docile to humans unless provoked. When a victim becomes ensnared in RPC-XXX’s vines, high concentrations of RPC-XXX-1 are injected into the victim’s bloodstream causing them to be infected and under the control of RPC-XXX within minutes. An ensnared victim is usually released after eight seconds as this time is all that is necessary for RPC-XXX to inject a sufficient quantity of RPC-XXX-1 into the victim through the vines they are entangled in. Footage has shown that RPC-XXX lays dormant in a fetal position while in containment. When in this state, RPC-XXX gradually grows thick vines out of its body which spread across the surface of its containment cell.



[[collapsible show="+ Open Extra Information" hide="- Close Extra Information"]]

**Discovery:** An isolated town in Peru was reported to have gone completely missing. The Authority was alerted immediately and recon drones were sent into the area. RPC-XXX was discovered to have taken over a water treatment plant in the area. RPC-XXX was spotted roaming the area with a metal collar attached to it. MST Echo-08[[footnote]]”Plague Doctors”.[[/footnote]] was sent in with hazmat suits to detain RPC-XXX, it was during this that the metal collar on RPC-XXX was destroyed. RPC-XXX entered a docile state once the collar had been broken. The population of the missing town was found dead at the water treatment plant. During the removal of RPC-XXX from the area, a man in a hazmat suit was spotted but MST forces failed to detain the figure.
