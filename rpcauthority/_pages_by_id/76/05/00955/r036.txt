**Registered Phenomena Code:** 618

**Object Class:** Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** Aggression, Animated, Bio-Hazard, Emotional, Grouped, Sentient

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-618 is to be kept in a 5x5x5 m containment cell at [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/site-007 Site-007]. As RPC-618-P, -W, -F, and -D cannot be separately contained, RPC-618-HESIOD events are to be monitored closely when they begin.

Investigation into the individual known as "Pandora" is currently underway.

**Description:** RPC-618 is a crudely made 26x26x26 cm cardboard box, held in place with tape and adhesives. It is decorated with a floral pattern, and has "PANDORA'S" written on the side in purple crayon. Around it are stylized hearts, aces, spades, and diamonds drawn in red and black crayon. The left side has the address: ████████, St. ██████, London[[footnote]] The precise location where RPC-618 was discovered.[[/footnote]] printed on in black ink. RPC-618 is extremely resistant to damage, and cannot be destroyed. On the back side of the box, a note is written:

if all evil is open call +44 ██████████[[footnote]] Consultation with British multinational telecommunications conglomerate, Vodafone™ in [REDACTED] reveal that the number described does not yet exist as of ██/03/2014. It was only on ██/██/2017 the number was given to a subject with no apparent connection with RPC-618. The phone number is most likely misinterpreted. [[/footnote]]
[[/div]]
@@@@

RPC-618 primarily behaves as a normal object. It is only during an active state that RPC-618's main anomalous properties manifest. This anomalous event will now be designated as an RPC-618-HESOID event. An entity will emerge out of RPC-618, along with numerous other smaller entities. These smaller entities are designated RPC-618-A instances and are mostly made out of paper. Along with those entities, 1 of 4 entities will appear. These entities are designated as RPC-618-P, RPC-618-W, RPC-618-F and RPC-618-D accordingly. Each RPC-618 instances behaves differently to one another, and lack high logical reasoning and thought.

An RPC-618-HESIOD event will normally last in between 30 minutes and 4 hours.

[[tab RPC-618-P]]
[[include component:image-enlarge name= 
http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-618/verysmartsickness.jpg|width=300px|caption={{fig. 2.1:}} RPC-618-P. {{(circa 2014)}}]]
[[=]]
[[size 110%]]**Description**[[/size]]
[[/=]]
RPC-618-P is a 140 cm tall figure made in the shape of a "plague doctor" from the 17th - 19th centuries. It is made out of plastic, colored paper, and held together with masking tape. A purple balloon takes the place of its "head", with black paper joined to the top appearing as a hood. RPC-618-P's main body structure is made from 2 plastic boards, blue in coloration. On RPC-618-P's chest is "Mr. Pestens" [sic] written in black marker.
------
[[=]]
[[size 110%]]**Behavior**[[/size]]
[[/=]]
After manifestation, RPC-618-P will sporadically move around the room[[footnote]] It is unknown how this is achieved, as RPC-618-P is observed to have no appendages.[[/footnote]] at high speeds, uttering grammatically poor sentences such as "Hello yes I am pestilence", "I am the sick. I make sick", "I will make sickness, you" etc. RPC-618-A instances which take the form of rats and flies will follow RPC-618-P wherever it goes. Individuals within 3 meters of RPC-618-P will invariably contract acute viral nasopharyngitis[[footnote]] The common cold.[[/footnote]]. When asked politely, RPC-618-P will remove the effects of acute viral nasopharyngitis from the individual.

[[tab RPC-618-W]]
[[include component:image-enlarge name= 
http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-618/angrydude.jpg|width=300px|caption={{fig. 2.2:}} RPC-618-W. {{(circa 2015)}}]]

[[size 110%]]**Description**[[/size]]
[[/=]]
RPC-618-W is a 155 cm tall pillow, with a blue plastic bucket sitting on the top of it. It has been cut out in the shape of a knight's helmet, and has been covered in tinfoil. A utility belt has been fastened around RPC-618-W, and has been proven difficult to remove. The utility belt has three holsters, which hold various items. On the back of RPC-618-W is "Lutenant War" [sic] written in red marker.
------
[[=]]
[[size 110%]]**Behavior**[[/size]]
[[/=]]
RPC-618-W during an RPC-618-HESIOD event will slowly trudge around the room, using the two bottom corners of its pillow body to maintain movement. It has been heard screaming various battle cries that have been heard throughout History, such as "Deus vult", "Vive la France", etc. Other times, it will simply exclaim "I am powerful", "I can fight you" and other derivatives. RPC-618-A instances follow RPC-618-W, and take the form of miniature tanks and figures in the shape of heavily stylized humans. These RPC-618-A instances will sometimes fight each other. When any individual comes within 3 meters of RPC-618-W, RPC-618-W will immediately start attacking the subject in various ways, including: Throwing small paper balls, running directly into the subject, etc. These attempts have never resulted in harm being done to the subject. Individuals within 3 meters of RPC-618-W will express mild annoyance, and mild violent tendencies. When asked, RPC-618-W will stop attacking the subject.

[[tab RPC-618-F]]
[[include component:image-enlarge name=

[[size 110%]]**Description**[[/size]]
[[/=]]
RPC-618-F appears as a paper plate, with a stereotypical "angry face" drawn on the front in black marker. The plate is fastened to a cardboard box. Affixed to it are 4 appendages, which act as arms and legs. These appendages are made out of paper, and support RPC-618-F's weight. "Famen" [sic] is written twice in green marker on RPC-618-F's chest.
------
[[=]]
[[size 110%]]**Behavior**[[/size]]
[[/=]]
During an RPC-618-HESIOD event, RPC-618-F will drag itself around the area using its appendages. Shallow breathing can occasionally be heard originating from RPC-618-F. It utters sentences that only consist of single words, such as "Hungry", "Eat. Become", "Skinny", etc. The RPC-618-A instances that follow RPC-618-F take the form of pigs and cows. Individuals within 3 meters of RPC-618-F will express great hunger[[footnote]] This can affect subjects which had expressed satiety not long before coming into contact with RPC-618-F.[[/footnote]], and a desire to consume edible objects. When asked, RPC-618-F can remove the feelings of hunger from an individual.

[[tab RPC-618-D]]
[[include component:image-enlarge name=

[[size 110%]]**Description**[[/size]]
[[/=]]
RPC-618-D is a 120 cm tall figure made in the shape of a stereotypical hunched “Grim Reaper” It is made out of black plastic boards, papers, held together by white duct tape. A blue balloon takes the form of its head, with a crude mask of a skull masking it. On RPC-618-D’s chest is “Death” written in red marker. RPC-618-D carries a paper scythe permanently taped to its hands. Multiple paper strips surround the object's body used as an extra limb.
------
[[=]]
[[size 110%]]**Behavior**[[/size]]
[[/=]]
Upon manifestation, RPC-618-D will haphazardly drag itself around the area using its scythe. Individuals within 3 meters of RPC-618-D report feeling drowsy and weak. The RPC-618-A instances that follow RPC-618-D take the form of stereotypical ghosts and gravestones[[footnote]] Despite being inanimate objects, these gravestones are capable of movement.[[/footnote]]. Upon eye contact with a human or animal, RPC-618-D will attempt to inflict harm on the subject with its scythe by hitting the subject until RPC-618-D runs out of energy, which usually takes 40 seconds. Despite RPC-618-D’s high aggression, it is mostly harmless and will stop if the subject requests so. RPC-618-D is incapable of speech.
