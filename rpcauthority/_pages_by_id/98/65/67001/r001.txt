**Registered Phenomena Code:** 967

**Object Class:** --Alpha-Yellow-- Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** Psychotronic, Sapient, Radiation, Extra-dimensional

**Containment Protocols:** As RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 have been damaged beyond operational capabilities no containment protocols are necessary. The remains RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 are currently being transported to [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/site-038 Site-038] for further study. Additionally, RPC-967 has been returned to the Vatican for containment under its previous containment protocols.   

[[collapsible show="[+] Show Previous Containment Protocols" hide="[-] Close File"]]
Due to the religious importance of RPC-967 to the Vatican, it's to be stored within a lead-lined containment unit within the Vatican archives. Personnel, be it Authority or ranking members of the Catholic order, are allowed to freely visit and talk to RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2. However, personnel that choose to do so must wear a Type-A radiation protection suit when in the presence of RPC-967. 

As to comply with the wishes of the Vatican, any recorded interviews with RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 must be approved by the Board and blessed by the acting Pope or no less than three Arch Bishops in its stead. And as a final stipulation on behalf of the Vatican, at least one high priest must be present for the entirety of the interview.
[[/collapsible]]

**Description:** RPC-967 is the title given to a painting first discovered by the Auctoritas Impertus at an unknown date. However, Authority historians currently believe that RPC-967 was first discovered sometime in the 17th century, though confirmation of this has proven impossible to gather due to the loss of records due to time. 

RPC-967 itself is a painting that depicts two humanoid entities wielding glaives and clad in grey metal armor; individually designated as RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 respectively. These entities are portrayed standing guard at either side of a wooden door that leads into an erupting mountain.

The armor worn by RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 is similar to the prominent style used by knights during the supposed time period of RPC-967's discovery. However, the armor worn by the two is notably bulkier and the symbol of the Auctoritas Impertus is featured on the shoulder plates (on the left shoulder of RPC-967-1 and on the right shoulder of RPC-967-2).

The glaives of RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 glow a light blue color and have been determined to have been painted with a radioactive isotope of Uranium. It is unknown how this paint has remained radioactive since its initial acquisition by the Impertus.

RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 are capable of communicating via a form of telepathy. This telepathy only works if the subject is within RPC-967's direct line of sight. When engaged in telepathic communication, RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 claim to be brothers and to have had positions in one of the Auctoritas Impertus's secret knight orders. Specifically RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 claim to have held positions in {{**Ut daemonium de venatores**}} (the demon hunters[[footnote]]An order of knights within the Auctoritas Impertus dedicated to containment and/or destruction of "demonic" artifacts and entities.[[/footnote]]).

Additionally, RPC-976-1 and RPC-967-2 are fanatically devoted to christ and will commonly try to convert subjects communicating with them to Christianity if they are not already. Specifically, RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 have both claimed to be deeply Catholic, a fact that has been confirmed by the examination of their mannerisms by Authority historians.

> = //For further details on the nature of RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 please see the interview logs in Addendum.1//

Within the records of the Auctoritas Impertus there exist a plethora of documents claiming that RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 possess the ability to exit RPC-967. Patterns within the records suggest RPC-967 has never used this ability. Instead, it is claimed that the presence of this ability was relayed to the Auctoritas by the Arclight, an allied group to the Impertus in the 17th century and the creators of RPC-967.

+ **Interview Log: RPC-967 A** 
[[/=]]

**Interviewer:** Dr. Edward Bellucci, High Priest Colacino

**Interviewee:** RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2

**Date:** 13/06/1954

**Foreword:** The following interview was undertaken to update the Authority database on RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2's story since the last interview with the pair occurred in 1897 and was considered to be below minimum readability due to its archaic language. 

> **Note:** Due to the method that RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 communicate all of their language will appear modern. 
-----

**<Begin Log>**

**Dr. Bellucci:** Hello, RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2, I am Dr. Bellucci.

**Colacino:** And I am High Priest Colacino.

**RPC-967-2:** Doctor, High Priest; please state the purpose of your interview.

**RPC-967-1:** You obviously aren't here for your own enjoyment, are you perhaps getting an updated testimony from us? 

**Dr. Bellucci:** Yes, that is why I am here today.

**RPC-967-2:** While we do not appreciate the direction the Impertus has taken recently, we are oblig-

**RPC-967-1:** Actually, I think it is smart to disassociate from the church, considering your RPC Authority is now operating all over this world. 

**RPC-967-2:** Really?

**RPC-967-1:** Think about it, not everyone in this world believes in the miracles of Christ. Furthermore, not everyone who worships the Lord does it under the Pope. 

**RPC-967-2:** I think we should agree to disagree on this matter for now. But we will be discussing this later. 

**Colacino:** I'd hate to interrupt the Sorcerer Paladins of the Impertus, but we must get back on the topic of the interview. 

**Colacino:** If the Doctor would be so kind. 

**Dr. Bellucci:** Right, let's start off with your childhoods.

**RPC-967-1:** If you insist, then allow me to begin. 

**RPC-967-1:** We were born into a noble family as twins. However, we were born with... Unique talents, as a way to put it. These talents caught the attention of the church, who thought they were a blessing from god. This would eventually lead me and my brother to join the Impertus. 

**RPC-967-2:** Essentially, it all started when our local priest sent a message to the Vatican. About a month after the letter was sent some knights arrived and carted us off to the Vatican, which is where we were indoctrinated into the Impertus. 

**RPC-967-1:** From that point on, until we became adults, they trained us in the arts of combat, thaumaturgy, and praying to god. After we graduated, we were given a choice about what we wanted to do from that point forward. 

**Dr. Bellucci:** What were your options?

**RPC-967-2:** We were allowed to join any of the inquisitorial orders. The types of orders where we would proactively seek out the strange, rather than reacting to it. Since at the time demonic sightings were all the rage we joined the demon hunter order.

**RPC-967-2:** We received more training and schooling on demons. Though not just from the Impertus, we also received some training from the Arclight, of all organizations. 

**Dr. Bellucci:** The what?

**RPC-967-1:** The Arclight, they were runic blacksmiths, allies of the Impertus, and the foremost experts on demons and hunting demons.

**RPC-967-1:** Regardless, they trained us on their knowledge of demons. And they even taught us rituals that could completely annihilate demons, preventing them from reforming in Hell. And yes, demons regrow in Hell after you kill their body. And because demons can learn like us, it's rare for someone to become a veteran of the demon-hunting order. 

**RPC-967-2:** But that is really it for the more interesting parts of our history. I mean everything else was just going to towns suspected of harboring demon cults and purging all of the members of those cults. And we had... special ways of gathering field data, let's say. So it was relatively easy to determine where demonic cults actually resided. 

**RPC-967-1:** Aren't you forgetting about the Subvesuvia incursion?

**RPC-967-2:** Oh... yes... Those morons

**RPC-967-1:** You really hated that mission didn't you. 

**RPC-967-2:** Personally I... I don't like to talk about that mission. Not out of shame mind you, we have failed other missions. But... it is the mission where we died. 

**Dr. Bellucci:** -I am going to have to ask you to elaborate what you meant by 'the mission where we died'.

**RPC-967-1:** He means exactly that, we died in that monastery fighting off a demonic incursion. And it was an extraordinarily large incursion at that. The type of incursion that would take the cooperation of various orders within the Impertus to fight off and destroy. If it reached full strength that is. 

**RPC-967-2:** But before that point me and my brother went into the monastery with a team of twenty-eight fellow paladins and nearly defeated the demons right there.  If we had received any reinforcements or supplies from our fellow demon hunters the battle would've been decisively won. 

**RPC-967-2:**But because the demons had been mildly more intelligent and had chosen to do what the biggest one of them said, we lost twenty-eight of our fellow paladins to starvation and exhaustion. 

**RPC-967-2:** Instead, everyone in the power-hungry clergy of the Impertus were trying to position themselves to claim glory for a victory over this seemingly organized demonic enemy. And not even the felling of the two most seasoned sorcerer paladins in the entirety of the Impertus could unite those petty clerks. Two sorcerer paladins that had their weapons and armor blessed by both the Emperor and the Pope...

**RPC-967-2:**  And it wasn't the Impertus that defeated the demons, it was the Arclight and their comparably pitiful forces. They had done it alone while the divine bureaucracy of the Impertus were trying to position themselves to claim the glory. 

**RPC-967-1:** Calm down brother, you are getting worked up.

**RPC-967-2:** Why wouldn't I? 

**RPC-967-2:** If we had received any reinforcements or supplies from our fellow demon hunters early on, the battle would've been decisively won. But because the demons had been mildly more intelligent and did what the biggest one of them said, we lost twenty-eight of our fellow paladins to starvation and exhaustion. 

**RPC-967-1:** We made it out of there, did we not?

**RPC-967-2:** No, we didn't, we stopped drawing breath during our defeat there. These contraptions the Arclight put us into simply allow us to continue the fight without breath.

**RPC-967-1:** Brother, there was nothing that could've been done about it. If anything the death of our force allowed the Impertus to change up its documents on the responsibilities of the orders.

**RPC-967-2:** I suppose you're right, but I believe we have told the fair doctor everything interesting about our past now. 

**RPC-967-1:** Yes, that is about everything interesting about our past. Now if you will be so kind as to leave me and my brother we can get back to our previous discussion. 

**Dr. Bellucci:** Thank you for your time and cooperation. It has been a pleasure.

**<End Log>**

= {{**Addendum.2 - In relation to the Sub Vesuvia**}}
[[/size]]
------

In 1982 The Board decided that relations with the independent [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/sub-vesuvia-event Sub Vesuvia] facility were at an unacceptable position. Because of this the Authority gifted RPC-967 along with various other minor helpful anomalous objects to the Sub Vesuvia in an attempt to improve relations. This resulted in no noticeable improvement in relations with the Sub Vesuvia. 

Following the complete failure of Sub Vesuvia and the subsequent exploration of the interior of the facility by Authority forces, RPC-967, RPC-967-1, and RPC-967-2 were all found destroyed within the facility. The destruction of RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 is of particular interest, as they were both discovered to have exited RPC-967 in physical forms that were almost identical to their appearance in RPC-967.

RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2 were both found to have been slain near the destroyed remains of an unknown artifact made out of coal and limestone. The artifact was believed to be a ring-like object with a radius of approximately three meters that featured various pyramidic protrusions along its surface. 

Additionally, large amounts of deceased [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-503 RPC-503-2] instances of various types were found slain around RPC-967-1 and RPC-967-2. The bodies of RPC-503-2 instances were also found leading up to the room in which the bodies of the pair were found.

The corpse of RPC-967-1 was found to have had their armor ripped open, revealing that it contained the remains of a badly scarred human male with various copper pipes and mechanical parts connected to it. This body inside of RPC-967-1 was missing its left arm and right leg, though these were believed to have been past injuries. Additionally, it was found that the heart of the human body within RPC-967-1 had been ripped from its chest cavity. 

However, the body of RPC-967-2 was found in a kneeling position in front of RPC-967-1. RPC-967-2 appears to have suffered various puncture wounds at seemingly random points of its body. With some of the wounds leaking blood, while a few of the wounds were leaking a black oil.
