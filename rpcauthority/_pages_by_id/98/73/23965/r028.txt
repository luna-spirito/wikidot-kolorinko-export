+++ **Containment Protocols:**

Due to the care established by St. John's monks, further containment efforts by the Authority are unnecessary. RPC-628 is currently kept within Mount Vesuvius. Said monks currently tend to RPC-628's necessities, such as sustenance, cleaning, and removal of parasites embedded within its skin.

Physical interaction with RPC-628 by staff is strictly limited. Personnel are to carry out bimonthly visits wherein RPC-628 is subject to routine examination and testing. Efforts to assist the local monks are to also be conducted. Supplies are to be provided if necessary.
[[include component:image-block-static name=https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/611298145829126334/676206891130683397/unknown.png|width=…px|caption=  Monks during routine containment measures.]]

All staff must wear Level-A hazmat suits [[footnote]]This standard presents full body protection besides an enclosed oxygen supply.[[/footnote]] when in the vicinity of RPC-628 due to the moss particulate present in the air and on surfaces. The suits must be fully worn at all times when interacting with the area in order to not transfer moss to the outside world.[[footnote]]This is due to the unknown types of flora that might cause unwanted changes in the surrounding ecosystem if introduced.[[/footnote]] after the end of any activities, Authority Personnel are to go through an obligatory decontamination procedure.

RPC-628 is to be fed three times per day with aquatic produce obtained from local suppliers. 50%-70% humidity is to be additionally sustained within the containment enclosure. Said humidity is maintained by a constructed hypocaust. [[footnote]] A Roman heating technique utilizing an open space below a building to disperse hot air, often used in Roman baths. [[/footnote]] The hypocaust and the adjacent ventilated wood-burning furnace are to be active for at least eight hours a day. Authority personnel is currently working to produce a machine to produce steam without the need for fire.

+++ **Description:**

RPC-628 is an aquatic mammalian organism measuring 430m long and weighing approximately 524,000kg.[[footnote]]Over thirty times larger and seventeen times heavier than the average Humpback whale.[[/footnote]] The entity is composed of both animal and plant cells.[[footnote]]These plantae cell presumed to be moss or moss-related.[[/footnote]] RPC-628 resembles, and is proportionally similar to a common //Megaptera Novaeangliae//[[footnote]] Humpback Whale. [[/footnote]]. Notable differences include its pigmentation, which produces a bright green colouration, besides that the entity's underbelly is white in coloration, with a darker tone on the affected area of RPC-628-A. RPC-628 exhibits commensalist behavior[[footnote]]An association between two organisms in which one benefits and the other derives neither benefit nor harm.[[/footnote]] consistent with members of its resembled species. Additionally, RPC-628 exhibits external growths resembling various marine invertebrate exoskeletons as well as plant matter.

The sea urchins, barnacles and moss found in the dermis of RPC-628 are capable of a great spread and growth if left unchecked. These growths vary in appearance, however, they maintain a consistent measurement of 5-10 centimeters. The moss is primarily, due to agglomerates, found on RPC-628's back although it has spread as far as its eye cavities, effectively blinding it. It is currently undetermined if RPC-628 employs conventional sight or uses an alternative method of interpreting its environment. The moss appears to be mundane until an object comes into proximity of RPC-628 with hostile intent. When this is the case, it will attach to any beings or objects and increase their own weight, holding down the object[[footnote]]Eg. A bullet coming in the proximity of the affected area will appear to slow down and not be as effective in contact.[[/footnote]]. The moss found in RPC-628's body is designated RPC-628-1. These mosses are able to absorb high amounts of humidity out of the body of any organic being.[[footnote]]It is capable of absorbing upwards of 60% of moisture in a human body.[[/footnote]] Unprotected individuals within the enclosure will invariably be dehydrated by the moss.

[[include component:image-block-static name= https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/496449587880525824/677654433122877460/unknown.png|width=px|caption= RPC-628.]]


RPC-628-A is a pitch-black two-flue harpoon.[[footnote]]Typically used in whaling before the nineteenth century and first appearing in artistic depictions in the fourteenth century.[[/footnote]] Necrotic and infected tissue has formed surrounding the site of its impalement on RPC-628. RPC-628-A is physically impossible to remove due to its anomalous properties.[[footnote]]It is theorized that the harpoon somehow merged with RPC-628 anatomy itself. For more details, view Addendum 1.[[/footnote]] When a living subject touches RPC-628-A their skin will begin to rapidly grow and mutate. The mutation closely resembles those found in tumors and certain types of cancer cells. Areas adjacent to the wound exhibit lack of moss growth. The injury does not cicatrize or appear to heal. This is theorized to be another anomalous effect afflicted by the RPC-628-A.

The following are the primary/secondary anomalous characteristics of RPC-628:

* The primary sound produced by RPC-628 is a whistle produced by its vocal cords. [[footnote]]This whistle appears to be unconventional and advanced relative to other cetacean vocalizations as it is capable of speech, including proficiency in English, Latin and Italian, as well as complex forms of singing.[[/footnote]]This whistle can produce an anomalous sound that resembles the human vocal range but was noted to reach higher frequencies as well. RPC-628's vocalization is able to have effects on all types of sentient beings. It is noted to have different effects depending on the emotional state of the listening subject. For example, those in distress happen to feel calm and those filled with hatred appear to feel physical fatigue and mental exhaustion. This effect appears to scale by intensity. It is able to perform songs but these appear to have minimal if any anomalous effect.

* Because the injury caused by RPC-628-A is unable to cicatrize or heal in any form, RPC-628 constantly bleeds. The blood from RPC-628 appears to have healing effects.[[footnote]]However the blood spilling from the harmed area around the wounded area, close to RPC-628-A  was noted to have greater effects closer.[[/footnote]] It appears that RPC-628 itself is subject to this effect as it is able to regenerate itself in a greater extent. The blood spilled by RPC-628 appears to possess rejuvenating qualities when ingested, inducing anomalous cell regeneration and temporarily bolstering the imbiber's fortitude. Besides that, it appears to raise the longevity of those who consume it, slowing the aging process on the affected body. However, it was discovered that the consumption of over 327mL[[footnote]] The consumption of any volume below 327 mL is currently being researched for medicinal benefits [[/footnote]] for usage is extremely dangerous. Consumption exceeding the limit has a high risk of generating tumors and abnormally differentiated cells resulting in tissue[[footnote]]Including epithelial, connective, muscular and nervous tissue.[[/footnote]], appendages[[footnote]]Including legs, arms and in rare occasions malformed heads.[[/footnote]], facial structures[[footnote]]Including oversized eyes, ears and mouth structures.[[/footnote]] and skeletal growth rupturing the epidermis. It is to be noted that those recently affected by RPC-628 blood will appear paler than usual, including tones of white or gray.

* RPC-628 was denoted to be capable of increasing and decreasing its weight, mass and density like RPC-628-1 instances in anomalous ways. It's theorized that its accumulation and loss of characteristics are interchangeable with RPC-628-1 instances. (For further details, see Addendum 2.)

+++ **Addendum:**
[[collapsible show="Addendum 1: Vivisection and Analysis" hide="RPC-628 analysis and vivisection"]]
> After weeks of conversation with RPC-628, permission was given from the entity to dissect and analyze its inner anatomy. A laser scalpel was used for the first operation, revealing samples of a digestive system, a nervous system and other vital organs. After 10 minutes of cutting through the flesh, sounds of distress came from RPC-628, with people who were able to hear its vocalisations were bleeding from their ears and experienced tinnitus[[footnote]]Tinnitus is the perception of noise or ringing in the ears.[[/footnote]]. After 2 minutes, the staff was able to find a heart. The laser malfunctioned and slit the heart. Upon cutting it, screams and wailing were heard coming from RPC-628, making the researchers in the vicinity temporarily go deaf, and forcing them to turn the laser off. After a few seconds, RPC-628 had healed from its injuries and started to pump blood normally.
>
> The operation was concluded shortly after.
>
> The results indicated that RPC-628 is close to immortality due to the effects of RPC-628-A.

[[collapsible show="Addendum 2: Interview with RPC-628" hide="Interview performed with RPC-628"]]
> **Interviewed:** RPC-628
> 
> **Interviewer:** Dr. Joseph
> 
> **Foreword:** Authority staff enter the part of the cave where RPC-628 currently stays in, they are being guided by one of the monks who show them around. When arriving, the monk notiflies them that only one person should enter at a time. Dr. Joseph was asked to use a hazmat suit by the research team, however, he refuses the offer so Joseph would look as human as possible to RPC-628. Mentioning he wishes to be "face to face."
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Monk D███:** I will have to ask, Mr. Joseph, will you need anything before the interview?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Absolutely, a cup of water would be enough, I haven't drank anything in a few hours.
> 
> **RPC-628:** Joseph, is it?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Oh, hello, I didn't know you were awake already, I'm sorry to disturb. I am just here to ask some questions. Now, we usually call you by Registered Phenomena Code 628, but do you have any names you would like to be called by while I'm performing this interview?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Names. I haven't heard those in a long time. Where I came from I used to be called Jonah, but you can call me by how they call me now. What was it? RPC-684?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** RPC-628.
> 
> **RPC-628:** Right, sorry, I was never good with numbers, not my forte if I say so myself, I'm usually more of a... uh...
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Maybe a musician? Given your vocal abilities.
> 
> //The monk comes in bringing a cup of water to the researcher, he thanks by nodding his head//
> 
> **RPC-628:** That's the word, sorry, my mind hasn't been in perfect shape after 200, 300... 400?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Weeks?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Years.
> 
> //Researcher Joseph, who was currently drinking in his cup of water, chokes from the surprise, after a few coughs he continue the interview.//
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Sorry, this caught me off-guard. I thought Humpback whales lived up to 50, even the maximum possible for a whale would be 90 for Blue whales.
> 
> **RPC-628:** I guess you haven't lived enough until you make people shocked by your age.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** It's the first time I am talking to something this ancient, no offence, but I thought you would be a bit younger. So that proves you are not exactly a whale, at least not a normal one, so what are you?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Well, my species when reaching maturity are supposed to pick a form to use for the rest of their lives, a hard choice for most, but for me it was the easiest answer to make; those majestic creatures swimming at the surface of the sea, so free and released of the shackles that life has, they just swim. No great number of predators or prey, the concept of survival are just unknown for them as they are the biggest creatures they know... Unconfined.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** I see, it's an interesting way to see things. Speaking of which, could you tell me more about yourself? Let's go by steps.
> 
> **RPC-628:** I already told you one curiosity about myself, It is your turn to speak about yourself, Mr. Joseph.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Oh? I wasn't expecting this. Caught off guard once more, but to clarify, I am here to ask questions about you. The interview is about the ancient moss whale creature, not the boring researcher.
> 
> **RPC-628:** I refuse to continue this interview unless you start saying something about yourself first.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Well, fine,  I'm 36, I have a wife and a daughter, Rose and Lizabeth, who I love with all my heart.
> 
> **RPC-628:** Children...
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Something that comes onto your mind?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Had two; one boy and a girl. I sometimes wonder, where are they now...
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Please tell me more about them; what are their names, what are the last memories you have of them?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Oh, Jehortes and Jalihya, sometimes Ephren. Those silly kids, they were never much of the explorer kind, no matter how much I tried to show them the outside world they always had more fun at home.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Home? Where is that?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Far... Eden.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Where can you find that place?
> 
> **RPC-628:** It's not anywhere close to here. It's a home I was thrown out of and into this abandoned world. They thought it was destroyed after a certain incident.. I was temporarly assigned as a Guardian to come here and scout the area, I was supposed to come back in a week or two. What they weren't expecting is that I would ever fall in love with this place and the people inside it.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Earth?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Earth.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So you never came back?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Yes, one of my many regrets, maybe my main regret, leaving my children behind, especially Ephraim...
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So can you tell me more about tha-...
> 
> **RPC-628:** Please change topics, it's too painful to think about what I left, and what I will never come back to.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** I am sorry, could you please talk about your uh, anomalous effects?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Anomalous effects? Never heard of those.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Thaumaturgy? I think you call it magic?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Yes. Absolutely, Nowadays I am not half of what I once was. Long story short, the gods gave me those abilities temporarily to make me able to explore and so I did. I saw the blue sky as I floated through the clouds, jumping from wind to wind. Its where life wanted to take me, with the birds at day and with the stars at night, the most pure form of freedom. It was the unknown, a pure kind of freedom, sometimes magical, sometimes dangerous.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So one of the examples of your anomalous effects was free mobility due to mass alteration.
> 
> **RPC-628:** What hard words. To be more specific, mass interchanging with me and the moss that follows me. I produce it by myself, most of which are currently staying in the cave, sometimes my only company. I had many abilities, most related to the nature of movement. Which is ironic, after I got inflicted with this.
> 
> //RPC-628 raises its head slightly, pointing to the harpoon currently embedded in its stomach.//
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Oh. Well, do you wanna talk about that?
> 
> **RPC-628:** The Harpoon, right. What is there to say;  considerable pain that I don't even notice anymore unless I move. Speaking of which, it took all my movement from my head down to my whole body. Oh! And I almost forgot, it won't let me die no matter how hard I try.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** You tried to kill yourself?
> 
> **RPC-628:** In the first years, then I tried with help, but the anomalous effects of the moss provided who was provided to me as a "blessing". Now leaves me incapable of of interacting physically with other beings without getting their bones crushed or their water absorbed completely, leaving them as dry as my soul.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** A blessing? But I thought you mentioned it as a being Thaumaturgic, sorry, "Magic." If that were the case, wouldn't you be able to control the effects?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Yes, when I was able to control myself, my thoughts, or my own body. Now look at my pathetic state. Do you think I can do anything else other than whine and complain?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So you can't even enact most of your anomalous effects due to your body limitations.
> 
> **RPC-628:** You don't understand, I was the happiest being alive, I've entered this place. And I found love, a love almost harder than any seen before, the love for discovery. I found humans, I discovered their ways. I watched them closely, befriended each and every one I found, their ingenuity, their love, grief, connections, I loved every piece of it, the good and the bad, I adored it... I wanted it.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So you ran away. Leaving all behind.
> 
> **RPC-628:** And never looked back, worst mistake of my life.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** And how did it happen?
> 
> **RPC-628:** They came looking. Of course they did, they were to bring me back, relegate me, I was lucky that I saw them first, I ran to a place where they would never get me, the deep dark parts of the ocean.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** And? What happened next?
> 
> **RPC-628:** I found the best and the worst that the unknown could ever offer me, the kindest soul I've ever met and the most foul one that would lay his presence upon me. I remember that day clearly, how everything started and how everything ended. The glowing creature, something like a child, he didn't knew the sea and was lost in its own innocence. It was almost like it was just born, one of many lost in the world it was put in, surrounded by darkness. I felt like that once, I would never come back to it. So I didn't let him come back to it.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Wait, so you stole a child?
> 
> **RPC-628:** I freed a child. From a life of darkness and eternal suffering.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Saving a stranger? At the cost of your own life?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Yes, However I was too slow, the creature in the throne noticed it too soon. From its own body came a spear, a harpoon, he threw immediately. It was like the laws of physics didn't apply to him, he moved so quickly I couldn't even tell when I was been pierced by the cursed item, then he looked me. Those eyes... They..
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** What about them?
> 
> **RPC-628:** They were soulless and yet, I saw so many things from just looking at them, none of which were good things, I was quickly stuck as he pulled back. I made the glowing child as light as he could be, he floated, screaming for a name he didn't knew, pointing at me with suffering as I slowly died. Desperate screams echoed through the voidfull ocean. I closed my eyes as I heard its voice. It told me: "Live."
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So he just let you go?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Exactly, It freed me to the world, when I opened my eyes I was unable to move around, beached at a fishing town in Italy. The first person walking around was this one bearded man, a special person, behind him were some fisherman coming to check if I were dead.
>  
> **Dr. Joseph:** Do you have any ideas why he would ever do that?
> 
> **RPC-628** His motives are unknown, but I knew from that moment on I would never be the same. I still have nightmares of that day. Its presence sits still at the hollow throne as it looked into the nothingness. Sometimes hear his voice in my darkest times, he talks about his one child, he says he is coming. He told me he'd let me go to watch with him as the day of his child arrives.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Uh... wow, so, who was that "special person" you mentioned just now?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Oh, David, or as your people call them, Davinci?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Da-Davinci?
> 
> **RPC-628:** That one, such a kind soul. He drove the fisherman away as he tried calling for help to bring me back to the sea. They failed of course, because of my weight.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** How wonderful of an artist to be so kind to you.
> 
> **RPC-628:** Yes, he was the last one to leave and when they all did. He just stood there, saying sorry for not being able to help, such a sensitive and good soul.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** What did you do then?
> 
> **RPC-628:** I talked to him, and there we stood, watching the stars, for hours to come, talking, and the more I felt free from the obligations of life, the more we talked, the more unchained I felt. slowly, I float to the skies, to meet the stars, like a balloon I was, going up, he stared as he started to think.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** You two seemed like close friends, how did you get in here then?
> 
> **RPC-628:** After I started floating he came with a machine to help me alocate, with the help of the monks of this place, I slowly came to this. My home.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** And are you happy with the current state you are in?
> 
> //After some minutes of silence, RPC-628 looks up to the roof of the cave//
> 
> **RPC-628:** After years of thought, I can say for sure it became repetitive, no matter how many changes or people come, Its still all the same, I guess, I'm just tired.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** If you could do one last thing before we go what would it be?
> 
> **RPC-628:** I want to look the stars... one last time.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** After the cooperation with RPC-628 and Authority with the interview. Authority staff is planning on creating an engine to move the whale to the outside of the cave without much movement and bring it to a safe place to help the entity and attempt to remove RPC-628-A from its stomach. By Request, to be any more visits to be made to RPC-628's current location. Such actions were requested by the monks for a defined amount of time. It is unknown what motives were behind such actions, but they are to be respected.

The status of AEDF recovery operations for RPC-628 are ongoing, but the state being of RPC-628 is unknown. For these particular reasons, previous containment protocols, excluding recovery, are no longer required.

**Description:** RPC-628 was an aquatic mammalian organism measuring 430m long and weighing approximately 524,000kg that resembled a common //Megaptera Novaeangliae//[[footnote]] Humpback Whale. [[/footnote]]. 

Even though the Authority does not have the corpse of RPC-628 in its possession currently, it was noted that RPC-628-A was removed due to unknown circumstances speculated to be related to the GOI "Eden's Garden", RPC-628-A is currently in the monks' possession and Authority personnel are currently attempting to get permission for the research of the object. 

While RPC-628 possessed a varity of anomalous abilities, the removal of RPC-628-A is believed to have neutralized the entity, and effectively caused the cessation of all anomalous properties of the entity.

When speaking to one of the St. Johns monks, responding agents was brought outside of the cave and shown a massive tree with no leaves. Engraved into the tree are the words:

[[collapsible show=""Forever In The Stars J.C."" hide=""Forever In The Stars, J. C."" hideLocation=""]]
The cathedral in the distance overshadows the man walking towards it. Those were the early hours of the morning, after midnight and before dawn. Walking along with the town with a serious expression on his face, a hooded man. Wearing a silken handmade robe that was clearly frayed with time and muddied by effort, he has been walking for hours maybe days. It was clear the traveler has been through a lot to reach that place. His clothes were far from clean, and ripped in some parts, clearly that place was not close to home. His reasons for being at St. John's church were unknown, maybe finishing some business, maybe starting some more.

In the middle of the road a hooded man, he walked through a common town. He appeared to not like to show his face, he wouldn't show it, the man on its surrounding nervously talked about him. He answered completely patiently and with a calm tone in his voice, he appeared to know what's going to happen next, like a gut feeling. And when he reached the church, he felt a cold gust down his spine. Only one step forward, he knocked on the door three times with the huge door knocker. Only one man answered the door, he opened it slightly. The man showed an annoyed expression, he asked their motives, the wooden man, full of hope, answered clearly:

"I am here to make a visit."

The young one was quickly silenced by the raising of the hand of the elder figure. The elder watched that figure, judgementally as the hooded figure slowly pulled down his hood, revealing a wooden body, and a wooden mask, with wooden horns, that almost reached the sky from the elders perspective, and, of those horns, he broke each one in half and offered them as he bowed.

He handed the branches on his horns to the priest.

"The motives I'm here for are not important, what is important is who is inside there, a long time friend of mine is currently in need of help and I came here to do so."

The old man still with a doubting face slightly closed the door, as he stopped was stopped by the wooden man who quickly put his hand on the door, to anyone else, that would have been 4 broken fingers, but to the wooden man? Only 1. As he mumbled in pain he pleads once more, now looking directly into the man's eyes.

"I haven't seen Jonah in way more than a decade, way more than 2, I can't remember his face, but I do remember my name, tell him Ephraim is here."

The man had a slight discomfort to see that type of circumstance happening in front of him as if he had lived enough to know what is coming next. And even so, he closed his eye, sighed one time, and said, as he closed the door.

"Stay here, and put that hood back on."

After some minutes of the man waiting impatiently sitting on the stairs, looking at the stars of that cold night. And the sound of the door opening could be heard slowly, the wooden man quickly raised to hear what the man had to talk about, once again, the door only opened slightly.

"He says he remembers it all, please wear this and enter."

As the group of priests starts to look from afar, the wooden humanoid starts moving towards the door, the man in the door raised a hand and said.

"I said to him, not you lot."

The group of priests had mixed reactions, some saddened, other, suspicious, for the man had walked for hours, sleepless to meet the one called Jonah on the church, and now he would finally be able to enter.

With an understanding reaction, they started walking away. The door revealed a huge room as it opened, the home to Jonah. The creature changed his clothes into a traditional monk uniform and started to follow the elderly man. On the way, they start talking."Ephraim? He has been spelling your name wrong for a long time eh?"

Ephraim laughed.

"He always did, Used N instead of M or E instead of A… We used to laugh those off in his house as we waited for Jehortes and Jalihya to get home."
The elder man smiled, but his smile quickly became one of doubt and concern.

"Can you truly help him like you say you can? I do not know your ways but I have known Jonah since I've entered this establishment.”

He stopped and looked directly into the Wooden man's mask and his eyes. Ephraim says "If I get punished for what I am about to do, I do not care…" Both were silent for half a minute.

"All I want is to make a friend smile one last time." The Ephraim put his hand on the old man’s shoulder as he raised his head once more.

"Please allow me to show you, there is nothing to worry about."

After that interaction, they went back to walking, to the deepest depths of the cave, where many mysteries awaited discovery. Soon enough, they reach a large portion of the cave where a giant whale lived. His name was Jonah Charte.

The hooded man didn't want his presence to be known, so his steps were silent as he slowly approached the whale in the pond. But once he reached the moss area, Jonah realized who was approaching.

"I can't believe it… Ephrein, Ephram is that you?" He smiled, when with it his mask did too. The wooden man came running to hug his long lost friend. Time might have passed but their friendship was never closer than right now. Tears came down the whale’s eyes.

"I thought I would never see you again, I missed you so fucking much."

The whale scolded him playfully.

"Watch the language boy… even though you are too old for the title."

Ephraim stopped hugging his friend and quickly started to lay on the whale’s back.

"Just grew a few inches… but god, I have so much to tell you, where should I even start?"

The whale was quick to answer.

"My children, Jehortes and Jalihya, where are they right now?"

Ephraim realized that Jonah hasn't seen his children in decades. Ephraim started to remember what the children did recently.

"Jehortes entered the academy in first or second place, Jalihya has chosen a more free-spirited path and decided to join the scouting team."

"But I thought she hated"

"Exploring? She did… And also going outside, but since you left, she started realizing those were the things you loved the most, and to come closer to you, she started practicing to become as big as her dad one day. Back in the day you were a big deal though… so when we heard the news, It was…"

Silence took over the room as both started to think of something to say.

"I am so sorry."

"You shouldn't be."

Ephraim put his hand on the creature's stomach.

"You did something you loved more than anything.”

He starts to pass his hand around the body, through the moss, as they started to float in the air.

"You left us and only left your memory but I understand. You shouldn’t have been punished.”

The whale pointed his head to the roof as he said.

"No you’re wrong, I should have been. I was naive enough to go to what I found out to be the forbidden lands."

Ephraim started tapping slightly on the part of his stomach close to the harpoon, then he starts talking with a worried tone.

"I heard it, the deep is alive and well then… doesn't matter much though, the only problem is, I knew you were alive, somewhere, and when I found out you were hiding…"

"I'm not hiding I swear."

"You were running from your responsibilities Jonah, people thought you died in battle, in this dangerous world, instead you ran never coming back… you…"

"Felt in love with humanity."

A silence came upon the room as Ephraim moved his hand close to the harpoon.

"And so did I."

Ephraim grabbed the Harpoon and started screaming in agony as soon as he did, glyphs appeared on his hand as he wailed in pain to his friend who was completely caught off guard.

"I finally understand Jonah, I really do."

"What are you doing Ephraim!"

He grabs the harpoon with both hands.

"What I should have done long ago."

The surrounding starts to shift and the air starts to become heavier as the presence of both entities start to clash severely as Ephraim's eyes dilated, he started to put roots around the harpoon and slowly pull it out, his hand starts to slowly get absorbed by the harpoon itself, Ephraim is quick to slash off his hand before it affects the rest of his body, and removes the Harpoon by infusing his vital energy to Jonah's body.

"I see why you love this so much more than your home… It's so full of… mysteries and undiscovered truths… It's literally the perfect home for someone like you Jonah."

"Ephraim… the Harpoon, y- your arm!"

"Eve blessed you with the ability to explore, she blessed me with abilities of healing, this is what I will do with this world, Jonah…"

"I'm not feeling good Ephraim… My vision is getting blurry, my body is getting light, Ephraim, I feel… I feel scared…"

"Jonah? Jonah no, you just came back, you can’t die here!"

After heavy breathing from the whale, Ephraim threw the harpoon to the wall with his one hand as he started to concentrate, and roots came from the cave floor.

"If we reach the area in time we can bring you to Eden and… and.."

"Please listen to me, Ephraim, I want you to hear what I need to say before I die."

"You’re not going to die!"

"Tell them I'm sorry, I was a coward… Naive… and extremely stupid-…"

"Shut up!"

The roots start to raise to the roof, where they started to dig to the outside.

"You are none of those things, you are my friend… and our dearest family, they need to see you one last time, you need to continue your…"

"I don't want to continue… I have lived through too much already."

"Jonah stop saying that, for all, that's glory stop, you have so much ahead of you."

There’s silence for a few seconds, followed by Jonah's voice saying.

"If this life has taught me anything it’s that we plant what we want, but we don't always want what we get."

Ephraim is silent.

"I want to see the stars, Ephraim, it is my last wish to see them as close as one can be."

The silence continues for a minute and Ephraim stops digging into the roof. He lays on the floor and begins to quietly laugh.

"You want to die in space?"

The whale looks up.

"Forever in the stars."

Ephraim starts to sit and look down, then he gets up and starts digging once more.

"I'm not doing that."

"You can't deny my last request."

"It's NOT your last request!”

"Ephraim I am dying."

Ephraim comes up to Jonah and starts to lay his hand on him, a glow of green light starts coming up from his body and hands. He attempts to heal him in any way possible.

"This won't work."

"It will.”

"I needed the harpoon to keep me alive Ephraim, I'm at my limit…"

"No, you’re not.”

A slight pause is made as Ephraim grasps Jonah’s body.

"Don’t bullshit me!”

“Ephraim…"

Ephraim starts crying as the glow gets stronger and begins to weep…

"I never been closer to someone, you can't die, Jonah, they need you, we need you… I need you."

The whale laid his full body on the floor, breathing heavier.

"Things change, even if you want it or not Ephraim, those are my last moments, do you want to spend them fighting?"

Ephraim stops the glow and starts hugging him as strong as he can, and unwillingly, the roots start surrounding the whale too. A minute pass as they continue digging. Ephraim stops hugging and looks up, as they finally reach the surface.

"Next step… the stars."

After some practicing with stones, Ephraim manages to throw one with roots so far it appears to reach a place where he couldn't see it any longer.

"Are you sure you want this?"

He hesitates at first, but Jonah quickly answers.

"Never been surer."

Ephraim looks at the stars, the soon to be the new home of Jonah, he got lost in the stars as he looked, he has never been more focused on something.

"What if you don't come back?"

"What if I do?"

He thought about it, but his mind wasn't working correctly at the moment.

Ephraim looked down at the floor, in dismay, as he started kicking stones around to the edge of the cliff, he seemed lost, almost like a child.

"My child..."

The whale starts looking up to the stars as he wondered what he is leaving behind, better, what he left behind, and with that in mind, he says:

"Tell them..."

"Please don't say what I think you are about to say"

They sit in silence, both staring at each other for a few moments, as Ephraim comes back looking at the stars, before Jonah continues speaking.

"Tell them I'm sorry when you meet them, will you?"

"You know I will, old man… You know I’ll miss you too.”

The roots start surrounding the whale, and they start rising from the floor, it wouldn't be needed though, Jonah would just float until he reaches there.

"I hope you do, it makes me feel younger"

A quietude comes as their eyes meet for one last time.

"I can feel your soul, you aren't really dying now are you?"

"Maybe in a few days."

They smile at each other as they look at the stars.

"I do feel alive now though, this was the most intense day I had since the accident."

Both start laughing as they know this will be their last conversation, the grip on the roots start getting harder to make sure the throw is certain.

"My unofficial third child, this was the best life one could ask… no matter what happened in between. Thank you, Ephraim."

"I'll never forget you, Jonah. Thank you for everything you've done."

Ephraim bows as the roots start preparing, after a minute, a ground crushing sound is heard as the roots throw Jonah into the sky, in the direction of the sea.

"Goodbye… dad."

Ephraim starts walking back to the door of the cathedral, to his surprise, all the members were waiting for him outside, shocked to see him on the outside, they start asking questions

"What happened to your arm?"

"Did you find Jonah? How is he? Can we help?"

"Were you kicked out of the church?"

Ephraim makes a happy expression through the mask and starts walking in the other direction of the church. The members of the church followed him through.

"Hold those who are dearest to you closest to your heart, and never let go."

On the way, he comes close to a forest, there he creates a wood structure, appearing to resemble a tree. The monks were shocked with such a performance, as they realized the tree had no leaves or fruits, only a stalk where he wrote a message with his remaining arm.


+++ Forever In The Stars, J. C.

Ephraim placed his hand on the tree, putting the moss inside it and walked off.

"I'm going to go home, this was a long day, and I need some rest."

The monks waved a last time as they wondered, and Ephraim smiled, looking at the sky, the stars, the clouds, and all in between, saying the last goodbye to his dear friend.
