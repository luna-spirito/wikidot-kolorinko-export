[[tab Overview]]
= [[size large]]**Overview**[[/size]]

The Remnants are the fractured remains of an Authority from another timeline whose actions led to a sudden and inexplicable event (dubbed the the ALPHA-Trigger) which cause a single, irreparable, universe-wide calamity and subsequent cascade of anomalies that fundamentally changed life forever for those residing within it. According to acquired documents, most, if not all attempts at trying to counteract the ensuing chaos failed, as did most world governments and the veil of normalcy itself. Time, reality, memories, and the fabric of reality made into aberrations far beyond recognition; combined with wide-spread containment breaches, the Authority's chain of command quickly broke down.

The remnants of the Authority, led by the fractured remains of its former rulers, managed to survive the calamity with a substantial amount of technology and personnel. Those leaders who had access to transdimensional technologies quickly used the last of their resources to evacuate the remaining personnel to pre ALPHA-trigger timelines of relative normality. The preamble signs of their ALPHA-Trigger are beginning to show in our world.

As for those few who remained, some have begun to jump into our timeline, influencing the world in unseen ways. Playing with variables, attempting to pinpoint the source of the errors and actions that lead to their downfall—a source they cannot even agree on among themselves—all in an attempt to amend their failures in our reality, and hopefully prevent what they see as inevitable from occurring. Be it by causing "black-outs" in several sites only for their databases to come back missing more than one RPC, or utilizing advanced and anomalous equipment from their reality to aid the Authority, as the purported "source" varies from Authority failures to outside meddling these groups know no bounds. Driven by a maniacal denial of their world's fate, jealousy and disbelief that our Authority endures sans "failure", or a genuine desire to help avoid calamity, they can be both helpful allies or ruthless foes.

For this reason, and despite their usual cooperation with the Authority, they are often seen on both sides of conflict, changing based on what they think might affect the future in a beneficial manner. Additionally, they are almost impossible to locate or find by reliable means due to their constant paranoia, foresight of our timeline’s continuity, and guerilla tactics, and use this advantage to blindside Authority agents, GoIs, and AoIs in the field. There is a considerable debate among top level bureaucrats as to whether to directly interfere in their actions, or allow them to continue to surgically remove "errors" they believe may play a part in the ALPHA-Trigger. As of now, the Intranet has been fortified substantially, but Remnant "black-outs" still occur once every few years.

[[tab Status Report]]
Very little is known about the current state of the Remnant's world, and all reliable information, obtained from Ark documents and eye-witness accounts, has been compiled below. It should be noted that only reports of currently studied areas have been added into the continental status segment.

> = [[size large]]**Timeline Report**[[/size]]
>
> The Remnants timeline is chronologically fragmented, ahead or behind by ~20 years contingent on location, weather, means of entry, and lesser known factors—with the difficulties and contradictions that implies. While their world was similar in history, culture, geography, and more prior to the disaster, fragmentary evidence suggests a technological gap further agitated in their favor by rapid technological/anomalous development following the ALPHA-Trigger. This is not uniform across the timeline: many areas and groups are strikingly regressive, while others wield equipment superior to anything in our Authority's possession.
>
> -----
>
> = [[size large]]**Continental Status**[[/size]]
> 
> **North America** - In the western world, those embers of civilization that prevailed fight with one another, as well as with their new environment, for survival. Anomalies render a grand part of the continent inhospitable, dangerous, or simply threatening to acknowledge. Yet, society still manages to thrive within the rubble.
> 
> **South America**  - The ALPHA-Trigger equivalent of a nuclear wasteland, anomalies now reign over South America and its inhabitants; countries turned to ash, reshaped into low-coherence nightmare lands or haunted by the creatures of their every myth. Only few centers of society remain, and their approach to the unnatural can be quite aggressive.
> 
> **Africa** - Often described as hell on earth, active volcanoes cover the land, and demon-like beings crawl out the ground only to be hunted down for blood by those who remain. And for those few who dared explore, Egypt is recorded as having fallen into primordial chaos.
> 
> **Europe** - Scrap-Knights and metallic dragons roam what is left of Europe and communes fall and rise with the wars of mages and the sudden appearance of temporal aberrations, all in the blink of an eye. Some reports claim that the remains of various governments and AoIs still attempt to bring a sense of order to the lands.
> 
> **Asia** - Claims of those who have ventured into Asia vary greatly. Some claim it to be a dreamlike land where only madness is constant, while others retell the falling and rising empires of Siberia. Although all remain consistent with the non-existence of Korea.
> 
> **Australia** - Reports of super-fauna and anomalous behemoths are common across the Sandy Desert, cities and people melting into one another, and even some claims about Sydney being ravaged by broods of parasitic grubs.

[[tab Factions]]

The following is an incomplete list of the factions that still remain, or have formed, within the Remnants timeline, separated by their abilities to travel between dimensions.

**Temporally Active**

[[div class="content-box"]]

= **Auctoritas Intelligencia**

----

The Auctoritas Intelligencia is led by the remnants counterpart to our current GD-CMDR, "Lightning Rod". According to Remnants documentation, he coup'd the current GD-CMDR in his timeline, after agents and soldiers around him held a private vote of no-confidence. After which, they accumulated a fair amount of Authority-grade equipment, including experimental Authority equipment that can only be theorized due to the advanced nature of ALTR-D2AJ, and the Intelligencia's clandestine behavior.

The Auctoritas Intelligencia is currently operating out of the Russian Extreme North, where they have established a base with a permanent temporal rift that allows them to freely travel between both timelines. They are locked in a permanent war with an anomalous self-proclaimed 'god' known only as Mars Aeris, what is presumed based on residual reports to be the Remnants version of "GD-WAR", a deranged shell of his former self whose mind and body have mutated beyond recognition resultant from the anomalous effects of the ALPHA-Trigger—thereby taking it as his personal mission to strike down supposed enemies of the long-gone Authority, using the corrupted minds and flesh of those he conquered to do so.
[[/div]]

[[div class="content-box"]]

= **The Eyes of the Ark**

-----

The Ark is a group of traveling caravans, researchers, mercenaries, and sometimes anomalous humanoids led by the Department Heads of the Research Division prior to the ALPHA-Trigger. There is no clear date for the foundation, as its members, goals, and name have frequently changed throughout the years following the dissolution of the Authority. Only recently have they managed to properly organize with the discovery of Site-112 and Project Dominion.

Thanks to its secluded nature and anomalous-powered defenses, Site-112 only suffered mild casualties during the ALPHA-Trigger, possessing prototypes of advanced technology and running data servers—commonly carried by caravans to archive their discoveries. Most important of all, however, were the remains of Project Dominion and Mobile Study Station #33, an unfinished research naval ship capable of self-propelled flight, commonly known as the “Ark”. Over the years, the Ark has improved upon its ship’s design, using scrap technology and anomalies to power their endeavors across the globe—even going as far as to develop functional inter-dimensional rifts. This allowed the previously de-centralized groups to communicate with one another, as well as acquiring a permanent base of operations to share and store information and artifacts once excursions are over.

The Ark travels around the remaining continents of the world in order to catalog and study the many anomalies that have appeared since. This is done to warn nearby outposts and travelers of the dangers of each region or its inhabiting phenomena, leaving documents close by in newspaper stands or setting up warning signs, often including a crudely-drawn eye to signify their involvement. The Ark has also shown to be cooperative with Baseline Authority, providing documents on the effects a given anomaly may have should it breach containment.

[[/div]]

**Temporally Inactive/Ambiguous:**

[[collapsible show="[+] Access Current Database" hide="[-] Close Database"]]
**The Refounding Fathers:** A religious extremist GoI based in a nation-state located in and around Colorado, USA. The RF centers its ideals on exaggerated views and interpretations of the actions and texts left by the Founding Fathers of the United States. The most important asset owned by this group is 0B4-AI, a powerful technological anomaly believing itself to be former President Barack Obama.

**New Zanzibar:** Following the ALPHA-Trigger, Malthusian cells all around the world took up arms due to the believed confirmation of their ideology. Over time, different groups began to band together to form a "perfect commune" around the philosophy of Thomas Malthus, eventually transforming into feudal-like societies. Practicing eugenics and genetic modification, New Zanzibar has adapted to the new nature of the world rather than fight against it.

**Polar Sector-17:** Isolated from the bigger impact of the ALPHA-Trigger, GD-ANTC and the Authority Polar Reverse 17 (APR-17) have taken hold of their new icy fortress. Here, with the natural protection of the elements, they hope to slowly rebuild the Authority, or at least, try to bring humanity back in action—and if possible, revert the events that led to its current condition. Their success in this task is, however, unclear.

**Amazing! Co:** Despite all the odds, Amazing! Co keeps functioning in the same manner it had prior to the ALPHA-Trigger, if less unrestricted. Abandoned store shelves and trucks keep being filled by their products every month, although few are brave enough to take them. Merchandise packages sent to the remaining outposts and air-dropped product magazines have become day-to-day occurrences, with no end in sight.

**Children of Nihil:** Much like their unchanging deities, Nihil cults around the world have stayed consistent, thriving in their new-found freedom and connection with the anomalous. From small caravans to towns around heretical churches, it is not uncommon to see believers of the Suns wander around the remains of the Earth.

[[tab Writer’s Guide]]

When writing for remnants, there are a few things one should keep in mind:


[[div class="content-box"]]
= [[size large]]**The ALPHA-Trigger and the “World Before”**[[/size]]

The old world, and its blissful normality, are all gone because of the ALPHA-Trigger. But what is the ALPHA-Trigger? Well, it was an event caused in part by the Authority, as you’ve read in the Overview, that affected all of Remnant’s reality. This is all the information you’ll ever get on it.

We’ll never talk about what exactly was the ALPHA-Trigger, nor what led to it or what caused it. The people who know the exact details regarding the ALPHA-Trigger are dead or gone mad beyond reason. You can talk about what happened during or after it, but never attempt to **explain** it. This is the single most important taboo when writing for Remnants.

In a writing sense, the ALPHA-Trigger is the universal hand-wave that allows writers to imagine all the weird anomalies that plague this reality. Go wild with your ideas, it’s not like there’s any world left to destroy further.
[[/div]]
@@@@
[[div class="content-box"]]
= [[size large]]**The World of Remnants**[[/size]]

The sky isn’t perpetually brown-colored, nor is most of it a barren wasteland taken from the latest Fallout game. Is the world fucked? Yes, very much so. Do anomalies rule over the world? Well, they have become the new normal. However, this doesn’t mean the world is truly over, people simply need to adapt to it. Be it a group of friends making their way across the New York City rainforest or an order of scrap knights killing the hordes of demons inhabiting France. Overlooking the remains of Russia, a Pilot worshipper shares a beer or two with winged, angelic gopniks.

While nothing will ever be the same in the world of Remnants, this doesn’t mean that their whole landscape has become empty deserts and depressive rubble. It’s an aesthetic type of apocalypse, and using that to your advantage, rather than simply depicting the Mojave Desert over all of Earth, will help you on the journey to make a good Remnants article.
[[/div]]
@@@@
[[div class="content-box"]]
= [[size large]]**Time Travel, Dimensions, and You**[[/size]]
Be it former agents of the Authority trying to prevent the ALPHA-Trigger from occurring in our universe, the remains of the Research Division opening rifts in time and space to send documents and refugees to our reality, or fools trying to return back in time before their universe went down the sewer, messing with the fabric of reality has become a key element of the world.

From clandestine agents of the intelligencia, to friendly faces, or portals, from the Ark, writers have a lot at their disposal when seeking ways to make our two realities interact with each-other. This is also the main way in which documents are acquired into the database.

[[/div]]
@@@@

In short, people have a lot of options when it comes to writing. You can play anomalous politics with the factions, write about Authority studying this reality, write about documents written WITHIN the Remnants universe by its inhabitants, or make tales about cowboys killing demon lords. Creative freedom is the key-word within this reality, and we hope you make good use of it.

And as for formatting, there isn’t anything set in stone. Do whatever you want, or take examples from already-existing articles. Good luck!
