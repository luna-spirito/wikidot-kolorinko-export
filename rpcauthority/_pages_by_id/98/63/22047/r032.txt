+++ GROUPS OF INTEREST

> Since the dark ages, much of humanity has sought to control or hide the anomalous for its own selfish and unrighteous benefit. The Authority has kept extensive records on the actions and state of these groups, and has in recent years destroyed many that sought to do civilization harm. Many have been discovered or changed in the modern age, and may be grouped into multiple distinct categories. We have provided a useful code system for GOI distinction.
>

+ RPC Authority
++ ###f54242|A2-Unrighteous­­##

* Authority-Aware
* Veiled
* Seeker
* Sculptor
* Structured
* Multinational

The RPC Authority is an alternate timeline iteration of the Authority that has not passed the 1921 Berlin Charter. Reluctant in their use of the anomalous to their own ends, the Authority instead possesses an incorrect interpretation of its founding principles, being willing to delay the advancement of the human race or overlook major humanitarian disasters in its twisted sense of "Research", "Protection", and "Containment".

While the RPC Authority lacks control over major geopolitical figures, they remain one of the most dominant organizations within their local anomalous community. Other than a lower access to funding and other resources, the RPC Authority is structurally similar to the Authority.

**Verdict:**

This group has proven to be extremely unrighteous. While seemingly a clone of the RCPA (a hypothesis we were led to believe during our brief cooperation in the early 1950s) the RPC Authority has proven to be highly detrimental to the human race of the RPC Authority's universe. To forcefully liquidate the RPC Authority would open up the RCPA's access to double the resources and double the manpower, thereby progressing humanity of both universes further. Due to the unrighteous nature of the RPC Authority, rebellions are to be expected. The aforementioned rebellions are to be supported whenever possible.

Due to limits in our inter-universal technology, no major offensives into the RPC Authority is to be planned. However, based on our current understanding of inter-universal communication, only small-scale skirmishes may even be possible, rendering any plan for forced liquidation as impractical.

If you're reading this from an RPC Authority terminal, you have a limited time to repent. Cease your unrighteousness this instant, and we will not come for you.
[[/div]]

+ Amazing! Co.
++ ##purple|A4-Righteous##

* Authority-Aware
* Unveiled
* Sculptor
* Fractured
* Multinational

Amazing! Co. is a worldwide corporate conglomerate specializing in anomaly production. Their employment record is immaculate, their product list duly so. They produce weaponry, consumables, beauty products, toys, furniture- all in the name of Righteousness. Amazing! Co. is truly anomalous, it possesses no true CEO or organizational council. However, since the transformation of the Authority into its modern self, Amazing! Co. has become one of our most valued assets in the modern age of anomaly consumption.

**Verdict:** 

Despite its dubious origins, Amazing! Co. is one of the most important allies to the Authority's protection efforts, and its contributions to the overall status of humanity cannot be understated. Our militaries utilize Amazing! Co. branded guns, our civilians eat beans from an Amazing! Co. can, and our infrastructure is riddled with Amazing! Co. jobs. Above all else, friendly relations with this group must not fail, lest our cause be held back generations.
[[/div]]

+ Kabushiki Kawaii
++ ##blue|A2-Proxy##

* Authority-Aware
* Unveiled
* Sculptor
* Structured
* Multinational

Kabushiki Kawaii, informally referred to as Kawaii Korps in some sects, is a corporation specializing in anomalous cosmetic and sexual appeasement products. Much of Kabushiki Kawaii's catalogue may be considered immoral by some, yet their ability to create organic life from anomalous means is unchallenged by most other groups. 

**Verdict:** 

Righteous, but no longer needed. Kabushiki Kawaii's anomalous organic construction technology has proved incredibly valuable in the outfitting of our militaries with Protector units. Thought not truly needed in the modern age, as the technology is found in Authority sites across the globe, Kabushiki Kawaii has been allowed to continue producing anomalous entities for sexual and/or cosmetic purposes due to its usefulness. Authority operatives have been put in place within the corporation's CEO board, to ensure its complacency.
