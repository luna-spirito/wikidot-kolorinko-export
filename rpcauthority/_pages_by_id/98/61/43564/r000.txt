**Registered Phenomena Code:** XXX

**Object Class:** Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** Sapient Hazard, Sentient Hazard, Organic Hazard, Extra-dimensional Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** As RPC-XXX poses no threat, there is no requirement for active containment to be in place at this time. If RPC-XXX inadvertently manifests during low priority experimentation, then researchers are to record RPC-XXX. If the number of attempts to manifest exceeds ██, then testing is to cease so as not to waste Authority resources.

**Description:** RPC-XXX is the designation for an alternate reality that is only accessible via random chance when using realty jumping objects or technology (anomalous or otherwise). Consists of a black void containing a small fire, and three seemingly immortal male humans named John, Jim, and Bob (dubbed RPC-XXX-1 to 3 respectfully) that sit around the fire. They only spend their time making conversation and mocking each other jokingly. Through multiple recordings of their interactions, it is clear they possess extensive knowledge of the anomalous world, which includes comprehension of the RPC Authority's function. Although they are knowledgable about different alternate realities, they seem to be more familiar with the baseline universe then anything else. How they know this information is unknown. There is no known way to access inside of RPC-XXX, and RPC-XXX-1, 2, and 3 don’t respond to any attempts of communication despite the fact it seems as if they’re aware of the researchers watching them. When a portal or “window” leading to RPC-XXX is opened it will stay open for an undefined amount of time. Afterward it will close or power off at a random point in time, because of this RPC-XXX cannot stay open indefinitely.
