**Registered Phenomena Code:** 097

**Object Class:** Beta-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Visual Hazard, Sensory Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** A perimeter fence is to be erected at a 30-meters diameter surrounding the structure containing RPC-097. Security personnel are to patrol the perimeter to assure the structural integrity of the fence, and to prevent civilians from accessing the containment site. The containment site must operate under the guise of a private mining operation. The entrance to RPC-097 must be concealed at all times to prevent accidental observation. No personnel are to enter RPC-097 under any circumstances.

**Description:** RPC-097 is a hole in the floor of a small concrete structure, this hole has a diameter of 1.5 meters at its opening. The passageway within RPC-097 leads down at a 45-degree angle into the ground, the walls of this passageway consist of heavily rusted ribbed steel sheets similar to those used in roadside drains. No light sources exist within RPC-097, and its ultimate depth has yet to be determined.

Any subject who directly views the opening of RPC-097 invariably feels a compulsion to enter RPC-097, though this can be resisted with effort. Subjects who enter RPC-097 tend to prefer crawling headfirst down the passageway, often justifying their position based on visibility, regardless of if they’d been provided a light source before entry.

The passageway of RPC-097 is theorized to have a conical structure due to the fact that the diameter of the passageway was observed to have narrowed the deeper it extends. This narrowing is often unnoticed by subjects, leading to them becoming stuck within RPC-097. Subjects who become stuck exhibit great distress which escalates the longer they remain within RPC-097. Leaving stuck subjects within RPC-097 in excess of 4 hours has a 100% fatality rate. Subjects extracted from RPC-097 after becoming stuck tend to suffer several fractured bones, especially the pelvis.

>

> 
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** Please switch on your flashlight and tell us if you see anything.
> 
> **CSD-4932:** Oh, right!
> 
> //CSD-4932 switches on his flashlight and illuminates the passage within RPC-097.//
> 
> **CSD-4932:** It’s really rusty in here.
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** Noted. Please continue.
> 
> //CSD-4932 crawls further into RPC-097, the rusted steel catching on his CSD uniform, causing minor hindrances in his progress. Subject continues for several minutes before stopping, estimated distance travelled: 100 meters.//
> 
> **CSD-4932:** There’s a bunch of jagged edges sticking out from the floor. They smell like puke.
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** Noted. Keep going, but try not to hurt yourself.
> 
> //CSD-4932 is heard attempting to crawl over the hazard, followed by a low ripping sound.//
> 
> **CSD-4932:** Shit! Ripped the uniform.
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** It can be replaced. Please continue.
> 
> //CSD-4932 descends RPC-097 for 40 minutes without incident, estimated distance travelled: 2km//
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** Are you currently feeling anything? Any unusual thoughts or emotions?
> 
> **CSD-4932:** Yeah... This place makes me feel like I’m a little kid again. Before I went to jail, y’know?
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** Could you please elaborate?
> 
> **CSD-4932:** You ever explore the wilderness with your friends? Playing stuff like hide-and-seek? Well I’m getting that same kinda thrill.
>

> 
> //The exploration continues for approximately 35 minutes without anything of note. Estimated distance traveled: 3.5km//
> 
> **CSD-4932:** Uh, doc?
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** What did you find, CSD-4932?
> 
> **CSD-4932:** There’s this uhh...
> 
> //Radio silence for ten seconds.//
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** Well?
> 
> **CSD-4932:** There’s like, a thing here.
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** Be more specific.
> 
> **CSD-4932:** It’s, uhh. Running water.
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** That’s it?
> 
> **CSD-4932:** Well it’s like... Flowing wrong.
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** Flowing wrong?
> 
> **CSD-4932:** Yeah. It’s coming out of a little hole to my left, but it’s not flowing to the ground. It’s flowing to the ceiling. It’s like gravity isn’t working right.
> 
> **Researcher Hawco:** Odd. Please continue.
> 
> //CSD-4932 spends two hours descending RPC-097 without incident. Audio has confirmed the diameter of RPC-097 has narrowed enough to force CSD-4932 to crawl on his stomach. CSD-4932 does not notify mission control of these changes. Estimated distance travelled 6.75km.//

> **CSD-4932:** I-uhh. I think something is behind me.

> **Researcher Hawco:** Can you see anything?

>

>

> 
> **CSD-4932:** I-I’m fucking stuck! I can’t move!
>

> 
> **CSD-4932:** Help! Get me the fuck out of here! Please!
