**Containment Protocols**: RPC-001 cannot be contained due to it's distance. Confirmed instances of RPC-001 are to be handled by Authority Agents immediately with the use of Extergito Glyphs. All encounters, both unconfirmed and confirmed are to be logged immediately. Media reports of RPC-001 are to be hijacked/confiscated.

Authority Agents are to remain on standby from █████ to ████████.

**Description**: RPC-001 is an extremely dense space-time anomaly weighing approx. //7528.0 × 10^34 kg//. It is estimated to measure 720 km in diameter. It does not seem to orbit any object, and instead seems to be moving entirely on it's own at an average rate of 3100 m/s. It is usually an estimate of 200 million km away from Earth, 820 km when near Earth, 680 km when least visible to the naked eye and 570 km when objects are least affected (minor turbulence). Sightings of RPC-001 occur from August 15th to December 19th.

For clarification purposes, below is a list of distances / events.
* 200,000,000 km - usual distance
* 1100 km - near Earth
* 930 km - least visible
* 680 km - objects are least affected (small turbulence), sunlight noticeably dims

Objects within a 450 km radius of RPC-001 will noticeably gravitate towards it. Regardless of friction and gravity, objects will continuously accelerate towards RPC-001, constantly increasing in speed. Objects within a 10 km radius of it will spontaneously become physically distorted. Despite the gravitational force of RPC-001, damage to nearby terrain is minimal on a local scale.

The substance of RPC-001 is unknown. It is commonly theorized to be composed of dark-matter, though this theory is opposed by the theory that the hypothetical density of dark-matter does not match that of RPC-001.

Multiple sightings of RPC-001 have been made, most of them being historical encounters dating as far back as 3000 B.C.

**HISTORICAL LOGS**
 
RPC-001 has been described in multiple historical mediums such as stone tablets, hieroglyphics, cave paintings and carvings. It has been reported to have been described in Norse scriptures, Ancient Egyptian hieroglyphics, etc. RPC-001 has even been reported to have been described in the stones of Stonehenge.

Any reported descriptions of a "black sun" in ancient scriptures are to be confiscated and replaced with replicas, along with extergito glyph exposure.

**Incident 001-1** 

An example of one of the many cover-ups was the implementation of Aztec deities known as the "Tzitzimime". The cover-up was barely a success as a partial Aztec description of RPC-001 was logged and released to the public by archaeologists after discovery of Aztec scriptures and books originally containing information of RPC-001, before Authority action could take place. Said scriptures and books were confiscated by the Authority, and all citizens that had known of the scriptures were exposed to extergito glyphs. The scriptures were altered by the Authority with the use of anomalous objects and then sent back to the location where they were held after discovery. The altered scripture is widely regarded as the original scripture.

> The stars that can be seen around the Sun during a solar eclipse. This caused the belief that the Tzitzimime were deities that would descend upon Earth to devour human beings during solar eclipses.
