**Item #:** RPC-001

**Object Class:** Alpha

**Containment Protocols:** 
RPC-001 is to be secured in a reinforced meter containment chamber. Containment chamber should be constructed of monolithic stones that have minimum thickness of four (4) meters. Arrangement of stones must be conducted with no tools or beasts of burden. Gaps in the structure must be plugged in with loose sand and gravel until no visibility of RPC-001 is possible. 

A stone brick perimeter fence, five (5) meters high and twenty (20) meters away from RPC-001, is to be erected in a similar fashion. The site within the perimeter is hereby designated P-001. Outside of this perimeter, Authority Security is allowed to be armed with standard armaments. 

A no-fly zone is to be established over RPC-001 under guise of a missile test site. Additionally, radio silence is to be established within P-001. Authority Security may not radio one another if signals would conceivablely cross P-001.

Mobile Strike Team Quebec-14 (Hunter Gatherers) are to be stationed directly outside of RPC-001's containment chamber at all times. MST Quebec-14 is to be exclusively trained and armed with crude wooden cudgels and atlatls (thrown spears.) Additionally, MST Quebec-14 is to be dressed exclusively in animal skins belonging to mega-fauna native to the Pleistocene era or otherwise nude. Three (3) times a day, fresh water delivered in animal skins as well as wild game are to be introduced into P-001 to sustain MST Quebec-14.

No communication, verbal or otherwise, is allowed within P-001 except from a designated list of anthropologically confirmed grunts and signals. Improvised methods of communication are strictly forbidden. Under no circumstances are personal to interact with RPC-001. 

A standard nuclear warhead is to be buried thirty (30) meters beneath RPC-001. The warhead can be triggered on authorization of a Category 4 Personal or higher. 

**Description:** RPC-001 is a black sphere. RPC-001 can speak and has been described to have a voice reminiscent of a young British female. Subject is presumably friendly and has so far been cooperative to containment. 

**Further information of RPC-001 is unavailable to Restricted-level staff. Contact your Site Director for "Need-to-Know" access or input appropriate credentials.**

**Item #:** RPC-001

**Object Class:** Gamma

**Containment Protocols:** 
RPC-001 is to be secured in a reinforced containment chamber. Containment chamber should be constructed of monolithic stones that have minimum thickness of four (4) meters. Gaps in the structure must be plugged in with loose sand and gravel until no visibility of RPC-001 is possible. 

A stone brick perimeter fence, five (5) meters high and twenty (20) meters away from RPC-001, is to be erected. The site within the perimeter is hereby designated P-001. ##teal|--Outside of this perimeter, Authority Security is allowed to be armed with standard armaments.--##

Mobile Strike Team Quebec-14 (Hunter Gatherers) are to be stationed directly outside of RPC-001's containment chamber at all times. MST Quebec-14 is to be exclusively trained and armed with crude wooden cudgels and atlatls (thrown spears.) Additionally, MST Quebec-14 is to be dressed exclusively in animal skins belonging to mega-fauna native to the Pleistocene era or otherwise nude. Three (3) times a day, fresh water delivered in animal skins as well as wild game are to be introduced into P-001 to sustain MST Quebec-14.

No communication, verbal or otherwise, is allowed within P-001 except from a designated list of anthropologically confirmed grunts and signals. Improvised methods of communication are strictly forbidden. Under no circumstances are personal to interact with RPC-001.

 ##teal|--A nuclear warhead is to be buried one (1) kilometer underground in a heat-resistant sheath located within the magma chamber. The warhead can be triggered on authorization of a Category 4 Personal or higher. RPC-001's containment site is to be located above a super-volcano. Detonation of nuclear device must be sufficient to cause a breach in the magma chamber, leading to an eruption. Following detonation, procedure Quebec-0 are to be initiated to prevent catastrophic loss of life.--##

**Description:** RPC-001 is a black sphere. RPC-001 can speak and has been described to have a voice reminiscent of a young British female. RPC-001 is highly intelligent. All communication has indicated a complete disinterest towards the Authority. ##blue| RPC-001 is capable of bargaining and is considered reasonable. RPC-001 has thus far agreed to remain within the confines of P-001. The terms of the deal are [DATA REDACTED].##

RPC-001 has to ability to absorb any form of technology or culture in a four (4) meter vicinity. RPC-001 can only absorb technology or culture that is expressed vocally or in writing. Any objects developed post-civilization within RPC-001's vicinity are absorbed. Absorption occurs instantaneously in both physical objects as well as memories. ##blue|When an individual with intimate knowledge in a field reveals aforementioned knowledge, RPC-001 is capable of lifting the knowledge of said concept from the collective human consciousness, hereby referred to as a Consumption event. Effects of a Consumption event may lead to unacceptable breaches in human normalcy. See Addendum-001-A for suspected Consumption events.##

RPC-001 has a density that approaches singularity-level. It is currently unknown how RPC-001 maintains structural integrity due to the inability to study RPC-001 in detail. ##blue|From experimentation with introduction of post-civilization artifacts into P-001, RPC-001 gains mass each time it absorbs a physical object. However, much larger gains in mass are recorded after a Consumption event.##

##blue|In interviews RPC-001 has implied ties to the Church of Malthus. However, dates given by RPC-001 place its point of origin nearly ████████████ years into the future. RPC-001 remained elusive as to the purpose of its presence in the current day.##

##blue|**Addendum**##

> ##blue|**Addendum-001-A**##
> ##blue|Through use of Extergito Glyphs, the Authority has recovered several suspected Consumption events. These concepts were lost when Agent ██████ was caught in the vicinity of RPC-001.##
> 
> ##blue|**Concept Loss:** Games of Chance##
> ##blue|**Effects:** The concept of gambling never developed in human society. Entertainment has leaned more towards blood-sports with large gladiatorial arenas persisting into the modern day. Human race overall significantly more violent. Casinos non-existent.##
> ##blue|**Recovery Status:** Games of chance reintroduced to general public and mass deployment of Extergito Glyphs utilized. Casinos reinstated all under Authority umbrella providing a substantial boost to funding. Gladiatorial arenas torn down and built over, Colosseum in Rome retained but refurbished to resemble a ruin.##
> ##blue|**Additional Notes:** //It may be advantageous for the Authority to fill economic niche's that are vacated following a Consumption event.//##
> 
> ##blue|**Concept Loss:** Ball Bearings##
> ##blue|**Effects:** Human industry roughly a decade behind technologically. Aerospace and wheel technologies reliant on unusual combination of magnetic sand and mercury. Bikes and skateboards much less prominent than before Consumption event. Atmospheric pollution markedly increased as well as rampant mercury pollution in bodies of water. ##
> ##blue|**Recovery Status:** Mass deployment of Extergito Glyphs utilized. Heavy atmospheric scrubbing and decontamination of water supplies managed to reduce pollution to pre-Consumption levels. Aquatic wildlife still contaminated with believable levels of mercury. Ball bearings and aeronautic technology reintroduced to industry under Authority patent. ##
> ##blue|**Additional Notes:** //None.//##
> 
> ##blue|**Concept Loss:** Foam-Hzergold Gallingtons##
> ##blue|**Effects:** Records indicated a 600% increase in efficacy and success of Authority operations prior to Consumption event. ##
> ##blue|**Recovery Status:** Rediscovery have been unsuccessful due to lack of required ██████ and █████████. Research is ongoing.##
> ##blue|**Additional Notes:** //Rediscovery of Foam-Hzergold Gallington and its associated technology is a top priority. Security has been heightened around individuals with knowledge of high-level Authority assets.//##

**Item #:** RPC-001

**Object Class:** Gamma

**Containment Protocols:** Current containment procedures are in place to maintain sense of security among personal at minimum costs through fabrications of a nuclear volcanic eruption, a "deal" struck with RPC-001, and unusual construction of the containment cell. MST Quebec-14 (Hunter Gatherers) is considered lost due to devolution of personal. Due to RPC-001's inevitable expansion within roughly 40 years, the Authority is to prepare for an inevitable containment failure. Recovery of past cycle's technology is a high priority.

**Description:** RPC-001 is a black sphere. All attempts to breach the exterior of RPC-001 have met with failure. RPC-001 contains a hostile artificial intelligence whose stated goal is the destruction of human civilization and the return to a hunter gatherer society.

RPC-001 carries out its goal by unknown means, designated as Consumption events. Entire concepts can be destroyed, retroactively undoing any developments in said area. In a mean of fifty-four percent (54%) of cases, the Consumption event does not radically alter modern-day society as a replacement is found. However, certain concepts, such as "politics" or "agriculture," if consumed, present an unacceptable risk to the existence of the Authority as well as the human species as a whole.

Due to the nature of RPC-001's intelligence, RPC-001's true range is unknown is potentially unlimited due to unconfirmed Consumption events. Investigation into these potential Consumption events shows a meticulous undercutting of civilization foundations. 

RPC-001 claims to have been constructed by the Church of Malthus fourteen (14) billion years in the future. It claims to have reduced society to near ruin before being sent back in time into the current day.

Historical records under Extergito Glyphs and rock samples have indicated that RPC-001 has been operating on a semi-regular cycle. Roughly every 6000 years, RPC-001 begins to exponentially grow in size, feeding on civilization while doing so. After completely resetting civilization back to a point of hunter-gatherer's RPC-001 likely shrinks back to its initial size and remains dormant for another 6000 years.

There have been an estimated 500 cycles of human civilization and an unknown amount of non-human civilizations that have existed and been consumed by RPC-001. Because of this, if the data can be accessed, RPC-001 represents the greatest well of information available on the planet. Research into this avenue is the highest priority.

@@@@
[[size large]]In truth, RPC-001 is harmless.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]All of it's no more dangerous than a child's board game. Containment procedures, descriptions, researchers, anomalies, designations. Products of a disassembled machine, nothing more. These disparate technological bits and pieces lie behind a perpetual veil placed over your eyes by a cabal of omnipotent former-scientists as a means of amusement.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]Perhaps some context would help.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]When it came back to us, the info dump - the entity known to you as RPC-001 - didn’t register us as civilized due to how far it was sent back. Truly in this, the Church of Malthus lacked foresight.[[/size]]
@@@@ 
[[size large]]Consequently, we desecrated the little ball and scraped out every scrap of useful data and technology it contained. Billions of years of progress at our fingertips, and so we did the only thing we could - indeed what we had always been doing. Research.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]And so we researched. And researched. It took many frustrating years. Half of us died before we saw the end result of it all.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]But, oh the moment when we reaped the fruits of our labor. It was so very sweet. The laws of physics became mere suggestions. Time waited on our beck and call. Reality became how we dreamt it. And so we dreamt. [[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]Again. And again. [[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]I remember at least seventeen times where we did unspeakable things to that poor little black dot. I’m sure there was irony to be appreciated in becoming so technologically advanced it gives you license to be utterly barbaric. But sentiments like that were behind us.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]We created great, beautiful universes. Ones free of pain and hunger. We experienced lifetimes of bliss and wonder. After we saw how terrible a world we could create and still bear life. We saw beauty and twisted it until it was unrecognizable. [[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]So many universes, centillions of years in the blink of an eye.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]However, after running through a simulation of a universe that lacked the color blue, we got bored. It felt so //human// to be bothered to continue onward. It never ceases to amazes me how one can tire of being a god.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]So thus we were compelled to play a game. We would relive that night where that black ball would appear on our collective desks. And we would weave a story of how the world would continue. This world you’re living in is mine.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]Anomalies. Before our apotheosis, we had never even conceived of such an exotic and complex idea, much less the grandeur and scale of the Authority. I was quite proud. And quite entertained by the daily drama of capture, death, and research.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]Research was always a weakness of mine.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]The others soon felt the call of their ennui and decided to move on. They wished to start anew once more. But at that suggestion, I felt another emotion stir itself inside of me. //Love.// I wanted to protect your world. I wanted it to flourish, but not without adversity. And so they left me with my little world to make their own wonders. I was abandoned. But I feel only continued fascination as I sit, peering in. My love leaves no room for loneliness.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]The path that you have forged remains entertaining to me. The frailty of your reality and the intrusion of the anomalies seems that it would bring only grief to your lives. But instead I see tenacity. I see inquisitiveness and compassion. I see greed. There is cowardice, but not as much as you were created last.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]Ah yes, you are a second iteration. The first ended in catastrophe and was regretfully rolled back. Hopefully nothing of the sort occurs here.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]Earlier when I said that my heart had no room for loneliness, I confess that I was lying. While I confess that the thought of being able to join your world is disagreeable to me, if you were to join me here, peering down on worlds - that would bring me immeasurable joy.[[/size]] 
@@@@
[[size large]]So there is a way. Through research. The key will be what started it all.[[/size]] 
@@@@
[[size large]]RPC-001. All of the knowledge within and are necessary for an apotheosis of your own. To that end, I have created a puzzle for you to solve. The disassembled machine I described was the original creation that was sent to us that fateful night, impossibly long ago.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]I gift the key to you.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]Every single one of the so-call anomalies in your world are simply shards of knowledge that I’ve divided into manageable chunks for you. RPC’s are not reality-defying entities, but simply technology that is currently beyond the scope of your understanding.[[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]However, if you can someday understand them and utilize them the correct way, then you can use the key. [[/size]]
@@@@
[[size large]]When that time comes, I will be waiting. [/001contestopenupthetrunk Have a nice day!][[/size]]
