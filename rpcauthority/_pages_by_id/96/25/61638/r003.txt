**Registered Phenomena Code:** XXX

**Object Class:** ###808080|Gamma-Black##

**Hazard Types:** Animated Hazard, Auditory Hazard, Sentient Hazard, Transmutation Hazard. 

**Containment Protocols:** Authority Emergency Suppressors are to be stationed in California, USA, with MST Alpha-07 "Compellers" on standby if MST Bravo-39 "Ballroom Blitz" requires assistance or is otherwise incapacitated. CSD who have undergone endurance training or have experience in synchronized dance are to be readily available upon manifestation of RPC-XXX. Unless CSD personnel attempt to reestablish containment of RPC-XXX, no personnel are to remove their soundproof equipment.

Unless CSD personnel attempt to reestablish containment of RPC-XXX, no personnel are to remove their soundproof equipment. A mass evacuation order is to be permitted by RPC Authority and government officials. Upon confirmation of RPC-XXX manifesting, Authority personnel are to silence media outlets, shut down news broadcasts, detain reporters, and ensure denial of civilian transportation in the areas affected by RPC-XXX with a cover story provided falsifying reports of an experimental government device malfunctioning while transitioning through the area.

**Description:** RPC-XXX manifests every midnight on June 3rd and inconsistent dates in the month of October within a random graveyard located in areas surrounding California, USA. The entity's appearance is that of a human skeleton measuring 1.9 meters in height, with a dark pigmentation in the color of its bones, the letters "A.G." protruding from its cranium, glowing in a fluorescent hue in addition to a white hue around its figure.

RPC-XXX is fully animate, and is capable of moving in motions not possible for most skeletal structures; bending, shrinking, extending, and modifying its physical form to seem fully elastic when a situation allows it to do so, causing it to be indestructible by current means, albeit capable of being temporarily delayed in its progress by MST-Alpha-07 "Compellers" through thaumaturgic and religious iconography. 

RPC-XXX's primary anomalous properties manifest when a living organism with a skeletal structure is within a range of which it is capable of perceiving audio caused by RPC-XXX, its skeleton will be expelled via [DATA REDACTED] in the event that the organism refuses to dance or collapses due to exhaustion. This leaves affected individuals fully conscious, still capable of breathing through unknown means, but unable to move in any considerable amount due to the lack of a skeletal system until eventual expiration due to accelerated decomposition. [[footnote]]Results with animals are inconsistent, as those that attempt to dance do so presumably as a survival instinct, while others immediately collapse and expulse their skeleton.[[/footnote]] The skeleton that has been expulsed have been classified as RPC-XXX-1 instances.

The RPC-XXX-1 instance that has exited the subject will begin a synchronized dance, in which all RPC-XXX-1 instances will adopt the same physical properties of the primary RPC-XXX anomaly, with a fluorescent glow to each RPC-XXX-1 instance, perfectly synchronizing their movements until reaching the primary RPC-XXX instance, where each instance of RPC-XXX-1 will begin the dance routine anew, while other RPC-XXX-1 instances attempt to pull other living creatures into RPC-XXX's area of effect via forcefully dancing with the creatures with until they enter RPC-XXX's area of effect, continuing to dance for 40 minutes thereafter, and then continuing to seek more living creatures[[footnote]]Field Agents have reported RPC-XXX-1 instances of having incredible grip strength and exert force above-average for the creature the RPC-XXX-1 instance originates from. This has resulted in many field agents being lost to the anomaly. [[/footnote]]. 

RPC-XXX's emitted audio is an acapella of Andrew Gold's "Spooky Scary Skeletons," with each instrument being played by multiple skeletons that have arranged other skeletons into instruments. Every skeleton added to RPC-XXX's entourage increases RPC-XXX's dB by 0.1 with each human skeleton, and 0.001 dB with each non-human skeleton.

Given the amount of RPC-XXX-1 instances in RPC-XXX's entorage manifesting with RPC-XXX, its current dB is hypothesized of being ███ dB with ███████ current RPC-XXX-1 instances, including a variety of non-human RPC-XXX-1 instances.

RPC-XXX's manifestation requires a grace period with its exponential increase in dB, increasing by 1 dB every 10 minutes until it reaches its former dB and entourage count prior to containment being reestablished. RPC-XXX's entourage will appear from the ground below RPC-XXX during the grace period, joining it in its synchronized dance and continuing their attempts to bring more creatures into RPC-XXX's Area of Effect. 

RPC-XXX dissipates if its skull is removed during its dance by anything other than itself. The only possible way to remove RPC-XXX's skull is to implement CSD personnel with prior experience in synchronized dance and/or personnel with exceptional endurance into RPC-XXX's entourage; If they are able to reach RPC-XXX, they are able to remove its skull and cause RPC-XXX to dissipate[[footnote]]This has proven ineffective with deaf personnel, as their incapability to hear RPC-XXX's audio results in RPC-XXX completely ignoring their existence, and unaffected by their physical interactions.[[/footnote]]. Upon RPC-XXX dissipating, all remaining creatures affected by RPC-XXX will have their skeleton immediately return to them --with no observable discrepancies-- with enhanced physical properties[[footnote]]Properties of enhancements are identical to RPC-XXX-1 instances, and all affected individuals are to be given RPC-XXX-1 classification and given Type II Memory Suppressants.[[/footnote]].
