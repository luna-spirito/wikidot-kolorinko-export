++ News

[[collapsible show="+ October 2018" hide="Hide"]]

> October 10th
> 
> The new containment and lethality ratings have rolled out. If you find any errors on your article feel free to correct it. You are also welcome to start placing Hazard Types on your articles. Thank you to the lore team for the hard work. Also small reminder that the Halloween contest submissions open on the 21st! 
> 
> - The Board 

> October 6th
> 
> Tomorrow evening the new object classes and danger ratings will roll out. This change has been worked on by the lore team for quite some time. See the winning submission [http://rpcsandbox.wikidot.com/agent-star-2 Here]! Many articles will be edited to introduce this change. 
> 
> - The Board 

> October 1st 
> 
> Hi everyone. Following recent staff chats and the creation of a site curation team, we have decided to impose a new rule relating to feedback. All feedback must be done on the forums, we will no longer be accepting screenshots of discord feedback as proof. Going forward, if you wish you have your article critiqued please post a link to your forum post in the article-drafts channel.
> All pieces of writing must have at least two pieces of solid feedback. Having two people say, "This is cool," does not count. We recommending receiving more than the two required rounds of critique.
> 
> -The Board

[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="+ August 2018" hide="Hide"]]
> **August 31st**
> 
> Hello Writers. The Board of admins and mods has voted on how to end the 001 contest. We have chosen to scrap the contest. We know that many people were upset with the themed contest and we apologize for that. This is one of the last remnants of CFO's rash decisions and we ask that you understand our disposal of this contest. We do not believe that the way the 001 contest took place fostered an opportunity to make quality articles. We thank all of the writers who contributed for their time and effort. You are encouraged to rewrite your proposals and form them into other RPCs using our normal system.
> 
> -The Board

> **August 27th**
> 
> Voting has been re-enabled with a new five star system. You may now rate articles on a scale of one to five stars. Please make sure to go through and revote as previous rating have been reset. We hope that this change in voting will help both established and new writers.
> 
> -The Board

> **August 26th**
> 
> RPC now has an official [https://www.redbubble.com/people/rpcauthority?ref=account-nav-dropdown&asc=u RedBubble store]. All profits will be donated to a different charity each month. This month we will be donating to the Cancer Research Foundation. 
> 
> -The Board

> **August 18th**
> 
> The featured articles have been updated, we now have a steam group. We also have a new section of the site [[[acquired-media|Acquired Media]]]! Please enjoy!
> 
> -The Board

> **August 1st**
> 
> We're allowing users to submit ideas for new hazards. Once completed, these new hazards will be used in a revamped tagging system. If you wish to submit your ideas click [[[http://www.rpc-wiki.com/forum/t-6682896/official-hazards-thread|here]]].
