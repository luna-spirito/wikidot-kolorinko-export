October 1st 

Hi everyone. Following recent staff chats and the creation of a site curation team, we have decided to impose a new rule relating to feedback. All feedback must be done on the forums, we will no longer be accepting screenshots of discord feedback as proof. Going forward, if you wish you have your article critiqued please post a link to your forum post in the article-drafts channel.
All pieces of writing must have at least two pieces of solid feedback. Having two people say, "This is cool," does not count. We recommending receiving more than the two required rounds of critique.

[[collapsible show="+ August" hide="Hide"]]
August 31st
Hello Writers. The Board of admins and mods has voted on how to end the 001 contest. We have chosen to scrap the contest. We know that many people were upset with the themed contest and we apologize for that. This is one of the last remnants of CFO's rash decisions and we ask that you understand our disposal of this contest. We do not believe that the way the 001 contest took place fostered an opportunity to make quality articles. We thank all of the writers who contributed for their time and effort. You are encouraged to rewrite your proposals and form them into other RPCs using our normal system.
