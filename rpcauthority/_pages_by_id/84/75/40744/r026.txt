RPC Authority Staff List
[[/div]]

------

[[/div]]

The following is a comprehensive list of Staff positions, who is in them, and their responsibilities. Note that some positions will overlap with one another as some of the staff members work over niches or have multiple jobs. For a comprehensive list of which specific staff is governing over, please look at the final list in the staff info. Also please note that just because the admins and mods of the RPC Authority Wikidot have power on the site, does not mean that all of them have power everywhere else. Not all site staff have power over the discord, reddit, etc.

The following is a comprehensive list of all the roles and the users in them:

----

__**Master Admin:**__ Master Admin is not necessarily an official position that is individually held by a single user on the site, but instead is just an Empty Account meant to hold the position for the Admin Team. All Admins have access to the account. **[IMPORTANT: DO NOT CONTACT THIS USER FOR ANY SITE-RELATED PROBLEMS!]**

//Staff Member:// [[*user theRPCoperator]] 

----

__**Site Admin:**__ Site Administrators are the staff members with the most power on the site. They have the ability to accept and deny applications, kick and ban users, modify almost any articles without a lock, and more. Some Admins also carry over a niche job meant for the community. **(Contact them for any general information, or to report users that seem to be committing a bannable offense.)**

//Staff Members:// [[*user Blairin]], [[*user Von Pincier]], [[*user ThermicLine]], and [[*user Croal]]

----
 
__**Site Mod:**__ Site Moderators carry a similar job to admins, but a lot more limited. They are able to lift and add locks, stop threads in forums, and change articles without worrying about locks. Unlike admins, they can not accept applications, kick or ban, and manipulate Site coding without permission. But, like admins, they will often work over a niche for the community that needs to be done. **(Contact them for any general help, or for any problems that may be occurring in the Forums.)**

//Staff Members:// [[*user MrPines]], [[*user ICFD]], [[*user Vizlox]], [[*user H F Dummie]], and [[*user JerryJohn]]

----

__**Junior Site Mod:**__ Junior Site Moderators are users that are under consideration to be moderators and carry some authority over both the general site and the forums. Unlike moderators, they care unable to perform actions such as lock threads, change articles with locks, and add or remove locks on pages. Despite this, users must listen to them if they are issuing a stop order, give warnings or are editing articles. **(Contact them for any general questions, or for any problems that may be occurring in the Forums.)**

//Staff Members:// [[*user DrPierson]], and [[*user JimmyBoyHaha]] 

----

__**Lore Council:**__ Lore Council members are members of the staff in charge of the specific goal of maintaining consistency within the lore. They are also in charge of approving or denying giant changes in lore. It should be noted that while it is the case that most lore building happens on the Lore Coordination Discord, most of the activities of the Lore Councilors are done through the office. Some moderators and admins may also be found to govern over this job. **(Contact them for any lore relating matters such if you want a big change in lore to be approved or questions of what may break canon.)**

//Staff in Group:// [[*user Blairin]], [[*user Vizlox]]

//User Members:// [[*user Redtide]], [[*user Doctor Lupin]], [[*user bXluYW1lamVmZg]]

----

__**Curation Team:**__ Curation Team members are responsible for maintaining the quality of the RPC wiki. This often goes under the ability to edit articles for slight grammar mistakes, contact users if their articles have problems, and request a deletion for articles if contacting the user is impossible. Additionally, these members may also have the ability to mark deletion on articles which were posted without criticism. If an article has received critisism and is still notably low quality or has significant issues the curation team will remove the article and assist the writer in improvement in the draft stage. Some moderators and admins may also be found to govern over this job. **(Contact them if you would like to report a below-average article or an article that has not received criticism.)**

//Staff in Group:// [[*user Croal]], [[*user Von Pincier]], [[*user JerryJohn]], [[*user JimmyBoyHaha]], and [[*user ThermicLine]]

//User Members:// [[*user Enkrum]], and [[*user Superspambot]]

----

__**Development Team:**__ Development team members are staff members that have chosen to assist with the development of code for the site and page themes. These members often present a commitment to work with the main staff to ensure that the site can look as great as it can be. **(Contact them for help with any code you do not know, or if you need code for a certain page or event.)**

//Staff in Group:// [[*user Blairin]], [[*user DrPierson]], [[*user MrPines]]

//User Members:// [[*user c0debreak]], [[*user James Pitchford]], [[*user MrBazzle]], [[*user Logangreenjeans]], [[*user Dr Vaughn]], and [[*user Doctor Lupin]]

----

__**Community Managers:**__ Community Managers are users that are either tasked with the management of some social media outlet used by the RPC-Wiki or are members commemorated for their contributions to RPC's social media presence. **(Contact them if you have any concerns over any of the social media outlets.)**

//Staff in Group:// [[*user ThermicLine]]

//User Members:// [[*user Ion_12]], and [[*user Liberonscien]]
