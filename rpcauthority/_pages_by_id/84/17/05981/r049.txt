Secure Authority Facilities
[[/div]]

------

[[/div]]

To support and sustain the Authority's operational security, the Authority maintains a worldwide network of facilities and infrastructures to contain and research anomalies known as RPCs. While many facilities vary in terms of human resources, responsibility, and jurisdiction, the Authority employs personnel worldwide, ranging from hundreds to thousands of researchers and security forces.

Note: If you are looking for a list of Site facilities, redirect over [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/site-index here].

[[collapsible show="+ Frequently Asked Questions" hide="- Close"]]

> //Q. What are Authority facilities?//
> 
> Authority facilities are installations maintained by the RPC Authority to ensure that anomalies are researched and contained to prevent them from causing breaches and disrupting society's normalcy. There are two common facilities used by the Authority:
> 
> * **Sites:** These facilities are responsible for the research, containment, and housing of Alpha-class, Beta-class, and Gamma-class RPCs.
> 
> * **Areas:** These facilities operate in various roles that don't involve the housing or containing RPCs onsite. Some of these roles include Observation & Data, Research & Development, Garrisoned Operations Base, and many more.

> //Q. How many facilities are there?//
> 
> The estimated amount of facilities maintained by the Authority vary from author to author. Since we don't precisely have exact numbers, authors can believe that the organization maintains a certain number of facilities in a continent or maintains hundreds of thousands in every continent. 
> 
> Overall, the answer to this question is entirely up to writers to interpret. 

> //Q. Can I create my own Site?//
> 
> Of course, you can. We highly encourage and recommend every writer to contribute and create their Site or Area facility. However, we recommend getting some critique first before taking the liberty of creating and finalizing it into a draft. 
> 
> For more information about creating a Site or an Area facility, please direct these inquiries to the Lore Team at the Discord server.

+ [[span style="color:#2766BB;"]]**Primary Facilities**[[/span]]

++ **Site**

Site facilities are responsible for the containment and research of RPC anomalies. These facilities may include sectors, including research, maintenance, medical, and security. 

On-site operations and management are directly overseen by the Site Administration and headed by the Site Director themselves. 

Site Directors report directly to Regional Directors.

++ **Area**

Area facilities are specialized locations that perform various functions. These functions include: Observations & Data, Storage & Logistics, Research & Development, Garrisoned Operations Base, Proving Grounds, or Medical Center. 

These facilities are strictly prohibited from housing any RPCs onsite.

++ **Regional**

Regional facilities are the logistics and administrative hubs for Sites and Areas within a specific geographic region, often an entire continent. These facilities coordinate with Sites and Areas within their adjacent regions. RPCs are strictly excluded from these facilities. 

Regional Directors report directly to the Global Directors.

+ [[span style="color:#2766BB;"]]**Secondary Facilities**[[/span]]

++ **On-Location Site**


On-Location Sites, or OL-Sites, are referred to as secondary facilities. These locations are built to contain specific anomalies that can't be transported to a Site facility or are too unpredictable to be present with other RPCs or personnel onsite. 

In addition to this, OL-Sites are supervised by its most proximate Site facility. In some situations, OL-Sites would facilitate as an independent location and directly reporting to the Regional Director.
