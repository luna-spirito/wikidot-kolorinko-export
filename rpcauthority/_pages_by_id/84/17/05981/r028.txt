Secure Authority Facilities
[[/div]]

------

[[/div]]

To support and sustain the operational security of the Authority, the Authority maintains a worldwide network of facilities and infrastructures to contain and research anomalies known as RPCs. While many facilities vary in terms of manpower, responsibility, and jurisdiction, the Authority employs personnel across the world ranging from hundreds to thousands of researchers and security forces.

Note: If you are looking for a list of Site facilities, redirect over [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/site-index here].

[[collapsible show="+ Frequently Asked Questions" hide="- Close"]]

> //Q. What are Authority facilities?//
> 
> Authority facilities are installations maintained by the RPC Authority to ensure that anomalies are researched and contained to prevent them from causing breaches and disrupting society's normalcy. There are two common facilities used by the Authority:
> 
> * **Sites:** These facilities are responsible for the research, containment, and housing of Alpha-class, Beta-class, and Gamma-class RPCs.
> 
> * **Areas:** These facilities operate in a variety of roles that don't involve the housing or containing of RPCs onsite. Some of these roles include Observation & Data, Research & Development, Garrisoned Operations Base, and many more.

> //Q. How many facilities are there?//
> 
> The estimated amount of facilities maintained by the Authority vary from author to author. While we don't exactly have a canon in regards to the exact numbers, an author can believe that the Authority maintains a certain amount of facilities in a continent, or an author can believe that the Authority maintains hundreds of thousands in every continent. 
> 
> Overall, the answer to this question is entirely up to writers to interpret. 

> //Q. Can I create my own Site?//
> 
> Of course you can. We highly encourage and recommend every writer to contribute and create their own Site, or Area facility. We, however, do recommend to get some criticism first before creating and finalizing it into a draft. 
> 
> For more information in regards to creating a Site or an Area facility, please direct these inquiries to the Lore Team at the Discord server.

+ [[span style="color:#2766BB;"]]**Primary Facilities**[[/span]]

++ **Site**

Site facilities are responsible for the containment and research of RPC anomalies. These facilities may often include sectors including research, maintenance, medical, and security. 

Onsite operations and management is directly overseen by the Site Administration, and headed by the Site Director themselves. 

Site Directors report directly to the Regional Director.

++ **Area**

Area facilities are specialized installations that perform various responsibilities, such as Observations & Data, Storage & Logistics, Research & Development, Garrisoned Operations Base, Proving Grounds, or Medical Center. Area facilities are strictly forbidden to house any RPCs onsite. 

Area Directors report directly to the Regional Director.

++ **Regional**

Regional facilities are the logistics and administrative hubs for Sites and Areas within a specific geographic region, often an entire continent. These facilities coordinate with Sites and Areas within their adjacent regions. RPCs are strictly excluded from these facilities. 

Regional Directors report directly to the Global Directors.

+ [[span style="color:#2766BB;"]]**Secondary Facilities**[[/span]]

++ **On-Location Site**

On-Location Sites, or OL-Sites, are referred as secondary facilities as these facilities are established to specifically contain anomalies that cannot be transferred to a primary Site facility, or are simply too unpredictable to be present with other anomalies or personnel on site. 

In addition, OL-Sites are overseen by its closest primary facility, and in some cases would facilitate itself as an independent facility.
