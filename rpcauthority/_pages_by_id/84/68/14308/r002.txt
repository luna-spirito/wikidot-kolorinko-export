**Registered Phenomena Code:** 549

**Object Class:** --Epsilon-Orange-- Neutralized.

**Hazard Types:** Sentient Hazard (?), Geological Hazard, Mechanical Hazard, Destabilization Hazard, Extra-Dimensional Hazard, Grouped Hazard, Ecological Hazard.

**Containment Protocols:** None, as object is assumed lost. See below for previous protocols. 

[[collapsible hideLocation="both" show="+ Old Containment Protocols v1.22" hide="- Close"]]

RPC-549 is contained, unpowered, in a specially modified, 10x10x5, Coherency Isolation Chamber (CIC) [[footnote]] A CIC is designed to keep the interior space at a constant specific coherency level. It is constructed mainly from stainless iron, and several anomalous components. Each CIC has a coherency exchange chamber, similar to cabin pressurization systems, that works as the only entry and exit to the chamber interior. Entrying objects and entities' ACS level must be changed to the CIC coherency level for entry, and vice versa.[[/footnote]] set to ACS-3 in order to prevent RPC-549 from activating. 

RPC-549 is only to be extracted through a portable CIC of identical characteristics, size negligible as long as larger than 5x5x3. 

Should RPC-549 be activated, either due to CIC failure or negligent extraction, it is to be transported to a replacement 549-Specific CIC (Construction pending), or the closest availible general CIC. After recontainment, if feasible, RPC-549 should be examined for potential damage or missing parts, and any changes reported to assigned Level-4 researcher.

RPC-549 is currently contained in OL-Site-376, located in Madagascar. It is kept separate from the main site in order to minimize the consequences of an activation event.

[[/collapsible]]

**Description:** RPC-549 is a set of two interconnected anomalous machinery, designated RPC-549-1 and -2. Both components are damaged to an unknown degree, and have lost as many as four related components, including input interface. Each individual component of RPC-549 acts independently, administered by an attached non-anomalous AI. This AI, designated RPC-549-3N, is considered Neutralized due to destroyed/missing/corrupted vital code. Due to the neutralization of RPC-549-3N, both other components act in what is considered a "Standby Mode," allowing the simultaneous activation of both components through their individual control systems only during the following circumstances;

* ACS level drops below 3. [[footnote]] This is theorized to be a system failure. Supposedly, RPC-549 would activate if ACS level were to drop under 4, but under unknown circumstances, RPC-549 has set the "normal" ACS value to 3. [[/footnote]]
* ACS level rises to 4.
* Certain components of RPC-549-2 are damaged.
* RPC-549-2 is destroyed.
* RPC-549 considers its current situation lethal to itself. [[footnote]] This mechanism is largely inconsistent and inpredictable, and assumed to be related to RPC-549-3N. Any "sudden" changes to RPC-549's environment are considered lethal. [[/footnote]]
* Removal of internal power storages is attempted.

Each invidual component of RPC-549 must be powered down through an internal switch located next to the internal control CPU.

RPC-549-1 is the larger component of RPC-549, and consists of two sensor systems (Visual), an inactive radiocommunication system, an internal CPU, two emergency power storages, and an interdimensional rift generator, consistent to current Authority development projections for year 21██. It is theorized that RPC-549-1 would be able to change the rift frequency, allowing travel to a number of dimensions. However, as the input interface is missing, RPC-549-1 is constantly set to a frequency of 345-223-XXX, corresponding either to the current Authority dimension or [DATA EXPUNGED] (See Addendum 549-2.) RPC-549-1 holds an internal server, in which RPC-549-3N is contained. RPC-549-1 is also capable of transporting itself, by generating a rift .50m below its exact location.

RPC-549-1-Generated rifts are largely stable. However, due to circumstances related to RPC-549-2 (Described below), access to said rifts is often impossible.

RPC-549-2 is a localized coherency stabilizer, built mainly of an unknown steel alloy, possesing an internal cooling system, an internal CPU and an emergency power storage. RPC-549-2 has suffered immense heat damage, resulting in unstable functioning. When active, RPC-549-2 immediately attempts to lower local ACS level to an estimate of 0.7. This results in an affected area of 1km with unstable topology, rendering movement within unfeasable. RPC-549-2-Affected areas tend to cause spontaneous mutation in local biology, and in less than 10% of cases, anomalous construction of topologically inconsistent buildings of variable nature. RPC-549-2-Affected animals are always able to traverse the affected topology easily, and sometimes capable of teleportation.

When RPC-549 is activated, both components fire their properties simultaneously. RPC-549-1 will immediately attempt to transport itself to a different location, always located within planet Earth. Once RPC-549-1 has been succesfully activated, RPC-549-2, if intact, will enter an indefinite operation mode, constantly lowering local ACS level. This causes the activation of RPC-549-1, continuing until both components run out of power. 

RPC-549 is vulnerable to most natural hazards, including impact, heat, and water, making its activation inherently dangerous to itself. Destruction of RPC-549-1 is theorized to cause a localized rift of unknown characteristics and capabilities.

**Discovery:** RPC-549 was the focal point to a spatial anomaly localized within an anomalous metal tower, extending 25 meters above ground, located in [REDACTED], Africa. Said anomalous building, classified RPC-549-AN, was unaccesible due to topological abnormalities, except through direct entry through what was considered the "main doors," a 2x2 rectangular hole five meters above ground. RPC-549 was located in an undergound basement below the main floor. Exterior of the tower showed a number of incoherent inscriptions, all in English. An engraved unknown sigil was located above the main entrance. 

Local biology had suffered a large number of mutations, including but not limited to;

* Any number of additional sets of arms.
* Hardening of exteriors, akin to an exoskeleton.
* Production of acidic fluids replacing blood and clorophyll.
* Ceasing of reproduction in any recognizable fashion.
* Indefinite growth.
* Increasing of aggressiveness.
* Development of additional appendages used for aggression.

A number of scorched, unidentifiable human corpses, designated RPC-549-AN-1 through -27, were located inside RPC-549-AN. All of them wore what is assumed to be a military outfit of some kind. A few DNA tests were conducted on RPC-549-AN-1 through -6, and RPC-549-AN-9 through -12, all matching up to 80% of known Authority members' DNA.

When RPC-549 was deactivated and relocated to nearby Site-077, RPC-549-AN, RPC-549-AN-1 through -27, and all affected biology vanished. Topological abnormalities remained for a period of six hours.

[[collapsible hideLocation="both" show="+ Addendum 549-2 - Loss of Contact" hide="- Close"]]

During 12/5/2018, 18:54, RPC-549 had an unexpected activation event. RPC-549-2 was found to operate at an exponentially increasing rate, overwhelming its containment chamber in less than 15 minutes. CIC emergency systems failed to activate under coherency breakdown. OL-Site-376 was soon evacuated, and rendered unoperable due to topologic unstability. RPC-549-1's radiocommunication component was activated at this time, under unknown circumstances. It is theorized that RPC-549-3N is related to this unexpected behavior.

By 19:33, RPC-549-1 was activated, and dissapeared. RPC-549 is assumed to have transported to its original dimension, due to the following message broadcasted via what's assumed to be text-to-speech by RPC-549-1 before activation.

> im going back home where the verdant fields grew and [[[rpc-101|volcanoes now feast]]]

Involvement with GoI GA-01 ("The Remnants") is being investigated.
