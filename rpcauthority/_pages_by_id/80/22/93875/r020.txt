+ **Hydrozen's Author Page**
-----
[[/=]]

Welcome to my author page!

My name is [[*user Hydrozen]], I go by //"Arbitrium"// on the RPC Discord. I joined the RPC Authority on July 4th, 2018, around half a month after it was created. I used to write SCP drafts for the SCP Foundation before the whole controversy happened.

I began writing for RPC almost immediately after I joined, my first article being RPC-650. Since then I have been writing for RPC.

I was born in 2004 and my personal hobbies are drawing and making music. The kind of music I make varies; for a while, I may feel like making drum and bass. For another while, I may feel like making slushwave instead.

Here is my main [https://soundcloud.com/hypertext137 Soundcloud.]
Here is my alternate [https://soundcloud.com/saerow Soundcloud.]

[[collapsible show="[+] Other Thoughts" hide="[-] Other Thoughts"]]

I sometimes think about why I am me, y'know? Why this body, mind, and life? I could have been someone else, but I was put into this body. Anyways...

Sometimes I also think about how someday, this site and its community will be inactive, and all the people who were involved will be out there in the world, doing their own things while sometimes wondering where all the other members are.

What if I reincarnate and come across my own work - my articles and my music - without even knowing that I made those things? Wouldn't that be something?

Eh, just some thoughts.

[[/collapsible]]
