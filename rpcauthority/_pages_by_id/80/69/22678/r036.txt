**Registered Phenomena Code:** 794

**Object Class:** Gamma-Red

[[include component:hazards radioactive=show|climatological=show|transmutation=show|ecological=show|grouped=show|biohazard=show|extra-dimensional=show|auditory=show|sensory=show|visual=show|temporal=show]]


**Containment Protocols:** RPC-794 is largely considered self-contained inside AZ-014. Manifestations of RPC-794's anomalous effects outside AZ-014 are to be contained, or terminated if the former is impossible.

A Class-S hazmat suit[[footnote]]Constructed after the detonation of Site-014, it can withstand up to 100000 Grays, any kind of air contamination and 2000°C with minor damage and comes equipped with a mounted flashlight, a fuel lighter and a self-termination system. [[/footnote]] must be worn at all times when operating near RPC-794's immediate effect zone; all expeditions or containment missions must be carried out by no less than 3 members, ensuring that in the event of damage or rupture of the Class-S hazmat suit, another member will be present to verify and/or assist in immediate termination of the compromised squadmate.

Should more anomalies affected by RPC-794 be discovered, MST-Golf-5 (Black Snow) is to be deployed for its classification, containment and/or termination; all kinds of expeditions that involve anomalies residing within the 100m range of RPC-794 are strictly forbidden unless authorized by the Board of Global Directors.

**Description:** RPC-794 is the anomalous mass resulting from the explosion at Site-014 and the subsequent collapse of the Research headquarters (See incident log-794-0), which at the time were cross-testing anomalous materials from RPC-███, RPC-███, RPC-███, and RPC-███ (All of which are to be considered neutralized or unrecoverable.). The explosion caused said anomalous materials to be contaminated due to exposure to high amounts of radiation, this contamination process mixed with high temperatures caused the anomalous materials to merge and create RPC-794.

[[collapsible show="Incident log-794-0" hide="Close incident log"]]
> **The following transcript was obtained from security cameras before the detonation of Site-014.**
> **3:50 AM:** There is an ongoing cross-test of 3 different Alpha class anomalies on the -5th floor of Site-014, the Research headquarters. At the same time, seeds from RPC-███ are being grown for harvesting. The intent of said cross-test was in the development of anti-memetic anomalous protection equipment and weaponization via reverse engineering.
> **4:05 AM:** Cross-testing of the previously mentioned RPCs had already begun
> **4:12 AM:** The site detonation alarms activate, quickly followed the stand-down alert, explaining said activation being caused by an error. All researchers return to their working stations.
> **4:24 AM:** The 3 RPCs are put together in the same testing room to test their reactions to one another.
> **4:36 AM:** Said RPC's are showing signs of mutual cooperation and compatibility. While the test is about to be concluded, all cameras shut down at 4:37 AM, that being the hour of detonation of Site-014.
[[/collapsible]]

Despite the destruction of the majority of Site-014, the anomalous properties of RPC-794-1 and RPC-794-2 caused an anomalous remnant of Site-014 lower floors to be created (now referred to as Anomalous Zone 014 or AZ-014). This remnant does not exist in our dimension, but rather a non-euclidean version of it; only accessible by a door that previously lead to the -5th floor of Site-014, but that has now been left in the open due to the destruction of the upper structure. Said door does not show signs of damage despite having suffered from said explosion, and as such it should be considered a byproduct of AZ-014.

It is not known if previously contained anomalies in the lower floors of Site-014 now reside inside AZ-014, or that all anomalies encountered within AZ-014 are byproducts of RPC-794's anomalous properties. Whichever is the case, all anomalies found inside AZ-014 must pass a test for them to be classified as RPC-794 instances.

For an anomaly to be considered a subproduct of RPC-794 it must follow one or more of the following criteria:

* The anomaly must be localized in AZ-014 or in a 100m radius of RPC-794.

* For the anomaly's effects to be theorized or confirmed to be caused by one or more of the anomalous materials being studied on the Research headquarters at the time of detonation.

* For the anomaly to be a mutation of a pre-existing anomaly by RPC-794. This can be confirmed by measuring the Roentgen levels around said anomaly, with 30000 being a confirmed instance.

Should one to find themselves lost without a Scintillation detector, all Class-S hazmat suits come equipped with a termination button, which will release a neurotoxin inside the helmet, resulting in a painless death in less than 30 seconds.  ||

This has lead to the expansion of RPC-794's immediate effect zone and the subsequent expansion of RPC-794-6. The latest measurements have shown that, at its current pace, the effects from RPC-794 and RPC-794-6 will completely escape the fallout area of Site-014 in 2 months. As a result, Project Paradise has been created with the help of GOI-12(Nucorp Industries). The project consists of the following:
> * A remote controlled robot designated as Paradise-005 will be created with the capability to withstand sufficiently high amounts of radiation to get within a 10m range of RPC-794. It will also carry a mounted firearm and incendiary rounds.
> 
> * A bomb created by reverse engineering anomalous objects and the help of Nucorp, with the effect of destabilizing all surrounding reality caught within its blast radius. This bomb will be mounted onto Paradise-005 and detonated once it reaches RPC-794.
> 
> * Should Paradise-005 and subsequent attempts fail to stop RPC-794-6's immediate effect zone from reaching the outside of Site-014, a non-nuclear weapon of mass destruction will be detonated inside AZ-014, with the intent to destroy RPC-794.
>
