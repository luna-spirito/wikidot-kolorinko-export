**Containment Protocols:** RPC-794 is considered self-contained, however, manifestations of RPC-794's anomalous effects outside of the safe area are to be terminated or contained when the former is impossible.

A Class-S hazmat suit[[footnote]]Constructed after the detonation of Site-014, it can withstand up to 100000 Roentgens, any kind of air contamination and 2000°C with minor damage and comes equipped with a mounted flashlight, a fuel lighter and a self-termination system.[[/footnote]] must be worn at all times when operating near RPC-794's immediate effect zone; all expeditions or containment missions must be carried out by no less than 3 members, this is to ensure that in case of damage or rupture of the Class-S hazmat suit, another member will be present for immediate termination.

Should more anomalies effected by RPC-794 be discovered, MST-Golf-5 (Black Snow) is to be deployed for its classification, containment and/or termination; all kinds of expeditions that involve anomalies residing within the 100m range of RPC-794 are strictly forbidden unless authorized by the Board of Global Directors.
For an anomaly to be considered a subproduct of RPC-794 it must follow one or more of the following criteria:

* The anomaly must be localized on the -5th floor of Site-014 or in a 100m radius of RPC-794

* For the anomaly's effects to be theorized or confirmed to be caused by one or more of the anomalous materials being studied on the Research headquarters at the time of detonation.

* For the anomaly to be a mutation of a pre-existing anomaly by RPC-794. This can be confirmed by measuring the Roentgen levels around said anomaly, with 30000 being a confirmed instance.

**Description:** RPC-794 is the result of the explosion at Site-014 and the subsequent collapse of the Research headquarters (See incident log-794-0), which at the time were cross-testing anomalous materials from RPC-███, RPC-███, RPC-███, and RPC-███ (All of which are to be considered neutralized or unrecoverable.). The explosion caused said anomalous materials to be contaminated due to exposure to high amounts of radiation, this contamination process caused the anomalous materials to merge and create RPC-794.

RPC-794 is the anomalous mass resulted by the merging of the different anomalies being cross-tested in Site-014, measuring 300kg and spreading all around the debris of the Research headquarters. RPC-794's Anomalous propeties manifest all around the fallout caused by the explosion of Site-014 and range from RPC-794-1 to RPC-794-7; two of RPC-794's anomalous effects are uncontainable and only manifest themselves in a 100m radius around RPC-794. Attempts to reach or contain said instances is strictly prohibited due to the massive amounts of radiation[[footnote]]80000 roentgens/h.[[/footnote]] and cognitohazard effects those anomalies have on individuals. 

[[collapsible show="Incident log-794-0" hide="Close incident log"]]
> **The following transcript was obtained from security cameras before the detonation of Site-014.**
> **3:50 PM:** There is an ongoing cross-test of 3 different Alpha class anomalies on the -5th floor of Site-014, the Research headquarters. At the same time, seeds from RPC-███ are being grown for harvesting. The intent of said cross-test was in the development of anti-memetic anomalous protection equipment and weaponization via reverse engineering.
> **4:10 PM:** The site detonation alarms activate, quickly followed by speakers, saying said activation was caused by an error. All researchers return to their working station.
> **4:20 PM:** Cross-testing of the previously mentioned RPC´s had already begun
> **4:35 PM:** The 3 RPC´s are put together in the same testing room to test their reactions to one another.
> **4:36 PM:** Said RPC's are showing signs of mutual cooperation and compatibility. While the test is about to be terminated, all cameras shut down at 4:37 PM, that being the hour of detonation of Site-014.

|| RPC-794-1 || RPC-794-1 is an anomalous cloud formation only encountered within the -5 floor of Site-014. This cloud formation will constantly pour liquid metal at a temperature of 1700°C. Despite the constant rain, RPC-794-1´s liquid metal droplets will evaporate in contact with any surface and return to the cloud. || Dispensers of cooling chemicals are being sent into the -5th floor to be released into the cloud. || RPC-794-2 || RPC-794-2 is a group of humanoids appearing to be made entirely of concrete. While these entities are slow and show very low levels of sapience, they have also shown to be extremely aggressive in proximity of humans, being able to accelerate to 30km/h when in sight of one. However, a single fire arm shot is enough for them to crumble apart and be rendered harmless. RPC-794-2 is theorized to be product of RPC-███, which at the time was being tested in the Research headquarters. ||  Should anyone encounter an RPC-794-2 instance outside or inside Site-014, immediate neutralization is authorized with its subsequent recovery for research purposes.||
|| RPC-794-3 ||  RPC-794-3 manifests itself inside Site-014, causing the -5th floor, in which RPC-794 is located, to become non-euclidean. This causes corridors to extend far beyond what they should, doors leading to unconnected rooms or back to themselves when entering and seemingly infinite stairways. This phenomenon becomes more severe the closer one gets to RPC-794. || An advanced Roentgens detector must be carried at all times, as this has been shown to be the only reliable way to locate oneself, providing a warning should one get too close to RPC-794 immediate area and allowing one to find a way back by distancing itself from the high amounts of radiation. ||
|| RPC-794-4 || RPC-794-4 are the roots and branches originating from the seeds of RPC-███ which were being grown inside the Research headquarters at the time of detonation. These roots and branches are spread all around Site-014, emanating from the -5th floor and have been shown to emite hallucinogens, causing any unprotected individual to be put in a trance and navigate themselves towards RPC-794. The leaves of RPC-794-4 are extremely sharp, having shown to be able to penetrate Class-S hazmat suits. || All personnel venturing into the -5th floor of Site-014 are recommended to act with extreme caution around RPC-794-4 leaves. it is recommend to set all roots and branches on fire, as this has shown to negate the spread of hallucinogens, taking around 5-6 days to grow again.||
|| RPC-794-5 || RPC-794-5 is a visual anomaly manifesting itself every 3 days, believed to be caused by the photo-sensory properties of RPC-███. This visual anomaly will cause the explosion of Site-014 to repeat itself, being visible only by those inside of the fallout zone. While the mushroom head and sonic wave caused by it are visible they do not cause any harm. This event will take place in a span of 5 seconds. || Every individual working around the fallout of Site-014 is to be made known of the anomaly, if said anomaly causes severe emotional distress, a dose of Class-C amnestics is allowed to be administered.||
|| RPC-794-6 || This anomaly occurs upon entering the 100m radius of RPC-794. In this area, normal laws of physics will cease to function properly, causing objects to defy gravity, accelerate or decelerate time in certain areas and the creation of miniature black holes. This is theorized to be caused by the destruction of RPC-███ and the subsequent release of its anomalous effects mixed with the high amounts of radiation and other anomalous materials found in RPC-794. || No personnel are allowed on this area and all those who trespass in it are to be considered lost. ||
|| RPC-794-7 ||  RPC-794-7 is a series of hallucinations occurring in the 100m radius of RPC-794. Said hallucinations will lead the affected individual towards RPC-794. These hallucinations range from far away voices to visual manifestations of personnel lost in the explosion. Witnesses have reported said hallucinations to be calling for help. While it is theorized that this is a subproduct of RPC-794-4, lack of testing makes this theory hard to proof.|| Where any personnel found themselves inside RPC-794 100m radius, the use of Roentgens detectors is to be authorized to find a way out of the zone. No other form of navigation aside from this is recommended, no auditory or visual cues are to be used for navigation. Where one to find themselves lost without a Roentgens detector, all Class-S hazmat suits come equipped with a termination button, which will release a neurotoxin inside the helmet, resulting in a painless death in less than 30 seconds.  ||
