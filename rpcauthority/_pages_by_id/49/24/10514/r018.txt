**Registered Phenomena Code:** 119

**Object Class:** Beta-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Organic Hazard, Sapient Hazard, Gravitational Hazard,

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-119 is currently kept within a standard humanoid containment cell. RPC-119 may be subject to testing on a bi-weekly basis and is to receive therapeutic and medical checkups after said testing. In the event that RPC-119 displaces any form of matter within its containment, it should be coerced into replenishing the displacement. Should RPC-119 display any potentially hazardous displacement, it is to be sedated after replenishing said displacement, and moved into Site-009's strict containment wing; therapy sessions will be held on a bi-daily basis. Interaction is to be limited and the activity of the anomaly is to be monitored at all times.

**Description:** RPC-119 is a human male, 45 years of age. It has a height of 185.42cm and weighs approximately 91kg. RPC-119 is balding and requires prescription glasses. RPC-119 is able to create minor displacements in matter (within 5 meters of itself). RPC-119 can only make minor displacements, with an estimated maximum size of 15cm^^3^^. RPC-119 expresses extreme discontent with its anomalous trait, and will often ask if said trait can be reversed. RPC-119 shows an inability to cope with the trait; therapy was requested and was later accepted.

Displacements in matter consist of a perfect black body, with a cubed shape. Attempts to make contact with the displacement(s) will result in a lack of gravity being placed onto the object(s). Displacements can cause a multitude of effects, including (but not limited to):
* Shrinking in size, yet consistent in mass;
* Mixing with other materials[[footnote]] The best example of this can be found in Test 119-004. [[/footnote]];
* Objects catching fire (if possible);
* Death;
* Reconfiguration of certain appendages[[footnote]] Example: Fingers being located at improper locations. [[/footnote]];
* Distortions[[footnote]] Example: Left eye being larger than the right eye. [[/footnote]].

**Addendum:** The following Addenda describe testing of RPC-119, alongside following therapy sessions and medical examinations. In the event that a discrepancy is detected within testing records, a member of [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/the-office-of-analysis-and-science the Office of Analysis and Science], under the [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/research-hub Research Division] should be contacted. Please note that not all test records have been displayed, as upon order of the Office of Analysis and Science. Questions in regards to this order may be taken up with your local adviser of said Office.

__**Test 119-001**__
[[/=]]
> **Subject:** RPC-119
> **Procedure:** RPC-119 is to make a minor displacement, and is to attempt to destroy a picture of a ball. RPC-119 may be coerced into doing so if it is not complying.
> **Results:** Once the displacement was completed, the picture vanished. When the object was replenished it materialized as a pile of ash.
> **Analysis:** //Though this may have been due to the flammability of the paper, more research should be conducted on this phenomena. - Dr. Blacks//
> 
> ------
> **Follow-up:** //RPC-119 appeared more excited this time since it was given a reward: a handshake. Though it wasn't advised to make contact previously, RPC-119 and Dr. Jonah seemed really connected at that point. RPC-119 requested a few more books to read since it was close to finishing the one it had read. Request was sent. - Dr. Isiah, RPC-119's designated therapist.//
> **Request Status:** Accepted.

[[=]]
__**Test 119-002**__
[[/=]]
> **Subject:** RPC-119
> **Procedure:** RPC-119 is to create a minor displacement, and is to drop a cup into said displacement. RPC-119 may be coerced into doing so if it is not complying.
> **Results:** Initially, RPC-119 disregards demands to create a displacement, and after being coerced into doing so, complies. Once the cup of water is dropped into the displacement, it appears to float.
> **Analysis:** //It is unknown if the displacement can remove already existent materials, and once the displacement was replenished, [us researchers] were already being escorted out. - Dr. Jonah//
> 
> ------
> **Follow-up:** //RPC-119 was generally distressed after the test, and only wished to go home. I told it the facility was his home, and it became hostile. It wasn't physical, but I did fear for what it could do. I suggested some form of minor entertainment to keep it occupied between now and the next test; something like a book would help it. - Dr. Isiah, RPC-119's designated therapist.//

__**Test 119-003**__
[[/=]]
> **Subject:** RPC-119
> **Procedure:** RPC-119 must create a slightly larger displacement over a bowling ball, and is to quickly replenish the displacement. RPC-119 may be coerced into doing so if it is not complying.
> **Results:** Once the displacement was replenished, the bowling ball appeared approximately 2cm smaller, though had an equal mass as before the displacement was created.
> **Analysis:** //More research is requested, as to continue to observe how far this rabbit hole goes. - Dr. Calvin//
> 
> ------
> **Follow-up:** //RPC-119 didn't have much to talk about, and was generally ay-okay as of the time of the test, though it did ask me "when would [it] go home" again. I do believe some form of...stern talking may be required. - Dr. Isiah, RPC-119's designated therapist.//

__**Test 119-004**__
[[/=]]
> **Subject:** RPC-119
> **Procedure:** RPC-119 is to create a displacement over a hooded CSD-3432, and must not be told of the subject, whom should be sedated prior to testing. Should CSD-3432 awake before displacement, it,-- alongside RPC-119,-- must be sedated with Class-II amnestics.
> **Results:** No disruption occurs, and once the displacement is replenished, CSD-3432's skull and organic tissue are evidently mixed with the hood.
> **Analysis:** //RPC-119 entered a shock-like state, and began having a nervous breakdown after seeing this. RPC-119 was sedated and brought to Dr. Isiah's office. - Dr. Orion//
> 
> ------
> **Follow-up:** //RPC-119 wouldn't stop mumbling about the incident, and once I broke the news to it, that our facilities house expendable personnel like CSD-3432, RPC-119 began sobbing uncontrollably. I comforted it to the best of my ability. I have sent a request for a 6-pack of bottled water to be sent to its containment. - Dr. Isiah, RPC-119's designated therapist.// 
> **Request Status:** Denied.
