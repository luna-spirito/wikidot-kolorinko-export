**Registered Phenomena Code:** 316

**Object Class:** Alpha-White (Neutralized)

**Hazard Types:** Emotional, Visual

**Containment Protocol:** RPC-316's corpse is stored in Site-089's morgue, in a clearly demarcated freezer unit isolated from the other units. Markings upon its locked doors are to include notices that the corpse of RPC-316 bears mild cognitohazardous properties that manifest upon observing it, and that it was never a member of significance within the RPC Authority. Any inquiries for the study of RPC-316's cadaver is to be cleared by Senior Researcher Junichiro.

If cleared for study, RPC-316's corpse is to be examined while under the influence of viderics - only light doses are required. Should foreign medical research personnel of non-Japanese ethnicity be present, they should be prioritized to participate or lead said examination.

**Description:** RPC-316 is a corpse of a deceased entity that bore the same numbering. Prior to its neutralization, RPC-316 possessed two advanced perception-altering properties: The first allowed it to travel anywhere without being noticed by human senses, while the second caused any human who noticed RPC-316 to perceive it as the utmost leader of said individual's affiliation.

Prior to contact with the RPC Authority, RPC-316 was imprisoned within RPC-257[[footnote]] PC-257 summarized: **//"Ama-No-Iwato, the Lightless Vault"//**, a cave system converted into a primitive containment facility once maintained by the now-defunct Hidden Sun Clan, now an Authority property ever since their destruction. Houses a large number of increasingly dangerous anomalies in temporal stasis. [[/footnote]], reanimating and escaping its anomalous confines during RPC-257's own discovery event.

RPC-316 proceeded use its anomalous powers to great effect, causing disruptions across Japanese Authority Sites. It went on to order and consume exorbitant amounts of high-class food whilst commissioning extravagant goods and services at the severe expense of Authority funding. Authority accountants have so far estimated a grand total of USD $124+ billion in food and commissioned luxuries ordered by RPC-316, with $440k of Authority finances being successfully spent. Attempts to reclaim these expenditures are ongoing, with an estimated writeoff of ██%.

As it further explored what it could do with its influence over the Authority, RPC-316 escalated its abuse of power to attempt a series of disastrous events for its own amusement: 

* Attempting to commission prohibitively expensive custom-built luxury vehicles and site amenities for its own personal use, including a walking stick featuring the British royal crown jewels in its handle,
* Attempting to order the detonation of on-site nuclear warheads to //"enjoy the fireworks"//,
* Attempting to order the execution of key personnel as a test of mesmerized personnel's loyalty,
* Attempting to take direct control of MST movements and command,
* Attempting to acquire other RPCs for its own personal use and cross-interaction, 
* Attempting to take full control of the RPC Authority by supplanting itself over the Global Directorate, 
* The repeated access of level 4 and 5 information and numerous breaches of their classification in the process of "telling jokes", and more.

While it thankfully did not succeed in the aforementioned attempts above thanks to the Authority's bureaucratic checks and protocols, RPC-316 succeeded in temporarily unsealing RPC-257 by learning the appropriate ritual from surviving archaic documentation kept in Site-089. This resulted in uncountable entities reanimating from RPC-257's temporal stasis effect, successfully escaping the cavern complex. 

RPC-316 marshaled a large number of these escapees to begin a parade known in Japanese mythology as a //Hyakki-Yakko//, bearing the goal of traveling through Japan's urban civilian centers and inciting panic. Through an unknown displacement method involving doorways only visible to these entities, the parade rapidly traveled from the outskirts of Takachiho towards Kyoto within the span of an hour, resurfacing in various settlements and Osaka to loot, vandalize and assault locals. The end-goal within Kyoto was to harass the Japanese Emperor himself, incorrectly believed to be residing within Kyoto Imperial Palace.[[footnote]] The Imperial Family moved residences to Tokyo in the mid-1800s, with the Kyoto Imperial Palace serving as a tourist attraction. [[/footnote]]

The //Hyakki-Yakko// would go on to be intercepted by a Project Blue Book exterminator battalion in Kyoto, who responded with lethal force leading to a 99.9% termination rate of anomalies and heavy infrastructural damage about the //Kawaramachi-Imadegawa// traffic junction. RPC-316 escaped the assault and fled into a nearby alleyway, only to be neutralized at the hands of another anomalous being.

In its current deceased state, RPC-316's anomalous properties appear to have greatly diminished. Personnel who view the corpse perceive fleeting senses of sadness or shock, but recover soon after. A sub-therapeutic dose of low-strength viderics nullifies this effect entirely.

**Termination Log:** The following is a conjectural step-by-step timeline of RPC-316's neutralization, based on the following: Eyewitness accounts, recordings of Project Blue Book's extermination operation of RPC-316's anomalous congregation, forensics investigation of RPC-316's site of death and an autopsy examination of RPC-316's body and injuries.

# RPC-316's gathering is halted by a blockade of Blue Book exterminators organized in a pincer ambush.[[footnote]] It is not known how Blue Book was made aware of this gathering and assembled in the correct location - it is suspected a mole within Authority ranks may have tipped them off. [[/footnote]]
# Blue Book opens fire, followed by reinforcements from squads based in separate points around the Imperial Palace.[[footnote]] Having being abducted as a 'guest of honour' and surviving the night, Senior Researcher Junichiro explained during debriefing that RPC-316's cognitohazardous effects were suppressed by the vast presence of fellow anomalous beings, allowing Blue Book personnel to open fire without hesitation. [[/footnote]] RPC-316's right side is caught in a blast radius, sustaining burn, shrapnel and explosion injuries.
# RPC-316 abandons its panicking congregation to Blue Book's exterminators. Unnoticed, it is trampled and injured further in the chaos.
# RPC-316 escapes into an alleyway shortly before Blue Book command authorizes the usage of an orbital strike to wipe out the remaining escapees in a show of overwhelming force. RPC-486, RPC-886's sister, is waiting for it.
# RPC-486 stabs RPC-316 through the back with a knife taken from a nearby restaurant.
# RPC-486 follows up by puncturing RPC-316's throat, cutting its neck open while severing its jugulars and vocal cords.
# RPC-486 throws RPC-316 to the ground, stabbing it in the chest repeatedly while it attempts to fend RPC-486 off.
# RPC-486 issues a final stab into RPC-316's eye, penetrating its brain. RPC-316 is neutralized.

RPC-316's corpse would be found afterwards by a Blue Book cleanup crew, causing confusion and agitation among their ranks as to why the corpse of their "Alpha-Class Founder" was among the neutralized entities. During a heated dispute with Blue Book command over accusations of being manipulated into assassinating said "Founder", Authority infiltrators successfully exit the outpost with RPC-316's corpse. An investigation of the murder site found the discarded knife.

> **Interviewed:** RPC-486
> 
> **Interviewer:** Dr. Yoshizawa Hinako
> 
> **Foreword:** Dr. Hinako approached RPC-486's den without being subjected to the customary insult or taunting. RPC-486 appeared listless, sitting beside its burrow entry with vulpine ears folded. To prevent any faux sensations of grief/anger Dr. Hinako examined RPC-316's corpse prior while dosed on viderics, affirming that RPC-316 was an anomalous infiltrator and not a high ranking RPC personnel as she was initially manipulated into believing.
> 
> Note that despite the above preventatives and the rapid diminishing of RPC-316's anomalous effect post-termination, Dr. Hinako occasionally displays residual signs of reverence towards RPC-316, albeit catching herself in the act of doing so and correcting herself accordingly.
> 
> ------
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** Hello, Mishio. You really look like you've seen better days.
> 
> **RPC-486:** If you're going to call me by my name and not by that stupid number, it's obvious that you're playing nice for something. Go away.
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** You know that's not how this works. I have to talk to you about something urgent. I'll wait here, and if you don't jinx or throw something at me I'll proceed. Okay?
> 
> //[ RPC-486 does not respond. ]// 
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** All right. I'll start. What can you tell me of what happened at Kyoto? 
> 
> **RPC-486:** The bastards who tried to kill Shiomi, then went on to nearly kill me in Tokyo had the time of their lives splitting apart a few hundred other //Yōkai//. What else do you want to know?
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** Aside from that, we want to know what happened to RPC-316, the Grand Dire- I mean, the //Nurarihyon//. He was killed, you see. But not by gunfire. We found a bloody knife close to where his corpse was. The fingerprints on its handle are a headache to read, suggestive of being held by a shapeshifter. Bits of your hair, or fur, in his fingers...
> 
> //[ RPC-486 does not respond. ]//
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** Mishio... Was it you who killed him?
> 
> //[ RPC-486 remains silent. ]//
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** We're not angry or anything like that. We just want to know what happened that night, if you are willing to tell us. You were there in Kyoto when everything went down, weren't you? You tried to warn them all and stop the Night Parade from taking off from Outpost eighty-nine, according to survivor accounts.
> 
> **RPC-486:** I tried. Everybody who stayed in that //Hyakki-Yakko//, none of them would listen. They all paid for it with their lives.
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** Why did you do it, Mishio? Why did you do what you did to his maj- to him?
> 
> **RPC-486:** The Massacre at Kyoto... It wasn't going to stop him. Sooner or later, he'd wave it all away, crawl out of wherever he's hiding in, make another stupid party somewhere else. Do it all over again. Or worse, he'd try and seek revenge. Not even for the deaths. Just for ruining his fun.
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** So you stopped him before he became a threat to us all.
> 
> **RPC-486:** Don't misunderstand! I didn't do it for you humans... I did it for Shiomi. For my family. If that old idiot started a war between //Yōkai// and humans, we'd... None of us would survive. Shiomi wouldn't be safe. I did it to protect Shiomi and our family. I don't care about anything else!
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** A week ago I would not believe I'd ever actually say this to you, but... Regardless of your intents, thank you on behalf of mankind and the Authority.
> 
> **RPC-486:** Yeah, yeah, it was nothing... Killing a stupid, weak old man...
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** That aside. In consideration of your deed, we have had a talk and... decided to grant you scheduled visitation rights with your sister. And we will also be toning down on the, ah, //antics// to get you out when you overstay your welcome-
> 
> **RPC-486:** //<Drastically perks up>// No more //'Dominance-Posing'//? **WOO!**
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** Wait-Before you celebrate, I have one last important question for you: When you killed him, do you think there could be some ramifications to his death?
> 
> **RPC-486:** Not really. If you mean friends who'd seek revenge or something similar he only had idiots and sycophants who followed his parties and buttered him up, and they were all a bit too busy dying to notice. I'll be fine.
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** I see. Again, thank you, Mishio.
> 
> **RPC-486:** Mmmm, yes. Praise me again!
> 
> **Yoshizawa:** Don't push your luck.
> 
> **<End Log>**

Kill-squads, assassins, snipers. The Authority's most ruthless ready to do whatever it takes to kill off that old man, granted various methods and weapons I don't care to describe. There's a casualty log we've bloodied our hands with: Innocent personnel who got caught in the crossfire, a list that is never going to be made known of.

And in the end we find him dead, reduced to a pincushion. By a kitchen knife. Wielded by one of his own 'subjects', no less.

Ironic, isn't it?

I do believe everyone knows this by now but it bears repeating: This was a massive disaster on all our parts. But all things considered, //it could have been worse.//

RPC-316 was a living, breathing cognitohazard of a scale none of us were truly prepared for. And yet, did we not keep it contained to a certain degree of success?

It kept coming back to our installations to mess with us because the very concept of the Authority amused it so much: All this secrecy, that we bear out of necessity.

It considered us a puzzle box, a toy to crack open for little more than idle entertainment. We hindered its attempts at total control with our shrouded identities and bureaucratic checks meant to prevent exactly what it was doing: __The unlimited abuse of power__. This kept it intrigued enough to focus its attentions almost exclusively on us, leading to this double-Ouroboros of us vexing one another over and over. 

As I said when I spoke to the displeased Japanese federal government officials on behalf of directors Genichi and Fuyutsumi: What could the alternatives have been if we weren't there to preoccupy it?

What if a bored RPC-316 chanced upon the correct Imperial Palace at Tokyo, or just as equally bad, wandered into the National Diet?

Emperor of Emperors. The Primest Prime Minister. Other ridiculous titles, you can imagine. Instant control of all of Japan, followed by unfettered access to the rest of the planet to spread his brand of disruptive hedonism. Let that sink in, I told the representatives.

With us, he wanted to initiate a nuclear failsafe. Imagine instead if he started World War 3 with Japan's military at his fingertips?

We've learned a valuable lesson out of all this: //Never again.//

If we ever get any hint that another Class IX entity like him ever shows up once more? We'll be unleashing [REDACTED] from the get-go.
