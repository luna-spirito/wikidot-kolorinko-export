**Registered Phenomena Code:** 562

**Object Class:** Omega-Red [[[footnote]] Explained sub-class. Designates anomalous items that were originally deemed anomalous, but are now considered mundane.[[/footnote]]]

**Hazard Types:** --Memory-Alteration--[[footnote]]Rendered incorrect after Incident 12022005/562.[[/footnote]], Aggression, Grouped, Sentient, Sapient?, Organic
[[/div]]
------

**Containment Protocols:** While RPC-562 itself is considered Explained, it is now known that its causes are anomalous and hostile, and remain active and uncontained. As such, an outstanding capture order has been issued for RPC-562-A.

RPC-562 remains a large threat to operational security, and as such gaslight protocols are in full effect for the Madrid Autonomous Community. Webcrawlers deployed in the European Union region are to seek potential references to Group of Interest "Worried Citizens of Plaza Mayor" as well as Madrid Capital City missing person reports filed between 2001 and 2005. Findings are then to be reported to EUROCOM Protection Division authorities.

> ##red|**OUTSTANDING CAPTURE ORDER:**## **06/07/2006 - PRESENT**
> **Authority:** EUROCOM PD Reg. Director Lauro Osorio
> **Current Focus:** RPC-562-A, Spain, Portugal, France

------

**Description:** RPC-562 designates a series of disappearances occurring in the vicinities of Madrid, capital city of Spain, between 2001 and 2005. While potential cases have been identified as early as 1999, only 162 disappearances have been confirmed as being a consequence of the phenomenon, each individually designated RPC-562-1 through -162. In order to view a full list of cases and evidence associated to each, view ###5295e1|Document 562-A/#814-3-B##.

RPC-562 cases follow an invariable three-phase pattern of events, organized as follows;

# Subject, hereon referred to as RPC-562-A, will claim to feel stalked by a single human figure every time they leave their house. This figure is always on-foot, and its face remains obscured in all descriptions. It appears to be innately aware of the exact location and field of view of surveillance devices placed in its vicinity, and successfuly exploits their limitations in order to remain unseen or quickly flee.
# After an undefined amount of time, RPC-562-A will disappear in unclear circumstances, with evidence being completely absent or insufficient to discern the exact moment of disappearance. Progressions to phase 2 occur when RPC-562-A is alone outside their home, but may not immediately trigger.
# Between 3 and 6 months after disappearance, RPC-562-A will reappear, unconscious, near the location of disappearance. Phase 3 RPC-562-A instances wear identical clothing as they did before disappearing, albeit slightly damaged and torn. RPC-562-A display an amnestic condition similar to Korsakoff Syndrome[[footnote]] Normally developed as a consequence of malnutrition or prolonged use of alcohol [[/footnote]], whose main symptom is generalized retrograde amnesia affecting all memories prior to onset: individuals are unable to recall most events prior to Phase 3, and fail to remember their prior relationship with individuals they recognize, such as family members or other loved ones.

Progression of Phase 1 into Phase 2 has been successfully halted temporarily by some affected individuals by avoiding unpopulated areas or remaining inside their homes, but eventual disappearance appears to be inevitable.

RPC-562 is considered Explained after Incident 12022005/562, but investigation regarding causes of the phenomenon are still ongoing.

While RPC-562 was known to the Authority since 2003, initial research of RPC-562 was conducted by an internet forum-based community self-designated "Ciudadanos Preocupados de Plaza Mayor".[[footnote]] Worried Citizens of Plaza Mayor. This name is derived from a similar organization based in Argentina, Mothers of Plaza de Mayo, founded during 1977 as a response to forced disappearances caused by the then-standing military dictatorship. [[/footnote]] Founded in 2001 after RPC-562-29 and originally composed of only 26 members, it underwent significant expansion after Incident 12022005/562, reaching 279 registered members prior to Authority intervention.

Original members of the group believed that RPC-562 were a series of forced disappearances carried out by the Madrid police force in potential collaboration with the Spanish government and other international governments or organizations for unknown purposes, potentially targeting individuals in possession of sensitive data. However, following a minor rise in popularity after RPC-562-98, the group's focus shifted to paranatural belief, attempting to explain RPC-562 as a paranormal phenomenon. Popular explanations produced by the group range from extraterrestrial abduction to a kind of "temporary rapture" or religious experience akin to the events of the //Divine Comedy.//

The group never gained enough traction to pose a significant threat to security, as Spanish media largely failed to publicize RPC-562 cases, and failed to pick up on similarities between those cases that were publicized.

The Worried Citizens were officially classified a Group of Interest after November of 2005, and were considered dismantled by July of the following year, following capture, questioning, and release of involved individuals. The group's understanding of RPC-562 was flawed, and none of their members had managed to contact RPC-562-A instances or their families prior to Authority intervention, with one major exception. However, their now-defunct website housed a large amount of evidence regarding events they believed associated with RPC-562, of which a significant amount has proven essential to Authority research on the phenomenon.

++ FILE #2: 29/06/2004

On the 29th of June, 2004, a 34 minute long video was uploaded to an off-topic thread in the Worried Citizens forum by an anonymous user[[footnote]] Going by the username "Fobos" [[/footnote]], claiming to be an exploration of an abandoned building in the outskirts of Madrid. When questioned by other forum members, the user refused to elaborate on the location the video was recorded in, instead assuring other users that "the place was dangerous". Four hours after publishing, it appears a user identified the location in question, resulting in the video being taken down and posts mentioning it deleted due to safety concerns. 

Before the video was taken down, a second user identified a location similar to the photograph attached to File #1, potentially connecting the anonymous user with RPC-562-98. Certain forum users believe that the uploader was Julián Ortega employing a different device, but had found no evidence to substantiate that claim.

As the original file could not be acquired by the Authority, its contents are unclear. However, a fragmented summary has been constructed through analysis of the thread.

> **00:00 -- 00:22:** Subject enters building through double doors, encountering what appears to be a mansion hall.
> 
> **14:55:** Panning shot: the outside of a large, unidentified machine in operation. It is coated in rust, and several sections appear to be nonfunctional or failing to work properly. It is mainly composed of liquid or gas storage tanks, as well as tubes and gears. Several tubes extend from its mainframe, a few of which leak small amounts of vapor.
> 
> **22:10 -- 26:30:** Subject explores what appears to be a meeting room, with a long table in its center. The table is covered with an assortment of papers and photographies. Along the opposite side to the entrance a piece of the wall has been removed, revealing an inactive furnace. Its inside holds partially-burned pieces of paper.
> 
> **28:11:** Brief shot of a weapons arsenal. Most weapons in it remain unknown, but at least a single modified M16 assault rifle was identified by forum users. This rifle is generally believed to be an early M16A2 model manufactured between 1983 and 1986, but the nature of its modifications remains unclear.
> 
> **33:42 -- 34:00:** Subject carefully records their surroundings, being inside an observation room with a single glass panel over a dark space. A reinforced metal door is located next to the glass panel. Subject later struggles to open this door, and descends a grated staircase. Forum users commenting on this segment note that the staircase is affixed to a wall to its left, which apears to be composed of rock but is extensively polished, with no rugosities or irregularities visible on camera.
> 
> **34:00 -- 34:47:** Subject finishes descending, and proceeds through a narrow nondescript pathway, similarly polished to the above described wall. Echoes of steps are audible, and grow in volume and proximity until the recording ends.

++ FILE #3: OPERATION HAMMER

The following documents were acquired between 07/2005 and 06/2007 during Operation Hammer, a joint effort by EUROCOM Spain Protection Division and a limited number of Worried Citizens members to locate potential causes for RPC-562 following Incident 12022005/562. 

Assistance from these individuals was vital to the operation's success as they had kept careful records of unexplained activity of armed individuals and unmarked vehicles around several buildings believed to be abandoned. Such movement is known to have been widespread in Madrid up until 1998, when all unexplained activity abruptly ceased. 

It is currently believed that certain elements of the City Council of Madrid were in tight collaboration with a small number of known independent an-orgs[[footnote]] Anomalous organizations [[/footnote]] operating publicly as a variety of small businesses, including construction, demolition, and pest control. However, careful raking of abandoned buildings known to be epicenters of the aforementioned unexplained activity managed to recover a limited amount of correspondence and documentation linking them together. It is believed that correspondence between these organizations uses a unique method of encryption or argot designed to obscure their intended meaning, masking it as logistical data or friendly communication between individual members.

While successful in demonstrating the existence of anomalous activity in Madrid prior to 1998, data acquired during the beginning stages of Operation Hammer was limited and failed to describe its purpose or link it to RPC-562, as it is believed that truly sensitive documents were destroyed prior to Authority involvement.

Operation Hammer led to a major breakthrough during 08/02/2006, when a series of improperly destroyed documents were discovered in a hidden furnace in the Sanatorio de la Marina. These documents directly linked pre-1998 anomalous activity to a subsidiary of the Vinland-Pyre Research Institute and RPC-562, and allowed the Authority to properly understand the causes and consequences of the anomalous phenomenon. Newer research discovered widespread activity of the Institute across Madrid between 1980 and 1984.

Of note, these letters prominently feature the Greek letters omega and iota in the top right. It is currently believed that this symbol represents the group of associated organizations, or their operations conducted in Madrid.

On the 12th of June 2005, a fire was detected in Floor 21 of the Windsor Tower, an office building in the financial center of Madrid. The fire spread quickly to the rest of the building, causing its outermost steel layer to collapse. The destruction of the Windsor Tower was nearly complete, and lead to its remains being demolished later.

However, during assessment by the City Council, a hole in its foundations was detected, connecting the lowest floor with an immense, unlit underground cavity. This discovery prompted Authority involvement, and a 20-man exploration team was sent into the cavity, and encountered a massive, rectangular space, approximately 2km in length, 600m in width, and 5km in depth. What appear to be portions of twenty six-story apartment buildings are carved from the stone, all empty save for thirty-six naked human bodies in various locations. These bodies are in various states of decomposition, and have been partially devoured by an unknown, carnivorous entity.

Once gaslight protocols were put in place, all thirty-six bodies were identified as victims of RPC-562 that had resurfaced and been questioned by the Authority in the past months. Further investigation of the Madrid underground managed to discover 53 cavities similar to that on Windsor Tower, populated with the bodies of all 162 RPC-562 victims.

However, when attempts were made to contact or capture the "resurfaced" individuals, it was discovered that they had disappeared again less than 24 hours after the Windsor Tower fire, last sighted running away from their homes. Attempts to track them have been unsuccessful, and close relatives have uniformly claimed unusual and hostile behaviour since their return to their households. Investigations attempting to connect a recent spike in cannibalistic murders across Western Europe with the resurfacing of RPC-562 victims are ongoing.

All "resurfaced" RPC-562 victims are under an ##red|outstanding capture order##, and are to be considered hostile Gamma-Red anomalies.
