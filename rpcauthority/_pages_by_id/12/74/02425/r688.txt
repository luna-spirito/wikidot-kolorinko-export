**Foreword:** In addition to containing major anomalous objects, the Authority also contains numerous Lesser Anomalous Items. These items are always Alpha in classification and frequently mundane in nature. The majority of lesser anomalous items are contained at [[[Site-016]]].[[footnote]]"The Warehouse."[[/footnote]]

The Lesser Object Format can be found [[[formatting-guide|here]]] under the "Lesser Object" section.

**Designation:** LO-348
**Description:** A traditional Chinese butterfly knife. 
**Site Location:** Site-038
Properties: Any object or substance cut by either blade of LO-348 will ignite approximately 1-3 seconds after being severed. The resulting combustion originates directly from the cut surface and rapidly spreads throughout the affected material. The ignition occurs regardless of the target's flammability; documented tests have resulted in the combustion of wood, concrete, steel, glass, water-saturated materials, and biological tissue.
**Note:** LO-348 was recovered following a fatal apartment fire in Guangdong Province, China, on 14/08/19██. Local authorities reported that the fire originated simultaneously in multiple rooms without an identifiable ignition source. An undercover Authority agent embedded within the provincial fire investigation bureau discovered the object among the debris.The previous owner of LO-348 remains unidentified. No historical records concerning LO-348 have been located, though decorative markings on LO-348 suggest manufacture during the late Qing Dynasty.

**Designation:** LO-351
**Description:** A collection of 4,670 cards from the Yu-Gi-Oh! Duel Monsters trading card game. All cards originate from the original Yu-Gi-Oh! series and its sequel series, Yu-Gi-Oh! GX. The cards show no anomalous properties when not being used in gameplay.
**Site Location:** Site-016
**Properties:** When a legal duel is conducted using LO-351 cards, miniature animated manifestations of the monsters depicted on summoned cards will appear above their designated cards. These manifestations, designated LO-351-A instances, resemble scaled-down versions of their depicted monsters and act out card effects, attacks, and other game actions in an "overlay dramatic" manner. LO-351-A instances are fully sapient and are fluent in both English and Japanese. During matches, instances frequently converse with duel players and spectators, offering strategic advice, insults, complaints, observations, and commentary regarding the state of the game. Interactions between different LO-351-A instances are regular and often reflect rivalries or alliances based on their respective card lore. All attacks and effects performed by LO-351-A instances are purely visual and auditory in nature and do not cause physical damage to players, cards, or surrounding objects.

LO-351-A instances will only manifest in duels that follow the official rules of the game. If a participant makes an illegal move, the affected instances will refuse to carry out the action and will halt progression of the duel until a legal move is made. Repeated violations cause LO-351-A instances to become uncooperative toward the offending player in future matches  often ignoring commands. Staff of Site-016 are allowed to use LO-351 for recreational purposes during break hours.

[[div class="content-panel"]]

**Designation:** LO-352
**Description:** A collection of modified plant species, primarily derived from the domesticated apple tree (Malus domestica), common sweet orange tree (Citrus × sinensis), Eastern White Pine (Pinus strobus), European pear (Pyrus communis), and Creeping Bent (Agrostis stolonifera). LO-352 instances were created by the Descendants of Yggdrasil.
**Site Location:** N/A
**Properties:** LO-352 instances are capable of producing various forms of confectioneries in place of their normal fruits, foliage, or plant tissues. These confectioneries contain no artificial ingredients and have been found to possess greater nutritional value than their artificially manufactured counterparts.

[[include :scp-wiki:component:coltop
| show=+ Known products produced by LO-352 include:
| hide=- Known products produced by LO-352 include:
]]

• Chocolate pinecones produced by modified Eastern White Pines.

• Rock candy formations resembling pears produced by modified European pear trees.

• Jelly beans produced in place of fruit on modified orange trees.

• Caramel candies produced in place of apples on modified apple trees.

• Liquorice-flavored roots and stems produced by modified Creeping Bent specimens.
[[include :scp-wiki:component:colend]]

LO-352 instances derive nutrients from pollutant substances, allowing them to thrive in heavily contaminated soil and water. Creating a gradual reduction of pollution in these areas. When cultivated within pots or similar containers, LO-352 instances will greatly decrease in size to fit the container while retaining normal productivity. This effect ceases once the specimen is transplanted into an open environment.

LO-352 instances do not produce viable seeds. Reproduction occurs exclusively through vegetative propagation, including cuttings and grafting.

Due to their utility and environmental benefits among the Descendants of Yggdrasil, LO-352 specimens are commonly exchanged as gestures of goodwill, appreciation, and diplomacy. Authority personnel are authorized to cultivate approved LO-352 specimens for personal usage.
