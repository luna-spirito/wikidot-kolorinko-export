**Registered Phenomena Code:** 190

**Object Class:** Beta-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Ballistic, Extreme Temperature, Organic, Sapient

**Containment Protocols:** Due to its location in the anomalous space on the island of Pabbay, Scotland, RPC-190 is effectively self-containing. In addition, there is currently no threat to normalcy posed by RPC-190.

Authority units stationed at OL-Site-CA have made an agreement with residents of the Hinterlands Village to aid in the rebuilding of houses destroyed by RPC-190-A and -B, in exchange for information on how to traverse Cair Aisling.

**Description:** RPC-190 is the designation for two reptilian entities, referred to as RPC-190-A and RPC-190-B respectively. Both entities inhabit the anomalous area located on the island of Pabbay, Scotland[[footnote]] Hereafter referred to as "Cair Aisling".[[/footnote]], in two separate caves. Both entities are able to speak in British English[[footnote]] Believed to be achieved because of how Randolph Gowering wrote dragons in the Cair Aisling series.[[/footnote]].

RPC-190-A and -B are believed to be the two dragons mentioned in Volume 3 of the Cair Aisling novel series, written by Randolph Gowering. In Volume 3, the entities are described by a character[[footnote]] Mr. Teuman, a human historian/dragonologist living in the Hinterlands Village. Mr. Teuman was a friend of Hasan Maza, and was killed off in Volume 5.[[/footnote]] to have had grudges against each other for over 2 decades, "always bickering and fighting over the tiniest things".

In addition to this, RPC-190-A and -B have been reported by residents of Cair Aisling to exit their caves and fly to each other in order to quarrel with one another. These altercations have mainly occurred in areas between/including the Plains and the First Forest. The entities have left much destruction in the wake of these encounters. The exact reason for the entities' hatred of one another is unclear at the moment, but it may be a large variety of reasons.

[[div class="dragons"]]

= [[size 130%]]**RPC-190-A**[[/size]]

----

RPC-190-A inhabits a cave in a cliff face at the Gulf of Wethir. RPC-190-A is ~5.8 meters in height, and resembles common depictions of dragons within European folklore. RPC-190-A possesses scales that are dark-green in coloration. Protruding from the front of RPC-190-A's cranium is a pair of horns, similar to those of a //Bos mutus//[[footnote]] Wild yak[[/footnote]]. RPC-190-A possesses a long, slender tail which has a sharp point located on the tip.

RPC-190-A, like other dragons that inhabit Cair Aisling, is able to emit flames from its body. The entity emanates these flames orally, which reach distances of ~53.3 meters and temperatures of ~700 degrees celsius. Like many other elements of Cair Aisling, it is unknown how this is achieved and is assumed to be Thaumaturgy.

RPC-190-A is known for being a hoarder of precious items, which it keeps inside the cave it dwells in. The entirety of these items are constructed from gold[[footnote]] Gold is much more common in Cair Aisling than other areas in Europe.[[/footnote]], frequently encrusted with crystals and gems. The items are stored in a large pile at the end of the cave, which reaches the cave's ceiling due to the amount of objects in the pile. RPC-190-A mainly exits its cave in order to find more items to add to its pile, or prey to consume.

[[/div]]

[[div class="dragons"]]

= [[size 130%]]**RPC-190-B**[[/size]]

----

RPC-190-B inhabits a cave in a large limestone boulder[[footnote]] Presumed to have been hollowed out.[[/footnote]] in the Desert, near the Dragon's Graveyard. RPC-190-B is ~6.1 meters in height, and resembles depictions of a Lindworm[[footnote]] A dragon-like creature from Northern-European mythology.[[/footnote]]. RPC-190-B does not possess scales, instead being covered in light-tan colored fur. The entity lacks hind-legs, as well as wings. Despite this, it is able to achieve flight. Furthermore, RPC-190-B when "standing" places its front-legs on the ground while the rest of its body is kept aloft[[footnote]] RPC-190-B lets its body lay on the ground when resting.[[/footnote]].

RPC-190-B, unlike RPC-190-A, is not able to emit fire from its body and utilizes a separate defense mechanism. Attached to RPC-190-B's head are frills that surround it in a ring, akin to the //Chlamydosaurus kingii//[[footnote]] Frill-necked lizard[[/footnote]]. Dark-brown quills are embedded inside the skin of RPC-190-B's frills, which average ~15 centimeters in length. The entity is able to fire these quills out of its frills at high speeds.

Apart from fighting RPC-190-A, RPC-190-B has only been seen out of its cave a few times. RPC-190-B predominantly exits its dwelling to seek out prey to feast upon. Prey of RPC-190-B consists of animals native to the Desert and travelers in and near the Dragon's Graveyard.

[[/div]]

**Addendum I:** RPC-190-A Interview 2/7/2020[[footnote]] Logs have been transcribed using pencil and paper by an accompanying researcher.[[/footnote]]

[[collapsible show="|> ACCESS FILE" hide="<| CLOSE FILE"]]

[[div class="dragons2"]]

**Interviewer:** Researcher John Sheffield

**Interviewee:** RPC-190-A

----

//(Researcher Sheffield enters the cave in which RPC-190-A resides.)//

**SHEFFIELD:** Ahem. Hello?

//(RPC-190-A approaches Researcher Sheffield from out of the darkness of the cave.)//

**RPC-190-A:** Who goes there? Who are you?

**SHEFFIELD:** Erm, I am simply a man looking to ask you a few questions. For a report on Dragonology.

**RPC-190-A:** You are dressed in the raiment of one who would attempt to murder me.

**SHEFFIELD:** The armor is for if you decided to attack me as soon as I came here.

**RPC-190-A:** Do not worry, human.

**SHEFFIELD:** Good, good. Anyways, I have been told about another dragon you have… problems with.

**RPC-190-A:** Which? I must let you know, I have squabbled with many, many other beings of my kind before.

**SHEFFIELD:** The one from the Desert?

**RPC-190-A:** Beazere? //(Laughs)// Is he even a real dragon? Acts quite feminal for a supposed //real// dragon!

**SHEFFIELD:** Hm? Could you elaborate?

**RPC-190-A:** Oh, I can with ease. He is one of those… "new world" dragons. Unlike one of us, //actual// dragons, he looks nothing like us. Thinks he's better than us when he is obviously inferior.

**SHEFFIELD:** Anything else?

**RPC-190-A:** //(Inhales)// Do you know Beazere cannot breathe fire? He just has spines like some giant hedgehog. What a coward, he couldn't burn down a village if he tried!

**SHEFFIELD:** It seems R— Beazere only comes out of his cave when he needs to eat. Why would he want to attack any villages?

**RPC-190-A:** Well, he is a poltroon! He would never try to destroy a town, he thinks the locals would slay him, or send a legion of soldiers to find him and do the same thing.

//(RPC-190-A pauses for 2 seconds.)//

**RPC-190-A:** He just goes out for meat because it is not a risk. In fact, I believe he may have a hidden stockpile… the glutton.

**SHEFFIELD:** //(Leans to look behind RPC-190-A)// What about //your// stockpile of gold?

//(RPC-190-A backs away and covers the pile with its body.)//

**RPC-190-A:** You cannot have it! Avaunt!

**SHEFFIELD:** I wasn't–

**RPC-190-A:** //You will not get your hands on my treasure//.

//(Smoke begins rising out of RPC-190-A's nostrils.)//

**SHEFFIELD:** Fine, we'll… I'll leave.

//(Researcher Sheffield exits the cave.)//

**Addendum II:** RPC-190-B Interview 5/7/2020

[[collapsible show="|> ACCESS FILE" hide="<| CLOSE FILE"]]

[[div class="dragons2"]]

**Interviewer:** Researcher John Sheffield

**Interviewee:** RPC-190-B

----

**SHEFFIELD:** Hello? Uh, "Beazere"?

//(RPC-190-B abruptly crawls out of its cave and shoots its quills at Researcher Sheffield. The quills bounce off his armor.)//

**SHEFFIELD:** Hey, hey, I'm not looking to hurt you? Alright?

//(RPC-190-B stops, and stares at Researcher Sheffield.)//

**RPC-190-B:** Hrm. What is it you want.

**SHEFFIELD:** I'm a scholar looking to ask you a few questions, for a report on Dragonology.

**RPC-190-B:** …Right. Go ahead then, come in.

//(RPC-190-B turns around and crawls back into its cave.)//

**RPC-190-B:** //(Turns its head)// And do not ask any stupid questions, mooncalf.

//(Researcher Sheffield follows RPC-190-B into the cave.)//

**SHEFFIELD:** So, I'll get straight to the point. I've heard you have some issues with another dragon, over at the Gulf of Wethir.

**RPC-190-B:** Aye. Fernael?

**SHEFFIELD:** I… think that's his name.

**RPC-190-B:** Oh, let me tell you about him. He still has a pile of gold, even though treasure-piling has not been done for decades. He always boasts about how enormous his pile is… it's not huge at all!

**SHEFFIELD:** Really? I've seen it, it's… large.

**RPC-190-B:** //(Laughs)// It is not. It only seems that way to you, for you are but a tiny human. Fernael, his pile will never be as huge as the piles of the Old Hoarders[[footnote]] In the Cair Aisling series, the Old Hoarders were an ancient group of dragons that owned treasure piles which filled entire cave systems with their numbers.[[/footnote]]. He //wishes//.

**SHEFFIELD:** Well, why don't you have a treasure pile?

**RPC-190-B:** I am not interested in fancy things. Treasure-piling is not for me. Fernael, he is completely corrupted by greed. He will not let any one person near his pile. I believe he may be, and excuse my language, "natured".

//(Silence.)//

**SHEFFIELD:** …Anything else you'd like to add?

**RPC-190-B:** I would. Fernael has always stated that his kind is superior, simply because his kind is older. //(Scoffs)// My kind is verily well-equipped for surviving in this world, while his will go extinct in no less than five twelvemonths. People here know to use fire-proofing potions. I don't need to go and bombard villages with an ambuscade, there is plenty of meat out here.

**SHEFFIELD:** Well, Fernael said you don't attack villages because you're apparently a coward.

**RPC-190-B:** …//What?//

**SHEFFIELD:** Er, I've talked— I've talked to him. He said you're, uh, "feminal".

**RPC-190-B:** Wondrous. Now I despise him even more. //(Pauses)// Excuse me for a second.

//(RPC-190-B crawls past Researcher Sheffield, and out of the cave. It begins rising into the air.)//

**RPC-190-B:** I am going to tear his tail off.

//(RPC-190-B flies in the direction of the Gulf of Wethir.)//

**SHEFFIELD:** I didn't embark on this three-day expedition to the Desert for this.

**Addendum III:** Incident 190 8/7/2020

[[collapsible show="|> ACCESS FILE" hide="<| CLOSE FILE"]]

[[div class="dragons2"]]

**Foreword:** The following is a transcript of Incident X, which occurred after RPC-190-B reached RPC-190-A's cave.

----

//(RPC-190-B lands in the entrance to the cave.)//

**RPC-190-B:** Fernael!

//(Silence.)//

**RPC-190-B:** //Fernael!// Get up!

//(RPC-190-A emerges from the darkness. RPC-190-B is clenching its claws.)//

**RPC-190-A:** Oh. Greetings, Beazere. What is it y–

**RPC-190-B:** Do not ask me foolish questions. I did not fly hither to answer anything. You //know// why I am here.

**RPC-190-A:** …I do not.

**RPC-190-B:** //(Sighs, and puts a claw to its forehead)// Did you encounter a human a few days ago?

//(Silence.)//

**RPC-190-A:** Um, no?

**RPC-190-B:** I think you might not be telling the truth. Because a human visited me a few days ago, and //he// claimed that //you// said I was a… coward.

**RPC-190-A:** I… //(Splutters)// I never said that!

**RPC-190-B:** How am I supposed to believe you? When have you ever been truthful?

**RPC-190-A:** Now, why would I lie about that?

**RPC-190-B:** I know you insult me behind my back! Always mouthing off about how I don't respect treasure-piling. I find it strange that //you// respect it.

**RPC-190-A:** It is one of the oldest traditions in the land, Beazere!

**RPC-190-B:** Oh, because it is tradition? //(Scoffs)// How haughty. You and other dragons continue partaking in outdated practices… you… //rough-skins//.

**RPC-190-A:** //(Gasps)// Do not //say that!//

//(RPC-190-A suddenly rushes towards RPC-190-B, and digs its claws into its flesh. RPC-190-B lets out a screech, and retaliates by tightening its serpentine body around RPC-190-A.)//

**RPC-190-B:** I say… I say whatever I want abou— argh!

//(RPC-190-A bites into RPC-190-B's body, and it lets go of RPC-190-A. RPC-190-A begins to fall into the ocean below, but flaps its wings to rise up and shoot out a stream of fire towards RPC-190-B.)//

//(The stream of fire burns RPC-190-B's fur, causing it to retreat to the top of the cliff. RPC-190-A pauses for a moment, and then flies up to RPC-190-B. When it reaches the top of the cliff, it is shot with quills by RPC-190-B.)//

**RPC-190-A:** //(Roars, and pulls out the quills from its body)// You are quite the man-at-arms.

//(RPC-190-B floats up to meet RPC-190-A.)//

**RPC-190-B:** That is because I do not waste my time showing off flames to my associates.

**RPC-190-A:** //(Laughs)// That is because you spend most of your time all alone in your abode… gobbling up your stockpile of meat.

**RPC-190-B:** I have no "stockpile", the Desert does not have a sufficient amount of creatures to even put in one!

**RPC-190-A:** I have seen the Dragon's Graveyard, Beazere. There are plenty of scavenger-beasts to consume.

**RPC-190-B:** Well, if I even //did// have one, I would not expect you to be so upset over it. I //know// what you are attracted to, Beazere.

//(RPC-190-B reaches and pokes RPC-190-A's stomach region. RPC-190-A backs away with a look of disgust.)//

**RPC-190-A:** Don't— Don't comment on that!

//(RPC-190-A blows a cascade of fire towards RPC-190-B, who shoots a group of quills at RPC-190-A. RPC-190-B is burned, but the quills shoot through the fire and wound RPC-190-A's left eye. It clasps a claw over its eye and roars.)//

//(RPC-190-A rushes towards RPC-190-B. It scratches RPC-190-B's face with its claw, and RPC-190-B coils its body around RPC-190-A. It squeezes tighter. This makes both entities immobilized, resulting in them both colliding into the cliff face. There is a loud crunch. RPC-190-B roars.)//

//(RPC-190-B releases RPC-190-A from its constriction, and attempts to fly away from the area towards the ocean. RPC-190-A shoots a stream of fire towards RPC-190-B, and flies in the same direction. The fire envelops RPC-190-B. After a few moments the fire dissipates, and RPC-190-B is not visible.)//

**RPC-190-A:** Wha–

//(RPC-190-A reaches the boundary of the island, and ceases to exist[[footnote]] Although modern items are always blown back by strong winds when brought from the outside, the inside edges of Cair Aisling lack these winds. Instead of being blown back, entities are destroyed in a manner similar to the deterioration of matter at the Page's End.[[/footnote]].)//

**Addendum IV:** Clekta Deinwin Interview 9/7/2020

[[collapsible show="|> ACCESS FILE" hide="<| CLOSE FILE"]]

The following interview was conducted with a resident of the Hinterlands Village, an area which had been partially destroyed in the crossfire during battles between RPC-190-A and RPC-190-B.

[[div class="dragons2"]]

**Interviewer:** Researcher John Sheffield

**Interviewee:** Clekta Deinwin

----

**SHEFFIELD:** Clekta, are you ready to begin this interview?

**DEINWIN:** Yeah, yeah. Just make it quick.

**SHEFFIELD:** I'll try to.

//(Researcher Sheffield leans down and picks up a notepad from a bag on which the questions are written.)//

**SHEFFIELD:** //(Clears throat)// How familiar are you with Fernael and Beazere?

**DEINWIN:** Huh?

**SHEFFIELD:** The… two dragons.

**DEINWIN:** Oooh, right. Them.

**SHEFFIELD:** Yes. What are your experiences with them?

**DEINWIN:** //(Chuckles)// Hoo boy. Let me tell you.

//(Silence.)//

**DEINWIN:** …I don't know, which one do I start with? There's so many.

**SHEFFIELD:** Just chronologically.

**DEINWIN:** Okay, so the first time I saw them was maybe twenty or twenty-three years ago. Someone was shouting, saying that dragons were fighting in the sky. Me and some other people went outside and sure enough, those things were up in the sky above the village duking it out. Then, the light-brown dragon flew under the green one and the green one shot a big plume of fire at it. It missed, and that fire went //straight// down.

//(Clekta makes a downwards motion with her arm.)//

**DEINWIN:** Set one of the houses alight.

**SHEFFIELD:** When was the next incident?

**DEINWIN:** Uh, about a year later. They fought again, green one falls down onto the village. Boom. Three houses crushed just like that. Didn't even look like it noticed, just got up and flew away. Maybe just didn't care.

**SHEFFIELD:** Was anybody injured?

**DEINWIN:** //(Breathes in)// No.

**SHEFFIELD:** Right. Anymore incidents?

**DEINWIN:** Basically more of the same, really. Dragons fight, sometimes we're left alone, sometimes we aren't. One time the green dragon drank a whole heap of water from the lake and spat it out at the other one. We had to ask a mage to refill the lake.

**SHEFFIELD:** Erm. Has anyone been… //killed// by the dragons?

//(Silence.)//

**DEINWIN:** Well, not directly. I think about twenty people have been killed. Usually by burning.

//(Researcher Sheffield looks to a guard monitoring the interview. He mouths "should I tell her?". The guard nods, and Researcher Sheffield turns back to Clekta.)//

**SHEFFIELD:** Then I think you'll be pleased to know that the dragons died yesterday.

**DEINWIN:** Wh— What? How?

**SHEFFIELD:** They, uh, managed to… get each other killed by keeping each other underwater. Both of them drowned.

//(Clekta exhales in relief.)//

**DEINWIN:** Finally! I mean, that's the… the best thing I've heard today.

**SHEFFIELD:** I'm sure of it.

**DEINWIN:** I'm just glad they won't be bothering us anymore. //(Laughs)// Don't need to help with rebuilding houses anymore either.

**SHEFFIELD:** Well, you won't have to help when it's actually finished. Anyways, I think that does it for this interview now. Thank you for cooperating.

//(Researcher Sheffield gets up out of his seat, and heads towards the door. He stops, and turns around.)//

**SHEFFIELD:** Before I leave you, do you know why they hated each other?

**DEINWIN:** Er… not really. Most dragons are just assholes. Then again. When you can //breathe fire//, I wouldn't expect you to be very kind-hearted.
