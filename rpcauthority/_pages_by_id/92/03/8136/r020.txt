**Designation:** Storm Fortress

**Location:** Area 01A, 706 kilometers southwest off of Pitcairn Islands.

**Site Occupation:** Containment, Research, Medical and D-Class Holding Facility. 

**Established** 16^^th^^ January, 1985.

**Total Area:** 2,500 m^^2^^

**Background Information:** Site-001 is a large maximum security subaquatic facility used to contain potentially precarious anomalies with mostly Gamma and/or Omega class objects. Access is through a sham oil rig platform for helicopters and/or VTOL aircraft carrying personnel to land on. Only personnel with security clearance of Level 4 or higher and/or authorized selected personnel may be granted passage to enter the site.

Site-001 is powered by █ Uranium U-235 Pressurized Heavy-water Reactors and is defended with several on-site Bofors 57mm Anti-Aircraft guns, AIM-9 Sidewinder heatseeker missiles, AIM-120 AMRAAM active radar missiles, ███-█ "Stonehenge" █████████ with █ geostationary satellites providing GPS/INS target tracking.

Site-001's entire facility is located 10 kilometers underneath the Pacific Ocean. In the case of a containment breach, all of Site-001's sector can be flooded with seawater of enormous pressure in less than 20 seconds.

+ Offices & Wings Section
[[/=]]
-----

* **Administrative and Office Wing** - Features the main office for the Site Director and his/her assistants. Only personnel with security clearance of Level 5 or higher may enter this wing.

* **Medical Level** - Equipped with intermediate class medical equipment used only to treat on-site injuries.

* **Research and Laboratories Level** - Only features the //Anomaly Research Sector.//

* **Security Level** - Used to store several RPC object-specific recontainment/handling gear.

* **D-Class Personnel Level** - Located one level above the Containment Level.

* **Containment Level** - Separated into two sectors which include: //Aquatic Sector// and //Terran Sector,// in which each of the sectors are separated into three subsectors based on the object's classifications: //Alpha Sector, Beta Sector// and //Gamma Sector.//

AH-22 is a large subterranean/subaquatic base with a small sham town above used (mainly) for The 1st Pacific Fleet to resupply and refuel their ships. It also doubles as The Authority sailor's housing and a short-range air supply transit airport to other sites in East Russia.

In order to repair and/or maintain The Authority's flagship, AS Andromeda, a discreet dry dock can be opened just offshore off of AH-22.

AH-22 also maintains a research wing and is the main staging area for all RPC-998 recovery operations. So far, 12 wrecks have been salvaged and are on display or under study as of 2018.
