**Site #:** 001

**Location Code:** [01]

**Description:** Site-001 is the hardest and the most expensive site to maintain due to its complexity. Site-001 is a floating base located 705 km southwest off of Pitcairn Islands in the Pacific Ocean used to contain highly dangerous Gamma and Omega objects.

Access is through a sham oil rig platform for helicopters and/or VTOL aircraft carrying personnel to land on. Only personnel with security clearance of Level 4 or higher and/or authorized selected personnel may be granted passage to enter the site.

Site-001 is powered by █ Tokamak Self-sustaining D-T fusion reactors and is defended with several on-site Bofors 57mm Anti-Aircraft guns, AIM-9 Sidewinder heatseeker missiles, AIM-120 AMRAAM active radar missiles, ███-█ "Stonehenge" █████████ with geostationary satellites providing GPS/INS target tracking.

Site-001's entire facility is located 10 kilometers underneath the Pacific Ocean. As of July 2019, Site-001 holds ██ various Gamma objects. In the case of a containment breach, all of Site-001's sector can be flooded with seawater of enormous pressure in less than 20 seconds.
