**Item #:** RPC-082

**Object Class:** Beta

**Containment Protocols:** All instances of RPC-082 are to be kept in standard humanoid containment chambers located in Site 37. Under no circumstances are two or more instances of RPC-082 to come into contact with each other. Instances of RPC-082 are to be constantly monitored via security cameras, and any change in behavior are to be reported to Head Researcher Camden (see Document 082-01). At least one Authority Security agent is to be present and equipped with 160 volt electric stun rod at each containment chamber entrance at all times.

Any reports of uncontained instances of RPC-082 must be immediately investigated by Authority Security Team 'Tyrann'. All discovered instances of RPC-082-1 are to be immediately destroyed.

Currently, 18 instances of RPC-082 are held in containment.

**Description:** RPC-082 is an anomalous species mimicking the appearance of popular children's mascot Barney the Dinosaur presumably originating from ██████████ Alabama. All instances of RPC-082 vary in appearance and size, and are typically bootleg in design. Instances of RPC-082 require no form of hydration, rest, or nutrition to stay alive. Analysis of recovered samples of RPC-082 reveal that instances are organic, and the seemingly plastic exterior of RPC-082 instances is muscle tissue. Further analysis revealed that seams connecting parts of RPC-082 instances' 'skin' is held together by a species of unidentified microorganisms.

Instances of RPC-082 are shown to be highly vulnerable to electricity, and will attempt to flee from the source of aforementioned electricity. Instances of RPC-082 terminate upon being exposed to electricity with a voltage of 200 or higher.

Instances of RPC-082 are predatory, and exclusively hunt adolescent humans between the ages of 5 and 14. Instances of RPC-082 have shown to be hostile towards humans over the age of 14. Between the hours of 7:00 AM and 8:00 PM, instances of RPC-082 will begin to hunt for adolescent humans. After 8:00 PM, instances of RPC-082 will return to a 'hive', hereby referred to as RPC-082-1 and 'hibernate'. Upon capturing a human, instances of RPC-082 will strangle the victim until the victim has been incapacitated. Instances of RPC-082 have an apparent knowledge of the exact moment victims fall unconscious, and will proceed to transport them to instances of RPC-082-1. Instances of RPC-082-1 are typically quiet and dark locations, such as abandoned buildings and caves. Upon arrival to an instance of RPC-082-1, the victim will be restrained via a thick and web-like substance originating from the █████ of an RPC-082 instance. Through unknown means, victims will be impregnated with an 'egg'. After █ hours, a new instance of RPC-082 will emerge, [DATA EXPUNGED] the victim in the process. Currently, ██ RPC-082-1 instances have been destroyed.

> **Addendum Document 082-1:** Between the hours of 7:00 AM and 8:00 PM, instances of RPC-082 typically stand behind the containment chamber door in a predatory stance. Nocturnal activities involve attacking and attempting to break the containment chamber door in an attempt to breach containment. This will resume until RPC-082 resumes predatory activities.
