**Registered Phenomena Code:** 332

**Object Class:** Gamma-Purple

**Hazard Types:** Climatological, Geological, Organic, Corrosive, Electromagnetic Force, Ideological, Titanic

**Containment Protocols:** Containment of RPC-332 is focused on two objectives - researching its metabolism & biofeedback, and with aid by China's "People's Committee for the Acquisition of Anomalous Objects", minimizing damage dealt to civilization by MDW-Lucidity events, which are caused by RPC-332's anomalous behaviors & attributes. RPC-332 cannot be restrained by artificial structures due to its biology, however it is also left sedentary by this, and its active location is naturally remote. This allows for a perimeter on land to be designated surrounding RPC-332. Every 150 km along this perimeter, a monitoring station has been established, the primary of which being in northwestern Bayan Nur, Mongolia. Half of these stations are organized by the Authority, the other half by mostly the People's Committee in collaboration with the Authority, and the primary station entirely by the Committee. These monitoring stations are each the size of a small Alpha-Class storage facility, however the Bayan Nur station is approximately the size of a small Beta-Class containment facility.

[[collapsible show="Mandatory Level 2+ Clearance Protocols: "RPC-332 Monitoring Duty."" hide="Return"]]
> Each monitoring station on the perimeter of RPC-332's area is to be equipped with the best in seismographic and meteorological equipment, including [REDACTED] technology, and each station is built and to be maintained at the highest standard of resistance against seismic and meteorological damage. Class B Personnel trained in using and maintaining this equipment are to work in pairs per device[[footnote]]One diurnal shift & one nocturnal shift.[[/footnote]] and are to each have a minimum of one backup operator in reserve for emergencies and exceptional circumstances. At all times, monitoring of seismic activities for anomalous signatures within RPC-332's area is to be performed, and weather forecast instruments are to be checked for signs of storms anywhere up to 1500 km outside RPC-332's area perimeter. RPC-332's vitals are to be monitored at all times for signs or instances of increased synaptic activity. These surveillance reports are to be submitted to the Bayan Nur station every Saturday between 6 PM & 11:59 PM. If the earliest signs of a MDW-Lucidity event are detected, reports proving such are to be submitted immediately.

**Description:** RPC-332-1 is a cave fissure located in the Gobi Desert approximately 5.5 km deep and the only direct means by which to have line-of-sight of RPC-332. From an observation chamber[[footnote]] Approximately 100 m^^3^^ in volume & near the bottom of RPC-332-1. [[/footnote]], RPC-332 appears as a wall of semi-transparent calcified animal epidermis[[footnote]]Witnessed after preliminary survey & excavation was performed. Epidermis was later measured to be slightly less than a kilometer thick.[[/footnote]]. Survey study cross-referenced with People's Committee surveillance reports allowed researchers to estimate the dimensions of RPC-332. DNA samples acquired during RPC-332-1 Expedition █ revealed RPC-332's heredity.

RPC-332 is an individual subterranean megafauna residing below the Gobi Desert, approximately 966 km long from end to head, 462 km at its greatest point of lateral width, and just under 58 km at its greatest point of vertical width[[footnote]] However, RPC-332's vertical width is significantly more uniform than its lateral width, with its head estimated to only be about 273 km laterally, but vertically being 56 km. [[/footnote]]. These body dimensions make RPC-332 the largest creature on Earth[[footnote]] The previously largest being a clonal colony of //Posidonia oceanica// south of the island of Ibiza in the Mediterranean Sea, which measures in at 8 km across. [[/footnote]], and indicates that RPC-332's underside nearly breaches the bottom of the Eurasian continental plate[[footnote]]Thus meaning its epidermis is exposed to liquid magma from within Earth's lithosphere at all times.[[/footnote]].
[[include component:image-block name= http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-332/ocean%20bear%20length.png|caption= Approximation of RPC-332's length relative to the region it resides under.]]
RPC-332 is more than 90% water in body mass, confirmed by specialized instruments during RPC-332-1 Expedition ██, with most of its solid mass composing the thickness of its epidermis. Biological electricity has been & can be detected within RPC-332, the highest concentration at its eastern-most end[[footnote]] Presumed to be the head.[[/footnote]]. Because of its enormity and entrapment by solid landmass, RPC-332 is considered immobile and catatonic, with the exception of when it presents a minuscule amount of motion or increased synaptic activity during a MDW-Lucidity event.

Research has shown that haphazard penetration of RPC-332's epidermis triggers a fast-acting metabolic defense, observable as an immense vacuole that forms within RPC-332. The vacuole contains an acid possessing a pH of ██ capable of melting [REDACTED] at a rate of [REDACTED]. Once formed, the vacuole appears behind the point of penetration and presses against it. The vacuole seals & opens around the penetrative point before rapidly compressing, pushing the acid out & shooting it at high velocity. Afterwards, the vacuole membrane serves as a clot to cease any bleeding of vital fluid. If left undisturbed, the acid degrades and dissipates completely between 14 to 37 hours subsequent of ejection from RPC-332.

Another metabolic defense is observed upon haphazard attempt to access RPC-332's biological electricity, wherein a surge of biofeedback occurs within RPC-332's body to suddenly unleash a deadly electrical discharge[[footnote]]Approximately equivalent to the common lightning bolt.[[/footnote]]. 92% of these discharges are directed at the origin of interference within the RPC-332-1 observation chamber. This biofeedback has resulted in ███ casualties across all Authority expeditions into RPC-332-1, henceforth requiring RPC-332 bio-electricity experiments to have cautious oversight.
[[include component:image-block name= http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-332/Gobi-Altai%20Earthquake.jpg|caption= The Gobi-Altai Earthquake, caused by a Class 2 MDW-Lucidity event, circa 1957.]]
Over decades, technological & analogue observation alongside research of the Gobi's history revealed that RPC-332 can cause cataclysmic events, referred to as MDW-Lucidity events[[footnote]]Or "lucidity events," for brevity. [[/footnote]], circa 19██. The earliest lucidity event definitively identified in recorded history was the Gobi-Altai Earthquake of 1957[[footnote]]Which stands as a standard example of a Class 2 MDW-Lucidity event.[[/footnote]], an earthquake that scaled 8.1 in magnitude and created a fault-line as high as 8.85 m. Ascertainment of whether or not Class 2 MDW-Lucidity events have occurred prior to this is ongoing. The number of Class 1 MDW-Lucidity occurrences prior to 1957 is unknown.

After consequences of RPC-332's anomalous properties had been observed and measured adequately, classifications as of ██/██/████ were updated. To clarify: there are three Classes of MDW-Lucidity events, each being a specific event tiered upwards based upon threat level:

# Severe, dense, ionized lightning-storms.
# Earthquakes between 7 & 9 in magnitude.
# Tectonic bifurcation of East Asia and/or RPC-332's subterranean containment breach.

To conclude MDW-Lucidity briefing: lucidity event occurrence rate decreases as the tier of severity increases. To date since 1957, there have been █ dozen Class 1 MDW-Lucidity events, less than █ Class 2 MDW-Lucidity events, and there is no sign yet indicating that RPC-332 can enact a Class 3 MDW-Lucidity event. For extended description on MDW-Lucidity event research & causality, see Research Logs 332.1, 332.1B, & 332.MDW.1 through 3.

Agreement as to whether or not the Gobi Desert is an anomaly itself is pending. Classification as natural or anomalous is ambiguous, as it is an indirect consequence of RPC-332 rather than a direct consequence. However, 100% confirmation that the Gobi would not be a desert in absence of RPC-332 is also pending.
[[include component:image-block name= http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-332/echiniscus%20granulatus%20courtesy%20WordPress.jpg|caption= //Echiniscus granulatus//, a close relative of RPC-332.]]
Taxonomic reference of RPC-332's DNA match it as being part of the //Echiniscus// genus of Kingdom Animalia, making RPC-332 a sibling species to the common tardigrade. Colloquially "water bear", this animal is usually a microscopic extremophile, the previously largest species being only 1.2 mm in length. The Authority taxonomist who discovered this dubbed RPC-332's species //Echiniscus atlasi//. While this is officially recorded within Authority taxonomic records, information about RPC-332 has not and will not be released outside Authority jurisdiction in the foreseeable future.

[[collapsible show="CRL.332.MDW.A-Consolidated-Report-█/█/201█" hide="Return"]]Authored by former Lead Researcher & RPC-332-1 Expedition ██ Director Dr. █████ Huang, prior to reassignment as Lead Researcher to another Gamma-Class project after RPC-332-1 Expedition ██ successfully concluded without issue, circa 2/3/201█.

> What is critical in understanding RPC-332 is to change your outlook. For so long we have held ourselves in a box when examining it. We are mystified by it, marveling in its size, ability, and control it may have over the regional environment, nay, the world environment! Even when trying to use science to our advantage, we constantly reference it in our minds as some kind of supernatural being. Perhaps a god. And this has led to a LOT of mishaps, if you could call them that. While doing my time in that nearly literal hellhole of a cave, I came to realize that in order to gain more insight into RPC-332, I would ironically have to learn how to employ what I believe to be //Echiniscus atlasi//'s greatest trait: obstinance.
> 
> RPC-332 is NOT a god, or even a monster. It is an animal. A force of nature, as cliche as that sounds, no matter how much we otherwise would scramble to call its nature "impossible."
> 
> Why does it create storms? To bring in moisture when annual rainfall is low in the Gobi. It creates weather patterns dangerous enough to ravage villages, towns, and sometimes even cities, all so it does not dehydrate. Is it that simple? Well, after going through the data (while I still could), there was a clear pattern correlating the formation of these storms with extended drought. This also explains why the storms always drift inland towards and over the Gobi before dissipating. How does it do it? That's a bit harder to delve into, but I feel we made progress. We can say that we understand the "why", which I believe will be fundamental in figuring out how to lower the number of Class 1 MDW-Lucidity occurrences, even if previous Expedition Directors didn't think so.
> 
> Why does it create earthquakes? With this, I had to double-over my 180 degree thinking pattern. You'd think I'd walk right into my own backside, but I believe my theory works. One of the biggest saving graces of a Class 2 MDW-Lucidity event is the fact that they can be predicted faster than ordinary earthquakes. And this is because the tech-that-shall-not-be-named allows us at a distance to track the firing of RPC-332's neurons prior to that electrical information reaching another part of its body. Some may say my thinking is dangerous here, but I don't consider it a stretch to suggest RPC-332 is intentionally, on some kind of lower level of consciousness, commanding a part of its body to shift. So in other words, it twitches a bit because it's thinking; maybe trying to scratch an itch for all we know. Past researchers believed RPC-332 couldn't possibly have a true thinking brain because it is too big, and while conventionally you'd be inclined to agree, I - nay, we - have reason to believe that its size doesn't prevent thinking, but instead just makes it think //slower//. As a result, maybe we perceive a constant, unerring torpor, when in actuality, it could be wide awake.
> 
> I wouldn't stretch this much further though. Being able to think doesn't imply high levels of intelligence, just that it has the minimal requirements for advanced ganglia. We won't get to know more for probably a long while. I was only able to manage a single bio-electricity experiment with my time there, and it didn't yield much more than previous ones. This isn't a criticism, but the reason for this is the extremely cautious experiment parameters. We can't go deeper into our understanding if we play it too safe the whole time. If I were to actually criticize, I'd say the number of safe experiments performed should be increased on future ventures. I am confident we have explored the territory of this study enough to perform these procedures much more often than we presently do.
> 
> But the caution is understandable, given past expeditions. The early ones, to be exact. I'm not stupid, I know there is a mass expungement of certain RPC-332 documents taking place, so of course I did my homework before those incident reports & research logs were lost. Now I'm not going to blacklist my entire consolidatory essay by mentioning anything too extreme, so I'll keep it tongue-and-cheek. We know of RPC-332's metabolic defenses, as we observed them action. And we also know that expedition personnel have been KIA over the decades. You can put two & two together, without knowing little details. Suffice it to say, our history of experimentation with RPC-332 has been particularly costly.
> 
> I almost have a desire to do as past Expedition Leads have done and insist that a Class 3 MDW-Lucidity event is impossible. But at this point, I think that would make me inconsistent. It's not that it couldn't ever happen, but moreover seems to be something more preventable than we currently consider. Yes, that is largely conjecture and intuition. But despite the accursed adventure that is a 332-1 spelunking, despite the disturbing history behind merely examining this animal up close... I am somehow left feeling optimistic.
> 
> So, if I were to begin a conclusion to this report, I'd start by saying that our B Class working at RPC-332's play-pen are doing god's work. So much about //Echiniscus atlasi// can be explained due to the endless hours of work they put into cataloging every little pinprick shift in their equipment's sensors. And if I were to end a conclusion to this report, I would like to pose a query: Of all the ordinary animals in the animal kingdom, if any of them were to somehow end up so bizarrely adapted as this, was any researcher **actually** surprised that it was a blasted tardigrade?
