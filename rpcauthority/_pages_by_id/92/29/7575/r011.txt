**Item #:** RPC-140

**Object Class:** Gamma

**Containment Protocols:** OL-Site-140 is to be the center for study of RPC-140. If RPC-140 phenomenons appear inside Cambodia, MST Uniform-40 ("Walking Napalm") is to be deployed with the intention of incinerating as much of RPC-140-1 as possible. Affected settlements must be quarantined and Extergito Glyphs are to be used on surviving citizens. In the case of these protocols failings, Level-5 personnel are to enable Protocol-[REDACTED][[footnote]] Instructions in the Protocol include Mass Napalm Bombing, Earth Scorching policies, [DATA REDACTED]. [[/footnote]].

**Description:** RPC-140 is a phenomenon affecting locations in Cambodia with a high density of vegetation. RPC-140 seems to appear more frequently when a certain quantity of human corpses are present and buried in the soil[[footnote]] RPC-140 has sometimes reappeared in the same location used before. [[/footnote]]. RPC-140 causes vegetation to grow faster, and develop nomadic capabilities[[footnote]] These capabilities include moving roots capable of dragging the trunk, rapid expansion of itself, etc. [[/footnote]]. Affected plants are hereby called RPC-140-1. When RPC-140-1 reaches a human corpse[[footnote]] Non-human corpses are ignored by RPC-140-1. [[/footnote]], it will twist its body around it, until the corpse is bound to RPC-140-1. Corpses after this stage are hereby called RPC-140-2.

RPC-140-2 regain their sentience, but do not regain their sapience. RPC-140-2 works as a visual and thinking organ for RPC-140-1, as it directs RPC-140-1 which direction to take, and detects anything around RPC-140-1. RPC-140-2 becomes increasingly aggressive and active when more RPC-140-2 are added to RPC-140-1, in some unknown way, this accelerates RPC-140-1's growth. RPC-140-1 are hostile to any living being near it. When in an encounter with a living being, RPC-140-2 will react with growls and screeches, and RPC-140-1 will proceed to attack. RPC-140-2 always directs RPC-140-1 to the nearest human settlement.

**Discovery:** RPC-140 strains were first located in █/██/1997 in The ███-███████ Mass Grave Memorial in ██████████, Cambodia. Civilians reported the vegetation in the ditch of the mass grave rapidly expanding, and attacking people near it. One guard, Soth S. ███, survived the outbreak, detailing the functions of RPC-140 and aiding the Authority in containment.
