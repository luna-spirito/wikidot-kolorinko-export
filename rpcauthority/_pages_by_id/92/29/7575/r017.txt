**Item #:** RPC-140

**Object Class:** Gamma

**Containment Protocols:** OL-Site-140 is to be the center for study of RPC-140. If RPC-140 phenomenons appear inside Cambodia, MST Uniform-40 ("Walking Napalm") is to be deployed with the intention of incinerating as much of RPC-140-1 as possible. Affected settlements must be quarantined and Extergito Glyphs are to be used on surviving citizens. In the case of these protocols failings, Level-5 personnel are to enable Protocol-[REDACTED][[footnote]] Instructions in the Protocol include Mass Napalm Bombing, Earth Scorching policies, [DATA REDACTED]. [[/footnote]].

**Description:** RPC-140 is a phenomenon affecting locations in Cambodia with a high density of vegetation. RPC-140 seems to appear more frequently when a certain quantity of human corpses are present and buried in the soil[[footnote]] RPC-140 has somtimes reappeared in locations where it has been known to have previously manifested. [[/footnote]]. RPC-140 causes vegetation to grow faster, and develop nomadic capabilities[[footnote]] These capabilities include moving roots capable of dragging the trunk, rapid expansion of itself, etc. [[/footnote]]. Affected plants are hereby called RPC-140-1. When RPC-140-1 reaches a human corpse[[footnote]] Non-human corpses are ignored by RPC-140-1. [[/footnote]], it will twist its body around it, until the corpse is bound to RPC-140-1. Corpses after this stage are referred to as RPC-140-2.

RPC-140-2 regain their sentience, but do not regain their sapience. RPC-140-2 works as a visual and thinking organ for RPC-140-1, helping guide RPC-140-1 and assisting RPC-140-1 in discerning what is located in its immediate environment. RPC-140-2 becomes increasingly aggressive and active when more RPC-140-2 are added to RPC-140-1. RPC-140-2 also accelerates the growth of RPC-140-1 instances by currently unknown means. All instances of RPC-140-1 are extremely hostile towards any animals in their vicinity. Upon detecting any nearby animals, RPC-140-2 will react with congested vocalizations that will alert its RPC-140-1 instance to the organism, causing it to attack. RPC-140-2 always directs RPC-140-1 to the nearest human settlement.

**Discovery:** RPC-140 strains were first located in █/██/1997 in The ███-███████ Mass Grave Memorial in ██████████, Cambodia. Civilians reported the vegetation in the ditch of the mass grave rapidly expanding, and attacking people near it. One guard, Soth S. ███, survived the outbreak, detailing the functions of RPC-140 and aiding the Authority in containment.

[[collapsible show="+ 140 Interview Log 001 - ██:██ to ██:██ " hide="- [ACCESS GRANTED]"]]

> **Interviewer:** {{Head Researcher Jozef ████}}
> 
> **Interviewed:** {{Soth ███}}
> 
> **Foreword:** {{[See Discovery.]}}
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Dr. Jozef:** Hello, Mr. ███. Should I call you by first name or?
> 
> **Soth ███:** If you want to.
> 
> **Dr. Jozef:** Alright, well. Now Soth, you are currently the only one we could pull out from that anomaly, can you tell us the things that were out there?
> 
> **Soth ███:** Honestly, I don't know how I managed to live. The trees were breaking the building I got in, and the grass, how it was slipping through the wall-cracks, that'll will haunt me. I only got to see one face in the horror, and it wasn't even human.
> 
> **Dr. Jozef:** In what way was that face not human?
> 
> **Soth ███:** It was rotten and putrid. It looked hungry, but also bloodthirsty. It looked at me, and I didn't have enough time to aim and shoot at that face, I was staggered.
> 
> **Dr. Jozef:** Anything else you want to describe about that face?
> 
> **Soth ███:** I was running when the jungle began spreading, I saw huge roots coming out of the ditch, all carrying the dead bodies. I just... [Soth ███ makes a gesture indicating something huge dug up from the ditch.] There were so many corpses, how did any of them still have flesh on their bones?
> 
> **Dr. Jozef:** It must be something ugly to see, isn't it? Well Soth, tell me how this situation ends. 
> 
> **Soth ███:** When I heard the planes you people had, I started hugging the wall closest to me. My father told me about the Napalm bombings when the Sahchip Indauchen[[footnote]] Khmer term for the Indochinese People's Republic. [[/footnote]] was around, how much it took away from the land, and I was going to get tangled in what I knew was going to be yet another type of Hell.
> 
> **Dr. Jozef:** Sad thing to see, that war was damaging to everyone that was there. Our helicopters reported you chasing this plant being that attacked the place, why was that happening?
> 
> **Soth ███:** I needed to run, I didn't want to be killed by accident, so I just decided to chase the face that saw me. I wanted to show I was with them, it's what my father told me to do if another war happens again in here. I saw it again, I knew that was the face, the image was burned into my mind. The whole body dragged around by roots that could impale a cow, I shot it this time. But it returned to the tall grass, into the mud. I don't know where the roots went, but they must be out there.
> 
> **Dr. Jozef:** That's quite a lot of info.Thank you for the information, Soth, your bravery is commendable. However, I believe we are out of time.
> 
> **Soth ███:** It felt to get that all off my chest, doctor. Where do I get my badge? The exit?
> 
> **Dr. Jozef:** What badge?
> 
> **Soth ███:** When your people brought me in, they said I did a pretty good job out there. That I needed a badge, maybe.
> 
> **Dr. Jozef:** They were joking, I'm pretty sure, but they aren't wrong. Once again, I would like to thank you for your time.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** Due to Soth ███'s skills, Researcher Jozef ████ asked permission from the Protection Department if Mr. ███ should be promoted into an ASF Unit. Soth ███ was appointed as a guard in OL Site-███ months later after the approval of various members of the Protection Wing.
