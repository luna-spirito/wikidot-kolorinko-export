**Item #:** RPC-301

[[f<image http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-format/beta]]

**Object Class:** Beta
 
[[include component:image-block name= Image_URL_Location|caption= Infrared imaging of RPC-301.]]


**Containment Protocols:**  At this time, remote-controlled auditory bait stations within Site-15 have been placed to contain the anomaly in a controlled outdoor environment. Audio-seal exterior containment walls around RPC-301 within a 1km perimeter to prevent external stimuli responses of sound. Research personnel are required to wear noise-cancelling headphones to protect themselves from the containment process should they enter the area. Backup generators are put in place in the event of a power-outage to ensure RPC-301's containment. These stations include speakers that must keep a constant level of 200 dbs every hour for RPC-301 to gravitate between each speaker during their activation intervals. Should RPC-301 ever breach containment, Site-15 are to launch a fighter jet bearing a speaker, broadcasting at 200+ dbs to draw RPC-301 away from any populated regions.

**Description:** RPC-301 is classified as a terrestrial-level distortion of space-time. The object, while not entirely visible to naked eye, can be seen with infrared vision devices or by the distortions in the atmosphere in one's field of vision. RPC-X is capable of absorbing all frequencies of sound within a 500m radius. Though it is possible it can respond to percieved sources of sound up to 10 to 15 miles as evident in the recovery operation. [[footnote]]See Interview logs for more information.[[/footnote]]

RPC-301 will usually linger within the affected region for a total of five minutes and thirty-seven seconds if there are no other external stimuli available to attract RPC-301. Within this time frame, RPC-301 will leave behind an anomalous residue carrying traces of hawking radiation, that will distort space-time within the region and individuals affected by RPC-301. The molecules within those affected are unable to vibrate, and thus cannot produce any auditory feedback. This same phenomenon extends towards individuals directly affected by RPC-301. Even when outside the affected regions, the afflicted will be unable to perceive any sound due to the bones in the middle ear failing to transfer the vibrations. Those not affected by RPC-301 will still find themselves incapable of perceiving and producing sound within the regions, but the residual effect will not carry over onto them should they leave.

RPC-301 was recovered in [REDACTED], Arizona in 2009, in which a small town suffered from a localized societal breakdown five days after what is believed to be RPC-301 occupied the region. Casualties were estimated to be around 52. TRT-21-"Sleeping Sirens" were tasked to assess the situation and contain the anomaly. Upon their arrival, RPC-301 approached the TRT at ██mph, prompting them to open fire on contact. Audio communications with TRT-21 were lost. However, visual reports showed the squad were still active during the events.

Five minutes after initial contact, RPC-301 left the premises, heading north. RPC-301 was then successfully contained 4.5 hours following the initial incident. The anomaly was observed to gravitate towards high levels of sound when a geyser erupted approximately 10.2 miles from its initial location.

TRT-21 and the remaining population of the town were brought in by the Authority for examination. Their ability to communicate vocally and perceive sounds were removed and, but interviews were still conducted through instant messaging. The effects of the residue are irreversible at this point and time.

> **Date:** 1/25/17 11:50 AM. 2.5 hours after initial encounter.
> 
> **Foreword:** ██████ is a survivor from the town involved in Incident-301-1 during the initial encounter with RPC-301. The survivor cannot speak nor emit any auditory responses from his person. The room  has been installed with computers with direct instant messaging applications to allow a steady flow of conversation. Dr. Apth has been called from Site-11 to interview ██████.
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** Greetings ██████, I am Dr. Apth. I am here to help you in anyway I can. Given the events that had transpired this morning, I need you to try and describe everything that occurred prior to the entity attacking.
> 
> **██████:** //Shows signs of visual distress and anxiety before taking the moment to type on the screen.// I don't know what to tell you doc. The way was quite normal  for us. Nothing really happened outside a car accident on 5th and Weston. 
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** When did the entity show up exactly?
> 
> **██████:** Shortly after the accident occurred. I want to say five... maybe ten minutes? I'm not sure. It came out of nowhere, like from above or something. There wasn't anything to see though but, there were like, ripples in the air. Large, giant ripples like solid tar exhuming heat from the making mid-summer sun. We just found out that I couldn't hear my own voice, or anyone else's. I was not the only one it seemed. There just... was silence. This eerie creepy silence that hovered over us. The only thing we DID hear though is this deep, low-tone rumbling ringing sound from that thing.
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** How long did the anomaly stay?
> 
> **██████:** For about... four... five days now. Then it sped towards the edge of town.
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** How have things been during those five days?
> 
> **██████:** //Shows signs of anger as he hesitates for a moment before typing.// Everything sucks now.
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** I'm sure that must be an exaggeration. The good news is you're alive. That's what counts the most I believe.
> 
> **██████:** //Visibly shows distress and anger at Dr. Apth, slamming on the keyboard. For a moment,  ██████ calms down and begins to type aggressively.// hjnm, ugkyuy Not bad!? Doctor, do you even know what we had to live like for five days? My daughter is part of the Church choir! I played the guitar! Me and my wife visit the local cafe every morning before we head off to work! You don't know what its like. She and her friends will never hang out or speak together ever again. I'll never hear her sing again. I can't play my guitar anymore and I'll never hear my wife say 'I love you' again! We all sit there, angry, depressed, losing our minds because there is nothing there now. There is nothing to hear anymore. Nothing but the dead silence. You can't hear the break of wind crashing at your ears anymore, nor can you hear the sound of your neighbors talking. Complete and utter silence. It's not a natural silence. Its like... its like what my uncle described during his accident on the ISS. He drifted around orbit for hours. He said how the silence felt so cold, cold and dead. There was nothing, a great big empty void where there is no sound at all. It almost drove him mad. I only now understood what that silence felt like to him. So don't you DARE fucking tell me I am exaggerating. 
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** I apologize for that. It is just, we've never had any sort of psychological damage involving hearing loss or anything of this caliber.
> 
> **██████:** I know what deaf people go through. Most are born with that problem, and others have an accident one point in their life. But imagine having all that suddenly taken away from you... and there is nothing. Absolutely nothing. The silence is damning. The ones who died? They couldn't take the silence. We tried to do the best we could, find a solution. Nothing worked. The cops couldn't do anything about it. They've no voice to show their authority. The township couldn't reach out to anyone across the continent. No one could hear them. People were panicking. The silence was getting to them. There were suicides, accidents, even fights. People were looting the stores. Many took advantage of what was happening. But you know what? They were doing it because they couldn't handle the silence. Those who killed themselves couldn't cope with it. The eerie dead quietness... the lack of social contact could push young folks like that to the brink. The others? They found it to be an alternative to stop themselves from going mad. We all miss the noise doc. We miss the voices, the wind, the music, our families. We try to imagine our own noises in our head, hear things we wish we could hear but are not already there. We even begin to forget what names we call ourselves because you are so used to using and hearing your own voice and the voices of others. Can you imagine what that is like?
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** I think I do now. Thank you for your time  ██████. Dr. Chaplin will escort you to your quarters now.
> 
> **<End Log.>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:**  ██████'s interview shows there to be damaging psychological and emotional effects for the afflicted. Based upon psychological maturity, age, social interactivity, there is a minimum chance for the afflicted to adjust and adapt without long-term complications and dysfunctions.

> **Foreword:** Dr. Apth interviews TRT captain Markov of "Sleeping Sirens" over the incident with RPC-301's initial recovery. Dr. Apth and Captain Markov took their respective seats as they began to type.
>
> **Dr. Apth:** I want you to explain to me your encounter with RPC-301.
> 
> **Captain. Markov:** About as much as you can expect from the reports. We touched down by VTOL on the outskirts of town. Barlow took half the squad and had him move through the town to track and draw the entity away. We did not expect anything at first until it came barreling our way. Barlow tried to warn us. Based on the anomaly's behavior, I can only assume it was attracted to the propellers. We never saw it at first. But we knew we were already under the effects of RPC-301. We switched to our infrared vision once we found unusual distortions in the air. It... well, I can't say it looked exactly like a black hole because it was emitting flares. Outside of the complete and utter silence, we heard ringing.
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** You said you heard... ringing?
> 
> **Captain Markov:** Yes.
> 
> **Dr. Apth:**  ██████ reported the same thing.
> 
> **Captain Markov:** Yeah. I was not sure about what that either. We opened fire on the object. We thought it was hostile and it was attacking, so we reacted without thinking. Broke protocol momentarily.
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** The reports said that RPC-301 remained in position for about several minutes until it sped away. 
> 
> **Captain Markov:** Yeah, and it stopped at a geyser about ten miles from the town. From what I understand, the geyser had a very violent eruption. It must had been noisy enough to catch the entity's attention. 
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** Yeah. You tracked the anomaly towards that location. How were you able to report this back to the site?
> 
> **Captain Markov:** Sign language. Barlow was proficient in it and one of the operators knew enough to interpret the information we were signing to come up with an effective containment strategy for the anomaly.
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** Must have worked out well in your favor then.
> 
> **Captain Markov:** Yeah. But my squad nearly fell apart. It took Barlow and I a good ten solid minutes to keep their heads on straight. We were fine once we got back to base though. But we're sure as hell going to miss our voices. 
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** Anything else you'd like to add before we conclude this session?
> 
> **Captain Markov:** Can't say it is all too bad though. RPC-301 did give us a leverage.
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** What do you mean by thaT?
> 
> **Captain Markov:** //Stands up and begins to maneuver around the room. The operative moved to be in full view for Dr. Apth as he stamps his foot hard on the floor with each step he took. Then he gestures his hand downward to indicate the lack of sound from his steps.//
> 
> **<End Log.>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** TRT-21 has been refurbished into a tactical espionage response team given the light of recent developments. Research into RPC-301 is being investigated, primarily focused on what the ringing sensation described by TRT-21 is.

[[collapsible show="Interview Log-301-3." hide="Close"]]
> **Foreword:** //Additional information of the events have been provided by Second in Command, Barlow after observing the effects of the town's collapse.//
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** When I spoke with Captain Markov, he mentioned how your squad nearly fell apart due to the lack of communication. What would you describe the psychological state of your teammates?
> 
> **TRT-Barlow:** Shit was fucked. No one knew what anyone was saying. No one could hear anyone or anything. Mouths were moving but no one could hear anyone say anything, like forcing the deaf and mute to speak in a blank white room about which jenga piece to pull out.
> 
> **Dr. Apth:**  An unusual analogy.
> 
> **TRT-Barlow:** The hostile entity continued to remain immobile in the air. There were no indication of the entity being sentient or sapient. But our real concern was what the effects had done to the town. It was a sheer stroke of luck that the anomaly remained in the area for as long as it did before we arrived, and then the geyser eruption.
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** Give me the full details of the effects.
> 
> **TRT-Barlow:** Despite the physical presence of other individuals, the affected populace showed complete communicative isolation from the rest of their relatives and peers. I estimated  the town populace had about two days in of rationality before folks began to feel the effects. By the time we arrived, the town had already suffered from a localized societal breakdown. Cops lost control of everything. There was no local government anymore. The township, the mayor, all the big-heads could not get in touch with anyone for help and they couldn't establish order. We saw looting, officers failing to diffuse fights and getting assaulted themselves. Everyone was trying to call out to someone for help and none would come. RPC-301 has proven to be more of a threat just by its presence alone. We're not looking at the long term mental and cultural effects doc. The creepy eerie silence does more than make people go nuts. With no effective means of contact or communication, how can you expect to keep everyone calm and together? Imagine if this hits a major population center. What do you think is going to happen to the infrastructure of our bureaucracies or our governments?
> 
> **Dr. Apth:** I think I am starting to see the full picture now. Thank you Barlow. You are dismissed.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statements:** RPC-X poses an extreme danger to society if the object ever breaches containment. If the object ever reaches a major population center, the resulting  panic could result in a full-scale Societal Collapse Scenario. Any areas suspected to be affected by RPC-X are to be quarantined and inspected in an effort to counteract it's anomalous effects.
