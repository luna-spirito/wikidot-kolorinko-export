**Registered Phenomena Code:** 650

**Object Class:** Beta-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Aggression Hazard, Animated Hazard, Sapient Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-650 is to be contained in a standard 7 meter x 7 meter humanoid containment chamber along with a tempered glass observation window at [http://www.rpc-wiki.com/sites:site-002-02 Site-002]. No personnel are allowed access to RPC-650 at any time unless authorized by a Level 3 person.

Personnel assigned for handling RPC-650 must pass with a score of at least an 80% on the Personnel Panic Test.[[footnote]]A test typically given to MST personnel to train units to remain calm when encountering dangerous RPCs.[[/footnote]]

**Description:** RPC-650 is a scarecrow, fashioned to appear as a human skeleton. The lower body from the hips are missing, with the torso, arms and head remaining. RPC-650 is clothed with a hat, hood and brown cloth covering the torso and arms.

RPC-650 is animate, sentient and hostile, however, it has been reported to remain inanimate for periods of time[[footnote]]The purpose of this behavior is unknown, but it is theorized that it may be either a resting state or a method to invoke uneasiness/fear in the viewer.[[/footnote]], even in the presence of fearful individuals. It is known to attack by lacerating and stabbing mainly at the chest, with occasional wounds upon the neck and face.

RPC-650 seems to be fueled by any sort of human fear. Individuals who do not demonstrate any fear of RPC-650 may render it innocuous. However, people who are fearful of it, but do not show any clear signs of fear are still however vulnerable as RPC-650 seems to be omniscient of their mental response, and may or may not physically assault the victim.

**Origin:** RPC-650 was recovered from a wooded area in the suburban neighborhood of █████████, in ████, Iowa after 11 accounts of murder with each victim assaulted within 1 hour, each suffering deep laceration and stab wounds.

The unusual quick activity of the culprit along with victims claiming to be attacked by a "scarecrow" had brought the attention of the Authority. Task Force AIR was deployed to the neighborhood and had begun the search for RPC-650. Upon discovery, RPC-650 was taken into temporary containment before being located to Site-002. A replica of RPC-650 was set up within the neighborhood.

The public was informed of a serial killer that would often bring a scarecrow while attacking citizens.

**Addendum 1:** Multiple interviews of former residents of the neighborhood were conducted with two people providing important leads.

**Interview Log 650-3**

> **Interviewed:** Jacob ████, former resident of █████████, 46 years of age.
> 
> **Interviewer:** Agent Christopher
> ----
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Agent Christopher:** So, tell me everything you remember. Anything.
> 
> **Jacob:** Okay... It was about 3:30. The kids just got home from school. I let my son go outside to play with his friends. They would always play on the other side of the crop field. About 15 minutes later, my son came back home to get some water. He started to tell me about this little myth.
> 
> **Agent Christopher:** Myth? Can you describe it? How old is your son?
> 
> **Jacob:** He was about 6. He said that there was a scarecrow in the crop field, and that it would chase you if you looked at it. He said that the only way to get away from it was to think of something that you like.
> 
> **Agent Christopher:** Did your son ever enter the crop field?
> 
> **Jacob:** I think so. One day, when he went out to play with his friends, he came running home crying about 5 minutes after he left. He was telling me about how he saw the scarecrow and that it chased him. I told him that the scarecrow isn't real and that it was probably some other kids trying to scare him.
> 
> **Agent Christopher:** Was he harmed?
> 
> **Jacob:** No, he was fine. No scrapes, bruises or cuts.
> 
> **Agent Christopher:** Did you ever enter the field?
> 
> **Jacob:** No, but I wanted to prove that it wasn't real. I left him home with my wife to walk to the field. It was about a 5 minute walk. I looked around but I didn't enter the field since the farmers did not like anyone interfering with the crops. The scarecrow wasn't there, so I started walking back home. While walking, I saw the same scarecrow standing on the sidewalk. At first, I was creeped out, but I thought it was just some teenagers playing a prank.
> 
> **Agent Christopher:** Thank you for your cooperation. You may leave.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** Jacob ████ was then taken into Authority custody and amnesticized and then released into public.

----
**Interview Log 650-4**

> **Interviewed:** Jonah ████, son of Jacob ████, 16 years of age.
> 
> **Interviewer:** Agent Christopher
> ----
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Agent Christopher:** Hello. I just want to ask you some questions. You're not in trouble. Please describe the myth of the scarecrow.
> 
> **Jonah:** Oh that old thing? //laughter// Okay.
> 
> **Jonah:** The scarecrow was said to be in the farmers field. It creeped out a lot of kids. Some kids who were older than me, probably 14 or 15 at the time said that it was a dead body and that a ghost possessed it. They said that when you enter that field, the scarecrow can get you. The only way to get away was not to be afraid of it. You're not supposed to think of hitting it because that would tell it that you're afraid. You're not supposed to run either. You just have to think of something you like, like a friend or anything, and then you just walk out of the field.
> 
> **Agent Christopher:** Did you enter the field?
> 
> **Jonah:** Yeah, they said that I would have to go into the field or I couldn't be their friend anymore. I was like, 6, so I thought they were my friends. I went in, I saw the scarecrow, and I could've pissed myself right there. It actually started to chase me for real. I immediately started running as fast as I could. While I was running, some kids were laughing.
> 
> **Agent Christopher:** Thank you for cooperating. You may leave. Remember, you're not in trouble.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** Jonah ████ was then taken into Authority custody and amnesticized and released into public.

**Addendum 2:** Multiple tests to invoke potential sapience with RPC-650 have concluded RPC-650 is capable of verbal communication, especially after Incident 650-1 when it had commented on it's containment, with CCTV footage confirming this event.

**Interview Log 650-5**

> **Interviewed:** RPC-650
> 
> **Interviewer:** Doctor █████
> 
> **Foreword:** The interview was conducted within RPC-650's containment chamber, Doctor █████ remaining behind the glass.
> 
> ----
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Doctor █████:** Hello, 650. We-
> 
> **RPC-650:** That is not my name. Do not call me 650.
> 
> **Doctor █████:** I am required to refer to you by your designation.
> 
> **RPC-650:** You WILL call me by ███████.
> 
> **Doctor █████:** If I do, will you cooperate with this interview?
> 
> **RPC-650:** Yes.
> 
> **Doctor █████:** Okay, ███████. We would just like to give you some questions for you to answer. 
> 
> **RPC-650:** Go ahead.
> 
> **Doctor █████:** We'll start off simple. Why do you harm others? Do you generally dislike humans?
> 
> **RPC-650:** It mainly started off with a few kids. 13, or 15. The bastards would always, always come into my field, and stomp on the crops. I would always chase them out, me yelling, them laughing and running. I would sometimes come out with a pitchfork, just to scare them.
> 
> **Doctor █████:** Alright, did you do anything else to stop this?
> 
> **RPC-650:** Of course, I would tell their parents to keep them inside and tell them not to enter my field. Of course, the bastards would just sneak out of their house, and run into my field, sometimes even burning the crops, risking a mass fire, the bastards were completely clueless as to what would happen.
> 
> **Doctor █████:** Did they ever hurt you physically?
> 
> **RPC-650:** Oh, no. Never, so long as I brought a pitchfork. Of course, that would lead to another. They began saying I was some sort of psycho killer, and that the scarecrow in the field was a dead child. The delinquents could've ruined my reputation.
> 
> **Doctor █████:** Did you ever get in trouble?
> 
> **RPC-650:** //laughter// Yes, whenever I bring a pitchfork, they would run home, yelling. First thinking they were smart and cool, next, they're running like a bunch of cowards.
> 
> **Doctor █████:** Did you ever.. hurt them?
> 
> **RPC-650:** No, I couldn't. I would be removed from the field, my life would be ruined.
> 
> **Doctor █████:** The scarecrow in the field, what was your relation with it?
> 
> **RPC-650:** I set it up just to scare some of the kids off. The myth of it being a dead body may have worked, just for the children. Their parents never believed it was a body, which it wasn't.
> 
> **RPC-650:** Soon... I died off. I was just a ghost, just to wander. But then it hit me, I remembered that scarecrow...
> 
> **<End Log>**
