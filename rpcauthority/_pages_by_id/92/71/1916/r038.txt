**Registered Phenomena Code:** 899

**Object Class:** Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** Meta-Physical Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** Due to the nature of RPC-899, active containment is considered detrimental to the core imperatives of the Authority. Efforts should instead be focused towards ensuring the continued maintenance of RPC-899 into perpetuity, or until such a time that Authority research replicates the effects of RPC-899.

All staff given level-3 or above security clearance are to be made aware of RPC-899 and understand the role of level-4 and above personnel in identification of RPC-899-2 instances, transfer of instances to, and maintenance of RPC-899-1.

A team of no more than 15 senior researchers are to be personally selected and assigned to RPC-899 by Site-42’s director and are to remain as the sole on-site permanent staff.

**Description:** RPC-899 is a class-10 reality altering phenomenon localized within the boundaries of Authority Site-42, designated RPC-899-1. It is not yet understood how or when RPC-899 manifested, but research into primary memetic, technological, and gestalt triggers is ongoing and considered a significant priority. The phenomenon is characterised by the complete neutralisation of all anomalous properties held by certain objects and entities inside of its sphere of influence[[footnote]]Currently confined to the outer walls of Site-42’s main structure.[[/footnote]] this effect is limited to a subset of anomalous items and entities collectively designated RPC-899-2.

While no concrete shared history or function between instances of RPC-899-2 has been identified, it is generally understood that patterns seem apparent within RPC-899-2 instances that allow for their identification within the Authority’s RPC collection. These include, but are not limited to; a generally understood anomalous function[[footnote]]Defined as an anomalous effect understood to work in a consistent way, with a vector understood to the best of the Authority’s ability.[[/footnote]], an ability to be reliably or feasibly transported to Site-42, and a lack of major significance to the Authority as a whole[[footnote]]Defined as an object or entity with a lack of prospect for long term research, or negligible value in active containment.[[/footnote]].

Once an RPC-899-2 instance enters RPC-899-1 and becomes subject to the phenomenon’s sphere of influence, all anomalous effects are completely neutralized until such a time as the instance exits the phenomenon. As such, the Authority is committed to actively utilizing RPC-899 to contain identified RPC-899-2 instances and has repurposed Site-42 for this directive.

**Registered Phenomena Code:** 899

**Object Class:** Beta-White

**Hazard Types:** Grouped Hazard, Meta-Physical Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** Due to the nature of RPC-899, active containment is considered detrimental to the core imperatives of the Authority. Efforts should instead be focused towards ensuring the continued function of RPC-899 into perpetuity, or until such a time that Authority research renders RPC-899 uneccesary.

Smokescreen Protocol 42-022 is to remain in effect for the foreseeable future, limiting full knowledge of RPC-899 to staff with security clearance below level-4. All staff given level-4 security clearance are to be immediately made aware of RPC-899 and tasked with identifying RPC objects under their administration as possible candidates for integration into RPC-899.

A team of no more than 15 senior researchers are to be personally selected and assigned to RPC-899 by Site-42’s director and are to remain as the sole on-site permanent staff. A team of exactly 90 Authority researchers are to be tasked with cataloging the construct’s visible components and identifying any that have been damaged or rendered inert through the continued function of RPC-899. All non-permanent personnel are to be cycled out bi-monthly, with class-B amnestics and standard record destruction applied upon cessation of their service on Site-42.

**Description:** RPC-899 is an anomalous construct centralised within Site-42, and extending approximately ███km along the Atlantic seaboard of the United States. Ostensibly built by the Authority[[footnote]]The most likely option, given the construct being centralised within an otherwise standard Authority facility.[[/footnote]] the construct currently contains approximately 1453 anomalous objects and entities linked together by a complex variety of memetic, technological, gestalt and thaumaturgical methods, many of which are currently either too poorly understood to replicate or require materials for construction known only from their presence within RPC-899. No full schematic of RPC-899 exists, and much information regarding its exact functionality has been lost due to the significant passage of time between construction and re-acquisition by the Authority.

While the limited information regarding RPC-899, collected during the initial reclamation of Site-42, holds no explicit clues to the exact effects of the construct, several possible functions are speculated, including but not limited to; memetic properties[[footnote]]Assumed from the lack of records or knowledge of both Site-42 and RPC-899 prior to reacquisition.[[/footnote]], the ability to cause significant alterations to baseline reality[[footnote]]Assumed from references to cooperation with Groups of Interest that do not and have never, to the Authority’s best understanding, existed. Examples include the “██████ ██████ █████████”, “██████ ██ ███ ██████ ███”, and “███████’s ████”.[[/footnote]], as well as some form of temporal displacement[[footnote]]Speculated due to Site-42 being reacquired ███ years before widespread Authority adoption of several non-anomalous technological components involved the site’s construction[[/footnote]]. The continued activity within the components of RPC-899 imply that the construct is currently active and asserting it's effects.

Despite lack of clarity on the function of RPC-899, surviving texts from Site-42 collectively cite the construct’s purpose as [DATA EXPUNGED]. This is considered a direct extension of the Authority’s central tenets, and by extension the continued function of RPC-899 is a concern of the highest priority.

**Addendum RPC-899-A:** Since re-acquisition of Site-42 and RPC-899 by Authority interests on ██/██/17██, approximately 261 component pieces have become inert or otherwise nonfunctional. In turn, 3██ anomalous objects and entities have been requisitioned from Authority inventories and successfully integrated into the main structure of RPC-899 as replacements.

**Registered Phenomena Code:** 899

**Object Class:** Omega-Black

**Hazard Types:** Reality Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** Due to the nature of RPC-899, active containment is considered impossible without irreparable damage to the core imperatives of the Authority. Efforts should instead be focused towards obscuring knowledge of the construct within Authority ranks for as long as feasibly possible.

Smokescreen Protocol 42-001 is to remain in effect indefinitely, limiting full knowledge of RPC-899 to staff with security clearance of level-5. All staff given level-5 security clearance are to be immediately made aware of the true nature of RPC-899.

Containment beyond this is irrelevant.

**Description:** RPC-899 is an anomalous construct centralised within Site-42, extending approximately 35 km directly downwards and 2971 km along the Atlantic seaboard of the United States. The construct currently contains approximately 1453 anomalous objects and entities linked together by a complex variety of memetic, technological, gestalt and thaumaturgical methods, all of which are currently either beyond the scope of the Authority’s understanding and thus irreplicable, or require construction materials that are completely foreign to baseline reality. No full schematic of RPC-899 exists, and much information regarding its exact functionality has been lost due to the significant passage of time between activation and acquisition by the Authority.

While the limited information regarding RPC-899, collected during initial Authority acquisition of Site-42, hold few explicit clues to the exact effects of the construct, surviving texts from Site-42 collectively cite the construct’s purpose as initiating and maintaining a “CKK-Class Reality Shift Scenario[[footnote]]Defined in-text as a total restructuring and condensation of multiversal reality[[/footnote]]”, to the best of the Authority’s current understanding, the construct has been activated for this purpose, and will continue to maintain this effect as long as it remains functional.

Texts recovered from Site-42 detail an incomplete history of RPC-899, claiming it’s construction to be a cooperative effort on the part of several dozen groups not present in current baseline reality[[footnote]]See document 33-X-21 for a complete list of referenced groups.[[/footnote]] working under a compartmentalized range of half-truths and under partially fabricated situations, without full understanding of individual contribution’s purposes or the overall goal. A single rogue collective, presumed to be the progenitor of the Authority in current baseline reality, is listed as being responsible for the design, complete construction, and activation of both RPC-899 and Site-42. Significant differences in central ideological tenets are assumed, given the nature of activating RPC-899 being fundamentally incompatible with the Authority's current interpretation of its ideological goals. For the sole surviving documentation from this collective not related to the technical aspects of  RPC-899, see document 001-42.

Since reaquistion of Site-42 and RPC-899 by Authority interests on 12/04/1733, approximately 261 component pieces have become inert or otherwise nonfunctional. In turn, 383 anomalous objects and entities have been requisitioned from Authority inventories and attempted integration into the main structure of RPC-899 as replacements. No replacement components have thus far successfully integrated into the construct, and repair is assumed to be impossible under current Authority understanding.

Due to the poorly understood nature of RPC-899, estimation of a timespan for the cessation of it's function is considered impossible, but inevitable.
