**Site #:** 002

**Location Code:** [02]

**Description:** Site-002 is the largest subterranean site in operation. Site-02 is located entirely beneath the Bald Mountain, Nevada used to contain numerous Alpha, Beta and Gamma objects. Access is through the several skygates for helicopters, VTOL aircrafts, and ground vehicles not exceeding 500 tons that can be lowered by the elevator.

Site-002 is powered by ██ Tokamak Self-sustaining D-T fusion reactors. Although Site-002's security isn't as heavy as Site-001's, it has substantial housing for the Authority MSF and ASF that as of July 2019, the number of combat ready agents exceeds the entire Norwegian active servicemen.

The massive size of Site-002 is also utilized for a secondary military hub featuring VTOL AFB, The Authority US West Coast headquarters, The Authority's primary containment site and several artificially controlled climate chambers for MSF and ASF training.

Sector F located in the southwest of the site was closed off in ██/██/1974 after instances of RPC-███ broke containment and had infested the site's secondary laboratory.

Site-002-A "The Ziggurat" is a remote secondary confinement pen located 70 kilometers northeast used only to contain RPC-005 from any form of liquids.
