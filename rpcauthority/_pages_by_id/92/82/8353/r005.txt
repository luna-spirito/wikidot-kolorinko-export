**Item** #: RPC-122

**Object Class**: Alpha

**Containment Protocols**: RPC-122 is to be contained in a 15x15 containment chamber with weekly maintenance to ensure the state of RPC-122 does not alter. 
If RPC-122's state alters, location is to be changed and Protocol-122-Alpha executed to prevent more incidents to happen.
Upon testing concluded, Protocol-122-Alpha, the usual routine of safely placing the dice to the pattern //1, 1, 1, 1// is to be done by an ASF operative to stop the anomalous effects. Normally, dice placement and order does not matter, but to ensure an easy maintenance, they're placed next to each other in a line.
**No testing on living beings is to be done on RPC-122 by any means. Personnel caught testing RPC-122 on living beings will recieve severe punishment and coercion measures.**

**Description**: RPC-122 consists of four white wooden dice dating from 1844 recovered from a closed factory by MST-██████ in Moscow after locals reported a factory to be releasing yellow smoke although it had been closed for many years.

RPC-122 does not trigger it's anomalous effects until the four dice represent their original summed value of 12. Many testings have been conducted to investigate how such value could be achieved. It is yet not clear if different patterns cause any different reaction in the anomalous effects of RPC-12.

RPC-122 represents a number of different reality-altering anomalous effects that seem to be random at times.

Upon anomalous effect triggering, any object or surface below the dice recieve the anomalous effects until an estimated range of 5 meters.

--RPC-122 is completely safe for living beings testing and represents no danger to humanity. In fact, it's effects may not trigger at all in human beings. There have been three tests conducted on this matter to comprove such fact.--

Following RPC-122-Incident-1, RPC-122 has been proved to be lethal at times. There is no current clear way of knowing how such fatal effects are triggered.

[[collapsible show="RPC-122-Incident-1" hide="Hide RPC-122-Incident-1"]]
In February ██th ████, Senior Researcher █████ conducted a test on RPC-122.

The test consisted of 2 Class-D trying different patterns with the four dice until it activated it's anomalous effects. The Class-Ds were not informed about the total sum value required for RPC-122's anomalous effects to trigger.

Upon triggering, both Class-Ds suddently collapsed.
ASF Operative-███ was dispatched to the CC to follow **Protocol-122-Alpha**. Once the protocol was concluded, there was a verification on the Class-D status. After extensive exams, it was concluded that both Class-Ds no longer had a brain.

Following this incident, testing on living beings has been forbidden.[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="RPC-122 Experiment Logs" hide="Hide RPC-122 Experiment Logs"]]
During the following Experiments, the four dice were placed next to each other and set to a specific pattern by a Class-D.

++ **Experiment #1**:
> **Affected Object**: Wooden table
> 
> **Procedure**: The four dice were placed on top of a wooden table and their pattern was changed. Pairing was random.
> 
> **Pattern**: 1, 1, 4, 6
> 
> **Change**: Wooden table converted into a metal table. Material appears to be Iron.


++ **Experiment #2**:
> **Affected Object**: Red carpet
> 
> **Procedure**: The four dice were rolled inside a red carpet until their sum equaled 12.
> 
> **Pattern**: 2, 2, 4, 4
> 
> **Change**: Carpet became yellow and sparkly.


++ **Experiment #3**:
> **Affected Object**: Glass
> 
> **Procedure**: The four dice were carefully stacked next to a window brought inside the Containment Cell.
> 
> **Pattern**: 2, 2, 3, 5
> 
> **Change**: Glass became bullet proof and bright red. No known color tint could be found on the glass.

++ **Experiment #4**:
> **Affected Object**: Cup of glass
> 
> **Procedure**: The four dice were placed inside a cup of glass.
> 
> **Pattern**: 1, 1, 4, 6
> 
> **Change**: Cup of glass begun melting. Temperatures estimated to be around 5000º celcius.

++ **Experiment #5**:
> **Affected Object**: Calico Kitten
> 
> **Procedure**: A wool ball was placed next to the kitten to entertain it. Following this, the four dice were placed in the requested pattern by the researcher next to the kitten.
> 
> **Pattern**: 2, 2, 4, 4
> 
> **Change**: Kitten became a scottish full-grown cat.

++ **Experiment #6**:
> **Affected Object**: Human Male
> 
> **Procedure**: The Class-D was requested to place two dice on his left hand and the remaining ones in his right hand. After placed, their patterns were adjusted.
> 
> **Pattern**: 3, 3, 3, 3
> 
> **Change**: Class-D gained 10 IQ points.

++ **Experiment #7**:
> **Affected Object**: Human Female
> 
> **Procedure**: The Class-D was requested to place two dice next to her left hand and the remaining ones in her right hand. After placed, their patterns were adjusted.
> 
> **Pattern**: 1, 1, 4, 6
> 
> **Change**: Class-D became blonde.
