**Full Name:** Dr. Nicholas S. Rieper

**Date Of Birth:** 23rd August, ████

**Position:** Assistant Director of Research Division
**Clearance Level:** Level 4 Clearance

**Description:** Dr. Rieper is currently ██ years old, and measures to be 1.75 meters tall and weighing at 92 kilograms. He has black hair, brown eyes and has no facial hair. 

**Biography:** Born in Seattle, Oregon, Dr. Rieper graduated from the University of Michigan with a Bachelor of Science degree in //Psychology//, and a Doctorate degree in //Biology// (PhD). Despite his achievements, Dr. Rieper enrolled in the University again to graduate with a Bachelor of Engineering degree in //Aerospace Engineering//, and decided to use this degree to be employed in the United States Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) in September 23rd, ████.  Additionally, during his tenure within the AFRL, Dr. Rieper was primarily stationed within Tonopah Test Range working on the development of stealth technology and other sensitive black projects at that time. 

In November 8th, ████, Dr. Rieper had been transferred over to the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) to head over a classified research program known solely as 'Icarus'[[footnote]] Icarus was a classified research program that dedicated to the responsibility of scientific research on anomalous phenomena[[/footnote]]. However, the Icarus Program had faced scrutiny throughout the years when congressional support slowly decreased funding, and by ████, the Icarus Program had been officially disbanded. 

On June 21st, ████, Dr. Rieper had resigned from the organization after he was approached by Authority representatives to be recruited into the Research Department.
