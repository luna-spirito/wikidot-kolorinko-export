**Item #:** RPC-224

**Object Class:** Alpha

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-224 is to be contained in a simple envelope within a locker at site-002. Any testing is to be done with headphones in windowless rooms, monitored over cameras with muted footage. Any testing done elsewhere should be considered an auditory hazard and punishment will follow to perpetrators. Any personnel able to hear 224 should close their eyes until any other personnel are able to stop 224.

[[include component:image-block name= 	http://rpcsandbox.wikidot.com/local--files/redtide/Crcle.jpeg|caption= Manuscript for Act 2 song 8: The Magic Circle of Infinity]]

**Description:** RPC-224 is a CD of George Crumb's Makrokosmos 1. The anomalous activity begins in act 2 song 8, titled "The Magic Circle of Infinity". The subject listening to the song will start bending all physical structures in sight. Once the piece starts playing, it loops continuously, until the CD is stopped. Any damage done is permanent, even with the disruption of the piece.

Experimentation has concluded any material in a structure stared at for more than 5 seconds will be warped in a clockwise direction, with thin structures warping quicker than denser structures. Skinny structures like poles simply bend while structures like walls warp in a swirling motion. For a living creature, prolonged exposure[[footnote]] approximately 20 seconds [[/footnote]]  is required for anomalous properties to occur, with approximately 40% of subjects surviving with extreme and permanent spine damage, and approximately 60% dying later from internal bleeding.


**Discovery:** RPC-224 was originally discovered on the Greek island ████████, where local police responded to a report a violent murder on a remote part of the island [[footnote]] A lighthouse keeper was found mangled in front of a mirror with 224 playing [[/footnote]]. After the police discovered 224's anomalous properties, embedded agents recovered 224 and administered amnesiacs to all people relevant. A letter was delivered to Site-002 one day after 224 was contained. The letter was addressed to the Authority and signed by someone under the alias Schoenberg from L'istituto della vera arte[[footnote]] Translated means: The Institute of True Art. [[/footnote]].

**Experiment log:**
**Foreword:** The Experiments shown here were attempts to understand how RPC-224 worked when it was first obtained. There was an initial argument about whether to confirm if 224 could potentially cause bodily harm, or to test 224's effects on structures. Eventually an agreement was made with consideration to what was said in Document-224-1

**Experiment #1:**
**Subject:** D-224-1
**Aim:** To discern if self-mutilation with 224 is indeed possible with a mirror
**Findings:** Subject was ordered to stare at their reflection, after 20 seconds, subject bent violently sideways, when onlooking researchers tried write down results, their clipboards warped. With fear of a Containment breach, they ended the experiment. Researchers then reviewed footage[[footnote]] surveillance cameras in the test room captured sound before current experiment procedures were created [[/footnote]] trying to understand what happened, which resulted in the TV warping, terminating the music along with it. It was then understood that hearing 224 in any way would result in its anomalous properties. Autopsy on the Subject after he died an hour later, revealed his spine was bent to his left, causing internal bleeding and rupturing of vital organs. Experiment procedures were updated.

**Experiment #2**
**Subject:** D-224-2 and D-224-3
**Aim:** To discern the amount of time needed for mutilation to occur
**Findings:** D-224-2 is ordered to put on headphones playing 224 and stare at D-224-3. 20 seconds after D-224-2 put on his headphones, D-224-3 bent violently sideways. D-224-2 was distraught enough after testing concluded to attack Security personnel, which quickly terminated him. D-224-3 was given immediate medical attention, and survived. She was reassigned to handling RPC-████ due to her paralysis from the waist down. 20 seconds confirmed for human mutilation.

**Experiment #3**
**Subject:** Dr. Lewis (voluntary)
**Aim:** To discern how 224 functions on structures
**Findings:** 5 second stare is found to be enough to warp a steel wall. Different materials were then added, all walls started warped within the same amount of time, with density seemingly slowing 224's properties. Thin walls and materials were quickly warped, while denser and thicker walls took more time[[footnote]] Longest time was 2 mins [[/footnote]]

**Experiment #4**
**Subject:** Dr. Lewis (voluntary)
**Aim:** to see how 224's properties manifest on a skinny object/structure.
**Findings:** Skinny structures like poles bent similar to D class personnel in previous Experiments. Similar findings were met with material and density.

**Experiment #5**
**Subject:** D-244-4
**Aim:** To see how long the song lasts
**Findings:** Subject was ordered to put headphones and a blindfold on. Testing Concluded after 10 hrs and Subject falling asleep. Subject was taken back to his cell with no issues[[footnote]] That was a complete waste of time-Dr. Lewis [[/footnote]].

> Dear employees of the Authority:
> 
> It has come to my attention that you've recently found one of my many creations. We here at the Institute strive on creating new and exciting art for the public, however i wish to ask that your officials please return my art to where it was found. My art's effects shouldn't work on people, a small oversight that i can fix. And with it fixed i can release it in a more public area, sharing this glorious art with the comfort knowing that no one is at risk.
> I understand if you don't comply, but if you do, you'll be sharing a testament to how far mankind has come from scribbling paintings in caves.
> 
> I also understand that you might try and find us. Anonymity is one of our specialities, with every member provided an alias and a secluded workstation. We know our art might bring aggressors and we've prepared over the last [REDACTED] years to ensure we can work in peace.
> 
> And a small ask regarding the future: I implore you to allow our art to be viewed by the public. Other artists have spent years crafting some of their finest works. If any Art is to be contained by your organisation, it should be the artwork of those who abused or do not understand true art. Some of their artworks were created specifically to cause harm. Be on the lookout, as you always are.
> 
> Regards: Schoenberg - curator of Music
> L'istituto della vera arte
