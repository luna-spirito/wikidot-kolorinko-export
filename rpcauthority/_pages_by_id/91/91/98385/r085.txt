contents= [[size 120%]] Often, it is often hard to make a distinction between the hierarchy of departments and that of Authority personnel. Although there are some similarities, the contents of this info-graphic are to be used exclusively to rank specific departmental power within the organization. Under no circumstance is the lowest ranking staff members of the Presidium necessarily higher than the highest-ranking members of other divisions or the acting divisional director(s) of a specific site. 

Individual rank is more ascribed based on a combination of clearance, and sub-department/facility structure. To learn more about this subject, please contact your local divisional officer(s) and/or OIRS representative. [[/size]] |
