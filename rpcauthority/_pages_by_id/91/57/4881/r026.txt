**Registered Phenomena Code:** 107

**Object Class:** Beta-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Temporal Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-107 is to be contained in the exact configuration it was recovered in, within a room that is an ██m ██m by ██m cube, within Site-006's 'Hazardous' unit. Access to RPC-107 is to be strictly limited to researchers involved with either the Site-006 Reality Hazard Unit or personnel involved with [DATA EXPUNGED] 


**Description:** RPC-107 references two objects, RPC-107-1, and RPC-107-2. 

RPC-107-1 is a shard of wood[[footnote]] Cedarwood. [[/footnote]] that is a square based pyramid in shape, 20cm long with a 5 by 5cm base. RPC-107-1 is a reality hazard with a profound effect on its local reality. Upon removal from containment, RPC-107-1 will extend a field of influence in an █m radius around itself that reverts the reality inside to the state it was in the year ██AD[[footnote]] Based upon radioisotopic dating of material removed from inside the influence of RPC-107-1. [[/footnote]][[footnote]] The sphere of influence expands at a rate of 0.99998 c upon initialization based upon current testing. [[/footnote]]. 

The view into the sphere of influence remains the same as it was previous to the expansion of the anomalous field, with the exception of RPC-107-1, RPC-107-2, and any human beings within the field; these will all cease to be seen. It is a quirk of the anomaly that moving objects cannot be observed from outside of the field, the environment appears completely static. The view from within RPC-107-1s effect, of the landscape that is outside of the influence of RPC-107-1s effect, is the view of the environment as it was in ██AD.

Human beings and RPC-107-2 are the only objects that are not affected by the reality-altering effects of RPC-107-1; clothes, testing equipment, buildings and any other man-made object not present in ██AD will cease to exist in the local reality of RPC-107-1. Upon the cessation of RPC-107-1s reality-altering effect, whether by physically exiting the area of space that it resides in or by stopping the effect entirely, any immobile object[[footnote]] Buildings, static furniture, etc.[[/footnote]] will remain where it was placed, and where it was observed to be from outside of the effect. Any mobile object [[footnote]] Clothes that are being worn, objects in motion, etc. [[/footnote]] will come into existence where they would be expected to be factoring in motion, for example, clothes will remain worn by a person exiting the field of RPC-107-1 even when the clothes did not exist within the reality of RPC-107-1.

The effect of RPC-107-1 can be assumed to exist as an enterable layer that is on top of the regular layer of reality that is experienced, as posited in the ██████████ theory posited by Dr. ██████████ of the Site-006 Reality Hazard Unit. 

The reality inside of RPC-107-1's effect is highly unstable. Utilizing a ██████-based directed quantum entanglement device, the Site-006 Reality Hazard Unit was able to determine that the reality inside of RPC-107-1's effect has an instability factor of 5 Schrödinger Instability Units (Sch) when compared to baseline instability of our reality. The particles directly around the sphere of influence of RPC-107-1 have been measured to have an instability factor of 15 Sch, making the risk of unstable reality anomalies presenting in our reality considerable.[[footnote]] The Schrödinger Instability Unit is currently a deprecated unit of measurement, supplanted by the somewhat more reliable Anderson Coherency Scale. A value of 5 Sch is approximately equal to ACS 2.8, while 15 Sch equals ACS 2.1.[[/footnote]]

RPC-107-2 acts as the primary containment device of RPC-107-1. RPC-107-2 takes the form of an ornate, Catholic style reliquary that has been radioisotopically dated to be ███ years old. The object is made from gold and glass and is decorated with multiple crucifixes and Latin scriptures. RPC-107-2 has been specifically designed to hold RPC-107-1 within the glass section, and actively displays RPC-107-1. While RPC-107-1 is within the glass section and RPC-107-2 remains sealed, the effects of RPC-107-1 are nullified. 

**Recovery:** RPC-107 was recovered by [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/authority-security-force ASF] Rapid Response Team, 'Crucifix Chasers', along with attache from Dr. ████████'s Reality Hazard Containment Team, from a previously sealed crypt in █████, Israel; based upon information gathered from RPC-███. Radioisotopic in combination with decay-based aging techniques confirm that the crypt is ████ years old. Along with RPC-107, eleven (11) male bodies, ranging from 40 to 80 years old at the time of death, were found. The bodies have been radioisotopically dated to be ███ years old. 

**Addendum:** 

> **"Based upon details gathered about the instability of the RPC-107 anomaly, it is the conclusion of my teams report that RPC-107's effect has an inordinately high risk of spawning unintended reality based anomalies. It is our recommendation that testing is suspended immediately"** - The conclusion of the Site-006 Reality Hazard Unit's report on RPC-107.

RPC-107 is currently being actively pursued by [REDACTED]. Members of Rapid Response Team 'Crucifix Chasers' have been deployed in surveillance roles in [REDACTED] for preventative strike co-ordination.
