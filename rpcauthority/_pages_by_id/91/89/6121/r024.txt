**Registered Phenomena Code:** 093

**Object Class:** Alpha-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Extra-Dimensional Hazard, Mechanical Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-093 is to have a muzzle plug inserted into the barrel and stored within a locked handgun rack within the arms room of Site-033. Access is to be restricted to Level 03 research, and any usage is to be logged on form 38-7 for accountability purposes.

**Description:** RPC-093 is a 7.62mm Tokarev Semi-Automatic Pistol, produced in the year 1943 for usage in the Soviet Armed Forces, and modified at some point after that by Soviet Research Divisions, ostensibly for covert operations requiring discrete methods of infiltration and exfiltration. [[footnote]] Authority efforts are ongoing to locate and apprehend documents or Soviet-era scientists related to this project. [[/footnote]]

RPC-093’s anomalous effects were first discovered in ██████, the Republic of Vietnam on ██, ████, 1965 following a U.S. Army Military Police raid of a local brothel owned by the “█████ ████ Society” in order to apprehend soldiers that had gone AWOL. During the course of the raid, RPC-093 was fired by a Vietcong insurgent, resulting in a displacement of local reality grouping creating an opening to a separate pocket reality, hereafter referred to as RPC-093-1.  SSG ██████ ████████ was subsequently killed when half of his body was [DATA EXPUNGED] upon opening of RPC-093-1. Parallel structure exists due to yet unknown properties inherent within RPC-093 is able to be safely entered via insertion through the opening. (See Defense Intelligence Agency Radio Transmissions 093-1-1)

RPC-093-1 is an instance defined by a wide and open plane, with fauna matching biome found in the Kazakh Steppes. The longer RPC-093-1 is in existence, the more reality within RPC-093-1 begins to breakdown with interactions within the physical reality of RPC-093-1 becoming increasingly volatile eventually culminating in the full breakdown of RPC-093-1. Instances of RPC-093-2 are human entities within RPC-093-1, thought to be previous persons who have entered into RPC-093-1, and experienced a molecular deconstruction event upon the physical breakdown of RPC-093-1. To date, no form of communication has been established with RPC-093-2. (See Experiment Logs)

> **Test Number**: Experiment 093-1
> 
> **Subject**: Unmanned aerial drone.
> 
> **Procedure**: Unmanned aerial drone inserted through opening RPC-093-1, and set for programmed course flying north at 1,000 Meters. Test is conducted to determine max time personnel can be present within RPC-093-2 before molecular breakdown begins.
> 
> **Results**: 
> 
> **00:03:02** Unmanned aerial drone begins flight in RPC-093-1 with no noted abnormalities.
> 
> **14:09:28**: First signs of breakdown are noted when a tree is observed at ground level to dematerialize.
> 
> **19:04:36**: Research team observes a cirrocumulus cloud formation consisting of grass.
> 
> **19:53:21**: Significant static has become prevalent in video feed and renders clear observation impossible
> 
> **29:08:32**: Video feed to aerial drone is totally lost.
> 
> **56:12:43**: Research team loses all connection to aerial drone.
> 
> **Notes**: Fourteen hour mark established as maximum time for teams to operate within RPC-093-1. Request for One CSD-Class to be inserted into RPC-093-2 until full molecular breakdown in order to study effects on human anatomy. **[Request Approved]**

> **Test Number**: Experiment 093-2
> 
> **Subject**: One CSD Personnel (CSD-093-1)
> 
> **Procedure**: CSD-093-1 is given ration kit for forty-eight hours, a video feed, line of rope tethered to the entry point, and biometric monitors to measure effects on the body. CSD is instructed to enter RPC-093-1 and walk in a straight line for one day, before turning back and exiting RPC-093-1.
> 
> **Results**:
> 
> **2:05:38**: CSD-093-1 states he is able to identify an instance of RPC-093-2. CSD-093-1 is instructed by the research team to approach and attempt contact.
> 
> **2:12:04**: RPC-093-2 instance is unable to be located by CSD-093-1. CSD-093-1 instructed to continue walking further into RPC-093-1.
> 
> **10:32:45**: CSD-093-1 first begins to notice breakdown of reality contained within RPC-093-1 when a small tree branch passes through his right upper arm. CSD-093-1 becomes rapidly alarmed and begins return towards tether point. CSD-093-1 is instructed to continue for another twelve hours or be terminated upon return. CSD-093-1 disregards instructions and continues back to the insertion point. 
> 
> **10:34:28**: Research team cuts off tether to CSD-093-1 in order to prevent his return to the insertion point and premature conclusion of the test.
> 
> **14:04:28**: Reality within RPC-093-1 has broken down to the point where CSD-093-1 is no longer able to correctly orient himself towards exit point.
> 
> **14:23:38**: CSD-093-1 becomes lodged in a stone tree upon stopping in order to attempt to ascertain his orientation to exit.
> 
> **16:13:53**: CSD-093-1 begins to fall.
> 
> **78:32:19**: CSD-093-1 expires from dehydration.
> 
> **Notes**: Testing suspended at this time due to rapid destabilization of RPC-093-1 and high likelihood of losing valuable Authority Personnel.
