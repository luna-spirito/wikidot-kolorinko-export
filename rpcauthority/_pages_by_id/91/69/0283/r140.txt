[[/div]]

The Authority maintains a worldwide presence in its effort to research and contain RPCs. Authority facilities can range anywhere from remote outposts with only a handful of personnel to sprawling bases with thousands of staff members.

++ **Site Roles**

Site facilities operate, often carrying multiple roles, to conduct the mission capabilities of the Authority within their [[[authority-regional-sites| region]]]. While various sites may conduct one or two roles, such as containment or research, some take on multiple roles due to their vast funding, initiatives, strategic importance. 

[[collapsible show="+ Site Roles" hide="- Close"]]

||~ Role||~ Meaning||
||Garrison||Denotes that the Site houses an ASF detachment or MST, size may vary.|| 
||Biological||Denotes that the Site operates a biological facility or sector that contains bio-related anomalies.||
||Containment||Denotes that the Site operates a containment facility or sector.||
||Detention||Denotes that the Site operates a detention facility or sector to house CSD personnel.||
||Spatial||Denotes that the Site operates a facility or sector dedicated to spatial anomalies, often designated to those stationed in foreign, planetary bodies.||
||Weapons Testing||Denotes that the Site operates a facility or sector dedicated to weapons and testing.||
||Heavy Containment||Denotes that the Site operates a high containment facility or sector that contains Gamma-class anomalies, often Beta-class included.||
||Humanoid||Denotes that the Site operates a facility or sector dedicated to humanoid anomalies, often assigned to Gamma-level entities.||
||Logistics||Denotes that the Site operates a logistical facility or sector that provides logistical support to itself or other facilities nearby.||
||Low Containment||Denotes that the Site operates a low containment facility or sector that contain Alpha or Beta-class anomalies.||
||Medical||Denotes that the Site operates a medical facility or sector that treats injured personnel, or contain anomalies relating to medical.||
||Rapid Deployment||Denotes that the Site operates a quick reactionary force deployed to contain an anomaly, or render assistance to nearby Authority elements.||
||Research||Denotes that the Site operates a research facility or sector that research anomalies, often conducting research and development.||
||Training||Denotes that the Site operates a training facility or sector that conducts training or classes to Authority personnel.||
||Transport||Denotes that the Site operates an airstrip, hanger, or runway dedicated to transport, including VTOL.||
||Intelligence||Denotes that the Site operates an intelligence facility or sector that gathers information on discovered anomalies or Groups of Interests.||
||Exploration||Denotes that the Site operates a facility or sector dedicated to exploration, usually anomalies displaying non-euclidean geometry, anomalous structure or caverns.||
||Spiritual||Denotes that the Site operates a facility or sector dedicated to religious services, or containing anomalies relating to religion.||
||Storage||Denotes that the Site operates a storage facility or sector that stores anomalies in storage containers in warehouses.||

[[/collapsible]]
