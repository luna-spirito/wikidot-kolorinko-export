**Registered Phenomena Code:** 072

**Object Class:** Gamma-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Auditory Hazard


**Containment Protocols:** RPC-072 is to be kept on its display podium within its designated locker in the soundproofed containment chamber at Site-064. It is not advisable that any personnel who are not naturally deaf (excluding CSD personnel) handle RPC-072 directly, as the RPC Authority does not yet have sufficient equipment to guarantee personnel safety when in direct contact with the object.

**Description:** RPC-072 is a cluster of transparent crystals, measuring approximately 60x69x45 cm, with a (presumably artificially) flattened base. The crystals themselves appear to be similar in chemical composition to common quartz crystals, with the exception of the presence of [REDACTED]. This compound is thought to be the cause of the anomalous properties detailed below. However, attempts by the Authority to reproduce this chemical composition artificially have been unsuccessful.

The object itself produces a low-volume (45db approx.) sound which has been described as "bringing forward... similarities" in the other ambient sounds it combines with (even sounds presumably loud enough to drown it out). Those who have heard the sound describe it as a "low hum" or a "uniform buzz". However, spectrogram analysis of the sound reveals a complex pattern which does not conform to the provided description.

The effects of RPC-072 have proven addictive to those who have heard them, and CSD personnel subject to testing with RPC-072 have shown numerous withdrawal symptoms when not directly exposed to the sounds of RPC-072. Indirect exposure via recording has proven unsuccessful in relieving these withdrawal symptoms, even with sampling rates as high as ██████ Hz (the current limit of technology available to the Authority). Nonetheless, it is not advisable that personnel above CSD's be exposed to the sound produced by RPC-072, even indirectly.

Withdrawal symptoms associated with RPC-072 include: Depressive episodes, nausea, obsessive thinking, dermatillomania, [REDACTED], and an inability to recognize elements of one's immediate environment (temperature, spatial aspects, color, etc.)

[[collapsible show="+ Interview Log Excerpts" hide="- Interview Log Excerpts"]]
> **Interviewed:** CSD-0124
> 
> **Interviewer:** Dr. Yao
> 
> **Foreword:** This interview was conducted in two parts. In the first section of the interview, CSD-0124 was directly exposed to a cluster of crystals removed from RPC-072 (designated RPC-072-01). The subject was presented questions through a wireless communication device by Dr. Yao. To respond, the subject spoke through a wireless communication device to CSD-3403, who then manually transcribed his answers and presented them to Dr. Yao. In the second section of the interview, CSD-0124 was not exposed to the effects of RPC-072, and the interview was conducted in direct proximity with Dr. Yao, following standard Authority interview protocol. The excerpts presented here are part of a larger interview log which may be accessed at [REDACTED] by authorized personnel.
> 
> **<Begin Log 1>**
> 
> **Dr. Yao :** What does it sound like?
> 
> **CSD-0124 :** It's a low hum, kind of like a refrigerator. It's not really that noticeable by itself.
> 
> **Dr. Yao :** You previously described it as a pleasant sound, could you elaborate on that?
> 
> **CSD-0124 :** It's like, when you hear a choir singing. When all the voices come together, you know, they have a different feel than the voices one at a time. You know what I mean?
> 
> **Dr. Yao :** So, the sound is harmonizing with other sounds that you're hearing?
> 
> **CSD-0124 :** With every other sound that I hear. Sometimes, if I focus hard enough on it, I can't tell other sounds apart anymore. 
> 
> **Dr. Yao :** Are you having difficulty distinguishing my voice from other ambient sounds in the room now?
> 
> **CSD-0124 :** No, I'm not really thinking that hard about what I'm hearing. Sometimes I feel like it's putting me in a trance, and even when I do other things, I'm just focused on hearing. Does that make sense?
> 
> **<End Log 1 Excerpt>**
> 
> **<Begin Log 2>** 
> 
> **Dr. Yao :** How are you feeling?
> 
> **CSD-0124 :** Can I- <sil> My voice is really loud. Am I yelling?
> 
> **Dr. Yao :** No, you're actually speaking below what I'd consider a normal volume. Does it sound to you like I'm yelling?
> 
> **CSD-0124 :** No. Everything sounds really- <sigh> It's like I'm hearing everything through a telephone with bad signal. It's hard to tell what I'm hearing.
> 
> **Dr. Yao :** Could you describe that in more detail? <sil> Could you describe that effect in more detail? Sir? Can you hear me?
> 
> **CSD-0124 :** Can you play the sound again?
> 
> **Dr. Yao :** If you cooperate, you can hear it again when the interview is over.
> 
> **CSD-0124 :** I don't- <inaudible>
> 
> **Dr. Yao :** I'm sorry, could you repeat that?
> 
> **CSD-0124 :** My voice is too loud, it's hurting my ears. Can I write down my answers?
> 
> **<End Log 2 Excerpt>**
>  
> **Closing Statement:** During the remainder of the interview, the subject lost focus several times and proved difficult to communicate with. Further tests have been suggested by Dr.██████ which would include deafening subjects after exposure to prevent distraction and possibly alleviate the side-effects of RPC-072, but these tests have yet to be carried out (as of ██/██/████).
