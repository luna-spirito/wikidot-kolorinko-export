++ A contest has started to determine the first entry on the RPC-001 page. The theme is to be inspired by the above images, so you may include them in your article, but they are not required. Additionally, you are not required to make your RPC a literal sun; be creative. Object classes Omega and Theta are unlocked for this contest. Do your best!

+ Dates

**Posting Opens:** July 21th, 2018 12:00 AM Central Time
**Posting Closes:** August 11th, 2018, 12:00 AM Central Time

+ To Enter:

Post your entry to the site with the url '001contest<yourusername>' with your site user name in place of <yourusername.> For example, if a user named Chad were to post an entry it would have the url 001contestchad. This page must be posted by you (or, if it is a collaboration, one of the partners). Add the tag "001C" to your entry so it shows up on this page.

+ Rules
* Any contestant who takes unusual and/or negative actions to increase their vote total, or decrease that of their competitors, will be disqualified for the involved contest and potentially banned from further contests (and possibly further disciplinary action).
 * Do not do any of the following:
  * Downvoting all of your competitors' work within minutes of them being posted
  * Spamming people with PMs about your work
  * Spurring Malicious Upvoting/Downvoting, whether on your work or anyone else's
* One entry per user
* Collaborations are allowed, but that will count as the one entry for all participants
* Pre-existing drafts are allowed, so long as they have never been posted to the wiki
* Edits to works **are** allowed.

+ The winner will be decided by who gets the most upvotes. Standard rules and procedures apply regarding deletion and quality.
