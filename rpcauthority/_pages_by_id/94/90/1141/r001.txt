**Item #:** RPC-135

**Object Class:** Alpha

**Containment Protocols:**   RPC-135 is kept within a magnetically sealed double airlock containment chamber. Under no circumstances should research personnel enter the containment room. Test subjects selected for additional testing while under the effects of  RPC-135 are to be removed from containment chamber. They are to be psychologically evaluated following the tests as are required to attend to a psychiatrist for mandatory checkups.

**Description:** RPC-135 is a statue comprised entirely of iron representing a human. The object is approximately 1.8 meters, weighing 500(lbs), bearing a CRT TV in place between the clavicles. The anomaly constantly produces a rapid sequence of random cosmological imagery. RPC-135 will always remain motionless even upon activation when in the presence of humans. If there is no sign of a human present within the general vicinity, RPC-135 will instantaneously re-materialize near the nearest living human if there are no obstacles in its path. How this is possible regardless of obstructions is unclear. Research is ongoing.

Initial experiments show test subjects that were within 5.7 meters RPC-135's vicinity become increasingly curious or the anomaly. When subjects make direct visual contact with RPC-135, they will become entranced. They will continue to stare at the anomaly for approximately thirty minutes. In almost every case, the subjects will approach entity until they are within physical contact. Within range of the anomaly, the subjects will grab hold of RPC-135 and proceed to push themselves against its screen head first. RPC-135s screen will turn to static as the subjects continue to climb and force their bodies inside. Despite the dimensions of the television screen, subjects were quite capable of fitting themselves in. These test subjects disappear entirely from reality and are declared missing. Indirect surveillance observation outside of RPC-135's range does not prompt any of these effects. 

Several additional tests were conducted with retrieval harnesses and shoulder mounted cameras attached to the subjects in the hopes of recovering them and any visual data they can record. However, the harnesses were unable to exert sufficient force to prevent subjects from climbing into RPC-135's screen. The ropes attached to the subjects' harnesses indicate they were cleanly severed once they entered the screen. The test subjects and all visual data were lost.

Psychological evaluations of the test subjects who were removed from the anoamly's influence show unusually extreme levels of elation, euphoria and complete unresponsiveness. A few rare instances of the test subjects speaking express a desire to return to 'There.' They do not specify whether they meant RPC-135 or not. Surprisingly, the evaluations of the test subjects revealed that they were under no compulsive effects.
