**Registered Phenomena Code:** 252 

**Object Class:** Alpha-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Regenerative, Contact, Sensory

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-252 is to be contained inside a four-digit safe. RPC-252 is not to be handled outside of its case, --except for testing purposes--.

**Description:** RPC-252 consists of two parts: RPC-252-1 and RPC-252-2. RPC-252-1 is a brown feather quill with a gold, ornately engraved base. RPC-252-2 is a gold-based inkwell and stand for RPC-252-1. RPC-252-2 is perpetually filled with black iron gall ink. The ink within RPC-252-2 regenerates immediately upon its removal from the vessel. The origin of RPC-252 is currently unknown.

RPC-252 displays no anomalous effects until activated by human touch. Upon direct physical contact with RPC-252-1, The subject will enter a trance-like state and begin writing. The subject matter written about by those affected by RPC-252 varies greatly and consists of both original and reproduced texts on matters seemingly related to the personal interests of the affected subject. The subject will continue writing so long as they remain in physical contact with RPC-252-1. If no paper or parchment is available, the subject will write on any available surface, including tables, walls, and their own body. The subjects state will increase in severity, progressing through three stages before the subject eventually dies due to neglect of basic physical needs.

[[collapsible show="+ Experiment Log" hide="- Experiment Log" hideLocation="both"]]
**Test Number:** RPC-252-1
**Subject:** CSD-5603
**Procedure:** CSD-5603 is to make physical contact with RPC-252-1, inducing its effects for observation.
**Results:** After 2 hours, CSD-5603 fails to respond to auditory stimuli. After 36 hours, CSD-5603 fails to respond to visual and physical stimuli. After a continues writing period of 6 days and 19 hours, CSD-5603 is pronounced dead. Cause of death: dehydration. The literature produced by CSD-5603 appears to be an incomplete medieval fantasy novel of his own authorship. The completed chapters introduce the protagonist, a high-elven mage's apprentice by the name of Archon, and detail his journey across the land of Arcania, home to various diverse humanoid races as is typical in the medieval fantasy setting. The last pages describe an unfinished battle between an army of human warriors against the cliche horde of orcish marauders. In the final sentence Archon begins to cast a spell of unknown effect, but the story ends with the death of its author.

**Test Number:** RPC-252-2
**Subject:** RPC-252-2
**Procedure:** Dr. Matthews is to pour out the contents of RPC-252-2.
**Results:** After 4 minutes of continuous pouring it has been determined that the ink inside of RPC-252-2 has regenerative properties. Outside of RPC-252-2 the ink displays no anomalous properties. The regeneration appears to be directly caused by RPC-252-2.  It is theoretically possible to flood an entire facility with ink by keeping RPC-252-2 upside down and opened.

**Test Number:** RPC-252-3
**Subject:**  CSD-5609
**Procedure:** CSD-5609 is to be put under the influence of RPC-252 until failure to respond to auditory stimuli. CSD-5609 is then to be physically separated from RPC-252-1.
**Results:** After 2 hours, RPC-252-1 was forcefully removed from physical contact with CSD-5609, whom immediately regained full consciousness. CSD-5609 remained deaf for 2 days, before regaining his hearing. Examination of the subject's writings reveals a 5 page essay regarding the history of medicine with emphasis on devastating plagues such as the Black Death and Spanish flu.

**Test Number:** RPC-252-4
**Subject:** CSD-5618
**Procedure:** CSD-5618 is to be put under the influence of RPC-252 and subsequently physically separated upon failure to respond to visual and physical stimuli. 
**Results:** After 36 hours, forceful removal of RPC-252-1 from the hand of CSD-5618 was not possible. The subject was restrained and his hand amputated on-site. Upon amputation CSD-5618 regains consciousness. After a period of one month, CSD-5618 did not regain any lost senses and was terminated. Examination of the subjects writing reveals an unfinished 29 page memoir detailing the subject's criminal past, imprisonment, experience as a CSD and his remorse for the murder of Allison ████ in June of 199█.

**Test Number:** RPC-252-5
**Subject:** CSD-5921
**Procedure:** CSD-5921 is to be given nutrients through IV drip and connected to a brain activity monitoring device. The subject is to be subsequently put under the influence of RPC-252. The effects of RPC-252 are to be monitored into advanced stages, as the subject is kept alive. The subject is to be continuously supplied with paper. 
**Result:** After 30 days of uninterrupted writing all observed neurological activity had ceased, but the subject continued writing. After 45 days of uninterrupted writing CSD-5921 had produced several reproductions of various Marxist and anarchist books, as well as his own 356 page untitled manifesto, detailing his political motivations for his mailbombing spree during the spring of 20██. At 50 days having completed a fifth and final reproduction text, as well as several thousand short phrases regarding government affairs and anarchism, the hand of CSD-5921 was amputated and the subject was pronounced dead upon separation from RPC-252-1.

**Test Number:** RPC-252-6
**Subject:** CSD-6201
**Procedure:** CSD-6201 is to be under the influence of RPC-252 and allowed to write until stage 2 of RPC-252's effects.
**Result:** See Incident log RPC-252-6

[[collapsible show="+ Incident log RPC-252-6" hide="- Incident log RPC-252-6"]]
On 12/14/20██,6:29 PM, during testing, CSD-6201 reached stage 2 of RPC-252's effects and was subsequently separated from RPC-252-1. Examination of CSD-6201's writings has raised significant concerns due to its description of a grotesque headless entity collecting eyeballs in its neck cavity. This suggests CSD-6201 has some knowledge of [[[http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-225|RPC-225]]]. It is currently unknown as to if this is true or merely a disturbing coincidence. It is unknown as to whether or not RPC-252 can give subjects knowledge of new topics. It is also unknown as to if RPC-252 and RPC-225 are linked in some way. 
[[/collapsible]] 

 **Retrieval Log :** RPC-252 was discovered by local law enforcement during a welfare check in ██████ County, Georgia, United States on ██/██/19██. Professor ██████ was found dead in his office alongside several large stacks of written pages on the subject of Greek mythology, including English translations of Homer's //Illiad// and //Odyssey//. Sgt. ████ took notice of RPC-252-1 and picked it up to inspect it. Sgt. ████ fell under the effect of RPC-252 and subsequently began writing before being convinced by has fellow officers to return to work. RPC-252 was subsequently recovered by MST Lima-19.

**Addendum 252-1:** Following incident RPC-252-6, all testing of RPC-252 is to be suspended until further notice. An investigation will determine if CSD-6201 has knowledge of any other RPCs and how any such knowledge was obtained.
