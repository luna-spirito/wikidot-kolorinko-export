RPC-411811-J, when coming into contact with any paper document, changes the physical appearance of said paper. The item will adopt a color scheme of blue, white, and a dark grey. This change happens at a slow rate, with the effect slowly spreading from the point of contact. During initial discovery, 100 milliliters of RPC-411811-J were collected. At the current time of containment, only 32 milliliters of the liquid remain.
