**Registered Phenomena Code:** 138

**Object Class:** Alpha-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Emotional Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** Instances of RPC-138 are to be kept within Alpha Refrigerator 12 at Site-023 when not being used for testing or consumption. --RPC-138 may be used as an alternative beverage in the case of a security breach of RPC-███.-- Use of RPC-138 as an alternative beverage has been halted due to inappropriate handling from site employees.

**Description:** RPC-138 is a pale brown liquid contained within ███ 236mL boxes of ████████[[footnote]] Subsidiary of Amazing!..Co.[[/footnote]] brand chocolate milk. RPC-138 shares the same ingredients as leading brands of chocolate milk, except for a previously unknown ingredient, now referred to as ██████████.

Consumption of RPC-138 has a psychoactive effect on the subject. Drinking RPC-138 will induce the consumer to experience a random milk flavor they are familiar with. With each subsequent drink of RPC-138, the flavor alters. Subjects are not required to have previously consumed flavored milk to taste it through the effects of RPC-138. The subject will taste what they assumed the flavor to resemble.

Due to the effect of RPC-138 only being psychoactive, only the brain has to understand that a flavor is milk to taste it. CSD-4564 was convinced that Lemon Juice was a type of milk. Subsequently the taste of RPC-138 would sporadically be described as tasting acidic and tangy by CSD-4564.

Subjects have varied emotional reactions when tasting an unexpected flavor, appearing distraught, upset, or in extreme cases angered. These have yet been proven to be an effect of RPC-138 or ingredient ██████████.

Excessive consumption of RPC-138 incites a secondary effect, where for the coming █ hours to █ days the subject is only able to taste flavored milk. The flavors continue to shift in this state every time the subject swallows their own saliva, which also tastes like flavored milk. Subjects report this effect to be extremely distressing and sometimes traumatic.

**Addendum 138-1:** Recreating the taste shifting effects of RPC-138 using ingredient ██████████ has been unsuccessful; however, using RPC-138, on-site chefs have produced ice cream[[footnote]] RPC-138 Ice Cream was made for Dr. ████████'s birthday. This inspired other developments of RPC-138 styled substances. [[/footnote]], smoothies, milkshakes, and various alcoholic beverages[[footnote]] Use of RPC-138 to make alcoholic beverages is prohibited after Agent ██████ shot multiple CSD personnel in a fit of rage most likely due to the combined effects of RPC-138 and █ White Russians. [[/footnote]] with the taste shifting effects of the regular form.

**Addendum 138-2:** Although the boxes containing RPC-138 have expiration dates, not a single instance of RPC-138 has gone sour. It is unsure whether this is another effect of ingredient ██████████, or a separate anomalous effect.

**Addendum 138-3:** RPC-138 is not lactose-free.
