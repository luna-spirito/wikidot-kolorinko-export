**Containment Protocols:** RPC-764 is contained at at Site-012. After numerous escape attempts, RPC-764's enclosure has been downgraded to a hermetically sealed 5x5x10 m reinforced containment cell surrounded by at least 2 security personnel armed with tranquilizer guns. A camera, a microphone, and a speaker have been placed on the ceiling for the purpose of monitoring and communicating with RPC-764. The containment cell is accessible through a NIJ 0108.01 blast door. Before entry, a combination of Sarin/VX paralytic gas must be discharged for a minimum of 10 minutes. ASF must also perform a precautionary test by piercing RPC-764-A's shoulder to ensure loss of consciousness.

**Description:** RPC-764 is a humanoid entity standing at 2.3 meters tall and weighing approximately 90 kg at the time of writing. It possesses two pairs of arms and one pair of legs. Although it prefers to move bipedally, its height and body structure often force it to engage in quadrupedal and occasionally hexapedal motion. RPC-764 possesses disproportionate strength relative to its weight, being capable of lifting upwards of 500 kg. It also displays extreme flexibility, though this is achieved through the contortion and breakage of its bones. Due to its anomalous regenerative capabilities, it does not appear to age, nor does it require sustenance.

RPC-764's body is subdivided into two segments, which are designated RPC-764-A and RPC-764-B. RPC-764-A denotes RPC-764's upper half, a Caucasian male named Evan Colton. Psychological analysis of RPC-764-A indicates an extremely narcissistic and combative personality. RPC-764-A appears to control the entirety of RPC-764. RPC-764-A's legs are severely atrophied, as they are fused to the shoulders of RPC-764-B in a manner that fuses their skeletal structure and nervous systems. RPC-764-B is the body of RPC-764-A's identical twin brother, Brayden Colton. Its chest and shoulders are covered in severe chemical burns where it has been fused to RPC-764-A. It also possesses overdeveloped lower body muscles. On rare occasions, RPC-764-B will begin scratching and ripping at RPC-764-A's skin.

**Discovery:** On 07/12/2022 local police in Loudon, Tennessee contacted the Authority after RPC-764 attacked the town, resulting in the deaths of 4 civilians and 1 police officer, as well as approximately US$210,000 in property damage. MST Romeo-7 "Suited Gentlemen" subdued and contained the anomaly without incident and issued Class A-1 Amnestics to all witnesses. The Authority later launched an investigation into the Colton household, resulting in the recovery of several documents of interest.

I am a real person drowning in an ocean of the undeserving. There are too many "people" in this world (mostly ███████ and █████ but I'll get to that later) taking up resources from those destined for godhood. When I have completed my ultimate transformation maybe you'll see me as a weirdo even moreso than you do now, so let me tell you I have always been this way. I have always been a psychopath dedicated to the art of destruction. When I was a kid I would torture animals and break toys. **Incident Log 764-I:** On 10/01/2023, RPC-764 breached the walls of its initial containment cell and attempted to escape Site-012. Within 30 minutes, it unintentionally locked itself in an unused secure storage room. This allowed ASF agents to ventilate the room with paralytic gas. Security footage showed that upon discovering that the storage room door was locked from the outside, RPC-764-A began cursing, sobbing, and apologizing profusely. RPC-764-B was observed to be capable of movement while RPC-764 was incapacitated. Review of the security footage of RPC-764's containment revealed that RPC-764-B was using American Sign Language. Researchers interpreted the following message from the footage:

> Thank you for keeping my brother in check.
