+++ **Containment Protocol:**
The whereabouts of RPC-961 and any active members of GoI-961: "//the Holy Knights of Love, Truth, and Justice//" (HKLTJ) remain under active scrutiny. Due to RPC-961's "incognito veil" property, conducting investigations into entities associated with the case has proven difficult. Multiple Mobile Specialized Teams (MSTs) and Authority Central Intelligence (ACI) cells throughout Japan have been assigned to assist with the investigation.

Should Authority personnel encounter an RPC-961-1 instance or a HKLTJ affiliate, they are to exercise precautionary measures and immediately report all observed activity to the ACI. Personnel are prohibited from establishing personal relationships or otherwise fraternizing with RPC-961-1 instances or HKLTJ affiliates.

Although the HKLTJ has not displayed outward enmity toward the Authority, the organization remains highly antagonistic toward Authority interests. Combined with the group’s potential to disrupt the veil, ASIACOM has officially declared it as a hostile entity of interest. Regardless of the benevolent intentions displayed by individual RPC-961-1 instances, GoI-961 is to be regarded as an active threat.

+++ **Description:**
[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/rpc-961/MagicMascot.jpg|width=230px|caption=RPC-961 {{(2008)}}]]
[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/rpc-961/wandia.jpg|width=230px|caption=RPC-961-2 wands belonging to Team Exusiai {{(2009)}}]]

RPC-961 is a reality-bender assuming the form of an animated stuffed plush doll resembling a featureless humanoid figure. The entity is distinguished by a pair of pink glass eyes and a pink ribbon affixed on its head. RPC-961 communicates using a childlike voice and mannerisms, identifying itself as a fairy named "Princess Claudette" sent to Earth to correct the wrongs of humanity.

The primary objective of RPC-961 is to protect humans from supernatural threats and enact moral justice into society. The entity searches for human subjects it considers “worthy” and grants them the opportunity to become a "Holy Knight of Love, Truth, and Justice" in exchange for granting them the "incredible power to uphold goodness in the world." Once subjects agree to these terms, they permanently inherit a portion of the entity's powers.

Individuals who accept RPC-961's contract are given the power to transform into an RPC-961-1 instance, an class entities referred to as “magical girls”. Despite their human appearance, the internal anatomy of these entities is malleable and psychotronic in nature. This altered anatomy allows them to resist cognito-hazards and physically withstand injuries that would otherwise result in immediate incapacitation or death.

RPC-961-1 instances possess various psychotronic and/or reality-warping abilities, which are channeled through plastic toys designed around the user's role, personality, and personal interests. These objects, designated RPC-961-2, can be manifested or demanifested by the instance at any time. When separated from its owner, the item will retain a small fraction of its owner’s anomalous properties, which is active regardless of who is wielding it. 

The capabilities of RPC-961-1 instances are fueled by the expression of positive emotions such as joy, willpower, pride, love, etc. Conversely, they are susceptible to the adverse effects of physical and mental stress resulting from emotional pain and mood disorders. Prolonged exposure to negative emotions may result in the RPC-961-1 instance becoming more vulnerable to physical harm.

What is theorized to be an “Incognito Veil” surrounds RPC-961-1 instances, which helps conceal their civilian identities and obscures any information that connects their personas. This property only deactivates when the RPC-961-1 instance directly confesses their identity to an individual.

[[div class="blockquote"]]

Each RPC-961-1 instance has special properties unique to their own. However, among them, there is a common trend of properties inherent to their character. The list is as follows:

* Enhanced senses
* Heightened Durability
* Flight and levitation[[footnote]] The method varies from shifting one’s own gravity, stepping on air, or aviation. Some instances like RPC-961-1-4 “Glimmer Green” lack this power. [[/footnote]]
* Superhuman strength and speed
* Accelerated healing factor
* Heightened stamina 
* Energy conduct and projection
* Cognitohazard and memetic resistance
* Materialize RPC-961-2 costumes and toys out of thin air
* Innate awareness of the approximate location of RPC-961 and nearby RPC-961-1 instances

+++ **Addendum 961.A:** GoI-961

[[div class="content-panel"]]
++ HKLTJ / "The Holy Knights of Love, Truth, and Justice"
+++ ##red|Hostile## 

* --Authority-Unaware--[[footnote]] For a long period of time, the HKLTJ assumed Hotel-11 was the entire Authority force. [[/footnote]]
* Unveiled
* Fractured
* Local

The //Holy Knights of Love, Truth, and Justice// (愛と真実と正義の聖騎士), sometimes known as Claudetta's Magical Girls, is a Japanese vigilante group composed primarily of juvenile human girls that have entered RPC-961’s contract. While all contracted individuals share a common benefactor (RPC-961), no centralized command structure exists. Instead, they operate in small, independent teams that rarely coordinate with other teams outside of exceptional circumstances.

The overarching objective of the HKLTJ is to eliminate both anomalous and non-anomalous threats to humanity. Their motivations stem from RPC-961's concept of righteousness and each individual's personal understanding of justice. This has led the HKLTJ to make enemies of various GoIs such as Kabushiki Kawaii and Togen Biotech.[[footnote]] A private, but publicly visible industrial biotechnological company. It is a major [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/church-of-malthus-hub Church of Malthus] Chapter within Japan. [[/footnote]]

The first documented RPC-961-1 instance was created in early 2007, following RPC-961's initial manifestation, which the entity claims occurred after it "fell down from Wonderland." RPC-961 selected 17-year-old Meiko ████ as its first "Magical Girl" and directed her to carry out vigilante activities throughout Yokohama. 

Very little information regarding RPC-961-1-1's activities exists, largely due to the discretion with which the instance conducted its operations. The vigilante known by the alias ##white|”Exusiai”## was primarily documented through eyewitness testimonies and urban rumors.

The instance remained active for approximately 6 months before retiring; citing a spinal injury and the pressure to finish secondary school entrance examinations as a reason. Upon retiring, the majority of RPC-961-1-1's anomalous abilities and contractual obligations to RPC-961 were relinquished; however, traces of its original capabilities have continued to manifest intermittently.

RPC-961 was first encountered by the Authority on 15/11/2007, after the account of "a living plush doll" was reported to the local police in ███████. Authority agents would quickly dispatch to assess the situation and find RPC-961 hopping through the marketplace while singing.

+++ **The Incognito Veil**

[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/rpc-961/claudettachomu.jpg|width=200px|caption=Questionably sourced ID card]]

The incognito veil extends to both digital and physical records that may reveal the identities of RPC-961-1 instances, including those who have since retired. It was initially theorized that this effect resulted from an antimemetic phenomenon which removed or obscured information deemed harmful to RPC-961.

However, following a 2008 investigation by ASIACOM into this phenomenon, it was discovered that the data was manually altered or deleted by personnel. Further inquiry identified the responsible individual as RPC-961 itself, acting under legitimate Authority credentials.

Subsequent review revealed that RPC-961 had obtained an official appointment within the Authority Central Intelligence (ACI), holding the position of Inspector General with Level 4 Security Clearance. An internal audit found no evidence of credential forgery, unauthorized access, or procedural misconduct; instead, indicating that RPC-961 obtained the position through standard administrative and legal appointment procedures.

+++ **Addendum 961.B-1:** Team Exusiai

[[div class="content-panel"]]

+++ **Team Exusiai**

"Team Exusiai" (チームエクシア) was founded in February 2008 in Kyoto, and would continue operations until 2015. The team is comprised of a tight-knit group of friends that stayed together all throughout Elementary School to High School, accruing various associates and anomalous artifacts throughout their time.

Throughout their tenure, Team Exusiai primarily opposed the joint operations of Kabushiki Kawaii and Togen Biotech, disrupting human trafficking and narcotics distribution networks operated through affiliated yakuza organizations. As the team gradually uncovered the anomalous world, their operations became increasingly effective. During their period of activity, anomalous incidents throughout Kyoto declined significantly, with much of the reduction attributed to their intervention.

[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/rpc-961/casualExusiai.jpg|width=400px|align=center|caption=RPC-961 and Team Exusiai {{(2012)}}]]

Unlike RPC-961-1-1 before them, the first few months of Team Exusiai's activities were highly public and theatrical, frequently drawing attention from civilians and local media. Their battles often resulted in significant property damage and widespread public disturbances, requiring extensive efforts from the Japanese government and the Authority to suppress information and maintain the integrity of the Veil.

This has caused an antagonistic relationship between Team Exusiai and the Authority, which originally tried to suppress and confine them through peaceful means. The group would remain hostile, however, as they were responsible for destroying several Authority On-Location Sites and garrisons under the belief that the confinement of anomalous subjects was "inhumane".

+++ **Addendum 961.B-2:** Kabushiki Kawaii

[[div class="content-panel"]]

+++ **Kabushiki Kawaii**

Team Exusiai would especially target Kabushiki Kawaii, as they were active in Kyoto and operated a major underground human trafficking ring during the mid to late 2000s, after the {{[REDACTED]}} incident quietly destabilized the local government and left numerous blind spots that bad actors have taken advantage of. Although public life remained largely the same, the Authority branch and the Japanese government have been scrambling to put things back in order before the veil could be ripped.

Nobuyuki Kurobane (黒羽伸行), a senior executive of the company and the chief logistics officer of the Kyoto network, became a recurring figure within Team Exusiai's tenure. Kurobane has gained a fascination with RPC-961 and Team Exusiai. He was responsible for orchestrating many of the group's battles in what he called an "experiment in engaging narratives". This "experiment" includes the creation of a group of specialized, often young SecuPro[[footnote]] "Security Product" - a modified subject created for military purposes [[/footnote]] subjects, which often underwent rigorous upgrades and modifications to catch up to the growing power of Team Exusiai.

**Underground Notoriety**

Through the effort of the Authority and the Japanese government, knowledge of RPC-961 and Team Exusiai has largely been repressed from the general public. Meanwhile, KK, under the direction of Kurobane, began advertising Team Exusiai and their exploits, seeding their niche popularity within underground communities in Japan in the effort to let the group's guard down and potentially turn them into a valuable asset of the company.

Much of Team Exusiai's niche popularity has been orchestrated by KK under Kurobane's direction, utilizing shell circles, front organizations, and third-party vendors in Comiket to distribute Team Exusiai-related media and encourage direct interaction between a select group of civilians, despite the Authority's continued effort of information suppression.

Investigation found no evidence that the Comic Market Preparatory Committee was aware of these activities; KK instead exploited the event's decentralized structure to establish a substantial covert presence without the knowledge or cooperation of its organizers.

**Final Encounter with Kurobane**

Over the following three years, Team Exusiai was involved in several hundred documented and undocumented engagements against KK assets across western Japan, further inflicting significant losses against KK infrastructure, particularly throughout the Kansai region. Post-incident analysis suggests that very few of these operations were coincidental. 

Kurobane increasingly fell out of favor with other KK executives, many of whom accused him of deliberately enabling Team Exusiai's continued activities at the expense of the company's long-term interests. Internal communications repeatedly criticized his willingness to sacrifice personnel, facilities, and revenue in pursuit of what he described as a "complete narrative."

After several years of repeated encounters, Team Exusiai had established the trust of numerous former KK assets, many of whom voluntarily provided intelligence on the organization's operations. The most recently identified collective, designated "Team Melody", was established in 2023 within Tokyo. The group seems to be a direct continuation of Team Exusiai's original motivation of dismantling GoI activities within the country.
