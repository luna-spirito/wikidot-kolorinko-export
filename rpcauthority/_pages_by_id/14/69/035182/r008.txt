------

+++ Containment Protocols:

RPC-645 is presently considered uncontainable. Containment efforts are instead directed toward monitoring manifestations, recovering interview transcripts, and minimizing disruption to Authority operations. Security personnel are to identify the intended recipient from the envelope's exterior and notify them of its arrival.

Investigations into the identity and whereabouts of its apparent creator and operator, designated PoI-6566 ("Ophelia C."), are ongoing.

------
+++ Description:
[[include component:image-block name=https://images.pexels.com/photos/4198074/pexels-photo-4198074.jpeg|caption= PoI-6566 seen through RPC-645|width=250px|align=right]]

RPC-645 is a collection of animated paper envelopes with small green plastic ribbons glued to them, with the signature "Ophelia C." written in ink on the back, alongside an assigned address. The object can reach its designated address by flapping like a pair of wings. Capturing these letters is difficult, as they appear to automatically avoid anyone they were not addressed to.

The insides of RPC-645 consist of a random assortment of drawings, newspaper clippings, and pages taken from various books and other pieces of paper media. No coherent message or pattern has been found among these contents. After it reaches its recipient, the contents of these papers disappear, leaving them blank.

After its intended recipient takes hold of RPC-645, the anomaly will interview them, asking a variety of personal questions. When asked about the purpose of these interviews, RPC-645 will either claim to be part of a journalistic report for a news agency or that it is collecting stories for inspiration for its novels.

Throughout the interview, the blank sheet of paper will record the conversation, transcribing it in handwriting without the use of a typewriter or other writing instrument. Once the interview has concluded, the paper will return to the envelope before demanifesting via spontaneous combustion.

RPC-645 is believed to serve as a means of communication for "Ophelia C." (designated PoI-6566), who appears to exercise direct control over RPC-645. If a hole is punctured through the envelope or any paper associated with RPC-645, PoI-6566 can briefly be observed through the opening, regardless of the angle from which it is viewed. The anomaly will automatically demanifest shortly afterward.

After the interview, PoI-6566 returns a copy of the original transcript to the appropriate mail address in around 1-2 weeks.
