++ The Nature of Psychics

Inquiring one hundred psychics about the mechanisms of their paranormal abilities will produce an equal amount of different answers, none of which stand up to scrutiny. In truth, few psychics understand the source of their talent. Therefore, replicating any psychic’s specific influence through heredation or training has proven futile; it is utterly unique to each individual. Some of these methods may outwardly appear similar to traditional occult techniques, but are set apart upon scientific observation. 

The Authority has come to understand psychic abilities as ontostratic[[footnote]]**//Ontostrata//:** Umbrella term describing all facets of supra-physical ontology (Infoplane/sphere, Sea of Forms, Memetic/Anti-Memetic Chemistry) as a holistic, interconnected apparatus.[[/footnote]] interruptions in the standard flow of conceptualization & creation. While an idea would normally diffusely manifest in the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/memotics-hub Infoplane], then flow through the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-632 Sea of Forms] in its ideal, perfect state, and finally achieve physical identity through the process of its creation, psychics can manipulate, skip and/or reverse this interaction between mind and reality. Indeed, some psychics can even palpate this interplay within minds that aren't their own, thus operating as [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/memetics-analysis memetic] agents. 

Joseph B. Raine of the Parapsychological Association explains psychic abilities as divided into two branches, those of psychokinesis and extra-sensory perception (ESP). 
* **Psychokinesis**: //Outwards Psychism// (telekinesis, teleportation, levitation, biokinesis, automatic writing), performed by overlaying the Sea of forms onto the physical plane,  superseding flawed acts of creation.
* **ESP**: //Inwards Psychism// (mediumship, remote viewing, psychometry, dowsing, astral projection), performed by directly witnessing the Infoplane, in opposition to the physical.

After the founding of the Authority’s own Department of Parapsychology, this model was developed to include a subtype distinct from Raine’s original classification:
* **Empathics**: //Sidewards Psychism// (telepathy, pathokinesis,[[footnote]]Pathokinesis can be further divided into: aggression- odi- phobi- and amokinesis.[[/footnote]] mesmerism), performed by implanting memetics into foreign minds through their connection to the Ontostrata.

In general, all psychic abilities are performed by expression of the psychic's qualia onto perceived reality. This manifests as eccentric views on the mechanisms of reality, ultimately resulting in the eccentric forms of ontostratic relations listed above.

It is as such that many known psychics are famous.[[footnote]]Daniel Douglas Home, A.W Underwood, Janet Lee, Ingo Swann, Miss Cleo are examples of psychics well-known to the public.[[/footnote]] They have therefore proven a hindrance to the veil, and are to be treated according to the I, I, I and U model.

A common practice among prominent psychics is to create groups of laymen organized around their abilities. [[footnote]]Richard, A. ++ Psychism; the Universal Model
A paradigm shift overturned the parapsychological field when Dr. Wolfgang Metzger proposed that //qualia//, the metaphorical fuel of psychics, exists in all sapient beings, however latent. Psychics are therefore only separated from the normal man by the ability to express qualia, not by innate supremacy or talent.

The idea of 'latent qualia', while initially rejected by the parapsychological community, gained traction within various French-artistic groups in the aftermath of the Great War. Further development by both Metzger and these groups resulted in the so-far only reliable methods of reconstructing psychic influence: the VidiCap and Creative Expression. 

----

To contemporary parapsychologists, there are three types of psychic actors:

1. The natural, born psychic: who lacks the barriers of mind that maintain qualia within the mind. 
2. The artificial, created psychic: the normal man whose qualia has been forced out. 
3. The tool-using, **not** psychic: who uses the VidiCap or creative expression to deliver qualia. 

All three actors can practice qualia recruitment; the usage and metamorphosis of the fellow's qualia to amplify and enforce the self's worldview, albeit in various manifestations.

++ The Mystic Age
Up until the turmoil of the first world war, psychics were seen as natural concentrations of universal power. Their truths were scarcely questioned, and psychics thrived as directors of human belief, culture and development.

[[=]]
+++++ Pythia and the Temple of Apollo
[[/=]]
[[include component:image-block
|name=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fa/John_William_Waterhouse_-_Consulting_the_Oracle_-_Christie%27s.jpg/1920px-John_William_Waterhouse_-_Consulting_the_Oracle_-_Christie%27s.jpg
|align=center
|caption=Oracle's Prophecy]]
One of the most powerful titles of the classical world was Pythia, the high priestess and oracle of Delphi. Delphic oracles were believed to shower penitents with prophecies and divination directly from Mount Olympus and its gods, attributing vast and immediate authority to the position. Pythia issued from the Temple of Apollo, one of the most recognized religious institutions of the antique period. 

The oracles were of the first psychic-like phenomena studied by the Auctoritas Imperitus. The infancy of the field led to much confusion regarding the existence of the thaumaturgic/psychic distinction, and we remain unaware if the oracles were entirely psychic, despite operating through similar means.

[[=]]
+++++ Nostradamus and the preservation of //Les Prophéties//
[[/=]]
A controversial figure, Nostradamus was an active futurewriter in 16th century France. He produced over 6000 predictions routinely published in sets of almanacs. Nostradamus was accused by the church for practicing magic, but was not prosecuted due to a lack of evidence. 

His prophecies have been heavily studied, republished and translated since their release, and the greater efforts of suppressing psychic fame in the 21st century have done little to alleviate their influence. The prophecies have caused many instances of cyclic history, and their accuracy is therefore oftentimes denounced as a Barnum effect by skeptics. 

> **Accusal of Michel de Nostradame**
> 
> Scribe Michel de Nostradame writes of the future. Fellow catholics have begun to turn towards his almanacs. Your Holiness, he may speak with forbidden tongue, I fear.
> 
> //-Letter of concern sent to Pope Clement VII by an unknown poster-//

The Nostradamus example became vital for deepening psychic understanding; by disproving previous misconceptions. The Auctoritas at this point still classified psychic power as a form of extramission, however the persistence of Nostramdamus’ influence post-mortem called this into question.

[[=]]
+++++ Helena Blavatsky and the Theosophical Society
[[/=]]
[[include component:image-block
|name=https://images.theconversation.com/files/524497/original/file-20230504-25-s5uehz.jpg?ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=45&auto=format&w=1000&fit=clip 
|align=right
|caption=Helena Blavatsky

Many of the fundamental mystic and spiritualist concepts studied by the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/the-bureau-of-acquisition#toc3 Prometheus Primogenitus] were developed by Helena Blavatsky and following Theosophical leaders. Claiming to be divinely inspired, Blavatsky formed in the late 19th century the Theosophical society; an organization dedicated to studying comparative religion and reach universal truths under notions of hidden, ancient secrets waiting to be exposed. 

Blavatsky led a nomadic life, traveling east and accumulating excotic occultism previously foreign to the western world. It is during this time she claims to have met the Masters; emanations of the esoteric and bestowers of knowledge. Later analysts have hypothesized a form of astral projection to have assisted Blavatsky in her travels, as well as being an explanation to these purported meetings.

After returning to Europe, Blavatsky compiled her knowledge into numerous books. Her bibliography was one of the first to recognize the multi-faceted nature of the paranormal, contributing heavily to an improved understanding of both the practised occult and the innate psychism. 

The Theosophical Society was founded to continue aggregating esoterics, and attracted numerous mystics that further engrained Europe as a hub of occult study. This coincided well with the Authority’s semi-recent conversion from the Auctoritas - which was greatly inspired by the Society to observe anomalous phenomena, and therefore psychism, through a critically secular lens.

++ The Transitional Period

[[=]]
+++++ Grigori Rasputin’s infiltration of the Russian Imperial Family
[[/=]]
[[include component:image-block
|name=https://cldnr.prod.webx.talpa.digital/talpa-network/image/fetch/f_webp,c_limit,w_3840,r_8/https://images.ctfassets.net/3p0bn61n86ty/7z4DbFAh1g9FMuzdBS5kOF/dd9cd8cc41b749be7ba21723e3452c4a/shutterstock_248215210.jpg

The network which lifted Grigori Rasputin from peasantry to an imperial influence originated from belief in his stature as a faith healer. Presence in his vicinity,  companionship and predominantly physical contact with Rasputin purportedly cured illnesses and banished corruption. As such, he became affixed as a mandatory presence for the health of and by the Imperial family. 

Unfortunately for Rasputin, his presence became conflated with the failures of Russian governance at the time. Therefore, after resisting multiple toxins, Rasputin was shot by noble conservatives while fleeing, and he died on the city streets. The Bolsheviks portrayed the assassination as a revolutionary act against individual consolidation of power and legitimacy. 

Certain ACI investigations reveal Rasputin to have been a posthumous inspiration and object of study for the Iron Initiate. Some Parapsychologists suggest his origin in peasantry to have influenced the character of the following Soviet psychic project: the Proletarskiy Dom. Nevertheless, Rasputin shows that psychics have an affinity for social upheaval, which both enforces and destabilizes their position.

[[=]]
+++++ German wartime occultism
[[/=]]
The German Empire of the Central Powers was not the first to utilize psychics for combat purposes, and the many attempts before it failed as the mania of battle is proven to be too hectic of an environment for most psychics to exert their influence. However, few successful specimens, such as the Siebte Pest (Seventh Plague, a combat psychic who left numerous survivors and allowed rumors of himself to spread within the allied ranks as a force of infernal terror), taught the Germans how to best harness the power of psychics. 

Goebbels and the Third Reich propaganda machine lent itself well to [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/gard-hub GARD]’s experiments with combat psychics during WW2. Inspired by Armanist thought,[[footnote]]An Austrian occult tradition partly inspired by Theosophy, it was adopted by the nationalist Völkist movement and contributed to the rise of ethnic supremacy in the eventual Third Reich.[[/footnote]] and supported by occultists such as Karl Maria Wiligut, Hitler sponsored internal and external promotion which equipped the Wehrmacht with outwards psychic abilities for offensive and defensive plays, as well as inwards abilities for intelligence and surveillance, among whom was Ludwig Straniak. 

Aryanist ideology embedded within the German population a belief in ethnic superiority, which was in some cases used to signify supernatural abilities exclusive to Aryans. Further promoting the psychics as natural expressions of Aryan dominance, it eliminated much skepticism regarding the authenticity of psychism.

While the German Empire sewed the seeds for anti-psychic groups,[[footnote]]Monise, P. //French Artistry after the Battle of Verdun; A study of people-based psychism and the birthplace of L'Académie du Véritable Art//. Psychology Dept. [[/footnote]] the portrayal of psychics by GARD was the final nail in the coffin. Many psychics lost global fame, and became dependent on more niche groups to sustain their influence. Certain regions, predominantly French mobs, began harassing and persecuting known psychics, claiming no single individual bears the right to dictate the direction of humanity.

Prominent French artists dabbling in Dadaism, readymade, and found art came together after WW1 to postulate the //Psychic Constant//; a theory claiming the total psychic influence possible at any given time to be constant, and that psychics concentrate this power and steal it from the general populace. The theory gained traction, and remains a monolith of French-psychic relations.

The French art-niches which popped up after WW1 and defined the Psychic Constant grew even closer during WW2. With the early theorems of Wolfgang Metzger,[[footnote]]Metzger was not directly involved in these groups. However, his idea of “latents” found itself in them, aligning nicely with the idea of the Psychic Constant.[[/footnote]] these artists managed to emit psychic influence through artistic expression. After an eventual growth in size and increase in production of anomalous art, they garnered the attention of many anti-psychic French initiatives. 

In order to de-escalate the situation, these diffuse groups who had united under the Psychic Constant formed an organization dedicated to three main ideas:

> //To distribute the sum of the Psychic Constant in order to prohibit the culmination and monopolization of psychic influence by an oligarchy of people.//

> //To make reality our canvas.//

As such, an institution teaching psychism through creative expression was born. It is believed to have been one of the founding schools for the conglomerate of anomalous artistry known as: //[http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/academy-hub L'Académie du Véritable Art]//.

[[/=]]
These national scandals set the scene for [[[Site-074]]] to adopt an eccentric method of psychic synthesis: the Memory Wax project. It resided in the belief that the primary hindrance of artificial psychism were minds “tainted” by a set-in-stone, qualia-poor worldview. As such, several volunteers within Site-074 Department of Psychology[[footnote]]C&D Resources and the Office of Ethics and Review barred the use of CSD-class personnel for speculative paranormal development.[[/footnote]] were prescribed a series of dosages consisting of repeating [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/viderics-guideb videric] and amnestic substances. 

The head researchers of the project believed that this would allow progressive erasure of psychic-incompatible worldviews unreachable by solely amnestics, using the mechanism of viderics networking with native engrams[[footnote]]To current research, engrams are networks of neurons whose firing represents recall and remembrance of a memory.[[/footnote]] and allowing the totality to be banished. Multiple Shaved Purities were created, and some even managed to re-develop fine motor functions. Of these specimens, those turned into psychics proved ineffective; as they manifested seemingly frivolous psychic influences.[[footnote]]Three main abilities were observed: A white light emerging from the eyes, invisibility in mirrors and other indirect non-lens passages of light, as well as minor anomalous suppression of watched objects.[[/footnote]] The Memory Wax project was forced to shut down, and Site-074 Board of Site Directors is as of writing facing gross negligence charges by the OEaR on behalf of widowed families.

After both witnessing and experiencing the failures of artificial psychism, the Parapsychological sciences of the Authority began to stagnate. Laboratory based study of psychism showed itself not only futile, but frequently dangerous. This begot critical review of previous conceptions, and sewed the seeds for the eventual I, I, I and U.

In the end, no attempt to elevate a normal individual to psychic agency and stature has succeeded. However, modern trials to create a newborn psychic spearheaded by bioengineering sculpturers such as [[[The Church of Malthus]]] and [[[Kabushiki Kawaii]]] show promise.

++ The I, I, I, and U
The Authority's relationship with psychism is complex, and various schools of thought exist. Some argue that direct persecution and containment of psychic based anomalies is a futile and unethical pursuit, while others believe that psychics must be treated as any other threat to the veil; with research and imprisonment. Generally, the former perspective is more prevalent. 

It has led to the protocol known as the I, I, I and U: Isolate, Ignominy, Individualize and see Unity; an approach to psychics that specifically targets their tendencies on the conscious gestalt. A focus on such elements has predominantly shifted the science of Parapsychology from laboratory-based quantitative methods, to the more qualitative disciplines of anthropology, game theory and crowd psychology.

The Department of Parapsychology’s main labor. We profile Damocles’ means of power and psychic abilities. Common is the description of Damocles’ purported psychism; in, out, and sideward.
