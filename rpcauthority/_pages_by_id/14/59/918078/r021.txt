++ ###FFA500|**Containment Protocols:**##

The containment cell of RPC-605 is to be maintained using materials recovered from the Vilomah Black Mountain facility. This containment cell is the most reliable method of halting RPC-605’s anomalous effects. If RPC-605 is ever to be moved from his chamber, he must be required to wear a specialized suit constructed using Black Mountain alloys. RPC-605 is forbidden from directly deciding visitation requests, diet, or personal items; instead, staff will pre-plan any of these out to prevent RPC-605 from warping reality.

Due to RPC-605’s anomalously induced multiple personality disorder, medical coverage has been extended to support RPC-605 in the realm of psychology. His current therapist, Dr. Cortes, is housed on site and is expected to visit with him at least twice a day.     

++ ###FFA500|**Descrption:**##     

RPC-605 is an Asian male. Their legal name is Akeno Abe, formerly an employee of the Authority. They are currently 51 years old as of July 13, 2025, the date of the article's publication. However, Mr. Cortes exhibits anomalous properties that not only overlap physical space, but also his mind, with concepts and places from different timelines.  As such, RPC-605 suffers from false memories of alternate lives. When first admitted to screening, RPC-605 was diagnosed as psychotic; yet, he is now considered relatively rehabilitated.

> ++ __PERSONAL HISTORY__
> [[/=]]
> 
> Uppbringing (1974-1991): Akeno Abe was born to a stable immigrant family living in the state of Maine. Throughout most of his youth, Akeno would go exploring abandoned buildings with his friends. One day, he'd somehow sneaked his way past the security perimeter into Black Mountain Base during an RPC raid. RPC personnel would find Mr. Abe unconscious in one of the facility's rooms. After a background check, they were amnesticized and returned to their parents. After this point, their life would continue normally. At the age of 15, he assisted his father's handyman repair service by performing both physical and socializing with employees. Despite his age, many workers in the company came to enjoy Abe's company and saw him as a valuable asset rather than a nuisance.        
> 
> Successful Career (1992-2001): When Mr. Abe turned 18, he inherited his father's business. At that time, Mr. Abe senior had unfortunately died of Colorectal Cancer. In hindsight, Akeno Abe believed his father had worked him from such a young age as a form of teaching—a passing on of knowledge from one generation to the next. In new hands, the business did as well as it had been prior. No significant changes would occur for Mr. Abe and his company until RPC contracted the small business for some minor, non-containment-related work on a front company that had major plumbing and electrical issues. Mr. Abe's work was of such high quality and affordability that it gained the attention of the Containment Division. Mr. Corte's company was bought shortly after as a front company, and he, along with his crew, was brought on board as a member of the RPC Authority.           
> 
> RPC Career (2002 - 2019): With a history of being a considerably skilled worker, it didn't take long for Mr. Abe to gain a reputation as a well-respected engineer among his colleagues. In time, he'd even try his hand at becoming an elected representative for the Maintenance Union, which he barely won. It is unknown why, but Mr. Abe submitted a transfer request to Site-014 shortly after his loss, where he took the role of a janitor. Even without official authority, many Site-014 personnel still considered Mr. Abe an unofficial Union Representative, at least among the other Containment personnel at the site. Life would carry on for Mr. Abe up until 04:37 on June 13th, 2019, when a five-kiloton nuclear bomb was detonated within Site-014.

++ ###FFA500|**Discovery Log:**##

Akeno Abe would be presumed dead following the days after the bombing. However, a rescue team would discover him, alive and intact. Somehow, the physical space around Mr. Abe was untouched by the blast, but rather, modified into a clear gelatinous gel with pressure absorption like nothing on earth. While considered a miracle by some, this is the moment the effects of RPC-605 began to manifest. Mr. Abe would claim to be a traveler from another timeline, searching for someone. While trying to calm down Mr. Abe, multiple etherial copies were detected on infrared, manifesting around Mr. Abe. The variants seemed normal, but a few outliers were documented, such as a neanderthal, an officer of the RCPA, and a being that vaguely resembled a Howler.    

RPC-605 was forcefully given a sedative and transported to a rehabilitation center. At the time, it was believed Mr. Abe was suffering from a form of PTSD induced dissociation after losing everyone he once knew at Site-014. However, it became clear over time that Mr. Abe truly believed himself to be an interuniversal being. While Researchers agreed that Mr. Abe was indeed a conduit for multiple universes, his travels had left him psychologically damaged. In addition, he kept calling out to a female figure that we have yet to find.
