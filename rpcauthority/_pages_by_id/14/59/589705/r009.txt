**Containment Protocols:** RPC-705 is to be stored in a 5x3 containment room in Site-015's Habitat Containment Wing. A tracking chip has been implanted in RPC-705 to monitor its current whereabouts. RPC-705 may roam the facility freely when active. Personnel with a history of repeat animal or child abuse are not to be assigned stations within Site-015's West Wing. 

[[collapsible show="+ RESTRICTED: ASF, LEVEL 3/705 AND LEVEL 4 PERSONNEL ONLY" hide="- ACCESS GRANTED"]]
The following revisions to RPC-705's containment protocols were approved by the Office of Ethics and Review:

RPC-705's properties has allowed for streamlining of screening procedures for Site-015's duty roster. Personnel caught by RPC-705 are to be questioned and reassigned to CSD duty if found guilty.
[[/collapsible]]

**Description:** RPC-705 is the corpse of a domestic dog, believed to be a golden retriever. RPC-705 appears to be in an early stage of decomposition, showing signs of bloating and decaying flesh. A collar with an attached locket remains affixed to RPC-705's neck containing a photo of a domestic dog sitting next to two children, one male and one female,[[footnote]] Later identified as the late ███ and ██████ ██████, aged 6 and 8 at the time of their disappearance. [[/footnote]] captioned  //Spike and the Kids//. Attempts to remove the collar result in RPC-705 becoming animate and aggressive.

RPC-705 remains otherwise inert until the life of a child[[footnote]] Between the years of 0 and 15. [[/footnote]] or pet within 1 kilometer of RPC-705 is under threat. RPC-705 will attempt to remove the subject from its current situation, often at its own expense. If a child or pet is under threat from an --adult human-- sapient individual, RPC-705 will attempt to terminate or incapacitate the culprit. It is unknown how RPC-705 discriminates individuals based on these criteria, however, it is not known to err in its attempts to identify "guilty" individuals. 

RPC-705 is capable of reconstituting itself, however, it will never leave a decomposing state. While active, RPC-705 is capable of perceiving its environment despite lacking eyes. Additionally, the entity resumes biological functions despite lacking many organs vital to perform these tasks and has been known to feed upon human remains and small animals such as rodents and birds. Children rescued by RPC-705 perceive the entity as an ordinary golden retriever[[footnote]] This effect is known to occur with photographs and recordings of RPC-705. [[/footnote]] and do not detect any foul odors from RPC-705.

**Discovery:** RPC-705 was discovered in ███████, Nevada following an increase in missing persons cases within a period of two months between 21/2/2█ and 22/4/2█, starting with ███ and █████ ██████ and the ██████ family dog. RPC-705's first recorded encounter was documented by Brandon Henderson, a high school freshman who was assailed by 18 year old Brian Cortez at ███ Alley while Brandon was making his way back home. Brandon was the son of Jonathan Henderson, a local police officer, while Brian was the younger brother of Randall Cortez, a drug trafficker affiliated with a local gang.

Following RPC-705's first recorded appearance, three more people were filed as missing by local authorities. All missing persons were known to have a repeat history of abuse towards children and animals, or were alleged to. The abnormal amount of disappearances prompted the Authority to dispatch MST Charlie-9 ("Countryside Hypocrites") to retrieve RPC-705 before discovering it in an inactive state in the  ███████ County Junkyard. RPC-705 became active but was quickly sedated and relocated to Site-015.

RPC-705 resumed activity ██ hours later when it attempted to terminate Doctor ███████ ████ and various CSD personnel, resulting in 1 casualty and 3 injuries. RPC-705 was tranquilized and remained inert for the remainder of the day. It was later discovered that all victims of RPC-705's attacks had a history of child or animal abuse as adults.

[[collapsible show="+ INPUT CREDENTIALS" hide="- ACCESS GRANTED"]]
The containment of RPC-705 proved to be a source of controversy among Site-015 staff. Prior to entering Authority custody, RPC-705 was responsible for the disappearance of 10 local criminals and likely prevented future crimes against children and animals. Ethical debates surrounding the issue of RPC-705's continued containment continued for weeks. Eventually, the manner was brought to the Office of Ethics and Review by Site Director Hines. Director Hines was initially reluctant to utilize RPC-705 in an official capacity due to past misuse of Utility-class objects by renegade Authority staff, but was persuaded by the staff under his supervision to integrate RPC-705 into site security. Two weeks later, OEaR approved the proposed updates to RPC-705's containment protocols.

RPC-705 has identified 3 problematic employees since the updates to its containment protocols. The first RPC-705-2 instance identified on the premises of Site-015, Junior Researcher Jim Hoyt, was spotted near the containment cell of RPC-███ before Inadvertently activating RPC-705. Researcher Hoyt was interviewed upon completing medical treatment.
