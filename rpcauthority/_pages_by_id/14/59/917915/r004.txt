++ ###FFA500|**Hunting Strategy:**##
[[/=]]

Many dangerous spirits haunt RPC-605-A man and RPC-605-C woman. They stalk Northern Forests of Laurentia. Forest region encompasses Black Mountain, Tall Rock, and Red Valley. Keep young away. Every seven nights, Chieftan and hunting party roam forest. Arm hunter with obsidian spear blessed by warrior spirit.  Seek out picked berries, blood trails, and campfires. Must leave forest before sunfall. Hunting party that does not return, presumed dead.

[[=]]
++ ###FFA500|**Description:**##  
[[/=]]

RPC-605-A is humanoid male Neanderthal that hunts in Tall Rock Region. RPC-605-C is Cro-Magnon female who hunts in Red Valley Region. Both bodies are meek and frail, not a threat to our warriors. Minds are mush. Beleive they are a long-dead race of hominids. Both wander aimlessly, trying to find their other. However, spirit forces surround the two RPC-605s. Spirits are dangerous. They alter landscapes, disrupt the natural order.  

------
[[=]]
++ ###FFA500|**Story:**##
[[/=]]

RPC-605-A was tribal prince of RPC Head Chieftain. He hunts in Northern Forest for fun. Then, abducted by Vilomah sorcerers who lived on Black Mountain. RPC attempted to recover Chieftain's son, but could not find Black Mountain. Obfuscated by evil runes. RPC Chieftain recruit Cro-Magnon nomad warrior. Woman with no name. Good nose she had, best in the Laurentia lands. Promissed her a seven moons feast if son was brought back unharmed. She goes looking for Black Mountain, and returns with Chieftain prince. Both are changed. Haunted by evil spirits.

His son acted unusual, not like himself. RPC Chief Prince grew worried. Unnamed Cro-Magnon woman also acted unusual. Carried herself differently. RPC Shammens believed evil spirits possessed both. Unnamed woman was refused payment and banished. Chief believed Shammens could cure his son. They titled him RPC-605-A, in accordance with the ancient scrolls. That's when the changes began. Chaos consumed village. RPC-605-A escaped into the forests, searching for other women. At the same time, hunting parties were sent out to kill women. Shammens believed her spirit and emotions impacted RPC-605-A's. They dubbed her RPC-605-C, ordered by the ancient scroll. RPC-605-A and RPC-605-C spotted periodically by hunting parties. Each roams different parts of Northern Forest. Neither finds the other. Every day we get closer. Will find both before they find each other.               

            

[[=]]
++ ###FFA500|**Follow Up:**##
[[/=]]

RPC-605-A and RPC-605-C found. Both dead. Die from poison berries. Neither together, both perish, separated by great stretches of forest. Shamans believe [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-605/offset/2 that other world spirits caused death.] Only explanation.
