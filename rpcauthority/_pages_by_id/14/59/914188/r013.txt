[[collapsible show="[+] Display: Former Containment Protocols" hide="[–] Hide: Former Containment Protocols"]]
> **Containment Protocols:** All known instances have been enclosed around soundproof plastic cages and placed in controlled habitats for experimentation. OL-Site-489 has been built in Hibbing, Minnesota, in the general area of RPC-489 crash sites. Nearby officials are to support the narrative of OL-Site-489 being an environmental research station for the Hibbing Rust Hull mine.
> 
> Current research endeavors are under the directive of Dr. Kurt Riese. Testing is focused on the implementation of artificial RPC-489-A replication as a method of subduing for dangerous organic anomalies. Researchers are not allowed inside enclosures during testing or at times when the instance is exposed to air.
> 
> Organic RPC-489 instances are to be incinerated and their remains safely disposed of at the end of allotted research time.
[[/collapsible]]

**Containment Protocols:** The original five RPC-489 instances have been encased in concrete and buried 20m underneath former OL-Site-489 inside a 3m thick lead box. All new discovered instances are to be disposed of and incinerated when possible, with their remains shipped to OL-Site-489 in concrete-filled barrels.

The immediate area around OL-Site-489 is guarded by remote artillery units, and by ASF personnel in a perimeter surrounding the Hibbing Rust Hull mine.

> ##red|**BLANKET TESTING BAN:**## **07/21/2025 -- PRESENT**
> **Authority:** NORTHCOM Command
> **Reasoning:** As a means to prevent the events of July 2025 from repeating, personnel are to be notified of the following: attempts to replicate and/or experiment on RPC-489 instances will result in immediate termination. Unauthorized attempts to approach OL-Site-489 will also result in immediate termination.

[[include component:image-block name=enclosure-4.jpg|caption=RPC-489-4 Habitat / General Flora Enclosure.]]

**Description:** A group of five meteorites composed primarily of wolframite, hollowed-out for use as Helmholtz resonators prior to impact. For all intents and purposes, instances act as vessel flutes when provided with a constant stream of air, producing an oscillating resonance (RPC-489-A) from one of many holes along their surface.

Material within audible range of RPC-489-A will experience accelerated necrosis (or erosion) and cicatrization.[[footnote]]Synthetic material such as plastic is unaffected.[[/footnote]] Lesions take the form of orifices spanning the material's length, and the formation of an inner resonance chamber, should a similar structure not be already present. Organic life will not expire as a result of said injuries inside this area. The effect's range and speed is proportional to the given area's sound exposure.[[footnote]]Defined as the integral, over time, of squared sound pressure.[[/footnote]]

In order to maintain this area, affected mammals will gather around RPC-489 instances and take turns blowing air into its central windway using their hollowed-out organs as biological bellows. At random intervals, they will also press body parts against the instance's holes to increase/decrease its pitch and frequency. Testing shows that prolonged exposure to RPC-489-A will result in permanent memory loss, and the development of dementia. Personal soundproofing (i.e earplugs) is unable to inhibit this particular effect. Recordings of RPC-489-A show no anomalous effects.

-----

**Discovery:** On the 3rd of May, 2025, a cluster of five Near Earth Objects were detected by NASA radars to have changed trajectory unexpectedly into a collision course with Earth. It was asserted that, due to their small size, the NEOs would rapidly disintegrate upon entry. NEOs did not disintegrate upon entry, instead appearing to have remained perfectly intact up to their collision.

Two days following impact, a team of students from the University of Minnesota volunteered to study the meteorites in their landing site around the Hibbing Rust Hull. Students failed to report back to the university after five days following their arrival at Hibbing, prompting police investigation. Subsequently, police officers failed to report back to precinct after confirming their arrival at the area of disappearance.

Subsequent disappearance of state police triggered Authority alarm systems, prompting the discrete deployment of [/protectorate RE-200 Rescue Rangers] into the area as facilitated by state officials. All missing individuals were found gathered around different RPC-489 instances around the area. The cause of the individuals' current state was hypothesized to be sound-based following close examination via remotely-controlled drones. Individuals expired shortly after their removal from the area. Remaining instances were incinerated or covered in Safe-Tissue.[[footnote]]Flexible lead-polymer sheets set to wrap and harden around stationary objects for temporary containment.[[/footnote]]

||~ "IMPACT KILLZONE" INCIDENT REPORT ||

|| **Date:** 07/21/2025 || **Location:** Hibbing, Minnesota || **Sender:** Dir. Cecil (Site-120) ||

||||||**Priority:** ##642C90|**GAMMA-PURPLE**##||

[[include component:image-block name=webs.png|caption=Auditory Killzone - Remote Drone Photograph]]

Following a cease in communication from OL-Site-489 on the 07/21/2025, an ACI team was dispatched to asses the area and its current condition. After confirming the death of all present staff and a breach of containment, NT-100 Neutralization and MST Echo-8 ("Plague Doctors") units were deployed to the assessed location of the escaped anomalies.

Satellite footage shows the affected area to have spanned eight kilometers across the western expanse of the Hibbing Rust Hull mine. Post-report examination identifies the "sheet" to be been composed of spider silk, gelatinous saliva, feces, larvae and dead skin. The expanse was covered in orifices of varying size, with some leading into what was later found to be hollowed out chambers beneath the ground, layered with similar material.

It is currently believe this expanse acted as large colonial form of RPC-489. The sound produced by this instance reached a radius of ~12km around its epicenter. This instance resulted in the discovery of RPC-489's particular effects on sapient beings, and the inability to prevent it through protective gear.

Following several failed attempts by deployed units to harm or disrupt the structure in any capicity, NORTHCOM officials called forth an emergency meeting with US representatives to request the use of military-grade aerial weaponry on national soil to prevent a cascading containment breach into the surrounding settlements.

As negotiations reached closing statements, a flash blizzard began to form over North Minnesota, following a trend of out-of-season weather the area had been subject to for the past few weeks.

While initially able to dislodge or divert away snow and rain, the colonial instance soon became overwhelmed as the blizzard grew more intense. Roughly 40 minutes after formation, the majority of the expanse had been covered in snow, or destroyed as a result of water.

Effects ceased shortly after, and units were able to dispose of the instance through mass incineration and corrosive chemical dispersal as the storm began to wane.

Concurrently with RPC-489's containment breach, AEDF vessels stationed around the asteroid belt began to relay emergency signals at the sudden appearance of an M-type asteroid 243km in diameter, heading for collision with Earth roughly four hours after detection. Spectrographic analysis revealed a high concentration of tungsten on its surface.

AEDFS Bodhi and Excelsior attempted to intercept the asteroid and divert its course with the use of shuttle crafts. Anomalous emergency alerts were relayed across the Inner-System Fleet as the asteroid managed to redirect itself without any visible forms of propulsion following interception.

[/rpc-598 101955 Bennu], too, attempted to intercept the asteroid. Its surface, however, did not appear to sustain any damage as a result of its ionic weaponry. RPC-598-1 continued to try to disrupt the asteroid's trajectory until a near-collision, after which attempts ceased. Whether or not RPC-598/-1 sustained permanent damage is unknown at the time of reporting.

The asteroid continued to evade assault by nearby vessels, as the Terrestrial Fleet and informed political bodies were made aware of the situation and asked to ready nuclear payloads should collision be imminent. Global Directors called forwards a meeting to discuss the usage of Antarctic weaponry should nuclear payloads fail, regardless of possible consequences.

However, as it reached Mars' orbit, the asteroid was caught in its gravitational pull and thrown off-course towards the Sun. The asteroid did not right its trajectory as expected. This event occurred at 5:12 PM, the time at which the colonial RPC-489 instance was overwhelmed by the blizzard.

At 6:44 PM, the asteroid collided with the Sun, being destroyed in the process.

This abnormality has been documented by regular news outlets, but has not drawn any attention into RPC-489. Solar abnormalities are expected to decrease and ultimately stop in the following months.
