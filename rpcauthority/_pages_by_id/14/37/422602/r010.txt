[[span class="listresize"]]Heroes of Justice™[[/span]]

[[include component:rate]]

[[f<image http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/component:icons/beta-orange.png]]

**Registered Phenomena Code:** 572

**Object Class:** Beta-Orange

[[include component:hazards grouped=true|contact=true|mind-regression=true|mind-control=true|ideological=true|emotional=true]]
------

**Containment Protocols:** Forty-eight boxes of RPC-572 are currently in storage at Site-██.  Testing can be done with appropriate authorisation from a Researcher Level Two (2) or higher. RPC-572-1 instances are to be taken to Site-██'s Psychiatric Wing for evaluation and research into a potential method of reversing their effects.

All remaining instances of RPC-572 and their whereabouts are currently being tracked by MST Unit Echo-9 "Toy Soldiers."  Undercover operatives are searching toy store outlets across the US for RPC-572 instances. Instances of RPC-572 are to be seized from the stores and taken into RPC custody.

------

**Description:** RPC-572 is an anonymously outsourced bootleg brand of superhero action figures from Amazing! Co., with the words "Heroes of Justice" printed on the cardboard box in bold letters. Each figure is based on a pre-existing character, repainted or fitted with alternate accessories that crudely differentiate them from the original products. The box contains seven (7) figures in total, hereby identified as RPC-572-A through G (see "Transcript of Known RPC-572-1 Instances" for more information).

When a child (typically within the age range of 3-12 years of age) plays with an instance of RPC-572, they will begin to act out alongside the figure with the perceived persona of the character they're holding. While this initial behaviour is no different from the usual activity of imaginative play typical of children, the actions of the child (now designated as RPC-572-1) will take on drastically different personality traits than before. RPC-572-1 will often show signs of extreme psychological personality fluctuations and conditions, such as DID[[footnote]] Dissociative Identity Disorder  [[/footnote]] and schizophrenia, culminating in the RPC-572-1 subject believing that they are the 'superhero' they are currently playing as. 

While no further anomalous effects have been attributed to individual RPC-572/X instances, all seven figures are known to have distinguishing behavioural traits observed in RPC-572-1 instances using them:

[[collapsible show="View Transcript of Known RPC-572 instances" hide="Close Transcript"]]
* **"Captain Super"** RPC-572-A is a repurposed, DC Superman action figure repainted to have inverse costume colors and with a green, glow-in-the-dark flesh tone.[[footnote]] Its tagline reads, //"The best superhero in the galaxie! To Infinity and Beyond!"// [[/footnote]] When RPC-572-1/A instances take on the 'Captain Super' persona, and tend to display the most life-threatening actions, including attempting to jump off of high places in an effort to '//fly//', swim in deep waters and engage in physical altercations with RPC-572-2 subjects. RPC-572-1/A instances also tend to be the 'leaders' of a group of RPC-572-1 instances and will tend to give out directives to other RPC-572-1 instances who will act accordingly. RPC-572-1/A instances also are most likely to engage in conflict with RPC-572-2 subjects and instigate other known RPC-572-1 to do so.

* **"Night Man:"** RPC-572-B consists of a Batman action figure that has been completely painted black (except for the face and eyes) and lacks the cape.[[footnote]] Its tagline reads, //"He is the Shadow Knight! He is...Night Man!"// [[/footnote]] The majority of actions taken by RPC-572-1/B instances usually involve attempting to scale buildings, driving vehicles, and operating heavy machinery, as well as getting into violent altercations with perceived 'criminals' at night.

* **"Speed Ranger:"** RPC-572-C is a Blue Power Ranger action figure, repainted with a painted red visor, white gloves, and red boots.[[footnote]] Its tagline reads, //"He's super fast AND Super cool!"// [[/footnote]] RPC-572-1/C instances tend to attempt outrunning traffic or pursue moving vehicles on foot or attempt to leap great distances between gaps or drops between tall structures.

* **"The Amazing Bulking Ogre:"** RPC-572-D is an action figure of Marvel's 'The Incredible Hulk' with the torso repainted to resemble the outfit worn by the Dreamworks character, 'Shrek' and renamed 'Amazing Bulking Ogre.'[[footnote]] Its tagline reads, //"Somebody once told him...HE was the strongest hero in the planet!"// [[/footnote]] Individuals classified as RPC-572-1/D act under the delusion of having greater strength than originally perceived, and will attempt to violently attack structures, vehicles, and RPC-572-2 instances. One instance of these delusional displays of '//strength//' occurs when RPC-572-1/D instances will attempt to halt moving vehicles with their own body mass.

* **"Lone Wolf:"** RPC-572-E is a repurposed G.I Joe 'Shipwreck' and the smallest toy within the collection. The figure is made to resemble a frontiersman, complete with a wolf fur cap in place of Shipwreck's original sailor cap.[[footnote]] Its tagline reads, //"No one tells him what to do!"// [[/footnote]] Instances of RPC-572-1/E tend to go missing from their homes and have been discovered in rural locations several kilometers away from their home, apparently having succumbed to the elements, poisoning, or starvation. It's believed that this is due to RPC-572-1/E attempting survival techniques beyond their current understanding, having ingested poisonous plants or raw food. Several crude man-made traps and 'battle tactics' have also been located near the bodies of deceased RPC-572-1/E instances.

* **"Wizard Sword:"** RPC-572-F is a poseable figurine of Goku from the Dragon Ball franchise, fitted with a black vinyl cloak with a small 'Gryffindor' coat of arms upon the left side. The character comes with a small blue clear-plastic katana.[[footnote]] Its tagline reads, //"He is the master of magical Japanese Combat!"// [[/footnote]] Individuals under the "Wizard Sword" persona tend to collect and store weapons, from impromptu tools to acquiring several antique or modern weapons through unknown means, though RPC-572-1/F instances tend to favour curved oriental swords. RPC-572-1/F utilises these weapons against perceived 'criminals' and RPC-572-2 instances, causing the most lethal damage to others out of the other RPC-572-1 instances.

* **"Iron Car Man:"** RPC-572-G is the amalgamation of an //Avengers: Iron Man// and //Transformers "Mech Machines"// action figure that transforms into an 'ARC race car,' repackaged with a blue 'IRON CAR MAN' sticker on its chest.[[footnote]] Its tagline reads, //"Half man, half Car, ALL HERO!!"// [[/footnote]] Active instances of RPC-572-1/G are most likely to be found in the construction of industrial areas, such as factories or vehicle maintenance and/or manufacturing facilities. While these instances also tend to engage in life-threatening stunts and acts of aggression towards RPC-572-2 instances, one of the more notable traits of RPC-572-1/G is the apparent need to //'upgrade'// themselves with mechanical parts taken from the aforementioned areas, and would usually result in fatalities or serious bodily injury or mutilation. If an instance survives this self-augmentation process without sustaining serious injury (usually if said augmentation is a minor one, such as wearing wire mesh as crude armor or wearing metallic piping on their arms), RPC-572-1/G will utilise these crude augmentations as weapons against 'criminals' and RPC-572-2 instances.

[[/collapsible]]

Actions of RPC-572-1 will become increasingly hazardous and life-threatening. Subjects have been reported attempting dangerous stunts and actions with various apparatus and environments such as construction sites and abandoned urban areas. Each subject is observed to be under the delusion that these actions are typical of the cable of their instance's personas. RPC-572-1 subjects have been reported attempting feats such as jumping from tall buildings, stopping traffic with their bodies, and apprehending 'criminals,[[footnote]] This varies as an RPC-572-1 may misunderstand certain acts as 'criminal activity' and attempt to confront them regardless of initial intention. [[/footnote]] operating vehicles (including construction equipment), and engaging in physical fights with both RPC-572-1 and RPC-572-2 instances utilizing a variety of improvised weapons.

RPC-572-2 instances are the closely related/acquainted adults of RPC-572-1 instances, including the parents, teachers, legal guardians, or peers wearing costumes (See Halloween Party Incident below). RPC-572-1 will regard these individuals as antagonistic figures,[[footnote]] Commonly, these individuals are regarded as supervillains by RPC-572-1 instances. [[/footnote]] dubbing them with a variety of elaborate names and titles.[[footnote]] As of yet, none of the noted 'supervillain' names exclaimed by RPC-572-1 instances have been discovered relating to RPC-572, except for one instance, referring to [[[http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-237 |RPC-237]]]. Whether this is an incredible coincidence that these two RPC objects are related somehow is currently unknown. [[/footnote]] RPC-572-1 will become increasingly hostile towards an RPC-572-2 instance, eventually culminating in the RPC-572-1 instance attempting to violently attack RPC-572-2. 

The level of aggressive behavior towards an RPC-572-2 seems to vary but has been theorized to be linked to subtle and usually mundane levels of provocation of an adult towards a child (i.e, asking to do chores, homework, etc.) This aggression increases the RPC-572-1's perception of RPC-572-2 as a 'villain.'

[[=]]
**REPORT DESCRIPTION:**
[[/=]]

Outside of [REDACTED] Elementary School, seven separate instances of RPC-572-1 had been observed gathering outside, appearing to be 'conspiring' with one another as a group, according to several witnesses. Instances appeared wearing crudely constructed 'superhero' costumes while carrying action figures of their respective 'hero' they had dressed up as. 

Reports of the physical state of the RPC-572-1 instances consisted of various minor to moderate injuries, including visible bruises and cuts that were initially assumed to be make-up. The RPC-572-1/E instance and the RPC-572-1/G instance appeared to have the worst inflictions. Witness reports of the RPC-572-1/E instance having a pale complexion and a bloated stomach, with clear liquid discharge oozing from their mouth and nose. 

The RPC-572-1/G, according to bystander testimonies, seemed to have had the most severe damage, having apparently "augmented" various pieces of sharp scrap metal in various wounds in the arms, legs, and chest. Although showing clear signs of physical pain, RPC-572-1/G seemed to either be oblivious or wilfully ignoring the self-inflicted injuries.
All of these physical abnormalities were sighted as "make-up and effects" by witnesses.
-----
[[=]]
**EVENTS TRANSPIRED:**
[[/=]]

__20:34:__ All instances of RPC-572-1 gathered outside of [REDACTED] Elementary School. Exaggerated speech and gestures were also noted, as instances pointed towards the school several times, indicating their “plan.”

__20:37:__ Instances gave a unified "salute" and cheer before running towards the school doors, passing a school custodian tasked as the usher. RPC-572-1/E and RPC-572-1/G were reported to be the slowest of the group, owing to their aforementioned physical complications. 

__20:38:__ Both instances physically apprehended the school custodian worker. RPC-572-1/E pulled out a hunting knife on the civilian, while RPC-572-1/G instances punched the civilian in the side, heavily rupturing both the custodian's right lung and RPC-572-1/G's right arm. The rupture was caused by a large metal spike that had been forcibly inserted into the upper forearm of RPC-572-1/G.

__20:39:__ First reported 911 call after bystanders witnessed the assault. Five of the initial RPC-572-1 instances had entered the school's main gym, where the Halloween Dance was being held.

__20:40:__ Survivor accounts for the RPC-572-1/A instance standing in front of the group, holding out his arm in an exaggerated gesture and aggressively proclaiming the following;
> "Evildoers! We have finally found your Lair of Doom! Now the Heroes of Justice will finally bring you all to justice!"
This in turn prompted mixed reactions, from laughter to overall confusion. Some apparently even clapped and cheered at this supposed 'event.'

__20:41:__ RPC-572-1/A instance was observed becoming highly agitated at this response, and in return engaged the nearest student, assaulting her with a punch, before turning on another.
In response to the assault, the other instances (excluding RPC-572-1/E and RPC-572-1/G, who were still outside) were prompted into attacking both students and teachers, who at this point began to panic, though many were still not aware of the full danger. Some were reported to still be laughing, believing the result to be an elaborate Halloween prank.

__20:41:__ First termination occurs. A literary teacher, 41, was stabbed in the throat by a blade carried by the RPC-572-1/F instance.

__20:42:__ Second termination. RPC-572-1/G. He bled to death as he lay beside the rapidly bleeding body of the 52-year-old school custodian he had assaulted. The custodian would also later be deceased by the time the authorities arrived at the scene.

__20:42:-20:51__ Within the timespan, twenty-seven (27) civilians had been assaulted and injured as they panicked and attempted to escape their attacks. 11 had been in critical condition when the authorities arrived, and nine (9) had been terminated. Injuries include stab wounds, lacerations, and broken necks. One victim had their nasal cartilage forced into their frontal lobe with massive blunt force.
In the main hallway outside of the gym, RPC-572-1/E had expired. His body was discovered leaning against the wall. The cause of death was poisoning from raw food and various toxic chemicals.

__20:52:__ MST Unit Echo-9 arrived on the scene along with other civilian police and paramedics, posing as first-response units. The five surviving instances of RPC-572-1 had exited the school in a "frenzied" manner, continuing to pursue and attack the fleeing civilians. Police and Echo-9 attempted to apprehend the instances but were themselves attacked in return, resulting in a slashed throat and femoral artery of two police officers by RPC-572-1/F and RPC-572-1/B respectively. 

__20:52:__ Echo-9 agents attempted to forcibly apprehend with tasers, but two agents were assaulted from behind by RPC-572-1/D, resulting in Agent ████ having her neck broken. Captain ████████ in response terminated RPC-572-1/D with a .45 handgun.

__20:53:__ After resisting substantial physical attempts at subduction, including tasers, all instances of RPC-572-1 suddenly expired simultaneously. Autopsy reports indicated that the deaths were caused by extreme stress, leading to cardiac arrest.

All survivors and families of the RPC-572-1 instances have received/are currently receiving psychiatric therapy with the civilian RPC Authority Psych-Trauma Response Unit. All civilian personnel affected by the incident are currently being monitored.

Captain ████████ of MST Echo-9 has been debriefed and demoted by Site Command for his actions during the incident, including the slow response time, inability to act during the first signs of a potential anomalous event, and the termination of the RPC-572-1/D instances.

The 'Halloween Incident' has since gone on record to show future MST Units the importance of sighting and discreetly containing possible anomalous threats while assessing the local populace's risk for civilian harm

[[collapsible show="View Addendum-01:" hide="Close Addendum"]]

It has since been discovered that RPC-572 can spread its influence when RPC-572-1 subjects successfully attempt to engage other children in playing with the figures, and RPC-572 has no anomalous effect on adults, even those with mental deficiencies and learning disabilities.

However, it has also been noted that as of yet, there has been no discernible method to reverse the psychological effects in RPC-572-1 subjects, and an obsessive need to hold onto an RPC-572 figure has been observed, and an RPC-572-1 instance will make any attempt to reclaim said figure when parted with it. Although the prolonged separation between RPC-572-1 and their RPC-572 figure has proven only to worsen the psychological effects and cause the subjects to become increasingly mentally unstable, proving to be a considerable threat to their person when not constantly observed and apprehended at any sign of self-inflicted harm. 

To date, research into a possible treatment/cure is still ongoing.
