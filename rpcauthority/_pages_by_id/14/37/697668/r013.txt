**Registered Phenomena Code:** 809

**Object Class:** Beta-Yellow (Neutralized)

**Hazard Types:** Sensory

----

**Archived Containment Protocols:** A three meter tall fence has been installed around the perimeter of RPC-809's active zone, deemed OL-Site-809. Due to RPC-809's specific activation prerequisites, further containment is considered unnecessary.

----

**Description:** RPC-809 is a square "No Bicycling" sign, fastened to the ground by a metal pole near [REDACTED].

Subjects riding bicycles within an approximately 40 meter radius of RPC-809 will report untraceable bodily pain and discomfort. Effects are known to last anywhere from several minutes to an entire week and seem to be exacerbated by the duration of time for which RPC-809's conditions are met.

Further research is ongoing.

[[collapsible show="[+] RPC-809 Experiment Log" hide="[-] RPC-809 Experiment Log" hideLocation="both"]]
> **Trial 809-01**
> ----
> **Materials:** One L-1 CSD subject, supplied with a bicycle and a helmet.
> 
> **Procedure:** Subject was instructed to circle around RPC-809 for 60 seconds.
> 
> **Result:** Subject reported groin pain for the next 15 minutes.

> **Trial 809-02**
> ----
> **Materials:** One L-1 CSD subject, supplied with a bicycle.
> 
> **Procedure:** Subject was instructed to hold the bicycle without riding it for the duration of the trial.
> 
> **Result:** No immediate effect. After ten minutes, subject requested the test to cease, citing an inexplicable sensation of being "watched and unwelcome".

> **Trial 809-03**
> ----
> **Materials:** One L-2 CSD subject, supplied with a bicycle, a helmet, and a bottle of water.
> 
> **Procedure:** Subject was instructed to circle around RPC-809 for 20 minutes.
> 
> **Result:** Trial was aborted nine minutes in, as subject complained of vertigo, nausea, and shortness of breath. Subject vomited during relocation back to Site-███. After one week of observation, all effects subsided without incident.

> **Trial 809-04**
> ----
> **Materials:** One L-2 CSD subject, supplied with a can of spray paint.
> 
> **Procedure:** Subject was instructed to vandalize RPC-809.
> 
> **Result:** Subject recoiled in shock upon attempt, reporting a jolt of pain across his body. Trial was aborted.

> **Trial 809-05**
> ----
> **Materials:** 30 L-2 CSD subjects, supplied with bicycles and helmets.
> 
> **Procedure:** Subjects were instructed to circle around RPC-809 for two minutes.
> 
> **Result:** //See Incident 809-01.//
[[/collapsible]]

----

**Incident 809-01:** One minute and 29 seconds into Trial 809-05, RPC-809 unexpectedly detonated. 12 subjects were injured by flying shrapnel. The debris shows no anomalous properties, and RPC-809 is considered neutralized.
