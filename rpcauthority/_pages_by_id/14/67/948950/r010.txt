**Containment Protocols:** Throughout the months of December and January, MST Whiskey-9 "Hightime" [[footnote]]Mobile Specialized Team deployed to handle holiday associated/adjacent anomalous items.[[/footnote]] are to be deployed on active search duty for manifestations of RPC-891. Witnesses must be amnesticized and leaks suppressed. Recovered shells are delivered to the nearest site capable of containing Alpha-level anomalous objects.


Each item must have its emitted tone recorded and registered in relation to this file. As multiple researchers have proposed usage of the anomaly during workplace festivities, the choice has been relegated to the jurisdiction of individual sites.

**Description:** RPC-891 denotes the appearance of a //Strombus pugilis// (West Indian fighting conch) shell within the abdomens of human cadavers. A wide-range study arrived at the following findings regarding manifestation: 

* An average of 1000 occurrences each year.
* 93% of instances are coupled with individuals aged 65 or older at the time of death.
* All deaths occurred due to natural causes.
* Individuals expired on one of the following dates: {{ADVENT SUNDAYS, DEC 24, DEC 25, DEC 26, JAN 6, JAN 7}}

The conch shells exhibit audio-spatial manipulation; transforming ambient noise into complex musical acoustics. These sounds are heard if the shell's aperture is brought to a subject's ear, and can be recorded by precise condenser microphones. Each harmony is unique to a given instance.

**Addendum 2 - Christmas Park:** 

On 01/12/2025 an advertisement was published online for a Christmas-themed event taking place in Qinnqua, Greenland on 25/12/2025. It consisted of a video displaying different attractions in "Christmas Park", which marketed itself towards adults. One highlighted experience was the "Thousand Voice Carol"; a musical concert promising "The Christmas Carol of your childhood's wonder." During this segment, a large pile of RPC-891 conch shells was visible. The event was suppressed by accusing it of having an exaggerated scale, supported by illegitimate users accusing the advertisement of including AI-generated content.

Further investigation revealed major financial activity within the region attributed to DreamsDates.[[footnote]]DreamsDates is a a pseudo Group of Interest which produces and organizes anomalous items or events linked to various holidays under the self-proclaimed goal of "Making Magic, Day by Day". Despite the organization leaving paper trails, no physical location or person has been found orchestrating their practices. Debate is ongoing regarding whether DreamsDates qualifies as a GoI, as numerous records chronologically align it with Amazing! Co. activity. Therefore, in spite of the more traditional methods, some consider DreamsDates to be a subsidiary of A!Co.[[/footnote]] Large sums of money from known DreamsDates company accounts had been transferred to a private person under the name of Arav Vihaan[[footnote]]Mr. Vihaan would refuse to answer when addressing him by his legal name, preferring the calling "Babi Noeli".[[/footnote]] (PoI-0891). Among inconspicuous purchases of festive decorations and signed contracts with construction companies, numerous cash withdrawals and transferences to accounts under unknown actors were found. 

An operation to retrieve the RPC-891 shells was organized for 09/12. However, further research uncovered the previously undetermined accounts to belong to various Demonymless fronts. The involvement of a potentially dangerous GoI resulted in postponing the mission in order to allocate MST resources. Facial analysis of the elf-dressed actors in the original advertisement matched them to known Demonymless agents. It was therefore suspected that the group was equipped with DreamsDate-supplied anomalous armaments. MST Whiskey-9 was chosen to raid the Christmas Park compound on 22/12.
