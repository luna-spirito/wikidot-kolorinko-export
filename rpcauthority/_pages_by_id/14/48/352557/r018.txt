++++ **Abstract:** 
RPC-714, otherwise known as Carcinmetalloplasia,[[footnote]] //Carcin// (cancer) //metallo-// (metal) //plasia// (growth) [[/footnote]] is a cancerous disease characterized by the ability of subjects to transform their bones into fully functional weapons. This condition results from years of study by the Church of Malthus to create a series of "Weapon-Vampires."
------

++++ **Safe Handling and Usage:** 

RPC-714 stem cells are preserved in dedicated storage units with an internal temperature of -80°C. Biology department staff are tasked with reviewing the stem cells for genetic stability weekly. Candidates for RPC-714-1 must conduct comprehensive physical and mental health assessments to ensure the subjects' suitability to handle RPC-714 injection.

RPC-714-1 subjects are confined in standard humanoid containment chambers equipped with sedative morphine implants for the safety of the subject and on-site personnel. The subject must follow a strict dietary protocol and regular medical and psychological evaluations.

++++ **Description:** 

RPC-714 is a modified cell infection that morphs a human subject’s bones through the synthesis of metallic compounds on the bone matrix. This process results in the formation of a dense and compact structure called the //Carcinmetalloplastic Osteoid// {{(fig. 1.1)}}. This transformation condenses the subject’s skeletal and muscular mass, heightening their body’s ability to rapidly regenerate through the temperament of malignant cell proliferation.  

RPC-714 is caused by an injection of osteoprogenitor cells[[footnote]] Stem cells in the bone that play a prodigal role in bone repair and growth. [[/footnote]] carrying RPC-714 RNA templates into the subject's spinal cord. Depending on which bone RPC-714 targets, full systemization may take from 2 weeks to 6 months. Completely synthesized subjects are designated as RPC-714-1.

In the event that RPC-714-1 reaches a certain threshold of pain, the subject activates the trigger-phase. The trigger-phase is a transformational phase where the subject’s bones expand, fracture, and transform into a variety of sharp, pointed, or blunt objects that replicate the appearance of existing weapons {{(fig. 1.2)}}. Once the trigger-phase is over, RPC-714 will morph back to its original form, suffering severe hypovolemia[[footnote]] Hypovolemia refers to a state of decreased blood and extracellular fluid volume in the body. [[/footnote]] in the process.

The trigger phase shifts the structure of the brain. This brain deformation heightens RPC-714-1’s aggression and contorts their hypothalamus and pituitary gland, resulting in involuntarily laughing and/or crying.

The trigger-phase necessitates a substantial amount of biomass.[[footnote]] Mainly myofibrillar proteins such as actin and myosin, and metals such as iron2+. [[/footnote]] As a consequence of its increased hunger, a significant portion of RPC-714-1 instances are innately driven by an insatiable craving for human flesh and blood.[[footnote]] As any kind of meat and blood can facilitate RPC-714-1's muscular and skeletal regeneration, there is no substantial reason for this phenomenon. The leading theory behind this craving might derive from the fact that human flesh is the most abundant kind of meat in an active war zone. [[/footnote]]


[[collapsible show="[+] List of variant templates" hide="[-] List of variant templates"]]
Carcinmetalloplasia subjects can only materialize one kind of weapon depending on the genetic template they fused with. Some ballistic variant templates exist as well. Ballistic protrusions lack clips or magazines, instead relying on an anomalous enzyme called "PyroCatalase" to synthesize gunpowder in the body. These RPC-714-1 instances use waste, fat and condensed blood as substitutes for bullets. Despite being composed of organic material, it appears to be just as effective as regular ammo.

------
++++ Overview of the Physiology of RPC-714
------
[[/=]]
**(a) Preface:** RPC-714, also known as Carcinmetalloplasia, is a phenomenon characterized by the transformation of bone into a metal-like substance. This document aims to provide a comprehensive explanation of the physiology RPC-714.

------

**(b) Synthesis:** To achieve a full synthesis, a dose of 4.0 million stem cells per 60 ml is required. These cells are injected into the hypothalamus to help facilitate the distribution of RPC-714 throughout the body.

The hypothalamus enables RPC-714 to modify the subject's thyroid gland, which is responsible for triggering the subsequent trigger-phase.

The trigger-phase activates when hormones such as cortisol and adrenaline are released into the bloodstream and perfuse into a targeted bone.

------

**(d) Osteogenesis:** During the trigger-phase, the subject's bones undergo expansion, fracture, and transformation, facilitated by osteoclasts.[[footnote]] Osteoclasts are specialized cells that break down and dissolve bone. [[/footnote]] These osteoclasts were modified to possess a specialized acidic enzyme that targets the metallic compounds found in the Carcinmetalloplastic Osteoid.

By secreting the acidic enzyme, the modified osteoclasts dissolve and expand the bones as the metal components are released. This process triggers osteoblastic[[footnote]] Osteoblasts are cells responsible for the formation and synthesis of new bone. [[/footnote]] rearrangement and regeneration, leading to the formation of skeletal weapons observed in RPC-714-1 instances. 

Considering the heat generated during rapid weapon creation and disassembly, the osteoclasts and osteoblasts were engineered with a more stable protein structure to help mitigate the effects of hyperthermia.

------

**(e) Neuroplasticity:** Carcinmetalloplasia induces structural modification in the brain, specifically in the hypothalamus, pituitary gland, and brainstem. 
------

**Summary:** The synthesis of RPC-714 involves the injection of osteoprogenitor cells to facilitate its distribution and modification of the thyroid gland via the hypothalamus. The trigger-phase triggers bone expansion and transformation through the action of modified osteoclasts, while neuroplasticity affects key brain structures, leading to cognitive and behavioral changes observed in RPC-714-1 instances.

+ Experiment 714/16
[[/=]]

**Resources:** (4) Adult Pigs, (4) RPC-714-v5

**Purpose:** To observe the effect of Carcinmetalloplastic stem cells in animal models.

**Procedure:** 4 different variants of RPC-714-v5 were injected into each of the four pigs, following the established protocol. The pigs were closely monitored for any physical changes or alterations in bone density and muscle development over time. After fully synthesizing, the pigs are to be shocked into a trigger-phase.

**Log:** It was observed that the pigs' bone density increased significantly following the injection of the Carcinmetalloplastic Stem Cells. Additionally, their bones and muscles exhibited noticeable growth and increased mass {{(fig. 2.1)}}.

However, despite these physiological changes, the pigs did not display the expected response when subjected to the trigger-phase stimulus. During the shock stimulus, the pigs exhibited minor leg tremors but did not progress beyond that initial response.

[[include component:image-enlarge name= 714-3.png|align=center|width=300px|caption= {{fig. 2.1:}} Adult pig post-synthesis.]]

**Conclusion:** The experiment revealed that Carcinmetalloplastic Stem Cells, specifically the variants of RPC-714-v5 used in this study, were compatible with Artiodactyla mammals such as pigs.

However, the limitation of the experiment became apparent as it was discovered that the pigs' brain structure was not sufficiently developed to facilitate the trigger-phase activation. It was evident that their brain stem lacked the necessary modifications to activate the trigger-phase effectively. 

------

**Notes:** //This is rather troubling. RPC-714's inability to synthesize with lower-intelligence mammals means that we'll have to go back to resorting to CSD-testing. I'll take a meeting with the board of Ethical Reviews to continue the Carcinmetalloplastic experiment program.//

+ Experiment 714/17
[[/=]]

**Resources:** RPC-714-1-44, RPC-714-1-53, RPC-714-1-55, IQ and EQ test evaluations, morphine

**Purpose:** To observe the cognitive and behavioral effects of RPC-714.

**Procedure:** The RPC-714-1 subjects were closely monitored during and before the activation of a trigger-phase. For the experiment, their emotional and logical intelligence was assessed through various problem solving, reasoning, and social tasks.

**Log:** Subjects have lost 60% of their logical processing skills has been diminished compared to their pre-trigger phase state, displaying an inability to engage in complex cognitive tasks.

Subjects also displayed emotional regression comparable to that of a 6-year-old. Characterized by impulsive and reactive behavior, limited perspective-taking abilities, and a greater reliance on external support for emotional regulation. This instability appeared to diminish when the subjects were given morphine shots. 

RPC-714-1 subjects that stayed in the trigger-phase for more than 3 hours displayed pronounced aggression towards the researchers, as they made inquiries regarding the possibility of consuming the researchers.

**Conclusion:** The cognitive and behavioral effects observed in RPC-714-1 are likely a result of significant neural modifications in the brainstem, a property added by the Church of Malthus to maximize RPC-714-1's pain tolerance. 

The emotional instability observed in RPC-714-1 subjects can be attributed to both hormonal imbalances and changes in neural circuitry.

+ Experiment 714/18

**Resources:** CSD-4758 (RPC-714-56), CSD-5848 (RPC-714-58), CSD-7675 (RPC-714-60), RPC-714-v8

**Purpose:** The purpose of this experiment is to observe the effect of different doses of RPC-714 on human subjects. 

**Procedure:** 5 different RPC-714-v8 samples are to be injected into CSD subjects, each with differing quantities of RPC-714. The sample dosage is as follows:

Sample A: 4.0 million stem cells / 60 ml (control group)
Sample C: 0.8 million stem cells / 60 ml (underdose)
Sample E: 9 million stem cells / 60 ml (overdose)

Subjects will be monitored for any anomalous changes post-injection for the next 4 months. Each subject will be given special treatment according to their metabolic need.

**Log:**  Subject CSD-4758 (RPC-714-56) was given Sample A. After 2 months, RPC-714-56 successfully synthesized RPC-714, exhibiting the expected bone and muscle density. Upon a shock stimulus, RPC-714-56 triggered the transformation phase and manifested dagger blades from its fingers. No significant adverse effects were noted.

Subject CSD-5848 (RPC-714-58) was given Sample B. RPC-714-58 wasn’t able to fully synthesize RPC-714 after 4 months. The low dosage led to RPC-714-58 undergoing osteoporosis. When exposed to a shock stimulus, RPC-714-58's bones prematurely fractured but didn’t heal. The subject was later diagnosed with stage 2 brain cancer.

Subject CSD-3422 (RPC-714-60) was given Sample C. RPC-714-59 fully synthesized RPC-714 after 3 weeks. The subject's body mass grew by 1600%. The subject's brain stem underwent significant modification, resulting in profound mental retardation and the inability to feel pain. Shock stimuli yielded no observable response.

Approximately ██% of the subject's bones, including the skull, have been fractured and transformed into various bladed weapons, protruding out of the subject's flesh. It is unknown how RPC-714-60 managed to survive in this state for ██ months.

**Analysis:** The subject that was underdosed, despite being given a similar high-protein as CSD-4758, was unable to maintain its cell's conversion into Carcinmetalloplastic tissue. CSD-3422, on the other hand, has undergone significant transformation in the brain stem, resulting in the body perpetually being stuck on a trigger-phase.

------

**Notes:** //CSD-3422's ability to survive so much modification is interesting. It doesn't seem like we've scratched the surface of the limit the human body can take RPC-714 before immediately dying, and it's possible that the recommended dosage we rely upon results in a watered-down version of the RPC-714-1 instances the Church of Malthus employs.//

//What's more, I find it curious, is that 4.0 million cells seem to be the universal dosage for anyone regardless of size, health, sex, and age. It appears that the Church of Malthus was careful in crafting the optimal template when creating RPC-714.//

**Addendum 714.3: History Log** 

RPC-714 was initially created in Austro-Hungary in 1914, with the creation of Version 1-2 templates by Dr. ███ Kaufmann, who aimed to engineer super-soldiers for the Great War. These early attempts proved unsuccessful, resulting in the death and permanent disfigurement of subjects injected with the variant. 

Advancements in RPC-714 continued, leading to the emergence of Version 3 in 1942. This was the first stable variant and gained widespread distribution by anonymous sects of the Church of Malthus in Eastern Europe. In 1946, Version 4 was developed and quickly became the most commonly used iteration of RPC-714, as transformation is no longer permanent and subjects can retract their weapons back.
