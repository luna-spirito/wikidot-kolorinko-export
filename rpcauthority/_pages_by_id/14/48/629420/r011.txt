**Registered Phenomena Code:** 812

**Object Class:** --Alpha-White-- Alpha-Yellow

[[include component:hazards top-spacing=none|transmutation=show|contact=show]]

**Containment Protocols:** Instances of RPC-812 are to be kept in separate Alpha-class storage containers at Site-086. --Testing of anomalous properties by 2R personnel is allowed.-- Testing of anomalous properties requires approval and supervision by 3R personnel.[[footnote]]See Incident Log.[[/footnote]]

**Description:** RPC-812 is a collection of 5 different cast iron frying-pans. Each instance measures 30 cm in diameter and weighs 1.3 kg with a chemical composition functionally identical to that of a non-anomalous pan. Additionally, each instance of RPC-812 carries the logo of GoI Amazing! Co. on the underside.[[footnote]]MST Echo-9 "Toy Soldiers" has launched a search for other RPC-812 products manufactured by Amazing! Co.[[/footnote]] Below the logo resides an imprint which is the only distinguishing factor between instances. 

Following are the imprints with the corresponding instance.

* RPC-812-1: RARE
* RPC-812-2: MEDIUM RARE
* RPC-812-3: MEDIUM
* RPC-812-4: MEDIUM WELL
* RPC-812-5: WELL DONE

The anomalous effects of RPC-812 manifest when --organic tissue-- a physical system consisting of at least ███ grams of matter make contact with the intended cooking surface of the instance.[[footnote]]Hereby referred to as "The activity plane".[[/footnote]]

Upon contact with the activity plane, the subject and the RPC-812 instance become inseparable for a period of 2 minutes. When enough time passes, the subject is separable and --has been cooked according to the imprint. The result is often considered ideal by researchers. How the tissue is cooked without heat is as of yet unknown.-- has changed in a semi-predictable[[footnote]]See Testing Log.[[/footnote]] way according to the instance's imprint.

[[collapsible show="[+] Discovery Log" hide="[-] Discovery Log"]]
RPC-812 was discovered in a crate by MST Echo-9 "Toy Soldiers" during a raid of a suspect warehouse located in █████████, Russia. A note was attached to said crate, relevant information translated to English is presented below.
> 
> //Introducing the first Amazing! Co. and Golden boys collaboration product! The perfect pans for all of your steak cooking needs, why engage in the hassle of cooking an ideal medium rare when these bad boys can do it for you? Save yourself some dignity at the next family get-together and buy the Perfect Pans bundle today!//
> 
The identity of the entity referred to as "Golden boys" is currently undetermined.

[[collapsible show="[+] Incident Log 23/05/████" hide="[-] Incident Log 23/05/████"]]
Authority surveillance cameras in Site-086 recorded Junior Researcher Luther removing RPC-812-2 from its container for a "testing session" with a piece of Japanese wagyu. However, the researchers finger made contact with the activity plane following a mindless attempt of removing a porcelain plate accidentally dropped onto the plane. During the following 2 minutes, Junior Researcher Luther attempted to remove the body part from the activity plane whilst showing signs of immense distress. At the end of the reaction, the entirety of Junior Researcher Luthers skin was exhibiting signs of irritation, as well as a permanent change to lighter, almost white tone.
[[/collapsible]]

Following the events of Incident Log 23/05/████, Containment Protocols and Object Class have been updated.
