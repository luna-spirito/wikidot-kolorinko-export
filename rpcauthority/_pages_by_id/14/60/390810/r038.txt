CR0100 is confined within St. John’s Gate, under the supervision of the Subjugation Office at North London Station. Its holding cell has been adapted to suit the specimen's nature,[[footnote]] The Gates are an impenetrable containment vault reserved exclusively for entities and artefacts deemed too hazardous for standard confinement. Each cell within the vault generates an illusory reality, tailored to the psyche or nature of the subject. [[/footnote]] manifesting as an illusory environment named the “//Dark, Endless Field of Rabbits and Foxes.//” This habitat provides the specimen with an appropriate setting in which it can be both secured and stimulated. 

The specimen is to be provisioned, weekly, with a live mature bovine of any weight and species. In cases where the Black Hunters Corp (BHC) secures a thaumaturgic specimen designated for termination, the subject may be fed to CR0100 as an alternative form of execution.

Interaction with CR0100 necessitates a controlled ritual to ensure personnel safety. Assigned staff are required to enter the dark field and follow the entity’s trail. Within the illusory environment, a rabbit or fox must be apprehended and formally presented as a sacrificial offering. CR0100 will only manifest upon receipt of this offering.

In the event that CR0100 is presented for exhibition or institutional display, strict oversight measures are required. The specimen must at all times be accompanied by:
* __Captain of the Black Hunters Corp 2nd Company__, responsible for armed containment, emergency intervention, and the enforcement of termination protocols should the specimen display signs of aggression or breach.
* __Member of the Thaumarch__, responsible for the maintenance of ritual controls, and the safe mediation of any thaumaturgic manifestations.

Out of formality, CR0100 is to be referred to as "Madame Boye" or "Lady Device" by Monarch Security staff.

++ Synopsis

[[include component:mi13image2 width=300px|align=right
|image=oldboy.png
|card=fig.2
|caption= **Entity Notes:**

CR0100 is an intelligent, reality-bending thaumaturge with an amorphous body that shifts continuously between canine and humanoid forms. It is most frequently encountered in the guise of a white hunting poodle.

CR0100 exhibits traits of both a biological organism and [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/a-report-on-the-history-of-ectoplasm-spirits ectoplasmic apparition]. Despite its amorphous nature, the specimen possesses a complete set of fully human internal organs. This human structure remains consistent regardless of its outward transformations.

Evaluations of the specimen's spiritual physiology likewise reveal it to have a human pattern, placing it closer to the class of beings known as Bacchanals[[footnote]] Thaumaturges that succumbed to the Hanover Curse and are physiologically, psychologically, and spiritually transformed into a malevolent form. [[/footnote]] than a conventional daemon.[[footnote]] A class of spirits that are the intermediate between gods and humans[[/footnote]]  The specimen's stature and spiritual influence are comparable to those of a minor deity. Evidence suggests it possesses cognitive access to the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/memotics-hub spiritual realm], enabling it to communicate with humans through hallucinations and dreams.

Witnesses consistently describe its presence as terrifying and unnerving, reporting an  overwhelming sensation of dread from the specimen's “malevolent aura." Prolonged exposure is observed to induce delusion, hysteria, and paranoia, often accompanied by auditory hallucinations resembling musket fire, battle cries, and distant human screams. It should be noted that, unlike other Bacchanal entities, thaumaturges under Britain’s curseship do not experience the acceleration of the Hanover Curse[[footnote]] The Hanover Curse is a curse that affects all thaumaturges within Britain’s Curseship. It accelerates one's advancement in thaumaturgy but carries the risk of the afflicted succumbing to the curse and transforming into a Bacchanal. [[/footnote]] in CR0100’s presence. On the contrary, prolonged exposure has been observed to slow the progression of the curse and, in some cases, reverse it.

After performing CR0100’s ritual, participants are presented with the option of offering their own blood to the specimen. Offering blood causes CR0100 to reciprocate with its own bodily fluids. CR0100 will leap onto the subject’s face and administer the exchange directly, expelling its blood into the subject’s mouth. This “black blood” substance consists of a complex ectoplasmic admixture of blood, gut microbiota, and neural tissue. Ingestion triggers a genetic and physiological transformation, reclassifying the subject into a CR0100-1 variant.

CR0100-1 variants retain their baseline cognitive identity but undergo significant morphological and metabolic alterations. These include:

* Enhanced regenerative capacity and resistance to conventional trauma.
* Heightened predatory drives, with compulsive fixation on both hunting behaviour and blood consumption.
* Subtle restructuring of neural architecture, often leading to behavioural instability and dissociation.

++ History

The origin of CR0100 can be traced back to the Pendle witch trial[[footnote]] The Pendle witch trial of 1612 in Lancashire, England, saw the prosecution of individuals, primarily members of rival families like the Demdikes and the Chattoxes, on charges of witchcraft, resulting in the execution of ten people. [[/footnote]] in 1612, where Elizabeth Device was charged with the murders of 3 people through witchcraft. Her nine-year-old daughter, Jennet Device, was the accuser in Elizabeth's trial. Jennet claimed that her mother had a familiar dog named "Ball" help her in her murders. Faced with her own child making accusations that would lead to her execution, began to curse and scream at her daughter before her eventual execution along with 9 other accused witches. 

Jennet Device would later be given custody to the royal court and serve as a thaumaturgist under the tutelage of Black Hunter Thomas Howard, 1st Curse of Maltravers. During the Scottish-Faelic War,[[footnote]] An event in English History called the Rightful Cause (1590 - 1625) that saw King James VII and I wage a crusade against witches and the faeries of Elfame. [[/footnote]] Lady Device distinguished herself with feats of medical alchemy and exorcism. Praised by her peers as a prodigal thaumaturgist indispensable to the king's Rightful Cause despite her young age, she was granted peerage, the Curse of Pendle Hill.

However, in 1637, a pregnant Lady Device was stricken by spontaneous deformations and madness,[[footnote]] This is believed to be an early form of the Bacchanal phenomena brought about by the Hanover Curse. [[/footnote]] thought to be a curse by the Elphane faeries. Thomas Howard claimed that Lady Device, along with her unborn child, perished days after. This was contradicted by future evidence.

++ Acquisition Notes

The specimen was apprehended by senior thaumarchs of the HMSO in 1833 within a Germanic Otherworld after its fight with [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-197 another minor-deity]. Following its formal acquisition, it was inducted into the Royal Congregation (RCS) and granted full pardon for its prior actions by Lord Protector Jasmine I, in 1835.

Since its induction into the Black Coats, the specimen has served the 2nd Company of the Black Hunters Corp for almost two centuries, producing hundreds of CR0100-1 variants, each deployed as highly effective monster-killers.

++ Project Outline

CR0100 is officially a member of the Black Hunters Corp, serving as the main patron of a curseship known as the "Hunter's Pact." The specimen's curseship allows CR0100's "hunters" to operate a hunt without the fear of being inebriated by the Hanover Curse.

The Hunter’s Pact is a thaumaturgic discipline that channels the power of CR0100, an allied Bacchanal contained beneath St. John’s Gate. Its practice grants enhanced physicality, enabling feats of speed, agility, strength, and instinct beyond human capacity. It also confers resistance to both the Hanover Curse and the corrosive spiritual degradation typically inflicted by Bacchanals.

Progression within the art is achieved through the consumption of a victim’s “flesh and blood.” This term is not limited to literal blood, but refers broadly to the essential life-substance of a target: ectoplasm in the case of spirits, energy cores or circuitry in mechanical entities, or sap in plant-based entities. Through this consumption, practitioners metabolise the essence of their prey, refining both body and spirit, and thereby amplifying their thaumaturgic potential.

This art has long been a source of controversy within Monarch Security. Practitioners are frequently reported to emit a repulsive aura, with their spiritual presence described as corrupted or diseased. Despite these accounts, there remains insufficient evidence to formally classify the practice as black magic.

The curseship also enhances the arms, armour, and martial arts of the variants through theogenics. It is for this reason that the 2nd Company BHC has taken the reputation of being the most versatile unit within the Black Coats.

++ On the Object's Utility

As you may already know, the entity we know as "Madame Boye" is the origin of the 2nd company of the Black Hunters Corp. One important detail I'm sure you're well aware of but isn't included in this document is how its "black blood" gives CR0100-1 immunity to the Hanover Curse. It gives the Boye Device's "blessed" immunity to every malevolent curse but its own--this //Black Hunt// taints the souls of those who follow its pathway and become a spirit beast, not unlike the Bacchanals the hunters fight against again and again.

And, as they depend on Old Boy's blessings more and more, and take in the doctrines of hundreds of thaumaturges to aid them in the hunt that satiates their bloodlust, more loyal they are to us.

For hundreds of years, the Black Hunters have served us with utmost loyalty, like a dog trying to please its master. Their loyalty to our cause is unparalleled, but of course, there are a few bad apples among the pack. Not all dogs are willing to listen to their master and will try to fight back and bite them with their sharpened fangs.

A rabid dog is dangerous. Wrathful, aggressive, unpredictable. A bite from one is fatal. I give you permission to put them down. Even if they show the slightest signs of anathema.

**CR0100:** So. Is it thy turn now?

**Off. Schwungrad:** This is only a part of office formality as per Captain Obscura's supervision, Madame Boye. 

**CR0100:** This year hath been a dreich and tedious thing. No blood, no hunt. //I wither from hunger in this silence.//

**Off. Schwungrad:** This is good news, madame. After all, it is our duty to protect the peace in our realm.

**CR0100:** Peace… //A soldier hath no part in peace.// That belongeth to his children, not to him. Thou’rt hunter, art thou not? A hunter must provide for his kin from the meat of his quarry. 

(//CR0100 shifts to a human form in order to appear less threatening.//) 

**CR0100:** (//It starts softening its voice.//) Tell me, what hast thou taken this year?

**Off. Schwungrad:** (//Discomforted, Off. Schwungrad looks down.//) Eight arrests. Four thaumaturges put down.

**CR0100:** I remember now... [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-758 red imps]… nay, such vermin count not. They're too ill-witted. (//CR0100 growls and returns to canine form.//)

**Off. Schwungrad:** Forgive me, I would've hunted more if I could, it's just--

**CR0100:** (//Barks//) Thou growest not stronger. Since thou hast turned thy hunt away from men, thy kind hath grown frail. //Thy kin weaken with every passing year.//

**Off. Schwungrad:** It is a regrettable situation we're in. However, it is a necessary step towards peace in our time.

**CR0100:** The spawn of evil slumber not, even in peace. Where be they now? //Fomorians, Bacchanals, evil gods accursed…//

**Off. Schwungrad:** Their kind has not crossed our borders for two years. Records show only scattered sightings, none confirmed. (//Off. Schwungrad offers CR0100 a file of the BHC's reports.//)

**CR0100:** //Records.// Thou dost bind the hunt with ledgers and signatures. (//CR0100 manifests a human arm out of itself to read the file.//) 

**Off. Schwungrad:** As you can see here, we--

**CR0100:** //Predictable.// Hast thou had strife with those {{[WITHHELD]}} once more? This congregation lieth stagnant.

**Off. Schwungrad:** With respect, madame, our duty lies in containment, not indulgence. The Royal Congregation deems the threat diminished.

**CR0100:** So be it.

(//CR0100 lets the file crumble into smoke.//)

**CR0100:** Thou art diligent, soldier, yet blind. Dost thou not ken how thy masters play at this game? They call me forth when it pleaseth them. (//Growls//) the White Witch of Pendle Hill made into a mere toy, aye? //God of the witch-hunters, paraded to remind their rivals what power yet slumbereth in their vaults.//

**Off. Schwungrad:** M-Madame Boye, y-you exaggerate. Your place within Monarch Security is one of honour, not spectacle.

**CR0100:** //Honour?// (//Laughs//) Nay, ’tis but convenience. When Parliament clamoureth, they brandish me to still their noise: “//See, even the Old Boy bends to our law.//”

(//Distant musketfire and shouting can be heard in the distance.//)

**CR0100:** When Parliament retreateth, I am chained once more to the field. I am pawn, not partner. //And pawns are spent.//

(//CR0100 shifts into its human form, looking Schwungrad eye-level.//)

**CR0100:** //And thou...// Thou servest men who stray ever further from the Crown, yet dare not confess it. Thy Monarch Security waxeth apart, a kingdom within the kingdom. Thou swearest fealty to the Lord Protector, yet dost scoff at thine own king. Tell me, soldier… when the hunt returneth, shall thy foe be the Bacchanal — //or the Parliament?//

**Off. Schwungrad:** I //serve// my oath.

**CR0100:** (//Laughs//) Spoken like a dutiful hound.

(//CR0100 slowly demanifests, its body dissapating into black mist.//)

**CR0100:** Prithee remember: even the stoutest leash doth fray when the masters fall to quarrel. And when they do, thou shalt be the first cast into the pit. Mistake not thy place, child. I know mine. And //I will not suffer to be spent lightly, Gate be damned.//

(//CR0100 dissolves back into smoke, leaving the endless field empty.//)
