This is the reworked MI13 Format. Much of the necessary CSS has been converted to components already. MI13 files are organized into a collection under tabs

To begin, use the URL __@@http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/no-####@@__ when making new MI13 articles. Then title your article No. ####.
First off, add this at the very top of your article. This will allow the hub to read your title.
> @@[[span class="listresize" style="font-size: 0px; line-height: 0px;"]] TITLE [[/span]]@@
Below, add the CSS module.
> [[module css]]
> @import url(http://rpcsandbox.wikidot.com/component:mi13-theme/code/1);
> [[/module]]

Secondly, there's the __optional__ Memorandum Notice, the things to keep in mind here are its **Clearance Level** (and their color code) which are as follows: ##royalblue|**UNCLASSIFIED (OFFICIAL)** (royalblue)##, ##lime|**PROTECTED** (lime)##, ##greenyellow|**RESTRICTED** (greenyellow)##, ##yellow|**CONFIDENTIAL** (yellow)##, ##orange|**SECRET** (orange)##, ##red|**TOP SECRET** (red)##[[footnote]] Keep in mind that the GPMS security format has been retired in 2014. ##royalblue|**OFFICIAL**## now encompass anything below ##orange|**SECRET**## clearance. [[/footnote]]; and a signature from the Prime Minister of UK[[footnote]] Which you can find on Wikipedia [[/footnote]]

++ Safety Procedure

The 5 **Threat Levels** are:
* minimal
* substantial
* moderate
* severe
* critical

The 5 **Display Status** are:
* stationed
* displayed
* exhibited
* gated
* removed 

LOCATION indicates the object’s specific residence, which may be a building, database, or individual custodian.
