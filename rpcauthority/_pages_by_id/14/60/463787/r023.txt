++ Safety Procedures

Artefact No. 0288 remains under the custody of MI-13 Junior Investigator Yushatyr. Historical assessment indicates that neither the artefact nor its associated entity presents any substantive risk to Monarch Security operations. On the contrary, the entity has consistently contributed to the agency’s objectives for over a century.

Although formally classified as an artefact, MI0288-01 has been recognised as a serving member of Monarch Security since her acquisition in 1898. She holds an established and respected position within His Majesty’s Subjugation Office (HMSO) and the Royal Congregation of Sorcerers (RCS), and is to be afforded the courtesies expected of a senior officer.

In recognition of her long-standing service, MI0288-01 is granted the full privileges of an agency staff. These include salaries, insurance and welfare benefits, access to training and development programs, entitlement to annual leave, and the right to judicial representation through the agency’s union in the United Kingdom.

Special accommodations have been made for MI0288-01’s apparitional nature, including the provision of silver-backed mirrors and spectral-motion-sensor camera systems to make her presence visible to personnel, as well as spectral microphones for clear auditory communication. Per MI0288-01’s request, financial support is extended to her custodian, Yushatyr, to fund her education, training, and approved projects under the supervision of MI-13.

As seen in {{Fig.1}}[[footnote]] Image taken from the [https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/H_WB-170 © The Trustees of the British Museum.] Shared under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) licence.[[/footnote]] MI0288 is an oval-shaped locket crafted from silver, features identical faces adorned with intricate gold scrolls against a backdrop of black enamel. The artefact is mainly composed of porcelain enamel, engraved and adorned in gold, pearl, and diamond. Inside the artefact is a silver mirror that displays a normally blurred reflection.

The artefact passively emits a faint field of spirituality on an 8 m diameter surrounding it, protecting subjects from auditory and transitory curses, as well as moderate malevolent influences. Participants report feeling either a sense of serenity or ease while in MI0228's area of effect.

Periodically, an apparition designated MI0288-01 appears on the reflection of the mirror ({{Fig.2}}). The entity would usually be seen standing next to the participant's reflection or sitting idly by, sometimes holding a silver utensil. The apparition manifests as a Caucasian woman in her early thirties, exhibiting pallor and marked malnourishment, characteristic of the “consumptive look” associated with incipient pulmonary tuberculosis.

The entity most commonly manifests in a European nightgown characteristic of the late 17th century. On occasion, however, she exhibits the ability to replicate the attire and personal belongings of those around her. These manifestations are illusory in nature, being an extension of MI0288’s own body rather than independent objects. Surrounding MI0288-01 are translucent ribbons of silver mist that are purported to be the residue of spirits and thaumaturgic properties the entity has absorbed throughout her two centuries of operative service.

If MI0288 is placed near a silver mirror for 4–6 minutes, entity MI0288-01 may fully materialise from the mirror’s reflection. Its image vanishes immediately if the mirror is covered, cracked, or otherwise disturbed. In other reflective surfaces, such as glass, metal, or water, MI0288-01 manifests as an indistinct silver cloud of a vaguely humanoid shape before turning into a clearer form after approximately half an hour. 

MI0288-01 is capable of physically interacting with other entities within reflective surfaces. Participants can hear her voice and feel her touch through the reflection, though direct interaction with her remains impossible. If MI0288-01 attempts to inflict harm, participants experience the associated pain in their physical bodies, yet no physical wounds manifest in reality. Instead, the injuries appear solely within the reflection as illusory marks.

It is speculated that MI0288-01’s touch interfaces directly with a subject’s spiritual essence. This interaction appears to induce corresponding neural responses, producing the sensation of physical contact or pain despite the absence of actual bodily harm. While harmless to living material beings such as humans, this property is highly effective against other spiritual entities, including incorporeal anomalies and malevolent influences.

++ History

MI0288 was crafted in 1616 by the Nuremberg goldsmith and thaumaturgist Corvinianus Saur for Amelia Brown (1610–1679), an illegitimate descendant of the Boleyn family. Her lineage traces back to George Boleyn, brother of Queen Anne Boleyn, the second wife of King Henry VIII.

The object was originally commissioned as a birthday gift for her estranged elder sister, Sophia Boleyn (1599–1633), but Sophia died of tuberculosis before the piece was completed. According to Saur’s surviving accounts, Amelia instructed that //“the locket be fashioned in the likeness of my sister’s soul, that it might never fade.”//

The House of Boleyn was rumoured to have practised a now-lost thaumaturgic art known as Silver Remembrance, a discipline said to allow practitioners to imprint memories, emotions, and even thaumaturgic signatures onto silver surfaces. Though dismissed by most scholars of the time, such techniques were whispered about in thaumaturge circles during the reign of Elizabeth I. Saur, intrigued by the commission, crafted a pair of mirror earring lockets, said to contain remnants of Silver Remembrance.

The lockets remained in the possession of Boleyn descendants until the late 19th century. According to MI0288-01, the remembrance property of the second earring has lost its essence and transported its spirituality to the MI0288 artefact, which is currently under Monarch Security's display.

++ Acquisition Notes

MI0288 was an important heirloom of the Boleyn family for centuries, up until the earrings were mistakenly sold off to the Rothschild family in 1799. Baron Ferdinand Rothschild sent the locket and nearly 300 other antique artefacts to the British Museum, which catalogued and curated the items for anomalous appraisal. MI0288, together with **█** other artefacts, was identified as anomalous and subsequently transferred to various Monarch Security stations. Since then, MI0288 has remained under custody of Norwich Station.

++ Project Outline
MI0288 was first assigned as a sentry at Norwich Station in 1922 and routinely deployed for field missions throughout Norfolk County. As of October 2022, however, MI0288-01 formally requested a reassignment of her occupation in order to assume a mentorship role for one of RCS’s young thaumaturges. MI0288 is presently under the supervision of Codicesian “Yushatyr” and continues to accompany her during active field operations.

[http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/monarch-security-personnel#yushatyr + Yushatyr's Personnel File]

When MI0288 was first transferred to Norwich Station, most custodians struggled to engage productively with MI0288-01. Madame Sophia Boleyn remained aloof in the presence of staff, distant and uncooperative until her first encounter with Yushatyr. While the girl polished the locket’s silver according to Sakha silversmithing rites, MI0288-01 remarked upon her skill and, in an unexpected turn, declared her worthy of becoming a protégé.

Since then, MI0288-01 has referred to Yushatyr explicitly as her “apprentice,” and requested that Yushatyr be transferred from the Codicesia to the Commission for Thaumaturgy as part of her training. In formal recognition of this singular rapport, custodianship of MI0288 was placed directly under Yushatyr’s charge.

+++ Petition for Review of Spirit and Artefact Rights
[[/=]]
**Statement of:** MI0288-01

**Curated by:** Julius Camden - Curia Arcanum Magistrate

**Sir Carousel:** The Curia calls MI0288-01, Lady Sophia Boleyn, to stand. You have petitioned the Curia for a hearing on the treatment of spectral entities and artefacts. As the 27th Grand Sorcerer of the Royal Congregation, I will be hearing your testimony... Lady Boleyn, you may proceed.

**MI0288-01:** (//Materialises in the full-length mirror placed at the stand.//) Thank you, Sir Carousel. (//Curtsey//) I speak not merely for myself, on behalf of the voices that have yet to reach the Curia. I was given an opportunity to listen to many spirits bound to the relics and phantasms that your ledgers call //confined//, and I must emphasize many of these artefacts are not merely objects. Many of them are minds, memories, the echoes of lives. I find your treatment of these creatures to be inappropriate.

**Camden:** Please, elaborate.

**MI0288-01:**  //You leave them to gather dust, or cast them into the Gate as if they were objects to be put in storage.//

**Camden:** Lady Sophia, Monarch Security is sworn to safeguard mankind. Confinement is our number one priority. And there is a difference between spirits and the human soul.

**MI0288-01:** Human soul? And what of mine, Magister? (//Steps closer to the mirror.//) I am not an original human soul. I am a copy. If you call me human, it is only by courtesy. Do you call me such because I look pleasing to your eye?

(//MI0288-01’s reflected image undergoes distortion, with bodily proportions elongating beyond human anatomical limits. Ocular regions recess into empty eye sockets.//)

**MI0288-01:** Would you still call me human if I looked thus?

**Camden:** (//Visibly uneas//y) Your appearance is not in question—

**MI0288-01:** But it is. If I may be accorded rights because I look like a lady, then your judgment is vanity, not justice.

**Sir Carousel:** Lady Sophia—

**MI0288-01:** (//Returns to her baseline form.//) Forgive me, Carousel. (//Turns back to Camden//) But consider this: these artefacts, these spirits, were never given a chance. They are locked away, their histories never read, their words never heard. I myself was nearly left to rot in a glass cabinet until I was brought by the HMSO. 

**Camden:** We cannot endanger the living for the sake of the dead. Many of these artefacts are volatile. We don’t have the unlimited expense of the Authority.

**MI0288-01:** You… you would risk nothing, then. Better ignorance than danger?

**Camden:** Our charge is to protect the Kingdom and her subjects. That is our first and highest law.

**MI0288-01:** And what of the subjects who are no longer living?

**Sir Carousel:** (//Clears throat//) Your honor, if I may, Lady Sophia’s service to the Crown has proven that even anomalous spirits may be reasoned with, and even be made allies. Perhaps… there is merit in what she says.

**Camden:** Your petition will be taken under advisement. But understand, Lady Sophia, the safety of the living cannot be bartered.

**MI0288-01:** I understand. But I shall not cease to speak for those who cannot.

(//Yushatyr is in a Cornwall Institute laboratory, inserting sodium hydroxide drain cleaner into a silver-ammonia solution.//)

**MI0288-01:** What is the latest project you are doing?

(//Yushatyr steps back from the chemical solution and opens her respirator mask.//)

**Yushatyr:** A mirror. Pure silver. I have been studying other silver apparitions and poltergeists. I think I know how to make your physical presence more--eh... physical.

**MI0288-01:** You have a habit of crafting things that bring me closer to a physical presence. (//Sits down next to Yushatyr and pats her head.//) I wonder why that is?

**Yushatyr:** (//Looks down, and swipes MI0288-01's hand away, flustered.//) How was the hearing?

**MI0288-01:** Delayed, as it was ten years ago and ten before that. (//Sigh//) It took time to grasp that I am not human.

**Yushatyr:** But you are human. I think the Curia is wrong. They cannot reckon what is and is not human. (//Shakes head//)  [[span style="font-family: helvetica, sans-serif;"]]Неа.[[/span]] ##8f8f8f|Nope.## I do not agree.

**MI0288-01:** The RCS speaks from a place of law, and law fears uncertainty. That was true in my day, and it is true now.

**Yushatyr:** For me, humanity is memory and care. If you remember and you cry and you keep other people's story, you are human--I think... [[span style="font-family: helvetica, sans-serif;"]]Ты понимаешь?[[/span]] ##8f8f8f|Do you understand?##

**MI0288-01:** (//Chuckles//) That's quite a broad definition.

**Yushatyr:** It's—eh—why do you doubt yourself about being human?

**MI0288-01:** I don't deny it. (//Leans forward, palms flat on the table.//) Because I remember. Not only my own remembrance. I remember the hands that made this locket, the breath in a winter room, the lullaby hummed by a woman who never saw the ocean. Memory is the living of a life. If a life remembers, who are we to deny it human shape?

**Yushatyr:** When I first arrived, I was ashamed. My family sent me away. I am far from Krasnoyarsk. People call you an artefact, and I felt small. I thought perhaps I chose the wrong company.

**MI0288-01:** You chose to put your faith to its conclusion over ease. That is no small thing. You have learned to see what others dismiss.

**Yushatyr:** Why did you choose me to be your apprentice?

**MI0288-01:** (//Smiles//) God only knows. //I must have forgotten.//
