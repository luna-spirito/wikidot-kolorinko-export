[[[RPC-996]]] was published by user [[user Applegate]] on June 25th, 2018, a mere twelve days after the RPC Wiki was founded. The story outlines a silent patch of land that takes away those who climb upward within.

Prominently featured is a red fire tower, which facelessly tempts its onlookers to scale the ladder and pay witness to the land's effects. The original image frames the tower from below, which I have replicated to convey its lofty mystique.
