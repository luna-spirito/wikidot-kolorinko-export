**Registered Phenomena Code:** 058-ARC

**Object Class:** Beta-Black

**Hazard Types:** Sapient Hazard, Geological Hazard, Transmutation Hazard, Mechanical Hazard, Extra-Terrestrial Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** Should the anomaly be deemed a "sufficient threat" by the UN, Project Blue Book has informed the Authority that it is capable of deploying a "measured response," the nature of which is currently restricted to AEDF[[footnote]]The [*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/aedfmanifest AEDF] represents the sum total of the Authority's extraterrestrial assets.[[/footnote]] personnel.

To prevent the risk of UN intervention, Protocol 058-ARC-Sigma has been enacted. RPC-058-ARC is contained via in-person communications on a regular basis to encourage the current routine of the entities. Ares II[[footnote]]The [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/coldandsteele05 Ares II base] is a research/containment outpost established on Mars.[[/footnote]] is assigned to maintain relations.

In no way can the true location of Earth nor its inhabitants be acknowledged while in proximity to RPC-058-ARC.

In the event of the "measured response" by Project Blue Book, AEDF forces are to remain on standby in Low Martian Orbit to intercept and destroy. Type-5 Defensive Resources are authorized.

**Description:** RPC-058-ARC (known as ET-003 by Project Blue Book) is the designation for a group of anomalous mechanical entities (RPC-058-ARC-1), a large cylinder of unknown composition (RPC-058-ARC-2) and a seemingly infinite number of nanomachine particles (RPC-058-ARC-3) currently located within the Amenthes region of Mars.

RPC-058-ARC-1 are two dozen cylindrical mechanical entities that are approximately five meters tall, comprised of an unknown alloy that is believed to feature titanium as a constituent material. RPC-058-ARC-1 instances feature three appendages affixed to the bottom. Two of these appendages are utilized for movement in a humanoid pattern while the third is used for manipulation.

RPC-058-ARC-1 instances are equipped with a four meter long steel pole with a curved sharp end that is utilized to mine various rocks and ore at random. Removed 'samples' are carried by their appendage once the tool is set unsecured atop their bodies. These samples are brought to RPC-058-ARC-2, pressed against its outer surface, and then returned and reaffixed to the ground via RPC-058-ARC-3.

RPC-058-ARC-2 is believed to be the facility that recharges, repairs, and retrofits RPC-058-ARC-1 instances, made with the same unidentified titanium alloy as its mobile counterparts, and with several openings for said counterparts to enter and exit. Its power source is currently unknown. 

Both anomalies are capable of advanced matter manipulation via the dispersal of unidentified microscopic particles (RPC-058-ARC-3). These particles can rapidly disintegrate and materialize any material upon contact, limited by the direction of RPC-058-ARC-2.

RPC-058-ARC-2 is capable of communication through radio transmission through itself or RPC-058-ARC-1, usually towards Ares II.

**Addendum 2: Deimos Incident**

[[div class="blockquote"]]

+++ Log 058-ARC-29

------

In 2014, a transmission from RPC-058-ARC-2 was intercepted by AEDF //Patmos//, an unmanned observation drone stationed upon Deimos. The transmission was received as a radio signal that, when deciphered, was an elaborate string of code that was eventually revealed to be a .txt file. The following is a transcription.

> HELLO SPEAKER OF MOON
> 
> MOON-SPEAKER
> 
> WE SEE YOU
> 
> YOU ARE THREAT TO OUR SERVANTS
> 
> NEVER SPEAK AGAIN

Shortly after this signal was received, all RPC-058-ARC-1 instances immediately moved inside of the facility. A large sphere of RPC-058-ARC-3 particles aggregated in Low Martian Orbit and, over the course of 1.3 seconds, collided with the surface of Deimos.

Observation from Martian equipment recorded that the moon's surface was scoured by the anomaly, removing any artificial structure or device through its transmutative properties, including the AEDF //Patmos//, before aggregating once more and returning to RPC-058-ARC-2. The anomaly was reclassified as Beta-Black.

[[/div]]

This event brought RPC-058-ARC-3 and subsequently RPC-058-ARC as a whole to the attention of Project Blue Book, which made efforts to deploy a "measured response". After tense negotiation, the Project was convinced of RPC-058-ARC's nature and brought it under their jurisdiction in exchange for maintaining peaceful relations with the anomaly.
