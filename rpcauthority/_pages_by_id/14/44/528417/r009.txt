**Registered Phenomena Code:** 919

**Object Class:** Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** Animated Hazard, Sentient Hazard

**Containment Protocols:**  RPC-919 is to be kept in a standard anomalous item locker. The object is to be restrained with a paper clip to prevent mobility.

**Description:** RPC-919 is a folded paper airplane. It will randomly begin to propel itself through unknown means towards a chosen target at a speed of approx. 30 m/s. RPC-919 is capable of navigating around obstacles to reach its target, unless obstructed or restrained, and appears to be capable of traversing indefinitely.

Locations reported to have been visited by RPC-919 prior to containment commonly include prominent landmarks and popular tourist destinations. A list of its confirmed sightings is attached below.

* London, England
* Paris, France
* Barcelona, Spain
* Sydney, Australia
* Cape Town, South Africa
* New York, United States
* Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
* Tokyo, Japan

**Addendum:** The following is a message found imprinted on the piece of paper which makes up RPC-919. To avoid the risk of neutralization, the text was gathered from an X-ray imaging of the object as opposed to unfolding it.  

The transcript appears to be written in broken Russian. This, combined with the content, suggests the text to be a foreign language writing exercise. An altered translation is provided.

> My name is Petra. I am 11 years old. I come from Germany. I live in Berlin.
>   
> Yesterday, I baked a cake with my mom. We had enough sugar because my mom traded her beer coupons for sugar coupons with Mr. Willems. He needs a lot of beer coupons. My mom said to not be angry at him, even if he sometimes kicks me for no reason when I want to buy something and smells like pee.
>  
> In the future, I want to become a doctor or a pilot. I haven't decided yet. I want to be a doctor because they help people, and I want to be a pilot because they visit a lot of places. Before, I wanted to become a singer like Elvis, but my teacher told me to not talk about him. Elvis is an American singer, and I like his songs.
>  
> My aspiration is to travel the world. I want to visit a lot of places, on all the continents. We sometimes travel, but we don't travel a lot. I visit my grandparents every year, but they do not live very far from Berlin. I want to go to other countries like France or America. I think that would be interesting.
>  
> -Petra
