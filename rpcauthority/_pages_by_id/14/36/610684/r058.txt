+++ **Containment Protocol:** 
RPC-929 is to be contained in a 4m^^3^^ chamber paneled with lead. The cell is regularly sterilized to prevent RPC-929 from contacting foreign plants or animal matter. As of 31st March 2020, human exploration of ALTR-929 is strictly prohibited until further notice. 

The LCQ drones stationed at ALTR-929 are to be hidden from RPC-929-1 and RPC-929-2's view. The drones are set to destruct its mold layer as soon as either entity notice it.

------
[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-929/GeorgesDelusion.jpeg|width=260px|caption=ALTR-929.]]

+++ **Description:** 
RPC-929 is a cross-dimensional rift situated in the disarranged cadaver of 34-year-old George █████, a former CSD-class personnel. Its near vicinity radiates a hazardous fluctuation calculated to be around 3.79 ACS.

Active organic compounds that are submerged inside the coherency bubble for a period of 30-45 minutes (depending on mass) are broken down and reconstructed into a particle-waveform state within the infoplane. This process is akin to being transported into a dream: where the succession of images, ideas, emotions, and sensations are given the illusion of corporeal substance.

ALTR-929 is a meta-imaginarium[[footnote]]  Meta (from Greek μετά, “beyond”, “outside of”) and Imaginarium (A place dedicated to stimuli to human imagination) An imaginarium is an area of low coherency in which one’s subconscious preconceptions cross into the tangible world. [[/footnote]] situated within the inner axis of the baseline reality. Despite behaving like a physical reality, it is structured in a wave-form state, and thus is prone to the distortion of human perception. ALTR-929's construction is modeled after the county of ████████, Kansas. Its native inhabitants (designated ALTR-929-e) are each designed to resemble the various people in George █████'s life.

ALTR-929 is controlled and maintained by three major anomalies, designated RPC-929-1, RPC-929-2, and RPC-929-3 respectively. The descriptions of these entities are as follows:

+++ **Description:** 
RPC-929-1 is the corporeal personification of RPC-929's remaining consciousness as George █████. It retains █████'s personality as well as his memories before his arrest in ██/██/2017. 

RPC-929-1's nature is to create and maintain creations within ALTR-929. Unlike its previous manifestation, it is unaware of its reality-warping ability, opting to simulate George █████'s life in ████████, Kansas instead.

+++ **Description:** 

RPC-929-2 is an idiosyncratic Epiales-class Oneiric entity concentrated on a single consciousness. It is characterized as the inevitable recession of all creation within ALTR-929. It pursuits the acceleration of entropy to bring ALTR-929 back to its primordial state.[[footnote]] Otherwise known as Chaos. A void state that precedes the invasion of organic matter which eventually lead to the introduction of RPC-929-1 and its subsequent creations. [[/footnote]]

The entity takes on the appearance of a 24-years-old French woman by the name of "Esmée Jean Carina" (designated PoI-929A), who was involved in the previous incarnation of ALTR-929 as well as the death of George █████. Unlike its predecessor, RPC-929-2 is, for the time being, sapient and docile.

------
+++ **Description:** 

RPC-929-3 is a collective consciousness formed from RPC-929's previous quanta residue. Information that are destroyed or forgotten is absorbed into this entity and distorted. Its nature is to continuously destroy and reconstruct ALTR-929, while also returning foreign entities back to the baseline reality.

Its presence is characterized by the appearance of the //Danaus plexippus//,[[footnote]] Monarch Butterfly. It was likely the first incarnation of RPC-929-1 to ever materialize. [[/footnote]] but are known to take on the form of RPC-929's previous vessels such as the 11-year-old British student "Camille █████", who inhabited the role of RPC-929-1 from 1940 to 2019.

[[/tabview]]

The mantle of RPC-929-1 can be passed from person to person, influencing RPC-929-2's appearance as well as ALTR-929's. The exact method of this transfer is unknown.

Early into the creation of a new ALTR-929 model, the imaginarium will attempt to simulate the continuation of its current ego's memories. Over time, the framework will corrupt into an illogical dreamscape as seen in the previous ALTR-929 model.

During Camille █████'s incarnation as RPC-929-1, only two humans were confirmed to have been transported to ALTR-929. Those being Esmée Carina (PoI-929A) and George █████ (CSD-6868). Archeological analysis suggest that there might've been many other versions of ALTR-929 that was under the control of humans and animals alike.

According to PoI-929A's account, Camille █████'s older brother, Lewis █████, was her predecessor to the RPC-929-1 mantle, a reign that lasted only a few hours before his termination under RPC-929-2.
