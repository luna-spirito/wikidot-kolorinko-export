+++ **Containment Protocol:** 
RPC-929 is to be contained in a sterilized 4m^^3^^ chamber paneled with lead. Exploration of ALTR-929 is to be limited to survey drones under Dr. Alexander Milhouse's approval.

------
[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-929/Wonderland1.jpeg|width=260px|caption=ALTR-929.]]

+++ **Description:** 
RPC-929 is a cross-dimensional rift encased in the disarranged cadaver of 11-year-old Camille █████, a British student who died during the Battle of France (10 May – 25 June 1940).[[footnote]] The German invasion of France, Belgium, Luxembourg, and the Netherlands during the Second World War. [[/footnote]] Its relative vicinity radiates a coherency bubble calculated to be around 3.73 ACS.

Active organic compounds that are submerged inside the coherency bubble for a period of 30-45 minutes (depending on mass) are abruptly teleported into a sub-dreamscape designated as ALTR-929. While displaying physical laws and other properties typical of baseline reality, it is structured in a particle-waveform state. The exact nature of ALTR-929's framework and the validity of its designation as an "alternate reality" is still debated.

ALTR-929, otherwise known as //le Royaume Souverain de la Reine Carina// (English: the Sovereign Kingdom of Queen Carina), is a landmass with a biosphere similar to Western Europe. Its inhabitants, designated ALTR-929-e, are entities modeled after typical fauna, cryptids and fictional creatures sourced from myths, storybooks, and/or RPC-929-1’s imagination. 

All forms of life, whether native or foreign, are unable to die while inside ALTR-929’s barring the presence and intervention of RPC-929-2.

Three major entities occupy the reality as forces of ALTR-929's nature, designated RPC-929-1, RPC-929-2, and RPC-929-3. They regulate and control the state of ALTR-929 and its interaction with the baseline reality. Descriptions of these entities are as follows:

   RPC-929-1 appears to be the corporeal personification of RPC-929’s remaining consciousness as Camille █████. Despite theoretically being conscious for over 95 years, it is developmentally stunted, exhibiting a juvenile outlook on its surroundings.

RPC-929-1's nature is to create and maintain its creation as RPC-929's "ego". The exercise of its reality-warping property can be identified by the disembodied sound of a ringing bell. RPC-929-1 is formally known as "Her Sovereign Grace" and the "Sorceress Queen" as a title of endearment by the native inhabitants.

RPC-929-2's near presence temporarily paralyzes nearby entities, facilitating neutralization. Entities that are not native to ALTR-929 terminated by RPC-929-2 are transported back to baseline reality as preserved cadavers.
