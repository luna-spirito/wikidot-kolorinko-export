[[size 140%]] **Science of Thaumaturgy** [[/size]]

The science of thaumaturgy is divided into 4 branches of sciences. 

* **Formal Science** - mathematics, sacred geometry, systems theory, numerology
* **Natural Science** - astronomy, alchemy, natural philosophy, botany, physiology/medicine
* **Human Science** - history, linguistics and symbolism, music/poetry, psychology
* **Spiritual Science** - theology, hermeneutics, philosophy

------

[[size 140%]] **Systems of Thaumaturgy** [[/size]]

The application of thaumaturgy is categorized under 3 systems.

* **Personal** - Thaumaturgic art that only requires the thaumaturge's own spirituality.
* **Instrumental** - Thaumaturgic art that requires the help of instruments.
* **External** - Thaumaturgic art that seeks the help of higher powers.

------

[[size 140%]] **Doctrine** [[/size]]

Doctrine is the main principle of thaumaturgy, as practitioners must spiritually transform themselves to apply their art. An example of doctrine is the discipline of the martial artist--or the trance-like flow of focus of the musician.

++ How to write a thaumaturgic art

------
The list of thaumaturgic arts in the [/the-framework-of-thaumaturgy framework of thaumaturgy]  is based on real-life magical practises across history and culture; hence why you're not gonna see an actual step-by-step guide to practising it—as it's not the point of the document. Rather, see it more as a tone-setter for this page. This page will be used for collaborative and referential purposes, so anyone can add their own thaumaturgic art to the page.

An important theme of thaumaturgy within MI13 is the subtlety of its process. To outsiders, the practise seems completely supernatural, but a closer inspection of it at first will seem to lose its magic. Thaumaturgy, at least in the context of MI13's definition, isn't the same as reality-bending or anomalous abilities, as there is an actual science into how it works.

You can write any art as ludicrous as the magic systems you'd see in say, Harry Potter or Dungeons & Dragons. What's important is that there is an explanation for how it works. The explanation can be pseudoscientific or otherworldly. As long as it's an art that the average person can pick up and perform with enough practice.

+ Fire Breath
To breathe as a dragon
------
[[/=]]
[[include component:image-block name= mmagic1.jpg|caption= //Belgian fire breath practisioner in the Jaipur Maharaja Brass Band.// |width=220px]]

**Composer:** //Achaemenid Empire (5th century BC)//

**Instruments:** //Lighter, cigarette, torch, alcoholic drinks//

**Key:** //Natural science, performance art//

**Catalogue:** //n/a//

**Style:** //Breathing technique//

**Movements:** //n/a//

------

Fire breath is a breathing technique that allows practitioners to spit fire out of their mouths. Through breathing and meditation, they can exhaust alcohol vapor and methane out of their throat, which is then lit up with an instrument such as a cigarette or lighter. 

Experts of the art are all invariably alcoholics and smokers either before or due to their practise. Certain breathing techniques can give different properties to the fire. Adept practitioners could conjure short-burst flames, focused fire beams, or fireballs with enough training. 

This art has been known to cause serious injuries to novice practitioners, and long-term use is known to cause burn scars on the side of their cheeks.

+ The King's Command
//Dieu et mon droit//
------
[[/=]]
[[include component:image-block name= mmagic2.jpg|caption= //King Richard the Lionheart.// |width=220px]]

**Composer:** //Charlemagne (768 - 814), King Richard I of England (1157 - 1199)//

**Instruments:** //Talismans associated with royalty//

**Key:** //Spiritual science, incantation//

**Catalogue:** //Patriarcha; or the Natural Power of Kings, Lex Rex (The Law and the King)//

**Style:** //Incantation hypnosis//

**Movements:** //That the first Kings were Fathers of Families, Positive Laws do not infringe the Natural and Fatherly Power of Kings.//

------

A powerful art that can only be used by people with direct lineage to Charlemagne the great or King Richard of England, the King’s Command is a linguistic art that gives practitioners the ability to command humans and spirits alike to follow their orders for a short period.

To invoke this command, the practitioner must articulate orders with an authoritative voice, inducing an aura that causes temporary mental disorientation among nearby individuals. This proclamation elicits an abrupt sensation of fear and intimidation, instilling an immediate desire to please the speaker.

This art has been historically used by kings and high nobles to intimidate their subjects and inspire soldiers. Despite being a powerful incantation, it spiritually exhausts the practitioner, who uses it to posture the presence of a king. The art demands its users to feel an ontological superiority over others.

Notably, entities such as faeries, monarchs, and clergymen with heightened spiritual reservoirs exhibit a capacity to wield the King's Command more routinely, mitigating the risk of significant spiritual depletion due to their inherent authority, requiring less initiative to project dominance.

An emulated version of this spell, known as the //Windsor Vow//, can be performed by any practitioner of thaumaturgy. However, lacking the authority of lineage, the Windsor Vow demands greater spiritual exertion while producing only diminished effects.

The movements of the art's doctrine are as follows:

**I: //That the first Kings were Fathers of Families//**
Fathers have the power to consent to the uniting or conferring of their Fatherly Right of Sovereign Authority on whom they please and thus is the responsibility of the king.

**II: //Positive Laws do not infringe the Natural and Fatherly Power of Kings.//**
Power is given by the multitude to one man, thus they maintain the natural liberty of mankind through the ordinance of God. That the Law which enjoys obedience to Kings as is delivered in the terms of 'Honour thy Father,' as all power were originally in the Father.

+ Rat Tyrant
Dominion over vermin and calamity
------
[[/=]]
[[include component:image-block name= mmagic3.jpg|caption= //A 'Rat King' talisman used by Rat Tyrant practitioners// |width=220px]]

**Composer:** //The English and French covens of the mid-14th century//

**Instruments:** //Rat king, rat hide//

**Key:** //Natural and human science, linguistic//

**Catalogue:** //n/a//

**Style:** //Incantation hypnosis//

**Movements:** //##red|[REDACTED]##//

------
##red|THE PRACTISE OF BLACK MAGIC IS FORBIDDEN.##

A lethal form of witchcraft that gained notoriety during the high medieval age. Practitioners of this adopt the 'doctrine of rats', following their philosophy and speaking their language, giving them control over small rat colonies by dominating their spirits. 

Central to the Rat Tyrant's methodology is the adoption of the 'doctrine of rats.' Practitioners immerse themselves in the language, behavior, and philosophy of rodents which gives the tyrant dominion over their spirituality. This domination usually takes the form of rat king talismans, which exude pheromones that control an even larger colony of rats.

The huge swath of rats is harmful both to the target and the practitioner. The unsanitary conditions and miasma emanating from the rat colonies necessitate practitioners to don protective clothing and air-filtering masks.

The art was historically employed by witches during the 15th and 17th centuries, this malevolent craft aimed to sow famine and disease in cities and villages. The victims of the attacks would suffocate under the miasma of the colony and later have their corpses devoured.

+ Face of Flagellation
To fool the world through many faces
------

[[/=]]
[[include component:image-enlarge name= mmagic4.jpg|width=200px|caption= Young member of the Flagellation Pathway.]]

**Composer:** //Joseph Grimaldi (1778 – 1837), Italian carnival cults of the 16th century//

**Instruments:** //Painted mask, greasepaint, motley garb, bells, mirrors//

**Key:** //Human science, performance art//

**Catalogue:** //The Flagellation Pathway (1830), Carnivale Obscura, Deus Risus: On the Laughing God//

**Style:** //Spirit glamour, ego inversion//

**Movements:** //Covet no purpose, Individuality is a paradox, Wisdom is your enemy, et al.//

------
The Face of Flagellation is a thaumaturgic art based on the philosophical framework of "Flagellatism". It is said to draw its power from a deity known as the [/rpc-571 God of Flayed Clowns]. By donning a painted face or mask, practitioners may distort their own identities, creating a glamour that emotionally manipulates their audience.

The essence of this art lies in ego inversion. The mask functions simultaneously as a mirror and a distortion, embodying both self and not-self. The painted face and/or mask becomes a vessel through which the thaumaturgist bends social and spiritual laws; lies may acquire the weight of truth, while hidden truths are uncovered by the thaumaturgist.

Historically, jesters and clowns were granted the unique license to ridicule rulers and nobles. The Face of Flagellation extends this “fool’s privilege” into thaumaturgic practice, enabling the practitioner to reveal or obscure through mockery and performance. The art was especially feared in France and Italy during the 17th century, when carnival tricksters were rumored to conjure grand lies and illusions that changed the fate of kingdoms.

**I. Covet no purpose.**
Attachment to grand meanings and desire embeds us in the lie of permanence and order. 

**II. Individuality is a paradox.**
Do not let the pursuit of individuality blind you from the simple truth of oneness.

**III. Wisdom is your enemy.**
As existence is ever-changing, so is truth.

**IV. Death is absurd, it is foolish to infer it.**
Death is the final curtain of liberation. 

**V. Subject yourself to mockery and sympathy.**
Your purpose is to be a slave of mankind. 

**VI. Smile and laugh, in spite of your pain.**
Life plays with comedy and tragedy in equal measures.

+ Lift of Flight
To be free with the wind
------

[[/=]]
[[include component:image-enlarge name= SirFerrisUmbrella.jpg|width=200px|caption= Sir Theodore Benjamin attempting to fly.]]

**Composer:** //The 1864 Flight Club, Sir Theodore Benjamin//

**Instruments:** //Umbrella, large cloth, hellium balloons//

**Key:** //Natural science, performance art//

**Catalogue:** //How to Fly with an Umbrella//

**Style:** //Aviation physics, athletic art//

**Movements:** //Subverting the Lift Equation, Wind Buoyancy, Ultralight Paragliding Aviation//

------
The Lift of Flight is a thaumaturgic discipline enabling controlled buoyancy and limited vertical ascent through the use of lightweight, drag-producing instruments. When properly enacted, ordinary objects such as umbrellas, cloth sheets, or helium balloons exhibit aerodynamic properties, generating lift sufficient to support human weight.

It is humanity’s nature to want to soar in the sky. It was a common phenomenon for children to leap from high places with makeshift devices such as umbrellas, often resulting in grave injuries. The 1864 Flight Club was formed by children working in London factories who, in their pastime, experimented with flight. Through a miracle, they succeeded in creating the discipline of the "Lift of Flight".

Sir Theodore Benjamin later codified this thaumaturgic discipline in his 1979 work: "How to Fly with an Umbrella", providing detailed instructions for reliably and safely practising the art.
