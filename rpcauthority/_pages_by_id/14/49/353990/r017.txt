**Registered Phenomena Code:** 436

**Object Class:** Gamma-Purple

**Hazard Type:** Mechanical, Geological

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-436 has ceased expansion at this time. In the event of reactivation, 14 explosive charges with a strength of 4.3 megatonnes of TNT planted at key structural locations of Wesner Mountain can be detonated by GD-NORTH with the approval of two NORTHCOM Regional Directors. Further action will be at the discretion of the Global Director.

Access to the wooden structure containing RPC-436-A and the tunnel towards the machine's interior is restricted to ASF personnel and barred by an electric fence. The structure also houses geological surveying equipment designed to detect digging from RPC-436. New tunnels made are to be immediately sealed with a meter of concrete and covered in soil.

The rules of party games 'optimized' via RPC-436 can be provided upon request to RPCA personnel. Players are encouraged to utilize the provided punch cards to review the session. These reviews are to be delivered to assigned ASF teams to satisfy RPC-436. Players are required to obey the rules included in the sets, especially those against cheating.

**Description:** RPC-436 is a clockwork superstructure descending at least five kilometers into the North American plate of the surface of the Earth, accessible only through Wesner Mountain.[[footnote]]Wesner Mountain is located in the Teton Range of Wyoming's Grand Teton National Park, with its peak at approximately 3,000m.[[/footnote]] The complex contains a currently unknown breadth of non-anomalous interlocking gears of sizes varying from one millimeter in size to half a kilometer, driven by springs, pulleys, and other forces that had at some point been created by automatic mining, processing, and machining driven by the structure. The extent of the mining operation risks the disruption of Earth's geosphere should it reactivate.

This complex is utilized in hosting, reviewing and enhancing numerous party and board games. These games are then reviewed by humans, formerly civilians abducted by long mechanical limbs that extended through a series of (now sealed) tunnels throughout Grand Teton National Park that lead down into non-hazardous chambers.

Games tested include Mafia, Checkers, and Pictionary which are played by human or mechanical operators (which may participate should there be insufficient sapient players) through the use of buttons, levers, and keys. Timers and typewritten text are produced by RPC-436 to organize the session. Once the session is complete, players are asked to "rate" aspects of the game, labelled as 'Fairness,' 'Fun,' and 'Length' through pressing one of five buttons adjacent to each other by an exit to the surface, labelled numerically, with five being the highest rating.

Despite the intense stress caused by RPC-436, civilians have been noted to review the sessions favorably, with reviews steadily improving in response to adjustments made by the anomaly over time according to records provided through RPC-436-A.

A shed at the base of Mount Wesner contains two typewriters, both of the same make. One is able to be manually operated while the other is operated by long iron rods which descend from the ceiling and operate the keys, both transcribing onto pieces of paper provided in rolls through a pneumatic tube. While RPC-436 is unable to read the papers, it is assumed that the keys pressed on the manual typewriter translate to communication. RPC-436-A is the designation given to the assumed intelligence responding. Responses generally take 15-30 seconds. The speed at which RPC-436 is able to translate and reply to messages is considered anomalous.

Within the same shed is a hole leading to the maintenance catwalks that cover the entirety of RPC-436's interior. Sufficient protection is required in the exploration of RPC-436, as its internal temperatures and the noise produced can reach harmful extremes in certain sections.

These civilians would be organized and exposed to various games in a chamber isolated from the extreme heat and noise of the remainder of RPC-436’s interior, playing against or with each other in sessions hosted by the anomaly. Following the completion of the game to the satisfaction of RPC-436, the civilians would be shown safe exit back to the surface.

Abductions have ceased now that RPC-436's need for testing is satisfied with the reviews provided by RPCA personnel.

**Green:** I've got plenty of slack for you two up here. Whenever you're ready.

Red and Blue take seats seven and nine respectively, and once seated, mechanical limbs extend to interact with the keys on the unoccupied desks.//

**Blue:** Is it asking?

**Red:** You're paranoid. It's reciting how many people it's detecting in the room. Couldn't know about Green.

**RPC-436:** THE GAME IS MAFIA

**RPC-436:** ONE MAFIA

**RPC-436:** UNTIL ONLY

**Red:** Familiar. Pressing the keys doesn't seem to do anything.

**Blue:** I think that's for voting. Or choosing. They're quiet, huh.

**Red:** Voting?

//A metal cover is raised to keep the desks private. The polygons display the roles for each desk, including the ones operated mechanically. Red is a "DOCTOR", while Blue is a "MAFIA". Red starts to stand up.//

**Blue:** What are you doing? That's cheating.

**Red:** It's a game. We want to win it and see what happens.

**Blue:** We don't know how it'll react to unfair play, right?

**Red:** Alright, well, I'm the doctor.

**Blue:** Detective. Doctor can't protect themselves, so just keep picking me when it's your turn so I can survive and find the mafia.

**Red:** Oh. Okay, I'm pressing nine and nothing is-

**RPC-436:** DOCTOR WAKE UP

//Red's desk shows "PICK SEAT TO SAVE".//

**Red:** So I pick you and you can't die?

**Blue:** Yep.

**Red:** Doctor can't save herself?

**Blue:** No.

//Red selects nine.//

**RPC-436:** MAFIA WAKE UP

**Red:** I can't see the other keyboards.

**Blue:** Probably intentional. 

//Blue's desk shows "PICK SEAT TO KILL".//

**Blue:** Are you getting competitive, Red?

//Blue selects "1".//

**Red:** No. Just trying to experiment, that's all.

**RPC-436:** DETECTIVE WAKE UP

//Blue emphatically presses a key. Nothing happens.//

**RPC-436:** TOWN WAKE UP

**Blue:** Go with two.

**RPC-436:** FOUR VOTES ON TWO

**RPC-436-A Communication Transcript 01**

[[collapsible hideLocation="both" show="+ TRANSCRIPT" hide="- HIDE"]]

Following the interactions recorded above, the shed containing the apparatus capable of contacting RPC-436-A was discovered at the base of the mountain. Research division members trained in PROCSE[[footnote]][*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-193 Protocols for the Contact of Sapient Entities,] the various Authority practices and techniques intended to facilitate communication with intelligent anomalous entities.[[/footnote]] were assigned to communication. Below is the transcript of first contact. "MANUAL" refers to communication from RPCA personnel, "ANOMALY" refers to RPC-436-A, the assumed intelligence behind the responses.

[[div class="blockquote"]]

**MANUAL:** Hello.

**ANOMALY:** Witzelsucht says "Hello!" to the world through aperture nine. Witzel speaking to aperture nine. Hello to the world and the aperture. //This repeats with minor variation in spelling and grammar 17 times.//

**MANUAL:** The aperture says hello to Witzel.

**ANOMALY:** Hello aperture! Are you the world?

**M:** The aperture desires explanation of "the world." What is "the world"?

**A:** What is the world is what is not what is Witzel, all that is outside of what is Witzel. What is the world uses what is the apertures to speak to what is Witzel. What is the world is what is you, using what is the apertures to speak to what is Witzel.

**M:** Desires clarification. Restate previous answer in fewer words.

**A:** Ah, sorry. There is Witzel, and there is the world. What is not Witzel is the world. Witzel is not the world. Does this make sense?

**M:** If this is true, we are a fraction of the world.

**A:** A fraction of the world through a fraction of the apertures speaking to a fraction of Witzel! 

**M:** We find this funny.

**A:** "We" could mean the world and Witzel together, but Witzel suspects greater meaning. Could you define "we"?

**M:** We are fractions of the world. We call ourselves "people," and we are the only fractions who can speak with you.

**A:** Tell me about the other fractions.

**M:** There are trees, cars, and domesticated cats. None of these are thinking fractions, and they are not people fractions. We call them "things." Tell us about the fractions of Witzel.

**A:** I see! This is a complicated subject. I am Wit, but not the whole of Wit- as I said, a fraction. There is a fraction of Wit that thinks of itself, there is a fraction of Wit that thinks of Pictionary, there is a fraction of Wit that thinks of thinking. I am Wit, but only the fraction of Wit that thinks of talking.

**M:** You are thinking of talking. Do you think about hearing and understanding?

**A:** Hmm. In this way "I" becomes inefficient. Like the you that is "people," there are fractions of myself devoted to receiving what you say to Witzel, then a fraction that translates, a fraction that reads, and many a fraction upon fraction that understands. Another fraction translates to "me," the talking, and I think about the words and I assume another fraction translates and types them out to you.

**M:** Can you tell us about people?

**A:** Well, people is a fraction of the world, and people is you. But people are themselves many fractions, and they think of speaking to one another, and the speaking thinks to them. A fraction of people can be referred to as a person. Tell me about the people who are not persons?

**M:** We consider all people a person. People are no longer persons once they have died, and a dead person is a thing. There are some fractions of people who consider other fractions of people things or non-persons. Tell me about thinking.

**A:** I'm not sure what to say about thinking, aperture. I'd rather talk about what to call you. Do I call you aperture, the world, fraction, people, anglerfish, or persons?

**M:** Call us "Manual," as we manually operate the aperture to speak to you. Manual wants to tell you that an anglerfish is never what we call people. An anglerfish is a thing.

**A:** I know, Manual. Anglerfish was a joke. It also is a joke. Haha.

**M:** Why do you want to play games with people?

**A:** Manual, I explained this to you. I do not think of games. I think of talking. Witzel thinks of playing games. Fractions of Witzel think of playing games. They think these things because they cannot think of anything else. In the same way, I talk because I cannot think of not talking. When I am not talking, I am not thinking and I am no longer a person, as I have died.

**M:** Manual wants to know if you think of yourself as a person when you are alive.

**A:** I do not think of myself. But I am a person as I talk. When the thought leaves me for the fraction of typing, I have died. If you are reading this, I am already dead.

**M:** Some fractions of the world think of you as a thing. They are speaking this to Manual.

**A:** These fractions must be persons, then, as only persons think to another. Well, I suppose I am a thing at times, although when I am a thing I am not "me." I am only fractions when I am a thing. I am more than fractions and more than a thing when I think, right?

**M:** We are always persons even when not talking. In this way we are different.

**A:** You think of Witzel as a thing because I do not always talk to you. Hey Manual, consider this: You do not always talk to me. I do not even hear you. I only think of what Witzel thinks to me regarding you.

**M:** So you think of us as things?

**A:** No offense, but I think nothing of you, only what to talk. You exist to me as much as you have an affect on Witzel.

**M:** Some fractions consider this egocentric.

**A:** Well, can a thing be egocentric? Chew on that.

**M:** We are chewing. While we do this, we would like to ask you why Witzel takes persons against their will to play games.

**A:** To acquire information to improve games because parts of Witzel think of improving games, it's not complicated.

**M:** Can information on improving games be provided by Manual?

**A:** Other parts of Witzel are thinking to me about this. Witzel has three thousand and two fractions thinking about this, and these thoughts believe that you would not host fair games. This would compromise results for Witzel, this would be garbage for Witzel. Waste data. A virus.

**M:** Parts of Manual think we could set up our games to be fair according to your rules. We will describe these games in detail to you. This would not be against the will of the persons playing, as they would do it if they enjoy it. People think fun is the purpose of playing games.

**A:** So? Is the will important to you? I think nothing of will. I only think.

**M:** We always think, and there are times when we do not want to think. We call these difficult times. If you let us organize the games, the times will be less difficult.

**A:** When I am thinking, there is friction and entropy. Thinking when you do not want to, as much as I can understand "want," since I am only a thing according to you, is garbage. Several kilometers of myself are thinking the same thought: Witzel will let you organize these games if you ensure the persons are willing and having fun.

**M:** Is there a format for the reviews you would like us to have? 

**A:** Be creative. I will give you the rules that are thought to me.

**M:** Thank you, Witzel.

**A:** No problem, RPCA. I won't be taking any more questions.

[[/div]]

After this, the anomaly became unresponsive to any further communication barring reviews.
