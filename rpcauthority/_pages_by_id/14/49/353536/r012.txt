**Containment Protocols:** No organisms with a cardiovascular system are to be allowed entry into RPC-819's current sphere of influence. Whenever Authority personnel must enter RPC-819, wearing a garlic necklace[[footnote]]Garlic has proven to be a sufficient deterrent.[[/footnote]] is mandatory for every individual. The area of influence will be advertised as an indigenous reserve to civilians under the guise of "Protecting a local Tarahumara tribe.” Protectorate personnel acting as national guard are to prevent any unauthorized entry through the redirection of visitors and the deployment of animal-deterring pheromones.

To discourage entry into the area during the recent surge in cryptid hunters, Authority agents initiated several secretly-controlled blazes that were later blamed on local tourists. Additionally, a cover-up story involving violent shaman rituals has been created to excuse any victims of RPC-819 prior to containment.

**Description:** RPC-819 is a sapient[[footnote]]Considered on par with that of //Homo sapiens//.[[/footnote]] forested region in Copper Canyon, Mexico. RPC-819 can manipulate the flora's roots within its area of influence. The exact size of the area is currently undetermined but is thought to be circular with a diameter of around 75 km.[[footnote]]Approximately 5.5% of Copper Canyon's total area.[[/footnote]]  RPC-819 is proficient in the movements of its roots, as it is capable of fine motions, seizing objects, and exerting forces tremendous enough to displace the terrain. Furthermore, the epidermis of affected vegetation can extract blood from the environment, which generally occurs after RPC-819 has incapacitated its victims. Consumed blood is then used to expand the area of influence, converting increased amounts of vegetation into RPC-819.

Moreover, the anomaly is known to be highly intelligent and capable of sadism. Additionally, it has shown competency in social manipulation;  it has occasionally abstained from attacking prey to attract more en masse by utilizing the superstitions of its targets.
