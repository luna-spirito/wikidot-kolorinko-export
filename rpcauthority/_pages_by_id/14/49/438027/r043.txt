**Description:** RPC-698[[footnote]]Locally referred to as [[[rpc-419|"The Teton Lights"]]] in certain parts of Wyoming.[[/footnote]] is an unidentified aircraft that measures roughly 20 meters in width and 10 meters in height and is similar in design to that of a stereotypical alien saucer. RPC-698 was first spotted in 1959 near Nellis Air Force Base. Several photographs taken of RPC-698 reveal tentacle-like appendages protruding out of the bottom of the saucer, which has lead Authority researchers to assume that RPC-698 is not being piloted on the inside as originally thought, but is instead a living organism. RPC-698 rarely travels into densely populated areas and is frequently seen flying over isolated communities, remote forests, national parks, although it seems to frequent the Nevada Desert the most.

Authority researchers believe that RPC-698 is the direct cause of a phenomenon that affects 0.2% of the total human population[[footnote]]2 million people.[[/footnote]]. Direct exposure to RPC-698's strobe lights seems to induce this phenomenon in individuals who observe it in person. RPC-698 causes affected individuals to suddenly believe in the existence of extraterrestrial lifeforms and that Area 51 is not a United States Air Force testing range[[footnote]]The front cover of Site-002.[[/footnote]], but is actually a supposed top-secret facility operated by the U.S. Government for the sole purpose of capturing, studying, and containing extraterrestrial lifeforms and their technology.

[[include component:image-block name=12736890_web1_Alienstock_Sept19-22_19-102.jpg|caption=Several individuals under the influence of RPC-698 protesting outside of [[[Site-002]]] in 2019|enlarge=false]]

Over the course of 5-7 hours, RPC-698 will slowly begin to negatively affect how individuals perceive the world around them, often implanting false memories of encounters with extraterrestrial lifeforms, such as abductions, UFO sightings, and even memories of working at Area 51.

The majority of the individuals that find themselves affected by RPC-698 are curently considered by Site-002 management as "Generally harmless" and currently do not pose a direct threat to either the Authority or The Veil. However, there have been several instances of affected individuals attempting to break into or infiltrate Site-002 with varying levels of success. Individuals that have been affected by RPC-698 also suffer from a psychological effect known as belief perseverance, which makes any attempts by security staff to reason with them significantly difficult as they will continuously attempt to reiterate the belief that the United States government is conducting unlawful and unethical experimentation on extraterrestrial beings that are being held against will inside of a top-secret facility located beneath Area-51.
