**Containment Protocols:** RPC-526 is secured in a standard storage locker in Site-033. Testing on RPC-526 may only be authorised by personnel with Security Clearance 3 and above. RPC-526 must always be set to a mass of 1x10^^15^^ kg or less[[footnote]] Any mass above that has shown to have significant stress on the infrastructure of the Site. [[/footnote]]. Testing of RPC-526 is to be conducted in a secure, airtight room, where only RPC-526 and CSD personnel are allowed. CSD personnel are to be monitored via a surveillance camera at all times. CSD personnel are to be terminated if they begin to use RPC-526, whether to breach containment or escape the facility.

All tests are to be logged, including the date, CSD code, and additional information, as well as the results of the test.

In light of Incident-026-1, RPC-526 is not to be brought onto any vehicle, and it to be carried and transported on foot if ever deemed necessary. RPC-526 is to be sealed in a lead-lined suitcase at all times during transportation, and is to be carried alone, with no other object in the same suitcase.

-----

**Description:** RPC-526 is a white cylindrical disc measuring a width of 6.0 cm and a height of 0.75 cm. RPC-526 has a logo imprinted on the center of its backside, that seems unable to be removed by any normal means. The logo consists of a ring, and the latter ‘A’ on top of it, stylized to appear similar to the symbol closely related with Anarchism.

Upon human contact with RPC-526, a light beep is heard and an interface appears on the opposite side of RPC-526. The interface is made up of a circular slider bar, inside of which is a tick as well as a cross. Sliding the bar to any position, then selecting the tick activates its anomalous effects. Selecting the cross causes the interface to disappear, supposedly switching RPC-526 off.

Selecting the tick causes the person who has activated RPC-526, hereby referred to as RPC-526-1, to suddenly increase in density. After this, RPC-526-1 will weigh anywhere between 1500 kg and 7.3x10^^22^^ kg[[footnote]] This corresponds to the mass of the moon. [[/footnote]]. The slider determines how much mass the individual will gain, based on a logarithmic scale.

The increase in mass of the individual can occur regardless of the distance between said individual and RPC-526, and will cause the same problems expected with such an increase in mass, with the exception that the individual does not appear to suffer any side-effects from the sudden increase in mass, and has no problem with movement. Due to the significant increase in gravitational force, objects will become gravitationally attracted to RPC-526-1. At the highest setting, RPC-526-1 can cause significant impact on the tides and manmade structures around them. Tsunamis will begin to form if RPC-526-1 is near the coast, and buildings will topple under the strong gravitational force of attraction towards RPC-526-1[[footnote]] Should any of the mentioned objects make impact with RPC-526-1, the person can, and will still expire due to the sheer force of impact. [[/footnote]]. This likely leads to death en masse for the population within ███ km of RPC-526-1.

These effects last for varying amounts of time, with this amount of time decreasing as the mass of RPC-526-1 increases. At lowest settings, RPC-526's effects can last up to 24 hours, while at the highest setting, RPC-526's effects last only 5 minutes. It is unknown if these two factors are related in a linear fashion, or in some other way.

**Addendums**:

[[collapsible show="+ Show Discovery Log" hide="- Hide Discovery Log"]] RPC-526 was first discovered during an RPC Authority raid of Synther Laboratories on ██ July 19██, under the suspicion that anomalous technology was being designed. During the raid, RPC-526, as well as █ other similar discs were found in a locker belonging to Researcher ███████, who was later found out to be involved with the development of anomalous weaponry.

RPC-526, along with the other discs, were transported to Site-033, where RPC-526's anomalous propeerties were eventually discovered after closer observation. [[/collapsible]]

__**Test 02: 24/10/19██**__

> **Subject**: CSD-4167
> **Test Materials**: RPC-526, 1 blue ballpoint pen, 1 piece of blank A4 paper
> **Procedure**: Subject was asked to set RPC-526 to the lowest setting, and write his name on the A4 paper. 
> 
> **Result**: Subject activated RPC-526 and wrote his name onto the piece of paper without incident.

__**Test 03: 25/10/19██**__

> **Subject**: CSD-4167
> **Test Materials**: RPC-526, 1 blue ballpoint pen, 1 piece of blank A4 paper
> **Procedure**: Subject was asked to set RPC-526 to 1x10^^8^^ kg, and write his name on the A4 paper. 
> 
> **Result**: The piece of paper immediately became stuck onto CSD-4167's shoulder as he activated RPC-526. The ballpoit pen was also observed moving towards CSD-4167, along the table on which it was set. CSD-4167 expressed mild difficulty in removing the paper from his shoulder, however managed to do so after 2 minutes and wrote his name without incident.

__**Test 05: 07/04/19██**__

> **Subject**: CSD-4209
> **Test Materials**: RPC-526, 1 9mm pistol, loaded with two bullets.
> **Procedure**: Subject was asked to set RPC-526 to 1x10^^10^^ kg, before holding the gun away from him and firing at a mounted target 20m away. Subject was then asked to increase RPC-526 to 1x10^^12^^ kg, and to do the same
> 
> **Result**: Subject activated RPC-526, at which point the revolver appeared to become stuck to CSD-4209's chest. Subject expressed considerable difficulty in removing the pistol, but managed to do so after 3 minutes. Subject fired the pistol without incident, hitting the target.
> 
> Subsequently, subject increased RPC-526's setting to 1x10^^12^^ kg and reactivated it. The pistol was violently pulled from subject's hand, towards his chest with considerable force. Subject expressed that it was significantly more difficult to remove the pistol. Subject fired the pistol without incident, hitting the target. However as subject moved closer to the target, both bullets became dislodged and moved towards the subject at high velocity. Both bullets pierced through the subject's sleeve, and became embedded into the subject's skin. Later analysis of the test footage revealed the bullets to be travelling at a speed comparable to a gunshot.

__**Test 06: 19/04/19██**__

> **Subject**: CSD-4209
> **Test Materials**: RPC-526, 1 9mm pistol, loaded with a single bullet.
> **Procedure**: Subject was asked to set RPC-526 to 1x10^^15^^ kg, before holding the gun away from him and firing at a mounted target 20m away.
> 
> **Result**: Subject activated RPC-526, at which point the pistol was violently ripped from subject's hand, towards his chest. This resulted in a broken rib.

__**Test 07: 24/05/19██**__

> **Subject**: CSD-4209
> **Test Materials**: RPC-526, 1 9mm pistol, loaded with a single bullet.
> **Procedure**: The pistol was mounted on a pedestal, 1.3m above the ground, and secured with a steel holder. Subject was asked to set RPC-526 to 1x10^^15^^ kg, before pulling the trigger of the pistol to fire it at a mounted target 20m away.
> 
> **Result**: Subject activated RPC-526, and noticed significant bending of several sections of the steel holder. Subject also noticed a significant force of attraction towards the pedestal. Upon firing the pistol, the pistol exploded, causing pieces of metal to be ejected around the room. Subject was wounded by 3 pieces of steel which had each lodged themselves inside the subject's thigh, shoulder and face.
> 
> Frame-by-frame analysis of test footage later revealed that the bullet that was fired managed to reach a displacement of 70 cm from the pistol before being pulled backwards by CSD-4209's force of attraction. The bullet had returned into the nose of the pistol with a velocity considerably higher than that of a gunshot, igniting the leftover gunpowder and causing the pistol to explode.

__**Test 12: 03/12/19██**__

> **Subject**: CSD-4545
> **Test Materials**: RPC-526, CSD-4546
> **Procedure**: Subject was asked to set RPC-526 to 1x10^^20^^ kg.
> 
> **Result**: Subject activated RPC-526, causing CSD-4546 to be violently pulled towards him, with an estimated velocity of ██ m s^^-1^^. The impact terminated both personnel. However, the significant impact flung subject into a wall of the room, resulting in massive structural failure of the light containment sector of Site-033. It is to be noted that this is not only due to the subject's extreme weight being thrown against the wall, but also the large gravitational force of the subject pulling the room's walls towards him. This led to the collapse of several buildings within the light containment sector, along with the containment breach of █ other anomalies. The resulting damage has been estimated to cost around ██ billion dollars to repair.

//7 entries abridged.//

**Note from Head Researcher**: Any mass above 1x10^^15^^ kg has shown to cause significant stress on the infrastructure of the buildings around it. While we have been able to repair much of the damage, and have further strengthened a large portion of the infrastructure, it is important that we make sure such an event does not occur again. Researchers are now prohibited from setting RPC-526 to any mass above 1x10^^15^^ kg, and will meet severe disciplinary action if found doing so.

> **13 06**: RPC-526 is secured in a small lead-lined container. Five (5) Clearance Level 1 personnel from board an Authority helicopter[[footnote]] Model Boeing CH-47 Chinook. [[/footnote]]. It is unknown which agent was in possession of the container at this time.

PoI-526-4 is wearing thick cotton gloves with rubber padding. Security cameras show no reaction induced in RPC-526. PoI-526-4 returns objects into the container after 1 minute and 3 seconds of examination. PoI-526-4 sets the container in a baggage compartment near the ceiling of the helicopter. This caused all loose items, personnel and scraps of metal to be gravitationally attracted to PoI-526-4. Due to unknown reasons, the helicopter engine also ceases working, likely due to components within being broken by the sheer gravitational force of PoI-526-4.

> **13 53**: Helicopter begins to descend at a speed estimated to be ██ m s^^-1^^. Pieces of metal are ripped of the helicopter and flung towards PoI-526-4, who is presumably dead at this point.
> 
> **13 55**: Helicopter crashes into an undisclosed location in the Pacific Ocean. Crash witnessed by a nearby cruise ship said to be much larger than expected. Nearby cruise ship also appeared to experience the gravitational effects of PoI-526-4, moving uncontrollably towards the sinking aircraft. Waves are reported to form more than 5 km away from PoI-526-4, and reach at maximum height of around 60 m.
> 
> **13 57**: Cruise ship is hit by a multitude of waves, and is overturned. Of the estimated 3500 individuals on board, 4 survivors were found and rescued by Authority personnel.
> 
> **14 00**: Gravitational effects of PoI-526-4 cease. Helicopter estimated to be around 320 m below sea level at this point. No survivors from the helicopter have been found.
