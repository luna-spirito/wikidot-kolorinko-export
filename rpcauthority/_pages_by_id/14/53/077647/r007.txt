**Registered Phenomena Code:** CN-023

**Object Class:** Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** N/A

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-CN-023 is contained in a blast-resistant glass display case in an Administration Sector of Site-CN-006, with no necessities of further containment measures.

Once RPC-CN-023 activates, the Administration Area should correspond with the affected Site sector and react accordingly.

**Description:** RPC-CN-023 is a 25cm*18cm monthly calendar containing 13 monthly sheets. It is labeled with the month, date, lunar calendar date and important holidays and festivals in Chinese.

On the first day of each Gregorian month, the calendar automatically flips to the corresponding month, and the previous sheet disappears. A new sheet is added instead, retaining RPC-CN-023's 13 sheets count. Additionally, Japanese verbs written in black gel pen will appear on the current month's sheet and distributed across the dates. These words[[footnote]]Depending on the month, the verbs displayed by RPC-CN-023 will show different conjugation, of which no patterns had been found yet[[/footnote]] are written in hiragana only, with no kanji or katakana appearing at this time.

On dates marked by RPC-CN-023, anomalous events related to the verbs occurs in Site-CN-006. See Experimental Log RES-CN-023-990 for more details.
