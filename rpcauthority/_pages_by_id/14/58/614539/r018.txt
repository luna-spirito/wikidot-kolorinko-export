My name is Jr. Researcher Jones! I really love the RPC Authority. I was assigned to Site-045 in ████ ██████, ████████, █████ █████, ████ █████, ████.  I love to contain anomalies with my friends! The Site Director kept telling me that GD-Hermano is getting fed up with me, but I let a Terror Parasite loose in his office, so we'll see who's sloshing!

Jokes aside, it really has been a while. I was once known as Ianofdoom2, but after losing interest in SCP/RPC, I deleted my Wikidot account (Sad!). While my overall contributions to the site have been minor, I did contribute a few things to the lore:

* Site-045
* The name of MST Zeta/Zulu-45 Backwater Filterer (Ascriber did everything else, though)
* The names Selohssa and Soshi (The bulk of their lore was done by my good friend Moist Bread and the good people at Cosmic Embers)
* LO-025, 177 and 344.
