There has been an uptick in online content creators known as "cosplayers", previously unassociated with Kabushiki Kawaii or other groups of interest, that have begun taking photoshoots while dressed as various Kabushiki Kawaii creations or products. This is likely in an attempt to normalize the concepts and visual identifiers of these entities to the public. While Authority staff have successfully taken down any related posts they can find, a select few have managed to evade notice before being seen by 20% of the individual creator's follower base. While the outfits themselves do not contain any memetic or info hazardous effects, nor do any of the photos in their online posts, the outfits depict entities that have been confirmed to be highly dangerous as well as known RPCs currently in containment, and even previously unidentified or unassociated anomalies. Upon contacting the cosplayers in question, approximately 72% have little to no memory regarding the photo shoot despite it being fairly recent. Those who do retain memories of it have praised the professionalism and felt safe in the environment.
