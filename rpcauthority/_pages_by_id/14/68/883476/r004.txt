The Autoredactor version 3.0011EV9 is an artificial intelligence program dedicated to veil maintenance developed by NuCorp for Authority use at the beginning of the year. The program excelled during the previous quarter, redacting documents and physical manifestations of the anomalous even before discovery by the Authority, leading to record-low risk of paranormalcy within civil society. 

Authority representatives therefore backed the continued use of the service with the approach of the July deadline, supporting it as a revolutionary tool that must be integrated into the veilkeeping workflow. Some dissidents protested against trusting the artificial mind to discern the natural from the anomalous, however were silenced by rising charts and cut bureaucracy.  

With the deadline approaching, certain reports have risen from niche unveiled press communities with cause for concern. While facing monstrosities made of black bars lurking in small town museums, libraries with amnesiac books, and civilians that spontaneously lose the ability to speak, read or write - field agents ask: //what is the anomalous, if not just what I don't get?//
