**Registered Phenomena Code:** 6969-J

**Object Class:** Gamma-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Sapient Hazard, Transmutation Hazard, Destabilization Hazard, Emotional Hazard, Ideological Hazard, Mind Control Hazard, Titanic Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-6969-J is currently in Authority custody at [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/site-002 Site-002]. It is not allowed access to any Authority terminal. Authority database monitoring systems are to report any and all articles with unauthorized changes. Upon notification of any unauthorized changes, MST-Echo-621 "Kink-Shamers" must promptly report to RPC-6969-J's containment cell and revert said edits.

[[include component:image-block name=melvin2.png|caption=RPC-6969-J before termination of employment.]]

**Description:** RPC-6969-J is former Junior Researcher Melvin Mateos, a 21-year-old Hispanic male standing at 1.7 m and weighing 136.1 kg at the time of writing. RPC-6969-J was terminated from Authority employment on 04/02/2022 after making numerous unauthorized edits on various anomalies in the database. However, since then, RPC-6969-J has anomalously maintained connection to the database and continues to make unauthorized edits to articles. Edits by RPC-6969-J tend to be of a sexual nature and range from simple augmentations in anomalies' appearances to entire incident logs detailing anomalies' personal sex life.[[footnote]] RPC-6969-J often inserts itself in these incident logs. [[/footnote]] In addition, RPC-6969-J appears to have reality-bending properties, as whatever RPC-6969-J adds into the articles manifests within the physical world.[[footnote]] This effect can be reversed by simply reverting the article to a previous version. [[/footnote]] It has been speculated that RPC-6969-J could use its anomalous properties to pacify or even neutralize dangerous entities. However, at the time of writing, RPC-6969-J has used them solely for writing erotic fanfiction. ██ humanoid anomalies have complained to researchers of notable changes in appearance overnight, and it is estimated that 60%-70% of all humanoid anomalies have suffered the effects of RPC-6969-J at least once.
