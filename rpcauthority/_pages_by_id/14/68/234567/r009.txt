++ Foreward 
This volume serves as a restricted record of Monarch Security personnel, prepared on behalf of the UK Civil Service Commission. Entries have been curated for internal reference, archival preservation, and operational review.

All staff are strongly encouraged to adopt an operational alias for use in all official matters. Use of one’s legal name is to be minimized whenever possible. While lower-level personnel may, in limited circumstances, continue to operate under their legal name, all senior staff and individuals engaged in thaumaturgical disciplines are required to employ a designated alias at all times.

Numerous hidden existences possess the ability to exert influence or control over individuals through knowledge of their true names. The careless disclosure of such information may compromise not only your security but also the integrity of your soul. //This protocol exists for your protection.//

**Date of Birth:** 14 March, 1960
**Date of Enlistment:** 14 March, 1970

**Department:** RCS, Round Table

------
+++ Overview:

**Sir Joseph Jéan Coleridge** is the 28th Grand Sorcerer of the //Thaumarch//[[footnote]] Seat for the leader and representative of the Royal Congregation within the Round Table. [[/footnote]] and the //Royal Congregation of Sorcerers//. True to the traditions of his predecessors, he celebrates the art of theatrical showmanship, presenting himself to the public as what he calls a “humbug magician".

His path to the Congregation began in 1970, the day after his tenth birthday, when his father was recruited into Monarch Security. That same day, he was introduced to the organisation’s arcane culture by the 27th Grand Sorcerer, //Sir Ferris//,[[footnote]] Sir Theodore Benjamin [[/footnote]] who took him under private tutelage. Under Ferris’s guidance, the young Coleridge quickly set forth exceptional talent and work ethic during his tenure in the //New Council of Cavallon.//

In 1992, Sir Ferris succumbed to the //Hanover Curse//,[[footnote]] The Hanover Curse is a curse that affects all practitioners within Britain’s Curseship. It accelerates one's advancement in thaumaturgy but carries the risk of the afflicted succumbing to the curse and transforming into Bacchanals. [[/footnote]] and entrusted Coleridge with the duty of ensuring his death. Following this event, and in accordance with Sir Ferris’s wishes, Coleridge was appointed Grand Sorcerer of the Royal Congregation.

------
+++ Overview:

**Dr. Sleep Author** hails from the Unseelie city of Bontnewydd, Elfame. Born to a family of "dream-eaters", she spent her youth studying the discipline of psycho-oneirology at her local medical school.

Her formal training began at the Bontnewydd Collegium of Neurothaumic Sciences, where she specialised in psycho-oneirology. In her thesis: //The Liminal States and the Codification of Prefrontal Symbolic Speech//, she documented over 400 cross-species dream architectures, including those of sapient fungi and minor spirits. Her studies would help bring oneirology to the limelight of Eccleston University,[[footnote]] Founded in 1830 and named in honour of the late Sir Johan Eccleston, Eccleston University is a research university dedicated to the exploration and study of thaumaturgic arts. [[/footnote]] bringing them one step closer to combining the thaumaturgic arts and conventional science.

A //sleeping mist// passively seeps out of the doctor, a side effect of her hereditary neurothaumic condition. While harmless in trace amounts, prolonged exposure induces soporific states in most humans, ranging from light drowsiness to full catatonia. For this reason, she has strict protocols when interacting with humans.

**Off. Wilson:** Please state your identity. Miss. Sleep Author.

**Dr. Sleep Author:** Yes. That’s where I’ll be based. The entities in that Gate need ongoing care.

**Off. Wilson:** Alright. 

[...]

**Dr. Sleep Author:** You look sleep-deprived. There’s darkening under your eyes, and your blink rate is lower than normal. Your facial tension suggests reduced alertness. This diagnosis is consistent with at least forty-eight hours of cumulative sleep loss.

**Off. Wilson:** I haven’t slept in a while, yes. Cheers for noticing.

**Dr. Sleep Author:** Chronic sleep loss impairs executive function and short-term memory. It’s not ideal for conducting official interviews. //It is an offense to the virtue of civil service.//

Might I suggest a treatment of Lemborexant or a distilled koala bear dream essence? While human and elfish neurophysiology diverge at the pineal gland modulation stage, my cross-species trials show a 72% efficacy overlap. Furthermore, I know of several local lullabies from my hometown that are effective in inducing sleep in humans.

**Off. Wilson:** I already have treatment for it, thank you for offering, Miss Sleep Author.

**Dr. Sleep Author:** I don’t mean to offend, but I am the best in my field. I’m confident my recommendation would help.

**Off. Wilson:** And what does your field entail, exactly?

**Dr. Sleep Author:** Humans and animals aren’t the only creatures in need of sleep. Plants, fungi, and even microbes need the continual day and night cycle to thrive. I can put anything to sleep. Be it humans, elfs, demons, or gods. Sleep is the universal prophylaxis. We all need a good night’s rest to stay happy and healthy.

**Off. Wilson:** Noted. Will you be wearing that mask the entire time? Not saying it's inappropriate, but it doesn't exactly make you look approachable.

**Dr. Sleep Author:** Approachable? Amply. I didn't design this mask to be scary. I designed it to be as approachable as possible to my patients, especially when children are involved. It reminds them of elephants. And the dream-essence of elephants is far richer and more nourishing than the pedestrian flavour notes of cats and dogs.

**Off. Wilson:** Have you interacted with children in your service?

**Dr. Sleep Author:** No. Not human ones, at least. 

**Off. Wilson:** //That's good to hear.//

**Dr. Sleep Author:** I beg your pardo--

**Off. Wilson:** So, you're a dream-eater.

**Dr. Sleep Author:** ... That's my family's art. It's in my interest that people have pleasant dreams. Unlike with medicine and thaumaturgy, you can't force that upon people.

**Birthplace:** Paris, France
**Date of Birth:** 17 August, 1982
**Date of Enlistment:** 9 July, 2012

**Department:** 4th Company, Black Hunters Corp
**Position:** Intelligence Officer, Diviner
**Religion:** Roman Catholic

------
+++ Overview: 
**Officer Forget-me-not** was born to a family of Parisian thaumaturgists and members of the //Descendants of Yggdrasil//.[[footnote]] The Descendants of Yggdrasil is an organisation based in the northern regions of Europe, specialised in research and developing botanical and/or environmental anomalies. [[/footnote]] However, after an undisclosed incident that caused a rift in his family, he enlisted in the Black Hunters Corp of Monarch Security. To protect himself, he made a pact with an //Anthousai//,[[footnote]] Nymph of flowers. [[/footnote]] exchanging his body for the insurance of his mind.

His service as a field hunter ended abruptly when he was afflicted by the Hanover Curse during an engagement with a Bacchanal entity. The affliction corroded his body, turned him into a living host for conscious Myosotis. Although his physiology and mental health exhibited characteristics of inebriation, psychological assessment confirmed that his cognition remained intact and free from clinical psychosis, in spite of his increasingly strange and sporadic behaviour.

Following this incident, Forget-me-not moved into the role of Intelligence Officer within the Black Hunters Corp, where he has since repurposed his anomalous condition as an asset, using his Myosotis growths in the gathering and divination of intelligence.

**Forget-me-not:** (//Forget-me-not is seen watering some flowers in the garden.//) What brings you here, Miss Teacup?

**Teacup:** Sir Carousel, sir. He told me to come to you to ask you about... stuff.

**Forget-me-not:** Coleridge brought you here? (//Forget-me-not pauses.//) Then tell him my mind still holds. The roots haven’t reached it yet. Not all the way down.

**Teacup:** We're all worried about you, sir. It's been **█** years since... since y'know...

**Forget-me-not:** Are you afraid of me, little miss? 

**Teacup:** (//Teacup looks down, then focuses on Off. Forget-me-not's flowers instead.//) No, sir. I think you’re… beautiful.

**Forget-me-not:** (//Sigh//) To you, flowers are pressed between book pages, harmless little souvenirs. But beauty is a mask evil loves best. You’ll have to learn that, if you want to wear Coleridge’s mantle.

**Teacup:** I—I’ll learn. I promise.

(//Forget-me-not hands his watering can to Teacup, who proceeds to water the garden in his stead.//)

**Teacup:** It’s not his fault you’re like this. It’s that Bacchanal’s.  

**Forget-me-not:** (//Forget-me-not laughs coursely before coughing//) Blame? I don’t blame him. 

**Teacup:** But you don't like him very much, do you?

**Forget-me-not:** He drowned in his wine of knowing. I only sipped--and it was enough to split me open. (//Leans closer//) Look closely, child. 

**Teacup:** I um...

**Forget-me-not:** Look. Coat, skin, bone, thought. Blossoms wherever I am not vigilant. Roots tangled in marrow. Petals blooming in silence between words. This body isn't mine anymore.

**Teacup:** (//Quietly//) Do they… hurt?  

**Forget-me-not:** You've talked to them before. Old names, dead scents, secrets pressed flat between centuries. They crawl through my sleep and play me like a harp of veins. 

(//Forget-me-not sits down on a nearby chair.//)

**Forget-me-not:** //Forget me not,// they say. Always. Forget. Me. Not. (//Sigh//) And I cannot. I remember everything.

**Teacup:**  Still… at least you kept your mind. That’s good, isn’t it?  

**Forget-me-not:** (//Course laughter//) Good? //Yes.// The Anthousai are merciful gardeners. My thoughts remain whole. No Pact-madness, no raving. //God bless the Daughters of Yggdrasil// you'll hear those of my kin say! Only corrosion of everything else. My bones rot cleanly. My flesh remembers too much. But the mind… ah, the mind endures. (//Voice lowers//) Long enough, perhaps, to outlast Coleridge. To watch you take his place.  

**Teacup:** (//Suddenly trembling//) Don’t say that.  

**Forget-me-not:** Why not? The flowers tell me. I will see your coronation. And when your hands shake with the crown, I will end myself in bloom and blood. I will spare you the spectacle.  

**Teacup:** //Stop it.//  

**Forget-me-not:** (//Smiling, Forget-me-not leans closer.//) Child, you must learn that endings are not kind. Gardens are not kind. They grow from rot. From bodies. Even you, yes, even you, your mantle will sprout from his grave. And from mine.  

**Teacup:** //Stop it!// I’m not—I’m not listening anymore! 

(//Teacup puts the watering can down hard and abruptly leaves.//)

------
+++ Overview:
Originally a student thaumaturgist and intern with the Russian government, Yushatyr’s family immigrated to Norwich, England, in 2018. Her family was of particular interest to Monarch Security due to their heritage, Thaumaturgic art, which involves silversmithing and gilded craftsmanship.

Upon her arrival, Yushatyr enrolled at the Cornwall Institute,[[footnote]] The Cornwall Institute is a college and research institution for the study of thaumaturgic art, backed and funded by Eccleston University. [[/footnote]] where the Royal Congregation’s Codicesia took particular interest in her work. The Codicesia sought both to preserve her family’s thaumaturgic techniques and to glean insight into the inner workings of the Ministry of Paranormality.[[footnote]] The Ministry of Paranormality (Russian: Министерство Паранормальности "МП") is the chief governing body for the research and containment of anomalous entities in the Russian Federation under President Vladimir Putin.[[/footnote]] Despite their inquiries, Yushatyr consistently declined to share sensitive information, citing loyalty to her nation.

Her personal interests, however, lay not in archival research but in fieldwork. Fascinated by MI-13’s investigative operations and eager to engage with entities and active thaumaturgists, Yushatyr petitioned to transfer into field service. This request was only granted upon the personal recommendation of Lady Sophia Boleyn ([[[no-0288|MI0288-01]]]), who vouched for her aptitude and potential.
