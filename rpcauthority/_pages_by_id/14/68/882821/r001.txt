**Tip from agent embedded in regional post distributor:** A local mail truck driver operating in East Idaho reported a curious flyer that he found in every mailbox on his route; one he'd never delivered himself. According to the agent who secured this flyer, this never went through their distribution system.

"The flyer's cardstock has the same material quality and colors used as their usual packaging materials; and its prominently labeled on the top with the word Recall. Correction, it’s labeled with “Recale.”

It’s undoubtedly from Amazing! Co. But, as much as I want to be correct, there's a lot about this flyer that doesn't fit their style. Their logo is not present on the flyer in any capacity, nor is any sort of visual or descriptive information. And since when have you known A! Co. to care about quality controls of any kind?!

This "Recale" notice doesn't have much to go off of. Perhaps it uses advanced memetics? I don't know, but assuming its from them, what the hell did they make that they're so afraid of?"
