[[div class="content-panel"]]

**Date:** May 30, 2019

**Location:** Site-██
 
**Subject(s):** Agent Jonah Morice, [[[RPC-002]]], One (1) Black-footed Ferret
------
**Procedure:** Agent Morice was provided with a cage containing a black-footed ferret and equipped with RPC-002 on his person. He then contacted RPC-467 using its temporary phone number to disclose the ferret’s location.
------
**Log:** Approximately two hours after the call, RPC-467 arrived on-site, driving an older-model Ford F-Series pickup truck. Upon observing Agent Morice positioned with the black-footed ferret while holding RPC-002, RPC-467 determined that no viable opportunity existed to terminate the specimen without risking harm to Agent Morice.

He subsequently ran out of the car and tried to run away. Before it could fully escape, Agent Morice discharged his weapon at RPC-467 but struck the vehicle instead, triggering an explosion that released a cloud of mustard gas and allowed RPC-467 to escape.
------
**Closing Statement:** Agent Morice was immediately transported to Site-███’s medical wing for treatment of chemical exposure and has since made a full recovery.
[[/div]]

[[div class="content-panel"]]

**Date:** April 6, 2019

**Location:** Elatia Mountain, Greece
 
**Subject(s):** [[[RPC-022]]], One (1) Cretan Wildcat
------
**Procedure:** A Cretan wildcat was placed inside a reinforced containment cage as bait, with RPC-022 positioned nearby on guard duty. Information regarding the specimen was deliberately leaked to an online black-market forum frequented by RPC-467.
------
**Log:** Approximately six hours after the information was posted, RPC-467 materialized within the surrounding woodland and fired a round from an SVD Dragunov rifle at the wildcat. RPC-022 intercepted the projectile before impact and immediately charged RPC-467. RPC-022 struck RPC-467 before he could escape and successfully pinned him to the ground. 

During the struggle, RPC-467 activated a concealed flashbang device hidden within his coat, disorienting RPC-022 and forcing him to release his hold, allowing RPC-467 to escape. Before fully retreating, he threw an additional flashbang at the wildcat’s cage, resulting in its death.
------
**Closing Statement:** RPC-022 expressed brief remorse over the failure to protect the wildcat but quickly forgot about the incident.

[[/div]]
