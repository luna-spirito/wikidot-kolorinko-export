It seems like Kabushiki Kawaii could make a fortune performing extensive plastic surgery or other transformations on willing subjects to their benefit and command a lucrative price doing so. Yet for all intents and purposes, they seem to be insistent on carrying out a slave trade that, as lucrative as it seems, could land them in hot water legally if people became aware of their activity.

Do they have some bias against anomalous people that's pushed them to this point, and they have some twisted sense of morals that make sure anomalies are kept under their thumb? Is it just their ideal money making strategy to sell slaves instead of legitimate services? Or is there something else we don't know about them?
