This rumor comes from a potentially unreliable source, so take all information with a grain of salt: Nucorp maintains an unknowable number of corporate branches in an untrackable number of universes. One of those universes is, or was, "ALTR-XL22." Discovered on 6/12/1976, initial monitoring concerned a nexus event between the RCPA Reality and theirs. To Research's surprise, the engagement ended after 20 days; the rift somehow closed on the defending timeline's side following a high-yield energy transfer flaring into the RCPA timeline. 

On 6/14/26, an anonymous person(s) of the UNAAC Interdimension Resettlement Service reached out to the Authority accusing NuCorp of transferring a large sum of borderline paranatural weapons to baseline. Per accusation, NuCorp has begun aggressively marketing orbital cannons with interplanetary cruise capacity at private Saudi Defense Shows. Wiretaps in Ukrainian Defense reveal payment plans for Heavy Strike Fighters with a 500-year fuel capacity. Large shipments of "battery compact pistols" are being rebranded as manufactured in China rather than an unknown "People's Republic of Manchukuo."

The source revealed that the major convergence of ALTR-XL22 was the commercialization of nuclear fusion-based technology. By 1942, the Manhattan Project had not yet fully materialized — nuclear physics developed more methodically — and the first atomic weaponry was developed by 2232. By 2249, a Nuclear arms race kicked off, and by 2256, a 40-minute nuclear exchange wiped out ~87.6% of the Human Population. The administration remained suspicious of how meticulous and organized the informant was, given that the informant was a supposed refugee escaping an apocalyptic timeline. Moreover,  the person(s)' identity was obscured from operatives, with the only pre-UNAAC activity tracked to a bank wire to a GEAR-owned subsidiary.
