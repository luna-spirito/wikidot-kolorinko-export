Entity No. 1571 is confined within the Gate of the Forgotten Saint under the supervision of the Subjugation Office at East London Station. Its holding cell[[footnote]] The Gates are an impenetrable containment vault reserved exclusively for entities and artefacts deemed too hazardous for standard confinement. Each cell within the vault generates an illusory reality, tailored to the psyche or nature of the subject. [[/footnote]] has adapted to resist the entity’s memetic nature. The chamber no longer attempts to simulate a physical plane, and instead has allowed the surrounding environment to slowly lose coherence and metamorphose into a dreamlike atmosphere.[[footnote]] ACS-2.9 within the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/acs-guide Anderson Coherency Scale.] [[/footnote]] Over time, the chamber has ceased to exist as a physical space at all. It is now a manifestation shaped by SE1571 itself, appearing as an autumn forest drowned in endless silver mist.

SE1571 is a cognitohazard. All qualitative information of the entity is to be expunged upon discovery. Even objective and empirical data concerning SE1571 are subject to rigorous scrutiny. The most reliable defence against its influence is the complete denial of its existence. Any information about SE1571, whether concerning its origins or its nature, may only be disclosed through metaphor and allegory.

All attempted termination protocols have failed. The entity is effectively indestructible and exhibits complete resistance to every form of thaumaturgic weaponry tested to date. No research or communication is to be conducted with the entity.

++ Synopsis

A mutated humanoid cadaver, perpetually suspended by a noose of indeterminate and seemingly infinite length. The entity remains unmoving, yet is capable of teleporting to any location and manifesting simultaneously before multiple observers. Although a physical body is present, its form is inherently unstable. It transitions into an incorporeal spectral body upon any contact with a human subject.

SE1571 is a Bacchanal[[footnote]] Thaumaturges that succumbed to the //Hanover Curse// and are physiologically, psychologically, and spiritually transformed into a malevolent form; a process called //inebriation.// [[/footnote]] that emerged from the fusion of the former artefact [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/no-1567 MO1567] with the corpse of former Codicesia member ██████ ██████. The entity lacks a skull; in its place, a colossal ████ ████ with ███ pairs of wings is integrated into its anatomy. Each wing contains Greek script accompanied by diagrams of ████████████ formulae, ██████ ████████, and detailed illustrations of █████ based around the Nihil rites.

As with most contained high-grade Bacchanals, SE1571 does not metabolize material food. Instead, the entity is theorized to subsist primarily on human cognition and sobriety. Individuals exposed to the full diameter of the entity’s aura of influence will exhibit alcoholic intoxication and disorientation. If exposure is prolonged, it may even result in permanent neurological damage.

SE1571’s true appearance is a severe cognitohazard, and direct visual exposure causes the subject to come under its influence. Any information that depicts SE1571 or the contents of its wings may transform into a spiritual larva designated SE1571-01, with material related to the Nihil rites being especially susceptible. The information must be sufficiently detailed for the transformation to occur, and this mechanism constitutes the primary manifestation of SE1571’s curse.

SE1571-01 is a conceptual entity[[footnote]] Also known as a memotic entity. They are creatures that primarily subsist in the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/memotics-hub spiritual realm] and manifest through cognition. [[/footnote]] that manifests as a worm-like form and consumes human cognition to sustain growth and metabolic activity. Although fully invisible and non-corporeal, it may manifest in the physical environment as a cloud of fog. Inhalation of this fog results in cognitive dulling, rendering reality dreamlike and distant. Subjects exposed for extended periods typically lapse into dissociative wandering until SE1571 has fully consumed their minds. Only Dr. Aesopica and ██████ ██████ are seemingly immune to this effect.

Humans within a 6 km radius experience recurrent dreams of an endless autumn plain suffused with fog. Affected individuals display an increased compulsion toward alcohol, and while intoxicated, become markedly more vulnerable to SE1571’s influence.

The interview was conducted Calpar, Curia Arcanum Liaison. The record takes place in the King’s Harrow, Stratford, during the 2019 FA Cup Final.

(//Crowd erupts in cheers. Aesopica jolts awake from the commotion and is startled by Calpar's sudden appearance.//)

**Calpar:** Dr. Aesopica, thank you for agreeing to speak with me. I understand this isn’t a formal setting.

**Dr. Aesopica:** You’re the one who asked for quiet... This is quiet. In my head, at least.

**Calpar:** I’d like to ask about the incident surrounding MO1567 and Mr. ██████. Can we begin with--

**Dr. Aesopica:**  Which one?

**Calpar:** … There was only one incident.

**Aesopica:** (//Dr. Aesopica begins laughing uncontrollably.//) //Oh, sir, no. No.// There were at least-- (//Dr. Aesopica taps his glabella.//) --seven. Eight. Depends if you count the dreams. //And I do. I have to.// Someone has to. (//He wipes his mouth with his sleeve, exhausted.//)

**Calpar:** Dr. Aesopica, how much have you had to drink today?

**Dr. Aesopica:** (//Dr. Aesopica at his glass//) I don't know. I've lost-- I've lost count. Everything’s so sharp, you know? Since the fog. Since him. Since the fucking… //everything.//

**Calpar:** You were never one to drink.

**Dr. Aesopica:** Didn’t drink at all back then. //I had a change of heart.//

(//Dr. Aesopica's hand trembles slightly as he attempts to drink another shot, but was promptly stopped by Calpar.//)

**Calpar:** This subject might be a sensitive matter, but I want your recollection of what happened during the final ritual attempt--in your own words.

**Dr. Aesopica:** Have you ever… read something so long it starts reading you back?

**Calpar:** Please stay on track, Doctor.

**Dr. Aesopica:** We both had our own motivations, but he didn’t know how they differed. It had to be me in that device, if it was anyone else they would’ve suffered. 

A dream, and a memory, one I’ve heard over and over again. //An autumn field of silver fog… an autumn field of silver fog….// A beast behind but one who never reveals anything.

It wasn’t a book. Wasn’t a codex. Was a starving lava trying to survive and prosper. We were both just like it, hungry for more, we wanted to learn more about this little worm that had captured our mind. And ██████— (//voice softens//) he thought… he thought it was just research. Just fun. Just--

**Calpar:** Are you saying you anticipated the catastrophic event?

**Dr. Aesopica:** He had… that look. Like someone already half-dreaming. Like he was listening to something I couldn’t hear. Even though the Wyrm chose me, //no// it should’ve chosen me. 

(//Sound of people shouting at the TV as someone misses a goal. Aesopica flinches hard again.//)

[...]

**Dr. Aesopica:** After the ritual, he began to fester in his fears and regrets; he knew he was going to inebriate. But he wanted to go out on his own terms. Swallowed the pieces and readied himself for the total annihilation of both himself and the worm. Said he didn’t want to die as something he didn’t choose to be. And he said-- (//He swallows hard.//) He said I’d forget him when it was over.

(//Dr. Aesopica wipes his nose with the back of his hand.//)

**Dr. Aesopica:** All that sentimentality, some selfless sacrifice, //and it was all for nothing.// (//Dr. Aesopica begins laughing manically.//)

**Calpar:** Dr. Aesopica, please be quiet, there are other people--

**Dr. Aesopica:** Nothing we did mattered, not the research, not the destruction of the notebook's remains. //It made them worse! And now here he comes, the new god of Bacchus! A thing you can't even kill if you tried! All for worse, all for nothing!//

**Calpar:** Quiet down.

(//Dr. Aesopica slowly calms down.//)

[...]

**Calpar:** Dr. Aesopica, I believe this is enough for now. We can continue another day, I’ll arrange transport back to East London Station.

[...]

**Calpar:** Thank you for everything, doctor. 

[...]

**Dr. Aesopica:** It was all for nothing. And all that's left is an autumn field of silver fog.
