**Registered Phenomena Code:** 824

**Object Class:** Gamma - Orange

**Hazard Types:** Aggression, Psychotronic, Emotional, Mind-Control

**Containment Protocols:** Due to its nature as an incorporeal phenomenon, RPC-824 cannot be physically contained. Authority agents embedded in the local law enforcement and mental health institutions of Crenshaw, Virginia are to report any possible targets of RPC-824 for monitoring. Authority agents are to relocate any affected civilians from Crenshaw unless ordered otherwise. Affected Authority agents are to be immediately removed from Crenshaw, admitted to therapy, and reassigned to a different anomaly.

**Description:** RPC-824 is a phenomenon which affects residents of the city of Crenshaw, Virginia. Over a period of time ranging from 2 months to 10 years, RPC-824 will cause a series of dramatic psychological changes in affected individuals. The following conditions are common in >90% of affected individuals:
* Hallucinations of humanoid entities. Affected individuals often consider these hallucinations friendly, though a few have described them as "demons."
* Severe delusions. The most common delusions in affected individuals are persecutory delusions, erotomania,[[footnote]] Delusion characterized by an individual's belief that someone else is in love with them. [[/footnote]] and delusions of grandeur.
* Heightened irritability and aggression, often towards individuals offering help.
* Paraphilias and paraphilic disorders.
* A strong desire to remain in Crenshaw, Virginia.
These traits cause individuals affected by RPC-824 to exhibit eccentric and often destructive behaviors, which result in their gradual ostracization from society. Affected individuals invariably disappear, often after achieving near-complete social withdrawal. At the time of writing, the criteria by which RPC-824 targets Crenshaw residents is unknown.

This thread was reported to the Authority during an ongoing investigation into the unusually high amount of mentally ill and missing persons in the city of Crenshaw, Virginia. In addition, Authority agents embedded in the Crenshaw Police Department noted that all missing persons cases involved Crenshaw residents who exhibited eccentric behavior and/or symptoms of mental illness, with some instances occurring as early as the 1810s. The Authority ran a disinformation campaign to dismiss RPC-824 as the actions of an unidentified serial killer and current containment procedures were implemented on 10/12/2021.

**Addendum 824-I: Abridged List of Notable Individuals Affected by RPC-824**

> **Name:** Constantine Wright
> **Description:** Uneducated and illiterate eccentric who wrote and published several books in broken English. Primary sources describe him as arrogant, irritable, and unpleasant. Disappeared September 1812 after the death of his wife. Believed to be the earliest known individual affected by RPC-824.

> **Name:** Alyssa Kevorkian
> **Description:** Fringe political activist who expressed delusions of grandeur and was known to make grandiose threats directed at her opposition. Disappeared November 2019 shortly after an interview where she reported hearing voices.

> **Name:** Ian "AzerbaiJohnny" Aliyev
> **Description:** Live streamer known for his unkempt appearance, poor hygiene, and sub-standard living condition. Expressed unique spiritual beliefs influenced by various television shows. Reportedly never left his house. Disappeared sometime between July 2021 and January 2022.

> **Name:** Phillip "FlipChan" Chandler
> **Description:** Internet personality known for his webcomic "Empress Sarah." Notably obsessed with an original character of his named Sarah Sunshine, who he insisted was a real person. Disappeared November 2016 shortly after his arrest for aggravated assault.

> **Name:** Christina "Nivaldi" Neufeld
> **Description:** Cult leader who convinced her followers they were characters from the video game //Princess Quest IV// in their past lives. Every member of her cult identified as their corresponding characters, but she identified as an original character named "Nivaldi." Disappeared May 2011, after which her cult was disbanded. Over the next two years, every member of her cult disappeared as well.

> **Name:** Herman Strouzenberger
> **Description:** Internet personality known for his advocacy of [DATA EXPUNGED]. Disappeared in solitary confinement on 12/01/2012 while serving a 40-year prison sentence.

[[tab Corrected Text]]
5 November, 1810

My friend Jeremy is an accomplished historian, having graduated from Harvard up north in Massachusetts. He is mighty fascinated in the history of our great country and especially Old Virginia like no other man I've seen.

The other day Jeremy came to me and told me a legend he documented from a nearby Indian tribe. When the White Man came to these lands, he fought a great war with the Indians and took their land. When the Indian chief was sent away, he called upon demons of vengeance to curse the White Man's greatest asset, his superior brain, with madness.

I suspect this legend to be true, for I have seen these demons with my very eyes.
