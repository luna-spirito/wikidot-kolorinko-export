Kabushiki Kawaii never conducts raids on Authority sites in an attempt to steal anomalies or other materiel. Its small size and lack of firepower forces it to instead organize much more subtle and manipulative operations.

On July 2018, the UNAAC accused the Authority of falsifying anomaly termination records after they discovered a previously registered anomaly among the ranks of Ambazonian warlord known as “General Tuckin”.

The anomaly in question, previously registered as RPC-███, was terminated during a firefight after escaping containment and site grounds with a hostage. It had been beforehand marked for termination due to its overwhelmingly aggressive behavior and multiple lethal site breaches it orchestrated alongside the site’s CSD population. MST Echo-11 “Securities” reported that an explosion had terminated both the anomaly and the hostage, leaving behind only charred remains.

The UNAAC rediscovered the anomaly in Cameroon during a coordination effort. It had appeared leading a small unit of rebels, assaulting the UN representatives - notably, video footage shows that a number of modification typical to Kabushiki Kawaii victims have been installed into the body of the anomaly. The Authority heard rumors of a “bodyguard” program being offered to select individuals - with this event, it was all but confirmed.
