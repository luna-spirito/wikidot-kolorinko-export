The file you are about to access describes events, procedures, or items relevant within Site-101. This disclaimer will familiarize you with protocols and traditions unique to the Site.

----

||~ Naming Conventions || 

All personnel directly involved with guarding inmates are given a simplified designation, consisting of their faction and a number.

> GXX: ASF guards.
> MXX: Protection Division Arrest Units
> PXX: Shaved Purities

All inmates are designated according to the type of their psychic abilities and cell location.

> Letters describe the abilities, and numbers the location.
> 
> **P - Psychokinesis:** Psychic abilities that output a force or modification on physical reality. 
> 
> **S - Extra Sensory Perception (ESP):** Psychic abilities that allow the paranormal intake of information.
> 
> **E - Empathics:** Psychic abilities that modify the emotional response or perception of sapient subjects. 
> 
> **Example:** SE-3/115 can perform ESP and Empathics, incarcerated within the 115th cell of the 3rd prison level.

Referring to designated entities with personal names or aliases poses a severe risk of breach and is **##red|strictly##** prohibited.
----

||~ The Duchamp Psalm || 

Colloquially known as the **“Song of Grounding”** or **"Resistance Chanting”**

> The Duchamp Psalm is a verbal exercise used to hinder psychics. It was composed by the French artist Marcel Duchamp in the aftermath of WW1. To the tune of //Au clair de la lune//, the psalm features satirical lyrics aiming to ridicule psychics, making heavy use of the pejorative //psitain//. Despite modern trials failing to verify its efficacy, the Duchamp Psalm remains as a traditional practice in West European anti-psychic spheres.

The Duchamp Psalm is considered vital for morale. Committing the Psalm to memory is heavily encouraged.

+++ **Description:**
RPC-266 is Site-101 and its accompanying anomalous characteristics, manifesting as a result of the RPC-266-QUILL and RPC-266-LAW interplay. 

[[include component:image-block name=https://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-266/Cover|caption=Site-101, 2003. Front cover: Vendin-le-Vieil Penintentiary Center|align=right]]

RPC-266-QUILL is a humanoid anoetic seated at the escritoire in the Warden’s office of Site-101. The bodily apparatus of RPC-266-QUILL is entirely composed of parchment and vellum paper. The white hair, however, is organic, and comprises a full-bottom peruke. The papers are adorned with written text; detailing the personal profiles, investigations, sentences, crimes, and penalties of all the psychics incarcerated at Site-101. RPC-266-QUILL will update this content with general developments accordingly. 

Notably, RPC-266-QUILL can not be mirrored in the reflective surfaces covering the Warden’s Office. It does not respond to any attempted interaction, except for acknowledging given documents and cleaning a gold plated engraving on the front of its desk, if it is tainted. The engraving reads:

> [[=]]
> This DEAF Judge
> This BLIND Jury
> This INNOCENT Executioner
> 
> WILL ADMIRE NO EVIL
> [[/=]]

When unoccupied, RPC-266-QUILL will write on an empty space on its desk. Despite inking the quill, it will not leave any calligraphy on the wood. Observing the penmanship, however, reveals it to align with simultaneous edits of the //Code D'incarcération Psychique//.

RPC-266-LAW, officially known as the Psychic Incarceration Code, is a penal code with causal relationships to the functions of Site-101 originally composed under the previous Site Director and Warden Jean-Baptiste De Pontes. The content of RPC-266-LAW is dynamic, and the frequent editing of its articles results in a continuous metamorphosis of Site-101’s logical conditions. However, not every clause is subject to change, as three main titles have never been modified since the original writing of the Psychic Incarceration Code, even before the manifestation of its anomalous properties. 

The three main titles of RPC-266-LAW have given rise to various conditions characterizing Site-101. Listed in order of ratification:
> [[=]]
> **TITLE .1**
> **The Officers must be immune to the temptations of psychic interference by means of Shaved Purities, to rid capacity for admiration of evil.**
> 
> -
> [[/=]]
> All Correctional Officers employed at Site-101 will adopt the properties of a Shaved Purity.[[footnote]]An anoetic entity created by the Authority with minor anomalous suppression-related psychic abilities.[[/footnote]]
> 
> ----
> [[=]]
> **TITLE .2**
> **The inmates are disbarred from internal social interaction. Looking Glass Isolation limits admiration to the self.**
> 
> -
> [[/=]]
> All surfaces within Site-101 have a minimum reflectance of 95%. Inmate cells are completely enclosed in mirrors, which show captromanic[[footnote]]The display of imagery on reflective surfaces.[[/footnote]] illusions generally engineered to elicit ego death.
> 
> ----
> 
> [[=]]
> **TITLE .3**
> **The Warden must admire no evil, only reflect the white light of law.**
> 
> -
> [[/=]]
> Currently unknown. Suspected relation to the manifestation of RPC-266 and the disappearance of previous Site Director/Warden Jean-Baptiste De Pontes.
> ----

The content of RPC-266-LAW is not absolute. Its clauses can be modified by personnel by presenting well-reasoned legal arguments to RPC-266, reminiscent of a civil law system. Additionally, documents can be handed to the entity as evidential support for the main proposition. Using correct legal terminology and etiquette has been proven to increase the success-rate of amending RPC-266-LAW. This applies only to the smaller provisions; the main titles have never been changed.

RPC-266-LAW’s constant metamorphosis mandates personnel to remain vigilant on the spatiotemporal variances of Site-101 owing to changing architectural statutes.[[footnote]]Anti-emetics are recommended for personnel with risk of motion sickness.[[/footnote]] Judiciary units are to record and report drastic changes and updated draft proposals for RPC-266. Due to its aesthetic and mechanical characteristics, Site-101 is colloquially referred to as the //Looking Glass Labyrinth//.

RPC-266-LAW has, according to researchers, abandoned its previous form of physical documentation written by Site Director Jean-Baptiste De Pontes. It can currently only be accessed through the reflective surfaces of Site-101. Planes with a reflectance of 95% or higher will emit modified images containing RPC-266-LAW content displayed over the mirrored subjects. Personnel have reported slight disorientation due to this effect, which has been exacerbated by the large amounts of reflective surfaces in Site-101 established under the second title of the Psychic Incarceration Code.

[[=]]
+ HISTORY
[[/=]]
----

The Directorate of Supernatural Defense traces its beginnings to the Catholic magistrates of the 16th century. There was no formal understanding of psychics, and no distinction between them and practitioners of the occult arts. Nostradamus, for example, was not so much as persecuted for his futuretouching almanacs, due to the lack of strict evidence for the explicit, well-defined craft of magic. 

The witches of the northern trials, however, were not spared. Many accounts describe eye-witnesses of the impossible, but no clear occult circle or script to back it up. These trials set the foundation for official, organized lawkeeping units of the Church.

The French Revolution temporarily castrated this force, until Napoleon Bonaparte’s supernatural [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/the-history-of-monarch-security Légion Fantôme], with their unprecedentedly preserved sources. In between the Napoleonic wars, the Legion would nourish their numbers with yet more members of the anomalous. Those who refused their offer - which we today know would fit the profile of the stereotypical egocentric psychic - were arrested, starved, and reconditioned to heed orders. 

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcsandbox.wikidot.com/local--files/looking-glass-labyrinth/Escape|caption=//The Witch No.1// — Joseph E. Baker, 1892, in respect to the “mystical powers” that prohibited incarceration of certain occultists, today known as psychics.|align=right]]

Failure defines this pursuit. Reports describe that certain magicmen grew more able, more intelligent, more magnificent; more powerful specifically //after// capture. Every escape left a prison camp in cinders, or an oubliette collapsed. These we today know would be psychics under the disguise of thaumaturgy.

The Legion could not understand these uncontainable entities, and its post-Bonaparte successor, the //Direction de la Défense Surnaturelle//, struggled similarly against the combat psychics employed by the Central Powers and Third Reich across both World Wars. The trauma which the French population suffered at the hands of psychics cemented anti-psychic sentiment, and various groups rose to further persecute psychics.

----
[[=]]
+ THE PROBLEM
[[/=]]
----

Most of these groups, save for [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/academy-hub  one exception], have totally failed. This is due to a fundamental misunderstanding. Psychics, as the Directorate of Supernatural Defense now knows, thrive on attention. The more influence a psychic gathers, the more powerful they grow. The substance known as qualia, they steal as their own. Take Nostradamus, The Feuerpest, even Starmother of Baraize: all icons of influence.

[[include component:image-block name=https://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/looking-glass-labyrinth/Admire|caption=A popular show.|align=left]]

We need to understand the allure of evil. The superstitious rants of the witch trials, the public executions under the Reign of Terror, //Le génie du mal//, all of De Sade’s debauchery; evil is given our entire being, and all the space available in our heads. We give qualia out as candy, and criminals are not satisfied with only what’s free.

Every historical attempt to incarcerate the psychic falls for the same trap. Elongated and theatrical trials, records describing crime in detail, guards whose sole purpose is to know and confront the prisoner; we may as well pull it out of our skulls and donate qualia directly.

----
[[=]]
+ MY SOLUTION

These new memories can again be targeted with amnestics, gradually removing the axioms with each repetition.
