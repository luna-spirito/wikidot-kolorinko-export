An anonymous tip given by "A Concerned Researcher" described witnessing a secretive meeting in Site-██'s lvl 4 board room.

2 Nucorp Engineers were seen with at least 10 personnel spanning all 4 divisions of the Authority. Many of these personnel belonged to MST-Golf-11, the other personnel were later identified through their descriptions as Authority Remnant personnel reintegrated into our reality.

Upon monitoring said personnel, no change in schedules or opinions either for or against Authority protocols and standards have been expressed. When questioned they expressed no memory of the meeting. All attempts to identify the engineers have failed, with even Nucorp denying their existence. Security cameras failed to capture any footage of said engineers
