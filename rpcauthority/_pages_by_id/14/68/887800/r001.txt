While investigating past reports and cataloguing known subsidiaries of Amazing Co, a new thread was discovered: Amazing Co., using third parties and shell corporations, have supplied the Fast Food chain McDonald's with toys and ingredients for meal preparation. The earliest recording of such a partnership happened in 1991 and has kept going allegedly to this day. How McDonald's was able to retain a seemingly normal work culture and why Amazing Co keeps the company at arm’s length remains unclear.
