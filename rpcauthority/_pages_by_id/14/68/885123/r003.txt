Amazing Co. often creates poor imitations of real products that misunderstand the original intent. This remains true for items with a practical purpose, but can be debated in the case of adolescent entertainment.

Recent investigations into A!Co. popularity with children has led to the discovery of "Golden Tickets" contained in certain toy packages, inviting consumers to "beta-trials" of future roll-outs. The ongoings of beta-trials are unknown, with no discernible location, schedule, or even age demographic despite seemingly only existing for toys.

Beta testers have a high likelihood of developing an affinity for locating A!Co. toys, and preferring them over ordinary playthings. As a result of this phenomenon, some affluent households have accumulated a high amount of anomalous products, which may result in complex multi-anomalous incidents.
