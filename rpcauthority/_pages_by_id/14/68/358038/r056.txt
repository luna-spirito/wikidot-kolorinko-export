**Registered Phenomena Code:** 767

**Object Class:** ##red|Gamma-Red##

[[include component:hazards auditory=true|organic=true|aggression=true|sentient=true|regenerative=true|microscopic=true]]

**Containment Protocols:** Authority webcrawlers are to track news of "Evangeline cuisine" food joints opening and report the results to Site-037 computer technicians. The ACI (Authority Central Intelligence) is to track and report any individuals in lower parishes of Louisiana suffering from chronic mental illness admitted to an EMS unit with all the following ailments:

* Severe abdominal pain
* Bowel obstruction
* Vomiting
* Spinal cord injury
* Lapses in body movement volition
Following a positive match, embedded ASF personnel are to reach and assess the subject within 48 hours of admittance. If an RPC-767 infestation is confirmed, the on-team ASF medic is to anesthetize the subject and perform emergency cordotomy. Subject is to be considered lost once the allotted time passes, at which point personnel must focus on neutralizing the instance. Witnesses are to be administered Class A-2 amnestics. If needed, a disinformation campaign may be set at Command's discretion.

ASF tasked with RPC-767 operations are to undergo psychological evaluation for any mental illnesses or personality disorders before deployment.
----
**Description:** RPC-767 are 1-3μm hyperelastic quadrupeds with superficial taxonomic resemblance to the Annelida phylum[[footnote]]Also known as segmented worms[[/footnote]] endemic to the lower Mississippi river in Louisiana, United States. Their internal anatomy is indistinguishable from that of a non-anomalous //Hirudo medicinalis//[[footnote]]European Medicinal Leech[[/footnote]]. However, the anomaly's entire cell makeup is of mucus-producing cells [[footnote]]Analogous to goblet cells present in the stomach lining[[/footnote]], making each cell functionally interchangeable and allowing for rapid cellular regeneration, even post-mortis. At this stage, RPC-767 is unable to ambulate properly, instead they locomote by elastic propulsion. Longitudinal regional sampling indicates immature instances have a life cycle of 72-112 hours and a population size between 30,000 to 1,400,000.

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-767/exhibit12b|caption=The tadpole-like RPC-767 fetus extracted in its second gestation stage]]
RPC-767 will actively seek to inject itself into local fish and miscellaneous edible fauna, the probability of successful injection being extremely low. Anomalous properties manifest once a human suffering from at least one severe mental disorder ingests said item. Therein, RPC-767 will seek its way towards the stomach and commence a 120hr gestation period encompassing three stages[[footnote]]each stage lasting 40 hours[[/footnote]]:
# RPC-767 stretches its entire body to coat the host's entire stomach lining and feed off the host's nutrients.
# The tadpole then burrows through the stomach lining's inner most layer, into the gastric pits and towards the Meissner's plexus, connecting its nervous system to the host's sensory neurons.
# The instance hijacks spinal interneurons, thus physically paralyzing the subject till the end of the gestation period.
Once gestation ends, the anomaly then signals motor neurons to induce intragastric pressure, violently propelling itself through the esophagus and out of the mouth, completing the birth cycle. Hereafter, the newborn will act upon its first instinct and consume the nearest source of soft tissue, predominantly beginning with the host. As an instance feeds, it will grow in accordance to the mass consumed, fully maturing after ingesting 50-300kg. Autopsies reveal mature RPC-767's internal organ structures are a crude facsimile of the host's organs, with abnormal size, shape, and placement.

The host's mental disorder is causal to a wide variance between 4 RPC-767 instances. Below is a table distinguishing physiological and behavioral mannerisms:
||~ Instance variant ||~ Diagnosis ||~ Mass to maturity ||~ Distinctions ||
|| α || Mood disorders || 200-280kg || Dense muscular build with dark maroon coloration. 60cm / 20cm, 45kg. Extraordinary physical ability, able to lift up to 4 times its weight. Extremely violent and compulsive aggressor, most are neutralized within 14 days from hemorrhagic shock caused by its high blood pressure.||
|| β || Trauma and stressor related disorders || 90-140kg || Vantablack long slender build with ~20-50 large pores across its body. 4.4-5.2m long / 7cm thick, 5kg. Reticent, prefers to conceal itself in dim environments to eat cadavers than to hunt. If none are available or the instance is threatened, it will coil itself and spout a continuous ~153dB high whistle from its pores, causing a concussive shockwave incapacitating nearby animals; subsequently forcing the instance into premature hibernation which lasts 23-57 days.||
|| γ || Dissociative disorders || 50-75kg || Grey, translucent body. 20cm / 5cm, 10kg. Variant contorts and camouflages itself to blend into a conspecific group's morphology, copycatting the cohort's mannerisms albeit poorly. Animals are not deceived except for amphibians, making them prime prey.||
|| ω || Schizophrenia and psychotic disorders || 260-300kg || Light pink bulbous build. 30cm diameter, 16kg. Locomotion by rolling. Feeds through psychotronic manipulation of animals to enter its maw. Similarly able to influence other variants into cooperating albeit with limited capacity.||

As of writing, there are 91 confirmed mature instances, 32 currently uncontained.

**Discovery:** On 13/04/2008, Authority personnel were alerted to suspected anomalous activity around Wilton E. Legrand's residence in Turkey Creek, Louisiana. 12 people were found dead with scarring consistent with RPC-767 affliction, with Mr. Legrand nowhere to be found.

A food party event was set a month prior for Turkey Creek's community. A poster advert wrote:
> In these trying times, one needs recess from all their worries if only for a week.. Together, we'll make it happen. Next April 7th, you can come and stay at councilman Legrand's mansion this spring break! Offering a feast with all Louisiana favorites such as: Étouffée, Banana Fosters and soul-healing Gumbo prepared by the man himself. Mark your calendars and do. not. MISS IT!
Further investigation has led to fellow councilmen retelling Mr. Legrand having suddenly lost his front teeth and developing severe back problems, with no indication of them beforehand; as well as actively proclaiming he was cured from insomnia. Wilton E. Legrand was later found dead on 27/12/2009, floating in a wetland 6km south from Turkey Creek.

**Incident-767-01:** On 04/02/2015, Terrebonne Parish municipal police received several noise complaints in a local neighborhood. During investigation, the noise source led to 2 γ instances, playing dead in imitation of a close-by incapacitated dog, next to 1 β instance. After witnesses were amnesticized, Authority personnel were quick to contain the instances en route to a local containment site.

The Authority vehicle transporting the aforementioned instances crashed and rolled over by an interstate road, heavily injuring onboard personnel. Agents reported an "impossibly loud whistle" from within the vehicle to have caused the collision. As they found their footing, Agent Bernard was ambushed on the back and swiftly killed by an α instance. Agents Derrick and Gerald shot multiple rounds at the instance before it was incapacitated. Hostile was deemed neutralized until 2 minutes later, when it regenerated from much of the trauma and attacked Agent Derrick. Gerald, in a decisive act, rolled the vehicle to its side in the hostile's direction, crushing it on top of Derrick's arm.

The instances on transit could not be found on vehicle, presumed escaped.

**Incident-767-04:** 09/08/2022, home security footage of an α instance, sodden in blood, roaming a premise's backyard went viral, prompting the dispatchment of an ASF team under a neutralization order. Personnel were able to terminate it and retrieve the body for site transfer. Investigating the premise, agents found the skeletal remnants of Catherine Thibodeaux, a 38 year old woman who was recovering from a previous suicide attempt. A disinformation campaign was setup to discredit the credibility of the video, purporting it using 3D effects and AI image manipulation to achieve the effect. 

**Incident-767-07:** On 10/07/2023, webcrawlers recovered █████ Hospital CCTV footage of 3 RPC-767 instances, 2 α and 1 ω, exiting a morgue freezer unit which held the body of Bellamy Marciniak. The deceased had been previously diagnosed with schizoaffective disorder and had committed suicide 14 hours prior, purportedly from pain induced by the anomaly. After termination of the 2 α instances and containment of the ω instance, it was reported a Jonathon ████████ was admitted to ████████ ████████ Hospital 60kms away from a RPC-767 infection. Due to vehicular failure in transit, an α instance was birthed by the time personnel arrived to the scene. After agents terminated the instance, subject was found to have survived the process but in critical condition. He was subsequently administered emergency treatment before transferal. Authority medics were successful in stabilizing ████████'s condition after intensive trauma surgery.
