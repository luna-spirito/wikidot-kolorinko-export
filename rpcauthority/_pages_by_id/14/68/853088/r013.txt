**Containment Protocols:** Testimonies are to be analyzed to identify legitimate subjects who may be recovered from RPC-581 by Heartbreaker Task Force (HTF) agents under the command of --Cormac Marshal-- Alexander Marshal, --in coordination with USOC efforts--. The Task Force Head has demonstrated exceptional proficiency in distinguishing legitimate accounts from fabricated reports; whose recommendations are to be followed whenever applicable.

The implementation of cover stories and the rapid suppression of related snuff films are considered essential in cases where a subject cannot be recovered prior to abduction by RPC-581. As of 2005, specialized web crawlers configured to detect the title cards associated with RPC-581-TAPE have been deployed. Most importantly, the following quote:

> Before us is the passive soldier of the Apathetic War. He will be broken, suffocated, and opened, for you to remember the blood that must be shed, and the skin that must be shred. Pray hear the Drums of Salvation.

If a legitimate target is identified prior to their appearance in a snuff film, the responding officer is authorized to physically or cognitively incapacitate the individual, as such measures have been demonstrated to prevent manifestations of RPC-581. When circumstances permit, the victim may be consulted regarding their preferred method of incapacitation. If unable to reach a preference, amnestic-induced retardation is the standard.

**Description:** RPC-581 is a dismembered human heart manifesting in the vicinity of individuals within the North American continent. RPC-581 steadily produces blood, and displays a normal cardiac rhythm, despite the lack of a complete hematopoietic system. It targets a single person at a time.

Due to the anomaly's unpredictable behavior and inability to be reliably contained, direct experimentation has been limited. Historical records suggest that RPC-581 has been active throughout the continent since at least the pre-Columbian era. Numerous theories have been proposed to explain RPC-581's behavior, the most widely accepted of which originate from the Nihilite belief framework.[[footnote]]**Nihil:** Both a theological framework and religion, Nihil categorizes archetypes of divinity as conceptual actors.[[/footnote]]

Scholars of the //Savage Redness// believe RPC-581 to be an aspect of the archetype. Prophecies of such cults, mainly those active in North America, often attribute it a messianic role, mainly through its secondary anomalous property.

> [[=]]
> //The War of Joy wages from the Pyramid Tomb by the selection of God Hearts//
> ----
> Nihil-aligned prophecy. God Hearts is believed to be RPC-581, and the War of Joy a fabled, new age. The Pyramid Tomb is less determined, but likely a Mesoamerican Temple.
> [[/=]]

Most knowledge on RPC-581 is gathered through scattered online testimony by individuals being actively targeted by the anomaly. Informational suppression of RPC-581  have largely proven ineffective, as RPC-581 has achieved widespread status as an urban legend within occult and media-focused communities, resulting in a substantial volume of unreliable commentary. However, as all targets expire shortly after confronting the heart, it is by posthumous verification that real witnesses to RPC-581 can be determined.

RPC-581 related disappearances are followed by the release of a motion picture depicting the subject in a gladiatorial snuff film, designated RPC-581-TAPE. Since the year 2000, this has manifested as a digital video posted on unregulated, dark web websites. The content of these videos is formulaic, and follows the pattern described below:

> The subject is chained by the ankle to the center of what appears to be an Aztec temalacatl.[[footnote]]A circular stone used by various Mesoamerican cultures to platform gladiator prisoners during fighting.[[/footnote]] They will attempt to defend themselves against multiple waves of aggressors in bare-handed combat. Despite the accumulation of numerous fatal injuries, the subject will remain alive. The attackers, meanwhile, don't share the subject's anomalous property. Their corpses often begin amassing on the platform as the subject defends themself. RPC-581-TAPE ends with the subject accepting defeat and surrendering, and soon after being overrun. 

Currently, the Heartbreaker Task Force is responsible for the management of all RPC-581 related affairs. Its former head, Decagon Office[[footnote]]Under which lies the Department of Nihilite Studies.[[/footnote]] researcher Cormac Marshal, discovered the only known method of preventing an RPC-581 abduction: the induction of a permanent disability. RPC-581 has never targeted physically or mentally disabled persons.

After Cormac’s death, leadership of the Task Force was transferred over to Alexander Marshal on the 4th of July 1985. Alexander has demonstrated exceptional proficiency in distinguishing legitimate accounts from fabricated reports and has partially mitigated the operational difficulties created by the loss of USOC support following the conclusion of the Newark Convention.

RPC-581 does not have a traditional discovery, as it has been prevalent throughout recorded North American history. The Auctoritas Imperatus first encountered it together with the Spanish conquistadors during the colonization of the New World, in the form of archaic media.

||~ Addendum .3 Exploration of Teotihuacan ||

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcsandbox.wikidot.com/local--files/art-the-saviour-art-the-gladiator/2020-02-11_Teotihuacan_la_Avenida_de_los_Muertos_y_la_Pir%C3%A1mide_del_Sol.jpg|caption=Pyramid of the Sun, largest structure of the Teotihuacan compound.]]

Instigated by Alexander’s testimony, various Mesoamerican pyramids in Mexico were investigated. Filtering for the areas with sightings of Alexander, the Teotihuacan Archeological Site with the Pyramid of the Sun was deemed the most likely candidate. [[[MST Hotel -1]]] “Highlanders” were sent to investigate, together with HTF head Cormac Marshal.

Referencing an archeological atlas, the team found a previously undocumented chamber within the Pyramid of the Sun’s underground cave system. Hotel-1 began preliminary mapping of the enclosure to aid a future research initiative. However, due to a cave in, this remains the only available documentation of the space.

**OBJECT:** A chiseled stone wall with ancient text.

Oftentimes used as a proof of spirituality, as only select, and in some cases lone, individuals can let the influence manifest.
