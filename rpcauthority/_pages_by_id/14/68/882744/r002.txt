Kabushiki Kawaii is known to have a hand in the recent growth of AI roleplaying sites. New intel suggests that the group has begun utilizing forcibly digitized extra dimensional entities and enslaving them within company data servers for use in erotic or sexually-focused chatbots. The exact methods utilized are uncertain but it is believed that Kabushiki Kawaii has developed a method of performing summoning rituals within an isolated digital environment, causing these entities to manifest in the form of a set of serialized parameter data files and likely greatly constraining any anomalous abilities they may possess. Sources also suggested that while this process is deliberately designed to give the company leverage over the entities, it is also mutually beneficial in some manner, possibly by allowing entities to “feed” off of a wide number of human victims in exchange for servitude. Research is ongoing.
