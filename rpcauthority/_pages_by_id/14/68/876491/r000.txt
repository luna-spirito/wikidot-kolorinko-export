**From a trusted source:** When searching for potential victims, Kabushiki Kawaii does not only rely on medical records.

While blood types, genealogy and other forms of data are useful, the group does not shy away from invading the digital privacy of others. In more recent times there has been an influx of "visual novel" video games to various digital storefronts which hold a number of invasive and subtle data collectors inside of their code that passively analyze and send the contents of the devices they are installed on to Kabushiki data centers. Often pornographic in nature with a intricately designed game loop, the goal of these games is twofold. If the game owner is a modification target, it is an attempt to map out the behavior and frequent locations of her that will make the abduction and breaking process easier. Otherwise, the group uses the owner as an outpost, scanning contacts, messages and GPS data to identify other targets.
