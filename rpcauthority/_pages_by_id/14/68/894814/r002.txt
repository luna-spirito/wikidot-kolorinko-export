Hi, I’m an anonymous source. I’m posting this at some obscure location. Remember Failsafe? I’ve recently been going through the archives and found a memorandum from a couple of years ago between the Deputy Site Director at 14 and a technician. The technician complained about the lack of budget for infrastructure improvements and referred to an ‘AEGIS system that was forgotten years ago.’ I asked around, and it turns out AEGIS was co-developed by NuCorp. 

It’s a bit strange that NuCorp developed the technology, and the FailSafes were activated. Strange.
