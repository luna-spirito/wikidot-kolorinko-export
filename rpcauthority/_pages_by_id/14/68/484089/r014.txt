**Registered Phenomena Code:** 461

**Object Class:** Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** Mechanical, Mind-Control

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-461 is stored in a standard Alpha-class containment locker at Site-044. All experimentation on RPC-461 is to be authorized by Head Researcher Dr. --Melissa Jackson-- William Berwick. The test chamber for RPC-461 is an 8x8x3 m room containing RPC-461's containment locker and a statue to take photos of.[[footnote]] Researchers chose a replica of //The Thinker// by Auguste Rodin with the belief that a well-known piece of art would stimulate the imagination of whoever is using RPC-461, leading to more vivid pictures. [[/footnote]] All testing on RPC-461 is to be conducted by a subject who will use RPC-461 and an instructor who will oversee the experiment.

**Description:** RPC-461 is a black press camera with a built-in flash bulb attachment. It has no apparent branding and appears to have been manufactured in the 1950's. All attempts to disassemble RPC-461 have been met with failure.

When RPC-461 is used to take a photograph, it does not capture an image of the physical environment opposite to its lens. Rather, it generates an image based on the thoughts of the user at the moment the photograph is taken. Upon observing the image, the user will be compelled to use RPC-461 again. Repeated usage of RPC-461 results in the user experiencing a Wedgwood Event[[footnote]] Named after the English photography pioneer Thomas Wedgwood. [[/footnote]] at random intervals.

A Wedgwood Event begins with the user's eyes emitting a burst of light, akin to a camera's flash. The Wedgwood Event ends once the user blinks, at which point their eyelids emit the sound of a camera's shutter.[[footnote]] Wedgwood Events do not affect the user's eyesight, regardless of frequency. [[/footnote]] The frequency of Wedgwood Events increases with the frequency of RPC-461's use.[[footnote]] Wedgwood Events grow less frequent with continued abstinence from RPC-461, and eventually cease in some cases. [[/footnote]]

**Addendum I: Discovery Log**

----

On 08/08/2020, Authority agents responded to reports of an anomalous event which occurred at the Lexington Photography and Film Museum in Lexington, Massachusetts, United States. Local newspapers attested that the museum's curator, Gerald Wright, suffered from an "inexplicable flashing condition."

> **Interviewed:** Gerald Wright
> 
> **Interviewer:** Field Agent William Holtz
> 
> **Foreword:** The following interview was recorded in the museum's executive office by an Authority agent disguised as a journalist.
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Holtz:** Tell me about this condition, Mr. Wright.
> 
> **Wright:** Well, it's very rare, but every once in a while my eyes do this thing where they flash and shutter like a camera. When it first happened to me I thought I was going crazy. I'd spent too much time around the cameras and I must be seeing things, you know? But then, the janitor saw it.
> 
> //Wright experiences a Wedgwood Event.//
> 
> **Wright:** Right on cue!
> 
> **Holtz:** That's really interesting! Now, can you tell me how you think this started? Like, did anything weird happen to you that might be linked to this condition?
> 
> **Wright:** I'm really not sure, but there's something about the Zapruder Camera...
> 
> **Holtz:** Zapruder? Like the guy who filmed Kennedy's-
> 
> **Wright:** No! No, not that one. You see, I have a camera that was donated to the museum by an anonymous collector. It came with a note saying it was made in 1951 by a man named Hermann Zapruder. I haven't made an exhibit for it yet because I cannot for the life of me figure out who this "Hermann Zapruder" is.
> 
> **Holtz:** I see. Can you tell me more about the camera?
> 
> **Wright:** Well, when I use it to take pictures, it barely ever captures what's in front of it, so it doesn't really work as a camera at all. Nevertheless, I do enjoy using it; testing it out, seeing what new image it'll print out. It fascinates me.
> 
> **Holtz:** So how's this camera linked to your condition?
> 
> **Wright:** Oh! Yes, well! My eyes began doing things only a week after I got the camera.
> 
> **Holtz:** Interesting. Can you show me the camera?
> 
> **Wright:** Certainly! Come with me.
> 
> //Wright begins leading Holtz to the museum's storage room.//
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** After this interview, Gerald Wright was taken into Authority custody under the guise of medical treatment for his condition and a disinformation campaign was created to dismiss the event as mass hysteria. The camera he mentioned in the interview was confiscated and designated RPC-461. Wright was eventually released after he ceased experiencing Wedgwood Events.

**Addendum II: RPC-461 Experiment Log**

----

> **Subject:** Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson
> 
> **Instructor:** N/A
> 
> **Details:** Control experiment. Subject to use RPC-461 to take three photos of the statue.
> 
> **Results:** Three (3) images of an apple in varying levels of detail, three (3) images of the subject holding RPC-461.
> 
> **Notes:** Subject was compelled to take three additional photos of the statue.
> 
> **Comments:** //Junior Researcher Yang was out sick, so I conducted the experiment alone. The photos were, unsurprisingly, exactly what I was thinking about. I planned on only taking three photos, but as per RPC-461's anomalous compulsion, I took three more before I stopped myself.// --Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson

> **Subject:** CSD-118574
> 
> **Instructor:** Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson
> 
> **Details:** Subject instructed to use RPC-461 to take ten (10) photos of the statue after reading a partially-redacted copy of [[[RPC-705]]]'s documentation.
> 
> **Results:** Seven (7) images of a golden retriever bearing a resemblance to RPC-705, three (3) images of said golden retriever attacking CSD-3564.
> 
> **Notes:** Test shows that the camera can capture photo-accurate images of anomalous objects based on the user's thoughts after reading a physical description of said objects.
> 
> **Comments:** N/A

> **Subject:** CSD-118574
> 
> **Instructor:** Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson
> 
> **Details:** Subject instructed to use RPC-461 to take thirty (30) photos of the statue after reading a copy of [[[RPC-456]]]'s documentation. Several miscellaneous images were hung on the walls of RPC-461's containment chamber to distract the subject and induce intrusive thoughts.
> 
> **Results:** Thirty (30) images of wooden gravestones with flowers of varying species growing in front of them. 25 of the images contained chrysanthemums.
> 
> **Notes:** Test shows that distractions and intrusive thoughts do not affect the contents of the photos generated by RPC-461.
> 
> **Comments:** //My compliments to CSD-118574's taste in flowers. Chrysanthemums are my favorite, too.// --Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson

> **Subject:** CSD-118574
> 
> **Instructor:** Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson
> 
> **Details:** Subject instructed to use RPC-461 to take forty (40) photos of the statue after reading a partially-redacted copy of [[[RPC-506]]]'s documentation.
> 
> **Results:** Forty (40) miscellaneous images unrelated to RPC-506. Several of the photos were related to Dr. Jackson's personal life. One (1) image of RPC-506-2.
> 
> **Notes:** CSD-118574 insisted that he had no knowledge of Dr. Jackson's personal life. After the test concluded, CSD-118574 used RPC-461 without Dr. Jackson's authorization and yielded a photo of an instance of RPC-506-2.
> 
> **Comments:** //This changes a lot of what we know about RPC-461. In the past few experiments, I had read the articles with CSD-118574. Because of this, we were likely thinking of the same things. This time, however, I was called into another room during the experiment. All these experiments, RPC-461 has been capturing my thoughts, not CSD-118574's, because I was instructing him to take the pictures. That explains why I kept demanding more samples each experiment. In addition, I experienced my first Wedgwood Event.// --Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson

> **Subject:** Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson
> 
> **Instructor:** N/A
> 
> **Details:** Subject entered the test chamber at 23:21 and exited at 2:05.
> 
> **Results:** Wedgwood Event frequency increased in subject.
> 
> **Notes:** N/A
> 
> **Comments:** //I was compelled. When I put that camera away, I felt a pit in my stomach, as if something really bad was going to happen if I stopped taking photos then. Something possessed me to return to RPC-461's testing chamber that evening. I emailed the site director for approval, and then spent a few hours taking photos. My eyes now flash and shutter like a camera every 4-5 minutes.// --Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson

> **Subject:** Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson
> 
> **Instructor:** Junior Researcher Dr. Kevin Yang
> 
> **Details:** Subject instructed to test the maximum frequency of Wedgwood Events by taking as many photos as possible using RPC-461, only being stopped at the instructor's discretion.

> 
> **Notes:** Subject now experiences Wedgwood Events several times per second. Subject is now capable of projecting three-dimensional moving pictures.
> 
> **Comments:** //I know this was against proper Authority protocol, but I was curious. After a few days of involuntarily "flashing" my higher-ups, I wanted to see how far this anomaly could go. I could have used a CSD for this test, but I'm already in too deep. I was taking photos over the course of the whole day, only breaking for lunch. I kept Dr. Yang with me so that I wouldn't get enthralled by RPC-461. I've discovered a new effect. Every Wedgwood Event isn't just a flash of light. Within the light is a 3-D projection of what I'm thinking, so my eyes now act like a projector.// --Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson

**Addendum III: Incident Log 461-01**

----

On 01/04/2021, The day following Dr. Jackson's last experiment with RPC-461, several anomalous events occurred at Site-044:
* The appearance of a doppelganger of Dr. [REDACTED].
* >100 anomaly report copies often containing incorrect and unrelated information manifesting on the desks of researchers unaffiliated with the source documentation.
* Unintelligible writing manifesting on the interior of Site-044.
* A non-anomalous version of [[[RPC-042]]] appearing in the mess hall, resulting in the injury of 50 personnel.
Incident disoriented personnel and disrupted general site operation. Internal investigation revealed Dr. Jackson's Wedgwood projections occasionally became corporeal after the respective Wedgwood Event concluded. In response, Site Director Philip Meyer ordered Dr. Jackson be held under Authority custody.

> **Interviewed:** Former RPC-461 Head Researcher Dr. Melissa Jackson
> 
> **Interviewer:** Site Director Dr. Phillip Meyer
> 
> **Foreword:** This conversation was recorded following several termination requests from Dr. Jackson.
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Meyer:** Good afternoon, Dr. Jackson.
> 
> **Jackson:** Dr. Meyer, can you please do what needs to be done? At least put me in a coma or something.
> 
> **Meyer:** I see no reason to do anything of the sort. I don't understand why you keep asking.
> 
> **Jackson:** Haven't you seen what I'm capable of? My mind, my imagination, is out of control. What if I end up thinking about...something dangerous?
> 
> **Meyer:** Do you often think about world-ending-
> 
> **Jackson:** Don't say it like that! I don't want to think of it! But, yes, I fear these Wedgwood Events may...you know.
> 
> **Meyer:** Dr. Jackson, you wrote that intrusive thoughts do not affect the photos RPC-461 generates. Do they affect the images your eyes project?
> 
> **Jackson:** No.
> 
> **Meyer:** Do you deliberately think about world...about dangerous things?
> 
> **Jackson:** No. I mean, not deliberately, but you saw that day, how even innocuous thoughts can mess everything up. I'm worried that it will happen again.
> 
> **Meyer:** I think you worry too much. What have you thought of recently, that has become real?
> 
> **Jackson:** Well, I...made myself some coffee earlier today. Yesterday I manifested a mannequin in my bed; scared the shit out of me. I-
> 
> **Meyer:** Anything dangerous?
> 
> //Dr. Jackson begins rapidly blinking while projecting several three-dimensional images onto Dr. Meyer's desk.//
> 
> **Jackson:** No, but I could think of something dangerous right now! I could think of a live bomb right in front of you, or...or a big stone in the air, about to fall on your head!
> 
> **Meyer:** Melissa, stop! Both you and I know you're in perfect control of your thoughts!
> 
> **Jackson:** The camera, my eyes, they're fucking with my head, Dr. Meyer! I don't know what's real anymore! The other day I greeted a handler, but he disappeared with a blink! When I drank my coffee this morning, I felt it disappear in my stomach! I try to open doors that aren't there! I just don't want to think anymore!
> 
> //Dr. Jackson shrinks down in her seat. She blinks.//
> 
> **Jackson:** And I'm tired of hearing that shutter noise.
> 
> **Meyer:** Consider everything you've documented about RPC-461. It'll take a while, but staying away from the thing will make the Wedgwood Events lessen in severity and frequency.
> 
> //Dr. Jackson blinks again. A corpse resembling Dr. Jackson manifests slumped over in a nearby chair.//
> 
> **Jackson:** I'm sorry.
> 
> **Meyer:** No need to say sorry, Dr. Jackson. Nothing that happened was your fault. I know you've suffered a lot this past month, and something's got to change. Head back to your quarters.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** Shortly after, Dr. Jackson was dismissed from her role and placed on extended suicide watch. The assigned psychologist has reported no suicidal behavior after 2 months since admittance. RPC-461 reclassification to Alpha-Yellow pending.
