During an excursion to Tyresta National Park, one class 2A student of Lögdea school gained the ability to "control plants", according to teachers. The student, Hugo Alfons, could reportedly manipulate the anatomy of vegetation, sprout berries, as well as heal certain injuries in his classmates. After growing familiar with his abilities, Hugo decided to drive the teachers out of the camp. 

[[=]]
[[image http://rpcsandbox.wikidot.com/local--files/soulless-sanctuary/Hugo style="width: 90%; margin: 0 auto;"]]

//##6b1010|Hugo Alfons##, Lögdea class 2A photo//

[[/=]]

Alfons refused to allow adults into his enclave, announcing it a “country without grown-ups”. A thick foliage soon enveloped the area, prohibiting entry. Truly, any kid's dream.

"The kids are our responsibility, and the police don't believe us." As contacting the local authorities were of no use, one of the teachers reached out to our office for unusual and mystical happenings. This is when I responded, eager to see if this new country had any merit.

Three uneventful days after my arrival, the teachers' call for help was answered by the neighboring Society of Body and Mind. Priests from the Society approached Hugo's enclave as "envoys" wanting to engage in "diplomatic talks". After negotiations, the two groups merged.

The teachers initially attempted to still reach the children, but were soon acquiesced by the Global Authorities. The specifics of this is unknown. The parents of the children have refused to be interviewed.

**Description:** RPC-788 is the collective designation for two types of corpses left behind by an ongoing series of homicides. the homicides bear similar or identical traits in their pathology and anomalous nature, that began on February 28th, 2026. Over 87% of RPC-788 cases have occurred within Stockholm County and surrounding residential areas. 

A study observing RPC-788 victims concluded:

* RPC-788 victims are between the ages of 20 and 50 years old.
* All cases have occurred at night.
* The targeted residence was vacant, save for the victim.
* The cadaver is always located adjacent to a window, casement, or other aperture to the outside. 
* The cadaver does not bleed.
* The cadaver does not decompose. 
* The cadaver is missing either the head or the rest of the body. 
* A handwritten note was always present nearby the cadaver.

RPC-788 was originally assumed to be caused by mundane serial killings without anomalous intervention. However, investigations failed to ascertain how the perpetrator entered the scene and lacerated their victims without causing them to bleed, nor why the victims' bodies did not decompose. A later biological analysis revealed additional anomalous properties attributed to the corpses, which are demonstrated in addenda.

The quintessential RPC-788 mutilations are surgically complex. In cases where the head is left at the scene (Type A - more common at 80% of cases), the brainstem is conspicuously absent. Correspondingly, in the rarer Type B cases, the brainstem remains attached to the spinal cord and is exposed cranially given the lack of other cranial structures.

The note found on RPC-788 sites always contains the same text. It has been translated and attached below:

> [[=]]
> Här kvarstår Ängeln's puppa 
> ##8f8f8f|Here remains the Angel's chrysalis##

+++ ADDENDUM:3 [The Society of Body and Mind]


The emblem adorned by the figure in Lindell’s home surveillance belongs to a group named the **Society of Body and Mind**. A brief history of the organization is attached below, mainly segments relevant to the RPC-788 phenomenon. 

[[collapsible show="[+] Society of Body and Mind History" hide="[-] Society of Body and Mind History"]]

**Excerpt from:** //The Unveiled Groups of Rural Scandinavia, Chapter 3 - Eriksson Gävle (2010)//

''//Samhället för Kropp och Sinne//, previously //Societas Corporis et Mentis//, is a secluded enclave of Christendom within Tyresta National Reserve, Stockholm County. It lies on the fringes of Swedish policy, and has been politically ignored in recent times due to its passive nature. Acting as a secluded micro-nation, the Society of Body and Mind enforces its own laws, economy, and moral worldview, heavily inspired by the teachings of the mystic theologian //Emmanuel Swedenborg//’s work. 

[[include component:image-block name=https://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/soulless-sanctuary/Emanuel_Swedenborg.PNG|align=right|width=200px]]

The Society has been historically varied in ídeology, originally being created by the Auctoritas Imperitus to detain and manage prisoners of the anomalous through divine arts. The Society, with union-wide centers, began evangelical work, converting or otherwise neutralizing humanoid entities. 

After the Stockholm Bloodbath in 1520, the Church became ostracised by the public. A relative of one of the massacre's victims, Gustav Vasa, soon organized an uproar, ultimately ending the Kalmar Union and overthrowing the Catholic leadership. During this period, the Auctoritas was persecuted by the protestant reformed Kingdom. The Society of Body and Mind was similarly targeted, however were spared in return for giving up all their anomalous assets to Gustav Vasa's replacement unveiled authority: //Försvaret mot Avvikande Objekt//[[footnote]]//The Defense against Aberrant Objects//[[/footnote]]

Without Auctoritas support, the Society continued to dwindle throughout the 16th century. Local protestant thought additionally disillusioned the group, culminating in a largely isolationist culture hidden in its previous headquarters of Tyresta. Secluded, the Society’s thinkers began to discuss what led to the decline of Church power, paving the way for Emmanuel Swedenborg’s influences.

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcsandbox.wikidot.com/local--files/soulless-sanctuary/Sunday-School.jpg|align=left|width=300px]]

Swedenborg preached divine inspiration, claiming to be a prophetic dreamer. Swedenborgism as a belief focuses on moral frameworks, and pictures God as an entity of pure love and affection, seen in all the good of earth. Any evil is as such the absence of divinity. Swedenborgism therefore rejects multiple aspects of the Trinity and Sola Fide.[[footnote]]//Faith Only//: Swedenborgism claims both faith and charity to be mandatory faces of worship.[[/footnote]]

The Society of Body and Mind applied Swedenborgism to the failures of the Church; an exaggerated emphasis on faith alone. In order to express God, who lies at the heart of all human good, one needed to exercise both the labor of body and mind; both physical charity and spiritual devotion. Anthropocentrism became a staple tenant, insisting that the human soul’s capacity for free will puts it at a higher position than angels.

> ##black|Mankind was given the choice; toil and devote, or embrace avarice and sloth? It is by making that choice, resisting the temptation, and ultimately expressing God through the goodwill of our souls, that we achieve feats planes above even that of choiceless Angels! Yes, God is in all humans! All of us, in this very chapel! For God, is the soul! All hail the spirit!##
> [[>]]
> //##black|Verse from a Society of Body and Mind sermon##//
> [[/>]]

Today, the Society remains a secluded enclave in Tyresta. It mainly engages in various charities, and orphan relief. In fact, the Society of Body and Mind often adopts homeless, orphaned, and even anomalous adolescents. 

Other sources describe it as a culture of prolific honor-related oppression and shaming. This is exemplified in that the Society constructed catacombs, as the grim profile of graveyards conflicted with the obsession over purity and innocence.
