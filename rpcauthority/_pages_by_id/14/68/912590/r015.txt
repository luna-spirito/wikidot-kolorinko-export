**Containment Protocols:** RPC-481 is to be housed in a standard humanoid containment chamber with no chamber modifications. As of writing, RPC-481 resides within Site-120 located in the Red Lake State Wildlife Management Area in northern Minnesota. In the event RPC-481 must be relocated to another site, the prospective site must reside within or near a densely wooded area.

In the event RPC-481 requests a meal substitution, or has not expressed any desire to consume its typically provided meal, a substitution of raw beef, pork, chicken, or [[[RPC-286]]] is to be provided instead.[[footnote]]See Addendum 481-2 for details.[[/footnote]] Otherwise, RPC-481 will receive a nutritionally balanced diet for herbivorous creatures consisting of fresh grasses, leaves, nuts, and other suitable dietary components for each meal. Documentation and observation is required whenever a meal substitution has been requested. Should an escalation in the frequency of these requests be observed, security personnel are to conduct regular check-ups on RPC-481 at intervals of fifteen minutes, providing an extra portion of the aforementioned types of flesh upon request. Until the 481-A event is complete, it is to be monitored via camera feed by at least one member of the RPC-481 research team at any given time.

When RPC-481 cannot be located within its unit, MST Victor-8  “It’s All a Game”[[footnote]]Specialized in game tracking, 3-5 members are to be trained in de-escalation.[[/footnote]] are to be dispatched to the wooded area nearest to the site. Upon discovery, MST Victor-8 is to refrain from using force and attempt to coax RPC-481 back into Site-120 to ensure its continued trust in Authority staff. During retrieval, tranquilization of RPC-481 may only be performed in the sole case that it becomes aggressive or attempts to escape the Site-120 grounds. 481-A events are not considered containment breaches unless the point of manifestation is outside of Site-120 grounds or if RPC-481 escapes past the outer fences of Site-120.

As of 04/14/20██, due to concerns of stereotypic behavior,[[footnote]]Also known as “Zoochosis”; Repetitive, invariant behaviour patterns with no obvious goal or function. [https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0003347205806402 See Stereotypies: a critical review][[/footnote]] containment protocols are pending changes to allow RPC-481 to exist in a more natural environment.

**Description:** RPC-481 is a female member of a critically endangered species known as Pedestrocervus tridactylus.[[footnote]] Stiltwalker Deer, also known as the "Not Deer" cryptid popular in online circles. [[/footnote]] The species is similar in appearance to the Odocoileus virginianus,[[footnote]]//White-tailed Deer//[[/footnote]] with the primary difference being that Stiltwalkers have the capabilities to ambulate on their hind legs, and possess small tridactyl hand-like appendages[[footnote]]Two fingers and a thumb, all three of which have spheroid joints to allow for holding and grasping objects such as branches, sticks, and even rocks.[[/footnote]] on their forelimbs, which they can walk on. Aside from RPC-481, the remaining four discovered members of its species are currently held on an Authority-owned preservation for the purpose of observation and safeguarding the species from extinction and monitoring any resulting offspring for potential similarities to RPC-481, despite exhibiting otherwise mundane characteristics. 

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-481/P-00481-forestedpond|caption=Discovery location of RPC-481 (not pictured) after the first 481-A event|width=###]]

RPC-481 appears to be a young adult of its species, as the white spots indicative of a juvenile are still visible on the hindquarters of RPC-481. Unlike others of its species, RPC-481 exhibits notable humanoid characteristics surpassing those of other Pedestrocervus tridactylus members, characterized by a distinctively human-like facial structure. Other features include a pair of four-inch stunted antlers that grow and shed naturally, similar to other deer species. Of particular significance is RPC-481's peculiar and inconsistent dietary requirement of meat. If RPC-481 were to transition to a solely carnivorous diet, it would undergo a translocation phenomenon, dubbed RPC-481-A, to the nearest wooded area. If found relatively quickly, RPC-481 will display signs of confusion or fear. RPC-481 has never displayed any memory of craving, asking for, or consuming raw meat of any kind. After recovering from the shock of a 481-A event, RPC-481 will resume its herbivorous diet until once again reverting to its carnivorous tendencies sometime in the future. RPC-481 can go from as many as eight months without this event taking place to as little as thirty-two hours. As of writing, no attempts to prevent RPC-481's teleportation have been successful (See Addendum 481-3).

Cognitively, RPC-481 possesses the intellect of a grade-school child, having a rudimentary grasp of the English and Dakota languages. To test the limits of RPC-481’s intelligence gain, it has been enrolled in game-based learning programs currently overseen by Junior Researcher Maela Kinnd. RPC-481 has demonstrated overall receptiveness and compliance with these instructional sessions, as well as displaying cooperative behavior in its containment environment. For ease of reference, RPC-481 has expressed a preference for the name "Chanhassen," meaning "Sugar Maple Tree" in the Dakota language. In order to facilitate interactions with RPC-481, it is recommended that all personnel refer to RPC-481 as "Chanhassen" while conversing due to its aversion to its official designation.

**Addendum 481-1: Discovery** 
On 9/27/20██, a report of a hunted deer with a humanoid face was made to the Fish and Wildlife Department of Minnesota. During the initial investigation, it was found by a local who had been hunting illegally on a native reservation. Authority staff operating within the department seized the entity and administered amnestics to the hunter. At the time of acquisition, RPC-481 had suffered three gunshot wounds to its torso, resulting in severe internal injuries. RPC-481's condition necessitated surgery and multiple resuscitation attempts. Eventually, RPC-481's condition was successfully stabilized. It was then officially registered on the Authority Database as Nonanomalous Abnormality (NAA) #98, before being reclassified as RPC-481 after the first 481-A event.

**Further Addenda:**
[[collapsible show="Addendum 481-2: RE:Carnivorous Tendencies" hide="- Return"]]
Below is a personal recounting of RPC-481 Lead Researcher Jason Reids’ experience regarding RPC-481’s dietary tendencies. At the time of the events described, Mister Reids was a Junior Researcher newly assigned to Site-120.
> The lead-up to that first 481-A event started about a month after we got it. We didn’t notice it at first and thought she was being picky about her food, so we switched it from simple plant-based nutrition bricks to more natural foods found in the stiltwalker deer’s habitat. Nothing, didn’t even touch it. She was frantic whenever we brought her food, running up to the door when it was delivered like a starving dog to seem disappointed at something. We couldn’t figure anything out. She looked sick, constantly shaking like she was suffering withdrawals from some hard drug. Deer can go about a month without food, and she looked like she'd gone nearly three without it. I have to be honest, I didn't know what we were supposed to do. Whenever we asked 481, or #98 as she was known then, all she could say was Dakota for "squirrel" or "rabbit". I brought up the issue to an old friend of mine, Stanley Nohmann, who was working on [[[RPC-286]]] at the time. We tried giving her some leftover flesh from it, and well... it worked for sure. We gave her as much as she wanted. I swear she ate enough to feed a Thanksgiving table. And as soon as she finished, we looked away for just one second, and she just, poof, vanished. Nothing but the smell of a pond left behind. 
> 
> After that first instance, the containment breach and everything, I left the site with some of the other staff, but I got separated from the group. I was the one who found her, as you may know. I was just a Junior then, bright eyes and bushy tail. I didn't even think of the dangers and forgot protocol. Got a hell of a talking to, sure, but I also got 481 back in containment. She- //It// was just scared. Needed someone there to be able to calm down. I... Turned out to be that person.

The following table shows all tests attempted to prevent the 481-A event phenomenon.

||~ Event # ||~ Experiment ||~ Result ||~ Notes ||
|| 481-A-5 || Replace meal substitutions with plant-based meat alternatives. || Failure || RPC-481 exhibited signs of extreme food poisoning after consuming the plant-based meat alternative. Translocation occurred immediately after being given a block of processed [[[RPC-286]]] flesh. ||
|| 481-A-8 || Substitute raw meats with cooked meats. || Failure || RPC-481 had difficulty consuming the cooked meat, forcing it to eat more slowly. No adverse/unusual effects. ||
|| 481-A-13 || CSD-19042 is restrained within RPC-481's holding cell, while meal substitutions were withheld, to test if RPC-481 would see humans as an alternate source of raw meat. || Failure || RPC-481 never reacted violently towards the CSD personnel, only begging to be given food. After 2 days of this behavior, RPC-481 was provided a meal substitute, and CSD-19042 was removed. ||
|| N/A || Provide meal substitutions without request, in an attempt to cause a 481-A event artificially. || Failure || RPC-481 refused to consume the meal substitute. ||
|| 481-A-20 || RPC-481 is to be fitted with a geo-location collar capable of recording video, audio, and vital signs. || Failure || Collar did not translocate with RPC-481, remaining in its cell while RPC-481 teleported as usual. See Addendum 481-4. ||
|| 481-A-21 || Lace replacement meals with anti-anxiety medication. || Semi-Success || While this did not outright prevent a 481-A event, it successfully delayed it by a total of 10 days and 11 nights. RPC-481 suffered lasting effects after the event's conclusion, temporarily displaying an inability to properly convey complex thoughts for 24 hours following the event. Noted for use in the future, but consistent utilization of this method could result in a loss of cognitive function. ||
|| 481-A-27 || Images of a target location within the bounds of Site-120 were placed around RPC-481's room in an attempt to subliminally influence the teleportation location. || Failure || RPC-481 was found in the opposite quadrant of Site-120. ||
|| 481-A-30 || RPC-481 is to be fitted with a geo-locator chip inserted at the base of the left ear for ease of tracking. || Failure || Upon teleportation, the chip previously embedded under RPC-481's skin was found on the floor of its cell. RPC-481 was found on Site-120's grounds, nursing a wound where the chip had been inserted. ||
|| 481-A-43 || Include a map of the forest surrounding Site-120 in RPC-481's study material, with natural landmarks labeled in an attempt to facilitate retrieval || Semi-Failure || RPC-481 managed to avoid MST Victor-8 for 18 hours, before being found drinking from the pond it had manifested nearby during the 481-A-1 event alongside one other members of its species. ||
|| 481-A-44 || Provide RPC-481 with an "all you can eat" style spread of various meats. || Success || 481-A event concluded within 30 minutes of initial signs. RPC-481 notably consumed nearly all of the available raw and processed [[[RPC-286]]] flesh, as well as some venison. While the spread was not an exhaustive list of all meat types, untouched meat types include: human, horse, lamb, veal, fish, and both cured and cooked meats. ||

[[collapsible show="Addendum 481-4: 481-A Event Recording" hide="- Return"]]
The following is a transcription of a video taken via a recording device embedded within the tracking collar around RPC-481's neck, during event 481-A-20.

> **0:00:00 -** The camera's view is obstructed as RPC-481 violently devours its meal. Its vocalizations are seemingly a mixture of animalistic and human noises. 
> **0:02:15 -** Upon finishing the meal of raw [[[RPC-286]]] flesh, RPC-481 moves frantically to the corner of its room and sinks. The view is obscured. 
> **0:07:43 -** It begins rocking in place, its breathing catching and becoming shallow. From the angle of the collar, RPC-481 is seemingly staring at the hidden camera in its cell. 
> **0:10:22 -** After looking away from the camera, RPC-481's breaths become erratic and it begins uttering the phrases "No" and "Please" in the Dakota language repeatedly, occasionally mentioning Reids in these mutterings.
> **0:13:45 -** RPC-481 reaches into its nightstand, retrieving the human biology flashcards from its education program and flipping through them. Its voice is shaky, unable to properly articulate the answers, but seemingly understanding them. 
> **0:15:02 -** RPC-481 flips to the final flashcard, displaying a human heart and its location. It panics upon seeing the image and throws the card away.
> **0:15:45 -** RPC-481 begins to scream, but the noise is suddenly cut off. The collar falls to the floor, flipping over as it falls to reveal that RPC-481 is no longer present in the corner.

> **During the next lead-up to a 481-A event, let's try anxiety medication in 481's substitute meals. If it properly prevents the event, then we may have found our answer. - Researcher Reids**

[[collapsible show="Addendum 481-5: Notable Advancements" hide="- Return"]]
> **__11/05/20██__**
> First 481-A event. NAA #98 reclassified as RPC-481, containment protocol updated, and investigations begin.

> **__12/27/20██__**
> RPC-481 develops a trusting bond with Junior Researcher Reids. Shows signs of learning aptitude.

> **__2/02/20██__**
> Second 481-A event. RPC-481 refused to accompany MST Victor-8 and was tranquilized and taken by force. Refused to cooperate after. Containment Protocol updated after Junior Researcher Reids was able to re-establish trust.

> **__5/14/20██__**
> RPC-481 enrolled in reading-based exercises, with teaching duties delegated to Junior Researcher Kinnd due to past experience in the field.

> **__6/20/20██__**
> Fifth 481-A event. Came willingly after a game of hide-and-seek with MST Victor-8. Noticeable morale improvement for all parties.

> **__3/01/20██__**
> RPC-481 now reading and speaking at an 8th-grade level. Expresses desire to “do what you do” to Lead Researcher Reids.

> **__8/12/20██__**
> Twentieth 481-A event. The longest recorded time between events is now eight months, twelve days.

> **__9/22/20██__**
> RPC-481 has graduated from all game-based learning programs available through the middle school level, and now has a fluent grasp of the English language, a rudimentary knowledge of mathematics, and a basic understanding of both physics and biology. Current academic interest: human biology and genetics.
> 
> **Refrain from enrolling RPC-481 in any higher levels of education.**
> **- Site Director Massone**

> **__12/19/20██__**
> RPC-481 has been seen pacing in tight lines from its latrine to the head of its bed with regularity.

> **__12/27/20██__**
> Pacing pattern has not changed. RPC-481 has been seen occasionally picking at a specific portion of its elbow.

> **__1/04/20██__**
> 30th 481-A event, first official containment breach since event 481-A-1. RPC-481 manifested just outside the Site-120 outer fence as captured by surveillance cameras. Due to the confusion that often comes to RPC-481 after teleportation, it thought it was still inside Site-120 and remained in place. RPC-481 came willingly, but stopped to watch a member of the Pedestrocervus tridactylus on site for several minutes before finally acquiescing to one of MST Victor-8's unit's urges to return indoors.

> **__4/13/20██__**
> 47th 481-A event, the second within only 48 hours. RPC-481 was found at the edge of Site-120's property, pacing the fence and rubbing the previously mentioned spot on its elbow. Reacted with shock when MST Victor-8 made contact and fled. It was found desperately attempting to enter Site-120's main building.
> 
> **It is important to note RPC-481's mental state has significantly deteriorated over the past four months, and this behavior in particular has progressively worsened; It is suspected that this is the cause of recent cognitive regression. Additionally, it has grown reclusive to personnel previously amicable in its eyes. Based on this development, I agree with Junior Researcher Kinnd's request that the education program for RPC-481 be reinstated as a containment protocol. - Lead Researcher Reids**
