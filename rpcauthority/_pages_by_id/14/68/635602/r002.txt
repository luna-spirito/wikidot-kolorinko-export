**Containment Protocols:** OL-Site-675 is to maintain the cover of an oil terminal owned by an Authority front. Personnel are stationed to defend against possible USOC[[footnote]]**The United States Occult Community:** a conglomerate of federal US departments responding to anomalous threats and opportunities. Despite its fragmented nature, the USOC is largely hostile to the Authority.[[/footnote]] infiltration.

Under no circumstances is RPC-675's oil content allowed to be ignited. Utilization must be approved by OL-Site Head, submerging in the oil is otherwise forbidden. 

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcsandbox.wikidot.com/local--files/well-oiled-world/Oiling.jpg|caption=RPC-675.|width=300px]]

**Description:** RPC-675 is an aboveground petroleum storage tank with an open roof. It constitutes the 103rd unit of the Inyo County Oil Terminal, formerly owned by TerraRoll Engines prior to acquisition by the Authority. The oil terminal is now designated OL-Site-675.

Siphoning the oil will not decrease the liquid level. Measuring has not reached the bottom of the tank; suggesting a geometric distortion giving rise to a limitless supply of petroleum. 

Exploitation of this supply has been proven dangerous, as the stored material’s greenhouse emissions cause severe psychological disturbance to residents of areas polluted by the smoke. The most commonly observed symptom is dyschronometria, frequently presenting at intensities sufficient to eliminate all temporal perception.

A primary psychological effect occurs when an individual is submerged in RPC-675's content. When submerged, the subject will perceive their future self making a decision of personal significance. Observed scenarios range from private financial decisions to complex military strategies. However, all illusions will depict the subject making the quantifiably favorable choice. Subjects often state that they will base their future actions based on this vision.

Occasionally, the vision will break from this pattern. Instead, the individual may perceive a fragment of what researchers hypothesize to be a singular, greater scene. The aberrant illusion has so far not been reconstructed. View addenda for further information.

**Discovery:** TerraRoll Engines is a subsidiary of the TerraRoll Affiliation: an American investment company specializing in enterprise risk and fixed income institutional asset management. Its leadership positions are occupied by artificial psychics created by the USOC as a response to similar Soviet programs. However, USOC cut its support of the company  in 1974 once TerraRoll’s artificial psychics began working for personal benefit.

TerraRoll Engines was once a giant within the fossil fuel sector, with influences globally. However, their success came to a halt soon after ending relations with USOC. The cause of their economic downfall is unknown, as TerraRoll subsidiaries typically obscure internal operations. Due to this, details regarding the nature of TerraRoll and its subsidiaries often come from witness testimony or acquired media. Even in these cases, employees are often hesitant to provide information.

RPC-675 was brought to the attention of the Authority on June 15th, 2000 by a letter sent from TerraRoll Engines Secretary Donald Remy to an Authority front. An oil stain made deciphering the contents difficult, but analysis of the paper revealed the following text:

> //Tank 103 in Inyo County Oil Terminal is a pool of scrying oil. Take it off my hands. Let me finally be free of the Oilman’s grease.//
> 
> //I should have let the company collapse after what happened at the party. I should have let it all go. I drowned in money, and found it neither green nor gold, but the bottomless black of crude oil. Today I resurface, I rub the oil off, and with it the guilt.//

5 hours after receiving this letter, TerraRoll Engines declared bankruptcy. Donald Remy remains missing as of June 30th, 2000.

+ Later Pursuits
[[/=]]

----

In just a few years time, TerraRoll Engines expanded to cover over 75% of fossil fuel production and imports in the USA. Remarkably, this was not reflected in the company’s public records, which described generally mundane ventures.

Upon further investigation, however, it was discovered that many of the records detailing failed pursuits were fraudulent. In reality, TerraRoll Engines was on an exceptional streak of successful investments. Certain outside parties attempted to persecute the company with charges of insider trading, but no such case ever flourished.[[footnote]]USOC involvement is suspected.[[/footnote]] Most employees were extremely loyal, and resisted outreach by ACI[[footnote]]**Authority Central Intelligence**[[/footnote]] investigators. 

At times, whistleblowers would voice concerns regarding the corporate culture of TerraRoll Engines. These individuals were similarly repressed, as tight non-disclosure agreements prevented them from sharing their experience with a large audience. These statistically improbable events eventually led to Authority interest. One informant managed to deliver a roll of film, containing an unedited video on TerraRoll Engines corporate etiquette.

TerraRoll Engines experienced an abrupt downward shift in growth in 1974. The cause of this remains unknown, however investigating the notekeeps of private personnel reveals the trend of a “party” or social gathering, often with omitted details. Most sources reference the event with a reluctant tone, as exemplified in Secretary Donald Remy’s correspondence regarding RPC-675.

This aligns with a police investigation on the company. The case was born out of numerous hydrocarbon poisoning-related hospitalizations of employees. Victims testify the cause to have been “a company party gone out of hand”, but refused to elaborate further. 

Shortly after this, the USOC stopped supporting TerraRoll Engines. The case became a large scandal. It was also around this time that Copper Lawrence disappeared from future company records.

+++ ADDENDUM.3 // Incident “Eternal Oblivion”

||~ Incident 675-0021 “Eternal Oblivion” ||
//02-28-2021//

Amidst early 2021 Authority [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/budget-cuts budget constraints], stationed Protection Division personnel sought to utilize RPC-675’s fortune telling abilities to outperform the other Divisions. 

Survivors attest that due to the sporadic nature of RPC-675 future sight, very few participants obtained visions regarding the institutional success of the Protection Division, instead seeing personally relevant scenes. 

The OL-Site Head hypothesized that if multiple personnel entered in RPC-675 together, RPC-675 would display a scene relevant to all divers simultaneously; ergo one beneficial for the division.

Upon entering the anomaly, the participants began to act erratically. Later interviews revealed that they witnessed the same illusion; that being the previously fragmented foreign vision in its complete form. 

During the hysteria, an as of yet undetermined armament went off and ignited RPC-675. 4 personnel had not exited beforehand, and expired.
