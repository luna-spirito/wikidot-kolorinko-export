**From a trusted source:** Specialists and researchers have argued about the exact origin of the anomalous material used in Amazing! Co. products. Some deem the topic irrelevant as to whether or not the raw material comes from an Alternate Reality or from our baseline, while the insistent few see it as a crucial piece of the containment protocol puzzle. Certain anomalies are limited in their number within the baseline and knowing whether or not Amazing! Co. has begun targeting these limited resources is a crucial step in adjusting security measures. Certain products have been known to contain material present in Alternate Realities, but it has been difficult to ascertain which ones. Besides obvious signs such as finding marked packaging or Amazing! Co. outright crediting the "donator" of the material, it is nigh impossible to identify the exact Alternate Reality material originates from. Due to this, it is extremely important to screen all Amazing! Co. products for AC scale discrepancies even if they all come from the same shipment.
