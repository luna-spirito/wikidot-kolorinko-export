**Summary of recovered documents from abandoned warehouse:** A recent investigation of an abandoned Amazing! Co. Warehouse has shown multiple posters by the company addressing complaints about certain products like [[[RPC-298]]] and [[[RPC-237]]], mainly in regards to their lethality. These seem to be targeted at an unknown group that has become aware of the company and it's products. The group is named inconsistently in the materials, as is consistent with Amazing! Co spelling and grammar, known only as "the opposition food group" in the most complete form of address that has been given, providing assurance in varying forms, oftentimes seemingly unrelated to the complaints they have received, and describing the group in question as trying to push their own products.

An investigation is ongoing to determine the identity of the group that has been targeted by this new ad campaign. It remains unknown if there is an anomalous effect to these advertisements, but researchers suspect they may make identifying the group in question much more difficult. Common suspects for the group in question have included Mne's chocolates and [REDACTED], though rumors of a hithero-unknown group that promotes food of it's own have yet to be corroborated.
