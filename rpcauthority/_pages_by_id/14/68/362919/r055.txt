**Containment Protocols:** (Updated 12/2/2021) RPC-832 is contained in an opaque 30×30×10m ACS calibration chamber[[footnote]]A room located 5,200+ meters below ground, but can be exposed to sunlight through a series of reflective tubes[[/footnote]] in Site-074. Parts of the containment chamber floor can drop down into a basement and be swapped out for cleaned tiles. This second room, located below the main room, is to be cleaned by 2C clearance maintenance staff bi-weekly after testing. Maintenance staff are to be outfitted with standard hazmat suits before entering the containment unit. Extraneous flesh is to be incinerated, and cleaners are to be decontaminated before exiting the cleaning floor.

RPC-832 is to be activated bi-weekly by --a security dog--[[footnote]]See Experiment-832-008[[/footnote]] an aquarium with various fish[[footnote]]of the Teleostei class, with the species rotated each cycle[[/footnote]]. The aquarium’s specimens are to be spilled from the upper hatch of the containment unit till RPC-832 is reduced to 50m^^3^^ in volume. 3R Clearance for RPC-832 is required for direct access and activation of the anomaly, with de-manifestation rates not exceeding 25m^^3^^/s.

Six of these chambers have been constructed around Site-074 with identical setups. An animal carcass should be placed and left unattended in all cells currently not occupied by RPC-832. When a complete de-manifestation of RPC-832 occurs, it is likely to appear in one of the chambers. Waste disposal by RPC-832 will be used to determine the newly occupied chamber. If RPC-832 does not appear in any chamber, ecological researchers must monitor local wildlife patterns. If a region with ecosystems is noted to have experienced an unexplained decline in sentient life, MST November 7 “Hammer The Gamma” is to be dispatched to the newly designated hazard zone to reacquire RPC-832. 

RPC-832-S instances are to be contained in 3m^^3^^ standard containment units. The units are to be cleaned weekly, and the instances tranquilized beforehand. Under no circumstances is access to these containment units permitted without ASF personnel.

**Description:** RPC-832 is a levitating gestalt of porous soft tissue lumps, with a long proboscis-like appendage. RPC-832 utilizes its porous structure to absorb and inflate itself with surrounding gases, demonstrating a preference for inhaling oxygen but anomalously exhaling sulfur dioxide. On baseline ACS levels, the subject pulsates at a rate of 20-22 bpm depending on its mass, and constantly expands in volume by gas when inactive from 50m^^3^^ to █████m^^3^^. This rate of pulsation will increase as the ACS decreases, with a ratio of 0.01 ACS to +5 bpm. Ultrasound screening indicates multiple amorphous structures within each mass, interconnected by a network of nervous tissue that connect to black spots on its surface. It is postulated that this network functions as the subject’s brain and its spots as rudimentary eyes.

If a sentient or sapient lifeform is observed by RPC-832, its appendage will point directly towards the noticed individual. The likeness or characteristics of the observed will begin to form as an impression on RPC-832’s body before rapidly taking shape and falling off. After a period of time passes[[footnote]]Recorded periods range from 40 seconds to 9 days.[[/footnote]], RPC-832 will inject a red gaseous substance from its appendage into one of its dropped lumps. This will rapidly mutate the lump into a sentient being (hereinafter referred to as an RPC-832-S instance). Excess lumps not injected by the red gas decompose over 3-10 days.

In this state, hereafter designated the “nurturing” phase, RPC-832 exhibits an elevated pulsation rate. Recorded values range from 40 to █00 bpm, correlating with the anomaly's ability to lower ACS as the bpm rate increases. When the last of its lumps falls apart, RPC-832 will slowly fade away, de-manifesting and reemerging from the remains of a wild animal. This process increases exponentially the longer RPC-832 observes another living individual, but will occur regardless due to natural shedding of lumps.

RPC-832-S instances’ biology and behavior are derived from the lifeforms observed by RPC-832. Once fully formed, the creature will mimic the original ecosystem, to varying degrees of accuracy. RPC-832-S instances do not trigger RPC-832 to form further instances. RPC-832-S’s qualities are dependent on the following parameters:

* Period of observation
* Number of observers
* The observers’ degree of sapience
* ACS Level
All recorded instances exhibit an innate flight-or-fight response towards --human-- non-832-S sapient life and hostility towards any perceived threat to RPC-832. To note is a universal lack of reproductive organs or cell regeneration in 832-S instances.

RPC-832 will actively seek out sentient observation if it has been inactive for longer than 10-20 days.

**Discovery:** On 09/02/2015, RPC-832 was first brought to the Authority’s attention when locals in [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/report-on-the-esoteric-state-of-appalachia Cherokee, Appalachia] reported a “red mist” coming from the Great Smoky Mountains National Park, with reported sightings of a “red stoat”[[footnote]]Later designated as RPC-832-S03[[/footnote]].

**Exploration Event Timeline**

**Date:** 10/02/2015

**Subject:** Scouting suspected anomalous disturbance within the Appalachian Great Smoky mountains.

**Command:** N/A[[footnote]]Appalachian State authorities allow the arbitration of barring recording equipment from entering protected zones, citing the Appalachian Regional Development Act of 1965. 23 P.L. 25 20 Stat. 50, 1966 Enacted H. R. 1.[[/footnote]]

**11:25** - ASF team enters the National Park by the main entryway and follows beyond the unrestricted trail and deeper into the mountain range.

**12:02** - Agent Fillmore points out a vertical silhouette on top of a mountain cliff in the horizon before “propelling” itself away from view. The group moves to hike the mountain to investigate.

**12:38** - The team stands down for a few minutes to rest and hydrate. Agent Thurman advises they take a longer, less steep path, citing the unusually quiet conditions. Captain Gilmore dismisses this concern, and the team follows the same steep path.

**15:10** - Having reached the top of the mountain, the group could not find the entity Fillmore pointed out to their dismay. However, Agent Mark notices what appears to be a green Kestrel flying erratically before diving at Gilmore, missing him, and then diving back into the woods. Deliberations on an alternative path are made before the team spends an hour scouting.

**16:13** - The group prepares to move after reporting nothing significant aside from a stark absence of any animal fauna and the oddly fungal-looking plant life. As the team packs up, Agent Mark gets his foot caught in a tangle of weeds and falls off a steep cliff and into the woods. The team put up their guard before Fillmore could look down; Seeing that the drop is about 100m, Agent Evgeny Mark is presumed KIA. A goat-like figure is glimpsed jumping from within a recess in the mountain and running over to Mark’s body.

**16:25** - Agent Fillmore is helped back up onto the ridge by Thurman, who informs him about having noticed a series of small “blurs” as he was pulling him out. As he says this, both agents simultaneously perceive said blur for a split moment, as it jumps across a tree gap. Captain Gilmore formally declares early withdrawal from the mission.

**17:37** - Agent Thurman asks the team to stop to survey the mountains’ relation to their trails. The forest is significantly dimmer than it should be, and the trail they followed is no longer there. Before an argument ensues, 5-7 squirrel-like entities attack the squad from above. They are quickly knifed down and neutralized, at which point they are deemed too malformed and mutated to be [[[RPC-286]]] instances. A tremor evidently surges from swarms of entities rushing towards them, at which point they run aimlessly. Captain Gilmore uses an Authority distress signal device to call upon local Protectorate rescue personnel.

**17:58** - The team enter a cave in attempt to lose the swarm. Running in a panic, Fillmore falls into a recess in the ground, the two cover him by shooting the incoming hostiles. The Agent tries to get his bearings, but is confronted by a faceless, malformed goat with a large, extended abdomen. The entity rushes towards Fillmore, who quickly maneuvers out of its way, causing it to ram into a rock. Seeing its brains splatter across the stone, Fillmore assumes it died, and encroaches on the creature for a better look. Before he realizes it, the thing cranes its neck backward, shoving a horn into Fillmore's side. The Agent bends over, likely winded, giving the deformed goat just enough time to ram into his head, knocking him out cold. The two agents escape before they're completely overwhelmed.

**19:12** - Agent Thurman and Captain Gilmore hide within a mountain cave, with Agent Patrick Fillmore, having been lost in the chase, being presumed KIA by the rest of his team. The duo take the opportunity to rest, drink, and refill from the cave waters. This prompts them to rest for an hour, with each member taking turns keeping watch while the others rest.

**20:55** - Captain Gilmore is restless and, suspecting rescue won’t arrive in due time, orders they continue their expedition till they find an exit back. Gilmore signals Thurman to dim their lights and adopt caution.

**23:25** - A glimmer of red light is seen at a distance, to which the duo remain cautious as they move towards the source. The duo crouch behind the bushes. Revealed beyond it is a plethora of mutated animals encircling and staring into the light, including Elks, Hares, Stoats, Hellbenders, Barred Owls, etc. Most animals are unfamiliar and amalgams of one another. At the center is a hairless bear standing on its hind feet, with an exposed, torn-open chest and head, and large, unidentifiable viscera illuminating the swarm. Soon after, two unrecognizable entities pull Agent Fillmore by his pants up and towards a large levitating mound of flesh. Three entities join to direct Fillmore’s head toward the mound, which warps and melts the environment, likely an ACS disturbance. After 30 minutes, the mound then drops 5 of its lumps and injects them with red liquid. The mounds of flesh warp and distend, forming legs, arms, and eventually standing up wholly formed as the second Patrick Fillmore. The entity then looked down at its double, grabbed a rock, and beat its head to pulp. After the act is done, in a slow but deliberate gesture, it points its finger at the two remaining survivors. In a voice that was not Patrick Fillmore’s, it bellowed, “Come forward!”

**00:01** - This prompts a horde to pin down and bring the two living agents towards the circle to repeat the ritual. Two minutes pass before an Authority helicopter lowers itself as an onboard Protectorate indiscriminately opens fire at the horde. Protectorate rescue agents retrieve the remaining scouting team and pull them up into the vehicle. A red Stoat had clawed its way up to inside the helicopter, which is swiftly shoved into an ammunition container.
[[/div]]
MST November-7 “Hammer The Gamma” is deployed with incendiary weapons to blast through RPC-832 instances to contain RPC-832. At which point, it is discovered that the anomaly de-manifests upon confrontation, teleporting further north. The anomaly manifests a second time before capture in Shining Rock Wilderness.

Following the anomalies containment, uncontrolled fires deforested 142 km² of protected area, sparking a minor diplomatic incident between the Authority and the Appalachian State Government. In a site breach of the arbitration provision in the Appalachian Regional Development Act, the Authority was expected to compensate locals with a $██████ reparations check and permission to use the anomaly to make more RPC-832-S’s for commercial hunting purposes. The Authority obliged the financial demand, but denied the subsequent request to use the anomaly for recreational purposes.

[[# addendumi]]**Addendum I:** On 08/16/2021 after routine feeding, RPC-832 has not produced an instance as expected. On the second day, a lump fell out. This continued for 9 days, after which no RPC-832-S instances spawned. Diagnostics have shown minute fluctuations in ACS in the chamber, below normal levels.

**Incident-832-1:** On 12/1/2021 1:12AM, several RPC-832-S instances were breached out of containment. In most instances, attempts were made to flee and avoid contact with ASF. RPC-832-S14 and RPC-832-S37 actively coordinated towards reaching RPC-832’s containment zone. According to one report, RPC-832-S37 was observed entering RPC-832-S14’s nostril opening, appearing to investigate its internals before making a jerky motion, causing the instance to cover it in dense phlegm. The aforementioned instances were terminated by ASF personnel, at which point RPC-832-S37 armed itself with a pistol and shot while maneuvering within RPC-832-S14’s swarm of appendages. The incident caused 12 casualties, 3 deaths, and led to RPC-832 escaping containment.

Senior Researcher Rayyan Palmer was found dead in RPC-832-S37’s containment room. On his person were a scalpel, a medical drill, a journal, and 3 plastic pouches filled with thermite. Autopsy has shown several self-inflicted wounds on the left shoulder and arm, a corneal ulcer in the right eye, and the cause of death was determined to be a hemorrhagic stroke halfway through an auto-trepanation procedure.
