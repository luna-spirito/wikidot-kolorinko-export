Members of a heretical Nihilist sect have been slowly working their way through Nucorp's hierarchy. Their numbers are not many - fewer than a hundred, perhaps fewer than a dozen. Some have been of the faith all their lives, others were indoctrinated due to their key placement within the company. With access to Nucorp's advanced technology, the followers of the Engineer seek to gain an edge over their Nihilist enemies and ensure their place in the sun.
