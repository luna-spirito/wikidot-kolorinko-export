On 20/06/2026 at 5:39 EST, the above image was sent to several Authority terminals,[[footnote]]It is worth noting that all terminals pertaining to MST Echo-9 and Hotel-11 were hacked to display this image.[[/footnote]] predominantly in NORTHCOM sites. Featured are the logos of Amazing Co. and NuCorp, as well as what appears to be a personification of the Kabushiki Kawaii logo, all of which are imposed on top of the Authority logo. Several documents produced by the aforementioned Groups of Interest are superimposed on the Authority logo.

It is unknown what the logo's purpose is or what it is meant to represent. A preliminary hypothesis suggests this is a provocation tactic directed at the Authority and its operations, but Protection infiltrators report that the groups of interest depicted express confusion about this incident, suggesting malicious intent from a third party.
