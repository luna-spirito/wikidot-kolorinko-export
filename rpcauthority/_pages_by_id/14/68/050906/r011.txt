+++ Abstract

Model blueprint created by //Dr. Melania N. Camilleri// and //Dr. Ivan S. Barkov// in [[[Site-070]]]. An autonomous bronze guardian made in the female form. These models are surrounded by floral growth and capable of transmuting dead organic matter into various species of flora. These entities are colloquially referred to as “Little Nikushas.” Nearly indestructible and unfailingly obedient, they assist facility personnel in both sentry duties and domestic work.

RPC-846's basic directive is as follows:
> //You will be the guardian of our garden. Protect all that is beautiful, that is your directive.//

------
+++ Safe-Handling and Usage
Their main base of operation is the West Wing greenhouse, where their creation and maintenance are managed by Dr. Ivan S. Barkov. Should an RPC-846 instance model require repair or modification, refer to the instructional addendum later in the document.

Each model’s bio-mechanical system requires both glucose and nitrogen for metabolic function, allowing it to process virtually any organic matter as sustenance. Its artificial mind will deteriorate and cease cognition without auditory stimuli. Thus, the maintenance of the item's internal music box is of top priority. 

The RPC-846 models are assigned to serve personnel within the [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]Suppression and Sustainment[[/span]] Department. Their capacity for [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]higher thought and emotional experience[[/span]] is to remain classified and concealed from the general Authority. Each unit is authorized to bear and operate any [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]standard-issue or custom weapon[[/span]] provided by supervising personnel, with the explicit exception of [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]memetic and/or psychotronic-class weaponry[[/span]].

+++ Description

A bio-mechanical thaumaturgic automaton engineered by Senior Researchers Dr. Melania Camilleri and Dr. Ivan Barkov. The original RPC-846 model was made after the image of assistant researcher [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]Nika Petrova[[/span]] to serve as her companion. Since then, derivative models have been made and customized for additional tasks. 

RPC-846 was constructed using material and analytical samples derived from [[[RPC-022]]],[[footnote]] **RPC-022**
[[include component:image-enlarge name= http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/rpc-022/RPC.png|align=left|width=100px|caption= RPC-022 on its pedestal.]]
**Object Class:** Beta-Yellow (Utility)

RPC-022 is a late Roman Empire-era marble statue of an unnamed soldier. It is animate and sapient, and is capable of expert combat and speech. It expresses strong loyalty to the Authority, which it refers to as the "Vanguard of Hercules."
 [[/footnote]] [[[RPC-224]]],[[footnote]] **RPC-224**
[[include component:image-block name= http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/rpc-224/Crcle.jpeg|align=left|width=100px|caption= Manuscript for the Magic Circle of Infinity]]
**Object Class:** Alpha-Yellow

RPC-224 is a CD of George Crumb's Makrokosmos 1 with the words “For Plato” written in cursive writing by a felt tip pen.  Any subject listening to the Act 2 Song 8, titled "The Magic Circle of Infinity will start bending structures and objects after 5 seconds or more of staring.
 [[/footnote]] and [[[RPC-550]]].[[footnote]] **RPC-550**
[[include component:image-enlarge name= http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-550/550.jpg|align=left|width=100px|caption= A rat parasitized by RPC-550-A.]]
**Object Class:** Beta-Red

**Abstract:** RPC-550 is the sapient, animate cadaver of a female child. Different species of small flora can be seen extruding from the entity’s body. RPC-550‘s eyes are filled with a black, acidic substance. When a subject makes physical contact with RPC-550, a random species of flora, designated RPC-550-A, will begin to grow out of the inside of the subject until the subject is terminated.
 [[/footnote]] Its shell is made of a copper-alloy that's been self-nullified, making it virtually indestructible. Beneath its shell is a mechanical endoskeleton overlaid with synthetic muscle tissue composed primarily of starch.

The biological component of RPC-846 originates from the blood of RPC-550. Each instance possesses a circulatory system filled with synthetic blood, synthesized from a universal RPC-550-A substance. Under the effects of the //Scholar’s Influence//, the biohazardous properties of this substance are neutralized, allowing for safe internal function. It gains sustenance through absorbing dead organic matter; the residue of its feed later blooming into various species of floral growth.

RPC-846’s brain analogue [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]operates through a thaumaturgic engine housed within a modified music disc, which continuously[[/span]] relays a melodic hum. This melody incorporates the memetic resonance of RPC-224. The music enables the synthetic muscle fibers to contract and manipulate their bronze shell with fluid, lifelike motion. It is also with this system that RPC-846 is able to communicate with humans through rudimentary speech.

Each RPC-846 instance’s intelligence processor is maintained by RPC-846-1, the central mainframe governing all units. RPC-846-1 is currently in an inert state and is encircled by a dense proliferation of floral growth originating from the entity itself, forming a small indoor garden.

[[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]When properly nurtured, RPC-846 exhibits accelerated cognitive growth, including emotional expression, higher-order thought, and pain[[/span]].[[footnote]] This phenomenon is fully memetic, as RPC-846 has no neural tissue to facilitate it. [[/footnote]] [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]These capabilities are attributed to floral growth derived from the brain tissue of Researcher Petrova, which has been implanted into every RPC-846 unit, allowing it to connect to the RPC-846-1 mainframe[[/span]]. [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]With this system in mind, RPC-846 is able to mentally mature the same as a human[[/span]].

The flora that grows around RPC-846 depends on the model's directive. These flora bloom at a rapid pace and will die after their current directive has either failed, changed, or completed. Likewise, the [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]music disc[[/span]] inside RPC-846 will also change according to the directive.

+++ Addendum 1 - On Diagnostic Concerns

[[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]At its most basic form, RPC-846's personality is identical to Researcher Petrova's[[/span]]. [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]It is likely that, if left to their own devices, the models will recreate their original creator--a consequence of RPC-846's reliance on RPC-846-1[[/span]]. [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]There have been concerns within the Engineering Department that their sapience will cause trouble within Site-070. This concern has been addressed, as recent models are affected by the diagnostic phrases[[/span]]. [[span style="background:#aaaaaa;"]]Should the diagnosis not work, a low dose of Class R Amnestics will suffice[[/span]].
