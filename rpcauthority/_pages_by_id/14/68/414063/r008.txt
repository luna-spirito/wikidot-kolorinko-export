+++ **Containment Protocols** 

The primary RPC-580 samples are handled by the biological research staff at [[[Site-008]]][[footnote]] Intensive Care Hub, General Hospital & Research Facility. [[/footnote]] for storage. A total of ██ clones of RPC-580-1 were produced by the Biology Department between 2011 - 2021 for testing purposes. No further cloning is permitted without the explicit approval of Site-008 Director Dr. Wilhelm Grau and Dr. Johan Mustermann. 

The 12 surviving RPC-580-1 instances created by the Authority were transferred from Site-008 to [[[Site-026]]][[footnote]] Containment, Humanoid [[/footnote]] to be adopted by the personnel stationed there. Resources may be utilized for the welfare, life support, and protection of these instances under the site’s Adolescent Care system.

Investigation of the whereabouts of PoI-4883 and the remaining RPC-580-1 instances scattered across the world is still ongoing.

+++ **Description** 

RPC-580 is an incomplete genomic template of //Homo sapiens// created by the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/church-of-malthus-hub Church of Malthus] in //Waisenhaus Nr. 9//. Genomic analysis suggests the source specimen was a male in the Haplogroup R-M269,[[footnote]] Phylogenetically classified as R1b1a1b, it signifies the haploid group of Western Europe. RPC-580 is specifically theorized to be of English descent. [[/footnote]] though large segments of the sequence are missing. The template appears to have been purposefully made to be modular.[[footnote]] A “modular genome” refers to an artificially structured genetic template in which segments of DNA are organized into interchangeable units, allowing for targeted insertion, removal, or substitution without destabilizing the entire sequence. [[/footnote]]

The template is barely suitable for complete human development. When issued to standard cloning equipment, the resulting organism (designated RPC-580-1) exhibits severe multisystem developmental failure. Observed characteristics include: oculocutaneous albinism, ███████, incomplete organ redundancy, stunted physical and emotional development, and a compromised immune system;[[footnote]] RPC-580-1 instances display an abnormal immune profile characterized by the absence or dysfunction of cytotoxic immune responses, including Natural Killer cells and cytotoxic T lymphocytes, resulting in near-total vulnerability to viral pathogens. [[/footnote]] often leading to more fatal symptoms. Without life support, RPC-580-1 segments are bound to expire early.

Despite these physiological disabilities, both male and female RPC-580-1 instances demonstrate exceptional IQ scores and can quickly learn and adapt to physical and environmental challenges. Regardless of how they are raised, children 3-5 years of age will quickly acquiesce language and invariably speak in a clinical manner, and have an innate understanding of their own biology.[[footnote]] This proficiency varies. The clinical culture of Site-026 may be a contributing factor. [[/footnote]] 

It was initially assumed that RPC-580-1 instances are incapable of experiencing pain. However, microelectrode measurements confirm that nociceptive pathways, including both Aδ and C fiber activity, are intact and fully responsive to external stimuli. Instead, the CoM has selectively suppressed the instance's ability to express or emote their emotional response to pain, effectively muting their ability to register and express suffering.

+++ **Discovery** 

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/ricmod/Orphans.png|caption= RPC-580-1 clones studying in Waisenhaus Nr. 9 {{(circa. 1960)}}|width=300px|align=right]]

RPC-580 was acquired from a villa in ██████, Austria called Waisenhaus Nr. 9. It was an orphanage funded by [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/gard-hub GARD][[footnote]] The German Anomalous Division. Nazi Germany's former veiled agency. [[/footnote]] in 1940 to create the next generation of Übermensch through eugenics, psychological, and physical conditioning. The program officially dissolved alongside the Nazi Party in 1945, with the villa and the facilities inside later being bought by Prof. Albrecht Thiel, the founder of a sect of the CoM called  ‘//the Lost Children of Malthus//.’ Records on the events between abandonment by GARD and acquisition by the Authority are fragmented.

It was not until July 2010 that Authority Central Intelligence became aware of Waisenhaus Nr. 9 through a Malthusian whistleblower, prompting a subsequent raid on the villa. Upon entry, the facility was found completely abandoned. Personnel were absent, and a significant portion of documentation had been removed in the days leading up to the operation. The only entity recovered on-site was a single RPC-580-1 instance, believed to have been unintentionally abandoned.

A significant amount of charred biomatter has been discovered buried around the perimeter of the villa, with each sample containing partial genetic sequences of RPC-580. However, these sequences are heavily degraded and cluttered by foreign, inhuman genetic material.

+++ **Addendum 580.1:** [[span style="font-size: 90%; font-weight: lighter!important;"]] Psychological assessment [[/span]]

RPC-580-1 instances are born with behavior coded in by the Church of Malthus. They have a preference for numerical designations (e.g., Registered Phenomena Codes) over assigned personal names, and exhibit heightened suggestibility, often accepting instructions or information without critical thought.

Segments compensate their inability to express pain through self-diagnosis with notable accuracy. This nature may be cause for long-term trauma within the instances if left untreated.

**Overview:**


[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/ricmod/ProfessorThiel.png|caption= Prof. Albrecht Thiel {{(circa. 1972)}}|width=200px|align=right]]


PoI-4883, “Prof. Albrecht Thiel,” is a high-ranking member of the Church of Malthus and the founder of the sect known as the Lost Children of Malthus, a sect originating from //Darwin’s Hand//[[footnote]] Darwin's Hand is a prominent sect split from the original Malthusiae cult. They seek to replicate the biological processes in organisms they deem superior to augment the evolution of humanity. [[/footnote]] that rose to prominence following the initiation of the Waisenhaus Nr. 9 project in 1945. He served as the sole director of the project until his disappearance shortly before the Authority raid in 2010.

The full extent of PoI-4883’s activities remains unknown; however, it is highly likely that he personally oversaw the mass cloning, experimentation, and disposal of the ████ known RPC-580-1 instances housed within Waisenhaus Nr. 9. RPC-580-1 are therefore designated as 'disposable humanoid clone', and are used as the primary subject of human experiment within the various sects of Malthus.

Like other senior figures within the Church of Malthus, PoI-4883 is believed to have undergone extensive bodily modification to extend his own lifespan. The individual has also been observed with the ability to quickly adjust to his height and muscle mass, making him a dangerous unarmed combatant.

The exact history of PoI-4883 before 1945 is unknown, as no record of an ‘Albrecht Marcus Thiel’ exists in any official documents.

**To my dearest Johan**,

This is an abrupt letter, considering your young age. Yet I know that one day you will read these words, and that the paths the RPC Authority charts will bring us together again.

I know you have many questions and suspicions about your true origins. Let me put them to rest: though you certainly were a part of Waisenhaus Nr. 9, and I never intended for you to be a DHC. You are not alone in that. Lost children are scattered across the world, living ordinary lives. You can try to look for your lost brothers and sisters, if you want. But among them, it’s only you, who I believe, will find me.

For decades, I have kept a secret from the wider Church of Malthus. An experiment, if you will, but not one of science or empiricism. Rather, it is an experiment born of my intuition, a test of the nature of fate itself. A foolish one, perhaps, yet I have pursued it nonetheless. I believe that fate will guide you to this letter, and that it will guide us to meet again.

Are you aware of how our Church was founded? It began as nothing more than a simple experiment. An idea conceived by Thomas Malthus, passed down to his disciples, and left to take root in the minds of those who followed them. Over time, it festered, growing beyond its origin into something monstrous.

Now it is broken, a distorted reflection of what it once was. Its chapters turn on one another, each convinced they alone are Malthus’s true successor. And yet, they remain bound by the same blood that sustains them. It is the experiment that began it all: an eternal legacy that will remain pure no matter how many times it was desecrated.

You are a continuation of that experiment, as were your brothers and sisters.

Yours, always, Albrecht
