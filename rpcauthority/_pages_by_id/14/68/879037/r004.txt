One of the biggest tells of an Amazing Co. product apart from its logo is the rough, poorly translated text that decorate its packaging. 
No matter the language it's in the text is always off, a detail that has made it difficult to track this GOI and one that the Authority has quickly dismissed as manufacturing blunders, but with every collected anomalous item a pattern started to emerge from its titles, rulebooks, instructions and the rare safety warning.

Every misspelled word, every awkward sentence, every strange grammatical slip on these anomalous products were more than cheap packaging, they were a code hidden in plain sight. 
The errors begin to form patterns, turning bargain-bin labels into secret messages. Only questions are; what are these messages saying, who are they from? and who are they for?

Another marketing memetic agent? 
A whistle-blower on the design team?
Some cognitohazardous remnant of whatever language Amazing Co. operates with?

There's only one thing to expect with this trail and it's the same thing to always remember when it comes to Amazing Co. 
The 'C' in "Co." does not stand for consistency.
