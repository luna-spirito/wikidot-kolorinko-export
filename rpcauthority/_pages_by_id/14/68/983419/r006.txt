------

**Containment Protocols:** Authority web-crawler (AmazingFan-035) is to monitor social media for any mentions of RPC-759 or grand-scale pickle/cucumber promotions. All RPC-759 instances found in physical venues are to be cremated and disposed of per standard anomalous biomatter guidelines. A single 749ml "Especial Edision" jar is kept in cold storage at [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/site-036 Site-036]'s freezer for study.

Any subjects in late-stage addiction are to be humanely euthanized at range, and the resulting RPC-759 instances disposed of. UNAAC has agreed to financially reimburse affected families from their Anomalous Disasters Aid fund. Personnel found to have bought RPC-759 from official Amazing! Co. channels will face severe punitive infractions and demotion, if not outright termination of their contract. Amazing! Co.'s Catalog(ue) N°144 is banned from staff access below L-4R clearance.

------

[[include component:image-block name=pickle.jpg|caption=RPC-759's Catalog(ue) N°144 Advertisement.]]

**Description:** An [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/amazingco-hub Amazing! Co.] line of red, brine-based pickled cucumbers. Ingesting one or more pickles results in a sudden spike in cortisol, a release of adrenaline and testosterone, a tripling in blood pressure, as well as increased aggression and clouding of judgment. These effects are attributed to "carpcaibsin",[[footnote]]Which Amazing! Co. advertises as being harvested from "meanie beanies".[[/footnote]] an anomalous, sweet-tasting capsaicin-like chemical.

RPC-759 is highly addictive, and continued consumption will result in deformations and the atrophy of the limbic system. While effective at suppressing anger following its initial outburst, atrophy will lead to an inability to express negative emotions unless through ingestion of RPC-759. Withdrawal symptoms include low blood pressure and panic attacks, and subjects may resort to murder or theft as a means to acquire RPC-759 when commercial means are unavailable.

Continued ingestion will permanently taint the skin red, cause profuse sweating, and spread welts outwards from the stomach across the body. Hair will fall out, and psychotic breakdowns may result in self-mutilating or heart failure. Eventually, the subject's limbs and head will begin to inflate as sweat accumulates inside the skin and organs. They will attack anyone on sight until expiration, usually through complete vessel implosion and heart detonation. New, sweat-brined RPC-759 instances can be found inside the subject's body.

------

**Discovery:** As part of a large promotional event, Amazing! Co. began to partner with veiled content creators for the review of their newest product lines, amongst which was RPC-759. Due to its individual-level threat, this was not placed at the top of containment and disinformation operatives until subject addiction reached a point of no-return, resulting in multiple explosion-born casualties.
