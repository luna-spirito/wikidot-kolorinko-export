**From a trusted source:** In its dedicated frenzy for interreality research, NuCorp does not ensure the safety of its employees and the wider world solely through radiation shields and protective suits. The company has a trained legal department that is in charge of charting the intricate legal world of the unveiled community, battling against disputes, protecting invention rights and securing fair pay for freelancers and contractors. NuCorp is aware that without its legal division many of its projects would not have survived to see the light of day, let alone allow the company to profit from them.

But, not everything is as it seems within the halls of these departments. NuCorp cannot afford for something as inconsequential as red tape or laws to slow down humanity's development, whether it comes from our reality - or someone else's. To make sure that they have the best of the best, NuCorp utilizes its breakthrough to modify and equip select members of their legal team with anomalous means available to them. Certain members do not even originate from our baseline, coming from realities with logics and laws of their own which they tirelessly work to explain and translate to their new employers.

Is this approach dangerous? Most likely.
But is it legal? Most definitely.
