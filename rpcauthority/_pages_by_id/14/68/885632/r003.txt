During the "30th Decennial Research World's Fair" hosted at Site-067, Nucorp was invited by the Authority to participate and showcase Patent NT-211: A 360-degree digital camera attached to a modified antenna and a refraction device introduced by the delegation as a "Maximillian Mirror", capable of dislocating the viewpoint of the camera backwards in time on a fixed position.

After showcasing a few collages of photographs taken as far back as 1695 (Authority supervisors forbade in-site use of NT-211), the presentation encountered technical difficulties when the files from 10/5/1869 to 27/6/1869 were found to be blank. The delegation made no explanation of the incident.
