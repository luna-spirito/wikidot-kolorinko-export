**Containment Protocols:** RPC-694 is currently contained in an inlet within OL-Site-694 located by the shores of Inamuragasaki, Japan. The site entrance is sealed in moisture-resistant concrete, interior clad in 20×20×2 cm lead panels. An extra layer of 3 cm thick foam glass soundproofing is added on top of these panels.

Personnel within 2 kilometers of RPC-694 must wear noise-cancelling headsets at all times. Those found not wearing them at any point are to be relieved of their position and transferred off-site. If any personnel are caught not reporting on this containment violation, both the insubordinate and those in violation will be terminated from employment. In the case of RPC-694 affliction, persons must be quarantined for 6 hours before they are released. In the case of containment failure, MST Alpha-2 "Maritime Praesidio Subvehi" are to be dispatched towards the location of the Authority positional receiver embedded within RPC-694.

RPC-694 entrants are to be considered lost.
[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-694/P-00694|caption=RPC-694 initial manifestation inside the Luzon rainforest in Ditawini, Philippines]]
**Description:** RPC-694 is a 10.6m long cadaver of a male //Megaptera novaeangliae//[[footnote]]Humpback whale[[/footnote]] in its second stage of decomposition, estimated to be 12 years old at the time of death. The whale is branded under its left fin with what appears to be an identification number (1170883) and an unknown script.

RPC-694 anomalously manifests itself on shores in human-populated areas on the Pacific Ocean coastline. Shortly after manifestation, it will produce a ~<130dB[[footnote]]Equivalent amplitude to an operating jet engine[[/footnote]] wail for 6-21 seconds. If less than 10 people are lured to its proximity within 4 hours, it will reproduce its call. This effect has been documented to reach up to a radius of 1.8 km.

RPC-694 constantly emits alpha radiation at 30 mSv/hr, as well as a low-frequency hum from within itself. This hum has a Lvl. 2 Siren memetic effect on those exposed to it, causing an obsessive compulsion to that sound. 

Affected individuals will gather around RPC-694 and auscultate it. They would later express the source sound as dampened yet euphonic from within RPC-694, prompting discussion on how to further enhance it, and conclusively who is most suited to enter RPC-694. Once a subject has entered the anomaly, the group covers RPC-694 to auscultate it and the person within. After 2-3 minutes, RPC-694 proceeds to swell until it violently bursts, terminating everyone within a 2 meter range.

After approx. 24 days, RPC-694 will re-manifest on a different shore. Confirmed cities where RPC-694 manifested before containment are as follows:
* Ishinomaki, Japan
* Jiangpingzhen, China
* Los Osos, USA
* Bagnell Ferry, USA
* Inamuragasaki, Japan

**Discovery:** On 25/02/2012, during a recreational expedition by civilians around the Luzon rainforest, RPC-694 was discovered in the village of Ditawini 15 meters from the ocean shore. Multiple images and videos of the event were posted on social media, with one live stream showing a man enthusiastically agreeing to enter RPC-694's mouth while equipped with a lavalier microphone. They then auscultate RPC-694 as the microphone picks up their ovations and a continuous low-frequency noise. The video ends with the anomaly bloating until it bursts, subsequently cutting the visual feed. Notably, the live feed picked up aquatic sounds for 10 seconds after the explosion before disconnecting.
