**Containment Protocols:** As of 02/20/2007, RPC-462 is held in a 5m^^3^^ steel-reinforced concrete chamber at Site-036's Heavy Containment Wing under Level 3 clearance. During regular hours, 4 ASF personnel armed with high caliber ammunition must routinely inspect the containment's structural integrity; any compromises found are to be reported to maintenance staff for repair. Personnel assigned to RPC-462 are to don AET-specialized uniforms[[footnote]]These uniforms are made from Teflon and silicone.[[/footnote]] as to prevent clinging onto the anomaly's semi-adhesive surface. Consumption of RPC-462's excess matter is strictly prohibited. 

In the event of a breach, the Heavy Containment Wing is to be placed under lockdown until RPC-462 has been secured. ASF personnel are tasked routing a mixture of water and soap to the sprinkler system and activating it as to temporarily incapacitate RPC-462, allowing containment.

**Description:** RPC-462 is a 2.1 meter tall humanoid entity composed entirely of consolidated rock candy. Its body consists of a vivid polychromatic arrangement of faceted crystals of various colors such as reds, yellows, oranges, greens, blues, and greys. RPC-462 has no visible facial features, or any internal anatomy.  Due to its structure, RPC-462 is very slow with its maximum walking speed clocking at .002 m/s. 

When moving, RPC-462's surface will emit a low-level acoustic crackling as a result of microfracturing followed by rapid recrystallization. Damages upon RPC-462's form of any kind result in the entity becoming inactive until its natural recrystallization regenerates all sustained damage. Its durability is comparable to reinforced granite, allowing for substantial defense against melee weaponry and small projectiles of lower caliber. 

Upon noticing a target, RPC-462 will gradually approach it, attacking once in striking range. Due to RPC-462's semi-adhesive surface, all physical contact results in the subject attaching to the entity, allowing for RPC-462 to strangle the subject.  Chemically inert materials prove effective for handling RPC-462, allowing for safe physical interaction with the entity.

**Discovery:** On 06/09/2006, an abandoned confectionery factory in █████, Colorado was discovered by a group of urban explorers who were travelling through a nearby forest. Upon entering, they documented their excursion via video camera. Multiple recordings were shot, mostly of vintage factory machinery with occasional commentary. During their exploration, they stumbled upon various posters and documents mentioning the "Greatest Candy Man That'll Rock Your Socks Off, The Rock Candy Sweetheart!". The group reported having fled the premise after encountering RPC-462 to a local police department, leading to Authority attention.

Mobile Specialized Team Charlie-9 ("Countryside Hypocrites") located the factory after following the group's trail by the countryside. Charlie-9 personnel then conducted an investigation of the complex to locate RPC-462. 

For the first three hours of exploration, RPC-462 did not appear, but instead Charlie-9 discovered various merchandise and banners portraying the entity in a promotional light, being drawn in the "Rubberhose" animation style. RPC-462 was located where it was last sighted and contained without incident. It was then transported to Site-036. RPC-462 was transported to Site-036 for further study and permanent containment.

**Addendum.462.01:**
[[collapsible show="+ Open Experiment Log" hide="- Close Log"]]
[[div class="site-doc" style="background: var(--background-4); border: var(--thick-border); padding: 1px 15px;"]]
**Overview:** RPC-462 was successfully placed within a 3m^^3^^ testing chamber. Before the entity could activate, a CO,,2,, laser was fired in the chamber, cutting off a piece of RPC-462. CSD-5541 was instructed to enter the chamber and acquire the piece, then exit. The subject performed this task swiftly and obeyed all orders as expected. 

CSD-5541 was then instructed to consume the chunk. 

-----

**12:45 PM:** CSD-5541 consumes RPC-462 chunk. No effects reported.

**12:50 PM:** Loud gurgling can be heard inside CSD-5541's stomach from the observation chamber. Subject reports extreme pain throughout their digestive tract.

**1:05 PM:** CSD-5541 collapses onto the ground, holding their stomach. Continued reports of extreme pain, now mixed with nausea and migraines. 

**1:10 PM:** The subject reports a sudden outburst of euphoria and begins laughing.

**1:15 PM:** CSD-5541's body begins crackling comparably to RPC-462 in tone. The subject's laughter substantially grows in volume.

**1:20 PM:** After one last laugh, CSD-5541's body detonates into thousands of rock candies of varying colors, ultimately terminating them.

The remains of CSD-5541 were gathered by personnel and stored in an Alpha-class locker in Site-036's Light Containment Wing. 

------
**Note:** It appears that despite being composed of nonanomalous materials, the effects post-consumption couldn't be more bizarre. From here on out, consumption of chunks derived from RPC-462 is strictly prohibited. This protocol has already been approved by the Head Researcher.

Signed,
Dr. █████.

**Addendum.462.02:**
[[collapsible show="+ Open Experiment Log" hide="- Close Log"]]
[[div class="site-doc" style="background: var(--background-4); border: var(--thick-border); padding: 1px 15px;"]]
**Overview:** To test RPC-462's semi-adhesive surface, a mixture of water and soap was routed to the containment's sprinkler system in an attempt to neutralize its adhesion. Upon activation, RPC-462 was doused in the liquid and suffered enough trauma from the sprinkler's pressure to enter an inactive state. A drone was sent into the chamber and made physical contact with RPC-462, resulting in it getting stuck despite the solvent liquid still present on its surface. The experiment was concluded a failure.

-----

**Aftermath:** RPC-462 activated and crushed the drone using its arms. It henceforth moved to the southwesternmost wall and repeatedly struck it until it breached containment. Stationed ASF personnel attempted to subdue the entity, only to miss >95% of all shots. (View Breach Report "482-01" for more information.)

BREACH REPORT: 462-01

----

On 02/18/2007 at 7:08 PM, RPC-462 breached containment for approximately thirty minutes in part due to its aggressive nature and durability, and was contained with some difficulty by ASF personnel. The entity was held in temporary containment before damages were repaired in 2 days.

RPC-462's containment protocols have been updated to include higher grade walling consisting of steel-reinforced concrete. 

**Approximate casualties from incident:** 0.
