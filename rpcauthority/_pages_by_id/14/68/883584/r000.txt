At 3:00am, 18/05/26, a warehouse in █████, ██████████ was the subject of noise complaints from nearby residents. The complaint described what seemed to be screaming children, cats or some mix of the two. The screaming was also described as "in unison" and didn't end till thirty minutes later. Upon reaching the location, Authority personnel found no trace of Kabushiki Kawaii operatives or victims and the case was subsequently closed. Roughly five days later, a similar complaint was filed by the same people in the same location over the same issue.
