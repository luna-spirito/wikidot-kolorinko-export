------

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-578 is to be contained within a standard large containment unit fitted with impact-resistant glass connecting an observation deck, and RPC-578 is to be located along the opposite wall. RPC-578 must be placed on a standard large pallet and secured onto it using no less than 3 pallet safety straps to permit safe relocation of RPC-578 via forklift. The aforementioned straps must be: 
* secured over the top of the frame of RPC-578,
* secured across the 5cm dimension of the frame, and
* distributed equidistantly from each other to maintain a consistent secure hold.

No less than --three-- ten Class A-2 amnestics are to be placed within an emergency amnestics locker located within the observation deck. 

In the event of unauthorized RPC-578-1 exposure, ASF personnel are to be immediately notified and briefed on the nature of the containment breach. They are then to equip Encapsulated Level A Hazmat suits and are authorized to administer only Class A-2 amnestics to the appropriate RPC-578-A instances. Instances must be handled in a calm and consoling manner in order to elongate the time until egress. Amnesticized RPC-578-A instances are to be quarantined for a period of 12 hours since the last observation of remaining signs of RPC-578-1, and are subsequently to be removed of RPC-578-A classification.

Instances of RPC-578-2 are to be handled using any tool that grants personnel at least 12 feet of separation. An 8-meter-wide large shutter door is to be installed in the RPC-578 containment chamber to connect to another standard large containment unit for the purposes of storing instances of RPC-578-2. This chamber requires Level 3 access under the sole purpose of storing additional instances.

Testing of RPC-578 is not permitted without written approval from Level 4 personnel.

**Description:** RPC-578 is the designation for a metal frame measuring 230cm by 146cm by 28.8cm in dimension, with a 34cm moulding. X-ray fluorescence testing indicates that RPC-578 is composed of 99.7% tungsten metal. Weld marks cover a seam along the middle of RPC-578, indicating a previous fusion of two frames into one. RPC-578 displays signs of consistent degradation and surface contamination, although from what source has not been ascertained; the surface of RPC-578 is covered with what appears to be the residue from tape and other adhesive equipment, and consistent scoring and gouging is observed across RPC-578.

The hazardous nature of RPC-578 prevents the thorough cleaning of its surface. 

RPC-578 is braced with 4 black circumferential clamps made of PA66-GF30 composite[[footnote]]It is not known for what purpose a temperature-resistant nylon plastic was chosen to brace RPC-578. Its advantage in maintaining structural stability under high static load suggests properties in RPC-578 that have to date not currently manifested. Research is ongoing.[[/footnote]] on its left and right sides. 12 colored wires cover one side of the vertical surface of RPC-578, constrained by and/or emerging from its clamps, many of which are frayed and splay outward. Testing has confirmed that none of these wires are live. RPC-578 is mounted on four black steel diagonally-strutted gusset brackets fixed at 90 degrees to its base, and extend outward by 42cm to allow it to stand upright.



Despite appearing to be man-made, no fingerprint residue has been identified on RPC-578. The only intelligible marking on RPC-578 consists of simplified Chinese characters printed on white tape. The exact meaning of which is hard to ascertain, but provisionally translates to
> PREVENT TAMPERING - SWOLLEN ORIENTAL NET-HOLE 

[[div style="text-align: center;"]]
or
[[/div]]


> PREVENT TAMPERING - TIGHT ORIENTAL NET-ORIFICE

Given the word choice, it is not certain whether this is referring to RPC-578 or to an individual.

------


[[include component:image-block | align=right| width=300px | name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-578/dash2.png| caption=Discovered RPC-578-2 instance. Viscera redacted.]]

RPC-578's effect is triggered when any living dermis or bone tissue passes through its opening. All tissue demonstrating the effect is designated RPC-578-1. RPC-578-1 is described as an immediate deteriorative physical expansion in all directions resulting in an observable increasing transparency of the body, the severity of which scales with time. Investigation under microscopy demonstrates that the space between all atoms expand, despite no observable force nor structure to keep affected atoms together. Furthermore, the effect propagates across the rest of the body save for visceral and vascular tissue over time, starting from the initial exposure site. Subjects exposed to RPC-578-1 are to be considered persona exuta and designated RPC-578-A. The final stages of RPC-578-1 invariably results in the termination of all RPC-578-A instances, marked by conversion into RPC-578-2. RPC-578-1 and therefore RPC-578-2 are contagious through contact.


The expanded body cells of RPC-578-1 display a remarkable visual effect that does not comport with scientific understanding of material expansion, RPC-578-1/2 become fully transparent and invisible. RPC-578-1/2 can only be seen through its effect on the objects that are behind it; specifically optically-diffusing objects behind it in a manner most consistent with a digital image-processing blur convolution.[[footnote]] Testing has confirmed this effect can be most accurately likened to a Gaussian blur. [[/footnote]] 


Below is a chronological description of the effects of RPC-578-1, including psychological report of RPC-578-A instances.


||~ Phase ||~ Physical Description ||~ Psychological Report ||
|| Exposure || Subjects are instructed to insert an extremity into RPC-578 and then withdraw it. During immediate exposure, RPC-578-A instances appear in ordinary physical condition as RPC-578-1 is local to the extremity's extent of insertion. RPC-578-1 immediately manifests, albeit in a slow and subtle capacity: The epidermis of the exposed extremit(ies) start to expand roughly 1 to 2 cm above the surface of RPC-578-A's body. Progression is slow, and the phase elapses over a period of 5 minutes on average.|| RPC-578-A do not report pain, but describe the affected location(s) as weighing less and report paresthesia therein. RPC-578-A typically report alarm and an overwhelming feeling of dread regardless of their knowledge of the stages to follow.||
|| Propagation || During propagation phase, the progression of RPC-578-1 accelerates. Entire regions begin to manifest RPC-578-1, lifting over the surface of RPC-578-A at increasing speed, including the torso and pelvis. Expansion of exterior cells measures 10 to 30cm. Optically-diffusive effect becomes apparent. This phase elapses over a period of 12 minutes on average.|| RPC-578-A report deep terror and confusion, and display erratic behavior. Instances are known to attempt to reverse the expansion of RPC-578-1 by forceful application of pressure downward on the affected regions to no success, and typically using non-affected extremities, which then also manifest RPC-578-1. Instances report the loss of function of already-affected extremities and a general feeling of increasing weightlessness. ||
|| Exsanguination || During exsanguination, RPC-578-1 appears to penetrate RPC-578-A deep into the vascular layers, and expansion is so severe that instances appear to display a deep red change in color, as blood begins to seep out through space caused by RPC-578-1. After ~4 minutes elapse, blood begins to drain out of RPC-578-A under the force of gravity. RPC-578-1 typically spans the feet of the subject to the head, but expansion of each region depends on time since exposure. Average expansion measures 1.1 m. Optically-diffusive effect becomes severe, as objects behind RPC-578-A become visible. The draining of blood typically completes ~13 minutes after the phase begins. || Instances of RPC-578-A that can still speak demonstrate symptoms consistent with hypoperfusion during the first half of the exsanguination phase, followed by the display of extreme confusion and related symptoms associated with hypovolemic shock. In the final interval of exsanguination, RPC-578-A become non-responsive.||
|| Egress || During egress, the degree of RPC-578-1 progression is so severe that enough space is created for the entirety of the visceral system and the brain to disconnect from RPC-578-A, simultaneously falling out of the mass of RPC-578-1 and piling onto the floor. _
 _
This phase is immediate.|| Not applicable. ||

Post-egress, RPC-578-A is designated RPC-578-2. After this, instances appear to span roughly 3m in diameter in a spheroidal shape, and begin to float due to buoyancy.

It is to be documented that RPC-578-1 onset is highly psychosomatic. The mental state of RPC-578-A instances directly affects the rate of RPC-578-1 progression. While standard completion to RPC-578-2 requires an average of 30 minutes, instances that assume a calm, measured disposition (or even one that is apathetic if possible) consistently slow the progression to RPC-578-2 to several hours. By contrast, exceptionally anxious and fearful instances of RPC-578-A complete within 7 minutes on average, with one recorded instance achieving egress within 15 seconds.[[footnote]] According to medical records, this instance of RPC-578-A had been diagnosed with hematophobia, which may have affected onset.[[/footnote]] It is possible to completely and permanently reverse the effect of RPC-578-1 with high-grade amnestics if treated in time. No less than class A-2 amnestics are authorized for recovery, and under absolutely no circumstances are Class-T[[footnote]] [*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/amnestics Temporary amnestics.][[/footnote]] amnestics authorized to be used to treat RPC-578-1 (See Addendum: AX-1).

**Addendum AX-1**
|phenomena-code=578
]]



[[==]]

Routine provisional testing of RPC-578 on 6/12/2026 inadvertently resulted in the exposure of one Researcher █████ Angler to RPC-578-1. Having been familiar with standard RPC-578 containment protocols, Mr. Angler displayed remarkable composure despite the imminent threat on his life. Being aware of the psychosomatic property of RPC-578-1 progression, Mr. Angler was able to slow the time until egress to an estimated period of 38 hours, and was noted as behaving so tactfully that he was reportedly reminding personnel to refer to him as RPC-578-A-31. RPC-578-1 reached 15% propagation phase before personnel were fully equipped with personal protective equipment to administer amnestics to the unaffected lower hip of Mr. Angler. The effects of RPC-578-1 emancipated within 12 minutes, and Mr. Angler's RPC-578-A classification was revoked once quarantine was no longer deemed necessary. Mr. Angler was commended for his behavior and returned to normal duties within the week.

On 6/23/2026, Mr. Angler's brain and viscera were found in his personal quarters directly below an instance of RPC-578-2. 

It was later reported to the Office of Ethics and Review that responding ASF personnel "lacked the appropriate amnestics to respond" to the situation posed by the minor containment breach, despite it later being ascertained that the Site's amnestic containment reserves were nominal according to inventory records. It was determined that responding ASF personnel would not, in fact, "lack the appropriate amnestics to respond" if they simply meandered over to the South Wing of the Site where plenty of life-saving amnestics are consolidated. 

The investigation ascertained that because the dedicated in-room emergency amnestics locker was not stocked as per operational due diligence, that containment personnel expediently deployed the use of at-the-time proximate Class-T amnestics. It was ruled that Mr. Angler's disposition had permitted more than enough time for containment personnel to retrieve the appropriate amnestics, and yet his efforts were made in vain since he had mistakenly placed his trust in the Containment Department of his Site. The Office concludes with great disappointment that the loss of life is patently attributed to a lethal and highly immoral lack of operational diligence on the part of Site containment personnel.

**Addendum AX-2:** It has been discovered that instances of RPC-578-2 universally emit radio-frequency electromagnetic waves in the 300 GHz band in a manner that is physically impossible. The emissions occur in discrete, packet-like bursts resembling Wi-Fi traffic, albeit at an incredibly high frequency that is uncharacteristic and ostensibly impractical for such a purpose. The emissions are not attenuated, scattered, or occluded by intervening physical obstructions despite their approximately 1 mm wavelength. These emissions are formed in the rough shape of an approximately 1.25-meter-diameter cylindrical emission envelope that appears to point in a manner that suggests deliberate targeting, and changes frequently. The intended recipient(s) of these packets are, at this time, unknown, however RPC-578-2 instances have been recorded as receiving responses from the same direction, and RPC-578-2 instances are even documented to have communicated between each other. Decryption of these intercepted signals has remained unsuccessful. 

RPC-578-2 do not respond to any stimulus, with the sole exception that they are known to seldom attempt to communicate with Wi-Fi access points such as wireless routers within ~20 meters. Research into attempting communication in this manner is ongoing.
