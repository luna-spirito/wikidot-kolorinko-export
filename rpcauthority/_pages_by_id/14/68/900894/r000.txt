An as of yet unverified source has provided details that anomalous technology conglomerate Nucorp is currently embroiled in a legal dispute with interdimensional rivals GEAR. Numerous patents and copyrights filed by Nucorp bear striking resemblances to products produced by GEAR in other dimensions. 

It is alleged that Nucorp are capitalising on their status as a multiversal corporation to effectively steal ideas that have not been patented within other realities to get around copyright law.

Is there any substance to these claims, or is this rumour a vicious lie spread by their competitors in an effort to destabilise this reality's foremost developer of anomalous technologies? Nucorp has so far refrained from commenting.
