The Authority raided a Kabushiki Kawaii warehouse. They must have known we were coming because their records were wiped. we did find a few leads of note though, including a partial internal message:

[[=]]
> 
> "-will return shortly, please inform the royal family that His Highness, the Prince will be quite pleased with our progress.-"
> 
> "-reduce accumulated genetic deficiencies without-"
> 
> "-a more perfect addition to the royal bloodline could not-"
> 
[[/=]]

Apparently whoever was overseeing this location has a high profile client that they are cooking something custom for.

The tech heads at the lab are still trying to recover any more info, but right now it's impossible to tell if they are playing match maker, or making enhancements to existing members of the royal family.

There is also still some debate as to **which** royal family they are getting into bed with, but it's a relatively short list.
