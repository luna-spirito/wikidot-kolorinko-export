Recent raw intelligence gained from Veiled assets suggests Nucorp has begun expanding their customer base. Several groups, including China’s People’s Committee for the Acquisition of Anomalous Objects, Russia’s Ministry of Paranormality, and even North Korea’s Room 28 have apparently made overtures to allow Nucorp to run field tests of certain prototypes of questionable reliability in exchange for discounts on future purchases. Agents in the field are to be aware of potential unseen weapons or defense capabilities that members of these organizations may utilize. Agents are also warned these prototypes likely pose as much if not more danger to their users as they do to their targets.
