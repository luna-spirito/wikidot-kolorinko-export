[[include component:hazards top-spacing=none|teleportation=true|temporal=true|destabilization=true|tychokinetic=true]]

------


**Containment Protocols:** No permanent containment protocols for GRP-002 instances have been established as of yet. GRP-002-1 through -1318 is currently in the hands of the Karza. Instances are scattered across various worlds within the Karzyioe' Empire[[footnote]]Situated around the Orion Nebula.[[/footnote]], and are being used to benefit both the Karza and the Corps in general, specifically for FTL travel and the protection of high-ranking personnel. Any questions or concerns relating to GRP-002 are to be forwarded to the Karza directly. 

**Description:** GRP-002 refers to a group of roughly 1318 small, shiny orbs, all weighing in at around 3 pounds. The orb itself is made up of a combination of unknown or undiscovered materials.

Whenever physical contact is made with an instance of GRP-002, the person making contact will be transported to a separate plane of existence[[footnote]]Theoretically.[[/footnote]] and will encounter GRP-002-A, which resembles a large, eyeless, vaguely humanoid entity with male characteristics.

So far, GRP-002-A has been very cooperative with both the Corps and the Karza. Occasionally, GRP-002-A will, most likely accidentally, cause unnecessarily high levels of stress within rangers. This usually happens after being asked a specific question.

Upon request, GRP-002-A will grant a single wish to the person holding the orb. This is presumably done by somehow influencing the probability of the series of events leading to the wish occurring[[footnote]]GRP-002-A will only accept requests in the realm of possibility. For example, he can make you fall in love, but he cannot make you part of a different species.[[/footnote]].

**Discovery:** While the Karza has used GRP-002 for thousands of orbits, some factions in the GT inferred its existence through its demonstrations. The GRPC would not become fully aware of GRP-002-A until a Lithium mining operation of Heurískō III[[footnote]]Third planet of the Isko's Home System, which the Karzyioe' Empire had briefly enslaved for a time eons ago.[[/footnote]]. During the oversight operation, a Senior Ranger would find an instance of GRP-002, specifically GRP-002-39.
