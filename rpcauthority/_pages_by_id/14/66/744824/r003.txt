**Media ID#:** M-00557

> **Foreword:** A team expedition into [[[RPC-557]]] on November 28th, 2012 found a fallen air vent on platform 6, enabling convenient access to a then-uncharted network of ceiling ducts. The group radioed for command to deliver a heat-resistant surveillance walker while they held the area.
> 
> The robot, one of two retrieved for the second round of RPC-557 investigations, is a model of adapted drone originally designed by Protocol engineers. Its insulated aluminum coating lends extensive heat resistance but weighs down the device to such a degree that it cannot navigate aerially, instead using four maneuverable legs.
> ----
> **<Begin Video>**
> 
> **00:00 -** The walker is placed up into the shaft and proceeds straight ahead. {{Temperature reading: 23°C.}}
> 
> **02:28 -** After approx. 30 meters, a three-way junction is reached. Command directs to first take left. Spots of mold are visible on the walls. {{Temperature reading: 61°C.}}
> 
> **02:55 -** A metal thump is heard, origin unclear. The walker stops for 2 seconds before proceeding. {{Temperature reading: 77°C.}}
> 
> **04:25 -** The walker travels approx. 18 meters before reaching a dead end. It takes a moment to scan this corner: there are no distinguishing features, not even a vent cover. {{Temperature reading: 85°C.}}
> 
> **04:37 -** Command begins a 180° camera rotation. As the camera finishes its pivot, it views the face of a spectral white figure 5 meters down the tunnel, staring it down and mouthing silently. {{Temperature reading: 90°C.}}
> 
> **04:45 -** The figure blurs into a dissolving fog. {{Temperature reading: 68°C.}}
> 
> **04:52 -** The walker continues straight for approx. 50 meters, during which no forks or turns occur. {{Temperature reading: 61°C.}}
> 
> **09:06 -** After extended travel, the next vent cover appears on the shaft's floor, immediately before a sharp right turn. {{Temperature reading: 75°C.}}
> 
> **09:16 -** Before the walker can reach the vent, the duct begins to clatter. A dark shape scurries from right to left across the other side of the vent cover in 2 seconds; the camera does not get a clear view. {{Temperature reading: 128°C.}}
> 
> **09:36 -** The commotion subsides, and the walker inspects the vent cover. Through the dense grating is a narrow passageway that appears to be a similar air duct. Dust still swirls from the recent disturbance within. The drone angles itself to view further but cannot see either end of the passage, only a floor and two spanning walls. {{Temperature reading: 79°C.}}
> 
> **10:28 -** The walker reorients itself, taking the right turn and continuing onwards. Mold presence is considerable for the following span. {{Temperature reading: 88°C.}}
> 
> **13:28 -** After approx. 36 meters of travel, mold abates and a dimly lit opening becomes visible at the end of the tunnel. {{Temperature reading: 105°C.}}
> 
> **14:27 -** The walker reaches the opening. Exiting the shaft, it falls out onto the floor of a small room.
> 
> The camera pans back and forth, using its light to observe details: the room is plain, with peeling white paint, uneven gray carpeting, and dusty trim. The floor is littered with discarded cigarettes and food packaging. {{Temperature reading: 144°C.}}
> 
> **14:45 -** Shortly before the lens is a cracked eggshell, its yolk poured out on the floor and boiling. Near the corner of the room is a bare white mattress marked by red and brown stains; at one edge rests a stuffed doll, possibly of a horse or donkey.
> 
> The camera malfunctions for 2 seconds at this point, causing a gray bar to obscure most of the right side of the screen. {{Temperature reading: 196°C.}}
> 
> **15:02 -** The camera pivots towards the room's opposite corner and spots a lit doorway, its door slightly ajar. Command directs the walker forward. {{Temperature reading: 241°C.}}
> 
> **15:17 -** The door is pulled open from the other side by a human figure cast in black. {{Temperature reading: 294°C.}}
> 
> **15:19 -** Camera fails. The lens melts over and the feed breaks.
