[[include component:image-block | align=right| width=250px | name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-397/image.png| caption= Image of RPC-397 in standard containment (Top & Bottom) split into two 1 liter containers, alongside relevant medical equipment]]

**Containment Protocols:** Only a single batch of RPC-397 may exist at any given time. Each batch must consist of no more than 2 liters of liquid and must be stored in one or more transparent BPA-free plastic containers. RPC-397 is to be stored securely and refrigerated, with a constant temperature of below 32°C. The refrigerator must have access to its own individual generator at all times, and 24-hour monitoring must also be ensured via camera.

No experimentation or testing with RPC-397 is presently authorized, and this will remain until further notice. In anticipation of its decay rate, a new batch of RPC-397 is to be created every 72 hours, while the former batch must be disposed of through the use of a furnace. The creation process must be supervised by at least one Level-4 employee.

Only personnel approved by the acting Research and Development Supervisor[[footnote]] Position currently occupied by Dr. ███ [[/footnote]] may access RPC-397 for anything outside of what is stated above.

------
**Description:** RPC-397 is a chemical product with a short lifespan that, when administered to humans, enhances their physical prowess and induces intense tolerance for labor. RPC-397 is composed of a mixture called "the Vitalian Formula,"[[footnote]] Recovered from the //Deutschen Anomal Forschung Abteilung// and modified by Dr. ███ [[/footnote]] the mixture thereof being known only to the acting Research and Development Committee and the select group of scientists tasked with producing the formula. These scientists are required to undergo the administration of amnestics after their work is completed to ensure continued secrecy.

Upon synthesis, RPC-397 will begin to ferment, a process that will conclude in 36 hours. Upon fermentation, RPC-397 will work as mentioned previously. After this, however, RPC-397 will begin to deteriorate rapidly, losing its effectiveness in the same duration it took to ferment. Therefore, it is necessary to consistently recreate the formula.

Upon administration, RPC-397 initiates rapid hypertrophy of bodily muscle and mass, which is accompanied by enhanced endurance and cognitive abilities. Thorough medical evaluations have established that these changes arise from rapid chemical processes within the body, coupled with neurological rewiring. However, how RPC-397 induces these processes remains to be fully understood.

RPC-397 is an ongoing development under the jurisdiction of the RPC Authority, and though it has demonstrated certain adverse effects, including depression and withdrawal, these have only been an issue during its current stage of development, and are being worked to be mitigated. Certain subjects have also reported hearing a singular voice, usually commanding or talking to them. Likewise, these effects are unintended, and ongoing efforts are being made to address and mitigate them as part of the developmental process. Upon completion of this process, RPC-397 will no longer pose any danger or threat and will serve exclusively for the betterment of the Authority.

> **Testing:** RPC-397-A, Formula #1[[footnote]] The first to have any noticeable effect [[/footnote]]
> **Subject:** CSD-7842. Race, age, height, and weight: Caucasian, 56, 1.7 meters, and 95 kilograms.
> **Procedure:** The subject is administered 7.5 milligrams of RPC-397 in the form of an IV three times in eight hours, followed by tests of physical and mental state. Notes of progress are taken as the experiment is ongoing by Dr. ███, starting immediately after the first administration.
> **Notes:** 
> 
> **1st Hour:** CSD-7842 showed no visible change but expressed discomfort with the intravenous (IV) needles and requested to leave. **Request denied.**
> 
> **2nd Hour:** CSD-7842 reported pain in his limbs and abdominal region. Tests revealed rapid muscle growth in his body. CSD-7842 requested painkillers. **Request denied.**[[footnote]] Comment: The effects of RPC-397 had to be seen in full. We can't have them minimized by other drugs. [[/footnote]]
> 
> **3rd Hour:** CSD-7842's pain slightly decreased, and visible muscle mass appeared, totaling around 2.3 kilograms. He also burned a relatively large amount of fat. CSD-7842 requested food. **Request approved.**
> 
> **4th Hour:** The second administration was resisted by CSD-7842. He was strapped down to prevent resistance. Tests were taken[[footnote]] Comment: Including both physicals and X-rays [[/footnote]] and CSD-7842 showed considerable muscle and height growth, with a gain of 6.8 kilograms of muscle and 5 centimeters of height.[[footnote]] Comment: During this time, CSD-7842's metabolism significantly increased, and he received appropriate intravenous fluids and parenteral feeding. [[/footnote]] After considerable questioning, CSD-7842 requested silence from "you f██king baboons". **Request declined**[[footnote]] Comment: A vocal restraint was administered to CSD-7842, which was removed after an hour. [[/footnote]]
> 
> **5th-6th:** Hours: CSD-7842 reported a considerable decrease in pain, despite physical tests indicating an increase in pain. He exhibited a significantly higher pain tolerance than the average human. His weight decreased, accompanied by a gain of 15.9 kilograms of muscle. Liquid and parenteral feeding were increased.[[footnote]] Comment: No further requests were made by the CSD-7842 from that point on. [[/footnote]]
> 
> **7th-10th Hours:** CSD-7842 gained 3.2 kilograms of muscle. CSD-7842 was released from his restraints and acted highly agreeable, likely due to RPC-397. Neurological scans showed significant rewiring in his brain, particularly in the prefrontal cortex. His weight and height were measured as 84.8 kg and 198 cm.
> 
> **11th-12th:** Hours: CSD-7842 was taken to the gym to exercise. His height and weight had stabilized, but his metabolism remained high. He demonstrated superhuman strength, breaking established records.[[footnote]] Comment: CSD-7842 benched 544 kg and squatted 909. [[/footnote]] He also showed professional-level ability in other exercises. CSD-7842 consumed around 4.5 kilograms of food and 5.5 liters of water.
> 
> **13th Hour:** CSD-7842 was brought outside to Site ███'s track and made to run as hard and long as he could. He completed one mile in 3 minutes, making 4 perfectly even 45-second 400s. 
>
> **14th Hour:** CSD-7842 collapsed and was a few minutes thereafter declared dead. The cause of death was marasmus, with the subject having lost over 40% of his body mass by the time of death. 
> 
> **15th Hour:** CSD-7842 expired in the 14th hour due to extreme dehydration and starvation. He lost around 27% of the water in his body and over 40% of his body mass, weighing 57.2 kilograms at the time of death. This unfortunate outcome does not appear to have been preventable and must be addressed in the future.
> 
> **Statement:** Great success! I don't like to think of this as a failure… I don't want to think of this as a failure, but a success. We got what we wanted out of it. It worked. It worked. We just need to fix it and get it working. We just need to make it work better, and that's… all we need to do.
