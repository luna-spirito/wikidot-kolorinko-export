**Containment Protocols:**

[[include component:image-block | align=right| width=350px | name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-075/RPC-075| caption=FLIR imaging of RPC-075's latest form, taken from an MST Zulu-05 aircraft.]]

RPC-075 is currently at large, and taking the form of **a close-coupled canard delta fighter aircraft of an unknown model (NATO reporting name: "Firefly").** The anomaly has been detected to typically patrol and engage in combat within contested airspaces over Eastern Europe.

Containment personnel are to treat RPC-075 as nuclear-capable, and under no circumstances should it be allowed to engage targets near populated areas. Furthermore, the form RPC-075 currently assumes exhibits stealth/semi-stealth characteristics and should be treated as such during engagements.

Unlike the previous containment protocols, RPC-075 does not respond to any disengagement order relayed from any known secure military frequencies. Do not attempt to establish contact with the anomaly.

A joint containment operation has been formed between the ASF 1st Air Wing, UN Peacekeeping, UNAAC Anomalous Assistance Force, and the Russian FSB. RPC-075 containment efforts are currently being led by **Maj. Erich Yeager** of the re-instated **MST Zulu-05 "Speed Demons."**

**Containment Protocols:**

[[include component:image-block | align=right| width=350px | name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-075/QF-16C| caption=RPC-075 during its patrol sortie along the coast of Iraq, 2015.]]

RPC-075 is currently bound to **Homey Airport (KXTA), Hangar 21,** taking the form of a **General Dynamics F-16C Block 50 Fighting Falcon.** Containment personnel may refer to Addendum 075/05 (Form Logs) for updates on the latest anomaly form and the airbase it is bound to.

In the case of a translocation event, containment personnel may refer to the Tactical Control Department to request real-time tracking of RPC-075 through its onboard GPS/INS tracker. Under no circumstances should RPC-075 be allowed to engage any airborne contact in its vicinity.

RPC-075 does not show aggression unless provoked; radar locks, weapon launches (active, semi-active, heat-seeking, and electro-optical), and combat turns are considered forms of aggression, and the anomaly may engage if necessary. RPC-075 can be forced to exfiltrate the area by introducing it to sheer air presence.

If the anomaly is not in an active combat state, containment personnel is to relay orders in allied frequencies to disengage and return to base. RPC-075 will then attempt to land at the nearest allied air force base; containment personnel must prevent contact between non-Authority personnel and the anomaly until it can translocate back to its bound airbase.[[footnote]]Note that "allied" in this case refers to factions considered allies with the registered nation assumed by the anomaly at the time.[[/footnote]]

Following incident 075-096, onboard research of RPC-075 using pilots is suspended until further notice. RPC-075 is to be transported back to its bound or allied airbase by cargo. Under no circumstances should personnel enter the cockpit area of RPC-075 for extended periods of time.

Due to the irregular nature of its translocation event, a specialized interception squadron has been assigned to track and escort RPC-075, designated MST Zulu-05 "Speed Demons."

**Description:**

[[tabview]]

[[tab Revised]]

As of 24/02/2022, RPC-075 assumes the form of an unidentified close-coupled canard delta fighter aircraft of an unknown model. Based on satellite imaging and combat footage of the anomaly, the current form measures approximately 18m in length, 10m in width, and 3m in height.

As with its previous forms, RPC-075 retains its locomotion, transmogrification, and translocation capabilities, with seemingly no deviation or variation. The anomaly also retains its deep understanding and knowledge of air-to-air combat, both within and beyond the visual range scope.

However, the anomaly now exhibits a different behavior in identifying targets; RPC-075 will engage any airborne contact in its operation area (approx. 2,000 km^^2^^) regardless of the IFF signal transmitted from the aggressing aircraft. Furthermore, it also treats airborne contact not carrying an IFF transmitter (i.e. commercial airliners) as hostile.

RPC-075 also retains its weapon materialization ability, and has been recorded in multiple instances[[footnote]]Wreckage analysis of fallen peacekeeping aircraft found fragments of the French MBDA Meteor components in one instance, while in another, a Russian R-77 missile was seen in a live gunsight recording footage to have struck the cockpit of a UNAAC EF-2000.[[/footnote]] to have launched missiles of various makes and models, with no signs of system incompatibility. Although unconfirmed, the anomaly is also theorized to possess the ability to materialize standoff nuclear weapons in addition to the aforementioned dumb-fire and guided munitions.

**Assessment Subject:** RPC-075
**Date of Assessment:** 22/02/2023

Based on the chamber security footage during the incident, RPC-075 seems to utilize its typical increased-probability quantum entanglement method to translocate. However, its containment chamber was equipped with the standard Bell-State Disentanglement device and was logged as being active, and fully operational during the incident.

A thorough inspection of the BSD device showed no signs of component failure or evidence of tampering. Furthermore, at least 2 personnel were stationed inside the chamber at the time, with both of them reporting no signs of intrusion or extraordinary events occurring within the timeframe.

Due to the nature of its translocation method, RPC-075 did not leave any physical traces for analysis. The only effect the anomaly exhibited in its immediate area was the instantaneous and sudden shattering of the BSD connective devices due to rapid cooling.

Assessment personnel did not find evidence of any other method of translocation utilized by the anomaly, as the recording of the incident showed the exact modus operandi used in its previous translocation events. More research is needed in order to ascertain the exact cause of RPC-075's containment breach.

[[/div]]

[[/collapsible]]

Recounts from Authority personnel engaged with RPC-075 mentioned that the anomaly exhibits excellent energy retention and climb rate, indicating its current form either has a low overall mass, or a high thrust-to-weight ratio. Furthermore, the exterior paint scheme consists of a plain sky-grey camouflage scheme with no roundels or other identifying marks.

Its current form seems to follow the contemporary design philosophies of many modern canard delta fighter aircraft. Limited wind-tunnel research with wooden mockups suggests the anomaly has the ability to exhibit unparalleled high-AoA performance, although its turn rate proved difficult to measure, as the exact mass of the aircraft is currently unknown.

Furthermore, the design of its diverterless air intakes, coupled with the overall shape of its primary wings, empennage, and canards, suggests the airframe has a degree of stealth capability. Further theories include that the cockpit is equipped with radar-reflecting Indium Oxide plating, seen in many contemporary stealth designs.

The full extent of its stealth capability can be measured from the recounts of numerous engagements with containment personnel. The anomaly has been measured to be virtually undetectable by radar in the front and rear aspects, partially visible in the side aspect, and is at its weakest and most visible in the top and bottom aspects.

[[tab Original]]

As of 01/01/2022, RPC-075 assumes the form of a General Dynamics F-16C Block 50 Fighting Falcon. The exterior of RPC-075 is virtually identical to the former USAF-operated units, save for parts of the wingtips, empennage, elevons, and ventral fins being painted high-visibility orange instead of the typical two-tone gray.[[footnote]]Deliberate modification by the Authority to ease visual acquisition, as part of the approved modifications per Document 075/01-13.[[/footnote]]

RPC-075 is capable of anomalous locomotion and instantaneous translocation towards a random point in the world and has shown a preference to manifest in the airspace of contested areas to seemingly perform patrol duties along their borders. The duration of these sorties can take anywhere between 1-18 hours, and RPC-075 will then dematerialize and translocate back to its bound airbase.

The anomaly will engage airborne and surface targets considered hostile, but it will only do so if provoked. As such, Authority personnel has stripped the exterior of RPC-075 of any distinguishing markings, such as roundels and tail numbers, to prevent international controversy in the case of an aggression event.

[[include component:image-block | align=left| width=300px | name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-075/F5F-5K| caption=Unconfirmed RPC-075 sighting, 1942.[[footnote]]Theorized to have been a result of the former pilot bailing out while the aircraft maintained level flight, judging from the open cockpit.[[/footnote]]]]

The anomaly also possesses the ability to transform its physical components down to its molecular makeup, but always seems to choose the form of a combat-capable aircraft. This ability has been researched extensively and determined to be part of a parondemenal[[footnote]]Essentially binding its incorporeal form to an existing object.[[/footnote]] transmogrification process instead of a simple intermolecular/subatomic transformation method, as the forms it assumed in the past vary greatly from mass and molecular makeup.

[[collapsible show="+ Notes on RPC-075 Transmogrification Abilities" hide="-hide Notes on RPC-075 Transmogrification Abilities"]]

[[div style="border: var(--blockquote-border); color: var(--text-color); background: var(--blockquote-background); border-radius: var(--blockquote-border-radius); padding: var(--blockquote-padding); box-shadow: var(--blockquote-box-shadow); margin: 0px 50px 0px 50px; padding: 3px 20px;"]]

**ID:** 075/01

**Log Date:** 14/11/1991
**Researching Personnel:** Dr. Robert J. Langston, Par.D

----

Following up on our analysis of a discarded radar part of RPC-075 following its disarmament procedure, we have found no evidence that the individual atoms of RPC-075 possess anomalous properties. In other words, this discarded radar part is virtually indistinguishable from a regular AN/APG-63 radar set.

Spatial anomaly sensors used inside the containing hangar the moment RPC-075 underwent a transmog event to assume Form 9 does not show an increased reading of interdimensional energy. This partially confirms the theory that RPC-075's transmogrification ability stems from an incorporeal form rearranging the physical components down to their atomic level.

However, because the forms RPC-075 assumes over the years vary greatly in size and mass, the conclusion of this research leans more on a parondemenal process rather than a simple conscious decision by the incorporeal form's part to rearrange its molecules to resemble a different object.

Meaning, the incorporeal form of RPC-075, essentially its "soul," would bind itself to another object. The objects it chooses to bind to do not necessarily have to be a complete and functional aircraft, as its leftover mass from the previous form can be recycled to repair a damaged fuselage.

This lines up with the increased reports of missing components in aircraft graveyards and the fact that the USAF did not report any mysterious loss of active-duty F-15C aircraft. Moreover, most components currently mounted to RPC-075 and in previous forms show serial numbers belonging to decommissioned and damaged airframes.

RPC-075 does not show a preference for a particular aircraft manufacturer in determining which form to assume, nor its national or political affiliation. The type (role) of the combat aircraft forms it assumed in the past were completely random, but a common pattern observed among the forms was that they had a form of air-to-air capability.

With each new form RPC-075 assumes, any work done to its appearance and internal workings, including non-standard additions such as GPS/INS trackers, will have to be redone, as the object will purge any components not listed in its manufacturer's specifications.

[[/div]]

[[/collapsible]]

As for its translocation trait, thorough research has determined that RPC-075 utilizes a form of increased-probability quantum entanglement to transport its mass into a different location. However, this does not conclude that the anomaly possesses Tychokinetic properties, as this trait only seems to affect its translocation method.

In addition, the Authority has found that the anomaly can be "bound" to a certain airbase by marking the exterior with the standard USAF tail code along with a unit number.[[footnote]]Or equivalent systems if the anomaly assumes the form of a non-USAF vehicle.[[/footnote]] The exact placement of the tail code does not hold significance, and a decision has been made to place the markings on the interior of its cockpit frame to prevent identification.

The exact mechanism of how this binding trait operates is currently unknown. However, limited paranatural research suggests that a form of past parondemenal procedure has had an effect on how RPC-075 behaves in the anomalous space (refer to Document 075/03 "Theory on RPC-075 Manifestation Events").

Moreover, evidence of limited intrinsic sentience in RPC-075 has been observed in how the anomaly responds to commands relayed through an escorting aircraft. In this case, the anomaly more often than not displayed compliance to any and all commands relayed through an allied radio frequency to land or disengage from the operation area, if the anomaly is not engaging in active combat.

[[include component:image-block | align=right| width=320px | name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-075/QF-86F| caption=RPC-075 as a USAF North American F-86F Sabre, at the time believed to be intercepting a Douglas D-558-2 Skyrocket research aircraft. Nevada, 1953.]]

RPC-075 has undergone a disarmament procedure with each new form as is standard practice since May 1989. This procedure primarily focuses on removing/decreasing the anomaly's combat capability, such as removing its autocannon(s), avionics, radar, fire control system, and electrical wiring to its weapons pylons.[[footnote]]A decision has been made to leave its radar warning receiver, flares and chaff launchers, as well as its electronic warfare suite to ensure anomaly perseverance.[[/footnote]]

However, this does not guarantee complete anomaly disarmament, as RPC-075 has been observed to materialize various weapon systems during its materialization events (refer to Document 075/02 "Analysis of the Combat Capabilities of RPC-075 [Form 11]").

[[collapsible show="+ Analysis on the Combat Capabilities of RPC-075 (Form 11)" hide="-hide Analysis on the Combat Capabilities of RPC-075 (Form 11)"]]

[[div style="border: var(--blockquote-border); color: var(--text-color); background: var(--blockquote-background); border-radius: var(--blockquote-border-radius); padding: var(--blockquote-padding); box-shadow: var(--blockquote-box-shadow); margin: 0px 50px 0px 50px; padding: 3px 20px;"]]

**ID:** 075/02

**Log Date:** 22/07/2006
**Researching Personnel:** Dr. Kelly Forger, D.E.S

----

Please note that this combat analysis was conducted while RPC-075 assumed Form 11, a General Dynamics F-16C Block 50; data gathered in this research paper will be null and void if the anomaly assumes a different form in the future.

RPC-075 has been observed to have an excellent understanding of Basic Fighter Maneuver (BFM) and Beyond Visual Range (BVR) combat procedures. The lack of a controlling pilot gives RPC-075 an insurmountable edge in its dogfighting capability due to the elimination of G-LOC[[footnote]]G-force-induced loss of consciousness.[[/footnote]] as a variable.

In the case of Within Visual Range combat, the anomaly leans towards the two-circle dogfighting flow, as is standard practice with USAF F-16 pilots. However, RPC-075 has also been observed to succeed in the one-circle flow, as seen in one of its engagements against a US Navy-operated F/A-18E Super Hornet.

RPC-075 achieves this feat by sustaining a maximum Angle of Attack beyond 55 degrees; normally, the flight control system of a typical F-16 will take over all control and reduce the elevon authority if the aircraft reaches this AoA number. This indicates that the anomaly is flying without an active FCS, which is not impossible to do, but certainly requires a skilled pilot to perform.

Moreover, onboard accelerometer telemetry has shown that RPC-075 can sustain G overloads exceeding 15G; far higher than the manufacturer's set limitations. Normally, this would cause catastrophic damage to the aircraft's superstructure, and in extreme cases, will outright tear the wings of the aircraft mid-flight.

However, after-sortie analysis of the fuselage and wing spars does not show any evidence of stress on the metals and composite plastics. This is theorized to have been a byproduct of the anomaly's transmogrification properties, but more research needs to be conducted on this topic before drawing conclusions.

In the case of Beyond Visual Range combat, the anomaly has a deep understanding of the optimal Doppler Notch maneuver angle while maintaining a radar lock on the opposing target. In addition, RPC-075 has been observed to materialize certain missiles not available to USAF-operated F-16 variants, such as the MBDA Meteor ARH missiles, giving it an excellent edge in BVR capability.

It is unknown how the anomaly can utilize these non-standard weapons, as the fire control system does not permit typical aircraft of this variant to carry such weapons and would require thorough upgrades in order to mount them. Research on this topic would require on-board observations, however, it is currently not possible with the 2004 research embargo.

The anomaly has also been observed to engage surface targets using a combination of strafing runs, and standoff missiles. The most notable air-to-surface engagement involving RPC-075 took place in ████, where the anomaly utilized six AGM-65 Maverick and two AGM-88 HARM missiles to engage a formation of MBTs and missile-equipped SPAAs along the border of ████████.

**Discovery:**

The first manifestation event of RPC-075 was theorized to have taken place during WWI, however, the sheer number of air battles taking place at the time, and the lack of reliable information sources have proved the effort to confirm this theory to be difficult.

The first recorded instance of an anomalous appearance of a single opposing fighter aircraft took place in 1967, during the height of the Vietnam War. In ██/██/1967, a formation of six F-4C Phantom II as part of the 555th Tactical Fighter Squadron was flying a routine patrol sortie in the skies above Vietnam.

[[include component:image-block | align=left| width=300px | name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-075/MiG-21F-13| caption=Gunsight camera footage of RPC-075, 1967.]]

Lt. Vizcarra, at the time acting as a Weapons System Officer for the lead aircraft, reported the sudden appearance of a radar contact 10 Miles (16 Km) ahead of their formation. He reported utilizing the combat tree to determine hostility. When no response was received, Lt. Vizcarra acquired a radar lock on the contact and prepared an AIM-7C SARH missile for launch.

However, Capt. Townsend, acting as the squadron leader, insisted on acquiring visual contact before engaging. Capt. Townsend, Lt. Vizcarra, and Lt. Callahan (WSO for the third F-4C in their squadron), all confirmed the opposing aircraft was a Mikoyan-Gurevich MiG-21 carrying North Vietnamese roundels.

The opposing MiG-21 managed to shoot down a single F-4C before outrunning the rest of the squadron and disappearing off the radar coverage using terrain masking. Lt. Callahan was the only survivor of the downed aircraft, as his pilot's ejection seat had failed to ignite. Lt. Callahan later recalled that the opposing MiG-21 "had no pilot in the cockpit," citing the reason being "[the cockpit] was entirely teal."

Reports of the sudden appearance of a lone opposing fighter aircraft would be received at an increased frequency in most major air-to-air engagements since then, with the most notable event taking place in the 2001 invasion of Iraq. On 20/03/2003, a squadron of USAF F-15Es spotted a single aircraft carrying the radar signatures of a MiG-29 flying along the southern border of Iraq.

The opposing MiG-29 simply left the area when two of the F-15E performed a combat turn and acquired a radar lock. This was the event that caught the attention of the Authority, as both coalition intelligence and Iraqi sources stated that the IQAF had zero presence in 2003, due to Saddam Hussein had ordered the bulk of his air force to be disassembled and buried.

[[include component:image-block | align=right| width=400px | name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-075/QF-4J| caption=RPC-075 as a US Navy McDonnell Douglas F-4J Phantom II. Panama, 1989.]]

The Authority made official contact with RPC-075 when a report of a lone McDonnell Douglas F-4J Phantom II carrying US Navy markings and roundels was spotted flying along the southern coast of Panama during the opening stages of the 1989 US Invasion of Panama.

On December 20, 1989, a USAF-operated Boeing E-3 Sentry, at the time acting as the AWACS overseeing the execution of Operation Just Cause, spotted an unscheduled flight of a lone US Navy F-4J flying below 2,000 meters just off the coast of Panama City. Two USAF F-16Cs were dispatched from Howard AFB to intercept and escort the aircraft out of Panama airspace.

RPC-075 was compliant in following the interception squadron's orders to divert and land back at Howard AFB. When the ground crew observed the absence of pilots inside the cockpit, Gen. Thurman notified the CIA, which in turn notified Authority personnel stationed in Central America.

[[collapsible show="+ Theory on RPC-075 Manifestation Events" hide="-hide Theory on RPC-075 Manifestation Events"]]

[[div style="border: var(--blockquote-border); color: var(--text-color); background: var(--blockquote-background); border-radius: var(--blockquote-border-radius); padding: var(--blockquote-padding); box-shadow: var(--blockquote-box-shadow); margin: 0px 50px 0px 50px; padding: 3px 20px;"]]

**ID:** 075/03

**Log Date:** 19/02/1992
**Researching Personnel:** Dr. Ryan Thompson, Ph.D

----

We can always chalk up the appearance events of RPC-075 to random happenstance, or what the Anomaly Department refers to as "pre-parondemenal behavior."  They theorize that most of the anomalous objects manifested over the years have been a result of parondemenal rituals; the theory that human minds and ideas being the very source of energy that causes anomalies to be borne onto the world.

However, I believe that RPC-075 is something much more extraordinary—not just a result of a person's rage and anger fueling the creation of it. I believe, that RPC-075 is the embodiment of peace itself. If we were to align the appearances of RPC-075 in past conflicts, it is clear that the anomaly has always chosen to fight the aggressing force, on behalf of the defending faction.

For example, take the US Invasion of Panama: The main objective was the deposition of the //de facto// Panamian leader, General Manuel Noriega, accused of drug trafficking. RPC-075 appeared and fought on behalf of the US Navy. The recent Persian Gulf War in 1991: to prevent Saddam Hussein's forces from invading and occupying Kuwait. RPC-075 appeared and fought on behalf of the US Air Force.

Even with all the more minor and undetermined reports of RPC-075 appearances, a common pattern can be observed that it chose to fight the aggressing faction. Whether RPC-075 has a degree of omniscience—that it somehow knows deeper details about the coming of wars, I do not know.

I know it is not my place to analyze the capability of RPC-075 or the combat strategies of the world's militaries for that matter, but what if RPC-075 appeared to shoot down a liaison aircraft carrying important national figures? What if it figured out that it can effectively end a war by killing its leaders?

Again, it is not my place to direct the Authority's containment operations, but I strongly suggest that RPC-075 be contained permanently. Sure, we can always use a peacekeeping force during these chaotic times, but what if it decides to kill the //wrong// target?

The first appearance of RPC-075 as Form 10 was theorized to have taken place in 2001, during the opening stages of the Invasion of Afghanistan. On 12/10/2001, US intelligence claimed that heightened air presence was detected on the border between Afghanistan and Uzbekistan.

The fate of the rogue aircraft, and whether or not it was RPC-075 was never confirmed. Some sources claim that Uzbekistan officials sent a decommissioned MiG-29 to an aircraft graveyard around that timeframe.

This was unmistakable evidence of RPC-075 manifesting as Form 10 as neither Iraq, Saudi Arabia, nor Kuwait, the point from which the contact was reported to have originated, had no MiG-29s in their inventory at that time.

MST Zulu-05 personnel were quick on the scene but were not allowed to pursue the contact as their inventory of US-produced fighters might cause an international controversy if they were to enter Kuwaiti airspace. The Authority later reestablished contact with the anomaly by ordering it to land at Ayn Al Asad Airbase using Authority-operated AWACS through Iraqi radio frequencies.

Authority personnel made preparations on March 26, 2003, to transport RPC-075 by flying it out of Iraqi airspace to an allied air force base in Saudi Arabia. However, a translocation event took place shortly after takeoff, and RPC-075 manifested in the outskirts of Najaf and shot down two US Navy F/A-18F Super Hornets. The pilot controlling RPC-075 was nowhere to be found.

A decision was made to only transport RPC-075 back to its bound or allied airbase by cargo on April 1, 2003, and to restrict any onboard research using Authority personnel for extended periods of time.
