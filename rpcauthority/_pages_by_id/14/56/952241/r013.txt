**Containment Protocols:** RPC-711 is kept atop a lead pedestal in Site-068's Alpha-level containment vault as an anomalous suppressor. Gold tubes (12mm x 2mm) are spread around the vault next to containment lockers and wired to Groves[[footnote]]**Groves Radiation:** Measured in Viottos/Vi(s); a type of radiation created as the result of spatial alteration through anomalous means, typically associated with thaumaturgy.[[/footnote]] counters. While RPC-711's properties are enough to neutralize any sporadic anomalous activity in the vault, manual removal of anomalies may be required should any enter a "passive" state (indicated by an internal count of ~71 Vi on nearby tubes).

If RPC-711 reaches a critical Vi of ~140, a gold-lined sheet will be remotely lowered over the statue. Containment personnel must then remove all anomalies from the vault and into auxiliary cells. Anomalies will only be returned to their designated lockers once aberrations have ceased occurring and ambient Vi returns to base levels.

-----

**Description:** RPC-711 is a marble statue made by Fabricio Olavarria de Bardales, a deceased Spanish sculptor and [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/academy-hub Accademia della Vera Arte] alumnus. The statue depicts two people embracing; a female sculpted in Carrara and a male in Nero Marquina. The female head and male hands of the sculpture have both been removed by blunt force. An elegy can be found etched onto the back of both figures:

RPC-711 is inert unless in the presence of anomalies or anomalously-altered spaces. When in the presence of an active anomaly, RPC-711 will create an area of altered reality around the anomaly such that its effects are nullified. This area will continue to exist until the offending anomaly is removed from the vicinity, destroyed or otherwise deactivated. Exposure to multiple active anomalies or passively active anomalies will lead to the creation of aberrations.[[footnote]] **Aberrations:** Sporadic spatial anomalies generated within areas of high Groves radiation. Typically ranging from sudden combustion of the air to pockets of distorted reality.[[/footnote]]


-----

**Discovery:** RPC-711 was the main art piece displayed at the Guggenheim Museum Bilbao's "2024 Exhibit of Spanish Art & History" hosted on the 21st of June in Biscay, Spain. It was advertised as the long-awaited return of famous sculptor Fabricio “Grajilla” Olavarria de Bardales following a decade-long retirement. 748 people, including well-known critics and artists, attended the afternoon exhibition, which was televised by local news channels.

As an annual opportunity for anomalous artists to share their works with the greater public, several dozen low-level anomalous artworks were previously approved by the Ministry of Paranormal Defence and Regulation for safe display. RPC-711 was not included in this list. It is unknown whether or not Bardales was aware of the exponential increase of his sculpture's area of effect in such a crowded space and with more than three dozen passive anomalies. 

The incident following RPC-711's unveiling resulted in, accounting for Authority and MPDR personnel, 489 casualties, 132 injuries, and 36 missing persons.[[footnote]]In large part caused by internal bleeding/organ failure from distorted pockets of space inside the victim's bodies.[[/footnote]] It is believed the area of effect could have expanded beyond the museum if not for the combined efforts of MPDR and Authority assets in evacuation after anomalous activity in the area was confirmed.

The event, recorded by various media sources, was covered as a large-scale biochemical attack from an unknown terrorist cell. Casualties not explainable by the cover-up were discretely disposed of and then added to the "missing" body count. Surviving civilians were administered Class G-1 amnestics. The museum was closed to the public until the MPDR successfully transported and contained the anomaly in their facility. Fabricio was found dead, resting against RPC-711. Following autopsy revealed the cause of death to be a seizure. Blisters found around his torso, arms and legs appear to be unrelated to the effects of RPC-711. 

Despite several attempts by the Ministry to bring Academy officials to a UNAAC hearing for compliance in terrorism (unrelated to the nature of the cover-up story), no clear motive for Fabricio's actions has been identified, and records corroborate the Academy's claims of no official contact with and from the artist in over a decade. Insider information regarding Fabricio has similarly failed to shed light into the nature of the attack.

**REPORT DESCRIPTION:**
[[/=]]

During regular maintenance of Site-068's Alpha-level containment vault by EMC-A3, personnel failed to follow procedure and remove RPC-711 from the area before disconnecting all electronics. At some point during electrical maintenance, one or more of the Lesser Objects housed in the vault entered a passive state, triggering RPC-711's properties. Over the three hours it took for EMC-A3 to finish electrical repairs, the automatic alert and emergency systems for RPC-711 had been rendered useless from Groves overexposure. In the ensuing hours, Site-068's infrastructure and personnel were severely harmed until the neutralization of the anomaly.

Director Mauricio Aquinas, Regional Director Arnold Denton, and AET-711 are awaiting a formal hearing at UNAAC on charges of gross negligence in the management of anomalies for utilitarian purposes. A (symbolic) funerary service in honour of Officer Garcia Funes and all personnel lost during the incident will be held the 21st of August in Site-068's general armoury. The cremated physical remains of all terminated personnel have been stored in hazardous material lockers until further notice.
