[[size 80%]]The Jenkins Index (JI) estimates an article's popularity. JI = votes * (rating - 3)
The Vizlox Index (VI) estimates an article's quality. VI = rating * (1 - 2 / (votes + 2))
Ratings on articles from 2018-2020 may be skewed by invalid votes[[/size]]
