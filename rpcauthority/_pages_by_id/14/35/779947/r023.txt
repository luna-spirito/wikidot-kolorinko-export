**Containment Protocols:** In accordance with the 2001 Anomalous Trafficking Agreement, containment and rehabilitation of RPC-614 instances is entirely under the jurisdiction of MST Hotel-11 "Karasu" across East Asia and Oceania, the specifics of which are classified to Hotel-11 personnel.

In the event that RPC-614 instances are encountered, MST Hotel-11 is to be contacted for retrieval and containment. Do not approach or interact with confirmed RPC-614 instances without the authorization of MST Hotel-11 personnel. Protectorate NT teams are trained and equipped with nonlethal weaponry if Hotel-11 is unavailable.

**Description:** RPC-614 is a designation for a variety of humanoid constructs created and distributed by [*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/kabushiki-kawaii-hub Kabushiki Kawaii].

RPC-614 outwardly resemble Japanese citizens[[footnote]]Statistics suggest over 60 percent of all RPC-614 instances are female, aged 14-22.[[/footnote]], usually modified with several features reminiscent of //Felis catus//, or domesticated cat, such as a tail attached to the lower spine or ears atop of the head, both largely non-functional pardon limited unconscious muscle movements, and at the expense of the victim's mobility. Modifications to the eye to appear similar to the iris of a domesticated cat are rare and often induce almost total blindness.

The contents of the chest cavities of RPC-614 instances have been entirely removed and replaced with a complex series of mechanical and cybernetic components. While some are direct replacements of the original organs, the majority are lethal weapons designed to assault and hinder personnel assigned to the containment of Kabushiki Kawaii and their activities.

The composition of these weapons varies on a case-by-case basis. They include and are not limited to blunt instruments, bladed weapons that erupt from the chest or arms, and tanks stored in the diaphragm which can disperse a variety of chemical weapons, such as ███████ █████, neurotoxins, and tear gas.

RPC-614's aggression is the product of a chemical reaction, originating from a small chip implanted on each instance's occipital lobe. While RPC-614 instances can be hostile towards the UNAAC or PCAAO depending on circumstance, they are primarily designed to target Authority personnel.

If engaged, the anomaly's aggressive behavior will not stop unless they are rendered unconscious or neutralized. EMP detonations can disable the occipital implant, at the risk of lethal damage to the brain. Once rendered unconscious, the chip in the victim's occipital lobe can be extracted and destroyed in order to pacify the instance.

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-614/Victim2|caption=Photograph of several Kabushiki Kawaii victims on auction, one of which was later revealed to be an RPC-614 instance after it was brought into PTSU custody.|width=290px]]

RPC-614 instances are generally unaware of the chip's nature and are unable to control their behavior once the occipital implant has been triggered.[[footnote]]In some encounters, however, they have attempted to delay its activation.[[/footnote]] RPC-614 entities often have no memory of the past 3 days before their "deployment" in a public area [[footnote]]Usually nearby a police station or an area where assistance will be provided once the entity is located.[[/footnote]] making information extraction difficult.

**Addendum 1: 公安九課 (Paranormal Trafficking Surveillance Unit)**

[[collapsible show="[+] Recovered PTSU Case Reports" hide="[-] Close"]]

Dozens of individuals were heavily monitored and tracked down by the PTSU[[footnote]]Paranormal Trafficking Surveillance Unit, the precursor to MST Hotel-11.[[/footnote]] due to their possible connection to and involvement with Kabushiki Kawaii and the anomalous human trafficking operations across Japan and East Asia. Several are still at large and are included in this document due to their relation to RPC-614's creation.

[[div class="blockquote"]]

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-614/Family|caption= Last known photograph of Itsuko Mori circled in red alongside her family.|width=290px]]

[[size 120%]] **CASE #18: Itsuko Mori** [[/size]]
 
------
 
**SEX:** F
 
**AGE:** 86-90~ (AS OF 1984)
 
**BLOOD TYPE:** AB
 
**STATUS:** DETAINED (STATUS UNCLEAR)
 
**REPORT:** CASE #18 is Itsuko Mori, also known as the "Kokeshi Woman", a secretive yet influential textile magnate whose father heavily invested in the industrialization of cotton milling in the late 19th century. After her father's death by unknown causes in 1920, Mori inherited the factories and finances and began to dominate the industry.
 
It is believed that not too long after her rapid horizontal and vertical business expansions created a formidable monopoly across the northern regions of Japan, she began investing in the illegal trade of captured and enslaved women across East Asia, further developing her economic expansion and influence in regional politics.

This involvement eventually led her to encounter several other persons of interest, such as CASE #21 (Shunji Yamada) and CASE #13 (████ ██████). When Kabushiki Kawaii was created, she financially and politically backed the nascent organization allowing for it to operate unimpeded. Several of her commissioned projects eventually came to fruition long after her detainment, one of which was the "Motomachi Hunter"[[footnote]]Unofficial title of RPC-614.[[/footnote]]. The first mention of the concept dates back to 1972, in a series of personal letters exchanged between CASE #18 and her grandson.
 
While Mori evaded capture for several decades, exacerbated by her frequently "changing faces" via sophisticated plastic surgery procedures by CASE #1 (Kabushiki Kawaii) staff, she was eventually arrested by Soviet authorities at a 1984 "auction" in the southern regions of the Sakhalin Oblast. While requests for her transfer into Japanese custody have been recognized by the USSR, they have not been fulfilled.[[footnote]]Itsuko Mori's location is unknown to this day. With the collapse of the Soviet Union, it is rumored but not confirmed she is now in the employ of the Ministry of Paranormality, the anomalous agency of the Russian Federation.[[/footnote]]
 
Mori is a titular example of the anti-aging capabilities of Kabushiki medical procedures. By 1980, her face was compared to a "porcelain mask" due to the paralysis of several facial muscles.

[[size 120%]] **CASE #21: Shunji Yamada** [[/size]]
 
------
 
**SEX:** M
 
**AGE:** 108 (AS OF 1982)
 
**BLOOD TYPE:** B
 
**STATUS:** !! AT LARGE !!
  
**REPORT:** CASE #21 is Shunji Yamada, a notorious war criminal who served in the Imperial Army of Japan from 1916 to 1947, with the first records of his activities dating to the Japanese Siberian Intervention (シベリア出兵, Shiberia Shuppei) of 1918–1922. By 1939, Yamada was employed by Unit 1644 (A satellite unit of Unit 731 based in Japanese-occupied Nanjing) after reports of his performance in his medical education.
 
Yamada's proficiency in human experimentation began with his experiments in the "domestication and control of forced laborers within occupied territories." and the "biological enhancement of military comfort women[[footnote]]Referring to women forced into sexual slavery by Japanese forces.[[/footnote]] for the purposes of the Imperial Japanese Army."
 
Yamada's experiments formed the medical and technological basis for several Kabushiki Kawaii assets seen today, and it is believed he was one of the several founders of the organization in its early years.
 
Despite his age, Yamada maintains a somewhat youthful appearance, due to extensive modification more invasive than the operations performed on other Cases. Unlike these other cases, however, Yamada is not visibly affected by the more common side effects.
 
It is believed that Yamada is responsible for several, if not the majority of, inhumane experiments produced by CASE #1 (Kabushiki Kawaii), particularly RPC-614.

As the current acting head of Kabushiki's "Water Purification Department", Yamada directs the vast majority of the organization's biological experimentation bureau, dedicated to the "enhancement of desired qualities [in] sexual/labor products."

[[size 120%]] **CASE #45: Toshiko Nakahara** [[/size]]
 
------
 
**SEX:** M
 
**AGE:** 76 (AT TIME OF DEATH IN 1988)
 
**BLOOD TYPE:** O
 
**STATUS:** CLOSED
 
**REPORT:** CASE #45 is Toshiko Nakahara, a member of Unit 731 shortly before its neutralization at the end of World War 2. While on trial alongside several other members of Unit 731, Nakahara was acquitted due to "lack of convincing evidence [of] Nakahara's involvement in Unit 731's operations" and American intervention, as Nakahara's talents in biological warfare were sought after by US military interests.
 
After several years in the employment of the United States Biological Weapons Program, Nakahara returned to Japan after President Richard Nixon's ban on the offensive application of biological weapons in 1969. While returning to Japan risked detainment, Nakahara claims that he was "contacted by a close friend" to join Kabushiki Kawaii and assist in its rapid expansion across the Asian human trafficking ring.
 
Kabushiki agents/units equipped with gas dispersion modifications were likely inspired and/or developed by Nakahara, and certain chemicals employed in the creation of several Kabushiki assets (such as the development of specific animalistic features and body parts via ███████ █████) were created during his employment with CASE #1.
 
Nakahara's "recruitment programs" for Kabushiki Kawaii via lacing several consumer goods with ███████ █████ made him a highly desired target among PTSU personnel. Once he was sighted in a public park in Hiroshima in 1988, he was arrested by local police who transferred him to PTSU custody.

Due to an unforeseen prison incident following his intraosseous cannulation interrogation, Nakahara acquired an officer's sidearm and shot himself in the head several times. The case was declared closed.

**Addendum 2: RPC-614 Analysis and Logs**

[[collapsible show="[+] The Motomachi Incident" hide="[-] Close"]]

[[div class="blockquote"]]

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-614/facility|caption= The "███████" residence, which was the Kabushiki facility raided by PTSU forces prior to the Motomachi Incident.|width=290px]]

[[size 120%]] **CASE #958** [[/size]]
 
------

By 2001, PTSU had become increasingly effective at suppressing Kabushiki Kawaii activity in the region, with several facilities and labs being raided and neutralized. This forced Kabushiki Kawaii to move most of their operations away from Japan and East Asia in general. However, due to both the decreased presence of Kabushiki Kawaii in the area and increased pressure from the Authority on Japan to cede anomalous assets and agencies, PTSU was progressively defunded and was limited to only a few stations, most of which were poorly supplied and manned. Nonetheless, PTSU's efforts had a substantial effect on Kabushiki operations in the region.

In an unexpectedly aggressive move, Kabushiki had created a "bait lab" near an PTSU station in the Motomachi (元町) district of the Naka Ward. Anonymous tips and continued observation of suspicious activity in the area led to an authorization of a raid on the building.

While the raid was mostly successful, there was a notable lack of information or data relevant to PTSU's efforts recovered and very few actual victims of Kabushiki operations. All 7 of those victims were RPC-614 instances, which at the time were unknown to the PTSU.

The main base in the Motomachi district was able to take in 3 of the 7 victims, and the remaining 4 were allotted to 2 other bases in central Japan. These 3 buildings hosted most of PTSU's personnel and resources and facilitated their foothold beyond northern Japan.

Only recordings from the Motomachi base were preserved, and are provided below, translated from Japanese:

**[04:36]** {{Authority forces arrive at the facility. Remainder of recording classified.}}

The 2 RPC-614 instances in transit to the facility in Nagano had activated while in the transport vehicle and eliminated all personnel within before escaping, leaving Nagano the only major Kyu base still intact and manned, as the third base that had received RPC-614 instances had failed to prevent its instance from killing the majority of its staff.

Notably, all outgoing calls from the stations during the Motomachi Incident were either blocked or outright ignored for reasons currently unknown. It is suspected that Kabushiki Kawaii may have also caused this, but it is pending further analysis.

Subsequently, the Motomachi base was cordoned off. The Authority was entrusted with the containment/cleanup of the scene, and surviving members of Kyu were taken into custody for questioning.

**Addendum 3: MST Hotel-11 "Karasu/カラス"**

[[collapsible show="[+] MST Dossier" hide="[-] Close"]]

[[div class="blockquote"]]

[[f>image http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-614/hotel-11 width="250px"]]

After the Motomachi incident had utterly devastated the already weakened PTSU, it was deemed a “lost cause” by the Japanese government due to the belief that its operation should be entrusted to the capabilities of existing police forces and the Authority itself, and ultimately disbanded.

However, the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/gd-asia Global Director of Asia] believed that the remnants of PTSU presented an opportunity to the Authority. After approaching the Japanese Government and the surviving members of Kyu, GD-ASIA drafted the Anomalous Trafficking Agreement of February 22nd, 2001.

This agreement between Japan, several other Asian nations (excluding Mainland China), and the Authority (specifically ASIACOM) outlined the size and scope of anomalous human trafficking across East Asia and entrusted the Authority with their “removal”.

> “While Kabushiki Kawaii has been stretched far beyond the Asian sphere, it still possesses a strong grip on the criminal underworld in the region that must be contained and excised. It poses a risk, not only to the human lives trapped in its horrific trade, but the security and character of the nations where its facilities are allowed to exist.”
> 
> - Excerpt from GD-ASIA’s //“Anomalous Trafficking Agreement”//

With this agreement, a new MST was formed and employed in several Kabushiki raids. Hotel-11 inherited a majority of the personnel, buildings, and assets of the defunct PTSU.
