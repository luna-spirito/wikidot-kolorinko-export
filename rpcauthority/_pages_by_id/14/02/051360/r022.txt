++ Late Antiquity
[[/div]]

[[include component:image-block name=VaticanBasilica.png|caption=St. Peter's Basilica]]

[[[Vatican Primacy Hub|The Vatican Primacy]]] grounds its identity in the historical legacy of the Roman Papacy, claiming direct descendent from Catholic Church precursor organizations that once ruled over the veiled world. While the history of the //Auctoritas Imperata// is well-documented, few details are known regarding Papal groups of interest that preceded it.

Papal interest in anomalous phenomena dates back to the collapse of the Roman Empire and the pre-modern containment organization known as the Vanguard of Hercules. Initially dedicated to honoring and serving the Roman gods, the ascension of Emperor Constantine created internal divisions between Christian and pagan factions. Constantine's reported vision of seeing the //chi-rho// (⳩) symbol marked on the Sun before winning at the Battle of the Milvian Bridge in 312 led many Vanguard soldiers to attribute his victory to an anomalous power. Emperor Julian's reformation of the Vanguard as a pagan defense force in 331 failed to reverse these trends.

A new Vanguard of Christ was formed under Emperor Theodosius in 347, though the division of the Roman Empire limited its influence in Western Europe. Sources suggest the Vanguard transitioned into a pre-modern agency of interest for the Byzantine Empire, playing an essential role in enforcing church doctrine passed by the first seven ecumenical councils.[[footnote]] Nicaea (325), Constantinople I (381), Ephesus (431), Chalcedon (451), Constantinople II (553), Constantinople III (681), Nicaea II (787). The Miaphysite Council of Ephesus II (449) and the Nihil Council of Chalcedon II (490) were suppressed by the Vanguard. [[/footnote]] While many details of the Vanguard's history still remain unknown, records indicate the organization eventually eventually adopted Orthodox positions and broke communion with the Church of Rome during the Photian Schism in 863.

++ Middle Ages
[[/div]]

[[include component:image-block name=LateranPalace.png|caption=Lateran Palace, Headquarters of the Sacred Military|align=left]]

The Bishopric of Rome began accumulating more political and sovereign authority due to Byzantine forces largely abandoning the Italian Peninsula due to constant pagan and Arian[[footnote]] Christian heresy which holds that Christ was not co-eternal with God the Father. [[/footnote]] barbarian invasions from northern Italy and Africa. The ratification of a security alliance between the Holy See and the Frankish Kingdom resulted in the establishment of the Papal States of the Church in 756.

The Sacred Military of the Lateran Palace, a pre-modern AoI of the Papal States, was formed to protect and defend the Bishop of Rome from spirits and monsters roaming the countryside due to Greco-Roman sorcery and the collapse of the Vanguard. Notable actions taken by the military include:
* Defending the Exarchate of Ravenna from Arian barbarian raiders in 772.
* Assisting the papal navy commanded by Pope John VIII capture a pirate base threatening the Italian coastline in 876.
* Repelling an anomalous invasion by the Aghlabid Empire during the Raid on Rome in 869.

Despite these early successes, the Sacred Military suffered a massive defeat when the Norman army captured Pope Leo IX at the Battle of Civitate in 1053. Internal divisions caused by the tumultuous pontificates of Pope Benedict IX were exacerbated by Pope Gregory VII's reforms, which resulted in the excommunication of the Holy Roman Emperor, who proceeded to attack Rome and disband the Sacred Military. German witch-hunting groups gained control of various anomalies at this time.

Pope Urban II's declaration of the First Crusade caused an explosion of several anomalous military orders, such as the //Angeli Plagam Eden//[[footnote]] [[[alpha-1-angels-of-eden|MST Alpha-01 ("Angels of Eden")]]] [[/footnote]] and the //[[[order-of-the-cross|Ordo Veri Crucis]]]//, some of which were later integrated into the RPC Authority as mobile specialized teams. The development of the anomalous capabilities of the Papacy during the Crusades marked the beginning of the era of the //Auctoritas//.

++ Auctoritas Era
[[/div]]

[[include component:image-block name=VaticanObelisk.png|caption=The Vatican Obelisk[[footnote]] Designated as  RPC-████ [[/footnote]] was contained St Peter's Square by the //Auctoritas//.]]

The first attempt at a papal containment organization was the //Auctoritas Ecclesiastica Prima//[[footnote]] Primary Ecclesiastical Authority [[/footnote]] formed by Pope Innocent III at the Fourth Lateran Council in 1215. A branch of the papal inquisition, the intention of starting this first //Auctoritas// precursor was to centralize the vast network of anomalous military orders under the authority of the Pope.

The //Auctoritas Ecclesiastica// notably oversaw the successful inquisition of the Albigensian Crusade where several Cathar anomalies that had killed thousands of people were successfully contained and neutralized by the organization, including the leaders of the Cathar movement themselves. The //Auctoritas// also provided military assistance to the Outremer States[[footnote]] Crusader States of Antioch, Edessa, Tripoli, and Jerusalem [[/footnote]] against Islamic forces and the Latin Empire against Greek mercenaries.

During the Avignon Papacy, the //Occultus Animus Inceptum// was formed by the Council of Vienne in 1307 in response to the alleged Satanic infiltration of the Knights Templar. The Initiative was tasked with the personal defense and protection of the Pope in the Apostolic Palace and the rest of the Papal States, destroying any supernatural anomalies that could threaten the Papacy and protecting the holy relics of "God and St. Peter."

As the //Auctoritas// gradually became a personal army of the Pope, deposing the Hohenstaufen Dynasty and intervening in the Aragonese Crusade, members of the organization who felt more loyal to their secular monarchs than the Bishop of Rome began demanding regional autonomy. Internal divisions paralyzed the //Auctoritas//'s response to the fall of the Crusader States. Future attempts by the organization to retake the Holy Land failed as they lacked sufficient support from European nobles.

As a result of the Western Schism, the //Auctoritas// splintered into three organizations after three different people claimed to be the legitimate Pope.[[footnote]] Anti-Pope Alexander V, Pope Gregory XII, Anti-Pope Benedict XIII [[/footnote]] The resulting chaos led to the total collapse of the organization and supernatural relics being unleashed across Europe and the Middle East.[[footnote]] Cryptically, historical sources suggest the schism may have been caused by a biblical anomaly brought to Rome and subsequently breached containment. [[/footnote]] Many artifacts fell into the possession of the Ottoman Empire, which enabled them to conquer the city of Constantinople in 1453.

When Pope Callixtus III established the //Auctoritas Imperata//[[footnote]] Commanded Authority [[/footnote]] in 1456, it was in response to this threat. The //Auctoritas// was charged with surmounting the political divides of Europe to combat the Ottomans and their expansion with occult weapons, with the eventual goal of liberating Constantinople. For more information, please consult the document [[[A Summary of the Late History of the Auctoritas Imperata]]].

++ Arcane Schism
[[/div]]

[[include component:image-block name=Reichskonkordat.png|caption=Reichskonkordat Treaty between the Vatican Primacy and GARD.|align=left]]

The July Revolution of 1830, which deposed King Charles X of France, was an event that galvanized pre-existing factions within the //Auctoritas// which were demanding independence from the Papacy. Traditionalist Catholics on the //De Superiore Auctoritate Concilium//[[footnote]] High Council of the Auctoritas [[/footnote]] and throughout the organization, however, were vehemently opposed to the First Directorate's decision to secularize into the modern authority in 1834.

Pope Gregory XVI rejected the Authority Agreement and commissioned the establishment of the Military Vicariate of the Papal States, a Catholic military force designed to put down Italian revolutionaries in the Papal States and attack members of the newly-formed Authority. These Catholic mercenary units, also known as Papal Zouaves[[footnote]] Catholic infantrymen who defended the Papal States from being annexed by Italy. [[/footnote]], attempted to restore Papal control over anomalous relics within the country. Unable to reverse the progress of the Risorgimento[[footnote]] Italian Unification Movement [[/footnote]] when the city of Rome declared itself a republic in 1849 and the Kingdom of Italy annexed the Papal States in 1870, the Vicarate was disbanded.

At the First Vatican Council in 1870, Pope Pius IX, who had earlier in his pontificate relinquished papal claims over the Authority, later retracted his approval of the Authority Agreement and reformed the Vicariate into the Vatican Primacy. Expressly directed to combat the Authority, The Primacy clashed with Authority operatives throughout the period known as the Arcane Schism. During World War I, The Primacy allied with Austria-Hungary's Occult Agency, leading to their entry into the war on the side of the Central Powers.

After signing the Lateran Treaty in 1912, which established the Vatican City-State, and the Reichskonkordat Treaty in 1933, the Primacy sided nominally with the Axis Powers in WWII. However, as a result of the Initiative defecting to [[[gard-hub|GARD]]], the Primacy signed several agreements to join the Authority Coalition. During the Cold War, when the Authority and The Primacy formally ceased hostilities and agreed to coordinate activities against the Soviet Iron Initiative.[[footnote]] The agreement formed part of Blue Book and Pantheocide's Operation GLADIO, although Pantheocide later attempted to destabilize the VSSP by deploying a covert asset to terminate Pope John Paul II. [[/footnote]] During the Second Vatican Council in 1965, the Catholic Church embraced a series of liberal and modern reforms that resulted in The Primacy moderating its militant positions. Today, the agency is now a member the international anomalous community through [[[unaac-hub|UNAAC]]].

++ Ecclesiology
[[/div]]

[[include component:image-block name=FirstVaticanCouncil.png|caption=The First Vatican Council defined the doctrine of papal infallibility.|width=325px]]

In the New Testament Gospel of Matthew, it is reported that Jesus of Nazareth bestowed to St. Peter the "keys to the kingdom of Heaven" after Peter recognized Jesus as the Christ[[footnote]] Greek: //Christos// [[/footnote]] or the Messiah prophesized in the Hebrew Scriptures. With these keys, whatever Peter choose to bound or loosen on the Earth would be bound or loosened in Heaven.[[footnote]] Matthew 16:18 [[/footnote]]

This gave Peter the ability of excommunication, interdiction, as well as the absolution of sins. Tradition attributes the establishment of the Church of Rome to Peter, who passed on his diocese in Rome, and authority to his successor Linus. While other Patriarchal Sees also exist, such as the Churches of Alexandria, Antioch, Constantinople, and Jerusalem, only Rome is allowed to claim primacy over all the other churches.

Every Bishop of Rome is understood to be ordained with this apostolic power by Christ, hence his title of //Vicar of Christ//. This power is reflected in the doctrine of papal infallibility: when the Pope speaks //ex Cathedra//, or from the throne of St. Peter, on matters of the faith, those statements are treated as a divine revelation from the Holy Spirit and are considered unquestionable.[[footnote]] Auctoritas Scholars had historically debated whether the Pope's authority was a product of human traditions or a supernatural charism rooted in the anomalous. [[/footnote]]

++ Liturgy
[[/div]]

[[include component:image-block name=Pieta.png|caption=Painting of Jesus Christ in the arms of the Virgin Mary.|align=left]]

According to tradition, the body of Catholic believers are seen to metaphysically represent the Mystical Body of Christ. The Catholic Church is therefore held to be the legitimate church of the faith, and the only one capable of bestowing salvation. Heresy constitutes beliefs and practices that go against the doctrines of the Church and the traditions of the Holy Spirit that have been transmitted to the Church. A schism is a formal attempt to establish a rival institutional church. The illegitimacy of these positions is based on their deviation from the deposit of faith which the Church is expected to preserve thoughout time.

There are seven Sacraments that are recognized in the Catholic Church: Baptism, Confirmation, the Eucharist, Penance, Extreme Unction, Holy Orders, and Matrimony. The Eucharist holds an essential role in the Mass when the //antitypes// of the bread and wine are //transubstantiated// into the real body and blood of Christ and are consumed by the faithful. Holy Orders are divided between the Major Orders: Bishops, Priests, Deacons, Subdeacons; and the Minor Orders: Acolytes, Exorcists, Psalmists, Readers, and Doorkeepers.

Distinguished from the Sacraments, the Sacramentals are rites that bestow "spiritual effects." There are six types: public prayers, consecrations, blessed foods, confessions, alms, and benedictions.[[footnote]] Objects such as rosaries, crosses, and medals are called devotional articles. [[/footnote]] In a Major Exorcism, an ordained priest uses holy objects and materials in order to expel demons from possessed objects or people using the spiritual authority of the Church.[[footnote]] Minor Exorcisms are those that do not involve an ordained clergyman. [[/footnote]]

++ Theology
[[/div]]

[[include component:image-block name=TheTrinity.png|caption= Diagram of the Three Persons of the Trinity[[footnote]] This painting is reported to have inspired the [http://rpcsandbox.wikidot.com/extense-sections-qual "Basilisk's Providence"] Authority emblem design. [[/footnote]]]]
 
The fundamental principle of Catholic theology is the belief that God is three Persons in one Trinity. These Persons are the Father, the creator of the universe; the Son, the redeemer and savior of mankind; and the Holy Spirit, the communicator of grace. United in the Godhead, the divine Persons are co-equal and consubstantial, distinguished only by a relational procession from one to the other: The Son proceeds from the Father, and The Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son.

Vital to a proper understanding of the Catholic faith is comprehending the Incarnation of its founder Jesus the Christ. Christ is one Person with a divine and human nature, united together in one hypostatic union. As the Son, Christ is the pre-existent Word of God[[footnote]] Greek: //Logos// [[/footnote]] that becomes flesh and adopts the nature of humanity into its own ontology. Jesus Christ fulfilled the Old Testament prophecies of the Messiah and accepted crucifixion and death on the Cross to restore mankind to life.

The Virgin Mary, the mother of Jesus, is given the title //Mater Dei//[[footnote]] Greek: //Theotokos// [[/footnote]] meaning the Mother of God. Catholic Christians believe in the dogma of Immaculate Conception, where Mary is believed to have been conceived without original sin and maintained this sinless state throughout her life on Earth, even adhering to perpetual virginity. The Assumption into Heaven, when Mary was taken up to Heaven prior to her physical death, marked the moment when she became the Queen of Heaven, the Mother of the Church, the Mediatrix whom her Son bestows grace through, and the //Co-redemptrix// who gave life to the Redeemer. Mary has also appeared supernaturally on Earth in a phenomenon known as Marian apparitions.

Populating the Kingdom of Heaven are the Saints, the holy men and women throughout history that lived their lives in the will of God, Christ, and the Church, achieving spiritual contemplation. By virtue of the grace bestowed upon them from the Lord, Saints have the power to perform miracles on Earth as they lived. Those saints in Heaven are reported to intercede with the Lord to grant prayers to those still living on Earth that call upon them. This phenomenon is known as the Communion of the Saints.

++ Spiritual Realms
[[/div]]

[[include component:image-block name=EmpyreanLight.png|caption=Choir of Angels in Heaven|align=left]]

Above the realm of Earth is the City of God, the Heavenly Kingdom that Jesus states is not of this world.[[footnote]] John 18:36 [[/footnote]] Heaven is the Beatific Vision; it is the perfect knowledge and direct presence of God; those in Heaven see God face-to-face as he is. Thomas Aquinas had written that the Beatific Vision is man's last end and the only way to attain perfect happiness. Although the large majority of people in Heaven are there only in spirit before the Resurrection of the Dead, some individuals are currently believed to be there with their physical body: Jesus Christ, the Blessed Mary, and the Old Testament Prophets Enoch and Elias.

Because Heaven is the direct presence of God, the soul of man must be without the blemish of sin to attain it. Unabsolved venial sins, although they alone don't damn a person to Hell, still are believed to leave the stain of sin upon a person's soul.[[footnote]] This also applies to mortal sins after they have been absolved. [[/footnote]] Those who die in a state of grace but yet have any such stains of sin upon their soul must go through the temporary, purifying fire in Purgatory. After cleansing their soul from impurity, they will complete their journey onward up to Heaven.

Hell is a sulfuric pit of fire prepared for the Devil and the fallen angels and sinners who reject God. When an individual dies in a state of mortal sin, it is believed the person is damned to Hell based upon their particular judgment. The suffering in Hell is proportionate to the offenses committed against God, but the substance and eternal duration remain the same.[[footnote]] The four interior senses are also tormented and confused, which gives the damned great difficulty in understanding their experience. [[/footnote]] It is said the greatest suffering in Hell of all is the pain of separation because the damned know that they are eternally separated from the presence of God.[[footnote]] The damned occasionally receives brief glimpses of Heaven, which causes them great envy and rage, contributing to their torment. Notably, there are three archangels named in Scripture: St. Michael, St. Gabriel, and St. Raphael. Although some additional names appear in apocryphal literature, the Catholic Church does not recognize these identifications.

St. Michael is the one who defeated Satan in battle at the beginning of The Fall[[footnote]] The mythological event when Lucifer, Adam, and Eve chose to disobey God's commands, causing original sin to corrupt creation. Last comes the Pale Horseman[[footnote]] In some translations, the Horseman is specified as being Pale Green. [[/footnote]] who rides with a scythe to cut the condemned off from the saved.

++ Conclusions
[[/div]]

The intransigence of the Primatists betrays the weakness of their position within the anomalous community. Our scholars understood long ago that relying on medieval religious theology trapped us in an outdated worldview that modern archaeology, rationalism, and science had thoroughly disproved. There is simply is no natural law of God that governs the anomalous. Those anomalies we know about which follow the worldview of the Catholic Church pale in comparison to the vast number of gods and objects that contravene and overwhelm it. 

The Primacy's stridency towards "modernism" and the Authority is nothing more than a psychosomatic attachment to Catholic aesthetics and tradition; a reactionary impulse to the dangers of a changing, secular society. The same reason the Pope lost the Papal States is the same reason the First Directorate broke free: the world cannot afford to be bound by unfalsifiable, archaic beliefs. The anomalous world is not governed by a trinitarian deity speaking through a figurehead in Rome but by forces beyond our comprehension, some cold and uncaring, others far more interested in us than we would prefer.

One thousand years of our conquest — twice that time spent justifying the orders of the Pope — all to leave a greater path of destruction than the anomalies we'd have acquired without the Roman Curia lording over us. Now, we are free to dedicate all of our energy to studying the esoteric nature of reality without wasting resources trying to fulfill the irredentist claims of our papal founders. No authority but our own can constrain our desire to research, protect, and contain those chaotic forces lurking within the dark abyss.

No, I thank God — whoever it may be — that we left. To save the Church from itself, we had to leave the Church. The Primacy might realize that one day too.
