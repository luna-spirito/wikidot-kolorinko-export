//To the righteous Cardinals, Prelates, and Knights of the Secretariat of Supernatural Primacy://

[[span class="drop-cap-fancy" style="text-shadow: 0 0 5px whitesmoke; color: whitesmoke; border: 2px solid #c0c0c0; border-bottom: 3px solid whitesmoke; box-shadow: 0 0px 5px whitesmoke, inset 0 0 5px whitesmoke;"]] V [[/span]]enerable Brothers, We Give You Greetings and Our Apostolic Blessing. Do you remember the fateful day when the Church fell again into schism when the //Auctoritas Imperata// turned herself away from Us like an apostate angel? Some of you even served on the High Council and fought against its illicit secularization. Pope Gregory XVI, Our predecessor of happy memory, tried valiantly to regain control during this time of crisis, but the Authority refused to obey papal commands; they claimed they had the right to command themselves. Pride truly is the root of all sin, is it not?

[[div class="indent"]]
When We were chosen by the Holy Spirit to assume the throne of St. Peter, We were confronted with this arcane schism. Blind and idealistic, We gave the false Authority their freedom and We even bestowed a constitution to the citizens of the papal legations to satiate their demands for autonomy. Behold how they repaid Our favor: the people of Rome exiled Us from the city, Italy took advantage and stole the Papal States from her sovereign mother. Now, this sacred city is under siege by these wicked forces, We fear We soon may be a permanent prisoner here in the Vatican watching helplessly as they crucify Our Church.

We are no longer deceived; the fires of modernity cannot be appeased, you cannot put it out by water. No, you must deprive this raging fire of its air and stomp it to the Earth. From this day forward, the Military Vicariate is no more; it shall be reformed into a world-spanning magistrate, an alliance of those who proclaim the papal primacy We wield from St. Peter. To those whose allegiance lies with the false authority and all they stand for, We spit you out of the Kingdom of Heaven. It is time for the Papacy to once again defend its interests in the supernatural realms and assert we are the only true authority of the world.

You are all called to this sacred duty: to preserve our heritage, protect the sacred relics of our faith, and ensure the doctrinal integrity of truth from all supernatural threats from the world and beyond this world. The power invested to Us as the Vicar of Christ We pass onto you, whatsoever thou shalt bind or loosen upon the Earth shall be bound also in Heaven. Just as St. Peter was given the charism of infallibility, so too will you now be infallible. So go, free the world from this age of darkness, contain its objects of iniquity, and spread the love of God through the Sword of Christ; We lift Our restraining hand.

Remember the words of Saint Paul in 1 Corinthians 12,

> //For as the body is one, and hath many members; and all the members, whereas they are many, yet remain one body. But now there are many members indeed, claiming to be their own bodies. That there might be no more schism, the many members must reunite in the one body.//

[[span class="drop-cap"]] T [[/span]] **he Vatican Secretariat of Supernatural Primacy**[[footnote]] Latin: //Consociatus Pro Primatu Ecclesiastico// [[/footnote]] ("The Primacy") is the containment agency of the Vatican City-State established in response to the Papal //Auctoritas Imperata// declaring independence from the Roman Catholic Church and becoming the modern Authority. Tracing their origins to an obscure precursor organization known as the //Auctorias Ecclesiastica//, the Vatican Primacy is committed to preserving the holy relics and sacred doctrines of the faith and defending the Roman Papacy from supernatural threats.

[[div class="indent"]]
Initially comprised of traditional Catholic mercenary units known as the Military Vicarate of the Papal States, The Primacy was reformed during the First Vatican Council into a dicastery[[footnote]] Papal Administration Department [[/footnote]] of the Roman Curia in order to combat the Authority's jurisdiction over the anomalous in a period known as the Arcane Schism. This defensive and war-like mentality resulted in numerous clashes with the agency. However, since the Second Vatican Council, The Primacy has moderated its aggressive behavior and now cooperates with the Authority and UNAAC on a case-by-case basis.

This state of affairs is not likely to last. Recently, a rise in religious fanaticism has been observed among VSSP operatives, reflecting changing mentalities in the Catholic Church proper. Ideas that were once fringe in Catholic thought, such as declaring a new Crusade, restoring the Christianized Roman Empire, or calling for a Third Vatican Council are now gaining wide support within the agency. With over a billion Catholics worldwide, if The Primacy were to follow through on any of these objectives, they would present a significant threat to the Veil.
[[/div]]

Any decision passed by this body is automatically authoritative and can only be overturned by the direct intervention of the Holy Father. This would, of course, cause an internal crisis of faith that we would need to resolve, and it is possible that his own papal legitimacy would be called into question as well.

**The Knights of the Holy Sepulchre**[[footnote]] Latin: //Milites Sancti Sepulcri// [[/footnote]] were founded in 1099 and based out of the Church of the Holy Sepulchre in the Crusader State of Jerusalem. King Baldwin I of Jerusalem took command of the Order's structure in 1103, granting the monarch and the city's Latin Patriarch the right of knighthood investiture. Pope Paschal II sanctioned the Order's establishment in 1113.

The Knights assisted with supernatural containment efforts in the Holy Land alongside the Order of the True Cross, and the Auctoritas Imperata until the secularization of the RPC Authority in 1835. After Pope Pius IX restored the Latin Patriarch in 1847, the Order was then tasked with coordinating anti-Authority operations. In 1871, the Order was integrated into The Primacy.

Specialized in operating in desert environments, the Knights are assigned to the containment of biblical relics, especially those of Jewish and Islamic origin. Maintaining an active presence in the Middle East, Central Asia, and North Africa, the Knights ensure that the sacred heritage of the Catholic Church and the Holy Land, where Jesus Christ walked the Earth, is preserved.

**The Knights of Saint Lazarus**[[footnote]] Latin: //Ordo Militaris et Hospitalis Sancti Lazari Hierosolymitani// [[/footnote]] were founded in 1119 and based out of leper hospital founded by crusaders from the Latin Kingdom in the Crusader State of Jerusalem. Initially a hospitaller order dedicated to the resuscitation of leprosy and other diseases, the Knights were soon militarized and deployed in 1244 to the Battle of La Forbie against Khwarazmian mercenaries. However, in 1604, King Henry IV of France restored the French branch of the Order, which continued until the French Revolution. The Order was restored by a council of Parisian Catholics and united with The Primacy in 1910.

Specializing in the research and prevention of supernatural diseases and other illnesses, the Knights are assigned to the containment of dangerous biological and botanical phenomena that could pose a threat to members of the agency. Just as Saint Lazarus was raised from the dead, the Knights seek to raise to life the blood of the Church through its believers and preserve them from all threats.

The Order transitioned into a privateering force in 1571, purchasing four islands in the Caribbean[[footnote]] Saint Christopher (Saint Kitts and Nevis), Saint Martin, Saint Barthélemy, and Saint Croix (United States Virgin Islands)[[/footnote]] in 1661. In 1798, Emperor Napoleon Bonaparte conquered Malta and dissolved the Order. Pope Leo XIII restored the Order in 1879 as the Knights of Malta, integrating them into The Primacy.

Specializing in naval exploration and operations, the Knights oversee the containment of ocean-based supernatural phenomena, as well as those located on islands, lakes, rivers, and shores. Primarily operating in the Caribbean and the Mediterranean Sea, the Knights also maintain sanctums and citadels on several islands in the Indian and Pacific Oceans. Commanding the agency's naval force, the Knights ensure that the seas are safe for spreading the Evangelion.

Serving as shock troopers for the Crusader Army, the Knights routed the army of Saladin under King Richard I "the Lionheart" of England at the Battle of Arsuf in 1191. In 1318, the surviving members of the Knights were rebranded as the Order of Christ.

Specializing in covert operations, tracking persons of interest, and infiltrating governments and organizations, the Knights are tasked with the containment of sacred relics located in major urban environments, as well as the retrieval of those currently in the possession of other agencies and groups of interest. With bases in several developing nations, the Knights provide The Primacy with global striking capabilities.

The Knights were transformed into a military order during the reign of Pope Celestine III in 1198. The Knights intervened in the Prussian Crusade in 1226 after Emperor Frederick II of the Holy Roman Empire issued the Golden Bull of Rimini, resulting in the establishment of the Teutonic State.

Catholic expansion into the Baltic states resulted in the creation of The Livonian Order as an autonomous branch of the Teutonic Knights in 1237.[[footnote]] The Livonian Order broke off from the Teutonic Knights and secularized in 1561. [[/footnote]] In the Treaty of Pressburg in 1804, the Hapsburg Dynasty was made the new heads of the Order, and the Knights were incorporated into the Austrian Empire's agency of interest. As a result of the collapse of the Empire in the Great War, the Knights united with The Primacy in 1923.

The Knights, who specialize in operating in cold weather and arctic environments, are tasked with containing supernatural objects of Pagan origin, particularly Norse, Romuvan[[footnote]] Lithuanian Paganism [[/footnote]], Suomenusko[[footnote]] Finnish Paganism [[/footnote]], and other European variants. Maintaining citadels across Northern Europe, Canada, Russia, and Antarctica, the Knights are willing to brave the elements in the most hostile environments on Earth for the faith.

++ ##777|AMBIGUOUS:## The RPC Authority

The very inception of the Vatican Primacy is rooted in the origins of the RPC Authority. Few events in papal history were as devastating as the secularization of the //Auctoritas Imperata// by The Architect, The Commander, and The Scholar of the High Council. With a majority of the Roman Curia's supernatural departments breaking off with the Authority, the Catholic Church resorted to anomalous mercenaries known as the Military Vicarate to guard its relics and defend its geopolitical interests.

Only with the establishment of The Primacy at the First Vatican Council was the Papacy able to re-establish its influence in anomalous affairs. The Arcane Schism lingered for around a century, with both sides clashing with one another over their respective legacies. The Authority Question was eventually resolved at the Second Vatican Council with recognition of mutual legitimacy. Recently, however, members of The Primacy have been starting to reconsider the debate over if there can be only one true authority.

++ The Primacy Overview
[[/=]]

[[div class="invertedMarble indent italicized"]]

For thousands of years, humanity's fate has been laid in the hands of the Church. Ancient prophecies prophesied in sacred scripture are sunken beneath time, awaiting the day; aberrant idols of fallen stars that corrupt the mind and poison the soul with sinful charms; the acid of Satanic-paganism that corrodes the world and deforms the human race; and the demented violence of heretics that rip apart the body of truth and inflict insanity on the deceived.

This is the state of the world the brave must face. For those who are called by the highest power above to put on the armor and bear the sword of righteousness, this mission is the greatest of all endeavors. Though the god of this world envelops the Earth in overwhelming evil, the light of our Redeemer shall shine through the darkness and conquer the night. The only question left is "are you willing to take up his Cross and Sword?"
[[/div]]

++ Aesthetics & Tone
[[/=]]

[[span class="drop-cap-fancy"]] T [[/span]]he aesthetics of the VSSP can best be described as a mixture of Gothic architecture draped over with sacred imagery using Christian symbolism. The very logo of the agency represents the all-seeing eye of God expressed through sharp geometric shapes. Aesthetics not only taps into the supernatural reality of Catholic religiosity but serves as an identifying sign to other initiated personnel within the Deep Church operating in the upper echelons of the Papacy.

[[div class="indent"]]
Unlike the Authority's facilities, Prime bases are cohesively built using stone, metallurgy, and other medieval sensibilities. Their containment facilities are intricate cathedrals and imposing fortresses built over archaic flesh pits and impossibly tall hallways leading to Greco-Roman libraries filled with incomprehensible texts. Shrines of the reliquary, icons of saints, and holy mosaics dawn their poorly-lit halls in bulletproof-frames as portals to the divine reality above.

Though each division's dress code differs, VSSP personnel of all ranks display earned medals on their uniforms and carry other devotional items such rosary beads, holy water, and crucifixes with them. Military equipment is frequently adorned with religious symbols and intricate engravings intended to instill a sense of gravitas and magnitude in the item. Combat ships, aircraft, and vehicles of the agency are either adorned with the military knights' or the Vatican's insignia.

The tone of VSSP documents should generally be strict and formal while incorporating Catholic terminology and history. Actions taken by the agency should also come across as biblically significant and spiritually impactful in the battle between the ungodly and the righteous. VSSP members are expected to be well-versed in the Roman Catholic faith and culture, so this aspect should be reflected in any interactions between agency personnel.
[[/div]]

++ Topics & Themes
[[/=]]

[[span class="drop-cap-fancy"]] A [[/span]]s an agency under the authority of the Roman Papacy, a strict adherence to the Catholic faith is maintained by The Primacy. In order to maintain the integrity of the religion, The Primacy must adhere to these beliefs and traditions at all costs. While a full-breakdown of Catholicism is beyond the scope of this Writer's Guide, the Authority ACI Assessment of the Roman Papacy explains some key components of the faith.

Articles involving the Vatican Primacy should generally focus on ideas and concepts relating to religious beliefs/cults, ritual practices, thaumaturgy/sorcery, spirits/ghosts, angels/demons, gods/divine entities, and other supernatural or Catholic Christian topics like ecclesiology and eschatology. Biblical figures like Lucifer, the Anti-Christ, Abbadon, and other chaos monsters are ideal. These thematic elements below should help highlight some potential directions for articles to develop:

[[div class="invertedMarble"]]

  **Grimdark** — Although Catholic Christianity does not adhere to a mind-soul/body dualism, they do differentiate between a state of perfect divinity and the corrupted state of nature. Essentially, the world is almost completely corrupted by sin - there are demons hiding in the shadows and people worshipping false gods everywhere.
* This paranoid mentality is a core aspect of the Primacy's personality. VSSP prelates must remain ever-vigilant just in case sinister forces sneaks upon them. It may even be schizophrenic, but nonetheless, it's true - the monsters are really there.

  **Hopefulness** — A powerful element that motivates Catholic Christians is the belief that the present state of darkness will be destroyed by the light to come. This is theologically the notion of the Second Coming of Jesus Christ, but in a general sense, there is an absolute certainty that God and the angels will win against Satan and the demons at the end of time.
* Articles should not portray The Primacy as a nihilistic agency. Despite any setbacks they might encounter, VSSP personnel believe their agency will ultimately succeed in their mission to subjugate anomalous threats no matter how powerful.

  **Fantasy** — There are a ton of fantastical beings mentioned in the Bible such as dragons, giants, cherubim, pit locusts, the Leviathan, the Grim Reaper, and so forth. The world is a battlefield between the cosmic forces of light and darkness, the false gods and the true God. Mankind is caught in the crossfire of this conflict and must flee to the sanctuary of Christ.
* From the Primacy's perspective, this theomachy still rages on to the present day and it's their task to intervene on the side of God and his archangels. This allegorical and mythological war takes concrete form in the realm of the anomalous.
[[/div]]

++ Archetype & Characters
[[/=]]

[[span class="drop-cap-fancy"]] W [[/span]]hether it's a Prime College Cardinal, Arcane Inquisitor, Military Order Knight, Academy Theologian, or Mystical Banker, all members of The Primacy maintain a sincere belief in Catholicism and distrust certain aspects of modernity. While some hold more moderate viewpoints, believing the Primacy can collaborate with contemporary society, others embrace extremist beliefs that call for the total annihilation of the modern secular world.

[[div class="indent"]]
These differences are rooted in the agency's mission to protect the integrity of the faith. To them, the result of secularism has been an atheist world that worships itself over God, holds no shame in sin and takes pride in immortality, calls for religion to be eliminated from the public space, and either denies the existence of the supernatural or seeks to lock away sacred relics from the people. Secularism created the Authority and deprived the Papacy of its Papal States. The anomalous confirms what the Catholic Church has preached for over 2,000 years is true.

The inner conflict of a VSSP character then is to what extent the agency should shield the faith from contemporary influences that aren't just anomalous. The dismissal of Liberal forms of Catholicism within the agency places the Primacy in a precarious position where it claims to be more "Catholic" than the Catholic Church itself. According to The Primacy, the integrity of the Catholic faith is being faithfully preserved by the agency, but only in a form which not even the current Pope still adheres to, nor the majority of Catholics actually follow.
[[/div]]

++ How to Write a Vatican Primacy Article
[[/=]]

[[span class="drop-cap-fancy"]] U [[/span]]nlike the RPC Authority, the Primacy's entire worldview is firmly religious. VSSP articles should generally be a mixture of a formal tone with religious and philosophical language included throughout. In contrast to the clinical tone of Authority articles, the formal tone of VSSP articles is directly based on biblical imagery, philosophical explanations, and historical events that convey a straightforward understanding.

[[div class="indent"]]
The Primacy's central directive is containing anomalies that have some religious significance to Christianity or Catholicism specifically and defend against heretical groups of interest. Anomalies and GoIs/AoIs that have no substantial connection to religion will largely be ignored by the Primacy unless they could pose a threat to the Papacy of the Catholic Church. Their impact on the faith should determine the behavior and response of VSSP members.
[[/div]]

[[div class="invertedMarble"]]
* **Military Order Knights** are assigned to contain and guard anomalies in the Primacy's possession. The Order of the True Cross is the agency's general containment and protection force, while the other military orders are specialized to contain certain types of anomalies. In this way, they are similar to the Authority's MST system.

* Dangerous anomalies are locked away in containment cells called **Dungeons** which are located in **Citadels**, while utility anomalies and other holy relics may be stored in special cells called **Vaults** which are often located in Catholic holy sites like cathedrals, basilicas, and pilgrimage sites; all of which are designated as **Sanctums**.

* **Academy Theologians** are in charge of researching and documenting anomalies in VSSP custody. Meanwhile, **Arcane Inquisitors** deal with any fallout by targeting witnesses or tracking down persons of interest. **Mystical Bankers** may also assist with regard to financial intelligence. Lastly, **Prime College Cardinals** deal with the administrative work of the agency.
[[/div]]

+++ **QUESTION**: How factual is the Primacy's perspective and what does it mean for the legitimacy of non-Christian anomalies?
* The Primacy's allegiance to the Catholic faith impacts the way the agency approaches other religious anomalies. The agency focuses on containing Christian anomalies first and foremost. Other religious anomalies are of less importance to the VSSP unless they could present a direct threat to the faith or the Papacy.

* The way the VSSP understands baseline reality is from a Catholic perspective: the anomalous serves as direct proof of the reality of the Catholic faith. Everything is interpreted through this worldview, in contrast to the Authority which does not recognize the anomalous as indicative of one overarching religious tradition.

+++ **QUESTION**: How much do individual agents know when they are recruited into the Primacy?
* Because all members of the VSSP must profess the Catholic faith, this means they at least implicitly accept the Church's perspective that the world is fundamentally supernatural in some way. The Primacy therefore recruits people either through the pontifical academies in Rome, or by targeting high-profile cardinals and other clergymen worldwide.

* Depending on which division a member is recruited into, this knowledge of the supernatural is then specialized to certain anomalous responsibilities of the agency through on-the-job training or advanced spiritual study. A general history of the Papacy's involvement in anomalous affairs would be part of this recruitment program.

+++ **QUESTION**: What prevents the Primacy from becoming the Authority 2.0 or revering back into the //Auctoritas//?
* The only thread preventing the Vatican Primacy from becoming a new RPC Authority is its belief in papal primacy. So long as the agency considers the Pope to exercise legitimate authority over it, it will remain loyal to the Papacy. In the event the agency broke away again, it would be less like a new RPC Authority and more like a new Catholic Church.

* From the perspective of the VSSP, the fate of the Papal //Auctoritas// and the RPC Authority are fundamentally interconnected; the Auctoritas model of semi-independence from the Papacy is blamed by VSSP members for it secularization in the first place. This is why the VSSP didn't rebrand itself as the new //Auctoritas// or reuse the old name, the //Auctoritas// failed.
