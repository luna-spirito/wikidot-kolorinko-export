**Registered Phenomena Code:** 470

**Object Class:** Omega-White

**Hazard Types:** Digital Hazard, Ideological Hazard, Info-Hazard, Mind-Control Hazard
[[/div]]

------

**Containment Protocols:** All Authority Web Crawlers dedicated to detecting RPC-470 are to do tri-daily sweeps of all social media and other communication platforms on the internet to scan for RPC-470-positive content. Any content that is given a rating of 20% or higher as being RPC-470-positive is to be purged and if the site is not a central social media platform (Twitter, Facebook, etc.), the website is to be taken down for at least 10 minutes to scan for any other potential RPC-470 vectors.

An active misinformation campaign against the existence of RPC-470, in which it is framed as a conspiracy theory based on a canceled alternate reality game, is to be maintained throughout the internet as a secondary safeguard against the anomaly.

Civilian pilgrimages to the Antarctic continent are to be disrupted without direct interference if possible. Possible redirection and subtle suggestion/redirection to official Antarctic touring groups is encouraged.

**Containment Addendum -- Operation ICE WALKER:** Under no circumstances are ANTARCOM personnel to approach the exclusion zone surrounding the Sentinel Mountain Range.

The website @@"http://www.fallenangels.███"@@ has been quarantined.

------

**Description:** RPC-470 refers to a tandem of behavioral phenomena relating to the Antarctic Continent. These specific behaviors can be roughly divided into two distinct sets:

# The development of a conscious desire to travel to the Antarctic Continent or any specific area or geographical feature of it, which exhibits certain compulsive characteristics.
# The development of social groups of people that share the previous desire and cooperate to that end, justifying it with various reasons that are often created after the establishment of the group.

RPC-470 possesses poorly understood memetic qualities that allow it to spread among persons that are aware of its existence,[[footnote]] If not necessarily its anomalous properties: being aware that specific individuals or groups desire to travel to the Antarctic is sufficient to trigger an infection, with effectiveness proportional to the number of members of such a group. [[/footnote]] although the effective infectiousness of the anomaly is inconsistent; it is not known what determines a successful infection.

Despite its compulsive properties, RPC-470 does not guarantee that an individual will travel to the Antarctic or develop an obsession with such a prospect. Awareness of RPC-470's status as anomalous or self-doubt regarding the possibility of travel to the Antarctic is often sufficient to dispel its effects, although relapse is relatively common temporarily.

RPC-470's potency increases with the size of groups of infected that actively display its effects. At sizes greater than 20 individuals, non-invasive methods are incapable of combatting the infection; attempts to induce doubt or prevent such groups from operating together will result in members reaffirming each other of their desire and reconstituting with whichever means are available to cooperate (I.e. message services).

RPC-470 is especially prevalent across social media, having found much greater success than other means. It is believed that this is an indirect result of the non-committal nature of online communities and the spread of information therein.

A common reoccurrence in RPC-470 media is a figure known as "The Companion". Whether drawn or in ASCII Art form[[footnote]] A form of graphic design where images are created using digital text characters. [[/footnote]], the design is consistent: a front-facing depiction of an angel with pointed feet.

The reason for the Companion's prevalence in RPC-470-related media is entirely unknown, but it has increased the efficiency of locating infected groups. It is hypothesized that the symbol may serve as a psychological conduit for the concepts and desires expressed due to RPC-470, directly increasing the likelihood of successful infection. It is also unknown whether the Companion is a development exclusive to social media or whether the symbol is present in older infected material in a different form.

**History:** Prior to the popularity of the internet, there were sparse mentions of RPC-470 throughout the Authority archives. One of the earliest possible references to RPC-470 was an encounter with GoI-7760 (The Stars of ██████)[[footnote]] A minor Group of Interest that existed briefly within the American South from 1963 to 1967. See Chapter 5 of //Helter Skelter: Cold War CounterCULTure and the Anomalous// by Darren K. Wimbleton for more information.  [[/footnote]] in 1966. The group sent out an abundance of letters to people in the town of, ████ ████, South Carolina and several other nearby towns. The letter's contents discussed a crowdfunded exploration to Antarctica to acquire "new wisdom."

Local police investigated the origin of the letters, believing them to be related to a potential fraud case. However, upon arrival to the letters’ original address -- the ██████ Ranch, being used by GoI-7760 as a headquarters -- they discovered several cognitohazardous pictograms displaying winged humanoids, which would cause the viewing viewer to vomit blood and ████████████ █████. 

Eyewitness accounts from several surviving officers make a note of a bulletin board linking photographs of several world events, including the Valdivia Earthquake, to an unknown major Antarctic occurrence that took place in 1960. None of the people present could decipher what was being displayed, and when the Authority sent personnel to investigate, the bulletin board was burned, along with several documents that were possibly related to the phenomena GoI-7760 was centered around.

The first confirmed sighting of RPC-470 on the internet occurred on 07/07/1989 in the "Tartarus: Satan, Heavy Metal, and more!" Usenet Newsgroup. It was a post by the user "H3l1b0und" in reply to the post "Angels, your thoughts?" by "WrongDong88"

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-470/angel2.png|caption=First internet sighting of RPC-470 (Hover to enlarge)|align=center|width=750px]]

User "MorgueSlave" (real name Ivan Bolz) was found to have lived in ██████████, Germany; however he was reported missing one day after posting his reply to "H3l1b0und". He was never found and is assumed to have expired shortly afterward.

__**a. Hoax-Mail Outbreak (1989-1993)**__
[[/cell]]
[[/row]]
[[/table]]

The popularization of email services and mailing lists brought what is believed to be the first modern outbreak of RPC-470, resulting in around 21 groups of infected individuals across the European continent. Authority knowledge of RPC-470 across social media began only in the latter half of the 1990s, with indirect intervention succeeding in dismantling the majority of these groups.

Later investigation of the initial outbreak revealed its root to be a streak of spam emails prominently featuring forged news articles relaying discoveries within the Antarctic continent. Around 60% of these have been successfully traced to a singular individual who lived on Prague's[[footnote]] Capital of the Czech Republic [[/footnote]] outskirts. Known as Ms. Lana Edlow by her neighbors, she would disappear in unknown circumstances before the Authority took notice of her involvement with RPC-470.

Interviews of neighbors uniformly give the impression that Ms. Edlow was retrieved from the Václav Havel Airport through luxury transportation in mid-1993, potentially after being hired. She would repeatedly talk about finding a "real job" in the months prior to her disappearance, referring to a research institution of unknown name. 

The motives behind her hiring are subject to speculation, given that she possessed no known higher education or noteworthy professional skills, save for relative knowledge regarding computing and internet services.

__**b. Forum User Excerpts (1994-2003)**__
[[/cell]]
[[/row]]
[[/table]]


After the spread of RPC-470 via email, various internet communities would engage in discussion regarding the anomaly across various forums and chat rooms. These groups developed numerous disparate hypotheses about the phenomenon, characterizing it as a viral marketing campaign, the work of a modern cult, or simply the written delusions of a mentally ill individual.

For containment, the Authority encouraged the development of these hypotheses to prevent the pursuit of more accurate descriptions of the phenomenon, and dissuade internet users from engaging with the anomaly and thus falling victim to its effects. These attempts failed: due to what is now understood as form of the Streisand effect, the phenomenon would only increase in popularity toward the end of the century. 

Lacking modern equipment and techniques for effective containment (such as webcrawler services and automatic filtering), hubs of activity relating to the phenomenon would be engaged via Distributed-Denial-of-Service (DDOS) attacks. Despite initially counterproductive effects, poor cybersecurity typical of the time would ensure many targeted sites would become permanently offline. Relevant discussions and pages found to be informative or exemplary of activity at the time were archived, some of which are attached below.

__**c. Decrease in Activity and Revival**__
[[/cell]]
[[/row]]
[[/table]]

As methods of containing digital anomalies became more efficient and effective, RPC-470's presence shrank significantly throughout the 2000s. Information concerning the anomaly was scrubbed from public archives and blacklisted from popular search engines, forcing discussions on obscure peer-to-peer and other means of anonymous communication such as I2P. Because of the difficulty in gaining access to such networks, as well as the abundance of similarly disparate and fringe conspiracy theories discussed within, their spread was effectively stifled.

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-470/woodrowilson.png|width=700px |caption=A forum thread from early 2006 discussing RPC-470. [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-470/woodrowilson.png Click to enlarge.] ]]

However, an incident on 01/03/2021 resulted in Authority agents being discovered. Initially thought to be a failure of operations security, it was later discovered that no sensitive information was ever shared by agents that could reasonably tip off members of THE REAL, nor were there any signs of awareness of the Authority's existence.[[footnote]] Awareness of other EoIs, such as the Academy of True Art, was known to be a factor. However, no relationship between either Electronomicon or THE REAL and any of them was known to exist. [[/footnote]] This led to suspicions that the community, or at least its owners, were tipped off by a third party: --this hypothesis has since been dismissed and associated investigations closed.--[[footnote]] Later events suggest that dismissal of this possibility may have been too hasty. Despite Regional Commands not being established until the late 50's, the Antarctic region was given a special denomination due to its unique self-governing nature and exemplary circumstances during the time. [[/footnote]] at the time for forwarding operating outposts.
* **Repurposing took place no later than 1970.** Structure and materials used for both reinforced gates match special plans for Purple-class entities that were deprecated after 1971.
* **Abandonment by the rediscovering party took place no later than 2019.** Recovered materials from the destroyed helicopter and temporary camp have suffered little wind erosion despite strong air currents.
* **The party responsible for rediscovery is not a member of, or affiliated with, the Authority.** Torches and explosives were used to destroy both reinforced gates. Should the operation have been carried out by Authority members or affiliates, external disassembly would have been possible without disabling them.

|| REPORT ||

------

* __**FIRST FLOOR**__

Entered foyer area at 1140. Debriefing/dressing room past second blast door: stocked with new civilian-grade gear, although DYSEN noted astronomical price for this particular brand of set, then stored one for lab analysis. Proceeded through the conventional door.

Office/comms complex on the other side. PATZ noted a great similarity to old logistical sites built during WW2 era: corroborated by presence of old, unused radio equipment. Several office rooms appear to have unusual disposition and size, namely the reinforced glass panels to the sides and metal doors connecting them to larger comms rooms. PATZ believes these to be repurposed humanoid containment cells, which is consistent with the tentative timeline of use of the facility.

One major discovery: the remains of one destroyed server rack of modern make inside a partially vacated comms room. Old radios found in the abandoned camp believed to have been removed from here.

Found ground hatch at 1310.

* __**SECOND FLOOR**__

Descended at 1315 into the supply closet/armory. Newer construction noted. Labyrinthine in structure: long, winding halls with numerous offices and empty rooms. Exploration continued until 1450 without anything of note. PATZ and FEDER agree that this floor must have been built with the intent of disorienting unfamiliar explorers, whether upon entry or exit.

At 1410, an incinerator room was discovered. Features twelve (12) gas incinerators with sizable piles of ash. Entire team agrees this to be a document disposal room. Presence of ash on the ground is evidence of recent activity: an unknown party most likely looking for improperly disposed documents. It is not known if any were found.

Rest of floor features great amounts of unidentified, noisy machinery protruding from the walls. PATZ and FEDER do not agree on their nature: PATZ believes them to generate energy of some kind, FEDER thinks it is a single machine, either mechanically hydraulic or distributing a liquid substance. Noise generated by the machine(s) can't be discerned to be either energetic or hydraulic.

At 1450, a reinforced door is discovered open at the end of a hallway, leading to the downward staircase. Deprecated hazard markings at the side of the wall:

* EXTREME
* RED
* SAPIENT/AGGRESSIVE
* VISION-INDUCED/IDEOLOGICAL
* MASSIVE SIZE

PATZ and FEDER discuss safest route to follow. FEDER says that he and DYSEN should go: PATZ and I are to wait and scan them for abnormalities upon return. FEDER and DYSEN descend at 1510.

* __**TOMB CHAMBER**__

FEDER exclaims the other side to be empty, and asks PATZ and I to come in. Ensuing infohazard test returns negative. Structure markedly different: appears characteristic of European crypts. Age is unknown, but minimal deterioration suggests newer construction than initially assumed.

Chamber is several dozen meters in height. Thirty (30) giant, sloped, rectangular holes in the walls to each side. PATZ suggests them to be open tombs, although they are empty. Bas-reliefs inside each hole support this: vague representations of humanoid skeletons, with the most notable characteristic being large wings extruding from where the shoulders should be. There are wide holes where the shoulders should be, proceeding into much larger empty chambers.

DYSEN notices faint trails of chalk on the floor around each hole. FEDER observes them to be ritual circles. They have been deteriorated both by a change in temperature, and by human destruction in the form of straight lines, seemingly corresponding to persons dragging their shoes across the circles.

No further abnormalities are noted. Team exits facility at 1650, finding difficulty with second floor.
