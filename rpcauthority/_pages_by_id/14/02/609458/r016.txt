**Registered Phenomena Code:** 691

**Object Class:** Alpha-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Visual Hazard, Animated, Organic, Digital, Contact Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** Instances of RPC-691 are to be contained via the cooperation of various media sharing websites in an effort to covertly remove the videos from the sharing algorithm. Said media sharing websites are to alert the Authority upon an individual’s viewership of the video. The processes of downloading or sharing instances of RPC-691 are to be monitored, and acted upon appropriately. The Authority has included spyware in some files of the video circulating throughout the internet, allowing the Authority to monitor and contain instances of RPC-691-A if necessary.

**Description:** RPC-691 is an MP4 video file distributed throughout Internet websites, specifically those that host pornographic content or whose users engage in online sexual activities. Auditory contents vary depending on the particular proclivities of the watcher. Video duration also varies for this reason, but is generally in the range of 10-12 hours. RPC-691 follows a format of continued affirmations directed toward the watcher — for instance, "you are chubby" or "you are beautiful" — accompanied by a cacophony of droning synthetic noises. The visual portion of the video consists of a repeating pattern of pink nested heart symbols moving inward rapidly, set upon a backdrop depicting outer space. To be noted is that the background is not a real satellite photograph, and further analysis reveals it is in fact a heavily edited picture of a vessel containing bile.

Repeated watchers and listeners incur a number of nonspecific physiological changes. Superficial mutations to the body structure of the affected individual are most noticeable, mirroring the physical form desired by them. Subjects are at this point designated RPC-691-A. These alterations are brought about via the slow cancerous growth of a silicone-like material under the skin. This is accompanied by a conversion of muscle to skin tissue concentrated in the extremities and an abnormally copious secretion of oils, the combination of which leads to the formation of painful boils after approximately a month’s time. Nerve tissue becomes increasingly dense across the body, leading to an estimated fivefold enhancement in tactile sensitivity. This particular change is often viewed positively by RPC-691-A in the early stages, and leads to a further indulgence in pleasure-seeking behavior. Due to aforementioned complications, however, the increased sensitivity quickly becomes a source of continuous irritation. 

The combination of increased pleasure-seeking and the anomalous effect of RPC-691 causes acutely addictive pathology. Removed from RPC-691 for a span of as little as two hours, RPC-691-A will suffer vomiting, hallucinations, irritability, and other symptoms of severe withdrawal. Regressive tendencies in the afflicted are also documented, especially in cases where RPC-691-A shares lodgings with their parents. During the final stages of RPC-691-A, addiction progresses into an overwhelming necessity to travel to a specific location. The points in question are shared across instances originating from identical or proximate social circles, likely as a result of viewing identical versions of RPC-691. An anomalous event is understood to occur upon arrival, but it has as of yet gone unobserved. Its end result is the congealment of RPC-691-A instances into a singular mass of bodies, designated RPC-691-B, followed by eventual death. Recovered masses of cerebral tissue demonstrate significant neural activity long after expiration of other bodily functions.

**DISCOVERY LOG :**

The Authority was alerted to an abandoned warehouse in [REDACTED], Pennsylvania after concerned locals reported a noxious odor and screams emanating from inside. Mobile Specialized Team Units Sgt. Clark and Corporal Green were dispatched to the site equipped with light armament. Upon entering, the operatives were met with an overwhelmingly foul smell. 

Evidence of extensive hematolagnia and coprophilia was noted, including symbols scrawled in excrement on walls bearing similarity to various pop culture characters and slogans in common usage on the Internet. Properties identified included //My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic// and //My Life As a Teenage Robot//.

 Further exploration revealed severed human limbs, coated in cystic boils. The flesh was wholly lacking in blood, having instead a copious amount of grease contained within numerous uneven layers of skin. This grease varied in composition, containing oils of a seemingly artificial nature. Examination of the limbs revealed that nearly 70% of their volume consisted of dermis. Given this physiology, these organs appeared to have been shed rather than cut off. 

While performing tissue samples in the west wing of the building, agent Clark was alerted to a steady dripping noise coming from behind a door in the hallway across from him. Preparing for the possibility of an encounter, the agent contacted his superior to reinforce him while approaching with caution.


Entering into a spacious storage room, the operatives came first upon a pile of organs and other remains in a pool of fluids, matching the makeup of those found previously. Hanging by chains above them was a cylindrical mass of flesh, measuring 16.5 feet in length and comprised of roughly 34 human bodies, the exact number of which could not be ascertained due to their amorphous state. It showed no signs of continued life. 


Joined most resolutely at the ribs downward, the bodies were fused in a manner which was blunt and would not have allowed for effective sharing of bodily function. Neither nerves nor blood vessels had been connected, though larger organs such as the stomach in some areas were welded. Covering the mass were boils of the type documented previously, accompanied by subcutaneous protuberances of a material resembling polysiloxane in composition. Analysis of skin revealed intense stretching, which indicated a rapid onset of physiological changes which was not accompanied by increased growth factor. Internal organs in many places had been pushed outside the body, being kept from exposure only by a thin layer of taut skin. 

The heads and hands of the bodies had undergone structural changes which made them superficially resemble various animals and objects, but the features were crude and resembled their respective objects more symbolically than anatomically. One head, assumed to depict a wolf, had a swelled cleft orbicularis oris and an elongated nasal area. The swelled mouth area was dotted with pimples in four parallel lines, presumably intended to denote vibrissal follicles. A significant amount of tissue had been added to the ears, extending them outward. Due to their simplicity, these ears could not have allowed for efficient hearing. 

The base of the structure had sustained considerable trauma, suggesting a forceful movement from within which ruptured the flesh. The death of the organism was likely a direct consequence. Discovered additionally were a trail of footprints in blood leading directly out from the pile of organs on the floor. The prints followed a path exiting the warehouse, and could not be traced further. Cursory inspection determined the tracks to be of an adult human female, but revealed through more thorough examination was the fact that they were wholly lacking in toeprints or any irregularity in texture. The Authority conducted a subsequent search of the surrounding area, but no leads of substantial value were uncovered.




**Closing:**

Motions have been made within Authority management that the process of RPC-691’s production of RPC-691-A should be allowed to temporarily continue in a supervised manner. This would be executed so that the entity which seemingly escaped RPC-691-B could be apprehended or monitored in its geographic movement. As this could potentially lead to the discovery and closure of unlawful operations by the Kabushiki Kawaii organization, this proposal has gained notable traction. The uncomfortable ethical ramifications of this plan, however, are apparent. Of critical concern is the fact that it is unknown how many instances of RPC-691-A must be created for RPC-691-B to form, nor the mechanisms by which the transformation occurs. As a result, the casualties of such an operation could quite easily reach unacceptable levels. Some supporters of the proposal have posited, having recovered and been exposed to substantial illegal materials from the personal devices of affected subjects, that expiration as a result of RPC-691 is neither apparently painful or undeserved. Such ethical matters are currently under consideration. It is not known if coalescence into RPC-691-B is painless or otherwise.

**[ Foreword: The Logs of this addendum detail an interview with Sgt. Clark, who recounts his experience when deployed to investigate RPC-691.]**

**[TRANSCRIPTION BEGIN]**

**Sgt. Clark:** Recording has started, correct?

**Dr. Boll:** Yes.

**Dr. Boll:** What was it that struck you first, upon entering the warehouse?

**Sgt. Clark:** Doctor, I think you know the answer to that question as well as I do. The smell. Oh God, the smell. I didn't react as badly as Green did, poor guy, but it still hit me like some kind of humid hurricane. I don't think I've ever smelled anything like it before or since, so I don't know how to properly describe it. It was like... You know, I grew up in Michigan. And when I was a kid I'd delve really deep into the woods in the summer. A regular Livingstone, I was. And there were some areas, by the riverside, where the odor of rotting logs, decomposing fish, washed-up trash and fungi all mixed together into this choking miasma, baked by the summer heat in the oven of the dense foliage. It wasn't painful, it wasn't the sort of thing that would knock you out or make you panic. But it was that oppressive, omnipresent fog that made you feel, deep down in your gut, that the entire world you were experiencing was intolerable. That's how it felt.

**Dr. Boll:** I see. Can you tell me about what happened during your inspection of the warehouse?

**Sgt. Clark:** Most of it was uneventful... it was just bagging and scanning the various crumbs the thing left behind. I couldn't help but be a bit impressed by the drawings. They weren't technically impressive by any means, but because of the sheer dedication and intricacy put into them I couldn't help but find myself staring at times. It at least provided a mild but welcome relief from the constant miasma baring down on us. I tried to crack a few jokes to my partner, but they fell flat. It should have been funny - I mean, the whole thing was absurd - but neither of us could really laugh. Didn't help that I think Green was a little too intimidated by me for us to shoot the shit, yknow. He's just a kid, I shouldn't be so harsh on him. Anyway, the arms we found... were just weird. I don't know what to make of them, maybe the boys at the lab can. Very mushy to the touch, like dough. When I picked one up, my fingers left pretty deep indents on it. I can't even imagine how something with flesh like that would move.

While I was doing tissue collection, I heard a faint sound coming from the room opposite me. I thought it was just some miscellaneous noise at first, but it was too steady. Dripping. Given the viscera we had slogged through so far, it wasn't hard to guess what it was. I called Green over, we disengaged our safeties and hesitantly went in. The door was a pain to open, that's for certain. Thing was rusted to hell, and heavy too. We get in, and it's this big garage. And near the ceiling was this horrible... thing. Limbs jutting out everywhere, gaping mouths, faces swelled so badly you couldn't see the eyes. Busted wide open, like a pinata. We sat there for a moment, not quite comprehending what we were seeing. The thing wasn't moving. The only movement in the room was that steady.. drip ... drip ... drip. 

After this delay, Green immediately screamed obscenities, opened fire on it and kept at it for a while. I reprimanded him afterwards. Not with much severity mind you - The thing was clearly dead, but I think it would have been more comforting if it was still alive. Because then you'd have the certainty, yknow? Even if there's a high risk that you're going to die, you'd still have the certainty that you're the human fighting this hostile monster, and that it's going to end with either a victory or a defeat. As I watched him load that thing with bullets, I really kind of hoped it would spring from its chains and roar at us. As it was, it was just this pathetic mangled carcass staring at us with its glassy eyes. Total, uncomfortable, undistracted stillness.  Green tried to make some crude remarks about the thing. I glared at him and he went quiet. This wasn't some alien - it was all too human. 

I mean, not only were these very distinctly human bodies, but even their deformity was more like some sick art piece than something otherworldly. Reminded me of some native American totems I'd seen in the museum. I don't know what went on in there before we got there, but I felt like I was intruding on an abusive household or something, watching the parents scream at each other. I was somewhere private, where I didn't belong, too intimate for comfort, but too removed for it to affect me viscerally. All I could really do is observe in a sort of daze. God, I mean, it goes without saying, but the bodies were disgusting. So many of those boils, it began to look a bit like a yellow raspberry. The dried pus formed a sort of lacquer that made parts of the thing blindingly shiny, while others were so coarse and bumpy it was nearly rocklike.

**[As he was recounting this, Clark had been looking upwards and to his right. With a sudden movement he affixes his eyes directly on Dr. Boll.]**

**Sgt. Clark:** Green cried in my arms halfway back to the pickup site. I told him gruffly that we needed to get going and that he'd need to be tougher than that to survive as a recruit. But I knew why he cried. I wanted to cry too. I wish I could have told him that he'd never have to feel that again. There's a filth in this world, a deep filth that fills your soul and gives you just enough clean air to survive but not enough to live. Creeps in slowly, and once you're in deep enough to realize what's going on it's too late, it's already coated your lungs and no amount of coughing will expel it. We got back out into the fresh air. Whoever - or whatever - was in that garage, didn't have the same privilege. It was monstrous, but the only thing I really felt when I looked at it was profound sadness. It had been choking too. It had been choking all its life, probably. 


**Sgt. Clark:** After we cleared the garage, I followed those prints. They looked female, but something was off about them... although, that might just have been my mind making up stuff, considering that with the context I'd be surprised if they //were// normal footprints. Anyway, I followed them off the property and they led to a river. Whatever it was, the damned thing knew what it was doing. As I was about to turn back, I saw it. A head in the water. Skin pale white, inhumanly huge eyes, flowing blonde hair. It didn't seem real. and this thing - this thing //winked at me//. It fucking winked at me. And then it just disappeared under the water, as if it didn't need to breathe. I couldn't chase after it at that point.
