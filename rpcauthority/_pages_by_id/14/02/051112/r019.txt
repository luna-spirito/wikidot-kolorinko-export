[[tab Facility Index]]

[[span class="drop-cap"]] T [[/span]] he Primacy maintains a network of facilities throughout the world. Various monasteries, supernatural pilgrimage sites, and cathedrals provide logistical and liturgical functions for the agency. Citadels, containment castles, and Sanctums, holy buildings and locations, are utilized to protect Catholic artifacts and contain dangerous entities from harming the faithful.
------
[[=]]
++ Facility Designations
[[/=]]

||~ Designation||~ Description||
|| Citadel || Agency bases, military castles, and other stand-alone facilities used to store supernatural phenomena. Citadels may also have holding cells for heretical Persons of Interest and weapons storage. ||
|| Dungeon || Containment rooms reserved for the storage of demonic and other unknown phenomena in Citadels. Only in incredibly rare instances are dungeons located near Sanctums, and only for neutralization purposes. ||
|| Sanctum || Church buildings, pilgrimage sites, and other holy locations are used to house sacred relics and other holy artifacts. Sanctums may also be used for non-supernatural purposes as well. ||
|| Vault || Containment rooms reserved for the storage of sacred phenomena in Sanctums. Some vaults may be found in Citadels that contain multiple categories of supernatural phenomena. ||

[[tab Classification Index]]

[[span class="drop-cap"]] T [[/span]] he Primacy uses a variety of classifications in order to catalog supernatural phenomena. This information assists the agency in containment efforts, as well as potential use cases an anomaly may provide to the Roman Papacy. One of the six military orders are assigned responsibility for protecting the agency's supernatural objects based on their specialization.
------
[[=]]
++ Inquiry Categories
[[/=]]

||~ Category||~ Description||
|| Sacred || The anomaly is of Christian origin and/or is venerated by the Catholic Church. ||
|| Demonic || The anomaly is not of Christian origin and/or is potentially dangerous to the Catholic Church. ||
|| Obscure || The anomaly's origins are unknown at the time of the inquiry. ||
------
[[=]]
++ Phenomenon Classes
[[/=]]

||~ Class Types||~ Description||
|| RELIC || Supernatural Items, Objects ||
|| LOCATION || Supernatural Buildings, Areas, Spaces ||
|| ENTITY || Supernatural Persons, Spirits, Creatures ||
|| BELIEF || Supernatural Ideas, Thoughtforms, Memes ||
------

------
[[=]]
++ Sanction Ranks
[[/=]]

||~ Ranks||~ Description||
|| LAITY || Access to the anomaly is available to any ordained members of the Catholic Church. Sacred relics are often classified under this sanction if the general public can access them. ||
|| MAGISTERIUM || Access to the anomaly is available for any VSSP personnel. Demonic phenomena is automatically classified under this sanction, as well as more dangerous relics. ||
|| CATHEDRA || Access to the anomaly is restricted to the VSSP Prelates and the Bishop of Rome. Direct approval from the Pope is required for all personnel below this ranking. ||
------
[[=]]
++ Ability Classes
[[/=]]

||~ Ability Designation (Power Level) ||~ Designation||~ Description ||
|| DIGT-(0-5) || Digital || Any supernatural phenomena that is digital in nature and uses a digital medium for its effects. ||
|| DIMT-(0-5) || Dimensional || Any supernatural phenomena that is from another dimensional plane or has the power to manipulate the nature of baseline reality. ||
|| CHEM-(0-5) || Chemical || Any supernatural phenomena that is chemically based or uses a chemical reaction for its effects. ||
|| COGN-(0-5) || Cognitive || Any supernatural phenomena that is activated or transferred through mental cognition or memetic influence. ||
|| PHYS-(0-5) || Physical || Any supernatural phenomena that exhibits unnatural durability or non-conformity with the laws of physics. ||
|| PATH-(0-5) || Pathogen || Any supernatural phenomena that is a virus or bacterium in nature or can inflict a disease that has anomalous effects or properties. ||
|| VITA-(0-5) || Vita || Any supernatural phenomena that is beneficial to the Catholic Church or sanctified in nature. This designation is reserved for utility anomalies. ||
|| NULL-(0-5) || Non-Designated || If your VSSP article requires a particular supernatural hazard that is not currently listed, you are free to add one. Make sure to keep it preferably around 4 characters long and add it to this hub. ||
------
[[=]]
++ Power Levels
[[/=]]

||~ Power Level ||~ Description ||
|| 0 || The supernatural phenomenon presents no threat to the Primacy or to the Roman Papacy. Utility anomalies are typically classified under this level. ||
|| 1 || The supernatural phenomenon is minimally hazardous to the Primacy or the Papacy. Nominal security procedures will prevent any threat from this anomaly. ||
|| 2 || The supernatural phenomenon is somewhat hazardous to the Primacy or the Papacy. More complex security procedures than usual will be needed to minimize the threat from this anomaly. ||
|| 3 || The supernatural phenomenon is moderately hazardous to the Primacy or the Papacy. Extensive measures need to be taken to reduce the threat of these anomalies from the public. ||
|| 4 || The supernatural phenomenon is dangerously hazardous to the Primacy or the Papacy. The full resources of the VSSP should be used to contain these anomalies that have the power to threaten the Veil or cause widespread devastation. ||
|| 5 || The supernatural phenomenon is catastrophically hazardous to the Primacy or the Papacy. Any anomaly that could wipe out Catholicism from existence, resulting in the eschatological defeat of the Christian God, or abolish the office of the Bishop of Rome is classified under this level. ||
