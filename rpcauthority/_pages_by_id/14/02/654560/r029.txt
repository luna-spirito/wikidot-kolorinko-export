**Containment Protocols:**

RPC-005 is currently at large in an unknown location; most likely to be in constant motion. The entity was last seen entering the waters along the coast of Faria Beach, California USA, following its containment breach on 03/12/1963.

RPC-005 is speculated to be roaming the surrounding areas of the city of Yogyakarta, Indonesia. Search parties assigned to the city should exercise utmost discretion, and to never mention the entity by name.
 
Recontainment personnel should utilize plasmatic accelerators to encourage quantum decoherence in RPC-005 and to prevent it from attaining entanglement. Do not attempt entity recontainment around bodies of water. Do not attempt lone recontainment.

In the case of a successful recontainment, RPC-005 is to be transported inside a standard Bell-State Disentanglement Chamber, and permanently kept in the specialized containment chamber located in Site-002-A. If possible, the entity should be sedated and maintained in an artificially induced comatose state prior to transportation.

In the case of an encounter by non-essential personnel: Maintain distance, discard any clothing articles in aqua green[[footnote]]Hex-code: #03BB85.[[/footnote]] (if any), and immediately report the last known location of the entity to Colonel Johan McKiernan, or to the desk of MST Lima-09 "Edgerunners."

**Description:**

RPC-005 is a humanoid entity, appearing as an adolescent female human measuring 165 cm in height with a slim build and an approximate weight of 55 kg. Phylogenetics study suggests that RPC-005 descended from a southeastern Asian ancestor, specifically from the Indonesian island of Java.

Histology studies of its tissue samples suggest that RPC-005 does not age. This is evident in the telomeres[[footnote]]A genetic feature associated with preventing DNA malformations in the chromosomes.[[/footnote]] at the end of its chromosomes not showing degradation typically exhibited in common human species. Furthermore, the length of its telomeres was considered to be far longer than that of any known human being on Earth, giving RPC-005 an increased resistance to genetic defects caused by chromosomal nondisjunction.[[footnote]]i.e. RPC-005 has a natural resistance to genetic malformations caused by ionizing radiation.[[/footnote]]

[[include component:image-block | align=left| width=250px | name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-005/Thyroid| caption=Proton Density weighted MRI imaging of RPC-005's larynx.]]

EEG scanning of the brain indicates heightened activity on the cerebrum, which may correlate to the anomalous abilities exhibited by RPC-005. One such case was that RPC-005 was observed to exhibit the ability to manipulate its larynx to produce sounds in frequencies generally not possible for a human being to reliably produce without damage.

This is due to the thyroid cartilage of RPC-005 exhibiting an abnormal amount of chondrocytes,[[footnote]]Cells that produce and maintain cartilaginous bones.[[/footnote]] with the vestibular cord and the skeletal structure of its true vocal cords showing similar thickening of muscle mass. This, in theory, allows RPC-005 to produce specific sounds with minimal damage to its larynx.

Furthermore, in a tomography scan of RPC-005's skull, it was found that a small flap of soft tissue inside the ear canal can be moved at will by the entity that completely covers its eardrums. This means that the entity's hearing is completely immune to its own bouts of high-frequency screeches.

**Assessment Subject:** RPC-005
**Date of Assessment:** 09/10/1963

Based on the surveillance footage of the incident, RPC-005 was shown to exhibit extra-dimensional properties. The exact mechanism in which the entity attains this feat is currently unknown. However, the entity seemed to have escaped containment using a form of quantum teleportation.

Limited research suggests that the entity possesses the ability to incorporate itself into a pure quantum state, most likely a form of instantaneous cellular disarrangement into a Bose-Einstein condensate. Then, the entity would entangle all of its atoms in another location, effectively teleporting at will.

However, in order for the entity to attain a quantum state, every single atom that makes up the entity must be completely isolated from its surrounding environment, as a pure quantum state is extremely fragile and any contaminant could induce decoherence in its entanglement.

As such, recontainment personnel are advised to utilize a means to introduce quantum decoherence in the entity's state of matter, such as a sudden disparage of temperature commonly found in plasmatic accelerators. This should prevent RPC-005 from entering a quantum state, disabling its extra-dimensional properties albeit for a limited time.

In regards to its permanent containment, a chamber equipped with a standard Bell-State Disentanglement measure, or a similar quantum disentanglement module would suffice in disabling entity teleportation. Note that the disentanglement measure must be a form of direct physical-to-entity method.

An example of the application of this increased pitch control is the ability of RPC-005 to produce a low-frequency sound that resonates with various substances. In this case, RPC-005 showed a preference to produce a continuous 19Hz sound that resonates perfectly with human eyes. This allows RPC-005 to disable an observer's visual acquisition of itself.

In another case, RPC-005 has been observed to generate a continuous 24Hz sine wave to manipulate the fluidic properties and dynamics of liquid matter. With precise pitch control, RPC-005 can extend this ability to deflect the movement of water and even imbibe a fluid source with non-Newtonian properties.[[footnote]]e.g. Reversing the flow of a running water stream, eliminating the friction between atoms (hot superfluid), suspending water droplets in mid-air, etc.[[/footnote]]

One extreme case showed that RPC-005 possesses the ability to break down the covalent/ionic bonds between molecules, as shown in an experiment where RPC-005 separated a body of distilled water into its fundamental elements of hydrogen and oxygen gasses. However, the full scope of this ability is limited in both knowledge and documentation.

RPC-005 has continuously shown animosity towards containment and research personnel, oftentimes attempting to breach containment which makes research and experimentation sessions difficult. Attempting to communicate with RPC-005 requires a special procedure in which the entity must be provided items or services to ease its emotional state.

However, the most notable trait of RPC-005 observed heretofore is its seemingly religious, or even fanatical, affinity towards the color aqua green. The entity has requested time after time various items and clothing in said color, such as the lining of bed sheets, wallpaper, nightgowns, and furniture upholstery.

The entity refuses to acknowledge questions pertaining to its affinity with the color. Dr. Emma Keyes, the acting psychologist for RPC-005, cited rationales that the entity may have developed an obsession caused by a lack, or restriction, of personal ownership during childhood.

Nevertheless, RPC-005 will display extreme hostile behavior toward personnel wearing clothing in aqua green, so much so that the entity will attempt to breach containment in order to terminate the offender. Dr. Keyes further added that this behavior may have been caused by a hormonal spike commonly exhibited among teenage women.

Another notable behavior of RPC-005 is a shift in gestures and mannerisms in presence of male personnel. In this case, the entity was observed to have been analyzing the physical properties of the personnel and speaking to them in a receptive manner. --Dr. Keyes speculated that this was caused by the entity's biological compulsion to mate.--[[footnote]]This theory has been discredited by other behavioral researchers due to a lack of evidence.[[/footnote]]

At times, RPC-005 can be heard humming various nondescript melodies. This is suspected to be an effort to breach containment, in which the entity attempts to find the resonant frequency of the chamber walls. This is evident in the microphone being rendered inoperable after picking up dangerously high frequencies emanating from the entity.

**Discovery:**

During the height of the United States' anti-communist sentiments, the CIA was keen on overthrowing the Indonesian government for their alignment with the Soviet Union. In September 1957, the CIA set up bases in the Philippines and began funding rebels with weapons in the Sumatra and Sulawesi areas.

However, Soviet counterintelligence learned of the plan almost immediately and notified the Indonesian government accordingly. Soon after, the CIA received reports of a singular operative terminating said rebels and sabotaging vessels in the Philippines base. This was later confirmed to be the act of RPC-005, working in tandem with the top generals of the Indonesian Army.[[footnote]]Often referring to themselves as "The Sons of Eisenhower," due to the nature of their extensive American training.[[/footnote]]

On 21/02/1958, the Indonesian military managed to bomb CIA-funded rebel radio masts in Sumatera and established a naval blockade along the coast in the hopes of regaining intelligence advantage. However, the CIA soon learned that RPC-005 was seen to be among the elements of the naval blockade, which prompted them to throw in a last-ditch effort in eliminating the entity.

[[include component:image-block | align=right| width=300px | name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-005/Dublon| caption=April 19th bombing.]]

On 19/04/1958, CIA attack aircraft began bombing various outlying islands which resulted in the deaths of both civilian and military lives. The CIA officially requested Authority assistance not too long after and on 24/04/1958, a joint effort between the CIA and the Authority was established, resulting in Operation Kingfisher.

As with the complete failure of the CIA in hiding their involvement in the coup effort, Authority commanders advised the CIA to conduct covert ops instead. Approximately 16 intelligence agents were embedded in various Sumatran settlements, with almost 70 natives acting as informants.

After about 20 days of intelligence gathering, the CIA learned that RPC-005 was stationed temporarily in the Hinako Islands, west of Nias. To prevent further knowledge of their involvement in Indonesia, the CIA agreed to relinquish all containment efforts to the Authority's hands.

On the night of 26/05/1958, elements of Authority's 3rd Pacific Fleet moved into the waters south of the Hinako Islands. The containment effort took 3 hours to complete and resulted in the deaths of 1357 Authority agents,[[footnote]]Mostly figures from lives lost aboard the ANV Voortrekker battlecruiser.[[/footnote]] the destruction of 1 cruiser, 3 destroyers, and the complete obliteration of Hamutala Island.

RPC-005 was then transported by air in an artificially induced comatose state to a temporary containment site on the outskirts of Site-002. The construction for Site-002-A began 4 months after the initial containment on 29/11/1958.

[[collapsible show="+ Historical Records and Findings Pertaining to RPC-005" hide="-hide Historical Records and Findings Pertaining to RPC-005"]]

[[div class="blockquote"]]

Research into the origin of RPC-005 indicates that the entity may have a semblance, or even a direct connection to, the local mythos of Indonesian folklore: //“Nyai Roro Kidul.”//[[footnote]]Old Javanese. Literally: Her Majesty Lady of the South.[[/footnote]] In it, the figure was said to have been the ruler of the Yogyakarta Sultanate and remains the rightful heir to its throne.

However, official records are scarce and most documents pertaining to the lineage of various Javanese monarchs have been lost in raids or sackings. The only known reliable source of information is the recollection from the entity itself, reinforced by a minuscule amount of surviving evidence and elements of local legends.

According to RPC-005, the entity was a direct descendant of Sri Baduga Maharaja (1401-1521), the founder and ruler of the Sunda Kingdom. This directly contradicts the //“Nyai Roro Kidul”// legend, as the Yogyakarta Craton was located far beyond the Sunda Kingdom's sphere of influence.

Nevertheless, RPC-005 recalls having a role in the establishment of the Yogyakarta Sultanate in the 18th century. As the entity had stated that it was fleeing from an abusive maternal figure, it is entirely possible that the entity chose to forego its claim to the family throne and instead establish its own royal lineage.

A memoir from the Craton annalist recounts that RPC-005 had a prominent role within the sultanate as a royal guard, and should any harm befalls the sultan and his family, RPC-005 would have the rightful claim to his throne.

RPC-005 disappears from public view in 1812, following the British occupation of Yogyakarta led by Colonel Gillespie. According to the entity, this was caused by the failure of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II in defending the city, and differing political opinions with his successor.

RPC-005 surfaces from time to time as brief sightings along the southern coast of Java. In 1942, the entity recalls attempting to make contact with the Craton following the Dutch colonies' defeat against Japanese forces.

**Date:** 17/06/1958

**Foreword:** Dr. Reynolds is tasked to evaluate RPC-005's motives in order to better understand the entity's standing against the Authority. Sgt. **Dr. Reynolds:** Begin log, interview session 005-0056. For the record, I have received explicit permission from Director Allenby to forego dehumanization protocols when conversing with the entity. In other words, I will be calling RPC-005 by its given name, instead of its object number.

**Dr. Reynolds:** Not yet, we still need to-

**Dr. Reynolds:** I don't kno-

I've never wanted any of this. This... this gift? It was not my choice... but I owe this to them, and they entrusted me with their lives. I don't want any more bloodshed, and I will try with every power I have to end this peacefully. But as it seems, violence is the only language that humans can truly understand.

**Dr. Reynolds:** //[Stammering]// I'm... I'm sorry.

**RPC-005:** Leave me alone.

//[RPC-005 turns around, facing the interviewers]//

**RPC-005:** ...Our children won't suffer as I did.

//[The surveillance camera shows RPC-005 dematerializing inside the chamber into a gel-like substance before vanishing. It then rematerializes outside, right next to Dr. MacIntyre]//

**Dr. MacIntyre:** What the-

**Unidentified ASF Personnel:** Loose anomaly on sight, please advise.
