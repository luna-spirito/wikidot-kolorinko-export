**Registered Phenomena Code:** 356

**Object Class:** Alpha-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Contact Hazard, Teleportation Hazard/Extra-Terrestrial Hazard (?), Destabilization Hazard, Ideological Hazard, Aquatic Hazard

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcsandboxcn.wdfiles.com/local--files/okaypj-tmp/hut.jpg|caption=RPC-356]]

**Containment Protocols:** ASF patrol teams consisting of five men each will be stationed around RPC-356 24 hours a  day. The patrol teams are to keep unauthorized personnel away from entering within a 500m radius of the subject and are allowed to use force if necessary. ACI will continuously monitor the surroundings for potential Nihil group activities and prepare for the possible attacks by aggressive groups.

RPC-356-1 is stored in stainless-steel containers no smaller than 200m^^3^^. These containers, which will be placed in advance near the containment location, should be examined and maintained twice a quarter. --Once a container is almost full, it should be transported and stored in Site-CN-006.-- The containers with RPC-356-1, whether it's full or not, should be transported and stored in [http://rpc-wiki-cn.wikidot.com/sites:site-cn-006 Site-CN-006] immediately after "It's Raining Inside" events.

CSD personnel infected by RPC-356-1 should be administered amnestics and sent to Site-066 for further re-evaluation and training. --Other infected personnel will be reassigned to the Research Division Unit-119.-- Since March 2021, due to the limited budget of the Research Division, RDU119 will not longer accept further infected personnel. Currently, ASIACOM is in discussion for an alternative place for the relocation of infected personnel.

**Description:** RPC-356 is a small-sized wooden hut, occupying an area of 10m^^2^^ externally, located in Ngawa, Sichuan. RPC-356's surface is charred black. The subject was still sturdy after the lastest earthquake due to it's anti-seismic structure. The front door of RPC-356 measures 1.8m in height from which the internal structure is not observable. Contact with personnel will be lost immediately upon passing through the door. CSDs reported that even though they could not visibly see the interior structure while passing through the doorway, by relying on their touch senses, they were able to detect a rough-surfaced columnar object situated approximately 40m away. The space beyond the door is thus determined to be larger than the exterior of RPC-356.

At least one "It's Raining Inside" event occurs twice a year; during which the surroundings of RPC-356 will experience Coherency disturbances of ACS ranging between 3.8~2.2. Personnel who remain with RPC-356 will begin to feel significant humidity. After a brief period (about 12 hours in general), large amounts of liquid of unknown composition (designated as RPC-356-1) will manifest and drop from the upper part of RPC-356. RPC-356-1 is observed to be colourless outside of RPC-356. The event will subsequently continue for another 2~3.5 days in general.

RPC-356-1 will spill out from the aforementioned door structure due to normal liquid piling. Fogs will will start developing around RPC-356 during the event, reducing visibility to less than a quarter metre. This fog is currently believed to be composed of RPC-356-1 droplets. Each "It's Raining Inside" event will produce 20m^^3^^ to [[span style="color: #ccc; background-color: #ccc;"]]980[[/span]]m^^3^^ amount of RPC-356-1.

Personnel exposed to RPC-356-1 unprotected will become obsessed with entities or events related to the Children of Nihil. In the pre-infective stage, personnel typically will not become aware of the infection by themselves. Although many of the beliefs are difficult to classify, worships related to Exiled Sun are most common among infected personnel (above 93%) among them, while a very few individuals have beliefs similar to Orrery of Ages and Void Unending. Amnestics are not effective against infected personnel. It is agreed that the infection itself is not memetic in nature.

Personnel may experience the following conditions in the pre-infection stage:
* Reduced efficiency in work related to Nihil entities;
* Refusal to approach RPC-356;
* Increased occurrence of going into trances.
In the late stages of infection, these behaviours will become violent, with some instances of personnel defecting to join nearby Nihil groups.

**Discovery:** In early 2009, the Authority cooperated with the South Sea Enlightenment Movement to conduct a raid on numerous small Nihil-Adjacent organizations in mountainous regions of southwest China. The operation was successfully executed, capturing a few Nihil personals.

The interrogation of prisoners began in March. The individuals provided the approximate location of RPC-356. MST-Sinchou-01 "Seeking in the Woods" was sent to search the area, and eventually found it on a hillside nearby █████████ town.

There are a large number of Nihil groups throughout China. Most of these Nihil groups are small in number, the smallest has about 5 people and the largest has just under a hundred, most hold anomalous objects. Since 2002, the Authority has been conducting a set of investigations into these groups. Since 2009, the Authority, together with the South Sea Enlightenment Movement, an allied Pilot-worship group, launched a series of raids against some of the larger groups.

Most of these Nihil groups are located in areas with hills and jungles, with two rare known cases of groups in the Qaidam Basin. All of them have activities in areas far from population. There are two types of groups; the first consists of members who live in nearby villages and gather at an agreed time, while the second does not allow contact with civilians, making their members completely dependent on anomalous means. Some of the areas where these groups operate have seen civilians worshipping Nihil deities, but most of them are unrelated to anomalies.

Most of the Nihil groups are engaged in spreading anomalous objects in populated places. A set of worldviews related to anomalous entities are disseminated. Their worldviews are varied, with a few having developed into a complete system [[footnote]] Note that most of these Nihil groups claim that their interpretation of worldviews are derived from their possessed anomalous objects. [[/footnote]]. Some of the most common are as follows:
* **Hou Yi sun-shooting worldview:** This worldview claims that the ten deities of Nihil originated from the ten sons of Dijun and Xihe, also knowns as the Golden Crows. At a point, ten suns rose at the same time and were shot down by Hou Yi's arrows. The concept of "flood" does not exist in this worldview.
* **Kua Fu sun-chasing worldview:** Some groups hold this worldview or a variation of it, which claim there are two deities, the Sun and Kua Fu. The Kua Fu was killed in a confrontation. These groups believe that the "flood" will bring Kua Fu back to life. The existence of the ten deities is rare in this worldview, but there are groups who explain that "Kua Fu's Sun-Chasing" as The Orrery of Ages.
* **"Nine States" worldview:** Some of the Early Light groups hold this worldview or a variation of it. These groups are unique in that they reject the coming "flood" and their members generate a lot of interaction with anomalous entities.

The Nihil beliefs of these groups are mixed in the original Buddhist, Taoist, folk religions and even Confucianism in the areas, differing greatly from the typical modus operandi of other Nihil groups. As a result, these groups are classified as Nihil-Adjacent groups.

Recently, Site-066 reported that the CSD recruits provided by our Site showed signs of anomalies and caused an riot, resulting in some disruptions. These CSD recruits all originated from Nihil groups from southwest China, and were all recently affected by the properties of RPC-356.

These prisoners were infected before we capture them, so no one had ever noticed their problem. Newly inducted researchers processed these individuals mistakenly, believing that a period of observation was sufficient to prove the absence of anomalous properties, when in fact most of the Nihil anomalous effects were unrelated to memetics. Post investigation shows that these personnel had already planned this insurrection prior to their capture, and purposefully hid their anomaly properties.

The following protocols will soon be executed, which are valid for all CN Branch sites:
* Members of the Children of Nihil groups captured within the jurisdiction of CN Branch sites should not serve as CSD recruits, they may be inducted as cooperative personnel or be executed;
* Personnel who have been affected by anomalies should not act as CSD recruiting personnel, they may be treated as a subproduct of the anomaly and interned, inducted as cooperative personnel or executed;
* Each Site should have specialised personnel to record information on captured Children of Nihil members for future testing;
* Minimize the opportunity for Children of Nihil members in custody to interact outside sources.
