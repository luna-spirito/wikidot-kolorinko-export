**Registered Phenomena Code:** 872

**Object Class:** Gamma-White

**Hazard Types:** Electromagnetic Hazard, Sentient Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** Due to its simple procedure and material requirements, only senior researchers and one L1 CSD are to be informed on how to perform RPC-872. Personnel outside of this group will require authorization from the head researcher to access instructions for the anomaly. During experimentation, RPC-872-A instances are not to be removed from the testing area and all devices present must be disconnected from external networks.

Authority Security Force Rapid Response Teams Echo-5, Charlie-10, and Whiskey-2 have been assigned to locate and terminate RPC-872-A instances with the assistance of 631-Anomaly Guard Platoon.[[footnote]] Anomaly Guard is the wing of the PCAAO (People's Committee for the Acquisition of Anomalous Objects) responsible for the retrieval and long-term containment of anomalies under their authority. [[/footnote]] Should these personnel be confronted by civilians during operations, they are to claim to be law enforcement enforcing a recall of defective products. The Chinese government has agreed to support this story with misinformation campaigns.

Any individual not affiliated with the Authority or the PCAAO discovered to be performing RPC-872 is to be captured and handed over to the PCAAO for questioning.

Facilities and personnel located in China are not to purchase any items that could be affected by RPC-872.

Discussions to revise protocols will open following confirmation that less than 4,000 unaccounted RPC-872-A instances exist.

**Description:** RPC-872 is a ritual performed on specific objects that causes the development of basic sentience and an ability to control electromagnetic fields within a 0.6-meter radius. For the purposes of performing RPC-872, any small, bowl shaped, ceramic container with a handle qualifies as a suitable object for successful execution. Contact the head researcher, currently Dr. Lee Stewart, for information regarding how to perform RPC-872.

Objects subjected to RPC-872, hereby referred to as instances of RPC-872-A, will attempt to identify the individual responsible for performing RPC-872 on them and will subsequently execute any provided instruction within their faculty, if provided by the individual. To communicate, RPC-872-A instances will use their radius of control to stimulate the subject's Wernicke's area[[footnote]] Area of the brain primarily responsible for language comprehension. [[/footnote]]. However, instances are unable to control mental processes outside of this.

RPC-872-A instances will perform instructed tasks through manipulating electronic devices within their radius of control. They demonstrate the ability to rapidly learn how to utilize the electronics that they are exposed to and duplicate or memorize data as they deem necessary to accomplish tasks.

**Discovery:** RPC-872 initially came to Authority attention on ██/██/2016, when Site-038 suffered a containment breach due to security systems repeatedly crashing. During the following investigation, it was discovered that Dr. ██████'s personal electronics had been suffering similar systematic failures. Through exposing his possessions to computers disconnected from the main network, the cause was determined to have been a mug he had purchased a week prior to the incident.

Initially, it was to be classified as a lesser anomalous object, however the PCAAO came forward and explained the nature of the item. Reportedly, the item had been created by Committee agents that defected to the Church of Malthus and that a number of other, identical items had also been created and distributed.

After trading and discussing proposals, it was decided that containment of RPC-872-A instances would be best achieved by a partnership between the Authority and the PCAAO.

As part of containment efforts for RPC-872, the PCAAO has agreed to give as much information as they can to help while not sabotaging their own security. This includes some experiment logs, incident logs, and the documentation for the anomaly itself. Attached to this addendum is the most recent iteration of the main documentation of RPC-872, designated Anomaly No.631 by the PCAAO.

++ ##Black|**1. Anomalous Characteristics**##
Anomaly No.631 differs from the Consensus in 4 ways:

# It is a ritual that can alter Consensus objects to make them anomalous.
# Altered objects exhibit basic thought.
# Altered objects can communicate telepathically with people.
# Altered objects can control electronics.

++ ##Black|**2. History**##
The Committee originally came upon Anomaly No.631 during investigations following [REDACTED] in the summer of 198█. Anomaly No.631 was created by a group of anti-communist extremists in an apparent plot to hijack control of news networks and spread disinformation among the public. The plot was foiled by Anomaly Guard personnel and instructions for how to perform it were extracted from the conspirators. These instructions were archived for several years until the anomaly's use as a means of creating tools that could aid agents in infiltrating the ever-increasing number of digital systems present both locally and internationally became apparent.

This use of Anomaly No.631 would be approved in ██/██/199█ and the teaching of its steps would be incorporated into introductory training for most field personnel. Throughout the 90s and early 2000s, Anomaly No.631 played an important part in sabotage, intelligence, counterintelligence, and acquisition operations.

Going into the late 2000s, however, many of its uses would be outdone by non-anomalous technology. The Communist Party had begun to integrate protocols into most civilian programs and devices that would allow government access when necessary. At this point, the Committee began to phase out Anomaly No.631's use in operations. However, following a raid on a Church of Malthus facility in ██/██/2016, a developing issue was brought to light. While the Committee was already aware that the anomaly was being utilized by defecting agents for their own goals, the raid had revealed that the process of performing Anomaly No.631 could be industrialized. 1500 altered objects were discovered at the facility with another 5000 having been unaccounted for at the time.

Just two months after this raid, an RPC Authority facility in Hong Kong suffered a containment breach linked to an object altered by No.631. Quickly realizing the severity of the situation, the Authority was approached, discussions were held, and it was agreed that a joint effort to contain objects altered by No.631 was the best course of action.

Two years following the initial containment of RPC-872, Authority Central Intelligence began to file concerns regarding the anomaly. These concerns were caused by their personnel outlining a noticeable and predictable pattern in the rate at which uncontained RPC-872-A instances were being discovered. ACI was, as such, charged with investigating the issue. Attached to this addendum will be all relevant documents and notes where necessary.

++ ##Black|**Security Measures**##
To distract potentially hostile groups attempting to obtain Anomaly No.631, a secure locker is to be maintained in the Historic Items section of 1007 Storage Facility under the guise of containing a set of instructions for performing the anomaly. No actual documents detailing the steps are to be created. Staff that require its knowledge are to contact the project lead, currently Dr. Chen Wang, to be taught how to perform it and to be assisted in memorizing the steps.

At least three facilities responsible for mass performance of Anomaly No.631 are to be active at all times with altered objects they produce to be moved to 1052 Storage Facility. Every six months, one of these facilities is to undergo a false raid in which 631-Anomaly Guard Platoon will retrieve staff and confiscate any equipment so it may be relocated. In the event one of the facilities is discovered by an outside organization, a raid is to be conducted in which all nonessential staff are to be terminated and all equipment present is to be destroyed.

631-Anomaly Guard Platoon has been charged with planting and maintaining caches of objects altered by Anomaly No.631. Once every three months or shortly before important operations, one or more caches are to be "discovered" and brought to the attention of rival organizations such as the INDIRA, UNAAC, and RPC Authority for a means of diverting their attention and resources. When moving to recover and terminate the objects in these caches, 631-Anomaly Guard platoon is to disguise themselves as the area’s law enforcement and act as though the operations are to enforce recalls of defective products.
