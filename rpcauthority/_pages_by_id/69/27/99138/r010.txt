+ ##705381|Welcome to the "The World is our Oyster" Contest page. We are opening the following two separate contests.##

##717E78|**004 CONTEST**## - This contest has no theme. Make the best article you can to win the RPC-004 slot. 

##717E78|**DIVISION TALE**## - You must make an article that prominently features at least one of the three divisions [[[research-hub |Research]]], [[[protection-hub |Protection]]] and [[[containment-hub |Containment]]]. The top RPC will receive the RPC-543 slot. The top tale will win a featured slot. 

On the RPC Official [*https://discordapp.com/invite/wkXBuxt Community Discord] there will be an art contest! Draw and submit your favorite RPC. Message [[*user croal]]/Coupon#1773 or see the discord announcements for more information. 

Contact [[*user Croal]] if you have any questions about the contests. 

+ ##705381|The Deadline##
##717E78|**Submissions Open:**## May 10th 12AM EST
##717E78|**Submissions Close:**## May 11th
##717E78|**Winners are to be announced on:**## May 15th

+ ##705381|Rules##
* Anyone is allowed to participate
* Do not coldpost. You must receive feedback in the drafts forum for RPC's and Tales 
* Wait until the submission period begins to upload your page
* You may submit to multiple contests, but only one work to each individual contest 
* Anyone found using alt accounts to upvote their submissions will be disqualified and banned from entering any further contests 
* Submissions must match the theme in a justifiable way
* Pre-existing drafts are allowed, so long as they have never been posted to the wiki
* Edits to works are allowed

+ ##705381|To Enter##
##717E78|**Add the proper tag to your entry so it shows up on this page.**## 
To enter into the 004 Contest contest use the tag "004"
To enter into the Division Contest contest use the tag "DivisionContest"

Do not coldpost. You must receive feedback before posting. For the 004 contest, post your entry to the site with the URL "Contest:004Contest<yourusername>" with your site user name in place of <yourusername.> For the Divisions contest post your entry to the URL "Contest:DivisionContest<yourusername>". For example, if a user named Chad were to post an 004 entry it would have the URL Contest:004ContestChad. This page must be posted by you (or, if it is a collaboration, one of the partners). 

The submission for each category with the most upvotes will win. The winners of each contest will win bragging rights and a featured spot on the front page. The winner of the 004 RPC will receive the RPC-004 slot. The winner of the Division RPC will receive the RPC-543 slot. Those who do not win are encouraged to upload their submissions.
