Site Rules
[[/div]]

[[div class="content-panel"]]
++ General Conduct
* Be civil and respectful to all members.
* Do not raid or dox. [[footnote]]Raiding includes the brigade, spam or otherwise malicious actions towards other wikis. Doxing includes the release of others' personal information.[[/footnote]]
* Do not post illegal or pornographic content. 
* Do not brigade, call for downvotes, or vote maliciously on an article.
* Do not advertise external sites or products.
* Do not engage in targeted harassment. [[footnote]] Harassment: Repeated and/or continuous actions performed by user(s) with the intent to disrupt, hinder, disturb, harm, or otherwise disparage the target after being asked to stop by the user or staff.[[/footnote]]
* Do not vandalize or delete articles.
* Do not incite conversations about politics or push your ideologies on others.
* Do not register and use multiple accounts on the site. 
* Do not role-play.
* Do not impersonate any staff members.
* You must be 15 years or older to join.

++ Forum Conduct
* Keep threads on topic.
* Do not spam.
* Post threads in their proper forum category.

++ Writing Conduct
* Do not coldpost.[[footnote]]Coldposting is posting an article onto the series list without first posting to the draft forum and receiving at least two pieces of feedback. You must apply at least some of the criticism you receive or seek out more to apply to your article. If you're transferring your articles from the SCP website you must receive approval from staff or criticism before posting.[[/footnote]] For your draft to be accepted on the main series you must have at least two people give feedback on the forum.
* Please link your criticism thread on the discussion page of your articles. 
* Self-Inserts are not allowed.[[footnote]]For example, do not create an in-universe character that is named after your wikidot account.[[/footnote]]
* Do not post low-effort content. Try your best.
* Do not post fetish material or make RPCs needlessly sexual. 
* Do not post Joke RPCs to the main page.
* Do not link to RPC sandboxes from canonical pages.[[footnote]]This rule does not apply to metatext, such as guides and author pages. Use of assets hosted on the sandbox (e.g. images, themes) is also permissible on any page, though "localizing" them to the main site is recommended.[[/footnote]]
* Do not crosspost.[[footnote]]Crossposting is posting your article on multiple mediums (Holders or SCP) without change of concept. For example, do not take an SCP and submit it as an RPC, or vice-versa.[[/footnote]]
* Tangential or implied references to the SCP Foundation and/or its canon are permitted in canonical RPC Authority articles and Tales. Articles or Tales which directly or explicitly reference the SCP Foundation and/or its canon, except in the metatextual context[[footnote]] For example, non-fictional articles about the history of the Authority. [[/footnote]], must be tagged as non-canon.
* If you are using art in your work receive permission from the artist beforehand and source it correctly.[[footnote]]Get consent from authors before you reference or collaborate with their article. (However, if they do not respond in 1 month, you are allowed to write a collaboration under the assumption of abandoned media.)[[/footnote]]
* Do not plagiarize. Ensure that you credit both code and media.[[footnote]]**Above all else, do not use another person's work without giving clear credit.** Plagiarism is a major offense and could result in severe punishment if you do not provide proper attribution for content included in your works. If you use a piece of text or media created by someone else, even if paraphrased or modified, you must state the name of the original author and the name of the attributed work in an obvious location (such as the page's discussion forum). In certain cases—such as for including copyrighted material—you may be asked by staff to remove the content, but you will not receive a punishment if you had properly attributed the work.

When attributing work, you should always try to provide a direct link to the original content when possible. You should also try to credit for homages and major inspirational influences, but this is not strictly required.[[/footnote]]
* To create a hub page on the site, you must have at least three articles for it.[[footnote]]You are not required to receive feedback in the forum but it is highly encouraged to seek input if your hub has a lot of content.[[/footnote]]
* **DO** participate in the forums to get feedback on your writing.

++ Staff Conduct
* No RPC staff, be they Discord or Site, may present themselves as an authority figure or use their Staff influence while acting as a member of any sexual-content-based community, chat group, forum, or social space. Furthermore, no RPC Staff may found or claim a leadership position within such a community, chat group, forum or social space while acting in their RPC Staff capacity. Any RPC staff found to be acting in violation of this principle, regardless of their prior disciplinary record or behaviour, will be immediately removed from their Staff position and barred from assuming such positions in future. Any new Staff member should, before assuming said role, remove any links mentioning their associations to the RPC Authority community from relevant social media, forums, groups, chats and servers.
* If any user discovers evidence of an RPC staff member violating the above rule, they are strongly encouraged to pass that information on to the rest of staff and, if they deem it necessary, to post said information in a publicly-visible fashion in the RPC Office Discord server, in the interests of maximum transparency.

//The rules of this site are subject to change. To appeal a punishment message an admin.//
