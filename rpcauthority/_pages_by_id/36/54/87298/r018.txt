**Registered Phenomena Code:** 386

**Object Class:** Beta-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Organic Hazard, Bio-Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** All instances of RPC-386, including affected animals and individual instances of RPC-386 are to be contained in Authority Permafrost Containment inside [[[*http://www.rpc-wiki.net/site-008 |Site-008]]]. Under no circumstances are personnel Level-3 and under allowed any contact with RPC-386. All individuals found to be affected by RPC-386 are to be acquired solely by Authority agents who have undergone experimental TTN-Coating surgery and have scored at least a 24 of out of 32 on the Schweps-Bridger Neuropsychology Cognitohazard exam. All animals (excluding humans) are to be incinerated immediately upon acquirement.  

**Description:** RPC-386 is the designation given to the anomalous folding of the TTN Titin prion found in the sarcomere. [[footnote]] A structural unit of a myofibril in striated muscle. [[/footnote]] Normally, the TTN Titin protein acts as a spring for muscle contraction and allows for the return of muscle tissue into a relaxed state after compression and vise-versa. As the TTN Titin protein is approximately 1 nanometer in length, it is the largest organic protein currently found in animals (including humans). 

The anomalous properties of RPC-386 stem from its unpredictability in the contraction and relaxation of muscle tissue and its uncontrolled growth leading to various strength and energy-exertion effects. In animals who currently possess RPC-386, the muscle tissue is reinforced and improved by up to 200% normal strength than normal instances, and the seemingly impossible reproduction of muscle cells occurs at almost cancerous rates. The muscle tissue begins to expand and contract at inhuman proportions and begins to reproduce leading to extreme growths of muscles which leads to complete energy exertion within 24 hours of protein mutation. After individuals affected by RPC-386 expire, their bodies continue to produce heat and exert energy providing for an almost "reanimation" like the process where the individuals can complete various simple function such as movement and in some instances, acts of aggression. Instances of RPC-386 are found in high proportions of individuals near [[[*http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-616 |████████, Russia]]] in which high cases of various psychotic diseases have been found. These two findings; however, are assumed to be unrelated and only a product of coincidence. 

The effects of RPC-386 are exhibited as follows in time of occurrence:
* Extreme muscle growth leading to skin tearing.
* Densely packed growths appearing along the individual.
* Extreme elongation of protruding individuals arms, legs, and phalanges.
* Stroke and cardiac arrest followed by extreme heat production.
* "Reanimation" and extreme heat exertion followed by mindless movement and possible aggression. 
* Increased lactic acid production resulting in hypoxia[[footnote]] Deficiency in the amount of oxygen reaching the tissues.[[/footnote]] and muscle death. 

**Discovery:** RPC-386 was initially discovered in animal individuals (including humans) in the area surrounding Installation-616 following its construction around RPC-616. The anomalous mutation of the TTN Titin protein complex was discovered during an autopsy of a villager living around the area of Installation-616. They were to found to exhibit massive growths upon discovery and the internal protein environment was mapped only to discover the mutated muscle protein.

Upon discovery of the cognitohazardous effect of RPC-616 and RPC-███, it was hypothesized that the mutations arise as an unintended effect on the genetic integrity of the population surrounding RPC-616.

We have come face to face with the true forms of chaos, and it is in our best interest to contain them, because if we don't more of these things will sprout from this eldritch spawning ground, and more time will be wasted dwelling on chaos, something we are not prepared to handle.
