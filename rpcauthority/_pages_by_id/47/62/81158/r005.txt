[[==]]
**Numerical Name:** Romeo-7

**Codename:** Suited Gentlemen

**Motto:** Once more unto the breach, my friends.

**Garrisoned Site:** [[[Site-038]]]

**Task Force Priorities:** CQB Combat, Crowd Control, Asset Retrieval, High-Value Target (HVT) Extraction, Wetwork.

**Established:** 19th December, 1960

**Description:** Comprised mostly of former elite tactical unit operatives from urban environments. Romeo-7 predominately operates in metropolitan regions, where operatives retrieving assets or undertaking combat roles.

[[include component:image-block name=R7-MP5.jpg|width=150px|caption= Agent █████, photographed 03/11/08.]]

Due to the increasing urbanization of the world after the Second World War, as well as the most significant intelligence breach in the 1950s and the loss of support from 34 nations caused from the failure of Operation [DATA EXPUNGED][[footnote]]Operation [DATA EXPUNGED] was an operation conducted in Los Angeles in 195█. The operation resulted in ██ civilian casualties and the first coverage of the Authority by televised media.[[/footnote]], it was determined by Regional Director Armstrong in 1957 that the formation of a Mobile Security Team dedicated to operating within metropolitan areas was necessary.

The usefulness of Romeo-7 was quickly established during the Berlin Wall crisis, provoked due to the refusal of the German Democratic Republic to extract Authority assets and personnel in East Berlin after the erection of the Berlin Wall. Romeo-7 was successful in extracting all 80 personnel of Site-048-OL within 60 hours through various methods, with either the use of forged documents and bribery or through intimidation of border security being the most successful.

Romeo-7 was initially garrisoned in Site-002, operating within the U.S. West Coast. However, with the increased levels of urbanization in Eastern Asia, as well as the increasing relevance of the People's Committee for the Acquisition of Anomalous Objects within the global anomalous community, Romeo-7 was transferred to █████, Japan in 1972. Romeo-7 was subsequently relocated to Site-038 in 1984 and continues to oversee the conflicts between the PCAAO and other regional entities.

As Romeo-7 predominately operates within densely populated areas, it is not uncommon for Romeo-7 to be equipped with battledresses of local police tactical units or even two-piece suits. As a result, Romeo-7 has gained the nickname "Suited Gentlemen" within the Authority.

[[collapsible show="Operation Sandbox Wonder" hide="-hide Operation Sandbox Wonder"]]

**Mission Name:** Operation Sandbox Wonder

**Status:** Complete Success

**Date:** 25/06/1972

**Description:** Authority intelligence was notified that a highly prolific auction of the non-anomalous community that would involve several anomalous objects, including RPC-███. Due to the potential breaches in security that this auction would pose, as well as the potential involvement of other hostile GoIs, it was decided that MST Romeo-7 was to intervene. Romeo-7 was successful in neutralizing all threats by Amazing Co! and GEAR as well as retrieving all anomalous objects.

**Notes:** Operation Sandbox Wonder was the event in which Romeo-7 earned the nickname "Suited Gentlemen", as operatives were restricted to donning two-pieces suits during the operation.
