The Critic's Corner aims to acknowledge the hard work and dedication of site members who provide useful critique by giving them the chance to share what they believe is the cream of the crop of our site content. Here's how it all works!

After every month, we'll review all the crit posted on the drafts forum and on page discussion sections. The user we determine to have written the most thorough, detailed crit becomes Top Critic until the next rotation.

The Top Critic will be asked to pick one page on the site of their choice- **any page at all**, save for entries in contests/events whose voting period is currently open. A link to this page, as well as the Top Critic's username, is displayed on the sidebar for the month. A site member cannot be Top Critic [!--for more than two consecutive rotations--] twice in a row. [!-- We discussed the possibility of having repeat winners, but I'm excluding it for now --]

----

If you have any questions, don't hesitate to message a member of the Critic's Corner team.
