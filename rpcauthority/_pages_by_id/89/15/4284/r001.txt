**Item #:** RPC-682

**Object Class:** Beta {{[Gamma reclassification pending]}}

**Containment Procedures:** RPC-682 is to be contained by Mobile Strike Team Bravo-5 ("Nasty Fumigators") disguised as civilian fumigators, nests of RPC-682 must be sanitized and/or incinerated alongside with single RPC-682-1 instances, former nests must be buried in dirt and affacted buildings are to be surveyed for a minimum of 10-11 days including it's beauty products (including hygienic products as of 2/12/20██.//See Addendum XXXX-B//). If any unusual news about possible uncontained RPC-682-3 are to be found, Authority operatives are to search and cover information if RPC-682-3 and RPC-682-2 are confirmed. Said operatives must be wearing a gas mask at all times and avoid contact with RPC-682-2 and -3 instances. RPC-682-2 (including RPC-XXXX-3) instances are to be destroyed, their remains must be incinerated and their location sanitized to avoid any kind of infection.

**Description:** RPC-682 is the collective designation of an anomalous sub-species of the //Periplaneta australasiae//. RPC-682-1 are said instances which are distributed across Urban Areas. Nests of RPC-682-1 usually appear near supermarkets and shopping centers. RPC-682-1 are 23–35 mm in length and have an unusual bioluminescent line in the lower part of the prothorax[[footnote]] This line is blue in male instances and pink in female instances. [[/footnote]]. RPC-682's behaviour show nothing out of the ordinary[[footnote]] Breeding, nest forming and social interaction. [[/footnote]] with the exeption of horomone excretion as RPC-682-1 instances have a distinct smell extremly similar to shampoo and other washing products[[footnote]] This smell possesses hallucinatory effects.[[/footnote]]. Every summer season will cause RPC-682-1 to undergo Event 682-Alpha.

**Event XXXX-Alpha**: In this event, every RPC-682-1 instances affected by Event 682-Alpha will search for beauty products //(shampoo and perfume bottles, hand soap, etc.)// inside the nearest supermarket or shopping center and will then perforate them with a (as of now unknown to any other //periplaneta// species) probocsis and dispence a light brown substance into the beauty products. [[footnote]] Research has found that this substance is composed of cockroach fluids, large amounts of expired shampoo, [REDACTED] and [DATA EXPUNGED]. [[/footnote]] A secondary anomalous effect will cause the brand name of the beauty product to spontaneously change to "//Arnulf's Almond Metamorphosis©: Remake your beauty like a butterfly//" once RPC-682-1 inserts its mandibles. No known company shares this name. Infected products are hereby called RCP-682-2.
RCP-682 will then stop the event after they created ~10 RCP-682-2 instances and/or were found in the process.

RCP-682-1 will then rub its abdomen against the bottle, sectreting RCP-682-1 horomones. This attracts consumers into buying RPC-682-2. If the costumer (hereby called the subject) has entered a body of water and use RPC-682-2, Event 682-Beta will trigger.

**Event 682-Beta**: If a subject uses RPC-682-2 in a body of water, the fluids of RPC-682-2 will start to self-replicate over the subject, then it will harden and trap the subject inside a cocoon.[[footnote]] This process has of yet not been stopped by the subject since it has been found the smell of RPC-682-2 mixed with water works as a tranquilizer. [[/footnote]]. The cocoon possesses a brown tint, and it imitates the shape of a cockroach cocoon. The subject will then stay in a coma-state inside the recently formed cocoon for aprox. 2 months. Event 682-Beta can be stopped if the cocoon is broken prematurely[[footnote]] Subjects will still stay in their coma state for an indefinitely ammount of time. [[/footnote]].

After said 2 months, subjects will reach a "mature" state and break out of the cocoon. Subjects will mutate in the shape of several Periplantea species[[footnote]] This excludes anomalous species like RCP-682 itself, the //Gromphadorhina// ██████████ and [DATA EXPUNGED BY AUTHORITY COMMAND]. [[/footnote]]. Subjects affected will hereby be called RCP-682-3. RPC-682-3 are dazed momentarily and will then begin RPC-682-3 behaviour. Activities in the behaviour include:
* Constantly commenting on themes regarding beauty or prettyness.
* Seeking beauty products and quickly agitate in the lack thereof.
* Feed from said beauty products. How RPC-682-3 absorbs nurtients from them is unknown.
* Secreting RPC-682-1 horomones. Making them a dangerous hallucinatory threat to unprotected subjects.

**682-3 Interview Log 001 - ██:██ to ██:██ :** \

> ‍

> ‍

> ‍
> **RPC-682-3:** First, you put the shampoo, which is made from the dust of butterflies, isn't that something magical? After you put it on, it magnificently wraps around you and you start to be inside it, sleeping until you are reborn in beauty.
> ‍
> **Dr. Theodor:** How was it inside it?
> ‍

> ‍

They had nice and vibrant colors, you could easily see them. They didn't talk, but it //felt// like they told me something, something along the charming lines of "Share! Beauty!"

> **Dr. Theodor:** Stop, Ms. Kerlake! This behaviour is not going to get you anything. I am obligated to not give you-

> **RPC-682-3:** My beauty is rotting! You can't do this to me! You //won't// do this me!
> ‍
> **Dr. Theodor:** Ms. Kerlake do not force me to take violent action against you. Now let me finish me sentence and-
> ‍
> [RPC-682-3 jumps into the window and clutches itself onto it, RPC-XXXX-3 is seen biting and scratching the window inefficiently.]
> 
> [Dr. Theodor stands back from the window]
> ‍
> **Dr. Theodor:** Ms. Kerlake! Stop at this instant! You're getting nothing with this attitude. [Dr. Theodor backs up away from the window and aproaches the guard microphone.]

> **Dr. Theodor:** [Talking to Raulch ████ into the microphone.] Raulch. Please open the interview door and terminate Ms. Kerlake, she's not calming down. And i don't that thing thrashing around the chamber.

>

> ‍
>
> **<Begin Log>**

> ‍
>

> ‍

> ‍

> ‍
> **D-5639:** Those roaches, it's like if they were invading my god-fucking mind, they are still talking to me. They are saying something, but i don't understand what are they talking about, or how do I know they are talking at all.
> ‍
> **Dr. Javier ████:** Okay D-5639. Let's move on your dream experiences.

> **D-5639:** Oh so you don't even give a fuck about the shit I went through? Give some humanity for fuck's sake.
> ‍
> **Dr. Javier ████:** Calm yourself D-5639. If you don't, I will have no choice but to call security.

> ‍

> ‍

> ‍

Then it started, there were like, sudden pictures of cramped spaces, full of cockroaches, they had like this... neon lines in their body? Then I notice some few cockroaches running around, it gets weirder than that.

Suddenly I was sitting in a chair made of roach organs, eating my cereal made from roach organs, my son right next to me started mutating into some huge roach and felt into the wave of cockroaches that were reaching my neck. The smell was getting intense. I went to the cockroach-made bathroom to use my cockroach-made toothbrush like nothing is happening, my family were all extremly big cockroaches by now. But I didn't scream for help. I didn't want to escape. Because that smell, that smell, it's pure, it's...
