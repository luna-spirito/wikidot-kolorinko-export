**Registered Phenomena Code:** 563

**Object Class:** Beta-Orange

**Hazards:** Extreme Temperatures Hazard, Ideological Hazard, Sapient Hazard, Aggression Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-563's perimeter is to be maintained at all times. Assigned personnel are to check RPC-563 with thermal binoculars hourly for signs of activity. In the event RPC-563-2 is present, the site administrator is to be immediately notified. 

Only teams of 5 people or less may be sent into RPC-563 at once. Due to high fatality rates, these teams are to be comprised only of D-Class or low risk personnel. The equipment to be supplied per person includes: 1 lighter, 1 set of matches, 3 food rations, 1 water canteen, and a heavy winter outfit. Additional items can be assigned if necessary.

Any letters for RPC-563-1 are to be reviewed by a Level 4 official before delivery. This also applies to any received letters from RPC-563-1. 

**Description:** RPC-563 is an area with a circular radius of approximately 2,000 km located in Antarctica. Below are all of the the currently known anomalous properties inside of RPC-563.

* The average temperature within RPC-563 is -105°C[[footnote]]The weather inside of RPC-563 is in a constant blizzard state.[[/footnote]], deviating heavily from the surrounding area's temperature (-49°C).
* Most technology (including non-electronics) invented during or after the 17th century are rendered inoperative once inside RPC-563. This includes firearms, recording devices, cameras, heaters, etc.
* The plant life inside of RPC-563 consists of multiple pine tree and shrub species not native to Antarctica. This flora creates a large, dense forest encompassing most of RPC-563. 

Two known entities are present inside RPC-563, which are referred to as RPC-563-1 and RPC-563-2. 

RPC-563-1 are multiple sapient humanoid entities that are dressed in colonial era clothing[[footnote]]This was a common style for Europeans who traveled on ships.[[/footnote]]. A community of RPC-563-1 are located in a settlement roughly 5km from the northern edge of RPC-563. The area around this settlement is warm and humid with a high concentration of vegetation occupying it, contrasting with the rest of RPC-563. Several experiments have shown that RPC-563-1 refuse to leave RPC-563, even when threatened/bribed.

RPC-563-2 is an entity with an undocumented appearance that possesses telepathic abilities[[footnote]]The full scope of this ability is unknown, as the properties of RPC-563 make it extremely difficult to perform tests and accurately record data.[[/footnote]]. It is presumed that it uses this ability in order to subdue prey for consumption[[footnote]]While consumption has never been witnessed, several blood stains have been found near where subjects have disappeared.[[/footnote]]. All major expeditions (6 or more personnel) into RPC-563 have been halted due to interference from RPC-563-2. However, RPC-563-2 is much less likely to interfere with small operations (1 to 5 personnel). See document 563-B for details.

**Addendum 563-A:** RPC-563-1-12 Interview

[[collapsible show="+ Open File" hide="- Close File"]]

[[div style="background-color: #101010; border: solid 1px #333; box-shadow: 0px 0px 5px #333; margin: 10px 20px;"]]
[[div style="margin: 0px; text-align: center; background-color: #335B97; color: #fff;"]]
+ -- RPC-563-1-12 Interview --
[[/div]]
[[div style="padding: 0px 10px;"]]

**Interviewer:** Dr. ██████
**Interviewed:** RPC-563-1-12

**Date:** 3/10/9█
**Time:** 7:39 AM

**Foreword:** RPC-563-1-12 was forcibly taken from RPC-563.

[[div style="background-color: #1F2024; border-left: solid 2px #333; margin-left: 5px; padding: 5px 7px; border-radius: 0px 10px 10px 0px;"]]
**<Begin Log>**

**Dr. ██████ clears his throat.**

**Dr. ██████:** Let me start by saying I'm sorry about the handcuffs, but they--

**RPC-563-1-12:** Handcuffs? Is that was these things are? Bloody awful they are, chafing me hands up!

**Dr. ██████:** Yes... now, if you wouldn't mind I'd like to ask you a couple questions.

**RPC-563-1-12:** Since when does it matter if I minded anything, eh? I certainly minded when you lot picked me up outta me home and dragged me through the snow but that didn't stop ye, did it?

**Dr. ██████:** ... I understand that you're upset, but the faster you answer my questions, the faster you get home.

**RPC-563-1-12:** Fine then... just ask the damn questions.

**Dr. ██████:** Thank you. How would you describe your town, and general everyday life?

**RPC-563-1-12:** Nothing ever happens, to be honest. You just tend to yer farm and keep yer head down. Sure as hell beats going out the border though.

**Dr. ██████:** By "border" you mean going out into the snow, correct?

**RPC-563-1-12:** Aye.

**Dr. ██████:** What do you know about the outside of your town?

**RPC-563-1-12:** For one, you don't want to be going out there. Not only is it cold as hell, but... well, I don't like talking about it.

**Dr. ██████:** About what?

**RPC-563-1-12:** There's this--... it's complicated.

**Dr. ██████:** //What's// complicated?

**RPC-563-1-12:** I don't want to talk about it.

**Dr. ██████:** As I've said before, the faster you answer my que--

**RPC-563-1-12 slams their hands on the table.**

**RPC-563-1-12:** I said, I don't want to talk about it.

**Dr. ██████:** Okay... we'll revisit that. Can you tell me any important history you know about?

**RPC-563-1-12:** The only thing I can think of is when our ancestors first arrived here. We don't know much about them, only that they were looking for refuge from Britain. They were debtors of some sort.

**Dr. ██████:** Is that all?

**RPC-563-1-12:** No... I remember something about an outsider appearing once, from another town. He died of illness shortly after though.

**Dr. ██████:** Last question, can you tell me what you were going to say earlier, about what's beyond the border? 

**RPC-563-1-12 stays silent.**

**Dr. ██████:** ... Listen, if you don't answer the question, we're going to have to keep you here, and that's not good for either of us.

**RPC-563-1-12:** ... Fine, if you go outside of town, there's almost no chance of you returning. Not just because of the cold, but because of the Shepard.

**Dr. ██████:** "Shepard?"

**RPC-563-1-12:** I've never seen it myself, but there's plenty of folk who say they've seen it just on the outskirts of town. The legend is that if ye leave town, the Shepard 'll get ye. I've-- we've lost... a lot of people out there.

**Dr. ██████:** Hm... Well, thank you for your co-operation, we'll have you returned shortly.

**<End Log>**
[[/div]]

**Closing Statement:** Relations with the RPC-563-1 community have become exceedingly worse after the capture and death (during transit) of RPC-563-1-12. However, some valuable information about the lives of RPC-563-1 was gained. Additionally, the "Shepard" is believed to be RPC-563-2.

Several high ranking officials have been called in for damage control with RPC-563-1.

**Addendum 563-B:** RPC-563-2 Experiment Log

[[collapsible show="+ Open File" hide="- Close File"]]

[[div style="margin: 10px 30px; background-color: #333; color: #ccc; box-shadow: 0px 0px 10px #333; border: solid 3px #fff; font-family: monospace;"]]
[[div style="background-color: #1F2024; color: #333; padding-left: 20px; margin: 0px;"]]
+ RPC-563-2 Experiment 3
[[/div]]
[[div style="padding: 10px; margin: 0px; font-size: 1.25em;"]]
**Experiment Description:** Multiple subjects were placed inside RPC-563. A researcher transcribed the events via a nearby watchtower.

**Experiment Conducted By:** Dr. ██████
**Subjects:** 8 D-Class (D-8172, D-6483, D-4752, D-1932, D-6481, D-9363, D-7351, D-5392)

**Time:** 6:36 PM
**Date:** 5/4/9█ 

[[div style="padding: 5px; padding-left: 7px; border-left: solid 3px #fff; background-color: #3e3e3e; border-radius: 0px 10px 10px 0px;"]]
**<Begin Log>**

**6:36 --** Subjects have been successfully placed, and all personnel have exited the area.

**6:38 --** Agitated movement begins among the subjects, however, RPC-563-2 cannot be seen yet. Assumed to be an argument between them.

**6:42 --** 1 subject leaves, and another follows. Visual on them is lost.

**6:43 --** Physical confrontation between two subjects begins.

**6:46 --** The confrontation is stopped by another subject. The two appear to shake hands.

**6:49 --** A subject falls over, the others subjects investigate. They begin to stare disappointingly at the body. 

**6:50 --** They bury the terminated subject.

**6:54 --** 1 of the subjects that exited earlier returns. They appear to be panicking.

**6:55 --** The subjects all begin preparations to leave.

**6:56 --** RPC-563-2 is spotted near the subjects.

**6:57 --** RPC-563-2 appears and gestures at the subjects. 1 subject charges at RPC-563-2, but a meter away they collapse. 2 others make an attempt at escape, they also collapse.

**6:58 --** The remaining 3 subjects are struggling to stand while communicating with RPC-563-2.

**6:59 --** RPC-563-2 makes a motion with its hand, 2 of the 3 subjects stand up straight, and walk towards the third subject. They grapple and hold them down onto the snow.

**7:00 --** RPC-563-2 approaches and kneels down, it appears to be touching their chest and stomach.

**7:01 --** The subject begins struggling violently.

**7:03 --** The two subjects let go, and exit with RPC-563-2. The remaining subject is unconscious, a team is dispatched to retrieve them.

**<End Log>**
[[/div]]
**Closing Statement:** The surviving subject had a message from RPC-563-2 carved into their skin: "Your armies are not allowed. You must abide by my rules." The subject died shortly after from a combination of blood loss and frostbite.
[[/div]]
[[/div]]

[[div style="margin: 10px 30px; background-color: #333; color: #fff; box-shadow: 0px 0px 10px #333; border: solid 3px #fff; font-family: monospace;"]]
[[div style="background-color: #fff; color: #333; padding-left: 20px; margin: 0px;"]]
+ RPC-563-2 Experiment 7
[[/div]]
[[div style="padding: 10px; margin: 0px; font-size: 1.25em;"]]
**Experiment Description:** The subject was sent into RPC-563 equipped with a primitive "two cups and a string" method[[footnote]]Two paper cups (not created in a factory, but by hand) had a hole punched through them, then a string was tied through this hole. This allows for sound to travel  into the cup, through the string, out the other cup, and into the recorder outside of RPC-563.[[/footnote]] system to circumvent RPC-563's properties. On the other end is an audio recorder.

**Experiment Conducted By:** Dr. ███████
**Subject:** D-4790

**Time:** 7:08 AM
**Date:** 8/10/9█
[[div style="padding: 5px; padding-left: 7px; border-left: solid 3px #fff; background-color: #3e3e3e; border-radius: 0px 10px 10px 0px;"]]
**<Begin Log>**

**There is a large amount of audio static, most likely from the weather.**

**Dr. ███████:** Okay, how do you feel?

**Subject:** Cold.

**Dr. ███████:** Emotionally, I mean.

**Subject:** Oh, sorry. I'm feeling... I'm not sure, sad, I guess.

**Dr. ███████:** Alright, just wait for now.

**<Fast Forward>**

**Dr. ███████ (in the distance):** Well do you see it yet?

**Unintelligible response.**

**Dr. ███████ (in the distance):** I don--

**Subject:** Excuse me? How much longer do I have to wait?

**Dr. ███████ (in the distance):** What... actually.

**Dr. ███████ walks back.**

**Dr. ███████:** Could you make some noise?

**Subject:** What?

**Dr. ███████:** Make some noise, yell.

**Subject pauses.**

**Subject:** Sure... I guess.

**Subject clears throat.**

**Subject (loud):** Hello? Anybody?

**Dr. ███████:** Keep going, I'll be back.

**<Fast Forward>**

**Dr. ███████ (in the distance):** It's there? Good, I'll go check on the subject.

**Dr. ███████ walks back.**

**Dr. ███████:** Do you feel strange at all?

**Subject:** Not really.

**Dr. ███████:** Are you sure?

**Subject:** Well, maybe a bit warmer. Does that help?

**Dr. ███████:** No. Look around, do you see anything?

**Subject:** No... just trees and bushes.

**Dr. ███████:** Okay... keep looking.

**Dr. ███████ walks away.**

**Dr. ███████ (in the distance):** Where is it now?

**Unintelligible response.**

**Dr. ███████ (in the distance):** Really? He should be seeing it by now.

**Subject:** I'm still not seeing anything.

**Dr. ███████ walks back.**

**Dr. ███████:** How do you feel now? 

**Subject:** Kinda tired. And... I feel a lot... hotter.

**Dr. ███████:** Hotter? Like you're warm?

**Subject:** Yea...

**Dr. ███████:** Okay just--

**A loud thud is heard.**

**Dr. ███████:** Hello?

**Subject:** It's so... inviting.

**Dr. ███████:** What?

**Subject:** The snow. It just looked so comfortable.

**Dr. ███████:** ... How do you feel now?

**Subject:** I don't know... the snow, it's just so... comfy. It's even better than a bed.

**Dr. ███████:** Do you see anything?

**Subject:** Yea... a man in a robe, in the distance. It's really... calming. I guess it's calming to know that there's somebody looking after you.

**Dr. ███████:** Is there anything else you feel?

**Subject:** No... I just want to stay here.

**Dr. ███████ gets out a radio.**

**Dr. ███████:** Okay, we got it, go ahead.

**Subject:** Who are you talking to? 

**Dr. ███████:** Nobody, just wait there.

**<End Log>**
[[/div]]
**Closing Statement:** The subject was retrieved after RPC-563-2 was lured away. The subject was noticeably more docile after the experiment. They also requested to be sent back into RPC-563, the request was denied.

All subjects affected by RPC-563-2 have reacted in largely different ways. It is assumed that how RPC-563-2's ability affects someone varies from person to person.
