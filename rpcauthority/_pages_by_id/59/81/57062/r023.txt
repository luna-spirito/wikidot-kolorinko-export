**Registered Phenomena Code:** 365

**Object Class:** Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** Electromagnetic Force Hazard, Mechanical Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-365 is stored in the [[[*http://www.rpc-wiki.net/site-008|Site-008]]] anomalous items research wing, room 46-B.  Testing of RPC-365 is limited to personnel of Level 3 or higher.

Individuals who have undergone Caduceus Events are to be transferred to Site-008's medical wing and kept constantly monitored.

Unauthorized access of RPC-365 is to be met with detainment, amnesticization, and possible demotion. 
 
**Description:** RPC-365 is a modified air displacement plethysmograph (ADP), used to measure body composition.[[footnote]] Body fat, weight, density, etc. [[/footnote]] RPC-365 is connected to a computer that allows it to function as a standard ADP. In addition to its standard function, RPC-365 is also capable of initializing anomalous events known as Caduceus Events. During a Caduceus Event, RPC-365's window becomes opaque and the following events occur: 

* High magnetic attraction
* Increased energy usage
* Visual warping near RPC-365
* Induction of headaches to nearby individuals
* Decreased levels of humidity
* Increased room temperature

Individuals are capable of entering RPC-365 and resting within a seat inside. Near the seat is an emergency cancellation button used to stop RPC-365's standard function. Whether this button is functional or not is unknown, as individuals and items inside are unable to move while RPC-365 is active.

When activated, individuals inside are subjected to a mutation process in which they are transformed into infantile versions of themselves, designated RPC-365-1. --It is currently unknown if RPC-365-1 instances retain memories following Caduceus Events as they are placed into comatose states following their transformation.-- ##4f92ff|Individuals inside are placed into comatose states following a Caduceus Event. The resulting RPC-365-1 instances awaken from their comatose state anywhere between two and three weeks following the event. Testing has shown that all prior memories are retained, but RPC-365-1 instances are unable to communicate properly until they reach the age of two.##[[footnote]] Relative to their initial Caduceus Event. [[/footnote]]^^,^^[[footnote]] While instances retain memories, early life brain developments hinder language capability and complex thought.[[/footnote]]

Shortly after its discovery and transfer, two CSD personnel were tested with RPC-365 in an event known as Caduceus-PRIME. Details of this event can be found at --▓▚▓▙█▛▉▒▅▖--

**Interview Log 365/1 Recorded on 04/05/2008**
 [[/=]]
[[<]]
**Interviewed:** RPC-365-1-1 (Formerly D-1126)
 
**Interviewer:** Dr. Afton
 
**Foreword:** The following interview was conducted 2 years after D-1126 underwent transformation by a Caduceus Event. Dialogue edits have been made post-recording to correct vocal mistakes by RPC-365-1-1.

-----
**<BEGIN LOG>**

**Dr. Afton:** Good afternoon RPC-365-1-1.

**RPC-365-1-1:** Oh, so I'm one of those things now?

**Dr. Afton:** Well, following the event, yes.

**RPC-365-1-1:** Great. So what do you want me to talk about?

**Dr. Afton:** How do you feel?

**RPC-365-1-1:** Better than before. I can actually speak now. I tried like hell but this past week I've gotten better. I still stumble a bit, though.

**Dr. Afton:** That's perfectly normal for a chil- sorry, someone of //your// age.

**RPC-365-1-1:** See? That's my main issue with this. Before I was this intimidating badass. Now I'm a god damn toddler. People don't take me seriously. Just the other day I was practicing words. Y'know, the most useful ones and this doctor jackass told me to watch my mouth. Come on, man.

//RPC-365-1-1 shifts in its chair.//

**RPC-365-1-1:** God, this sucks. You don't think putting me in that thing will bring me back?

**Dr. Afton:** I'm absolutely positive it won't.

**RPC-365-1-1:** Anything else you need to know?

**Dr. Afton:** What was it like inside RPC-365?

**RPC-365-1-1:** Weren't you there? I remember telling you this already.

**Dr. Afton:** My memory of the event is uh, hazy to say the least.

//RPC-365-1-1 begins to speak, but pauses.//

**RPC-365-1-1:** Huh, I guess mine is too. Anyway, from what I remember, it wasn't like I thought it'd be like.

//RPC-365-1-1 laughs.//

**RPC-365-1-1:** I've seen those things before. Well-- not //those// things, the non-crazy versions. Used to measure-- uh, body... composition?

**Dr. Afton:** That's correct.

**RPC-365-1-1:** I thought I was just being taken for some sort of routine testing. I couldn't be more wrong. I faintly remember a massive amount of pain.

**Dr. Afton:** Anything else?

**RPC-365-1-1:** I went into a coma right?

**Dr. Afton:** Yes, that's correct. Do you remember anything from the coma? Dreams perhaps?

**RPC-365-1-1:** I faintly remember one. It was kinda like I was being... reborn, for lack of a better word.

**Dr. Afton:** Can you elaborate?

**RPC-365-1-1:** Well uh-- it was very dark. Then it was very bright.

**Dr. Afton:** Is that it?

**RPC-365-1-1:** I think there were stars? I'm not entirely sure.

**Dr. Afton:** Alright. Thank you for your time.

**<END LOG>**

-----
**Brief overview:** Following its initial test, RPC-365 was assumed to merely revert individuals to a state of infancy. However, this was disproven. RPC-365-1 instances retain memories prior to undergoing a Caduceus Event. Technology like this is inconceivable in the modern world. The practical applications of these properties are highly beneficial for the Authority, which is why I propose extensive testing of RPC-365. 

RPC-365 could potentially be beneficial to the Authority in that it can effectively and theoretically extend an individual's lifespan to an infinite degree. I also believe that RPC-365 is capable of curing diseases and physical damage to the body, as evidenced by CSD-1126's^^[((bibcite d1126))]^^ lack of knee pain post-Caduceus-A.

I wish to lead and conduct further tests on RPC-365 for the forseeable future to test the extent and limit of its anomalous abilities.

Frequent updates will be provided should this request be accepted.

I am hesitant to let you assume a lead position in the testing of RPC-365. At the present moment, it is nothing more than a machine to restart one's life. There still is the possibility of catastrophic outcomes in RPC-365-1 instances which I refuse to let occur.

Not to mention the fact that you have not attached any relevant files regarding RPC-365 test subjects when submitting your request.

I will, however, allow you to continue studying the lives and growth of RPC-365-1-1 and -2. Perhaps then we will discuss further testing.
