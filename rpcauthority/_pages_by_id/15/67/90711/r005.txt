**Summary:**
The Authority was created sometime ago (somewhere in the 1800’s though up to interpretation) by the Vatican, British Empire and maybe France, inspired by an Ancient Anomalous Vanguard created by the Roman Empire. The vatican proposed the creation of the Authority to the British cause their Empire is the biggest, thus has the best reach to find and contain new anomalies. The British empire also employed Indigenous Anomalous keepers into the Authority for their expertise in containment, either voluntarily or by force. The use of former Napoleonic forces helped contribute to make a sizeable military in case of extreme circumstances. During the Early 20th Century, The Authority split from the British Government and planned WW1 to fund themselves for the future. After WW1, the Authority was self sustaining and connected to all countries in the League of Nations. During WW2, they had to fight against the Ahnenerbe and the Nazi Occult Society and maybe allied with the Allies to help retake invaded sites throughout Europe. During the Cold war, they had to compete with a Russian and American GOI, an issue which wouldn’t be solved until the fall of the Soviet Union. Also during the Cold war, they took the time to update and Modernise everything, from Containment Protocols and Format to Logos and Equipment. A small rebellion happened in the Authority, only strengthened by an alternate version of the Authority (RCP) supporting the rebellion, which led to a pyrrhic victory for the RPC Authority. With the fall of the Soviet Union, they absorbed the Soviet GOI and overtake their sites and retake their old sites behind the iron curtain. They spend the last of the 20th Century negotiating with Countries and making deals with them.

**Ancient Anomalous Vanguard:**

Created by The Roman Empire to Destroy or Contain Anomalies and usually posted in the extended parts of the empire. Could’ve been created by Caesar and later made official by a paranoid emperor like Caligula (which records indicate he tried to fight gods) Slaves are used as Class D. Allied with some indigenous Anomalous keepers if they couldn’t destroy the Anomaly and in a friendly territory.

Some rival Vanguards seem to be Carthaginian Anomalous Keepers (possibly an ancient order though no other records exist), the apostles of Jesus, Gaul Druids, Sorcerer’s of Brittanica and Egyptian cults. 

Greeks had the idea to try and understand Anomalies, and had a vanguard dedicated to understanding them (could explain why their civilisation was so advanced). This was merged into the Roman Vanguard after the empire’s expansion into Greece. Thus began the use of slaves as more than feeding items/hazardous removal for anomalies, now they used them for testing.


**Medieval Times and Renaissance**

With the Fall of the Roman Empire, The Vanguard disbanded, but members kept Anomalies for further containment/protection. During the dark ages, Anomalies were seen as either sent from god or Satan and any humanoids were considered either cursed or witches. There were a few medieval vanguards created out of the Remnants of the Roman Vanguard and they protected /used “holy” items and destroyed/contained objects and people of Satan. Many of them lived nomadically, roaming around, destroying Anomalies where they could.

With the 1st Crusade, all knights in Europe were joined by the Pope to take back the holy land from the Sacrean Military, even the knights of Roaming Vanguards. And so they all pledged allegiance to the Vatican/Pope and all Vanguards merged into the Vatican GOI, The Swords of Christ. Some Anomalies tied themselves into the Crusades, and with it’s Conclusion, a sect of elite knights split off. They claimed the Vatican’s bias to “Satanic items” despite some having a helpful nature was irresponsible and create The Authoritas, a small Mercenary Vanguard that existed until the 1800’s.

The Protestant Reformation created a rivalling religious faction after Anomalies, with many Swords merging with the Protestant GOI and due to the Vatican losing much power over Europe in this time, it lead to a secret war. With an Agreement reached with the Protestant GOI, the Vatican eventually contacted the British empire for help in creating a stronger force to deal with Anomalies. At some point, the Authoritas are persuaded back and remerged into their 1st MST, The Angels of Eden.

**1800’s and the World Wars:**

The British empire used remnants of the French Imperial Army to help create the Authority’s military similar to the Roman Vanguard. They appointed Global directors from every part of the empire and ensured that the Directors could run the Authority with no biases to benefit any country in case of an uprising. This had to be a global effort so during the years countries were slowly added in: Authority mainly created by the Vatican and British Empire (maybe France, records unspecific) in its entirety. (The creation date is unclear with many wars happening during the first half of the century and with England going into “Splendid isolation” in 1871 until WW1) In the late 1800’s (during Splendid Isolation), the higher ups of the Government created MI13 in response to the Royal families growing concern of Anomalies. The Ministers that founded the Authority attempted to keep the Authority’s origins a secret from MI13 and proceeded to destroy all files linking them and the Vatican to the Authority. MI13 would not discover the Authority until the early 20th Century due to the Ministers covering up Authority meddling.

In 1900 the British Parliament attempted to secretly pass a decision to nationalise the Authority and merge it with MI13. The Board in protest broke all personal ties to the Government and became independent. Due to the split, the Authority started manufacturing a war to fund themselves for the next few decades. This came in the shape of the assassination of Franz Ferdinand by the Angels of Eden and thorough the years leading up, they ,manufactured many arms to fund themselves short term. They spent the war moving Anomalies to a fledgling site in the US, and manufacturing more army surplus to further help fuel their departure from the British Empire. With the end of the war and the League of Nations created, the Authority expands and is no longer connected exclusively to the British Empire, however Russia is giving no support to the Authority due to it’s revolution (records indicate that they never were, but who knows). During the Great Depression, the amount of unemployment helps the Authority, possibly with recruitment and possibly helps them make up the procedure of using businesses as fronts (could've had a long term impact in helping end the Great Depression?).

With WW2, the Authority tries to keep RPC’s hidden from the Nazi party, but Hitler's fascination with anomalies eventually gets them discovered and he creates an Anomalous tracking branch of the Ahnenerbe[[footnote]] The Nazi party's think tank [[/footnote]] (Anomalieerkennung division) to track some down for the war effort. His actions also inspire the German Branch of the Authority (FSE) to split in 1938 and become loyal to the Nazi Party. The FSE also change their name to the Nazi Occult Society (NOS) to prove their allegiance to Hitler's vision.
Predicting this split the Authority moved all RPC’s that could be transported safely out of Europe. Many Gamma and Beta RPC's were left on site, leading to the Authority to eventually team up with the Allies in 1941 to reclaim their sites, either through providing men for the war effort, or using RPCs to cut a way through to their occupied sites (sources are unspecific). With the end of the war, the Ahnenerbe and NOS are officially dissolved but some splinter cells hang around until 1947, when the Authority hunts down the last cells.

**The Cold War:**
During the Cold war, the Authority is missing some sites due to either the Nazi parties attempts to destroy them and the Iron Curtain. An agreement is made with Stalin allowing them to create their own GOI, and the Authority hand over all Eastern European sites to the Russian GOI. The Authority is primarily self sustaining now, and they modernise everything during the technology race, Containment procedures, Military equipment, Covert operations and even begin using RPC’s for their benefit, creating the Theta Classification. They also start writing themselves out of history, destroying any remaining evidence of their British and Vatican Origins, fabricating evidence to look like they crafted agreements with the British and Vatican back in those times.

They make covert agreements regarding RPC’s with the UN and EU. Only missing out on Countries that refused outside help (Russia, China, Cuba, Chile after the military Coup and other countries run by Dictators during this time) and the US agreement being amended when they implement their own (inexperienced) GOI to compete with the Soviet GOI (somewhat inexperienced), leaving the Authority to lose support from the US (but they’re not exclusively hostile). McCarthyism also made Containment for U.S. RPC’s difficult with many citizens trying to connect/ the Authority and Anomalies back to Russia and with a few even trying to expose the Authority. 

In 1979, a rebellion attempt happened inside the Authority, with many low ranking researchers and security personnel rebelling to forcefully push the Authority in a direction of helping the public and punishing crime. This rebellion happened over 3 main sites and was lead by a Doctor Yeung (access to the Doctor's files are restricted to lvl 5. No way i was finding that out). the rebellion only worsened with the introduction of an alternate version of the RPC Authority, from (this is a theory accepted by many in the Authority) an alternate timeline. They called themselves the Righteous Centralised Protection Authority, and attempted to aid the Rebellions. The interfering helped elongate the rebellion over the next year, leaving the Authority with heavy casualties but victorious in quelling the rebellion and stopping the RCPA from invading our world.

In the 1980’s with Gorbachev elected as leader of the USSR, he allowed Western culture into the Soviet Union. The Authority uses this as an entry into the USSR and gains agreements with them, maybe overtaking and merging with the Russian GOI.

With 1992, they gain agreements with the reunified Germany and former individual Soviet Union states (with its fall). China makes their GOI to make up for the dissolution of the USSR (which supplied them with their GOI). The US GOI is also dissolved due to ineffectiveness (maybe remade after 9/11, records unclear) and the Authority is given more freedom in the US. Any other countries previously closed also make agreements with the Authority.
