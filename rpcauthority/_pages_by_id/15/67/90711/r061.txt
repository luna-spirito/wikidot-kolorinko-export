**Introduction:**

--What if I told you that there was another world--
--When I was a boy--
Throughout history there have been several unexplained events and characters, we took to calling them anomalous. Whether these were elaborate hoaxes, I cannot say for certain. However, here under our noses lies a hidden world where people go everyday to study said anomalies, both weird and dangerous. (Add more later)

So I ask you dear reader, what is The Authority? To answer that question, we must go back to the times of the Roman Empire, and meet the first recorded instance of an organisation who face the unknown as though it was nothing more than another day in Rome: The Sons of Hercules.


**Ancient Organisations and the Sons of Hercules:**

The Sons of Hercules were created by generals and legionnaires of the Roman Empire to destroy anomalous hostile entities they consider a threat to the republic, or contain artifacts they deem to be gifts of the gods. By all known records found this was considered the first international organisation, usually posted in conquered lands, with an elite division stationed in Rome itself. Records are unclear when exactly this organisation was founded, created either just before or during the reign of Caesar as 1st Emperor of Rome. Many slaves are recorded in messages from officers as being used as a primitive version of Authority CSD, with a much higher mortality rate. The Sons of Hercules seemed to try and ally themselves with indigenous anomalous keepers if destruction of an entity was impossible and negotiations with previous keepers could reach an agreement. There are some mythological references to the organisation's involvement, such as the likes of Minerva being a primary influence in their formation. However, the validity of this claim is unclear given the number of contradicting reports from various sources from each time period.

A crucial turn in the goals of the Organisation came about with the invasion of Greece and their assimilation. With the similarities of culture and belief systems, it made integration  easier throughout the course of time. The Sons of Hercules came upon a prominent academy of scholars and philosophers in Athens. This organisation was dedicated to understanding and using artifacts they too saw some as gifts of the gods (could explain why their civilization was so advanced, check resources). The academy merged with the Vanguard, creating a more effective system that reduced mortality rates of slaves and ensure a more dedicated research institution. By the time the republic fell and the autocracy was formed, the Sons of Hercules retained much of their previous duties. Over time, many of the Roman emperors attempted to reduce the Vanguard's influence in the military, including their right to hold the artifacts and entities they procured over the years. This has lead to some altercations between the emperors and the vanguard, which has even lead to the vanguard being involved in the assassination of Emperor Caligula after he was deemed as a threat to the senate, nobility, military personnel and attempted to claim divinity by leaving Rome for Alexandria.


**The Precursor Order and the rise and fall of the Auctoritas**

With the fall of the Roman Empire, The Sons of Hercules were unofficially dissolved, with small groups from the organisation still around for containing/neutralizing anomalies. During the Dark Ages and due to the spread of Catholicism, most anomalies were seen as Holy or Satanic and various humanoids were considered either demons, cursed or witches (need stronger sources, look into witch hunts). A split within the Sons of Hercules remnants due to religious conversions, forming small groups that took to using artifacts they protected, considering them “holy” items while seeking out "Satanic" items to destroy or contain. Many of them lived nomadically, roaming around and destroying anomalies where they could.

With the 1st Crusade, all knights in Europe were united by the Pope to take back the holy land from the Saracen Military, even the knights of the roaming Vanguards. They all pledged allegiance to the Vatican/Pope and all Vanguards merged into an as of yet unknown order who employed and safeguarded many anomalies that existed during this time. Records indicate a few anomalies discovered/awoken by the Crusades (with one of the strongest sources mentioning the box of Pandora).

With the end of the Crusades, a sect of elite knights proclaimed grievances to the pope themselves, claiming the Vatican’s bias to destroying “Satanic items” at all costs was irresponsible and lead to many unneeded deaths. The council excommunicated all who complained (link grievance accord here) and dissolved the order. All anomalous assets were then handed over to a recently constructed group known as the Auctoritas Imperata (Need to find name of the creator, possible Pope?). The Auctoritas provided a wider expanse with various discovery reports of anomalous instances coming from priests conducting confessions (Best examples: Pgs 43, 200, 2455 and 3344). With a seemingly rise of demonic sightings, a small formation of demon hunters was seemingly created to combat this sudden rise (link letter to Auctoritas scholar here). The Auctoritas would stay as a dominant power in the Western world for centuries, untill the Protestant reformation and the creation of the Union.

The Union was initially created as a response to the Auctoritas, with many unhappy with the suppression of anomalous activity and knowledge of the anomalous. Due to the loss of the Vatican’s power throughout the years the Union quickly became large enough to rival the Auctoritas. A small but secret war occurred, which is suddenly brought to a halt in the late 1700's (All records seem to point to something called "The Anti-Christ”).

Soon after the supposed defeat of the "Anti-Christ", an agreement was reached with the Union and the Auctoritas to form a stronger force to deal with anomalies. While few in The Union agreed to establish the Authority, a majority of members left to establish a presence in the Americas. The planning and creation of the Authority took the rest of the century, with multiple meetings occurring and various disagreements taking years to resolve. Eventually, both parites agree to propose this to the British royal family and they agreed to help. (The mercenaries are persuaded back and remerged into their 1st MST, The Angels of Eden. **1800’s and the Creation of the Authority:**

The British Royal family use their influence to pull imperial guards and remnants of the French Imperial Army to help create the Authority’s military similar to the Roman Vanguard. They appointed a Global Leaders (later changed during the 50’s to Global Directors) from every part of the empire and ensured that the directors could run the Authority with no biases to benefit any country in case of an uprising.

During the Mid 1800’s, a declaration of manifest was allowed, giving the Authority permission to secretly work in countries and colonies outside of Britain’s reign and bring back anomalies to contain. This proved incredibly dangerous with the first ever establishment of the American branch, which was continuously harassed and persecuted by the Union. The Union, believing in sovereignty, attempted to destroy any Authority sites established. To the outside world this was seen as mass religious persecution, which was only ended with a negotiation and treaty signing. Hostilities between the Authority and The Union would stay, even as the Authority eventually obscured the Union during the following years to only occupy fringe groups and eventually blend in with newer forming Protestant groups, effectively hiding themselves to the present day.

In the late 1800’s (during splendid isolation), the higher ups of the government created The Bureau of Anomalous Research Operations (BARO) in response to the growing concern of anomalous events in Britain. The Royalty that founded the Authority attempted to keep the Authority’s origins a secret from BARO and proceeded to destroy all files they could linking them, the Vatican and the Protestant GOI to the Authority’s creation. The Authority would only be discovered in 1903 by MI13, (the current Anomalous agency in the UK)  due to the cover up of Authority meddling.


**The World Wars:**

The Imperial Defence Committee on discovering the Authority and MI13 in late 1903 secretly built up a decision to pass through parliament to nationalise the Authority and merge it with MI13 during its reorganisation in early 1904. The Board, in protest, broke all personal ties to the government and royal family to become independent.

Due to the split, the Authority started buying many fledgling industries and companies to fund themselves for the foreseeable future. This would be beneficial as WW1 started, with the Authority making massive profits from the war and using their ties in the industry to move all anomalies in predicted war zones to a fledgling site in the US (their first US site, named site-02).
With the end of the war and the League of Nations created, the Authority expands and is no longer connected exclusively to the British Empire, however Russia is giving no support to the Authority due to it’s revolution (records indicate that they never were, but who knows? There could have been a small window of time). During the Great Depression, the Authority is crippled with the value of money worsening, however recruitment skyrockets during this time. The Great Depression also helps the Authority establish multiple businesses to use as fronts (could've had a long term impact in helping end the Great Depression? Records unspecific).

[[collapsible show="Nucorp Industries and their Relationship with the Authority" hide="Close"]]

> Records found on Nucorp Industries indicate their creation being in 1914, with the company starting out as a scientific research company that sold plans and blueprints to buyers. The Authority initially tried to buy Nucorp in 1929, but negotiations fell through due to the stock market crashing and the Start of the Great Depression. Nucorp gained Authority attention again with the plans and copyright for the 1st nuclear reactor being sold to the US Government in 1939. The Authority approached Nucorp again after WW2 offering a partnership, promising it to be beneficial to both sides. An agreement was made and the Authority helped establish their first manufacturing plant in 1956. It was used during the construction of the Authority Battlecarrier, which lasted from the 60's to the 70's. Their current relationship is still friendly, with Nucorp even reverse engineering Anomalies for the Authority to be beneficial.
[[/collapsible]]

With WW2, the Authority strived to keep anomalies in general hidden from the Nazi party, but Hitler's fascination with the occult led to him forming special military expeditions to recover them. Records indicate this occurred with the help of the Gestapo spying on known Authority assets. Due to The board declining to speak with Hitler on using anomalies, Hitler commanded Heinrich Himmler to create an Anomalous Agency for containing, researching and weaponizing anomalies. This GOI was co-created by 3 other groups: The Ahnenerbe, which made up the scientific division, members of the Thule Society which made up the Occult division (known the the Black sun Division), and the German Branch of the Authority which went rogue to follow Hitler's vision in 1935 and used their expertise to make GARD a formidable opponent.

Predicting this split the Authority moved all remaining RPC’s that could be transported safely out of Europe. Many Gamma and Beta RPC's were left on site, leading to the Authority to eventually team up with the Allies in 1941 to reclaim their sites, either through providing men for the war effort, or using RPCs to cut a way through to their occupied sites. (these 2 options were discussed, but which one was implemented is unknown) With the end of the war, GARD was officially dissolved but some splinter cells hang around to this day.

**The Cold War:**

During the Cold war, the Authority had yet to reclaim all of their lost assets and facilities. Either due to the Nazi party's attempts to destroy them during the Allied advance, or the establishment of the Iron Curtain. The Authority was forced into a one sided agreement made with Stalin, allowing him to establish his own special division using Authority assets they claimed since the 2nd World War. The Authority had to hand over all Eastern European sites to the Russian GOI.

Other agreements made regarding RPCs were run through the newly established UNAAC .(Formed in 1944) Created with the sole purpose to certify Anomalous agencies in other countries and regulate their involvement to prevent militarized use of anomalies, in what would be known as the 1945 International Anomalous Agreement. Only a few countries refused to sign the Authority's agreement. (Cuba, Chile and other countries run by Dictators during this time) Shortly after however, controversy broke out in which the US agreement secretly broken their agreement to compete against the Soviets. This lead to the Authority losing a considerable amount of financial resources and political influence, and with McCarthyism, acquisition and containment of U.S. RPCs became a difficult endeavor. Many citizens attempted to connect the Anomalies back to Russia and with a few even trying to expose the Authority as a Communist occult organization.

This agency was created to document the anomalies left behind by the previous anomalous agency, and to work alongside other facilities created and funded by The People’s Liberation Army. (henceforth PLA).

The investigation would last for four years in part of the Soviet's backing of the PLA by impeding UNAAC's investigative efforts. It was likely that the investigation would lead to nowhere until reports of a PLA commander utilized weaponized anomalies against the UN forces during the Korean war. This was enough for UNAAC to force their emergency power doctrines to force the Soviets cooperation. Eventually the PLA was charged with unauthorized weaponization of anomalies for conflict in 1953. PLA was dismantled and had to relinquish their anomalies to the organization. The only other mentions of the PCAAO were during the Sino-Soviet split, which lead to the PCAAO no longer being financially backed by the USSR. The agency had to sign several agreements to allow the Authority into PCAAO's dig sites and containment facilities. As of 2000, the PCAAO voided all of their past treaties with the Authority, with relations being unsteady but neutral.

The Authority itself would slowly become a self-sustaining organization that we see now. With the modernization of computers, scientific research studies and techniques during the growing technological period in the cold war which included the 'space race,' the effectiveness of research and containment increased by 80%. Containment procedures, Military equipment, Covert operations even began using RPCs for their benefit, leading to the Theta Classification. In addition, due to the Soviet and US's own experiments into the occultism, supernaturalism, and the creation of human made anomalies, the Authority's internal division established new methods of its own information censorship to heavily obscure or augment any history mentioning them, destroying any remaining evidence of their British and Vatican Origins, fabricating evidence of their associations and agreements with the Vatican.


In the 70's, the Authority established their first orbital research station in due to an agreement between JFK and the Authority back in the 60s. (Lyndon B. Johnson attempted to retract from this agreement but due to blackmail, he sustained from his decision.) This lead to the creation of Site-99, the most expensive scientific endeavor preceding the ISS. Site-99 helped establish the first lunar facilities. Five years after the launch however, Site-99 was destroyed. (records on what happened are redacted) Reconstruction on Site-99 began the following decade with the fall of the Soviet Union. Reconstruction is still ongoing.

This coup occurred in 3 of the Authority's major sites, led by the then Site Director of Site-74, Stannum Yeung (access to the Doctor's files are restricted to lvl 5). Defectors from the RCPA depicted them them as dictators, utilizing RPCs to police and control the public, in an effort to enforce their idea of order and saftey.
> 
> Their interference left the Authority crippled after having suffered heavy casualties. Eventually, the coup was quelled and new intense security measures were enacted to prevent the RCPA from invading our timeline again. What achieved this victory was a massive counterattack by MST-01 the "Angels of Eden" against the RCPA officials who have integrated into the Authority's ranks.

In 1985, Gorbachev was elected leader of the USSR, allowing the influence of western culture into the Soviet Union. The Authority took advantage of the policy to infiltrate the USSR, gaining political and economic influence which helped them to take over and assimilate the Russian GOIs.

1992 saw the fall of the Soviet Union, and the Authority took this as an opportunity to establish treaties with the reunified Germany and former Soviet Union states and satellite states to expand and re-establish their presence across the eastern and western Bloc. The US GOI dissolved due to its ineffectiveness in preventing the exploitation and utilization of anomalies(maybe remade after 9/11, records unclear) and the Authority is given more freedom to conduct both research and military operations within the US. Other countries followed suit with treaties to establish Authority presence on their land.
