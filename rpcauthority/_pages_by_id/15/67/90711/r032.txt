**Summary:**
The Authority was created sometime ago (somewhere in the early 1800’s though records are unclear) by the Vatican, British empire and maybe remnants of the French Napoleonic forces (records unclear how), inspired by an ancient anomalous vanguard created by the Roman empire in ancient times. The Vatican proposed the creation of the Authority to the British with their Empire extending the furthest, thus has the best reach to find and contain new anomalies. The British empire employed Indigenous Anomalous keepers into the Authority for their expertise in containment, either voluntarily or by force. The use of former Napoleonic forces helped contribute to make a sizeable military in case of extreme circumstances. During the Early 20th Century, The Authority split from the British Government due to a controversial move on their part and in order to sustain themselves, bought out multiple industries and profited off of WW1. After WW1, the Authority was self sustaining and through the League of Nations, they created agreements with other countries regarding Containing Anomalies. During WW2, they fought against the Ahnenerbe and the Nazi Occult Society and allied with the Allies to help retake occupied sites throughout Europe. During the cold war, the Authority was able to Modernise all outdated portions and install new equipment. During nearly the rest of the 20th Century, the Authority had to compete with a Russian and US GOI which wouldn’t be dissolved until the early 90’s. A small rebellion happened in the Authority during the late 70's, only strengthened by an alternate version of the Authority (RCP) supporting the rebellion, leading to a pyrrhic victory for the RPC Authority. With the fall of the Soviet Union, they absorbed the Soviet GOI and overtake their sites and retake their old sites behind the iron curtain. They spend the last of the 20th Century negotiating with Countries for agreements.

**Ancient Anomalous Vanguard:**

Created by The Roman Empire to Destroy or Contain Anomalies, This early version of the Authority was usually posted in the extended parts of the empire, with an elite division stationed in Rome itself. All records are unclear when they were created, but many correlate with the Vanguard being created either just before or during the reign of Caesar as 1st Emperor of Rome. Many slaves are recorded in messages from officers as being used as a primitive version of Authority Class D, with a much higher mortality rate. The Vanguard also seem to ally themselves with some indigenous anomalous keepers if they couldn’t destroy the anomaly.

Some rival Vanguards mentioned seem to be Carthaginian anomalous keepers (possibly an ancient order though no other records exist), the apostles of Jesus, Gaul druids, the sorcerers of Brittanica and Egyptian cults.

A sub-division of the Roman Vanguard seems to originate from ancient Greece, with many various scholars and philosophers trying understand Anomalies. This led to a Greek vanguard dedicated to understanding and using anomalies (could explain why their civilisation was so advanced). This was merged into the Roman Vanguard after the empire’s expansion into Greece, thus began the use of slaves as more than feeding items/hazardous removal for anomalies, now they used them for testing. (records indicate some altercations between The Vanguard and various emperors. Which ones, though are so far undetermined). Records do indicate however vast storehouses containing various Anomalies and secluded in various outposts. Many of these warehouses have been looted, however The Authority have found many ancient anomalies still hidden inside.


**Medieval Times and Renaissance**

With the fall of the Roman Empire, The Vanguard disbanded, but small groups were still around for containing/protecting anomalies. During the Dark Ages and due to the spread of Catholicism, anomalies were seen as either Holy or Satanic and various anomalous humanoids were considered either cursed or witches. There were a few medieval vanguards created out of the remnants of the Roman Vanguard and they protected/used “holy” items and destroyed/contained "Satanic" items. Many of them lived nomadically, roaming around and destroying anomalies where they could.

With the 1st Crusade, all knights in Europe were joined by the Pope to take back the holy land from the Sacrean Military, even the knights of the roaming Vanguards. They all pledged allegiance to the Vatican/Pope and all Vanguards merged into the Vatican GOI, The Swords of Christ. There are some anomalies recorded to have been discovered/awoken by the Crusades (with one of the strongest sources mentioning the box of Pandora). With the conclusion of the 1st Crusade, a sect of elite knights split off, claiming the Vatican’s bias to “Satanic items” (despite some having a helpful nature) was irresponsible and they created The Authoritas, a small Mercenary Vanguard that existed until the 1800’s.

The Protestant Reformation created a rivalling religious faction after Anomalies, with many Swords merging with the Protestant GOI and due to the Vatican losing much power over Europe in this time. A small but secret war occurs, which is suddenly brought to a halt in the late 1700's (All records seem to point to something called "The Anti-Christ"). An agreement is reached with the Protestant GOI, and 
the Vatican eventually contacts the British empire for help in creating a stronger force to deal with anomalies. The planning of the Authority takes the rest of the century, with multiple meeting occurring and various disagreements taking years to resolve. The Authoritas are persuaded back and remerged into their 1st MST, The Angels of Eden.

**1800’s and the Creation of the Authority:**

The British empire used remnants of the French Imperial Army to help create the Authority’s military similar to the Roman Vanguard. They appointed Global Directors from every part of the empire and ensured that the directors could run the Authority with no biases to benefit any country in case of an uprising. This had to be a global effort so during the years countries were slowly added in: Authority mainly created by the Vatican and British Empire (maybe France, records unspecific) in its entirety. (The exact creation date is unclear with many wars happening during the first half of the century and with England going into “splendid isolation” in 1871 until WW1)

In the late 1800’s (during splendid isolation), the higher ups of the government created MI13 in response to the royal family's growing concern of anomalous events. The Ministers that founded the Authority attempted to keep the Authority’s origins a secret from MI13 and proceeded to destroy all files they could find linking them and the Vatican to the Authority. MI13 would not discover the Authority until the early 20th Century due to the Ministers covering up Authority meddling.

**The World Wars:**

In 1900, the British Parliament attempted to secretly pass a decision to nationalise the Authority and merge it with MI13. The Board, in protest, broke all personal ties to the government and became independent.

Due to the split, the Authority started buying many fledgling industries and companies to fund themselves for the foreseeable future. This would be beneficial as WW1 started, with the Authority making massive profits from the war and using their ties in the industry to move all anomalies in predicted war zones to a fledgling site in the US ( their first US site, named site-02). The Authority spent the war manufacturing more army surplus to further help fuel their departure from the British empire.

With the end of the war and the League of Nations created, the Authority expands and is no longer connected exclusively to the British Empire, however Russia is giving no support to the Authority due to it’s revolution (records indicate that they never were, but who knows? There could have been a small window of time). During the Great Depression,  the Authority is crippled with the value of money worsening, however recruitment skyrockets during this time. The Great Depression also helps the Authority establish multiple businesses to use as fronts  (could've had a long term impact in helping end the Great Depression? Records unspecific).

[[collapsible show="Nucorp Industries and their Relationship with the Authority" hide="Close"]]

> Records found on Nucorp Industries indicate their creation being in 1914, with the company starting out as a scientific research company that sold plans and blueprints to buyers. The Authority initially tried to buy Nucorp in 1929, but negotiations fell through due to the stock market crashing and the Start of the Great Depression. Nucorp gained Authority attention again with the plans and copyright for the 1st nuclear reactor being sold to the US Government in 1939. The Authority approached Nucorp again after WW2 offering a partnership, promising it to be beneficial to both sides. An agreement was made and the Authority helped establish their first manufacturing plant in 1956. It was used during the construction of the Authority Battlecarrier, which lasted from the 60's to the 70's. Their current relationship is still friendly, with Nucorp even reverse engineering Anomalies for the Authority to be beneficial.
[[/collapsible]]

With WW2, the Authority tries to keep RPC’s and anomalies hidden from the Nazi party, but Hitler's fascination with the occult eventually gets them discovered. Records indicate this occurred with the help of the Gestapo tracking Authority agents back to a site near Berlin. Due to The board declining to speak with Hitler on using anomalies, he creates an anomalous tracking branch of the Ahnenerbe (the Nazi party's think tank), known as the Anomalieerkennung division, to track some down for the war effort. His actions also inspire the German branch of the Authority (the FSE) to split in 1938 and become loyal to the Nazi Party. The FSE also change their name to the Nazi Occult Society (NOS) to prove their allegiance to Hitler's vision.

Predicting this split the Authority moved all remaining RPC’s that could be transported safely out of Europe. Many Gamma and Beta RPC's were left on site, leading to the Authority to eventually team up with the Allies in 1941 to reclaim their sites, either through providing men for the war effort, or using RPCs to cut a way through to their occupied sites (these 2 options were discussed, but which one was implemented is unknown). With the end of the war, the Ahnenerbe and NOS are officially dissolved but some splinter cells hang around until 1947, when the Authority hunts down the last cells.

**The Cold War:**
During the Cold war, the Authority is missing some sites due to either the Nazi party's attempts to destroy them during the Allied advance and the establishing of the Iron Curtain. An agreement is made with Stalin allowing them to create their own GOI, and the Authority hand over all Eastern European sites to the Russian GOI.

[[collapsible show="The PCAAO's origins and their relationship with the Authority" hide="Close"]]
> The People’s Committee for the Acquisition of Anomalous Objects (Henceforth PCAAO) was one of the two agencies co-created by the Soviet Union and the People’s Republic of China in 1949. This agency was created to document the anomalies left behind by the previous anomalous agency, and to work alongside the other anomalous agency created and funded by The People’s Liberation Army (henceforth PLA).
> 
> The PLA’s Agency was created to contain and document weaponised anomalies, which quickly led to accusations of the Agency using weaponised anomalies freely in conflicts. The UNAAC suspended the Agency from UNAAC meetings for the accusations until an investigation was finished, but the Agency was quickly caught in another controversy after a commanding officer was convicted for using anomalies to combat UN forces during the Korean war. Due to the conviction, the PLA was officially punished in 1953 with the removal of the PLA's anomalous agency and the acquisition of their anomalies. (the trial of the commander, the amount of anomalies, their classifications and their distribution are restricted to lvl 5). 
> 
> With the PLA’s Anomalous agency gone and their methods shamed, the PCAAO rose to prominence as the dominant Anomalous agency in China, leading to a treaty with the Authority only known as: “The Joint Anomaly Agreement" (all other data like dates and negotiations of this treaty are classified). The only other mentions of the PCAAO are during the Sino-Soviet split, with the PCAAO no longer backed by the USSR GOI, they make several agreements to allow the Authority into PCAAO dig sites and containment facilities. These agreements would only gain controversy and minor conflicts as China grew it's economy and the PCAAO became self sustaining. As of 2000, the PCAAO has have voided all past treaties with the Authority, but is not openly hostile towards the Authority.
[[/collapsible]]


The Authority is primarily self sustaining now, and they modernise everything during the technology race, Containment procedures, Military equipment, Covert operations and even begin using RPC’s for their benefit, creating the Theta Classification. They also start writing themselves out of history, destroying any remaining evidence of their British and Vatican Origins, fabricating evidence to look like they crafted agreements with the British and Vatican back in those times.

They make covert agreements regarding RPC’s with the UN and EU. Only missing out on Countries that refused outside help (Russia, China, Cuba, Chile after the military Coup and other countries run by Dictators during this time) and the US agreement being amended when they implement their own (inexperienced) GOI to compete with the Soviet GOI (somewhat inexperienced), leaving the Authority to lose support from the US (but they’re not exclusively hostile). McCarthyism also made Containment for U.S. RPC’s difficult with many citizens trying to connect/ the Authority and Anomalies back to Russia and with a few even trying to expose the Authority. 

In the 70's, the Authority are able to establish their first Space site, Site-99. This would later in the decade be destroyed (records on what happened are redacted) and reconstruction on Site-99 would begin in the following decade and as of 2000, reconstruction is still ongoing.

[[collapsible show="The 1979 Rebellion and the Righteous Centralised Protection Authority" hide="Close"]]
> In 1979, a rebellion attempt happened inside the Authority, with many low ranking researchers and security personnel rebelling to forcefully push the Authority in a direction of helping the public and punishing crime. This rebellion happened over 3 main sites and was lead by the then Site Director of Site-74, Stannum Yeung (access to the Doctor's files are restricted to lvl 5). The rebellion only worsened with the introduction of an alternate version of the RPC Authority from an alternate timeline. While unconfirmed, this is a widely accepted theory alongside the theory of the rebels somehow contacting them to aid their rebellion attempt.
> 
> They called themselves the Righteous Centralised Protection Authority, and attempted to aid the Rebellions. Many refugees (or escapees) from their timeline depict them as dictators, ruling over society and using RPCs to police and punish the public, in an effort to enact their vision of justice.
> 
> The interfering helped elongate the rebellion attempts for the next year, leaving the Authority with heavy casualties but eventually victorious in quelling the rebellion and stopping the RCPA from invading our timeline. Documents specify this was achieved in a massive counterattack against RCPA members by MST-01 the "Angels of Eden".
[[/collapsible]]

In 1985, Gorbachev is elected leader of the USSR and allows western culture into the Soviet Union. The Authority uses this as an entry into the USSR and gains agreements with them, overtaking and merging with the Russian GOI.

With 1992 and the fall of the Soviet Union, they gain agreements with the reunified Germany and former individual Soviet Union states and satellite states. The US GOI is also dissolved due to ineffectiveness (maybe remade after 9/11, records unclear) and the Authority is given more freedom in the US. Any other countries previously closed also make agreements with the Authority.
