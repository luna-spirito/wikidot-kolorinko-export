**Registered Phenomena Code:** RPC-585

**Object Class:** Alpha-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Visual, Emotional

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-585 is currently stored in a standard .5 x .5 x .5 meter locker at Site-279. Experimentation with RPC-585 requires permission from research personnel with Level 3 or higher clearance. Unauthorized practices and circumvention of these protocols will result in project removal, followed by temporary suspension pending for audit. CSD personnel are to remain immobilized for the duration of the experiment. Security personnel are to be equipped with sedatives in the event of the subject's attempted escape.
 
**Description:** RPC-585 is a series of three standard VHS tapes, designated as RPC-585-1 through RPC-585-3, with each instance displaying different titles and their own sets of film. The tapes themselves depict an individual that is visually identical to Death Grips singer Stefan Burnett[[footnote]]More commonly known as his stage name, MC Ride[[/footnote]] designated as RPC-585-4, who is the only recurring figure throughout all tapes. Extensive analysis of the tapes contents through re-recordings has ruled out the possibility of DeepFake usage. The origins of the individual in the tapes is unknown, as medical records for Ride have never confirmed the existence of an identical twin[[footnote]]The possibility of cloning has not been ruled out, but ways that RPC-585-4 could have ever possibly come into contact with a method of human cloning and have this method stay unknown to the Authority or the US Government falls down to speculation.[[/footnote]]. It has been hypothesized by Authority researchers that, given the unknown nature of RPC-585 and the similarities between the world present in the videotapes and our own world, RPC-585 depicts events that take place within an alternate reality[[footnote]]This is limited to speculation, as RPC-585 does not appear to have any traceable links or history within our world aside from its discovery[[/footnote]]. Said instance is capable of inter-dimensional travel, accessing our current universe from a similar derivative. However, notable alterations in RPC-585-4's chronology include the following.

* The inhabitants appear to display high volumes of gullibility and a lack of free will, allowing them to be "molded" intellectually when coming into contact with an outlier force unknown to their reality. This has seemingly stunted their collective evolution as its prevented many major events seen throughout our own history from occurring, such as the Apollo 11 Moon landings, the civil rights movement, and 9/11[[footnote]]This data is based off of inferences from background footage and somewhat lacks substantial proof[[/footnote]].

* The societies of the new reality appear to function based off of traditional barter-and-trade economical systems, with no currency or exact good pricing visible within the footage. However despite these differences, the reality appears to still have evolved enough to contain similar technological and architectural aspects such as our own, with items like the desktop computer, automobile and cellphone being widely available commodities. America still appears to exist in a similar form, with standard governmental procedures. Further information of the chronology, including the potential existence of the Authority within it, is currently unknown.

[[include component:image-enlarge name= http://rpcsandbox.wikidot.com/local--files/jerryjohn/MC_Ride_%28cropped%29.jpg|width=200px|caption= Image still captured of RPC-585-4 from RPC-585-2. Timestamp: 10:34]]


RPC-585's anomalous properties manifest when an individual reads the titles on the videotapes in their entirety [[footnote]]The affect applies to both reading internally and reading aloud[[/footnote]]. Individuals will begin to display increased amounts of aggressive behavior and poor decision making immediately after the completion of the reading. However this is considered to be an entrance-level anomalous effect, as the contents of the videotapes themselves have shown to display greater yet similar anomalous effects during a viewing. The effects usually persist for 30-32 seconds upon viewing completion of either the tape titles or their contents. The anomalous effects of RPC-585's titles have shown to persist if they are being read via photographed or recorded media. However they have not shown to persist if re-written onto other surfaces[[footnote]]This includes surfaces such as paper, adhesives and electronic screens[[/footnote]]. The contents within RPC-585's tapes have not proven to reproduce anomalous effects if the videos are re-recorded through the usage of video capturing equipment. 

**Discovery:** RPC-585 was discovered following a field report being sent to the Authority from agents within Sacramento, California. It is believed that RPC-585 was anomalously scheduled for delivery. Due to its lack of a return address, RPC-585's package was seized and sent in for inspection. Three employees read the titles of RPC-585's tapes and subsequently experienced their anomalous abilities. All three individuals proceeded to assault each other, causing security to be notified. Upon the arrest of the employees, RPC-585 was seized by the Sacramento Police Department, where an agent from [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/authority-security-force ASF] Rapid Response Team stationed there notified the Authority.

**Addendum 585-1: Tape Contents** 
The following are the transcripts of each tape in their numerical orders. With the exception of the "entering" videotape, all tapes play out in a single take. "Entering" differs due to the fact that during specific timestamps the video will abruptly freeze, causing stagnation and slight distortion of the video. After a short period, usually two seconds, the tape will resume but with the tape contents displaying RPC-585-4 in a different location. It is believed that this is meant to represent the passing of time, however due to the linear design of VHS tapes it is unknown how this is possible, as it directly contradicts its own design.

//The first few video feed seconds are completely black, with the exception of some light distortion. What appears to be a high wind speed causes most other sounds to be drowned out. However faint yelling can be heard for short periods of time. After more seconds pass the black disappears and the video feed opens to a view of a blue sky visible over a small hill. A highway network, skyscrapers, and a stadium are visible a distance behind the hill. A soft hum of numerous car engines can be heard. The video feed during this time is shaky, and heavy breathing from the individual holding the camera is audible from a short distance.//

**RPC-585-4:** Oh my fucking god. Holy fucking shit. Jesus fucking christ! What the fuck was that? God, oh god. I never want to go through that again. 

//The camera slowly steadies. After a deep breath RPC-585-4 turns the video feed to his face. His forehead is visibly caked in sweat and his eyes seem to be puffing out due to the large dark circles under his eyes. RPC-585-4 stares at the camera for a moment until looking up.//

**RPC-585-4:** Where the hell am I? I was just… I was just outside the studio… back in LA. 

//RPC-585-4 pauses for a few more moments until he speaks again.//

**RPC-585-4:** Holy shit. Am I in fucking St. Louis? No, no… this isn't happening. This shit ain't fucking happening. Ok, ok. I'm gonna call Zach, I'll call Zach and he'll help me sort all this crazy shit out. Because I'm either fucking dreaming or high off my ass. 

//The camera shakes again as RPC-585-4 runs through his pockets.//

**RPC-585-4:** No, no! Fuck! Fuck, fuck- it's not fucking there. I'm in the middle of St. Louis without my fucking phone. 

//Shortly after RPC-585-4 stops talking, the video feed begins its first time skip. The feed freezes for a short moment. When it resumes, RPC-585-4 is seen walking down St. Louis' Market street, with numerous pedestrians lining the sidewalks. RPC-585-4 taps one of these passersby on the shoulder.//

**RPC-585-4:** Hey man, can you tell me what year it is?

**Passerby:** Twenty-nineteen. 

**RPC-585-4:** It is? Oh thank god. You know, I've been through some shit recently and I feel like I've aged ten fuckin' years. Fuckin' crazy right?

**Passerby:** You have? I had no idea that was possible. It must be truly remarkable for a person like you to age at such an accelerated rate.

**RPC-585-4:** What? No man, I was just messin around. You didn't actually think that I…

**Passerby:** I was mistaken then? What a shame. That would've really been quite a spectacle. 

//The passerby then walks away.//

**RPC-585-4:** What the fuck? The hell is wrong with that guy? Crazy ass…

//The second time skip occurs. When the feed resumes, RPC-585-4 is seen sitting on a hotel room bed.//

**RPC-585-4:** Something's… something's really fucked with this place. Can't believe this is even happening. I walked up Market Street and waltzed into this Marriott. Told the lady at the desk my situation. She had the craziest fucking look on her face as I was telling it. I guess she believed every word I said. She told me how horrible it was that I had just sat my fat ass down in the middle of St. Louis with nothing to my name. That ain't even the weirdest shit though. The weirdest thing is that no one's noticed who I am yet. Didn't even get a goddamn name mention, like I don't even fucking exist. I asked the receptionist if she had heard of the band "Death Grips". Girl was confused as all hell. So I'm gonna guess that wherever the hell I am, whatever this piece of shit place is, it means I got no leverage. Got no way to go back home. No way to call Zach. (//Pauses//) Does Zach even know I exist here? If the band doesn't exist, then have we ever fucking met before? Am I…

//RPC-585-4 pauses and stares at the ground.//

**RPC-585-4:** Am I alone?

**<End Transcript>**

 //The video feed opens to a debate stage, in which RPC-585-4 and an unidentified individual are standing opposite of each other behind separate podiums. In between the two podiums, on a flat screen behind both individuals, are the words "Independent Presidential Debate". The logos of numerous presumed sponsoring news organizations such as "MSDNC" and "CNM" can be seen rotating along the screen's background. A reflection of a table of two debate moderators is visible behind the two podiums.//
 
 **Female Moderator:** Mr. Burnett, in the past you've stated that, and I quote, "I will not have the dirt-crusted hands of these "shithead" politicians hamper the American people's process of freeing themselves from tyranny". Can you elaborate on this?

 **RPC-585-4:** Yea, yea. I think people need to find their own way in the world. You can't have any "government" or ruling system control the citizens of this country. If they're controlled then they'll never be able to find out how they really fit in. Under my presidency I say that the government will truly operate by the people, and for the people. Let the people decide what they want, and don't let any motherfucker get in your way. 

//The crowd applauds.//

**Male Moderator:** So would you say you're in favor of decreased government control over the operations of society?

**RPC-585-4:** No no no… none of this "decreased" bullshit. I'm talking about full on cancellation. If the American people say they don't want it, then they don't have to get it because-

**Political Opponent:** Moderators please! Forgive me for m-my interruption, but what my opponent is saying here is simply madness! Without a government we would be subjected to complete and total anarchy. There would be no order, much less any peace, within our society! These- these outlandish proposals from my opponent are simply chaotic and should be unwelcome on a debate stage such as this. We are a civilized country! And without civilized order, we will lose ourselves to chaos! 

//The crowd falls silent after the conclusion of the speech. Both the moderators and RPC-585-4 appear to be visibly shocked. A few moments of silence pass before RPC-585-4 reaches behind a compartment within his podium and pulls out a pistol. RPC-585-4 then shoots the individual on the opposite podium in the head, killing him instantly. As the crowd remains silent, RPC-585-4 walks over to the opposite podium and drags the body in front of and between the two podiums. Upon finishing he makes a hand signal in the direction of the back right corner of the room.//
 
**RPC-585-4:** Bring it over!

//A sledgehammer is then placed on stage by an individual from the crowd. RPC-585-4 retrieves it and proceeds to stand over the corpse of the politician. RPC-585-4 raises the sledgehammer over himself and brings it down onto the corpse's head, crushing it. A sound similar to a "wet crunch" is heard as blood splatters over RPC-585-4 and the area around the corpse. He then repeatedly smashes the corpse with the sledgehammer, continuing to mutilate it until no more crunching can be heard. Once the sound ceases, RPC-585-4 finally pauses, lifting his head up to face the audience. He uses his right hand to wipe masses of caked blood off of his face, which has now been completely drenched in red. After a few short moments of wiping the blood off, RPC-585-4 drops the sledgehammer and addresses the audience.//

**RPC-585-4:** FUCKIN' BUREAUCRATS AM I RIGHT?
 
//The audience erupts into a fury of loud cheering. As they cheer, RPC-585-4 returns to his original podium spot.//

**RPC-585-4:** With a new government, none of us will have to worry about chickenshits like him telling us what to do. We will be free to make our own destiny, where every man, woman and child can be what they see fit! We don't need anyone lording over us anymore, you hear me? We don't want any control! 

//The crowd cheers louder.//

**RPC-585-4:** No more!

**Audience:** NO CONTROL! NO CONTROL! NO CONTROL! NO CONTROL!

//Feed opens to a front-facing shot of RPC-585-4's face. He breathes heavily and appears to be in a state of emotional distress. Around him is a somewhat dilapidated and structurally unsound room, with numerous cracks within the white-painted walls. The room is illuminated by a small light bulb hung from the ceiling. After a short period RPC-585-4 begins to speak.//

**RPC-585-4:** I wanted to open this by saying… I'm sorry. 

//RPC-585-4 sighs.//

**RPC-585-4:** I just… I just wanted to make something great, you know? Something everyone could work with…

**RPC-585-4:** I thought that maybe, maybe I could get perfection. Maybe I could mold this world into something better, but… but something's just not right with these people.

From the moment I fucking got here… it was… it was just a bubble waiting to burst. Everything was just waiting to explode. And I tipped everything over the edge.

**RPC-585-4:** //*Whispers*// I got all those people killed…

//RPC-585-4 looks up and stares at the ceiling. Tears can be seen falling from his eyes.//

**RPC-585-4:** So it's all just chaos now. No… rule of law or anything like that. People kill for sport and they kill for fun. And there ain't nobody there to stop them. I guess it was only a matter of time before they turned on me, seeing as I let them run around doin' their bullshit when things got hard. I can't help but wonder why the hell I'm even sayin' this. Not like I was a beacon of hope here or any shit like that. Am I trying to repent for my fucking sins? Make good with god or something like that? I'm so deep in shit I can't fuckin’ breathe…

//RPC-585-4 looks down at the floor and shudders.//

**RPC-585-4:** I'd take it all back if I could, you know. I miss the band, I miss my old life and Zach… I miss the shitty tours, the long ass bus rides, the asshole fans. All of that… all of that seems so small compared to all of this though. And I don't think I'll ever get to experience it again. 

//RPC-585-4 closes his eyes and clasps his hands together in a position similar to that of a prayer. After a short period, a loud banging is heard from a door across the room, surprising RPC-585-4 and causing him to stand up.//

**Unknown Crowd:** Come out! Come out from your little hidey-hole! We know you're in there! 

**RPC-585-4:** No… no! Get the fuck away from me! Leave me alone!

**Unknown:** Aww, that's so cute..but I'm afraid we can't do that. You got a helluva lot riding on your head, and it would be mighty nice sight to see you impaled on the front of my F150!

//The door hinge finally breaks and the door falls down. The unknown crowd begin rushing into the room next to RPC-585-4.//

**RPC-585-4:** Don't be like me.
