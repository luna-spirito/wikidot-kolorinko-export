**Registered Phenomena Code:** 840-NC

**Object Class:** Presumably Neutralized/Gamma [[footnote]] It is unknown if RPC-840-NC is a valid threat to humanity at this time. --Reclassification will be made when more information surfaces.-- Reclassification made in revision 0.02v. [[/footnote]]

[[include component:hazards grouped=show|extra-terrestrial=show|bio-hazard=show|]]

**Containment Protocols:** Use of [[[RPC-991]]] has proven incredibly useful for research into RPC-840-NC as a whole. Exact numbers and dates have been discovered and proven with the help of RPC-991. Researchers assigned to RPC-840-NC are advised to make use of RPC-991 to conduct research. Additionally, RPC-840-NC's anomalous properties had presumably ceased before the establishment of the Authority; thus no containment procedures are currently in place.

As for preventing knowledge of RPC-840-NC's anomalous attributes spreading to the populace, information concerning the habitability of Venus at a previous time has been revealed to the public, but not the RPC-840-NC events themselves. Additionally, NASA has allowed the Authority to remotely control advanced rovers on the surface of Venus which can be used to research the many anomalies RPC-840-NC is associated with, along with the NASA Anomalous Research Division (NASA ARD) which is currently funding and making multiple contributions towards projects relating to RPC-840-NC.

**Description:** RPC-840-NC is the designation given to multiple anomalous events and entities located on the planet of Venus. These Venusian[[footnote]] Relating to or denoting to the planet Venus or a hypothetical/theoretical inhabitant of Venus. [[/footnote]] anomalies have been designated separately and will be explained in greater detail. RPC-840-NC-1[[footnote]] Colloquially referred to as 'Venusians'. [[/footnote]] is the designation given to a decimated civilization that inhabited Venus since approximately 2 billion years ago and up until approximately 65 million years ago. Evidence shows that RPC-840-NC-1 was a technologically advanced civilization similar to humans; however, it is presumed that they are entirely separate from the human species despite this. [[footnote]] All information about RPC-840-NC-1's presence on Earth or their genus is merely hypothetical. Their relation to human beings is obscured. [[/footnote]] Use of RPC-991 has shown that Venus originally supported life; much similarly to Earth; along with an aerial view of an RPC-840-NC-1 civilization before an apocalyptic event. [[footnote]] Said apocalyptic event has been designated RPC-840-NC-2. [[/footnote]]

[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/anomalousducky/arial|width=350px|align=left|caption=Aerial photograph of an RPC-840-NC-1 civilization.]]

@@ @@

RPC-840-NC-2 is an apocalyptic event that occurred on Venus approximately 65 million years ago. All but 2 RPC-840-NC-1 specimens had perished over the course of 2 years. These 2 RPC-840-NC-1 specimens have been designated the names Adam and Eve by other RPC-840-NC-1 specimens. These 2 instances of RPC-840-NC-1 were considered of high importance to RPC-840-NC-1 as a whole.

RPC-840-NC-3 is the designation given to the Chicxulub asteroid. [[footnote]] The asteroid responsible for the extinction event on Earth approximately 65 million years ago, wiping all Dinosauria life from the planet. [[/footnote]] High importance RPC-840-NC-1 members designated Adam and Eve in texts found on Venus [[footnote]] Hereafter designated RPC-840-NC-6 for Adam, and RPC-840-NC-7 for Eve. [[/footnote]] were aboard RPC-840-NC-3 when it impacted Earth. The entities then proceeded to bring sapient/human life to planet Earth by unknown means. Human life-forms existing directly because of RPC-840-NC-6 and -7's arrival to planet Earth would also suggest that the Panspermia [[footnote]] The Panspermia hypothesis proposes that life was brought to planet Earth via extra-terrestrial means, or an asteroid that impacted the planet. This supposed asteroid is the reason life exists on Earth according to the Panspermia theory. [[/footnote]] theory is scientifically true.

[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/anomalousducky/car|width=300px|caption=RPC-840-NC-5 infection on a RPC-840-NC-1 operated vehicle.]]

RPC-840-NC-4 is responsible for the mass extinction of RPC-840-NC-1, and the harsh conditions of planet Venus. RPC-840-NC-4 resembles a large cathedral constructed entirely out of remains from an unknown life-form. RPC-840-NC-4 landed on Venus, causing the immediate causalities of approximately 4,000 RPC-840-NC-1 specimens. Roughly 30 minutes later, RPC-840-NC-4 released a gas of indiscernible composition. This gas was toxic to RPC-840-NC-1, and approximately 85,000 casualties were recorded over a period of 12 days. This gas RPC-840-NC-4 produced had seemingly released a bacterial infection onto the RPC-840-NC-1 populace. [[footnote]] The bacterial infection created by RPC-840-NC-4 has been designated RPC-840-NC-5. [[/footnote]]

RPC-840-NC-5 is a highly lethal bacterial infection created by RPC-840-NC-4. RPC-840-NC-5 functions similar to a modern-day fungal infection, aside from the immense production of lesion-like spores appearing on the body. These spores can also attach and spread to and from inanimate objects, and has been seen covering large areas of the ground on Venus. RPC-840-NC-5 spores spread to the skin immediately after contact and will begin to travel to the cardiac muscle of the body.

RPC-840-NC-8 and RPC-840-NC-9 are 2 anomalous figures vaguely resembling a pyrotechnics mask.  [[footnote]] 'RPC-840-NC-9' will be used to refer to both RPC-840-NC-9A and RPC-840-NC-9B. [[/footnote]] RPC-840-NC-8 and -9 manifested on Venus approximately 2 hours after RPC-840-NC-4 had landed. RPC-840-NC-8 takes on the form of a dimly lit doorway, with a 'face' visible in the middle. Any biological lifeforms who approach RPC-840-NC-8 within approximately 3 meters will be drawn near RPC-840-NC-8, and will not be able to escape the grasp of the entity. RPC-840-NC-8 will then neutralize any life-form once it has entered the entity's 'door-frame'.

RPC-840-NC-9A takes on a similar appearance to RPC-840-NC-8, except for the fact RPC-840-NC-9A hovers over the ground. RPC-840-NC-9A is moved along by an unidentified humanoid much smaller than itself; designated 'RPC-840-NC-9B'. A single rope is connected to both RPC-840-NC-9 entities. RPC-840-NC-9 shows no signs of hostility, and mostly spend time wandering the wastelands of Venus.

RPC-840-NC-9B has been observed to wail uncontrollably and show extreme signs of depression. The humanoid displays severe signs of post-traumatic stress disorder and is constantly vocalizing in an unidentifiable language. It is unknown if RPC-840-NC-9B was created alongside RPC-840-NC-9A, or was attached to the entity after the initial appearance of it on Venus. It is likely that more anomalous entities such as RPC-840-NC-9 inhabited Venus after the apocalyptic event, however, no evidence to support this claim has surfaced.

**Registered Phenomena Code:** 840-NC

**Object Class:** Gamma-Black

[[include component:hazards grouped=show|extra-terrestrial=show|bio-hazard=show|]]

**Containment Protocols:** Humanity must not exceed a Type II civilization as presented on the Kardashev scale. [[footnote]][https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kardashev_scale The Kardashev scale] is a method of measuring a civilization's level of technological advancement based on the amount of energy they are able to utilize. [[/footnote]] It is hypothesized that humans will attain Type II status in the next couple thousand years. As of 2023, humanity has not yet reached Type I status, thus the threat RPC-840-NC poses is on low alert. The Gamma Black classification has been applied due to the immense threat RPC-840-NC-4 will pose when it is alerted to Earth's growing technological capabilities.

The Authority is to prevent humanity from exceeding into a Type II civilization. Several international laws are to be applied to prevent the rapid growth of technology when humanity reaches a Type I civilization. Any countries that refuse to abide by the new international law (which has been disguised as if it is meant to slow rapid inflation or economic failure) is to be dealt with by force if necessary. Containment Protocols will be updated if any major changes to the geopolitical and geographic structure affect them in any way in the future. Construction on an advanced rocket to allow up to 500 humans to escape to another planetary body was launched by NASA and the Authority in 2021. This will allow humanity to persevere on other nearby habitable planets (which will most likely be discovered before a Type II civilization is achieved) if RPC-840-NC-4 becomes a more imminent threat to human-kind.

If by any chance humanity exceeds this threshold, or RPC-840-NC-4 lands on Earth's surface unprecedentedly, it will be nearly impossible to deal with the threat as RPC-840-NC-4 is technologically superior, and its sole purpose is to exterminate all hyper-advanced civilizations in the universe. Defeat is to be admitted by Earth's forces, and the previously mentioned advanced rocket is to be boarded by all highly-intelligent and governmental figures at the current time as soon as possible.

**Description:** RPC-840-NC's relation to life on Earth, the species RPC-840-NC-1, and all other details have been omitted from this revision as they have not been modified. This revision will mostly focus on RPC-840-NC-4; the large cathedral-like structure mentioned in the previous revision of the RPC-840-NC document.

* **RPC-840-NC-1:** An advanced, human-like civilization that inhabited Venus up until 65 million years ago. It is hypothesized that RPC-840-NC-1 is responsible for the Genesis event which brought human life to planet Earth. RPC-840-NC-1 referred to themselves as the 'Venusian' species, and seem to have formed countries, cities, and governmental ideologies on the landscape of Venus.

* **RPC-840-NC-2:** RPC-840-NC-2 is the collective designation given to the apocalyptic event that decimated the RPC-840-NC-1 population. RPC-840-NC-2 is merely a codename for the event itself.

* **RPC-840-NC-3:** The designation given to the Chicxulub asteroid that is responsible for the extinction of the Dinosauria species 65 million years ago. 2 RPC-840-NC-1 specimens (RPC-840-NC-6 and RPC-840-NC-7) were aboard RPC-840-NC-3 when it impacted Earth.

* **RPC-840-NC-4:** A massive structure resembling a modern-day cathedral constructed entirely of remains from an unidentified species. RPC-840-NC-4 landed on Venus, and released a toxic gas which spread an infectious bacterium onto the RPC-840-NC-1 populace (RPC-840-NC-5). RPC-840-NC-4 is responsible for the mass extinction of RPC-840-NC-1, and the inhabitability of planet Venus.

* **RPC-840-NC-5:** RPC-840-NC-5 is the previously mentioned infectious disease released by RPC-840-NC-4 when it arrived on Venus. RPC-840-NC-5 operates similarly to a modern-day fungal infection, with multiple tumorous spores spreading to and from the body, inanimate objects, and the surface of the planet. It is presumed RPC-840-NC-5 is dormant as of now, and no longer poses a threat.

* **RPC-840-NC-6:** The RPC-840-NC-1 specimen referred to as 'Adam' in ancient texts recovered from Venus. RPC-840-NC-6 seems to be of high importance to RPC-840-NC-1's culture.

* **RPC-840-NC-7:** The RPC-840-NC-1 specimen referred to as 'Eve'. RPC-840-NC-7 is of high importance and seems to be related to RPC-840-NC-6 in some way.

* **RPC-840-NC-8:** An anomalous entity which appeared on Venus after RPC-840-NC-4 had initially appeared. RPC-840-NC-8 resembles a doorway that is protruding from the surface below it which is dimly lit with a single light. RPC-840-NC-8's 'facial area' resembles a modern-day pyrotechnics mask. Any individual who comes into proximity of RPC-840-NC-8 will be terminated as they will be unable to escape the grasp the entity has on them.

* **RPC-840-NC-9A:** RPC-840-NC-9A is one half of RPC-840-NC-9: The designation given to both RPC-840-NC-9A and -9B. RPC-840-NC-9A is a large porcelain entity similar in appearance to RPC-840-NC-8, except for the fact RPC-840-NC-9A hovers above the surface of Venus. RPC-840-NC-9A seems to pose no immediate threat.

* **RPC-840-NC-9B:** RPC-840-NC-9B is seen carrying RPC-840-NC-9A along by a single rope connected to the 2 entities. RPC-840-NC-9B wanders aimlessly and is seen emanating random distressed calls.

New hypothetical research about RPC-840-NC from Researcher Yash has been proven to be true. The RPC-840-NC-4 structure is controlled by a god-like figure which cannot be understood with current human knowledge. This figure/being will relocate RPC-840-NC-4 to any planetary body with a civilization that has exceeded a Type II civilization on the Kardashev scale, or has used any other means to gain complete technological prosperity which might exceed a Type II civilization. [[footnote]] This would make the '[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Filter Great Filter]' hypothesis accurate, as this theory is what supposedly prevents non-living matter from undergoing abiogenesis, in time, to expanding lasting life as measured by the Kardashev scale. [[/footnote]]

RPC-840-NC-4's purpose in the universe is to exterminate all life that has exceeded such technological boundaries, which keeps a balance of power within the universe. Currently, RPC-840-NC-4 is known for the mass extinction of the 'Venusian' race (RPC-840-NC-1) which inhabited Venus around 65 million years ago. RPC-840-NC-4 is able to summon a deadly gas (RPC-840-NC-5) which will then release a bio-logical hazard (highly-contagious disease) onto the populace of the planet it is currently landed on. Said disease is highly lethal, and no possible way to cure it has ever come to fruition, as it is highly likely that it can //not// be cured by any means. RPC-840-NC-4 will also summon anomalous beings (currently only RPC-840-NC-8, -9A, and -9B have been seen) which will aid in exterminating the civilization.
