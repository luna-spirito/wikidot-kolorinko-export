**Containment Protocols:** All RPC-861-1 instances have been catalogued and attached with remote tracking devices.

**Description:** RPC-861 is a phenomenon occurring in [[[Site-038]]]. All 184 security cameras installed within the facility are direct components of RPC-861, hereby designated as RPC-861-1 instances.

RPC-861-1 instances have been known to spontaneously disappear from their original positions and re-appear in front of Dr. Miles Savage's on-site residence in the Personnel Housing Wing. Each camera is always found facing the entrance to his room, and on rare occasion, have been seen turning towards Dr. Savage if he is within the RPC-861-1 instance's vicinity.

The RPC-861 phenomenon initially began on 5/19/2018, when the on-site security team lost control of all RPC-861-1 instances. Security cameras installed after RPC-861's activation have not been claimed by the anomaly, remaining non-anomalous and within the security team's control.

All attempts to remove RPC-861-1 instances from Site-038 have been unsuccessful, as all removed instances have simply re-appeared within Site-038.

**Addendum 861.1:** Incident 861.1

On 6/20/2018, Dr. Savage requested to move to Site-074 due to rising concern over RPC-861. This request was accepted; all RPC-861-1 instances remained inactive for 2 weeks after his departure.

However, on 7/16/2018, every known RPC-861-1 instance spontaneously relocated to Site-074, appearing in front of Dr. Savage's office. After 30 minutes, a piece of paper manifested on his desk. It read:

> its ok miles
> i have not forgotten
> and you should not try to forget either
> those times are over
> but i am still here for u
>
> youre beautiful
> never forget that

During Dr. Savage's questioning regarding the incident, he refused to comment on the note. Further investigation is ongoing.
