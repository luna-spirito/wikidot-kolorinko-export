[[f>image http://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/echo-08-plague-doctors/Echo-08.png width="264px"]]

**Numerical Code:** Echo-08

**Codename:** Plague Doctors

**Motto:** "In Gas noster exedra" (The gas is our living room)

**Garrison/HQ:** Varies

**Roles:** CBRN Response, Rapid Deployment, retrieval and containment of Bio-Hazard and Toxic Hazards, chemical warfare, direct action.

**Established:** June 8, 1974

**Description:** Echo-08 was formed on June 8, 1974 after a concerning lack of operatives capable of surviving biohazardous and/or chemically threatened environments. Echo-08 has operated in assistance with [*http://www.rpc-wiki.net/foxtrot-4-prey Foxtrot-4] for emergency reinforcements (if none from [*http://www.rpc-wiki.net/foxtrot-4-prey Foxtrot-4] are available) or assistance in operations conducted within toxic environments.

The original roles of Echo-08 consisted of CBRN Response, Rapid Deployment, and Chemical Warfare. The retrieval and containment of anomalies within or consisting of said environments was added after the introduction of [[[RPC-161]]] and lack of personnel capable of providing security to its containment.

Echo-08 was originally station in the major parts of [REDACTED] due to the concerning number of countries in possession of chemically lethal weapons within the region and the tension between said countries. Too avoid further advancements of these weapons to ready them for use, direct action was soon added to their official roles in March, 1998

Echo-08 is currently worldwide and is composed of 6 detachments for each region. The detachments are as follows: Detachment-Alpha (North America), Detachment-Bravo (South America), Detachment-Charlie (Europe), Detachment-Delta (Asia), Detachment-Echo (Africa), and Detachment-Foxtrot (Oceania). Each detachment consists of at least 70 operatives.
