+++ **Containment Protocols:**

Primary care of RPC-628 has been entrusted to the Order of St. John the Hesychast. Further containment efforts by the Authority are deemed unnecessary. RPC-628 is currently kept within Mount Vesuvius. Monks of the Order are currently tending to RPC-628's necessities, such as sustenance, cleaning, and removal of parasites embedded within its skin.

Physical interaction with RPC-628 by staff is strictly limited. Personnel are to carry out bimonthly visits wherein RPC-628 is subject to routine examination and testing. Efforts to assist the local monks are to be conducted and basic supplies are to be provided if necessary.

[[include component:image-block-static name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-628/628-1.png|width=…px|caption=  Monks during routine containment measures.]]

All staff must wear Level-A hazmat suits[[footnote]]Level-A hazmat suits present full body protection and are fitted with an enclosed oxygen supply.[[/footnote]] when in the vicinity of RPC-628. These suits must be properly worn at all times when interacting with the area.[[footnote]]This is due to the unknown types of flora that might cause unwanted changes to the surrounding ecosystem if introduced.[[/footnote]] After the end of any activities, Authority personnel and local monks are to go through a mandatory decontamination procedure in order to avoid cross-contamination of the moss with the outside world.

RPC-628 is to be fed three times per day with aquatic produce obtained from local suppliers. Relative humidity of 50%-70% within the containment enclosure is to be maintained by a hypocaust[[footnote]] A Roman heating technique utilizing an open space below a building to disperse hot air, often used in Roman baths. [[/footnote]] constructed below the containment enclosure. The hypocaust and the adjacent ventilated wood-burning furnace are to be active for at least eight hours a day. Authority personnel are currently working to produce and install a machine to create steam without the need for fire or electrical wires.

+++ **Description:**

RPC-628 is an aquatic mammalian organism measuring 430m long and weighing approximately 524,000kg.[[footnote]]Over 30 times larger and 17 times heavier than the average Humpback whale.[[/footnote]] After research, it was discovered that the entity is composed of both animal and plant cells.[[footnote]]These Plantae cells are presumed to be //Andreaeopsida// and //Andreaeobryopsida//.[[/footnote]] RPC-628 resembles and is proportionally similar to a common Megaptera Novaeangliae (Humpback Whale). Notable differences include its pigmentation, which produces a bright green colouration. The entity's underbelly is white in coloration, with a darker tone surrounding the affected area of RPC-628-A. RPC-628 exhibits commensalist behavior[[footnote]]An association between two organisms in which one benefits and the other derives neither benefit nor harm.[[/footnote]] consistent with members of its non-anomalous counterparts. Additionally, RPC-628 exhibits external growths resembling various marine invertebrate exoskeletons as well as plant matter.

Any biological material or sea life attached to RPC-628 found in its dermis (including moss, barnacles and others) are capable of growing and spreading at 0.7m/s.[[footnote]]The of moss is capable of spreading outside of the entity's body, however, the spread will stop after an approximate distance of 17m.[[/footnote]] These materials have been observed to grow to a consistent measurement of 10-20 cm. Due to agglomerates, the moss is primarily found on RPC-628's back. The moss can also be found in RPC-628's eye cavities, effectively rendering it blind. It is currently undetermined if RPC-628 employs conventional sight or uses an alternative method of interpreting its environment. The moss appears to be mundane until an object comes into proximity of RPC-628 with "hostile intent." When these conditions are met, it will attach to any surface, including beings or objects and increase their weight and density, holding down the subject.[[footnote]]Eg. A bullet coming into the proximity of the affected area will appear to slow down and experience a reduction in effectiveness on impact.[[/footnote]] The moss found in RPC-628's body is designated RPC-628-1. This moss is capable of absorption of high amounts of humidity out of the body of any organic being.[[footnote]]It is capable of absorbing upwards of 60% of moisture in a human body.[[/footnote]] Unprotected individuals within the enclosure may invariably be dehydrated by RPC-628-1. The moss particulate found in close proximity of RPC-628 is present in the air and on surfaces.

[[include component:image-block-static name= http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-628/628-2.png|width=px|caption= RPC-628.]]

RPC-628-A is a pitch-black two-flue harpoon[[footnote]]Typically used in whaling before the nineteenth century and first appearing in artistic depictions in the fourteenth century.[[/footnote]] located below the ribcage. Necrotic and infected tissue has formed surrounding the site of its impalement into RPC-628. RPC-628-A is currently physically impossible to be removed due to its anomalous properties. It is theorized that the harpoon has somehow merged with RPC-628 anatomy itself. (For further details, view Addendum 1.) Due to RPC-628-A effects, it is to be noted that RPC-628 is incapable of any movement.  When a living subject touches RPC-628-A, their skin will begin to rapidly grow and mutate. These mutations closely resemble those found in tumors and certain types of cancer cells. Areas adjacent to the wound exhibit a lack of moss growth. The injury does not cicatrize or appear to heal. This is assumed to be another anomalous effect afflicted by RPC-628-A.

The following are the secondary anomalous characteristics of RPC-628:

* The primary sound produced by RPC-628 is a whistle via vocal cords. This whistle can produce an anomalous sound that resembles human vocalizations but was noted to reach higher frequencies as well. This whistle appears to be unconventional and advanced relative to other cetacean vocalizations as it is capable of speech, including proficiency in English, Latin and Italian, as well as complex forms of singing. RPC-628's vocalization is able to have effects on sentient beings. It is noted to have different effects depending on the emotional state of the listening subject. For example, those in distress happen to feel calm and those filled with hatred appear to feel physical fatigue and mental exhaustion. This effect appears to scale by intensity. RPC-628 is able to perform songs but these appear to have minimal if any anomalous effect.

* As the injury caused by RPC-628-A is unable to cicatrize or heal in any form, RPC-628 constantly bleeds. The blood from RPC-628 appears to have healing effects.[[footnote]]This healing effect is in direct proportion to the collected blood's proximity to RPC-628-A.[[/footnote]] It appears that RPC-628 itself is subject to this effect as it is able to regenerate itself. The blood spilled by RPC-628 possess rejuvenating qualities when ingested, inducing anomalous cell regeneration and temporarily bolstering the imbiber's fortitude. Besides that, RPC-628’s blood shows a noticeable effect in raising the longevity of those who consume it, slowing the aging process on the affected body. However, it was discovered that the consumption of over 327mL[[footnote]] The consumption of any volume below 327 mL is currently being researched for medicinal benefits.[[/footnote]] is extremely dangerous. 
 * Consumption exceeding the limit has a high risk of generating tumors and abnormally differentiated cells resulting in neoplastic tissue(including epithelial, connective, muscular and nervous tissue.), appendages(including legs, arms and in rare occasions malformed heads.), facial structures(including oversized eyes, ears and mouth structures.) and skeletal growth rupturing the epidermis. It is to be noted that those recently affected by RPC-628's blood will appear paler than usual, including tones of white or gray.

* RPC-628 is capable of increasing and decreasing its weight, mass and density similar to RPC-628-1 instances. It's theorized that its accumulation and loss of characteristics are interchangeable with RPC-628-1 instances. (For further details, see Addendum 2.)

+++ **Addendum:**
[[collapsible show="Addendum 1: Vivisection and Analysis" hide="RPC-628 analysis and vivisection"]]
> After weeks of conversing with RPC-628, permission was given from the entity to dissect and analyze its inner anatomy. 
> 
> A laser scalpel was used for the first operation, revealing samples of a digestive system, a nervous system and other vital organs. After 10 minutes of cutting through the flesh, sounds of distress came from RPC-628.
> 
> After 2 minutes, the operating staff found the heart of RPC-628. At this point, the laser malfunctioned and perforated the tissue deeply. Upon cutting it, screams and wailing were heard coming from RPC-628. Personnel who heard its vocalisations began to bleed from their ears and experienced tinnitus[[footnote]]Tinnitus is the perception of noise or ringing in the ears.[[/footnote]]. And further listening lead to all researchers in the vicinity to temporarily go deaf and forcing them to turn the laser off. After a few seconds, RPC-628 had healed from its injuries and started to pump blood normally.
> 
> The operation was concluded shortly after.
> 
> The results indicated that RPC-628 is close to immortality due to the effects of RPC-628-A.
[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="Addendum 2: Interview with RPC-628" hide="Interview performed with RPC-628"]]
> **Interviewed:** RPC-628
> 
> **Interviewer:** Dr. Joseph
> 
> **Foreword:** Authority staff entered the cave where RPC-628 currently resides. Upon arrival, the guiding monk notifies them that only one person should enter at a time. Dr. Joseph is asked to use a hazmat suit by the research team, however he refuses the offer in order to remain as human as possible to RPC-628; he wishes to be "face to face" with RPC-628.
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Monk D███:** I must ask, Dr. Joseph, will you need anything before the interview?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Absolutely, a cup of water will be enough for me. It’s been hours since I drank anything.
> 
> **RPC-628:** Joseph, is it?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Oh! Hello... I'm sorry to sound surprised, it's just that... I've only read the documents and never heard you speak. I'm sorry to disturb you. I am just here to ask some questions. Now, we usually call you by Registered Phenomena Code 628, but do you have any names that you would like to be called by while I'm conducting this interview?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Names. I haven't heard those in a long time. Where I came from I used to be called Jonah, but you can call me what they call me now. What was it? RPC-684?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** RPC-628.
> 
> **RPC-628:** Apologies but I was never good with numbers, not my forte if I have to say so myself, I'm usually more of a... uh...
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** A musician I presumed? Given your vocal abilities.
> 
> //The monk comes in bringing a cup of water to the researcher, he thanks him by nodding his head.//
> 
> **RPC-628:** Ah, that is the word, I apologize, my mind has not exactly been in the best of conditions after, uh… 200, 300… 400? How many has it been?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Weeks?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Years. But--
> 
> //Researcher Joseph, who was drinking in his cup of water, chokes from the surprise. After a few coughs he continues the interview.//
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Sorry, this caught me off-guard. I haven't told you, but I am quite new to this whole anomalous thing. I thought humpback whales only live up to 50! Even for a blue whale[[footnote]]Blue whales have an average lifespan of 80-90 years.[[/footnote]], hundred years…? It's unheard of!
> 
> **RPC-628:** Then I guess you haven't lived long enough until you make people shocked by your age.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** It's the first time I am talking to something this ancient, no offense, but I thought you would be a bit younger. So, what are you, can you tell me in your own words?
> 
> **RPC-628:** I would prefer speaking of my own species. You see: When my species reach maturity we are supposed to pick a form to use for the rest of our lives. A hard choice for most, but for me it was the easiest answer;  those majestic creatures swimming at the surface of the sea, so free and released of the shackles that of life has, they just swim. No great number of predators, an abundance of prey [get rid of this comma]... they are the biggest creatures they know… and unconfined. 
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** I see, it's an interesting way to see things. Speaking of which, could you tell me more about yourself? Let's go by steps.
> 
> **RPC-628:** I already told you one curiosity about myself. It is your turn to speak about yourself, Mr. Joseph.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Oh? I wasn't expecting this. Caught off guard once more, but to clarify, I am here to ask questions about you. Forgive me but the interview is about the ancient moss whale creature, not the boring researcher.
> 
> **RPC-628:** Then I refuse to continue this interview unless you start saying something about yourself first.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Well, fine. Let’s see... I'm 36, I have a wife and a daughter, Rose and Lizabeth, who I love with all my heart.
> 
> **RPC-628:** Children...
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Something that comes to mind?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Had two; one boy and a girl. I sometimes wonder, where they are now...
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Please tell me more about them; what are their names, what are the last memories you have of them?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Oh, Jehortes and Jalihya, sometimes Ephren. Those silly kids, they were never much of the explorer kind, no matter how much I tried to show them the outside world they always had more fun at home.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Home? Where is that?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Far... Eden.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Where can you find that place?
> 
> **RPC-628:** It's not anywhere close to here. It's a home I was thrown out of and into this abandoned world. They thought it was destroyed after a certain incident.. I was temporarily assigned as a Guardian to come here and scout the area, I was supposed to come back in a week or two. What they weren't expecting is that I would ever fall in love with this place and the people inside it.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Earth?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Earth.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So you never came back?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Yes, one of my many regrets. Maybe my main regret, leaving my children behind… Jehortes and Jalihya... especially Eph-Ephraim…
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So can you tell me more about tha-...
> 
> **RPC-628:**  I apologise… I do not wish to talk of that topic. The pain pushed onto my mind by such a memory…
> 
> //RPC-628 groans weakly, and water starts surrounding Its eyes, possibly on the verge of tears.//
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** I am sorry. We could  talk about your uh, anomalous effects?
> 
> **RPC-628:**  "Anomalous effects"? I am not acquainted with such vernacular, Mr. Joseph.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Thaumaturgy? I think you call it magic?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Of course. Nowadays I am not half of what I once was. In short, the gods gave me these abilities temporarily to make me able to explore and so I did. I saw the blue sky as I floated through the clouds, jumping from wind to wind. It's where life wanted to take me, with the birds at day and with the stars at night, the most pure form of freedom. It was the unknown, sometimes magical, and others times, dangerous.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So one of the examples of your anomalous effects is... sorry, I mean, was free mobility due to the alteration of your mass.
> 
> **RPC-628:** Such hard words. To be more specific, mass interchanging with me and the moss that follows me. I produce it by myself, most of which are currently staying in the cave, sometimes my only company. I had many abilities, most related to the nature of movement. Which is ironic, after I got inflicted with this.
> 
> //RPC-628 raises its head slightly, pointing to the harpoon currently embedded in its stomach.//
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Oh. Well, do you wanna talk about that?
> 
> **RPC-628:** The Harpoon, indeed. What is there to say; considerable pain that I don't even notice anymore unless I move. Speaking of which, it took all my movement from my head down to my whole body. Oh! And I almost forgot, it won't let me die no matter how hard I try.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** You tried to kill yourself?
> 
> **RPC-628:** In the first years, then I tried with help, but the effects of the moss was a "blessing". Now it leaves me incapable of interacting physically with other beings without getting their bones crushed or their water absorbed completely, leaving them as dry as my soul.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** A blessing? But I thought you mentioned it as being thaumaturgic... sorry, "magic" if that were the case, wouldn't you be able to control the effects?
> 
> **RPC-628:** When my flesh and mind were my own, perhaps. Now gaze upon my flesh and ask yourself if 'control of thine destiny' is what you see written upon it? Do you think I can do anything else other than whine and feel sorry for myself?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So you can't even enact most of your anomalous effects due to your body limitations, is that right?
> 
> **RPC-628:** You don't understand, I was the happiest being alive, I've entered this place. And I found love, a love almost stronger than any seen before, the love for discovery. I found humans, I discovered their ways. I watched them closely, befriended each and every one I found, their ingenuity, their love, grief, connections. I loved every piece of it, the good and the bad, I adored it... I wanted it.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So you ran away - leaving it all behind.
> 
> **RPC-628:** And never looked back, the worst mistake of my life.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** And how did it happen?
> 
> **RPC-628:** They came looking, of course, they did. They were supposed to bring me back, relegate me. I was lucky that I saw them first. I ran to a place where they would never get me, the deep dark parts of the ocean.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** And? What happened next?
> 
> **RPC-628:** I found the best and the worst that the unknown could ever offer me, the kindest soul I've ever met and the foulest one that would lay his presence upon me. I remember that day clearly, how everything started, and how everything ended. The glowing creature, something like a child, it didn't know the sea and was lost in its own innocence. It was almost like it was just born, one of many lost in the world it was put in, surrounded by darkness. I felt like that once, I would never come back to it. So I didn't let him come back to it.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Wait, so you stole a child?
> 
> **RPC-628:** I freed a child. From a life of darkness and eternal suffering.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Saving a stranger? At the cost of your own life?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Yes, however, I was too slow, the creature on the throne noticed it too soon. From its own body came a spear, a harpoon, he threw at me immediately. It was like the laws of physics didn't apply to him, it moved so quickly I couldn't even tell when I was being pierced by the cursed relic, then he looked at me. Those eyes... They..
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** What about them?
> 
> **RPC-628:** They were soulless and yet, I saw so many things from just looking at them - none of which were good. I was quickly struck as he pulled back. I made the glowing child become as little dense as he could be! He floated, screaming for a name he didn't know, pointing at me with suffering as I slowly died. Desperate screams echoed through the voidful ocean. I closed my eyes as I heard its voice. It told me: "Live."
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** So he just let you go?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Exactly. It freed me to the world. When I opened my eyes, I was unable to move, beached at a fishing town in Italy. The first person walking around was this one bearded man, a special person. Behind him were some fisherman coming to check if I were dead.
> 
> //A brief pause is heard in the recording, followed by a sigh from RPC-628.//
> 
> **RPC-628** However, the motives of that monster are unknown to me, but I knew from that moment on I would never be the same. I still have nightmares of that day. Its presence sits still at the hollow throne as it looked into the nothingness. Sometimes hear his voice in my darkest times, he talks about his one child, he says he is coming. He told me he'd let me go so I could watch with him as the day of his child arrives.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Uh... wow, so, who was that "special person" you mentioned just now?
> 
> **RPC-628:** Oh, David, or as your people call him, Davinci?
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** Da-Davinci?
> 
> **RPC-628:** That's the one, such a kind soul. He drove the fisherman away as he tried calling for help to bring me back to the sea. They failed of course, because I couldn't swim.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** How wonderful of an artist to be so kind to you.
> 
> **RPC-628:** Yes, he was the last one to leave, and when they all did, he just stood there, saying sorry for not being able to help. Such a sensitive and good soul.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** What did you do then?
> 
> **RPC-628:** I talked to him, and there we stood, watching the stars, for hours to come, talking. And the more we talked, the more I felt free from the obligations of life, the more unchained I felt. Slowly I floated to the skies to meet the stars, like a balloon going up. He looked at me as he started to think, a plan, I smiled at him.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** You two seemed like close friends. But how did you get in here then?
> 
> **RPC-628:** After I started floating he came with some ropes to help me relocate. He didn't have a place at his home to keep such a huge whale like me, but he had an idea, the monks who kept his anomalies could've helped him. Now, they didn't see eye to eye with each other, but for my well being, he, with the help of the monks of this place, slowly moved me to this cave. My current abode…
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** And are you happy with the current state you are in?
> 
> //After some minutes of silence, RPC-628 looks up to the roof of the cave.//
> 
> **RPC-628:** After years of thought, I can say for sure it became repetitive. No matter how many changes or people come, it's still all the same. I guess… I guess I'm just tired.
> 
> **Dr. Joseph:** If you could do one last thing before we go what would it be?
> 
> **RPC-628:** I want to look at the stars... one last time.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** Authority staff are planning on creating a device to move RPC-628 to the outside of the cave and bring it to a safe location. Attempts to remove RPC-628-A from its stomach are being made. The monks requested that the Authority have more visits of a longer defined amount of time with RPC-628.
 It is unknown what benefits the monks seek to gain by this request. Request granted by GD-0█.

+++ **Containment Protocols:** 

Due to recent circumstances, physical containment of RPC-628 has been withdrawn. The status of AEDF recovery operations for RPC-628 are ongoing, but the current state of RPC-628 is currently unknown. For these particular reasons, previous containment protocols, excluding recovery and the suppression of public knowledge, are no longer required.

+++ **Description:**

 RPC-628 was an aquatic mammalian organism measuring 430m long and weighing approximately 524,000kg that resembled a common //Megaptera Novaeangliae// (humpback whale).

While RPC-628 possessed a variety of anomalous abilities, the removal of RPC-628-A is believed to have caused the cessation of all anomalous properties as a result of the neutralization of the entity.

+++ **Addendum:**

On 23/07/2020, the Authority was notified by the local monks about RPC-628's recent death. Agents sent to Mt. Vesuvius soon realized that RPC-628 was not present at the containment enclosure anymore, the time of the incident roughly corresponded with a report from Authority Extraterrestrial Defense Force of an --unidentified object-- of what was presumed to be RPC-628 leaving Earth.[[footnote]]The location of --the object-- RPC-628 has yet to be determined.[[/footnote]]

Although the Authority does not have the corpse of RPC-628 in its possession currently, it is noted that RPC-628-A was removed due to unknown circumstances. RPC-628-A is currently in the monks' possession and Authority personnel are currently attempting to acquire the object for further research.

When speaking to one of the St. Johns monks, responding agents were brought outside of the cave and shown a massive tree with no leaves. Engraved into the tree were the words: [[[http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-628/offset/1|Forever In The Stars, J. C.]]]
