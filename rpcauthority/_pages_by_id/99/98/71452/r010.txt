**Registered Phenomena Code:** 221

**Object Class:** Beta-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Sapient Hazard, Regenerative Hazard, Transmutation Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-221 is to be contained in a standard humanoid containment cell with basic necessities such as a bed, bathroom, and clean clothes in Site-009. One on-site ASF personnel is to be stationed outside of its containment cell. Pending good behaviour, RPC-221 is allowed to request for objects that won't trigger its anomalous properties such as paper, a pen, and a mechanical pencil. RPC-221 is to be provided with a standard three meals a day and its containment cell is to be cleaned every Sunday at 17:00. Testing of RPC-221 is to be approved of by Head Researcher Harold R. Ames through a signed letter of authorisation.

Any RPC-221-A instances created during testing are to be obstructed from their stretcher and transferred to Site-016, contained in a standard containment locker of Level 3 access that is equipped with humidity and temperature-controlled devices. No RPC-221-A instances are to be taken out of containment under any circumstance. Any personnel caught taking RPC-221-A instances out of containment are to be apprehended and questioned. The RPC-221-A instance depicting RPC-221 will not be obstructed under any circumstance; Failure to act in accordance with this will result in demotion to Level 1.

[[include component:image-block name= https://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-221/Aussie.png|width=…px|caption= RPC-221 upon discovery.]]

**Description:** RPC-221 is a 30-year-old Caucasian male of Australian descent, standing at 190 centimetres tall and weighing at 76 kilograms. RPC-221 has the ability to paint pictures that appear photorealistic, whether it be a person, object or a landscape, and link the subject's physical properties to the painting. These paintings are to be referred to as RPC-221-A instances. Subjects of RPC-221-A instances cannot be altered in any physical way but are able to be altered through altering the image depicted on RPC-221-A instances. This has caused RPC-221's first painting of itself to alter its appearance with an absent right forearm. The right forearm however merely appears absent, with RPC-221 demonstrating on multiple occasions its ability to be used despite the lack of visual appearance.

RPC-221 is only able to manifest its anomalous properties if it uses its desired materials such as paint mixtures of acrylics and pastels. RPC-221 is unable to produce the same effect with pencils, pens, crayons and chalk. RPC-221's anomalous properties also do not occur if it deems its RPC-221-A instance as 'unfinished' in some way. The easiest way for RPC-221 to deem its works as 'unfinished' is to obstruct them from their stretcher after testing. However, the changes done to RPC-221-A instances are permanent even after obstruction and require RPC-221 to revert the changes in order to restore the subject to its original state.

Humanoid subjects of RPC-221-A will gain the ability to avoid further physical maturity, such as RPC-221 being the age of 30 years old but retaining its physical features from when it was 19 years old. It is currently unknown what will occur if an older subject of RPC-221-A would have their instance obstructed due to current limited knowledge. This is why it is discouraged to obstruct RPC-221's painting of itself due to certain risk factors.

**Addendum-01:**
[[collapsible show="(+) Discovery" hide="(-) Discovery"]]
**Discovery:** RPC-221 was discovered on January 1st 20██ at █████ University, USA. A New Year's celebration was occurring at the university that RPC-221 attended when RPC-221's body began to suddenly alter. Witnesses claim that tattoos began to appear on RPC-221's body, then its hair began to alter between varying styles and its pupils began changing colours. Witnesses then began sharing these occurrences online which is when the Authority took notice. All online media of RPC-221 was wiped and a recovery team was sent to █████ University to escort RPC-221.
[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="(+) Interview Log" hide="(-) Interview Log"]]
> **Interviewed:** RPC-221
> 
> **Interviewer:** Agent ████
> 
> **Foreword:** Interview of RPC-221 of its origins and abilities.
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Agent ████:** Good morning.
> 
> **RPC-221:** Uhm... you too.
> 
>  **Agent ████:** I will be conducting an interview with you, will that be alright?
> 
> **RPC-221:** Sure sure... But can I ask my //own// question first?
> 
>  **Agent ████:** Any questions you have will surely be answered in due time. Now, about your abilities. Did you obtain them at some point or were you born with them?

> 
> **RPC-221:** I'm a self-taught artist, but I'd say my art prof helped me a lot. I miss going to his classes.

>  **Agent ████:** You miss going to his classes? Are you saying that you do not see this professor often now?
> 
> **RPC-221:** Yeah, he was my prof in my old school from years ago... I hop around universities a lot just to mainly sharpen my skills but also... I guess, move around more. Scholarships aren't a problem for me now.
> 
>  **Agent ████:** What was your old school? The one with the professor that taught you these abilities?
> 
> **RPC-221:** Uhm... Well... I can't say, man.
> 
>  **Agent ████:** You can't say?
> 
> **RPC-221:** Yes... but only because I can't pronounce the school's name. It's really European and my Aussie tongue can't for the life of me say it.
> 
>  **Agent ████:** Alright, odd but alright. Mind explaining what was happening at that party?

> 
> **Agent ████:** Are you saying that they altered that... painting of yourself?
> 
> **RPC-221:** Yeah, those shitheads were once friends of mine. I told them about what the painting could do and they started fucking everything up! God, they made my life hell. I thought I hid it well but they're as determined as ever.
> 
> **Agent ████:** Speaking of alterations... Is your missing arm one of them?
> 
> **RPC-221:** Ehh... More so an accident that happened when I first made the painting. The arm isn't visible in the drawing so it altered me like this. I don't mind it though since it's still there, you just can't see it.
> 
> **Agent ████:** Alright, that's all we need to know for now.
> 
> **<End Log>**

**Addendum-03:**
[[collapsible show="(+) Test Logs" hide="(-) Test Logs"]]
+ Test-221-01

**Subject:** CSD-0890

**Procedure:** CSD-0890 was ordered to pose as a subject for RPC-221. Once the painting has finished, on-site ASF personnel were ordered to fire rounds at CSD-0890.

**Result:** CSD-0890 expressed no pain and remained unharmed during the firing.

**Notes:** N/A

+ Test-221-02

**Subject:** CSD-0890

**Procedure:** Using the same painting, RPC-221 was given the task to alter CSD-0890's image in any way it desired.

**Result:** CSD-0890 began to alter once RPC-221 finished its task, her hair began to grow longer, freckles appeared across her cheeks and piercings appeared on her earlobe. Once the painting was obstructed from its stretcher, CSD-0890's appearance remained unchanged. When CSD-0890 attempted to remove her ear piercings, she was not able to despite her best efforts.

**Notes:** N/A

+ Test-221-03

**Subject:** The interior of RPC-221's containment cell.

**Procedure:** RPC-221 was tasked with painting its containment cell and to outfit it with whatever it pleased (within reason).

**Result:** RPC-221's containment cell began to be decorated with several furniture pieces such as a twin-sized bed, a drawer with three compartments, a bedside lamp, and a beanbag chair. RPC-221 also changed the colour of its containment cell's walls, turning it from grey to navy blue. The RPC-221-A instance was obstructed and the containment cell remained unchanged. Upon further inspection of each piece of furniture, it was discovered that none of the pieces of furniture were functional. An example of this was that the drawer did not have three hollow compartments and the drawers were simply carved in.

**Notes:** "Seems like he can't make many functional things, that bed was rock hard and the bean bag chair was just some solid statue." -//Head Researcher Harold R. Ames//

+ Test-221-04

**Subject:** Site-009 cafeteria area.

**Procedure:** RPC-221 was tasked with painting the cafeteria of Site-009 and outfit it with amenities.

**Result:** Once RPC-221 finished its task, three drink and snack machines appeared against an empty wall of the cafeteria. Two drinking fountains were added as well as food at the cafeteria station that RPC-221 claims to be its favourite. All changes were functional and remained unchanged when the RPC-221-A instance was obstructed. Testing of these new additions discovered that the food provided was not edible and that the other amenities were not functional.

**Notes:** "I'm guessing that it just makes additions for aesthetic purposes, a bit disappointing but that might only be because I've seen a lot of stuff while working here." -//Head Researcher Harold R. Ames//

+ Test-221-05

**Subject:** Containment cell wall

**Procedure:** RPC-221 was tasked to paint its containment cell wall and then add an animal of its choosing.

**Results:** RPC-221 finished its task, painting a wolf in front of the wall. Upon inspection of the 'wolf', it appeared that the wolf was not able to move and showed no signs of life. RPC-221 removed the wolf from its painting before it was obstructed.

**Notes:** "Seems like all of its paintings lack any life or function. I wonder if this would improve if he got more schooling or something." -//Head Researcher Harold R. Ames//

+ Test-221-06

**Subject:** A white adult feline

**Procedure:** RPC-221 was given the task to paint the subject and give it an extra tail of a different colour.

**Results:** RPC-221 finished its task and the subject sprouted an extra tail from its back. The tail was black in colour but was not animated. During an x-ray examination of the tail, it showed that no biological matter was present. When questioned as to why RPC-221 placed the tail on the cat's back, RPC-221 responded; "I wanted to see how far I can challenge Mother Nature." The painting was restored to its original state before being obstructed.

**Notes:** "Well, it did challenge Mother Nature." -//Head Researcher Harold R. Ames//
