**Registered Phenomena Code:** 627

**Object Class:** Alpha-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Mechanical Hazard, Contact Hazard, Electromagnetic Force Hazard, Teleportation Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-627 is to be housed in a clear acrylic box measuring 0.6 m x 0.6 m by 0.6 m (2ft. x 2ft. x 2ft.), with circular holes on one face to allow for minimal interaction with the object. The box containing RPC-627 should allow for the object to be plugged into an independent generator, and is to be stationed at the auxiliary kitchen of Site-036. This kitchen has been granted special priority privileges in regard to food shipments delivered to the Site. Unusual or exotic ingredients[[footnote]]Such as ingredients which are not present in the regular stock of the Site pantry, or ingredients which are not typically commercially available.[[/footnote]] which may be a part of the orders produced by RPC-627 are to be procured with utmost haste and care, and should be delivered to the auxiliary kitchen immediately for use in the orders made by the object.

Six (6) CSD personnel with experience in food service and preparation must be placed on standby and ready at a moment's notice to produce any and all orders printed out by RPC-627. If CSD personnel are successful in completing an order from RPC-627, all money manifested is to be confiscated and utilized by the Authority as it sees fit. If CSD personnel are unable to complete the order for any reason, be it lack of ingredients, time, or otherwise, new CSD personnel will be brought in as replacements. No personnel should attempt to damage or destroy RPC-627. The only time contact with the object is allowed is when order slips are removed for study and to produce the orders printed on them. Periods of activity and inactivity are to be recorded on a weekly basis to attempt to formulate a pattern of RPC-627's orders.

**Description:** RPC-627 is a common receipt printer[[footnote]]A receipt printer is a type of device utilized by retail establishments and restaurants which takes in information inputted by a register, i.e. product information and quantity, and prints them out onto paper for use in proof of sale.[[/footnote]] of seemingly average make and model, though the object bears no manufacturing markings whatsoever. RPC-627 has several anomalous traits, primarily, the object has shown to randomly print out various "orders" of various foodstuffs and non-foodstuffs which may or may not have special instructions on their preparation/presentation. These orders may vary wildly from one another, and some orders have been shown to repeat. The method by which RPC-627 prints out orders is unknown.

If an order is not completed as per the specific instructions, --any individual who completed the last order produced by RPC-627-- any individual within a --250 m-- 1km radius will instantly --vanish-- teleport to an as of yet unknown location, possibly existing [DATA EXPUNGED] (See Incident Report: 627-1 for details). If the order is completed within its defined parameters, however, the foodstuffs/non-foodstuffs of the order will instead teleport to the unknown location, and the money that the order states is the price will be instantly teleported into the possession of individuals responsible for the creation of the order, always as cash.

RPC-627 is also known to operate without an apparent external power source (though it is not recommended that personnel allow this), and appears to be indestructible, as multiple attempts at its destruction have been unsuccessful. To date, RPC-627 has survived blunt force trauma, fire, submergence in water, freezing, and even anomalous means of destruction. All such methods have shown no effect on the appearance or function of RPC-627.

Lastly, RPC-627 is capable of defending itself from potential or perceived threats, as several attempts to relocate it via direct contact have been met with RPC-627 discharging targeted electrical currents towards the individual making contact with it. "Sentient Hazard" classification is pending. However, it is unknown how the object achieves this. The ream of receipt paper within RPC-627's chassis appears to be inexhaustible, and the object's inkwells are in fact empty. Research is ongoing to determine the composition and source of both the paper and ink the object produces.

**Recovery Report:** RPC-627 was first recovered on ██/██/2007 from The ████████████ Restaurant, an establishment usually specializing in seafood, after several of its staff had seemingly vanished in the middle of a lunch rush, with patrons in the dining area also beginning to vanish at random shortly after. One line chef, identified as Tim █████, did not initially vanish, however, and continued to make the orders produced by RPC-627 until Authority agents were able to secure the location. A cover story of a gas leak which originated in the kitchen was implemented, and a heavy dose of amnestics was applied to all surviving patrons as an explanation for those which were lost. Mr. █████ was interviewed shortly afterwards about his explanation for his actions during the incident.

[[collapsible show="+ Addendum 627-2: Test Log of RPC-627 while in containment. Level II restricted." hide="- Addendum 627-2: Test Log of RPC-627 while in containment. Access granted."]]

After the acquisition of RPC-627, testing was conducted in an attempt to discern if the object possessed any more anomalous traits than those which it has already displayed. Findings from these tests are displayed as such.

**Test #:** 627-01
**Procedure:** One (1) order slip produced by RPC-627 is prepared by CSD-1678 per its specifications. Order consists of a quail egg sandwich with tomatillos and fig sauce, with a side of pan-fried frog legs. Order is stated to cost $13.99.
**Results:** Upon completion and plating of the order, said order instantly vanished, after which the exact amount of money the order is stated to cost materializes in CSD-1678's hands. Money is confiscated afterwards.
**Notes:** //Ok, so that seems simple enough, you make an order, you get its supposed cost, I wonder though what is the result if two people are responsible for the order?- Dr. ████████//

**Test #:** 627-02
**Procedure:** One (1) order slip produced by RPC-627 is prepared by CSD-1678 and CSD-4347. The order consists of roasted whole guinea pig, with a side of fried green tomatoes. The order cost $21.99. CSD-1678 is instructed to prepare the guinea pig, while CSD-4347 prepares the tomatoes.
**Results:** Order vanished upon completion; afterwards, both CSD personnel receive exactly $11, which is confiscated afterwards.
**Notes:** //Well, it looks like whoever is doing this knows about division of labor. That doesn't exactly help us understand how the object functions.- Dr. ████████//

**Test #:** 627-03
**Procedure:** One (1) order slip produced by RPC-627 is not prepared, CSD-1678 and CSD-4347 are kept in their assigned quarters and monitored. Order consists of one (1) 9 Volt battery and one (1) pack of golden raisins.
**Results:** 12 minutes after the order slip was produced, CSD-1678 and CSD-4347 vanished instantaneously at the exact same moment. It is unknown what happened to these personnel afterwards.
**Notes:** //It appears that this entity targets whoever was responsible for the last order completed, as it appears to expect them to keep producing orders until "it says so", whatever that means. - Dr. ████████//

**Test #:** 627-04
**Procedure:** One (1) order slip produced by RPC-627 is not prepared. CSD-7186, who had prepared several orders beforehand, is kept in his quarters and monitored. CSD-7186 is equipped with a shoulder-mounted camcorder and microphone in an attempt to observe what happens to individuals affected by RPC-627. Order consists of lamb chops apparently prepared in the style of General Tso's chicken, with jasmine rice on the side.
**Results:** CSD-7186 vanished at the expected time. However, the recording device did not teleport along with him. This slip was produced several minutes after the CSD personnel disappeared:

> **NOTICE**
>
> COME ON NOW, I THOUGHT WE WOULD NOT HAVE THIS PROBLEM ANYMORE.
>
> -CHEF GOBAI WANAKHA.
 
**Notes:** //I did not expect this level of precision. - Dr. ████████//

**Test #:** 627-05
**Procedure:** Technician Andrews attempts to disassemble RPC-627 in an attempt to find out how the object produces the anomalous order slips.
**Results:** As Andrews lifts the cover of the object and attempts to remove the ream of paper, he is struck by a bolt of electricity in the arms and torso. He is merely incapacitated by this action.
**Notes:** //Medical staff say that Andrews can't move his arms anymore. This thing knew how to disable a threat. I'm calling for a recommendation to classify this object as a Sentient-Hazard. -Dr. ████████//

**Test #:** 627-06
**Procedure:** RPC-627 is unplugged to observe how the object functions without electricity.
**Results:** RPC-627 began to print out various order slips which contained moderate to severe cognitohazards. RPC-627 was plugged back in and afterwards produced this slip:

> NOTICE
>
> HAS SOMETHING HAPPENED TO THE "RECEIPT PRINTER" AS YOU SO CALL IT? MY ORDERS YOU RECEIVED APPEARED TO STILL BE WRITTEN IN MY NATIVE LANGUAGE. HOPEFULLY YOU WERE NOT AFFECTED.
>
> -CHEF GOBAI WANAKHA.

**Notes:** //Looks like unplugging it only exacerbates problems, because those slips which contained the cognitohazards were still orders, so the CSD still disappeared. We must find another method of containment. -Dr. ████████//


**Test #:** 627-07
**Procedure:** CSD-2317 is instructed to lift the cover of the object to observe the ream of receipt paper and also to see whether the object will operate normally.
**Results:** RPC-627 fails to produce an order slip for 4 hours, after which CSD-2317 is instructed to place the cover back on the object. Immediately afterwards, a lethal electric bolt discharges from RPC-627, killing the CSD instantly. This slip was produced afterwards:

> **NOTICE**
>
> PLEASE DO NOT DO WHATEVER YOU DID TO THE "RECEIPT PRINTER" AS YOU SO CALL IT, EVER AGAIN. I WAS IN THE MIDDLE OF A "LUNCH RUSH" AS YOU SO CALL IT WITH MY CLIENTELE AND THEY WERE VERY UPSET THAT THEY DID NOT RECEIVE YOUR "FOOD" AS YOU SO CALL IT.
>
> -CHEF GOBAI WANAKHA.

**Notes:** //Now this is interesting. This entity appears to rely on the unobservance of the receipt paper to allow for the orders to be inputted and printed out on the object. Perhaps this can be an effective containment protocol. -Dr. ████████//

--Findings of this test are still under review as of ██/██/20██.-- --The findings of this test have been deemed an effective containment protocol and will be implemented as of ██/██/20██.-- As of Incident 627-1, these findings are now deemed an unacceptable containment protocol, and should never be implemented in the future.

[[collapsible show="+ Addendum: Incident Report 627-1: Mass disappearance of Site-036 personnel. Level III restricted." hide="- Addendum: Incident Report 627-1: Mass disappearance of Site-036 personnel. Access granted."]]

After the findings of Test 627-07, it was deemed acceptable by Site-036 Director ██████ that the most effective method of containment for RPC-627 was to leave its paper ream exposed, which apparently prevented the entity or entities affecting the object from producing the orders. The time at which the cover was lifted from the object is noted as being at 1325 hours local time on 04/12/201█. RPC-627 failed to produce order slips for a full 24 hours, and also did not discharge electricity when approached and handled. The object was moved to the storage wing of Site-036 and was placed on low priority observation. Three weeks passed without any activity reported by RPC-627. On 05/03/201█, security personnel in charge of the storage wing failed to give an hourly status report. Additional security personnel were sent to assess the condition of the personnel who were last known to be stationed at the storage wing.

Of the three personnel who were in charge of security of the storage wing, one member, identified as Craig █████████, was still alive. The other two personnel were killed, with their corpses badly mutilated and some organs seemingly missing. The cause of death was most likely determined to be massive bodily trauma, though it is unclear how this was caused. Mr. █████████ was found to be making several gestures, which were later determined to be similar to motions made whilst preparing food, such as whisking into the air and cutting seemingly nothing, all whilst standing in front of RPC-627, whose ream of paper was still exposed. Mr. █████████ is also noted to be repetitively stating a series of phrases, later deduced to be various "orders" commonly known to be printed out by RPC-627. Security teams moved in to attempt to subdue Mr. █████████. However, as soon as they came within 2 meters of the subject, they instantly vanished. Personnel then retreated to alert the Site Director of the situation.

The storage wing was sealed, and communication was attempted with Mr. █████████, who continued to make gestures and speak phrases. A drone was sent in to attempt to subdue the subject, which also disappeared. However, the distance between it and the subject when it disappeared was noted to be 5 meters now. Mr. █████████ then stopped speaking phrases and made contact with personnel observing from the Site cameras. Automatic distress beacons alerted nearby Sites of the situation, and MSTs were mobilized for fear of a possible breach. As they investigated the Site, they soon discovered RPC-627, with one slip printed out, which reads as follows:

> **NOTICE**
>
> THIS IS THE RESULT OF YOUR FAILURE TO COOPERATE WITH ME. DO NOT ALLOW THIS TO HAPPEN AGAIN.
>
> - CHEF GOBAI WANAKHA.

As recovery efforts continued, an assessment of the surrounding area revealed that a sizable amount of the population of a town, which was close to Site-036, had also vanished. Through approximations of the distance between individuals who are said to have disappeared and RPC-627, it was then deduced that anyone within a 1 km radius of RPC-627 as it closed teleported. Unfortunately, due to the large-scale and sudden nature of the incident, no viable cover story could be implemented, and amnestics were deployed over the remainder of the town. All Site-036 personnel have been deemed MIA. Research is still ongoing to deduce where matter that is transported via RPC-627 is placed.

As of Incident 627-1, current containment protocols have been implemented, and thus far minimal CSD personnel have been teleported by RPC-627.
