**Name:** Captain Erin McKinley

**Clearance:** Level-3

**Class:** MST Field Commander

**Current Assignment:** Hotel-01 "Highlanders" -  [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/sites:site-007 Site-007]

**Age:** ██

**Physical Description:** Captain McKinley is of Scottish decent. Currently she is 1.82m tall and weighs 76.4kg. She has dark auburn hair and blue eyes.

**Background:** Born in Glasgow, Scotland in 19██, Erin Elrick McKinley to Mrs. ███ McKinley and Mr. ██ McKinley. She let an uneventful childhood and graduated school with an interest in architectural. However financial issues prevented further schooling and so she opted to join the military. She found the environment to be to her liking and became a talented soldier of the Royal Regiment of Scotland. She earned her commission only █ years later and was promoted to Captain.

An incident in 20█with an anomaly killed six members of her squad that had been deployed in ████, Iraq.[[footnote]] Declassification of this Anomalous Event is still ongoing [[/footnote]] Agents responding to the event rounded up the survivors for interrogation and amnestics. Captain McKinley expressed interest to join the Authority under the desire to further protect the world. [[footnote]] Exact Quote: //"What in the fuck is the point of fighting terrorists and nutjobs in this sandy hell if it can all go to shit when some demon horror just crawls out of your friend's face and lays waste to a damn city?!"// ~Captain McKinley[[/footnote]] Agent ███ contacted command and was given approval on account of Captain McKinley's stellar record. She was assigned to Hotel-01 and was allowed to retain rank following introduction to the Authority as well as anomalous combat and containment training.

Captain McKinley has served the Authority for ██ years and has led ███ successful missions. She is considered one of Hotel-01's most distinguished soldiers.
