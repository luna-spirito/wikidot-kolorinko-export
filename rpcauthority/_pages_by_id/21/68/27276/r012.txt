**Registered Phenomena Code:** 540

**Object Class:** Beta-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Organic Hazard, Sapient Hazard, Auditory Hazard, Info-Hazard

**Containment Protocols (current revision):** RPC-540 must remain within a soundproofed humanoid containment chamber with the addition of pressure sensors on the walls and the floor. Additional monitoring must be made through constant observation of pressure sensor readings and thermal imaging. Outside of specific and strictly supervised testing conditions with prior authorization from the current Site Director, no visual contact or communication is to be made with RPC-540.

[[collapsible show="Previous protocols (archived)" hide="Close"]]
RPC-540 must remain within a standard humanoid containment chamber and monitored through standard surveillance equipment. Limited, regular interaction through external means is recommended as a way to mitigate RPC-540's effects.

Outside regular interaction, RPC-540 must be provided access to a collection of fictional literature and writing material as requested, with original pieces being retrieved, filed, and kept for testing purposes. All testing must be authorized by current Project Lead.
[[/collapsible]]

**Description:** RPC-540 is a pale caucasian male, 17 years in age, with average black hair, and a thin body standing 1.72 cm. in height. RPC-540's anomalous properties manifest whenever it attempts verbal or written communication.

RPC-540's written and spoken words are subject to a powerful Cognito-hazardous effect, though such an effect is dependent on both the subject matter being discussed and RPC-540's emotional state. Conversations held with RPC-540 broadcast its current emotional state, imprinting it upon the other participant(s), eliciting an empathetic response. Subjects sustaining prolonged contact with RPC-540 (usually within the span of one or two minutes) will generally become agreeable with it and will keep engaged out of empathy instead of outright compulsion.

After perceiving this, RPC-540 will request that any participant(s) read or listen to an original literary work produced by it, regularly in the form of a short story or poem. Such works focus on overtly dramatic displays of emotion, with the intent of engaging the audience and invoking an equally strong response. These works, however, are considered to be lacking in quality and result in them being panned by whatever audience is currently gathered around RPC-540.

By agreeing to be part of the audience, subjects expose themselves to the next phase of RPC-540's effect. After reading or listening to any literary work produced by it (and proceeding to haze it), subjects report being strongly affected by an unseen force. Descriptions have included suffering deep lacerations, penetration or puncture wounds, prolonged loss of hearing and/or sight, and concussions. In spite of this, and outside the distress or disorientation endured, no harm ever comes to the audience, with subjects eventually recovering from whatever damage claimed to be felt.

RPC-540 states that writing serves as an outlet for its emotions and it is advised by Authority researchers that this claim is to be taken in a literal sense, as RPC-540 is seemingly able to imbue its written work with its emotions. Such effects do not manifest when played from recording devices.

**Recovery:** RPC-540 was recovered after a reading session during an event at █████████ High School located in █████, ███. The mass confusion and hysteria caused by the manifestation of RPC-540's anomalous properties among a wide audience led to intervention by Authority agents. 

A cover story centered around an unusual case of collective hysteria was fed to the media, RPC-540 was secured and contained, witnesses were gathered for exposure to amnestics.

[[collapsible show="Addendum 540-1: Initial investigation efforts" hide="Close"]]
After reviewing media produced by the audience gathered during the incident, no anomalous entities or effects were observed before RPC-540 began reading. Further analysis focused on memetic or information hazards revealed no immediate results, forcing researchers to conclude that the main cause of the event was RPC-540 itself.

Before amnestic treatment, members of RPC-540's family remained certain that it never previously displayed any anomalies. Outside of statements regarding its hobbies, namely its marked interest in reading and writing, no origin of the anomalous effects could be determined. Authority personnel then proceeded to interview RPC-540.

After displaying disgust, Dr. Black vocalizes distress, stating multiple times that something has cut through his eyes. Interview abruptly terminated.//

Subsequent interviews yielded similar results, which led to the Authority's initial understanding of RPC-540's effects. Due to the nature of its anomalous properties interfering with information gathering, interviews with RPC-540 were suspended until better procedures could be enacted.

> Since it's apparent that there is an emotional component to RPC-540's effects, I suggest containment efforts focus on mitigating or eliminating any stressors within reason. Perhaps acclimating to its new surroundings might help in future interaction attempts.
> 
> -Dr. Black

[[collapsible show="Addendum 540-2: Recovered evidence" hide="Close"]]
With interviews unable to provide significant information regarding RPC-540, Authority agents seized RPC-540's belongings and began analysis of gathered evidence. It was confirmed that literary works produced before the recovery incident did not manifest any anomalies.

The remainder of RPC-540's belongings recovered from its home displayed no anomalous effects. The following portion of a chat recovered from RPC-540's personal computer became the only possible lead to determine an origin to its properties.

This prompted another attempt at obtaining additional information from RPC-540. Due to its nature and the fact that no feasible interview could be conducted without being hindered, RPC-540 was led to an observation chamber and asked to use a recording device in order to elaborate on the previous chat log. The following is RPC-540's audio transcript.

> **Testing parameters:**
> 
> To avoid being unnecessarily brought to any stress and/or anxiety, RPC-540 must not be present in any active test. Original pieces taken from RPC-540 are allowed to be used in testing, but if there is need for a specific tone or intended effect, RPC-540 is to be requested to create such work. Any original pieces by RPC-540 must be placed within folders and located in the dedicated filing cabinet. During tests, CSD subjects are to be escorted to a standard observation chamber, asked to read the intended piece, and recorded for an audio transcript.

> **Researcher:** Dr. Black

> Along with my team, I've tested RPC-540 in the attempt to find better ways to communicate with it in order to better understand the methods by which it gained its anomalous properties. Interaction has always been a major constraint precisely because said effects, but these tests have served the purpose of outlining ways in which we can manipulate or work around those effects. Brief interactions with personnel have also helped the acclimation process and RPC-540 has now grown accustomed to its new environment.
> 
> Not only have these brief interactions helped with integration with its surroundings, but we've also noted a sharp decrease in the intensity of RPC-540's anomalous properties whenever it displays a positive or peaceful mood. This leads us to conclude that a test with a carefully selected audience that might be more compliant with or otherwise resistant to the Cognito-hazardous writing will boost RPC-540's self-esteem which will highly likely allow us to better communicate with it and continue looking into its anomalous nature.
> 
> -Dr. Black
