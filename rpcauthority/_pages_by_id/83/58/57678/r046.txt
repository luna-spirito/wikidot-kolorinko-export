[[=]]
+ **Overview**
[[/=]]
-----

**Established:** Initially established 13 July, 1906, abandoned 2 December 1906. Reactivated 4 May 1926

**Front Cover:** None.

**Location:** Columbiad Mons, Shackleton Crater, 89.44°S 141.8°W, Luna 

**Codename:** Fort Columbiad

**Hex-code:** [13]

**Site Occupation:** AEDF Terrestrial Fleet (TERFLT) support facility, Beta/Gamma-level research and containment, Lunar power generation

**Division Jurisdiction:** Containment (AEDF/U&IIB)

**Total Area:** Approx. 15000 m^^2^^[[footnote]] This measurement does not include surface facilities. Much of the internal subterranean volume of Site-019 is currently unoccupied, with individual Site wings deliberately separated for the sake of atmospheric and informational compartmentalization.  [[/footnote]]

**Facility Access:** Regular personnel and cargo transfers to and from Site-019 and low Earth orbit are performed monthly with a variety of vessels as part of the Authority Lunar Return Shuttle (LRS) Program. The Site maintains four U&IIB Cargo/Heavy standard landing pads.

**History:** The Authority came into possession of Null-gravity Generator technology in 1896[[footnote]] The theoretical model for practical gravity control was first developed by German glider engineer Otto Lillienthal in early August of that year.[[/footnote]] and immediately began a program of NG airship construction. Several notable flight accidents led to the conclusion of the Research personnel involved that any NG-based vehicle should necessarily be capable of travel and navigation through the extreme limits of the Terrestrial atmosphere, and by extension the vacuum of space. A lunar voyage was first proposed in 1901, with the final vessel, AV //Columbiad//, constructed over the next 5 years.[[footnote]] The //Columbiad// carried a crew of 12 in a hull adapted from a high-pressure steam boiler, and navigated by the combination of NG field manipulation and gyroscopes driven by a compact electrical motor. Inexperience in temperature regulation and atmospheric recycling led to unsanitary, overheated and humid onboard conditions, only exacerbated by the deleterious mental health effects of intense NG field exposure.[[/footnote]] 

Captained by experienced Protection Division submariner Etienne Pontchartain, the //Columbiad// was launched from Willowmore, South Africa on the 10th of July, 1906, making landfall on the lunar South Pole 3 days later. The serendipitous discovery of a preexisting network of caves in the immediate vicinity led to the decision to establish a "fort" on the site. Crews used two adapted tethered diving suits and a caisson system to both enlarge and seal the cave system over several weeks. Unfortunately, the combination of worsening atmospheric conditions about ship, as well as NG poisoning[[footnote]] Prolonged exposure to NG fields, a concept not well-understood at the time, induces mild radiation burns, and alterations in brain chemistry which cause intense paranoia, seizures, and face blindness. [[/footnote]], led to the death of most of the //Columbiad//'s crew by the middle of August. The three surviving members attempted to relaunch the vessel, putting it in a low Lunar orbit where it was recovered six years later.

Fort Columbiad, which remained sealed but empty, was reopened and expanded by the crew of AV //Cavor// in the spring of 1926, re-designated Site-019, and expanded to its present size over the next 50 years. Budget shortfalls in the 1970s and 1980s led to the halting of expansion works on Site-019 which have yet to be restarted, leading to the presence of a substantial network of sealed and unoccupied tunnels.

**Background Information:** To avoid detection and to maximize stellar radiation protection, more than 95% of the Site's habitable volume is subterranean, with minimal surface access. All surface facilities are camouflaged with layers of regospackle[[footnote]] A substance formed by the mixture of processed lunar regolith with a variety of industrial adhesives, creating a coating which is visually indistinguishable from the normal Lunar surface by telescope observation, and produces similar radar returns to Lunar regolith. [[/footnote]] and equipped with distributed cooling systems to keep the Site's surface temperatures comparable to surrounding Lunar surface.

[[=]]
+ Offices & Wings Section
[[/=]]
------

* **Lunar Logistics Control (LLC)**: Site-019 acts as the control center and coordination hub for the supply of electrical power to other Lunar sites, notably Sites ███, ███, ███ and [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-099 099]. Site-019 also acts as a warehouse and transit facility for supplies transferred from Earth via LRS and subsequently distributed by local transport.
* **Generator Wing:** Site-019 operates 30 km^^2^^ of low-visibility solar panels, as well as two Type 6 Stellarators with a combined maximum power output of 4100 mW. More than 90% of this power output is diverted to other Lunar facilities as part of LLC.
* **Department of Communications, Lunar Office (DEPCOMLUN):** The Site-019 Maintenance team is charged with the maintenance and upkeep of Authority communications satellites and relays on the Lunar surface and in Lunar orbit.
* **SoARn Laser Installation Control Facility:** More commonly known as [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/coldandsteele02 "Sauron"], the Solar Ambient Reflection Weapon is an anti-asteroid laser defense system. Physically located more than ████ km away from Site-019, within [REDACTED] Crater, its primary control, targeting and power management systems are emplaced on Site-019 to minimize the risk of damage to the Installation also causing permanent damage to inhabited Lunar infrastructure.[[footnote]] Since 2017, SoARn has been mothballed pending large-scale systems upgrade works. [[/footnote]]
* **Lunar Research and Containment Wing:** This isolated section of the Site contains hardened Beta and Gamma-level containment and research facilities designed for use on entities which require low-gravity or continuous vacuum environments for safe containment. The Research Wing currently houses ██ RPC entities and █ Lesser Anomalous Objects.
* **Habitation Wing:** Contains living quarters, recreational facilities and hydroponic farms sufficient to maintain Site-019's complement of personnel. In emergencies, the Habitation Wing can be directly linked to several different backup offsite power, communications, water and air filtration facilities, enabling it to survive independently of the rest of the Site. For security reasons, Habitation Wing is the deepest of the sections of Site-019.
* **AEDF Fleet Yard 019:** This dock facility contains all the equipment needed for long-term maintenance and repair of AEDF vessels capable of Lunar landings. It also maintains a suite of 8 drone repair craft used for maintenance work on vessels which are incapable of unassisted landings.
[[collapsible show="+LEVEL 4A/AEDF CLEARANCE REQUIRED" hide="-CLEARANCE ACCEPTED"]]
* **AEDF NG Drydock 1:** This subterranean facility, [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/motm concealed] as a series of hydrogen tanks on the southern edge of the Site, acts as the long-term dry dock for the AEDF's Inner-System Fleet Line Reserve ([http://www.rpc-wiki.net/aedfmanifest INFLTLNR]). For further detail on the special-use vessels currently stationed at Site-019, see "Vessels on Station", below.
[[/collapsible]]
