**Containment Protocols:** 
[[include :rpcsandbox:component:hazard-image-block-base
|hazards=[[image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/component%3Aicons/h-organic.png style="margin-top: 1px; width: 35px;" title="Organic"]][[image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/component%3Aicons/h-sentient.png style="margin-top: 1px; width: 35px;" title="Sentient"]][[image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/component%3Aicons/h-tychokinetic.png style="margin-top: 1px; width: 35px;" title="Tychokinetic"]][[image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/component%3Aicons/h-emotional.png style="margin-top: 1px; width: 35px;" title="Emotional"]][[image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/component%3Aicons/h-mind-control.png style="margin-top: 1px; width: 35px;" title="Mind Control"]]|align=right|width=350px|name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-696/696.png|caption= Image of RPC-696 taken by PVT Walker during the initial investigation.]]

RPC-696 is to be sprayed with one half-liter (0.13 gallons) of animal fluids every day at 18:00 GMT. At least once per week these fluids must consist of a specialized admixture of human origin[[footnote]]After watering RPC-696 with a purely nonhuman admixture for five days, anomalous effects restarted unexpectedly. As of ██/█ /████ it is unclear whether this uptick was the result of a satiety concern from RPC-696 or a manifestation of some kind of taste preference.[[/footnote]]. Contracts have been established with the nearest █████ bank at █████████ as well as with █████████ ████████ to ensure a steady supply of human admixture and animal fluids of acceptable quality. The bath should be made of █████, █████, and █████ [[footnote]]The exact specialized recipe is made available by request to Dr. █████ through their intranet email address or via any site management with a Level 3 clearance and a 3.2 or better on the Bryke Mental Resilience Index.[[/footnote]] in a 3:2:1 ratio.

RPC-696 must be recorded at all times; retrieved footage should be scanned for unexpected growth at least once per hour, even at night. Daily growth or shrinkage within a range of five centimeters (two inches) is to be considered normal and regular. //Approval of acting heads of staff and a Level 4 or higher security override is required before any attempt to physically alter RPC-696//. It is similarly important, before raising alarm, to review footage to determine if a branch has changed in size or if RPC-696 moved it from another location.

Detailed reports covering RPC-696's cumulative growth, movement, and health must be emailed to Dr. █████ or the acting head of staff through their intranet address at least twice a week. As of ██/█ /████, these regular reports are scheduled for Wednesday and Sunday. If these dates are missed and immediate contact fails, the facility will be presumed lost and a team will be dispatched for armed investigation.

While RPC-696 is relatively easy to contain[[footnote]]Staff turnover at the Paramour Garden facility makes containment pricey and the retraining process laborious, however presently the containment process is considered both manageable and regular.[[/footnote]] and may be reclassified to Alpha at a later date, at no point should this encourage dereliction of duties. Current Beta classification is primarily motivated by a general lack of understanding of RPC-696's motives, needs, capabilities, limitations, and weaknesses. If containment of RPC-696 fails again, it could have dire consequences.

Background information about RPC-696 is available to Research staff via Description files "Physical Description" and "Anomalous Behavior." Additionally, //all personnel, acting or transient, must thoroughly review "Safety Considerations" before physically entering the facility//.

**Description:** 
 
[[collapsible show="[Access File: Physical Description]" hide="[Close]" hideLocation="both"]]
[[div style="background-color: #000000; border-left: 8px solid #777; border-top: 4px solid #777; border-bottom: 1px solid #777; border-right: 1px solid #777; margin: 5; padding: 7px;"]]
RPC-696 is a perennial tree mostly[[footnote]]Bark scrapings taken from RPC-696 were sequenced and compared with a sample taken from a nearby seed bank. The two genomes had a correlation of 99.8%; experiments to determine the effect of this 0.2% genetic deviation on RPC-696's anomalous nature have been underway since █/██/████. As of ██/██/████, there have been no scientifically significant discoveries.[[/footnote]] genetically equivalent to the //Populus nigra//[[footnote]]Black Poplar[[/footnote]] common through central and eastern Europe. RPC-696 is ten and a half meters (34.4 feet) tall with a trunk approximately 2 meters (6.6 feet) in diameter. In appearance alone, RPC-696 is not overtly noteworthy. The most visually distinct feature of the tree is its wild, decurrent[[footnote]]Extending downwards; branching limbs frequently attached to other branching limbs rather than a trunk or stem.[[/footnote]] branches wholly unlike that of many modern cultivars, which is only notable to horticulturists, and barely visible "heart-shaped imprints"[[footnote]]Shapes similar to an ideographic representation of a heart, as in Unicode: ❤. Several shapes reportedly resembling human or animal hearts were observed during the initial investigation, however as of ██/██/████, no similar imprints have since been observed.[[/footnote]] spread over most of the trunk between one and two meters (3.3 and 6.6 feet) from the ground. These imprints are suspected to be traces of healed-over scars generated by civilian activity. No concrete origin of these imprints has been determined; it has also been argued that they occur naturally on RPC-696's surface to function as a lure. 

Based on the outcome of an attempted incineration of bark scrapings taken prior to containment, RPC-696 may be unusually flame retardant. This property is anticipated to share common origin with its apparent ability to absorb fluids more quickly and thoroughly than typical trees, even though its bark. Such absorption would imply that the tree's whole husk was made up of living or fibrous tissue. As of ██/██/████ this assessment has not yet been tested. Planned research initiatives on the subject were cancelled due to the somewhat precarious nature of RPC-696's containment. 

RPC-696 appears to derive sustenance from animal fluids. RPC-696 does not appear to need sunlight or water to grow, unlike most plants.
//At least// when exposed to the bodily fluids of an animal or animals, RPC-696 is capable of limited motion and remarkable rapid growth. Any practical limits on these capabilities have not been yet been determined. Going forward, this particular factor is anticipated to be the deciding element in any future re-classifications of RPC-696's lethality or containment indices. It is imperative that any qualified personnel that observes RPC-696 growing or moving in a way that exceeds expected bounds report the incident immediately to the nearest accessible site, or failing this the first available MST. Any such request should be made as a ██████ priority alert.[[/div]]

[[collapsible show="[Access File: Anomalous Behavior]" hide="[Close]" hideLocation="both"]]
[[div style="background-color: #000000; border-left: 8px solid #777; border-top: 4px solid #777; border-bottom: 1px solid #777; border-right: 1px solid #777; margin: 5; padding: 7px;"]] 
Fashioned "the Paramour's Poplar," RPC-696 has been a famous matchmaking aide for ███ years. RPC-696 is particularly popular with those already in unsteady or hostile relationships. Historically, prospective lovers would couch letters in its branches; these letters would demand whoever read it meet for a tryst beneath the tree's low branches. 

Over the years, no less than four lone operatives investigated these urban legends. Unanimously it was determined that they were baseless assertions, or at least that the effects were non-anomalous.
█████ ████████ █████████ ███ █████████ from the office of █████████████████, statistical analysis caught a previously unnoticed trend: of the four independent operatives who investigated RPC-696, each had untimely and undesired break-ups or divorces. This was the case even with the two in engaged in Authority-enforced cover-up relationships. Operative █████████, the only one of the four with unguided international travel clearance, made several return trips to a town in immediate proximity to RPC-696. The justification for these trips was determined to be falsified, which was the first such infraction on █████████'s report.
In a follow-up investigation, it was revealed that Operative █████████, who had been in a relatively stable homosexual relationship, was in fact taking these trips to meet a female lover under the branches of RPC-696. This sudden tryst occurred despite █████████ having, at no point, expressed or demonstrated any interest in the opposite sex. 

Psychosomatic profiling found none of the expected neurological markers associated with such a dramatic change in sexual proclivities. When interrogated about these affairs, Operative █████████ had no explanation for their sudden change in sexuality other than, to quote, "finding true love".
During containment it was confirmed that via unknown means[[footnote]]Pheromones, electromagnetic signals, and aural waves were eliminated as possible explanations via testing during the Paramour Garden phase.[[/footnote]], proximity to RPC-696 induces unusual, and often wanton, sexual acts that almost universally result in new couples. Experiments with staff and CSD personnel deliberately exposed to RPC-696's influence revealed that this seemingly random compulsion in fact had components of both Emotional and Mind Control mental hazards. 

On discovery of Operative █████████'s sudden change in proclivities it was suspected that RPC-696's anomalous coercion generated exclusively heterosexual relationships; however, on review, numerous reports of homosexual relationships were obtained from the nearby populace and later corroborated. Building on those reports, several spontaneous and anomalous homosexual relationships were confirmed and recorded during the Paramour Garden phase, making a heterosexual coupling a non-essential component for RPC-696's coercion to take effect. The specific circumstances needed to generate, or prevent, a RPC-696 event were further complicated by the fact that all surviving relationships spawned by RPC-696 involve at least one male participant. This apparent discrimination in relationship composition is suspected to be evidence for RPC-696's sentient status and specifically motivated by RPC-696's base utilization of █████ for sustenance. The mechanisms used in determining which people are paired and their sexes are unclear.

RPC-696's anomalous effect of generating new relationships has been determined to have at least two components. If a subject has left a letter in RPC-696's branches, when any individual makes contact with that letter, both parties feel a compulsion to enter and remain within sight of RPC-696, or secondarily to enter under its branches at a coordinated time that ranges from three hours to six weeks of the event. Secondarily, any individual who has seen RPC-696 in person within a proximity of at least █ kilometers[[footnote]]Initially suspected to be 'still within eyeshot of RPC-696', this number has increased continuously over time.[[/footnote]] can be spontaneously coerced into a relationship, most frequently when RPC-696 has not seen a tryst within █ days. When an afflicted subject is deprived of their ability to approach or arrive at the coordinated time spontaneously arranged, they will became violent, often to the point of recklessness. This violence appears to be the direct result of a mind control class hazard. 

During testing, as afflicted subjects were forcibly moved farther away from RPC-696, in raw distance[[footnote]]Number of obstacles, width of obstacles, quantity of mass placed between the afflicted subject and the RPC-696, and duration kept out of line of sight all had no apparent impact on the degree of violent compulsion in any experiment; adding distance in any direction did.[[/footnote]], their violent tendency diminished rapidly, eventually fading entirely at ██ kilometers. The second component of RPC-696's anomalous effect, a purely emotional longing for a new partner and desire to visit RPC-696 at a point in the future, did not fade at any distance. Interviews with civilians who experienced a RPC-696 event reveal such feelings even fifty to sixty years later, with some making regular visits. A test with a pair CSD personnel forced into a RPC-696 event revealed that, even when amnesticized this emotional desire remained; when put into solitary confinement at site ████ and asked to point to where their 'ideal vacation' would be, both personnel pointed within meters of RPC-696, an occurrence that was rated to have a likelihood lower than █████%. While this emotional hazard is manageable with attention, based on all tests, it is impossible to remove.
Both of RPC-696's coercive effects were observed even in individuals already in relationships who reported total satisfaction with their circumstances.

When exposed in a similar way, individuals with no recent romantic or sexual relationship frequently, but not always, demonstrated a sudden longing to enter a new relationship; they also demonstrated the inexplicable violent tendencies when restrained. The remainder of individuals with no recent romantic or sexual relationships appear to be totally immune to both RPC-696's Emotional and Mind Control hazards. Dr. █████'s own seeming immunity[[footnote]]The probable cause of this immunity has since been identified following the Paramour Garden incident. At the direct request of Dr. █████ and allegedly to 'ensure incoming personnel take RPC-696's containment seriously', this expected cause is listed in Addendum 1.4 via interview and nowhere else in RPC-696's immediate documentation.[[/footnote]] was the motive for their initial promotion to the position of Paramour Gardens facility public face and director. 

The final tests before the the Paramour Garden Incident were devised to deprive RPC-696 of visitors for escalating periods of time. This was intended to gain an understanding of its feeding patterns and the upper limits of its coercive range. During these tests, a strong Tychokinetic hazard was identified. If even only a single person in a █ kilometer radius of the tree had at any point placed a letter on its branches,  then without fail they would attempt to enter the facility. Eventually this impulse became so strong it extended even to some people who had merely laid eyes on RPC-696. Multiple civilians successfully bypassed entire task forces in what was ███████████ to ███████████████ █████████ ████, or in essence, virtually impossible. 

When totally deprived of animal fluids for a duration exceeding eighteen hours, RPC-696 demonstrates new and potentially hostile anomalous effects. While it does not appear to be the result of deliberate animosity toward humans[[footnote]]No danger is observed when RPC-696 is allowed to attain fluids at a rate equivalent to that it typically amassed prior to being placed in containment //or// if it is fed fluids in ratios proportional to that typical yield.[[/footnote]], these hazards could lead to mass loss of life if left unchecked. RPC-696 is strongly suspected to be a Sentient hazard; there is presently no body of evidence for escalation to Sapient status.

[[div style="background-color: #000000; border-left: 8px solid #777; border-top: 4px solid #777; border-bottom: 1px solid #777; border-right: 1px solid #777; margin: 5; padding: 7px;"]]
* Avoid physical contact with RPC-696 at all times[[footnote]]While it is unclear whether contact with RPC-696 encourages anomalous effects, until more effective containment protocols have been developed, ████████████████████ has deemed it too risky to make regular or irregular contact of any kind.[[/footnote]]. Make note of any contact with RPC-696 and the extenuating circumstances leading to that contact at first opportunity.
* Avoid physical contact with other personnel at all times. Make note of any contact with other personnel and the extenuating circumstances leading to that contact at first opportunity.
* If you have any unreported or otherwise unaccounted for preexisting relationship when you are ordered to relocate to the facility premises, you must describe these relationships in detail to the nearest qualified personnel and report to site █████████ for reprimand and immediate transfer.
* If you are approached by one or more potential romantic partners inexplicably while working on site, describe this encounter or these encounters to the nearest qualified personnel in detail and report to site █████████ for immediate transfer.
* If you feel sudden or pervasive romantic feelings toward any individual within a 5 kilometer radius of the RPC-696 facility, describe these feelings in detail to the nearest qualified personnel and report to site █████████ for immediate transfer.
* If you find yourself near or within the facility premises after transfer due to any of the aforementioned risk conditions, alert all available Level 4 or higher authorities, the current facility manager (as of ██/██/████ still Dr. █████), as well as all available staff. Once the Authority has been informed, you must terminate yourself at first opportunity.

> When contacted, the Descendants of Yggdrasil have denied any involvement in the incident, as well as disavowed ████████████████ according to descriptions of his actions. They followed this disavowal with offers to "care for" RPC-696 if containment proved "too specialized" for the Authority. While mass murder is not in keeping with their typical mode of operation, based on circumstantial evidence, I believe that the SoY - or perhaps, rogue elements within it - were in critically involved.
> ██████████████████████████████████. This is concerning, as containment is already tenuous without external threat. We, of Monast Garden, are not typically affected by the neurological hazards RPC-696 presents. This is by design. At the same time, the tree is fickle. It is impossible to tell when it is "taking offense" or "taking action" in the way you might be able to with a fauna or humanoid anomaly, and it is not as rigorous and predictable as most of the inanimate. Experiments using Monast Garden in care of ██████████████ have had superior containment outcomes to RPC-696, the very anomaly we were organized around. It seems to grow ever more demanding, in real time, even as we study it and contain it.
> 
> Worryingly, based on regular height and branch length measurements, RPC-696 had a period of growth following the Paramour Garden Incident, which was apparently unprecedented, and has not been matched since. That observation is made while taking into account the regular application of the "specialized recipe," which marks this as a clear case of anomalous growth. RPC-696 feeds on ████████████████ as much as █████ and ██. Consequently, when functional testing was resumed, it was determined qualitatively that the intensity of the neurological hazards had //increased// after this period coinciding with the growth, perhaps proportionally (exponentially?) to the increase in size of the tree. 
> Attempts to prune the Poplar have seen mixed results. An attempt to prune back a bough appeared to pass safely, but local statisticians observed that the rate of sexual assault increased by three standard deviations in a ██ kilometer radius, a deviation that lasted four months before gradually returning to the baseline. This event cannot be demonstrably linked to RPC-696, but it does not by any means appear to be a coincidence, either. It paints a grim picture of the potency of RPC-696, as well as the Authority's ability to contain it using a "clear-cut" approach. As designated HSLR, I strongly recommend maintaining the current designation and procedure, but I would not call this unsteady state a "successful containment".
> 
> On that note, were another incident on the order of magnitude of Paramour Garden to occur, RPC-696 would be impossible for the Authority to contain by any reasonable means without dramatic consequences. In a situation like that, enlisting the help of, or conscripting, more specialized personnel - as in the case of the Descendants of Yggdrasil -  would in fact be an effective solution. Perhaps the only effective solution.
> 
> Given the subject of this particular investigation, one is forced to ask: 
> //Was that the plan?//
