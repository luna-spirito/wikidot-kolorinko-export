**Registered Phenomena Code:** 441

**Object Class:** Neutralized (formerly Beta-Yellow)

[[include component:hazards psychotronic=show | sentient=show | extra-dimensional=show | tychokinetic=show | immeasurable=show | temporal=show | info-hazard=show | mind-regression=show]]

**Revised Containment Protocols:** All related information to RPC-441 has been reclassified to Level 5 access, including all related source material to RPC-441's history. Due to info-hazard concerns, RPC-441's previous and dated locations have been erased from the database at the directions of the Directorate. 

As of April 23, 2006, RPC-441 has been considered Neutralized. 

[[collapsible show="+ Containment Revision 441-01" hide="- Close File"]]

> **Containment Protocols (██/██/████):** RPC-441 is to be contained at its present location with security supervision managed by Mobile Specialized Team Delta-1. 
> 
> To protect the sensitive nature of RPC-441, its whereabouts are to be strictly under a need-to-know basis. Personnel involved in the research and analysis are to be kept under constant surveillance on a bi-monthly basis.  
> 
> Personnel who have been discovered to remove source materials from RPC-441 without authorization, or act in an unscrupulous behavior, are to be reported and disicplined accordingly. 
> 
> Furthermore, RPC-441 is to not be entered unless clearance is explicitly granted by RPC-441-1, or a person with Level 5 clearance. Failure to abide by these procedures will result in amnesticization and departmental transfer.

**Description:** RPC-441 is a spatial anomaly that takes the form of a library, speculated to be located in a pocket universe. The dimension's entrance has been recorded to shift and appear at random locations, though the entrance has since been permanent at [DATA EXPUNGED], since 1964. 

RPC-441's interior appears to be physically similar to that of the Strahov Monastery in Prague, Czech Republic. While RPC-441 is within the confines of a pocket dimension, the spacetime is perceived to be affected by a time distortion, meaning that time moves at an accelerating pace. Various observations conducted revealed that a minute passing by is the equivalent to 12 hours outside of the dimension.

It is encompassed by long hallways and large wooden shelves, measured to be at 8 meters tall, that contain various scriptures and other materials. These materials in particular are considered "lost materials" from our reality, which include ancient Chinese texts, Biblical text, the Gospel of Eve, and much more. It's speculated that RPC-441 houses thousands of these lost materials that date back to 285 B.C, the oldest recorded date that it may have originated.[[footnote]]A few architectural notes provided by RPC-441-1 revealed that RPC-441 has undergone numerous changes in the past, some of which have hinted Egyptian origin, specifically the Ptolemaic Kingdom. Further notations of the designs may have further hinted that RPC-441 was an offshoot of the Library of Alexandria, though this is merely a hypothesis.[[/footnote]]

In an unpredictable manner, RPC-441 undergoes an event where its extensive contents would be manipulated by a telekinetic force of unknown source. During this event, said contents will be removed from shelves, transferred across the library and re-categorized and sorted in different shelves. The purpose of this event is unknown. 

Current measurements appear to exceed over the limited dimensions of the host location, which has been determined at about 5km^^2^^, suggesting that RPC-441 does not follow Euclidean geometry. When analyzed for ACS levels, the results for RPC-441's levels were determined to be [REDACTED]. With results inconclusive, it was concluded that the space may be infinite. 

RPC-441-1 is a sapient humanoid that appears to be the pocket dimension's only inhabitant. Not much is known about RPC-441-1, but it is known that RPC-441-1 appears to have originated from this reality. They were manifested by RPC-441 to be a gatekeeper of the place and its materials, though how RPC-441 selects its instances remains unspecified.[[footnote]]Further understanding of RPC-441 showed that previous RPC-441-1 instances from the past had a commonality of academia background, mostly in Philosophy and Epistemology. See Addendum 441.02 for further details.[[/footnote]] RPC-441-1 does not display any anomalous properties at first glance, but various observations have hinted that they may display psychokinetic properties, ranging from telekinetic to telepathic. 

While RPC-441-1 is usually docile, subjects who are deemed a threat or perceived to be a threat to RPC-441 will be forcefully removed by RPC-441-1. Subjects who've been forcefully removed do not sustain any physical or psychological damage, though they may sustain minor bruises. 

Materials brought outside of RPC-441 will cause the material to combust and disintegrate within a matter of seconds. However, the flames do not appear to spread onto the carrier or the environment around it, suggesting that the flame only manifests on materials brought from the pocket dimension. Further analysis revealed that the materials are cohesively reactive once environmentally exposed to the change of ACS levels in our reality. In addition, the materials burnt and disintegrated reemerged within RPC-441. 

[[include component:image-block name= http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-441/441-2.jpg| width=280px|caption= **Fig 1.1.** Lost material, dated in 200 B.C.]]

In rare occurrences, when a subject intentionally tears or destroys a material, subjects will experience a variety of illusions that may lead to neurodevelopmental disorder.[[footnote]]The disorder in-pairs with various other disorders and affects the development of the nervous system, leading to abnormal brain function which may affect emotion, learning ability, self-control, and memory.[[/footnote]] 

Since its discovery and initial containment, RPC-441-1 has been reluctant to provide or allow any materials to be removed from the premise. Negotiations between the appointed liaison, Researcher Kelsey Fisher, and RPC-441-1 have allowed the entry of Authority personnel into RPC-441. --Entry by personnel was restricted only to researchers with high security clearance.-- Following an incident, RPC-441-1 discontinued any entry by outside entities unless permitted by the Directorate. (See Addendum 441.04)

**Addendum 441.01:** Discovery

In July 9, 1958, the Swedish branch discovered a dimensional gateway, designated as RPC-441, during the construction of a new site facility at [REDACTED], Sweden. Construction was halted as a result and containment procedures were implemented the following day. Various field analysis were conducted over the weeks, but by July 26, RPC-441 had dematerialized and was declared inactive. Due to the circumstances of RPC-441's initial manifestation and neutralization, the site's construction was delayed for a seven month period. 

In 1964, an incident occurred at Site-███, Italy, when RPC-441 appeared at a subsection of the facility. The subsection was initially evacuated at the orders of the Site Director, though security presence was maintained at a high-level during the anomaly's manifestation. An unidentified humanoid appeared, designated as RPC-441-1, from the aperture and made contact with an Authority liaison, prompting negotiations for a permanent containment settlement. 

Containment was discussed with a Directorate representative, and approved for RPC-441 to be permanently contained at Site-███. Following this incident, RPC-441 and RPC-441-1 was designated soon afterwards.

**Addendum 441.03:** Interview with RPC-441-1 

Dr. Simmons Baker, at the orders of Site Management, was tasked to conduct an interview to gather background information about RPC-441 and RPC-441-1. Interview was commenced on [REDACTED] with minimal security presence. 

The following is an interview transcript between Dr. Baker and RPC-441-1. 

[[collapsible show="+ Attachment 441-02.1" hide="- Close File"]]

[[div class="paragraph1"]]

**Interviewer:** Dr. Simmons Baker, Office of Analysis and Science 

**Interviewee:** RPC-441-1

-----

[BEGIN LOG]

**Dr. Baker:** How are we doing today?

**RPC-441-1:** Oh, I'm doing well. Shall we proceed? 

**Dr. Baker:** Yes, indeed. Now, again, you're not obligated to be here as my colleagues have stated-

**RPC-441-1:** Please, I insist. 

**Dr. Baker:** If you say so. So, what can you tell me about RPC-441? 

**RPC-441-1:** In what way?

**Dr. Baker:** Background, basically. Like, when did it first manifest? Why did it take the form of a library?- those kinds specifically. 

**RPC-441-1:** I really can't say much for obvious reasons. But what I can say is that the place been around for a long time. Uh, the place values history and knowledge as I do, and I guess it selected me for that common reason. 

**Dr. Baker:** Have you ever directly communicated with RPC-441?

**RPC-441-1:** Not in words, no. Alexius and I have communicated through sentences, which appear on a book that is located in the place's altar stand. 

**Dr. Baker:** (//Writes//) Is that who you refer to personally, or that a given nickname?

**RPC-441-1:** Given. 

**Dr. Baker:** Do you know why they prefer that?

**RPC-441-1:** Not quite. I like to think it's a reference to Alexander the Great. Supposedly, he and ...

**Dr. Baker:** He and what?

**RPC-441-1:** (//Pauses, visibly uncomfortable//) ... No-nothing. Let's move on.

**Dr. Baker:** Uh, alright? How about you? What can you tell me about yourself?

**RPC-441-1:** Nothing much. I'm a former teacher and knowledgeable in a lot of languages.

**Dr. Baker:** Multilingual. Interesting, and what language do you speak?

**RPC-441-1:** (//In German//) A lot, but I like German. 

**Dr. Baker:** Ah, German. Anything else? 

**RPC-441-1:** Well. Let's just say many, since I'll be taking up most of your time if I do name them all.

**Dr. Baker:** Oh, no no. Let's not. 

//Dr. Baker writes down a few notations, flickering through a few pages.//

**Dr. Baker:** I actually want to discuss your first encounter with RPC-441 if that is okay with you?

**RPC-441-1:** I'm sorry. I can't.

**Dr. Baker:** Can I ask why?-

**RPC-441-1:** I have other things to do if you don't mind. 

**Dr. Baker:** Uh, of course. That's fine, but may we may have to ask you again when we do call you.

**RPC-441-1:** That is fine, thank you. 

[END LOG]

**Addendum 441.4:** Incident 08/16/████

On August 19, ████, Senior Researcher Markus Kovalyov entered RPC-441, accompanied by his associates and a security detail. Researcher Kovalyov was reviewing materials at the Russo-Asia category for most of his time until he was called to return back after an hour. He took two books from the shelves as he was about to exit, but was stopped by RPC-441-1, who inquired him why he took the books. Researcher Kovalyov told RPC-441-1 that he was authorized by Site Management to remove the books from the premise, after which RPC-441-1 took the books away from him and insisted that he leave. 

An argument occurred during this time which resulted in Researcher Kovalyov to physically assault RPC-441-1. The supervising security detail intervened and removed Researcher Kovalyov from RPC-441. However, when Researcher Kovalyov was removed from RPC-441, he experienced several nosebleeds and eventually fell into a coma within the hour.
