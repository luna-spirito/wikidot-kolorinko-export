**Registered Phenomena Code:** 438

**Object Class:** Beta-Red (Utility)

**Hazard Types:** Aquatic Hazard, Toxic Hazard, Memory-Alteration Hazard, Mind-Regression Hazard, Extra-Dimensional Hazard.

-----

**Containment Protocols:** Black Site-438 has been built on the now-deserted town of Archaia Feneos, Corinthia, Greece. Black Site-438 is to be used for the manufacturing and exportation of amnestic products to Authority assets around the globe.[[footnote]] For more information, refer to: "Manufacturing Process"[[/footnote]]

Neighboring settlements are made to believe that Archaia Feneos has been deserted due to a massive leakage of toxic waste into the town's water supply, becoming too dangerous for human hospitability. Unauthorized individuals attempting to enter Archaia Feneos are to be detained, questioned and amnesticized.

Access to the lake is only permitted to MST X-Ray-11 ("Dream Catchers"). Any individuals attempting to enter the lake without permission are to be shot on sight. All unauthorized individuals that enter the lake are to be considered unrecoverable. On the rare occasion that individuals manage to escape the lake by their own volition, they are to be captured and euthanized.

-----

**Description:** RPC-438 is a high-magnitude memotic ground[[footnote]] A fairly recent discovery in the field of Memotics, commonly known as "Memotic Dumps" [[/footnote]] residing at the floor of lake Doxa in Archaia Feneos, Corinthia, Greece. Memotic Dumps like RPC-438 act as pseudo-singularities, allowing for the passive entry of memotic vectors into the physical space. In this case, vectors will mix with the water, toxic waste and organic remains in the lake to form RPC-438-1.

[[include component:image-enlarge name= http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/fragment:lethium-2/lethium1.png|caption=The currently accepted version of RPC-438-1.]]

RPC-438-1, or otherwise known as Lethium,[[footnote]] From the Greek word //Lethe//, meaning "to forget", and in reference to one of the five rivers of the Greek underworld.[[/footnote]] is the result of said chemical reaction, that being intangible information converting into a physical material by merit of its transition between the two planes, as they both meet at the entryway of RPC-438. Lethium, in its non-physical form, is believed to be what makes up most of the chemistry inside the Infoplane. RPC-438-1 is an element of unnatural creation, found only as the result of a selected number of Memotic Dumps, being designated as both a heavy metal (for its atomic weight of 280.21), and as a metalloid (for its ability to easily bond with organic elements such as carbon).[[footnote]] Natural lethium from the lake will refuse to bond with any element that is not carbon and without certain types of catalysts. See: "Lethium's Reaction to Organic Material."[[/footnote]] RPC-438-1 has a melting point of 28 °C, and easily filters through skin, natural or synthetic fabrics, and elements possessing weak atomic bonds.

Memotic Dumps similar to RPC-438 can be found in several places around the world, although in far smaller proportions. Lethium secreted by each Memotic Dump is drastically different in composition and reactivity, while sharing other basic properties. RPC-438 is the only Lethium deposit of sufficient concentration to enable filtration, harvesting and analysis.

The only way to remove pure Lethium from the lake is with the use of an organic host, as attempts to remove it by common methods will result in the element dispersing and returning to the Infoplane. The possible use of artificial memotic grounds for its retrieval is undergoing research.

On February 11th, 1991, the local authorities of Archaia Feneos were informed of several bodies floating in the nearby //lake Doxa//. Authority agents stationed in Greece were quickly informed of the occurrence by the government due to the unusual characteristics of the event.

At their arrival, seven corpses were found floating in the lake affected by RPC-438. The lake was found to be severely contaminated as the result of mass toxic waste dump by local factories.

It is theorized the deaths resulted in a sudden release of a large amount of memotic scalars into the Infoplane; when combined with a low stability zone, and adequate chemical environment, created a stable Memotic Dump in the lake and the perfect conditions for the manifestation of Lethium. Autopsy of the teenagers revealed a compound of the element that was, at the time, thought to be impossible to remove from the lake.

After study of the compound ended, jurisdiction of the anomaly was handed to Black Site with the approval of GD-WEST.

++ Trilethium Carbide
[[/=]]
-----
Trilethium carbide[[footnote]]Lh,,3,,C,,2,,[[/footnote]] is the name given to the compound created when Lethium interacts with organic materials. More specifically, those found in sentient beings.

[[=]]
@@  Lh          Lh        Lh@@
@@      \      /      \    /     @@
@@        C             C        @@
[[/=]]

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/fragment:lethium-2/acetyl_chloride_25ml.png|caption=A sample of Trilethium carbide with its distinctive lack of color|align=right]]

Lethium is never treated as a foreign agent by human organisms, allowing its free circulation through the bloodstream after ingestion or skin absorption. Once there, Lethium will make its way through the body until it reaches the brain.
 
Upon reaching the brain, a chemical process will begin between Lethium and carbon, with neurons and their respective electric signals acting as a catalyst during the process.[[footnote]] The study of Lethium reactions inside the brain has led to the theory that memotic scalars are created inside the brain by methods of Intra-Dimensional chemical reactions, where elements exclusive to the Infoplane react with those in the brain, acting as a memotic ground for both memotic quanta and the aforementioned elements.[[/footnote]] This process can take from two hours to one week to finish, depending on the brain activity of the subject, with lower activity taking far less time to complete.
 
This reaction will result in the creation of Trilethium bicarbonate. The quantity produced depends on the size of the subject. Trilethium carbide, unlike pure Lethium, will not demanifest into the Infoplane once removed from the body. This is believed to be caused by its bond with carbon, which now ties it to the physical space in a chemical sense.

It is to be noted that this reaction only occurs in the brain of living organisms; as such, any organic material lacking the aforementioned, such as meat, plants, and fungi, will not react with Lethium.

> **Stage 1: Fabrication of Trilethium carbide**
> 
> This process is undertaken monthly for Authority-wide supply usage. One batch of stockpiled material for manufacturing is to be kept at all times in case of delayed or halted production.
> 
> While this compound can be created with the use of livestock and other animals, in 90% of cases, the Trilethium carbide created by this method is highly unstable when taken outside the lake, or can lead to undesired or dangerous effects when distilled. As such, the only completely reliable material that can be used in manufacturing has been deemed the following:
> 
> * One (1) Unconscious Human Adult.[[footnote]] As long as all higher brain activities are present, age is not a factor. [[/footnote]]
> -----
> The subject, commonly an elderly adult, is to be put into an induced coma. Once completely unconscious, MST X-Ray-11 ("Dream Catchers") is to carry the subject into the lake and submerge it for no more than 20 seconds, after which it is returned to the facility.
> 
> Once inside, the subject is left to rest in a bed until leakage of trilethium bicarbonate (denoted by a colorless liquid coming out of the nose) starts occurring. At this point the subject is considered deceased, as the majority of its brain has been consumed by the reaction.
> 
> The subject is to be hung inverted above a collecting pool. A notch is cut across the parietal and frontal skull bone, allowing for the compound to drain.

> **Stage 2: Distillation into Pure Lethium:**
> 
> Materials needed:
> 
> * 20grs of Titanium-50.
> 
> -----
> 
> After all Trilethium carbide has been gathered and the body disposed of, the resulting material is to be taken into the distillation room. There, the compound will be placed into the Finkleton Chamber[[footnote]] Named after its creator, Dr. Damian Finkleton. It is one of the most advanced artificial memotic grounds ever devised, with several magnetic generators and high-speed atom catapults built inside it.[[/footnote]] where the trilethium carbide will be bombarded with Titanium-50 in order to separate it from bonded carbon. It is then magnetically captured, while the artificial memotic ground prevents it from dissipating into the info-plane.
> 
> 20 grams of Titanium-50 are sufficient to produce six batches of pure Lethium, with the process itself taking 34 hours to create two liters of the useable Lethium.
> 
> Normally, every month's batch produces approximately 14 liters, which are then transported to the amnestic assembler.

> **Stage 3: Amnestic Manufacturing:**
> 
> Materials needed:
> 
> * Twice as much bromine as Lethium produced in the last stage.
> 
> -----
> 
> Bromine will be introduced into the Finkleton Chamber to bond with lethium, creating Lethium Dibromide,[[footnote]] LhBr,,2,, [[/footnote]] making its stable existence outside of the artificial Memotic Ground possible again.
> 
> [[=]]

> 
> From this point forward the production is very simple; to produce pill-form amnestics, half will be filled with the recently created compound while the other half will contain a simple hydrocarbon. Once inside the body, the hydrocarbon and bromine molecules will violently react with each other, freeing the Lethium from the compound and allowing it to travel to the brain, where due to the lack of material for a proper reaction, will vanish into the info-plane, taking with it several memotic scalars connected to the subject's brain during the process.[[footnote]] The amount of which is determined by the hydrocarbon/monolethium dibromine ratio, as long term memory requires more Lethium to be targeted.
