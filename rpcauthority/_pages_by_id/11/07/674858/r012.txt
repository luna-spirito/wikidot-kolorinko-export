**Containment Protocols:** All instances of RPC-298 are to be kept in an airtight, temperature-controlled, anomalous-objects containment locker at a constant temperature of 2°C at [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/site-002 Site-002]. Although occurrences of RPC-298 instances melting have proven non-hazardous, such an event is to be considered a containment breach and treated as such. In such an occurrence, the temperature of RPC-298's containment locker is to be brought down to -10°C and is only to be reset with the validation of at least --two personnel with level 2 security clearance or higher-- one Head Researcher. --All testing to be done involving RPC-298 instances must first be approved by at least two personnel with level 2 security clearances or higher.-- 

As of ██/██/20██, all testing to be done involving RPC-298 must first be approved by at least one Head Researcher and must be carried out in reinforced testing chambers only (See Incident Log-298-2).   

[[include component:image-block-static name= http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-298/rpc298.png|width= 300px|caption= A pack of RPC-298-A instances.]]

**Description:** RPC-298 is the designation given to ready-to-eat gelatin products by [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/groups-of-interest#amazing-co Amazing! Co.] under the guise of Kraft Foods Jell-O[[footnote]]Save for the name being misspelled as "Jello-O".[[/footnote]], with the words "It never ends!" written on the box the instances were discovered in. 

The box contains thirty-eight (38) packs of ready-to-eat gelatin products, produced in four (4) different flavors: Strawberry (RPC-298-A), Madagascan Vanilla (RPC-298-B), Golden Pineapple (RPC-298-C) and Crispy Cola (RPC-298-D), all provided with exclusive designations. 

Once an entire instance of RPC-298 is consumed by a subject belonging to the Hominidae[[footnote]]Collective designation for greater primates, including but not limited to Chimpanzees and Homo Sapiens.[[/footnote]] family, its anomalous effects will manifest in the gradual, irreversible transmutation of the subject, as shown by the script tabulate below:

||||||~ Transmutation process||
||~ Minutes||~ Notes||
||5 Minutes|| (Stage 1) Subject experiences a slight case of fever, with the subject's core temperature elevating to 38°C. || 
||7 Minutes|| (Stage 2) Subject's core temperature will dramatically increase, reaching temperatures of 50-55°C. The subject also loses its inherent capability to breathe due to respiratory failure. || 
||10 Minutes || (Stage 3) Subject undergoes necrosis at this stage due to asphyxiation and hyperthermia, reaching a core temperature of 100°C. Subject's skeletal structure rapidly transforms at this stage, the sudden increase in the subject's body temperature gelling the collagen reserves. The resulting gel will begin to spread to and contaminate tissue, incapable of gelling by, anomalous means. || 
||10-15 Minutes|| (Stage 4) Subject is now fully transformed, all bodily tissue being converted to the anomalous gel substance. ||
|| +15 Minutes|| (Stage 5) Collagen bonds within the gel mass will rapidly begin to deteriorate, resulting in a horizontal spread of the gel mass. This process cannot be prevented by any means and all attempts to preserve un-deteriorated gel masses thus far have proven unsuccessful. ||

**Discovery:** RPC-298 was discovered in a residential home in ████████, USA on ██/██/20██ after an EMS call was logged by a suspect who's daughter started suffering from hyperthermia after consuming an instance. The victim was later confirmed deceased mid-transit, which is when she started showing signs of "gelling" and was brought to the attention of the Authority. The caller and EMS team were immediately called in for questioning and administered amnestics directly afterward (See Interview Log-298-1).

[[collapsible show="Addendum 3: Incident Logs+" hide="Access Granted-"]]

**Incident Log-298-1:**  After an exaggerated period spent in the restrooms, the Authority escorts forced their way into Mr. ████'s cubicle to find his deceased body and a photograph of his late daughter palmed in his hand, with the words "Thansk you for your service." written on its back in red marker, and a partially consumed instance of RPC-298. Upon the removal of Mr. ████, his body began to rapidly heat and show late third-stage symptoms of the transmutation process. The Authority escorts were immediately evacuated from the scene and quarantined until deemed uncontaminated. It was later confirmed that Mr. ████  had consumed a small portion of an undocumented instance of RPC-298-A. Authority Researchers observed how despite consuming a portion of RPC-298, Mr. ████ did not complete the transmutation process. Further research and testing into why such an event occurred have been set into place.

**Incident Log-298-2:**  At approximately 20:19, RPC-298-4 entered a state of extreme mental distress, running around the test chamber and beating on the walls and observation screen. RPC-298-4's hair rapidly fell out, revealing that its skin turned translucent. RPC-298-4's upper appendages gradually extended by a meter in length, measuring 163cm in total. Due to RPC-298-4's translucent skin, on-site researchers could conclude that RPC-298-4's skeletal system was then completely absent, save for its maxillary and mandibular sets. RPC-298-4 lay still for approximately 2 minutes. RPC-298-4 then spontaneously leapt from a standstill at the observation screen, resulting in a containment breach and the death of two (2) on-site researchers and four (4) ASF security personnel. After having breached containment for ██ minutes, RPC-298-4 was dispatched and disposed of by means of incineration.
