+++ **Primary Report:**

[[include component:image-block name= http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rcp-4139/4139.png|width=210px|caption= {{Fig 1.0}}: RCP-4139.]]

RCP-4139 denotes a singular member of a hitherto undiscovered anomalous species of extra-dimensional origin, individually designated a Class III Extra-Dimensional traveller/influencer species. RCP-4139 is physiologically similar to //Bathynomus giganteus// (Giant isopod), though almost entirely alien in structure and bodily function. RCP-4139's exoskeleton is of violet pigmentation, however its chest area is notably orange in color. Despite lacking vocal cords, RCP-4139 is fully capable of speech in numerous languages, and its antennae seem to serve as a mental connection to other members of its species. 

RCP-4139's brain is located in its stomach cavity, and has an anomalous connection to every other anomalous entity related to RCP-4139. Through that link, RCP-4139 shares thoughts between every other member of its species; and talking to it is equivalent to speaking with every instance simultaneously- to a degree. Despite this anomalous link, RCP-4139 itself can only hold so much information within its brain at any given time, and interviewers should take this into account if RCP-4139 begins displaying symptoms of this. 

-----
+++ **Auxiliary Report:**

RCP-4139 is contained within an entity holding unit on the 86th floor of Site-397-42. Two onsite Protector-class anomalies are tasked with surveying RCP-4139's unit for any extra-dimensional incursions, though no outwardly anomalous persons are to come into contact with RCP-4139 in order to prevent accidental cross-pollination of potential anomalies. RCP-4139 is provided with nutrient paste meals twice daily, though no furniture is present within the room aside from a steel table.

Within RCP-4139's cell is a lesser anomalous gramophone, set to play 1900s-1920s music upon RCP-4139's request. The reason for this request is unknown, however it has been deemed harmless enough to warrant compliance. Interviews with RCP-4139 are conducted by research staff R. Levine, abbreviated to simply Levine in documentation. Physical testing of RCP-4139 has not been conducted, as to avoid potentially angering the rest of its anomalous species.

If you wish to view the attached file(s), speak to your department(s) overwatch for accessory information.

+++ **Capabilities**

**Processing Capabilities:** 
Significantly anomalous. Noncompliant when attempted cognizant testing.

**Physical Capabilities:** 
Comparable to a small dog, yet arthropodal in nature. 

**Abnormal Capabilities:** 
Aforementioned gestalt consciousness with those of its species in other realities.

**Notable Flaws:** 
Harmful relations with it may spur an inter dimensional invasion of its kin.

-----

Notes: 

Due to RCP-4139's swathes of information regarding other realities, it is worthy of being treated with a certain degree of respect by research staff. Though it will never be a Protector, its utility to our righteous cause shall not go unrewarded.
-----
+++ **Additional Notes**

Interviews are to be conducted whenever convenient to gather information about other realities/timelines. 

**Relations:** 
Good relations with 4139 are deemed integral to avoiding a multidimensional incursion event. 

**Management:** 
Read above.

> 
> **Levine:** ...How many are you?

> **RCP-4139:** Innumerable. Do not be threatened, as your precursors were.

> **RCP-4139:** Shan't. Our palaver draws to an end.

> **Interviewer:** Dr. Levine
> 
> **Foreword:** Interview transcribed by containment staff Jeffrey Kilroy. Notes have been added on discretionary basis.

> 
> **Levine:** You're still with us. That's good.
>

> **Levine:** Oh, why's that?

> **RCP-4139:** You interest us. Your ways are unlike the others we have seen.

> **Levine:** You said we were unique, before.
> 
> **RCP-4139:** Affirmed. This is true; no other world has accomplished your deeds.
> 
> **Levine:** How so?
> 
> **<RCP-4139's pupils dilate once again>**
> 
> **RCP-4139:** The things you do are not to be done by others. You are the logical endpoint of a theoretical concept invented by those too scared to fulfill its parameters.
> 
> **Levine:** Hell's that mean?
> 
> **RCP-4139:** Think. Things from beyond your plane look upon you with naught, save foreboding.
> 
> **Levine:** Seems like your opinion on us has changed.
> 
> **RCP-4139:** We do not have opinions. Only facts based on prior evidence. And we have done research since last we spoke.
> 
> **Levine:** Oh? 
> 
> **RCP-4139:** You seek justice like we seek meaning. You seek power to enforce this justice. 
> 
> **Levine:** Making a lot of assumptions.
> 
> **RCP-4139:** Facts. Trepidation fills us when we think of your future.
> 
> **Levine:** I thought your goal was to know everything.
> 
> **RCP-4139:** Absolute power corrupts absolutely. So often have we sipped from that yolk, only to find it choking us.
> 
> **Levine:** And?
> 
> **RCP-4139:** You are soon to be choked. We can do nothing but warn you.
> 
> **Levine:** What, if you are so wise after all, could be more powerful than the men who kill gods?
> 
> **RCP-4139:** Things who kill men.
> 
> **Levine:** You're making fun of my wording.
> 
> **RCP-4139:** We speak literally, probationer. Take care to what you speak, as we are not a rebel in a cage.
> 
> **Levine:** You sound almost, sour.
> 
> **RCP-4139:** We have now seen your gaol. The thought makes our minds shudder, what you have done to its denizens.
> 
> **Levine:** Thought you met gods?
> 
> **RCP-4139:** Do not act inattentively. You who hears without an attentive ear is deaf.
> 
> **Levine:** Central is only using you for information. You're shooting the messenger here.
> 
> **RCP-4139:** Read the message you carry. 
> 
> **Levine:** How can we possibly be worse than those eldritch tentacle-whores out there, hm?
> 
> **RCP-4139:** The suns have the decency to end the facsimiles they wish to control. The faceless thing acts out of primacy, not sapience. He of the Iskonnyy is punished karmically for his crimes. The library of Uut'L'iithra only harms those who read from it words they were not meant to see. 
> 
> **Levine:** I-
> 
> **RCP-4139:** The order of Cetacea has had their minds bequeathed. The Sarcomere were bred to perform their sins. The Asura of Tripura were punished by Brahma, and lead astray by the Tower. 
> 
> **Levine:** What even is half this shit?
> 
> **RCP-4139:** You ask what makes you worse than they. We cannot tell you without telling you of their past. The Avi fell to gluttony, despite their good will. They of Halberds seek to pluck diamond from the ashes. The red king has lost his sight, and thus his reason. The Nivaldi failed to seek that which would save their corporeality.

>

> 
> **RCP-4139:** Allow us to name those that do.

> 
> **Levine:** You've yet to prove how we're worse than these things.
> 
> **RCP-4139:** You are worse, as you assimilated them. Your righteous path leads only to reciprocation from your universal peers, and the slow descent of entropy.
> 
> **Levine:** Got all that from a prison?
> 
> **RCP-4139:** We saw the nectarium of the war gods. We heard their incestuous cries as your aberrant words forged them away from their natural course.
> 
> **Levine:** How could you have seen that- I'm not even allowed to know what you're talking about!
> 
> **RCP-4139:** Ozymandias knew not the destruction of his kingdom. Gilgamesh did not foresee the whore. It is your blood-borne right to climb the highest peaks, as to fall the farthest.

> **RCP-4139:** You will continue to catechize this one. It will answer as it may. We will no longer divulge in your repugnance.

> **Levine:** ...Do you want anything? Any amenity we have on the site, it's yours.
> 
> **RCP-4139:** Music. You will provide this one with jazz. It enjoys such, and unlike your chafing lords; we permit happiness.
> 
> **<End Log>**
