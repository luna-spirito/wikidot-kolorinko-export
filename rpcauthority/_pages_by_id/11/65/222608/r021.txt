**Containment Protocols:** As RPC-370 and all related phenomena are located solely within Cair Aisling, traditional technology is unable to be utilized for containment purposes.

RPC-370 is self-contained within RPC-370-1[[footnote]] Meaning RPC-370's containment relies on assets unrelated to the Authority. [[/footnote]]. The perimeter of RPC-370-1 is to be guarded by at least 10 ASF personnel at all times. An outpost has been established 34 metres north of RPC-370-1's entrance, where personnel are instructed to reside when researching RPC-370.

**Description:** RPC-370 is a humanoid entity measuring 4.5 metres in height[[footnote]] 3.2 when in its usual slouching stance. [[/footnote]]. Two 1.42 metre long horns, which each resemble that of a male //Ovis//, jut from both sides of the head, which are black in coloration, and are nearly identical to obsidian when viewed on the molecular level[[footnote]] RPC-370 allowed Authority researchers to collect samples of its horns. [[/footnote]]. Its skull is similar to that of a //Megaloceros giganteus//[[footnote]] Irish elk (extinct). [[/footnote]], measuring 0.2 metres across. RPC-370 is covered in a thick layer of grey, matted fur, which has not been observed shedding or growing past 24 centimetres.

While not being particularly agile, RPC-370 has the ability to lift objects weighing more than 2000 kilograms. In addition, RPC-370's stomach houses an anomalous flame, orange light clearly visible within RPC-370's throat and stomach. Embers and smoke are regularly expelled from RPC-370's mouth.

In RPC-370's possession is a large leather cloak, and a walking staff composed of bone. RPC-370 claims that its staff is a dysfunctional "magical catalyst," a "Philosopher's stone" once situated at its tip. The staff is noticeably damaged.

RPC-370 has identified itself as "Malibos Kannocte," the previous ruler of a now-defunct kingdom known as "Great Exaltia." According to RPC-370, the entity belongs to a race of semi-divine bipeds called "Archdragons," which were rendered near-extinct by the Empire during a war which took place approximately 700 years before the Corrupting Wars.

RPC-370-1 is a large sinkhole located 3 kilometres north of the northernmost tip of the Sirencoves, surrounded by the Desert region. Suspended 3 metres below RPC-370-1's mouth is a flat, spacial anomaly, appearing as a wall of spectral fire stretched along the rim of the sinkhole. This anomaly is orange in coloration, and does not interact with most organisms which pass through; personnel are able to enter RPC-370-1 without complication. However, in the event that RPC-370 is within its immediate vicinity, the anomaly will blacken. RPC-370 is unable to exit RPC-370-1, instead coming into contact with the spacial anomaly as if it were a solid object.

Approximately 700 thaumaturgical runes have been carved into the sediment along the spacial anomaly's edges, resembling those found in the Spirelands.

**Discovery:** The Authority learned of RPC-370-1 after ASF personnel were informed by Taur Ingleic, an elven sorcerer working in Thievesport. After more word of a "Dark Lord" was received by locals at OL-Site-CA, Researcher Strauss elected to investigate the area where RPC-370-1 was told to be located.

After travelling to the Sirencoves on horseback, Strauss met an elderly gnomish woman living in the small, relatively unknown village of Esthir. After a short talk, the woman agreed to give Strauss directions to RPC-370-1.

//August 12, 2020//

[...] When I arrived at this so-called "Dark Barrier," I was admittedly surprised at how big it was. It was almost as wide as the diamond mine in Russia, where [REDACTED]. The sinkhole must have spanned two miles on each end. I got off my horse and looked down the hole. I thanked the Lord that Locke told me to bring my rappelling gear, because it must have been a mile deep at least. The sunlight couldn't reach the bottom, and it was //noon//.

The sides were made of jet black brimstone, like the kind of lava stones people dig up in Hawaii. It was smooth, as if someone had cut the hole open with a mechanical drill. Luckily, there was this huge boulder next to the pit that I could fasten my rope to. The small lady told me the wall of fire didn't burn, and I tested it by sticking a twig in it. It didn't burn.

Going down there was absolutely terrifying. I must have descended for a good half hour before my boots touched the fortress. At the bottom of the abyss was this stone castle looking thing, with these gnarly gargoyles on every ledge. Straight out of Dark Crystal, especially when this Skeksis-looking motherfucker came out from the shadows.

The rumors are true. Malibos Kannocte, the Dark Lord of Great Exaltia, pranced up to me from the gate of his submerged castle and introduced itself. It even bowed. It smelled like a decade's worth of piss. Of course I asked him if it was this "dark lord" everyone on the surface was talking about. It affirmed, and invited me inside. I was pleasantly surprised to find out that the place was lit. Smokeless torches hung on the walls, and there were four small [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-433  golems] tidying the place up. It apologized for the "little helpers," as it had apparently made lunch an hour ago. One of the golems waved to me. I sat at the table and Malibos poured me a cup of steaming tea. It was green, so I didn't drink it.

I asked him about why it was down here. It kind of stared into space for a moment before telling me: "For my conquests, the great elven sorcerer Alek the Snake-eyed has imprisoned me here. It's a long story, sonny."

I asked him to elaborate on his species.

"An Archdragon I am. And through black magic, the first Spire Elves turned us into dragons, such foul beasts."

I digress. It eventually asked me who I was, and I told him I was just an explorer who thought it would be interesting to come and check the place out. "No mere explorer has the gall to come here," it replied. "Most are too scared. A strange smell lingers around you."

He kept repeating something about a smell.

I eventually told him "more humans would be stopping by." I hope I wasn't too obvious? But it knows something. Malibos has a secret, and I feel like it would serve us well to know. I'm setting up camp near the boulder. The sinkhole is four miles north of Esthir in the Sirencoves.

Strauss: "Yes. A persistent stuttering problem from this one."

Strauss: "Is... something wrong?"

Strauss: "What do you mean?"

RPC-370: "... Ah, nothing. The old Dark Lord's superstition, I suppose. Carry on with yer questionin'."

Strauss: "Then, why did they call you the 'Dark Lord?' That sounds awfully evil, wouldn't you say?"

RPC-370: "I wouldn't go runnin' yer mouth about me crown, mud-man. I am the Dark Lord, king of Great Exaltia. They called me Dark for t' night sky, which masked the obsidian pillars of its walls. Back in me day I summoned great storms, plagues, with this thing." //RPC-370 waves its staff in the air.// "Y' know what this is made from, laddies?"

RPC-370: "The bone of Veddhor Waxbrog, old king of the humans. And this cloak? The skin of dwarves, elfs, and humans. Crafted with me own sweat and blood when yer kind tried invading Exaltia. I killed your kind before, and I'll do it again if ye got the gall to insult her."

It got awkward after that, so we left. RPC-370 told it would "anticipate our return."
