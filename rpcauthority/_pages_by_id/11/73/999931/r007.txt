**Containment Protocols:** RPC-459 no longer requires permanent containment. Occasionally, RPC-459 will 'visit' Earth, arriving at Site-096, following approval from the Directorate and Site Director Thomas Ridge. Upon arrival, Mobile Specialized Team Whiskey-7 (Space Force) are to escort RPC-459 to research head Dr. Waylon Murke.

A standard humanoid containment cell should be prepared for RPC-459, along with numerous pieces of literature, a painting, and an assortment of films of the snuff[[footnote]] Films in a purported genre of movies in which a person is actually murdered or commits suicide. [[/footnote]] genre, all of which should be rotated each time RPC-459 departs from the Authority's custody.

Occasionally, RPC-459 may request to engage in conversation about an assigned topic. Specialists in said topics may be approved for a 3-hour maximum conversation.

RPC-459 is forbidden from returning to Earth with other members of its race. Should a member of RPC-459's race accompany it to Site-296-49C-Heligoland, MST Whiskey-7 are to utilise a Jessop-Disruptor[[footnote]] A generator capable of disturbing the photons that make up RPC-459's biological structure, restricting it to a state of paralysis until the device is powered down. Created by Dr. Wilson Jessop. [[/footnote]].

------

**Description:** RPC-459 is a sapient, humanoid light construct. RPC-459 will dress in various Victorian-era attire, most notably a suit, top hat, and black leather shoes. RPC-459 maintains a physical presence, but prefers the use of a light-based telekinetic ability to interact with nearby objects.

RPC-459 is capable of creating malleable constructions of light by utilizing nearby light sources. It uses this ability to create attire, preferring to construct humanoid clothing.

RPC-459 possesses the ability to convert any form of organic life into a light construct similar to itself via reconstruction at the atomic level. Such instances are designated RPC-459-1. RPC-459-1 retains its physical shape and consciousness, but will acquire abilities similar to RPC-459.

**Addendum 459.1:** RPC-459 was interviewed by Dr. Murke on █.█.20██ to further understand its motives. This interview is attached below.

[[collapsible show="+ Interview 459.1" hide="- Hide Interview"]]

> **Interviewed:** RPC-459
>
> **Interviewer:** Dr. Murke, RPC-459 Research Head
>  
> **<Begin Log>**
>  
> **Dr. Murke:** Now, what did you say your name was again?
>  
> **RPC-459:** Nemphis.
>  
> **Dr. Murke:** Right. I'd like you to explain your purpose on this planet. Why are you here?
> 
> **RPC-459:** Well, I... I am a part of a collective, so to speak. We are a society of intelligent, creative individuals, and we seek to grow this society. Many inhabitants of this society are creatively gifted individuals, wouldn't you say?
> 
> **Dr. Murke:** I... Yes, I suppose so. But why convert them into these beings?
> 
> **RPC-459:** Allow me to explain, good doctor… Life, biologically, is bound to darkness. But light, you see… It destroys this darkness. It destroys what threatens its existence. I want the lives of those gifted with creativity to continue, just as light does.
> 
> **Dr. Murke:** Hm... Seems rather cruel, does it not?
> 
> **RPC-459:** In what regard?
> 
> **Dr. Murke:** Well, a great many of these creative minds have family and friends. If you take those away, what does that leave them with?
> 
> **RPC-459:** Other organisms have not expressed this sorrow, so I didn't deem it a possibility. I suppose the family of a creative mind would surely inherit such creativity, no?
> 
> **Dr. Murke:** That's not how it works at all. Creativity is not a trait of biology. It is unique to the mind.
> 
> **RPC-459:** ...   
> 
> **Dr. Murke:** I understand. You feel you're being very generous, using your powers to essentially immortalise us, but it can end up causing more pain than if we had just died.
> 
> **RPC-459:** ...
> 
> **Dr. Murke:** I will have site security escort you to your cell now. This interview is over. Thank you for your cooperation, RPC-459.
> 
> **RPC-459:** ... You are welcome, Doctor.
> 
> **<End Log>**
>  
> **Closing Statement:** RPC-459 returned to containment without resistance. Upon questioning, RPC-459 described the interview as "enlightening", and expressed wishes to converse with Dr. Murke further.
[[/collapsible]]

> 
> **Interviewer:** Dr. Murke
>

> 
> **Dr. Murke:** I'm glad to hear it. Where would you be departing to, and how far away is your collective stationed?
> 
> **RPC-459:** [REDACTED]
> 
> **Dr. Murke:** Good. That should be a far enough distance.
> 
> **RPC-459:** ... Dr. Murke, may I make an additional request?
> 
> **Dr. Murke:** Yes, RPC-459?
> 
> **RPC-459:** Should my departure be approved by your higher-ups, would it be possible for me to return to this planet, to explore it further? I feel as if there are many other aspects to human life I have yet to discover, and I am eager to learn more about your race. I... I have never encountered anything like it. I mean, creativity is a broad thing indeed, and every planet has a new form of it, but... The inspiration you draw from death, I... I am afraid that I found it incomprehensible. I feel... Inspired by what you have taught me. I would like to know more. I //know// that there is more to learn.
> 
> **Dr. Murke:** Tell you what, RPC-459; I'll include that in my request, and we'll see.
> 
> **RPC-459:** Thank you, Dr. Murke.
>
