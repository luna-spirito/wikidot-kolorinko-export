**Containment Protocols:** RPC-236 cannot comprehend or interact with most mentally stable humans, leaving research and containment to a specific class of unstable individuals requiring handlers to prevent occult phenomenon breach. RPC-236 may be dealt with via a number of effective methods based on behavior and RPC protocol:

* **Mundane:** Banishment to their plane of existence. This method is reserved for relatively cooperative instances that have become coincidentally trapped in our dimension. Look for signs of fear to outright panic and confusion. Such instances are timid, and will typically ask to be returned home, often the victims of illicit or accidental occult activity. These instances are not often ideal test subjects, due to the extenuating circumstances surrounding their presence here.

* **Curious:** Banishment, or containment. Methods of containment range from entrapping the subject in any means of abstract, two-dimensional media, be they books (with at least one landscape present in the work), a map (does not have to depict an existing location), a computer program within any battery-backed memory device, etc. A second means of containment is soul-binding; Effectively, controlled possession of transigent personnel trained to deal with occult entities. **WARNING! Do not bind more than //one// (1) instance of RPC-236 to any transigent individual!** Such instances are chatty, inquisitive, and somewhat distracted; This implies a lack of planning or intent beyond (usually) exploration. Such instances are //ideal test subjects//, and should not be dissected, due to the likelihood of extended cooperation.

* **Suspicious:** Containment or termination. Such instances are potentially dangerous, and often come in pairs. If one is found, immediately perform a sweep for the second. These instances will exhibit suspicious behavior, atypical to their kind. While it may seem counter-intuitive, look for direct, non-convoluted speech, an aura of sincerity, willingness to make eye contact, and a general calm about their behavior. Contain these instances as soon as possible before determining if dissection and/or termination is the best course of action.

* **Hostile:** Terminate. While RPC-236 is often cowardly and defensive, these instances will be outwardly aggressive and violent. They've come for a reason, and if you witness them in an aggressive state such as this, you (or someone nearby) is probably their target. These instances cannot be trusted even in containment.

Official documentation must be formed with joint cooperation of personnel experienced in handling transigent individuals. Some instances of documentation may be rendered incomprehensible to intransigent personnel, but are retained on recommendation of at least three transigent agents assigned to the case.

Given the non-linear nature of said object classification and the potential risk for occult phenomenon breach, employment of obfuscating and symbolic terminology when referring to various instances is required.

Due to the unclear line of distinction between mental illness and genuine anomaly, psychiatric evaluation is required to distinguish cases of schizophrenia or shared psychosis. Cameras and electronic recording devices cannot reliably capture most subjects, with rare instances easily dismissed as artifact or optical illusion.

**Description:** RPC-236 is a class of liminal beings, existing beyond levels of common perception. It initially appears as an opaque, black vapor, usually only visible through peripheral observation. Upon deeper observation, transigent researchers have described a species of androgynous humanoids, showing no discernible genitalia or mammary glands, smooth blue/violet skin, and a human face with soft, rounded features. Voices are described as warbling, melodic, and high-pitched. They stand roughly one meter in height, often with a tangled mane of coarse, black hair, in its natural state. They possess two large, almond-shaped, yellow eyes, emitting a faint glow, and a pair of broad, rounded spiral horns, which begin above two nodules on the forehead, curve around, and eventually point forward, resembling those of a ram[[footnote]]Instances often coppice their horns to be tall and straight. As well, larger horns indicate a less physically violent lifestyle, as the keratinous objects are somewhat hollow and easily broken. **NOTE: This is //not// an indicator for an individual's threat level**[[/footnote]]. Their hands bear two large, gnarled fingers, and one opposable digit, much like talons, and small, flat feet with two broad, claw-like toes, in a manner similar to a cloven hoof; The skin on the hands and feet appears rough and calloused. Finally of note, they possess an extremely flexible, tapering prehensile tail[[footnote]]RPC-236 is often defensive when inquired about their tail, and may even grow hostile on repeat investigation; Physical contact unadvised. See "Reproductive System" for details[[/footnote]], almost as long as their body, and as thick as an average human arm.

[[collapsible show="+ RPC-236 Reproductive System" hide="- Clearance Level: 1" hideLocation="both"]]
[[==]]
RPC-236 houses its reproductive organs at the base of its tail. The appendage itself functions as a sheath for a hidden, retractable stinger, which serves to breach a victim's heart, allowing RPC-236 to implant 3-7 spherical, white eggs; Substance within described as a viscous, translucent yellow. While suspected to be some form of parasitic reproduction, RPC-236-1 is equally invisible to intransigent individuals, and those capable of observing have stated seen clutches of varying maturity simply disappear when the host dies; Always for reasons unrelated to the eggs, which do not appear to be physically harmful[[footnote]]To this day, no samples of RPC-236-1 in captivity have hatched[[/footnote]].

[[collapsible show="+ Behavior and Social Structure" hide="- Clearance Level: 1" hideLocation="both"]]
[[==]]
Upon first meeting, RPC-236 may appear docile, and even amicable toward humans, but will often require some form of reciprocation to guarantee continued cooperation. It should be noted that such actions should never be referred to as gifts or favors, as RPC-236 will immediately reject them; Insistence could be met with hostility or violence.

Given their personality as a whole, it is inadvisable to believe anything they say; Instances of RPC-236 should be considered compulsive liars. However, this seems to be a defense mechanism, and not actively malicious, as RPC-236 is inherently reclusive and secretive, only ever congregating in small, tribal clans. Witnesses claim such groups will engage in ritual cattle mutilation, tearing the animals apart with their talons, or crude, cutting implements. This action appears to bear religious or social significance, as they do not process or consume the cattle in any way.

RPC-236 shows a natural affinity for children, often forming bonds with certain individuals, well into adulthood; Classified RPC-236-2. While the lasting psychological effects of this are still debated, it should be noted that no instance of this union has ever displayed signs of RPC-236-1. Even those expressing annoyance or outright contempt toward adolescents will not harm them, claiming a maternal, primordial deity (or a giant sea serpent; reports vary) would punish them for such actions.

[[==]]
Juan Vasquez was a senior at █████████ High School during the time of the █████████ massacre. Vasquez came from a Catholic family, claiming on several occasions to hear voices and see demons. On the day of the aforementioned incident, an instance of RPC-236 instructed Vasquez to steal his father's handgun and smuggle it in his backpack. Vasquez fled the classroom during an outbreak of RPC-███, firing on several infected students and faculty, before eventually preparing a ritual in the gymnasium which prevented the infected from entering long enough for him to dispatch several more before he ran out of bullets. Mobile Quarantine[[footnote]]Headed by Dr. Mallory Heston[[/footnote]] was able to put down the remaining infected at the last second, before apprehending Vasquez for questioning. Investigation revealed there was no way Vasquez could have known the contents of this ritual, or even the existence of RPC-███, nor the mass poisoning that had occurred the day before in the lunchroom.
