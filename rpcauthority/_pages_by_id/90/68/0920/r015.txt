**Item #:** RPC-132

**Object Class:** --Alpha-- Beta

**Containment Protocols:** Media propaganda initiative “Tea Leaves” is to be iterated and deployed by embedded agents in all major media networks in the United Kingdom. Agents must deploy at least four daily //Addams-Meywether// Subliminal Memetic Payloads. For information on the “Tea Leaves” initiative, see Secure Document #132. --Due to the overwhelming success of the “Tea Leaves” initiative, this RPC is to be classed as Alpha.-- //(See Addendum 132-4.)//

While manifestation sites of RPC-132 have so far only been confirmed in the United Kingdom, all Authority staff are to immediately report to their superiors if they come across any instances of RPC-132 outside of the United Kingdom. Due to Authority efforts every other country around the globe recognises RPC-132 as contraband, and should be treated as such. Media propaganda initiatives based on the “Tea Leaves” initiative are ready to deploy in each country should the need arise. 

In the rare occurrence of an instance of RPC-132-1 manifesting, Authority agents must engage and terminate the RPC-132-1 on sight, and incinerate the corpse. --All instances of RPC-132-1 must be pursued and terminated no matter the cost.-- Under no circumstances should Authority personnel pursue RPC-132-1 instances into any kind of tunnel system. This includes, but is not limited to: sewers, storm drains, subways, and metro networks. //(See Addendum 132-1.)//

Any non-Authority witnesses to any instance of RPC-132-1 are to be detained at Site 19 until amnestics can be administered and a suitable cover story implanted, if necessary.

> [[=]]
> **Authority Media Propaganda Initiative #132**
> **CODENAME - "TEA LEAVES"**
> 
> //Infiltrate all news and media networks in the country, insert/deploy subliminal messages and memetic agents in all media formats, in order to achieve the desired outcome on a country-wide scale.//
> [[/=]]
> 
> Originally conceived by Dr. S███████, and first deployed on the 6th of February 190█.
> 
> The original focus of the initiative was the consumption of tea products over coffee products, and implanting a negative view of the RPC-132 'brand'. However, this lead to a small number of the populace testing the product out of spite, or human bloody-mindedness. The focus of the initiative rapidly switched to ignorance of any RPC-132 instances, while the consumption of tea products over coffee products aspect remained the same in order to minimise the risk of memetic dissonance.
> 
> A government agency was created to sweep up any instances of RPC-132 across the country, and destroy them via incineration. To that end the government agency is to be provided instructional material with updated anti-memetic payloads on a regular basis, so that members of the agency will not be affected by the initiative while employed.
> 
> Long term effects of exposure to the initiative have proven acceptable, with the worst recorded side effect causing brain aneurysms in an acceptable percentage of the populace.
> Additionally and unexpectedly, the initiative has been adopted as a trope of UK citizens by the rest of the world. This trope has been positively embraced by a large percentage of the UK population, and has boosted the effectiveness of the initiative considerably.

[[/collapsible]]

**Description:** RPC-132 is a brand of instant coffee powder contained within a 32.7 x 24.3 x 19.7 cm rectangular glass container, holding almost exactly 200g of RPC-132 powder, topped by a red plastic cap. The glass container is emblazoned with a distinctive logo of a large cartoon rodent that makes it easily recognisable.[[footnote]]Exhaustive testing has proven the containers themselves to be completely non-anomalous. - Dr. H██████[[/footnote]]
The first known instance of RPC-132 was secured by Authority agents on the 8th of August 189█, from the family run convenience store located on the corner of ███████ street in Finsbury, London.

RPC-132 anomalously materialises onto the shelves of supermarkets and convenience stores all over the United Kingdom. Manifestation events happen every 8 hours, however the exact store (and shelf) RPC-132 materialises at has been concluded as ‘entirely random’. Between 3 to ██ instances of RPC-132 will materialise each time, and will neatly fit onto a shelf as if put there by hand. CCTV footage from before and after materialisation, shows that other products on the shelves are moved to make room for RPC-132 instances, however the other products are left neatly arranged with their labels facing forward as normal.
Materialisation is instantaneous, and all CCTV footage has been unable to give any useful data. One frame RPC-132 is not present then the very next frame it is.

When questioned, no member of staff from any store has any memory of ordering, receiving, or placing RPC-132 on their shelves. Testing performed on staff, recording equipment, and the building at multiple manifestation sites has found no other anomalous properties or activity of any kind.

The anomalous properties of RPC-132 appear after an organic entity consumes the powder in some way. RPC-132 can be ingested in its powdered form, or mixed with liquid. The effects remain the same. Any organic entity that ingests any amount of RPC-132 is immediately and irrecoverably considered a Stage 1 RPC-132-1 instance. 

Upon ingestion, subjects (henceforth Stage 1 RPC-132-1s) will exhibit no abnormal properties or behaviour for the first 24 hours. However after the first 24 hours have passed Stage 1 RPC-132-1s will enter Stage 2, where they become photosensitive, skittish, and more rodent-like in their mannerisms and overall behaviour. The speed of this change is dependent on the total amount of RPC-132 consumed by the Stage 1 RPC-132-1 in the first 24 hours, although all Stage 1 RPC-132-1s will aggressively refuse to consume any more RPC-132 after exactly 24 hours from first consumption.[[footnote]]Additional testing has proven this really is //exactly// 24 hours, down to the second. - Dr. F██████[[/footnote]]

48 hours after initial consumption all RPC-132-1s will enter the 3rd --and final-- Stage. In this Stage, RPC-132-1’s become frantic and aggressive, and will attempt to reach the nearest sewer system, subway system, or any other tunnel-like system, to the exclusion of any other goal, including self preservation actions such as eating, drinking, or sleeping. Stage 3 RPC-132-1 become extremely hazardous as they show signs of reduced cognitive functions, increased physical strength, increased speed, and increased resistance to trauma. All attempts to communicate with human Stage 3 RPC-132-1’s have ended in failure. Either they do not understand, or they ignore the attempts to communicate.

All attempts to halt or reverse the progression of RPC-132-1 stages has met with failure, --however testing is ongoing. Long term containment and testing is currently impossible due to Stage 3 RPC-132-1’s refusal to eat or drink to sustain themselves, however they will survive for 7 days on average upon reaching Stage 3.-- //(See Addendum 132-3)//

Post-mortem autopsies report no changes to any biological structures or systems of RPC-132-1s. How RPC-132 affects behaviours of Stage 2 and Stage 3 RPC-132-1s is unknown. How Stage 3 RPC-132-1s gain their increased physical abilities is also unknown.

**Addendum 132-1:** Due to the events of Incident 132-62, the containment procedures of RPC-132 have been updated. For more information, see Incident Report 132-62.

[[collapsible show="+ Incident Report 132-62" hide="- Incident Report 132-62"]]

> **Brief:** On June 6th 19██, members of MSF November-14 were tasked with helping Dr. T███████ with an experiment intent on finding exactly where the RPC-132-1s went after entering the local sewers and/or underground tunnel network.
> 
> **Log:** Four D class personnel were implanted with tracking devices, each given a hot cup of RPC-132, and told to drink. The RPC-132-1’s were then restrained, fitted with explosive collars, and put into standard mobile humanoid containment cells. After 48 hours, the four RPC-132-1s were transported to four different London Underground train stations that the Authority had closed ‘for maintenance’. Each RPC-132-1 had one squad of ten agents assigned to them, that would track them through the tunnels.
> 
> Experiment begins and for the first hour, nothing unusual happens. Each squad reports every 5 minutes as planned, and confirm no problems tracking their assigned RPC-132-1. According to tracking data, the RPC-132-1s have seemingly moved at random.
> After the second hour squads 1, 2, and 4 report slight difficulty keeping up with their assigned RPC-132-1s due to structural designs in the tunnels and maintenance corridors/shafts. Squad 3 fails to report at the 20 minute mark, however contact is re-established after 10 minutes and after a quick equipment check, the squad continues on. At this point the RPC-132-1s appear to be moving towards the same general location, however due to the distances of the RPC-132-1s, the exact location is still indeterminable.
> After three hours have elapsed, squads 2 and 3 both report fatigue and increased difficulty in tracking their assigned RPC-132-1. Squad 1 fails to report after the 15 minute mark, and squad 4 fails to report after the 25 minute mark. Both squads are not heard from again. The tracking beacon for the RPC-132-1 that squad 1 was assigned to stops responding at the 35 minute mark. Tracking data shows the three remaining RPC-132-1s began converging on the same location. The three signals are triangulated and the destination is extrapolated to be roughly 82m (269ft) directly below the popular tourist location [REDACTED] in Central London. Site designated OL-Site-132/██.
> 7 minutes and 4 seconds later a member of squad 3 contacted Command screaming intelligibly. [DATA EXPUNGED] can be heard in the background before the transmission is abruptly cut 8 seconds later. At this point no further contact with any members of the MSF November-14 squads can be made. 2 minutes and 41 seconds later the single remaining implanted tracking device appeared to malfunction, as it pulses on and off for the next 3 minutes and 32 seconds before cutting off completely.
> 
> **Outcome:** All members of the MSF November-14 squads considered MIA after search and rescue requests were refused by Command. Containment procedures updated. All data and information on the movement and convergence location of the RPC-132-1s is restricted to level 4 personnel. Measures to contain OL-Site-132/██ are being handled by London Regional Director [REDACTED].

**Addendum 132-3:** Based on events of Incident 132-183, all further testing and experiments with RPC-132 on organic subjects are immediately placed on hold pending Level 4 review.

[[collapsible show="+ Level 4 access required to view Incident Report 132-183" hide="- Incident Report 132-183"]]
> **Brief:** On the ██th of ████████ 20██, Dr. K████ proposed an experiment intent on prolonging the lifespan of a human Stage 3 RPC-132-1 held in containment. The goal was to see if there were any other Stages, to assess their threat, and to research ways to contain if necessary.
> 
> **Log:** Through the use of artificial feeding pipes that were surgically implanted into RPC-132-1 #334 before it reached Stage 3, Dr. K████ was able to keep #334 alive past the average 7 days. 
> #334 was fed approximately 13 cups of RPC-132 during the first 24 hours of exposure.
> Aside from the fastest recorded change from Stage 1 to Stage 2[[footnote]]4 minutes and 12 seconds. - Dr. K████[[/footnote]], the first 10 days of the experiment were uneventful.
> On the 11th day of the experiment (approximately 2██ hours after initial consumption of RPC-132) #334 entered a new phase of development, Stage 4. Before Dr. K████ or his staff were able to perform any meaningful tests [DATA EXPUNGED]
> 
> **Outcome:** Incident culminated in a site wide Containment Breach. Reports indicate ██ fatalities, ███ injuries, and █ RPCs unaccounted for as of ██/██/20██. Video footage confirmed Dr. K████ was KIA during the breach. 
> //While this resulted in what I can only describe as a horrible tragedy, I feel it is my duty to say that we must try this again, with obviously increased precautions. If Stage 4 or even further Stages are possible out in the wild, then we absolutely *must* work out a way to contain or at the very least put them down. - Dr. O████████//
