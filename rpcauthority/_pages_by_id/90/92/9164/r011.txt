**Item #:** RPC-074

**Object Class:** Beta
[[f<image http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-format/beta]]

[[include component:image-block name= http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-074/simo_hayna.jpg|caption= An image of RPC-074 during the Winter War.]]


**Containment Protocols:** RPC-074 is to be contained in a magnetically locked 5x5x5 meter containment cell. No windows or vents have been installed to prevent the frequency of potential containment breaches. Four security personnel, with two mediating between each shift for six hours, must remain attentive and present at all times. Security operatives are required to be in groups of four and are not to be allowed out in the open field in the event RPC-074 breaches containment.

**Description:** 
RPC-074 is a humanoid anomalous entity with pale or snowy-white skin pigmentation. The anomaly is mute; no facial recognition can be determined as the anomaly is always covered by a mask. When personnel attempted to lift the mask, they are struck with a torrential blizzard. Temperatures within the room dropped to -123° Celsius, causing severe frostbite and other deliberating effects. 

RPC-074 displays inhuman strength, incredible durability and is immune to any sedative effects administered. While RPC-074 has been observed to be less active during daylight, it has attempted several times to escape from the containment cell at nightfall. Considering incident 074-01, in which the lights in the cell failed due to an outage, RPC-074 escaped from its cell between the time it took to replace the lights. During periods of snowfall in winter or whenever present in cold winter environments, RPC-074 is able to phase into the snow and vanish from visual contact and appear just as well without alerting the presence of others. No tracks or traces of its presence are left behind.

Discovery of RPC-074 arose during the midst of The Winter War. Prior to its participation in the conflict, there had been no report of any entity matching the description of RPC-074. There had been folk talk from delirious injured Finnish hunters however, about a pale man in a mask, dressed in white camouflage pulling them to safety. It is uncertain if these reports have any correlation to RPC-074.

Visual reports of RPC-074 stemmed from Finnish soldiers during the Battle of Kollaa, but no one could properly identify the entity. Both Russian and Finnish intelligence detailed the anomaly’s unusually high kill streak which totaled to five hundred and forty-two. Two hundred and fifty-nine of these kills were confirmed sniper kills. The entity has also displayed high resilience and survivability witnessed by containment operatives after evading carpet bombing through its phasing ability and having survived an explosive round without any indication of damage.

RPC-074 also displayed unusual habits and actions throughout the course of the war. While there is no indication of the anomaly showing emotion, RPC-074 does express aggression and shows a rather morbid sense of humor revealing the anomaly bears a personality. Several frozen corpses of Red Army troops that fell by sniper fire were posed as signposts.  The names of the towns, those destroyed by carpet-bombing or burned during the Red Army's advances, were written in paper and found in the grip of the Russians pointing in the direction of said towns. The Finnish were not responsible for these incidents but claimed it was their doing nonetheless. However, it is likely that RPC-074 was responsible given reports of a Finnish soldier vanishing into the snow after posing the corpses. In some of these cases, ears and teeth and fingers were missing from the frozen carcasses.

Agent ████ █████, an RPC Operative of the Finnish Authority branch, was integrated into the Finnish Army as a cover operative to replace the Entity while containment teams worked behind the conflict for several weeks to contain RPC-074.  Given ████’s own successful hunting and rifle expertise, ████ operated within the White Guard’s 6th Company of JR 34 as a sniper to contemplate the narrative.

**Addendum-074-A:** 
RPC-074's sentience calls into question its understanding of human concepts and behavior patterns. It has shown an ideal understanding of demoralization and psychological warfare. I.E. butchering corpses of their ears, fingers and teeth as trophies, posing stiff frozen bodies as signposts directing towards towns. There had been not a single instance of RPC-074 killing anyone beyond the Russians during the war. Given its hunter like nature and cold proficient killing, RPC-074 shows elements of compassion and empathy. Minutemen that were recovered after being declared MIA claim to have been rescued by a masked figure and were medically attended to. Many who made these reports were considered delirious with pain and hypothermia. Though these reports do not contradict each other, and all share the same descriptions of the anomaly. 


**Addendum-074-B:** 
Several items were retrieved during RPC-074's initial recovery. However, a written scrap of paper was not accounted for during containment processing.
*	A broken M/28-30 rifle, a Mosin-Nagant variant.
*	A Suomi KP/-31 Submachine gun.
*      Bracelets and necklaces with teeth, fingers, and ears stringed together.
*	One survival pack equipped with rifle ammunition and medical supplies and one small scrap of paper unaccounted for during initial containment. The scrap, written in Finnish, reads as followed:
