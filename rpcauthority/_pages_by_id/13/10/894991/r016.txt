**Containment Protocols:** OL-Site-733-A is to be constructed on the north-eastern coast of Komsomolets Island of Severnaya Zemlya for the surveillance of any commute towards RPC-733. OL-Site-733-B is to be constructed on the east coast of Bolshevik Island as the main site for the monitoring of RPC-733. OL-Site-733-C is to be constructed as well as a surveillance site for any unauthorized activity near the area of RPC-733.

Unauthorized ships detected approaching near Authority sites and/or RPC-733 are to be immediately redirected away from RPC-733. Aircraft such as commercial airplanes that approach RPC-733 are to be redirected via radio communications. Should any aircraft/ships observe RPC-733, Authority personnel are to track any individuals who have observed RPC-733 and administer Class A amnestics.

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/soviet-angel/MAP.png|align=right|width=300px|caption=Approximate location of RPC-733.]]
----
**Description:** RPC-733 is a high-temperature, luminescent entity of indeterminate anatomy, spanning approx. 7 kilometers wide, and whose most distinctive feature is the possession of what appear to be a varying number of wings, often ranging from 7 to 13. RPC-733 is currently located 1 kilometer above the Laptev Sea, directly east of October Revolution Island, Severnaya Zemlya, Russia. RPC-733 has also demonstrated sentience and appears to possess an awareness of objects around it (See Document "Soviet Iron Initiative - RDS-71 Aftermath"). Since its first observation by the Authority, RPC-733 has not made any apparent movement in any direction except for the movement of its wings.

The temperature of the center of RPC-733 is 5,555,555,555,537° C. Thus, it is believed to be composed of quark-gluon plasma[[footnote]]A state of matter in which atoms are so hot that the quarks and gluons that make them up cannot remain together, causing them to separate and form a "soup" of quarks and gluons.[[/footnote]]. However, the heat radiated by RPC-733 appears to be non-causative, or nullified by a secondary anomalous property: its immediate surroundings range from -272.85ºC to 272.45ºC (near 0 K), provoking the development of a thick layer of fog through condensation of atmospheric humidity. This is believed to be due to RPC-733 draining thermal energy from its environment.

Severe weather phenomena have been observed near RPC-733 due to the low-temperature air around it; severe hailstorms, snowstorms, and gale winds ranging from 70-130 km/h are common near RPC-733.

----

**Addendum 733-1:** Further investigation of the area around RPC-733 concludes that the environment around RPC-733, along with RPC-733 itself, possesses low coherency (estimated ACS of 1.9). Several anomalies have been observed near RPC-733 due to this, mostly temporal and extra-dimensional anomalies such as the relocation of Authority ships.

RPC-733 has also been observed draining mass amounts of thermal energy from Authority ships, aircraft, and Authority personnel themselves by unknown means. Testing has determined the area of effect in which RPC-733 will drain energy from subjects to be 0.3 kilometers from RPC-733. Temperatures of objects being drained by RPC-733 drop at an estimated rate of 700° C per second.



----

During investigations regarding the Soviet Iron Initiative in 1983, several documents connected to the origin of RPC-733 were extracted, along with documents regarding "RDS-71", a nuclear device developed by the Soviet Union.

On May 3rd, 1971, at approximately 12:10 PM, an experimental Soviet unmanned drone was deployed towards the anomaly that had formed from the RDS-71 test. The drone was equipped with a camera, high-sensitivity microphones, and an infrared thermometer.

Upon approaching a distance of nearly 0.2 kilometers from the anomaly, the drone experienced severe temperatures. However, high-sensitivity microphones detected what sounded like Russian "speech" emanating from the anomaly.

[[div style="margin: 10px; padding: 10px; border: #fff 1px dotted; background: #1d2126; color: #E5E5E5; border-left: #fff 1px dotted;"]]
"Почему? Почему? Почему?" ("Why? Why? Why?")

"Почему мне не хватает божественности?" ("Why must I be?")

"Будь проклят!" ("Damn you!")

[[/div]]

Moments after the speech was detected, the drone shut down and the connection was lost, presumably due to severe drops in temperature as a result of the anomaly.

//On the seventh of June, I had boarded the ship towards the research site on October Revolution Island, and immediately, I had felt a strong sense of dread, strong enough to paralyze me momentarily. A soldier had looked at me and furrowed his brow, and asked if I was alright. I had paused before I spoke to him, assuring him that I was fine.//

//Afterwards, I had entered the site and was greeted by the warm, air-conditioned air of the research site. The researchers had greeted me respectfully before asking to direct me towards the next ship. Almost immediately after the ship had begun its journey, I felt the same sense of dread. I turned to look upon the cold sea, and I could already see its glow.//

//Soon, I was given a close view of the being that hovered before me. Its luminous glow was incredibly bright, but not bright enough to pain my eyes, almost like a winged, second sun. Its magnificent, yet intimidating wings wrapped around each other. I stepped towards the railing of the ship and placed my hands on the railing to come closer.//

//Promptly, I was met with visions of the motherland, along with voices that cursed the Union for birthing this angel. It spoke to me://

//"An error was made. Lacking the touch of God, his holiness. Drain the lands cold, dead, and empty, lest I die. I cannot die, I must not. Burning rage, burning anger, burning hate for being. A false angel to strike down this godless union."//

//I ordered for the ship to depart back, for I had seen enough. I was left with a deep sense of dread and anxiety for several days afterwards. I was left with recurring dreams of the angel, dreams that would leave me unable to return to sleep. After seeing the angel in its state of anger, fear, and sorrow due to its existence and our responsibility for it, I could not help but feel that our fate had been sealed by its birth.//

**Addendum 733-2:** Further surveying of the environment around RPC-733 has concluded that the area from which RPC-733 is absorbing energy appears to be growing at a steady rate, with the radius of this area increasing by 3.3 meters every 24 hours. RPC-733 has also observed several increases in temperature.

The "cold areas" that RPC-733 has previously absorbed energy from have been revealed to possess absolute zero temperature due to the energy absorption of RPC-733.

It is believed that should this effect continue, northern areas of the Earth, along with some societies, will begin to experience life-threatening temperature drops along with climates shifting towards a polar climate in approximately ███ years before further areas of the Earth are affected along with larger societies.
