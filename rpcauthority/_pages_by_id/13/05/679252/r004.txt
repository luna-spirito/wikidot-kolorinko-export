**Registered Phenomena Code:** 866

**Object Class:** Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** Extra-Terrestrial? Hazard, Incorporeal Hazard


**Description:** RPC-866 designates a past event viewed by all human beings in an approx. 20km^^2^^ area, located within the Hala'ib Triangle disputed territory, between Egypt and Sudan, and along the Red Sea coast. Research is ongoing by Dr. Samuel Hussain, and any queries surrounding RPC-866 should be referred to him.
