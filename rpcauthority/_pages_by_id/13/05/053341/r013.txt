+++ **Containment Protocols:** 

RPC-975 instances are to be contained inside the containment facility of Site-031-1 in a standard containment room filled with soil possessing conditions that are preferred by non-anomalous members of the Leucanthemum genus and surrounded by glass walls, with an overhead glass opening for daily sunlight access. RPC-975 are to be watered via 4 overhead sprinklers thrice per day. A packet of RPC-975 seeds are to be stored in nearby storage locker 01A.

RPC-975-1 specimens are to be contained in Site-008's containment wing in a 5-by-5 centimeter sample tube stored in a standard pathogen vault inside a category one refrigerator locked with a level 4 hermetic seal. 

Personnel volunteering to make use of RPC-975-1 must provide documented proof of their pulmonary disease as either produced by an outside physician or Site-008's own medical personnel. Volunteers who are allergic to pollen are not allowed to make use of RPC-975-1. Once inserted, said volunteer is to be checked bi-weekly for any new developments inside the lungs of said volunteer. After approximately 2 months, Site-008's medical personnel are to check on said volunteer for an overall physical examination. Any tumors formed by RPC-975-1 found inside the volunteer's body are to be surgically removed.

+++ **Description:** 

RPC-975 refers to an anomalous subspecies of //Leucanthemum vulgare//[[footnote]]Commonly known as Oxeye Daisy[[/footnote]]. Despite their genetic makeup, RPC-975 are superficially identical to //Chamaemelum nobile//[[footnote]]Commonly known as Chamomile[[/footnote]], and can only be identified as RPC-975 via DNA, growth patterns and the anomalous regenerative properties it possesses for humans. 

In its reproductive life cycle, RPC-975 releases RPC-975-1 into the atmosphere, which are anomalous pollen spores that travel in the air. Once RPC-975-1 has landed on the carpels or stamens of a non-anomalous //Leucanthemum vulgare// or //Chamaemelum nobile// it will turn it into another RPC-975 instance. When a human with a pulmonary disease[[footnote]]Pneumonia, Lung cancer, Asthma, etc.[[/footnote]] inhales RPC-975-1, the human (henceforth referred to as the Subject) will proceed to experience various regenerative effects to their lungs, which takes place in the course of three phases. These following phases can be found below. If the Subject is allergic to pollen and inhales RPC-975-1, they will suffer from a sudden allergic reaction which has proven to be usually life-threatening; urgent medical attention may be required in such cases.

[[tabview]]
  [[tab Phase 1]]
    Having landed on the airway lining of the Subject, RPC-975-1 will enter Phase 1 of growth, characterized by germinating via unknown means and shooting microscopic roots that dig through the tissue of the epithelium. At this point, the roots will release a protein which has been identified as a unique variation of TMED7 only found in RPC-975-1: RPC-975-TMED7 which signals the immune system against attacking the foreign growth. The roots of RPC-975-1 will then proceed to travel downward to the trachea and into the two primary bronchi.
  [[/tab]]
  [[tab Phase 2]]
    Once reaching inside the bronchi, the roots of RPC-975-1 will start to grow by feeding in direct contact with the bloodstream, giving step to Phase 2 of growth. Once connected to the arteries inside the lungs, RPC-975-1 will sprout small petals to repair any damage in the interior of the pleura for a period of 7 days. Once all damaged tissue is covered, the roots of RPC-975-1 will begin to merge into the bloodstream and start to transform into alveols, replacing dead or dysfunctional alveoli in the process.
  [[/tab]]
  [[tab Phase 3]]
   At the end of the process of regeneration, Phase 3 of growth begins, where RPC-975-1 will create minuscule tendrils to connect to the main bronchioles so the newly generated alveolis will gain access to the respiratory tract in a time period of 15 days. If there are still dead/dysfunctional alveoli on the lungs, some tendrils will extend to the dead instances and try to deconstruct them until the lungs no longer have any traces of damaged tissue. Most dead tissue will be released via coughing and in other instances, in urine or sweat.
  [[/tab]]
[[/tabview]]

In the often rare event that RPC-975-1 does not initially make contact with respiratory organs, RPC-975-1 will travel in the bloodstream until it arrives at a large organ. Once located in the organ it will rest and latch onto the internal skin, beginning to absorb nutrients and vitamins which are located inside the organ. After a period of 4 to 6 weeks: if RPC-975 has not reached the respiratory tract yet, it will begin to send a chemical compound to the immune system which signals it to attack RPC-975-1; which at this point has become a benign tumor. This tumor is not life-threatening, and will shrink in approximately 2 months.

After Phase 3, the lungs of the User (designated as RPC-975-2) are fully 'healed', and will function as a healthy pair of human lungs, despite plant matter being present inside. When RPC-975-2 suffers damage post-regeneration, the newly-generated alveoli and RPC-975-1 still present in RPC-975-2 will begin to rapidly fix the damage via means similar to Phase 2 and 3 of regeneration. It is currently unknown if regeneration continues until RPC-975-2 is entirely composed of plant matter or if it automatically stops after reaching a certain number of 'regenerations'. The highest number of 'regenerations' recorded by the Authority is 5 times, inside Senior Researcher ██████.

----

+++ **Discovery:**

RPC-975 was brought to the Authority's attention after a sudden decrease of lung disease in a public hospital belonging the city of ██████, Massachusetts on March 12th, 200█. At first, this did not catch the interest of the Authority until two weeks later, where an image of a chest radiograph depicting artifacts recognizable as daisy petals growing on the inside of a patient's lungs spread on the internet. After the leaked radiography reached the public, a massive influx of lung cancer patients admitted themselves into the hospital in hopes to be healed.

An Authority-conducted raid under the guise of an illegal drug search of the local hospital uncovered a ring consisting of members of the Group of Interest "Descendants Of Yggdrasil' working in the hospital as oncologist doctors who were giving samples of RPC-975-1 to patients in need. On the rooftop of the hospital, an artificial greenhouse where RPC-975 was produced and RPC-975-1 extracted was also discovered. DoY agents declared all events were true and proved their illicity by showing that the petals in the lungs were a harmless effect upon inhaling RPC-975-1. The Authority raid took place in the span of 2 days.

To avoid a more widespread release to the public of RPC-975 and RPC-975-1, the chest radiograph image was quickly given a cover story under the guise of a hoax, and all samples of RPC-975 and RPC-975-1 were confiscated by the Authority. All DoY agents who were present at the scene were taken into Authority custody for questioning, then released with no repercussions as per request of DoY. All doctors and patients at the site who had their lungs healed by RPC-975-1 were given amnestics and had all outgoing treatments for lung cancer using RPC-975-1 sent to Site-008 for future research and investigation.

How he knew he should choose that specific patient is something I'll never know.

We checked the recordings of security to try and search who the doctor was but we came empty handed. The only lead I collected was this card, some sort of an invitation to join some kind of a botany group. At first I thought it was a religious cult; boy was I was wrong on that one. When we got inside █████'s room we saw him looking happy and he only requested to be taken off his meds and for the daisy to be kept around him.

The only thing we didn't made him sign off was to leave the hospital for the month since it was already paid off by the insurance company.

At first we thought we got some mixed results from another patient or a malfunction in our equipment, but when I saw the test in eye to eye, I realized that those test were legitimate.
>

All of the personnel of the oncology ward were left shocked that a terminal cancer patient was cured and we didn't even know how. Before he left I asked him if I could take some lung samples for future testing; he said yes and we extracted a little piece of it. The weird thing about this was that the lung tissue had this greenish color to it.
>

It was filled with people who were cultivating food and giving it to poor people and even building toys for kids with wood. > **Sjöström:** You mean the other daisies you found? They were prototypes and in the process of being tested on people. Since I saw its raw potential on treating the lungs against everything, I handled the proposal to extend the creation of more daisies to treat even bigger threads, like making the daisies turn other daisies around them into more of themselves. We also had a [*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-603* potato against infertility] but that project, from what I heard, went slow.

> [[=]]
> **Test #1: Lung Cancer**
> [[/=]]
> 
> **Attending Division wing:** Research Division
> 
> **Designated Researcher for the test:** Axel Sjöström
> 
> **Site where the test was performed:** Site-008
> 
> **Items used to perform the test:** CSD-28374 (who had a tumor in the left lung as a result of lung cancer), RPC-975-1
> 
> **Hypothesis:** If the info that the Descendants Of Yggdrasil provided in Interview Log 975-1 is true, then RPC-975-1 will able to treat tumors caused by lung cancer without causing side effects.
> 
> **Experiment:** CSD-28374 inhales a dose of RPC-975-1 via a BA mask and has his lungs checked on daily by on-site researchers.
> 
> **Results:** Through the course of roughly 3 weeks, RPC-975-1 was observed to target the tumor in CSD-28374's left lung, growing roots which dug themselves into the tumorous tissue and 'draining' it via unknown means, which was evidenced by the tumor shrinking entirely in the span of 2 days. CSD-28374 reported no pain during this process, but rather a slightly uncomfortable sensation of pressure in the respective area. The newly-generated RPC-975-2 instance proved to be functional, and CSD-28374 was sent back to his cell in the Research Wing after the experiment.

> [[=]]
> **Test #2: Acute Bronchitis**
> [[/=]]
> 
> **Attending Division wing:** Research Division
> 
> **Designated Researcher for the test:** Axel Sjöström
> 
> **Site where the test was performed:** Site-008
> 
> **Items used to perform the test:** CSD-51432 (who, at the time, was suffering from a chest cold[[footnote]]acute bronchitis[[/footnote]] caused by a viral infection), RPC-975-1
> 
> **Hypothesis:** While the veracity of RPC-975-1 has proven to be completely harmless, now we focus towards making sure that RPC-975-1 is capable not only of performing healing to lung cancer but it is also able to treat acute lung diseases, and not necessarily only chronic ones.
> 
> **Experiment:** CSD-51432 inhales a dose of RPC-975-1 via a BA mask and has his lungs checked on daily by on-site researchers.
> 
> **Results:** Through the course of roughly 2 days, RPC-975-1 was observed to spread roots through the bronchi affected by the viral infection, and produce antibiotic compounds which managed to kill off the infection. The roots were also observed to drain any sputum and phlegm out of the bronchi, and send in anti-inflammatory agents to soothe any inflamed tube walls. The resulting RPC-975-2 instance was noted to be small/limited in terms of plant matter inside the lungs. CSD-51432 was sent back to his cell in the Research Wing after the experiment.

> [[=]]
> **Test #3: Diarrhea**
> [[/=]]
> 
> **Attending Division wing:** Research Division
> 
> **Designated Researcher for the test:** Axel Sjöström
> 
> **Site where the test was performed:** Site-008
> 
> **Items used to perform the test:** CSD-34842 (reason of diarrhea was caused by minor poisoning from improperly-cooked food), RPC-975-1
> 
> **Hypothesis:** Although it was not mentioned in Interview Log 975-1, RPC-975-1 may be able to cure other, non-pulmonary ailments; this is based upon the aforementioned idea of RPC-975 evolving to cure more illnesses.
> 
> **Experiment:** CSD-34842 inhales a dose of RPC-975-1 via a BA mask and has his stomach area checked on daily by on-site researchers.
> 
> **Results:** Throughout the course of two weeks, no visible improvements to CSD-34842 were recorded in the digestive tract. After the 2 week period passed out, various RPC-975-2 instances were detected inside the small intestine acting as benign cancer cells, absorbing nutrients and growing inside the cavity. Once researchers took one sample from CSD-34842's intestinal region and analyzed it; it was concluded from the sample that the RPC-975-2 instances were sending hormones which were indicating to be attacked by white blood cells.
> 
> After giving medication for the diarrhea and for symptoms akin to fever and mild pain, the existing RPC-975-2 instances were reduced by a 80% in 3 days and completely gone by the 10th day. CSD-51432 was sent back to his cell in the Research Wing after the experiment.
> 

> [[=]]
> **Test #4: Oxeye Daisy**
> [[/=]]
> 
> **Attending Division wing:** Research Division
> 
> **Designated Researcher for the test:** Eryn Petersen
> 
> **Site where the test was performed:** Site-031-1
> 
> **Items used to perform the test:** A collection of Oxeye Daisies (7), a single instance of RPC-975
> 
> **Hypothesis:** If the additional information that the Descendants of Yggdrasil provided in Interview Log 975-1 is true, then RPC-975-1 can transmutate non-anomalous oxeye daisies into instances of RPC-975-1
> 
> **Experiment:** The collection of oxeye daisies were planted around the RPC-975 instance. A large fan with airflow mimicking a forest breeze was then installed to encourage the spread of RPC-975-1.
> 
> **Results:** Within minutes of the fan turning on, the oxeye daisies anomalously transmuted into RPC-975 instances, which was characterized by a sudden spurt of growth of the stem and the changing of the leaves into the bipinnate shape that instances of RPC-975 and non-anomalous members of the //Roman Chamomile// species possess. The transmutation was in the span of roughly 2 and a half minutes. The newly generated instances of RPC-975 were replanted into their respective containment chamber.
