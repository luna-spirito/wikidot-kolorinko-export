When possible, amnestization should take place gradually and in isolated areas.

[[div class="content-panel"]]

[[f<image http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/component:icons/object-white.png]]

**Registered Phenomena Code:** 013-1

**Item Type:** Object

**Lethality Rating:** White

**Hazard Types:** Info-Hazard, Visual, Memory Alteration

**Abstract:** The anomaly is a short 13-page book with visual properties, under experimentation following a special request from Black Site Department. Development of potential applications is unnecessary.

**Safe Handling and Usage:** RPC-013-1 is to be treated as a lethal cognitohazard; visual exposure should be avoided.

------

> ##red|**TESTING BAN:**## **13/07/2013 - PRESENT**
> **Authority:** [IDENTITY CLASSIFIED], Black Site Department
> **Reason:** //To prevent potential spread of classified information.//

------


**Description:**  RPC-013-1 is a unique children's storybook titled "Bob's Sleepy Time". RPC-013-1 spans 13 pages, with its first 12 folio exhibiting no anomalous properties. However, page 13 is suspected to contain a visual memetic agent, exposure to which would result in the immediate reception of large amounts of data. Of note, RPC-013-1's contents are unusual for similar non-anomalous books in regards to phrasing and narrative. 

The flipside of page 13 contains a monochrome image unrelated to the book's content: this has been determined to not be hazardous on its own, but is placed to accentuate the effects of the memetic agent in question. Attempts to track down the origin of the image have failed. Location pictured and date of photography are unknown.

**Discovery:** RPC-013-1 was recovered after the disappearance of Mr. Julián Abascal, inhabitant of Mexico City, classified PoI-7916. The subject in question had displayed unusual behaviour towards his son and grandchildren, repeatedly attempting to expose them to RPC-013-1. After his family attempted to contact Mexico City law enforcement, the subject left his home, abandoning most of his belongings save for a small choice of apparel suited for travel. RPC-013-1 was left in possession of his grandchildren, later retrieved by law enforcement and repossessed by the Authority. RPC-013-1's anomalous properties had apparently not been noticed by the police force.

Reports of PoI-7916's disappearance and unusual circumstances involved reached Authority informants in Mexico City, prompting NORTHCOM Authority involvement. Further action regarding operational security was deemed unnecessary, as local news media failed to pick up on the case and Mexico City's high crime rate quickly distracted law enforcement.

PoI-7916 was last sighted by local law enforcement in the Plaza de Constitución (Constitution Square), heading northwest.

__RPC-013-2 Black Site File.__

[[div class="content-panel"]]

[[f<image http://www.rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/component:icons/gamma-white.png]]

**Registered Phenomena Code:** 013-2

**Object Class:** Gamma-White

**Hazard Types:** Auditory, Info-Hazard, Memory Alteration, Mechanical

**Containment Protocols:** Mexican Authority personnel are to report unusually high rates of migraine or headache, as well as general radio jamming to regional NORTHCOM supervisors who are then to relay the information in question to Black Site Department representatives. Black Site Department is responsible for intervention on each given case.

Personnel born before 1960 are disallowed from participating in RPC-013-2 operations.

**Description:** RPC-013-2 refers to a series of makeshift radio transmission devices produced by PoI-7916. RPC-013-2 instances work simultaneously as jammers and transmitters, overriding any transmissions on a certain frequency, provided they are in a 5km radius. While jamming, RPC-013-2 transmit variable sequences of numbers and phrases, all of which share similar anomalous properties that have not been successfully replicated under any conditions.

In a minor percentage of cases, audible reception of signals transmitted by RPC-013-2 can result in immediate and severe sensory overload, primarily manifesting as migraine, which subdues over the course of 20 minutes. Once this timespan is over, affected subjects will have incorporated large amounts of unfamiliar data. Data received through RPC-013-2 is always highly sensitive, and especially hazardous to operational security.

Of note, these properties are more consistently triggered and powerful in subjects born before the year 1960. In these subjects, amnestic treatment regarding received information is rarely effective. As such, sanitization protocols require extensively damaging the amygdala[[footnote]] Neuronal clusters in the brain responsible for the development of memories associated with strong emotions, such as fear or trauma. [[/footnote]] for the same effect as regular amnestics.

RPC-013-2 instances are mainly located throughout Northern Mexico, especially through the provinces of Sonora and Chihuahua, although a limited number of instances have been found in Arizona.

**Discovery:** The first known instance of RPC-013-2 was discovered near Santiago de Querétaro, northwest of Mexico City, during October 2013. Local Authority personnel were alerted following reports of general malfunction of radio devices along the northmost edge of the city. It was found that these malfunctions were identical across all reports, always consisting of devices being jammed by an identical broadcast not unlike that of a numbers station. [[footnote]] Radio stations characterized by the transmission of messages encoded in a numerical fashion, sometimes accompanied by vocal phrases. Most commonly associated with intelligence operatives operating outside their country. [[/footnote]]

It was ultimately decided not to intervene, having failed to find any anomalous properties inherent to the broadcast. No attempt was made to alert Mexican intelligence.

However, as October progressed, an abrupt increase in medical reports regarding headaches was noticed across the same population being affected by the phenomenon. Further investigation of specific cases prompted Black Site Department intervention, as symptoms experienced by the population of Querétaro had significant overlap with those provoked by RPC-013-1. 

Interviews with affected individuals confirmed suspicions of a link between both phenomena, resulting in incognito search parties being deployed north of Querétaro. RPC-013-2/1 was found on the 14th of October. PoI-7916 was briefly identified on the following day, installing an RPC-013-2 instance near the city of Torreón.

> **PoI:** I want a lawyer.
> 
> **B:** You do not have access to one. The circumstances of your apprehension exempt you from any rights you may normally have during questioning.

> 
> //**[Pause; 8 seconds.]**//
>

>

> 
> **B:** State from where or whom you acquired the components to produce the psychological properties of the radio devices.
> 
> **PoI:** Wha-- Psychological properties? They have no special powers.

> **B:** State the intent with which you produced the radio devices.
> 
> **PoI:** I-- One minute, please.

> **B:** Do not avoid the order you have been given.
> 
> **PoI:** Wa-wait, wait for a moment. I'll collaborate, but let's talk in more humane terms, can we?
> 
> //**[Pause; 15 seconds. PoI's mouth is moving, but his speech isn't registered.]**//
> 
> **B:** Please respond to the order. State the intent with which you produced the radio devices.
> 
> **PoI:** Alright, alright. I did it to make people remember like me. Can you please take me out of here? The symbols are making me dizzy and my head aches. I can't talk to you like this.

> **B:** Elaborate.
> 
> **PoI:** Wait, wait. I'm not feeling well. Please just let me out and I'll talk.
> 
> **B:** No. Please respond to the order.
> 
> **PoI:** Please! I'll talk just fine elsewhere, just let me out! The symbols are making me sick.
> 
> **B:** If you were allowed access to a face-to-face interview, would you be able to elaborate?
> 
> **PoI:** Yes, yes! I would elaborate.
> 
> //**[Brief pause.]**//
> 
> **B:** Please await, the door is opening soon.

>

> 
> **PoI:** I... I don't remember. Did I... Buy it? No, it couldn't have been. I think it was my granddaughter's.[[footnote]] This claim is believed to be false; PoI's daughter as well as her parents have failed to recognize RPC-013-1 upon questioning. [[/footnote]]

> **PoI:** I felt dizzy and grumpy and my head ached. It subsided once it came to me... Epiphany? That's the word, right?
> 
> **B:** Yes, it is. Do you recognize the image on the last page?
> 
> **PoI:** Come again?
> 
> //**[B takes the photograph of RPC-013-1, and hands PoI a replica of the image in question. PoI takes it with his left hand, using his right to floss his front teeth.]**//
> 
> **PoI:** Oh! Yes, I do. It's the... The uh... The walk back. We had to walk from my city to somewhere else. I had three other photos from the book, but well... You have them now.
> 
> **B:** When did this happen? Do you remember where you were walking to?
> 
> **PoI:** January, nineteen-sixty. We were walking to... Uhm.

> **PoI:** No, I don't remember.
> 
> **B:** What is the mechanical structure on the left side?
> 
> **PoI:** A weapon.
> 
> **B:** A weapon?
> 
> **PoI:** Wait, uh... I'm not sure now.
>

> 
> **PoI:** Oh, so soon?
>

>

> **B:** Whispers?
> 
> **PoI:** Oh! Nothing to be worried about. They can't be here anyways.
> 
> **B:** I'll ask my superiors.

People everywhere around us would keep staring and listening for the entire length of the transmission. I had a hunch people would still do the same here. The numbers don't mean anything.

>

> 
> **PoI:** Oh God... You people! The guys outside! He's sick!

[[tab ADDENDUM]]
[[div class="content-panel"]]

Following PoI-7916's death, a large number of behavioral anomalies have been detected across the Atlantic coast of the United States and Mexico, closely resembling PoI-7916's own irregularities. No clear cause or pattern for the anomaly has been found despite extensive questioning and analysis of affected populations.

An investigation campaign focused on RPC-013 and PoI-7916 found consistent mentions to a series of mechanical containment devices located below sea across the Atlantic coast, dubbed "Lightswitches" by individuals involved. While scarce evidence of the prior existence of at least one submarine Black Site facility near the Atlantic coast exists, no clear outline of its purpose and systems remains.

Communication with UNAAC archivists remains similarly fruitless, although evidence for the relocation of a number of their Subsurface Resonator containment devices to coastal Authority sites between 1960 and 1963 exists. Redacted mentions to an unspecified third party involved in the operation are considered a cause for concern.

An explorative operation in conjunction with UNAAC is undergoing in hopes to find "Lightswitch" facilities and assess what occurred to them, if anything, and what their relationship with the above described phenomenon is.

Of note, numerous cavities across the Atlantic floor have been found to be overrun with ectoplasmic presence.
