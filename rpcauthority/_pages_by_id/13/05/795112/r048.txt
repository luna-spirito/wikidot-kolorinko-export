++ HOW TO USE FONTS

First, take the class of the font and the class of the paper you want. These are in the tabs. Then put them in a div block like this:

> @@[[div class="marck paper3"]]@@
> This produces a div with the "marck" font and the "paper3" background. 
> @@[[/div]]@@

See:

[[div class="marck paper3"]]
This produces a div with the "marck" font and the "paper3" background.
[[/div]]

The typewrite1 and typewrite2 fonts are meant to be used with the paperGray background to create a typewritten text look. They don't look great on the other paper backgrounds.

The patrick font is meant to be used for titles. It may not look that great for regular text.
