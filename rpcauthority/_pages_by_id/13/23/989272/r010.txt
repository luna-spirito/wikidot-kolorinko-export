Founded in 1992 by Robert H. Sharrock, Conscius Labs Inc (CNSL), part of the “Great Medical Arm,”[[footnote]] The common group-naming for various medical sister companies owned by partnered monopolis, such as: T&NY, Grey’s International, Vinland-Pyre RI, U.R.T.M., and The O’Connor Scientific Fund.[[/footnote]] is an R&D company specialized in the field of biotechnology, being one of the leading centers of study in the field across the USA. This company centers their research into the highly-experimental field of Necrobiotics—the manipulation of dead matter for its use in technology and medicine. They are most well known by the public for their recent contributions to regenerative medicine, neuro-reconstructive therapy, and the now-infamous "1998 Strafford Experiment.” During said experiment, researchers were able to fully reanimate the neural synapses of a five-day deceased monkey utilizing then-groundbreaking Necrobiotic chemicals and surgery. While the monkey died of health complications three days later, the experiment set up the reputation of the company as one of the leading scientific institutions of the 21st century.

In recent times, Conscius Labs Inc has become famous for its advancements in the field of computing, neural network development, and artificial intelligence. This has come as a surprise to many, as the company never involved itself in such fields.

Within the 359 documents, it is shown that shortly after the announcement of such advancements, the laboratories signed an agreement with Mirage Inc for the use of a new security algorithm. Further documentation shows an increase of missing cases within the region of the newly-built CNSL HQs. Further documentation shows CNSL’s founders to have been involved in the manufacturing of illegal substances within Germany during WW2. Further documentation shows ██████, Austria, to be one of their main laboratories.

There is, however, no one in ██████, Austria, pardon RPC-839.

Post-mortem autopsy showed that Dr. Cowells would've expired regardless of his choice to commit suicide, as lethal doses of [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/viderics-guide Leptosiderine] were found inside his body.
