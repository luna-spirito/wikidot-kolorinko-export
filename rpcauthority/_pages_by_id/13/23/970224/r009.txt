[[=]]
+++ ABSTRACT
[[/=]]

The Anti-Hex Body Armor System vIII (AHA3), informally named the "Holy Armor" is the third incarnation of the anti-thaumaturgic armor system. The body armor is engraved with a series of theo-alchemic glyphs and sigils to repel and intercept various thaumaturgic threats. Originally created by [[[RPC-503]]] blacksmiths, the design was improved by the FOA and later patented to the Authority.

[[=]]
+++ (I) BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION
[[/=]]
**(I) Original Design**
The first incarnation of the Anti-Hex Body Armor System[[footnote]] More broadly known as "Anti-Thaumaturgic Armor". [[/footnote]] was allegedly created in 1864 by an  RPC-503 blacksmith. It was a sophisticated 15th century Gothic Armor engraved with holy sigils to protect its user from thaumaturgic harm. The utilization of applied theogonics[[footnote]] The thaumaturgic art of belief. [[/footnote]] allowed for the amplification of its anti-hex properties. This special armor became the default garb of the Teutonic Order.[[footnote]] The original title of RPC-503's militia. [[/footnote]] Due to RPC-503's isolation from the rest of the world, the arts of sigil composition have been lost from mainstream thaumaturgy and science.

**(II) FOA Adoption**
After RPC-503 was discovered by the FOA in 19██, several of the anomalous order's instruments were adopted into the BündesOkkultAbteilung's arsenal. The agency improved upon the instruments in the possession of RPC-503, both to aid the Order in their conflict with RPC-503-2 and to enhance currently available protective equipment for combat personnel. The Anti-Hex Body Armor was upgraded via integration with a standardized Multi-Function Tactical Armor set in use by the FOA, engraving with the exact sigil composition employed by the Order. Even after the Authority acquisition, the FOA has the most advanced version of the Anti-Hex Body Armor System.

**(III) Authority Acquisition**
The Authority eventually bought the rights to use the armor on Jun. 26, 2011, during a UNAAC conference. The invention would later be sold to the MI13 and PCAAO a few months later after both agencies filed several complaints to the FOA.[[footnote]] The complaints were filed under the claim of "unfairness". [[/footnote]] Since the acquisition, only minor improvements have been made to the armor's anomalous properties as the arts of effective sigil composition remains a mystery to the Authority as a whole. Inversely, the body armor system template has been significantly improved upon, specialized for ASF and MST operations.

[[=]]
+++ (II) DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION
[[/=]]

AHA3 is primarily made of tungsten reinforced steel composite metal foam covered in thin glass. It was made to loosely replicate the 15th Century Gothic style of armor both for aesthetic and applied theogonic purposes.[[footnote]] The psychological sway of "feeling like a proper knight" has been shown to enhances belief. [[/footnote]] It meets the standard criteria for a bullet-resistant body armor system. In total, the armor weighs 2.8 kg without extra equipment. The numbers listed in {{Fig. 1}} designates the 4 sigils which make up AHA3's property.


**(1) Sigil 1: Self-Nullification**
Self-nullification thaumaturgic property that makes objects virtually indestructible down to their molecular structure. Though it is a common characteristic of several anomalous objects, replicating the property is difficult and costly due to the microscopic and geometric precision required of sigil composition. Note that self-nullification is a property that protects the armor’s molecular structure, not its wearer. A fatal impact will still be dangerous as the armor won't be able to suppress blunt trauma.

Sigil 1 is the backbone of AHA3's properties. It allows for the alchemical glyphs and sigils to endure indefinitely without the inconvenience of deterioration. The sigil is engraved on the armor's breastplate to magnify its effects. It is composed to extend to the entire body armor.

**(2-3) Sigil 2 & 3: Tychokinetic Field**
A minor tychokinetic glyph is engraved to protect its wearer from imminent harm by projecting a stochastic field.[[footnote]] Randomly determined probability pattern. The pattern of which is controlled within a circumference, otherwise known as a field. [[/footnote]] A total of 4 tychokinetic field sigils is drawn around the armor. Two on the plackart (one in front and one in the back), one on the fold, and the last one is located in the back of the helmet.

**(4-5) Sigil 4 & 5: Minor Psychotronic Repulse**
A minor psychotronic glyph is engraved to protect its wearer from telekinetic, telepathic, and pyrokinetic threats. The effectiveness of this property is amplified when stacked with the tychokinetic sigils. A total of 4 psychotronic sigils are engraved on the armor. Each shoulder pad has one, the gorget has one engraved on the front and back. The helmet has one composed on its behind.

**(6) Sigil 6: Emotional Suggestion Intercept**
An Emotional suggestion intercept sigil is engraved on the front of the helmet. Be the emotion negative, the helmet will repel hypnotic manipulation by intercepting external brainwaves. This glyph has been shown to increase productivity and willpower among its wearer.

[[=]]
+++ (III) INSTRUCTION AND USE OF THE INVENTION
[[/=]]
AHA3 has a wide array of applications both in the Protection division and the Containment division for its versatile nature. With the exception of the self-nullification sigil, the property of each glyph can be amplified or reduced by the wearer. This process utilizes the arts of applied theogony. While the original models only required religious faith to magnify its potency, modern systems require intensive psychological training before mastery.

**(I) Wearing the Armor**
The armor can be worn and fastened like typical hard body armor. It is to be put on over standard Authority uniforms. The hooks around the armor allow room for more utilities.

**(II) Passive Properties**
By simply wearing it, all the effects of the sigils are projected.

**(III) Active Properties**
The wearer can amplify or decrease the effects of all of their glyphs with the exception of the self-nullification sigil. This can be mixed and matched to produce different effects.

**(IV) Mixed Properties**
The tycho-psychotronic fuse combination has the potential to shield personnel from the destabilization hazards of high-class reality-bending. The emotional-psychotronic repulse fuse can disorient sapient threats with an excess of emotional suggestions. The tycho-emotional fuse expands the wearer's stochastic field's circumference.
