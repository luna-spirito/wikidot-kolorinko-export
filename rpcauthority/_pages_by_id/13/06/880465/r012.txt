---------

**Containment Protocols:** --RPC-151 is permitted access to the Administrative and Office Wing of Site-015. Personnel are permitted to use RPC-151 as a standard trash can, and communicate with it. Under no circumstance should RPC-151 be mishandled, harassed, or abused. Personnel are not to dispose of recyclables in RPC-151.-- //See **Incident Report 151-1.**//

RPC-151 is to be kept in a small (8m x 6m) entity locker. A microphone is to be installed into the locker for communication with RPC-151. All personnel requesting access into the locker must undergo a brief search to ensure no disposable items[[footnote]]Wrappers, broken items, used items, bags, desecrated foodstuffs, and other disposable materiel.[[/footnote]] enter the locker. 

Those desiring to conduct an experiment on RPC-151 with disposable items must request RPC-151 be transported to a suitable testing chamber. Individuals conducting a physical interview outside of a testing chamber must comply with RPC-151 if given orders by the entity until a member of the Security Force can safely escort them out of the locker.

------

**Item Description:** RPC-151 is a decommissioned //Disneyworld// attraction previously known as, "Push the Talking Trash Can." Numerous instances of this attraction were manufactured and distributed across the world to numerous Disney theme parks. However, the attraction was decommissioned on February 14th, 2014. No other cases of anomalous properties manifesting in any of these other instances have been reported. RPC-151 has a small ID inscription on its rear, reading 'Model-██'. Any inanimate object deposited into RPC-151 will be removed by unknown and anomalous processes after 3-5 days have passed. RPC-151 is capable of being damaged by non-anomalous means. It is not known if RPC-151 can be destroyed. 

RPC-151 is capable of self-locomotion, and will wander the space it presides in aimlessly. It will move to avoid obstacles and walls, though. RPC-151 is also capable of speech, which is accompanied by the mechanical movement of its cover flap. When alone, this speech primarily composes of quotes from //Disney// produced films, or statistics about the benefits of recycling. RPC-151 will engage in discussion if prompted, but the contents of this discussion rarely delve beyond the realm of waste management, recycling, and //Disney// related topics.

In the instance an individual deposits a recyclable[[footnote]]Dry paper, cardboard, glass bottles, rigid plastic, and metal containers.[[/footnote]] into RPC-151, it becomes agitated, and will chastise the individual. If a disposable is dropped onto the ground, or placed in an inappropriate spot, RPC-151 will become aggressive and demand the waste be instead put into RPC-151, or a nearby waste bin. If these commands are not followed, RPC-151 will proceed to attack the individual by ramming itself into them. Due to the varying weight of RPC-151 based on its contents, injuries can range from light bruising and scratches, to broken bones.

**File Title:** Incident Report 151-1
------
**Contents:** On 8/██/2017, RPC-151 bore witness to janitorial personnel Robert ███ ██████ dispose of a ███-brand candy wrapper onto the floor. RPC-151 gave immediate command towards Mr. ██████ to place the wrapper in an appropriate waste disposal bin. Mr. ██████ ignored this command, and walked into the nearby washroom. Approximately 1 minute and 17 seconds later, Mr. ██████ can be heard shouting expletives at RPC-151 from the washroom. Mr. ██████ swiftly emerges from the washroom, and is pursued by RPC-151. Mr. ██████ finds himself cornered at the end of the hallway, and [DATA EXPUNGED]. 

Mr. ██████ was treated for four traversely fractured ribs, a dislocated shoulder, and an obliquely fractured tibia. Mr. ██████ had the injury bill deducted out of his paycheck, and was issued a four-month suspension of duties. 
------

**File Title:** Interview Log 151-1
------
**Preface:** On 12/██/2018, Junior Researcher Vincent ███████, under the supervision of Head Researcher Dr. Pedram Adaredi, conducted an interview with RPC-151. Mr. ███████ was properly searched for disposable items, and none were found on his persons. A small plastic chair was also brought into the locker for Mr. ███████ to sit on.

**Interview Log:**
> <Begin Log, 14:27:19, 12/██/2018>
> 
> //Mr. ███████ adjusts the chair into a convenient position, and sits on it.//
> 
> **Mr. ███████:** Hello RPC-151. I'm going to be asking you a few questions. Can you answer them for me?
> 
> **RPC-151:** I always love to help! Did you know that this year people have been producing 8% more trash than last year? It's terrible!
> 
> **Mr. ███████:** I did not know that. That is some curious information. 
> 
> **RPC-151:** Of course! We always love to help! Disappointingly, people are recycling at a rate 2.9% lower than last--
> 
> **Mr. ███████:** We, who is "we"?
> 
> **RPC-151:** It seems that, this year, 2.56 million tons of food waste has been composted, as opposed to 35.14 million tons being thrown into landfills.
> 
> **Mr. ███████:** RPC-151, please do not avoid the question.
> 
> **RPC-151:** I am here to help clean your space clean! If you need any help, don't hesitate to ask us!
> 
> //At this point, Dr. Adaredi notices that Mr. ███████ is chewing on a piece of bubble gum, and signals that the interview be concluded soon.//
> 
> **Mr. ███████:** Once again, who are you referring to when you say "we" and "us"?
> 
> //RPC-151 makes a momentary pause in its usual motion.//
> 
> **RPC-151:** Food makes up about 21.59% of the waste we produce, only second behind paper and cardb--
> 
> //Mr. ███████ attempts to make a bubble with his bubble gum. He fails at this, and propels the chewed bubble gum onto RPC-151, which immediately stops speaking.//
> 
> //RPC-151's speaking manner changes from its usual high-pitched electronic speech to a low and booming voice almost indistinguishable from that of a human.//
> 
> **RPC-151:** You little mind-prying, nosy hooligan. You've gone and dirtied up not only this space but me. I will not tolerate this anymore! Remove this filth from me at once!
> 
> //Mr. ███████ jumps up from his chair, looking over at Dr. Adaredi only to receive a furious scowl. He hastily removes the bubble gum from RPC-151, and cleans up the saliva with his coat.//
> 
> **Dr. Adaredi:** Mr. ███████, please exit the chamber. RPC-151, my apologies for this incident.
> 
> **RPC-151:** This is immature and wasteful, but I accept your apology. Fun fact: In 1960 plastics only made up 0.4% of our total waste, but grew to 10% as of today?
> 
> //Mr. ███████ exits the chamber, and RPC-151 continues to espouse statistics about civil waste management.//
------
//I suspended Mr. ███████ to cleanup duty for the next two months. Because of him, everyone has to have their mouth searched before entering. I believe you can understand why I find that annoying.//

//I've also ordered the confiscation of all other decommissioned models of the attraction. None of them have exhibited anomalous properties, but better safe than sorry.//
-Dr. Pedram Adaredi
