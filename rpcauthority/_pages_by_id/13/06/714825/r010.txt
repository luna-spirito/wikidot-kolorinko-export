+++ **Containment Protocols**

RPC-063 is to be secured in a Class III containment unit with an interlocking door system. Material specifications are to be arranged(?) for a Ruthenium-Aluminum alloy to counteract any ruptures caused by the anomaly's extradimensional properties.

Digital photographs and digital recordings are not to be taken of the anomaly under any circumstances. Any forms of media that resemble RPC-063 are to be confiscated and destroyed immediately. The only photographic image of RPC-063 is to be kept within a sealed folder, accessible only by personnel above Level 3. 

Due to its passive behavior and personality, RPC-063 is to be interviewed biweekly to maintain a social and psychological connection. Personnel requesting to organize interviews must be pre-approved in advance by a Level 4 staff member from the Anthropology Department. 

However, it is to be noted that any requests may be rejected for any reason by Lead Researcher Dr. Harri Victor.

During these interview sessions, personnel with religious affiliations, specifically Catholicism, are to be barred from conducting interviews, including in the proximity of RPC-063's containment unit. 

Any unusual behavior is to be reported to Dr. Victor or a staff member from the Anthropology Department. If behavior has been determined to be life-threatening, the Amber-Red Protocol is to be enacted unless specified otherwise by Site Management. 

//Protocol Update: Due to an incident in 06/1998, containment protocol has been updated. See the attachment below for further details.//

* 2. An Authority quick reaction force element is to be deployed to suppress and reestablish containment in the wake of a Blackfall.

+++ **Description**

[[include component:image-block name= http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-063/RPC-063.jpg| caption= RPC-063 
//(Non-Hazardous Photograph via RPC-268)// | width=300px]]

RPC-063 is a Class IV extradimensional humanoid entity that seems to have manifested sometime during the Protestant Reformation. Measuring approximately 1.43m in height, the subject does not display any form of clothing or discernable physical structure, which can be attributed to its darkened epidermis. 

Due to its unusual skin nature, the subject becomes almost indistinguishable from its facial and physical form, only apparent by its darkened epidermis and body outline. 

The only identifiable features are the subject's distinct radiance of its eyes, specifically the ulna and radius of its eyes. Whether the luminosity is caused by external light exposure is currently unknown. It also sports translucent hands and arms.
Strangely, light does not appear to be absorbed or deflected when any illumination is directed at RPC-063's skin. This results in light passing through the entire body, with light appearing visible through its skin.

RPC-063-1 instances are individuals that were affected by RPC-063. When RPC-063-1 is within the presence of RPC-063, RPC-063 will manifest and influence RPC-063-1 to enter Stage 5 REM sleep.[[footnote]] Rapid Eye Movement (REM) is a phase of sleep within mammals and birds. Stage 5 refers to the "dreaming" stage of sleep. [[/footnote]]

Other physical symptoms include:

* Displaying high blood pressure along with an irregular acceleration of heartbeats. 
* Blood and oxygen flow rapidly decreasing, causing paleness of the skin. 
* Muscle spasms and convulsions around the neck and upper body. 

RPC-063-1 will be unaware that they've entered a state of deep sleep and will visualize random dreams manifested by their own cerebrum. However, these illusions will begin to shift into RPC-063-1's phobias, displayed by RPC-063, who will materialize at random intervals to RPC-063-1 in various appearances but often in their natural form.

RPC-063-1's phobias will develop to a critical point where they will experience circulatory shock, followed by an immediate cardiac arrest. Within hours, RPC-063-1's body will cease to exist and be substituted by an umbra outline of their body.

Photographic samples were taken in 02/1999 of RPC-063's appearance. However, unknowingly, the photographs were cognitohazardous after four out of the five photographists were found unconscious on the floor after suffering a brain hemorrhage.[[footnote]]As of 01/03/19██, no deaths have since been recorded when directly viewing at RPC-063.
[[/footnote]]

Further testing conducted on viewing RPC-063's appearance through photographic images has resulted in death. However, a photo of RPC-063 was successfully captured on film and developed using [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-268 RPC-268]. This developed film was considered non-hazardous, though additional copies using RPC-268 were discovered to be cognitohazardous.[[footnote]]See Addendum 063.04[[/footnote]]

Following a series of tests, various devices revealed different results, each manifesting different effects. It's unclear if this particular photograph was somehow unaffected by RPC-063 or RPC-268's anomalous effects, but results are currently inconclusive.[[footnote]]See Addendum 063.04[[/footnote]]

--It was under the assumption that RPC-063 was incapable of articulating, as evident by RPC-063 illustrating indecipherable messages on the observation glass.-- On 08/████, it was revealed that RPC-063 was capable of speech.[[footnote]]See Addendum 063.03[[/footnote]]

+++ **Addendum 063.01.** [[span style="font-size: 0.80em;"]]Discovery 01/03/19██[[/span]]

[[include component:image-block name= http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-063/RPC-063-1.jpg| caption= RPC-063-1 instances in the distance from the cemetery.| width=225px]]

On January 3, 19██, Head Research Staff Dr. Gabrielle was discovered floating above the ceiling of Site-014's Office Wing. The initial cause of death was inconclusive among medical experts. Security footage captured an unidentified figure, designated as RPC-063, glancing at the camera before the footage abruptly corrupted. 

//Note: Initial personnel who came into contact with the footage were exposed to its cognitohazardous effects and died an hour after inspecting the footage. The footage was briefly confiscated as a result.//

Further investigations uncovered that the attendees at the cathedral church located at [REDACTED], Massachusetts, reportedly disappeared on separate occasions by local authorities. Mobile Specialized Team X-Ray-6 ("Annulifers") were deployed to search the premises. However, X-Ray-6 reportedly encountered Vatican operatives during their investigations.

With the suspicion that the Holy See was involved, the Authority contacted the Church for information concerning RPC-063. Communications between the Authority and the Holy See lasted for two days until the Holy See agreed to distribute reports dating back to the Protestant Reformation.[[footnote]]See Addendum 063.06[[/footnote]]

Following several days of searching, RPC-063 was tracked down at an abandoned cemetery, located not far from the cathedral. A containment team was deployed and managed to contain RPC-063 with no incident. 

However, upon the team's arrival, the Anderson Coherency Scale (ACS) was reported to be irregular by the team.

+++ **Addendum 063.02.** [[span style="font-size: 0.80em;"]]Blackfall Event[[/span]]

//Note: This appendix covers the manifested effects that took place in 06/1998, for information pertaining to the incident; See Addendum 063.05.//

[[collapsible show="📎 Access Attachment 063-02.1" hide="- Close File"]]

Due to indeterminable factors that cannot be accurately described or identified, a Blackfall manifestation cannot be predicted at this time. 

A Blackfall or Blackfall Event refers to a phenomenon manifested by RPC-063. The causes for the phenomena remain widely debated. Dr. Maria Mayson from the Astronomy Department hypothesized that the event may be correlated to the Earth's orbital inclination and a solar eclipse. 

Before the incident on 06/1998, solar eclipses continuously occurred four days in a row; the cause of said eclipses was unexplainable at the time, but during these occurrences the behavior of RPC-063 was reported to have been erratic by security personnel.

//The following is an [theorized] outline event of a Blackfall manifestation, based on information and on-site instrument recordings during the time of the incident://

[[div class="paperGray typewrite1"]]

= **A Study Concerning the manifestation of a Blackfall**
= //Written by the Department of Physics//

= ...

In the event of a solar eclipse, the Anderson Coherency Scale (ACS) levels will begin to sharply shift wildly in intensity, the numbers of which would fall below 1 and above 5. It's to be noted that ACS measurements may be inconsistent and/or unreliable. 

RPC-063 will, if possible, attempt to seek out an enclosed area where it will manifest and reconstruct the occupying space's geometry to a non-Euclidean state.  

Once the occupied space becomes non-Euclidean in nature, an unknown wave of energy will be released and projected within a two-hundred-meter radius, causing any person within the proximity to succumb to a deep state of sleep.

While under this deep state of sleep, persons will begin to gravitate upwards as an unknown phenomenon of black haze develops around their ankle joint that attaches itself to the ground. It is assumed that this phenomenon is present to prevent those affected from drifting away from their current position. 

Due to the intense fluctuations in the area's geometry and shift in non-Euclidean form, the affected area will start to experience a fracture in our reality, causing numerous distortions and spatial decay in the area. It's theorized that when this occurs, our reality may be exposed and opened as a Bridgeway to other realities. 

[[/div]]

Samples taken from the incident were examined by the Nuclear Physics Department. They concluded to not be nuclear material or emit any hazardous radiation. However, large quantities of unidentified particles were detected, but not precisely enough to cause immense harm to personnel. 

While the particles were mostly present in various samples, it is most likely that they may have originated due to the manifestation.

+++ **Addendum 063.03.** [[span style="font-size: 0.80em;"]]Interview 08/24/████[[/span]]

Following its initial discovery, several interviews were administered by Researcher Fraser. Attempts to extract information, such as its history and other relevant knowledge, were initially unsuccessful. 

Previous sessions have demonstrated RPC-063 to be uncooperative, often leaving indecipherable statements on the observation glass.  However, on 08/24/████, RPC-063 was observed to verbally communicate with Researcher Fraser. 

The following is an interview between Researcher Fraser and RPC-063.

[[collapsible show="📎 Access Attachment 063-03.1" hide="- Close File"]]

[[div class="paragraph1"]]

**Interview Log 063.01 -- Audio Transcript**

**Interviewer:** Researcher Emilia Fraser

**Interviewee:** RPC-063

//Note: The interview has been in session for 16 minutes. RPC-063 has been noted to continuously glance and glare at Researcher Fraser for nearly the session's entirety.//

-----

[BEGIN LOG]

**Researcher Fraser:** //[Checks their watch]// Almost 16 minutes to the clock. You haven't said anything to me for the past week. //[Murmurs]// Guess the guys over at containment were right about wasting my time with you.

**RPC-063:** //[Nods forwardly]// Yes. It is how I utter my tongue to you and among!

**RPC-063:** //[Interrupts]// It is not my place to affirm your conclusions, for they may be all an illusion. 

//[Researcher Fraser moves the recorder closer to the observation glass]//

**Researcher Fraser:** Why are you talking to me now? What made you hesitate? 

**RPC-063:** I held no faith in your trust when I was summoned before. That is, until I sensed honesty and efforts for rapport.

**Researcher Fraser:** Do you not trust the Authority? 

//[Interview room lights suddenly flicker rapidly, the observation glass visibly shaking as unidentified whispers became apparent and intensified. RPC-063 is visibly shaken.]//

**RPC-063:** //Auctoritas?//

**Researcher Fraser:** No, no! There's no Auctoritas here. We're friends!

//[Room lights gradually stop flickering as the whispers reverberated and faded]//

**Researcher Fraser:** Okay. Let's avoid //that// question ...

//[Researcher Fraser re-organizes the table and flips through the clipboard]//

**Researcher Fraser:** Where did you come from, 063?

**RPC-063:** I was deceived long ago by my primordial heir, who sent me to a world to face affliction and tear. That is, till I saw an estranged hand reaching out to me, comforting and heaving me from the despairing realm. A beacon of reasoning, he proclaimed, that was against the Church's lust and so mustered me to fight, or I would become dust.

**Researcher Fraser:** Who was this person you're referring to?

**RPC-063:** A protestant man who shook the Pope, aiding a reformation against the Church's strings of rope. 

**Researcher Fraser:** Strings of rope ... //[Pauses]// You were summoned by someone from the Protestant Reformation?

**RPC-063:** Yes, I'd been feared and turned his ethos to be revered!

**Researcher Fraser:** Why were you summoned during that time period, and what did he do with you?

//[RPC-063 does not respond. The room's light repeatedly flickers once more]//

**Researcher Fraser:** 063, can you please answer my question-

**RPC-063:** I wish to not speak further, for I must be an observer.

**Researcher Fraser:** I'm sorry, but what do you mean by that?

//[RPC-063 becomes non-responsive after a minute, and Researcher Fraser terminates the interview shortly after.]//

[END LOG]

**Interviewer's Statement:** //It's progress, I'd say. We haven't had any information from RPC-063 since it was contained, and here I nearly lost my faith in these sessions until today. I wasn't expecting 063 to speak rhythmically at first.//
