**Registered Phenomena Code:** 424

**Object Class:** Alpha-Yellow

**Hazards:** Bio-Hazard, Emotional (RPC-424-A[d]), Explosive (RPC-424-B), Extreme Temperature (RPC-424-B), Microscopic, Organic (Fungal), Regenerative, Sensory (RPC-424-A[d]), Transmutation

**Containment Protocol:** Unused and unopened RPC-424-A and RPC-424-C variants of RPC-424 are kept sealed within their original crates, which are kept in a locked alpha containment room. Standard dehumidification is to be maintained within the room.

RPC-424-B is to be kept in a separate alpha containment room adjacent to that holding the other two, with its interior walls modified for blast-resistance. Absolutely no organic waste is to be left in either room, especially RPC-424-B's.

Usage of any packet requires research authorization and the attendance of a mycologist to assist in the identification of produce. Personnel are not to consume anything produced from RPC-424-A nor C, excluding CSD-class personnel strictly for research purposes. 

Should an RPC-424-A instance produce mushrooms of the //Psilocybe// genus, that instance should be accordingly marked as RPC-424-A[d] and seperated from RPC-424-A instances who do not. Any non-CSD personnel caught consuming RPC-424-A produce, particularly //Psilocybes// from RPC-424-A[d]s, are to be subjected to a disciplinary hearing. 

Experiments involving RPC-424-B are to occur within a medium-grade blast-resistant experimentation chamber (or to take place at an outdoors testing field) with adequate shielding for all on-site personnel.

**Description:** RPC-424 is the formal designation of three packets manufactured by the hostile GoI "Church of Malthus". RPC-424 packets are categorized by the GoI as Packet A, Packet B and Packet C, respectively reclassified by the RPC Authority as RPC-424-A, RPC-424-B and RPC-424-C. All three packets weigh an average of 2g each, with equal dimensions of 100mm x 80mm x 3mm.

One of each RPC-424 instances are bundled together into an opaque plastic sheet alongside non-anomalous utensils[[footnote]] A packeted hand-sanitizing napkin, a biodegradable spork, salt and pepper sachets,  powdered milk-and-coffee mix. [[/footnote]] and are meant to be used in combination with one another. The packets bear no decorative features save applied adhesive labeling bearing a red star and usage/storage instructions. The packets feature a tear line at the top, and are resealable. Each packet's interior is lined with what appears to be a mould-soaked substrate. 

Basic advice printed on the back labels state to keep the packets sealed when not in use, to keep the interior lining moist to prevent any delay in activation, and bear further instructions unique to each packet. Barring minor variations, the instructions for each packet generally state to shake each packet over moist organic material[[footnote]] Pulped wood, mulch substrate, plant/meat matter, etc. [[/footnote]], then wait for a small period of time.

[[tab RPC-424-A]]
[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-424/1-s2.png|width=300px|caption=An assortment of edible fungi produced through the application of RPC-424-A upon the corpse of CSD-5603 (terminated in an unrelated incident).]]

**__Packet A: Solid Food Production__** 

Instructions upon this resealable sachet state to //'...shake packet over moist organic material, wait 10 minutes, then harvest.'// Additionally, a disclaimer states that the packet may be used once every 12 hours, and reminds the user to //"make the most of it"//. A nutritional table lists the basic daily calorific requirements for adults and adolescents, along with a chart of estimated calories from various food sources.[[footnote]] Among others, it is noted that an average human adult male can provide 125k calories. [[/footnote]]

Shaking an opened RPC-424-A causes it to eject spores of an indeterminable genus. Attempts to analyze these spores are unsuccessful due to rapid self-denaturation. Should the packet be shaken directly over a suitable growth substrate as instructed, the substrate is then observed to be rapidly consumed within minutes, producing a diverse assortment of edible fruiting bodies. With the exception of a few packets (see below), the fungi produced all belong to edible species and are rich in nutrients, vitamins and calories. 

The mechanism of growth defies logic as the base substrate can be completely incompatible with the growth of non-anomalous edible fungi; tests with conventional mushroom substrate, vegetative mulch, decaying wood, rotting meat and human corpses produce generally similar results. Interestingly, an experiment that involved "luxury goods" substrates was observed to fruit an overwhelming majority of fungi commonly perceived as "high-end gourmet food"[[footnote]] A mixture of fresh wagyu beef, caviar and pink ivory mulch translated into an abundance of King Boletes, Chanterelles, Matsutakes, Morels, European White and Black Truffles. [[/footnote]].

Due to experiment failures, it remains a hypothesis that the originating spores are some form of "predecessor" within the fungi kingdom's phylogenetic tree, designed to rapidly convert the base substrate into a more compatible growth medium before differentiating between Ascomycotic and Basidiomycotic fruiting bodies. Document 424-1 suggests that a fungus-themed faction within the Church of Malthus' Microbes Division "pre-programs" these spores with a "genetic memory" of the edible fungi that they may grow into upon usage.

Certain RPC-424-As are observed to produce mushrooms of the //Psilocybe// genus, capable of producing Psilocybin and inducing the expected psychedelic state in consumers. Document 424-1 implies this to be due to internal negligent tampering with the RPC-424-A creation process. As such, RPC-424-As found to be capable of this feat (Current est. less than 20% of the seized quantity) are labeled accordingly as **defective** (RPC-424-A**[d]**) and separated from the others.

[[tab RPC-424-B]]
[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-424/Scarlet_elf_cap_cadnant_dingle.jpg|width=300px|caption=//Sarcoscypha coccinea//, believed to be the base template of RPC-424-B's organism.]]

**__Packet B: Heater Unit__** 

Features flammable and explosive hazard symbol labeling. Instructions state to seed RPC-424-B spores on moist organic substrate, wait for the "heater unit" to finish growing, then to insert food into cavity and pinch the opening shut, maintain a safe distance of 5 meters and await the reopening of the cavity. Additional instructions list a range of substrate quantity/quality for best results, and to proportionally increase/decrease said substrates for greater/lesser amounts of food requiring cooking. A warning is mentioned to not supply more than 3kg of substrate per growth.

Upon performing the instructions, RPC-424-B deposits spores that lead to clustered growth of nodules. These nodules appear to detect and converge onto each other, forming a spherical scarlet fruiting body with a hollow interior. While bearing resemblances to the Scarlet Elf Cup mushroom, this variant features a significantly greater size, shorter stem, reinforced hooping surrounding a thick and sturdy cup and a malleable adhesive membrane surrounding the upper edges of the cup.

Upon sealing the membrane shut, a reaction is induced within the cup that generates an exothermic reaction that may reach up to ███ degrees Celsius. The mushroom neutralizes itself in this process, and once sufficiently cooled the cup can be broken off from its now-brittle stem and opened to reveal the "cooked" contents.

When grown on substrate beyond the 3kg limit, the organism generated becomes increasingly unstable in proportion to its increased size, with its exothermic reaction possibly resulting in either a conflagration that consumes the entire cup, or an explosion. The most powerful explosion so far is estimated to match 12kg of TnT, with RPC-424-B being seeded on a whole cow carcass as an experiment.[[footnote]] Due to insufficient safety preparations, █ research personnel were killed during observation with a further ██ casualties. Safety guidelines involving RPC-424-B testing have been updated. [[/footnote]]

Attempts to understand the mechanisms of this extreme bioexothermic mechanism are ongoing. Dissection of non-activated specimens do not reveal any spore-producing tissues.
[[/tab]]
[[tab RPC-424-C]]
[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-424/iu.png|width=300px|caption=A control sample of brackish water side-by-side with water filtered through RPC-424-C's organism.]]

**__Packet C: Hydration__**

The instructions state to shake the RPC-424-C packet onto any body of water (slower process with higher yield), or soggy/moisture-rich organic material (faster process but potentially reduced yield) matching standard room temperatures.

Upon performing the instructions, RPC-424-C deposits spores that form a film across water bodies/wet substrate. This film gradually thickens to produce an organism that appears based on puffball fungi. One primary "puffball" is produced, with smaller buds possibly forming around its base. This growth appears to visibly dehydrate the substrate it is grown upon. The primary "puffball" growth adopts a faintly translucent texture upon maturation, becoming partly rubbery in the process. This nodule can be removed from the growth without rupture/spillage.

The topmost tip of the "puffball" can be snapped off to squeeze out filtered water, or the puffball can be carefully retained as a portable single-use water sac in the absence of suitable storage containers. The membrane is fairly resistant to moisture loss and damage, though it can still be ruptured with a sufficiently sharp object/amount of force. 

In testing RPC-424-C with various forms of liquids polluted with organic and chemical waste, the water extracted from RPC-424-C has been observed to meet all potability tests while being free of toxins, heavy metals and microbes. Inspection of the fungal base post-filtration shows heavy concentrations of pollutants, contained within an elaborate tubular myconic filtration system.

**Discovery Log:** Ongoing intel gathering on Church of Malthus activity suggested increased movement around an established Church of Malthus front, previously dismissed and kept under minimal observation for being disused. Visual observation showed a frequency spike of visiting Malthusian operatives, typically arriving in trucks, vans and cars to deposit various boxes and cargo.

With suspicions arising that the Church of Malthus may be stockpiling biological weaponry for an attack, a motion was unanimously passed to raid the premises. A collaboration of MST Echo-8 "Plague Doctors" and MST Foxtrot-04 "Prey" stormed the location, only to find it undefended and understaffed. Rather than fight the operatives, the sole Malthusian personnel on-site chose to flee the premises.

A detailed sweep of the premises revealed this "abandoned church" was hastily repurposed into a holding facility for what could possibly be considered a product recall, as evidenced by a single note seized from the fled personnel's quarters:
