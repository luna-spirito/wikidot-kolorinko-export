+++ Tips to using this format:

* Be very clinical when you work and don't use words like "don't" use "do not" and such
* Use a lot of footnotes and bibliography (check ACS Dreamscape for an example on the latter)
* Try to use or make up scientific terms, or look real ones up and try to apply them to the anomaly

+++ CODENAME: WOOD CHIPS
[[/=]]
[[=]]
Dr. Roger Eberton and Dr. Michael Mcdonald
Research Division - OAS's Physics Department
█████, ███
[[/=]]
[[=]]
**ABSTRACT**
[[/=]]
The following document is a study on RPC-9999, its properties, reality of origin, scientific explanation for its anomalous properties.

This document also contains a newly found object similar in nature to RPC-9999, as well as an explanation for it. Despite current understanding, testing on this anomaly shall continue.
//Keywords:// RPC-9999, RS, RI, Subatomic, Extra-Dimensional

-----
[[=]]
**INTRODUCTION**
[[/=]]
RPC-9999 is one of the oldest anomalies the Authority is in possession of, and one of the most mysterious ones at that, having baffled our every scientist with its incoherent properties, and not only its anomalous ones. Most of our understanding of RPC-9999 comes from old research papers, going as far back as when the Authority was still under the rule of the papacy.

In this document, we will analyze RPC-9999 with the latest technology available to us, as well as the use of other non-conventional objects for this goal. We have discovered a lot from RPC-9999 over our time as an organization, such as its Reality Signature (RS) being that of RS1>00.000.01 regardless of its state or condition, the fact the wood making it's contents seems to withhold the test of time, and the countless theories regarding its interaction with subatomic particles as a means to explain the properties it possesses. But despite all of this, we yet lack a full understanding of its properties and origins, unlike the many objects we have come to study.

-----
[[=]]
**MATERIALS AND METHODS**
[[/=]]
The M&M section of this document will focus on 3 main aspects: A complete and accurate reading of RPC-9999’s Reality Signature (RS) reading with newly developed scanners and tools, a study of RPC-9999’s properties at a sub-atomic level for a plausible explanation for its transmutable properties, and a study of RPC-9999's origins, if it has any.

//**Reality Signature Reading:**//

Reality Signature (RS) readings on RPC-9999 have always been a mystery due to their unusual results as stated before. This was thought to be due to underdeveloped tools on the field, with theories stating that it may also possess extra-dimensional properties. However, recent testing with AB55-RSR machinery has shown both of these assumptions to be incorrect.

Unlike any other object or location known to the Authority, RPC-9999’s RS has stayed the same, regardless of any influence placed upon it. Even mundane objects show slight differences in their RS readings due to several factors (gravitational influence, mass changes, element decay) but RPC-9999 remains stable at RS1>00.000.01. However, unlike other objects, this exorbitant reading does not exhibit the same properties as a normal object with it would. Normally, such a reading would mean that the object is only hardy existing in our reality, without taking into account present Reality Decay (RD) theories, which could cause it to decay into its basic components. RPC-9999, however, stays with the same basic coherency as any normal object would.
Should a dimension with RPC-9999’s RS reading exist, it would appear as a black void, devoid of any matter, where the smallest influence forced upon it could cause massive shifts in the immediate reality. This theory has been widely accepted by most of Research Division, as it would fit with the unknown letter’s description found within RPC-9999.

//**RPC-9999’s Interaction with Particles in a Sub-Atomic Level:**//

Thanks to the latest developments in non-photonic recording devices, we are able for the first time in history to witness RPC-9999’s effects on Sub-Atomic particles without having to worry about the uncertainty principle as a nuisance.
The following will be a line of events taking place during this test:

1- A single splinter is removed from RPC-9999, it is then covered in tracking liquid for accurate readings using E.M.O.N.D cameras.
2- A 5 centimeter^^3^^ block of wood is placed upon the test panel.
3- A robotic arm takes a single simpler, which it then uses to puncture the woodblock at a speed of 35mph.

Results:

Upon being introduced into the test object, the splinter begins to fade away as per normal expectations. However, thanks to E.M.O.N.D cameras, we can see the true reaction taking place upon the woodblock. 3 milliseconds after puncturing, matter originating from the splinter is seen being unbounded from each other, before completely enveloping the test object.

4 milliseconds after puncturing, the object’s RS readings seem to take those normally associated with RPC-9999. But unlike it, the object seems to become immediately subject to the forces such a reading would cause, oscillating between signatures until stabilizing at baseline levels. However, before this can occur, the object begins to split in two as expected. Our current theories hold that thanks to RPC-9999's naturally high Reason Index,^^[((bibcite reasoning))]^^ the object reacts to the sudden change in its coherency by splitting into two similar entities (with some matter being lost due to the energy required for this process) of similar making. To be noted, 1 millisecond before creating, the wooden block seemed to vanish from the test panel, as to where it went before manifesting the 2 new instances, we don’t know, as both objects lacked any type of residual RS.

//**Reality Signature Origin Tracking:**//

As stated before, RPC-9999’s signature is RS1>00.000.01, and this value has never changed in the course of the Authority's history, which would objectively place the reality it originates from a prime candidate for exploration, requiring minimal math to open a spatial rift towards the location. However, due to it also being such an infinitely low value, this reality would not be able to exist at all, but as seen with the existence of RPC-9999, it does. And if a spatial rift of any kind were to be open towards this reality, the difference between baseline signatures and this location would be so drastically different that it could cause damage to the fabric of reality, however, the creation of a brief enough rift could allow for any objects in the other end to access out reality, and if following the characteristics of RPC-9999, remain stable.

The following will be a line of events taking place during the test:

1- A vacuum chamber containing a single rift mechanism was prepared for the test to prevent cross-contamination or dangerous exposure to extra-dimensional material.
2- The coordinates for RPC-9999’s RS were remotely placed in the mechanism, the half-life for the rift was set to 0.5 seconds.
3- The spatial rift was activated, and the rift opened. Slow-motion cameras show a single piece of paper being sucked from the rift thanks to the vacuum, no discernible features can be made from the rift, only black.

Results:

Despite a common theory that this test would yield no results at all, a single item from this reality was able to get brought to baseline with only minimal damage to the surrounding coherency. How a document like the one collected, or even paper, were able to exist in this reality is yet unknown and more tests would be needed. The contents of the recovered document can be found in a separate file.

-----
[[=]]
**RESULTS**
[[/=]]

The results from this test have allowed us to shed new light on RPC-9999, its effects, origins, and significance for the Authority as per the facts here present:

|| RPC-9999 || RS1>00.000.01 || 5 ||
|| Recovered Document || RS1>00.000.01 || 5 ||
[[=]]
Basic Readings
[[/=]]

It was also shown that the way in which RPC-9999 functions can be explained by currently known laws of physics and extra-dimensional theorems understood by the Research Division. As such, discoveries brought forth by RPC-9999 may be subject to distribution within the general public.

Despite these incredible discoveries, we still have little information about the origins of RPC-9999, who made it, who found it, and who lost it, only to be found again by ASF personnel within the hallways of now long-gone sites. These questions will remain unanswered until a time where we develop advanced enough technology to fully explore the reality from which RPC-9999 originated from.

-----
[[=]]
**DISCUSSION:**
[[/=]]

Finally understanding RPC-9999 enough to consider parts of its information available for public distribution is a great success within our department's modern history. But despite knowing so much, we still know so little, and with the discovery of this document from RPC-9999’s reality, a new door has opened into the possibility that we still have much to learn about how dimensions as a whole work, possibly even starting the resurgence of the Constants Theory^^[((bibcite constant))]^^ as a whole.

As always, our work is not yet done, and we must strive to find the answers hidden from us.

Making sense of the impossible.

[[/=]]

[Everything else regarding the anomaly that does not fit on the previous sections such as interview logs, exploration or encounter logs, and current operations rearding the anomaly.]

+++ Tips for this format/tone:

* You don't wanna go to much on tangents or use any type or purple prose, this is meant to deliver information quicly to someone who needs it.
* As before, keep it clinical.
* You don't need to call it an RPC yet, just an event, anomaly, object, or animal.

[[div class="revisionStamp"]]
RBOA 21 MARCH 2010
[[/div]]
[[=]]
Dr. Federico Santos and Cpt. Fath Abdo
Research Division - Office of Analysis and Science: Biology Department - Fungal Research Sub-Department
Protection Division - Authority Central Intelligence: DEP-010
Souq al-Hamidiyah, Damascus, Syria
[[/=]]
[[=]]
**DISCOVERY**
[[/=]]

[[include component:image-block name=https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/457426775031676950/780442374228279326/Soul_Truffles.jpg|caption=Fig. 1 taken with a concealed camera]]

On March the 3rd, 2010, Authority inside agent ████ informed AFRICOM's DEP-010 personnel of a possible anomalous object making its way into the local black market, being nicknamed "Dijin Hopes" by standing merchants. ████ bought a single instance for research and classification.

The instance in question, now classified as //Terfezia spectris//, is an unknown species of truffle similar in appearance to a normal desert truffle (//Terfeziaceae//) when dried, that being the state ████ bought it in. The truffle was being sold alongside a jar containing a blue liquid, similar to bee honey. ████ was unable to afford this substance, supposedly required to activate the "magical properties" of the truffles, due to having run out of funds.

//Terfezia spectris// at first glance seem to be able reflect 90% of the visible light spectrum with the exception of blue, causing its characteristic blue hue. Upon consumption, the truffle appears to produce a psychotronic effect upon the subject's brain similar to common hallucinogenic fungi. Finally, //Terfezia spectris// have been described as smelling like “Oak mixed with sugar” by personnel. It should be noted that, for these properties to become active, the truffle must be rehydrated.

Further testing with the unknown blue substance is required upon future acquisition. Efforts to locate the original distributor of the truffles is underway in conjunction with Protection's ACI DEP-010, led by Cpt. Fath Abdo.

-----
[[=]]
**BACKGROUND**
[[/=]]

[[include component:image-block name=https://www.mssf.org/cookbook/graphics/Truffle.jpg|caption=Fig. 2 |align=left]]

@@@@
@@@@

Insider information claims that, despite having only made its way to the common black market, it had been a common commodity within anomalous spheres of commerce for decades. The reason for its sudden shift into the former is yet unknown.

Sightings and documentation of the truffle itself is scarce, but records of it have been found in EASTCOM's "Archival of Paranormal Documentation" dating back to 1889 when biologist and fungi enthusiast Gregory Kuznetsov reported the sighting of a new species of truffles (Fig. 2) found deep within an unnamed Russian forest.

@@@@
@@@@

Further information is unknown, as records of Kuznetsov's work stop shortly after the making of this document. His disappearance has yet to be correlated to this discovery, and not simple casualty.

-----
[[=]]
**PRELIMINARY MEASURES**
[[/=]]

In accordance with the "Middle Eastern Non-Anomalous Intervention Act" of 1950, Authority officials within the area are authorized to cease and desist all objects of anomalous found within the area provided by ████ with the support of local Syrian authorities. Anomalous objects are then to be transported to the nearest Authority Site available.

ACI - DEP-010 personnel are then to track and locate the original distributor of the truffles, and if possible, their source. Once located, ASF will be deployed for preliminary containment of the anomaly.

Instances of the blue substance are to be delivered to Authority Sites with truffles in storage for their combined testing.

-----
[[=]]
**ASSET VALUE**
[[/=]]

It is currently too early to devise a clear economic or scientific value that these truffles may hold, aside from their sole existence as a new species. Several researchers studying this instance have come to an argument about the true nature of the truffles as an object that is anomalous in nature, or just an undiscovered property that species of fungi can acquire naturally, sprawling from the fact that fungi species are still heavily understudied across the world.

As for its economical value, the “Authority Statue#1329” prohibits the marketing or distribution of recreational drugs that are anomalous in origin, and its recreational use for personnel has shown no signs of increased productivity or anomalous levels of insight. Although further research is needed to comprehend the chemical making of the truffles and their possible application in the medical field.

[[bibliography title="References"]]
: reasoning : **Reasoning Index:** The measure by which the coherency and stability of an anomaly’s properties are quantified in a scale of 0 to 5. The higher the Index, the more “sense” its effects will make, the lower the index, the more random and unpredictable they will be.
: constant : **Constants Theory:** The notion that, despite radical differences between each other, all realities have several concepts or objects that stay consistent between one another, even when this would not be normally allowed under currently understood laws of extra-dimensional physics, e.g. Canada. Due to a lack of concrete evidence, this theory has been dismissed by the major part of the anomalous scientific community.
