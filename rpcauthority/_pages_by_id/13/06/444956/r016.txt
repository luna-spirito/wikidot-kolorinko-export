**Containment Protocols:** An airtight 46m^^3^^ steel cube has been erected around RPC-336 in OL-Site-336.[[footnote]]OL-Site-336 is a [[[Site-004]]] On-Location Site.[[/footnote]] with one steel door placed at the cell's entrance. The cube's walls should be 5 inches thick to minimalize breaches. A humidity level of 60% is to be maintained at all times within the cube to neutralize the anomalous properties of RPC-336-2. Should the humidity levels inside the chamber show a humidity level of 75% or higher, all CSD and ASF personnel are to be evacuated or otherwise equipped with gas masks and/or sufficient breathing apparatuses. 

The same containment protocols are required for the humanoid entities designated RPC-336-1A that arise from RPC-336. The tentacles that may occasionally arise from RPC-336, designated RPC-336-1B, must be kept in RPC-336 using an electric fence that borders RPC-336's edges. All subjects who have expired inside RPC-336's containment must be recovered as quickly as possible to avoid "feeding" RPC-336-1B.

[[collapsible show="Show Containment Attempt [FAILED]" hide="Close"]]
> RPC-336 is to be contained by a 3 layer steel covering over the surface at OL-Site-336. Cameras are to be placed within RPC-336 to monitor all appearances of the tentacle-like appendages designated RPC-336-1B. Cameras are also to be placed on the surface of RPC-336 to monitor the integrity of said covering and to record breaches. Two ASF officers are to be patrolling RPC-336 to fend off RPC-336-1B momentarily in the instance of a breach. An O2 monitor is placed on the edge of the covering for any instance of the O2 level decreasing in that area. In the event of O2 levels dropping all personnel are to be evacuated or put on sufficient breathing apparatuses. 
[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="Show Containment Attempt [FAILED] Reason" hide="Close"]]
> This attempt was a failure due to RPC-336-1B adapting to this method and manifesting multiple sets of tentacle-like appendages to force its way out allowing RPC-336-1A and RPC-336-1B to breach causing multiple ASF casualties. A new containment method is to be in place describing a cube with air being pumped into it to level out RPC-336-2's effects and to minimalize pressure. Although RPC-336-6A will not be securely contained within RPC-336, it was later found that they expire quickly. The protocol affects RPC-336-1B by containing RPC-336 in such a large container that RPC-336-1B will not be able to reach its surroundings.
[[/collapsible]]
------

**Description:** RPC-336 is a backyard in-ground swimming pool of indeterminate depth, with only 5km having been explored so far. From an unidentified point in RPC-336 anomalous vapor is exuded, designated RPC-336-2.

RPC-336-2 has a potent mind-controlling effect on victims inhaling it: affected subjects will be driven to enter RPC-336 and swim downward until they lose consciousness via asphyxiation or excess pressure. Once the subject's vital functions cease while in this state, they will begin a gradual transformative reanimation process.

Reanimated subjects, designated RPC-336-1A, lack facial features and bodily hair. Their skin will form a blueish pigmentation and will have a similar texture to bone with separations for the joints needed to retain movement. While RPC-336-1A loses most of their original senses, they still retain spatial awareness and balance after their reanimation. They also preserve their capability of intelligent thought and can perceive human beings that are in close proximity to RPC-336. Other vital functions such as sleeping and eating are lost during the reanimation process.

RPC-336-1A instances have been observed to patrol RPC-336 when not being interacted with. After a continuous 15-minute patrol, RPC-336-1A will abruptly expire.

Should an individual be within 1.5m of RPC-336-1A, it will become hostile and attempt to kill the subject in order to drag them into RPC-336. Doing so will cause the lifespan of RPC-336-1A to extend by 15 minutes.

RPC-336 contains an unknown collective amount of tentacles pigmented red or orange. These tentacles can occasionally be seen extending from an indeterminate point to the surface.

It was observed that when a subject expires within RPC-336,  their body is some kind of food source for RPC-336-1B, causing them to grow in strength, size, and population. This also causes RPC-336-1B to surface more in quicker intervals.

If anything were to go through the walls such as drilling, exploding, and mining, RPC-336 will only appear to be a 1.5-meter deep pool. But once subjects who observed this event surface and observe the pool once again, it will go back into its infinite state. This will trigger RPC-336-1B to surface and attempt to terminate all surrounding life as if it was harmed.

> //Note: RPC-336-1A and RPC-336-1B only respond to human/organic material. Thus far no reaction is shown to the drones by RPC-336-1A and RPC-336-1B. However, RPC-336-1B will not be visible to drones or any camera equipment not attached to a human subject, nor will it attempt to make an appearance or attack the drone.//

[[collapsible show="Incident Report 336-1" hide="Incident Report 336-1"]] 
> **Date: October 12^^th^^ 2013** 
> **Personnel Involved: CSD-3385**
> **Events transpired: ASF officer inhaled RPC-336's fumes and killed other officers.** 
> CSD-3385 was sent to RPC-336 for testing but before CSD-3385 could enter the chamber, A gas leak breach sounded an alarm as RPC-336's gas started leaking out of RPC-336's containment. An ASF officer went to inspect the leak but the gas mask equipped to him had a small hole in the bottom-most region and caused him to inhale the fumes, mind-controlling him. The ASF officer started walking into RPC-336's containment. Once inside he walked into RPC-336 and started swimming downward, at about 4.5 meters down the ASF officer had not been seen for 20 minutes. After 20 minutes past the ASF officer surfaced and appeared to have no face and had blue skin and walked as if he was intoxicated. When the surrounding ASF Units aimed their guns, RPC-336-1A became extremely hostile and killed most of the units. 
[[/collapsible]]

------

[[collapsible show="Incident Report 336-2" hide="Incident Report 336-2"]]
> **Date: October 23^^rd^^**
> **Personnel Involved: ASF Officer** 
> **Events Transpired: RPC-336-1B surfaced and became hostile against an ASF officer.**
> A single on-duty ASF officer was running usual security checks inside RPC-336 making sure there weren't any breaches and he heard movement in the water, RPC-336-1B surfaced and gripped the ASF officer and started using blunt force to likely damage the ASF officer's cranium, rib cage, and several vital organs at a speed of ████. After several minutes of the ASF officer suffering fatal blunt force trauma, RPC-336-1B threw the ASF officer onto the containment chamber floor and crushed most of his bones and he suffered multiple fatal organ lacerations. After the death of the ASF officer, RPC-336-1B dragged the deceased ASF officer into RPC-336.  He suffered internal bleeding until he expires 3 minutes after the event. 2 minutes after that officer had died reinforcements showed up late.
[[/collapsible]]
