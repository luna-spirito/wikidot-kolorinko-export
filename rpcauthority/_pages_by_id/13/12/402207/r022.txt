Squads must report attempted containment breaches and respond immediately, but in the event of multiple RPC-611-1 instances, teams must wait for assistance before engaging. Personnel cannot engage multiple RPC-611-1 instances without sufficient support. **Description:** RPC-611 is the Yuaskyag Corrective Labor Camp (Russian: горный-трудовой медицинский лагерь) and the approx. 1,800km^^2^^ area surrounding its epicenter. RPC-611 is located within the Magadan Oblast (Russian: Магаданская область) of the Russian Far East. The area of RPC-611 has been experiencing a slow decline in coherency and expanding for the past {{59}} years as of {{2/1/19}}. It is estimated that RPC-611 will reach a coherency of 0.01 and encompass the entirety of the Magadan Oblast by {{6/4/21}}.

RPC-611-1 are (assumed) former humans who have been produced by the processes of RPC-611-2. While the specifics of their nature and anatomy (if they possess one) are pending study, it is understood that RPC-611-1 instances are incorporeal and intangible. Observations of their behavior suggest instances suffer from bipolar schizoaffective disorder.

RPC-611-1 instances are unpredictable in their behavior, although they are to be assumed hostile at all times. A distance of no less than 25 meters must be kept at all times to ensure the safety of personnel. Under no circumstances are personnel to approach an instance of RPC-611-1 unless ordered by senior personnel.

RPC-611-1 instances are capable of limited telekinesis, contact-based (and possibly ranged) telepathic suggestion, possession of inanimate objects or living beings, as well as movement through any material. RPC-611-1 instances are possibly capable of limited manipulation of coherency levels in a specific area, raising it higher or lower. Pardon limited telepathic communication that cannot communicate in ways more complex than emotions or single words, RPC-611-1 instances cannot speak.

RPC-611-1 instances are humanoid in appearance but lack any and all discernable features, such as facial features, hair, eyes, and any visible body parts beyond the forearms or the lower leg. While transparent, RPC-611-1 instances give off a blue "glow" when observed.

RPC-611-2 is a large mechanical device designed by Hermann Vogt and constructed in 1959. The make and manufacture of RPC-611-2 are currently unknown. RPC-611-2 is currently located in the main medical building of the Yuaskyag Corrective Labor Camp, which itself lies near the epicenter of RPC-611.

RPC-611-2 is capable of creating instances of RPC-611-1 when provided with a human subject. While the process is currently unrecorded, it is understood that a human subject, once interred, can be used to produce an instance of RPC-611-1 over the course of an hour. How the machine is operated, how the instance is created, and the device's composition is unknown. RPC-611-2 cannot create more than one RPC-611-1 instance from a single human subject, and the original subject is rendered permanently comatose by the device.

No methods known to the Authority are able to reverse this operation.

[[collapsible show="[+] Addendum 2: RPC-611 History:" hide="[-] Close"]]

> RPC-611-2, or the “Cordatus Circuit” as it is called in [*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/monotony-and-alpha-1 recovered documents], is a device that is capable of seemingly paracausal creation of entities both intangible and incorporeal. But RPC-611-2 is not the first device of its kind, simply the most recent version. During the Third Reich, a doctor going by the name of Hermann Vogt had developed a machine not unlike the RPC-611-2 we know now in a concentration camp, the name of which has been expunged from all historical records.
> 
> We believe he was experimenting on the available populace for reasons that might have been military, medical, or otherwise, commissioned by the //Schwarze Sonne// of GARD[[footnote]][*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/gard-hub German Anomalous Research Division], a division of the German National Socialist Worker's Party created to study and apply anomalies and the occult.[[/footnote]] for a classified project. The project the machine was produced for was dubbed simply “Zwangslotse”, and the translation, “compulsory pilot”, suggests that Vogt was aware of the abilities of the entities prior to his arrival in the camp, but we do not know how or why.
> 
> The device he constructed was the primogenitor to the Cordatus. This machine was capable of the same paracasual creation, but the entities produced apparently “dissolved in moments” once “removed” from the victim of the machine. However, Vogt found that the entities could be coerced, via memes and cognitohazards, into what can best be described as possession of inanimate objects. The entities would be able to inhabit these objects and manipulate them to a limited degree, without dissipating. These experiments proved fruitful, but never able to be mass-produced. Nonetheless, it resulted in several anomalies that are not necessarily relevant to the history of RPC-611 insofar as the document is classified to contain.
> 
> This camp was near the Eastern Front. Once it was taken by the Russians, the soldiers found radios that screamed, televisions that cried, and lamps blinking in morse code, locked away in cells. The soldiers burned the camp to the ground. Conveniently, most, if not all witnesses we didn’t grab first died of a panic attack or PTSD-related delusions not too long after. However, Vogt escaped to Berlin long before the Soviets had arrived.
> 
> And once Berlin was in shambles, he was taken to the Soviet scientists, likely operating with the Iron Initiative[[footnote]] Anomalous research and application division of the USSR. [[/footnote]], who were very interested in this “Zwangslotse” program they’ve heard about. The deal was simple. Political asylum for scientific aid. The Russians brought him to the Magadan Oblast to perform his next series of experiments. This time, they wanted observers. Recon agents and the like. The Zwanglotse proved too unstable and unreliable, but a soldier that could walk through walls, never sleep, never eat, never die? That would prove useful. He constructed a new machine, the Cordatus Circuit, and this one could preserve the original body, supposedly a sort of link for the, ah, spirit to not dissolve as the others did. By 1959, it was in full operation.
> 
> However, the power of suggestion wasn’t yet discovered, and the creature’s ability to tap into the mind wasn’t detected until it was too late. The takeover was swift, and Vogt found himself once again at the mercy of the Russians, although in this case, the Russians were tortured inmates who found themselves unable to return to their original bodies at the hands of this doctor, and they had no intent to spare him for the sake of science. He was gutted like an animal.
> 
> It didn’t take long for the Soviets to find out, of course. No need to waste men, though. A swift bombing run should take care of it. The Russians thought that the comatose bodies kept the ghosts alive. Kill the bodies, the ghosts dissipate. This was proven incorrect, once the majority of the camp was reduced to rubble. Something in the machine's design is keeping them alive, and that machine is, according to the dossier, several levels beneath the main medical building.
> 
> That brings us to now. The collapse of the USSR has led to several anomalies being left uncontained and unheard of, and once we tracked down the Iron Initiative scientists that worked on the project, they let us know that they have been unable to contain a machine that threatens to melt most of midwestern Eurasia, and that it was effectively now our problem.
> 
> //- Site Director Daniel Reikowski//
