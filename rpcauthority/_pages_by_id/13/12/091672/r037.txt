**Galactic Registered Phenomena:** 014

**Object Class:** ##FFE100|Omega-Yellow## 

**Hazard Types:** Contact Hazard, Destabilization Hazard, Extra-Terrestrial Hazard, Teleportation Hazard, Sensory Hazard, Extra-Dimensional Hazard
----
**Containment Protocol:** GRP-014 no longer exists within our reality, and therefore containment is impossible. If contact is re-established, a blockade of 12 GRPC Jupiter-3[[footnote]]Standard Issue GRPC Rocket, one of the fastest man-made crafts.[[/footnote]] ships must be imposed around GRP-014 as soon as possible. This blockade has permission to halt any encroaching vessels from contacting GRP-014 by any means they deem necessary.   
----
**Description:** GRP-014-1 is an anomalous asteroid, 3.4 miles / 5.44 km at its widest, consisting of 80% iron, 5% nickel, 3% gold, and 2% platinum, taken from an automated mineral sample during GRPC Jupiter-3's touchdown. Rubidium-strontium dating of GRP-014-1’s materials places the age of the asteroid at 50,000 years old and potentially even older. The inside of GRP-014-1 has been hollowed out by unknown means, making room for GRP-014, a monolithic temple-like structure carved from the rock by unfamiliar tools. Carvings of some cosmic beast, humanoids, and nonhumanoid figures adorn the interior tunnels of GRP-014-1 and the exterior of GRP-014. Archeologists hypothesize that the carvings symbolize a ritualistic sacrifice of alien origin.

GRP-014 displays anomalous properties only when a sentient entity enters its field of effect. It's theorized this field pertains to GRP-014-1 and a 100mi / 160km radius encompassing it. Proximity to GRP-014 increased the likelihood of destabilization hazards, including removing matter from the universe, allowing humans to survive in a vacuum, and memetic-induced psychosis. The closer one gets to the source of GRP-014, the stronger these effects will manifest themselves. An eyewitness report claimed that the interior of GRP-014 was home to a haunting of incorporeal aliens. No video recordings can prove these sightings, but the odd circumstances surrounding its discovery add some legitimacy to the claim.

GRPC MSgt Ranger Denis Joh and Rocketeer Sgt Vanta Ngai sortied from GRPC Station 007 at Standard Galactic Time [Redacted] to the Vega system. Issued an FTL Drunkmenn Drive, with twice the amount of Drunkmenn Microbes than in a standard-issue Jupiter-3 Rocket. The two made a distance of 13.6 Light Years in 19,272 hrs/34 min/23 sec. Upon approaching the Vega system, both GRPC rangers reported a feeling of “being watched” and “an unusual feeling of Deja Vu'' about the situation.  

While looking for a spot to land, GRPC Rocketeer Vanta Ngai spotted the heat signature of a  spacecraft, which had its engines still running—parked on the dark side of the asteroid. Upon approach, a visual of the vessel was made. The craft was small, with dimensions of 200ft / 60.96m wide, 240ft / 73.152m long, and 180ft / 54.864m tall. It had two cylindrical thrusters on each side, a smooth rectangular body, and sported a bright pinkish-purple coloration. The GRPC Rangers, fearing a potential pirate threat, issued a status report to the GRPC NullCom Highland observation outpost in case of the worst possible outcome. 

Landing 570ft / 173.736m away, both Rangers hiked over to the craft to explore its interior. It consisted of an engine room on the top overlooking the two thrusters, a loading bay in the rear positioned between the two engines, and an enormous, lavish bedroom in the front, adorned with pseudo-Greco-roman architecture, including busts of Alexander the Great. Paraphernalia of a fictional ’90s timeline that did not undergo Sol Wars lined the walls, and a bathroom connected to the bedroom was plastered with palm tree wallpaper. Font types used were irregular, with an extraordinary effort put into aesthetics, yet were surprisingly in English. 

There were no manual control systems. Instead, all operations were carried out by an unknown AI known as Windows 9500, which made its presence known when GRPC Rocketeer Ngai jumped onto the bed, asking if he wished the bedroom lights to be adjusted. Questioning Windows 9500 made it clear that it had kept the ship operational since its captain's departure 146 days ago. In that time, it had reportedly fixed the life support systems two times and shot at a total of 15 incoming asteroids. Upon further questioning the AI's personal favorite colors and opinions on mundane topics, Windows 9500 was diagnosed as non-sentient and non-anomalous, making any moral or lawful questions about reprogramming null. 

After verbally reprogramming Windows 9500, whose protocol was changed from [follow the orders of anyone lying down in bed] to [follow orders from anyone wearing a GRPC logo], nothing else was noted as being of interest, aside from a small palm tree that was believed to have gone extinct after the extinction of all life on Earth. Both rangers took the palm tree back to the GRPC craft to determine if it was genuine or a synthetic copy.  

During their examination, both Space Rangers picked up two different Mk1 Drunkmen Drive signatures emanating just 2.1 miles away from GRP-014-1. Both ships fired off short radio wave bursts declaring their allegiances. 2min 12seconds later, the  S.S. Lunar Lover took aggressive actions against the S.S. Leonov, which took aggressive actions, forcing the S.S. Lunar Lover to maneuver behind GRP-014-1. As a response to the loss of direct line of sight, S.S. Leonov fired off three guided rockets in an attempt to destroy the S.S. Lunar Lover before the asteroid eclipsed it completely. All three missiles were jammed via the use of an advanced FSM jammer system, leading to an unguided and unintended hit on and around the landing site of the GRPC Jupiter-3 craft.

GRPC Ranger Denis Joh was struck in the back by craft shrapnel, puncturing his right lung, resulting in open tension pneumothorax. Lacking adhesive paper, Rocketeer Vanta Ngai used a plastic sandwich bag and electrical tape to patch up the back injury and relieve tension from the right lung. 

At this time, the S.S. //Leonov// had launched a dozen of its one-man interceptors in an attempt to outflank the S.S. //Lunar Lovere//, making escaping from the surface impossible. GRPC Rocketeer Vanta Ngai decided to abandon the already damaged and decompressing ship to shelter in the pink craft. Making their way outside back onto the surface, Rocketeer Vanta Ngai noticed rocket impact sites had created a tunnel leading inside GRP-014-1.

GRPC Rocketeer Vanta Ngai took refuge within the GRP-014-1 despite GRPC Ranger Denis Joh’s protests. In response to Ranger Denis Joh’s intense emotional distress, GRP-014 began to display its reality-bending properties resulting in the unexplainable disappearance of both the USSR and Mercenary ships. This perceived act of aggression compelled the USSR interceptors into attacking GRP-014-1 in desperation to save their lives. 

Suit camera footage shows GRPC Rocketeer Ngai was also affected by the anomalous burst, being inexplicably moved deeper into the cave with no entrance or exit in sight. GRPC Rocketeer Vanta Ngai seemed dazed by this experience, believing himself to be back inside his resting quarters; he began stripping off his spacesuit, ending his suit's cam recording. One of the last things Rocketeer Vanta Ngai’s Camera feed showed was a mural of a humanoid-like entity being crushed by an ever-shrinking swarm of alien monsters led by a massive squid-like beast with 42 legs. GRPC Ranger Denis Joh regained consciousness 32min and 15sec into the asteroid bombardment, in which he found a trail of GRPC Rocketeer Vanta Ngai’s gear leading deeper into GRP-014-1.  

Following the course, GRPC Ranger Denis Joh came across GRPC Rocketeer Vanta Ngai standing in the nude under the entryway arch of GRP-014. From what little footage was compiled, the exterior walls of GRP-014 were adorned with glyphs depicting and encouraging a wide range of species to enter this room, symbolizing it as an oven. Ashes that arose from the oven exited an enormous circle, accumulating into a smaller circle. Video recordings of the interior showed a dark room full of unusual dust particles suspended in zero gravity. Further video recordings showed GRPC Ranger Denis Joh ranting at GRPC Rocketeer Vanta Ngai before pulling out his standard-issue Rocket Gun, blasting Ngai in the upper right abdomen, and propelling him back into GRP-014. 

Body camera video recordings showed that Rocketeer Vanta Ngai got up and walked deeper into GRP-014 after being shot, sitting down on a pile of rocks, and rocking back and forth, mimicking the behavior of a rocking chair. Inexplicably, shortly before sitting down inside GRP-014, Vanta Ngai turned to stone before rapidly disintegrating into dust. Ranger Denis Joh tried to investigate, unknowingly passing the threshold of GRP-014, giving him a severe memetic attack. It’s strongly believed that Ranger Denis Joh’s perception of reality had been heavily compromised at this point due to the effects of GRP-014. Ranger Joh began ranting about figures, mostly non-humanoids, who were either sitting in the corners like Rocketeer Vanta Ngai or clawing at the walls in pain.
