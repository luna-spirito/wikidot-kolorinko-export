> In-universe, an Anomaly Processing Documents (APD) is utilized by Prolab to formulate a Containment Protocol for an anomaly. Often, it is a loose collection of paperwork, physical notes, scraps, and test logs collected by File Managers. Their paperwork is often rough around the edges: Usually broken into multiple pages at a time and riddled with numerous revisions and excerpts of team discussions debates. It is also common for the format to be [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-110-j modified.]
> 
> Out-of-universe, this is another way to approach an anomaly through trial and error. Observation is key for harder-to-contain anomalies, and conjecture is documented at every step of the process.

+ Extra Info:

[[collapsible show="[+] Tips on How-to-Write" hide="[-] Tips on How-to-Write"]]

+ **Tips on How-to-Write:**

* __Not every anomaly is worth investigation, especially if it's simple.__ Pre-built chambers exist for a reason. Usually, only specific anomalies require a lot of attention needed.

* Write from Containment's Perspective; be essentialist and inferential based on available data. Use excerpts to communicate viewpoints throughout the piece.

* __Use a blended/informal tone;__ be conversational and to-the-point. In-universe, this document is a collection of physical notes. While they're still scientists, excessive formalization only bogs down their fast-paced nature.

* Add lively debate -- but remember, show don't tell. Use a smart mix of excerpts to tell part of your story. Documented debates when deliberating finalized classes/lethality are common. 

* Testing is both the core of the article and the heart of Prolab. Everything in Containment is trial and error. Failure is common, and suggestions are frequent.

* __Reign testing in.__ While AET is an apt comparison, ProLab isn't Research. Above all else, their job is to test anomalies for exploits and to them into containment chambers. Every move is deliberate towards containing phenomena.

* Not even final proposals are __final.__ Even when finalized, Protocol is modified when new/substantial information comes to light. It is not uncommon for Prolab to revisit an anomaly more than once.

* //**Go hog-wild.**// Prolab is one of the few departments in containment not held down by many budgetary constraints when specifically testing. Use eccentric methods: Rocket launchers, actors, thaumaturgical explosives -- their Division is founded on a myth-busters-esque mantra of seeing whatever will get the job done.
