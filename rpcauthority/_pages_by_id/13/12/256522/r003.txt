**Containment Protocols:** 

[[tabview]]
[[tab RPC-854]]
Being exposed to RPC-854 is not automatically lethal, unless a non-practitioner has been exposed to the anomaly for around 11-15 minutes which can cause individuals to develop Polycythemia vera.[[footnote]]Polycythemia vera is a type of blood cancer. It causes your bone marrow to make too many red blood cells. These excess cells thicken your blood, slowing its flow, which may cause serious problems, such as blood clots.[[/footnote]]

Because of RPC-854’s beneficial properties, and the benefits of personnel learning to harness it, however, because of the high-risk factors that come into play when training to utilise RPC-854, the Authority will only allow for select members from the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/protection-hub| Protection] and [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/containment-hub| Containment] Divisions to learn how to harness RPC-854.
##Red| Notice:## Personnel in the Protection Division that have successfully learnt how to utilise RPC-854, will be contacted and encouraged to enlist in MST Echo-25[[footnote]]Specializes in containing and terminating undead or supernatural entities that required unique termination methods.[[/footnote]] ("Dreaded Out").

[[/tab]]
[[tab RPC-854-1]]
The following is a list on how to deal with specific RPC-854-1 sub-branches.

**RPC-854-1A:** Practitioners that are capable of utilising RPC-854-1A, should be crippled at a long-range distance, and protective armour composed out of flame-resistant materials such as kevlar is advised to be worn. Depending on the practitioner's skill in generating and manipulating the flames, fire-dampening tools are ill-advised because of the flame's capacity to quickly spread.

**RPC-854-1B:** Practitioners that are capable of utilising RPC-854-1B, should be targeted from long-range distance, preferably towards their upper limbs to neutralise their control. While an electrical charge may be enough to subdue the practitioner, this is advised against as the combatant may also be affected by this, therefore, it should only be used should the practitioner be able to make it to a safe distance.

**RPC-854-1C:** Practitioners that are capable of utilising RPC-854-1C, should be targeted from long-range distance, or a stealth attack before they're able to begin utilising the art. There is currently, no best method in regards to terminating practitioners other than targeting their mortality.

**RPC-854-1D:** It's best advised that practitioners who are capable of utilising RPC-854-1D be attacked through a well placed sniper bullet, or a distraction be enacted to catch them off guard. If these methods are unavailable, fire may also work as an option as practitioners may accidentally cause it to spread and consume all nearby oxygen

[[/tab]]
[[tab RPC-854-2]]
Practitioners that are capable of accessing RPC-854-2 should be attacked from a long distance through the use of a sniper rifle to cripple their limbs. Heavy weapon fire is also advised, along with explosives due to the practitioner's common traits to withstand blunt trauma for extended periods of time.

[[tab RPC-854-A]]
Due to the anonymity of the location that RPC-854-A is unable to leave and which RPC-854-B are settled at, minimal containment procedures are required to prevent knowledge of the anomalies from being leaked to the public. In the event RPC-854-A becomes able to leave their location, depending on their intentions, they should either be subdued and contained or allowed to remain within the boundaries of their location.

Interviews with RPC-854-A should be conducted every two months, with relations between the Authority and the entity being upheld through RPC-854-D instances under Authority employment. 

It's advised that personnel bring two of the gifts listed below and present them to RPC-854-A if they are selected to undergo the trials to learn RPC-854-2.
* Two bottles of red wine.
* Cooked ungulate meat.
* Spices.

Those selected that have agreed to becoming RPC-854-1, will be given some documents confirming that the Authority is not to be held liable for any damages sustained during their training, nor will any of their next-of-kin who are also employed in the Authority be eligible to file complaints to the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/authority-departmental-hierarchy| Authority Crimes Tribunal] for damages.

[[/tab]]
[[tab RPC-854-B]]
Because of RPC-854-B's relative obscurity within the Homolje Mountains, no additional containment procedures other than preventing trespassers to their location is required.

Site-286 has entered into diplomatic relations with RPC-854-B, the results of which have been positive. Because of this, exchange between RPC-854-B and Authority personnel has been successful, with RPC-854-B agreeing teach selected Authority personnel on how to use RPC-854, and the Authority in turn has agreed to provide RPC-854-B with materials to improve their society.

[[/tab]]
[[tab RPC-854-C]]
Unless otherwise stated, all RPC-854-C instances not found to belong to a member of RPC-854-B should be confiscated from Persons of Interest and withheld in Authority containment until further notice. RPC-854-C instances may be held in site lockers used for handling minor anomalous items.

[[/tab]]

RPC-854-D instances should be screened regarding their motives, and whether or not they are also able to utilise RPC-854-1 and -2. Depending on the analysis of their character, they should either be subdued and placed in Authority containment, or be allowed to function as normal so long as they sign an agreement to not disclose RPC-854, -1 and -2 to others.

Personnel are advised to keep a distance from RPC-854-D instances if engaged in a battle, due to a majority of -1 and -2 having long-range capabilities.

+ Description:

[[tabview]]
[[tab RPC-854]]
RPC-854 is an anomalous form of radiation, that is more commonly referred to as ‘Zemljina moć’[[footnote]]**Zemljina moć** (Serbian: Земљина моћ; Power of the Earth.[[/footnote]] by RPC-854-B, who were the first to have discovered it. It has been found that all living organisms are capable of harnessing, exerting and manipulating RPC-854, however, there are few who are able to achieve this to its fullest.[[footnote]]A current estimate of RPC-854-C is roughly 572 individuals.[[/footnote]]

Living organisms are able to harness RPC-854 through a mentally concentrated process, causing RPC-854 to then be transferred throughout the individual’s bloodstream. It has been found that practitioners of RPC-854 must be at least semi-conscious when attempting to generate even small amounts of RPC-854. RPC-854 appears to manifest as a green light.

RPC-854 can also be spread and held within condensed matters such as liquids and solids, although only solids that are composed out of silver and/or iron will be able to effectively contain any RPC-854 that is created. In all other cases, the practitioner must maintain contact with the matter in order to effectively conduct the anomaly in the material.

Additionally, practitioners may opt to produce RPC-854 throughout their bodies, granting their physical capabilities to become more noticeably enhanced after roughly 5-10 minutes of allowing it to be spread through the bloodstream. This can be concentrated to specific areas of the body, e.g. hands, arms, legs.

Any form of deceased matter that comes into immediate contact with RPC-854 will quickly begin to disintegrate.[[footnote]]Example: A recently deceased human body was recorded to have taken upon 9 seconds to completely disintegrate.[[/footnote]] However, substances such as petroleum have been found to not be susceptible, nor conductible to RPC-854, potentially suggesting that millions of years of decomposition will render RPC-854 null upon contact.

The estimated time for non-members of RPC-854-B to learn RPC-854 is roughly 5 years, although, there have been exceptions to this.

[[/tab]]
[[tab RPC-854-1]]
RPC-854-1 is the designation given to multiple sub-branches of RPC-854 that can be accessed through a set of specific training rituals, labelled RPC-854-1A to -1G. In order for a practitioner to make use of one of the sub-branches after completing the set of rituals, they are required to make use of a specific set of body techniques in orderly to properly maintain use of the branch. Noticeably, practitioners are not limited to one RPC-854-1 sub-branch, and can train to utilise multiple if they wish.

The distance and quantity of an element that a practitioner is able to manipulate when utilising one of the sub-branches is heavily dependant on their own skill, physical and mental strength, enabling them to manipulate and generate more as they become stronger. 

Most practitioners usually become adept at learning RPC-854-1 after around 10 years of specific training routines, which enables their bodies to become accustomed to the process of using one of the sub-branches.

----

+++++ Final Stage

In late 2009, it was discovered that there was an additional final stage to completing RPC-854-1 which enabled a practitioner to transmutate their own bodies into the substance that they were most closely affiliated with. A study showed that this was only possible for individuals that:

* Had a deep spiritual connection to the environment around them.
* Spend a majority of their life within their environment without leaving it.
* Only practiced one RPC-854-1 sub-branch in their lifetime.
* Become more accustomed to the environment around them and adapt to it.
* Disregard technological devices.
* Disregard certain emotions.

Not only will practitioners be able to transmutate themselves into their element, but will also obtain full telekinetic control over the same substance within a 20 m radius around them.

However, practitioners able to complete this stage are rare as the Authority has only discovered a total of 3 individuals to have done so. It was also discovered that despite their ability to change their bodies, they were unable to leave their environment or would slowly succumb to death after prolonged absence.

[[collapsible show="[+] Open RPC-854-1A" hide="[-] Close RPC-854-1A" hideLocation="both"]]

RPC-854-1A is the sub-branch of RPC-854 that grants the practitioner the capability to generate and manipulate fire. Practitioners will be capable of generating fire from any of their limbs and be able to manipulate it in almost any form, including changing its shape, temperature and length of the flames.

Practitioners that do choose to use RPC-854-1A do not have any immunity or resistance to the flames created, and are therefore susceptible to injury from the flames and/or heat produced. Because of these effects, practitioners are required to utilise the flames at a safe distance or risk injury to themselves.
[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="[+] Open RPC-854-1B" hide="[-] Close RPC-854-1B" hideLocation="both"]]

RPC-854-1B is the sub-branch of RPC-854 that grants the practitioner the capability to generate and manipulate water and/or any liquid found to contain water in it including flavoured waters, milk etc.

In order to use RPC-854-1B, practitioners are required to use their hands and fingers in order to control and move water in the direction that they want it to. RPC-854-1B can also be manipulated at the longest distance compared to other sub-branches, allowing practitioners better long-range distance attacks. More experienced practitioners have been found to be capable of manipulating the bodily liquids within an individual's body, although this has been found to require an immense amount of experience and control in order to do so.

One of the major limitations of RPC-854-1B, is that practitioners who suffer any form of upper limb impairment, or lose the use of their hands in any form will become unable to use the sub-branch as a result. This weakness cannot be overcome through the use of prosthetic limbs, as testing has shown. Another limitation is that the water being manipulated must be connected to the ground or other water sources, otherwise practitioners will lose control over it.

Because of these applications and a lack of offensive capabilities, a large majority of practitioners that choose to use RPC-854-1B will primarily take up positions within the medical community.
[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="[+] Open RPC-854-1C" hide="[-] Close RPC-854-1C" hideLocation="both"]]

RPC-854-1C is the sub-branch of RPC-854 that grants the practitioner the capability to generate and manipulate terrestrial elements.[[footnote]]Excluding water, fire, air and metals.[[/footnote]] Practitioners that choose to make use of RPC-854-1C, are able to control the terrestrial elements around them e.g. earth, stone, wood, sand etc.

Unlike most forms of RPC-854-1, practitioners are not required to perform any movements in order to utilise RPC-854-1C, but instead perform a series of concentrated breaths in order to upkeep the telekinetic manipulation of said substances. However, in order for a practitioner to utilise RPC-854-1C, they are required to keep constant contact with one of said substances underneath them.[[footnote]]Testing with practitioners has demonstrated that holding a piece of the required substances and/or standing on artificial materials will not allow for use of RPC-854-1C.[[/footnote]]

However, forms of movement as simple as walking or talking will immediately disrupt a practitioner's control over their elements.
[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="[+] Open RPC-854-1D" hide="[-] Close RPC-854-1D" hideLocation="both"]]

RPC-854-1D is the sub-branch of RPC-854 that grants the practitioner the capability to generate and manipulate wind.

In order to effectively manipulate RPC-854-1D, practitioners are required to make as little use of their bodies as possible, and only focus on limbs in order to use it. This involves the use of arms and legs making harsh and sudden movements in directions to generate gusts of wind, while generally moving in a direction in order to manipulate them. 

When practitioners utilise RPC-854-1D, the oxygen in the haemoglobin will start depleting.

It has been found that control over RPC-854-1D becomes stronger when practitioners are not making contact with any solid or liquid substance e.g. propelling themselves off the ground through the use of RPC-854-1D, or weather phenomena around them is strong.
[[/collapsible]]

[[/tab]]
[[tab RPC-854-2]]
RPC-854-2 is a unique manifestation of RPC-854 that can only be accessed through intense training and practice, requiring that a practitioner undergo various hardships before manifesting RPC-854-2. RPC-854-B have colloquially nicknamed the phenomena "Borbenduh".

RPC-854-2 manifests itself as red transparent flames, emerging from an individual's chest area before they continue to spiral outwards and cover the entire body except for the eyes. RPC-854-2 cannot be viewed ordinarily, only visible to other practitioners who have unlocked RPC-854-2 or those who possess the natural ability to see spectral entities and/or phenomena.

RPC-854-2 grants a tremendous increase to a practitioner's physical condition, allowing them to perform incredible feats through strength and endurance. Some of the greatest examples of these feats include lifting approximately 10,00 kg with some effort, sprinting at a top speed of 75 km/h and enduring approximately 1 hour and 30 minutes of continuous physical trauma.

RPC-854-2 will also boost a practitioner's the power in the sub-branches of RPC-854-1. This includes
* Practitioners with RPC-854-1A being able to conjure blue flames.
* Practitioners with RPC-854-1B being able to manipulate blood.
* Practitioners with RPC-854-1B being able to manipulate and generate magma.
* Practitioners with RPC-854-1D being able to manipulate the temperature.

RPC-854-2 does not appear to adhere to the standard chemical process and/or laws that applies to non-anomalous fire, however, the oxygen found in the haemoglobin is believed to serve as a fuel source for RPC-854-2, as oxygen in the surrounding environment does not appear to fuel RPC-854-2 in anyway. Instead, RPC-854-2 consumes the oxygen within red blood cells, requiring that practitioners keep control over their breathing while in use of RPC-854-2, otherwise they may undergo negative effects of lack of oxygen.

[[tab RPC-854-A and B]]
RPC-854-A is a small rural community of [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vlachs_of_Serbia| ethnic Vlachs] that have currently stationed themselves around the area that RPC-854-B resides, with an estimated population of around 216 individuals. All members of the community have proven themselves adept at using RPC-854, however, only 29% are able to manifest and manipulate RPC-854-1, furthermore less than 9% have the necessary capability to manipulate RPC-854-2.

Although a majority of RPC-854-A is adverse towards outside interaction, and members will only periodically leave to gather supplies, information and/or resources, but have been more open to interviews and communication with the Authority, preferring to do so through [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/site-286| Site-286.]

All members of RPC-854-A practice a form of paganism, with the most notable deity in their religion being RPC-854-B, whom they refer to as Svarog.[[footnote]]Svarog (Old East Slavic: Сваро́гъ; Russian: Сварог) is a Slavic god with unclear functions, but most often interpreted as a sky god or a god of fire and smithing.[[/footnote]] 

Onwards from the 15th century, until establishing a permanent camp around RPC-854-B in 1911, RPC-854-A pursued a hunter-gatherer lifestyle. Only a few members of RPC-854-A were ever permitted to leave at a time, usually for the aforementioned reasons, but would also seek out [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-502| 502 instances.] around the area and neutralise them.

Periodically, a small group of anywhere between 3-5 members of RPC-854-A would set out for an undesignated period of time to seek out [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-502| RPC-502 instances] to neutralise, seen as a right of passage by RPC-854-B. An elder in RPC-854-A would also be present to witness and intervene in the battle as they saw fit.

It wasn't until 1912, when RPC-854-A discarded this tradition for a new right of passage that was provided by the area in which RPC-854-B was situated. Once a practitioner had completed their basic training, RPC-854-B would give them a task to complete, the tasks varying between an individual based on their skills and weaknesses. Despite this, certain members of RPC-854-A were not permitted to engage in armed combat unless in self-defence, which usually applied to the community elder, doctors, teachers etc.

[[collapsible show="[+] Open RPC-854-B" hide="[-] Close RPC-854-B" hideLocation="both"]]
RPC-854-B is a male humanoid entity located in a secluded area of the Homolje Mountains, of which they are unable to leave. Whether this is caused by an outside force or by their own choice is unknown. 

RPC-854-B is elderly, appearing to be somewhere in their early-sixties while genetic testing has revealed them to be of Slavic descent. RPC-854-B has proven themselves to be capable of communicating in English and Serbian, as well as not having shown any physiological requirement for sustenance, hydration and/or sleep, although they have exhibited the desire to do so.

One of the more noticeable properties about RPC-854-B, is their ability to create RPC-854-C instances when provided with exemplary materials. RPC-854-B only chooses to do this for individuals that have managed to successfully unlock RPC-854-2, resulting in a small number of RPC-854-C objects made, furthermore, these objects will only work for whoever RPC-854-B made them for.

**Addendum 854.01: Discovery Log**

All anomalies relating to RPC-854 were first discovered on the 13th of February, 1999, by Site-286 during an excavation trip into the Homolje Mountains, after numerous reports of deceased RPC-502-1 instances close to the area caught the attention of the site. The trip was lead by Monk. Rajković who lead a small group of other monks and security personnel, who camped within the mountains near where one of the 502-1 instances had been killed.

All personnel who were selected for the trip wore signet rings[[footnote]]Known as the ‘gentleman’s ring’, the signet ring is traditionally seen as a symbol of family heritage.[[/footnote]] bearing the symbol of the archangel Michael on them. Around 15 minutes after staying in camp, one of the guards noticed a figure emerging from the forest and was wearing an RPC-854-C instance that bared the same symbol as that of the Monastery of the Holy Archangels.

Monk. Rajković carefully approached the figure while showcasing the signet ring to them, causing the figure to calmly approach the camp once they had taken notice of it, removing their RPC-854-C instance from their face. After a few minutes of conversing with the figure, they agreed to lead Monk. Rajković and the group to RPC-854-B.
