------

++++ **Containment Protocols:**

RPC-551 is contained in an oak coffin provided by ██████-Kapelle. Due to ethical concerns, RPC-551 is to only receive minor medical aid during a UHER-event. After which, its cell and coffin are to be maintained. Interaction between RPC-551 and RPC-551-2 instances must be monitored closely in case an insurrection plot occurs.

RPC-551-2 candidates must go over daily psychological examination a month before they are to unsheathe RPC-551-1. RPC-551-2 must stay at least 150 m away from any military vehicles or RPCs, especially if the RPC in question is of the aggression-hazard type. To terminate RPC-551-2's "worthiness" they are to be manipulated into repeatedly breaking RPC-551's trust.

++++ **Description:**

[[include component:image-enlarge name= http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/rpc-551/Karla.png|width=…px|caption= RPC-551]]

RPC-551 is the preserved cadaver of an adult female of north-Germanic descent identifying itself as Saint Karla (1499-1518). Its body is covered in wax and perfume to appear incorruptible.[[footnote]] A Roman Catholic and Eastern Orthodox belief that divine intervention allows some human bodies to avoid the normal process of decomposition after death as a sign of their holiness. [[/footnote]] The symbol of the "██████ ███" is engraved in RPC-551's forehead hidden by layers of wax. RPC-551 is well preserved internally and externally. Whether this preservation is the cause of sophisticated embalming techniques or is a part of RPC-551's anomalous property is unknown. 

RPC-551-1 is a ceremonial bastard sword weighing 1.5 kg and measuring 1.2 m. The Latin phrase: “Servus morte dormit. Electi eius vocationem et audient.”[[footnote]] (in English) "In death, the servant sleeps, for they will hear the call of her chosen ones." [[/footnote]] and floral iconography is engraved on the object's rain-guard. The sword itself impales through RPC-551's stomach. Before the discovery of RPC-551-2 instances, removing RPC-551-1 from RPC-551 was impossible without damaging RPC-551.

RPC-551-1 can be unsheathed only by certain "worthy" humanoid entities. At the time of writing[[footnote]] 11/07/19 [[/footnote]], 2,600 human subjects and 25 hand-picked humanoid RPCs were asked to pull out RPC-551-1. Only 10 humans and 2 humanoid anomalies succeeded. Common characteristics can be found among the 12 confirmed instances of RPC-551-2, the characteristics of which differ among males and females.

Upon the successful extraction of RPC-551-1 (classified as a UHER-event), white sparks will appear on RPC-551's stomach cavity. RPC-551 will undergo rapid cellular regeneration. Any injury RPC-551 receives prior to reanimation will heal with the exception of the cavity. In its fully animated state, RPC-551 has full brain function but is paralyzed from the neck down. 

Because the cavity is unable to heal, RPC-551 will only have 0.4-2 hours to communicate before expiring from blood loss. With the help of medical aid, it is possible for the UHER-event to last more than 2 days, but RPC-551 will suffer in the process.

> //Great be the vessel of Divine Judgement![[footnote]] RPC-551-1 [[/footnote]] My dear harbinger, I have returned from Hades as your servant for as long as I may live. Blessed be your heart, for a great burden now befalls on your soul.//
> 
> -**RPC-551** upon the activation of a UHER-event

RPC-551 speaks in the Ecclesiastic-Latin and Low-German[[footnote]] Also known as low Saxon. Was prominently used in the 12th-17th centuries.  [[/footnote]] languages. It has shown to be patient and affectionate towards RPC-551-2 instances, consoling them until RPC-551's eventual expiration. When interacting with Authority personnel, RPC-551 develops a stubborn and facetious attitude. During interviews, RPC-551 will often purposefully apply pressure to itself so as to bleed out faster and avoid further confrontation.

RPC-551-1’s blade is made of anti-thaumaturgic metal of the unconventional sort. When the edge of the blade comes in contact with a coherency field higher or lower than ACS-3.97, the edge will react by discharging an opposing coherency field in a strange attempt to "nullify" it. The effect only lasts a fraction of a second before diffusion but can have a detrimental effect on both RPC-551-2 and its target. This window of coherent vulnerability can be exploited by RPC-551-2 to bend the very physical and anomalous structure of RPC-551-1. In theory, RPC-551-1 can be used to terminate "indestructible" items.

RPC-551-2 instances report "hearing" RPC-551's voice coming out of RPC-551-1.[[footnote]] Most likely a form of telepathic communication. [[/footnote]] The accounts elude to this voice heavily influencing RPC-551-2's thoughts and actions. RPC-551-2's "worthiness" can be revoked if and only if it breaks RPC-551's trust.

__**Historical Account of RPC-551:**__

RPC-551 (formerly designated as PA-018 by the FOA) has identified itself and confirmed to be Saint Karla (1499-1518) was a member of the Exiled Knights: an assembly of former knights excommunicated for transgressions against the Holy Roman Empire and Papal Auctoritas. This group along with many other semi-organized witch-hunting operations are one of many predecessors to the modern BündesOkkultAbteilung. 

The document below is an archived letter from former knight Heynrich to an anonymous Auctoritas scholar in Habsburg in 1518.
[[/div]]

[[div style="background-color: #c9c4b7; color: black; font-family: Times; font-size: 16px; border: 10px; padding: 20px"]]

Six years ago, one of our knights, Fidel traveled across the country in search of a tool to aid us in our efforts against the beast. His journey lasted two years, most of us thought he was dead. He eventually came back, the first thing he told me after all these months was //I’ve finally found one.// That //one// he found, he said that //we can defeat them all! The emperor’s force, the Papal Inquisition, all of them!// Nothing can match our horror when we discovered that //one// was the witch that stood next to him. She was no mere ninny wench that makes their enemy's bread stale or their dogs ill. That girl he brought in was a daughter of Asmodeus,[[footnote]] Asmodeus is the prince of hell. "Daughter of Asmodeus" is believed to be an archaic term for female high-class reality-benders. [[/footnote]] a wretched god trapped in the body of a blissful waif, the only way we knew was by that putrid mark carved into her head. Although at any moment she can turn us into turds with only her thoughts, the lunatic Fidel found one who knew not her might. 

The waif’s name was Karla, a religious sister from a chapel near Yberg castle. She was chastised by her own village because of her mark. Pitifully found near death in the yard of the church, it's no mystery why she didn't hesitate to join us. And for six years, she was a treasured member of our group and we loved her dearly. But the day is growing near where the instrument will finally play her melody. 

To Fidel, this is no longer about ridding our earth of beasts but ridding this world of wickedness in its entirety. He wants to turn her into an instrument in this song of war. This war he schemes is a plot to destroy the Papal Inquisition and the Holy Emperor's army. My allegiance is true to the knights' goal, but I fear the lunatic's scheme will bring nothing but suffering to all of Christendom. 

There is a sword forged of Damascus steel[[footnote]] Anti-thaumaturgic metal. [[/footnote]] created by another witch long ago. Only with this blade have we been able to stand on even grounds against gods. With this blade, you can defeat her. I only avoid doing this myself because it would break my heart doing so. I accept my fate as a traitor of the knights. May God judge me for it. 

I've convinced six of my men to join me and stop Fidel, when you meet us in Habsburg we will tell you everything. You may judge us for our past crimes, but I beg of you to do it after the fight is over. I must reconcile my past mistakes. It is in good faith I bring you this message old friend.

She will thank you.

[[>]]
//God be with us.//

++++ **Addendum Discovery Log 551**

On the 16th of June, 1922, the FOA received reports of the disappearance of 12 people, suspected to be the cause of a "witch" terrorizing █████ Castle. The FOA began dispatching investigators on the 27th of June, but the suspect was never found. According to the local accounts, the witch has been killed by Oliver ██████ and "Saint Karla's magic sword", which led to the discovery of RPC-551. Although the FOA held legal custody of RPC-551, the item was transferred to the Authority to fulfill an old promise.

**Report:** On PA-018's Transfer

**Written Report:** Many of our men have questioned my decision on transferring PA-018 to one of our most controversial competitors. Hopefully, this report will explain my motivation for initiating.

Since antiquity, mankind fought against beasts of the unknown with instruments of science and mystics. Tales of brave men such as the //Exiled Knights// were one of many sparks that lead to the creation of the //Bundesokkultabteilung//. Although mankind’s bravery shines brighter than ever before, the courage of our ancestors faded into long-abandoned memories. This brings cultural significance to PA-018, a treasured member of the Exiled Knights, whom no doubt had a hand in shaping this agency to what it is today.


In 1518, the //Auctoritas Imperata// saved what was left of these our predecessors, and in turn, we pledged alliance to them. PA-018 is the chain that binds us to this old tradition. Therefore, for the sake of our ancestor's wishes, Saint Karla's covenant with the heir of Auctoritas must be treated with respect.
