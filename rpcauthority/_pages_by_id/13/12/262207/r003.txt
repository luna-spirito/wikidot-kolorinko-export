**Registered Phenomena Code:** RPC-009

**Item Type:** Object[[footnote]]This document is concerned with study of the RPC-009 item instances and not with RPC-009 itself.[[/footnote]] ##666|**|**## **Lethality Rating:** White

**Hazard Types:** Transmutation, Immeasurable, Incorporeal, Mechanical
[[/div]]
[[/div]]

**Abstract:** RPC-009 is a cross-dimensional refugee who is currently in --Engineering Component-- Protectorate possession and is unavailable for study. However, the //Department of Occult Concerns// has in its possession several thaumaturgy items originally belonging to RPC-009. It is very likely that within several years, we will be forced to return said items, and so our research must be efficient and timely.

**Safe Handling and Usage:** Each RPC-009 sub-item must be treated with proper care. RPC-009-1 is non-anomalous in properties but must be handled delicately to preserve its features in order to indirectly study the reality of origin and the other items based on the markings and construction of the item. Standard literature text applies to handling of RPC-009-2, -4 and -5. RPC-009-3 is not to be used wastefully; we only have the one supply of it for our studies. RPC-009-6 and -7 are to be handled with the utmost caution, as anomalous artifacts which can be unpredictable in nature.

= **Researcher Audio Log 009.1**

**Foreword:** The following meeting was recorded on May 31, 2014.

----

**[BEGIN LOG]**

**Dr. Pennington:** Are we recording this time? I don't want to start it unless we are recording.

**Dr. Vasquez:** Yes, we are actually recording.

**Dr. Pennington:** Alright. [Clears throat] This is the first meeting of Primogenitus Team //Novum Thaumatum//. For this first week, we will work on gaining an understanding of the RPC-009 itemset, not necessarily the actual magic system. Vasquez and Mccann will be in charge of RPC-009-1, RPC-009-2 and RPC-009-5, the satchel, the French book and the torn book respectively. Morris and Cartwright will be studying RPC-009-3, RPC-009-4 and RPC-009-6, the chalk, runes and helm respectively. Macgregor and I will be studying RPC-009-7, the pamphlet. Any questions?

**Dr. Macgregor:** 'Noval Thaumatum'? What a weird name for team. Who was came up wit' that again?

**Dr. Vasquez:** It was assigned. Who cares.

**Dr. Morris:** I think it's a good name.

**Dr. Cartwright:** You didn't even pronounce it right, dude. //Novum Thaumatum.//

**Dr. Pennington:** Are there any //actual// questions?

**Dr. Mccann:** No, sir.

**Dr. Morris:** Wait, what do we know about this RPC-009 character? Shouldn't we have access to him for testing and whatnot, if he's in Authority custody.

**Dr. Pennington:** We don't know much. We don't have access to him because he is currently in Containment Division's custody at an Engineering Component facility. Apparently they want to keep him there so that they can run their own experiments and see if he can be useful for containing their gamma-class anomalies. I have no idea how many protocols they are breaking by doing this -- probably at least a few -- but we have no power in the situation. I have a feeling we'll learn a lot about him through studying his items though.

**Dr. Morris:** Alright. Thank you.

**Dr. Pennington:** Any more questions?

**Dr. Morris:** No.

[The researchers shake their heads no.]

**Dr. Pennington:** Then let's get to work.

**[END LOG]**

+++ RPC-009-1: Emptied leather satchel (Inert)

[[f>image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b7/Instruments%2C_veterinary_%28AM_1962.54-4%29.jpg width="250px" style="border: 2px solid #99a; box-shadow: 0 0 2px 1px #003"]]

Pictured is RPC-009-1, belonging to RPC-009, which contained RPC-009-2, -3, -4 and -5. The exterior is made of a black leather and the interior is composed of beige cloth. The satchel has no additional compartments nor does it have a lock.

The satchel is closed by a button which no longer connects properly. The button is presumed to have been functional prior to its acquisition. Acquisition of RPC-009-1 and other items from RPC-009 were met with strong resistance and the satchel's fabric was damaged considerably, as visible.

[[div class="content-box"]]
+++ RPC-009-2: Book titled "Thaumaturgie aux États-Unis: histoire et culture" (Inert)

RPC-009-2 is a 40-page book which provides an overview of an alternate reality United States of America, believed to be the reality of origin of RPC-009. The book is written entirely in French which is presented as the official language for thaumaturgic discussion in the American thaumaturgic community.

The book is currently being translated to English in order to be used by the active involved researchers. The title of the book translates to "Thaumaturgy in the United States: History and Culture".

+++ RPC-009-3: Thaumaturgic-symbol drawing chalk (Anomalous)

RPC-009-3 is a set of drawing chalk used for thaumaturgic spellcasting based on the RPC-009 magic system. Each chalk color is intended for use with a specific class of ethereal organ, as described in later documents.

* Green: thaumaturgy related to RPC-009-A "The Mind"
* Yellow: thaumaturgy related to RPC-009-B "The Soul"
* Orange: thaumaturgy related to RPC-009-C "The Wyrth"
* Purple: thaumaturgy related to RPC-009-D "The Glove"
* Blue: thaumaturgy related to RPC-009-E "The Death"
* Red: thaumaturgy unrelated to the five ethereal organs

+++ RPC-009-4: Scroll titled "The Runic Alphabet" (Presumed inert)

This scroll does not actually list any runic characters as one would expect by the title. Instead, it provides instructions for discovering the various letters for ourselves. These letters are drawn along with geometric patterns using the thaumaturgic chalk in order to use the RPC-009 magic system. Currently, we have discovered 3 basic letters, 2 advanced ones, and several other markings.

[[=image https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/440652449641136128/858886349519716392/unknown.png width="100%" style="border: 2px solid #99a; box-shadow: 0 0 2px 1px #003"]]

The method of discovering new runes is somewhat random. The scroll instructs the user to equip the Priest's Eyes[[footnote]]Described in RPC-009-6[[/footnote]] and then to begin writing on parchment paper using an ink pen. If, by pure chance, you get most of the way toward a valid rune match, the Priest's Eyes take control of the pen, causing it to float outside your hand, and finish it for you. It also touches up some of the spots earlier that aren't quite right.
[[/div]]

[[div class="content-box"]]
+++ RPC-009-5: Book titled "Gun Magic" (Presumed anomalous)
Most pages of RPC-009-5 were torn out, presumably by RPC-009 prior to acquisition. Written on the inside of the back cover is the text: "One day I'll be back for the rest of my stuff. Fuck you." This text is also presumably written by RPC-009.

It is believed that this book details a specialization of RPC-009 magic relating to metallurgy and manipulation of firearms.
[[/div]]

[[div class="content-box"]]
+++ RPC-009-6: Thaumaturgic Helm & Goggles (Anomalous)

[[include component:image-block name=https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/440652449641136128/858465644172935218/unknown.png|width=250px|caption=The binoculars of RPC-009-6, separated from the helm.]]

RPC-009-6 is a set of helmet and goggles/binoculars which RPC-009 was wearing upon his arrival. They are described as the "Priest's Eyes" in a lot of the RPC-009 magic texts. Inward rotation of the binocular's lenses does not alter focus, but rather changes mode of thaumaturgic vision.

The helmet portion of RPC-009-6 is believed to be non-anomalous. The goggles may be detached from the helm for easier use. Modes of use are documented below.

~~~~

# 0° from center: Neutral; does not allow for vision of thaumaturgic effects of any kind
# 20° from center: Runic 1; allows for creation/discovery of new runes
# 40° from center: Runic 2; allows for advanced vision of runic symbols, providing additional information as holographic text (in French)
# 60° from center: Ethereal 1; allows the wearer to see RPC-009-A
# 80° from center: Ethereal 2; allows the wearer to see RPC-009-B
# 100° from center: Ethereal 3; allows the wearer to see RPC-009-C
# 120° from center: Ethereal 4; allows the wearer to see RPC-009-D
# 140° from center: Ethereal 5; allows the wearer to see RPC-009-E
[[/div]]

RPC-009-7 is a pamphlet which seems to act as a guide for Refugees exiting RPC-009's home dimension. The pamphlet is anomalous, with pages infused with magic that allow for text to be animated and interactive in superficial ways. The writing of the pamphlet is very idiosyncratic and whimsical. Here are some transcribed excerpts:

= **Researcher Audio Log 009.2**

**Foreword:** The following meeting was recorded on April 6, 2014.

----

**[BEGIN LOG]**

**Dr. Pennington:** Week one of our study has concluded. At the end of each study period, we will come to discuss and combine our acquired knowledge for each area of... RPC-009 magic. We really ought to come up with an actual name for this thing.

**Dr. Morris:** Is each study period going to last only a week? That seems rather short.

**Dr. Pennington:** No, just the first. Most will be a month or two.

**Dr. Morris:** I see.

**Dr. Mccann:** Well, Vasquez and I have made good progress on 009-1, -2 and -5. That is to say, 009-1 is just a satchel. It is quite unnotworthy. 009-2, however, is fascinating. We are still awaiting a full translation but what we have so far is a promising look into the history of... their timeline. Do we have a name for the alternate reality? How do ALTR codes come about anyhow?

**Dr. Pennington:** I believe they are arbitrary. Let's just come up with something.

**Dr. Cartwright:** How about 'ALTR-MTGS'?

**Dr. Pennington:** That works. Is there any meaning behind it?

**Dr. Cartwright:** Absolutely none.

**Dr. Pennington:** Right. We'll use that. Mccann, how's about 009-5?

**Dr. Mccann:** Well, uh. As you all know, it's pretty destroyed. Only thing of note is the ominous message directed at //us//, saying that he is going to come back for his stuff.

**Dr. Macgregor:** Should we be worried of that? He comes back, hurts us all, beats us up, et cetera? Could happen, yes? I have not good heath ensurance, 'specially for magic-related wounds.

**Dr. Pennington:** No, that's ridiculous. There's nothing to worry about. They've been providing regular communication and containment of him has been quite fine.

**Dr. Cartwright:** Doesn't he have really powerful magic though? That's what I heard.

**Dr. Pennington:** Er, possibly.

**Dr. Morris:** Yes I heard he does. And he's not really being contained? Just he doesn't know where else to go so he stays there and cooperates? If he ever found out where our research lab is, he could just leave and come here...

**Dr. Pennington:** No, that's wrong. He's fully contained in a steel thaum-proof chamber. 

**Dr. Morris:** Oh, apologies. I must have been mistaken or overly presumptuous.

**Dr. Vasquez:** I heard he used his powers to blow up an airplane. The plane was in //mid-air//.

**Dr. Pennington:** What? I've heard nothing of this. That sounds ridiculous.

**Dr. Morris:** No, I've heard that too.

**Dr. Cartwright:** Sounds like magic powerful enough to break out of any containment cell.

**Dr. Pennington:** Uhhhh, well, uh. Don't worry about it. I will make a call and double check what that is about. I'm sure it's nothing. 

**Dr. Vasquez:** We don't even know what kind of magic he has.

**Dr. Macgregor:** We know he has the pages from that Gun Magic book... What kind of gun blows up plane... Very dangerous.

**Dr. Pennington:** //Enough!// We don't have all day here. Morris and Cartwright, how did your studies go?

**Dr. Morris:** Well, we were able to produce a few runic symbols using the lens. These symbols can be used within circular patterns, not unlike alchemy.

**Dr. Vasquez:** Can this be used to perform magic?

**Dr. Morris:** Yes, presumably. We haven't dared test that yet. We need to study it more before we try it out, but I'd imagine it works similar to using alchemy circles.

**Dr. Cartwright:** Additionally, the goggles allow vision of 'ethereal organs' which are apparent external segments of the human body that all humans possess. They are only visible through this lens, and they are absolutely fascinating. 

**Dr. Macgregor:** Is possible that the 'ether organs' are not actually real, only imaginary images created by lens? I do not think everyone simple had these organs on their body for all time, and then now suddenly they are accessible through this lens from alternate reality traveller.

**Dr. Cartwright:** Well of course, but it's our job to study under the presumption that the organs actually matter. 

**Dr. Macgregor:** I suppose so.

**Dr. Cartwright:** Well, how did study of the pamphlet go?

**Dr. Macgregor:** The pamphlet is quite ludicrous. Does not shut up about United States.

**Dr. Vasquez:** Pardon?

**Dr. Pennington:** The pamphlet seems to be a guide for diplomacy between RPC-009 and people of our reality. It's written in a very offbeat way. Not much else to say. I suppose it provides some insight into how the ALTR-MTGS world operates but not in a clear sense.

**Dr. Mccann:** I believe that's it for the recap, then? What's next?

**Dr. Pennington:** I believe we should focus on studying the explicit thaumaturgic elements while we wait for the English translation of RPC-009-2 to come through. We need to learn more about these ethereal organs. They may be the most important part of this magic system.

**Dr. Mccann:** Yes, I agree completely.

**Dr. Pennington:** I think we are ready to get back to work. We won't be breaking up into teams this time so we will not need to record another meeting like this. Also, I will try find out about that plane that got blown up. I'm sure it is nothing but I will let you know the details.

**[END LOG]**

[[tab RPC-009-A: "The Mind"]]
The Mind is the ethereal organ believed to represent the human consciousness itself, including all thoughts and individual experiences. It appears attached to the brain and only exists inside the skull, but is still visible translucent within the Preist's Eyes.

[[tab RPC-009-B: "The Soul"]]
The Soul is the ethereal organ believed to represent a spiritual connection to a higher plane of existence. It appears as a radiating, teal-colored beam that stretches from eye to eye. Upon death, the Soul rises into the air and gradually dissipates, while other ethereal organs vanish almost immediately.

> ##88408D|Gossip about RPC-009 is apparently all over the Authority, especially in Antarctica where he is being contained. Apparently he's now transferred to the Protectorate, and he even fought a Howler and lived. I am not sure if this should be believed...##
> 
> ##88408D|Dr. Mccann##

[[/tab]]
[[tab RPC-009-C: "The Wyrth"]]
The Wyth is an orange-colored, cylindrical shape that slithers around the torso and rib cage like a snake. It represents all of a person's fundamental needs. The longer it is, the more needy a person is. People who are pampered and selfish, or are incredibly determined and fueled by motivation, have an unmoving Wyrth wrapped entirely around their abdomen. This provides a level of rigidity to their internal fortitude.
[[/tab]]
[[tab RPC-009-D: "The Glove"]]
[[include component:image-block name=https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/440652449641136128/805070375259340830/glove.png|caption=RPC-009-B as depicted by Dr. Morris]]
The Glove is a green-colored ethereal organ that surrounds a person's dominant hand. It acts as a physical connection between a person's 'ether' and the real world. One usually needs to use their Glove in order to cast spells and interact with this kind of magic. If you lose your dominant hand, you can't perform any RPC-009 magic anymore. People who do not have a naturally dominant hand actually have two Gloves which allows them to perform magic that normally requires two people.

~~~~

> ##88408D|I heard that the Gun Wizard is, how you say it, 'ambidextrous'. He must have two ethereal gloves... Very dangerous man. --Macgregor##

[[/tab]]
[[tab RPC-009-E: "The Death"]]
The Death: At the back of a person's neck is a small black orb which represents all of the evils within a person. Those who become enlightened and truly pure have their soul wrapped entirely around their neck, without any Death. Those who become corrupt and evil have their Death expand and grip their head with dark tentacles, eventually covering the entire head save for the two openings at the eyes for the Soul.
