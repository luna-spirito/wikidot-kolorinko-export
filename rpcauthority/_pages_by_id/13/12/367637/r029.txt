**Containment Protocols:** RPC-560 is stored in storage locker 7, a 10³ Beta-class containment locker at [[[Site-015]]]. Four members of [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/delta-1-the-keepers MST Unit Delta-1 "The Keepers"][[footnote]] Specializes in the containment and protection of high-value anomalous objects from external entities.[[/footnote]] and four members of MST Bravo-6 "Knockin' On Heaven's Door"[[footnote]]Formed to capture and protect anomalies resembling Christian religious entities.[[/footnote]] are to be guarding the entrance of RPC-560's containment chamber at all times. Shifts rotate every 5 hours.

The Department of History is to search for anything related to RPC-560 in historical texts, especially in the Bible. 

**Description:** RPC-560 is a semi-sentient[[footnote]]RPC-560 has been observed to move, albeit barely, and is capable of producing vocalizations.[[/footnote]] winged humanoid bearing white steel armor resembling that worn by 15th-century English knights. RPC-560 usually stands upright with both arms crossed. A Latin inscription has been carved onto the breastplate of RPC-560's armor "Acceptat Deus nisi dignus." which when translated reads "God only accepts the worthy."

When a human subject capable of comprehending the phrase says it aloud and makes physical contact with RPC-560, they will immediately begin floating upwards at a straight axis at a speed of approximately 175 kilometres per hour. Subjects will not experience any physical distress which would normally be brought on by the severe decrease in bodily temperature or increase in pressure caused by traveling at such altitudes. Subjects will instead remain conscious and capable of speaking despite any physical damages they may receive which would render them incapable of doing so.

Upon being launched into the air, subjects will respond with distress and worry. Upon reaching the troposphere,[[footnote]]The troposphere is the lowest level of the Earth's atmosphere.[[/footnote]] subjects will experience symptoms similar to severe psychosis as well as feelings of euphoria, initially claiming to perceive visual and auditory hallucinations described as being otherworldly, and often perceived as "divine in nature."

These feelings will increase as subjects rise further into the Earth's lower atmosphere, often to the point of hysteria and hypoxia.[[footnote]]A condition where not enough oxygen makes it to the cells and tissues in the body. This can be achieved by going too far into the atmosphere.[[/footnote]] As subjects rise into the stratosphere,[[footnote]]The stratosphere is the second-lowest level of the Earth's atmosphere.[[/footnote]] they will become increasingly nonverbose when asked to describe hallucinations. At this point, subjects will generally diverge into hysterics. Subjects are also observed speaking to a separate, unseen entity.

When the subject enters the mesosphere,[[footnote]]The mesosphere is the third level of the Earth's atmosphere.[[/footnote]] they will cease ascending and will begin to fall back toward the ground at a fast pace. Subjects at this point will begin to endure the full physical and mental effects of RPC-560 brought on by their ascent into the air, and will either expire before eventually hitting ground level, or hit the ground with immense force.

> **Recovery Log:**
> 
> RPC-560 was initially discovered by Group of Interest "The Reformed Clergy of the Spirits", or just The Reformed Clergy. Documents confiscated from the Clergy reveal that RPC-560 was initially discovered sometime around March of 1988. [[footnote]] The Clergy is a religious anomalous organization that follows similar teachings to the Christian religion but at a superficial level. Their beliefs are based around a unique variant of tritheism over the traditional monotheistic beliefs of Christianity, which means they see both God, Satan and the Holy Spirit as deities. They have been connected to a handful of religious anomalies, or are at least related to incidents involving religious anomalies. They have no connection with the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/vatican-primacy-hub Vatican Primacy].[[/footnote]]
> 
> Very little documentation exists detailing the origins of RPC-560, other than a small amount taken from The Clergy describing RPC-560 as a centrepiece of their religion, as it was the holy relic placed at the peak of a hill outside of Sedona, Arizona, and claimed RPC-560 had been placed outside of the town as a means of allowing its inhabitants to prove their devotion and rejecting those deemed unworthy of entering the ‘Kingdom of God' and worthy of entering the 'Domain of Satan' where they be punished for the rest of their days.
