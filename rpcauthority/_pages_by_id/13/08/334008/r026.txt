**Registered Phenomena Code:** 905

**Object Class:** Beta-Orange

[[include component:hazards
|aggression=show|sensory=show|top-spacing=none]]

**Containment Protocols:** The outer doors of RPC-905 are to be chained shut and padlocked [[footnote]]See Addendum-905-1 and Incident-905-1 for more details[[/footnote]], with signs instructing passing pedestrians to keep out. The outer perimeter must be patrolled by three ASF Officers wearing civilian security uniforms and carrying sidearms only. Shifts are to rotate every five hours. The chains and padlocks are only to be removed for testing purposes. Under no circumstances are Staff allowed to enter. Instances of RPC-905-1 are to be terminated unless required for testing purposes.

**Description:** RPC-905 is an abandoned warehouse located in the outskirts of Denver, Colorado. The building itself consists of white metal plates with a steel roof. Three doors allow entry to the building: one is a set of steel double doors, one is a delivery entrance, and one is a door right next to the delivery entrance. It is unknown as to what the purpose of the warehouse was before abandonment; researchers believe that it was most likely an item storage facility.

 Southeast of RPC-905 is a yard surrounded by a barbed chain-linked fence. Graffiti adorns both the yard and the side of the warehouse that faces the street. It is only the interior of the building where RPC-905's anomalous nature begins to manifest itself.

The interior of the warehouse is completely dark, with all of the windows being blocked and barricaded. Once inside RPC-905, the senses of the person who entered are diminished by unknown means, although the subject will retain their sense of touch. Subjects entering the building have expressed feelings of unease and vulnerability within the building, and a feeling of being watched. The following is a list of unusual occurrences within RPC-905. All these phenomena cease when the subject attempts to illuminate the source.

* Humans of unknown race or gender are seen on the periphery of flashlight beams. Some subjects state these people seem deformed or entirely inhuman altogether.

* When the interior of RPC-905 is illuminated, the lights will die out at an abnormal rate. Though the power of the light itself will not be reduced.

* Persons who enter RPC-905 bump into what feels like another person, or something else entirely. This causes stress in the subject.

* Mangled bodies found inside of RPC-905 are reported to reanimate approximately 24 hours after their supposed death.

* Subjects who enter RPC-905 and encounter the mangled creatures have a estimated 50% chance to return in a box. Subjects have been found both alive and deceased.

Fully illuminating the interior of RPC-905 negates all anomalous effects, but light sources   will begin to burn out in a quarter of the expected time. Once the lights in RPC-905 begin to dim to about 50%, the anomalous properties resume as normal.

RPC-905-1 instances are reanimated corpses of subjects who have been killed inside RPC-905, and are hostile to everyone they encounter but themselves and other instances. It is currently unknown who, or what is causing subjects to end up deceased inside of RPC-905.

> RPC-905 was found shortly after a group of three teenagers went missing inside of the Warehouse. Most likely trying to explore abandoned ruins. Two of the teens were found stuffed into boxes. They were unconscious, but injured.  The third body was mangled with both muscle and bone being exposed. The third teen later reanimated, screeched at the search team, and fled.

[[collapsible show="Show Incident 905-1" hide="Hide Incident 905-1"]]

> **Date:** 7/19/2016
> 
> **Time:** 12:36 PM, MDT
> 
> **Incident:** During both a new moon and a cloudy night, four RPC-905-1 instances forcefully broke down the steel double doors which lead into RPC-905. The 905-1 instances immediately began to harass on-site security and a fight immediately ensued between on-site ASF units and the 905-1 instances. 
> 
> Two ASF units were killed along with one RPC-905-1 instance. One ASF unit was injured. Two 905-1 instances escaped. And one 905-1 instance retreated into the warehouse.
> 
> The lights were turned on to search for the RPC-905-1 instance which had fled back into the warehouse. It was later discovered that the lights had negated all of the anomalous effects of the warehouse. The 905-1 instance was found cowering in a crate and was terminated immediately. The RPC-905-1 instances which had escaped were found not long after. They were also terminated.

[[collapsible show="Show Addendum 905-1" hide="Hide Addendum 905-1"]]

> Since Incident 905-1, the doors of RPC-905 are to be chained and padlocked and are only to be unlocked during testing. It has also been discovered that light will negate all anomalous properties inside of RPC-905. Under no circumstances are staff allowed to enter RPC-905. Testing must now be approved by a Senior Researcher or higher. We are sorry for the inconvenience.
> 
> I almost forgot to mention, apparently, this entire incident could have been prevented if there were precautions put in place to terminate instances of RPC-905-1. So, I have decided to make a minor change to the containment protocols. From now until the foreseeable future, any instance of RPC-905-1 is to be terminated unless required for testing purposes.
> 
> On that note, IF you need an instance of RPC-905-1 alive for testing purposes, please inform me. We do not want a repeat of Incident-905-1. That is all.
> 
> - Senior Researcher Jackson, Head of Research for RPC-905
