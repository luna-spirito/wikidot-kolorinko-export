**Containment Protocols**: As the phenomenon is isolated to a singular planet and its atmosphere, the containment efforts are to see GRPC forces enacting a quarantine of Cif, ARES VII planetary system. The quarantine is to be overseen by MST Hotel-8 combat, expeditionary, and diplomatic forces. 

Foreign life is not to be introduced to and allowed to reproduce on the surface of Cif and its atmosphere. All vessels entering or exiting the atmosphere of Cif are to be sterilized thoroughly.

**Description**: GRP-144 refers to the regional anomaly affecting the evolutionary pathways of organic life localized on the planet of Cif. GRP-144 was noted in all branches of life, presumed to have naturally developed on Cif, and is expressed by:

# The promotion and introduction of genes resulting in the phenotypical development of organic ballistic and chemical weapons.
# The repression of the genes not responsible for the phenotypes outlined above.

The majority of the life cycle of organisms affected by GRP-144 (hereby referred to as GRP-144-1) consists of engaging in semi-organized warfare with other species. Combat between subspecies has been noted prior but is considered an outlier. 

It must be noted that the said combat rarely possesses survival advantage and is performed more as a form of social requirements necessary for the organism's mental health. 

In single-cellular organisms, organelles capable of the rapid excretion of Chlorine gas as a form of chemical warfare and shooting pieces of condensed fibrous proteins at high velocity developed. Both are commonly used for ongoing combat between the species.

Multicellular life affected by GRP-144 with advanced nervous systems can enact actions most comparable to military drills and combat formations as a form of instinctive behavior. This is often accompanied by a brain capable of performing advanced calculations through the star's location to angle their shots in an artillery-like fashion.

[[collapsible show="[+] Addendum 1: Planetary Report" hide="[-] Hide Log"]]

**Overview**: Cif is a terrestrial planet located in the ARES VII planetary system. ARES VII is an A-type main-sequence star with a 1.7 solar mass. With an average surface temperature of 42°C, water exists in liquid form on Cif’s surface. It must be noted that the said water is acidic due to the high concentration of hydrochloric acid formed on account of the chlorine-rich atmosphere, having an average pH of 4.5.

The landscape of Cif is considerably flat due to the high level of chemical weathering affecting rock formations. As such, terrestrial sedimentary rocks are near non-existent and the landscape tends to be clay-like. The said clay is rarely abundant in minerals due to the dissolution of clay minerals in acidic conditions, sparking fierce competition between the species of flora.

[[collapsible show="[+] Addendum 3: GRP-144-1-ε - Extended Entry" hide="[-] Hide Log"]]
> GRP-144-1-ε are a species produced through selective breeding of human subjects on the surface of Cif. Originating from 80 human subjects first introduced to Cif on Standard Galactic Time 35432 hours, members of GRP-144-1-ε retain their humanoid shape. Due to the effects of GRP-144, the lack of primary genetic variety was not a detriment to their survival. Much of the fatty tissue was removed through exposure to GRP-144, with the subjects now weighing 32 kg on average. This, accompanied by the thinning of legs and development of hooves-like structures through the merge of talus and calcaneus bones, allows members of GRP-144-1-ε to move at speeds of roughly 97 km/h while traversing flatlands — their primary habitats.
>  
> At maturity, the subject's secondary stomach ruptures, revealing an 800 mm tube comprised of Teflon and Thulium which connects to the body by a thickened vein linked to the pellet-producing bone regions. As long as the connection remains unsevered, members of GRP-144-1-ε are capable of firing round pellets comprised primarily of collagen. The aforementioned pellets travel at an average speed of 600 m/s and are used offensively.

[[collapsible show="[+] Addendum 4: Archaeological Site-AR7-CIF-1 Exploration Log" hide="[-] Hide Log"]]
During the sampling aimed at investigating the properties of Cif's soil and its relation to GRP-144, a structure most comparable to an underground bunker was located. Further expeditionary teams were called to the location to help with a possible unearthing.

Upon entry, the structure — comprised primarily of an array of fluoropolymers — was found to be outfitted with crude electronic devices. From their design and organization, the bunker appears to be a targeting station of sorts. This hypothesis is supported by the discovery of several topographic maps of the area imprinted on sheets of white silicon. While heavily damaged, access to the lower levels was achieved.

Halls filled with records both religious and scientific were uncovered and analyzed by the linguistic sub-division of MST Hotel-8. While the texts were dated to be 2500-2600 years in age, no considerable damage was done due to the acidification-resistant writing material. With much of the texts preserved, a semi-complete lexicon of the language was created. For a complete report of the artifacts, see Addendum-5.

Fourteen instances of ballistic missiles with an expected range of 4'500 km were located on the lower levels. Promptly, after precautions relating to ballistic missiles code of conduct were undertaken, the devices were studied. Instead of a chemical fuel, the rocket was to be powered by an electric spring propulsion system. All of the weapons were of the chemical kind, filled with concentrated chlorine gas.

[[collapsible show="[+] Addendum 5: Archaeological Site-AR7-CIF-1 Artefact Analysis" hide="[-] Hide Log"]]
Religious symbolism relating to warfare is a persisting motif within the structure. There is a notable lack of imagery relating to other deities common in most polytheistic societies (harvest, fertility, etc.). While at first the tripedal creatures with round heads were presumed to be a depiction of the divine, a medical diagram of the species disproved this theory. Object A-2 further supports this interpretation.

**Object A-1**
A scenery of two tripedal creatures with featureless round heads. The scene is that of battle, with the two targeting each other with long tubes held by 2/3 of their thin appendages. The painting is more dynamic than most, suggesting it to of a different artistic style.

**Object A-2**
A mural detailing the mythicized history of tripedal creatures — from throwing rocks to bow-like boxes to intercontinental missiles. The selection of paints seems to be limited to black, white, and orange. Whether this is a stylistic choice or a limitation is unknown. Notably, a figure titled "R'irh" in the shape of the fighters oversees the battlefield. This is suspected to be a representation of the deity related to war.

**Object A-3**
An inscription found engraved on all ballistic missiles. The text is highly stylized and ornate. Irregularities in the inscriptions suggest that they have been engraved by hand rather than in a hypothetical armaments factory. This also disputed the prior assumptions that the text consists of manufacturing information, as it appears more religious in nature. 

The following is a transcript of the aforementioned inscription (note that the translation is incomplete).
