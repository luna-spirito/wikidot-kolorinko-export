+++ Containment Protocols:
All instances of RPC-758 must be tattooed with Videric sigils.[[footnote]] A ceremonial sigil composition named after the Videric class of drugs. It makes RPC-758 instances visible and was historically used by RPC-758 tribes to contact humans societies. [[/footnote]]  Aggressive instances are to be contained in a sanitized containment chamber paneled with Himalayan salt. Docile instances are to be contained in a standard humanoid compartment cell with minimal luxuries. Classical and gospel church music are played continuously for tranquilization purposes. 

At least 5 religious/spiritual items such as crucifixes, talismans, and/or beads are to be placed inside the containment cell per instance. The items must be of cultural significance to the RPC-758 species’ culture of origin. Feeding will be done once per week with CSD-class personnel wearing the [[[patent-426 |AHA3 “Holy Armor”]]] system.[[footnote]] The Anti-Hex Body Armor System vIII (AHA3), informally named the "Holy Armor" is the third incarnation of the anti-thaumaturgic armor system. [[/footnote]] Once RPC-758 is done feeding, the CSD-class personnel must report for immediate psychological diagnosis and possible treatment if necessary. 

All personnel stationed at RPC-758 must follow strict hygienic and dietary practices. Per supervision of the Office of Ethics, the culture around sites containing RPC-758 must demonstrate a healthy work environment.[[footnote]] As RPC-758 is repelled by the concept of “love and friendship” personnel are encouraged to display friendly interaction with one another. [[/footnote]] Adequate knowledge of the practice of exorcism will be required of all staff working with or next to RPC-758. Personnel of the Abrahamic faith are required to carry crucifixes if they are of a Christian denomination, or a bottle of honey and water if they are of the Islamic faith. If the RPC-758 instance is non-Abrahamic of origin, it may be exorcised according to the practices of their regional culture.

Subjects showing symptoms of RPC-758-A must meet with medical staff for treatment immediately. Therapy sessions will be compensated for. A specialized [[[viderics-guide |Leptosiderine drug]]] was made to magnify the stimulative effect of RPC-758-C and help affected subjects recover.

+++ Description: 
**Anatomy:** RPC-758 designates a genus of semi-incorporeal humanoid parasites with a loosely bio-sulfuric makeup. RPC-758 instances are invisible to the naked eye but can be viewed via tattooed sigils, rituals, Videric-class drugs…etc.

RPC-758 have no digestive system, respiratory system, nor reproductive system. Instead, an intricate mass of what appears to be fat, sulfuric compound, and neuron cells replicating the aforementioned systems take its place. The mass is theorized to operate its metabolism. RPC-758 gains sustenance through negative human emotions[[footnote]] "Emotions" aren't clearly defined, as RPC-758 cannot feed on hormones and brainwave signals. [[/footnote]] feeding and gaining mass through anomalous means. Different species of RPC-758 will prefer different emotional states to feed on, all of which revolve around human suffering. 

**Behavioral Characteristics:** Contemporary cultural norms influence the physical and mental health of RPC-758. Notions that are branded as "satanic" by large groups of people become another source of sustenance for the entities. RPC-758 instances that are participating in and/or are near "satanic objects" such as tarot cards or death metal albums will exhibit higher aggression, strength, and productivity. Inversely, RPC-758 is repelled by positive human emotions and concepts such as love, empathy, joy, etc. Exposure to these virtues will weaken the instance and in enough quantity, terminate them.

RPC-758 have developed a diverse culture,[[footnote]] See: Addendum-758-A1: Species and Culture [[/footnote]] prominently revolving around mischief and hatred towards humanity. Intellectually, RPC-758 is found to possess roughly human-level intelligence. IQ test results estimate their intellect from 50-70 points; however, some outliers have demonstrated possessing an IQ point above 1██. 

Despite their relatively low intellect, RPC-758 instances demonstrate a substantial understanding of complex thaumaturgic arts, particularly alchemy. It can transfigure alchemical constructions out of its own body parts, most of which exhibit minor anomalous properties.[[footnote]] See Addendum-758-A2: Anomaly Constructions [[/footnote]] Another talent present within the entire genus is a sophisticated understanding of sigil composition and creation, which they can use to brand intricate spells unto objects to exhibit anomalous properties.

**Life Cycle:** RPC-758 are parasites dependant on humans to continue their life cycle, starting with locating a human host.[[footnote]] Although preferences vary, most RPC-758 instances choose their host by their looks. [[/footnote]] The life cycle of the genus can be seen below:

> __**Stage 1:**__ RPC-758 will search for a human subject to infect. Once it finds a perfect host, it will follow them. RPC-758 will send its host subliminal messages, amplifying the subject’s instinctive drive and impulse to commit morally ill actions. If the subject is able to refrain from pursuing their desire, RPC-758 will leave in search of another host.
>

> 
> __**Stage 2:**__ After 2-4 weeks RPC-758 will have successfully conceived its offspring in its host. An embryo made of neural oscillation designated RPC-758-A materializes inside its host’s central nervous system. For the next 2 months, the RPC-758-A embryo is stagnant.
> 
> ------
> 
> __**Stage 3:**__ 2 months after infection, RPC-758-A will feed on its host’s continual psychological deterioration. The host will develop mental disorders reminiscent of GAD[[footnote]] Generalized anxiety disorder. [[/footnote]] and/or Major Depressive Episodes. The subject will develop focal seizures which will worsen over time.
> 
> ------
> 
> __**Stage 4:**__ After 3 months of being infected, RPC-758-A will damage the subject's right prefrontal cortex, prefrontal, and temporal lobe regions, allowing for RPC-758-B to manifest. RPC-758-B is a hallucinatory projection of both the subject’s instinctual desire for immediate pleasure as well as RPC-758-A’s subliminal messages. 
> 
> Subjects with stronger willpower and/or a healthier psychological status will manifest an entity designated RPC-758-C to fight against the RPC-758-A parasite. RPC-758-C is a hallucinatory projection of the subject’s internalization of moral agency. 
> 
> RPC-758-C is recounted to appear like "a tiny little angel", affirming to the subject’s moral conscience. If the subject were to follow RPC-758-C's directions consistently, the RPC-758-A parasite will eventually starve and expire. However, if the subject ignores RPC-758-C, they will inevitably follow into RPC-758-A's craving to its eventual birth.
> 
> ------
> 
> __**Stage 5:**__ After 4 months, the subject can no longer prevent RPC-758 from being born. Subjects will experience excruciating pain as their nervous system collapse into each other to form a new organism. The subject will then vomit out a mixture of blood, fat, and neuron cells, which will slowly morph into a fully developed RPC-758 instance. The subject at this point would have either expired from shock or undergo brain death.

RPC-758 will grow up until it reaches adulthood at 7 years old. The average lifespan of RPC-758 instances is 50 years.

+++ Addendum-758-A1: Species and culture
RPC-758 is the most abundant and widespread genus of bio-apparations. Currently, there are 178 known species of RPC-758, each with a unique set of behavior and characteristic. While RPC-758 is sexless, some species such as the "Alder Elf" (RPC-758-2) have gendered physiques, which implies the existence of sexual dimorphism among RPC-758.[[footnote]] Sexual dimorphism is the condition where the sexes of the same species exhibit different characteristics. [[/footnote]] The purpose of this separation is societal, as RPC-758 generally have similar gender roles as humans. 

RPC-758 are highly social and territorial beings with an affinity for hierarchal society. Their tools, culture, and language are borrowed directly from humans societies. They are just as likely to fight in inter-species conflict as they are with fighting humans. RPC-758 are prone to believe in religions of Abrahamic origins, but due to their satanic nature, they are impaired from practicing it. Instead, RPC-758 practice Shamanism, the Occult, and in some cases instances, sects of the Nihil faiths.

__**Object Description:**__ The masculine “Red Imp” appears quite similar to our modern depiction of a stereotypical devil. The primarily masculine Red Imp has animalistic features that can easily be compared to existing animals. Their horns and tails, for example, match up with the horns of Bovidaes such as the American bison or the Impala. These animalistic traits, however, are purely an aesthetic display, serving no function to the imp’s day-to-day life. Other notable anatomical features are its red skin and naturally long fingernails.

__**Behavioural Characteristics:**__ Culturally, the red imp is highly aggressive towards humans, leaving no second thought to attack humans on sight unless they’re looking for a host to infect. This nature isn’t determined by their inherited psychology, but rather, the various sects of Christianity’s attitude towards devils as a whole. The Red Imps have two violently opposing divides in their beliefs. One being theistic Satanism,[[footnote]] Umbrella term for religious groups that consider Satan to be an objectively existing deity. [[/footnote]] and the other being the worship of the "Savage Redness" deity.

##8f8f8f|__**Addendum- the Red Crusade:**__ Red imps can be found in many parts of the known world. They primarily reside in religiously Christian nations like Greece, Hungary, Ethiopia, etc. As they make up 66% of the known RPC-758 population,[[footnote]] Since the Red Crusade, the population has been reduced to 32%. [[/footnote]] it is safe to say they are the most common species of RPC-758. Since the 19██ ████████ incident, the Red Imps have proven themselves to be one of humanity's great hazards. The Authority and other intergovernmental agencies have declared ███ against the Nulamalla Concord and the entire RPC-758-1 species. ##

__**Object Description:**__ The “Alder Elf” are more comparable to fairies of European folktales than a devil. Their name comes from the coloration of their skin, which is pale like the bark of an Alder tree. Their antlers are shaped like an Alder tree’s branch as well. Though sexless, these devilfolks[[footnote]] Informal designate for the RPC-758 genus. [[/footnote]] come in both a masculine and feminine form, which is determined by the sex of their former host. The two physiques are vastly different from one another, which stands in stark contrast to other RPC-758 species that have gendered forms.

The masculine Alder elf is goblin-like in appearance. Its face can only be described as a grotesque exaggeration of the male form. It also has a naturally abdominous body, perhaps to keep itself warm during the winter season. The feminine instances meanwhile are distinctively human-like in appearance. Besides their invisibility, they can differentiate by their horns and skin discoloration. The masculine instances are infatuated by jewelry, while the feminine ones have an affinity for flowers.

__**Behavioural Characteristics:**__ By RPC-758 standards, this species is relatively passive towards humans; they only opt to attack us if provoked. While the Red Imps are fueled by humanity’s hatred for demons, the Alder Elfs are based on Europe’s fear of fairies and witches. There are many reports of Alder Elfs stealing human children to eat them or raise them as their own.

##8f8f8f|__**Addendum- Red Crusade:**__ The Alder Elfs have become a useful ally to the Authority and other European agencies in the ███ against the Red Imps. Approximately █,000 anti-thaumaturgic weaponry were commissioned out of Alder Elf communities to aid us in our crusade. ##

+++ Description:

__**Object Description:**__ The dominantly masculine “Red Goblin” is an umbrella term for the various RPC-758 species residing in Eastern Asia such as the Korean Dokkaebi, the Chinese Wangliang, and the Japanese Tengu. There is no set defining characteristic for Red Goblins, which despite its name, comes in a diverse set of skin coloration. This species of RPC-758 sports a heterogeneous body shape, culture, and extra-animalistic feature.

__**Behavioural Characteristics:**__ In general, Red Goblins don’t hold the same level of disdain for humanity compared to other RPC-758 species. While other devilfolks sustain themselves from making human subjects suffer the consequences of their moral obscenity, Red Goblins extract human suffering through mischievous pranks. These pranks are far from harmless, ramifying in permanent injury and even death. The Red Goblin’s main source of sustenance is pain.

Red Goblins as a whole are more concerned with interspecies conflict than humanity itself. It isn’t uncommon to see one aid humans gain the upper hand against other RPC-758 tribes. The Abrahamic religions, while effective, have a lower damage rate against the Red Goblins because they base their identity on Eastern Religions such as Buddhism, Hinduism, Sikhism and Confucianism. 

##8f8f8f|__**Addendum- The Red Crusade:**__ The Red Goblins have been a useful ally to some Eastern agencies with their ███ against the Red Imps. __**Object Description:**__ The Ash Ghoul of the Middle East are genetically the oldest species of RPC-758. Structurally, they are almost completely incorporeal, more similar to an apparition than an organism.

They inhabit desolate places far from human contact. As a result of their scarce environment, they are frail and blind, only bothering to move when they accidentally stumble upon a human subject.

The concept of ghouls and the undead have long since been forgotten in Islamic communities in the Middle East, their stronger devotion to faith has lead to the permanent degeneration of the Ash Ghoul species.

I write this message for my series of writings in opposition to GD-WAR's final stand against the Nulamalla in Arabia. All too many have asked me what the Demonstorm incident exactly was. Everyone deserves to know the truth of what happened on that very day so that history will not repeat.

It all began in the Great War. After the disastrous █████ Campaign, the Ottomans looked to occultism to gain any advantage they can muster. They would end up allying with a Red Imp tribe called the Nulamalla for weapons. In exchange, the Ottomans offered them human sacrifices. Those poor souls were prisoners of war and innocent Armenians who would become prey to the devils.

Their new weapons truly were something extraordinary. Iron trumpets which can deafen ears, muskets that can vaporize men; I remember watching a mercury missile drop into an enemy formation and gasp as those men melt into molten iron.

Enemies of the Ottoman began to fear our weapons and especially the Nulamalla. While the existence of devilfolks was common knowledge, it wasn’t known yet they grew with hatred. They demonized us, which only made the Nulamalla stronger and their weapons deadlier. New instruments of war will be produced, leading to our enemies hating us even more and thus, strengthening the red imps. This cycle of hate continued on and on, it seemed like there was no limit to how powerful a devilfolk can be.

This cycle reached a crescendo on January 30th, 1916. I saw it with my own eyes. Soldiers from both sides halting fire as unwavering dread succumbs their souls. They were unaware their true enemies were invisible, that the weapons they hold were feeding off of every ounce of hate, anger, frustration, desire. Then they began frantically shooting blindly, hundreds dying from stray bullets as disillusioned soldiers try to fight what they saw as demons crawling around their bodies. 

After that, they vomited. Blood, fat, pieces of their own brain. The battlefield glowed red, drowning grain of sand with blood. It all molded into one: the red king of all devils. It reigned earth for 2 minutes before collapsing into its own body from its weight. In that short amount of time, I counted its heartbeat. 1... 2... 3... It beat its heart 306 times. By the time it stopped beating, 26,000 men became all but blood.

The city was all covered in red as the voice of a thousand invisible infants cried on what remained of those bodies. I was one of the few men who survived. During the battle, I was praying with a group of Armenian soldiers. I told them God will protect us in our fight against the Russians, and we just needed to trust Him all things will be well. That simple prayer, an act of love and duty, shielded us from the red king.
