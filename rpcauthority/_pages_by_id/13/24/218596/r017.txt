**Registered Phenomena Code:** BUTT

**Object Class:** Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** Sentient, Sapient (?), Mechanical, Aggression

[[include component:image-block name= https://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/butter/bunnypclol.webp
 |caption=Photograph of RPC-BUTT-A in an inert state.]]

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-BUTT-A and BUTT-B are currently stored within the LO Anomalous Storage Losers of Site-305. Dossiers produced by RPC-BUTT are to be distributed among personnel for comedic purposes.

**Description:** RPC-BUTT designates two tangentially related anomalies, BUTT-A and BUTT-B.

RPC-BUTT-A is a "Bedtime Originals Blossom Plush Bunny Stuffed Animal Toy", animated by the ectoplasmic remains of Dr. Snood of Site-305 in Kucinoka, Florida.

RPC-BUTT-B is an Underwood Touch-Master 5 typewriter. While unobserved, RPC-BUTT-B manifests a sheet of paper through currently unknown means. When in proximity to one another, RPC-BUTT-A will utilize the typewriter to write out an entirely fictional and generally poorly-formatted attempt at an official Authority document (RPC-BUTT-C). Aberrations exist (RPC-BUTT-D) that deviate from the format to a greater extent, often in the form of short stories.

While their substance is rarely, if ever, consistent, RPC-BUTT-C/D instances are universally incredibly ridiculous in their implications, highly irreverent to Authority protocols and procedures, and possess little to no understanding of the titles and terminology used by Authority personnel.

This process varies wildly in the time taken to produce it and the content thereof, ranging from 3 days to like, a year, and once completed, the anomaly becomes completely inert for however long she feels like I guess.

**Addendum 1: Documented RPC-BUTT-C Instances**

* [*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-341 RPC-341] - **AMALGAMATE, Or Children of Wrath**

[[collapsible hideLocation="both" show="[+] Open" hide="[-] Close"]]

Amalgamate was the first actual SCP I ever started working on, and is the first draft I submitted to their greenlight forum. It never got greenlit whatsoever, but I had a strange affection for it given my love/fear of body horror and other more personal issues. I unironically enjoy this one, and I'm forever amazed that I managed to get it up, given everything that happened between it being written and it being posted here.

Its original working title was Cronenbaby. I think that was pretty funny.

Its also probably my best work, judging by the ratings, which is awesome since this took forever to write. My work after this didn't really have the same amount of effort put in, but at the same time they didn't have as rough a start. 341 is good, but the conditions and effort that made it good aren't really sustainable.

[[/collapsible]]

* RPC-YYY - ERLKOENIG, Or The Threat of Predestination

[[collapsible hideLocation="both" show="[+] Open" hide="[-] Close"]]

This one hasn't been posted yet, but it was what almost led to me being an SCP author instead of an RPC one. After AMALGAMATE wasn't well received, I put this one up on the forums and it was greenlit twice and approved of in a few days. In fact, I got a few popular SCP authors to look and it and they LIKED IT. I was over the moon.

Then a lot happened. Put a wrench in things. Definitely took a good look at the SCP community. 

I decided not to stick around. I still haven't been able to work on ERLKOENIG for a long time.

[[/collapsible]]

* [*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-611 RPC-611] - WRAITH, Or The Antidote to the Human Condition

[[collapsible hideLocation="both" show="[+] Open" hide="[-] Close"]]

611 is a tough one to go over. After I posted 341, something else came up, and among other things, led to me leaving the RPC community for a little while. My brother was the one who actually came up with the basic idea of ghosts in an abandoned Russian city, and the idea of a German scientist causing it, and the rest became a very drawn-out analysis of an anomaly that ends with it deciding it wanted to blow itself up.

The execution of my articles tends to be what brings them down, in the end, and I think 611 is a painfully obvious example of just that.

Also, over half of it was written ON THE SANDBOX on my PHONE in the CAR. I had to work with that CSS (I used the code from [https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-style-resource here] btw) on a tiny little screen. It was fun!

[[/collapsible]]

* [*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-612 RPC-612] - MONUMENT, Or History Without A Home

I think it was Almarduk who said this idea basically wrote itself. There isn't too much to say about 612 once you're done reading it. I was a little disappointed with it, but I still love it.

* [*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/rpc-613 RPC-613] - PASIGRAPH, Or Rise and Rend Asunder
