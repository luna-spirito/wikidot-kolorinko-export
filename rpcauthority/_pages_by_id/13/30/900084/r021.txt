**Containment Protocols:**

RPC-613-A is to be monitored from its home in Mosul. In the event RPC-613-A causes a breach of secrecy via the usage of RPC-613-B, all relevant witnesses are to be amneticized and media taken down per standard expunging protocols. Alpha-21 "Wolf Pack" is assigned to locating RPC-613-A in such an event.

**Description:**

RPC-613 is the group designation for two related anomalies. RPC-613-A, a human male in Mosul, and RPC-613-B, an anomalous language that has existed for approximately the past 5,000 years that only RPC-613-A can speak fluently.

RPC-613-A is an Arabic male approx. 2.14 meters tall identified as Ishaia Goriel, also known as "Aleimlaq",[[footnote]]"The Giant" in Arabic.[[/footnote]] a priest of a local sect of the Assyrian Church of the East in Mosul. RPC-613-A is a hyperpolyglot[[footnote]]A term referring to an individual unusually capable of becoming fluent in and retaining several languages[[/footnote]], fluent in English, French, Arabic, Spanish, Persian, Kurdish, Old Latin, Turkish, Koine Greek, Aramaic, Classical Hebrew, and Russian. Although appearing otherwise non-anomalous, RPC-613-A displays a remarkably high Theophan resistance of 4 [[footnote]]Theophan is a system of measurement utilized by the majority of the Authority to measure the strength of a cognitohazard's effect relative to the resistance of a sapient entity and the amount of influence it can exert. The majority of cognitohazards are Theophan 1-3, encountered non-anomalously, while the average human has resistance to Theophan 2 or higher. Refer to RCP Office of Analysis and Science. //Standard Categorization of Preternatural Cognitohazards, 2nd ed., Authority, 1993.//[[/footnote]] and possesses the unique ability to utilize RPC-613-B, which it refers to as "pasigraphy".

Using a combination of memes and archetypal phenomena, RPC-613-B forms a "universal language" that is communicated via verbal and gestural components, capable of conversing with any human being provided that they can both see and hear RPC-613-A. While limited and unable to directly communicate abstract or complicated ideas, RPC-613-A can string together several concepts to assist the listener in understanding.

For future reference, transcripts of RPC-613-B will be recorded as whatever concept is communicated within brackets, for example, [WEAPON], [SPEECH], or [LOVE].

**Addendum 1: Discovery**

Originally identified as PoI-3921, RPC-613-A was considered unremarkable pardon a few reports of "speaking in tongues". Due to the rising tensions in Mosul and eventually the 2008-2009 attacks on Mosul Christians, RPC-613-A took their church underground to avoid the rise in terrorist attacks in the area.

In 2014, once the Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant (ISIL) had taken control of Mosul, RPC-613-A was one of 100 or fewer Assyrian Christians remaining in the city that originally had 70,000. After the destruction of the Dair Mar Elia[[footnote]]St. Elijah's monastery, a notable Christian place of worship for the past 1,400 years.[[/footnote]], RPC-613-A had joined the local resistance movement against the regime.

Notably, RPC-613-A recorded several sermons despite the hostile terrorist occupation, calling for action against the ISIL and severely deviating from the Assyrian Church's doctrine on the subject of incredible violence. After they had been anonymously distributed throughout the internet at large, the Authority had taken notice due to the abundance of memes throughout the sermons. While mostly non-anomalous, their frequency and the fact that several were intentionally performed, as well as the usage of RPC-613-B, which was then unknown, resulted in Ishaia Goriel being flagged as PoI-3921 due to their possible understanding of the anomalous. An excerpt of one of the sermons is recorded below.

RPC-613-A utilized their fluency in several languages and on occasion RPC-613-B to assist in communication between the armies, as well as their combat proficiency. By December 9th, 2016, after the liberation of Mosul, Agent "Perigee" located and contacted RPC-613-A, who was at the time working at the farm of a family friend in Mosul. Agent Perigee utilized a camera hidden in one of their shirt's buttons to record the conversation.

**Addendum 2: RPC-613-B Analysis**

[[collapsible show="[+] Level 2/613 Access Required" hide="[-] Close"]]

> //From the linguistics office of [*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/site-286 Site-286], Fr. Antonije.//
> 
> While it is a (respectable) loss to not be able to bring him to our monastery for a more in-depth analysis, I have had the most excellent time analyzing the interviews and discussions with Goriel. What a rich past he has! I have taken great pleasure in this assignment, and I advocate for further study, although I am unsure of how much time we have with him.
> 
> Nonetheless.
> 
> RPC-613-B is a unique language with no definite origin. Records of RPC-613-B's existence besides our own are limited, pardon the (subpar) Vatican's documentation and Goriel's claims. As much as I enjoy his tales, I should clarify that we cannot be sure of their historical basis- Nimrod (or rather, his descendants) founded Babel, but we are unsure of who specifically "commissioned" the Tower, if it even was commissioned and not simply called for. But RPC-613-B is as elaborate as Goriel claims, this "pasigraphy" of his incorporates almost every sound the human tongue can make.
> 
> But this pasigraphy is incredibly difficult to record or describe, due to its nature. RPC-613-B relies on archetypal phenomena- as in, the roots of various words in various languages- and memes. Memes that simply...communicate. I cannot even attempt to record what the gesture or sound made was, beyond what it meant. RPC-613-B is less of a language and more of a string of...data, raw linguistical data, that the mind interprets and communicates to the consciousness. That is the final assessment. RPC-613-B can be taught, can be learned, but it does not come so easily. I can barely understand how I can understand it, that is how bizarre this is.
> 
> I highly entreat you to look into this further. I believe there is great potential in this anomaly for our purposes.

**Description:** PoI-341 is Erdoğan Özdemir, an Ottoman general and charismatic leader in the Middle East. While biologically non-anomalous, 341 is capable of communicating via a variety of memetic keywords and archetypal sounds. PoI-341's primary danger is their charismatic abilities and aggressive attitude towards propagating the military and spiritual conquests of the Ottoman Empire.

Notably, PoI-341's efforts are encouraging several Islamic revivals across Turkey, leading to concerns over dhimmis (non-Muslim subjects) within its borders. While the Capitulations of the Ottoman Empire, a series of contracts between the Ottomans and several European countries entrust the French with the safety of Catholics, the persecution of Christians within Ottoman borders continues, with the Constantinople and Batak massacres throughout the 19th century being notable examples.

Officially these "incidents" are a response to uprisings in the respective areas. However, Russian commissions in Batak have found evidence of mass burials near the church, with conservative estimates of at least 2,000 casualties, although this has been denied by Ottoman authorities. Intelligence networks based in French post offices have recently discovered that PoI-341 may be connected to the Hamidian massacres against the Armenians by Sultan Abdul Hamid II, which resulted in over 100,000 Armenian casualties. This action cannot be left unpunished, and it is believed that PoI-341 will only continue in his fanatic obedience to the Sultan.

[[include component:image-block name=https://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-613/ArmenianMassacre.jfif|caption=Photograph of Armenian victims at a mass burial site. PoI-341 is believed to be the figure on the far left.|width=290px]]

**Maintenance Protocols:** Currently, no known method of containing PoI-341 exists so long as the Ottoman Empire stands. However, intelligence networks have reported that PoI-341 has an only child, and after the death of his wife in 1886, never remarried. His daughter is rarely seen outside of his home, although reports of shouting and violence between the two have been recorded. Some agents state that PoI-341 has accused his daughter, Ceylan Özdemir, of becoming an apostate from Islam or possibly converting to another religion altogether, although to what is currently unknown.

It is believed that PoI-341's ability is inherited, and so Ceylan may have information relevant to the containment of PoI-341. Proposals are currently being developed.
 
**Value Assessment:** PoI-341 is a threat not only to the Catholic Church, but to Christianity as a whole as proved by his actions and activities. So long as PoI-341 lives and remains under the protection of his Sultan, the safety of Christians throughout the Ottoman Empire's domain cannot be guaranteed.
