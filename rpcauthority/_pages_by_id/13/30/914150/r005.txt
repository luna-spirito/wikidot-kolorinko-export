------

**Containment Protocols:** The main bridge heading towards ████████, Oregon is to be restricted from public use and a new one has been constructed 17.7 km west of the initial area. No personnel under level 3 security clearance are to have access to RPC-779-1.

Personnel are not to attempt to remove RPC-779-1 from its current position in order to prevent becoming an RPC-779-2 instance. RPC-779-2 instances that have been prevented from committing suicide are to be given psychiatric care until they have fully recovered.

------

**Description:** RPC-779-1 is a human male of slim build found on a bridge located south from ████████, Oregon.[[footnote]]RPC-779-1 has been seen sitting on the westernmost ledge of the aforementioned bridge.[[/footnote]] His identity has been confirmed to be 19-year-old Markus Boyles[[footnote]]A college student of ████████ University in █████, Oregon.[[/footnote]] according to the wallet found in its possession. The wallet belonging to RPC-779-1 only contains a state identification card, $4 USD, a receipt for flowers, and a photo of a woman's face scratched out.[[footnote]]The only visible feature on the woman is part of their blonde hair, a singe hair clip, and a small drawing of a heart on the bottom left side of the photo.[[/footnote]] Its current apparel includes a grey jacket, a pair of blue jeans, and white sneakers. Communication with RPC-779-1 is not possible as it does not respond to any personnel. RPC-779-1 is seen in a constant posture of slouching over, resting their fore arms on its lap and dangling its legs.

Subjects forcefully moving RPC-779-1 to safety will be met with a feeling of regret and anxiety, from here on afterward the subject is designated RPC-779-2. RPC-779-2 instances will be compelled to jump off of the bridge, though the effect will fade if they are prevented from doing so by outside intervention. Regardless of whether the instance commits suicide or not, RPC-779-1 will immediately stand up and return to their position prior to being removed from the ledge.

[[collapsible show="Interview File #1" hide="Close File"]]
> [[=]]
> __**Interview Transcript 779.1**__
> [[/=]]
> **Interviewer:** Dr. Pierce
> **Interviewee:** Dennis Kalleway
> **Date:** 6/9/2015
> 
> **Foreword:** Due to the Authority's inability to obtain answers from RPC-779-1, researchers attempt to uncover its past by questioning several people, specifically staff of the ████████ University. For reference, Dr. Pierce is the current Head Researcher for RPC-779 and Dennis Kalleway is a councilor at the University in question. Kalleway had claimed to be the councilor for Markus Boyle, this has been confirmed by the institution. Subjects were applied Class A-1 amnestics once the interview had been concluded.
> ------
> 
> [AUDIO BEGIN]
> 
> **Dr. Pierce:** Mr. Kalleway we have a few questions for you and we require that you answer as truthfully as possible. We need as much information on Markus Boyle as possible, do you understand?
> 
> **Kalleway:** Yes, doctor. I will answer with the best of my knowledge.
> 
> **Dr. Pierce:** For what reason did Markus require your help and when?
> 
> **Kalleway:** It was December 17th of last year,[[footnote]]2014[[/footnote]] he had arrived at my office early in the morning, around 6:54 AM. He seemed very overwhelmed with something, I could tell based on his facial expression and voice tone. He told me he was having trouble balancing between the classes he had been assigned to attend.
> 
> **Dr. Pierce:** Anxiety and overwhelmed with the classes, When did it begin?
> 
> **Kalleway:** Since classes began. He was a freshman.
> 
> **Dr. Pierce:** For how long did you meet with him?
> 
> **Kalleway:** I had set up a weekly meeting with him every Wednesday to ensure his mental state was stable. He attended all meetings up until May 13th this year.
> 
> **Dr. Pierce:** How did Markus behave during the last meeting, did he appear distressed or overwhelmed in any way?
> 
> **Kalleway:** No, as a matter of fact he had a smile on his face. I asked about what put him in a good mood that day and he told me he had gotten a girlfriend. We only talked for a few minutes before he left earlier than usual.
> 
> **Dr. Pierce:** Had Markus shared with you a photo of who it was?
> 
> **Kalleway:** Yes, she was a young blonde girl, she looked about his age. Her name was Samantha, though, I am not sure about the last name.
> 
> **Dr. Pierce:** Mr. Kalleway, we have recovered a photo from Markus's wallet, though a majority of the photo has been scratched out. Are you able to make out if this is the exact photo from that day?
> 
> (//Dr. Pierce hands the photo over to Mr. Kalleway for closer examination.//)
> 
> **Kalleway:** It is quite difficult to determine, though from what I can see I can confirm this is the same picture. As for the hair clip, I personally can't remember if she had a clip, but I am sure I didn't pay too much attention to it at the time.
> 
> (//Mr. Kalleway hands the photo back.//)
> 
> **Dr. Pierce:** I have one more question Mr. Kalleway, when Markus shared the photo with you, did he directly hand it to you?
> 
> **Kalleway:** As a matter of fact, yes he did.
> 
> **Dr. Pierce:** Thank you for your cooperation Mr. Kalleway, that is all we need.
> 
> [AUDIO END]
> 
> **Post Interview Note:** Once the interview was concluded, Mr. Kalleway was ordered to supply finger print samples in order to fully confirm this was the exact photo, despite the possibility that the finger prints could have been wiped away since then. Once results were clear, it was confirmed to be the same photo.[[footnote]]2 sets of finger prints had been found on the photo, the other has been confirmed to be Markus's.[[/footnote]]. Further inspection into the students attending ████████ University has lead researchers to believe the person in the photo to be 19-year-old Samantha Fetching, another Freshman student.
[[/collapsible]]
------

> **Addendum 779.1:** During further research, it was discovered that Ms. Fetching was one of the 3 victims involved in an apparent homicide which occured on May 29th, 2015 in a home on █████ Ave. in ████████, Oregon. It has been assumed that RPC-779-1 is in its current location due to a state of grief for Ms. Fetching.

> **Addendum 779.2:** Recent police reports have revealed the physical appearance of the suspect involved with the aforementioned homicide via security tapes of a home across from the crime scene. CCTV cameras had revealed a male figure leaving the home in which the homicide had taken place. The description of said suspect is identical to the clothing worn by RPC-779-1. The weapon used in the crime was a Beretta 92 pistol.
