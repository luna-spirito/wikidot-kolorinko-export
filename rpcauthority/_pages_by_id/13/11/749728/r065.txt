+ **Overview**
[[/=]]
----
**Established:** November 8th, 1957[[footnote]]Construction for the oil field began in 1995, and finished in 1998.[[/footnote]]


**Front Cover:** Shaybah Oil Field

**Location:** Rub' al Khali Desert, 22.5248° N, 54.0086° E

**Codename:** "The Strip"

**Hex-code:** [04]

**Site Occupation:** Low Containment, Object Containment, Research, CSD Holding and Anti-Terrorism

**Division Jurisdiction:** Research and Containment

**Total Area:** 7,435 m^^2^^
710 km (Surface Oil Field)

**Background Information:** Site-004 serves as the largest Authority secure containment facility in the EURASIACOM region, and is responsible for supervising any and all anomalous activity from within the Arabian Peninsula. The site also serves as the primary Authority containment facility for inanimate RPCs, although has made two exceptions.

Site-004 was a personally funded endeavor of the [[[GD-EURA]]] at the time, financed through his shares in the Anglo-Persian Oil Company following the Saudi Oil Boom in the late 1930's. Offically recognized as a Site in the 1950s in the midst of EURASIACOM's economic height, it logistically links up Europe and Asia and manages anomaly transport and logistics from ASIACOM to AFRICOM and WESTCOM.

In the early 1970s, rising extremism, anti-western ideology, and Malthus terrorism became a threat to the operational stability of Site-004, and increased activity from hostile groups of interest around Site-004 became an increasingly bigger problem for security forces, which ultimately culminated in an attempted terror attack on behalf of a regional Church of Malthus cell in 1981.

Site-004 has historically been the site most favoured by [[[GD-EURA]]]. During Authority [[[Budget Cuts]]], the [[[GD-EURA]]] at the time began purchasing both U.S. and Israeli military equipment using money out of his own pocket as an efficient and cheaper alternative to the sudden weapon rollbacks, benefiting ASF and MST units stationed at Site-004.

During Operation Dawn Initiative,[[footnote]]Operation Dawn Initiative was a worldwide counter-insurgency against terrorist groups that are involved in the trading, or usage of the anomalous.[[/footnote]] Site-004 was used by both Authority and Coalition forces as the main Forward Operating Base from the start of the operation in 2001 to the end of the operation in 2006. 

Site-004 doubles as both an oil field and a containment facility, operating under the guise of an oil field run by Saudi Aramco. Site-004 also has a private airstrip, which is utilized to transport Authority personnel and supplies to Site-004. Annual oil production rates are estimated at 14 million barrels, with 70% of the profits going directly towards EURASIACOM's budget.

[[=]]
+ **Offices & Wings Section**
[[/=]]

----

* **Surface Sector:** The surface section of Site-004. Site-004 is in control of an oil field which is a method Site-004 personnel use to gain revenue for the Site and the Authority as a whole. A private airstrip accompanies the oil field and transports both personnel and supplies to the Site. The surface is also home to a wildlife reserve for endangered Arabian animals.

* **Administrative Sector:** The command sector of Site-004. Offices of administrative personnel are located here. Only Level-4+ or specifically authorized personnel are allowed to enter.

* **Personnel/Recreational Sector:** The main housing sector of Site-004. Features a mess hall, multiple research labs, an infirmary, a housing sector able to house around 500 Authority personnel, and multiple rooms for recreational purposes. This sector leads to most of the other sections in Site-004.

* **Underground Farms:** Site-004, being located within the Rub' Al Khali desert, is one of the most remote Authority installations on the planet. Because of this, coupled with how expensive the logistics are to constantly be shipping goods to the site, Site-004 staff have taken it upon themselves to create a complex network of underground farms to make the site self-sufficient and less reliant on outside help.

* **Containment Sectors:** Unlike other major Authority sites, Site-004 utilizes the less common Low/High-risk based containment sector system where containment sectors are segregated based on how much danger any given anomaly may poses to on-site staff. 

* **Low-risk Containment Sector:** The Low-risk Containment Sector is utilized to contain primarily Alpha-class or Beta-class anomalies. Humanoid-type anomalies are infrequently contained at Site-004, being stored temporarily. The only instances of humanoid anomalies being contained at Site-004 indefinitely are RPC-505 and RPC-763.

* **High-risk Containment Sector:** Added to Site-004 in the early 2000's as apart of an expansion to the underground sections of site. This wing of Site-004 is primarily used to contain either Gamma-class anomalies or anomalies that possess a lethality rating of Purple or higher.

* **CSD Cell Block:** Renovated in 2010, Site-004's cell block primarily functions to house its CSD personnel. Due to Site-004 mainly being used to store objects, less than 30 CSD personnel are kept on-site at all times, making it one of if not the smallest cell block of any major Authority site.
