**Registered Phenomena Code:** CN-015

**Object Class:** Alpha-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Geological Hazard, Transmutation Hazard, Emotional Hazard, Animated Hazard, Mechanical Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** MST-Wuchen-15 "Night on the Galactic Railroad", disguised as station attendants, will block the wall of Exit B of [REDACTED] station[!--Guilin Park station--] (where RPC-CN-015-1 is located) at 23:00 every day for reasons of construction until the metro's closure that day. At least one member of MST-Wuchen-15 is to maintain surveillance of the area during the blockade to prevent accidental entry of uninvolved persons into RPC-CN-015. In the event of a misentry, the on-duty personnel is to guide the misentrant to take RPC-CN-015-2 to the Shanghai South Railway Station and then leave RPC-CN-015, and be amnesticized in accordance with standard procedure.

**Description:** RPC-CN-015 is an anomalous space, which can only be accessed through RPC-CN-015-1. Based on the transit map within RPC-CN-015 (see Addendum CN-015-██: Internal structure of RPC-CN-015), it is inferred that it is closely linked to the Shanghai Metro Line ██[!--Line 15--] currently under construction.

RPC-CN-015-1 is the [REDACTED] station's interchange point from the second basement level to the third basement level, which remains a normal wall void of cavity within while its anomalous properties are inert. RPC-CN-015-1 will manifest and be available for passage after the last daily Line ██ train departing for █████ Road[!--Qixin Road--] arrives at this station[[footnote]] As of the current version of this document (21/4/2019), the time is 23:33. [[/footnote]], which usually lasts about 10 minutes. RPC-CN-015-1 is considered "one-way" and cannot be used to leave RPC-CN-015. After passing RPC-CN-015-1 subjects will find themselves on an island platform[[footnote]] Such as Platform 1 Line 2 as shown below. [[/footnote]], subjects would be considered within RPC-CN-015 at this point.

Based on the access route in RPC-CN-015-1, the platform is believed to be situated at the same point as the ██ metro platform under-construction at [REDACTED] station. The platform is identical to other Shanghai Metro platforms and their sceneries. Staying within RPC-CN-015 for more than 3 hours will result in the loss of contact with the surface[[footnote]] This is possibly related to the Shanghai Metro's rule that the dwell time within the station should not exceed 3 hours.[[/footnote]].

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpc-wiki-cn.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-cn-015/Guilin_Park_Station.jpg|caption=Photo of RPC-CN-015-2 after its doors are opened]]

RPC-CN-015-2 is one or more AC09B EMU(s) manufactured by Changchun Bombardier[[footnote]] **Annotation:** The Changchun Bombardier Railway Vehicles Co. Ltd. [[/footnote]]. RPC-CN-015-2 arrives at the station and stops for about 20 seconds within 7 minutes after the first entrant (referred to as "travelers") enters into RPC-CN-015 through RPC-CN-015-1 that day. RPC-CN-015-2 will only appear on the side marked for the █████ ██-████ ████ station[!--Zizhu Hi-tech Park--]. No second train would arrive within 3 hours of RPC-CN-015-2 leaving the platform. 

RPC-CN-015-3 refers to humanoid entities that appear inside RPC-CN-015 which are indistinguishable from ordinary human beings by appearance alone. RPC-CN-015-3 can be divided into two categories, that of "Passengers" and "Attendants". "Passengers" will solely appear inside RPC-CN-015-2 in a similar fashion to station passengers, while "Attendants" only appear under certain situations (see Incident Report CN-015-A). Usually at least one of the "Passengers" shares the appearance of a deceased friend or relative of the traveler who entered RPC-CN-015.

RPC-CN-015 was initially brought to the attention of the Authority by the "sudden appearance" of passengers in front of a wall at the Shanghai South Railway Station. The Authority began an investigation after amnesticizing the people involved and creating cover-up footage.

[[collapsible show="Addendum: CN-015 Excepts of Relevant Experiments" hide="Hide Experiment Log"]]

> __**CN-015 Experiment A-1 (24/12/2015)**__
>
> **Subject:** CSD-05064
> **Procedure:** Subject was ordered to take RPC-CN-015-2 to Shanghai South Railway Station and leave RPC-CN-015 without conducting any additional experiments.
> **Result:** Screening following the subject's departure from the station showed no physiological abnormalities. Subjects showed emotional disturbance but refused to give a reason.
> **Analysis:** Theorization requires additional experiments.
> **Addenda:** Recovered footage shows that one of the RPC-CN-015-3 to be the wife accidentally killed by CSD-05064.

> __**CN-015 Experiment A-2 (3/1/2016)**__
>
> **Subject:** CSD-05064
> **Procedure:** Subject was ordered to take RPC-CN-015-2 to Hongmei South Road station and get off and leave RPC-CN-015, during which they may talk to any RPC-CN-015-3.
> **Result:** Subject lost contact with the surface when he got off at Hongmei South Road station and was presumed lost. Subject was instructed to talk to RPC-CN-015-3, during which RPC-CN-015-3 expressed confusion about the conversation.
> **Analysis:** It is believed that the reason for the loss of CSD personnel is due to the station not existing in reality[[footnote]] **Annotation:** South Hongmei Road only began operation on 23 January 2021. [[/footnote]]. Although RPC-CN-015-3 exhibited sapience during conversation, there appears to be no memory associated with the subject.
> **Notes:** Repeated experiments are needed to confirm the speculation. it is possible that RPC-CN-015-3 exhibits Emotional Hazard to some extent.
> **Addenda** Subjects repeatedly said "sorry" 12 times when conversing with RPC-CN-015-3。

> __**CN-015 Experiment A-7 (23/1/2016)**__[[footnote]]Incident CN-015-A.[[/footnote]]
>
> **Subject:** CSD-05129
> **Procedure:** Subject is instructed to press the emergency brake button on the side of the station after entering RPC-CN-015 and wait for further instructions.
> **Result:** A RPC-CN-015-3 "Attendant" instance appeared in the direction of RPC-CN-015-1 24 seconds after the brake button was pressed and wrestled with the subject, after which communication was severed. The video recording shows that RPC-CN-015-2 remained on track for more than 40 seconds and did not leave the platform even as communication was lost.
> **Analysis:** RPC-CN-015-3 "Attendant" instances will only appear when exhibiting destructive behavior.
> **Notes:** Almost figured out the temperament of RPC-CN-015, perhaps it can be used to catch up to the South Station in the future? But being on a train filled with the dead is definitely not appetizing.
> **Addenda** RPC-CN-015-1 no longer manifests within 2 days after the occurrence of Incident CN-015-A, with its anomalous properties reappearing on the third day.

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpc-wiki-cn.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-cn-015/National_Museum_of_Ethnology%252C_Osaka_-_Spirit_figures_%28Mushiokuri-no-Ningyo%CC%82%29_-_Nanto_City%252C_Toyama_Pref._-_Made_in_1978.jpg|caption=RPC-CN-015-4, cognitohazards removed]]
RPC-CN-015-4 are two dolls made of unknown plants and paper products held together by wire, designated as RPC-CN-015-4-A and RPC-CN-015-4-B.

RPC-CN-015-4-A's padding on the left arm is slightly detached, the paper at its waist is torn, and its legs show signs of pinprick and tearing damages. RPC-CN-015-4-B, on the other hand, is relatively intact. Each of the two figures has a slip of yellow paper, with Campanella's name and that of his grandmother written on the back in black. The right arm of RPC-CN-015-4-A is nailed to the left arm of RPC-CN-015-4-B and covered by the paper "costume". The faces of the figures are drawn on paper. RPC-CN-015-4-A has a grim, pained expression, with lacerations at the corners of the mouth, and rusted "tear stains" on the eyes of the figures. RPC-CN-015-4-B's face is intact, and displays a peaceful expression.

**30/1/2016 Addition:** At 23:34, a day after //Exploration#CN-015-B1//,  MST-Wuchen-15 operators found a note in front of RPC-CN-015-1, the note uses the same type of paper as the one found 
on the back of RPC-CN-015-4. There are writings on it in red. Examination confirmed the handwriting belonged to Campanella.
