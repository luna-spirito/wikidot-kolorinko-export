**Registered Phenomena Code:** 229

**Item Type:** Object

**Lethality Rating:** Alpha-Yellow

[[include component:hazards regenerative=true|immeasurable=true|explosive=true|extreme-temperature=true|visual=true|sensory=true]]

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-229 and RPC-229-1 must be contained in a 10³ meter, temperature-controlled, reinforced steel chamber, with a bulletproof two-way mirror installed in a connecting room for observation purposes within Site-007. No electronic devices are permitted inside the containment chamber with the exception of a single wireless speaker embedded within the outer wall for communication with the reader.

**Description:** RPC-229 is a three-quarter-bound leather book, with test samples from discarded entries that suggest it is composed of 94% human tissue and 6% human bone. RPC-229's features are minimal, with no deterioration regarding its external covers. Held within RPC-229's contents are 426 pages worth of literature told in languages that were both spoken and written from 0 AD to the mid 19th century. All currently translated and legible works within RPC-229 bear consistent changes between entries, suggesting that it was passed along by different authors over the course of its existence. Lastly, in the bottom-left corner of each page belonging to RPC-229, there exists a stamped crest that bears an insignia consisting of a shield with the letter C prominently displayed in the middle. Behind its crest are four avian-like wings which wrap around its furthermost tip and point to their opposite sides.

Although attempts to damage or open RPC-229 are ineffective when it is closed, said attempts do not apply to its contents when it is opened with the assistance of RPC-229-1. Upon opening RPC-229 and by ripping or obfuscating any page, RPC-229 will reform itself and a blank page will reappear to replace the lost parchment upon closing its covers and reopening. Any pages removed from RPC-229 are shown to lose any anomalous properties they once had during a reading.

RPC-229-1 is a 152 cm tall altar composed of cherry wood and inlaid with golden carvings which stand upon four stone feet. RPC-229-1 has three drawers, three mirrors, and two circular, flat surfaces for the placement of writing utensils for when RPC-229 is in use. Upon recovery, three items were found alongside RPC-229-1 and RPC-229. Of these were two sets of quill and ink and a single entry torn from the pages of 229, all of which have currently exhibited no anomalous properties. 

To open RPC-229, it must be placed within the rectangular slot indented upon RPC-229-1's angular roof. When affixed, the book will begin to levitate approximately 12.7 cm off the surface before rotating to face the reader. RPC-229 will then float as long as it is not forced or moved away from RPC-229-1. 

Further anomalous manifestations will not occur unless:

* The subject has an understanding of the language in the entry.
* The literature is read out loud in its entirety.
* The subject is not interrupted by outside interference during a reading.

Upon reading aloud an entry, the reader shall begin to develop symptoms of heightened hysteria before losing consciousness as higher brain functions cease and their motor skills are impaired. Any attempts to wake the reader will be unsuccessful until they have successfully finished their entry or RPC-229 is closed. Upon returning to consciousness, the reader will exhibit weariness and confusion which will last for roughly one hour, but specific knowledge regarding the story they had read will remain obscured.

After the reading is over, one of many anomalous occurrences will appear in the space above 229-1 (See Test Log 1-A through 1-C for more details). Any anomalous effects resulting from the reading of RPC-229 will have no effect on the reader even if the space around them has been significantly damaged.

[[collapsible show="+Discovery" hide="-Discovery"]]
**Discovery**: On 10/█/████ outside the town of ████ within the United Kingdom, local news outlets and surrounding occupants in a radius of 10 miles reported a magnitude 2.0 earthquake that shook the area for more than ten seconds. Triangulation using numerous observation outposts surrounding Site-007 revealed the source to be a large forest on the outskirts of ████. Following reports of the explosion in the county, MST Foxtrot-7 “Highland Cattle” were sent to investigate. Upon arriving, the appearance of the area seemed consistent with the reports of a wildfire, but all flames had been put out with seemingly no outside interference.

Investigations into the area revealed a log cabin that had been burned to its foundation, with three unidentified, charred bodies situated around the dwelling. The only undamaged items in the vicinity of the house were RPC-229 and RPC-229-1. Foxtrot-7 then encountered a fleeing local who was placed under Authority custody and suspected to be the culprit of the fire. An on-scene interrogation revealed that the man and three others had placed RPC-229 on RPC-229-1 when he had uncovered the once previously hidden cabin before reading a currently unknown entry. The resulting explosion burnt the household down and the three other culprits were killed in the ensuing fires. 

All nearby towns were issued Class-A amnestics, and the following cover-up story was printed about an unexpected earthquake and a dry wildfire. Recovery teams were sent out to retrieve RPC-229, RPC-229-1, and conduct further investigation of what remained of the cabin. Unfortunately, significant fire damage destroyed any evidence that might have been involved in the creation or origin of 229 and 229-1. No other bodies were found around the dwelling, suggesting that it had been abandoned for some time.[[/collapsible]]

- **Test Log 1-A conducted by Researcher Oberon on 2/█/████**

**Foreword**: CSD-00293 is instructed via the loudspeaker to acquire RPC-229 from the first drawer and place it atop RPC-229-1, then read from page 356.

**Title**: Infernus Ignis

**Page**: 356

**Estimated Age**: Mid-14th century

**Entry**:
> The twists in the wood cleave the bones from her skin, tied to and strung tight to witness the crowds of the wicked and fallible. The people gather to see…her, all howling in unison, in joyous praise as the blazing fire upon the quiet pyre rages and roars. Their black faces, amorphous and sickly like the depths of hell, speak as if they should be placed above the holy Father and us, his brethren. They seek to harm those who wish to help them, to burn them alive until their essence permeates throughout the rotting fields of bloated corpses and ill-worshipers. 
> 
> She wished to save them from themselves, from the dark plague that shall swallow them whole. It is why we have not reached from the shadows, and I had warned of this. Yet still, I mourn for her, and now I shall be furious for her. These holy men, the poor beggars, the unclean sinners, they will suffer like her, the scores of wretched humanity. For it is their souls that have cast doubt upon their savior, just like they did from our ancestor of Nazareth, and thus shall they be doomed to a far worse fate than even he. There is no hope for the children of God. I resign myself and my kin from this world, and the people of this world shall burn when the time has come.

**Result**: Upon completion, fire suddenly fills the room, incinerating all surfaces of the testing chamber and any internal electronic equipment. The fire spread before personnel were able to put it out with provided safety equipment. CSD-00293 is found unharmed and testing protocols are updated.

- **Test Log 1-B conducted by Researcher Oberon on 3/█/████**

**Foreword**: CSD-00293 is instructed via a loudspeaker to acquire RPC-229 from the first drawer and place it atop RPC-229-1, then read from page 420.

**Title**: Quiet Ice

**Page**: 420

**Estimated Age**: Late 18th Century

**Entry**:
> They have scorned me, thrust me from their household, dropped me into the abyss of sadness where I am only allowed to plead with the Devil himself. His hands are cold, but he understands what it is like to be cast out from a place he once belonged. I cannot be satisfied, and they will not allow me to be, as those I had once called my family have forsaken my future for the sake of staying hidden, as to not allow us to be revealed to those who are “unworthy” and “tainted.” 
>
> They will not allow me to have a future of my own and it is the fault of this book. It has forced us, with its corruption and heavy-handed memories, to lurk in the shadows until we are all that is left. They have been corrupted by its guiding hand and allowed to think that humans are so beneath them that tradition should be allowed to trample their graves. I do not care what happened to our ancestors, I only care about the future of this world, and my family shall not stand in my way. 
> 
> They can change, and we should let them become those who are worthier than even us. I only hope that my sadness shall bear fruit for their future, for our magics should ascend us to be their shepherds, but instead, it has deemed us to be their reapers. 

**Result**: Room temperature within the testing chamber suddenly dropped to below -18 degrees celsius. Immediate frost buildup upon the two-way window did not allow for any other effects to be recorded by supervising researchers.

- **Test Log 1-C conducted by Researcher Oberon on 14/█/████**

**Foreword**: CSD-00293 is instructed via a loudspeaker to acquire RPC-229 from the first drawer and place it atop RPC-229-1, then read from page 423.

**Title**: Bright Hopes

**Page**: 423

**Estimated Age**: 1918-1919

**Entry**:
> ████ is a beautiful town and the people here are just as splendid. The Great War seems like a dream away now, and those returning from the fight have settled back into their homes and with their families. I have welcomed them like the rest, but it has begun to make me worry for those I have left behind. To think if they are still out there only begins to make me wonder if they have returned to hiding, or are still searching for me and their precious holy book. Without their guide, they are lost, and I would assume they will stop at nothing of taking back what I have taken from them. Even if they are my family, I shall not let them harm others. For now, I return to the clinic tomorrow to help treat the wounded, and even those who are the worst off shall make it through. I will make sure of it.

**Result**: No immediate effect was recorded and the test was promptly ended. Medical post-analysis conducted by on site nurses showed that any scars/medical deformities were no longer present on CSD-00293's body. Further testing is required. [[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="+Interview 1" hide="-Interview 1"]]

Foreword: CSD-00293 is brought to an interrogation cell to meet with Researcher Oberon. He takes a seat and is strung using handcuffs to the middle of the table. He has deep bags under his eyes and seems to have been suffering from severe insomnia.

> <Begin Log>
> 
> Oberon: Welcome. I trust you’ve been sleeping well since our last test?
>
> 00293: Er, yeah. Kind of...not really.
> 
> Oberon: Uh-huh. Have you been experiencing any side effects or abnormalities since our last meeting?
> 
> [00293 is silent for a moment, fiddling with his hands.]
> 
> 00293: I mean, I guess…I’ve been getting these strange dreams recently ever since I read from, whaddya' call it, that book of yours? It's like... these dreams make me feel like a stranger, like they aren't mine. Whoever they were made for, they  definitely weren't made for any of us. We're all like these outsiders looking in.
> 
> [Oberon flips through her folder and writes something down.]
> 
> Oberon: I see. So you’ve been having nightmares I take it?
> 
> 00293: No, not nightmares. Hell, they feel like nightmares, but… they aren't menacing or scary. If anything, they feel like memories, like someone attached their brain to mine and shoved all their thoughts into me. I don’t remember much after I dream them, but we all feel that way, it ain't nothing special. However, these don't go away naturally, but rather...they were taken from me.
> 
> Oberon: Do you recall anything in these... memories? Names, places, times?
> 
> 00293: Not...really? All I remember are the voices... 
> 
> Oberon: The voices?
> 
> 00293: Yeah. A whole bunch of booming voices, asking me if I was a relative or a part of their ‘family' as if I was meeting some damn cult. 'Course, I had no bloody idea what the hell they were talking about, but they were incredibly aggressive, and they just got more pissed the more I told them no.
> 
> [00293 places his head into his hands to break eye contact]
> 
> Oberon: I have a few more questions to ask-
> 
> 00293: They said they were coming.
> 
> Oberon: Who is coming?
> 
> 00293: The voices! Whoever the hell they belong to! I alerted them by not **being** one of them. When I told them no that final time, they ripped a chunk of me away like I wasn't even a person. They saw into me, they knew who I was, and I know I didn't tell them a damn thing! They knew my name, why I'm here, my whole fuckin' life story! Everything I know and once knew all because I read from that bloody book! Th-they want it back, and... I don’t know what’s gonna happen to me.
> 
> Oberon: I see. We're done here.
> 
> <End Log>

[Afterword: CSD-00293 is to be placed under restrictive custody and shall be moved off-site pending termination.]
