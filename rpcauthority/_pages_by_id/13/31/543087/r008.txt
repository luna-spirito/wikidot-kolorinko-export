------

**Containment Protocols:** Given that RPC-617 has no major or anomalous impact on the real world, nor within a digital medium, and the recent "agreement" prevents its interference of civilian property and risking detection, there are no containment protocols provided or needed, pardon the maintenance of the device provided to RPC-617 and its private internet connection.

**Description:** RPC-617 is a sapient, incorporeal entity that is capable of accessing the Internet through any computer, provided that the device is capable of maintaining an Internet connection and --has a copy of //Garry's Mod//--[[footnote]]A "sandbox" video game developed on the Source engine by Facepunch Studios and published by Valve in 2006.[[/footnote]] --downloaded onto its hard drive--.

RPC-617 has no detectable presence within the device itself. There are no known methods of blocking RPC-617's access to any device pardon disconnecting that device from the Internet--, deleting //Garry's Mod//,-- or destroying the device in question. RPC-617 is effectively invulnerable, provided that the Internet both exists and that there is a computer that can access it along with a copy of //Garry's Mod//.

RPC-617's range also extends only as far as an Internet connection can be provided. RPC-617 was able to access Authority devices on Earth's moon, while it was unable to access [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/aedfmanifest OUTFLT] vessels of the AEDF.

RPC-617 had created its own //Steam// account in order to access //Garry's Mod//, but it has agreed to be provided with an Authority-created account[[footnote]]Dubbed "wanderer01", provided by on-site staff during creation.[[/footnote]] for both monitoring purposes and due to the fact that the original account was made using the inactive email address of former US President █████ ██████.

Other than its source of access, RPC-617 is typically unobtrusive (only communicating via text) once it has accessed a server. The entity usually attempts to simply "observe" the population, and while it may occasionally participate in certain activities available, RPC-617 is otherwise indistinguishable from a non-anomalous user of //Garry's Mod//.

**Addenda:**

[[collapsible show="[+] Discovery" hide="[-] Close"]]

[[div class="blockquote"]]

RPC-617 was first discovered in 2017 when a member of [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/romeo-7-suited-gentlemen Romeo-7] had reported that their personal computer had been accessed several times while they were away on mission, and that security records showed the computer activating "by itself" and starting //Garry's Mod// between 02:30 and 05:00 hours. After analysis by Maintenance Union staff, it was determined that the access was anomalous in nature. Research personnel were eventually contacted in order to determine the nature of the detected anomalous entity.

After examination revealed that the entity was utilizing the computer purely to access //Garry's Mod// and to join a variety of public servers, Dr. Carpenter (the responding Researcher) suggested that an interview with the entity be conducted, due to concerns that attempts to "remove" the anomaly may result in being unable to contact it again and/or inciting hostility.

The agent who discovered the anomaly (Dubbed "Weaver" as their name was redacted by request) agreed to assist in interviewing the entity in exchange for reduced disciplinary action due to possessing unapproved software on Authority property.

After locating the server RPC-617 was currently accessing, Agent Weaver began communication with RPC-617. After several attempts via voice chat, Weaver instead opted for utilizing text, to which RPC-617 immediately responded. Due to the public nature of the server, Agent Weaver suggested that they join a private game in order to properly conduct the interview and avoid disruption. RPC-617 agreed and a private server was created and joined by both parties.

After extensive questioning, it was determined that there was no feasible way to prevent RPC-617 from freely accessing public //Garry's Mod// servers nor hinder it in any capacity. It was agreed that the most effective way to reduce the entity's interference with the public was to provide a computer for RPC-617 to utilize without disrupting any other devices. After RPC-617 agreed to permanently reside in the provided device, Agent "Weaver" freely volunteered their computer for this purpose[[footnote]]In exchange for reduced disciplinary action.[[/footnote]] and RPC-617 continues to utilize it as of {{11/08/19}}.
