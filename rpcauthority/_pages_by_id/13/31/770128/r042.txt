**Description:** RPC-159 refers to a physiological phenomenon primarily affecting the human brain upon exposure to RPC-159-A. Presentation is in keeping with a mild neurodegenerative disorder bearing remarkable pathophysiological similarities to transmissible spongiform encelopathies (TSEs), as it is characterized by the progressive destruction of neuronal tissue throughout the brain. The nominal characteristic of TSEs, however, is absent; rather than the resultant manifestation of multiple voids across the cortex, RPC-159-A occupies these voids, progressing locally and steadily from the area of initial infection. 

Although the agent responsible for the neurodegeneration that occurs during RPC-159 infection is unidentified, it is strongly suspected to be a subspecies of //Spiroplasma// and is formally referred to as the //catalytic agent.// 

It is unique among bacterial diseases in that it produces two unique substances, formally named //pseudocurd// and //starter fluid//, as neurodegeneration progresses.

Both RPC-159-A and its catalytic agent are generally found and ingested together, with only exceptionally rare cases of infection including either one in absence of the other. Further details regarding RPC-159 spread and mechanisms of infection will be elaborated upon in the attached addenda.

**Pseudocurd** are the coagulated remains of cerebral tissue left by RPC-159 infection. Due to the speed at which the disease progresses, only minimal quantities of the substance exist at one time, complicating detailed analysis. It is similar in appearance to the curd obtained by the coagulation of milk, if substantially more complex in composition and produced from cerebral tissue. 

**Starter fluid** is a substance similar to cerebrospinal fluid, with the significant discrepancy of possessing large amounts of oxygen and certain nutrients not found in its normal counterpart. Throughout the progression of RPC-159-A, it will replace the entirety of cerebrospinal fluid normally found in the human skull.

RPC-159-A progresses remarkably quickly in the human brain, requiring 2 weeks to 1 month to fully spread. However, due to the properties of the catalytic agent, it rarely provokes the debilitating and/or life-threatening symptoms associated with TSEs. Certain neuronal pathways connecting the nervous system to the rest of the brain remain universally immune to RPC-159-A and are not displaced or deteriorated in any way during infection, as do most hormone-producing bodies.

**RPC-159-A** is an anomalous fungus of the //Penicillium// genus that is capable of rapid growth in the pseudocurd produced by the catalytic agent. Although highly similar to //Penicillium roqueforti//, it possesses two unique characteristics: 

* The capability to interface and successfully communicate with the neuronal pathways preserved by its catalytic agent, thus maintaining neuroelectric communication with the rest of the brain.
* The capability to substitute cerebral functions via a gestalt biosystem composed of a series of physiologically-disparate enzyme-based biotic analogues, in place of the biosystem that composes the standard neuroelectrically-based human brain.

While not fully understood, current analyses suggest that the exact means by which these characteristics are achieved are not entirely anomalous. 

The end result of RPC-159-A progression throughout pseudocurd is the replacement of the human brain by an analogous organ entirely composed of a substance identical in texture, smell, and taste to Rochefort cheese, with the major exception of greater humidity.

To the contrary, it is possible RPC-159 infection may partially restore cerebral capabilities lost due to disease, trauma or aging. One remarkable example of RPC-159-A's subcomponent analogues is its method of neural communication. Whereas the human brain achieves neural networking via the transmission of electric signals through a series of connected dendrite-axon cells, RPC-159-A accomplishes the same feat through emission of pheromones at particular concentrations orchestrated by high-density lattice of "networking" mold cells. 

This emission of pheromones is not unlike the pheromone systems employed by non-anomalous mold fungi used to mark the directions in which high-valence stimuli is likely to exist in accordant concentrations. However, RPC-159-A's pheromone system is instead used to designate, enforce, and modify networks as well as relay information across the biosystem such that it can develop an understanding of complex relationships between abstract concepts. 

Similarly to the typical neuroelectric process, evidence of the "strengthening" of understanding (i.e. learning, practicing, etc.) can be observed as a result of increases in particular pheromone network chains as those network cells are excited to produce pheromone responses repeatedly at progressively higher concentrations.

While these two systems accomplish the same task and for the most part share the same properties, pheromone transmitters produced by RPC-159-A have a theoretically-infinite set of variations and specialization when compared to conventional neurotransmitters. This is expressed as a dramatically heightened capacity to effectively distinguish relationships and concepts. The details of this process suggest that networking cells have individual "microbrains" of their own that are necessary to calculate the appropriate pheromone response depending on the disparate variants of stimuli.

These distinctions, while noticeable upon close investigation of affected subjects, are rarely apparent to an outside observer.

**Epidemiology:** 

RPC-159 is generally contracted by the consumption of infected cheese, which can be primarily found in North America and western Europe. Infected cheese is exceptionally rare, with public contraction occurring at a rate of less than a dozen cases per year at average. Pieces of infected cheese have rarely been recovered, but are generally subtypes of blue cheese or feature significant mold growth. Many confiscated samples are humid and irregular in shape when compared to commercial cheese.

Areas of statistical abundance correspond with areas of above-average commercial cheese production, primarily in the United States, Germany and France. Despite this correlation, no significant connection has been discovered between commercial producers and RPC-159 in spite of thorough inspection of supplies, production and transport systems between 1998 and 2005.

As the life-cycle of RPC-159 is not known, the causes behind the aforementioned statistical abundances are unclear: RPC-159 victims do not excrete, perspire, or exhale any abnormal material associated with the disease. It is thus strongly suspected that RPC-159 is of artificial design and artificial introduction into the population. This is substantiated by the comparative abundance of RPC-159 in certain circles associated with anomalous activity and the disparate complexity of its mechanisms.

**Description:** On this date it was discovered that in specific cases, instances of RPC-159 do not resemble that of Rochefort cheese. Rather, it was found that instances can resemble a variety of cheeses, to a substantive effect on behaviors:
* **Cases resembling Philadelphia cheese:** Subjects suffering head trauma will very likely report remarkably higher propensities of brain damage as compared to typical human subjects. This is as a result of the pliable nature of this cheese. The brain of the subject becomes severely deformed; large depressions of brain matter at the location of impact is observed. Compression between brain matter and skull is observed on the opposite end of the location of impact. Subjects suffering from this variant are to be handled carefully; application of force should be seen as a very last resort to facilitate compliance with personnel. 
* **Cases resembling Feta cheese:** Subjects are observed to be more detached from stimuli that would otherwise elicit emotional responses in typical human subjects. However, subjects also appear to be quick to anger, and are more likely to engage in antisocial aggression.
* **Cases resembling Camembert cheese:** Subjects acutely suffer brain damage, seizures, and subsequent death upon exposure to environments in excess of 28°C. Autopsies discover that the RPC-159 brain completely melts down beyond function. During expiry, subjects are observed to exhale brain matter particulate, positing a smell of rich ammonia consistent of melted brie cheese into the surrounding environment. This particulate is capable of infecting other subjects when inhaled; infected subjects will also manifest the Camembert variant.
* **Cases resembling Pule Donkey cheese:** Post-exposure, subjects are invariably diagnosed with psychopathy.
* **Subjects suffering from schizophrenia before RPC-159 exposure:** RPC-159 brain is composed of a composite of multiple different cultures resembling a combination of different cheeses.

**Date of Discovery:** 11/12/2021
**Consistency:** Isolated incident
**Description:** On this date, the Authority discovered that a Buddhist monk, living in a crude remote domicile in the Himalayas, was affected with a specific variant of RPC-159 that has not been observed before: The subject's brain is entirely identical in composition to mozzarella cheese. Furthermore, this cheese is very specifically identical to the Milkaut brand of commercial cheese. It is to be noted that, excluding this circumstance, no single case of RPC-159 composition resembles that of any known brand. 

Upon interviewing the subject, he claims to have not made contact with any human life for over 30 years, with the only exception of individuals who deposit food donations at a lower portion of the mountain on which he lived. Even then, the monk claims that he would "wait until the people are gone" to collect the donations. 

Observation of subject's brain demonstrates that initial introduction of RPC-159-A occurred during this period of isolation, specifically into the 17th year. Subject claims to have never once ingested a piece of any cheese, deeming it a "substance of decadent desire," and remains unconvinced of the existence of RPC-159 within him.

How RPC-159 manifested in an extremely remote incident, and the exact nature of how this incident manifested in a commercial brand of cheese is, at this time, unknown.

As a result of MST Foxtrot-4's findings, numerous investigations and subsequent sting operations relating to RPC-159 were deployed in the following months. Ensuing discoveries generally share the characteristics of CASEFILE 159#000121989-2: Upper-class clubs or groupings of limited size and knowledge of the anomalous harvesting of RPC-159 and/or testing its effects on human capabilities. The summation of these investigations has been retroactively named Operation ARCHFIEND for the sake of archival and future organization.

236kg of RPC-159 were recovered from Operation ARCHFIEND, 151 of which belong to a singular, massive cerebral structure emerging from a surgical aperture in the skull of Philip Wozniak, age 24. It is currently understood that he was inducted for undeclared employment under a minor Austrian GoI associated with RPC-159, who exploited his migrant background to coerce him to participate in experimentation with the anomaly.

It has been ascertained with a likelihood of 81% that the cerebral structure is primarily composed of material extracted from an unknown number of other, unidentified individuals and forcibly grafted onto Mr. Wozniak's own infected brain. The minimum estimated number is in excess of 47 persons, matching recent missing persons records from the city of Vienna.

At the time of finding, it was stored in a wooden wine barrel in the basement of █████ ███████, which Mr. Wozniak's body was propped against. No particular accommodations had been made to preserve the structure. as a result, it has suffered massive damage from drying, transportation, and malnutrition. Wozniak himself remains conscious and capable of speech and motion, but remains motionless most of the time.

Whenever Mr. Wozniak is spoken to, or attempts to move or speak, his body suffers from minor convulsions and fleeting moments of palsy throughout every extremity. This state is accompanied by uninterrupted, incoherent speech that reflects a rapidly-changing thought process.

It is believed that, when Mr. Wozniak attempts to vocalize, every neural center in the cerebral mass is doing so simultaneously or sequentially, thus returning numerous different responses at the same time. Psychological examinations highly suggest that the subsequent vocalization of Mr. Wozniak, when attempting to communicate, is an aggregate summation of the neuronal relationships belonging to their former component individuals.

This is supported by physical analysis of the structure, which presents nodular organization around "central" structures notoriously similar to a non-anomalous human brain. It is thus believed that the above described behavioral aberration corresponds to a multitude of persons, or a fusion thereof, attempting to speak simultaneously via Mr. Wozniak's body. Whether they are aware of their situation, or can be reasonably defined as unique individuals rather than disparate thought processes, is unknown.

Interrogations of Mr. Wozniak are currently ongoing in an effort to continue Operation ARCHFIEND. He is scheduled for indefinite and induced coma, or a suitably humane alternative, on 01/01/2022. Authorization by Office of Ethics and Review pending.
