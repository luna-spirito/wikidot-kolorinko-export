**Registered Phenomena Code:** 612

**Object Class:** Gamma-Purple

**Hazard Types:** Memory Alteration, Temporal, Teleportation, Transmutation

**Containment Protocols:** All non-Authority reports of RPC-612 instances and related information/media must be taken down and redacted from public records following standard operating procedures with assistance from MST X-Ray-6. Documentation regarding RPC-612 in any capacity must be classified under Level-3 authorization.

RPC-612 instances must be immediately neutralized and deconstructed. Upon detection via web crawlers, embedded agents, and civilian reports, MST Romeo-7 and Victor-1 are assigned to facilitate and ensure the deconstruction of RPC-612 instances and the removal of all related media. 

All personnel involved in the instances' destruction, or that have otherwise witnessed the construct, must be amnesticized of any related memories once neutralization is complete. While assigned personnel are to be aware of the nature and containment protocol for RPC-612,[[footnote]]Including the amnesticization of personnel.[[/footnote]] the effects and appearance of the manifestation must be erased from any record or memory outside of Addendum 1 and those permitted access.

Addendum 1 cannot be made available to more than one individual at a time.[[footnote]]Currently, only the selected Head Researcher of RPC-612, Dr. Agnes Welkard.[[/footnote]] Under no circumstances may Addendum 1 be distributed to unauthorized personnel or by any individual other than the current Head Researcher of RPC-612.

**Description:** RPC-612 is the classification of various monuments, statues, memorials, cenotaphs, and other commemorative constructs that can manifest in any location --on Earth--, depicting an ahistorical event. The events depicted do not seem to have any recognizable pattern, although most typically involve natural disaster, tragedy, battle, death, or disease related to the history of the region within the last 500 years, with some exceptions ranging from events in the future or before recorded history.[[footnote]]//While all manifestations are to be deconstructed with haste, events that affect earlier history are given higher priority.// - Dr. Agnes Welkard[[/footnote]]

An RPC-612 manifestation occurs typically once within a span of six months, producing a slowly expanding area of effect that manipulates reality to adhere to the event that the structure memorializes. As the majority of RPC-612 modifications adhere to scenarios that negatively impact human history, the affected area is often rendered uninhabitable or hostile.

Once inside the field, the border between the effected area and baseline reality is no longer visible. From the interior, the alterations appear to be worldwide, even as the effect is still only as large as its radius and can be exited at the same distance one enters.[[footnote]]Albeit accounting for where the field expanded during an expedition.[[/footnote]]

Generally, an individual can safely enter and exit the border of RPC-612 with no ill effects, but remaining within the field for longer than 2-4 hours can result in moderate to severe modification of the individual's memory and possible "integration" into RPC-612's modified reality, and they cannot be recovered until RPC-612 is destroyed.

The field has a consistent rate of expansion relative to its size, yet its impact can be exacerbated by any act that would commemorate the event depicted in the RPC-612 instance. This includes observing the construct, reading information related to the event, commemorative offerings such as flowers, the celebration of holidays related to RPC-612, and similar actions. Likewise, the growth can be slowed, stopped, and in some cases reversed by the opposite. Destruction of any media related to RPC-612, actively preventing the spread of information related to the event, and the demolition of the original RPC-612 instance can result in the minimization and neutralization of RPC-612's effects.

Once the effects have been neutralized, any temporal effects caused by RPC-612 are undone and the affected area returns to normality as if RPC-612 had never manifested. RPC-612 and any phenomena related to it will cease to exist. However, any external media not produced by RPC-612, such as any Authority documentation or memory related to the anomaly will persist, and possibly risk propagation of RPC-612's effects should another manifestation of the same ahistorical event appear.
