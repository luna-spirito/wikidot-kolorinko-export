++ DIVISIONAL MANAGEMENT CHANGES REGARDING RPC-416 AND ALTR-AC18
[[/=]]
-----
As of January 22nd, 2021, and in light of [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/budget-cuts recent events], the Presidium’s [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/office-of-financial-affairs Office of Financial Affairs] (OFA) has decided: material, scientific, and security spending jurisdictions, alongside current diplomatic and economic affairs regarding RPC-416, -1, and -1A, including all traded goods, will be transferred from their respective operational divisions to the Office of Financial Affairs (or other respective Presidium sub-departments) as a result of subpar financial handling by Division officers worldwide over the past months. The economic relevance of RPC-416 was deemed too crucial to allow mismanagement. Changes made at our discretion will be highlighted in orange.

For information or requests concerning RPC-416, contact Head of Finances Hector Magnus at hectormagnusofa@authority.rpc

- ###f05b07|The Presidium, Authority Administration##

-----

**Abstract:** RPC-416 is an extra-dimensional rift located in Whitestone, Russia. Different types of entities have been shown to exit this rift, as well as allowing entrance into its reality of origin. Interaction and commerce with entities exiting RPC-416 is only permitted to --selected personnel within the Research Division.--  ###f05b07|personnel authorized by the OFA.##

-----

**Safe Handling and Usage:** RPC-416 is currently self-contained within Whitestone, Russia, where OL-Site-416 has been constructed for the purpose of diplomatic improvement with RPC-416-1 and the exploration and archival of information regarding ALTR-AC18, which are under the jurisdiction of the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/the-bureau-of-acquisition Department for Cultural Affairs], the Department of Higher-Dimensional Studies, ###f05b07| and the Office of Financial Affairs.## Furthermore, small teams from the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/the-office-of-analysis-and-science History and Biology Departments of the OAS] as well as [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/protocol-laboratory Protocol Laboratory] have been permanently stationed at OL-Site-416 with the objective of studying and cataloguing all objects and data acquired from RPC-416-1 or any other manifesting entity. ##f05b07|A provisional office for the Anomalous Commerce department has been set up in OL-Site-416 as well for the study of traded goods with RPC-416-1.##

Containment Division’s [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/engineering-component Engineering Component] has been tasked with the construction and maintenance of the current Apatite mining operation in Whitestone with the monetary and manned support of RPC-416-1A as per the “Financial and Cooperation Act of Whitestone'', signed by RPC-416-1A and the Office Of Financial Affairs with the Office of Diplomatic Relations acting as an intermediary. This document outlines the exchange of Canadian Dollars[[footnote]]The only currency available in both baseline reality and ALTR-AC18.[[/footnote]] and anomalous technology from ALTR-AC18 in return for the continuous functioning of Whitestone’s mining operation.

###f05b07|Additionally, a two-way railway system going through ALTR-AC18, RPC-416, and Site-115[[footnote]]Closest Authority site to Whitestone[[/footnote]] has been constructed by the Engineering Component for the ease of material transport of goods and assets between the two parties, serving too as a transport method for RPC-416-1 instances.##

##f05b07|RPC-416-1A, alongside his chosen associates, have been granted permission by the Authority to work within the Office of Financial Affairs as economic consultants, salesmen, auditors, and trade workers. This permission does not possess a defined deadline, and shall be kept in place until such a time the Authority no longer suffers from the economic burdens caused by the recent loss of government funding.##

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-416/416-1.png|caption= Lyosha Rebrokost, an RPC-416-1 instance and FSN citizen.]]
**Description:** RPC-416 is a spatial rift located within the abandoned mining town of Whitestone, Sakha, Russia. RPC-416 leads to ALTR-AC18, an alternate reality in which the course of human history has been severely altered by the common knowledge and widespread availability of anomalies. The device and methods utilized to open and stabilize the rift are poorly understood due to RPC-416-1A's refusal to allow Authority personnel near it. The main property of RPC-416 is its function as a two-way passage for entities within ALTR-AC18, specifically those from the “Free Svith Necropolis”, a nation known to be ALTR-AC18’s analog of Soviet Russia, in which a libertarian revolution led against the then-reigning monarchy resulted in the creation of a highly capitalistic and industrial society. ##f05b07|As a result of this, many of its citizens are well versed within various fields of economics and finance, or are otherwise very skilled laborers.##

RPC-416-1 is the collective designation for the main inhabitants of the FSN, a species of humanoid entities resembling human skeletons, or decomposing bodies, in the appearance and biology of various ethnicities.[[footnote]]It should be noted that RPC-416-1 instances retain all human capabilities found in living organisms, even when the organs necessary for such are missing (e.g sight, digestion, speech).[[/footnote]] All RPC-416-1 instances appear to be comprised of naturally formed bones and muscle tissue ##f05b07|or commercially manufactured alternatives, referred to as "Luchshekost",[[footnote]] Roughly translating to “Better Bone”.[[/footnote]] an alloy comprised 80% of Apatite,[[footnote]]A very scarce element within ALTR-AC18.[[/footnote]] with the remaining 20% being made up of diverse metalloids such as silicon and carbon compounds, which are used in the creation and sustenance of RPC-416-1 instances by the use of both chemical and necromantic rituals.[[footnote]]All widely available to RPC-416 instances within their home reality.[[/footnote]]##

It is believed that RPC-416-1 instances are able to exist due to a permanent anomaly affecting the FSN region that causes sentient/sapient organic life to be unable to die of natural causes, instead gradually losing biological functions as the body decays and eventually leaving only the skeletal structure.[[footnote]]This, however, has not been shown to cause a decrease of strength or mobility in RPC-416-1 instances.[[/footnote]] Bones only decay a few years after the total loss of all other organic matter. This has led citizens of the nation to be, on average, more than 200 years old, with immigration and manual RPC-416-1 manufacturing as their only means of population growth.

RPC-416-1A designates the currently elected president[[footnote]]The FSN works on a system that is comparable to a liberal democracy in baseline reality.[[/footnote]] of the FSN, Vladimir Ilyich Znatkost: a prosperous textile company owner and economist, being the one who initially led the revolts against their previous government following the public execution of his sister at the hands of “Great Necromancer Nicholas the III'' for indulging in outlawed necromantic rituals.[[footnote]]RPC-416-1A can be easily distinguished from other instances by the possession of an artificial moustache and gold-plated jaw.[[/footnote]] RPC-416-1A is responsible for all actions carried out by the nation as a whole, which are almost unanimously followed by all RPC-416-1 instances, often citing a common saying: “Если не знаешь, Знаткост знает.”[[footnote]] Roughly translating to “If you don't know, Znatkost knows”[[/footnote]] As of the time of writing, RPC-416-1A has shown to be highly cooperative with Authority personnel.

Additionally, the FSN is in possession of an unknown number of anomalous objects native to ALTR-AC18, those which it has agreed to trade with the Authority in return for the benefits previously mentioned in the **Safe Handling and Usage** section of this document. A partial list of these objects can be found below;

Details: While RPC-416-2 cannot be publicly distributed by our front companies, its use inside the Authority has shown to be of great advantage for diverse reasons:

* RPC-416-2, by use of electrical currents, allows for the near-instantaneous transmission of information across great distances. Several terminals across the Authority, including those aboard AEDF vessels, have been outfitted with prototype versions utilizing RPC-416-2 in hopes of achieving wide-spread utilization.

* RPC-416-2 instances are 10 times cheaper than normal circuitry previously used by the Containment Department of Communications, and are currently being tested within the department as a replacement for current devices.

* RPC-416-2 also allows for the rudimentary communication of personnel across spaces of compromised coherency, pocket dimensions, or alternate realities. While not as efficient as current methods, it is immensely affordable, and safe from anomalies that may interfere with electrical devices.

Research AETs are currently looking into further applications of RPC-416-2 into the Authority, as well as within currently ongoing projects such as Delta-X.[[footnote]] A project by the Engineering Component for the construction of a functioning quantum computer.[[/footnote]]

[[tab RPC-416-3]]
[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-416/416-3.jpg|caption=Several minecarts containing RPC-416-3 being unloaded from a train on their way to Site-115.|width=100%|align=center|enlarge=false]]

RPC-416-3 is a mineral native to ALTR-AC18, most commonly found within the FSN's Siberian region, and dubbed “Molneugol”[[footnote]] From the Russian //molniya// (молния) meaning “lightning bolt” and //ugol'// (уголь) meaning “coal”.[[/footnote]] or “Lightning Coal” by mining and distributing companies. RPC-416-3 is unable to produce thermal energy like its non-anomalous counterpart, but instead generates high voltage currents and provokes electric discharges onto its surroundings when sufficiently heated. This process progressively converts solid material into energy until all of RPC-416-3 has been consumed. This material serves as the main source of energy for the FSN, as its energy output far outpaces that of normal electricity generators (although not those with sufficient output like nuclear plants). One of the main drawbacks of this material, however, is its ionization of the surrounding air, as well as the release of ozone as a waste material into the atmosphere, both of which are dangerous to us but neglectable by RPC-416-1 instances due to their lack of organs.

+++++ **Economic Value:** Medium - Limited

Details: Despite the health hazards posed by the use of RPC-416-3, they can still prove vital  in different scenarios common for the Authority, as well as being the bases of new research into easily accessible sources of energy at a low cost;

* The construction and maintenance of power plants utilizing RPC-416-3 as its main source of fuel is not only easier and safer, but also more convenient for regions with intensive maintenance necessities such as AFRICOM, which are known to heavily syphon monthly allowances. Use of RPC-416-3 requires only a simple understanding of its properties and minimal safety equipment (e.g gas masks) without requiring specialized personnel for its operation.

* 416-3 has been identified by a Component study to have potential as portable or backup batteries. The study concluded that a battery composed of 416-3 would be comparably less complex, and therefore would have a significantly lower cost to manufacture, when compared to contemporary baseline batteries. However, this would need to be weighed against their rather poor efficiency when compared to the same contemporary batteries. 1/10th of the cost for 4/10th's of the efficiency was their conclusion.

There are also several Research projects currently in the works that seek to replace internal batteries of High Frequency Tesla Coils (HFTC) used by the [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/protectorate Protectorate’s] Neutralization Team (NT-100). This project seeks to reduce the high cost required for their construction and upkeep while maintaining equal or greater levels of efficiency during operations.

[[tab RPC-416-4]]
[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/rpc-416/416-4.jpg|caption=A house made with 15 seeds of RPC-416-4 located in Eidyn, Scotland, The Pact.[[footnote]] Image provided by RPC-416-1A.[[/footnote]]|width=100%|align=center|enlarge=false]]

RPC-416-4 is a species of Sequoia called “Sequoiadendron Cambiantis”, a type of tree native to Scotland, The Pact.[[footnote]] According to history books provided by RPC-416-1A, “The Great Pact of Hereward” or just “The Pact” is a socioeconomic and political alliance between Ireland, Scotland, and Britannia created in the 1600’s during a joint war with the French that continued following the end of the conflict. The simplest comparison would be a smaller version of the European Union.[[/footnote]] The main quality of this species of tree is the ability for its growth to be manipulated and railroaded by the use of electricity, a technique that was commonly utilized during thunderstorms by ancient tribes within the region for religious purposes, but that has now been studied enough for the quick manufacturing of living residences across the country. The growth time for these trees is usually between 2 to 5 days for projects of normal size such as houses. Due to its reproductive cycles, constructs produced using RPC-416-4 last an average of 2 weeks before they begin to crumble, releasing high quantities of seeds in unpredictable patterns and becoming increasingly difficult to manage. Controlled fires are currently accepted to be the easiest way to suppress the growth and expansion of RPC-416-4.

**Additional Information:**

Following the release and subsequent deletion of the video by ACI personnel, surveillance drones began scouting the area in search of the instances. After 2 days, they were able to locate the place on which the instances appeared to originate from: an abandoned mining town named “Whitestone”. The town appeared to have been partially reconstructed by the instances, all of which emanated from a spatial rift in the town’s center. To assess the severity of the situation, an envoy from the Presidium, Valentino Romano,[[footnote]] Whom had been staying in a nearby town for unrelated businesses.[[/footnote]] was sent to attempt to interview the leading instance, and if possible, come to an agreement for containment.

**RPC-416-1A:** Excellent! Now allow me to accompany you back to your carriage.

++ __**Chapter 3: Death and Afterlife of Vladimir Znatkost__**

Deceased to a factory accident in the 22nd of April 1870 with his sister, Jekaterina Lebedeva, following a moderately wealthy life within Krasnoyarsk he was subject to the recently discovered arts of necromancy by Tsar Nicholas the III, later named “Great Necromancer Nicholas the III”, alongside the rest of his town and country in the years to follow. While life following death was not simple, especially due to the Tsar’s post-mortem wealth tax, this did not stop the newly renamed Vladimir Znatkost from continuing his economic ventures across Siberia, more now than ever given the certain benefits that semi-immortality provided both him and his workers.

“Znatkost Tethers” quickly became the first fully private business within the nation to the disdain of Nicholas the III, as it also advocated the freedom for trade and life that in Znatkost’s words, was the very soul of each skeleton. His ideology, however, was not fully manifested until his sister’s life was expelled from her body by lich enforcers following the discovery that she possessed outlawed books and texts pertaining to the sustainability of the soul following material death. This action led to the radicalization of Znatkost, who proceeded to move all his economic assets deep within the Siberian region to avoid state disturbance. It was during the following decade that he bought several of books from both The Pact and the Tuluwhai Empire to gather all possible knowledge of necromancy before taking decisive action against Nicholas, even ignoring events such as the 4-Point-War[[footnote]] A war against Germany by The Pact, The Napoleon Confederation, Byzantium, and The Tsarist Necropolis as noted in other volumes, which later turned into mixed conflict until the signage of “The Treaty of Roses”, with no side obtaining the clear victory.[[/footnote]] in Europe and the Heavenly War of Ascension[[footnote]] Unknown conflict amongst Chinese dynasties.[[/footnote]] not far off east. It was also during this time that he discovered the recipe for Luchshekost, which allowed him to quickly amass troops from single skulls or ribs.

It was in 1917 following the end of the 4-Point-War that the rebellion against Nicholas the III began. Most citizens believed at the start of the conflict for the altercation to end shortly thereafter, as all dissidents from the nation simply had their souls taken away by the liches, but unbeknownst to many at the time, Znatkost was able to develop a ritual to unbind the souls of a skeleton from the control of the necromancers, allowing them to fight without the imminent threat of death. Eight years did the war last, and while casualties were intense on both sides, with even Znatkost himself nearly dying again, the revolutionary troops eventually outnumbered the Tsarist forces by a factor of 5:1, a difference which only grew in later years of the war as supplies of inanimate bones did. And in September the 8th, 1925, the troops arrived at the Tsar's castle to find a nearly dead and agonizing Nicholas, using the last of his power to protect himself and his family from Znatkost's men; a futile effort, as he and his lineage were sentenced to hanging and their souls destroyed to never rise again, giving birth to a new and free Svith Necropolis.
