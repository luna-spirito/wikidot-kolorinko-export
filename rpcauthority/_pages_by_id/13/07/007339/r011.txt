+++ __Containment Protocols:__

[[/=]]
----

RPC-099-A's area of effect has, as of 2/21/2021, been measured to extend throughout the entire Lunar mantle and across the Lunar surface to a height of approximately 500 metres. The prospects of further expansion of the anomaly following Incident RPC-099-14 have been deemed unlikely, as RPC-099-1 appears to be unable to penetrate further into the lunar exosphere.

[[/footnote]] concentrations are believed to denote areas heavily contaminated by RPC-099-1. |width=310px|align=right]]

Members of [[[Site-019]]] and [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/site-099-decommissioned Site-099]'s staff are to be screened bi-weekly for traces of RPC-099's memetic influences. Additionally, Site-099 staff are not permitted within 100 metres of the remains of the now-defunct Site-099-ARC mining facility without proper clearance and PPE.[[footnote]] Level 4/AEDF clearance with long-term lunar exploration suits equipped with Mk. IV personal reality stabilization devices. [[/footnote]] All direct observation and exploration operations of the former site must be done as remotely as possible. All personnel assigned to these areas must be issued up to two 1000 mcg/ml [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/viderics-guide L-prosectide] vials for intramuscular injection in the event they come into contact with an instance of RPC-099-1.

RPC-099-A is currently contained within the lunar cave systems beneath Site-099-ARC. Although accurate mapping of these tunnels has been deemed impossible due to the spatial nature of the anomaly, RPC-099-A is believed to extend to a depth of approximately 229 kilometres into the Lunar mantle. Access to the cave systems must be restricted whenever possible, and on-site ASF personnel have been authorized the use of force to achieve this. All permanent and transitional personnel stationed on a landed AEDF vessel or the lunar surface proper must be briefed on the dangers associated and probable likelihood of anomalous incursion these sub-lunar constructs pose.

The dimensional rift RPC-099-A represents poses a significant danger to the entire AEDF and the Authority's interplanetary operations. Further destabilization and possible collapse have been deemed a Black-level high-priority risk. Following the destruction of the AEDFS //Kaiser Wilhelm//, Uniform-01, the Prometheus Primogenitus, and members of ProLab have been tasked with the closure or destruction of the rift by whatever means possible.

+++ __Description:__

[[/=]]
----

RPC-099 is the collective designation for a currently immeasurable reality coherency vacuum within the upper lunar mantle and its subsequent heterogeneous effects. RPC-099-A represents the AC singularity and its area of memetic influence; RPC-099-1 represents a //Celantur// "low-level" interdimensional ecosystem theorized to originate from the singularity.

RPC-099-A's immediate proximity is characterized by a series of rapid radical fluctuations in local reality coherency– currently anywhere from 0.5 to 4.7 on the Anderson Coherency Scale. Consequently, all current personal reality coherency stabilization apparatuses have proven useless within the lower sections of the anomaly's tunnel system. Based on extrapolated data from microscale reality vacuum collapse events triggered by RPC-███ and several repurposed explosive yield formulas initially proposed by Enrico Fermi,[[footnote]] Enrico Fermi (1901-1954) was an Italian-American Nobel Prize-winning theoretical physicist who briefly collaborated with NuCorp on their early nuclear projects in the 1930s, some of which utilized what would now be understood as Coherency-alteration technology.[[/footnote]] the near-instant destabilization and collapse of RPC-099-A would result in an explosive energy release equivalent to ██ megatons of TNT or ███ PJ.

It has been hypothesized, based on the unique traits exhibited by RPC-099-A and RPC-099-1 respectively, that both aspects of the anomaly act in tandem to trigger the onset of the Moon Madness phenomenon. Two operating hypotheses for this dual functionality are currently being studied by the Authority;
# RPC-099-1 has begun to contaminate the entirety of the lunar atmosphere at a molecular level.[[footnote]] Viderics have, as of yet, been unable to identify microscopic atmospheric contaminations of greater than 5% by volume without the aid of dangerous dosages of Prosectide. [[/footnote]]
# RPC-099-1 is a by-product of an active sub-dimensional entropic invasion. 

The Global Directorate has allocated additional funding to the AEDF to explore and address these hypotheses.

Dr. Aldous Merodak was removed from extended probation on Site-019 and transferred back to Site-099-ARC due to his expertise and extensive knowledge of the previous RPC-099 area, gained during joint experimentation performed on-site with Dr. Maria Sylvius from 2013-2019. 

A total of two expeditions were undertaken into the 099 tunnels. ------
**Captain**: Nigel Rowley
**Head Consultants**: Dr. Aldous Merodak, Dr. Nina Farange
**RSS personnel**: Sgt. Tyler Neyergel, Sgt. Blake Timoat, Sgt. Samuel Hash, Cpl. Brian Eanki (Protection), Jr. Researcher Emily Tuus (Viderics expert), Jr. Researcher Marguerite Schmidt (Seismology technician)

Having completed our initial exploration into the RPC-099 tunnel system, we have returned with very little by way of a consistent explanation on the anomaly's operations. All 9 members of the expedition were in full long-term lunar exploration suits, Dr. Merodak and myself carrying the portable [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/the-anderson-eckhardt-coherency-test AECRs] typical for local-area observations in the field. We conducted detailed coherency analyses every 4 hours, the logs of which are appended to this document. At no point were there any signs of Mr. Chatraparthi's presence.

Within minutes of entering the tunnels, all members of the expedition detected the distinct "misting" or "hazing" at the edges of our fields of vision that are consistent with a slight reduction in local Coherency. Fortunately, at no point did we encounter the more hazardous spatial or temporal distortions which are often associated with low-coherency spaces. 

Several members of the group, myself included, experienced intense night terrors and infrequent but similar auditory hallucinations of varying severity. Dr. Farange has notified me that hallucinations of this nature are not consistent with other low Coherency spaces. I could swear it almost felt alive - //personal//, even. None of us were able to clearly identify what we had dreamed about, or what the faint whispers were screaming.

The tunnel network demonstrates a spiral fractal pattern that certainly does not align with presently explained natural or geological phenomena on Luna -- nor do its branching, recursive paths indicate any obvious sign of artificial construction. We frequently found ourselves doubling back onto passages we had traversed days before; there are few sharp corners in the tunnels, but no end of long, barely-noticeable curves. 

Fortunately, the radio transceiver beacons we placed at regular intervals, combined with the deployment of several automated rovers, ensured that we were generally able to keep our bearings.

The tunnel walls were almost uniformly composed of heavily compacted, polished regolith, with a spiraling scalloped pattern consistent with the general fractal shape of the larger network. We encountered frequent deposits of a viscous, sap-like substance leaking from cracks or fissures in said pattern, of which we collected several samples for analysis upon our return to Site-099.

On the 11th day of the expedition, we were swarmed by thousands of hair-like worms, erupting from fissures in the tunnel walls and ceiling without warning. All of us have noted that the tearing or ripping motions they made have stuck with us since the experience. They seemed to be endless, in both quantity and length. Our hand weapons did nothing. Luckily, they couldn't break through our suit seals or harm us-- an uneasy stalemate. Regardless, they were dense enough that any further exploration would prove fruitless. We could only retreat.

It took two days to make it back to the surface by the most direct route, and we were constantly blocked by boluses of  worms. They ate at our tracks, came at random intervals, disappearing into the shadows without a trace every time. Every time they attacked the auditory hallucinations, which we all experienced as whispering, became stronger. We weren't sure whether the rovers or beacons were knocked off the network or outright destroyed. I fear the worst.

The moment we exited the sealed tunnels, our pursuers immediately retreated, leaving no trace of their presence. None of us experienced the loud screams or "ripping" sounds that previous witnesses to movement in the tunnels have reported. Thankfully, no serious physical injuries, but I suspect that we were all more shaken by the experience than we'd care to admit.

**Post-Mission Addendum**
//Dr. Aldous Merodak//

Analysis of fluid samples from the tunnel walls provided any number of chemical mysteries for us to unravel; the substance is a thick, brown-black liquid with a pH and odour not dissimilar to acetic acid. Structurally, it appears to be a suspension of primarily iron molecules in water, though with a variety of unidentifiable trace elements. 

The structure of the suspended molecules is what truly caught our attention; even at the molecular level, this substance is fractal, with interlocking iron and ruthenium atoms forming a graceful series of loops and circles.[[footnote]] Such fractal molecules have been artificially constructed using iron and ruthenium atoms on Earth, but not to such a degree of sophistication. For further information see Newkome & Wang, Nanoassembly of a Fractal Polymer: A Molecular "Sierpinski Hexagonal Gasket", //Science//, June 2006. [[/footnote]]

At Dr. Sylvius' suggestion, we both experimented with several doses of Leptosiderine viderics but were unable to discern any changes in the contents of one of the sample vials. Dr. Sylvius' assistant Emily Tuus suggested the use of Prosectide, and with our approval self-administered a small 15mcg dose. Within 3 minutes she reported seeing movement and fractal patterns within the chemicals in the vial and reported that the liquid was full of moving filaments or 'worms'.

Against our recommendations, she picked up the vial to examine it more closely, and immediately dropped it, screaming that the worms were "in her hand". 

We initially assumed that she was experiencing a negative reaction to the Prosectide, but during the cleanup process, she began coughing violently and repeating the phrase "A God in me in me a God." She also demonstrated an apparent hypersensitivity to cold, complaining constantly that the medical bay was not warm enough. When questioned about this, she responded by accusing me of lying before attempting to bludgeon me with a bunsen burner. Her behavior quickly became more erratic and violent, and her speech increasingly incoherent. Thankfully RSS personnel were on standby; she was restrained and tranquilized before she could deal any lasting damage.

We performed a thorough physical analysis on Ms. Tuus; even under sedation, she continues to cough and attempt to scratch at her skin.[[footnote]] These symptoms are consistent with what is now known as the advanced physical infection of RPC-099-1. [[/footnote]] After several hours under she began to vomit uncontrollably, the contents of her stomach riddled with hair-like worms not dissimilar to the ones we encountered in the tunnels. Thankfully, they appear to have died immediately upon contact with the air on-site.

We've confined her to one of the medical bay's isolation chambers. The possibility of more extensive memetic or biological contamination left unchecked troubles me deeply. I've destroyed the remaining samples. To minimize the risk of further spread, I've ordered the //Kaiser Wilhelm// to cancel their landing plans. Until we can understand what we're dealing with, and what happened to Tuus, I don't want anyone else entering or leaving the area. To leave lives up to fate, to chance -- //that// is not something I am willing to risk. --Fate, on the other hand...--[[footnote]] The final three sentences of this document were not present in the duplicate copy Dr. Merodak forwarded to Site-019. [[/footnote]]

> ##black| **Note:** The following document was transmitted by Dr. Merodak on the 10th day of Expedition II, along with several hundred megabytes of badly-corrupted digital images and sound files, and several files labelled "Full Mission Logs" which were found to contain only repetitions of the word "MERRU."##

------
**Captain**: --Nigel Rowley-- Dr. Aldous Merodak
**Head Consultants**: --Dr. Nina Farange--
**RSS personnel**: --Sgt. Tyler Neyergel--, --Sgt. Blake Timoat--, --Sgt. Samuel Hash--, --Cpl. Brian Eanki-- (Protection), Jr. Researcher Marguerite Schmidt (Seismology technician)
------

With the tragic death of Mr. Rowley, I have assumed command of all subsequent RPC-099 exploration. This videric-assisted exploration of the tunnels has provided us with a wealth of new data, and I feel that we're closer than ever to understanding the nature of RPC-099. We can also positively confirm the fate of the 1969 expedition.

Given the immediate results afforded by the interaction between Prosectide viderics and the RPC-099 phenomenon, I recommended to Mr. Rowley that our second expedition be conducted under the influence of a constant low-dosage drip of Prosectide, administered at intervals infrequent enough to minimize the risk of side effects. Dr. Farange expressed her displeasure with this course of action, but upon further discussion, it became apparent to Mr. Rowley and me that her objections were derived more from a fear of RPC-099 than any serious scientific qualms.

All members of the expedition team were fitted with suit auto-injectors, maintaining a regular dosage of 21 mcg of Prosectide every 6 hours. Within moments of entry, we began to notice substantial changes to the tunnel network.

When viewed under the influence of Viderics, RPC-099 is not merely a fractal tunnel system; it is an incredibly dense alien ecosystem slightly out of sync with conventional reality. This nonnative ecosystem is visibly "superimposed" over the previously-empty tunnels. It quickly became clear that what we had once prematurely assumed to be caves or passages in the rock were actually an extensive semitransparent network of fluid cavities that seem analogous to //mycelial hyphae// in terms of function - though we have encountered no fruiting bodies, surficial or otherwise, that imply the usage of spores for reproduction. Smaller threadlike fractal structures appear to be embedded into the walls of the greater "hyphae" (for lack of a better term), growing and withering at extraordinarily accelerated rates. 

The fractal pattern extends to a degree we had not previously understood; the hyphae themselves grow 'roots' outward, that subdivide and split until they are of the same fineness as the worms; it may be that the tunnels are themselves merely the physical extensions of the worms on a large enough scale.

I opted to observe the growth rates during one rest period, driving a marker flag into a chink in the tunnel wall. Within the hour, over half of the marker was covered in the spiraling fractal patterns. It took the help of SGTs Neyergel and Hash to extract the marker from the wall afterward - the spiraling biomass continued to grow and develop separately from the host for five hours following extraction, after which it shriveled up and disintegrated. Strange hook-shaped tooth-like structures had grown around the body of the marker, and caused deep scrapes in the metal when it was removed. Dr. Farange hypothesizes that the intensely coiled morphology of these structures is likely a highly-evolved function of the greater 'supercolony' to maximize surface contact with potential sources of nutrients. 

The hair-like worms we found in the fluid samples are present nearly everywhere, but are not strictly speaking a life-form in and of themselves; rather, they seem to be a conducive medium, the substance through which the other, more complex forms of life in the tunnels move. Visualize, if you will, an ocean composed of densely-packed crystalline hair-worms, the space between each worm being occupied by an infinitesimal number of smaller worms. Never doubt the worm.

Further on in the tunnels, we uncovered bulbous growths on the wall composed of the smaller threadlike structures that had been observed earlier. These growths randomly expel spurts of fluid from several small orifices at their base. We have collected samples, but I believe this substance to be the same liquid we recovered on the first expedition. Farange proposed that these could possibly be reproductive fluids, but it was too early and the environment far too uncontrolled for any conclusive statements to be made for or against such an assertion.[[footnote]] Similar samples gathered by U&IIB personnel post-Incident 099-14 have confirmed that these fluids are not dissimilar to proteolytic enzymes. [[/footnote]]

By the second day, we all again began to experience symptoms of varying severity inconsistent with Prosectide exposure: most notably, lucid dreams in which we explored the tunnels sans necessary equipment or suits. Three members of the team reported hearing the voices of their younger selves speaking to them, and both Rowley and myself had an inexplicable encounter with a figure wearing a 1940s-era space-suit who stepped calmly out of a wall, shook both of our hands, and then dissolved into an oily puddle. "Never doubt the Worm," he sang. 

My own dreams have been unsettled, but this is no place to analyze them. I noticed a degree of jitteriness or nerves on the part of all the expedition members, myself included, that could not be reconciled with the situation. We all had difficulty operating basic technical equipment, including our recording devices as we marveled at their modern sophistication. I fear that much of our recorded media is corrupted as a result of this.

Thankfully, we had no hostile encounters with the worms for much of the expedition, and despite the surreal environment were able to penetrate much deeper into the tunnels. By the sixth day we had covered almost twice the distance of our first expedition, and the character of the tunnels had begun to change; instead of a gentle slope downwards, they became increasingly steep, with regular swooping drops and semi-vertical shafts that slowed our progress. It is almost as if the network draws in towards a central point of hub, like the spokes of a wheel. --Never doubt the Worm.--

On the seventh day, our symptoms worsened considerably. Expedition members Sgt. Timoat and Cpl. Eanki were completely incoherent, failing to answer to their given names and insisting that they be called 'Newsom' and 'Lynn'.[[footnote]] Frederick Newsom and Laura Lynn were two technicians listed in the roster of the failed 1969 Fort Providence expedition. Their bodies were never recovered. [[/footnote]] Eanki bolted while we slept. We found him several klicks down from our last encampment, attempting to crack the faceplate of his helmet against the tunnel wall and screaming inarticulately. Before we could reach him, he emptied his sidearm into his chest. We examined the body, and found that the inside of his suit was caked in wormlike filaments, and strange bulbous fruiting bodies suspended on stalked growths had eaten away at his eyes, rupturing out of the front of his skull.

While rappelling down a vertical shaft on the eighth day, Dr. Farange noticed several dark masses embedded in the tunnel walls, visible through the haze of worms and fractal roots; by shining several portable spotlights against the tunnel wall, we were able to identify the silhouettes of several badly-damaged hardsuits, of a design consistent with those worn by the members of the 1969 expedition. Dr. Farange was quite shaken by the experience, and had to be dosed with sedatives before she was calm enough to keep moving. A loss of scientific objectivity such as this is extremely disappointing. We are meant to be seekers of truth here: without doubt. --Never--

I can only surmise, based on the veins of petrified rot frozen in place in and out of the breaches of their antique suits, that they were set upon and killed by the boluses of worms defending the tunnels. The psychological effects in this place would certainly account for their bizarre final messages. We are still unsure on what happened to the remaining members of that expedition, and have found no other evidence of their presence. Given Eanki's fate, it has become clear to me that there are more than just --God-- worms that can kill a man in this place.

The next day, while exploring a flatter section of tunnels, we were abruptly set upon again by a colony of worms, with much the same results as before; while they seemed unable to penetrate our suits, they physically prevented us from advancing and made moving back difficult. It was not yet time. While we were preparing to ascend a shaft away from the worms, Dr. Farange suddenly began to behave erratically, drawing her sidearm and firing several shots into the tunnel walls. When Rowley moved to restrain her, she addressed him as "Calver,"[[footnote]] I can only presume this to be Bedford Calver, leader of the 1969 expedition; I'm unclear if her hallucinations occurred as a result of RPC-099, or some kind of nervous breakdown as a result of the stress of exploring the tunnels. [[/footnote]] saying that "you brought him back here" and he was "already part of it all," before shooting him several times. Unfortunately, he was dead from the shock and hypoxia before we could re-seal his suit. One more gone.

Dr. Farange lost consciousness shortly thereafter. We were able to escape up a longer vertical shaft away from the worms. While we were taking stock of the situation, she abruptly unsealed her suit, though it is unclear whether she died as a result of exposure to the vacuum of space or as a result of the worm infestation that appeared to have been growing inside her for some time. The colony of worms that claims to be Nina Farange should not be trusted. A detailed vivisection overview is attached.

The expedition will continue. It must.

On the 12th day of Expedition II, the remaining members of the team voted to abandon Merodak, who had ignored several mission cancellation orders and begun to demonstrate increasingly unstable behavior. The events that followed are unclear as all communication between the team was lost after the 10th day; only seismology technician Marguerite Schmidt survived long enough to reach the surface. The sequence of events that lead to the deaths of the other members of the expedition team have remained inconclusive.

Though in a state of physical exhaustion, Ms. Schmidt was found to be otherwise physically unharmed, though she demonstrates symptoms consistent with severe post-traumatic stress disorder, and psychotic behaviour characterized by uncontrollable panic attacks and glossolalia.[[footnote]] A psychological phenomenon wherein a person begins to speak in a language unknown to them. On 2/13/2021 at 00:13 UTC +0, a massive expulsion of approx. 136 PJ of energy destroyed the entirety of Site-099-ARC and devastated the Lunar Southern hemisphere. A simultaneous drop in local reality coherency accompanied the blast and persisted throughout the fallout radius for several weeks thereafter. Reports from surviving personnel describe collective shared visions of bright multicoloured lights, accompanied by a "butterfly-like shape" visible in the dust clouds left by the explosion. These shapes and lights were seen at several different times in the week following the explosion.[[footnote]] The ejecta from the detonation - highly visible from earth - was easily explained away as an impact plume following an unanticipated meteor strike. [[/footnote]] The AEDFS //Kaiser Wilhelm//, which had been tasked with supporting the ongoing expedition operations, was instantly destroyed by high-velocity impact with lunar debris. Several other lunar Sites, including Site-019, were slightly damaged by the resultant seismic shockwave; the depth at which the explosion occurred appears to have somewhat mitigated its impact.

The resultant event is being addressed with standard AEDF protocols for Case MARZANNA disasters.[[footnote]] Case MARZANNA is an operational protocol for the sudden destruction of an AEDF station or facility whose loss cannot be entirely concealed from the public. In this event, the initial blast and Lunar debris substantiated the MARZANNA protocol. [[/footnote]] Lunar Logistics Command has undertaken the construction of a new Site-099 on the slope of Malapert Massif to better monitor the blast site. All Authority-allied AOIs capable of space travel have been briefed on Incident RPC-099-14 and the MARZANNA protocols, and have been provided falsified data to assist in the maintenance of the the asteroid impact cover story.
