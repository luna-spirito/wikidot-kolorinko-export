**Recommended Reading Order:**

I'm most proud of [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-137 137], [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-665 665] and [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-899 899] in that order, but I think [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-141 141] is only a little below them. I'm less proud of my first article,  [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-203 203], because it's kind of boring and a little all over the place, and  [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/rpc-134 134] sits in a strange place where I love the idea, and I love that it's canon, but I don't feel any pride in having written it in the way I did.

My tales i recommend reading after the articles they're about, although my two Kabushiki Kawaii articles can probably be read whenever as long as you've got the basics of the group down (infact, i'd sort of recommend reading them before some of the other stuff, just to characterize it in my very niche idea of what they should be).
