**Registered Phenomena Code:** 358

**Object Class:** Beta-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Sapient Hazard, Info-Hazard, Organic Hazard, Sensory Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-358 is to be stored in a locker in the Research Wing of Site-088, only accessible to personel cleared for research with RPC-358, interviews/lessons with RPC-358-2, or security personnel stationed to RPC-358-2. Persons designated RPC-358-1 are to be closely monitored by on-site security, and any ethical infractions towards RPC-358-2 are subject to disciplinary action as per the Stability and Integrity Commission.

RPC-358-2 is to be contained in a modified humanoid cell, and given basic needs and requests, but no request that compromises Authority security or containment of RPC-358 shall be granted. RPC-358-2 is to be fed thrice daily, with a diet comparable to modern Japanese cuisine, but can be given local foods if RPC-358-2 requests. RPC-358-2 is also to be given basic education daily, including lessons on English Language and Literature. In the event that RPC-358-2 must leave its containment chamber, it is to be accompanied by at least two armed guards, both of whom must RPC-358-1 instances.

**Description:** RPC-358 is a standard DVD copy of a Japanese animated series titled [REDACTED] produced by AMAZING! Co. Studios. The box containing RPC-358 measures 190mm x 135mm x 14mm and weighs approximately 74 grams, while RPC-358 itself resembles a standard DVD disc, the front of which is decorated with the title of the content and an uncolored AMAZING! Co. Studios Logo. RPC-358 and its box exhibit no anomalous properties until the DVD is placed into a DVD player with a connection to a viewing device. 

Upon an individual's full viewing of RPC-358's contents, the individual is hereby designated as RPC-358-1. Instances of RPC-358-1 then gain the ability to perceive RPC-358-2 visually and audibly[[footnote]]Without first viewing RPC-358's content, RPC-358-2 cannot be viewed by any of the human senses including vision, audition, gestation, and somatosensation. The method as to why this occurs seems to be that the brain itself refuses to register RPC-358-2's existence. Further research into this phenomena is ongoing.[[/footnote]]. RPC-358-2 is incapable of interacting with normal individuals by any means. RPC-358-2 is described as an 18-year-old brown-haired Asian woman of Japanese decent standing at 157.2cm and weighing 46.3kg. With this description, RPC-358-2 greatly resembles the heroine of [REDACTED], and refers to itself with the same name, Tomoko Hatanaka. RPC-358-2’s personality has been described by researchers as being mature, confident, and brash.

**Discovery Log:** RPC-358 was discovered when 22 reports of schizophrenia along with 12 cases of homicide had occurred at █████████, Japan. The circumstances behind each murder were identical; Blunt force followed by a complete inability to identify fingerprints to any individual. The Authority was eventually contacted by Japanese police after reports of a //Sōrei//[[footnote]]Sōrei, 騒霊, meaning poltergeist[[/footnote]] in a rural northern town led to panic from the local populace.

**Incident Log 358-1**

After RPC-358-2's lessons, the afternoon of 12/██/200█, two Site Security officers (both of whom were RPC-358-1 instances) reported hearing RPC-358-2 screaming from the inside of its cell whilst training new members of Site Security. Both officers, alongside three trainee guards whom were not RPC-358-1 instances, entered RPC-358-2's cell, where both officers were killed by Junior Researcher Takahashi, who bludgeoned them with a [REDACTED] before attacking the trainees. One trainee was killed and another injured before being shot and killed by the remaining with her sidearm. Both survivors reported that Junior Researcher Takahashi had been secreting a black, viscous, and corrosive fluid from his eyes, which he used to stick to the [REDACTED] he had been wielding. Autopsy of Junior Researcher Takahashi revealed the replacement of tear ducts with a gland that created a venomous protein solution, and multiple blood vessels and capillaries ruptured around the eyes, causing them to be filled with blood.
