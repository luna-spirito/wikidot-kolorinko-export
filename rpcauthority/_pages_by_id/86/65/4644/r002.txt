Here is what the RPC Authority is: a community of writers and artists dedicated to horror. That is it. There are no politics or ideologies here. No one here cares about anyone else's beliefs. If you are tired of the moderators at the SCP Foundation, this is your place. [[user CFOperator]] is the site admin and is only focused on putting out quality content.

This is what RPC Authority is not: A soapbox, a political party, a safe space, a fan fiction site, a blog, a movement, etc., etc. 

WE ARE THE AUTHORITY. WE RESEARCH. WE PROTECT. WE CONTAIN.
