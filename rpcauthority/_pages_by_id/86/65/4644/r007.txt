[[size 130%]]**If you are reading this, it means you are one of the select few chosen to join the ranks of The Authority.**

The common man fears many things: poverty, murder, death, nuclear war, etc, and he spends his whole life in the pursuit of safety. In truth, the man does not know true fear, but he does know true safety. The Authority is safety. The Authority is every invisible string attached to the man that keeps him from falling into the pit of Hell. The Authority knows true fear, and it is wise to fear The Authority. 

From the depths of the unknown comes crawling every manner of daemon and entity, subjecting mankind to their tyranny. The Authority is humanities only line of defense, entrusted ages ago to do what the weaker man could not. The Authority is in every nation, every space, monitoring and protecting the world from the unknown. There is no greater power; The Authority operates with unlimited power and will protect humanity, no matter the cost.

**The mankind must remain ignorant**. There is no one else capable of protecting this world. It our responsibility, and ours alone, to be the silent bulwark against the unknown.
