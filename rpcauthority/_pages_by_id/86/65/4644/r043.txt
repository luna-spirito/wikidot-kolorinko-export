[[size 130%]]**If you are reading this, it means you are one of the select few chosen to join the ranks of The Authority.**

The common man fears many things: poverty, murder, death, nuclear war, etc, and he spends his whole life in the pursuit of safety. In truth, the man does not know true fear, but he does know true safety. The Authority is safety. The Authority is every invisible string attached to that man that keeps him from falling into the pit of Hell. The Authority knows true fear, and it is wise to fear The Authority. 

From the depths of the unknown comes crawling every manner of daemon and entity, subjecting mankind to their tyranny. The Authority is humanity's only line of defense, entrusted ages ago to do what the weaker man could not. The Authority is in every nation, every space, monitoring and protecting the world from the unknown.It operates with unlimited power and will protect humanity, no matter the cost.

**Mankind must remain ignorant of our existence**. It is our responsibility—and ours alone—to be mankind's bulwark. Only The Authority has the reach, resources, and conviction to stand firm against the chaos of the unknown.

+ Mission Statement

"People are dumb, panicky dangerous animals and you know it. Fifteen hundred years ago everybody //knew// the Earth was the center of the universe. Five hundred years ago, everybody //knew// the Earth was flat, and fifteen minutes ago, you //knew// that humans were alone on this planet. Imagine what you'll know tomorrow." Agent K.

The Authority operates in total secrecy and works to protect and maintain the veil of normalcy that mankind operates under every day. By preventing the masses from knowing the existence of The Authority and the anomalies it protects against, the world is able to function without descending into chaos. The anomalies the Authority protects against can cause untold amounts of harm to the civilian populace and require eternal vigilance by the Authority's part.

Our mission is three-fold:

++ Research
The Authority can learn much from the anomalies it captures. More often than not, the study of anomalies grants valuable scientific insight that can improve the lives of humanity as a whole.

++ Protect
The Authority owes allegiance to no one, yet it is the assured defender of the human race against anomalous threats. The Authority secures anomalies in an effort to limit the harm done to civilians and to ensure there is no undue loss of human life.

++ Contain
The Authority contains anomalies with the goal of preventing their influence or effects from spreading and for research. The Authority works tirelessly to keep anomalies hidden and secure from the public eye by relocating, dismantling, or disguising RPCs. 

++ Authority Operations
The Authority maintains a presence across every inch of the globe. If an entity is detected in some remote location, The Authority will be there faster than even the most technologically advanced nation could ever deploy personnel. Nothing is hidden from our eyes.

++ Containment Protocols
The Authority maintains an extensive database of all captured and known anomalies, known colloquially as "RPCs". The Authority database provides succinct summaries and containment protocols for RPCs in order to facilitate research, containment, and emergency preparedness.


++ Operational Security
Maintaining secrecy at the Authority requires special care when divulging information to individuals. As a result, Authority staff are each assigned security clearance depending on their needs and function within the Authority. Personnel found violating security access protocols will be detained, interrogated, and otherwise "handled" at the discretion of Authority Administrators.

[[tab title="Who We Are"]]
The RPC Community is simply a collection of horror writers who wish to recapture the magic of the original SCP Foundation. We feel the moderation of the SCP site has destroyed the atmosphere that made the Foundation special, and RPC is here to rebuild. 

**What the RPC Authority Is**
* The RPC Authority is a creative writing community, in the vein of anomalous creative horror writing. This does not necessarily mean, however, that your article needs to be horror. There have been many takes on the genre that you can find throughout our articles, that take the idea of what needs to be contained as something different than horrifying. 

**What the RPC Authority Isn't**
* We are not a platform to push political beliefs, ideological debates, or other matters not related to creative writing. Please, for the sake of others, leave those at the door.

Thank you for reading.
