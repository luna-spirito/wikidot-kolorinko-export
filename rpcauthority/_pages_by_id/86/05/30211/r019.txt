[[==]]
**Nathan Harris Keller** was born to John Francis Keller and Eleanor Keller (formerly Eleanor McCarthy) in Albany, NY, USA. John Keller was a US Army soldier assigned to 5th Battalion, 87th Infantry Regiment and was among the 23 KIA during the US Invasion of Panama in 1989. Eleanor Keller was a teller in a Western Union outlet, located in 607 Broadway, Watervliet, NY 12189, USA. Eleanor Keller was murdered during a home invasion in ██/██/1992.

Nathan Keller then moved in with his uncle, Malcolm Keller—a teacher in the nearby Sheridan Preparatory Academy—in West Hill; a well known ghetto in Albany. Nathan Keller then had a rough childhood; forced to work at an early age as Malcolm Keller was unable to accommodate his daily needs. However, Nathan Keller earned a scholarship in Pace University, located in the nearby New York City and went for the Forensics major. Nathan Keller graduated at age 22 with a bachelor's degree and immediately enlisted in the New York Police Department.

After finishing the Academy, Nathan Keller was assigned as a patrol unit for 3 years, before his performance earned him a promotion as a homicide detective in ██/██/2007. Nathan Keller then engaged to Caitlín Léan Murphy shortly after his promotion and both were later married just 3 months after the engagement in ██/██/2007. Nathan and Caitlín Keller had a daughter, Mary-Anne Aleta Keller, in ██/██/2007.

[[include component:image-block
    name=KellerMarriage|
    caption=Nathan and Caitlín Keller during the wedding reception. |

In July 2012, Nathan Keller was investigating the murder of Emma Hopkins that was a part of The Black Tulip murders of 2012. The evidence in which Nathan Keller had gathered all pointed to Tony Armone, a well known mob boss and allegedly had bribed the rankings of the NYPD. During this investigation, Tony Armone had sent assassins to rig Nathan Keller's car with explosives. However, the explosion had claimed Caitlín and Mary Keller's lives instead.

Shortly after the funeral, Nathan Keller refused the commissioner's order to take a few weeks off and was later suspended. Nathan Keller then went on an unauthorized investigation on Tony Armone, aided off the records by his colleagues. The Board of Global Directors took interest in Detective Keller after he successfully killed Tony Armone, proven to be a high ranking member of the Raven Mafia and the mastermind behind The Black Tulip murders. Global Director 03 then ordered the transfer of Nathan Keller to MST Sierra-8 "Sundowners", seeing how he had obtained an intricate knowledge of the Raven Mafia and its inner workings.

Nathan Keller's colleagues had filed complains against him during his time in the Authority for being overtly cynic and hostile, often assaulting personnel who mentioned Caitlín and Mary Keller or The Black Tulip. Nathan Keller was later ordered to attend psychiatry sessions in the office of Dr. Genevieve Varghast. MST Sierra-8 investigators and a small portion of the protection division personnel had given Nathan Keller the nicknames such as "Revenant" and "The Authority's Own Bandog".

Dr. Varghast initially suspended Nathan Keller from active duty as he was diagnosed with severe depression. After approximately 3 months of consuming antidepressants and attending therapeutic sessions, Dr. Varghast observed significant progress and allowed Nathan Keller to return to active duty. However, Nathan Keller's cynical and nihilistic behavior was still apparent.

During his time working on the homicide department of MST Sierra-8, Nathan Keller demonstrated exceptional efficiency in tracking down Raven Mafia operations and was made evident by his comment: "I've worked for people like them, so I know how they think.". Initially, the Human Resources started an official investigation but was shortly terminated as there was no direct evidence.

In mid-2017, Nathan Keller's animosity against the Raven Mafia was getting progressively more apparent, as observable during a methamphetamine lab raid, where Nathan Keller executed every gunmen, even after they have surrendered. Dr. Varghast made an official request to Captain Hakob Bedrosian, the commanding officer of MST Sierra-8, to suspend Nathan Keller. However, Captain Bedrosian offered Nathan Keller a temporary transfer to the cartage department, in which he had accepted.

In ██/██/2017, as part of RPC Authority's MST Modernization program, The Board of Global Directors signed a contract with Nucorp Industries to develop a set of armor and weapons specialized for covert ops to blend in with civilians while retaining the same firepower and defensive capability of a typical MST loadout. 

[[include component:image-block
    name=NBP-1|
    caption=The Nucorp NBP-1 mod. 0, during the trial run. |
    width=310px
]]

The result was the Novusbellum Program, spawning the Nucorp NB/P-1 mod. 0 lightweight simple blowback, single-action semi-automatic pistol, chambered in .22 LR cartridge and a trench coat equipped with composite armor embedded within the linings, complementing the pistol to reduce print on the operator's clothes.

Nucorp Industries also designed a complementary collapsible PDW (Personal Defense Weapon), designated as NB/PDW-2 mod. 0. However, the design was rejected as it proved to be too complicated to deploy and the cartridge used (.45 ACP) was too inaccurate beyond 20 meters. 

The armored trench coat, designated as NB/AS-3 features a layer of Kevlar KM2 along the torso and arm area with added layer of Dyneema UHMWPE (Ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene) along the torso. The combined weight of the Teflon-coated cotton fabric and the body armor adds up to approximately 4kg (including the weight of the fabric). Nucorp Industries was also planning on adding an APS (Active Protection System) suite embedded into the breast pocket to defend the operator from remote explosives.

Investigator Nathan Keller was chosen to partake in the trial run, along with several other MST operatives. Nathan Keller received the NB/AS-3 trench coat positively, commenting on its relative light weight compared to its excellent protection capabilities. However, he did not favor the NB/P-1 mod. 0. After shooting 5 fully loaded 10-round magazines in the shooting range, Nathan Keller quoted "It's inaccurate compared to most .22 and the trigger creep is abysmal. Grip is too small for my hands, mag release button is weirdly placed and it's too light for my liking." and choosing to favor his personal Browning Hi-Power (chambered in 9x19mm Parabellum cartridges).
