+ Early Life

[[==]]
**Nathan Harris Keller** was born to John Francis Keller and Eleanor Keller (formerly Eleanor McCarthy) in Albany, NY, USA. John Keller was a US Army soldier assigned to 5th Battalion, 87th Infantry Regiment and was among the 23 KIA during the US Invasion of Panama in 1989. Eleanor Keller was a teller in a Western Union outlet, located in 607 Broadway, Watervliet, NY 12189, USA. Nathan Keller then lived with his mother until the age of 10, until Eleanor Keller was murdered during a home invasion in ██/██/1992.

Nathan Keller then moved in with his uncle, Malcolm Keller, in West Hill—a well known ghetto in Albany. He enrolled in Albany High School, along with Malcolm Keller working there as a teacher. Nathan Keller had a rough childhood; forced to work at an early age as Malcolm Keller was unable to accommodate his daily needs. Reports from his known associates indicate that Nathan Keller used to deal various narcotics and partake in petty thefts, however, this was unconfirmed. Nevertheless, Nathan Keller graduated in ██/██/2000 and earned a scholarship in Pace University, located in the nearby New York City.

He then graduated at age 22 with a bachelor's degree in Forensics and immediately enlisted in the New York Police Department. After finishing the Academy, Nathan Keller was assigned as a patrol unit for 3 years, before his performance earned him a promotion as a homicide detective in ██/██/2007. 

+ Marriage and children

During his first year as a NYPD Detective, Nathan Keller met Léa Murphy in a diner outskirts of New York City. then engaged to Léa Murphy shortly after his promotion and both were married just 3 months later in ██/██/2007. Nathan and Léa Keller had a daughter, named Mary-Anne Keller, in ██/██/2007.

+ The Black Tulip Murders

In July 2012, Nathan Keller was investigating the murder of Emma Hopkins that was a part of The Black Tulip murders of 2012. Eric Hopkins—the father of Emma Hopkins and later revealed as APAS art collectors—attempted to provide useful information to Nathan Keller before his wife, Jenna Hopkins murdered him and claimed her own life. Nevertheless, Jenna Hopkins stated that The Hopkins used to deal various weaponized art anomalies to Tony Armone, a well known mob boss that had allegedly bribed the rankings of the NYPD. However, this was not enough for a warrant. After months of investigation and several serial murders, Nathan Keller was able to confirm that the victims of The Black Tulip murders were connected to tony Armone—in business and private matters. 

Before Nathan Keller could file a warrant to the District Attorney, Tony Armone sent assassins to rig Nathan Keller's issued cruiser with explosives. However, the explosion had claimed Léa and Mary-Anne Keller's lives instead. Shortly after the funeral, Nathan Keller refused the commissioner's order to take a two-week vacation and was later suspended. He then went on an unauthorized investigation on Tony Armone, aided off the records by his colleagues. The behavior of his Captain stalling the arrest of Tony Armone added with several evidence Nathan Keller had obtained led to him uncovering the web of conspiracy involving not only the commissioner, but the Mayor of New York City as well.

The Board of Global Directors took interest in Detective Keller after he successfully killed Tony Armone, proven to be a high ranking member of the Raven Mafia and the mastermind behind The Black Tulip murders. Global Director 03 then offered Nathan Keller a transfer to MST Sierra-8 "Sundowners", believing the intricate knowledge of the Raven Mafia and its inner workings he had obtained would be a great asset to the Authority.

+ RPC Authority

During his first years in the Authority, Nathan Keller's colleagues had filed complaints against him for being overtly cynic and hostile, often assaulting personnel who tried to talk about or mentioned Léa and Mary-Anne Keller or The Black Tulip murders. Nathan Keller was later ordered to attend psychiatry sessions in the office of Dr. Genevieve Varghast. Due to his hostile behavior in the early years working in the Authority, MST Sierra-8 investigators and a small portion of the protection division personnel had given Nathan Keller nicknames such as "Revenant" and "The Authority's Own Bandog".

Dr. Varghast initially suspended Nathan Keller from active duty as he was diagnosed with Major depressive episode and post-traumatic stress disorder. After approximately 3 months of consuming antidepressants and attending therapeutic sessions, Dr. Varghast observed significant progress and allowed Nathan Keller to return to active duty. However, his cynical and nihilistic behavior was still apparent.

In mid-2017, Nathan Keller's animosity against the Raven Mafia was getting progressively more apparent, as observable during a methamphetamine lab raid in ████, ██, where Nathan Keller executed every gunmen—even after they have surrendered—without orders from his superiors. Dr. Varghast made an official request to Captain Hakob Bedrosian to suspend Nathan Keller from active duty for a complete psychological check-up. However, Captain Bedrosian instead ordered Nathan Keller a temporary transfer to the cartage department, believing the work atmosphere would relieve him of post-traumatic stress while also being productive.

In ██/██/2017, as part of RPC Authority's MST Modernization program, The Board of Global Directors signed a contract with Nucorp Industries to develop a set of armor and weapons specialized for covert ops to blend in with civilians while retaining the same firepower and defensive capability of a typical MST loadout. 

[[include component:image-block
    name=NBP-1|
    caption=The Nucorp NBP-1 mod. 0, during the trial run. |
    width=310px
]]

The result was the Novusbellum Program, spawning the Nucorp NB/P-1 mod. 0 lightweight simple blowback, single-action semi-automatic pistol, chambered in .22 LR cartridge and a trench coat equipped with composite armor embedded within the linings, complementing the pistol to reduce print on the operator's clothes.

Nucorp Industries also designed a complementary collapsible PDW (Personal Defense Weapon), designated as NB/PDW-2 mod. 0. However, the design was rejected as it proved to be too complicated to deploy and the cartridge used (.45 ACP) was too inaccurate beyond 20 meters. 

The armored trench coat, designated as NB/AS-3 features a layer of Kevlar KM2 along the torso and arm area with added layer of Dyneema UHMWPE (Ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene) along the torso. The combined weight of the Teflon-coated cotton fabric and the body armor adds up to approximately 4kg (including the weight of the fabric). Nucorp Industries was also planning on adding an APS (Active Protection System) suite embedded into the breast pocket to defend the operator from remote explosives.

Investigator Nathan Keller was chosen to partake in the trial run, along with several other MST operatives. Nathan Keller received the NB/AS-3 trench coat positively, commenting on its relative light weight compared to its excellent protection capabilities. However, he did not favor the NB/P-1 mod. 0. After shooting 5 fully loaded 10-round magazines in the shooting range, Nathan Keller quoted "It's inaccurate compared to most .22 and the trigger creep is abysmal. Grip is too small for my hands, mag release button is weirdly placed and it's too light for my liking." and choosing to favor his personal Browning Hi-Power (chambered in 9x19mm Parabellum cartridges).
