++ Join The Site

**Account Rules:**
* Only you can use your Wikidot account on our site. Do not share your account with anyone.
* You are only allowed to use one Wikidot account on our site. Any "extra" accounts will be removed.

**Roleplaying:**
* We are not a roleplaying site. Don't pretend to be a fictional character, and don't pretend to be a member of the fictional Authority.

**Age:** 
* This website contains mature themes and discussions. We expect members to behave in a mature and adult-like manner. You must be 16 or older to join.

**Applying:**
* Write the Site Passcode in the application below. If you still don't know the Site Passcode, then read the [[[Guide for Newbies]]], and you will find instructions letting you know what to do.
* Your application must include a single grammatically correct sentence explaining why you wish to write for us. 
* (Optional) Tell us where you heard of our site. 
If you application does not include an introductory sentence (with correct grammar) and the site passcode, it will be denied.

[[=]]
**By submitting an application below, you're agreeing to follow the [[[Site Rules|rules of this site]]].**
