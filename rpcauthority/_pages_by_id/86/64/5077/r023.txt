++ Join The Site

**Applying:**
* Write the Site Passcode in the application below. If you don't know the Site Passcode, then read the [[[Guide for Newbies]]] to find instructions.
* Your application must include a grammatically correct sentence explaining why you wish to join. 
* (Optional) Tell us where you heard of our site. 
If your application is not grammatically correct or does not contain an introductory sentence and the site passcode, it will be denied.

[[=]]
