++ Join The Site

**Account Rules:**
* Only you can use your Wikidot account on our site. Do not share your account with anyone.
* You are only allowed to use one Wikidot account on our site. Any "extra" accounts will be removed.

**Roleplaying:**
* We are not a roleplaying site. Don't pretend to be a fictional character, and don't pretend to be a member of the fictional Foundation.

**Age:** 
* This website contains mature themes and discussions. We expect members to behave in a mature and adult-like manner. You must be 15 or older to join.

**Applying:**
* Please write the Site Passcode in the application below, then submit the application. If you still don't know the Site Passcode, then read the [[[Guide for Newbies]]], and you will find instructions letting you know what to do.
* You //only// need to send us the Site Passcode in your application, nothing more.

[[=]]
**By submitting an application below, you're agreeing to follow the [[[Site Rules|rules of this site]]].**
