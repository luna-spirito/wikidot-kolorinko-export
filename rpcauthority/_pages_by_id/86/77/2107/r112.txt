**Registered Phenomena Code:** 002-ARC

**Object Class:** Alpha-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Mechanical Hazard, Newtonian Hazard, Memory Alteration Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-002-ARC is to be contained in a standard Alpha-class container. --RPC-002-ARC is only to be removed for testing purposes, which must be cleared by any level 3 research staff. RPC-002-ARC is not to be used outside of approved testing areas, and only on approved targets. All organic based testing must be approved by the Site-███ Director.-- See Addendum 002-ARC-1.

**Description:** RPC-002-ARC is a Colt Single-Action Army Revolver, painted silver, with a wooden handle. RPC-002-ARC displays no anomalous properties when unloaded. The material, quality or velocity of any bullet loaded into and fired by RPC-002-ARC does not alter RPC-002-ARC’s anomalous effects, only acting as a vehicle for its transport. Any bullet fired from RPC-002-ARC will alter materials it comes in contact with on a subatomic level, the changes themselves varying based on which cylinder has been fired. The anomalous effect do not require penetration. While it affects more complex compounds, it is far less effective, spreading its effect out in a much smaller area, and often not converting the entire compound to the new element, due to impurities.

When used on basic chemical elements, RPC-002-ARC alters them into another a differing element, state, or form by changing the position, charge and energy of subatomic particles. The change is based off of what cylinder the RPC-002-ARC is currently rotated to. While multiple attempts have been made to mark and catalogue which chamber has what effect, the item anomalously clears and repairs, preventing all alterations. The extent of self-repair seemingly allowed it to recover from a blast that vaporized everything else in a ███m radius. The item appears have a minor cognitohazard effect; anyone focusing their mind on keeping the exact rotations of each cylinder remember's incorrectly despite conviction of being correct, even after writing or otherwise noting the order previously. Though testing is difficult, the reference point of the only constant, the destroyed chamber, makes it possible to mark during a single session, but the markings will be later removed afterwards. Attempts to observe the markings disappearing through video recordings have shown the disappearance happen after seemingly random intervals with no visible change period.

Each cylinder is denoted with a dash at the end of the test log, in order of cylinder tested by distance from the control destroyed cylinder.

[[collapsible show="+ Experiment Log" hide="- Experiment Log"]]

**Test Number:** RPC-002-ARC-1
**Subject:** CSD-4867, Asian male, aged 38.
**Procedure:** Agent Jameson is ordered to point and fire the revolver at CSD-4867.
**Results:** CSD-4867's body temperature increases to 1670 °C, causing all water in their body to violently rupture the body's tissues, which shortly after are reduced to ashes. No anomalous properties were found within the ash. Agent Jameson is hit by the vapor expanding, causing minor burns.
**Notes:** From the temperature recording, it seemed CSD-4867 was increased to the melting point for human bones. From the recording, it seems that the bullet does not need to penetrate the skin, making contact is enough to induce this effect.

**Notice from Head Researcher:** Organic testing is to be discontinued, and it's advised to use CSDs to operate with RPC-002-ARC-1. 

**Test Number:** RPC-002-ARC-2
**Subject:** One (1) bar of steel alloy.
**Procedure:** RPC-002-ARC is fired by CSD-4869 into the subject.
**Results:** Upon collision of the bullet with the steel alloy, carbon and iron violently slam into the right and left walls of the testing chamber. It is assumed that RPC-002-ARC separated the steel bar's base original elements, carbon and iron, to the opposite sides of the room. The remaining materials are left unmoved.
**Notes:** The steel jacket casing of the bullet used in testing was not separated, although it was malformed from contact. Interestingly, even if the payload is the same as the subject, it will not be affected. Why the iron and carbon were violently separated, while other components such as Manganese (which was present at a higher percentage than carbon) were simply left behind with no additional kinetic energy is unknown.

**Test Number:** RPC-002-ARC-3
**Subject:** One (1) kg of firewood, from an English oak.
**Procedure:** CSD-4867 is asked to fire RPC-002-ARC into the firewood.
**Results:** Upon the bullet's impact with the firewood, it vaporized, and fog immediately covered observation windows, obstructing vision. The testing room is locked while heat and movement sensors are observed. The heat in the room dropped drastically, and no movement was noticeable. Upon the fog dispersing, CSD-4867 is found dead to frostbite
**Notes:** After reviewing heat sensor showings, it's assumed RPC-002-ARC reduced the firewood's heat to Absolute Zero or near such heats, which caused it's atoms to collapse and spread, dropping the overall heat in the room to below -20 °C.

**Notice from Site Director:** RPC-002-ARC is to be fired with a remotely controlled trigger for all further testing.

**Test Number:** RPC-002-ARC-4
**Subject:** One (1) rubber sheet.
**Procedure:** RPC-002-ARC was triggered remotely using a mechanical pull system, firing at the subject.
**Results:** When the bullet connected with the rubber sheet, rather than experiencing any resistance, the bullet traveled straight through, the sheet collapsing into small fragments. It was discovered on investigation that the pieces the rubber sheet broke into crumbled and broke when any pressure was applied. These pieces, while small, were unusually heavy. Further testing of the properties revealed a high conduction of electricity in the object.
**Notes:** The new material created by RPC-002-ARC has an electricity conduction far greater than any normal materials available. In the short distance tests we were able to perform, the electricity lost amount to only .0003% over a 5km closed circuit.

**Test Number:** RPC-002-ARC-5
**Subject:** One (1) cup of water.
**Procedure:** RPC-002-ARC was loaded with a bullet into the destroyed chamber. It was fired remotely.
**Results:** RPC-002-ARC’s hammer did not trigger the bullet inside of it, due to the internal damage of the chamber.
**Notes:** On closer examination of the chamber, the damage seems similar to that of a backfire. Whether this is due to its own effects, or damage from external sources, it is unable to be fired. Repair options are being explored, but have thus far been repelled by the guns anomalous properties to maintain itself.

**Test Number:** RPC-002-ARC-6
**Subject:** One (1) cup of water.
**Procedure:** RPC-002-ARC was triggered remotely from the observation room, angled downward towards the cup to impact the water before the cup.
**Results:** See Incident log RPC-002-ARC-6.
**Notes:** It’s nothing more than luck that a cup of water was selected for this test. Had any object larger than that been selected, it would have leveled all of ███████.

[[collapsible show="+ Incident log RPC-002-ARC-6" hide="- Incident log RPC-002-ARC-6"]]

On 4/12/20██, 8:22 am, RPC-002-ARC was undergoing testing of its final chamber. Subject chosen was a cup of water, fired downwards to connect with the water rather than the cup. At 8:23 am, all contact was lost with Site-███, which at the current time had multiple researchers in communication with other sites. An automatic distress signal was sent out, signalling that one or more RPCs had breached containment. MST recovery teams were immediately sent out to recontain and neutralize any threats, anomalous or otherwise. On first-responder arrival to Site-███, one MST operator noticed a sinkhole near the site’s entrance. It was discovered at that time that all but the exterior framework of the east side of Site-███ had been destroyed.

MST operatives secured and neutralized all on-site anomalous threats, but were unable to recover the black box. It is unsure exactly what occurred, but it appears that matter has been vaporized in a ███m radius around the initial detonation area. RPC-002-ARC was recovered successfully during search and rescue operations, and no lasting damage was found. Multiple RPCs were unconfirmed as neutralized, and at this time it is unknown whether the blast neutralized them or facilitated their escape.

[[/collapsible]]

**Addendum 002-ARC-1:** All testing suspended on orders of Global Director 04. Due to the items anomalous self-concealing properties, and incident RPC-002-ARC-6, further testing has been considered too dangerous to continue.

**Addendum 002-ARC-2:** RPC-002-ARC has been transferred to Site-001 in ██/██/████ by order of the Board.
