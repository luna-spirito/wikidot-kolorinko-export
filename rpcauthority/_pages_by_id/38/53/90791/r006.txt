Hello, all you boy wonders and supergirls! 
@@@@
We're hosting a community contest to honor one of the most influential creators of fiction, 
**Stan Lee**.
"How are we doing this?" I hear you ask using my super hearing. 
Well, it's quite simple, we're using creative fiction, but not just any creative fiction, we're hosting a superhero/villain contest.
@@@@
And here are the rules:
# The character must be either an original hero or villain. 
# The character must either be anomalous or use the anomalous but origin story is up to you.
# Entries must be in the form of a Tale.
# All entries must be tagged "Comic-Kaze".
# All entries will be judged by the community based on overall score
@@@@
The deadline for this contest is __Jan. 12th__ 
@@@@
Have fun, get creative, and prove you can be a super writer, **Excelsior**!
