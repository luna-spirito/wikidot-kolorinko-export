+ **Overview**
[[/=]]
-----

[[f>image http://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/cognitive-i/Foxtrot-4%281%29.png width="264px";]]

**Numerical Code:** --Alpha-23-- Foxtrot-4 (Reassigned)

**Codename:** Prey

**Motto:** //Dum Vives Currere Potest Sine Pervenire Nusquam// ("You can run your whole life, but not go anywhere")

**Garrison/HQ:** Various 

**Roles:** Rapid Deployment, Containment and Retrieval, CBRN Specialist, Special Reconnaissance, and Direct Action

**Established:** --February 8, 1942-- September 29, ████ (Reactivated)

**Description:** Foxtrot-4 was formed as a special reconnaissance and industrial sabotage unit to combat GARD's operations in the middle east, in 1942. Previously codenamed as Alpha-23, Alpha-23 was lead by Colonel Cartwright in coordination with American and British intelligence.

By 1945, GARD operations within the middle east were dismantled by Allied forces. In ████, the Church of Malthus began to spread their influence throughout the Arabic states to establish a foothold for their organization. With Malthusian influence seen as a threat to Authority operations, the Authority assigned Alpha-23 to dismantle the Malthus network within the middle east.

With the assistance of Israeli intelligence from Mossad, Alpha-23 captured known Malthus cells operating within the Israeli Defense Force and the Israeli Government.

Malthusian influence had weakened within the Middle East, but complete neutralization proved difficult. Alpha-23 was deactivated as the threat of Malthus operations within the Arabic states became weaker in the 1980s, during the Soviet invasion and subsequent clash with Malthusian forces.

In ████, in the aftermath of the disastrous containment efforts of Site-███, the Protection Division reorganizes its security protocols and subsequently reactivates Alpha-23, and reassigns them to Foxtrot-4. Since ████, Foxtrot-4 has been the primary detachment for rapid deployment in situations where anomalies need to be contained and retrieved, following their discovery or initial containment breach within an Authority facility.

In addition, Foxtrot-4 has multiple response teams stationed across the world on standby at all times.

**Mission Codeword:** //Crowd Control//

**Status:** Active

**Date:** June 23rd, ████

**Summary:** Following a major containment breach within Site-███, Foxtrot-4 and various response units were deployed to re-contain the loose anomalies. Due to the gravity of the incident, Foxtrot-4 was authorized to use force whatever means necessary to contain the situation. Seven hours later, the situation resulted in most of the housed anomalies being re-contained.

Investigations into the incident were believed to be orchestrated by [DATA EXPUNGED], though evidence linked to the accused organization could not be confirmed. As of ████, current retrieval for uncontained anomalies from Site-███ are still ongoing.

> //I must emphasize that the operation is still ongoing. While I don't anticipate the operation to end within the coming months, I hope the results would please the higher-ups for the unit's progress since the incident.//
>
> - Colonel Abrams Hale

**Date:** December 8, 20██

**Summary:** Foxtrot-4 was sent to investigate and dismantle a facility in ███████, Oregon, after ACI intelligence confirmed the property to be registered by an international anomalous group, wanted by the World Security Council. Posing as an FBI SWAT team, Foxtrot-4 breached the facility and apprehended key members of the organization. Known persons of interest [REDACTED] was an ACI informant and was debriefed by ACI Agents in the aftermath of the operation.

Intelligence obtained from apprehended members revealed that the organization was established by an unknown former Authority researcher, though the extent of the organization's existence is said to have originated from the mid-1980s.

**Mission Codeword:** //Clockwork//

**Status:** Inactive; Success

**Date:** August 4, 20██

**Summary:** Foxtrot-4 tasked its CBRN Team to ████████, Colorado, following unconfirmed reports of a biological attack conducted by an anomalous group. With support from Air Operations, CBRN Team made contact and neutralized hostile forces within the metropolitan area.

--Situation proved difficult after the CBRN Team encountered friendly fire from ███████ Police Department, which resulted in numerous police officers being neutralized at the orders of the assigned tactical operations command.-- (Subject for reclassification)

With assistance from federal authorities, cleanup operations ensued after the situation lasted about two hours.

Following the incident, Mobile Specialized Team Sierra-7 ("Sundowners"), in coordination with American AOI-0812 ("Division 15"), were tasked to investigate the attacks and discovered that the individuals behind it were previously employed by the People's Committee for the Acquisition of Anomalous Objects (PCAAO).

When inquired about the attacks, the accusations were largely denied by the Chinese Government.
