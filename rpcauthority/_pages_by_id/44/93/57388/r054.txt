**The Anthropology Department**
-----
[[/=]]
**Description:** The Anthropology Department is mainly focused on the study of human reaction to anomalies encountered by the Authority. While most nations in the world are oblivious to the existence of anomalies encountered by the Authority, there are cases in which human beings come into contact with anomalies, and the Anthropology department seeks to document any interactions between humans and RPCs. 

In addition to this, the Anthropology department also studies organizations, both active and inactive, that is either anomalous in nature or deal with other anomalies. It should also be noted that the Anthropology department also encompasses human archeology and work closely with the History department to uncover and retrieve any anomalous object that appears throughout history.

**The Chemistry Department**
-----
[[/=]]

**Description:** The Chemistry Department is responsible for the research and analysis of chemical compounds composed of atoms, molecules and ions: their composition, structure, properties, behavior and the changes that they undergo during a reaction with other substances. While this department is considered a centralized department due to its reliability as a foundation for the understanding of both basic and applied scientific disciplines at a fundamental level. 

--Both the Physics and Biology Departments have recently established a committee regarding the existence of anomalies and their reasoning of such to defy against the scientific basis of which has been established.-- This committee was disbanded in [DATA EXPUNGED].

Furthermore, This department is in the jurisdiction of anomalies that have been exhibited to produce undocumented chemical compounds, elements, and anomalies that defy the fundamental foundation of our current knowledge on alchemy.

[[image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/the-office-of-analysis-and-science/Cybernetics2.png style="filter: invert(1); width: 18%;"]]

**The Cybernetics Department**
-----
[[/=]]

**Description:** The Cybernetics Department is dedicated to the study and development of Cybernetic technology. The goal of this department is to one day ensure their findings are published in order to improve the lives of the disabled in our society. The Cybernetics Department was developed on ██/██/████ at the request of Board Member ██████ ████████, which is currently exclusive to the Authority. 

Due to this, personnel entering the Cybernetics Department must first undergo educational training by senior members who have expertise in the field. Any personnel interested in joining the department, or considered as a candidate for entry, must have some educational background in Engineering and Biology.

[[tab The Geology Department]]

[[=]]

 [[image http://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/reseach-hub-2-0/Geology.png style="filter: invert(1); width: 18%;"]]

**The Geology Department**
-----
[[/=]]

**Description:** The Geology Department focuses on the study of the Earth's composition and Geography, the formation of geological bodies, along with the geological processes that occur in these two focuses. This department is well known for having not only having multiple locations around the world but also for its actively mobile Research personnel. The Geology Department, for all intents and purposes, encompasses both principles related to Geology and the Earth Sciences. 

The Geology Department contains a number of sub-departments focusing on disciplines that include but are not limited to, Geography, Paleontology, Oceanography, Meteorology, Astronomy, Mineralogy, Environmental Science, Geochemistry, et cetera. Due to this number of principles under the department, there is a prevalent history of overlap and joint projects had with other departments under The Office of Analysis and Science.

This department is responsible for research of RPCs that relates to the Geography of the planet(s), the climate of the biosphere, and/or effect the geological process that either cause or are the cause of the RPC in question. In addition, this department also researches evidence for any RPCs that may have existed, by performing paleontological research on areas suspected to contain fossils of what could have been categorized as RPCs (this causes some minor overlap with the Anthropology Department and Biology).

[[tab The History Department]]

[[=]]

[[image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/the-office-of-analysis-and-science/History.png style="filter: invert(1); width: 18%;"]]

**The History Department**
-----
[[/=]]

**Description:** The History Department is dedicated to studying the past in order to better understand RPCs that have shown evidence to be an occurrence for a large amount of time. The History Department's main priority is to both study the origins of these phenomena throughout history, research any possible anomalies, and investigate any anomalous occurrences throughout history to decide RPC classification. The History Department is not only concerned with historical studies, but also other fields in humanities such as philosophy, theology, literature, and language.

Within the History Department, the specialized Directorate for Historical and Pseudohistorical Studies (DHPS) is a working group dedicated to the study of the Authority's history, as well as the fabrication of false or deliberately misleading historical documents to maintain information security.

[[tab The Physics Department]]

[[=]]

[[image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/the-office-of-analysis-and-science/Physics.png style="filter: invert(1); width: 18%;"]]

**The Physics Department**
-----
[[/=]]

**Description:** The Physics Department is responsible for the research and studying of matter and motion and behavior through space and time and that studies the related entities of energy and force. While the department is one of the fundamental scientific disciplines, its main goal is to understand anomalies that defy the current known laws of our reality and universe. 

Additionally, the Physics Department also takes jurisdiction within matters on a very small scale or under extreme conditions and includes quantum physics, atomic and nuclear physics, solid state physics, and elementary particle physics. 

Due to the complexity that researchers within the Physics Department have to face, the Global Directors has given full authorization to the current Lead Director, ████████, to recruit one of the top experts in the field of physics across the world.

**The Robotics Department**
-----
[[/=]]

**Description:** The Robotics Department is responsible for the engineering and scientific advancements of mechanical engineering, electronic engineering, information engineering, computer science, and many more. This particular department is responsible for anomalies that have been developed under circumstantial means or contain unusual mechanical functionality. It should be noted, however, that this field is very limited in comparison to the cybernetics department, and engineering department. Due to this, The Robotics Department is mostly concerned with the development of robotic engineering and will concern themselves with robotic RPCs that may present a breakthrough in the field. Like The Engineering Department, Robotics work very closely with the containment division in the development of better containment techniques.
