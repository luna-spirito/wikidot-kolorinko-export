{{**Name:** Harold R. Ames}}

{{**Age:** 52}}

{{Division: Research}}

{{**Area of Occupation:** Authority Anthropological Department}}

{{**Rank:** Head}}

{{**Role:** Researcher}}

{{**Clearance Level:** Level IV}}

{{**Physical Description:** Harold R. Ames is a caucasian male, with ginger hair. He is 185 centimeters tall, and of slim build, weighing 6█ kilograms. He has no notable unique traits.}}

{{**Background:** Harold R. Ames was born on 11/12/1967 in ██████, WV, USA. He is the son of Cane Ames and Ashley Ames. At age 23 he worked in a model train factory, and soon became an amateur engineer. After 3 years of being an engineer, Harold decided to seek higher pay at NuCorp in 1985. In 1999, The Authority noticed Harold's skills and recruited him, after he accepted the offer of higher pay. Harold R. Ames' work at NuCorp was mostly making phone calls in the office, and fixing machines big or small. He currently has a collection of the tech and devices he has tweaked. He moved up the ranks, and was fixing important assets to NuCorp in 200█. It is unknown how much information Harold has of NuCorp, and he refuses to give out classified information.[[footnote]] If this is  for legal or personal reasons are unknown. [[/footnote]] Harold has been discovered to have majored in Anthropology in college and has been tested and promoted accordingly. Due to the passing of the Anthropological Head researcher Harold has been promoted accordingly.  Harold is not to be questioned about this until ██████. Harold is not to be given access to Gamma class RPCs after Incident A-███-0██.[[footnote]] Personnel not involved in this event are not to ask any personnel who were involved in this event,what it was per protocol. [[/footnote]]}}

{{**Current Duties:** Harold R. Ames' current duties are to research and assist in containment of various anomalies that are sapient, and/or relate to human interaction. He is skilled in interviews, and can get information out easily. Harold often expresses how he wanted to work on mechanical anomalies, but is often ignored.}}
