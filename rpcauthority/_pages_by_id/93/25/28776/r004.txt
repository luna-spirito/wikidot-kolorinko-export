= **Shutdown O-5582**

**Date:** March 25, 1955

**Objective:** Break into and terminate The Controller of RPC-558. Secondary Objective is to coordinate Artillery as a last resort.

**MST:** █████ (previously known as Strike team-Sigma)

**Team Members:** Sergeant Petrov of the Initiative, Corporal Lanning of the Authority, Private Rhine of the Authority, Private Kreng of the Initiative, Private Borisov of the Initiative 

**Forenote:** At 1000 hours RPC-558 inexplicably attacked with multiple groups of 558-2 instances, in an attempt to breach the fencing surrounding RPC-558. Due to the strength and amount of fencing, all on-site ASF personnel were able to take positions around RPC-558 and open fire at some instances before the fencing was fully breached. While defence began, Strike team Sigma (housed in the workers complex), was ordered to find an isolated segment of the perimeter and work their way to The Controller for termination. Time was crucial to ensure no 558-2 instances escaped, or production of 558-3 instances was escalated.

> **Closing statements:** Sargent Petrov was questioned on his reckless and needlessly risky behaviour. Petrov cited that the neutralisaiton of the RPC was the only possible choice to prevent an even worse catastrophe, and the rescue of the Researcher was due to a personal code of conduct, recorded quote: “No Comrade left behind”. After consulting the Initiative’s generals and viewing the testimony of Researcher Victor, a joint punishment of demotion was agreed upon, promoting Corporal Lanning to the Sargent rank. The rescued Researcher was able to relay the effect of the Controller’s seat (as seen in the attached document) and is scheduled for a 3 week break then (due to request) reassignment to The Office of Analysis and Ethics for Project GD-6 IE.[[footnote]] Further information is restricted to Analysis and Ethics personnel with clearance level 4 or higher [[/footnote]]

> **Interviewer:** Seymour Goule of the Analysis and Ethics Department.
> 
> **Interviewee:** Researcher Victor Skelj
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Seymour:** You’ve been cleared of any possible memetic properties that may have been inserted during your brief time on the chair. You’re incredibly lucky to be alive, if it wasn’t for Sargent Petrov’s improvisation you would’ve died in the artillery strike. Now tell me, what’s the last thing you remember?
> 
> **Victor:** I remember a lot...I wish I didn’t but almost everything.
> 
> **Seymour:** Let’s start from the beginning, what were you doing there.
> 
> **Victor:** I was interviewing the Controller of RPC-558, when he told me that he intentionally triggered Operation shutdown in an effort to die...It obviously succeeded.
> 
> **Seymour:** So Operation Shutdown occurred and the Sargent terminated the Controller, what happened next?
> 
> **Victor:** I attempted to tell him that the death of the Controller wouldn’t shut down the RPC immediately, but then...the chair ejected the controller. It was surprising to see but, then all these wires that were hanging from the ceiling suddenly grabbed me and threw me in the chair. Once the helmet was on then the...pain started.
> 
> **Seymour:** It physically injured you?
> 
> **Victor:** ...No...It more...Ah its difficult to explain..
> 
> **Seymour:** Take your time. Any information as to what it did can help.
> 
> **Victor:** ...It was like an electric shock. It didn’t hurt, but it sent these...images into my head...Images of these things happening to my family and loved ones...I saw the murder and rape of them.
> 
> **Seymour:** You don’t need to continue if this is getting too much.
> 
> **Victor:** No, I need to explain. it was like I was experiencing a nightmare. The Controller stated in one of his interviews that the chair tortured him in unspeakable ways...The chair showed these things...and used it as a way to get him to do what all controller’s need to do.
> 
> **Seymour:** Control the factory?
> 
> **Victor:** ...Yes I believe so. It was similar to lucid dreaming, I could see the Strike team debating my situation...and that chair was showing me these images. It wanted me to direct the zombies to kill them, I don’t know how I came to that conclusion but, in my head it seemed to make sense...order the Strike team be killed and the images would stop.
> 
> **Seymour:** It gave you a choice?
> 
> **Victor:** ...It’s rather cruel to do something like that, say the horrors you’re seeing will stop only if you kill a few people you barely know. But...I persevered, in an attempt to wait that they’ll run away...But as this part of my mind was trying to persuade the rest of me to give the order, these groups of strangers, instead of running away...they saved me. I lost consciousness right after they removed me from the chair.
> 
> **Seymour:** But how would images hurt a man like yourself?
> 
> **Victor:** But you didn’t see it! You didn’t see the...blood. The...bones cracking, the castration, the evisceration-
> 
> **Seymour:** Alright, you can stop there.
> 
> **Victor:** -The screams the bile the...the tearing of flesh and t-then the hooks-s...
> 
> **Seymour:** Doctor I think-
> 
> **Victor:** -A-And there was...nothing left except b-bloody pulps...
> 
> **[Brief Silence]**
> 
> **Seymour:** ...I see. I think you deserve a weeks break.
> 
> **Victor:** I...think you might be right. D-Do you need anything else from me?
> 
> **Seymour:** Only a request. Due to this experience you had, and due to the scarcity of having first hand experience and surviving, I believe we could use your expertise alongside a few others to enhance interrogation methods to a less...lethal.
> 
> **Victor:** Well this is...a surprising request. I’m somewhat...I’ll see how I feel after my 3 week break.
> 
> **Seymour:** ...3 Weeks?
> 
> **Victor:** Let me be clear. I’m supposed to be dead if it were not for Sargent Petrov and his team. This thought has been up in my mind for...ever since I woke up. I’ll need a substantial amount of time to be back in a proper mental condition.
> 
> **Seymour:** ...Alright. I’ll try and push this through.
> 
> **Victor:** Thank you. Just one question...Did they ever retrieve the Controller’s body from the wreckage?
> 
> **Seymour:** I wouldn’t know, but I believe they wouldn’t need to.
> 
> **Victor:** Right, right, just wondering.
> 
> **<End Log>**
