+ A Note from the Author

Hello, my name is Blake Pierson as every author and writer knows or refers me to in the RPC Community. It's not actually my name; it's more so a pseudonym that I'm more comfortable to be identified or addressed. Of course, others tend to refer me as Dr. Pierson-- //not a doctor of course.//

Currently a college student, I started writing for the RPC Authority on July 1, 2018. Since then, my works exclusively expanded outside as a writer, from artistry to video eding, and became a Site Moderator for a short period. In all honesty, I felt like I made several impacts in the community by helping those who are new to the RPC Community, and contributed to the expanding RPC Universe. //Perhaps, who knows.// 

Below further this page, you'll find additional information about my works on the site, and a link to my Youtube Channel where I posted 'Lore Explained' type of videos. Until then, see you around.

//Mhm,//

- Dr. Pierson
