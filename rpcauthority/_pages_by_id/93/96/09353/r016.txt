----

Every few months, a new challenge prompt is posted. This prompt could be a theme or a writing restriction. At the conclusion of the challenge, the highest rated submission will win a featured spot on the front page and a new prompt will be posted. Winners do not receive the contest-winner badge. They instead receive the challenge-winner badge.

To enter, post an article that works under the current prompt and send a Wikidot PM to [[*user vizlox]] with your article link.

Only entries that reach at least 8 votes before the challenge ends are considered for winner. It is recommended not to post your article near the end of a challenge so that it has time to accrue votes.

++ Rules

* Anyone is allowed to participate
* Do not coldpost. You must receive feedback in the drafts forum
* Submissions must match the theme in a justifiable way
* Pre-existing drafts are allowed, so long as they have never been posted to the wiki
* Edits to works after submitting are allowed
* Malicious or manipulative voting is strictly prohibited

++ FAQ

**Q:** Am I restricted to only one entry per challenge?
**A:** No, you may enter multiple times if you wish.

**Q:** Can I vote on my own entry?
**A:** Yes, but it wouldn't matter. Author votes are discounted when deciding the winner.

**Q:** Can I enter an article into both the challenge and another contest or event?
**A:** Yes, but not articles in the contest category (their URL begins with "contest:"). They use the Upvote/Downvote rating system which is not compatible.

**Q:** Do I have to submit an RPC?
**A:** You may submit any kind of article (tales, acquired media, etc.), as long as it is able to follow the prompt.
