**Registered Phenomena Code:** 989

**Object Class:** Beta-Yellow

**Hazard Types:** Organic Hazard, Toxic-Hazard, Memory Alteration Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-989 is to be contained at Site-029 within an airtight, reinforced steel cell measuring 6 m x 6 m x 6 m. Due to the perpetual risk of RPC-989's airborne toxins, the designated cell must undergo mandatory weekly inspections to ensure the absence of cracks or wear along concrete and steel material. Should any breaks be found, site 29 is to be placed on immediate lockdown until RPC-989 is transported into a backup cell. Hazmat protective gear, including breathing masks and goggles, is mandatory for all staff assigned to Site-029. A series of 24-hour surveillance feeds are to be installed within RPC-989's cell and security staff is to report any discrepancies in RPC-989's behavior to ██████. As of ██/██/20██, all tests involving the usage of RPC-989's blood are hereby suspended until further notice.

**Description:** RPC-989 is a humanoid specimen standing at 2.5 meters in height. Subject shows signs of severe emaciation with visible symptoms of muscular dystrophy present. RPC-989 lacks any discernible facial features, with the majority of its face being comprised of several layers of wrinkled flesh. Visible sections of the subject's skin are devoid of pigmentation, with metallic scale-like growths covering the entirety of the subject's limbs. A pair of chitinous stumps, measuring 1 meter in length each, are located at each side of RPC-989's shoulder blades. Protrusions are reminiscent of avian appendages though they appear to have been surgically amputated by unknown means. Due to the fact that the subject is able to move these protrusions through vestigial muscles on its backside, it is theorized that RPC-989 was once capable of flight.

RPC-989 does not react to any form of physical stimulation and further cognitive analysis has revealed that the subject remains in an indefinite vegetative state. Despite its inactive condition, RPC-989's body continues to function as expected though it does not appear to require sustenance. RPC-989's body also produces an airborne toxin which it releases through dermal pores in a manner reminiscent to sweat. The toxin has been shown to cause a variety of symptoms when inhaled repeatedly, including nausea, vomiting, memory loss, and ████. It is not yet known why the subject releases this miasma but it is theorized to be a defense mechanism.[[footnote]] Toxin is only released into the air when any living being approaches within 1.8 meters of RPC-989 [[/footnote]] 

Despite the averse effects of RPC-989's airborne toxin, consequential analysis has found evidence that the specimen's blood, hereby referred to as RPC-989-2, can provide a myriad of beneficial effects on those with severe medical ailments. Please see Addendum 1 for a documented archive of tests involving RPC-989-2.

[[collapsible show="+ Interview with Agent █████, 02/26/20██ -- INPUT CREDENTIALS" hide="- Close Interview Log"]]

> **Interviewed:** Agent █████
> 
> **Interviewer:** Dr. Helms
> 
> **Foreword:** Interview with Agent █████ following the initial retrieval of RPC-989
> 
> **<Begin Log, 0300 Hours>**
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** Start from the beginning. And please, be as detailed as possible. 
> 
> **Agent █████:** You saw the payload we brought back. You saw that... [Pauses] That thing. They called it an angel, don't look like any angel I've ever seen though. 
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** "They?" Who is "They?"
> 
> **Agent █████:** That goody-two-shoes organization. The one I was sent to infiltrate. Miracle Inc? My mind is still fuzzy from all the bullshit.
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** Do you mean Miracle Labs Inc?
> 
> **Agent █████:** Yeah, them. You see, our original mission was to investigate rumors of a so-called perfect medicine that was being distributed around the slums of ████████ in El Salvador. You saw the data we collected. The blind could see. The crippled could walk. Hell, some locals even told me that dead people were being brought back to life. It was like Jesus was wandering around town working his magic. Straight up biblical shit. 
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** And the source of this perfect cure was RPC-989?
> 
> **Agent █████:** Yup. Though, I wouldn't exactly call it a perfect cure. [Scoffs] Sure it worked wonders for some people, curing cancer and all that... but for every person it helped, three got fucked over. 
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** Tell me about the negative side effects of the cure. What did you see?
> 
> **Agent █████:** People were... [Coughs] transformed. Their bodies twisted and warped into all kinds of scary shit, I'm talking Lovecraft levels of shit. They hardly looked human by the time it was all over.
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** Was Miracle Labs Inc. aware of the effects their medicine was having on some people?
> 
> **Agent █████:** Yes. In fact, they believed that the negative side effects were a good thing. According to the guy in charge of the operation, those who were transformed were simply being punished by God for their lack of faith. According to him, God was changing them into a form that reflected their sinful nature.
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** What happened to those individuals transformed by the medicine?
> 
> **Agent █████:** They kept me out of the inner circle but from what I gathered, anyone who experienced negative side effects was detained and tossed into a giant furnace thing in the basement of the church. Absolution by fire, they called it. Turned to ash.
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** How did you learn of the existence of RPC-989?
> 
> **Agent █████:** Well, I managed to infiltrate this Miracle Labs group by posing as a missionary for a Christian charity. Took a while to gain their trust. Their operation was based inside a newly built church, had security goons stationed all over the place. Invited the locals in twice a week for a sermon, singing and worship and all that. Handed out free food and clothing too. Eventually, the head honcho, Pastor Mills, asked to meet with me directly and invited me to be a part of the operation.
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** Did this person reveal to you the existence of RPC-989?
> 
> **Agent █████:** Yes. He gave me a tour of the inner workings of the church. Took me into an elevator that went down beneath the building. That thing. The angel. It was bound by chains and kept inside a huge prison cell underneath the church. It didn't even move or make any sounds, kinda' just sat there all slumped. Had to wear gas masks the whole time too.
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** How did they manufacture the medicine?
> 
> **Agent █████:** They drained blood from the angel's body and then sent it to a lab upstairs. Not sure if they diluted it with anything though. The final product was always administered through syringes.
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** You could've moved up the chain of command and obtained much more intel in regards to Miracle Labs Inc. We know very little about this organization and additional information would've served us greatly. Why did you abandon your mission? Why did you decide to suddenly call in the Authority to retrieve RPC-989?
> 
> **Agent █████:** Those bastards were planning something big. Something called a 'Bloodletting' miracle. A new method to treat the entire local population at once. They were gonna contaminate the water supply with that thing's blood. Can you imagine the level of fuckery that would've resulted? It wouldn't just be thousands of people consuming the water. Animals and plants would too. Can you imagine that? Pigs and trees growing bigger and sprouting tentacles and shit? I think I did the right thing by breaking my cover and blowing that bastard's brains out. I'm glad I called in the task force. I'll happily take my punishment.
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** There will be repercussions, yes. Though at this time we are still investigating the incident before decisions are finalized.
> 
> **Agent █████:** Well, I suggest you finalize your decision soon. While you're sitting on your asses in here, those bastards have other operations scattered across the world. That pastor told me all about it. Artifacts that can control the weather and create natural disasters. Old books that can summon things from beyond the stars. Do your job, doc!
> 
> **Dr. Helms:** Dutifully noted, Agent █████. Thank you for your time. 
> 
> **<End Log, 0450 hours>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** Per Agent █████'s testimony, it appears that Miracle Labs Inc. poses enough of a global threat to merit further investigation by the Authority. I hereby request that Miracle Labs Inc. be placed on The Authority's watch list as a potential T-02 level entity.
