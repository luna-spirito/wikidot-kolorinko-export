**Registered Phenomena Code:** 974

**Object Class:** Omega-Purple

**Hazard Types:** Destabilization Hazard, Emotional Hazard, Mind-Regression Hazard, Ideological Hazard, Sensory Hazard, Visual Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** Due to its nature, RPC-974 cannot be contained. In order to counteract the effects of RPC-974, [http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/authority-security-force ASF] Rapid Response Team "Social Club" was formed. Romeo-84's main responsibilities consist of identifying and liquidating both RPC-974-1 and RPC-974-2 if they are present in an urban environment. In order to sense RPC-974-2 instances Romeo-84 members is equipped with special drugs called ''viderics''[[footnote]][/viderics-guide Viderics Guide][[/footnote]].

The Authority has created a number of self-supporting organizations whose activities are aimed at integrating people into social interaction[[footnote]]Additional information is available in the D:974C:K2:CL4R documentation.[[/footnote]].

Captured instances of RPC-974-1 and RPC-974-2 must be kept in a low ACR-resistant research biocells of zone ██, located at a distance of 8 meters from each other, and and kept under constant monitoring.

**Description:** RPC-974 is the designation for an anomalous phenomenon affecting people from 8 years of age around the world, mainly in the developed countries of Europe and Asia.
It is unknown in what way people initially become affected by RPC-974, however, due to the analysis of statistical information, RPC-974 risk factors were identified, including:

* Social exclusion
* Lack of social skills
* No children or living relatives
* Apathy, depression symptoms
* Psychological diseases such as: Dementia, schizophrenia, paranoia, OCD
* Long stay in one place
* Old age

Individuals affected by RPC-974 are hereby designated as RPC-974-1.
RPC-974-1 is effected by RPC-974 in the following way.

[[collapsible show="  Open Interview Log 974-1:13" hide=" Close Interview Log 974-1:13"]]
> **Interviewed:** RPC-974-1, known under the name Y███ T████. Subject is on 3 stage of RPC-974.
> 
> **Interviewer:** Doctor V████ T█████
> 
> **<BEGIN LOG>**
> 
> **Doctor V████:** Hello, Y███. I was instructed to conduct a survey with you. How do you feel?
> 
> **Y███ T████:** Hi, Doctor. I'm a little depressed in connection with my current situation, but otherwise I feel fine. Although... I've been sleeping badly.
> 
> **Doctor V████:** Please, continue.
> 
> **Y███ T████:** I mean, I sleep even little more than what's recommended, but I'm tormented by a repeating dream every night. It is very difficult to describe, but I will try to formulate it. //(Pause in a 6 seconds)// In this dream, I feel a limitless empty space, with only one object, located at an very far distance from my position. This object begins to expand, overgrown with an incredible pattern, and the larger it becomes, the faster it expands. Infinite expansion in infinite space. Strange, but that really horrifies me, doctor. Immediately after waking up, I try to open my eyes to take off this delusion and... calm down, or something.
> 
> **Doctor V████:** Thanks, Y███. Previous respondents provided similar information, but no one described the phenomenon in such detail.
> 
> **Y███ T████:** You know, sometimes it seems to me that I see this rubbish when I close my eyes too. It seems that it doesn't want to leave my mind. It resumes the expansion process... I know it sounds like nonsense, but I have to share these thoughts.
> 
> **Doctor V████:** At this point, I think the interview can be considered completed. Goodbye, Y███.
> 
> **Afterword:** After this interview, Y███ T████ was liquidated according to the containment protocols of RPC-974.

[[collapsible show=" Addendum 1: RPC-974-2. Clearance level 3 is required." hide=" Encryption key accepted."]]
The pattern covering the body of the RPC-974-2 has anti-memetic properties, making the RPC-974-2 undetectable without use of viderics or other special means. Around RPC-974-2, a low-ACS[[footnote]][/acs-guide A Word on the Anderson Coherency Scale][[/footnote]] field with a diameter of 4 meters begins to spread, starting with a value in SD 2.98 ACS at a distance of one meter from RPC-974-2 and ending with a value in 3.89 ACS at the edge of the field. A person who has fallen asleep within the boundaries of this field has a chance to be affected by RPC-974, increasing by █% for each risk factor observed in a person according to statistical information. RPC-974-2 instances exhibit abnormal immunity to low ACS, while maintaining stability within the field they generate.
[[/collapsible]]
-----
[[collapsible show=" Addendum 2: Clearance level 4 is required." hide=" Encryption key accepted."]]
During experiments with instances of RPC-974-2, it was found that the fields emanating from them are able to merge with each other, expanding their boundaries and amplifying in effect. Thus, 2 individuals of RPC-974-2 located one meter apart generate a field with a diameter of 7 meters, with an ACS value of 2.26 at the epicenter. Theoretically, the merging of the fields emanating from 4 instances of RPC-974-2 can provoke the emergence in our reality of a stable space with an ACS value of 1 or lower, which will entail [DATA EXPUNGED]. Any subsequent experiments of this kind are restricted by order of The Board.
