**Registered Phenomena Code:** 052

**Object Class:** Alpha-Orange

**Hazard Types:** Auditory Hazard, Gravitational Hazard, Mind-Control Hazard, Extra-Dimensional Hazard, Geological Hazard, Climatological Hazard.

**Containment Protocols:** Due to the proximity of RPC-052 to the local population, a 50x50 meter perimeter has been designated around RPC-052 for continuous research and secure isolation from public interference. Under the direction of Site-038, OL-Site-052 was assigned to act as the local jurisdiction with regards to the maintained care and oversight of RPC-052.

Though the area adjacent to OL-Site-052 remains open to the public, access to RPC-052 and its vicinity is prohibited. All visitations within the national park are to be supervised by Authority staff to ensure the safety of visitors. Any pathways leading to RPC-052 are to be blocked off in association with a standard disinformation campaign, several signs have been placed around the enclosure of RPC-052 to prohibit civilians from traveling into the designated regions.[[footnote]] The current cover story being that RPC-052 is a protected zone for several endangered species found within the local area under the imposition of heavy fines and jail time for illegal trespassing.[[/footnote]] 

MST Delta-4 "Garcas" has been assigned to RPC-052 and is directed to monitor the area on a weekly basis disguised as tourists or native civilians. Reporting for any deviant activities that may relate to the anomaly or trespassers.

When an RPC-052-1 instance is active, Authority personnel must immediately call upon reinforcements of any ASF available at the time. Any attempts at confrontation must be done out of the bounds of civilian sight. For that reason, RPC-052-1 instances must be transported to the periphery of the city, while heavily sedated. Delivery of sedation should be exclusive to long-range tranquilizer firearms with a gas operating propellant system.

In the event of containment failure, whether due to obstruction of transport or other third-party intervention, Delta-4 are instructed to use CS gas grenades, paint-ball tear gas ammunition, and long-range pepper spray to quickly subdue any and all threats. However if RPC-052-1 instances are confirmed to have entered its third stage, containment is considered impossible. In this stage, paramilitary/lethal force is to be permitted to all on-duty MST/ASF personnel, including the usage of DEW-based weaponry, Dazzler-type armament, type 5-XV gas canisters, and high capacity electric artillery.[[footnote]] All weapons watt-usage must range from 100 milliamps to 250 milliamps for certain extermination.[[/footnote]]


**Description:** RPC-052 is an Igneous type boulder located in ████ National Park, Thailand, measuring approximately 137m³ in volume. RPC-052 shows signs of moderate discoloration, likely due to eroding from exposure to acid rain. Radiocarbon dating shows that RPC-052 is approximately 284 years old. 

Upon initial discovery, RPC-052 displayed no anomalous effects in its original state. However, RPC-052 has shown to possess gravity-altering and physics-distorting properties. If any attempts to harm RPC-052 or the surrounding area[[footnote]] About 37m in radius.[[/footnote]] is made, "Force-altering" effects will be exerted onto the active threat. Under this effect, the aforementioned threats are unable to produce more than 5.2MPa of pressure. [[footnote]]The tensile strength of rocks is approximately 18Mpa-66MPa. [[/footnote]] If any sapient organism attempts to directly inflict damage onto RPC-052 or its surrounding perimeter at least three times within a span of one hour, RPC-052 will activate its first anomalous effect onto the aforementioned subject. Said subject will be designated as an RPC-052-1 instance.

RPC-052-1 instances are the sapient organisms affected by the anomalous effects of RPC-052. RPC-052-1 will obtain the ability of gravity alteration and the modification of other features of physics through unknown, anomalous methods. It's to be noted that RPC-052-1 will not retain --any-- some of its past memories. Instead, it will be mind-controlled under the influence of RPC-052 to carry out its tasks.[[footnote]]Mainly to protect RPC-052 and guard the surrounding area.[[/footnote]] RPC-052-1 abilities appear to strengthen over the span of a few hours, with RPC-052-1 gaining the capability of bending the relative trajectory of objects around them, such as changing the vectorial speed of those objects in relation to themselves. This effect appears similar to telekinesis to observers. [[footnote]]It could be considered as a form of gravity-kinesis. [[/footnote]] 


Within the first three hours of exposure using the aforementioned abilities, instances of RPC-052-1 will be capable of lifting up to a maximum weight of 1.65 kg and exert a force of 20 N on areas up to 4 m. Furthermore, RPC-052-1 instances will develop a type of force-field that encapsulates their bodies and raises their pressure tolerance to up to 5,000 psi.[[footnote]]Those include direct hits from physical objects. [[/footnote]] By the 3rd hour, the second stage of development will occur to RPC-052-1 instances. RPC-052-1 will be able to elevate objects up to 46 kg, exert a maximum force of 500 N and withstand a maximum force of 25,000 psi. After 7 hours, instances of RPC-052-1 will enter their third and final stage of development. RPC-052-1 will be capable of lifting objects up to 459 kg, exert a force of up to 4840 N, and withstand forces of approximately 95,000 PSI. [[footnote]]It is to be noted, the second stage of RPC-052-1 has an almost white iris, and the last stage appears to have no iris at all, with a complete white coloration that matches the sclera.[[/footnote]]

Additionally, RPC-052 possesses a third anomalous effect that is activated under specific circumstances. [[footnote]]The successful result of the ritual was denominated as RPC-052-A. [[/footnote]] Through recent research and testing, the Authority was able to identify at least 3 individual conditions sufficient to activate the anomalous event activation. The following are verified by Authority personnel to be necessary for its activation.

* RPC-052 must be in close proximity to a performance of 1900s western music. Said music must be played using instruments related to that time period. The music should start in a pitch of 13 dBA and slowly raises up to 70 dBA, and be played on a constantly changing frequency of 20-137 Hz.

* An instance of RPC-052-1 must be physically present within the area of effect RPC-052 exerts and, regardless of their status, laying on the ground.

* Through recent documented and recorded experimentation of RPC-052, the final condition of the third anomaly was discovered to be the near-future arrival of a world-ending event.[[footnote]] For more information, see Addendum 3 [[/footnote]]

> When the conditions are met for the ritual and the execution of said ritual is successful, the surrounding area within RPC-052’s perimeter begins to slowly ‘dissipate’ from material reality. When that happens, all objects will become brighter and start losing their current material properties, dissipating at the same rate as the amount of pigmentation and color on them. Following this, the affected area perimeter will increase to approximately 13m/s. This state of existence is identified as the “Gateway.” These effects will begin to spread further until it breaches the atmosphere and the Ozone layer.
> 
> The dimension designated as RPC-052-A can be described as having a white, colorless bright background. It appears to have no visible space limit. A main font of light is described to illuminate the space at the center of the firmament. The source appears to not be directly visible, yet it constantly produces luminescence. RPC-052-A also contains a great number of trees with a variety of fruits. They appear to cover the ground by at least 37 meters outside of the center. However, it is to be noted the aforementioned were all destroyed and burned beyond recognition. Research through the few remains of the plant cells indicates that none of the said trees or their offsprings are found on Earth's ecosystem. 
> 
> The center point is located on what was presumed to be the main focal point of the previous occupying civilization, of which provides evidence of previous life forms taking residence in RPC-052-A. The location is currently in complete ruins, with most architecture being in an unstable state and/or debilitated. There was no confirmation of organic life in the city or the surroundings beyond the aforementioned charred flora.
> 
> It is to be noted on the remaining surfaces, mostly on walls, there is a red dry liquid that has been identified to be a mixture of blood, feces, sweat, saliva, semen, mucus, urine, and other bodily fluids. RPC expeditionary personnel also discovered remnants of internal organ cells in a liquid state as well[[footnote]]Examples being, brain matter, intestinal tracts, entrails, etc. [[/footnote]]. It is uncertain how these parts were liquefied in such a manner, although it is theorized to be through anomalous methods. 
> 
> Additionally, RPC-052-A has a fountain in the center of the broken urban complex. What was previously speculated to be a water supply, is now filled with a mixture of the previously mentioned red liquid which appears to be undrinkable, and most assumed to be detrimental. 
>

**Discovery:** The first instance of RPC-052 anomalous effects were reported to be on the nearby Thai cities of ████ and █████ about a supposed individual able to alter "gravity" on their surrounding area. Personnel was quickly deployed to the area to prevent any more accidents and test for other anomalous effects that may appear from RPC-052. The subject was brought into Authority custody --and is currently being tested and Interview for extra information.-- after amnestics, the subject was freed. Through investigations of cameras of older dates, it has lead to the assumption that it was activated recently. Analysis of the park cameras pointed out that the subject was a "rockhound"[[footnote]]Term used usually for those who collect stones as a hobby.[[/footnote]] trying to acquire a sample from the rock, which would later be designated as RPC-052. After failing to take samples, the subject assaulted the stone, activating RPC-052's first anomalous effect. The subject was the first recorded RPC-052-1 instance.

After the incident, OL-Site-052 was created on the 07th of May of 201█.

##red|The following is the recovery text of the diary found by the exploration of the pocket dimension RPC-052-A##
--------------------------------------
> **Chapter ##red|__1__##: Into the White room.**
> 
> I suppose it is a man's job to communicate his experiences when entering into the unknown, keeping the flame of humanity ablaze. However, what flame is there to keep, when humanity itself is gone? My name is Richard Wellington, and I am the last remnant of humanity.
>
> I was never into diaries; some may say I was the opposite of a supposed writer. I loved to talk. In fact, I did it every morning with my assistant. Discussions without depths end, quite pleasing routines one could have, especially while your job was as dark as mine.
> 
> In my job, things could become quite grim from time to time, so having a light to spark once in every moonshine, is, I would say, quite calming to people. Someone to remove the weight off your shoulders.
> 
> That makes me think, the only person I didn't see as an object of study, but as a friend (a close one) was my dear assistant Margaret. Oh, what a bright light she was. So full of positivity and good comments for all the right times, she kept the patients unanxious and calm previous to my visits. I couldn't say I was the most calming figure, the opposite might be the truth, but with Margaret at my side, no man or woman would have a nervous heart.
> 
> And thinking about her, thinking about them… It just hurts thy soul more, as oneself could see the past and maybe even understand it. But to not feel a certain way for it, it is surely close to impossible.
> 
>The worst part is, the more I think about it, the harder it gets to accept that…
> 
> //They are all gone.//
> 
> As a psychologist, having no one but myself… is…
> 
> It might be the most ironic joke god has ever laid upon me… the dreams of a broken man.
--------------------------------------
> **Chapter 2: Adapting to your surroundings.**
> 
> The more I think of the past, the more I start to think of a future. I might be alone throughout my entire life, but by no means I will stay laying in this cold, harsh ground.
> 
> I shall eat of thy fruits, given from the trees surrounding the central lake, and shall drink of thy water, and build for myself a home. One home that I could never have in my world. No, not since the beast came.
> 
> It stood as tall as the skies could see. A creature so big, thou feet could cover entire parts of our city. I can still remember the day of the escape; London was on fire that day, as the London beast attacked. I could hear all the sirens as the beast stood tall, walking through the city and bridges like it was nothing. As our entire city crumbled at its feet, all I could do was stare at the fiery beast, as it's black flames did not burn, but crushed, devoured and assimilated.
> 
> I could remember as Margaret pushed me away, as her fragile body was pulverized by the mere size of thay colossal creature trotter.
> 
> Completely devastated by her death, I remembered how nothing else mattered to me. Enraged, but without anything to do, all I could do was lament her loss. And blame myself.
> 
> As I sobered to the loss of the only friend I ever had, I smashed the earth with my fists, screaming into the sky before ripping apart the ground. I just started digging a hole with my bare hands, completely insane. The insanity I studied for years now has finally struck my mind.
> 
> As I dug that hole, my own grave, I laid there in that dark, stormy night, waiting for the creature to reach me so he could bring me to the place it brought her. But he never did. I was but a mere ant to the creature, too pathetic to even be noticed of such extreme pain. So I decided to give up. And give in.
> As I closed my eyes, I could feel my body dissipating away. When I woke up, I was there, in the white room… Alone... free?
> 
> I stayed here for a long time, About 4 months, 8 weeks, and 3 hours. I know most of the mechanisms of this place, the presumably anti-aging magical waters of the lake, the enchanting fruits of the forest, The trees that keep coming back no matter how many times you bring them down. Sounds of night and day, announcements of a tomorrow. If I'm crazy, in my homeland. I am but a child in this world.
> 
> The only thing I could scientifically prove, was that I was actually, utterly alone.
--------------------------------------
> **Chapter 3: The arrival of the __##red|three__##-folk**
> 
> I hear noises. They are noises from neither the night nor the day, they aren't from the sky or the light, but from interaction. My body fills with excitement as I hear noises. 
> 
> I should feel scared, but interaction with literally any form of being has been lost for so long, I can only imagine how one would be. I go to the supposed source of the sounds.
> 
> And there, inside the woods, is where I found them, Hundreds of people, all so confused, so lost. I smiled, as wide as the face could be, I might have broken my face in the process of feelings that I haven't felt in a long time. In a run, I embraced the people in a long hug, they reciprocated, and as the time of night came upon us, the light became less bright. The sounds came, scaring them.
> 
> I tell them to follow me, as I brought the new Populi to their new home, As we sit around the lake, I brought their information of the world they live, the rules and the systems that run it, they listen to me, curious about this strange salvation, as one of their youngest ones come to me, kneeling, searching for any hope that he could acquire.
> 
> He tells me about the occurrence that brought his people to their current state, a ritual, made by those they call "The deep ones." A group of fishermen that always came to their surroundings weekly for fishing on their waters was weird for being a city with only one type of fish. One day, a creature roamed the streets, and the weird fisherman boats came in, invading, looking for the creature, telling the population the creature was a hibernating bear, and not approaching it.
> 
> However, as they got invaded, the creature appeared to disappear, and when we realized it was too late, the entire three interconnected cities were in complete darkness, and the creature started hacking away at the bodies, the only remains of the city, were of stone, and a few hundred scarred man. Those who stand with me. Those that my eyes see.
> 
> It was then that I realized one thing, the stone was not a prison, but instead, an option for salvation, for all humanity, and, this way, the new humanity would be made from the ground up, from the ashes.
> 
> I stood on top of my wooden house, and as I screamed words of inspiration, I told them to follow me as I guided humanity from the age of darkness to the age of light.
> 
> And as I did, by destiny, or extreme luck, the day came, as the lights came around.
> 
> They declared me King, and together, we built a kingdom, the new remains of humanity.
--------------------------------------
> **Chapter 4: The development of conflict.**
> 
> Like any relationship, a society will come into a point where it will have many great conflicts, and there we were, coming to the brink of war. It is truly hard to manage a civilization when it starts to become so divided. It's like keeping a broken glass together by putting the pieces back, you might do it with enough time, but the cracks will still appear, previous conflicts and recent ones have led to unbearable pressure.
> 
> For this and other reasons, recreational activities are at its highest. Projects of betting, creation of games, and generation of currency through our 7 fruits, has been generating a highly competitive city. The energy is certainly there; however, the direction is coming to a concerning path. I could not stop it, no one can stop development, and with pressures being this high, I can only attempt to orient them in the right way.
> 
> However, I fear I am only one against the will of many. I recently created a council of the civilians. They were the ones with the most time spent in the Library, who were also the ones who recently contributed most to the development of the books inside, helping me with decisions while also offering a voice to the people.
> 
> The council, which is on its third generation, is currently in quite a conflict. With the conflict of generations, It is becoming quite hard to maintain a stable relationship.
> 
> I fear I will soon have to enact some sort of control over the currency being created, I could not let this get out of control, as the blame would be put on me, I do need some sort of guidance, even though I was the one who offered them the option of guidance. 
> 
> The country is about to split, and I am currently trying to keep them all together.
> 
> Help is needed, and it is needed immediately.
--------------------------------------
> **Chapter 5: A solution to our pollution**
> 
> __##red|Seven##__  wise men came in today, they fell from the sky, six bodies, and one man with broken bones.
> 
> His screams were loud, as loud as one could be. All the city came to see the man. Unlike all the others, who I had invited to the town, he invited himself, stumbling into the city while we all watched from afar.
> 
> His name was █████ ████████ ███████, he was a young boy about 23 years old, and he was the youngest member of our society.
> 
> The boy got healed quickly with the medical treatment developed in our country. However, when he told us about the incident that led him to our world, he started crying. Quite the sensible person, and quite the religious one too, he preached about something that we forgot for a long time, religion. Something along the lines of Christianity and purity, now rules, but forms of changing our lives for the better. He, the addition that I wished, the help that was necessary to our population: The only thing that was remaining for our completion.
> 
> Purpose.
> 
> He talked about the Seven priests, and the religion they follow, those seven priests, wise men that commanded a church inspired by those of the commands of Christianity. And based around the number 7, the 7 Archangels, bringers of justice, purity, all that good thing. And about two weeks in, he already was teaching most of our population, while we constructed him a great church, combining it with the great Library, one place for religious and spiritual search, and knowledge research.
> 
> Life was good, and things were looking bright for the first time in years.
--------------------------------------
> **Chapter 6:** Death
> 
> Destruction, he blamed us for temptation, then he brought the death of an entire nation.
> 
> His mask fooled us all, its crocodile tears stood tall, we gave him our trust and gave death in return.
> 
> I am currently hiding… from this world of lies
> 
> But I was already struck, and it won't be long until I die.
> 
> Our ages might be unlimited, and our harm might be reduced, but… I can promise you, boy.
> 
> Our mortalities are but the same.
> 
> He called us impure.
> 
> As it killed us all.
> 
> His mercy, oh, so small.
> 
> It screamed, he smiled? It painted the wall with blood. A red flood.
> 
> The hope is no longer there, I wish it was here,
> 
> What a low blow, because our trust and hope were so high.
> 
> Is now nonexistent in this dark night.
> 
> I pity you, child of the light, a creature of darkness.
> 
> For you might never feel as we feel, see as we see.
> 
> We may be created by sin, but you are created by the judgment of thee
> 
> By the mistakes we learn, we grow.
> 
> By making mistakes, you kill and become shallow.
> 
> Humanity may not end, by you, we learned the earth is still alive and well
> 
> As the creature that brought our end is standing on his self-created hell.
> 
> We shall survive to see another day.
> 
> You will wait in chains to see our flame from far away.
> 
> Strained on these chains… Forever.
--------------------------------------
