**Registered Phenomena Code:** 314

**Object Class:** Gamma-Purple (Utility)

[[include component:hazards temporal=true|sapient=true]]

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-314 is to be contained in isolation in the underground facility built  ██ km northwest of Site-███ (designated as Site-███-A "Exolvuntur"). Its containment bunker must be fitted with all items the site manager deems necessary for proper humanoid containment, including all sensors and surveillance elements. The presence of any elements that may represent a danger to RPC-314's life and physical integrity are strictly prohibited, this includes any kind of lethal ammunition, as such operational ASF personnel are to be equipped with non-lethal rounds.

**Description:** RPC-314 is ████ ███████, an American male of Egyptian descent. It has symptoms of Post Traumatic Stress Disorder and Depression. RPC-314 claims to have the anomalous property of sending its consciousness to the moment of its birth upon death, causing its life to effectively loop. It has corroborated this claim by providing knowledge that could only be obtained through such means, and has advised authority personnel on how to avoid containment breaches on past occasions. RPC-314 claims to be sent from the Remnants' timeline, in order to secure this timeline for refugees, as it was told by Authority personnel from the Remnants' timeline to consider it a lost cause. 

**Discovery:** On ██/██/19██, Authority surveillance satellites detected a spike in low Anderson Coherency Scale activity in an uninhabited part of the Great Basin Desert, North America. Authority personnel were dispatched, and located RPC-314 amid some heavily damaged and seemingly improvisational machinery. RPC-314 was a child at this time, though due to the nature of RPC-314 it displayed behavior more expected of adults, and requested to be treated as such. RPC-314 was very cooperative with RPC personnel, though it declined to inform any of them of its status until it had contact with Level 3 personnel. A log of initial contact with RPC-314 can be found below. Once brought to the nearest site, and examined, it was interviewed by Dr. Vergara, and the interview log can be seen below.

[[collapsible show="Open Initial Contact Log" hide="Close Initial Contact Log" hideLocation="both"]]
> **Dispatched Agents:** Agent Charon and Agent Peterson
> 
> **Foreword:** After remote examination determined the mobilization of any MSTs was unnecessary, two Level 2 field agents were dispatched to examine the area and locate any anomalies for containment. This log begins from when the two agents had arrived at the site, and made their approach.
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Agent Charon:** Alright, guess that's it then. What do you think it is?
> 
> **Agent Peterson:** I dunno man, maybe some Amazing! Co. shit gone wrong.
> 
> **Agent Charon:** Has anything of theirs ever gone right?
> 
> //[There is a brief gap of silence as the agents make their way closer to the site, and more of it comes into view]//
> 
> **Agent Peterson:** Goddamn, what is all this? Looks like a car crash or some shit.
> 
> **Agent Charon:** Is that a refrigerator? I think this is a bicycle over here, or atleast what's left of one.
> 
> //[The aforementioned refrigerator opens up, and RPC-314 falls out]//
> 
> **Agent Peterson:** Fuck! Wha-
> 
> **Agent Charon:** Sh- I mean wow, it's a, uh, a kid.
> 
> //[RPC-314 stands up, and wipes dust from its eyes, before looking at both of the Agents]//
> 
> **Agent Peterson:** Fuck, you okay bu-
> 
> **Agent Charon:** Watch your mouth man. He's just a kid.
> 
> **RPC-314:** That you, Charon?
> 
> **Agent Charon:** You... what?
> 
> **RPC-314:** Ah, it is you. Listen, please drop the "kid" stuff. If your superiors are alright with it you'll probably get an explanation later, but first off I need to you take me to a Level 3 so I can explain things to them.
> 
> **Agent Peterson:** Wait, how do you know his name?
> 
> **RPC-314:** Unless you've gotten a promotion I don't know about Peterson, I'm not telling you or anyone who isn't atleast Level 3. Sorry.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** Dialogue from this point onward was largely RPC-314 refusing to answer queries. It wasn't until RPC-314 was debriefed by Dr. Vergara that it provided anything more of note. The site itself seemed only to contain heavily damaged machinery of improvised design, and its original structure is still undetermined.

[[collapsible show="Open Interview" hide="Close Interview" hideLocation="both"]]
> **Interviewed:** RPC-314
> 
> **Interviewer:** Dr. Vergara
> 
> **Foreword:** RPC-314 was given a brief examination by medical personnel before being debriefed by Dr. Vergara. While care was taken not to reveal unnecessary information to RPC-314, it was found to be far calmer than usual, and was later described by personnel as being far more relaxed than individuals under Authority care generally are.
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** Hello Mr. ███████.
> 
> **RPC-314:** Hello Dr. Vergara.
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** Ah, so it's true. You know people's names. Well, I am Level 3 personnel, as you requested. Are you willing to speak up now?
> 
> **RPC-314:** Gladly. Ask away.
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** So, first off, why don't you just tell me about yourself. Who are you?
> 
> **RPC-314:** My name is ████ ███████, I was born to a 1st generation Egyptian immigrant couple in Las Vegas. My mother is a housewife, while my father is a chiropractor. 
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** Mhm. Now, how did you get from there, to the middle of the Nevada desert?
> 
> **RPC-314:** Well, It was better to pick somewhere more isolated when making the cross from my home universe.
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** I'm sorry, home universe?
> 
> **RPC-314:** Yes. I'm not from this universe, but an alternate one. Remnants, is what you call it.
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** Hm. So, why did you leave then?
> 
> **RPC-314:** Well, let's just say things there aren't the best. Or, well, they won't be.
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** So, you're a refugee then?
> 
> **RPC-314:** Not exactly, more of a pioneer? Trailblazer? I'm here to prepare things. Or atleast make sure they stay nice and good.
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** Prepare? How so?
> 
> **RPC-314:** Well, a lot of bad things are going to happen.  lot of people are going to die, and the ones that are left are going to be desperate for safety. That's where this universe comes in. Unfortunately, there won't be too many who can make it.
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** You uh, you keep referring to things in the future. As though you know for certain they're going to happen. What makes you so sure that's all going to happen?
> 
> **RPC-314:** Because I've been there. Repeatedly.
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** You went to the future?
> 
> **RPC-314:** No, I lived through it. I grew up there, in that doomed world, and saw everything fall apart repeatedly. Nothing I did, nothing I said, nothing I tried ever changed it. I suppose I should explain what makes me anomalous. It's a bit of a long story, is that alright?
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** Please, go ahead.
> 
> **RPC-314:** You see, whenever I die, no matter how or when, my life restarts. I close my eyes for what should be the last time, and open them again to find my self in the delivery room, surrounded by my parents, doctors, nurses.Everything's the same each time, except for the fact that I have my memories of the life before that one, and the one before that, so on and so forth. At first I wasn't even aware of the danger to my reality, died young you see. But, as I lived longer, I saw it. I saw the great cataclysm that destroyed so much. At first I thought that was the purpose for my being. To prevent the disaster.
> 
> **RPC-314:** So that's what I did. I put my all into fighting it, gathered every piece of knowledge I could, and put it to use. Lifetime after lifetime, failed strategy after failed strategy. None of it ever worked. Not even an eternity was enough to stop it. It didn't matter who I tried to be, what I tried to do, what I tried to say. It all failed in the face of that calamity. Eventually I discovered the Authority, however.
> 
> **RPC-314:** At first I thought this was the missing puzzle piece. The answer to my struggle. That if I simply worked with them, this could be contained. I willingly put myself in front of them, explained who and what I was, and advised them as best I could. At first they were apprehensive of me, but as I learned more of their organization I was able to do more to convince them of my utility. I managed to prevent countless containment breaches. Those first few times I really thought it might work. No. Not even the Authority was enough for me to stop it all. Still, I was willing to keep trying. However many lifetimes it took.
> 
> **RPC-314:** Eventually, though, it all took its toll on me. While I'm of stronger stuff than most, just from centuries of experience, I am still human. There's only so much I can take. It started to show to the Authority. They could see it in me, the countless failures, the hopelessness of the situation. Eventually, the time came when they gave up on even me saving that reality. So they told me of their last resort. Here.
> 
> **RPC-314:** While my world was doomed, there was another that was not. I couldn't save mine, but I could protect this one atleast. Prepare it, and preserve it for the refugees of mine. And so, that's why I'm here, Dr. Vergara. I'm here to prepare the Authority for the refugees it's going to get, and to protect this world from ever suffering a fate like mine. I hope we can do so.
> 
> **Dr. Vergara:** I hope so too. That'll be all for today, though we of course will have more questions for you later after we've thought about what you've said here today.
> 
> **RPC-314:** See you then.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** This is when RPC-314's anomalous properties were first fully understood, and it was determined that it must be contained rather than processed as a non-anomalous human.

**Addendum:** Upon searching police records, there is a missing person's report for a ████ ███████ in Las Vegas. The case was never solved.
