[[f>image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/secure-authority-facilities/SOUTHCOM.png width="224px";]]

**Established:** 9^^th^^ November 1971.

**Front Cover:** Colombian base for various environmental NGOs

**Location:** Amazonas Provinces, Colombia [3° 49' 37.146'' S 70° 2' 41.9388'' W]

**Codename:** "Cabin in the Woods"

**Hex-code:** [1f]

**Site Occupation:** Natural Bio-climatic Research and Containment Facility for anomalous faunas and plantation.

**Division Jurisdiction:** SOUTHCOM [Research and Containment]

**Total Area:** 41,062 m^^2^^

**History:** After numerous appearances of anomalous creatures in the South American continent, especially within the Amazonian rainforest, the Brazilian, Peruvian and Colombian Government and other government's containment and neutralization of the anomalous within the region began to dwindle. A deal was made between the governments and the Authority Resources division in which the Authority would be allowed to covertly operate in designated regions of the Amazonian rainforest, unhindered by the government and local authorities for the continuous monitoring of Possible Anomalous Activity and the research and containment of said anomalies present at the region.

Initially established as "OL-Site-313" along with 6 other On-Location sites all across the Amazon rainforest. With the discovery of [REDACTED] by OL-Site-313, the administrative staff was able to acquire additional funds and a containment facility was constructed to utilize the anomalous capabilities of LA-1██. Upon the completion of Containment Biome Domes-A through -G and the reconstruction of OL-Site-313 as a water treatment facility for CBDs, the site was then officially re-designated as "Site-031" and given jurisdiction of its sister sites and other facilities present in the region[[footnote]]Collectively known as the "Amazon Network".[[/footnote]].

Since the discovery and subsequent containment of RPC-███, negotiations between the Authority and GOI "Descendants of Yggdrasil" has led to a research collaboration between the two groups. Currently, DoY-Affiliate personnel have been integrated into Site-031's research groups.

**Background Information:** Currently, Site-031-1 houses botanical and faunal anomalies that are classified from Alpha to Beta, with a Lethality Rating from White to Orange at Site-031's Natural Reserve. Anomalies classified as Gamma and with Lethality Ratings of Red are either contained at Isolated Containment Units located near the Natural Reserve, or contained at the Separated Containment Wing. Site-031-1 currently does not house any Black-lethality anomalies.

> Update (01/02/2014):
>
> Due to increasing suspicious activities, and increasing numbers of people spotted outside of Site-031 and facilities related or under the jurisdiction of Site-031, as of 1^^st^^ February 2014, an additional 60 Protection Personnel has been added into Mobile Specialised Team-Sierra-15 "The Wild Wranglers", and will be stationed all across Site-031-1. These changes will be noted in the containment protocols of each Registered Phenomena document.
>

-----
[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/site-031/Arnold.jpg|width=300px |caption=Site 031-1 Hydroelectric Power Plant.]]

* **Administrative Facility** - The primary facilities of Site-031-1 located north of the area's hydroelectric power plant. The administrative facility is responsible for the administration and coordination of the Site-031-1 and every other OL-Sites under its jurisdiction.

* **Aquatic Facility** - A facility located south of Site-031-1 and adjacent to Site-031-1's power plant. While the Aquatic Facilities is capable of housing and research of marine anomalies with its subterranean containment sector, the primary function of the Aquatic Facility is the transportation of personnel into other sites and facilities located near the Amazon River.

[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/site-031/climate_innovation_district_houses-solar-avenue-950x633.jpg|width=300px |caption=Site 031-1 Personnel Facility A.]]

* **Personnel Facility** - Located south-east of Site-031-1 is responsible for the housing of personnel such as CSDs, research personnel, and MST personnel, and the distribution of them into other facilities.

* **Research and Containment Facility** - A facility located east of Site-031-1 and is generally responsible for the research and containment of RPCs taken into Site-031-1. Site-031 Natural Reserve is contained within Research and Containement Facility

* **Medical Facility:** Located 33 km from Site-031, the Medical Facility serves as Site-031's major medical treatment facility for patients and injured personnel and anomalies. The facility is also used to store medical supplies, and as a checkpoint for supplies and anomalies' transportation into other sites located within Latin America from Site-008.

+++++ **Incident-S031: The Hastings Incident**

On 17/05/2014 at 23:44, unexpected weather conditions led to the start of a wildfire at OL-Site-357. The resultant generator failure which led to the shutdown of the perimeter’s electric fence effectively caused a breach in containment of RPC-███. Despite its believed passive nature, all of Mobile Specialised Team-Sierra-15 "The Wild Wranglers"'s 357-assigned personnel dispatched to OL-Site-357 in an attempt to re-establish containment fail to respond.

[[f>image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/secure-authority-facilities/WESTCOM.png width="224px";]]

[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/site-031/iceland-1611854_960_720.png|width=300px |caption=Site-031-2, Geothermic Power Plant-A]]

**Established:** 3^^th^^ of August 2016.

**Location:** Southern Region, Iceland [64°18'55.5876''N  20°18'19.7568''W]

**Front Cover:** Haukadalur Valley

**Codename:** "Wonderwall"

**Hex-code:** [1f]

**Site Occupation:** Natural Bioclimatic Containment Facility

**Division Jurisdiction:** WESTCOM[Research and Containment]

**Total Area:** 452,000 m^^2^^.

**History:** After the partial destruction of the former Site-031 on 17th May 2014 caused by the combination of both containment breaches of RPC held within the site and assault from hostile groups --believed to be related to GOI "Sons of Yggdrasil"--of unknown assailants. The subsequent collapse of the "Amazon Network" as a result of the destruction of the administrative site made coordination between facility difficult and Post-Incident Assessment Team of Site-031 deemed it necessary for major relocations of anomalies into neighbouring sites and facilities.

As noted by Site-031-1 Director Harrison Jhoulen[[footnote]]Assigned after the death of Director Gilda Jair.[[/footnote]] and Director Christian Huygens, the usage of Natrual Reserve to place multiple anomalies within with little control over them may have been one of the major cause for the multiple containment breaches during Incident-S031-357, and that per their recommendation, a new site to be constructed to either contain Site-031's faunas or flora portions of anomalies.

After evaluation, Authority Office of Financial Affairs decided to repurpose the proposal for the construction of "OL-Site-355"[[footnote]]A site focused around the creation of simulated environments for the containment of anomalies with specific needs.[[/footnote]] to handle the anomalous floras of Site-031.

**Background Information:** Multiple facilities had been established across the Haukadalur Valley, including Site-031-2's primary containment facility located at Geysir[[footnote]]Geysir, or "The Great Geysir" is the oldest active geyser in Iceland.[[/footnote]]. Site-031-2's facilities harness the innate geothermal activity of the area to act as the primary power source and are distributed between each other.

 Given the GOI "Sons of Yggdrasil"'s known usage of botanic anomalies and their operations throughout northern Europe, Site-031-2 will act as the primary response site for the research and containment of all anomalies related to the GOI and the monitoring of said Group of Interest.

As opposed to Site-031's BCDs, containment of anomalies at Site-031-2 would be reviewed in a case-by-case basis by current Director of Anomaly Containment for the construction of additional containment chambers should it be deemed necessary.

[[=]]
+ **Facilities And Sections**
[[/=]]
-----

Site-031-2 currently consisted of 2 primary facilities, categorized as Personnel Sector and Containment Sector. Each facility is stationed with at least one geothermal power plant or is given directly power from Geothermic Power Plant-A.

[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/site-031/entrance.jpg|width=300px |caption=Site-031-2, Entrance into underground facilities of the Personnel Sector]]

* **Personnel Sector:** The Personnel Sector included facilities for the administrative responsibilities of the site, anomalies research, personnel housing as well as MST stationing and deployment. The Surface portion of Personnel Sector was used as the general personnel housing and can hold a maximum amount of 500 people. Most of the Site-031-2's anomalous research is conducted at Sub-Level 1 of Personnel Sector and is connected to the Containment Sector via a tunnel that is collapsible in the event of a containment breach.

[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/local--files/site-031/glass-wall.jpeg|width=608.5px |align=center |caption=Instances of trees infected with RPC-355-1, contained at Containment Sector Sub-Level 2, Specialized Wing, Containment Unit-L1]]

[[include component:image-enlarge name=-Haukadalur_Valley.jpg|width=280px |caption=Entry point of Containment Sector]]

* **Containment Sector:** This Containment Sector is the containment facility for Site-031-2's anomalies, no surface facility is present at Containment Sector with the entirety of the facilities being built underground, entrance into CS is only available through the elevator shaft. Sub-Level 1 of CS is designed as an armed checkpoint for anomalies transportation into/out of Site-031-2. Sub-Level 2 and Sub-Level 3 handles the containment of Alpha to Beta Class and Gamma Class anomalies or anomalies that require specialized containment units respectively. While Sub-Level 2 consists of only containment units for anomalous floras or containment chambers for sapient anomalies such as [[[RPC-355]]], Sub-Level 3 is designed for anomalies considered to too hazardous to personnel and their containment chamber is equipped with oxidizing chemical agents that can be remotely released upon a containment breach. Sub-Level 3 also houses anomalies that require specific needs or simulated environment.

Containment Sector is to be given access to members of MST-Oscar-35 "Green Health", stationed there to assist containment of other botanical-based anomalies.

In the event of a large-scale containment breach, the elevator shaft will be destroyed via explosive charge placed there, effectively closing the only exit for any anomalies. In the event that an RPC is capable of reaching Sub-Level 1 or the surface, [REDACTED], cover story S312-CB: "Great Eruption" will be issued in the event of civilian inquiries.
