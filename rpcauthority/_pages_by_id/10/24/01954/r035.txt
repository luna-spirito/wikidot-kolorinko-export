[[tab Footage]]
**Footage showing anomalous phenomena can serve as a risk to the normalcy of the world. Such footage is either taken or seized by the Authority and uploaded here for archival and investigative purposes.**

The template can be found **[[[formatting-guide#toc0|here]]]**. 

All footage follows the following naming convention: M-XXXXX. The XXXXX can be comprised of uppercase letters and numbers. [[tab Acquired Photographs]]
**With the advent of home computing and sophisticated imaging software, images displaying anomalous phenomena have become harder to legitimize.**

**All photographs seized by the Authority have been thoroughly screened for validity. Said photos are displayed here along with photographs retrieved from locations of anomalous events.**

Please place information regarding the origin and history of the photograph in the caption below it. Photographs use the following naming convention: P-XXX.
