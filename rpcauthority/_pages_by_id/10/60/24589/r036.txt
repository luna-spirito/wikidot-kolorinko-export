**Object Number:** A-013

**Containment Requirements:** Medium

**Threat Level:** Medium

**Containment Procedures:** A-013 is to be contained within an artificial habitat measuring 25 meters by 25 meters by 5 meters in Site-16's Wildlife Sector.

**Description:** A-013 is a colony of twenty-three beetles belonging to the genus //Brachinus// [[footnote]]Bombardier beetles.[[/footnote]], initially discovered in the Amazon Rainforest. A-013 are genetically identical to other members of //Brachinus//, except one difference in the way that they defend themselves. Instead of producing and spraying 1,4-Benzoquinone within its explosion chamber, A-013 sprays molten gold. The method by which A-013 synthesizes the gold, as well as preventing injury to itself in the process, is currently unknown. Dissection and similar procedures are prohibited due to the limited number of instances of A-013.

A-013 was initially discovered by an expedition of coleopterologists[[footnote]]Coleopterologists are scientists that specialize in the study of beetles.[[/footnote]] attempting to track Bombardier Beetles. The coleopterologists were reported missing, and local authorities went searching for the party. The authorities found the coleopterologists severely burned and coated in molten gold, none of the coleopterologists survived. A GEAR task force was called to come in and contain A-013 after the authorities suffered 3 casualties.
