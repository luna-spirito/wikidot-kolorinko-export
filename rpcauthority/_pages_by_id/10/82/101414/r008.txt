**Registered Phenomena Code:** 052-J

**Object Class:** Neutralized, previously Alpha-Yellow (Utility)

**Hazard Types:** Mechanical, Electromagnetic Force, Geological.

**Archived Containment Protocols:** RPC-052-J is stationed at Garage 9 of Site-███. The use of RPC-052-J --as a standard transportation vehicle-- is strictly limited to Level 2 personnel. Maintenance of the vehicle is to be performed twice a week to preserve its operational value.

**Description:** RPC-052-J was a Nissan NV350 Urvan painted in black, used primarily for transportation and occasionally, drive-by shootings perpetrated by the previous owners[[footnote]]As noted by the seats in the passenger's area, with two of the double seats facing each side door, while one of the seats faces the rear window.[[/footnote]]. The most notable modification is in the driver's area of RPC-052-J, this being a built-in secondary gear stick. The words ''Chill Zone'', ''Outta Here'', and ''Drift By'', have been written with the white marker on the layout of the secondary gear knob. Upon starting RPC-052-J, various anomalous properties manifest according to the shift position of the van indicated by the secondary lever.

**Discovery:** RPC-052-J was discovered on 21/09/1996, in the Southwest region of the city of Los Angeles, California. Several motorized shootings had been perpetrated in the last 5 days, likely a result of increased gang conflicts in response to Tupac Shakur's death on 13/09/1996. During one of the aforementioned incidents, several police officers were subject to many of RPC-052-J's anomalous properties while chasing the vehicle, severely damaging 4 patrolling vehicles. A follow-up retrieval operation resulted in the recovery of RPC-052-J, however, the driver (suspected to be the owner of the van) was found deceased inside the vehicle and therefore could not be detained for interrogation[[footnote]]An autopsy revealed the subject expired due to a drug overdose of Cocaine.[[/footnote]].

[[collapsible show="+ Addenda 052 J.1 - Experimentation:" hide="ACCESS GRANTED" hideLocation="both"]]

> ------

> **Test Number:** 009.
> **Personnel Involved:** CSD-789, a 31 year old Hispanic male convict.
> **Objective:** Trigger any of RPC-052-J's anomalous effects while the vehicle moves in reverse.
> **Procedures:** CSD-789 was instructed to drive the van in reverse while shifting patterns.
> **Result:**  Due to the low velocity of the vehicle, shift patterns C.Z and D.B were the only ones whose effects triggered during the test. 
> **Notes:** Shift pattern C.Z persisted when the vehicle was motionless, as noted by Researcher Salazar upon entering the vehicle and demanding CSD-789 to //''Turn that thing off or I'll personally deport you back to Mariachiland!''//[[footnote]]//Junio Researcher Salazar requested immediate transference of CSD-789 to Site-███-MX. Request denied due to incompetent and unprofessional behavior, and the subject has been severely reprimanded as a result of it.  -Dr. Ramirez.//[[/footnote]] 

> **Test Number:** 010.
> **Personnel Involved:** CSD-790, a 27 year old Caucasian female convict.
> **Objective:** Measure the strength of RPC-052-J's magnetic field during shift pattern O.H.
> **Procedures:** CSD-790 was instructed to drive near various objects affected by drive pattern O.H while the aforementioned effect was triggered.
> **Result:** Inconclusive. RPC-052-J crashed into a police car during the third repetition of the test when CSD-790 confused the brake with the acceleration pedal.

> **Test Number:** 012.
> **Personnel Involved:** --CSD-791--[[footnote]]Averted, CSD-791 could not enter the vehicle from the driver's door due to the subject's severe obesity.[[/footnote]] CSD-792, a 17 year old African-American male convict.
> **Objective:** Determine the rate of gasoline consumption by RPC-052-J during shift pattern M.I.
> **Procedures:** CSD-792 was instructed to perform a test drive under shift pattern M.I. 
> **Result:** The rate of consumption was determined to increase varyingly between 13% and 43%.
> **Notes:** On the fifth recreation of the experiment, CSD-792 immediately attempted to flee by driving away from the test area shifting between drive patterns M.I and D.B. This escape attempt was unsuccessful due to the low gasoline levels resulting from the previous tests, and the individual was successfully recaptured by ASF Lieutenant ████ Jammar.

[[collapsible show="+ Addenda 052 J.3 - Neutralization Incident:" hide="ACCESS GRANTED" hideLocation="both"]]


> **- 03/01/1998 - 09:03:29 -**
> 
> During a second interrogation, CSD-791 accused Junior Researcher Salazar of being an acquaintance of RPC-052-J's previous owner. ASF Commander Jammar was tasked to investigate Junior Researcher Salazar's locker, while two Authority agents were dispatched at the subject's home. During this period, Researcher Salazar was informed of the operative when escorting CSD-791 to Garage 9 for a scheduled experiment with RPC-052-J. Once the vehicle was provided, CSD-791 entered the passenger's area and Researcher Salazar proceeded to drive away from Garage 9. 
> 
> 30 minutes after ASF personnel were notified of a Class-CSD Containment Failure, several Authority agents intercepted a radio message from the local police department, claiming that a van matching the description of RPC-052-J was spotted in the west region of Los Angeles. The subjects inside the van were reported to kidnap a 12 year old female child and hold her as a hostage. MST Romeo-7 (''Suited Gentlemen'') mobilized for an immediate containment operation.
> 
> RPC-052-J was found 10 minutes later by MST Romeo-7 on [REDACTED], a nearby parking lot. The vehicle was burning, and most of the essential components were incinerated beyond repair. Both Salazar and CSD-791 were found deceased inside the van, partially burnt, and the child reported on the police radio channel was missing from the scene. An autopsy revealed that both of the subjects expired due to internal hemorrhage caused by several gunshots in the cranium, torso, and lungs. The police department later reported that the child had been retrieved back to its family, deeming a rescue operation completely unnecessary.
> 
> A subsequent review of nearby security footage that captured RPC-052-J revealed various unidentified individuals surrounding RPC-052-J during its incineration, and one of the subjects was seen talking with the missing child. The subject was approximately 20 years old, and like the other individuals, has his eyes protected by dark sunglasses and his mouth covered by a black scarf. The only identifiable trait between all subjects was their close resemblance to a nearby Mexican gang.
