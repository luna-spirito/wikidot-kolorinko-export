+ **Overview**

-----
**Established:** 19^^th^^ December, 1984.

**Front Cover:** Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Hong Kong

**Location:** Hung Hom, Hong Kong

**Codename:** "Poly"

**Hex-code:** [26]

**Site Occupation:** Containment, Research, Intelligence

**Division Jurisdiction:** Research

**Speciality:** Mechanical Hazards, Extradimensional Hazards, Chronological Hazards, Tychokinetic Hazards, Electric Hazards

**Total Area:** 91600 m^^2^^[[footnote]]Site-038 does not include the entirety of The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.[[/footnote]]

**Background Information:** Site-038 is the Authority's primary research facility for Alpha and Beta Class objects with low threat levels, as well as temporary or long-term storage of Alpha and Beta Class objects retrieved within the Pearl River Delta metropolitan area [[footnote]]The collective term for cities near the Pearl River, such as Hong Kong and Guangdong[[/footnote]]. Discussions onto whether to expand Site-038 as a hub for all Authority activities within China or even the entirety of East Asia or to establish a proposed Site-039 at [DATA EXPUNGED] are being made among Global Directors.

Site-038's main power source is supplied by the CLP Corporation Limited alongside the non-anomalous sections of the Hong Kong Polytechnic University, with an on-site Natural Gas Thermal Power Station as a backup source.

Site-038 is also used for recruitment and training of research staff due to Site-038's location at a university. This includes [DATA EXPUNGED]. As a result, research staff are often professors at the Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

Site-038's non-anomalous facilities also make it inherently suited for the study of mechanical anomalies.

Due to Site-038's unique geographical location, it is also used as a forward base for operations against the People's Committee for the Acquisition of Anomalous Objects.

**History**: During the 1960s and 1970s, due to the increasing relevance of the People's Committee for the Acquisition of Anomalous Objects, the decision was made by the Authority to establish a forward base within the People's Republic of China in order to reduce the relevancy of the aforementioned Group of Interest, as well as increasing Authority presence in monitoring. As a result, the plans for a Site to be established in China were formulated.

After the announcement of plans for Hong Kong's handover to China in 1979, Global Director 09 proposed that the aforementioned Site be established in Hong Kong while under British influence, who were supportive of the RPC Authority. However, due to the large degree of instability predicted by Global Director 02, the Authority decided that the construction of a new Site from nothing would be too time-consuming, and opted for the modification of an institute for research purposes in order to conserve time. The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, then The Hong Kong Polytechnic, was chosen in November 1979, and construction began on 30/12/1979.

Site-038 was established 19/12/1984, though construction would not be fully completed until 16/07/1991. The Hong Kong Polytechnic gained full university status on 25/11/1994, becoming The Hong Kong Polytechnic University and allowing for a higher number of research staff to be recruited.

+ **Offices**[[footnote]]Due to the architecture of Site-038, the standard sector is referred to as a "core" instead of a "wing".[[/footnote]]
[[/=]]
-----

* **Administrative and Office Core** - This core is used to manage the entire facility of Site-038 and the entire Authority operations in Southern China. Only certain staff, MST leaders, researchers, and Global Directors may enter this area.

* **Intelligence Core** - Used to monitor all domestic People's Committee for the Acquisition of Anomalous Objects, as well as the housing of Mobile Security Teams and certain agents.

* **Mechanical Anomalies Core** - Separated into two sectors: //Department of Mechanical Research//, //Department of Electrical Research//, and //Department of Applied Anomalies.//

* **Medical Core** - Mainly used to store medical supplies for requesting sites. However, several advanced medical and surgical equipment are stationed in the event of an emergency.

* **Containment Core** - Storage of low-threat Alpha and Beta items with high value of research, as well as temporary storage for items retrieved from East Asia.

* **Extra-Dimensional Core** - Research of objects capable of extra-dimentional anomalies conduced here.

* **Chronological Core** - Research of objects capable of chronological displacement.

[[collapsible show="+ Security and Intelligence Breaches" hide="-hide Security Breaches"]]

**#001**
**12/10/1986**
**Transgressor:** The People's Committee for the Acquisition of Anomalous Objects
**Outcome:** Agents from the People's Committee for the Acquisition of Anomalous Objects were able to evade Site security through one of the incomplete segments of Site-038 and gained intel related to [[[RPC-041]]], [[[RPC-998]]] as well as the list of Authority Agents embedded within the Guangdong Province Government. The agents responsible were apprehended and transferred to Site-009. The agents were released on 08/08/1988 during a prisoner exchange alongside intel of RPC-998[[footnote]]It was determined that intel related to RPC-998 under Committee jurisdiction may benefit the Authority.[[/footnote]]

**002**
**10/11/1998**
**Transgressor:** The Church of Malthus
**Outcome:** A force of 303 operatives under the Church of Malthus were able to raid Site-038, resulting in the lockdown of Site-038's sub-terrain levels 1 to 4. This lockdown resulted in the siege of Site-038. The incident was resolved when MST Romeo-7, Echo-7 and Authority Security were able to regain control of Site-038, killing 98 Malthus Agents and capturing 76 more. 2 Romeo-7, 3 Echo-7 and 7 Authority Security staff were KIA, while 3 more research personnel were killed during the siege. All surviving Malthus agents were interrogated, which led to [DATA EXPUNGED], severely damaging Malthus activity within East Asia.

[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="+ Show Other Incidents" hide="-Hide Other Incidents"]]
**#001**
**June 1989 to September 1996**
**Incident:** A disproportionate amount of staff, including many senior staff, requested transfers from Site-038 to other Sites, or resigned during this period, causing massive staffing shortages due to increasing political instability within Hong Kong.
**Outcome:** The nature of this incident was determined to be non-anomalous and related to the political status of Hong Kong at the time. Site-038 went into emergency mode and 76% of all research was either met with significant delays or were postponed. Site-038 did not fully recover until September 2014.
**01/07/20 Update:** With the recent political climate, it is believed that this incident would resurge within the next 2 years.

**#002**
**16/11/2019**
**Incident:** The Hong Kong Polytechnic University was besieged by the Hong Kong Police Force, in an attempt to arrest protesters. Due to Dr. Erasmus Cheng's arrest when attempting to depart, staff determined that surface-level exits were not viable.
