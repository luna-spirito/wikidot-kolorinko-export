**Asset #:** A-070-J

**Object Class:** Gamma

**Containment Protocols:** A-70-J is to be contained under the guise of a healthy eating camp for those afflicted with anorexia. A-70-J is to be staffed with no less than 50 "enforcers" to help the "camp residents" eat their special diet of A-70-J-1 with various food items mixed in. 

A-70-J requires its campgrounds to be filled with 200 "camp residents" to be residing there at any given time to prevent a containment breach. If the number ever falls below this number, enforcers must eat A-70-J-1 until the Authority can find more "camp residents" to participate in the consummation of A-70-J-1. For this purpose, recruiting from our own employees under the guise of "reallocated to human resources".

The box, being an XK-class world-end scenario, must be patrolled by a standing guard of twenty. Designated "Cereal Crusaders" are to be rotated out of their shifts every 8 hours to avoid exhaustion and "get away from that tasteless shit".

**Description:** A-70-J is a cereal box that forcefully ejects cereal at a constant rate of .55 bowls per minute (bpm). The Authority has tried to monopolize this by creating their own brand of cereal, Special A. Unfortunately, A-70-J-1 is primarily made of grains of lightly toasted rice, wheat, and barley, and sold as badly as its namesake, Special █.

[[include component:image-block name=cerealgulag1|caption=Cereal "Camp" Circa 196█]]

The box, reads as follows, to "Part of a nutritious breakfast [[size 80%]]breakfast[[/size]][[size 60%]]breakfast[[/size]][[size 40%]]breakfast[[/size]][[size 20%]]breakfast[[/size]]" to ad infinitum. The box was discovered on site, after reports of a "avalanche of Special █" were reported to The Authority by an anonymous source. Agents later found an entire campsite drowned in the Special █, and established site "Cereal" there.

[[collapsible show="+ Experiment Log 070-J-1" hide="- Experiment Log 070-J-1"]]

**Experiment 070-J-A**
**Procedure:** A standard bird feeder was filled with A-070-J and a camera was set up in a nearby tree to record and document the experiment. 
**Results:** Five days after the test began, a total of eighteen various birds were found dead at the base of the feeder.

**Experiment 070-J-B**
**Procedure:** Two squirrels were locked in a cage, and a bowl filled with A-070-J was placed inside along with water and other necessities. 
**Results:** On the second day of testing, neither squirrel went to consume A-070-J. They did, however, express the standard symptoms of starvation. On the third day, one of the squirrels was found cannibalizing the other.

**Addendum 070-J-1:** It is safe to assume that 070-J cannot be fed to wildlife, and testing of A-070-J on animals is hereby suspended. It's also safe to assume this wouldn't sell very well.

[[/collapsible]]
