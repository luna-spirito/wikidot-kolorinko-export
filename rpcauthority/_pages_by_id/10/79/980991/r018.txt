##6C6766| //(Redirected from __Provisional Site 052__.)// ##


[[f<image http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/component:icons/alpha-yellow.png width="90px"]]
[[include component:image-block name= http://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/crepe-cake-i/RPC052 |caption=RPC-052 //in situ//, seen via viewport. | width=375px]]

**Registered Phenomena Code:** 052

**Object Class:** Alpha-Yellow

**Primary Hazard Types:** Corrosive Hazard, Contact Hazard

**Secondary Hazard Types:** Explosive Hazard, Visual Hazard, Ideological Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** Provisional Site 052 is to be made into an exclusion zone and sectioned off by 200m x 200m chain-link steel alloy fencing, with Grade II security bundle provisions in place. 
[[collapsible show="+ Show Standardized Grade II Security Bundle Provisions" hide="- Minimize" hideLocation="both"]] Cautionary signage is to be posted prominently along the perimeter indicating various hazards.[[footnote]]Currently warning of radiation poisoning and structural instability.[[/footnote]] Motion-triggered CCTV cameras are to remain fixed at predetermined areas of coverage at all times, maintaining a panoptic surveillance system in the event of any attempted unauthorized entry into the designated area. A standard duty of security personnel is to occupy an outpost adjacent to the barrier wall containing the sole entry into the site.
[[/collapsible]]

All personnel participating in research are prohibited from maintaining their field of vision towards or on the last known location of any object interacting with RPC-052, and must disengage visualization of this location after the object-RPC complex has become microscopic. Proxy viewing of the microscopic process is permissible via approved hypermicroscopy visualization tools. 

Materials made of or containing significant portions of xenon, cesium, uranium, or other periodic elements which display a wide variety of radioactive isotopes are prohibited from passing the fenced perimeter of Provisional Site 052.

**Description:** RPC-052 is an orb of a black, hyper-dense, and highly corrosive liquid measuring at approximately 15cm in diameter. Any material that comes into physical contact with RPC-052 will experience markedly accelerated corrosion.

The corrosive process enacted by RPC-052 does not terminate at a molecular level and will continue to reduce matter beyond the usual liquid or gaseous state, continuing well into the microscopic, atomic, subatomic, particle, and subparticular scales. The factor at which RPC-052 reduces matter is approximately 1x10^^1^^m (or a power of ten) per 30 seconds.[[footnote]]This means RPC-052 would corrode a blue whale into the size of an average mouse in one minute, or the observable universe into a mote of dust in about 18 minutes.[[/footnote]] Hypermicroscopy has confirmed degeneration of nuclear matter such as protons, neutrons, and even electrons into quarks, and those into a quantum scaffolding of dispersed probability waves. While visualization of the smallest scales is currently beyond the scope of the Authority's scientific technology, the rate of corrosion suggests matter is reduced beyond third-, second-, and first-dimensional meaningfulness, as well as the Planck length,[[footnote]]1.62×10^^−35^^ m; the smallest possible measurement of material reality.[[/footnote]] ultimately achieving a complete material and theoretical vacuum. Despite an observable measure of RPC-052 separating from the orb to corrode foreign matter, the volume and density of RPC-052 do not diminish.

Corrosion of the subatomic components of radioactive elements have been demonstrated to precipitate nuclear chain-reactions.[[footnote]]No personnel were harmed in the confirmation of this theory, as its effects were anticipated by Authority theoretical physicists, and sufficiently small quantities of radioactive materials were used in testing.[[/footnote]] As such, no radioactive materials or elements with a wide variety of isotopes are allowed in proximity to RPC-052; even trace macroscopic amounts of such materials may result in an explosion that would destroy current containment and cause loss of life.

Research has begun to investigate probable secondary anomalous effects of RPC-052, which are experienced by any who view a material object's complete corrosion into non-existence.[[footnote]]Warning to research personnel: This means approximately one minute after an object becomes microscopic.[[/footnote]]

[[tab Discovery]]
In early 2012, a report was forwarded to Authority case recruitment teams through established channels in the Bavarian state police force regarding an astronomy observatory in Munich, where a staff of eight -- four astronomers, two earth scientists, one communications engineer, and one theoretical physicist -- were reported missing with no supporting data or forensic evidence. The observatory was part of the COPENHAVEN global research complex.[[footnote]]Sought to provide fail-safe solutions to cosmological threats that would mean the destruction of the planet, were they to occur (e.g. a doomsday meteor, directed quasar, etc.).[[/footnote]] 

Upon scanning the facility, RPC-052 was discovered in the center of the observatory's primary communications transceiver, the relay of which was trained at the stellar coordinates of GRO J1655-40, 4U 1543-475, and additional cataloged black holes.[[footnote]]The specific reason for this is not made clear given the team's research notes.[[/footnote]] The area housing the transceiver's internal components was replaced with a hollow chamber, in which RPC-052 was suspended. 

[[include component:image-block name=http://rpcsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/crepe-cake-i/communications.jpg |caption=Primary communications transceiver housing RPC-052. | width=375px]]

Attempts to manipulate the object showed that it was an ink-like substance with a density similar to degenerate matter,[[footnote]]Nuclear matter whose behavior is dictated by quantum mechanics due to extreme compression of intra-atomic spaces.[[/footnote]] which is theorized to compose neutron stars. The object however had no gravitational presence or effects, nor measurable radiation signatures. When disturbed by a non-tactile force (e.g. pressurized air, gravity, magnets), the substance temporarily behaved in an anti-gravitational manner before it would reconfigure to its spherical shape. 

Containment proved difficult in that whatever touched the object was subject to its interminable corrosive properties. Initially a pipette, then a vacuum-equipped beaker were lost. The containment team at that time recommended the site be converted to a provisional site, as the anomaly was technically sufficiently contained already; this was approved and designated Provisional Site 052. Research and supportive containment directives were then initiated. The initial containment chamber was fitted with a suitable testing apparatus including a viewport and nuclear-grade glovebox.

[[tab Incident Report 052.1]]
{{Research Team 1}}
After initial testing was complete, Research Team 1 boarded a vehicle to be transported to their private vehicles. During the transport, a graveyard was passed by. The research personnel reacted excitedly to the sight, exclaiming to one another in apparent concern and shock, vocalizing wonder and fear at the graveyard, and discussing its possible purpose. Authority transport personnel did not report anything out of the ordinary during this drive, or with the graveyard specifically after attention was drawn to it. Members of the research team were piqued by the sight of the graveyard enough to break transfer protocols and open the passenger bay doors, stepping out in a casual fashion while the vehicle moved at approximately 85kph. Four members of the research team perished instantly, while two sustained career-ending injuries to their extremities. The latter personnel were treated appropriately at the medical ward of a nearby Authority asset, while the former were pronounced dead upon arrival. Of note, the living members did not understand that their colleagues had died as a result of the error, perhaps due to a sustained state of neuropsychological shock.

[[tab Incident Report 052.2]]
{{Research Team 2}} 
At 13:23 on day 3 of the second phase of initial testing, an accident in handling protocols occurred with the anomaly. An individual manipulating the object remotely did not wait sufficiently long enough for the substance to re-collect into the sphere and opened the safety hatch to retrieve testing materials that failed to contact the substance. An errant droplet of the anomaly then contacted the dorsal surface of her hand. Chemical spill protocols were enacted, however, panic ensued when these protocols were not effective, despite being followed appropriately. [[tab Incident Report 052.3]]
An individual previously on Research Team 2 and who made a full recovery from Incident 052.2 asked to rejoin the research efforts. Authority administration approved of this, as the experience had reportedly given the individual some insight that he claimed he needed the equipment to demonstrate scientifically.

In the initial and only trial, the researcher readies the recording equipment. After, he breaks protocol and inserts himself into the containment chamber; he quickly comes into contact with RPC-052 and begins to corrode. Video feeds are clipped during this process due to the suspected conceptual secondary anomalous effects of RPC-052, but audio feeds records the following statement from the researcher at 12:49, approximately one minute prior to complete dissolution:
[[div style="padding-left:2em"]]
"I talked with Lyigeti, he still knows of death. So I'll see you—“[[footnote]]The remainder of the audio is cut by Project Lead as a precautionary measure. Speech continued for an additional 10 seconds.[[/footnote]]
[[/div]]

Responding personnel subsequently discovered that the hypermicroscopy technology affixed to the containment chamber had been pre-programmed to track the researcher's center of gravity while inside, by reading graviton concentrations and mapping the coordinates' subatomic composition. A high concentration of ephemeral quarks and anti-quarks was found rotating in a disc-like pattern and with high velocity about an unseen center.[[footnote]]Presumably the last known locus of the researcher's center of gravity.[[/footnote]] Perpendicular to the rotating disc were two jets of similar material, extending outward at relatively massive distances; on the order of several powers of ten relative to the pseudo-particle matter itself. The particle and energy signature is markedly reminiscent of those seen around and originating from black holes. 

A directed scan of the facility discovered additional such signatures — one at the last known position of A. Dilland, and eight more at various positions throughout the observatory floor.
