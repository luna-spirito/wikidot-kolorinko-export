**Registered Phenomena Code:** 390

**Object Class:** --Beta-- Gamma-Red (See Incident Log 390)

**Hazard Types:** Visual Hazard, Memory Alteration Hazard, Ideological Hazard

**Revised Containment Protocols:** One instance of RPC-390-1 is to be contained in a zero-transparency page protector in Researcher Thompson's desk.  It is to be used strictly for testing. Further instances of RPC-390-1 are to be destroyed by any means possible. Any instances of RPC-390-2 are to be terminated with extreme prejudice. Film cameras are banned within Site-002.[[footnote]]See Experiment-390-4[[/footnote]]

[[collapsible show="+ Show Original Containment Protocols" hide="- Hide Original Containment Protocols"]]
**Containment Protocols:** RPC-390-1 is to be kept in a zero-transparency page protector in Researcher Thompsons desk. Instances of RPC-390-2 are to be kept in standard humanoid containment cells at Site-45 for interviewing and testing.
[[/collapsible]]
**Description:** --RPC-390-1 is a 215.9 by 279.4 mm white piece of paper with the words "George Boston" written on it with black marker. The paper exhibits a memetic effect that when read causes subjects to believe they are an entity named "George Boston."-- 

RPC-390-1 instances are the words "George Boston" written by instances of RPC-390-2. These writings exhibit a memetic effect that mentally turns subjects that read them into an entity named "George Boston." These converted subjects are referred to as RPC-390-2.

RPC-390-2 instances share similar memories, theorized to be the memories of the original "George Boston." --Beyond this point instance of RPC-390-2 have no observed anomalous properties.-- Instances of RPC-390-2 have displayed the ability to create instances of RPC-390-1 by writing the words "George Boston" in any physical medium[[footnote]]See Incident Log-390-1 and Experiment Log-390-1[[/footnote]].

RPC-390-2 instances display similar mental traits including:
* An increase in aggression against non-converted humans.
* A strong desire to convince non-converted humans to become instances of RPC-390-2.
* An obsession to collect anything featuring or displaying the name "George"
* An irrational hatred of any and all people named "John"[[footnote]]See Interview Log-390.
[[/footnote]]
* Religious tendencies.

**Discovery:** A report of spontaneous civil unrest in ██████, Ohio lead to Authority investigation. Instances of RPC-390-2 were found raiding homes in search of "Johns." Instances were restrained with great difficulty, leading to the termination of 7 subjects and the capture of 11 more.

**Addendum-390-1:**
[[collapsible show="+ Interview Log-390-1" hide="- Close Interview Log"]]
> **Interviewed:** An instance of RPC-390-2
> 
> **Interviewer:** Dr. █████
> 
> **Foreword:** This instance of RPC-390-2 was found during a missing persons case, where it turned out that it was abducting civilians to convert into other instances of  RPC-390-2. 
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Dr. █████:** Please state your name.
> 
> **RPC-390-2:** George Boston.
> 
> **Dr. █████ shuffles through his papers, pulling out a birth certificate**
> 
> **Dr. █████:** Your birth certificate states your name is Alan Resnik.
> 
> **RPC-390-2:** That document is a forgery.
> 
> **Dr. █████:** Do you have any proof for that?
> 
> **RPC-390-2:** It doesn't say George Boston.
> 
> **Dr. █████:** Noted. Would you mind telling me about yourself? Where were you born?
> 
> **RPC-390-2:** Boston.
> 
> **Dr. █████:** State?
> 
> **RPC-390-2:** Massachusetts.
> 
> **Dr. █████:** What year were you born?
> 
> **RPC-390-2:** What's with all the questions?
> 
> **Dr. █████:** It's part of the interview, would you kindly...
>  
> **RPC-390-2:** What is your name?
> 
> **Dr. █████:** That is not relevant to the interview.
> 
> **RPC-390-2:** What. Is. Your. Name?
> 
> **Dr. █████:** Dr. █████
>  
> **RPC-390-2:** WHAT IS YOUR FIRST NAME?
> 
> **Dr. █████:** I'm not at liberty to disclose that.
> 
> **RPC-390-2 starts to look visibly frustrated** 
> 
> **Dr. █████:** Mr. Boston, are you OK?
>  
> **The instance of RPC-390-2 stands up rapidly and forcibly takes Dr. █████'s papers**
> 
> **Dr. █████:** Security, I need assistance.
>  
> **RPC-390-2:** You're one of them... a fucking John.
>  
> **Dr. █████:** Security now.
> 
> **RPC-390-2:** YOU'RE GONNA PAY. YOU'RE ALL GONNA PAY FOR WHAT YOU DID.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** A security team arrived with in seconds of RPC-390 turning hostile and foiled its subsequent attempt at terminating Dr. █████. The instance of RPC-390-2 was terminated shortly after.
[[/collapsible]]

**Addendum-390-2:**
[[collapsible show="+ Incident Log-390-1" hide="- Close Incident Log"]]
At 1200 hours, during a routine inspection of cells containing instances of RPC-390-2, two members of site security and one janitor were turned into instance of RPC-390-2. This was found out 30 minutes later after all 3 instances failed to identify themselves correctly and were promptly terminated.

The source of this containment breach was found in the cell of Instance-12. Instance-12 had written the words "George Boston" on the wall using a mixture of fecal matter and urine. The writing exhibited similar memetic effects to RPC-390-1 and was cleaned after discovery.

Subsequently Head Researcher Thompsons ordered the testing of this effect and the reclassification of RPC-390 to Gamma.
[[/collapsible]]

**Addendum-390-3:**
[[collapsible show="+ Experiment Log-390-1" hide="- Close Experiment Log"]]
> **Experiment-390-01:**
> 
> **Materials:** 
> * One (1) Instance of RPC-390-1
> * One (1) CSD (Subject unable to read English.)
> 
> **Procedure:** CSD was instructed to observe instance of RPC-390-1.
> 
> **Results:** CSD personnel was converted into an instance of RPC-390-2. Instance spoke English without accent.

> **Experiment-390-2:**
> 
> **Materials:**
> * One (1) - Digital Camera
> * One (1) - Instance of RPC-390-1
> * One (1) - Instance of RPC-390-2
> * One (1) - CSD
> 
> **Procedure:** The instance of RPC-390-1 was photographed by instance of RPC-390-2. CSD was instructed to observe camera image.
> 
> **Results:** CSD was unaffected. Image file was saved for further testing.

> **Experiment-390-3:**
> 
> **Materials:**
> One (1) - Color Printer
> Two (2) - CSD
> 
> **Procedure:** Image file from Experiment-390-2 was printed in both color and monochrome. One CSD was instructed to observe color copy. One CSD was instructed to observe monochrome copy.
> 
> **Results:** Both CSD were unaffected.

> **Experiment-390-4:**
> 
> **Materials:**
> One (1) - Film-reel Camera
> One (1) - Instance of RPC-390-1
> One (1) - Instance of RPC-390-2
> Two (2) - CSD
> 
> **Procedure:** An instance of RPC-390-2 was instructed to photograph an instance of RPC-390-1 using the provided film camera. Film was developed in both monochrome and color via automatic development machine. One CSD was instructed to observe color copy. One CSD was instructed to observe monochrome copy.
> 
> **Results:** Both CSD were converted. Containment protocols updated accordingly.

> **Experiment-390-5:**
> 
> **Materials:**
> One (1) - Instance of RPC-390-1
> One (1) - Instance of RPC-███
> One (1) - CSD
> 
> **Procedure:** The CSD was sent into RPC-███'s attack radius and instructed to show RPC-390-1 to RPC-███.
> 
> **Results:** RPC-███ turns into an instance of RPC-390-2, and inquires on whether or not the CSD is a "John". RPC-███ terminates the CSD and disappears. Subsequent instances of RPC-███ appear to not be affected by RPC-390-1.

> **Experiment-390-6:**
> 
> **Materials:**
> One (1) - Instance of RPC-390-1
> Five (5) - Instances of RPC-003
> One (1) - CSD
> 
> **Procedure:** A CSD was instructed to present RPC-390-1 to the instances of RPC-003.
> 
> **Results:** The instances of RPC-003 ignore RPC-390-1 and attempt to shoot the CSD.
