+ **Over-Description**

------
RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 is a gold-covered wooden chest measuring approximately 1.3m x 0.8m x 0.8m. At the top of RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 are 2 golden humanoid figures with wings; in their current position the humanoids are facing each other although they have been known to invert their orientation at random. Muon tomography scans[[footnote]]Muons are subatomic particles similar to electrons and can be utilized in scans to reveal the interior of thick materials in greater detail than X-ray based tomography can.[[/footnote]] of RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 have revealed the contents within to be two jars containing unknown material, two stone tablets, and a wooden rod -- these items are hereby collectively referred to as RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎-1.

RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 exhibits the anomalous property of local reality manipulation resulting in it existing in multiple states at once while simultaneously having no determinable state. Due to this effect, RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 exhibits secondary anomalous properties which have been recorded into this document as “Property Sets”. There is no way to predict what effect RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 will adapt next nor does an analysis of previous data on RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 lead to any conclusive findings.

Constants persist throughout RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎's history, and are listed below:

* RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 will always produce something, such as items or creatures or a passive "effect".
* RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 will always display autonomy.
* Organic contact with RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 will result in instant termination through unknown means.
* Should RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 ever be damaged, it will regenerate within a 24 hour period.
* Attempts at damaging RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 with the intent of destroying it fail consistently. [[footnote]]Thus far there have been 398 attempts at destroying RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎. [[/footnote]] [[footnote]]damaging RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 accidentally has been observed to have worked, however when damaged with intent an unobservable force appears to "shield" RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 from the damage it would have sustained otherwise. [[/footnote]]

[[span style="color:green;"]]__**Containment Systems:**__[[/span]] As RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 does not move in its current state, it is to be placed in a special high-danger, high-threat containment chamber at OL-Site-𝟎𝟎𝟎.  RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 is to be propped onto its posterior side (face-up) to allow personnel to walk through it. Expeditions into location R-L9806 must be approved by the Site Administrator at OL-Site-𝟎𝟎𝟎.

[[span style="color:green;"]]__**Pacification Systems:**__[[/span]] Any entities that leaves RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 from R-L9806 are to be subdued or terminated as per standard Procedures.

[[span style="color:green;"]]__**Pacification Note:**__[[/span]] NA

[[span style="color:green;"]]__**Object Description:**__[[/span]] [Repetitive information removed] The interior of RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 acts as a "gateway" between our local reality and an alternate reality.[[footnote]]It is to be noted that Authority scientists at the time of writing did not have the same understanding of the structure of the universe as the Authority dose now thus information such as local realities is poorly known and explained. For an updated explanation please request the proper documentation from your local Site Administrator.[[/footnote]] Scans have shown that all instances of RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎-1 are still inside RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎, though there has been no way to reach these objects given the anomaly's current properties. The "gateway" appears to lead to a universe[[footnote]]Referred to as R-L9806 in accordance with the Lee Omniversal Classification System.[[/footnote]] where nothing exists save for a seemingly endless maze of rooms and hallways. These areas demonstrate variations amongst each other regarding their distance from "point zero"[[footnote]] The coordinate where RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎's portal is located within R-L9806.[[/footnote]] but generally share a similar style of construction with abnormalities spread among them.
[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/_eskalion/uto5528cck131.png|caption= An example image taken from R-L9806 (Restored)]]
The majority of the walls cataloged with R-L9806 have yellow wallpaper glued onto them with a small floral design. Despite every hall and room having functioning lights, to date no expedition in R-L9806 has found a light switch or an electrical room. Furthermore no electrical sockets found on the walls of R-L9806 functions with the exception of Room R-L9806-528, found 30 meters away from "point zero". As such, Room R-L9806-582 has been established as a base camp within R-L9806 and is helmed by Dr. Natasha Thompson.

R-L9806 is populated by humanoids of unknown origin and varying degrees of hostility with some possessing the ability to speak known languages, and with others showing no intelligence of any kind. Given the wide variety of the humanoids, a generalized formal description is impossible with the only commonality among them being yellow teeth.

[[span style="color:green;"]]__**Behavioural Characteristics:**__[[/span]] [Repetitive information removed] RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 is unpredictable in its behavior, with a generalized pattern yet to be discerned.

[[span style="color:green;"]]__**Incident Report 1913-𝟎𝟎𝟎:**__[[/span]] On April 1st, 1984 at 8:37 AM EST the doorway held in R-L9806 at RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 suddenly closed constituting one of its many "Property Resets". The only person within R-L9806 at the time was Dr. Natasha Thompson. despite the being disconnected due to technological limitations monitoring equipment left behind continues to send information to this day; however most of it has thus far been indecipherable.

Dr. Thompson was assumed to be dead as soon as RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 changed its properties. Shortly before at roughly 8:24, a camera near Room R-L9806-764 captured an image of her heading down the hallway attempting to establish contact with main Authority channels. Several frames later, one of the many humanoid inhabitants can be seen assaulting Dr. Thompson. Due to this incident the Authority has began to investigate inter-dimensional travel and has subsequently banned the establishment of any base of operation on the other side of any gateway that has been determined to be unstable.  Stability is to be tested in the future using the Lee Spacial Stability Test[[footnote]]In modern Authority this is referred to as the Lee-Anderson Spacial Stability Test.[[/footnote]]

[Repetitive information removed] Following the cessation of RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎's 1913 Property Set, RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 began to emit a humming sound at 30 dB, with a wavelength of 30 Hz. This is paired with a form of crude instantaneous movement, which is limited to a radius of only roughly three meters. Researchers believe the movement is the result of a shift in local reality, however the exact mechanism is currently unknown as it has never occurred while under direct observation.

[[include component:image-enlarge name=http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/_eskalion/1574210847741.jpg|caption= Structure constructed by a skulk of //Vulpe aegyptica//[[footnote]]Egyptian Red Fox[[/footnote]] around the corpse of a recently deceased specimen.]]

Animals that hear RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎's auditory signature for an extended period of time begin to exhibit behavior contrary to their normally observed behavior. Initially the behavior of affected fauna only deviates slightly, often in the form of erratic movement or slowed response to stimuli. Upon the death of any member of an affected group of organisms, more severe deviations become apparent.

RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎's hum causes affected individuals to perform elaborate death rituals, often far more elaborate than the species would be capable of under baseline conditions. This will manifest as graves, structures built around corpses out of local materials, or in many cases the arrangement of several corpses into intricate configurations.

For a time the mechanism that caused this was elusive for researchers. However following an account by Dr. ██████, it became clear that overexposure to the hum caused psychotropic effects, and the containment protocols have been changed accordingly.

|| April 14th, 1991 || //Vulpe aegyptica// || Wildlife researchers in the area reported a lack of //aegyptica// corpses being found, as they feared it was evidence of the population dwindling due to human interference. Irregular track patterns were also recorded at this time, which alerted Authority personnel to the possibility of RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 effects. Searches came across large circular structures[[footnote]]Pictured.[[/footnote]] built from driftwood and sticks, around //aegyptica// corpses, which were noticeably moved. Population could not be relocated in effectively, however the reports of irregular behaviors were successfully buried thanks to significant efforts by the Black Site Internal Intelligence Council. ||
|| June 16th, 1994 || //Peucetia viridans// [Green Lynx Spider] || Attempts to dig post holes in areas close to the RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 site revealed to workers a series of caves that lead beneath the RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 containment area. According to eyewitnesses, the caves were large enough for humans to traverse, however it was near impossible to do so as the space was taken up almost entirely by large and ornate spiderwebs which spanned between the cave walls. Researchers were contacted to observe the webs, noting that the web itself indicated the green lynx spider, though the web's shapes were irregular for the species. It was then that researchers discovered the webs were centered around small "nodes" which were eventually learned to be the corpses of deceased spiders wrapped in silk and placed in the web. Disturbed, researchers sealed the cave, and it has remained untouched following the incident. ||
|| December 17th, 1996 || //Monetaria moneta// [White Cowry] || Authority event monitors noted several locals and tourists complaining of a lack of white cowry shells appearing on beaches close to the RPC̄-𝟎𝟎𝟎 site. The normally abundant shells had almost completely stopped washing ashore, and in response researchers ordered an investigation in the waters near the beaches. It was subsequently discovered that the shells were being arranged in structures on the seafloor by living cowry specimen. Several formations were discovered of cowry shells, corpses still within, arranged on the seafloor in groups of ten, with trails made in between mimicking the shape of the Jewish "Qaballah" diagram. Researchers have proposed plans to relocate the cowry population, and a program to place shells on the beaches as to not disturb the local populace.||

> "I have not yet been able to reconcile my emotions regarding this terrible hum. No longer can I truly be sure whether the voice I hear is truly hers or some imitation, a cruel spectre merely using me to enact its strange desires. I wonder whether the humble creatures that we have observed carry the same uncertainty. Do they even have the capability? What must the voices of the dead sound like to a stork? To a snail? To a bee? The religious nature of some of these occurrences is also troubling to me, after hearing her speak to me. She also speaks of religious themes, asks me to express the ideals of God in the world, as she cannot. She was God fearing in her life, but is this really her? Do the snakes hear the voices of their deceased speak of God as well? What does their God look like?"
