[[collapsible show="+ Contest Info"]]

**Three Random Hazards -** Contact [[*user Croal]]/Coupon#1337 or [[*user Vizlox]]/Gask#4878 to be assigned your hazards. You will be assigned three random [[[*threats-hazards | hazards/hazardous properties]]]. Your article must include these hazards and can include more if you wish. 

You are allowed to trade your entire hazard set with another user if you both agree to the trade. If you trade hazard sets with another user you must inform either Coupon or Vizlox. You are only allowed to trade hazard sets **once**. 

As an extra challenge, you may also request a random image to be included in your article. You will be given a choice of two random CC images from Wikimedia Commons. Please contact Vizlox to request a random image. The highest rated entry to accept this challenge will be awarded with a special badge.

The submission with the most upvotes will win. The winner will get bragging rights, the RPC-052 slot and a featured spot on the front page. Those who do not win are encouraged to upload their submissions. The highest rated entry to accept the random image challenge will earn the challenge winner badge.

Winners will be announced on May 31st. 

++ ##BD2578|The Deadline##
**Submission Opens:** May 20th 12 AM EST
**Submission Close and Voting Opens:** May 25th

++ ##BD2578|To Enter##
1. Write your article and get feedback on the forums. Wait until the submission period opens on the 20th to upload to the main site. 

2. Post your entry to the site with the URL "Contest:TRHContest<YourUserName>" with your site user name in place of <YourUserName.> For example, if a user named Chad were to post an entry it would have the URL rpc-wiki.net/Contest:TRHContestChad. This page must be posted by you or a collaboration partner. You must receive feedback before posting. 

3. Add the proper tag to your entry so it shows up on this page.
Use the tag "TRH" on your entry
