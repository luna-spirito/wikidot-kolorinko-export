* Dr. Mario's Megavitamin
* Falco/Fox's Blaster
* Pretty much Ice Climber's whole kit
* Mario and Luigi's Fireball
* Pichu and Pikachu's thunder moves
* Mewtwo's entire kit
* Link's entire kit
* Ness's entire kit
* **Bowser's Fire Breathe** (Note: Nothing should reflect Bowser's Fire Breathe.)
