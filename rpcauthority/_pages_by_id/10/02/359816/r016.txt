**Containment Protocols:** --RPC-292 is contained within the ADI[[footnote]]Anomalous Document Isolation.[[/footnote]] wing of Site-015's Physics Department. RPC-292 is sealed within a completely opaque envelope bearing RPC Hazard iconography, locked in ADI Safe #437.-- 

As of 14/6/1959, RPC-292 has been reclassified as Neutralized.

**Description:** RPC-292 is a complex series of esoteric mathematical problems and incomplete proofs inscribed upon an otherwise mundane piece of white letter paper measuring approximately 8.5 x 11 inches, carefully folded and placed into a white envelope addressed "From Elucidator Kruschtya" in poor handwriting.


Subjects exposed to the equations inscribed upon RPC-292 will begin to exhibit manic symptoms such as insomnia, hyperactivity, and vastly degraded temporal sensitivity, all related to a developing obsession with solving RPC-292. At this point it should be noted that symptomatic progression is directly proportional to the amount of progress a given test subject can make in solving RPC-292; effectively limiting the worst of its effects to those with substantial mathematical knowledge.

Over the course of one week following exposure, the cognitive effect of RPC-292 will worsen, and within twelve days subjects will begin to present symptoms of acute schizophrenia including auditory hallucinations and clinical paranoia. It is not uncommon for subjects in this state to request exclusive access to RPC-292, citing concerns of "intellectual sabotage" or theft of the item. A single dose of low-grade amnestics is effective at treating the cognitive effects of RPC-292, as are other methods focused on targeting the subject's ability to recall the equations inscribed upon it. 

Subjects retain a perfect photographic memory of the page, recalling the equations written upon it accurately for up to twenty days after exposure or until anti-memetic treatment. 

--Despite the attention and subsequent infatuation of many of the Authority's finest academics, the equation remains unsolved.--

On 14 June 1959, Dr. Yanov successfully solved RPC-292 in his personal study. Upon completion, RPC-292 self-neutralized and Dr. Yanov █████ ██ ███████ █ ██████ ██ █████████ ████████ █████████.

Authority personnel embedded within academic institutions are to be informed of the former properties of RPC-292 and instructed to remain vigilant for its potential re-appearance.

[[collapsible show="Discovery Log" hide="Hide Discovery Log"]]

RPC-292 was discovered in 1947 by an Authority researcher embedded in an archaeological expedition set to excavate a number of tombs recently uncovered in the Egyptian "Valley of the Kings". Upon finding RPC-292, said researcher recognized the anachronistic nature of the item and reported it immediately. The Authority recovered the item and placed it in ADI storage soon after initial observations and RPC designation, where it remained until May 26, 1959.

[[collapsible show="Incident-292-1 5/12/1959 - 6/14/1959" hide="Hide Log"]]

On 12 May, Dr. Yanov █████, a previously renowned mathematician working at Site-015, requested unadulterated access to RPC-292 for an extended attempt at solving its anomalous equations. Access was granted, and site staff began to closely monitor Dr. Yanov's psychiatric status.

Initial results followed the existing prediction model, with symptoms developing in line with previous exposure timelines. After fourteen days of uninhibited exposure, Dr. Yanov requested to move RPC-292 from containment into his personal study. The request was granted; and following site procedure, a 24/7 CCTV monitoring system was installed in both Dr. Yanov's personal study and in Dr. Yanov's office. By this point Dr. Yanov's performance of work-related duties had begun to suffer severely in favor of constant deliberation over RPC-292, and Dr. Yanov received an official reprimand from Site Director ██████. Dr. Yanov did not respond. After twenty-six days of uninhibited exposure, Dr. Yanov had completely isolated himself in his study with RPC-292, failing to emerge for food and water despite struggling with the apparent symptoms of severe malnutrition and dehydration which were clearly visible over the CCTV system. Subsequently, staff overseeing the experiment began to deliver both necessities to Dr. Yanov's study directly. During this time, personnel assigned to deliver Dr. Yanov's meals reported a strange tendency for him to slip from English into indecipherable speech during their brief interactions. 

On June 9th Dr. Yanov was forcibly removed from his study by Site-015 Security after he was caught engaging in self-harm with eating utensils by the concealed CCTV system, and was subsequently brought before Authority psychiatric personnel for mental evaluation.

Inspection of Dr. Yanov's self-inflicted woulds proved them to be entirely superficial, consisting of scratched-on number sets and tables, determined to be the result of heightened mania and not any implicit desire for self harm. Following proper medical and psychiatric treatment Dr. Yanov was released. Two Site Security personnel were placed outside of his door in the case of a repeat scenario, and an additional supply of blank note paper was provided to Dr. Yanov. 

Dr. Yanov's final vocal interaction with facility personnel took place during his escorted return from the Psychiatric Health and Wellness department, when he stopped a former colleague in the hallway to congratulate them upon becoming a parent. At this time, neither Researcher ██████ nor their spouse was aware of the pregnancy.

Over the following four days Dr. Yanov's symptoms continued to worsen. By June 13th Dr. Yanov had been awake for a total of 129 hours, and could be seen relentlessly pacing his study while talking to himself. At approximately 6:30PM Dr. Yanov sat at his desk and resumed attempts to solve RPC-292, staying seated throughout the night.

At 1:17AM the following day, June 14th 1959, Dr. Yanov solved RPC-292. After fifteen seconds of frenzied celebration, RPC-292 vanished from Dr. Yanov's hands as he collapsed onto the floor with violent convulsions. The Security Officers outside of Dr. Yanov's study reported a disturbed wailing coming from the other side of the door, and were ordered to stay in place until the situation was deemed safe for intervention. After approximately forty-three seconds, █████ ██ ██ █████████ ████████ ██████████████ █████ ██ ██████ ██ ███ ███████ ███████████; ████ ███████ ███ ██████████ ██ ███ ████, ███ ████████████ ██ ███ █████, ███ ███ ██████████ ██ ███ ███████. Sixteen seconds later, Dr. Yanov's head snapped to face the camera concealed at the northwest corner of the ceiling in his study; at which point the personnel assigned to monitor the CCTV feed manually closed the transmission.[[footnote]]Later interrogation of personnel responsible for cutting the CCTV feed revealed no malicious intent nor dereliction of duty; incident filed as the result of exposure to one of RPC-███'s secondary effects.[[/footnote]] It was then that the Security Officers waiting outside were ordered to enter the study to re-establish visual contact with Dr. Yanov, leading to ██████████ █████ █████ and a Level-3 containment breach.

During the subsequent containment efforts, five Site Security personnel (including the two officers assigned to Dr. Yanov's study) and sixteen members of Site-015's Physics Department were exposed to RPC-███ and isolated for treatment.

Following Dr. Yanov's re-designation, the Authority released a statement to family and former colleagues announcing his death and the destruction of his remains.
