**Registered Phenomena Code:** 836

**Object Class:** Gamma-White

**Hazard Types:** Mechanical Hazard

**Containment Procedures:** Due to the seemingly random and unpredictable appearances of RPC-836, permanent containment is currently unfeasible. Instead, Authority personnel involved in Countermeasure “Call-In” are to be instructed to monitor all paid programming appearing on cable television channels for signs of anomalous objects being sold. Should an instance of RPC-836 be located on any channel, the Authority may utilize assets within the Federal Communications Commissions to facilitate interference with all satellite television networks. When the signal is jammed, it will be replaced with one of several public domain programs.

Should anyone not involved with the Authority make a call on-air to the instance, the Authority is to immediately interrupt the purchase. If the item must be retrieved directly from the buyer, Class-A amnestics are to be provided following the acquisition.

Anyone partaking in an example demonstration of an object advertised by an instance of RPC-836 is to immediately be questioned regarding their appearance in the advertisement. 

**Description:** RPC-836 is the designation for a series of anomalous advertisements, manifesting as a standard ‘Paid Programming’ style program. These advertisements vary in length from two to thirty minutes and can appear on any channel accessible to a standard television set with connection to a cable network. The source of these advertisements have been untraceable so far, and no mention of the host network or channel has been recorded during an instance of RPC-836. The only connection between the advertisements is the phone number viewers are instructed to call in order to purchase an item.

An instance of RPC-836 will typically override the program scheduled to run at the time, normally a non-anomalous sponsored program, but has been seen to override other types of television programs such as cartoons, news programs, and in one case the first segment of a feature-length film. Cable networks questioned by the Authority have reported no interference from the network aspects where instances of RPC-836 appear.

Advertisements classified as RPC-836 instances tend to follow the standard format of a paid programming advertisement, involving the host of the program, usually a television salesperson or celebrity, attempting to sell the viewers a product. The salespeople used in instances of RPC-836 tend to be well-known sales agents, and their current condition or location seem to have no effect on if they can appear in instances of RPC-836. Any notable individuals appearing in an instance of RPC-836 have no recollection of participating in the advertisement, nor any knowledge of the items they attempted to sell. 

All products sold during instances of RPC-836 have been shown to be anomalous in nature, particularly in aspects that improve the item’s basic functions. Anomalous items advertised by RPC-836 tend to be Alpha-class anomalies, but there have been instances where what would normally be classified as Beta-class are advertised through RPC-836. The possibility of unidentified anomalies sold through RPC-836 instances have been explored by the Authority, and agents are advised to constantly monitor where instances of these products have been delivered to ensure that any purchases made are safely recovered and cataloged. Authority staff in charge of monitoring RPC-836 instances are permitted to use the Authority’s funds to purchase the anomalous products sold by the instance for testing purposes.

[[collapsible show="Examples of RPC-836 Instances." hide="Hide Examples of RPC-836 Instances"]]

> **Date of Instance:** 9/17/2014- First recorded instance of RPC-836 manifesting, replacing a regularly scheduled news program on NBC.
> 
> **Product Advertised:** “Clean-All” Stain Remover Laundry Detergent
> 
> **Description of Advertisement:** As-seen-on-TV salesman Vince Offer attempts to sell a previously unknown brand of Laundry Detergent. Examples of displaying the product include pouring various substances, including tomato sauce, oil, grease, and human blood on a seemingly non-anomalous white cotton shirt and placing it into a standard-issue washing machine. Shirt is removed by Vince and appears to be completely clean. 
> 
> **Product Properties:** When poured into a washing machine, any stains on clothes in the machine will immediately disappear. Analysis reveals that the stains have been removed on a subatomic level and that all particles not relating to the clothing’s composition have disappeared. Proposition to study the liquid for use as an anti-chemical hazard measure is under consideration. 

> **Date of Instance**: 5/12/2015- Replacing a normally scheduled Paid Programming block on Denver News Channel 7.
> 
> **Product Advertised:** “Solar Slider” Brand Slider Cooker
> 
> **Description of Advertisement:** Famed as-seen-on-TV salesman Billy Mays appears and uses the Solar Slider Brand Cooker to heat many different food products and types of meat to make small sliders. Once an object that can be heated is placed into one of the cooker’s cylinders and the machine is closed, the product is shown to begin steaming and heating the objects placed within. Mays displays the product’s anomalous properties by using it to grill meats including beef, chicken, fish, elk, kangaroo and yak. Mays also places solids such as sand or coal into the cooker, resulting in pressurized solids such as glass or diamonds. These are all placed within typical slider buns. After cooking every material provided, Mays consumes each slider made with no issues that would normally come from trying to consume the heated objects, and expresses glee over the taste of the ingredients. It is worth noting that the first appearance of this advertisement was aired six years after the death of host Billy Mays. 
> 
> **Product Properties:** When an item is placed into the cooker and the lid is closed, each cylinder projects an amount of heat towards the object needed to properly cause it to grill or solidify. Temperature testing has shown that the machine is capable of heating up to at least 27 million degrees Fahrenheit, roughly the same temperature as the core of the sun.

> **Date of Instance:** 10/29/2016- Replacing a showing of “It’s the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown” on a public broadcasting station in Sacramento, California.
> 
> **Product Advertised:** All-Purpose “Killer Kutter” Kitchen Knife
> 
> **Description of Advertisement:** Host “Chef Antonio” and guest famed chef Gordon Ramsay cook a medium-sized cheese pizza while discussing the Killer Kutter Kitchen Knife. The two chefs are shown using the product to cut several ingredients as well as demonstrating the knife’s anomalous properties by cleanly slicing through wooden cutting boards and steel blocks. Notably, at one point in the advertisement, Chef Antonio displays the cutting ability of the product by amputating Ramsay’s left hand. Ramsay screams, presumably in pain, but is calmed when Chef Antonio announces that the pizza is finished cooking. The two discuss the product’s sharp edge while consuming the pizza (the wound on Ramsay's hand appears to have been cauterized by the knife) and the advertisement ends. When Mr. Ramsay was questioned about this event, he displayed no recollection of his appearance or product, and no damage to the chef’s hand was noted.
> 
> **Product Properties:** Knife works as described in advertisement, seemingly bisecting any object the blade touches when being used to slice or chop the object. This includes metals and items not shown in the advertisement, such as titanium, stone, and  ██████. Proposal to use an instance of this product to attempt termination of certain Beta or Gamma-class anomalies is being considered.

> **Date of Instance:** 1/15/17- Replacing two episodes of children's programming on Cartoon Network in Jackson Hole, Wyoming.
> 
> **Product Advertised:** Perma-Seal Brand Sealing Solution
> 
> **Description of Advertisement:** Television salesman Phil Swift promotes the many uses of 'Perma-Seal Sealing Solution', illustrating how it can be used to seal leaking faucets, cracks in porcelain, and even broken bones. In order to demonstrate the effectiveness of Perma-Seal, Phil calls a volunteer from the audience (which has never been seen in any instance of RPC-836 to date), who is revealed to be a young woman. A large glass dome is lowered onto the woman, and Phil appears to cover the dome with the product. The woman attempts to move the dome, but it does not budge. Phil stares at the woman in the dome as he explains that Perma-Seal can be used to keep guests in or out of certain areas. The woman at this time appears to be gasping for breath, and ramming her body in an attempt to break the glass. Approximately ten minutes pass before the woman falls to the floor, suffocated by the sealed dome. Phil continues to stare at the body for thirty seconds before the advertisement ends.The woman in the advertisement has since been identified as Maria Fernandes, a 27-year old banker living in Michigan. When questioned, Ms. Fernandes displayed no knowledge of the advertisement or her supposed death.
> 
> **Product Properties:** Any non-anomalous object covered with Perma-Seal will gain a massive amount of weight and density, and become extremely difficult to move or break. It is assumed that Perma-Seal tightens the molecular density of the affected substance. A proposal to re-engineer this product for use in Authority structures is in consideration.
[[/collapsible]]

As the public are also able to view these advertisements, it is possible for a subject to call into the program to request the product. Each instance of RPC-836 has a phone number which a subject can call to purchase the item on display. During these calls, the host will often talk and banter with the subject while constantly boasting about the effectiveness of the product and eventually informing the caller that the product is on the way to their location. By tracing back calls made to RPC-836 instances, agents have discovered that exactly twenty-four hours after placing the call, a package will appear next to the front door of the buyer with no return address containing the anomalous item of purchase. There has been no recorded instance of the package being delivered by any sort of postal service.

The following is a transcript of what is considered to be a standard advertisement displayed during an instance of RPC-836 appearing. This transcript was taken on June 21, 2018, at approximately 7 A.M on the Weather Channel. 

> *Advertisement begins. A large sound stage is shown, and camera focuses on a pedestal containing the product that the instance is attempting to sell- a small, orange, jellylike blob. Shortly after, a large, bald man wearing a green apron appears from behind the pedestal. Authority researchers have since been unable to identify this host.*
> 
> **Host:** Goooood morning, everybody! Hope you're having a great day! I'm your host for the program, *the sound cuts to static at this point*, and today we have something REALLY special to show you! Introducing the newest innovation from our laboratories- Impervi-Gel! 
> 
> *The camera zooms in on the orange blob as the host begins to speak again.*
> 
> **Host:** Have you ever had the unfortunate experience of being involved in an accident? Of course you have! But there are some accidents that even a bandage or a trip to the hospital won't fix. That's why there's Impervi-Gel! Just strap this little buddy on your wrist, and you're instantly safe from any accidents that come your way! 
> 
> *The camera pans back to the blob, which is now wiggling slightly.*
> 
> **Host:** Yes, the Impervi-Gel will shield you from any trips, slips, wounds, or deaths that may occur while you've got it with you! Now you never have to leave the house afraid again! But don't take our word for it- take theirs!
> 
> *The camera pans over to a previously unseen area of the sound stage, where two similar-looking men are standing fifteen feet apart from each other. One is wearing an orange bracelet around his wrist.*
> 
> **Host:** These two just can't seem to stay out of the way of trouble. Luckily, one of them purchased Impervi-Gel- now let's see what happens when danger strikes!
> 
> *Camera pans up to reveal two massive weights suspended above the two men. The host is seen crawling up to the top of the stage and cutting the two ropes with a large hunting knife.*
> 
> **Host:** And down they go! Now let's see how Impervi-Gel works to protect our lucky friend!
> 
> *Immediately before the weight makes contact with the first man, the orange bracelet around his wrist instantly forms into a cocoon-like structure around the man. When the weight lands on the structure, it slides off as if on a slope, causing no damage. The second man, however, is quickly crushed under the massive weight. A large puddle of blood and broken bones can be seen close to the weight's landing position. The camera focuses on the puddle for ten seconds before returning to the host. *
> 
> **Host:** Oh, poor fellow! If only he had an Impervi-Gel too! But there's no time to mourn him- we've got to explain to you all the other things this product can do!
> 
> *The host continues demonstrating all of the different hazards Impervi-Gel could prevent, including extreme temperatures, gunshots, knife wounds, and injuries sustained from ██████ ██████. These tests continue for ten minutes, with the man and the host constantly expressing surprise at the effectiveness of the item. This is interrupted when the host begins to discuss purchasing the product.*
> 
> **Host:** What's this? It seems like we have a buyer! May I have them on the line, please?
> 
> **Customer:** Hello! Am I on the air? My name is Agnes... I'm buying this as a gift for my grandson. He gets into all sorts of accidents, so I thought this might help him!
> 
> **Host:** Wonderful, Agnes! We've received your payment, and rest assured, your Impervi-Gel is on the way! Isn't  it a great product?
> 
> **Customer:** At first, I didn't think so... but the more I see of it, the more I like it! It's wonderful!
> 
> *Host and Customer continue to discuss the uses and benefits of the product for five minutes before customer hangs up.*
> 
> **Host:** Well, that's all the time we have folks. Remember: Stay safe with Impervi-Gel! This is *Unintelligible Static*, saying farewell for another day. But don't worry- we'll be back with another great product soon! Buy now, while you still can! Show us your thanks! We love all of you, so buy our products and love us back! Accept us! Why won't you accept-
> 
> *Camera cuts to static before normal programming resumes.*
