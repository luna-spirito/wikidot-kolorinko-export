[[span class="listresize"]]Memory Man[[/span]]

[[include component:rate | stars = none | decimal = show | translations=show | translationblock = [[image http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/component:icons/pt.png link="http://autoridaderpc-pt-br.wikidot.com/rpc-330"]] ]]

[[f<image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/component%3Aicons/beta-orange.png]]
**Registered Phenomena Code:** 330

**Object Class:** Beta-Orange (Pending ████████ classification. see Memorandum 330-GD))

**Hazard Types:** Organic Hazard, Sapient Hazard

**Containment Procedures:** RPC-330 is to be primarily contained in a standard humanoid containment cell at [[[Site-026]]], approximately 4.5 meters long and 4.5 meters wide. The containment cell is to be considered a primary target to defend in the event of a raid by a hostile GoI.

Testing on RPC-330 must be approved by Site Director Bechal. RPC-330 is permitted to request recreational items for their containment cell, but these requests must be within reason and approved by the Site Director.

[[collapsible show="Display Updated Containment Procedures (Level Three or higher Clearance required)" hide="Access Granted"]]

Due to RPC-330's cooperation and willingness to assist the Authority in Loci Procedures, RPC-330 has been granted limited Authority clearance level 4 in between Loci procedures. RPC-330 is permitted, under the supervision of at least two Authority guards at all times, to freely move around some areas of Site-026, including to the mess hall and staff break room.

Authority personnel are permitted during breaks to approach and converse with RPC-330, but are advised not to discuss subjects such as  RPC-330's abilities or involvement in any Loci Procedure. Unnecessarily upsetting RPC-330 is grounds for disciplinary action.  

Should the need for a Loci Procedure arise, RPC-330 is to be granted access to Authority records in order to cross-reference preexisting Authority information with any information recalled during the procedure. If classified information is recalled, RPC-330 has agreed to allow Class-A amnestics to be used in order to prevent any possible leaks. Therapy may also be provided depending on the nature of the memories recalled if they are particularly traumatic to RPC-330. RPC-330 has been classified as an asset to MST Sierra-8 'Sundowners' in the Investigatory Division during Loci Procedures.
[[/collapsible]]

**Description:** RPC-330 is a Caucasian male, formerly known as Michael Belrose, approximately 35 years of age, 72.5 kilograms in weight, and 2 meters in height. 

RPC-330 has displayed the ability to recall the memories of any deceased subject with near-perfect accuracy if provided a name, regardless of whether RPC-330 has made contact with the subject or not. This anomalous recollection has been tested and shown to allow RPC-330 to recall events and information known only by the subject, as well as bypass any mental blockers or cognitohazards used to conceal them. RPC-330 is able to take amnestics to temporarily forget a memory, but can recall the memory if needed and provided with the target's identity once again.

RPC-330 has stated that during periods in which he is not focusing on retrieving specific memories, he can 'receive' memories from subjects who have previously been unknown to RPC-330 at random (there appears to be no specific order in which anonymous memories are recieved). However, if the subject's name is located during these memories, RPC-330 will be able to focus and recall specific memories from the individual. RPC-330 reports that he experiences both known and unknown individuals' memories at random when not concentrating, and is not in control of which memories he cycles between during these periods.

RPC-330 states that he has limited control over which memories he is able to access, but through a brief period of concentration can attempt to 'force' himself to recall the memories of a certain individual, provided RPC-330 is aware of the individual's name. This process is described as being “extremely painful” to RPC-330, though the procedure has no identified harmful after-effects.

RPC-330 first contacted the Authority on July 8, 2015, claiming that he was attacked in his home several days prior by unknown assailants and was able to discover the location of Site-026 by accessing the memory of Herman Scheving, a former Authority Researcher killed during a containment breach of RPC-███. After being spotted by Authority guards, RPC-330 agreed to turn himself in provided he received a chance to meet with Site Director Bechal and explain the reason for his intrusion.

[[collapsible show="Show Interview Log 330-01" hide="Hide"]]

> **Interviewed:** RPC-330
> 
> **Interviewer:** Site Director Bechal
> 
> **Foreword:** This interview was requested by RPC-330 upon initial discovery. Interview was granted under the supervision of four ASF members. 
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** The circumstances of your arrival are extremely bizarre... tell me, how were you able to recall Mr. Scheving's past and our location?
> 
> **RPC-330:** It's a long story, really, one I hope you'll listen to. I was cycling through my memories when I came across something strange- some old guy, gray hair, suit like yours. Saw his name tag too- I'm guessing he was your Herman. He got out of some kind of vehicle and headed towards this building out in the wilderness- I recognized the area because I used to go hiking in the hills over there. I think it was around midnight, judging by the sky?
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** That was indeed the beginning of his shift. But what did you mean earlier? About cycling through your memories?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Oh, I suppose I should explain that. See, I've got this weird problem- I guess you all might call it an 'anomaly'. I have lots of memories in my head- and almost none of them are mine. It feels like I'm some kind of storage unit for the things. I can learn a lot from them- like that guy's knowledge of how to get here.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** A storage unit? So you 'can 'absorb' people's memories?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Not exactly. They need to be dead first, from what I've gathered. The way I see it is this: memories need to go somewhere after their holder dies. I guess that somewhere is me. 
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Hmm... So you are able to recall the memories of deceased individuals?
> 
> **RPC-330:** I guess you could say that, provided I know their names and I can focus for a minute or two. Thing is, if I don't focus on it, the ones I recall are a bit... random. Sometimes they can be pointless, or sometimes, like in the case of old Herman, I can get lucky and remember some useful info. 
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** I see. And when did you first discover this ability?
> 
> **RPC-330:** It started when I was very young. It was a day after my uncle's funeral, and I started to begin thinking about memories I couldn't have possibly had. Things like getting married to my aunt, getting my first job... nothing a 6-year-old could have done. After a while I started remembering more and more from people I had known, from co-workers to random people I passed on the street. This ability to remember stuff from all over the world is pretty new, if I'm being honest.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** How has this ability affected your life? Is this what caused you to seek us out?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Let's just say it hasn't exactly paid off in dividends- especially when you can't remember which assignments are yours. With every memory I get, it gets more and more difficult to pull out the ones I need. Constantly losing focus doesn't tend to resonate well with employers, and so I found myself stuck in a dead-end apartment. Well, until the break-in.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Break-in? 
> 
> **RPC-330:** Yeah. Some goons broke into my place a few days ago looking for me. Usually I keep this whole 'memory storage' thing hidden, but I must have gotten drunk one night and spilled my secret- probably just my luck that those guys were listening. Whoever these guys were, they wanted my ability for God knows what, and a few goons in black stormed my apartment to try and get it one night. Called themselves 'Church of Malthus' or something like that, and tried to grab me when I came home from the welfare office. 
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** How did you escape?
> 
> **RPC-330:** One of the good things about this memory shit is sometimes you get lucky. In this case, I focused for a bit and accessed the memories of Jim Crawnigan, the guy who lived in that apartment before me- turns out he had a hidden crawlspace I managed to squeeze into. I decided to wait it out, and before more came, tried to find something, anything, that could help me get to a safe place. That's when I remembered Herman walking out to that strange building in the middle of nowhere... I figured that it would be a decent place to hide.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** I understand. Tell me, do you have any prior connections with this group? 
> 
> **RPC-330:** Never heard of them. Maybe accessed their memories once or twice by accident, I don't know. Whatever the reason, they're after me, and if you guys want, I could turn myself in for study or whatever in exchange for some safety. I didn't just grab that one moment from Herman, after all- I know what you guys do here. You study and keep strange stuff in this place- and I'm stuff that wants to be kept here.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** And why the Authority, of all places?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Think of it like this. I either come here and be put in a cell, or go there and get killed. I know the stuff you guys do, and from what I can tell, you're the good guys. At least, I hope so.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** After taking a lie-detector test, RPC-330 was voluntarily contained at Site-026 under special circumstances.

[[collapsible show="Show Interview Log 330-02" hide="Hide"]]
> **Interviewed:** RPC-330
> 
> **Interviewer:** Site Director Bechal
> 
> **Foreword:** The following interview was conducted approximately one month after RPC-330’s initial containment:
> 
> **<Begin Log>**
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Good afternoon, RPC-330. How are you faring?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Actually, not bad. Hell, I'm doing better here than I was back at my old dump. Mighty kind of you guys to bring me here. Feels more like a five-star hotel than a containment cell if you ask me.... if you don't count the guards.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Interesting. So you are enjoying your time here?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Why not? You guys have been great to me. I've been thinking of a way to return the favor, and I think I've got something. How about I help you out?
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** I'm sorry?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Well, I don't like asking for free stuff. So I wanted to repay you guys. See, I think we could work out a win-win situation. The Authority is always looking for information, but sometimes you can't find it, right? That's where I come in.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Can you explain?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Well, if what I hear around the cell is right, sometimes your men will disappear and die on missions and experiments. But I can regain the memory of what happened during that time, if you give me the information. 
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Are you asking us to divulge classified information to you?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Not necessarily. All I need is a name, and to know what I'm looking for. Then I can drag something up, and if you guys really are serious about keeping it hidden, I could always take some of those amnesia drugs you give to people. Think of it as my way of repaying you for all the hospitality.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** That is an interesting proposal, but would certainly be a tremendous risk.  We'd need to speak with the Global Directors about it, and even then there's a large chance it won't be allowed. 
> 
> **RPC-330:** I get it. But hey, if they do come around, I'm always here to help. It's the least I can do for you guys. You gave me a new lease on life- and if this damn ability can actually be useful to help the world, I'd be happy to use it for something good.
> 
> **<End Log>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** Following severe psychological and loyalty testing by order of Global Director ██, Proposal 330-37 was drafted allowing Authority personnel of Clearance Level 4 or higher to cooperate with RPC-330 for the purpose of gathering information.

[[collapsible show="Display Loci Procedure Protocol (Level 4 or higher Clearance required)" hide="Access Granted"]]

With cooperation from Global Director ██, and the willingness of RPC-330, the Loci Procedure may be used in order to gain insight into anomalies that have the ability to neutralize cameras or audio recorders placed in their radius. In accordance with Proposal 330-37, Researchers at Site-026 have obtained consent from several Global Directors to utilize the capabilities of RPC-330 for research and intelligence-gathering purposes.

During a Loci Procedure, RPC-330 is to be given the name and Clearance Level (if applicable) of any individual who is known to the Authority in order to attempt to gather information regarding anomalies, Groups of Interest, or other knowledge. After focusing for a short period of time, ranging from one to ten minutes (depending on the specificity of the memory recalled), RPC-330 can attempt to recall information that could be beneficial to the Authority. Any memory recalled determined to be useful to Authority security or research staff is to be recorded and cross-referenced to confirm the accuracy of these memories.

The following is a transcript of Loci Procedure 330-01, overseen by Site Director Bechal as requested by RPC-330. 

> **Interviewed:** RPC-330
> 
> **Interviewer:** Site Director Bechal
> 
> **Foreword:** After gaining approval from Global Director ██, RPC-330 was permitted to attempt a Loci Procedure in order to gain insight into the whereabouts of an outpost belonging to GoI "Church of Malthus" believed to be on the outskirts of Ontario, Canada. RPC-330 asked for a meeting with Site Director Bechal during the procedure.
> 
> **<Begin Log, 12:20 P.M>**
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** RPC-330, are you certain you wish to go through with this so quickly? The Global Directors have said that we could start with something smaller if you'd prefer...
> 
> **RPC-330:** No thanks. I've always been the sort of guy to go right into the heavy situations. Better to just jump in the pool rather than put a foot in at a time, you know? Besides, if I really do see something that would scar me for life, you guys can just wipe it from my memory, can't you? Let's do it.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** ....I see. Always the optimistic one, aren't you? Shall we get started?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Whenever you're ready.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Very well. Your target is Jeremy ██████, Church of Malthus Agent. Killed by Authority agents on June 3rd of this year. We're looking for any information about Church of Malthus outposts nearby. Think you can dig something up?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Hmm... that's pretty specific. It might take a bit, but I think I could find something.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Take your time.
> 
> //(RPC-330 appears to rub his forehead and concentrate for nine minutes. At approximately 12:30 P.M, RPC-330 screams and appears to be in pain.)//
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** RPC-330! What's wrong?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Holy shit. Holy fucking shit. 
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Have you found anything about the outpost?
> 
> **RPC-330:** You might say that. I haven't got the coordinates just yet, but inside the place.... Jesus Christ. I've been around this site for a bit, and I haven't seen ANYTHING like this.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** What do you see?
> 
> **RPC-330:** Things. Horrible, terrible things I can't describe. Larvae of some sort, twisted layers of flesh and bone.... and humans. Well, I think they were at one point. Now they're mounds of meat and slime and god knows what else... and they're still alive. Looking at it, I can only imagine what else went down there that this poor sap couldn't see...... Oh god, it's horrific. I don't know if I can keep going.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Please try to focus. We need those coordinates. I'm sure I can talk to someone about giving you amnestics....
> 
> **RPC-330:** Alright, I'll keep trying. I think I got something- just give me a minute. It's a little fuzzy, but from what I'm gathering... ███ N, -███.██? 
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Are you sure?
> 
> (RPC-330 nods, still visibly disturbed)
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** I see... thank you. 
> 
> **RPC-330:** I need to stop. Please. I gave you what you needed....I can't be in here any longer. I could swear I just heard one of those things scream... from where I'm terrified to think of. I don't care what else you want- I need to leave. NOW.
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Very well. Whatever you wish. 
> 
> //(RPC-330 appears to stop concentrating, and sits in silence for a short time before speaking again.)//
> 
> **RPC-330:** Doc... be honest with me. Are there... are there more people like this? Ones even you guys can't hunt down?
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:**...Yes, unfortunately. That is why we need you here. I believe that you could be the key to stopping them. That is, if you are willing to work with us.
> 
> **RPC-330:**.... I guess so. If this is how I need to repay you guys for all the kindness the Authority has given me, then so be it. Now that I see what you guys are up against.... I think I can be a little more help. 
> 
> **Site Director Bechal:** Thank you for your cooperation, RPC-330.
> 
> **RPC-330:** Now.... about those amnestics....

> **<End Log, 12:45 P.M>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** After sending several Mobile Strike Forces to the geographic coordinates recalled by RPC-330, a raid on the area was conducted, leading to the capture and detainment of several Church of Malthus agents and larval stages of several known biological anomalies, including RPC-███ instances.
> 
> At the request of Site Director Bechal, RPC-330 was given Class-A amnestics following the interview. Permission to apply amnestics after any Loci Procedure is pending approval.
