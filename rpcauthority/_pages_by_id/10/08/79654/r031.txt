**Containment Protocols:** RPC-094 is to be kept in a humanoid cell at Site-088, comprised of stainless steel walls at least one (1) meter thick. The cell is to be monitored by infrared and optical cameras at all times, and any change in behavior by RPC-094 is to be reported to current Lead Researcher ████. RPC-094 does not require sustenance, so there is no need for RPC-094 to be given food and water, and interaction outside of testing is to be kept to an absolute minimum. In the event of a containment breach, Rapid Response Team "Caesar's Legion" are to be dispatched to re-secure RPC-094.

**Description:** RPC-094 is a statue seemingly made out of marble, although the composition appears to be fluid[[footnote]] It is currently suspected that RPC-094 is comprised of non-newtonian fluid, although how RPC-094 is able to keep itself solid is still speculated upon. [[/footnote]], somewhat organic in nature, and is unknown to the Authority at this time due to RPC-094's violent nature. Despite resistance to damage, RPC-094 can appear to "bruise," resulting in the injured area to appear more aged than surrounding "skin." Currently, RPC-094 takes the form of a standard Italian Infantryman, albeit with the face being perfectly smooth and no properties whatsoever (lack of eyes, nose, mouth, etc.), and a roman //pilus// attached to its backside. RPC-094 has been observed to “look” in seemingly random directions at times. RPC-094 is known to become exceedingly hostile to non-italian Europeans, especially those of Germanic or British descent, to the point of attacking without provocation. The reaction has also been observed to occur with those of Iranian or middle-eastern descent, as shown by the deaths of Dr. ██████ and Lieutenant ██████ during a containment breach 094-13-6-19██. The //pilus// used as a weapon, despite the marble-like appearance, appears to have no need for sharpening, and has been observed to pierce ██ cm of steel. RPC-094 does not speak, or communicate with personnel in any way. RPC-094 is extremely active, walking across the southern wall of it's containment cell and prodding at the walls with it's //pilus//.

**Incident Log:**

Incident 037-088-██:

At approximately 10:37 AM local time, Site-088 experienced mass and widespread containment breaches, resulting in RPC-094's escape. RPC-094 was shown to be very capable in combat, resulting in heavy loses to RRT "Caesar's Legion". RPC-094 eventually broke out of Site-088, and moved south at high speeds, running at a maximum recorded speed of ███ KPH. RPC-094 caused extensive damage to German infrastructure, which was passed off as a terrorist attack by Authority Misinformation Efforts. RPC-094 showed extensive resistance to damage, resisting attacks until it reached the Czech border until noticeable injury was expressed. Due to renewed efforts by the RRT, RPC-094 was captured 10 kilometers southeast of Regensburg, and recontained successfully. 

Note: //After Incident 037-088-██, we've elected to construct an OL-Site for RPC-094 where it was recovered, to reduce the chance of further damage to the Authority and civilian populations. Covering that up was our largest misinformation campaign in Central Europe, and with over ███ casualties, it has been deemed well worth the risk.//

-Site Director ██████

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-094 is to be kept in a humanoid cell at OL-Site-094, modified to have a 3.5 centimeter thick glass window overlooking Capitoline Hill, in Rome. The cell is to be monitored by infrared and optical cameras at all times and any change in behavior by RPC-094 is to be reported to current Lead Researcher ████. --All personnel assigned to RPC-094 are to be of Italian descent--. See addendum 094-27-6-██. RPC-094 does not require sustenance, so there is no need for RPC-094 to be given food and water, and interaction outside of testing and interviews is to be kept to an absolute minimum. Despite RPC-094's greater docility in its current conditions, [*http://rpcauthority.wikidot.com/authority-security-force ASF] Rapid Response Team "Caesar's Legion" are to be on standby at all times, ready to be dispatched to re-secure RPC-094 if it ever breaches containment.
 
**Description:** RPC-094 is a statue seemingly made out of marble, although the composition appears to be fluid, somewhat organic in nature, and is unknown to the Authority at this time due to RPC-094's --violent nature-- reluctance to cooperate. Despite resistance to damage, RPC-094 can appear to "bruise," resulting in the injured area to appear more aged than surrounding "skin." RPC-094 currently takes the form of a standard Italian Infantryman, albeit with the face being perfectly smooth and no properties whatsoever (lack of eyes, nose, mouth, etc.), and a roman //pilus// attached to its backside. --RPC-094 has been observed to “look” in seemingly random directions at times-- RPC-094, when active, faces towards the Capital of what it deems the “greatest threat” to Rome (see interview log 094-27-6-██). RPC-094 is known to become exceedingly hostile to non-italian Europeans, especially those of Germanic or British descent, to the point of attacking without provocation. The reaction has also been observed to occur with those of Iranian or middle-eastern descent, as shown by the deaths of Dr. ██████ and Lieutenant ██████ during a containment breach 094-13-6-19██. The //pilus// used as a weapon, despite the marble-like appearance, appears to have no need for sharpening, and has been observed to pierce ██ cm of steel. --RPC-094 does not speak, or communicate with personnel in any way.-- See interview log 094-27-6-██. RPC-094 has been shown to have knowledge of Italian, Latin, and Ancient Chinese. The degree of its fluency in these languages other than Italian is yet to be seen, as RPC-094 interjects Latin into its regular Italian speech habitually, and has only spoken in Ancient Chinese once. RPC-094 can be goaded into conversation when in a docile or passive state, which is to be acted upon promptly for informative potential.

**Addendum:** 

Addendum 094-27-6-██

In violation of protocol, Junior Researcher Yang, a shadow of former Lead Researcher ███████ and of Chinese descent, was present during observation on ██, ██, 20██. This lead to the first recorded communication from RPC-094, transcribed below and translated from Italian.

[[collapsible show="Show Interview Log 094-27-6-██ -- LEVEL 2R SECURITY CLEARANCE REQUIRED: INPUT CREDENTIALS" hide="CREDENTIALS ACCEPTED -- YOUR ACCESS HAS BEEN LOGGED"]]

> **Interviewed:** RPC-094
> 
> **Interviewer:** Junior Researcher Yang and Lead Researcher ███████
> 
> **Foreword:** Junior Researcher Yang and Lead Researcher ███████ enter observation room, security feeds show RPC-094 is in a docile state, looking downwards and kneeling.
> 
> **<Begin Log, 16:50 GMT+2>**
> 
> **Lead Researcher ███████:** So this is where we observe the RPC, through the glass, *knocking heard as Lead Researcher ███████ taps the glass* bulletproof, of course.
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang:** Is it safe? The reports said the spear could cut through steel.
> 
> //RPC-094 is observed to tilt its head on security footage, but this goes unnoticed by the two researchers.//
> 
> **Lead Researcher ███████:** A //pilus//, actually, but yes, it can. 
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang:** If it is hostile to non-italians, couldn’t it just kill us anyway? I do not see why it could not.
> 
> **RPC-094:** I am a warrior, //Sericanum//[[footnote]] Determined to be based of the Latin word //Serica//, which was used to describe the Han Dynasty. Lit. //land of silk//. [[/footnote]], not a murderer.
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang and Lead Researcher ███████** are observed to jolt upright, before turning to RPC-094
> 
> **Lead Researcher ███████:** Wait, you can speak?
> 
> //RPC-094 remains silent//
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang:** He asked you a question, 094.
> 
> **RPC-094:** I do not waste Jupiter’s gift of voice on barbaris, man of the East. You would be wise not to do the same.
> 
> //Lead Researcher ███████ motions to Junior Researcher Yang to continue conversation with RPC-094 while scrambling to find a notepad in his desk. The sound is discernible over the footage.//
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang:** Er… (he takes a pause for a moment) Then why talk to me? I am not a roman at all.
> 
> **RPC-094:** No, you are not. Any person can see that, including me.
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang:** But you have no eyes?
> 
> **RPC-094:** The gods granted me sight, //Sericanum.//
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang:** You keep calling me serica- sericahnoo- seri-
> 
> **RPC-094:** //Sericanum//. Your people come from //Serica//, or //Cina//, as they say in these times. Your ancestors called us //Dàqín//.
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang:** You know of China?
> 
> **RPC-094:** I have been made to gaze to Peking and [REDACTED] recently. Such a tragic fall from Empire.
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang:** Gaze? Why do you look at China?
> 
> **RPC-094** I look at the threats.
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang:** Threats?
> 
> **RPC-094:** The greatest to Rome and Her people.
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang:** Why do you do that? Why just look, and not speak?
> 
> **RPC-094:** I did. I once used my voice to serve Rome, until the //Barbarium// came.
> 
> **Junior Researcher Yang:** What do you mean by the //Barbarium//?
> 
> **RPC-094:** He came from //Germania//, and I failed in my duty. The Gods punished me for that, and now I can only watch, and never warn. The graecum did not take me to their homeland, and they too fell to the eastern hoar-
> 
> //RPC-094 stands up and abruptly faces north, and becomes unresponsive to all further attempts at communication//
> 
> **<End Log, [16:57 GMT+2]>**
> 
> **Closing Statement:** RPC-094 continued facing Northwards for approximately █ months before turning around and facing south. All attempts to communicate with RPC-094 during this time have failed. Lead Researcher ███████ reprimanded for violation of containment procedures.

Note: //I believe that RPC-094 is only open to communication during a “Docile” state, where it deems that there aren’t any threats to Rome. It looks like it will only respond to “Romans” and Sericanum, and not other races. We will need to look into a full psychological profile of RPC-094 ASAP, but given the current political climate in Italy, that might take a while. I’m very curious as to whom “the gods” are. Does it mean the old Roman Deities? If so, do the corresponding Greek legends have merit? That might explain some other anomalous objects in our possession if we can get to the bottom of this. And it talking about [REDACTED]... we might have some questions for our friends in the [http://www.rpc-wiki.net/unaac-hub committee]. We have a lot of research ahead of us.//
