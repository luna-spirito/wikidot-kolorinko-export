[[span class="listresize" style="font-size: 0px; line-height: 0px;"]]Shower Boi[[/span]]

[[include component:rate title=rpc-003-J]]

[[f<image http://www.rpc-wiki.net/local--files/component:icons/beta-white.png]]

**Registered Phenomena Code:** 003-J

**Object Class:** Beta-White --because the object is being a fucking beta about it--

**Hazard Types:** Aquatic Hazard, Organic Hazard, Sapient Hazard, Chronological Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-003-J is free to roam Site-██ with the exclusion of restrooms containing showers and/or hair hygiene products. If, however the entity enters restricted areas it is to be insulted[[footnote]]It is prefered to mention the entity's hair, its mother or the fact that it really sucks at strategy games despite playing it for hours on end.[[/footnote]] or in extreme cases slapped with a rolled-up towel until it leaves. At least two security guards armed with deodorant are to be stationed outside of RPC-003-J's bathroom[[footnote]]Which is also my bathroom, but the entity seems to forget about it[[/footnote]] after RPC-003-J finishes its work and returns to its --filthy fucking cave he calls a room-- dwelling. The security guards are to spray the entity with the content of their deodorant cans every time the entity leaves the shower.

**Description:** RPC-003-J is an --ape-like-- humanoid with several anomalous properties. The entity is 1,70m --in circumference-- tall, weighs 98kg making it so obese that it is theorized to be an anomaly in itself. 
The entity shows an almost sexual fascination with hygiene products and uses them profusely without noticeable effect.[[footnote]] It is theorized that during the usage of a hygiene product or when water coming out of a showerhead contacts the entity a localized Space-Time anomaly takes place on the entity's skin which temporarily turns its sweat glands into portals leading to a dimension full of horse sweat and pig tallow, which seep out onto the entity's skin[[/footnote]]
Its primary anomalous property is the ability to spend exorbiant amounts of time in the shower of up to two (2) --entire goddamn-- hours. It is theorized that RPC-003-J creates a space-time bubble when under the shower which disrupts the flow of time, making it pass slower inside. This is confirmed by the fact that when asked not to spend so much time in the bathroom, the entity shows confusion and claims to be spending "15 minutes max".
On the --frequently occuring-- occasion that the entity takes a shower a collection of items, hereafter reffered to as RPC-003-01-J spontaneously manifest in the siphon of the shower. Instances of RPC-003-01-J take the form of among others: human scalp, pubic and armpit hair; trace amounts of urine and feces; shame; dandruff and [REDACTED]. They display a cognitohazardous effect on RPC-003-J, as when confronted about them, the entity claims that he "doesn't know what you're talking about" even under pressure and threats.[[footnote]]To this date the entity was threatened with: 
* Locking the bathroom and not giving him a spare key
* Throwing his personal items out of the window
* Telling the landlord about it
* Me kicking his fucking ass
[[/footnote]] 
In the circumstance that RPC-003-J's flatmate[[footnote]]Me, that is[[/footnote]] attempts to utilize the bathroom while having limited time to spare, the entity senses his intentions and immediately scrambles to reach the shower. Multiple tests have shown that in this state the entity picks up speeds almost as high as 35 km/h. To date all attempts to reach the bathroom quicker than the entity have ended in failure. The amount of time RPC-003-J spends in the shower is proportional to the importance of the event that the victim has to take part in.[[footnote]]During one such incident the entity spent one hour and fourty seven minutes (1:47) in the bathroom, trapping the victim's keys inside. This caused the victim to miss a meeting with --the love of his life-- an acquaintance, ruining the individual's chance to --find happiness-- gain valuable assets. This incident ultimately ended with a Gamma Class Dominance Shift scenario within the victim's mind, 
mentally destabilizing him for an unspecified, albeit long, number of years.[[/footnote]]

The entity, followed by Researcher Kowalski, headed to the bathroom. After an unspecified time, the entity left the shower without RPC-003-02-J. The item's whereabouts are currently unknown, but are theorized to be --eaten by that fucking slob, for God's sake, he really sprayed a water-repellant on a sandwich and ATE IT IN THE SHOWER-- consumed by RPC-003-J.
