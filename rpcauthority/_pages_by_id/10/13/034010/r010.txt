**Containment Protocols:** 3 RPC-630 instances must be stored on Site-031-1 for research purposes and seeds are to be kept in cold storage on Sites worldwide in the event of sudden radioactive contamination.

For nourishment purposes, RPC-630 instances are to be planted near or above radiation sources derived from elements with a half-life of 10 years or less,[[footnote]]The half-life of a substance is the time it takes for radioactive decay to make the substance lose half its original mass.[[/footnote]] water/dirt contaminated with radiation, toxic elements, etc.

In the case of an RPC-630 E-01 Event, the generated RPC-630-2 seeds are to be collected and sent to normal agricultural farms and the non-anomalous RPC-630 instances to be sent to normal greenhouses stores. Gel harvested from the remaining leaves can be safely sold to cosmetic and pharmaceutical manufacturers.

Personnel around the world are to keep constant vigilance in natural and/or artificial radioactive zones to check for RPC-630. Personal is strictly advised to avoid consuming the gel generate from RPC-630 and the water from RPC-630-EX in radioactive filled zones. Only after post extraction analysis can both items be consumed safely

On the event that members of the Descendants of Yggdrasil are observed planting RPC-630 instances in the aforementioned areas, they are to be immediately detained fand have their instances of RPC-630 confiscated. Planted RPC-630 instances are to be removed from the area.

**Description:** RPC-630 is the designation for a group of seeds functionally similar to those of a variety of succulent plants, most commonly //Crassula ovata//[[footnote]]Jade plant or "lucky plant".[[/footnote]], //Aloe polyphylla//,[[footnote]]Spiral Aloe.[[/footnote]] //Aloe vera//, and //Astroloba tenax//[[footnote]]The "Prince Albert" plant.[[/footnote]].

When planted in normal conditions, RPC-630 will generate non-anomalous instances of the aforementioned plants. However, when RPC-630 is planted in zones with more than 5,000 millirems[[footnote]]5,000 millirems is the maximum support of radiation for an adult human body.[[/footnote]] its germination cycle will be completed in a period of 3 hours. Depending on the levels of radiation in the area RPC-630's seedling may grow up to 150% larger than their typical size.

During its anomalous growth cycle, RPC-630 will start to radiosynthesize[[footnote]]A process on which a plant transforms radiation into energy.[[/footnote]] said radioactive materials as nutrients. Seedlings will extend their abnormally large root structures towards any nearby radiation-contaminated water source.[[footnote]]Succulents generally grow notably short roots.[[/footnote]] The fertilization of the plant appears to depend on the amount of radiation absorbed, leading to the unnatural bearing of fruits designated RPC-630-2 at the tip of the leaves, and to a further 630-1 Event. 

The seeds generated from RPC-630-2 are identical to those from the order //eurosidae//, more specifically the //fabidae// group, including apple, pear, banana and other mundane fruit trees. These seeds have no anomalous properties and show a lack of radioactive elements. Most RPC-630 entities are slightly larger than average fruit sampled from mundane non-anomalous instances.

After a period of time, if all the radiation of the area has been absorbed into RPC-630 or RPC-630 has grown to a more bloated size, event RPC-630-E1 will begin.

[[collapsible show="Show RPC-630 Event-01" hide="- Credentials Accepted, displaying Event log."]]
Once the life cycle of RPC-630 comes to an end or the plant absorbed enough radiation, RPC-630 will start to become translucent, with RPC-630-2 instances increasing in size. Records indicate that given the conditions, some RPC-630-2 instances can grow up to 1 meter in diameter and 1.30 in length.

The roots of the plant will become solid and strong enough to support the weight of the now enormous 630-2 instances, with the branches of RPC-630 growing at a rapid rate. Once RPC-630 has grown double its original size, all 630-2 instances and a few prominent leaves of RPC-630 instance will naturally explode, spraying seeds (only if RPC-630-2 instances are present) and leaf remains around a radius of 7 to 12 meters.

One minute after the explosion RPC-630 will turn the remaining leaves into a form of solid plant matter, turning from RPC-630 into RPC-630-EX. At the top of RPC-630-EX, a leaf cupula can be located, where all the remaining water absorbed by RPC-630 will be located. Studies performed in the water after extraction has proven safe for consumption and provide minimal radiation levels.

Living entities in close proximity with RPC-630 at the moment of detonation would result in potentially injures and/or death. In testing, any human mannequin left within 2 meters of an RPC-630 instance undergoing an RPC-630 event had signs of severe injury, with Protection personnel remarking upon the similarity of injuries to those caused by fragmentation grenades.

If RPC-630 explodes in an area with more than 5000 millirems or overly contaminated area with radiation or toxic elements, the scattered RPC-630-2 seeds and remainings from the original RPC-630 will grow into new RPC-630 instances, repeating the process until radiation levels in the area become safe.

**Addendums:**

[[collapsible show="Descendants of Yggdrasil acquisition of RPC-630" hide="- Credentials Accepted, displaying log."]]
RPC-630 was initially conceived by Nucorp as an organic radiation eater, taking 15 years of study and research to create a test batch. Nucorp personnel were delivering instances of RPC-630 for testing as a new possible Theta class anomaly. Authority personnel were dispatched in armored vans to carry containers of RPC-630 and various low yield radioactive elements and isotopes. Testing of RPC-630 was organized on a remote section of Colombian grasslands previously used for testing of radioactive weaponry.

After arrival on the initial point of testing, the radioactive element containers were lowered and checked for any type of radioactive leakage. The containers of RPC-630 were left secured and organized by the security personal and development team. The zone around the area of testing was quickly scanned for any anomalous instances around the area in general. The only inconsistencies found were types of small subterranean tunnels around the testing grounds.

Video reports and voice recordings of the site indicated a minor success in the recollection of radioactive materials and environmental contamination. While a not groundbreaking achievement as the Nucorp agents expected, they concluded the test and ordered the security guards to store the remaining containers of RPC-630 back into the van to transport to Site-031-1.

An emergency broadcast was sent to Site-031-1, with a short message from Captain Millers reporting the theft of RPC-630 and other materials in the testing zone.

> //We're requesting for immediate backup! The testing area was infiltrated by an unknown group that is escaping right now!//
> 
> //Casualties are unknown and they have taken all the nuclear material and most containers of RPC-630.//

When Authority agents arrived at the testing site, most of the security guards keeping the containers of RPC-630 were found unconscious and tied together with vines. All radios, weapons, helmets and armor were removed and disposed of.

The vehicles were found picked clean of the nuclear materials with no signs of forced entry clear. The main van, however, was opened with the main key with half of the RPC-630 containers and a single note remaining.

> We are sorry for stealing these precious materials, but after watching the test of today we wanted to see if this little green guy can grow and be more useful.
> 
> Don’t worry about us or the agents, we left you half of the seeds so you can keep testing!
> 
> High regards
> -Lúðvík Jóhannsson

Captain Millers was found outside the parking lot, unconscious.

All Nucorp and Authority personnel were sent to Site-031-1 for medical care; to the surprise of the medical staff, no wounds were found. After the incident, all Nucorp personnel were dismissed and the remaining RPC-630 instances were temporarily contained for research and protection purposes.

[[collapsible show="Addendum-01: Re-discovery of RPC-630" hide="- Credentials accepted, displaying."]]
Modified varients of RPC-630 were first sighted in highly radioactive areas across the world, notably in the areas across the Chernobyl, the Fukushima power plant and the Goldsboro swamp area with witnesses and camera footage depicting 2 people in hazmat suits planting seeds of RPC-630 instances before rapidly growing into maturity. All RPC-630 instances were removed before any RPC-630 event occurred.

Authority agents stepped into action when an RPC-630-E1 event was recorded in the abandoned residential area of Pripyat. No deaths were recorded but 6 civilians were injured in the explosion. To avoid a possible leakage of information to the public, Authority agents contacted the forest rangers regarding the tourist area and confiscated all electronic devices and explained that it was some type of “mutation in the flora that shouldn’t be approached".

MST-Oscar-35 "Green Health" was dispatched from its base of operations in Site-031-2 to attend the newfound anomaly. When the team arrived at the abandoned civilian complex, various small instances of RPC-630 were found alongside with various expired fruits of different sizes. Because of the at-the-time unknown resemblance to RPC-630 with its original Nucorp counterpart, a minor containment area was placed, effectively closing the tourist area of Pripyat.

Nucorp agents were called to the containment area for inspection relating to the link between the newly found RPC-630 instances and the original Nucorp variant. Direct studies in the density of radiation absorption and consistency of the plant concluded that this new plant was RPC-630. After inspection reports of the small instances of RPC-630 indicated a natural evolution in relation to radiation eating and the reproduction of consumable fruit.

Days after the containment of RPC-630 various members of the Descendants of Yggdrasil were sighted in the forest of Pripyat planting more instances of RPC-630. They were captured and sent to the place of containment to interrogate in relationship with the creation and modification of the new strain of RPC-630.

After interviews and elaborated explanations, they explained that they saw the plant being useful for the restoration of human habitats and decided to modify it to fit the purpose of cleaning and “give birth to a nuclear fruit”. Yggdrasil members explained that the detonation part of RPC-630 was not on purpose and it was more of an evolutionary step that the plant naturally took.

Because of the success of the interview, the Yggdrasil members were left free but their equipment confiscated for future testing and containment. 2 notes from the PoI “Pétur Lúðvík Jóhannsson” were found in one of the bags of the captured members, one for the skirmishing group and the last one directed towards the authority.
