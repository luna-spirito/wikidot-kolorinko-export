**Established:** ██ March, 19██.

**Front Cover:** Water Reclamation Plant

**Location:** Kálfborgarárvatn, Iceland, 38.0 km Southwest of Reykjahlíð

**Codename:** "Crossroads"

**Hex-code:** [23]

**Site Occupation:** Containment, Research, Development, MSF & ASF Housing, Administrative, ICRU "Frávik" Division

**Division Jurisdiction:** General

**Total Area:** 19.4 km^^2^^

**Background Information:** OL Site-035 is currently built around the remains of the Black Lake Technical Institute. The campus came under Authority control following Incident A8-035. Due to the incredibly anomalous state of the campus, access of all personnel is restricted at the discretion of Site Director Magnússon. The facility is powered by the five AMOS Generators built around the Sub-Basement J11 Rift.

In order to effectively contain the constantly shifting realities that now exist within the campus, Site-035 has had over ██ expansions within the past decade. The Site has now nearly expanded to twice the size of the original institution, and is still getting larger.

Aside from containing the remains of BTI, Site-035 is also the foremost facility for research into alternate realities, metaphysics, and ontology. This can be done without much difficulty at Site-035 due to the large number of realities of various Anderson Constants which intersect the campus. Over 50 VMLs (Variable Modular Labs) have been installed in the "Safe" areas of the institution for the purposes of observing the realities present in the area. Research from Site-035 has already been productive, assisting in the creation of the Anderson Coherency Scale (a detailed summary of which can be found  [[[http://www.rpc-wiki.net/acs-guide | here]]]).

Research in the former Sherman Auditorium and Development Offices of the campus are strictly prohibited due to a majority of the realities present there being of Class ACS-1 or lower.

[[=]]
+ **Site Protocols**
[[/=]]
-----

[[collapsible show="Show Protocol #035-1 'Líkklæði'" hide="Access granted"]]
OL Site-035 personnel are permitted to utilize any means necessary to keep the Site's activities hidden from nearby civilian populations, particularly the community of Reykjahlíð. The Water Reclamation Plant front must be maintained in any interaction with a non-authorized individual. Air support is to be immediately utilized in the event that any visibly anomalous phenomena occur high enough above the Site to be seen by adjacent population centers.

Lethal force is permitted to deter any non-authorized individual attempting to breach OL Site-035 Security. Should the trespasser be caught alive, they are to be detained and immediately extradited to Site-███ for CSD processing.
[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="Show Protocol #035-2 'Wayfinder'" hide="Access granted"]]
[[include component:image-block name= arches| caption= Photograph of the arches outside Building-G7 appearing in █████, Brazil. | width=200px]]

The 'Wayfinder' team are to monitor satellite and news feeds for any instance of a section of the BTI campus appearing outside the confines of Site-035. If possible, any instance should be acquired and returned to Site-035, though if this method is deemed too costly the demolition of the structure in question will suffice.

'Wayfinder' wil be given the clearance required to obtain pieces of the campus should they appear in a restricted or high-clearance area under Authority Control.
[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="Show Protocol #035-3 'Panopticon'" hide="Access Granted"]]
The research staff of Site-035 are to closely monitor the rifts in case of non-baseline activity, such as the ejecting of objects or entities into our reality. In the event of this, given an object is not dangerous enough that an immediate return to its reality of origin is required, anomalous objects emitted from the rifts should be cataloged and contained in the Containment Wing of Site-035. Containment Protocols of each object will be deliberated by Site Administration on a per-object basis.

Any objects ejected from a rift which accesses an ACS-1 Class reality are restricted from containment and are required to be returned to their reality of origin, regardless of any perceived level of danger.
[[/collapsible]]

[[collapsible show="Show Protocol #035-4 'Jonah'" hide="Access Granted"]]
In the event that an entity emitted from the rifts is able to overwhelm Site-035 Security, Site Director Magnússon must be contacted directly in order to request higher Authority officials for assistance as soon as possible. In the meantime, the Security Perimeter is to be evacuated and remaining Security personnel are to be assigned to protect the nearby civilian population from the entity. If with Authority assistance Site-035 personnel are able to significantly subdue the entity, it may be held in the Containment Wing to be studied for a short time before being returned to its reality of origin.

If an emitted entity overwhelms both Site Security as well as any reinforcements the Authority can muster, the situation is officially no longer the responsibility of OL Site-035 and Global Director ██████ must be contacted directly in order to assess the threat. 
[[/collapsible]]

[[=]]
+ **Offices & Wings Section**
[[/=]]
-----

* **Administrative Wing** - This wing houses the offices of the Site-035 administators. This wing is off limits except for personnel with Level 5 security clearance, Head Researchers and/or admitted personnel.

* **Security Perimeter** - Maintained around the campus at a distance of 1 km. Should the campus itself expand due to its anomalous effects, the perimeter will have to expand accordingly. MSF are stationed at points along the perimeter to keep intruders out as well as to keep any entities from escaping the site. Should an entity overwhelm the available personnel, Security should refer to Protocol #035-4 'Jonah'.

* **Research and Laboratories Wing** - Comprised of established laboratories within the 'safe' sections of the campus and several VMLs built around - and in some cases within - the many rifts present on site. Research at Site-035 is primarily focused on metaphysics and ontology, utilizing the varied and ubiquitous rifts as a means to access alternate realities. The VMLs (designated "Direct Research" sectors) are off limits to researchers of clearance lower than Level-2 as well as researches that have not undergone the requisite Direct Research approval by Site Director Magnússen. 

* **Medical Wing** - Medical facilities are required to be present at OL Site-035 in accordance with Authority Safety Protocols B██-D█ due to the Site's ever-present safety hazards. The rifts can produce highly unsafe levels of structural instability on campus, though the entities emitted  from the rifts (especially those originating from Class ACS-2 or lower realities) pose the most potent threat to staff. The psychiatric ward has recently been expanded in response to a spike in profoundly negative cognitive effects reported by staff. Said effects have been hypothesized to have resulted from distortions of the Site's physical space by the rifts.

* **Containment Wing** - Considering no cataloged anomalies are contained at OL Site-035, the Containment Wing is much smaller compared to many other sites. This wing is mainly dedicated to containing anomalous objects and entities that have been ejected from the rifts, often in accordance with Protocol #035-3 'Panopticon' (or in more extreme situations, Protocol #035-4 'Jonah'). A list of currently contained anomalies can be found below.

[[tab 035-A]]
**Object Origin:** Rift B93 [ACS-3]

**Object Description:** Object is a standard 71.1cm by 91.4cm window set in a mahogany frame, painted white. Though the object can be manipulated and moved, when not acted upon by outside forces it will remain completely stationary in its location regardless of whether or not it should be physically possible for it to do so (it has been observed to consistently stay suspended in midair despite its orientation). When looking through the window, rather than seeing through to the other side of the glass the observer will instead perceive a vast, flat, reflective landscape (it has often been compared to a more pristine instance of a reflective salt flat). A single motionless figure is visible through the window as well - most accurately understood to be a large white-haired primate - at varying distances from the window. Several researchers have described the figure having pronounced red eyes, though there is no concrete evidence of this. 
[[/tab]]
[[tab 035-B]]
**Object Origin:** Rift K14 [ACS-3]

**Object Description:** Entity is an organism superficially resembling the //Tiburonia// genus of jellyfish, though considerably larger at 1.5 meters in height. Another anomalous property of the creature is its ability to suspend itself within any given area as if it were in water, keeping its shape regardless of its environment. Though it has shown itself to be capable of consuming human beings the creature is fairly easy to subdue and poses no immediate threat to staff as long as it is kept fed. It is able to regenerate its tentacles if any are damaged, and it will even periodically grow new tentacles. Close observation of the creature during the regeneration process have shown that the materials for each tentacle to not come from the creatures bell, but rather are discharged from an opening underneath the bell. Where the creatures tentacles actually come from are currently unknown, though research is ongoing.
[[/tab]]
[[tab 035-C]]
**Object Origin:** Rift E55 [ACS-3]

**Object Description:** Entity is an organism of the suborder Serpentes, measuring roughly 11 meters in length and weighing approximately 1,049 kg. 035-C differs from a baseline snake in that it displays the attributes of a standard lighthouse. Such attributes include: the end of 035-C's tail often being buried into the ground and kept anchored there, 035-C's scales colored in vibrant white and red stripes, and 035-C having an operational incandescent kerosene lighthouse lantern in the place of the average Serpentine head. How 035-C lives despite not having the means to take in nutrients is currently unknown.
[[/tab]]
[[tab 035-D]]
**Object Origin:** Rift T06 [ACS-2]

**Object Description:** Object is a small chunk of granite measuring 16 cm in length. The textures of the object's edges imply that it was removed from a much larger granite structure. On one side of the object a green human eye protrudes from the rock. Though the eye is organic, it does not respond to any stimuli and remains moistened despite being unable to blink. Any organism with functional ocular organs that keeps direct eye contact with the object for more that 5 seconds will immediately begin to emit cast iron chains of various sizes from every orifice on its body. During this process, a larger cast iron object will begin to form inside the organism's torso, slowly growing larger and displacing the organs as it expands. Once it grows large enough to escape the body cavity the object reveals itself to be in the shape of a //Salix alba// or "Willow" tree, from which the recently emitted chains hang. How the eye of 035-D is able induce the creation of such an object within organisms is currently unknown.
[[/tab]]
[[tab 035-E]]
**Object Origin:** Rift H29 [ACS-2]

**Object Description:** Entity is an humanoid organism in the exact likeness of Dr. ███████. While originally thought to be suffering from an immense malignant tumor, X-Rays have revealed 035-E's chest cavity to be entirely empty aside from a large arthopodal organism. Though the organism's species is unknown it has a decidedly insect-like appearance. Periodically, the creature will use 035-E's larynx and throat to emit an exceedingly intense ultrasound of a currently unknown frequency. Any native matter will animate and take on a distinctive insect-like arthropodal composition upon contact with the emitted sound wave. This effect is observed in all forms of matter - barring human beings and the wave's primary medium of travel - including samples comprised almost entirely of near mass-less particles such as photons. The wave has a unique effect on human beings. A human which comes in contact with the wave but cannot hear it (either due to a disability or a device impairing hearing) will experience the effects normally. Humans that are able to hear the frequency will begin to develop a similar creature to that within 035-E upon contact. This creature or the creature's species are currently hypothesized to be the reason the reality accessed by Rift H29 is physically composed entirely of insect-like organisms.
[[/tab]]

**Object Origin:** Y52 [ACS-1]

**Object Description:** Entity is a colonial organism in a liquid state. The single celled organisms which make up 035-█ undergo changes constantly as the mass changes shape, and are thus very difficult to identify. Though several researchers have noted the compounds of new cells much larger than the others forming on the entity's edge since its discovery. The organism itself is opaque and has a an exceedingly dark color and heavy consistency. It is generally compared to a thick crude oil or tar-like substance. The organisms most unique feature is its ability to expand rapidly without taking in any outside substances. Upon its discovery it was measured at roughly 5 liters but has since been measured around 23. Freezing the substance within ice is the only known method capable of halting its expansion. The organisms will emit vocalizations on rare occasions. At first they were believed to be indecipherable, however recent testing has shown it to be a heavily distorted and accented Icelandic dialect. The only known deciphered vocalizations are as follows:
* //Svarta hvalinn...//
* //Svarta hvalurinn verður að fæðast aftur...//
* //Það er pantað af áhorfandanum í hyldýpinu...//
Probes placed within the entity appear to show that an area much larger than could be contained within 035-█ can be entered through it. There also appear to be separate organisms in this area, --though how they are able to survive in an opaque liquid is still being researched--. Additionally, a second form of vocalizations can be heard within the cavity. These vocalizations are similar to that of whale songs, though they imply a creature entirely too large to exist on Earth. --Research on these vocalizations is ongoing as well.--

Due to the recent discovery of the Rift Y52 reality's exceptionally low Anderson Constant, further research into this object has been halted. It will remain in deep freeze containment until further notice.
