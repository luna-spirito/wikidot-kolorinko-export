+ **Overview**
[[/=]]
-----

[[f>image http://rpcauthority.wdfiles.com/local--files/x-ray-6-annulifers/Annullifier width="250px"]]

**Designation:** X-Ray-6

**Codename:** Annullifiers

**Garrison/HQ:** Site-002

**Role:** Data Expungement

**Motto:** //Venent, Vident, Oblitus// (They came, They saw, They forgot)

**Established:** 26^^th^^ March, 1919

[[==]]
**Description:** The primary assignment of X-Ray-6 operatives is to expunge any evidence of Authority's existence and its anomalies in the mass media and apprehending individuals with said evidence that is not involved within the anomalous community.

X-Ray-6 Operatives are mostly embedded into media offices and act as sleeper agents. They are given a new identity with full background, with previous education and family references fabricated by the Administrative Division. When the need arises, Expungement Operatives are to direct the media attention away from any Authority Operations and, in extreme cases, terminate individuals carrying sensitive information.

Mass amnesticization may be carried out by selected X-Ray-6 Operatives with direct permission from at least 3 (three) Global Directors. 

In ██/██/1961 following the building of the Berlin Wall (and later, the Cuban Missile Crisis), The Board of Global Directors temporarily repurposed X-Ray-6 as an espionage and counterintelligence task force due to their experience in corporate infiltration. The newly formed Counterintelligence Division operatives were assigned to minimize the risk of a global nuclear war that may hinder the containment of RPC Objects. 

The Board decommissioned X-Ray-6's Counterintelligence Division in 1991 following the collapse of the Soviet Union. However, there are plans to reactivate the Counterintelligence Division in late 2025.
[[/==]]
