**Registered Phenomena Code:** 095                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                           
**Object Class:** Alpha-Red

**Hazard Types:** Toxic Hazard, Mechanical Hazard                                                          
                                                                                     
**Containment Protocols:** RPC-095 is to be stored in a cushioned Alpha-Class Storage Unit at Site-004. Personnel with Level-2 clearance and above are allowed to conduct tests on RPC-095. Anyone who attempts to activate RPC-095 without permission will be apprehended and sent for questioning. --One armed security guard must accompany the tester at all times.-- No less than three armed security guards must accompany the tester at all times [See Testing Log 095-1].


**Description:** RPC-095 is a "Telechron Turn-About" clock manufactured by Telechron Electrical Clocks in 1954[[footnote]]It should be noted that the "Telechron Turn-About" model was discontinued in 1955 after it was discovered that the model had fewer lines for counting off of, making telling the time difficult[[/footnote]], with its time set at 4:15. Its anomalous properties manifest when an individual manually changes its time in a counter-clockwise direction by turning the bottom-right knob on its back, therefore changing it from its 4:15 standard time. Once RPC-095 is activated all objects within 20 meters will experience a form of degradation and disfigurement. RPC-095 will activate when placed outside or inside and can be activated by anyone. If RPC-095 is moved from its location while in an active state, the range of influence will stay the same distance but now have its source from its new location, potentially affecting different objects. Items will continue to degrade until they disintegrate as long as RPC-095 is off from its normal time. 

Data has shown that there are "distances" from RPC-095's normal state that affect the amount of time it takes for change to occur among objects, with 4:16 being the slowest time, and 4:14 being the fastest. Testing has shown that humans and animals are unaffected by RPC-095, however, clothing items worn during activation can be affected. When RPC-095 is returned to its normal time, all objects will anomalously repair themselves, but whatever disfigurement and distortion that has occurred will remain. Objects that are moved outside of the range during activation have been shown to not be affected by the restoration process.

RPC-095 was found after a news station in [REDACTED] published a story about a family that had found a "Magical Clock". The item was seized by Authority specialists and all individuals who had read the story and come into contact with the object were mind-wiped via Extergito Glyphs. The home of the family who had discovered RPC-095 was found to contain numerous rusted and warped items.

> **Closing Statement:** CSD-3091 recovered and had his mind subsequently wiped, where he was then returned to his cell for use in later tests.
