**Registered Phenomena Code:** 004

**Object Class:** Alpha-White

**Hazard Types:** Sapient Hazard

**Containment Protocols:** RPC-004-1 and RPC-004-2 are to be placed within adjacent storage lockers spaced 8 meters apart. In the event that either RPC-004-1 or RPC-004-2 are producing ink, ASF and janitorial personnel are to be alerted immediately.

**Description:** RPC-004 is a collection of anomalous books, two of which remain. RPC-004-1 is a red hardback notebook that contains writings from an unknown author; the writing itself, however, is not memetic. The text within RPC-004-1 describes anomalous events and criminal acts, the investigations of which are still ongoing. Evidence suggests that the author of RPC-004-1 possessed anomalous abilities, and used them to commit the aforementioned crimes.

RPC-004-2 is a blue hardback notebook with the name "Jenny" written on the cover. The writing inside of RPC-004-2 is noticeably more incoherent, but still lacks memetic properties. The name Jenny is believed to refer to Jenny R█████, a 2█ year old woman who disappeared on 10/20/18. The police case has since been closed due to the absence of evidence.

When RPC-004-2 is placed within 5 meters of RPC-004-1, they both begin to emit a large amount (approximately 4 liters) of ink[[footnote]]Tests have shown this fluid to be standard carbon black ink.[[/footnote]]. The fluid will begin to spill off the page until only a small amount is left in the form of words. These writings are only briefly visible however, as they dry and disappear after approximately 1 minute. In addition, the writings between the books appear to be connected, as if it were a conversation (See document RPC-004-D).

**Addendum RPC-004-B:** RPC-004-1 Excerpts

[[collapsible show="+ Open File" hide="- Close File"]]

**Foreword:** The following are poems taken from RPC-004-1, the dates preceding the poems appear to match with several missing person reports in the area Jenny R█████ disappeared.

[[div style="background-color:#CEBEA5; margin: 10px 2.5%; color: #333; font-family: 'Neucha', cursive; font-size: 1.65em; line-height: 1em; padding: 1px 10px; box-shadow: inset 0px 0px 7px #333; border-radius: 3px;"]]
09/12/17

Girl behind the counter,
of true love I am a doubter,
but you've caught my eye,
with your charms ever so shy,
madly I chase after you,
sadly I return, feeling blue,
but do net fret,
I need not catch you with a net,
with pen to paper,
I'm afraid I'll now never "see you later,"
now you're up on the shelf above,
added to the collection I so love.

[[div style="background-color:#CEBEA5; margin: 10px 2.5%; color: #333; font-family: 'Neucha', cursive; font-size: 1.65em; line-height: 1em; padding: 1px 10px; box-shadow: inset 0px 0px 7px #333; border-radius: 3px;"]]
9/27/17

Down down, onto the page,
where I release all my rage,
a poem, a look,
next a book,
and then it's done,
that's when I've won.

12/20/17

Immortality, it's what we crave,
we want it so badly, we'll change how we behave,
but with my gift, there's no need,
here's some words that you should heed,
there's only one way to have immortality,
accept my gift, lose the morality,
please, accept what I give,
up on my shelf you surely will live.
[[/div]]

3/15/18

pen going
ink flowing

tears flowing

5/11/18

I wonder what must it be like,
sitting up there all day,
watching from above as us mortals play,
thoughts buzzing to and fro,
causing the literature inside to grow,
the shelf must be a curious place,
especially with the newly added face.

10/20/18

The jig is up,
I'm about to throw up,
my collection burned,
all but two books were left unturned,
they're at the door,
they'll be in before four,
but do not worry,
even if I am in a hurry,
I will live on,
I won't be gone,
words are immortal,
and now I am too--

**Experiment Description:** RPC-004-1 and RPC-004-2 were placed in close proximity for 2 hours while researchers observed.

**Experiment Conducted By:** Dr. ███

**Time:** 9:22 PM
**Date:** 5/4/1█

@@@@
@@@@

[[span style="font-family: 'Neucha', cursive; font-size: 1.25em; line-height: 1.3em;"]]Ah, I see,
you're the only other in this world who is truly free,
how could one be so amiss,
in this, the most pure of bliss?[[/span]]

@@@@
@@@@

[[span style="font-family: 'Caveat', cursive; font-size: 1.35em; line-height: 1.2em;"]]you call it bliss?
a curse, that's what I call this,
a hatred unlike any other I've felt,
the feeling would make my skin welt,

instead I have leather, which I dread a lot.
[[/span]]

@@@@

[[span style="font-family: 'Neucha', cursive; font-size: 1.25em; line-height: 1.3em;"]]No, no, can't you see?

we're history that lives,
an opportunity that no one now gives,
humans only retell history, nothing more,
you've ascended from being a common whore,

done with only a mere rhyme,
and if you can't handle the price,

@@@@

[[span style="font-family: 'Caveat', cursive; font-size: 1.35em; line-height: 1.2em;"]]living? That must be a joke,

spending my days locked behind these pages,

what I would give to have my life back,
I'd happily lop off an arm or leg, things I already lack.
[[/span]]

@@@@
@@@@

[[span style="font-family: 'Neucha', cursive; font-size: 1.25em; line-height: 1.3em;"]]That's a shame,
you only have yourself to blame,
if your mind was more open,
perhaps you'd be less broken,
I've grown tired now,
although first I will take a bow,
we've put on quite a show,
for these strangers we do not know,
and with that I bid adieu,
I hope you seek to build yourself anew.
[[/span]]

[[span style="font-family: 'Caveat', cursive; font-size: 1.35em; line-height: 1.2em;"]]I hope you burn,

and while you're at it take me with you,
for me there is no building anew.
[[/span]]
