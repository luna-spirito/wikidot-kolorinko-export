Heya! So, you're probably wondering, what's this whole "Anomaly Data Room" thing about?
Well, if that's the case here's some info that might help answer your question:

+ General Site Info

Anomaly Data Room (ADR) is a **fictitious** community-driven data bank, and my personal collection, full of weird, strange, unexplainable, mysterious, obscure, [INSERT MORE WORDS HERE] things. And, of course, a way to creatively express yourself with little to no limitations.
The "weird things", which we call **Anomalies** can be anything from living entities to obscure concepts. Basically, anything that is considered anomalous is welcome here.

At ADR, our main focus is the anomaly itself, with studying and explaining it being our main goal. We want to see how creative a writer can get with not only their concepts but their whole presentation as well.

As the data room is relatively new, it's almost completely empty!
