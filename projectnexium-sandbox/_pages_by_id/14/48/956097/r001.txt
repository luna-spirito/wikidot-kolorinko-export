**Log-107:**
**The Serrated**
**Environment Class:**//Urban//, //Ruins//
**Hazard Class** //Physical//
**Hostility Class:**//Variable//
**Rarity:** //Level 2//

**Article:** A humanoid-type cryptid of varying heights[[footnote]] Shortest specimen found a 7 feet 2 inches and the tallest was found at 11 feet 7 inches. [[/footnote]], skin tones, builds, and other features, are most comparable to humans in terms of general complexion. Despite varying appearances, this cryptid is unified by the following traits: a usually hunched stance, a minor asymmetrical arm structure[[footnote]] Specimens have been found having one of their arms being 5-11% longer than their other arm. [[/footnote]], mottled skin, long digits compared to the size of the specimen's palms, and a mouth containing three rows of serrated teeth. 

**//Finding 1://**
**Internal Organs**

Despite having an organic external body, the cryptid contains a mechanical set of internal organs, with appear to have been externally inserted. Bones are made out of a mixture of organic calcium structures and cobalt-chromium alloy rod implements.
