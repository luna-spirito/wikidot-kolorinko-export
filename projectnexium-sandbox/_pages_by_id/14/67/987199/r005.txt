    <h4>Containment Procedures:</h4>
    <p>PNX-015 and 12 PNX-015-1 instances are to be held in an aviary designed to resemble the Susquehannock State Forest in Pennsylvania (50x50x100 meters). PNX-015-1 instances must not be viewed by any human via sight, photograph, or video recording. Any personnel who view a PNX-015-1 will be designated PNX-015-2 and must be terminated and incinerated immediately by security personnel.</p>

    <h4>Description:</h4>
    <p>PNX-015 is a sapient hivemind composed of American crows (Corvus brachyrhynchos). PNX-015 refers to itself as the hive’s Queen. PNX-015-1 instances are drones of the hive, and PNX-015-2 is any human in the transformation stages. PNX-015 and its drones communicate telepathically. PNX-015 is capable of speaking English, both directly and through PNX-015-1 instances.</p>

    <h4>Transformation Stages:</h4>

    <h4>Stage 1:</h4>
    <p>PNX-015-2 begins to grow small feathers on its body and hears faint, unintelligible voices from PNX-015-1s and the Queen. Subjects typically do not notice these changes. This stage occurs immediately after exposure.</p>

    <h4>Stage 2:</h4>
    <p>PNX-015-2's arms begin to morph into wings, more feathers grow, and the voices become louder and more understandable. Subjects often panic and attempt to remove feathers, which only accelerates growth. This stage occurs 10–30 minutes after exposure.</p>

    <h4>Stage 3:</h4>
    <p>PNX-015-2 grows a beak but retains the ability to speak. Their body becomes mostly covered in feathers, and their voices become louder. Subjects experience severe psychoPNXical distress as their humanity deteriorates. This stage occurs 30–60 minutes after exposure.</p>

    <h4>Final Stage:</h4>
    <p>PNX-015-2 screams in agony as bones shatter, organs rupture, and blood pours from the mouth. Beaks press against their skin from beneath the skin until it tears open. The body explodes, blood splatters, and organ bits spill, releasing twelve new PNX-015-1 instances covered in blood. Any observer will suffer the same fate. This stage occurs 90 minutes after exposure.</p>

      <h4>First Containment:</h4>
      <p>Many people have gone missing in the Susquehannock State Forest, Pennsylvania. Search teams recovered personal belongings and mutilated, crow-like human remains. All non-Nexuim witnesses were administered amnestics. Two K-Class personnel were sent into the forest equipped with video and audio recorders, radios, and survival supplies.</p>

      <h4>K-Class Personnel:</h4>
      <p>K-4827: 24-year-old male</p>
      <p>K-7293: 22-year-old male</p>
