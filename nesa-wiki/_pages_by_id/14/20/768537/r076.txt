**Anti-NESAs**
[[collapsible show="Info" hide=" Close "]]

[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

**Threat levels:** (unavailable)

**Locations:** Scattered across the Alpha Multiverse.

**Description**

Anti-NESAs are versions of NESA that are so different that they aren't exactly NESAs, some variants don't pose a threat as their underdeveloped and others, despite having differences, cooperate with us. However, there are variants who seem to branch off in ideologues, either taking over their entire universe as a cult, humans conquering and enslaving other races through war, or being a corporation and using anomalies as products and in the most simplest terms, buying the human race, and other races and planets. NESA itself has a balanced view, allowing us to spread among creation.

Anti-NESAs like these always lean more into Warrior/Soldier, Merchant, and Priest/Thinkers. But the fourth group, the Authority/Bureaucrat, are the most dangerous as not only will these variants seek to maintain complete control over their universe, but will seek similar variants to join forces and undermine NESA.

**NESA operations**

All NESA variants are monitored by the ATRD[[footnote]]Alternate Timeline Research Division[[/footnote]] in conjunction with the DRD. Hostile Anti-NESAs are also dealt with through espionage and sabotage.

**Arachnids**
[[collapsible show="Info" hide=" Close "]]

[[image Ara.jpeg style="max-width: 250px; margin-top: 12px;"]]

[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

**Threat levels:** A

**Locations:** their deity also called Queen of the Arachnids is a large scale space station with its size being unknown, the location of this station however is discovered to be in Universal Cornets-[Redacted].

**Description**

Arachnids are a very dangerous anomalous group of Robotic entities that wishes to destroy the omniverse and rule a single universe under their control, they’re deity which can be described as an omnipresent super computer simultaneously existing in the majority of space, universes, multiverses and realm’s.

The Arachnids as an organization is very organized and quite large, their military might is possible the strongest in the known Omniverse and are capable of Multiversal travel.

Some members of the Arachnids doesn’t share the same goals as the majority so they are considered outcast and are permanently stuck on the color red/FF0000 as a form of shame or “glitches” as they were called.

**NESA operations**

Red Arachnids are considered part of a group called Zeta-99, an SSD that composes of only Arachnids and act as the first line of defense against Arachnidien attacks.

**Asian Union**
[[collapsible show="Info" hide=" Close "]]

[[image AU.jpeg style="max-width: 250px; margin-top: 12px;"]]

[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

**Threat level:** C

**Locations:** The Independent Republic of Hong Kong.

**Description**

The Asian Union is a branch The United Eurasia, locally active in the Chinese coast, Japan, Korea, Hong Kong and Taiwan Regions. There are many Union branches of Eurasia, but what sets The AU apart is their pursuit in advancing technology.

This pursuit is considered problematic as they consistently work on anomalous projects, or will even create anomalies to advance the region. Hong Kong is the most technologically advanced city on Earth; but is also riddled with anomalous tech, including magic, that threatens the civilian population and threatens secrecy as well.

**Operations**

The region of Hong Kong is not under NESA jurisdiction as permitted by the Asian Union, however this doesn’t prevent NESA from being in the region. If a situation arises where The AU and Hong Kong as a whole is in danger from the Anomalous, NESA will have to work without interference from The AU.

**Nano-Farms Inc**
[[collapsible show="Info" hide=" Close "]]

[[image Nano.jpeg style="max-width: 250px; margin-top: 12px;"]]

[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

**Threat levels:** D

**Locations:** Sizu Island, Victoria City, Tanzania, Ethiopia, Earth.

**Description**

Nano-Farms Inc is one of the largest corporations on Earth with their headquarters located on Sizu Island [[footnote]]The island is quite small compared to the other 2 but the facility that the company operates in is made up of 3 mega towers interconnected by platforms of multiple parks representing each biom on earth.[[/footnote]] between the islands of Ukerewe and Ukora, both with very dense populations of civilians and wildlife. [[footnote]]The metropolitan area of Victoria City spreads to the 3 major islands as well as the surrounding islands and the main land, VC is what you’ll call a utopia of 50 million inhabitants of both citizens and animals.[[/footnote]]

Nano-Farms Inc is normally non-anomalous but are on the list as curtain department’s of the company turned to anomalies and entities to maximize production or produce anomalous foods and medicines that could have negative effects on the general public.

The company also works with the Ethiopian government and anyone that they can profit off of, NESA is neutral towards them as they provide food for sites on Earth but secrecy to them is in our best interest.

**NESA operations**

Even though the Ethiopian government has become corrupt in favor of Nano-Farms Inc, some officials who wish to work in the public’s interest are always kept in touch with NESA which makes it easier to respond to anomalies in Africa and in return provide protection.

Nano-Farms Inc will continue to work in the public eye as long as their anomalous activity doesn’t become a threat to secrecy.

The Oasis Refuge for Creatures Abroad is a group dedicated to freeing those enslaved by the Imperium, O.R.C.A is small but shouldn’t be underestimated due to the number of spies they have across Multiverse-3.

[[tab Killer Whale Imperium]]

[[image KW.jpeg style="max-width: 250px; margin-top: 12px;"]]

**Threat levels:** A

**Locations:** Multiverse-3

**Description**

The Imperium is a large scale empire that rules over the majority of Multiverse-3 using advanced technologies and magic to hold and spread this power, the Killer Whales which is the species that run the Imperium live on their own planet called Orcinus is the Capital and government officials seems to live in luxury, while civilian Killer Whales live comfortably though less.

As for non-Killer Whales they are considered slaves to the Imperium and those who goes against them are punished or executed, the group known as O.R.C.A is consistently at odds against the Imperium and have freed billions from slavery on the daily.

Unlike O.R.C.A the Killer Whales Imperium is actively seeking anomalous artifacts and utilizes them in their weapons programs as well as other branches of their Empire.

**NESA operations**

The Imperium is fully aware of NESA and is aggressive against us for various reasons, the Imperium has even attack NESA space station’s and stolen a number of anomalies for their weapon projects.

The Imperium knows that a multiversal war will destroy both of our organizations so they try their best to ease tensions between us, the Imperium is not interested in leaving their multiverse but is to be monitored at all times.

**Scaly™ Corporation**
[[collapsible show="Info" hide=" Close "]]

[[image SyCorp.png style="max-width: 250px; margin-top: 12px;"]]

[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

**Threat Level:** B

**Locations:** Flaming Bay, Drago; New York City, Earth; District 65, Kepler.

**Description**

Scaly™ is a Video Game Development and Media company created by 3 individuals, Obsidian, Donna and Jayce, with the intent to create faster and more reliable, internet connections between planets. Originally they were non anomalous until they invented A-033, which is only used for those working in the organization, Scaly™ Corp Headquarters is on Drago with two other offices on Earth and Kepler. Allowing them to run the game, GalaxyChat™ Where users from the three planets, and those neighboring them, can enter into VR through a Headset, Brain Link, or Holographic Platform, and chat with other users at far distances. A-033 also allows this.

**NESA Operations**

[[tabview]]
[[tab Obsidian]]
Hatched in 2929, Obsidian was a rich dragon along with the rest of his family, he founded the Scaly™ Corporation in 2975 first as a Media enterprise, operating several Social Media apps. As of now, Obsidian is operating in the shadows, though it is unclear where he is, with people associated with Scaly™ anomalous projects suggest that he put himself into GalaxyChat™ to attempt immortality.
[[/tab]]
[[tab Donna]]
Born in 2990, Donna is a human who runs the Earth branch in New York City, SSD’s has searched again and again to find the office and those working there, with those employed having access to the anomalous location. And despite how well known by everyone arrested by NESA, Donna can not be tracked down.
[[/tab]]
[[tab Jayce]]
Hatched in 2987, Jayce is a Plateosaurus who runs the Kepler branch in District 65, like Donna, Jayce can not be tracked down, with the last sighting of him being an image of him going into the Rax Building. The Kepler branch office is located between floors 131 to 150, with those working there mostly civilians. However, there is an anomalous location that appears to exist within these floors where Jayce is believed to be working in. Anyone from the PBD, NESA, and even TPAS, who successfully infiltrated the location has all since been missing in action.

**The Three Planetary Alignment System (TPAS)**
[[collapsible show="Info" hide=" Close "]]

[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

**Threat levels:** C

**Locations:** Intergalactic

**Description**

The Three Planetary Alignment System is an organization treaty made between 3 of the most powerful civilizations in the known universe, this organization is actively seeking out anomalies with the intent of either exploiting or to destroy them.

However they are aware of NESA and have on many occasions battle against us or conflicted with our SSD’s on missions, but they do understand that we like them have similar goals.

TPAS’s goals are to protect all life in the universe by all means necessary, as well as terraforming uninhabitable planets into beautiful environments for colonialism.

**NESA operations**

[[tabview]]
[[tab Capital Planet]]
The Capital Planet is against NESA but will work with us to protect civilians from dangerous anomalies, however TPAS wherever they may be, should be left to their affairs.
[[/tab]]

Even though the Bureau puts the lives of Dinosaurians first above all others.
[[/tab]]
