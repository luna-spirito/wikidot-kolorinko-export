The Dimensional Research Division (DRD) has operations across the Omniverse, the DRD has seen and learn of other Multiverse’s with their own organization, however we are in no position allowed to intervene with them in any way, this is for both our safety and their’s.

The mission of the DRD and NESA as a whole is to Explore, Analyze, Document and Preserve, so in turn keep their matters to them unless there’s a reason to get involved.

All missions that may take place with in other Multiverse organizations must be approved by 2 Level 1 Members.

The BPF is an organization that operates across their Multiverse and even have on occasion advanced into other Multiverses, they discovered just like us that the Omniverse as a whole is a fiction and has set a goal to keep all life forms of ever learning about this.

However despite similar goals the BPF had showed hostility towards NESA personnel so infiltration will not be possible, DRD-4 are to instead observe the BPF effects in their Multiverse from a distance and to report of any BPF operations outside their Multiverse.

Protocol’s have been put in place in order to prevent a war between our Multiverse’s if the BPF were to become fully aware of our existence.

The Federation is a human polity founded in 2054 by the remnants of the leaders of Europe after a nuclear war between the second Soviet Union and a highly aggressive America. Around 500 years later, the Federation is the largest and most powerful nation in the Milky Way. They try to provide an equal nation with plenty of rights for every citizen, even artificial intelligence. They also are trying to contain the Scourge. The federation is also at war with a hostile human empire called the Corpus Alliance.

NESA at the moment are allies with the Federation since we both have similar goals and technologies, there is no reason to infiltrate the Federation for there is an agreement on open communication. DRD-5 has established a permanent space station located in several hundred light years in deep space/outside the Milky Way Galaxy, it has also been agreed upon that the Federation will help keep DRD-5’s secrecy among the civil populations.

Unlike most DRD’s, DRD-8 has managed to become great allies with La Organization. La Organization is a group that protects both humans and anomalies, with the philosophy that both humans and anomalies have the same right to exist; But like the PBD,[[footnote]]The Planetary Bureau of Defense puts Dinosaurian lives first.[[/footnote]] La Organization puts humanity first.

However, what makes La Organization unique is Project PIAM. Project PIAM seeks to normalize Anomalies into the human population, this is the reason why DRD-8 and NESA as a whole are allies with La Organization.

DRD-8 also works in conjunction with the Dimensional Control Department (DCD) and the Dimensional Exploration Division (DED), with the hopes that Project PIAM will allow NESA to operate in their universes more easily over time.

The Void Observation Group is an organization whose goal is to rid their Multiverse of the Void, an entity capable of destroying entire universe’s, the VOG are aware of our existence and wishes to be allies with us because of our numbers combined would do better against the Void.

However the Void seems to be happy in its own Multiverse but has on occasion consumed universes outside of it, because of this threat NESA operations in 66 are to make sure that it is happy and remains in that Multiverse.

DRD-66 main goal is to come up with effective methods of predicting the Void’s next target as well as evacuation measures in as many universes as possible, DRD-66 base of operations are located universal code (66-237810) where information about the Void and the Multiverse as a whole is collected and is where refugees are put into the relocation program.

but if this universe were to be targeted emergency measures are to evacuate refugees to the Beta universe and all information must be uploaded to the Master Database, after which a new base of operations in 66 will be established. 

**Multiverse - 99**

[[image UIABblueflag.png style="max-width: 150px; margin-top: 12px;"]]

The UIAB is an empire and organization dedicated to the destruction of Normalcy and the Human Race, It is advice that humans **SHOULD NOT** come to any universe within the Multiverse 99 cluster. It should also be noted that the Killer Whales Imperium is allies with the UIAB, with the UIAB giving the Imperium resources and anomalies, and the Imperium giving weapons that served its purpose to the UIAB. It is unclear what relation O.R.C.A. has with UIAB, or if either groups are even aware of each other.

As for the Multiverse itself, it is one of the oldest discovered by NESA, with some universes estimated to be trillions of trillions of years old, but it is also the most chaotic Multiverse, similarly to Multiverse 65 with its Backrooms, with all Civilizations dead and the anomalous running rampant. 

The UIAB is also aware of us and hate us, making them extremely dangerous and hostile, non-human personnel should be armed and should expect to die when entering this Multiverse, always blending in, exploration and observation is not possible either. missions in this Multiverse must be discussed and unanimously approved by the Level 1 Members.

The DRD is not the only Division in NESA to travel between Alpha Universe’s, also crossover tales must be greenlighted both here and in the wiki of your choice.

You can make 2 articles each about the same story or phenomena, 1 here and 1 there or you could just make a single article here that references the other wiki, references of NESA in other wikis however only need to be greenlighted in that wiki.

If you want to make a article about 2 or more wiki’s, that is possible but is more likely to be denied.

The DRD can also have other Divisions with them if you want to include other Divisions that corresponds with the anomaly appropriately.

If there’s a wiki that isn’t on this list that you find go to the forum’s, to per page discussion and to the DRD thread, the wiki can be confi, liminal or similar genres, and be purely fictional, and a link (if possible) to the main or home page of the wiki should be provided.

Also check out the Containment Fiction wiki for a more updated list, this page wouldn’t be possible without them.
