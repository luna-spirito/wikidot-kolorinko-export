[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

The Technological Research Division is devoted to the study of Anomalous Tech as well as the developing effective containment protocol’s, making use of Anomalous Tech and separating it from Civilian Tech.

The TechRD has the hardest job yet as determining what is Anomalous and Civil can be a very gray area, so it is up to each individual universe to determine that for themselves.

Of course some forms of Tech are considered too Anomalous for a single universe to make available to the public, so this is here as a means to determine which is which.
