**Light Matter**

Light Matter is Matter that can be see with the naked eye, this would include the periodic table, Light Matter gets its name due to the fact that light waves reflect off of them creating color.

**Dark Matter**

Dark Matter is similar to Light except that light waves can’t reflect off of them, thereby making them non-existent, the reason why light can’t reflect off of Dark Matter is because they are a lot smaller than regular Atoms or Light Matter, because of their size going through a Dark Matter cluster can’t result in a collision.

What makes these Anomalous in nature is that they can still carry electricity, despite being undetected, Anomalous Entities seems to like using them for their abilities, almost like a portable battery, it should be noted that it makes these entities capable of mind control, teleportation, creating radiation, shape shifting and more.

It should also be noted that Dark Matter can be used as pocket dimensions, warping of space time, and simple straight up reality altering events when combined with Anti-matter and Light Matter.

**Dark Matter 0**

Dark Matter 0[[footnote]]For the purposes of secrecy, personnel are to refer to DM-0 as just Dark Matter and refer to other Dark Matter types DMs.[[/footnote]] is a type of Dark Matter that is the most widespread and least anomalous of the spectrum, DM-0 is used by the civilian population for purposes such as hyper engines and other highly advanced technologies.

Even though DM-0 is technically anomalous by nature, it has become so used and well known that it became no more anomalous than uranium-233, uranium-235 and plutonium-239.
