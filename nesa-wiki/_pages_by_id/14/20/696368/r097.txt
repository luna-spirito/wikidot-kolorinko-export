**Blitzes - Blitz**
[[collapsible show="Info" hide=" Close "]]

[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

**Appearance**

Blitzes are an insectoid species with 4 legs, 2 mandibles, 2 bulbous compound eyes and a beak like snout; their body is in 3 parts with the triangular shape head, the elongated thorax and an abdomen.

They can also have a par of wings depending on type and their colors can very from green, opal, blue, purple, pink and bronze.

**Government**

Blitzes live in a hierarchy society where there is no official government nor a police force; despite their lack of government, Blitzes become more self efficient and are effectively trying to better their own lives rather than others.

But as good as this is, mob mentality is a common thing to happen, especially with misinformation which is something that is completely frowned upon in the species. As for laws they are enforce by these mobs depending on where you are on world and there are kinda unspoken rules such for example stealing and murder.

**History/Culture**

Blitzes used to have major governments similar to that of earth, but at some point in their history blitzes in the masses realize that all of their government’s were useless constantly fighting in war and constantly trying to keep the majority of the population poor in order to control them.

Eventually multiple rebellions formed in every country and these rebellions went to war against the very government’s that was keeping them down, the blitzes completely over through these government’s and soon the rebels started their own country. However these countries didn’t last and over the course of several generations, all governments were destroyed and patch’s of civilization continued in towns and a few large cities.

Their main business is in mechanical engineering and even though there’s no currency, they will take food and spare parts as payment.

**NESA operations**

Area 21: due to how much they hate misinformation Area 21 is located in the most isolated place in Blitz, the reason why Area 21 is here despite the danger of mob mentality is because Blitzes are considered some of the best mechanics in the universe so Area 21 is mainly to repair ships and test the advancement of hyper engines.

**Chlorophyllians - Verdantia**

[[collapsible show="Info" hide=" Close "]]

[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

**Appearance**

Chlorophyllians are sentient plant-based beings that have evolved on the lush planet of Verdantia. They stand at an average height of 6 feet, with a slender, stalk-like body structure. Their skin is a vibrant green, filled with chlorophyll, which allows them to photosynthesize. They have leaf-like appendages that serve as arms and legs, and their heads are adorned with petal-like structures that change color based on their emotional state. Their eyes are luminescent, capable of seeing a wider spectrum of light than most species.

**Government**

The Chlorophyllians operate under a form of government known as Arborocracy. In this system, the oldest and wisest Chlorophyllians, known as the Elder Trees, make decisions for the community. These elders have lived for centuries, their wisdom and knowledge growing with each passing year. The Elder Trees are deeply respected and their decisions are rarely questioned, as they have the best interests of Verdantia and its inhabitants at heart.

**History/Culture**

Chlorophyllians have a rich history rooted in harmony with nature. They believe in the interconnectedness of all life and strive to maintain balance in their ecosystem. Their culture is centered around growth, both personal and communal. They value knowledge, wisdom, and the pursuit of enlightenment.
Their history is marked by periods of peaceful coexistence, with occasional conflicts arising over resources or territorial disputes. However, these conflicts are usually resolved through peaceful negotiation rather than warfare. The Chlorophyllians have a deep respect for life and abhor violence.

**Crawler - Senkkon**

[[collapsible show="Info" hide=" Close "]]

[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

**Appearance**

Crawlers are an isopod species that have a minimum of 9 segments with additional segments with age, each segment of their body has 4 limbs with 2 toes at the end for grabbing objects. Their exoskeleton can range from rusty to, blonde, or dark brown. And have 2 eye stalks and 3 antennas.

**Government**

(unavailable)

**History/Culture**

Crawlers are one of the most unique races in the universe, as they have a drive to protect their entire race, having no concept of individuality. Even though they do do things for themselves in terms of recreation, all activities involving necessities are viewed as how to help continue their species.

An example of this is their intimacy, where most races view it as something special and exciting, Crawlers view it as nothing more than a contribution to Senkkon, having no romantic feelings for each other, with their only concern for physical health of the female and the eggs she'll lay. This also makes it where Crawlers normally don't know their parents or offspring.

This and their lack of emotions of any type, makes the coldest species. But at the same time, the most reliable, trustworthy, and efficient species in the universe.

**NESA operations**

Even though there's no official government on Senkkon, there is a network of Vaults operated by NESA, allowing quick response time to anomalous activity on the world.

**Pathers - Phila**

[[collapsible show="Info" hide=" Close "]]

[[div style="background-color: #D7D7D7; border:solid 1px #666666; margin: 0.5rem 0; padding: 14px 24px; "]]

**Appearance**

Pathers are Macropus with little to no fur, a long, horizontal flat tail, and elongated hands and feet, as well as a long head with forward-facing eyes, massive muscular jaws with serrated teeth and they're the only intelligent species that jumps instead of walking. They also have a frill that is used for hearing, with folded being closed proximity and fully extended being able to hear a conversation from far away.[[footnote]]Pathers are also well versed in hearing a wider array of frequencies, which is utilized for non-corporal entities or invisible phenomena by both 9's and personnel.[[/footnote]]

**Government**

The Phila government revolves around the few cities that exist, specifically the space ports, because there are so few places to land a ship, these space ports started creating laws to allow people to get on and off world, as well as having to create a currency to be used by those visiting the planet, are on world transportation, with airplanes used for busy routs and buses for more on demand routs. 

**History/Culture**

Pathers is a Culture defined by local communities, in fact, their planet has the fewest cities ever, while simultaneously having the most towns and villages then any world discovered, this is because the planet has seasonal droughts that would cause bodies of waters to dry up and then rain somewhere else, this cycle has in a sense spread the population out evenly until most of the planet was occupied by rural areas. This way of urban planning also created a unique situation where towns adapt to nature instead of building over it. It also made it so that Pathers have multiple cultures.

**NESA operations**

Because of a Pather's frill, which is sensitive to sound vibrations and electromagnetic fields, Phila has the highest number of religious groups per capita than any other planet. Though most are non anomalous like any other civilization, this high volume unfortunately translates to anomalous cults, which is made worse by how rural they are and how many towns and villages are on world. At least the lack of major populations makes it easier to keep the veil of secrecy.

Stars have five arms that radiate from a central body giving them the shape of a star polygon, these arms are used to both grab and walk, with near perfect flexibility and grip, one side have plates that act as eyes covering the entire side, giving them 360-degree vision, while the other side have spines facing towards its orifice.

Most history on Luminor has either be destroyed or replaced in such a way that it's impossible to know the true history without using anomalous means. However, what we do know is that Star's aren't isolated as they have advanced enough for space travel, but are still quite behind other races, this led to the creation of independent states like the Ocean Pyramid in an attempt to bring tourism and in turn show the galaxy that they aren't technologically behind.

On the planet is a government agency call Project 5, which is aware of the anomalous but lacks the tech necessary to deal with them. Because of this, NESA has infiltrated Project 5 and use them to contain anomalies on Luminor.
