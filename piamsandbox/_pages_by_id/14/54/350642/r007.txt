++ 》__Safety and management parameters__:
An.C-3_001.B shall remain confined to its containment enclosure within the facilities of the //Alpha-007.B// base, at the request of the agency and based on its good behavior, its enclosure is a replica of its natural environment, following the instructions of an anomalous wildlife enclosure, including its management and maintenance.
Feeding schedules have been applied following a diet that is in accordance with An.C-3_001.B; and requests given by the subject should be reviewed to know if they will be authorized or denied.

Interviews and communications with the agency are made daily, access to their enclosure is allowed. Learning and teaching sections will be given with the organism by an assigned tutor/teacher.

Outings to the para-organic interaction yards are conducted twice a week. An.C-3_001.B may interact freely with the other anomalies.
