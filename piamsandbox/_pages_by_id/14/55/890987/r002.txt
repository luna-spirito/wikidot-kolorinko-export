I can't take it anymore. Make her go away. Please.
That woman... No. That thing has been at my window for hours, still, silent... expressionless.
How long has this demon been there?
I woke up and it was there, with its pale face, that empty stare, watching me.  It has done nothing but watch me, watch me. It's hunting me.
This specter... it chose me as its victim, why? Why me? Shit!

I want to run, I want to escape, but I won't stand a chance. I know she'll catch me, she'll do something and kill me sooner than expected. And even if I want to run away in search of the slightest hope of surviving and seeing the light of dawn once more, I can't, my legs... they don't respond.
I'm just... I'm sunk in my bed, burying my face in between my knees, in a helpless fetal position. My gaze wanders and meets he. There is no trace of emotion on this petrified face, but I am sure he is smiling inwardly at seeing me in this state.

Why did this horrifying yōkai have to appear in my district? Sure, she was always to blame for all those missing cats... she eats them, when a kitten appeared it was torn up and half-chewed. She doesn't seem to possess any trace of her former humanity... if she ever did.
Mujōkao, leave me alone, go away! Please...

Don't you get tired of this? Just watching, closely. Damn you, how the fuck are you standing at my window? My apartment is on the fourth floor of the building, and yet... you're so calm, peaceful and unmovable, like... like an angel.

At this point, I don't know what's worse?
You being this calm or you moving again.
Fuck, I almost had a fucking heart attack when you started moving... slamming your face against the window, over and over again, in a loop.
A loop of such insistent, desperate pounding, and yet without a wrinkle or expression on your face. So peaceful, calm, it made the pounding sound like a lullaby to sleep, especially to me; it was terrifying. Always looking me straight in the eyes, without damaging your makeup.

So quiet, since you showed up it seems like all the other noises in the world have disappeared. I don't even hear my own breathing.
You're quiet again, Mujōkao.
You have told me so much with your gaze and that non-existent expression. There is no tomorrow for me anymore, is there?
I want to laugh, I want to cry, but I can't, I'm just a mass of trembling.

You open my window, ready to end this.
And yet, the closer you get the silence itself, even more strident than before, seems to be drowned even more in that abyss of nothingness.

But... no, you have spoken.
You were talking to me from the beginning, weren't you, Mujōkao?
Your voice... it's beautiful, silent... metallic, inhuman.
You have been repeating the same thing since you manifested yourself, speaking so low, something less than a simple murmur or a whisper; a sound so nonexistent that not even your lips pick up that they should move.

I hear it too, it becomes clearer as you come closer to my bed. I hear it clearly. It's not worth praying for my salvation, what difference does it make when it's you?
You're right, I'm sure you are, but I don't want to think about it....

Standing in front of me, I hear you, Mujōkao.
Repeating, announcing, preaching the truth of your self-proclaiming words....

❝//**{{I... am... God.}}**//❞
