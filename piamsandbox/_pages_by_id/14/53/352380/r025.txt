> ------
> [[size 1.5em]]》Containment:[[/size]]
>  An.BI-002 is confined to a standard containment enclosure for sapient humanoid organisms. This enclosure has been furnished with basic and necessary implements and amenities. Due to the explicitly human nature of An.BI-002, treatment of An.BI-002 should be normal with respect to maintenance schedules and procedures.
> ------
> [[size 1.5em]]》Description:[[/size]]
> An.BI-002 corresponds to a U.S. female, approximately 25 years of age, of slim build and no notable physical abnormalities. An.BI-002 goes by the civilian name //Jenniffer Thompson//.
> An.BI-002's anomalous ability is a degree of logical alteration to make a change to reality and 'erase' itself from existence, modifying reality to eliminate all events/information related to it, following a timeline where it never existed. An.BI-002 is able to rematerialize, rewriting reality to its previous version where her person does exist. In An.BI-002's own words, when it 'removes' itself from the base reality, it rematerializes in an empty black space where no time seems to pass until it materializes again. This site described by An.BI-002 has been impossible to detect and seems to be indifferent to changes and logical alterations of high-level reality.
> An.BI-002 explains being from a non-existent city and state of the United States, explaining several irregularities of historical events and locations. It is theorized that in one of the interactions where An.BI-002 eliminated itself from reality, our reality suffered a cataclysmic event of the extinction of reality and a subsequent rewrite and reboot of it, eliminating what An.BI-002 explains and modifying it to be our current version. An.BI-002 did not seem to be affected by this alteration of reality due to its presence in that empty space.
> ------
> [[size 1.5em]]》Discovery and capture:[[/size]]
> An.BI-002 walked to the site of a space-time anomaly classified by The Organization as An.G-███. The subject was immediately detained and upon interrogation, reported that the site was the //__Alpha-001.A__// base and inquired about what happened to him, asking to speak with several friends and acquaintances of theirs who were members of the //Table of 11//. Subsequent investigations determined his anomalous history and abilities and he was successfully contained. Several of the events are unknown, such as why in this version of reality An.BI-002 was never contained, the relocation of the base in question and why the change made her acquaintances, who are now members of the high command of //The Table//.
